1. Offline Vector Database & Encryption

Hayagriva Law Vaults (.vlt) are compiled binary packages containing domain-specific vector embeddings and statutory legislation indexed for high-speed offline retrieval.

  • AES-256 GCM Cryptographic Container: Protects proprietary precedent databases.
  • HNSW Vector Indexing: Allows instantaneous semantic similarity matching across 500,000+ judgments.
