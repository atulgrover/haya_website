1. Multi-Agent Legal Reasoning Architecture

Hayagriva utilizes a multi-tiered agentic orchestration pattern combining specialized neural models (HayaParam 7B / 14B) with deterministic validation rules.

  • Cognitive Router Agent: Analyzes raw legal briefs and routes tasks to domain-specific subagents.
  • Statutory Compliance Agent: Validates extracted facts against statutory provisions (IBC 2016, Companies Act 2013).
  • Verification Agent: Ensures zero hallucination via double-check assertions against encrypted Law Vaults (.vlt).
