Data Privacy & Sovereignty

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 14, 2026  |  Domain: https://hayagriva.app  |  Platform: HAYAGRIVA IPE & Skillpedia
🔒 Core Privacy Guarantee: HAYAGRIVA is architected around a Local-First & Zero Cloud Data Transmission principle. All confidential case files, company domain records, financial ledgers, and local AI agent inferences execute strictly on your local device.

1. Introduction & Scope

HAYAGRIVA (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the HAYAGRIVA Universal Integrated Professional Environment (IPE) Platform, the NSQF Skillpedia learning hub, and the web portal hosted at https://hayagriva.app.

This Privacy Policy explains what data is processed when you access our web portal, download desktop binaries, use our NSQF micro-learning video reels, or purchase software licenses and marketplace intelligence reports.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide authentication, licensing, and access to educational materials:

  • Account & Profile Information: When you register on the portal, we store your full name, email address, password hash (encrypted using salt-hashed bcrypt), role persona (Student, Employee, or Professional), and optional firm/organization name.
  • Authentication & Session Data: We issue secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored locally in your browser’s localStorage to maintain authenticated sessions.
  • Licensing & Transaction Details: For licensed professionals, we store cryptographic license keys, purchase tiers, transaction identifiers, and expiration dates.
  • Marketplace Report Orders: When ordering valuation or intelligence reports, we collect order specifications (company name, report parameters, notes) to fulfill the requested analysis.

3. Local-First & Zero-Cloud Data Sovereignty

For users of the HAYAGRIVA Desktop IPE (Insolvency Professionals, Legal Counsel, and Enterprise Teams):

  • No Cloud Document Uploads: Case papers, valuation sheets, Section 29A verification files, and corporate debtor records processed in the Desktop IPE are stored exclusively in your local encrypted vaults (.vlt) and local SQLite instances.
  • Local AI Inferences: Multi-agent audits and LLM workflows run via local quantized models (GGUF / local inference runtimes) without transmitting sensitive prompts or documents over the public internet.

4. Third-Party Integrations & YouTube API Services

HAYAGRIVA Skillpedia integrates with YouTube API Services to provide relevant, verified vocational training demonstrations mapped to the National Skills Qualification Framework (NSQF).

5. Payments & Financial Security

Online transactions and subscription payments are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways (Razorpay). HAYAGRIVA does not store or process raw credit card numbers, CVVs, or banking PINs. All payment processing is securely tokenized through the payment gateway.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use standard browser local storage (localStorage) strictly for functional session management (JWT token persistence, active tab state, and theme preferences). We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. Data Security & Compliance

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures:

  • All web traffic is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS (256-bit).
  • Database backups are encrypted at rest.
  • We adhere to data protection principles set out under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India) and global privacy standards.
