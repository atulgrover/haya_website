### NSQF Data Pipeline & 3-JSON Master Lake Invariants
- **PDF → Markdown (`nsqf_pdf_to_md.py`) is Lossless & Complete**: 
  - It is **100% expected, intentional, and correct** for Markdown (`data/md/*.md`) to contain duplicate Performance Criteria (`PC1`, `PC2`, etc.) — once as bullet text in the syllabus/narrative section, and again as rows in the Assessment Criteria Table.
  - **Rule**: NEVER attempt to deduplicate or strip out the narrative PCs from the Markdown files or the Python converter. The `.md` file is the raw digital mirror of the complete PDF.
- **The 3-JSON Master Lake Architecture (`data/json/`)**:
  - `data/json/nsqf/${cleanCode}.json`: Canonical Assessment AST compiled from Markdown (`nsqf_md_to_json.js`).
  - `data/json/sop/${cleanCode}.json`: 10-Chapter Industrial SOP Master Dossier generated by `nsqf_sop_synthesizer.js` & enriched by `nsqf_enrichment_suiter.js`.
  - `data/json/msme/${cleanCode}.json`: 9-Chapter Bankable DPR & Machinery Tool BOM Master generated by `nsqf_msme_synthesizer.js` & enriched by `nsqf_enrichment_suiter.js`.
- **Interactive Micro-Learning & Assessment Invariants**:
  - `video_clip`: Timestamp bounds (`start_seconds`, `end_seconds`, `clip_duration_seconds`, `embed_url`) for 60-90s auto-bounded micro-reels.
  - `viva_quiz`: 3 bilingual (EN + HI) MCQs per unit with explanations, options, difficulty ratings.
  - `study_takeaways`: Technical pro-tips, common mistakes to avoid, and statutory safety rules.
  - Database support in `server/db.js`: `nsqf_pcs` has `start_seconds`, `end_seconds`, `viva_quiz_json`, `study_takeaways_json` columns; `nsqf_modules` and `nsqf_nos` use native JSONB trees.
