## Master Architecture & Overview

The **HPTI Compendium** is a multi-volume treatise establishing a sovereign, rigorous, and culturally grounded psychometric paradigm. Unlike Western psychometric models (such as MBTI, Big Five, and Enneagram) which view personality as a flat, static bundle of descriptive traits, **HPTI** approaches human individuality as a **multi-dimensional energetic state (*Prakriti*)** capable of systematic evolution (*Sadhana*) toward supreme clarity (*Sattva*) and ultimate purpose (*Swadharma*).

This compendium unites:
1. **Classical Metaphysics**: The *24 Tattvas* of Sāmkhya, the *Pancha Koshas* of the Taittiriya Upanishad, and the *Antahkarana Chatushtaya*.
2. **Dynamic Psychometrics**: The *Triguna* mechanics, Sri Krishna's 6-Factor psychometric analysis in *Bhagavad Gita Chapter 18*, and the *16 Manasa Prakritis* of the *Charaka Samhita*.
3. **The Proprietary ECCP Matrix**: A 4-dimensional taxonomy ($3 \times 3 \times 4 \times 4 = 144$ permutations) integrating **[E] Energy**, **[C] Cognition**, **[C] Competency**, and **[P] Purpose**.
4. **Living Epic Archetypes**: Concrete, relatable narrative mirrors from the *Ramayana* and *Mahabharata*.
5. **Modern Economic Reality**: Direct bridges to India’s **2,002 certified NCVET & NSQF National Qualification Packs (QPs)** and global vocational taxonomies (O*NET, ESCO).
6. **Sadhana & Leadership Protocols**: Actionable guidance for transcending burnout, mastering work, and cultivating high-trust Dharmic organizations.
