## 1. Introduction: The Universal Applicability of Vedic Ontology

A fundamental premise of the Sanatani tradition is that cosmic law (*Sanatana Dharma*) is not a localized, sectarian creed; it is **universal reality (*Apaurusheya*)**. 

The biological humors (*Tridosha*), the energetic forces of consciousness (*Triguna*), the cognitive functions of the inner mind (*Antahkarana*), and the four natural vocational competencies (*Varna-Swabhava*) operate in every human nervous system, whether an individual is born in Varanasi, Tokyo, London, or San Francisco.

While Chapter 18 and Chapter 19 established the direct interface between HPTI and India's sovereign national infrastructure (**NSQF / NCVET**), modern global professionals operate in an interconnected worldwide labor market.

In this chapter, we construct the **International Crosswalk**: mapping the HPTI ECCP matrix directly to the world's two largest international vocational classification standards:
1. **O*NET (Occupational Information Network)**: The official occupational taxonomy of the **United States Department of Labor (USDOL)**.
2. **ESCO (European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations)**: The multi-lingual taxonomy of the **European Commission (EU)**.
3. **The Holland RIASEC Crosswalk**: A comparative bridge to John Holland's hexagonal vocational model.
