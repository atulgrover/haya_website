## 2. Token Breakdown: The Four Dimensions

### Dimension 1: `[E]` Energy (Triguna Dynamic)
Measures the baseline qualitative frequency of the individual's mind-body battery:
- **`SS` (Sattva-Dominant)**: High equanimity, cognitive lucidity, low ego-attachment, ethical clarity, peaceful buoyancy.
- **`SR` (Sattva-Rajas / Strategic Kinetic)**: Sattvik wisdom dynamically propelled by Rajasik drive. The ultimate founder/reformer profile.
- **`RR` (Rajas-Dominant)**: Restless enterprise, high ambition, competitive hunger, transactional commercial velocity.
- **`RT` (Rajas-Tamas / Heavy Kinetic)**: Aggressive power coupled with stubborn defensiveness, burnout vulnerability, or reactive rigidity.

*(For standard 144 combinatorial modeling, the three primary energetic categories evaluated are Sattva [S], Rajas [R], and Tamas/Blended [T].)*

---

### Dimension 2: `[C]` Cognition (Antahkarana Locus)
Identifies the primary psychic organ through which the individual evaluates reality and reaches decisions:
- **`BM` (Buddhi-Dominant)**: Decisions governed by objective truth, ethical principle (*Viveka*), systemic logic, and long-range consequences. Highly analytical, principled, strategic.
- **`MM` (Manas-Dominant)**: Decisions governed by relational empathy, aesthetic resonance, emotional harmony, and sensory intuition. Highly adaptable, communicative, artistic.
- **`AM` (Ahankara-Dominant)**: Decisions governed by executive agency, personal sovereignty, competitive pride, and bold ownership. Highly charismatic, decisive, pioneering.

---

### Dimension 3: `[C]` Competency (Varna-Swabhava Core)
Identifies the natural, inborn operational gift (*Swabhava*) an individual brings to society (elaborated in Chapter 10):
- **`B` (Brahmana / Knowledge & Synthesis)**: Conceptual analysis, scientific research, teaching, ethical philosophy, architectural design, data modeling.
- **`K` (Kshatriya / Strategy & Execution)**: Military command, enterprise management, crisis intervention, governance, sovereign leadership, protective valor.
- **`V` (Vaishya / Commerce & Ecosystems)**: Capital allocation, trade, supply chain networks, venture funding, market creation, generative agriculture.
- **`S` (Shudra / Craft & Operations)**: Specialized technical craftsmanship, direct execution, operational delivery, artistic fabrication, healthcare support.

---

### Dimension 4: `[P]` Purpose (Purushartha Vector)
Identifies the ultimate teleological fuel that motivates the individual's sacrifices (elaborated in Chapter 11):
- **`D` (Dharma / Systemic Order & Ethics)**: Driven to uphold justice, preserve universal balance, enforce righteous systems, and serve the whole.
- **`A` (Artha / Material Wealth & Generative Capacity)**: Driven to build tangible assets, secure economic prosperity, allocate capital, and expand resources.
- **`K` (Kama / Aesthetics, Vitality & Cultural Joy)**: Driven to experience sensory beauty, emotional resonance, artistic expression, and cultural richness.
- **`M` (Moksha / Liberation, Autonomy & Transcendence)**: Driven to achieve intellectual freedom, spiritual liberation, self-sovereignty, and detachment from worldly illusions.
