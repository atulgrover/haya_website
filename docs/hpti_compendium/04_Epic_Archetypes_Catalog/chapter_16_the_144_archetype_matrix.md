$$\mathbf{3\ Energy\ Modes\ (S/R/T)} \times \mathbf{3\ Cognitive\ Loci\ (BM/MM/AM)} \times \mathbf{4\ Competencies\ (B/K/V/S)} \times \mathbf{4\ Purposes\ (D/A/K/M)} = \mathbf{144\ Unique\ Cells}$$

In this chapter, we outline the **Master Archetypal Lexicon**: dividing the 144 matrix into its **Three Great Sovereign Tiers** (Sattvik, Rajasik, and Tamasik), establishing the taxonomic nomenclature, and cataloging the principal archetypal families.
