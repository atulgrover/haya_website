WORKBENCH (ipe)

The Eclipse Theia Integrated Professional Environment (ipe) IDE engineered for legal practitioners.
Parse case files, query dual-vector RAG search indices, and execute subagent audit loops.
Operates 100% on-premise with air-gapped zero cloud transmission and private payload signing.

STAGE 1

Case File Ingestion & Indexing

Securely parses and structures heterogeneous legal documents into high-performance local SQLite vector & full-text search indices.

Multi-Format Ingestion PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), TiddlyWiki, and scanned OCR layout handling.
Dual-Vector RAG Engine Concurrently routes legal text to `InLegal-SBERT` and financial sheets to `Finance-Embeddings`.
Active-Context Control Matrix Filters search scopes with a live 2,048-token budget gauge to prevent context window overflow.
Bi-Directional Monaco-SQLite Sync Intercepts edits to markdown tables and syncs key-value dictionaries directly to database records.
STAGE 2

Professional Enhancements & Visual Analytics

Provides real-time LSP diagnostics, dynamic relationship graphs, and automated judicial layout compilers.

Monaco LSP Hover & Completion Auto-completes section citations, key claims, and statutory definitions directly in the editor.
D3.js 3D Case Graph Viewer Renders interactive 3D graphs mapping corporate entities, creditors, claims, and related parties.
Case Wiki Q&A Accordions Auto-scans `wiki/qna/` markdown files to build structured collapsible question-and-answer trees.
Supreme Court Layout Compiler Compiles drafts into Supreme Court compliant DOCX (A4, Times New Roman, 14pt, 1.5 line spacing, 4cm margins).
STAGE 3

Agentic Reporting & Critique Loops

Orchestrates specialized subagents via Eclipse Theia ChatAgent delegation to perform automated compliance audits.

`@coc-coordinator` Subagent Computes voting shares ($\text{Debt}_i / \sum \text{Unrelated Debt} \times 100\%$) excluding Section 5(24) related parties.
`@plan-evaluator` Subagent Audits Resolution Plans against Section 30(2) & Section 29A to compile Regulation 39(4) Form H certificates.
`DocumentAgent` & `FormsAgent` Executes autonomous critique loops: drafts documents, sends to FormsAgent for auditing, and auto-refines.
Offline LLM Hot-Swapping Executes `LegalParam-2.9B` and `FinanceParam-2.9B` on unified port 8090 with 50% RAM savings (~2.7 GB).

DATA PRIVACY

DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

Enforces complete data minimization and local data sovereignty under Section 6 & Section 8 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. All confidential case records, financial spreadsheets, and vector embeddings are stored and processed 100% on-premise without transferring data to external LLM cloud APIs.

  • Zero Cloud Data Leaks: Client documents remain strictly within local hardware boundaries.
  • Local Anonymization Engine: Automatic PII masking before local subagent execution.
  • Purpose Limitation: Case data is locked isolated to the active case workspace.
CYBERSECURITY

Information Technology Act 2000

Protects document integrity and guarantees an evidentiary chain of custody under Section 43A and Section 66A of the IT Act 2000. Utilizes local cryptographic hashing, asymmetric digital signatures, and encrypted on-premise storage.

  • Cryptographic Hashing: SHA-256 document fingerprinting at ingestion.
  • Ed25519 Digital Signatures: Cryptographically signed audit memos & Form H files.
  • Encrypted Vaults: AES-256-GCM encrypted `.vlt` databases with tamper-evident audit trails.
JUDICIAL DIRECTIVES

SC / RBI / IBBI AI Directives

Strictly adheres to Supreme Court legal ethics, RBI cybersecurity frameworks, and IBBI statutory codes governing AI deployment in judicial proceedings.

  • Attorney-Client Privilege: Confidential strategy is never transmitted offsite.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Audit: Mandates `verified_by_user = 1` verification flags on all AI drafts.
  • Air-Gapped Local Execution: Port 8090 local GGUF server isolates processing from public networks.
