Chapter 0: Sovereign Workspace Setup & myPIE IDE Architecture

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) iPIE Portal operates alongside your local myPIE (Hayagriva Desktop IDE). Confidential client records remain 100% private on your laptop while generating verified compliance payloads.

💡 4-Step Local IDE Execution Pipeline
  1. Ingestion & Slicing: Converts PDFs/Word/Excel into sliced companion markdown cards (concepts/).
  2. Monaco Audit: Live status dot indicators & bi-directional table sync (case_kv_dictionary.json).
  3. Subagent Execution: Run specialized domain agents (@coc, @evaluator, @avoidance).
  4. Regulator Payloads: Generates cryptographically signed Ed25519 JSON compliance files.
