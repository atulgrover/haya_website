RSC/Q3201: Rubber Product Finishing Operator
Brief Job Description
A Rubber Product Finishing Operator is responsible for preparing tools, equipment, and materials required
for rubber product finishing like carrying out trimming, buffing, and cleaning operations and inspecting
rubber products for visual and dimensional quality. The individual ensures that finished products are
defect-free, conform to quality standards, and are ready for packaging or dispatch. The individual also
adheres to workplace safety, hygiene, and documentation protocols during the finishing process.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to be attentive and demonstrate the attributes of a good examiner. A good
observer having a focused mindset, he should seek perfection in carrying out final finishing of the product.
An individual having an aptitude for learning should be able to work independently and co-ordinate with
other team members effectively.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N9801: Prepare for rubber product finishing operations
- 2. RSC/N9802: Perform Trimming, Buffing and Cleaning Operations
- 3. RSC/N9803: Inspect Products for Visual and Dimensional Quality
- 4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol

RSC/N9801: Prepare for rubber product finishing operations
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to prepare for rubber product finishing operations by
checking job specifications, selecting appropriate tools and consumables, ensuring readiness of equipment
and workplace, and confirming availability of components. It involves performing pre-operational checks,
minor machine settings, and reporting abnormalities before beginning the finishing activities.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan and prepare for finishing operations
Prepare tools, consumables and equipment
Coordinate material movement and quality check
Elements and Performance Criteria
Plan and prepare for finishing operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. interpret work instructions and job sheets for the finishing task
PC2. identify types of rubber products and their finishing requirements
PC3. plan the sequence of finishing activities based on product specifications
PC4. ensure availability of tools, accessories and consumables for the finishing process
PC5. confirm the cleaning and readiness of the workstation before starting operations
Prepare tools, consumables and equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. identify and select finishing tools such as knives, grinders, buffers, and cleaning agents
PC7. inspect tools and equipment for functional condition and cleanliness
PC8. demonstrate safe handling and storage of tools and chemicals used in finishing
PC9. carry out minor adjustments or report malfunctions in tools/machines
PC10. perform a trial run of equipment if required for smooth operations
Coordinate material movement and quality check
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. receive semi-finished rubber products from the production line
PC12. visually inspect products for any pre-existing defects before finishing
PC13. verify product tags, labels or batch identification as per company SOP
PC14. segregate items based on finishing type or priority requirements

RSC/N9802: Perform Trimming, Buffing and Cleaning Operations
Description
This NOS covers the competencies required to carry out finishing operations such as trimming, buffing, and
cleaning on rubber components using manual or semi-automated tools. It involves following defined
operating procedures, identifying and removing surface defects, maintaining product integrity, and
ensuring work is done within the specified cycle time and quality parameters.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Execute trimming and buffing activities
Perform cleaning and surface preparation
Maintain quality and productivity
Elements and Performance Criteria
Execute trimming and buffing activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify trimming requirements based on product specifications
PC2. operate manual and mechanized tools for trimming extra rubber
PC3. perform uniform trimming without damaging the product shape
PC4. operate buffing machine and adjust settings as per material type
PC5. control speed, pressure and direction during buffing
PC6. collect trimmed waste for disposal or recycling as per SOP
PC7. ensure trimmed/buffed surface meets finish quality standards
Perform cleaning and surface preparation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. select cleaning materials suitable for the rubber product surface
PC9. remove oil, dust, flash or other residues using approved methods
PC10. dry the product appropriately before moving to inspection
PC11. use vacuum, brushes or compressed air tools as applicable
PC12. ensure safety while working with chemicals or heated air
Maintain quality and productivity
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. check consistency of finishing across batches
PC14. identify surface defects like burrs, dents or cracks
PC15. rework or escalate non-conforming pieces as per defined SOP
PC16. record details of finished quantity, rework and rejection
PC17. maintain efficiency while ensuring finishing quality
PC18. clean and store tools and equipment after operations

RSC/N9803: Inspect Products for Visual and Dimensional Quality
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills needed to inspect finished rubber products for visual and
dimensional quality as per defined quality standards. It includes checking for surface defects, physical
deformities, flash marks, contamination, and critical dimensions using gauges, templates or measuring
instruments and segregating OK and NOT OK products accordingly.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct visual inspection
Check dimensional accuracy
Handle non-conforming items
Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct visual inspection
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. examine surface finish for cleanliness, consistency, and damage
PC2. identify common surface defects like bubbles, cracks or scratches
PC3. verify product aesthetics against sample or reference sheet
PC4. mark and segregate defective or rework pieces appropriately
Check dimensional accuracy
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. use measuring tools such as vernier calipers, templates, or gauges
PC6. compare dimensions with standard product drawings/specifications
PC7. perform calibration checks on gauges used
PC8. record measurements as per SOP
PC9. identify and report deviations from allowable tolerances
Handle non-conforming items
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. label defective or suspect products for further review
PC11. follow protocols for scrap, rework or repairable defects
PC12. maintain inspection log and generate quality reports
PC13. ensure traceability of inspected batches
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. visual and dimensional quality parameters in rubber products

RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
Description
This unit is about maintaining health and safety of self and others at workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
Render appropriate emergency procedures
Maintain standard safety procedures at the workplace
Participate in safety awareness campaigns
Understand potential sources of accidents
Use safety gears to avoid accidents
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. undertake basic safety checks before operation of all machinery and equipment and report
hazards to the appropriate supervisor
PC2. work for which protective clothing or equipment is required is identified and the appropriate
protective clothing or equipment is used in performing these duties in accordance with
workplace policy
PC3. read and understand the hazards of use and contamination mentioned on the labels of
chemicals, utilities etc
PC4. prior to performing manual handling jobs, risk is assessed and work is carried out according
to currently recommended safe practices.
PC5. use equipment and materials safely and correctly and return the same to designated storage
when not in use
PC6. dispose off waste safely and correctly in a designated area
PC7. risks to bystanders are recognized and action taken to reduce risk associated with jobs in the
workplace
PC8. perform work in a manner which minimizes environmental damage
PC9. all procedures and work instructions for controlling risk are followed closely
PC10. report any accidents, incidents or problems without delay to an appropriate person and take
immediate necessary action to reduce further danger
Render appropriate emergency procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. follow procedures for dealing with accidents, fires and emergencies, including
communicating location and directions to emergency.
PC12. follow emergency procedures as per company standards and workplace requirements
