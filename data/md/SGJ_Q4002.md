#### SGJ/Q4002: Junior Technician- Solar Cold Storage
Brief Job Description
Junior Technician-Solar Cold Storage performs installation along with operations and maintenance and of a
solar cold storage/cold room solution while meeting the performance and reliability needs of customers by
incorporating quality craftsmanship and complying with all applicable industry standards, quality and
safety requirements.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be punctual, attentive, sincere and should have an ability to bring a positive and
helpful attitude to work. The individual shall also be highly motivated towards achieving an organizational
goal so as to inspire others at work as well. The individual should also be knowledgeable enough to
diagnose the cause of an issue with regards to system installation and fully skilled to figure out how to fix
it.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SGJ/N4006: Basics of Solar cold storage system installation and its operation
2. SGJ/N0702: Site Survey and pre-requisites for installation of solar cold storage system
3. SGJ/N3901: Elements of solar cold storage system and their functions
4. SGJ/N0623: Technical aspects for installation, usage, performing O&M and cost of solar cold storage system
5. SGJ/N1301: Availing low- cost financing and Case studies showcasing best practices in system
installation and operation of solar cold storage system
6. SGJ/N0801: Maintain Health and Safety at Solar cold storage project site
7. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### SGJ/N4006: Basics of Solar cold storage system installation and its
operation
Description
This unit provides overall concept of solar cold storage system with its functions and key components. This
unit also provide basic skills and knowledge required to manage and operate cold storage system
installation work.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
key importance of cold storage for storing various agricultural/ horticulture produce key insights of solar cold storage system installation and operation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Solar cold storage system installation and its operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Discuss solar energy and its various applications, including in solar cold storage.
- PC2. Discuss the basic functions of a solar cold storage system and how that meet the
requirements for storing agricultural/horticultural produce by farmers/Farmers producers’ organisations (FPOs), etc at farm/Mandi level.
- PC3. Discuss in brief the key components of the solar cold storage system and their functions
- PC4. Discuss the overall concept of solar cold storage system, illustrate its schematic and explain
the main functions of main components
- PC5. Briefly explain the prospects, process, and practice of solar/ cold storage system in India
- PC6. Discuss key drivers, trends, and overall opportunities offered by solar cold storage system to
various stakeholders.
- PC7. Discuss the importance of this job role and the basic skills, knowledge and responsibility of a
technician in cold storage market space
- PC8. Discuss the evolving landscape of Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), system
assemblers and developers in solar cold storage business space
- PC9. Provide an overview of key stakeholders like Farmer Producer organisations (FPOs)/ Self Help
Groups (SHGs) and operators in mandi etc
- PC10. Explain concerned policies, schemes and regulations issued by the Central/State Government
along with Standards/benchmarks associated with solar cold room (including cost), if any
- PC11. Illustrate the significance of solar energy and its applications across various technologies
including solar cold storage solution.
- PC12. Show how solar cold storage solutions are useful for storing and extending the shelf life of
agricultural and horticultural produce
- PC13. Illustrate schematic of solar cold storage system and explain the functions of all key
components

#### SGJ/N0702: Site Survey and pre-requisites for installation of solar cold
storage system
Description
This unit is about analyzing parameters and other pre-requisites to be used in site survey for installation of
solar cold storage system. This unit also provides an overview of design considerations and handling of
tools/ instruments used in site survey and system installation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
analysis of various parameters for installation of solar cold storage system survey site for installation of solar cold storage system
Discuss basics of design considerations for solar cold storage system
Discuss pre-requisites for installing solar cold storage system
Describe various tools used in site survey and system installation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Analysis of parameters used in installation of solar cold storage system
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Discuss various parameters for system installation, any design considerations, create
suitable checklist and explain how to identify the location where solar cold storage system shall be installed
- PC2. Discuss how to analyse key information (e.g. quantity/type of produce, distance from mandi
etc) for identifying the sites for installation of solar cold storage
- PC3. Discuss how to assess the potential and demand for solar cold storage solution
- PC4. Discuss how to map the power requirement, assess system demand/ sizing/ usage and meet
supply provisions (from solar/ Grid/ DG etc) for cold storage system installation and its successful operation
- PC5. Discuss how to use various tools/instruments for site survey.
- PC6. Discuss the requirement for any civil work required for solar cold storage system installation
- PC7. Illustrate and Analyse various parameters including any design considerations for siting the
location for installing solar cold storage system.
- PC8. Show how to access required data and analyse key info for performing site survey and create
checklist for installing solar cold storage solution.
- PC9. Demonstrate how to perform calculations for estimating demand/potential for solar cold
storage in a given area
- PC10. Illustrate how to map the power requirement, assess system demand/ sizing and usage and
meet supply provisions (from solar/ Grid/ DG etc) for cold storage system installation and its successful operation
- PC11. Show how to perform site survey by using various tools/instruments.
- PC12. Show how to perform the required civil work for system installation, if any

#### SGJ/N3901: Elements of solar cold storage system and their functions
Description
This unit outlines the key elements of solar cold storage system, their functions as well as functions of all
key components.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Describe the key elements of solar cold storage system and their functions
Discuss functions of all key components of the system
#### Elements and Performance Criteria key elements and functions of solar cold storage system
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the detailed prospects, concept and practice of utilisation of solar cold storage
system in India
- PC2. Discuss how to perform planning for installation of solar cold storage solution
- PC3. Discuss how “plug and play” modular, solar-powered cold room is designed for 24/7 storage,
thereby addressing the problem of post- harvest losses in fruits, vegetables and other perishable food.
- PC4. Discuss how to identify various tools and tackles and utilise them for various assembly and
installation functions
- PC5. Discuss key elements of a solar cold storage unit, their specifications and overall installation
of the system
- PC6. Explain basic design and fabrication of solar cold storage system along with its key
components (e.g. refrigeration system, etc)
- PC7. Discuss any pre requisites for the overall system installation, as per the site requirements.
- PC8. Discuss key Information on storage/ cooling requirements of different fruits, vegetables and
other farm products [e.g. shelf life, temperature range, humidity etc.]
- PC9. Discuss how cooling requirement during the evening hours can be met from the thermal
backup system
- PC10. Demonstrate the functions of all key components of the solar cold storage system and
showcase its successful operation for storing a range of agricultural/horticultural produce
- PC11. Show how to use various tools and tackles safely for installation the system and all its
components
- PC12. Perform basic design and fabrication of solar cold storage system including of key
components like refrigeration, thermal energy system etc
- PC13. Analyse various specifications of the key components of solar cold storage system and how
to perform system assembly/ installation
- PC14. Analyse any pre- requisites for the overall system installation, as per the site requirements.

and cost of solar cold storage system
Description
This unit describes the technical parameters of all key components of solar cold storage system, design,
installation and operation & maintenance and how to analyse them.
Scope

Discuss the technical parameters of all key components of solar cold storage system
Discuss key parameters for solar cold storage system design, installation and O&M
