HYC/Q3602: Fire Safety Supervisor (Oil & Gas)
Brief Job Description
The main responsibility of the fire safety supervisor is to ensure adherence to fire safety systems and
procedures and supervise the conduction of firefighting operations in oil and gas industry. He/she develops
fire safety systems, procedures, testing and inspection of the firefighting systems and equipment for
ensuring workplace safety from fire hazards. The individual is also responsible for inspection & testing of
fire safety equipment and coordination with operation and maintenance team for organising safety
awareness programs to ensure the safety of the workers and surrounding places. The individual also
responsible for developing fire contingency plans.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have a good knowledge and communication skills. He/she must have supervisory
skills. The person should be proactive, disciplined, safety-conscious, courageous and a good team player.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HYC/N3603: Introduction to advanced firefighting system including codes & standards
- 2. HYC/N3604: Checks, inspection and testing of firefighting equipment for functional reliability
- 3. HYC/N3605: Emergency procedures and drills
- 4. HYC/N9301: Working Effectively in a team
- 5. HYC/N9302: Maintain health, safety and security procedures
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

HYC/N3603: Introduction to advanced firefighting system including codes
& standards
Description
This unit covers introduction to firefighting systems in line with advanced latest fire safety rules &
regulations and procedures specific to oil and gas installations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carryout safety systems and procedures as per applicable safety rules & regulations
Elements and Performance Criteria
Carryout safety systems and procedures as per applicable safety rules & regulations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Know and understand Oil Industry Safety Directorate (OISD) standards related to firefighting
procedures, systems, equipment and appliances in oil and gas industry
PC2. Define Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) policies
PC3. Applicable codes & standards related to fire safety
PC4. Latest developments in firefighting equipment
PC5. Techniques to handle Pressurised Fire Hoses
PC6. Hands-on-practice of using Foam making Equipment & different Nozzles
PC7. Survival in Smoke and Search & Rescue operation. PPE & Rescue Techniques
PC8. Combating Fire inside Oil Storage Tank with Foam System/Equipment
PC9. Prevention of Fire/Accidents & Safety Precautions.
PC10. Fire-causes, Prevention and Control
PC11. Major fire scenarios at site and contingency measures.
PC12. Operate firefighting equipment to a greater degree of competence through practice on use
of these equipment.
PC13. Fighting the road tanker spillage/leakage fire.
PC14. Demonstration of BLEVE fire in a pressure vessel.
PC15. Combating Fire inside Oil Storage Tank with Foam System/Equipment
PC16. Safe work practices while working at height including rescue procedure from height specially
during fire incident
PC17. Safe work practices during confined space entry job, including buddy system, distinct PTW,
JSA specially during fire incident in the confined space
PC18. Safe work practices during excavation job including earth collapse
PC19. Fire safety measures in high rise buildings
PC20. Fire safety measures during transportation of petroleum products through tankers including
arresting any leakages

Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
Assessment Guidelines
- 1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will also lay
down the proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each PC.
- 2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on the knowledge bank of questions created by the
SSC.
- 3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS and where applicable, on the selected
elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- 4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for the theory part for each
candidate at each examination/training centre (as per assessment criteria below).
- 5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training centre based on these criteria.
- 6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score a minimum of 70% of aggregate
marks to successfully clear the assessment.

| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
