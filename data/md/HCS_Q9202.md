HCS/Q9202: Leheriya Printing Artisan
Brief Job Description
The Leheriya Printing artisan is a skilled craftsperson who creates beautiful fabrics with wavy patterns.
Using special folding and dyeing techniques, they bring vibrant colours to life, showcasing the rich
traditions of Rajasthan. Each piece they make is unique, adding a special touch to clothing and
accessories.
Personal Attributes
A Leheriya tie-dye artisan is creative and has a keen eye for detail, enabling them to create beautiful,
colourful patterns. Their passion for this craft, along with patience and skilled hands, allows them to
preserve traditional techniques while also exploring new styles.

HCS/N9239: Introduction to Leheriya Tie-Dye Printing
Description
This unit introduces Leheriya, a traditional tie-dye technique renowned for its vibrant diagonal stripe
patterns, created by skillfully tying and dyeing the fabric.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the History and Cultural Significance of Leheriya
Identify the Geographical Distribution of Leheriya
Recognise Traditional and Contemporary Usage of Leheriya
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand the History and Cultural significance of Leheriya
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the origin and historical development of Leheriya in Rajasthan.
PC2. Describe the role of Leheriya in Rajasthani festivals, rituals, and ceremonies, such as
weddings and cultural events.
PC3. Recognise the traditional caste and artisan communities that have historically practised
Leheriya, including their specific roles and contributions to the craft.
PC4. Compare Leheriya with other resist-dyeing traditions such as Bandhani, highlighting
similarities and differences in techniques and cultural contexts.
PC5. Explain how Leheriya symbolises elements of nature, such as the monsoon and fertility, and
its connection to the agricultural cycle.
Identify the geographical distribution of Leheriya
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Locate key centres for Leheriya production, including Jaipur, Jodhpur, Udaipur, and other
prominent regions in Rajasthan.
PC7. Describe how the arid climate of Rajasthan plays a role in the dyeing and drying process,
allowing for natural, sun-based drying techniques.
PC8. Identify the local resources available for Leheriya production, such as water sources, dye
plants (e.g., indigo, turmeric), and fabric materials.
PC9. Discuss how the desert culture influences Leheriya’s colour choices, including vibrant hues
like yellow, red, and green, which reflect the desert environment and cultural symbolism.
Recognise traditional and contemporary usage of Leheriya
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. Identify garments and accessories where Leheriya is traditionally used, such as sarees,
turbans, dupattas, and odhanis.
PC11. Differentiate between the diagonal (waves) and chevron (V-shaped) patterns commonly
found in Leheriya, explaining the significance of each pattern.

HCS/N9240: Material Selection and Fabric Preparation
Description
This unit covers selecting appropriate materials, including suitable fabrics and dyes, and preparing the
fabric through processes like washing and stretching to achieve high-quality results in Leheriya tie-dye
printing.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify tools, raw materials and dyes
Choose suitable fabric types and assess the quality of fabric
Pre-wash and soften fabric
Prepare fabric for folding and tying
Ensure Workplace Safety and Maintain Tools in Leheriya Printing
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify tools, raw materials and dyes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. List fabrics suitable for Leheriya like cotton, silk, and voile.
PC2. Identify natural and synthetic dye types.
PC3. Identify tools like tying thread, plastic drums, dye vats, etc.
PC4. Understand the role of mordants and fixatives in the dyeing process.
Choose suitable fabric types and assess the quality of fabric
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Identify breathable and dye-absorbent textiles.
PC6. Ensure shrinkage and colourfast properties are known.
PC7. Check for even weave and fine texture.
PC8. Ensure no defects like holes, grease or stains.
PC9. Measure fabric width and straight grain alignment.
Pre-wash and soften fabric
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. Use cold water to remove starch.
PC11. Avoid harsh detergents that weaken fibres.
PC12. Dry fabric in shade after pre-wash.
PC13. Iron lightly to smooth creases and improve foldability.
Prepare fabric for folding and tying
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC14. Align fabric edges to ensure uniform folds.
PC15. Stretch or lightly iron the cloth for accurate folding.

Ensure workplace safety and maintain tools in Leheriya Printing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Know how to identify suitable fabrics for Leheriya tie-dye printing.
KU2. Understand the properties of natural and synthetic fibres used in dyeing.
KU3. Recognise the importance of fabric quality in achieving desired dye results.
KU4. Know the types of materials commonly used for Leheriya, such as cotton, silk, and chiffon.
KU5. Understand the role of fabric texture and weave in the dye absorption process.
KU6. Know how to conduct a basic fabric test for colourfastness and dye compatibility.
KU7. Understand the process of pre-washing fabric to remove sizing, dirt, and finishes.
KU8. Know the purpose of scouring and how it improves dye penetration.
KU9. Recognise the environmental considerations in selecting sustainable materials.
KU10. Understand the safety measures to be followed while handling fabrics and cleaning agents.
KU11. Know the correct storage methods for fabrics before and after preparation.
KU12. Understand the effect of water hardness and temperature on fabric preparation.
KU13. Identify the tools and equipment used in fabric preparation.
KU14. Understand the importance of handling fabric carefully to prevent damage.
KU15. Know how different fabrics respond to various tying techniques in Leheriya.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Select appropriate fabric based on fibre type, texture, and intended design.
GS2. Follow instructions to correctly pre-wash and prepare fabric for dyeing.
GS3. Handle fabrics with care to maintain their quality during preparation.
GS4. Use preparation tools and cleaning agents safely and responsibly.
