ASC/Q1501: Automotive Spare Parts Operations Assistant
Brief Job Description
The individual needs to assist in packing, storing and maintaining inventory in a storage facility.
Personal Attributes
An individual in this job should be a keen observer and have an eye for detail and quality. They should be
organised, team-oriented, customer centric, able to multi-task and have the ability to work for long hours
in adverse conditions. The person must have good communication and interpersonal skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. ASC/N9801: Organize work and resources (Service)
- 2. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
- 3. ASC/N1501: Assist in storage, issuance and maintenance of spare parts
Options(Not mandatory):
Option : Automation in logistics
- 1. ASC/N6114: Use of automation in logistics and material handling

ASC/N9801: Organize work and resources (Service)
Description
This NOS unit is about implementing safety, planning work, adopting sustainable practices for optimising
use of resources
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain safe and secure working environment
Perform work as per quality standards
Health and hygiene
Material/energy conservation practices
Effective waste management practices
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain safe and secure working environment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. organise work as per organisation’s current health, safety and security policies and
procedures
PC2. report any identified breaches in health, safety, and security policies and procedures to the
designated person
PC3. identify the risks and hazards associated with work activities, their causes and prevention
Perform work as per quality standards
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. ensure work area is clean and tidy
PC5. ensure that work is accomplished as per the requirements within the specified timeline
PC6. ensure team goals are given preference over individual goals
Health and hygiene
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. sanitize workstation and equipment regularly
PC8. clean hands with soap, alcohol-based sanitizer regularly
PC9. avoid contact with ill people and self-isolate in a similar situation
PC10. wear and dispose PPEs regularly and appropriately
PC11. report advanced hygiene and sanitation issues to appropriate authority
PC12. follow stress and anxiety management techniques
Material/energy conservation practices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. identify ways to optimise usage of material in various tasks/activities/processes
PC14. use resources, including water, in a responsible manner

ASC/N1501: Assist in storage, issuance and maintenance of spare parts
Description
This OS unit is about assisting in packaging, storing and maintaining inventory and labelling the spare
parts for easy retrieval.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform unloading and bining of spare parts as per layout
Maintain inventory and storage facility
Assist in processing orders
Assist in scraping damaged or obsolete material
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform unloading and bining of spare parts as per layout
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify work requirement and material (spare parts, lubricants, fluids etc.) as per the
instructions given by supervisor
PC2. take precautions to avoid damage to spare parts, lubricant/fluid containers during
unloading/loading and handling
PC3. use suitable tools, equipment, PPE and technique for unloading/loading the spare parts,
lubricant/fluid
PC4. carry out unboxing of received consignment and tally material as per invoice
PC5. report if notice any damage/discrepancies in received consignment to the person concerned
PC6. follow guidelines and carry out binning of items to designated place as per storage plan
Maintain inventory and storage facility
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. dispose left over packing material as per organization’s policies
PC8. follow 5S and maintain storage facility as per storage guideline/instruction given by
supervisor
PC9. assist supervisor to carry out physical inventory verification on regular intervals as per
organisation's policy
PC10. identify damaged, dead/obsolete items and move to a separate, difficult to reach location
and make room for moving stock
PC11. maintain documentation related to identified damaged or dead/obsolete items
PC12. segregate items from one another using storage boxes-bins, plastic separators etc., If mixed
storage (several different parts in one location) cannot be avoided
PC13. carry out periodic maintenance required for items stored such as rotate tyre in rack to avoid
flat spot
PC14. assist the supervisor in maintaining complete and up-to-date stock records
Assist in processing orders

Description
This NOS is about picking and issuing materials as per the BOM to meet production and shipment

To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. obtain the work requirement and material (spare parts, lubricants, fluids etc.) details need to

quantity, bin no. etc. by following SoP/WI

the inventory through automated inventory management system

PC10. assist supervisor in recording and maintaining data of stock records by following SoP/WI

PC11. obtain and check the product/material details/BOM need to be dispatch or process out from
inventory for manufacturing
PC12. feed the product/material picking details in the control system
PC13. monitor and ensure that robot is locating and picking correct product/material as per
order/parts requisition slip/instructions
PC14. collect the product/material at collection point from the robot and release the

in the inventory through automated inventory management system
