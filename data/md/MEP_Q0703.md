#### MEP/Q0703: Human  Resource Operations Executive
Brief Job Description
The Human Resource Operations Executive is responsible for managing and overseeing various HR functions including recruitment, selection, employee onboarding, training and development, performance
appraisal, and employee relations. This role requires a blend of strategic planning, effective communication, and organizational skills to ensure smooth HR operations and contribute to the overall
success of the organization.
Personal Attributes
Strong Communication Skills, Organizational Skills, Attention to Detail, Problem-Solving Ability,
Interpersonal Skills, Decision-Making Ability, Strategic Thinking, Adaptability
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### MEP/N0712: Organizational Behavior
Description
This NOS focuses on understanding individual and group behavior in organizations to enhance workplace
dynamics, employee engagement, and effective human resource management.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction of Organizational Behaviour (OB)
Perception and Learning
Groups and Teams
Motivation
Organizational Development (OD)
Leadership and Organizational Change
Organizational Processes
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction of Organizational Behaviour (OB)
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Define the nature and scope of OB
- PC2. Describe the disciplines, modes, challenges and opportunities of OB
- PC3. Explain the importance of organizational values and code of conduct
Perception and Learning
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Define perception and the process the of improving perception.
- PC5. Highlight different factors and errors (halo effect, stereotype, projection) influencing
perception
- PC6. Describe different components and functions of attitude
- PC7. Identify determinants of personality and discuss their impact on workplace behaviour.
- PC8. Explain the theories and principles of learning.
Groups and Teams
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Discuss the development stages of groups and the importance of group norms and
cohesiveness.
- PC10. Explain the processes and techniques of group decision-making, including brainstorming,
Nominal Group Technique, and Delphi Technique.
- PC11. Identify and discuss the types of conflicts (individual, interpersonal, group) and the role of
Transactional Analysis in conflict resolution.
- PC12. Explain the different types of teams and their significance in achieving organizational goals.

- KU4. Identify strategies for managing change and fostering a positive work environment.

#### MEP/N0401: Principles of Management
Description
This unit describes about various principles of management.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Management
Evolution of management thought
Planning & Decision Making
Organising & Staffing
Direction & Control
Leadership and Team Management
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Define management and explain its functions and processes.
- PC2. Analyze the nature of management and its significance in organizational effectiveness.
- PC3. Identify and differentiate between various managerial roles and essential managerial skills.
- PC4. Compare and contrast management and administration, highlighting their similarities and
differences.
- PC5. Evaluate historical developments and contemporary issues in management, understanding
their impact on organizational practices.
- PC6. Apply management principles to real-world case studies, demonstrating problem-solving and
decision-making skills.
- PC7. Discuss ethical considerations in management practices and their implications for
organizational behavior and culture.
- PC8. Examine global perspectives on management, recognizing cultural and contextual
differences in management practices.
- PC9. Identify emerging trends in management, such as digitalization, sustainability, and diversity,
and their implications for future organisational strategies.
- PC10. Synthesize knowledge of management principles to propose innovative solutions to
organisational challenges.
Evolution of management thought
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Explain the evolution of management thought from early philosophies to modern
approaches.
- PC12. Evaluate classical management approaches, including scientific management and
bureaucratic theory, and their relevance to contemporary organisations.

#### MEP/N0606: Public Relations (PR)
Description
This NOS focuses on developing and managing effective public relations strategies to enhance an
organisation's reputation, engage stakeholders, and handle communication across various media platforms
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding Public Relations
Public Relations Process
Tools of Public Relations
Public Relations Environment
Public Relations and Writing
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Public Relations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Define key concepts and theories in public relations, including publicity, propaganda, and
advertising.
- PC2. Describe the history and evolution of public relations, highlighting the contributions of key
pioneers and their works.
Public Relations Process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. Explain the process of defining a PR problem and selecting appropriate media for
communication.
- PC4. Analyze case studies to illustrate effective PR strategies and tactics.
- PC5. Discuss methods for gathering feedback and evaluating the effectiveness of PR campaigns.
Tools of Public Relations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Describe the steps involved in organizing press conferences and managing media relations.
- PC7. Identify barriers to organizing press events and strategies for overcoming these challenges.
- PC8. Explain the factors influencing media selection for PR activities.
- PC9. Discuss difficulties faced in reaching target publics and effective solutions.
Public Relations Environment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Identify current trends in the PR industry and their consequences for businesses.
- PC11. Explain the growth and influence of public opinion on PR strategies.
- PC12. Describe the role of PR in politics, sports, entertainment, and celebrity management.
