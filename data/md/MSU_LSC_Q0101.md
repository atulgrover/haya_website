MSU/LSC/Q0101: Last Mile Delivery Supervisor
Brief Job Description
Upon completion of this short-term qualification, candidates will be equipped to manage and optimize the
final stage of product delivery as Last Mile Delivery Supervisors. This role involves coordinating
transportation, logistics, and communication to ensure timely, accurate distribution, ultimately enhancing
customer satisfaction and operational efficiency. Supervisors will oversee the critical process of delivering
goods to end customers, addressing the growing demand for efficient last-mile delivery in the country.
Personal Attributes
A Last Mile Delivery Supervisor should possess strong analytical and organizational skills, with the ability to
manage multiple tasks and solve problems efficiently. Effective communication and leadership qualities
are essential for coordinating teams and ensuring timely deliveries. Attention to detail, adaptability, and a
focus on customer satisfaction are also crucial in maintaining accuracy and handling unexpected
challenges in a fast-paced environment. Time management and a proactive approach are key to
successfully optimizing the final stage of product delivery.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:

Arrangements

- 5. MSU/LSC/N6602: Analyze delivery performance data and implement enhancements
- 6. MSU/LSC/N6603: Control Quality and Manage Risks during delivery process

MSU/LSC/N0101: Develop Strategies for Planning and Optimization of
Delivery Routes
Description
Last mile managers are responsible for devising efficient route planning strategies to ensure timely and
cost-effective deliveries. This competency is crucial for logistics professionals aiming to minimize delivery
time and expenses, benefiting courier companies, e-commerce platforms, and delivery service providers
seeking to optimize their operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage and Analyze Orders
Plan and Optimize Routes
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage and Analyze Orders
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Oversee retrieval of daily delivery orders from central system
PC2. Ensure accurate categorization of orders by size, weight, and delivery time
PC3. Analyze historical data to identify delivery trends
PC4. Incorporate GPS data to not only optimize routes but also to avoid congested areas, reducing
idling time and emissions in traffic
PC5. Monitor route optimization software effectiveness
Plan and Optimize Routes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Assess routes not only for time and distance but also for their environmental impact,
selecting paths that minimize emissions and vehicle wear
PC7. Opt for routes that prioritize fuel efficiency, avoiding unnecessary detours and considering
factors like downhill stretches to reduce fuel consumption
PC8. Adapt routes in real-time based on traffic conditions to minimize fuel consumption and
contribute to a cleaner environment
PC9. Review and fine-tune optimized routes manually
PC10. Incorporate instructions for unique delivery locations
PC11. Allocate delivery time slots that align with lower traffic times,contributing to smoother traffic
flow and reduced emissions
PC12. Distribute finalized routes through mobile apps that also provide eco-friendly driving tips,
encouraging drivers to adopt sustainable practices

MSU/LSC/N0102: Organize and Oversee Delivery Team Operations and
Collaborations
Description
This OS unit is about coordinating and supervising delivery teams, fostering teamwork and efficient
collaboration. It is essential for Last mile managers in various industries, including retail, food delivery, and
parcel services, to ensure seamless delivery processes and enhance customer satisfaction.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assign Driver and Communicate
Track and Monitor in Real Time
Elements and Performance Criteria
Assign Driver and Communicate
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Host pre-shift meetings, set objectives.
PC2. Assign drivers based on expertise to specific routes.
PC3. Provide printed route manifests and navigation aids.
PC4. Address exceptional delivery requirements.
PC5. Share point-of-contact details at each delivery.
PC6. Handle driver queries promptly via various communication channels.
PC7. Offer guidance for alternate routes due to closures.
PC8. Provide real-time support to tackle unforeseen challenges.
Track and Monitor in Real Time
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Monitor real-time GPS data for driver tracking.
PC10. Analyze traffic conditions to predict potential delays.
PC11. Communicate updated estimated arrival times to customers.
PC12. Recognize drivers exceptional performance in debriefs.
PC13. Identify performance improvement areas using metrics.
PC14. Collaborate with drivers to develop solutions.
PC15. Schedule training sessions based on performance.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Conduct Pre-Shift Meetings to Establish Goals

MSU/LSC/N0103: Cultivate Relationships with Logistic Partners and
Oversee Contractual Arrangements
Description
Last mile managers need to build strong partnerships with logistics providers and manage contractual
agreements.This competency is applicable to professionals involved in supply chain management and
distribution, facilitating effective collaboration between companies and their logistics partners.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Collaborate with Partner Organizations
Negotiate and Review Contract Terms
Elements and Performance Criteria
Collaborate with Partner Organizations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Organize recurring meetings with logistics partners.
PC2. Share and discuss delivery performance data.
PC3. Network in industry conferences for connections.
PC4. Establish efficient communication channels for updates.
PC5. Coordinate daily order data sharing with partners.
Negotiate and Review Contract Terms
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Negotiate pricing and define service level agreements.
PC7. Specify exceptional situation handling in contracts.
PC8. Ensure contract language aligns with terms.
PC9. Implement APIs for seamless e-commerce data sharing.
PC10. Scrutinize carrier invoices for accuracy.
PC11. Validate invoicing against pricing and terms.
PC12. Resolve billing disputes promptly.

KU1. Organizing Regular Meetings with Logistics Partners
KU2. Sharing Data on Delivery Performance and Discussing Enhancements

KU4. Ensuring Timely and Effective Communication Updates

MSU/LSC/N6601: Leverage Technology Tools and Resources for delivery
processes
Description
Last mile managers must harness technological tools for optimized delivery operations. This competency is
valuable for logistics and transportation professionals utilizing software and hardware solutions to enhance
efficiency, suitable for businesses across multiple sectors.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Implement Technology and Train Teams
Implement Tracking and Communication Systems
Elements and Performance Criteria
Implement Technology and Train Teams
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Install and configure tracking devices and tools.
PC2. Train drivers for tracking and communication systems.
PC3. Respond immediately to alerts for timely solutions.
PC4. Engage drivers to overcome potential bottlenecks.
Implement Tracking and Communication Systems
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Monitor real-time tracking dashboards.
PC6. Analyze historical tracking data for insights.
PC7. Generate detailed reports on driver performance.
PC8. Utilize tracking data to update customers.
PC9. Send real-time delivery alerts to customers.
PC10. Offer real-time tracking link for transparency.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Expertise in Installing and Configuring Tracking Devices and Tools
KU2. Educating Drivers on Proficient Use of Tracking and Communication Systems
KU3. Prompt Response to Alerts and Notifications for Timely Action
KU4. Engaging Drivers to Effectively Resolve Operational Bottlenecks
KU5. Monitoring Real-Time Tracking Data for Accurate Driver Location Insights
KU6. Deriving Valuable Insights from Historical Tracking Data Analysis
