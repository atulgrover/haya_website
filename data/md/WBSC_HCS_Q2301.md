#### WBSC/HCS/Q2301: Mat Maker
Brief Job Description
Mat Maker is responsible for crafting high-quality mats using various materials such as fabric, Korai
Grass.The candidate will possess expertise in cutting, shaping and assembling materials to create
aesthetically pleasing and durable mats on handloom for various purposes including decoration, utility and
artistic expression.
Personal Attributes
A Mat Maker should possess patience, attention to detail and creativity. Good hand-eye coordination,
physical stamina and a sense of aesthetics are essential to ensure quality craftsmanship and precision in
weaving.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. WBSC/HCS/N2301: Apply safe working practices.

#### WBSC/HCS/N2301: Apply safe working practices.
Description
Apply safe working practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Usage of proper personal protective equipment.
Safety rules and procedures.
Application of safe working practices.
Application of basic first aid to address common accidents.
Maintenance of a clean, organized and safe work environment.
Practice of safe handling and usage of tools, chemical and equipment involved in mat crafting.

- PC2. Follow safety rules and procedures.

Description
Demonstrate processing of raw materials
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Key raw materials like mat grass, cotton yarn, cloth and stitching threads.

#### WBSC/HCS/N2305: Execute basic weaving operations for Mat products.
Description
Execute basic weaving operations for Mat products.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operation of the charkha efficiently for reeling yarn.
The drumming and warping of cotton yarn.
Weaving of mats and sheets in specified widths, lengths and design variations.
Inspection of woven items visually for errors and quality defects.
Verification of the size and design specifications of the woven mats.
Rectification of weaving errors as per supervisor instructions.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Operate the charkha efficiently for reeling yarn.
- PC2. Perform the drumming and warping of cotton yarn.
- PC3. Weave mats and sheets in specified widths, lengths and design variations.
- PC4. Inspect woven items visually for errors and quality defects.
- PC5. Verify the size and design specifications of the woven mats.
- PC6. Rectify weaving errors as per supervisor instructions.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The charka method for reeling different types of yarn.
- KU2. The drumming process of cotton yarn as per specific design-wise: Tana and Jhala (Warping in
drum and warping in wooden beam / Naraj).
- KU3. Various weaving patterns like diamond, fish bone, wave, check, honey comb, falls etc.
- KU4. The steps of visual check for any errors or damages in quality of weaved mat roll / sheet.
- KU5. The steps of verification of the mat roll / sheet weaved as per size and designs.
- KU6. The procedure for identifying and correcting weaving errors.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Practice reeling yarn using a charka safely and effectively.
- GS2. Execute the drumming process for cotton yarn on different setups.

#### Assessment Guidelines
Assessment will be based on the concept of Independent Assessors empaneled with West Bengal State
Council of Technical & Vocational Education & Skill Development (WBSCT&VE&SD), identified, selected,
trained and certified on Assessment techniques. These Assessors would be aligned to assess as per the
laid down criteria. WBSCT&VE&SD would conduct assessment only at the training centers or designated
testing centers authorized by WBSCT&VE&SD. Ideally, the assessment will be a continuous process
comprising of two distinct steps: A. Continuous assessment by Trainers, B. Term end /Final Assessment by
WBSCT&VE&SD. Each National Occupational Standard (NOS) in the respective QPs will be assigned weightage. Each Performance Criteria in the NOS will be assigned marks for theory and/or practical based
on relative importance and criticality of function. This will facilitate preparation of question bank / paper
sets for each of the QPs. Each of these papers sets/question banks created by subject matter experts
through WBSCT&VE&SD, especially with regard to the practical test and the defined tolerances, finish,
accuracy etc. The following tools are proposed to be used for final assessment: i. Written Test: This will
comprise of (i) True/False Statements and/or (ii)Multiple Choice Questions and/or (iii) Matching Type
Questions. Online system for this will be preferred.
ii. Practical Test: This will comprise a test job to be prepared as per project briefing following appropriate
working steps, using necessary tools, equipment and instruments. Through observation it will be possible
to ascertain candidate’s aptitude, attention to details, quality consciousness etc.
