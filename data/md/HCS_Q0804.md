#### HCS/Q0804: Leather Toy Maker
Brief Job Description
The Leather Toy Maker-Artisan selects galvanized wires or iron and mild steel and makes grass and wood
strands while using threads along with different synthetic water colors to make the final toy.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have: hand-eye coordination, ability to analyze and make the mixture,
ability to judge requisite amounts of mixture, judge if the prepared mixture looks right, no color blindness,
ability to sort the mixes.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N0818: Prepare the Raw materials for making the leather toy products
2. HCS/N0819: Carry out the processes for making the Leather toy products

#### HCS/N0818: Prepare the Raw materials for making the leather toy
products
Description
The unit discuss in details about the preparation of raw materials before beginning the production
processes. It informs about performance criteria, knowledge and understanding, and skills required to
become a Leather Toy Maker Master Craftsman.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
assemble required raw materials processing the raw materials check quality and productivity standards
#### Elements and Performance Criteria assemble required raw materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the raw materials for making the pattern and mold
- PC2. arrange the different tools and equipment at the workplace
- PC3. ensure raw material are as per product requirement both in terms of quality and quantity
- PC4. identify the appropriate amount of cardboard pulp and glue (form of leather) based on the
job specifications
- PC5. arrange the suitable synthetic water colors and polish as per the product requirement
required for coloring the objects
- PC6. arrange galvanized iron wires of appropriate size and dimensions required for making the
skeleton of toy based on the approved job specification
- PC7. place appropriate quantity of water in a drum based on the job specification
- PC8. place eyes, teeth and soles of an appropriate size and numbers required for making leather
toy
- PC9. deliver the remaining raw material to the appropriate storage locations
Processing the raw materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. explain the importance of wrapping galvanized wires of mild steel or iron of 1mm to 4mm
thick depending upon the size of the toy skeleton
- PC11. list the parameters required for covering the skeleton with grass and wood wool strands to
give mass to the body of the toy
- PC12. identify the type of thread is using on to the skeleton of toy to give the structure an
additional support
- PC13. place the appropriate quantity of copper sulphate paste (layee) for joining the joints and
edges of the skeleton and place in the sunlight for drying the same

#### HCS/N0819: Carry out the processes for making the Leather toy products
Description
This unit discusses in detail the different processes involved in making any leather craft product including
shaping of main frame of product with hard leather, wet leather, grain leather and bonded leather frame
and then finishing of the final product.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
carry out the process of making wire frame carry out the process of smoothening and finishing
#### Elements and Performance Criteria carry out the process of making wire frame
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the tools and equipment to be used for the preparation of the product in accordance
to the specifications provided
- PC2. apply the standard operating procedures to make the wire frame structure based on
thickness and materials used for making the final product
- PC3. perform the process of wrapping the grass and strands in the form of bunches with
appropriate thickness of threads to build the mass on the skeleton
- PC4. demonstrate the standard operating procedures to make the cardboard crushed and
moistened for further processing
- PC5. perform the standard mixing procedures for mixing the moistened and crushed cardboard
pulp with tamarina flour
- PC6. perform the process the tanning process on leather material for preserving through
putrefaction to avoid any loss to the properties of leather
- PC7. apply standard operating procedures for wrapping the leather material on the skeleton based
on thickness and skin type based on the job specification
- PC8. arrange the materials eyes, teeth, soles and nails along with appropriate colors and sizes
from 1/4th of an inch to 2 inch depending upon the size of the toy carry out the process of smoothening and finishing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. perform the standard operating procedures to place the finishing objects on the final
prepared product ensuring the correct dimensions and sizes are there
- PC10. select the suitable color combinations and the appropriate quantity of wax polish based on
the job specification
- PC11. apply standard organizational safety procedures while using sulphate oxides, synthetic
colors, wax polish and sharp tools and equipment
- PC12. select the appropriate quantity of glue for sticking leather over the model body by boiling
tamarind powder

#### HCS/N9805: Entrepreneurship Skills for making leather toys
Description
This unit focuses on developing essential entrepreneurship skills specifically tailored for artisans involved
in making leather toys. It aims to equip learners with the knowledge and abilities necessary to successfully
establish and manage a leather toy business while fostering creativity and craftsmanship
Scope
The scope covers the following :
creativity and design skills technical proficiency business management skills marketing and sales skills
networking and collaboration

Creativity and Design Skills

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
