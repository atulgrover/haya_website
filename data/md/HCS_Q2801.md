#### HCS/Q2801: Casting Operator - Metalware
Brief Job Description
A metalware casting operator is responsible for the precise operation of casting processes to produce high-
quality metal components. They ensure the accuracy of moulds and oversee the casting process, maintaining safety and efficiency throughout production.
Personal Attributes
A metalware casting operator is detail-oriented and safety-conscious, demonstrating strong problem-
solving skills and a commitment to quality. They are adaptable and efficient, with a solid understanding of
casting techniques and processes.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

1. HCS/N2801: Perform pre casting requirements

#### HCS/N2801: Perform pre casting requirements
Description
This OS unit is about preparing the pre casting requirements to cast the metal surface
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understand work requirement assemble the required raw materials set the cast machine
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand work requirement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Receive work instruction from the supervisor.
- PC2. Understand the job sheet and the casting method.
- PC3. Plan the target on the number of pieces to be completed.
Assemble the required raw materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

furnace, refrigerator bricks.

Set the cast machine

#### HCS/N0603: Carry out the process involved in Sandcasting Techniques
Description
This unit focuses on the Sandcasting is a metal casting process where molten metal is poured into a sand
mold to create objects. First, a pattern of the object is made and placed in a two-part mold filled with sand.
Once the pattern is removed, the mold is reassembled, and molten metal is poured into the cavity. After
the metal cools and hardens, the mold is broken, and the final metal part is cleaned and finished.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
design the pattern mold preparation pouring the molten metal cooling and removing the casting finishing touches
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Design the Pattern
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. create a pattern of the object to be cast
- PC2. model or pattern of the desired part is made from wood, metal, or plastic
Mold Preparation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. Place a pattern inside a two part frame called a "flask"
- PC4. mix casting sand with clay and water and tightly packed around the pattern
Pouring the Molten Metal
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. heat the metal in a furnace until it becomes melt
- PC6. close the mold and pour the molten metal through the gating system into the cavity
- PC7. fills the cavity and takes the shape of the part
Cooling and Removing the Casting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. allow the metal cool and solidfy inside the sand mold
- PC9. clean the casting and remove the excess sand or any unwanted metal (like the sprue and
gates) is cut off
Finishing Touches
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. polish and sand the casting to remove rough surfaces, imperfections, or sharp edges
- PC11. trim the excess metal or flash (the thin layer of metal along the mold lines) by using cutting
tools
