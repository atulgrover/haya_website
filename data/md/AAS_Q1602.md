AAS/Q1602: Aerospace Structural Fitter
Brief Job Description
Aerospace Structural Fitter is is responsible for carrying out structural assembly of aircrafts/ helicopters
and perform activities like drilling, reaming, riveting of structures, applying sealing compound etc.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have basic communication ability and should be responsible in order to maintain
procedures. He should have the ability of operation monitoring i.e., observing gauges,dials etc., ability to
quickly move hand to grasp and assemble objects (dexterity), reading, writing and communication skills
and sensitivity towards safety for self and equipment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. AAS/N1616: Understand the Assembly Drawings.
- 2. AAS/N1617: Understanding the Different Drilling Operations.
- 3. AAS/N1618: Understanding of Different types of Riveting Operations
- 4. AAS/N1619: Understand the Reaming Operation
- 5. AAS/N1620: Understand the Canopy Matching
- 6. AAS/N1621: Perform RMA installation of the Aircraft
- 7. AAS/N1622: Mixing of Sealants and Application of Sealants

AAS/N1616: Understand the Assembly Drawings.
Description
This module focuses on developing the ability to read and interpret assembly drawings used in aerospace
manufacturing. It trains individuals to understand various views, sections, symbols, scales, and part
identification in coordination with process sheets and technology, ensuring accurate interpretation and
execution of technical drawings.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Reading Assembly Drawing,
know-how to make use of the drawing signs, drawing scale, drawing number,
Description of drawing in coordination of Process sheet & Technology.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Basic Knowledge of the Drawing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Able to read the drawing
PC2. Know the views of drawing i.e. plan, isometric etc.
PC3. Able to identify sections of drawing.
PC4. Know the types of lines used to differentiate between the assembly part positions.
PC5. Know the signs of drawing like finishing triangles, hole size, thickness, length, width etc.
PC6. Know to identify the scale of drawing
PC7. Able to correlate the drawing and technology
PC8. Know to identify the position numbers of the part and able to identify the part number in
material schedule (MS)
PC9. Know how to see the section of drawing in different zones
PC10. Mark the center of the hole with a punch.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand the role of technical drawings in aerospace manufacturing
KU2. Recognize types of views (plan, front, side, isometric, sectional).
KU3. Interpret line types for assembly positions
KU4. Identify symbols for hole size, thickness, length, and width.
KU5. Read and apply drawing scales.
KU6. Understand drawing numbers, title blocks, and revisions.
KU7. Correlate assembly drawings with process sheets and technology.

AAS/N1617: Understanding the Different Drilling Operations.
Description
This module introduces key drilling operations such as drilling, reaming, tapping, and countersinking. It
focuses on their application, tool selection, and execution as per technical specifications in aerospace
assembly.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Responsible for conducting drilling activities like: Drilling, Counter sinking, Counter boring, Spot
facing, Reaming &Taping.
Responsible for measuring dimensions and inspecting work pieces.
Carry out machining activities to make Aerospace Assembly.
Interact with other technicians, supervisors, inspectors, process planner, design, tool planner etc.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform different drilling operation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Read the process information sheet & drawing and operations.
PC2. Check and inspect the parts supplied for physical / dimensional variation.
PC3. Check the documentation of all parts and its identification with respect to documents.
PC4. Marking on the part according to drawing / technology/ reference tool.
PC5. Check whether the component having proper edge distance and other dimensions prior to
carrying out drilling operation.
PC6. Select the proper tool as mentioned in the technology.
PC7. Check the machinability of tool i.e. like sharpness, size etc.
PC8. Decide the number of stages in which the hole is to be enlarges as per size and its steps.
PC9. Select the tools as per number of stages in which drilling is to be carried out.
PC10. Mark the centre of the hole by punch or perform drilling on pilot holes.
PC11. After each stage check the dimension of the operation performed by tool mentioned in the
technology.
PC12. Deburr the hole by tool as mentioned in technology.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand various drilling operations: drilling, reaming, tapping, countersinking.
KU2. Know how to read process sheets and technical drawings.
KU3. Identify and inspect parts for physical or dimensional issues.

AAS/N1618: Understanding of Different types of Riveting Operations
Description
This module focuses on performing riveting operations on aerospace panels. It includes reading technical
drawings, selecting appropriate tools and rivets, cleaning components, executing precise riveting, and
inspecting completed work. The technician is also responsible for documenting measurements and
coordinating with other departments to ensure quality and compliance with specifications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Responsible for conducting riveting activities on panels.
Responsible for measuring dimensions and inspecting work pieces.
Carry out machining activities to make Aerospace Assembly.
Interact with other technicians, supervisors, inspectors, process planner, design, tool planner etc.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform different riveting operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Read the process information sheet & drawing and operations.
PC2. Check and inspect the parts supplied for physical / dimensional variation.
PC3. Check the documentation of all parts and its identification with respect to documents.
PC4. Marking on the part according to drawing / technology/ reference tool.
PC5. Check whether the component having proper cleaning for riveting operation.
PC6. Select the proper tool as mentioned in the technology.
PC7. Select the rivet as mentioned in the technology.
PC8. Clean all the parts and rivets with naphtha for riveting.
PC9. Now parts are tech. bolted, and rivets are inserted on holes with primer.
PC10. If sealing compound is called on technology, then apply sealing instead of primer.
PC11. Do the riveting with the rivet gun on one side and bucking bar on another side.
PC12. Measure the bucking head and bucking length by Rivet Gauge.
PC13. If riveting is improper then rivets it and install new rivet and do the riveting.
PC14. Document the dimension.
PC15. Apply the non-corrosive chemical (Primer) as per the technology.
PC16. Offer to supervisor and inspect.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Knowledge of reading and interpreting process sheets, technical drawings, and operations.

AAS/N1619: Understand the Reaming Operation
Description
This module covers the execution of reaming operations in aerospace assembly, focusing on achieving
precise hole dimensions and surface finish. It includes selecting appropriate tools and coolants, setting
correct machining parameters, and inspecting workpieces per technical specifications. The role also
involves thorough documentation and maintaining cleanliness and corrosion prevention of tools and
components.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Responsible for conducting machining activities like reaming operations.
Responsible for measuring dimensions and inspecting work pieces.
Carry out machining activities to make Aerospace Assembly.
Interact with inspectors, process planner, design, and tool planner.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform reaming operation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Read the process sheet & drawing
PC2. Check and inspect the parts supplied for physical / dimensional variation.
PC3. Check the documentation of all parts its identification with respect to documents.
PC4. Check the internal diameter of bush/part by tool mentioned in technology.
PC5. Select proper tools and reamers as mentioned in technology.
PC6. Check the machinability of reamers i.e. like sharpness, size etc.
PC7. Select the proper coolant for reaming operation as mentioned in the technology.
PC8. Set the speed of rotation and feed rate for reaming operation as mentioned in the
technology.
PC9. Select the proper sequence of reamers as per dimension, tolerance and surface finish
mentioned in the technology.
PC10. Reaming of holes as per sequence of reamers selected.
PC11. Apply coolant in between the reaming operation as mentioned in the technology.

Description
This module/ unit is about Canopy matching ensuring the precise alignment and fitting of the canopy to
the aircraft structure. The technician is responsible for verifying the clearance and fit between the canopy
and the surrounding components. This process includes marking, trimming, and adjusting the canopy

securely installed, providing both functional and safety integrity, with clearances suitable for sealing hose
installation and other critical operations.
Scope

trolley, and crane.

Description
The technician will be responsible for mixing and applying various sealing compounds to aerospace panels,
ensuring proper preparation, inspection, and curing. They will conduct sealing, riveting, and bolting
operations while maintaining documentation and collaborating with other teams for seamless execution.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Responsible for mixing & preparation of variant of sealing compound such as spatula & brush variant
of sealing compound U30 MES 5NT, VITEF 1NT, UT32 NT, U2 28 NT, P9, ZP3, VK-9.
Responsible for conducting the sealing activities on panels.
Responsible for measuring dimensions and inspect work pieces.
The technician will carry out machining activities to make Aerospace Assembly.
Interact with other technicians, supervisors, inspectors, Process planner, Design, Tool Planner etc.
