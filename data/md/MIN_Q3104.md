#### MIN/Q3104: Electrician (Mineral Sands)
Brief Job Description
An Electrician (Mineral Sands) is responsible for the Operation and Maintenance (O&M) of the installed
equipment and electrical supply system.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be physically fit to work for long durations. The person should have attention to
detail, organizational and problem-solving skills. The individual should also have appropriate verbal and
written communication skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MIN/N3107: Test and operate electrical equipment and motors at the dredge and supplementary mining plant
2. MIN/N3108: Test and operate electrical equipment and motors at the mineral separation plant
3. MIN/N3109: Test and operate electrical equipment and motors at the rare earth extraction plant
4. MIN/N3110: Test and operate electrical equipment and motors at the main receiving sub-station
5. MIN/N1810: Follow the applicable health, safety and environmental guidelines (Placer Mining)
6. MIN/N1811: Work collaboratively and effectively in a team
7. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### MIN/N3107: Test and operate electrical equipment and motors at the
dredge and supplementary  mining plant
Description
This occupational standards unit is about testing and operating electrical equipment and motors at the
dredge and supplementary mining plant.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operate the processing equipment
Test cables, joints and switchgears
Perform equipment maintenance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Operate the processing equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. monitor the operation of dredge and different types of slurry pumps
- PC2. check the operation of different types of 3-phase induction motors at the dredge
- PC3. test and maintain the 3-phase Induction motors engaged in the winching system
- PC4. carry out overhauling of the motor including greasing, bearing replacement, etc
- PC5. use the appropriate electrical tools and measuring instruments for the installation of
electrical systems and equipment
- PC6. install electrical panels as per the relevant drawings
- PC7. check and ensure the correct operation of the newly installed equipment
- PC8. coordinate with the Main Receiving Sub-Station (MRSS) regarding power supply
- PC9. follow the appropriate safety measures to protect the electrical systems during extreme
weather and natural calamities
Test cables, joints and switchgears
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. check the condition of Cross-linked Polyethylene (XLPE) , and Polyvinyl Chloride (PVC)
insulated cables
- PC11. install and check the condition of different types of trailing cables, e.g. 11kV, 3.3kV, etc.
- PC12. connect cables to panels and sub-panels appropriately
- PC13. carry out cable end termination in control panels appropriately
- PC14. install different types of cable joints and cable trays
- PC15. inspect, identify and rectify abnormal conditions in the circuits
- PC16. check insulation resistance for different types of installations, e.g. control panel, motor and
cables
- PC17. check for the correct functioning of the toggle switch, push button switch, selector switch,
cam switch, etc.

mineral separation plant
Description
This occupational standards unit is about testing and operating electrical equipment and motors at the
mineral separation plant
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Supply electricity to the installed system
Perform maintenance and operations
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Supply electricity to the installed system
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. check the electrical layout and single-line circuit diagram
- PC2. check for the correct installation of the High-Tension Separator, Electrostatic Plate Separator,
Magnetic Separator, Induced Roll Magnetic Separator, Rare Earth Drum Magnetic Separator,
Cross Belt Magnetic Separator, High-Intensity Induced Roll Magnetic Separator, etc.
- PC3. follow the supervisor's work instructions/ work-related documents to determine the work
requirements and work accordingly
- PC4. measure current, voltage and resistance of single-phase and three-phase system
- PC5. identify the phase, neutral and earth connections in the supply system
- PC6. select and maintain ISO Standard hand tools and measuring tools required at the site, e.g.,
insulation resistance tester or megger, clamp meter, multimeter, voltage probe, etc
- PC7. ensure power system protection for induction motors, transformers, etc
Perform maintenance and operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. overhaul the motor
- PC9. grease and replace the bearing
- PC10. maintain the UPS, battery bank and capacitor bank in an appropriate condition
- PC11. conduct different types of tests on motors, e.g. IR Value, No load, Winding resistance,
Polarity Test, Blocked Rotor Test etc
- PC12. check cable laying, cable jointing and terminations, and carry out their maintenance
- PC13. carry out the maintenance of different types of breakers, panels etc
- PC14. check the rotation of the driving system in slurry pumps and booster pumps
- PC15. calibrate the ammeter, kilo-volt meter, etc
- PC16. conduct the insulation test
- PC17. check the transformer oil level and replenish it as required

#### MIN/N3109: Test and operate electrical equipment and motors at the rare
earth extraction plant
Description
This occupational standards unit is about testing and operating electrical equipment and motors at the
rare earth extraction plant
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Supply electricity to the installed system and equipment
Perform testing, maintenance and operations
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Supply electricity to the installed system and equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. use the appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) including Gum boots, Polyurethane
(PU) gloves, ear muffs and eye protection
- PC2. follow the appropriate electrical safety precautions at the rare earth extraction plant
- PC3. obtain the work permit from the authorized person following the applicable procedure
- PC4. maintain the electrical checkpoints
- PC5. ensure the relevant electrical parameters are aligned with the process requirement of 3-
phase induction motors at various locations in the rare earth extraction plant
- PC6. check the circuit breakers, isolators, bus bars, wiring, fuses, earthing, switchboard, control
panels, relays, and diesel generators for the correct functioning
- PC7. follow the applicable safety standards in the earthing system, armoured cables and flexible
cables as per the Indian Electricity Rules
- PC8. maintain the electrical equipment, such as motors, fans, lighting, ACs, heaters, compressors,
MCC Panels, LDB, PDB, etc
- PC9. inspect the electrical equipment to identify electrical risks, hazards, defects and the needs
for the adjustment/repair
- PC10. modify the parameters of electrical equipment, if required to ensure alignment with the
prescribed standards
Perform testing, maintenance and operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. carry out maintenance of different types of motors and pumps, e.g. agitators motors,
crystallize motors, centrifugal pumps, evaporator pumps, process water pumps, etc
- PC12. carry out predictive, preventive and breakdown maintenance of diesel generators, motors,
circuit breakers, isolators, bus bars, MCC panels, switchboards, wiring, protective relays, etc
- PC13. carry out testing and identification of electrical systems for proper functioning of electrical
wiring, equipment, and fixtures

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.

3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
