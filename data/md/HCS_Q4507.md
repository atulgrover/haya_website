#### HCS/Q4507: Paint Line Operator (Paper Mache)
Brief Job Description
The Paint Line Operator is responsible for painting papermache objects with different shades of colour as
per requirement. He should be capable of making appropriate colour combinations with the shades
available in the market. The Brushing is responsible for applying paints as per design requirement of final
pieces required by buyer
Personal Attributes
The Painter should have creativity with flair for sketch and design. He should be keen, vigilant, good
eyesight, patient, investigative and good knowledge of Materials. He should be free from defects of colour
vision. He should have keen interest on learnings. He should have keen interest on learnings.

#### HCS/N4506: Painting and finishing of object
Description
This unit is about inventory of skills, knowledge, tools and techniques needed to carry out the process of
painting right from colour selection, paint preparation, varnishing, drying to finishing of objects.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
carry out preparatory activities for mixing paint with shades varnish of Painted Objects drying
finishing
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out preparatory activities for mixing paint with shades
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Use suitable ppe like rubber hand gloves and nose mask as required.
- PC2. Ensure the place/room is clean, ventilated and well lit.
- PC3. Select suitable colour ingredients based on hue and tone of shade required.
- PC4. Transfer suitable amount of colours in a clean bowl.
- PC5. Dissolve colours in suitable glue (fevicol).
- PC6. Mix the ingredients (colours and glue) well with an stirrer or suitable paint brush.
- PC7. Check the colour mixture by sample testing on waste paper or suitable materials to ensure it
matches with the shade needed.
- PC8. Ensure the object is clean and free from dust, dirt etc.
- PC9. Ensure the object is not damp or wet.
- PC10. Paint the object uniformly as per shade required.
Varnish of Painted Objects
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Apply varnish on the product in order to get proper shine and make sure that color doesnt
fade away.
- PC12. Allow it to dry.
Drying
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. Keep the material in open atmosphere under shade.
- PC14. Leave for 24-48 hrs.
Finishing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC15. Cover the product with thin strips of butter paper to absorb extra unfixed paints from
surface.

#### HCS/N4504: Applying paint to Sakhta
Description
This unit is about inventory of skills, knowledge, tools and techniques needed to carry out the process of
applying paints to sakhta.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
preparation to brushing process applying paints and varnish to object drying final checking
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparation to brushing process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Use suitable ppe like rubber hand gloves and nose mask as required.
- PC2. Ensure the place/room is clean, ventilated and well lit.
- PC3. Take suitable quantity of paints in a bowl.
- PC4. Do test paints on separate object.
- PC5. Check for shade suitability for colour.
Applying paints and varnish to object
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Select suitable brush size. usually 1 or 2 inch brush is common.
- PC7. Identify the portion as main colour and a star.
- PC8. Do brushing as per shade and precaution needed.
- PC9. The process is continued till desired level of coating is attained.
- PC10. Do the varnishing where needed.
Drying
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Keep the material in open atmosphere under shade.
- PC12. Leave it to dry.
Final checking
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. Check to ensure no line or design mark is visible (should be covered up by paint colours).
- PC14. Check the objects matches with design as per customer requirements for shade and motif.

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.

4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
