CPC/CAP/Q0501: Plastics Recycling Machine Operator
Brief Job Description
The machine operator handles the recycling materials, sets up and operates the plastic processing
machines, finishes the product & stores in desired place
Personal Attributes
Technical aptitude, Safety-conscious, Reliability and punctuality, Organizational skills, Problem-solving
ability, Teamwork and communication, Quality-focused mindset, Willingness to learn
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CPC/CAP/N0501: Understand basic concepts, job requirements & basics know how related to
process.
- 2. CPC/CAP/N0502: Perform the Plastics Recycling related operations, monitor process parameters
and troubleshoot the process/product as per the final output specifications and the standards
specified by the organization.
- 3. CPC/CAP/N0503: To Conduct quality checks and inspection of the finished products with reference
to the approved product
- 4. CPC/CAP/N0504: Entrepreneurship in Plastics Recycling.
- 5. CPC/CAP/N0401: Basic health and safety practices at the workplace, 5S
- 6. CPC/CAP/N0402: Basics of computer and data entry in MS OFFICE/office Open source suite
Software
- 7. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

CPC/CAP/N0501: Understand basic concepts, job requirements & basics
know how related to process.
Description
This OS unit is about understanding the job requirement, operating the Plastics Recycling machine in order
to produce recycled granules raw material by recycling various plastic wastes
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/ task covers the following:
Understanding the work order and the process requirement from the supervisor
Arranging ,cleaning and drying the required Plastics waste for the process
Cleaning the required dies and equipment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand the work and the process requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. To interact with the supervisor in order to understand the production schedule
PC2. To plan the day’s production activities based on the supervisor’s instructions
PC3. To ensure availability of consumables and Plastics waste for production in sufficient quantity
as per production plan/supervisor instructions
PC4. Clearly understanding the does and don’ts of the manufacturing process as defined in SOPs/
Work Instructions or defined by supervisors
PC5. Check availability of the personal protective equipments (PPE) like Gloves, Goggles etc
PC6. Understand the Recycling procedure and process to be adopted for completing the work
order from the supervisor by referring the Work Instruction document/ SOP manual
Arrange the Plastic wastes for recycling and die required for the same
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Ensure that the required Plastic wastes is procured before starting the process
PC8. Understand the die required for executing the required operation and ensure that the same
is available for operation
PC9. If die is not available collect the die from tool room
PC10. Install and bolt the die in place and slide the safety door shut
PC11. Add the Plastics waste in the machine using material loader or by manual feeding
Clean the apparatus and the components before executing the process
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. Ensure dies are clean if not clean with soft cotton cloth
PC13. Ensure cleaning of the other auxiliaries tools, (if any) before the initiation of
grinding/crushing or recycling to form the plastics pellets

CPC/CAP/N0502: Perform the Plastics Recycling related operations,
monitor process parameters and troubleshoot the process/product as per
the final output specifications and the standards specified by the
organization.
Description
This NOS is about produce good quality recycled granules in line with the required specifications
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The recycling machine operator will be responsible for
Checking the operations of the equipment
Feeding the plastic wastes as per requirement
Set up and operate the plastics recycling machine
Perform visual inspection of the output products
Achieve productivity, quality and safety standards as per company norms
Report problems to supervisor
Elements and Performance Criteria
Check the operation of the equipment Used in plastics recycling process
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Check for operation of recycling apparatus like hopper, heaters,washing equipment etc. and
inspection of the plastics sorting equipment like sensors,ejectors etc.as per the checklist
provided
PC2. Fix the desired die to the extrusion machine in order to achieve the desired operation as per
the Work Instructions/ SOPs
PC3. Make modifications in the process parameters ( by selecting the right program from the
machine control system)
Feed the claned, dried and separated plastic waste in the hopper and conduct a trial with the setting of
the parameter
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Perform preheating of sorted plastic waste ( In case of Engineering plastics)
PC5. Ensure that the plastic waste are mixed with additives, fillers (if any) before being fed into
the hopper
PC6. Conduct a test process and produce a sample output as per requirement
PC7. Ensure that the inspection and dimension of the output pellets are inspected and measured
as per the process given in the Work Instructions/ SOP
PC8. In case the test product or pellet matches the quality of the final output, start the production
process
Conduct the actual process with final setting as per product approval
