WBSC/AMH/Q1901: Garment Maker
Brief Job Description
The Garment Maker is responsible for the end-to-end process of garment production from material
selection to final quality checks. This role requires proficiency in sewing techniques, pattern drafting and
cost estimation to ensure high-quality finished products that meet design specifications.
Personal Attributes
A Garment Maker demonstrates precision, creativity and attention to detail, along with strong technical
skills in drafting, stitching and finishing. They maintain a disciplined, safety-conscious and customer-
oriented attitude. Adaptability, teamwork and entrepreneurial thinking further enhance their professional
growth.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. WBSC/AMH/N1901: Describe the roles of garment maker.
- 2. WBSC/AMH/N1902: Identify garment-making tool.
- 3. WBSC/AMH/N1903: Operate sewing machines efficiently.
- 4. WBSC/AMH/N1904: Make basic garment patterns using drafting techniques.
- 5. WBSC/AMH/N1905: Apply hand and machine stitching techniques.
- 6. WBSC/AMH/N1906: Cut and stitch garments.
- 7. WBSC/AMH/N1907: Ensure proper finishing of the produced garments.
- 8. WBSC/AMH/N1908: Estimate the cost of garments.
- 9. WBSC/AMH/N1909: Work in real job situation with emphasis on basic safety and hazards.
- 10. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Set up new business
- 1. WBSC/AMH/N1910: Set up a small garment business unit.

WBSC/AMH/N1901: Describe the roles of garment maker.
Description
Describe the roles of garment maker.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Unsafe working conditions in line with site policies.
Punctuality, responsibility, and respect for materials in practice.
Ergonomic principles in workstation setup and posture awareness.
Handling of customer queries, complaints, and alterations effectively.
Role-playing exercises to simulate customer interactions and upselling.
Customer feedback for service improvement.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify unsafe working conditions in line with site policies.
PC2. Demonstrate punctuality, responsibility, and respect for materials in practice.
PC3. Apply ergonomic principles in workstation setup and posture awareness.
PC4. Handle customer queries, complaints, and alterations effectively.
PC5. Engage in role-playing exercises to simulate customer interactions and upselling.
PC6. Analyze customer feedback for service improvement.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Procedures to achieve a safe working environment in line with occupational health and
safety regulations and requirements according to site policy.
KU2. Work ethics such as Punctuality, Quality of Work, Responsibility, Honesty and Transparency,
Teamwork, respect for Materials and Resources, time management, adaptability etc.
KU3. Ergonomics like proper Workstation Design, Tool and Equipment Placement, Posture
Awareness, Lighting and Ventilation, Use of Ergonomic Tools etc.
KU4. First-Aid, nature and causes of injury and utilization of first-aid.
KU5. Various employment opportunities for the job holder in the apparel industry.
KU6. Garment production process and the role of Garment maker in the process.
KU7. Handling of customer queries and complaints.
KU8. Management of customer order and alterations.
KU9. Sales techniques and upselling.
KU10. Customer feedback and improvement.

WBSC/AMH/N1902: Identify garment-making tool.
Description
Identify garment-making tool.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Different types of fabrics, threads, buttons, and fasteners used in garment making.
Selection of appropriate materials based on the design.
Demonstration of correct use of measuring tools.
Conversion of units of measurement.
Taking of body measurements accurately using measuring charts and a dummy.
Usage of cutting tools and marking tools effectively for different fabrics.
Cutting of fabrics precisely following patterns.
Adjustment of cutting techniques as required.
Maintenance cutting tools through proper cleaning and sharpening procedures.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify different types of fabrics, threads, buttons, and fasteners used in garment making.
PC2. Select appropriate materials based on the design.
PC3. Demonstrate correct use of measuring tools.
PC4. Convert units of measurement.
PC5. Take body measurements accurately using measuring charts and a dummy.
PC6. Use cutting tools and marking tools effectively for different fabrics.
PC7. Cut fabrics precisely following patterns.
PC8. Adjust cutting techniques as required.
PC9. Maintain cutting tools through proper cleaning and sharpening procedures.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Types of fabrics and their use in garment industry (cotton, silk, polyester).
KU2. Different types of threads (cotton, polyester, nylon) and their uses based on fabric type.
KU3. Buttons and Fasteners: Types of buttons (plastic, metal, wooden) and their application.
KU4. Fabric grain and its importance in cutting.
KU5. Selection of materials based on the design.
KU6. Different measuring tools: measuring tape, ruler, French curve, and L-square.
KU7. How to read a measuring tape used by tailor.

WBSC/AMH/N1903: Operate sewing machines efficiently.
Description
Operate sewing machines efficiently.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Major sewing machine parts and their functions.
Difference between types of sewing machines and their uses.
Selection of suitable needle, thread, and machine for fabric types.
Demonstration of threading, tension adjustment, and basic machine operation.
Routine cleaning and oiling of machine components.
Common machine issues like thread breakage or skipped stitches.
Repairing of machine faults using correct methods and tools.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify major sewing machine parts and their functions.
PC2. Differentiate between types of sewing machines and their uses.
PC3. Select suitable needle, thread, and machine for fabric types.
PC4. Demonstrate threading, tension adjustment, and basic machine operation.
PC5. Perform routine cleaning and oiling of machine components.
PC6. Diagnose common machine issues like thread breakage or skipped stitches.
PC7. Repair machine faults using correct methods and tools.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Overview of sewing machines and their uses in garment production.
KU2. The major parts of the sewing machines such as needle, bobbin, presser foot, feed dogs etc
and their functions.
KU3. Types of machines: Manual & Motorized sewing machine, buttonhole machines
KU4. Types of Sewing Machines beds: flatbed, raised bed, post bed, cylindrical bed, and side bed.
KU5. Types of belts used: flat belt and V-belt.
KU6. The cleaning process and cleaning of oil, dirt from the machine.
KU7. Common machine problems such as thread breakage, skipped stitches.

WBSC/AMH/N1904: Make basic garment patterns using drafting
techniques.
Description
Make basic garment patterns using drafting techniques.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Drafting of accurate garment patterns using drafting techniques.
Key technical terms in pattern making.
Pattern manipulation and grading for different sizes.
Cutting of basic pattern blocks for various garments.
Transferring of paper patterns onto a fabric.
Sketching of key garment components such as necklines, sleeves, and collars.
Creation of patterns for men's, women's, and children's garments with proper measurements.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Draft accurate garment patterns using drafting techniques.
PC2. Explain key technical terms in pattern making.
PC3. Demonstrate pattern manipulation and grading for different sizes.
PC4. Cut basic pattern blocks for various garments.
PC5. Transfer paper patterns onto a fabric.
PC6. Sketch key garment components such as necklines, sleeves, and collars.
PC7. Create patterns for men's, women's, and children's garments with proper measurements.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Drafting and Paper Patterns: Types, Importance, Pattern drafting
KU2. Familiarization of technical terms used like pattern, block, dart, seam allowance, grain line,
notch, ease, drafting, yoke, placket, muslin etc.
KU3. Principles of pattern drafting.
KU4. Pattern a bodice block.
KU5. Patterns manipulation techniques.
KU6. Pattern grading Size M, L, XL.
KU7. Overview of different garments: Mens kurta, pyjama, shirt, pant, Womens petticoat, blouse,
nightwear, salwar, kurti, Childrens wear.
KU8. Drafting and patterns making on brown paper & transfer all on a cloth, cut with the scissor.
