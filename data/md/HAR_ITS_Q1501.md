#### HAR/ITS/N0908: IT Foundation and Digital Tools (2)
Description
The NOS provides basic knowledge and skills to operate computer and prepares you to perform basic
office jobs.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS builds essential skills in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Internet usage, preparing students
for basic office tasks and serving as a foundation for advanced computer applications.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand the meaning, characteristics, history, generations, and classification of
computers.
- PC2. Identify and explain the main components, input/output devices, and the working of a
computer system.
- PC3. Differentiate between data and information, and perform basic number conversions.
- PC4. Demonstrate understanding of operating systems, including the settings and functions of
Windows..
- PC5. Diagnose and resolve common peripheral malfunctions and connectivity issues.
- PC6. Create and edit documents using word processing tools with proper formatting and layout.
- PC7. Apply formulas and functions in spreadsheets and use spreadsheet tools effectively.
- PC8. Design and present information visually using digital presentation software.
- PC9. Develop correct typing posture and accuracy with improved speed.
- PC10. Utilize networking and internet skills to communicate online, manage email accounts, ensure
cybersecurity, and understand cloud storage
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Demonstrates understanding of the evolution, characteristics, and classification of computers,
including their historical development and technological generations.
- KU2. Identifies and explains the main hardware components, input/output devices, and the overall
functional process of a computer system.
- KU3. Explains the purpose, functions, and configuration of operating systems, particularly Windows,
in managing computer resources and settings.
- KU4. Understands methods for diagnosing and resolving common hardware, peripheral, and connectivity issues within a computer system.

#### HAR/ITS/N1501: Introduction to Accounting with Tally (90 hrs.)
Description
The NOS covers basics of accounting using Tally software, focusing on ledger creation, voucher management, and generating financial reports for efficient business accounting.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Covers the fundamentals of accounting principles and concepts along with practical application using Tally
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain basic accounting concepts, principles, and types of accounts.
- PC2. Apply rules of debit and credit to record financial transactions accurately.
- PC3. Prepare journal entries and post them into ledger accounts.
- PC4. Maintain subsidiary books such as cash book, purchase book, sales book, and journal proper.
- PC5. Prepare and analyze trial balance and identify and rectify accounting errors.
- PC6. Prepare final accounts including Trading Account, Profit & Loss Account, and Balance Sheet
with adjustments.
- PC7. Create and configure a company in Tally.ERP9 with appropriate features and manage
accounting masters, including groups and ledgers.
- PC8. Create and maintain inventory masters, and record accounting and inventory transactions
using appropriate vouchers and invoicing.
- PC9. Perform statutory compliance (GST, TDS, TCS, EPF, ESIC), manage payroll, ensure data
security, and generate financial, inventory, and statutory reports.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Basic accounting concepts, principles, and conventions, including types of accounts and rules
of debit and credit.
- KU2. Accounting processes such as journalizing, ledger posting, trial balance, rectification of errors,
and preparation of final accounts.
- KU3. Computerized accounting systems and working knowledge of Tally.ERP9, including company creation, masters, vouchers, and reporting.
- KU4. The role of an Accounts Assistant within an organization and coordination with finance, HR,
and operations teams.
- KU5. Workflow in accounting processes, reporting structures, and deadlines in a professional environment.

#### HAR/ITS/N1502: Forms  of Organization and Financial Statement Analysis
Description
This NOS covers the understanding of different forms of business organizations such as sole proprietorship,
partnership, HUF, and companies along with their features and significance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes knowledge of organizational structures, types of partnerships and companies,
and the importance of goodwill in business operations. It further covers analysis and interpretation
of financial statements using liquidity, solvency, activity, and profitability ratios to assess business
performance and financial health.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify and differentiate various forms of business organizations including sole proprietorship,
HUF, partnership, and companies based on their features and functions.
- PC2. Classify different types of partnerships and companies according to business requirements
and legal structure.
- PC3. Interpret the purpose, objectives, and users of financial statement analysis in business
decision-making.
- PC4. Apply appropriate financial statement analysis tools for evaluating business performance.
- PC5. Calculate liquidity, solvency, activity, and profitability ratios using financial data accurately.
- PC6. Analyze and interpret accounting ratios to assess the financial position and operational
efficiency of a business.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The meaning, characteristics, advantages, and limitations of different forms of business organizations.
- KU2. The concepts of sole proprietorship, HUF business, partnership, goodwill, and companies along
with their types.
- KU3. Different users and tools of financial statement analysis.
- KU4. methods of interpretation of financial statements for evaluating business performance and financial position.
- KU5. The role of different business organizations in commercial and economic activities.
- KU6. How to apply financial analysis concepts for supporting organizational planning and decision-
making.

#### HAR/ITS/N1503: Indian Financial System
Description
This NOS provides understanding of the structure, components, and functioning of the Indian Financial
System including financial institutions, markets, instruments, and regulatory bodies.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes knowledge of banking systems, NBFCs, insurance, mutual funds, financial markets, and regulatory frameworks governing the Indian financial sector.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the structure, components, and evolution of the Indian Financial System.
- PC2. Differentiate between various financial institutions such as commercial banks, cooperative
banks, NBFCs, development banks, insurance companies, and mutual funds.
- PC3. Identify the functions and features of money markets and capital markets in the Indian
economy.
- PC4. Distinguish between primary and secondary markets, and between money market and capital
market instruments.
- PC5. Identify and classify various financial instruments including deposit products, loans, stocks,
bonds, and insurance policies.
- PC6. Explain the objectives and importance of financial inclusion and government initiatives related
to financial access.
- PC7. Analyze challenges in the financial sector including NPAs, cybersecurity threats, and issues
related to financial inclusion.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The structure, components, functions, and evolution of the Indian Financial System including
financial institutions, markets, instruments, and regulatory authorities.
- KU2. The concepts of financial services, financial inclusion, government initiatives, and challenges
such as NPAs, cybersecurity threats, and access to credit in the financial sector.
- KU3. The role of financial institutions and markets in economic growth and business operations.
- KU4. how to interpret regulatory guidelines and compliance requirements applicable to financial
institutions and markets.
- KU5. how to demonstrate awareness of risk management, cybersecurity, and responsible financial practices in organizational contexts.
#### Generic Skills (GS)

#### HAR/ITS/N1504: Taxation (Income Tax & GST)
Description
This NOS provides knowledge of the taxation system including direct and indirect taxes, income tax
provisions, TDS, GST, and e-filing procedures
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes understanding of income tax concepts, residential status, taxable income computation, tax deductions, TDS procedures, and online filing of Income Tax Returns. It also covers
GST structure, registration, input tax credit, GST return filing processes, compliance requirements,
and challenges related to taxation practices.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify and differentiate various types of taxes, taxable income heads, deductions,
exemptions, and GST components applicable under the taxation system.
- PC2. understand and identify taxable income from salary, house property, business/profession,
capital gains, and other sources as per applicable tax provisions.
- PC3. Perform online filing of Income Tax Returns (ITR), TDS returns, and GST returns using the
prescribed forms and procedures.
- PC4. Interpret GST compliance requirements including registration, input tax credit, return filing,
due dates, and penalties for non-compliance.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The concepts of direct and indirect taxation, income tax provisions, GST structure, taxable
income, deductions, exemptions, TDS, and return filing procedures.
- KU2. The methods of computation of income under different heads, GST compliance requirements, e-filing processes, and applicable taxation rules and regulations.
- KU3. The importance of taxation compliance, financial record maintenance, and statutory reporting
in organizations and businesses.
- KU4. Taxation procedures, return filing practices, and regulatory requirements in accordance with
organizational and legal standards.

User/individual on the job needs to know how to:

| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |

| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
