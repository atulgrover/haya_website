TNSD/CSC/Q0201: Manufacturing Assembler - Electrical & Electronics.
Brief Job Description
This qualification pack for the Manufacturing Assembler - Electrical & Electronics job role is designed to
equip learners with the essential skills and knowledge required for efficient assembly of electrical and
electronic components.
Personal Attributes
Upon successful completion, learners will be able to: Perform skilled assembly of electrical and electronic
components to industry standards. Contribute effectively to manufacturing processes within
Electronics/Electrical engineering industries.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. TNSD/CSC/N0212: Perform Basic Manufacturing Operations.
- 2. TNSD/CSC/N0213: Conduct Engineering Measurements.

TNSD/CSC/N0212: Perform Basic Manufacturing Operations.
Description
Able to understand the different types of hand tools Able to understand machine tools used in machine
shop and their applications for making different jobs/exercises as per specified drawing. Able to check the
parts/exercises with the metrological instruments.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Learn about classification of metals with the properties & uses of ferrous and non-ferrous metals &
alloys with the scope of heat treatment of process.
Awareness of properties and uses of polymers and insulating materials.
Familiarize with Drilling and related operations.
Awareness of different machining practices like Turning, Milling and grinding.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand the basic safety rules, shop floor safety, and know about the use of personal
protective equipment, First Aid and OSH
PC2. Familiarize with ferrous metals and alloys
PC3. Familiarize with the scope of heat treatment of metals and alloys in Engineering field.
PC4. Perform Basic fitting operation - Filing, Marking, Hack sawing, Drilling, Taping, Chipping, and
Grinding as accuracy of 0.1mm
PC5. Understand different fit of components for assembling as per required tolerance , observing
principle of interchange ability and check for functionality
PC6. Perform different operations on Lathe, Milling, Grinding, following personal and equipment
safety with standard operating procedure.
PC7. Learn Different types of Fasteners and its applications
PC8. Familiarize with Different fit of components for assembling as per required tolerance
observing principle of interchangeability and check for functionality.
PC9. Familiarize with the properties & uses of ferrous and non-ferrous metals & alloys and
properties and uses of rubber, timber and insulating materials
PC10. Produce components involving bench work, different operations on Lathe, Milling and
Grinding machines observing standard procedure and check for accuracy.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand the basic safety rules, shop floor safety, and know about the use of personal
protective equipment, First Aid and OSH

TNSD/CSC/N0213: Conduct Engineering Measurements.
Description
Understand the usage of different measuring instruments Familiarization with different checking gauges
used in tool room/manufacturing. Understand the system of limits &fits used in the manufacturing
industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the usage of different measuring tools & familiarization with different checking methods
Understand the importance of limits and fits in engineering practices.
Understand the system of GD& T
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand the object of measurements & Familiarization with measuring Instruments
PC2. Understand the purpose of marking & distinguish different system of marking.
PC3. Familiarize with marking tools
PC4. Understand the usage of different angle measuring instruments
PC5. Understand the importance of Limits, Fits & Tolerance
PC6. Familiarize with different gauges used for checking
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand the object of measurements & Familiarization with measuring Instruments.
KU2. Understand the purpose of marking & distinguish different system of marking.
KU3. Understand the usage of different angle measuring instruments
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Familiarize with marking tools

TNSD/CSC/N0214: Interpret and Create Basic Engineering Drawings.
Description
Read and understand engineering drawing so that items can be manufactured as per dimensions and
specifications mentioned. Draw Board drawings of parts which are required to be manufactured.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Ability to read engineering drawing.
Understand the different types of threads and its representation.
Understand simple mechanical assemblies.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Learn the importance of engineering drawing, Practice on drawing instruments and write
engineering script.
PC2. Understand the importance of Drawing standards.
PC3. Construct standard geometrical shapes and curves in engineering practices
PC4. Draw Free Hand sketching.
PC5. Draw different views of Orthographic projection
PC6. Prepare 3 dimensional drawings of a given object.
PC7. Prepare Sectional views with dimensional drawing.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Learn the importance of engineering drawing, Practice on drawing instruments and write
engineering script.
KU2. Understand the importance of Drawing standards.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Prepare Sectional views with dimensional drawing.

TNSD/CSC/N0215: Execute Basic Electrical Wiring and Panel Assembly
Description
Able to understand the basic electrical components and their functions. Able to connect the circuits for
domestic and industrial applications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand different electrical sub system.
Understand the functionality of various Protective devices.
Understand the working procedure of AC & DC Machines
Familiarize electrical measuring instruments
Familiarize the Cables & Connectors used in AC &DC Circuits
Learn to Realize the need of Electrical Safety
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. learn the Construction of different electronics sub system and test electronic devices and sub
system.
PC2. Understand to Estimate and perform panel wiring using different types of cables & connectors
PC3. Understand the working procedure of AC & DC Machines
PC4. Understand the basics of different types of cores & connectors and usage
PC5. Familiarize Cables & Connectors used in DC Circuits & AC Circuits
PC6. Realize the need of Electrical Safety
PC7. Learn to Construct different electrical sub-systems and measure parameters.
PC8. Estimate and perform panel wiring using cables, connectors, Protective devices and test
functions
PC9. Demonstrate the troubleshoot and repair different Electrical, Electronic systems/ devices.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand to Estimate and perform panel wiring using different types of cables & connectors
KU2. Understand the working procedure of AC & DC Machines
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Familiarize Cables & Connectors used in DC Circuits & AC Circuits
