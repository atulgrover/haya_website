#### PSC/Q0603: Water  Treatment Plant Assistant Operator
Brief Job Description
A Water Treatment Plant Assistant Operator is responsible for treating wastewater in water treatment
plants. The individual carries out various processes for effective water treatment and effluent management
in compliance with the applicable environmental regulations. The person uses different water treatment
plant equipment and also carries out their basic repair and maintenance.
Personal Attributes
WTP Assistant operator requires meticulous attention to detail, strong problem-solving skills, and a
methodical approach to managing wastewater treatment processes. Adaptability to changing situations, a
commitment to environmental stewardship, and adherence to safety protocols are crucial. Excellent
analytical abilities, a proactive attitude, and effective communication skills are essential for coordinating
with teams and ensuring compliance. Additionally, the role demands a strong sense of responsibility,
integrity, and a willingness to learn and implement innovative solutions for sustainable wastewater
management.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PSC/N0610: Perform Various Processes to Treat Wastewater
2. PSC/N0611: Maintain the Equipment of Water Treatment Plant
3. PSC/N0612: Perform Support Activities in the Disposal of Treated Effluent
4. PSC/N0603: Maintain Records & Reporting at Site
5. PSC/N0602: Health and Safety Practices at the treatment Plant

#### PSC/N0610: Perform  Various Processes to Treat Wastewater
Description
Water Treatment Plant (WTP) operations entails managing treatment processes to purify wastewater. This
involves receiving effluent, applying primary, secondary, and tertiary treatment methods, ensuring proper
settling and filtration, and disinfecting the treated water. Regular equipment checks, chemical dosage, and
monitoring treatment efficiency are crucial. Adjusting operations as per variations in effluent
characteristics and maintaining compliance with environmental standards are key.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out preliminary, primary and secondary water treatment
Carry out advanced water treatment
Treat sludge and control the odour
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out preliminary, primary and secondary water treatment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Draw water in the appropriate quantity from the wastewater source, such as a river or lake.
- PC2. Carry out preliminary treatment of water, using bar screens and mechanical rakes to remove
large debris such as rags, plastics, and other solid waste.
- PC3. Carry out primary treatment of wastewater using primary clarifiers and sedimentation tanks.
- PC4. Skim off oils and greases floating on the top of the water using the appropriate tools and
equipment.
- PC5. Aerate the water using diffused/ mechanical aerators or oxidation ditches to break down
organic matter.
- PC6. Carry out secondary sedimentation by transferring water to secondary clarifiers where to
remove activated sludge.
Carry out advanced water treatment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Remove the remaining suspended solids and contaminants using sand filters, activated
carbon filters, or membrane filters
- PC8. Remove nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus by following the appropriate biological or
chemical processes to prevent eutrophication.
- PC9. Eliminate the remaining pathogens using chlorine, Ultraviolet (UV) light, or ozone, as
appropriate.
Treat sludge and control the odour
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Treat the sludge following the appropriate methods, such as thickening, digestion and
dewatering
- PC11. Dispose of the dewatered sludge or re-use it for appropriate purpose, e.g. soil conditioning

#### PSC/N0611: Maintain the Equipment  of Water Treatment Plant
Description
Monitoring and managing Water Treatment Plant (WTP) involves overseeing effluent quality, flow rates,
and treatment processes. This includes regular testing for compliance with set standards, optimizing
treatment efficiency, and maintaining equipment. Effective sludge management, data recording for
compliance, and emergency preparedness are essential.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out preventive maintenance
Carry out corrective maintenance
Carry out documentation and reporting
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out preventive maintenance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Treat the effluent following appropriate processes, such as additional filtration or advanced
oxidation to remove any contaminants
- PC2. Coordinate appropriate tests on the effluent to ensure it meets the regulatory standards for
safe disposal
- PC3. Dispose of the effluent following the appropriate disposal methods, such as Surface Water
Discharge, Groundwater Recharge, re-use and recycling, etc.
- PC4. Ensure compliance with the environmental regulations and standards applicable to effluent
disposal
- PC5. Follow contingency plans for handling accidental spills, equipment failures, or other
emergencies that could impact effluent disposal
Carry out corrective maintenance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Conduct a root cause analysis to determine the cause for the equipment malfunction or
failure
- PC7. Isolate the faulty equipment from the system and follow lockout/tagout procedures to ensure
safety
- PC8. Repair or replace the faulty equipment components, as appropriate
- PC9. Use the manufacturer-recommended spare parts and maintenance tools
- PC10. Calibrate the equipment to ensure it meets the required specifications and standards
- PC11. Conduct appropriate tests to check for the correct functioning of the repaired equipment
- PC12. Carry out repair and maintenance within the appropriate durations as per organizational
policy, to minimize potential water supply issues
- PC13. Coordinate with the equipment manufacturer for complex repair and maintenance needs
Carry out documentation and reporting

#### PSC/N0603: Maintain Records &  Reporting at Site
Description
This unit focuses on the importance of maintaining accurate records and effectively reporting activities at
a sewage treatment plant. It emphasizes the significance of documentation in ensuring compliance with
regulations, monitoring plant performance, and facilitating continuous improvement. Trainees will learn
how to record data, prepare reports, and communicate relevant information to stakeholders.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand how to maintain records and data
Reporting to the higher officials
Respond to higher officials
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand how to maintain records and data
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. able to read plan & specifications for different types of modifications in sewage treatment
plant.
- PC2. able to document data on pathogen reduction.
- PC3. able to record the data on sludge accumulated and sludge treated.
- PC4. able to maintain daily operational log.
- PC5. perform routine operational duties, flow meter readings, sample information with
corresponding analytical data, etc.
- PC6. able to identify unusual conditions at the site
- PC7. identify and report accident equipment hazard.
- PC8. perform preventive maintenance
Reporting to the higher officials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. reporting to the supervisor if any kind of problem occurs
- PC10. identify spare parts inventory when in need
- PC11. identify specifications on equipment from supplier
- PC12. equipment inventory
Respond to higher officials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. able to responding promptly to higher officials' inquiries or requests demonstrates a sense of
urgency and professionalism.
- PC14. able to provide clear and accurate information in responses to higher officials ensures
effective communication and minimizes misunderstandings.
