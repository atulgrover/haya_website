#### ELE/Q6706: Optoelectronic Technology
Brief Job Description
This comprehensive training program equips participants with the skills to design, install, test, maintain,
and optimize optoelectronic systems, including LEDs, sensors, displays, and photovoltaic applications.
Covering Health & Safety, customer interaction, system design, installation, maintenance, and energy
optimization, the program combines theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical experience using
industry-standard tools and software. Participants will gain the expertise to deliver efficient, safe, and
sustainable optoelectronic solutions in professional settings.
Personal Attributes
Detail-oriented, safety-conscious, and technically proficient professional with strong problem-solving
abilities, hands-on practical skills, and effective communication for delivering efficient and sustainable
optoelectronic solutions.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ELE/N6725: Work organization and management (Optoelectronic Technology)
2. ELE/N6726: Communication and interpersonal skills (Optoelectronic Technology)
3. ELE/N6727: Design and manufacturing of optoelectronic applications (Optoelectronic Technology)
4. ELE/N6728: Installation and implementation of optoelectronic applications (Optoelectronic
Technology)
5. ELE/N6729: Maintenance of optoelectronic applicationsof security systems (Optoelectronic
Technology)
6. ELE/N6730: Optimization of optoelectronic applications (Optoelectronic Technology)

Technology)
Description
This NOS is about to plan, organize, and prioritize tasks effectively to ensure timely design, installation,
and maintenance of optoelectronic systems while.
Scope

Work organization and management
#### Elements and Performance Criteria

documentation in all workshop operations.

systems.

#### ELE/N6727: Design and manufacturing  of optoelectronic applications
(Optoelectronic Technology)
Description
This NOS is about to design optoelectronic systems and applications by applying engineering principles,
component selection, circuit design, and performance analysis. Manufacture, assemble, and test optoelectronic devices such as LEDs, sensors, displays, and photovoltaic systems to ensure quality,
efficiency, and reliability.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Design and manufacturing of optoelectronic applications
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Analyse the important parameters and functional requirements of the optoelectronic
application environment.
- PC2. Design optoelectronic application schemes according to industry requirements and
application needs.
- PC3. Manufacture simple optoelectronic applications following the designed schemes.
- PC4. Inspect and ensure the manufacturing quality and operational standards of optoelectronic
applications.
- PC5. Test and select correct specifications for various optoelectronic products, including
photovoltaic products, LED luminaires, optoelectronic transmission and display equipment, sensors, and laser systems.
- PC6. Select appropriate control equipment, photovoltaic power generation systems, sensors, laser
systems, application terminals, and control platforms to achieve energy-saving and efficiency goals in integrated applications.
- PC7. Analyse the application environment to optimize manufacturing schemes, energy-saving
measures, and light efficiency design schemes.
- PC8. Select, configure, and optimize optical and electronic components, including drive and heat
dissipation modules, for high-performance optoelectronic applications.
- PC9. Test the function and performance parameters of optical and electronic components or
optoelectronic products to ensure compliance with specifications.
- PC10. Apply industry-aligned software tools (e.g., DIALux, LED simulation platforms, Arduino-based
controllers) to simulate, programme, and validate lighting and sensor-based applications.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

#### ELE/N6728: Installation and implementation of optoelectronic
applications (Optoelectronic Technology)
Description
This NOS is defines the competencies required to install, integrate, test, and commission optoelectronic
systems in accordance with technical specifications, safety standards, and application requirements
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Installation and implementation of optoelectronic applications
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Read and interpret engineering drawings, wiring diagrams, schematics, technical manuals,
and engineering instructions accurately.
- PC2. Analyze customer requirements for optoelectronic system applications and identify
functional and efficiency requirements.
- PC3. Select appropriate optical and electronic components according to application needs,
functional parameters, and efficiency design schemes.
- PC4. Test relevant parameters and characteristics of optical and electronic components, sensors,
laser systems, and control devices.
- PC5. Select and configure suitable optoelectronic application schemes according to demand
analysis and application environment.
- PC6. Select, configure, and optimize drive and heat dissipation modules based on application
terminal structure and power requirements.
- PC7. Provide system structure drawings, construction drawings, and related technical
documentation for installations.
- PC8. Install and commission equipment, components, devices, upgrades, or refurbished
optoelectronic equipment.
- PC9. Use a variety of optoelectronic display devices, LED panels, screens, sensors, and
photovoltaic power generation equipment effectively.
- PC10. Select energy-saving control methods and test the characteristics of optoelectronic
application equipment and system control platforms
- PC11. Install and perform basic configuration of opto sensors and cameras, including connection,
alignment, and computer-based setup.
- PC12. Install, debug, and operate the optoelectronic application system, ensuring normal operation
and functionality.
- PC13. Programme or configure Arduino-based smart lighting control systems and basic image
display devices.
- PC14. Programme or configure Arduino-based smart lighting control systems and basic image
display devices.

#### ELE/N6729: Maintenance  of optoelectronic applicationsof security
systems (Optoelectronic Technology)
Description
This NOS is about to defines the competencies required to inspect, troubleshoot, service, and maintain
optoelectronic-based security systems to ensure reliable performance and compliance with safety
standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintenance of optoelectronic applicationsof security systems
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Read and interpret construction drawings, technical data, and documents for optoelectronic
application systems.
- PC2. Analyze the working principles of optoelectronic applications, signal transmission processes,
and characteristic parameters of each system component.
- PC3. Identify the causes of faults in optoelectronic systems and select appropriate fault detection
schemes based on fault phenomena.
- PC4. Diagnose and replace faulty equipment, modules, lines, parts, and components in
optoelectronic applications.
- PC5. Use standard optoelectronic testing tools, instruments, and maintenance equipment safely
and effectively.
- PC6. Perform maintenance on each module of optoelectronic application systems to ensure proper
operation.
- PC7. Modify control platform parameters to optimize system performance after maintenance
operations.
- PC8. Debug and optimize optoelectronic application systems to ensure correct functionality.
- PC9. Test optoelectronic applications after maintenance to verify proper operation and
performance.
- PC10. Apply diagnostic methods to identify and correct errors in sensor/camera configurations and
smart lighting control systems.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Comprehensive knowledge of construction drawings, system layouts, wiring diagrams, and technical documentation to accurately interpret optoelectronic application system
requirements.
