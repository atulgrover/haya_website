ELE/Q6704: Autonomous Mobile Robotics
Brief Job Description
A Robotics Expert is a skilled professional involved in the design, development, maintenance, and
implementation of robotic systems, including Autonomous Mobile Robotics (AMR), across diverse
industries. They operate in various environments such as offices, manufacturing plants, and research
laboratories focusing on solving sector specific technical and operational challenges. In manufacturing, for
example, robotics technologists may develop robotic automation solutions to enhance productivity or
design autonomous mobile robots for tasks in hazardous, complex, or hard to reach areas. The role
requires strong expertise in robotics fundamentals, including logic design, microprocessors, programming,
and mechanical, electrical, and control systems. These professionals work collaboratively with
multidisciplinary teams to build efficient, reliable, and high performing robotic and AMR solutions.
Personal Attributes
In manufacturing, robotics technologists may create robots to automate tasks and increase productivity, or
they might design mobile robots for exploration in dangerous or hard-to-reach areas. The role requires a
strong understanding of robotics, including logic, microprocessors, computer programming, and
mechanical, electrical, and control systems. Robotics technologists collaborate with multiple disciplines to
design and build the most efficient and effective robots.

ELE/N6708: Work organization and management (Autonomous Mobile
Robotics)
Description
This NOS unit is about proper training, the correct use of equipment, and adherence to safety protocols in
manufacturing settings.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Principles and Applications of Safe Working
Purposes, Uses, Care, and Maintenance of Equipment and Materials
Environmental and Safety Principles in Good Housekeeping
Principles of Team Working and Their Applications
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Maintain a clean, safe, and well-organized work area in accordance with workplace
housekeeping and environmental guidelines.
PC2. Prepare self appropriately for assigned tasks, ensuring full compliance with health, safety, and
personal protective requirements.
PC3. Schedule work activities efficiently to optimize productivity and minimize disruption to ongoing
operations.
PC4. Follow all rules, regulations, and standard operating procedures applicable to robotics
technician/engineering tasks.
PC5. Select appropriate tools, equipment, and materials required for the task.
PC6. Use all tools, equipment, and materials safely and strictly as per manufacturer’s instructions.
PC7. Demonstrate adherence to safety standards for the environment, equipment, materials, and
work processes.
PC8. Restore the work area to its proper condition after completing tasks, ensuring safe and ready-
to-use status.
PC9. Contribute effectively to team tasks by providing support, receiving and giving feedback, and
demonstrating collaborative behaviour.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Knowledge of mobile robot design and development processes.
KU2. Familiarity with the diverse applications of mobile robotics in various industries like
autonomous vehicles, manufacturing, agriculture, aerospace, mining, and medicine.

ELE/N6707: Design (Autonomous Mobile Robotics)
Description
This NOS unit is about the principles and applications of project design, including the process of creating
detailed project specifications and evaluating manufactured items based on specific criteria.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Principles and Applications of Project Design
Nature and Formats of Project Specifications
Bases for Appraisal of Manufactured Items
Design Parameters
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Analyse project briefs and specifications to determine the required performance, functionality,
and operational characteristics of mobile robotic systems.
PC2. Identify and resolve uncertainties, ambiguities, or missing information within project briefs or
specifications.
PC3. Assess environmental conditions, operating constraints, and usage scenarios for which the
mobile robot is being designed.
PC4. Identify and define hardware requirements—including mechanical, electrical, electronic, and
control components—needed to support the robot’s performance.
PC5. Generate design concepts and develop detailed project designs for manufacturing a functional
robotic system within specified timelines.
PC6. Design tele-operation control systems independent of the robot’s base platform, ensuring
compatibility and performance.
PC7. Develop strategies for solving mobile robotics tasks such as navigation, obstacle avoidance,
localisation, orientation, and mission execution.
PC8. Evaluate and select optimal options for materials, components, add-ons, and manufacturing
processes based on cost, purpose, feasibility, and performance.
PC9. Complete the design and documentation process—including drawings, specifications, and
standards—within defined cost, purpose, and time constraints.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understanding the concept of project design and its phases
KU2. Application of design principles to various project types
KU3. Integration of functional, aesthetic, and practical considerations in design

ELE/N6711: Navigation and localization (Autonomous Mobile Robotics)
Description
This NOS unit is about navigation and localization of Robotics
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Implement Navigation Strategies
Develop Sensor-Based Environment Detection
Implement and Test Map-Based Navigation
Implement and Test Map Generation
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Implement navigation strategies for mobile robots based on orientation, mapping, and
environmental conditions.
PC2. Develop and use sensor-based methods to detect, interpret, and understand the robot’s
surroundings.
