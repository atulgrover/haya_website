#### CSC/Q0905: Mechanical  Automation & Maintenance  Engineer
Brief Job Description
A Mechanical Automation & Maintenance Engineer is a professional responsible for designing, installing,
maintaining, and upgrading mechanical systems, equipment, and machines that are controlled by automated systems.
Personal Attributes
The person should be result oriented with good technical and analytical skills, should have Excellent
Interpersonal Skills, communication and presentation skills and a good team player. They should have
ability to manage projects, prioritizing of work and mentoring the budding engineers.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CSC/N0920: Select & install new machinery and automation systems as per Requirements.
2. CSC/N0921: Manage mechanical issues with machinery and automation systems
3. CSC/N0333: Lead Maintenance Team for the Repair & Maintain of Mechanical Automation System
(including Controllers & Hydraulic/Pneumatic equipment)
4. CSC/N1339: Collaboratively coordinate with the team
5. CSC/N0505: Follow health, safety and environment guidelines at workplace
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### CSC/N0920: Select & install new machinery and automation systems as
per Requirements.
Description
This unit is about to Manage mechanical issues with machinery and automation systems.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform Inspection on Mechanical Automation System.
Implement diagnostic tests or experiments to validate the root cause of the mechanical issues.
Interpret Troubleshooting activities on Mechanical Automation Systems.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Select & Develop Mechanical Automation Systems Project Document and Component drawings.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify and understand the requirements and specifications of the mechanical automation
system project, including functional requirements, performance objectives, and constraints.
- PC2. Evaluate different design options, considering factors such as cost, manufacturability,
reliability, and scalability.
- PC3. Select appropriate mechanical components, subsystems, and materials based on project
requirements, performance criteria, and compatibility with existing systems and Ensure components meet relevant industry standards, regulations, and safety requirements.
Perform installation of Mechanical Automation system
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Review and understand technical documentation, including installation manuals, schematics,
and blueprints and Verify that all required components, parts, and tools are available before beginning the installation
- PC5. Assemble mechanical components, including actuators, conveyors, robotic arms, and other
machinery, according to manufacturer specifications and assembly instructions.
- PC6. Ensure proper alignment and positioning of mechanical components, using measuring tools
and alignment techniques to achieve accuracy and Install mounting brackets, fasteners, and support structures securely to provide stability and structural integrity
Perform post-installation activities of Mechanical Automation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Verify that all components of the mechanical automation system are installed correctly and
functioning as intended and Conduct functional tests to ensure that the system performs its intended tasks accurately and efficiently.
- PC8. Integrate the mechanical automation system with other systems and equipment within the
production environment and Ensure seamless communication and interoperability between different components and subsystems..

#### CSC/N0921: Manage   mechanical issues with machinery and automation
systems
Description
This unit is about to Manage mechanical issues with machinery and automation systems.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform Inspection on Mechanical Automation System.
implement diagnostic tests or experiments to validate the root cause of the mechanical issues.
Interpret Troubleshooting activities on Mechanical Automation Systems.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform Inspection on Mechanical Automation System.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Check for any visible signs of wear, damage, or corrosion on mechanical components such as
gears, belts, chains, and bearings and Inspect for loose fasteners, bolts, or screws that may indicate potential issues with the system's structural integrity
- PC2. Test safety features such as emergency stops, interlocks, and guards to verify they function
correctly and effectively interrupt the system's operation when activated.
- PC3. Verify the accuracy and repeatability of movements by checking positional accuracy and
alignment using measuring instruments or visual markers.
Implement diagnostic tests or experiments to validate the root cause of the mechanical issues.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Gather information from operators, maintenance logs, and observations to understand the
symptoms of the mechanical issue.
- PC5. Choose appropriate diagnostic tests or experiments to validate each hypothesis
- PC6. Perform functional tests on individual components or subsystems to assess their
performance under normal operating conditions.
Interpret Troubleshooting activities on Mechanical Automation Systems.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. identify and define the problem or issue affecting the mechanical automation system. Clearly
articulate the symptoms or anomalies observed during system operation.
- PC8. Optimize troubleshooting processes to achieve swift resolution without sacrificing accuracy.
- PC9. implement appropriate corrective actions based on the identified root cause and Ensure that
the corrective measures effectively address the problem and prevent its recurrence.
- PC10. Adhere to safety protocols and procedures throughout the troubleshooting process to
mitigate risks to personnel and equipment.Prioritize safety considerations when implementing corrective actions or conducting tests.

- PC1. systematically inspect mechanical automation systems to identify components that are

misalignment, improper lubrication, or material fatigue.

- PC8. avoid damage to sensitive electronic components due to negligence of ESD procedures

organisation

- PC11. ensure that other people follow all access control and perimeter safety procedures in
designated factory areas and help avoid accidents
- PC12. use emergency switches or other mechanisms of stopping a machine immediately in case
any emergency situation has developed or about to happen
- PC13. ensure that electrical equipment are properly grounded

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for the Qualification Pack will be created by CGSC.
2. Performance Criteria (PC) have been assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will also
lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each PC.
3. The assessment for the theory part will/may be based on knowledge bank of questions approved CGSC.
4. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
5. Assessment Agencies will create Assessor Guides comprising of Theory and Practical Assessment Set
and Guidelines for each examination/training centre (as per assessment criteria below). The same will be
approved by CGSC for adequacy.
6. To successfully attain Certification on the Qualification Pack, the trainee must score a minimum of 70%
in each Core NOS and minimum of 70% in all non-core NOS. In addition, a candidate needs to attain a
minimum overall pass percentage of 70% for certification.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
