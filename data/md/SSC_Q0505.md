#### SSC/Q0505: IT Technical Writer
Brief Job Description
An IT Technical Writer for IT services and application development is responsible for creating technical
documentation, such as job aids, help documents, user guides, reference manuals, training materials,
online help content, etc. These documents facilitate the transfer of knowledge between application
development teams and user teams. They collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather information,
utilize documentation tools, and ensure consistency with industry standards. Additionally, they format and
publish content across multiple platforms, maintaining and updating documents to reflect changes in
products or services.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have excellent collaboration skills to effectively engage with stakeholders and a
strong analytical mindset. They should stay informed about evolving technologies, demonstrate a self-
driven attitude, and manage tasks efficiently. Adaptability in a fast-paced environment and a continuous
drive for improvement and learning are also essential qualities.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SSC/N0703: Create and Manage Documentation for Knowledge Transfer
2. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### SSC/N0703: Create and Manage  Documentation  for Knowledge  Transfer
Description
This NOS focuses on creating, managing, and publishing technical documentation, including case studies,
best practices, project artefacts, reports, minutes, policies, procedures, and work instructions for
knowledge sharing with internal or external stakeholders. It involves collaborating with stakeholders, using
documentation tools, ensuring accuracy, and following organizational policies and guidelines.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand Documentation Requirements
Create Technical Documentation
Structure and Format Documentation
Collaborate with Development and User Teams
Use Documentation Tools and Technologies
Edit and Proofread Content
Publish Technical Documentation
Maintain and Update Documentation
Use GEN AI in Documentation Creation and Management
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand Documentation Requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the need for technical documentation such as job aids, help documents, user guides,
reference manuals, and training materials.
- PC2. collaborate with application development teams and user teams to understand the technical
and functional aspects of the application.
- PC3. conduct interviews or research to gather necessary information for documentation.
Create Technical Documentation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. write clear, concise, and accurate content for job aids, help documents, training guides, and
software manuals.
- PC5. alter the content to meet the needs of the target audience (end-users, developers, and
support teams).
- PC6. apply proper grammar, style, and tone for user-friendly documentation
Structure and Format Documentation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. organize content in a logical and easy-to-follow structure using headings, subheadings,
tables, and lists
- PC8. create navigable content with cross-references and indexing where necessary.
- PC9. ensure accessibility and usability, adhering to WCAG guidelines for users with disabilities

- PC26. incorporate GEN AI outputs into documentation workflows, ensuring the content is verified

#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the organizations standard operating procedures (SOPs), policies, procedures, and guidelines
for creating documents, especially for knowledge sharing
- KU2. the purpose and scope of the work to be carried out and the importance of staying within these boundaries for documentation tasks.
- KU3. how to identify the key stakeholders when developing documents (e.g., developers, subject
matter experts, end-users).
- KU4. the roles and responsibilities of the key stakeholders in the documentation process and how
to engage them effectively
- KU5. the procedures for approving, publishing, and updating the organization's knowledge base,
along with how to manage this knowledge effectively
- KU6. the importance of verifying and reviewing information for accuracy and relevance to ensure
it is fit for knowledge sharing
- KU7. how to gather feedback from stakeholders and users to improve documentation
- KU8. how to incorporate feedback to improve documents, enhancing clarity, quality, and utility
- KU9. the methods and techniques for publishing and approving documents, especially when working with others, ensuring content is accurate and aligned with organizational standards.
- KU10. how to use tools, templates, and language standards to convert raw technical information into organized, user-friendly documents.
- KU11. how to identify sources of information and reference materials for document creation, ensuring they are reliable.
- KU12. how to review documents to correct common editorial issues like grammar, formatting, and technical accuracy.
- KU13. how to follow change management procedures, including version control, approvals, and tracking document revisions to ensure documents are always up-to-date and correctly
versioned.
- KU14. the GEN AI technologies, particularly their applications in documentation tasks such as content generation, summarization, and editing
- KU15. how to use prompt engineering to interact effectively with GEN AI tools for generating accurate, relevant documentation content
#### Generic Skills (GS)

- GS1. maintain work-related notes and records
- GS2. read the relevant literature to learn about the latest developments in the work
- GS3. listen attentively to understand the information/ instructions being shared by the speaker
- GS4. communicate clearly and politely with co-workers and clients
- GS5. coordinate with co-workers to achieve work objectives
- GS6. plan and prioritize tasks to ensure timely completion
