SSC/Q8401: Product Manager - Web & Mobile
Brief Job Description
A Product Manager Web and Mobile is responsible for overseeing the full development lifecycle of web and
mobile solutions. The role involves creating detailed use cases, aligning business requirements, and
understanding customer needs. The individual also shapes the go-to-market strategies and drives
customer engagement and retention initiatives to ensure the success of the product
Personal Attributes
Individuals in this role must manage and collaborate with various stakeholders involved in the
development of Web or Mobile based solutions. They must lead team members and manage team
performance to achieve project success.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. SSC/N8401: Define product/solution strategy and oversee development lifecycle
- 2. SSC/N8127: Collect and define business requirements through client interaction
- 3. SSC/N8402: Finalize the MVP (Minimum Viable Product) based on the defined business
requirements
- 4. SSC/N8403: Define KPIs to monitor software performance, adoption, usability, and conversion
- 5. SSC/N8404: Define a go-to-market strategy for the product/solution
- 6. SSC/N8405: Define a customer engagement and retention strategy
- 7. SSC/N8406: Create and execute a project charter and monitor the project KPIs
- 8. SSC/N9011: Manage team performance to achieve project goals
- 9. SSC/N8452: Optimize Revenue, Security, and Accessibility in Web and Mobile Products
- 10. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

SSC/N8401: Define product/solution strategy and oversee development
lifecycle
Description
This unit is about creating a product/solution strategy for developing applications for web-based or mobile-
based platforms.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Product/Solution research
Product/solution strategy
Elements and Performance Criteria
Product/Solution research
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. evaluate the customer experience journey to identify and document key interaction points
and areas for improvement
PC2. conduct in-depth research on the competitive landscape, assessing competitors' products
and offerings to identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities
PC3. collaborate with cross-functional teams to define and propose solutions that address
identified customer needs, ensuring alignment with business goals
PC4. formulate a product differentiation and market positioning strategy by analyzing market
trends and customer segments, presenting at least two unique value propositions
PC5. select the appropriate platforms, regions, and languages for the application/solution based
on market research and target audience analysis
PC6. analyze market demand and customer feedback to identify and prioritize at least three key
product features
PC7. apply roadmap creation tools and prioritization techniques, such as MoSCoW and the Kano
Model, to develop and present a clear product roadmap
PC8. utilize prototyping and wireframing tools to visualize at least two early-stage product
features for stakeholder review
PC9. perform competitor product analysis by evaluating features, pricing, and user feedback to
support product differentiation strategy decisions
Product/Solution Strategy
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. determine the type of application/solution (e.g., web, native, hybrid, cross-platform) by
analyzing project requirements and customer needs, and justify the selection with
documented reasoning
PC11. develop a detailed product development roadmap, outlining key milestones, timelines, and
dependencies for each phase of the project

SSC/N8127: Collect and define business requirements through client
interaction
Description
This unit is about identifying the requirements of internal and external clients.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Determine the requirements
Define the requirements
Establish key success metrics
Elements and Performance Criteria
Determine the requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. assess the client's current business operations, identifying key processes, workflows, and
pain points
PC2. evaluate the client's technical capabilities, systems, and resources to ensure feasibility and
alignment with project goals
PC3. conduct business contextual discussions with the client to gather detailed insights into
business objectives and needs, ensuring a clear understanding of requirements
PC4. utilize tools and techniques such as user story mapping and affinity diagrams to structure
and prioritize requirements, ensuring comprehensive coverage of client needs
PC5. create user personas to represent the different end-users of the system, enhancing the
understanding of client requirements and guiding the development process
Define the requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. evaluate and clearly define the client’s business and technical requirements, ensuring they
are specific, measurable, and aligned with project goals
PC7. evaluate and clearly define the client’s business and technical requirements, ensuring they
are specific, measurable, and aligned with project goals
PC8. document the requirements comprehensively, using templates and standardized formats for
consistency and clarity
PC9. validate the requirements with internal and external stakeholders, ensuring alignment and
addressing any discrepancies
PC10. reinforce Agile methodology by breaking down requirements into actionable user stories or
tasks for iterative development and continuous feedback
Establish key success metrics

SSC/N8402: Finalize the MVP (Minimum Viable Product) based on the
defined business requirements
Description
This unit is about developing Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for different types of business requirements
Scope
The scope covers the following :
MVP research
MVP strategy
MVP development strategy
MVP feedback and iteration
Elements and Performance Criteria
MVP Research
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. conduct customer research and surveys to identify at least three major pain points or
problems faced by the target audience
PC2. propose solutions to address identified customer problems, ensuring they are feasible and
align with business objectives
PC3. collect and analyze feedback from customers on proposed solutions to refine the problem-
solving approach
MVP Strategy
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. set the design process and map out the user flow for the MVP, ensuring it is streamlined for
ease of use
PC5. identify and prioritize essential features for the MVP using the MoSCoW method, focusing on
"Must have" and "Should have" features for initial development
PC6. implement strategies for iterative testing and refinement of the MVP, incorporating feedback
loops to improve functionality
MVP development strategy
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. develop a detailed project plan with clear milestones, responsibilities, and timelines for
delivering the MVP
PC8. define and allocate the necessary budget and resources for MVP development, ensuring they
are in line with project goals
PC9. collaborate with cross-functional teams, including design, development, and marketing, to
ensure seamless MVP development and alignment with customer needs
PC10. conduct usability testing and A/B testing to validate design and feature choices, ensuring the
MVP meets user expectations
MVP feedback and iteration

PC11. release the MVP to early users and gather actionable feedback to evaluate the initial product
