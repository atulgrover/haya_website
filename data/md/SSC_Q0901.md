#### SSC/Q0901: Security Analyst
Brief Job Description
Individuals at this job are responsible for protecting organizational data by monitoring and analyzing
threats, coordinating responses to information security incidents, managing and securing cloud environments, and executing phishing and social engineering defenses. They ensure robust security
measures, mitigate risks, and maintain compliance with industry standards across digital platforms.
Personal Attributes
This job may require the individual to work independently and take decisions for his/her own area of work.
The individual should be result oriented and have a high attention for detail. The individual should also be
able to demonstrate communication skills, logical thinking along with willingness to undertake desk-based
job with long hours.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SSC/N0954: Monitor and Analyze Threats

3. SSC/N0956: Manage and Secure Cloud Data

#### SSC/N0954: Monitor and Analyze Threats
Description
The OS unit is about identifying, analyzing, and responding to potential security threats to ensure the
protection and integrity of digital systems.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Threat Monitoring and Analysis
Digital Forensics and Incident Investigation
Threat Analysis, Incident Investigation, and Reporting
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Threat Monitoring and Analysis
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. monitor security alerts from firewalls, IDS, SIEM, and other security platforms continuously,
ensuring immediate identification of potential threats
- PC2. perform real-time analysis of security alerts, correlating them with system data to detect
suspicious activities
- PC3. ensure alerts are cross-verified with other logs (e.g., network, application) to confirm the
legitimacy of the threat
- PC4. categorize identified threats based on severity, impact on critical business operations, and
sensitivity of data potentially compromised
- PC5. utilize frameworks like MITRE ATT&CK to classify and understand the tactics, techniques, and
procedures (TTPs) used in attacks
- PC6. apply a risk-based approach to determine the urgency and priority of threat response
Digital Forensics and Incident Investigation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. initiate incident investigations by collecting and preserving digital evidence from
compromised systems, network devices, and endpoints following best practices
- PC8. perform forensic analysis to reconstruct the sequence of events leading to a security breach
or attempted attack
- PC9. analyze collected data to pinpoint the root cause and identify attack vectors, aiding in
understanding how the system was compromised
- PC10. collaborate with the incident response team to isolate affected systems and mitigate ongoing
threats
- PC11. recommend remediation steps based on the investigation, including patching vulnerabilities,
hardening systems, or improving security controls
- PC12. use appropriate security tools (e.g., IDS, firewalls, SIEM) proficiently to detect, analyze, and
respond to cyber threats

compromise (IoCs).

- KU8. Regulatory compliance requirements, such as GDPR, ISO 27001, and NIST frameworks, for secure data handling and threat mitigation.

#### SSC/N0955: Coordinate with team  and respond to Incidents
Description
The OS unit is about collaborating with team members to address incidents efficiently, ensuring timely
resolution and minimal disruption to operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Incident Escalation and Threat Response Management
Post-Incident Analysis and Vulnerability Management
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Incident Escalation and Threat Response Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. establish and document clear escalation procedures for major security incidents, ensuring
timely notification of Incident Response Team (IRT) and Threat Hunting Team
- PC2. effectively communicate threat status, urgency, and required actions to relevant teams and
management during an incident
- PC3. isolate the compromised system
- PC4. implement and configure SOAR tools to automate threat detection, investigation, and
containment processes
- PC5. refer to the organization's knowledge base to review past information security incidents and
the methods used to handle them
- PC6. allocate information security incidents to the relevant personnel for investigation and
response
- PC7. evaluate the severity and potential impact of identified threats, prioritizing them based on
organizational risk and criticality
- PC8. monitor the progress of investigations into information security incidents and escalate to the
relevant personnel if the progress fails to meet established standards or service level agreements (SLAs)
Post-Incident Analysis and Vulnerability Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. conduct thorough post-incident investigations to determine the root cause of the security
breach or attempted intrusion
- PC10. provide input based on security trends and incident analysis to strengthen policy
development and enforcement mechanisms
- PC11. conduct regular vulnerability assessments to identify potential weaknesses in the
organization’s systems, networks, and applications
- PC12. coordinate with IT teams to prioritize and apply security patches to address known
vulnerabilities in a timely manner

#### SSC/N0956: Manage  and  Secure Cloud Data
Description
The OS unit is about implementing data security measures and managing cloud storage to ensure the
confidentiality, integrity, and availability of digital information.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Cloud Security Management and Threat Protection
Implement and Manage Identity, Access, and Data Protection Policies
Cloud Security Management and Disaster Recovery
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Cloud Security Management and Threat Protection
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. configure and manage threat protection, information protection, and identity protection
features within the Microsoft 365 Defender Suite
- PC2. deploy and manage Azure Security Center, Azure Firewall, and Azure Key Vault effectively
- PC3. regularly review and update security configurations to address emerging threats and
vulnerabilities
- PC4. implement security measures across multiple cloud providers and servers
- PC5. utilize SIEM tools and threat intelligence feeds to monitor cloud environments continuously
- PC6. identify and report suspicious activities or anomalies within specified response times
- PC7. investigate security incidents promptly and efficiently, documenting findings and
remediation steps
- PC8. implement corrective actions to mitigate the impact of incidents and prevent recurrence
- PC9. manage user accounts, ensuring appropriate access based on roles and responsibilities
- PC10. maintain records of user access changes and ensure timely de-provisioning of inactive
accounts
Implement and Manage Identity, Access, and Data Protection Policies
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. implement and manage Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) for all relevant user accounts,
achieving a specified compliance rate
- PC12. conduct regular access reviews, identifying and revoking unnecessary user permissions
- PC13. identify and classify sensitive data types (e.g., PII, PHI) effectively within cloud environments
- PC14. categorize data according to sensitivity levels and ensure appropriate protection measures
are in place
- PC15. implement and monitor DLP policies to prevent unauthorized data access and transmission
- PC16. ensure adherence to industry regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS) by conducting regular
audits and assessments

#### SSC/N0957: Execute  Phishing and Social Engineering Defense
Description
The OS unit is about identifying, preventing, and responding to phishing and social engineering attacks to
safeguard sensitive information and maintain cyber security.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Phishing Threat Analysis and Response Management
Phishing Threat Management and Incident Response
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Phishing Threat Analysis and Response Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. employ advanced tools (e.g., Phishme) to analyze and assess the risk level of incoming
emails
- PC2. identify phishing attempts by examining emails for red flags such as suspicious sender
addresses, urgent requests, and grammatical errors
- PC3. conduct thorough risk assessments for potential phishing threats, factoring in data sensitivity
and employee vulnerability
- PC4. design and implement phishing simulation exercises to educate employees about phishing
tactics
- PC5. analyze employee responses to phishing simulations to identify knowledge gaps and areas
needing improvement
- PC6. employ digital forensics techniques to trace the origin of suspicious emails, utilizing IP
addresses, DNS records, and metadata
- PC7. investigate incidents of successful phishing attacks to assess damage and identify corrective
