HYC/Q9403: Executive Finance - Oil & Gas
Brief Job Description
The individual will be able to perform variety of financial tasks viz. dealing with Financial Management and
Security Market, designing different kinds of formats, modules, reviews and feedback charts, handling
database in the organizations, banks, Financial Institutions and performing basic operations of banks,
financial markets, preparing different Financial Products and Financial Statements using General
Accounting Principles and trading practices at stock exchanges, NCFM Modules etc.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have good sense of responsibility, a good organizer and time keeper. The person
should have the ability to work in a team and maintain excellent interpersonal relationships

HYC/N9407: Corporate Governance and Ethics in Oil & Gas
Description
This unit is about integration of ethics into finance careers is a key focus, promoting responsible decision-
making. Participants develop effective communication strategies for disseminating governance solutions,
ensuring transparency and accountability within the dynamic Oil & Gas sector.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Corporate Governance Policies and Practices
Role of Ethics in Financial Decision-Making
Corporate Governance Challenges and Solutions
Ethics and Corporate Responsibility in Finance Careers
Elements and Performance Criteria
Corporate Governance Policies and Practices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify the concept of corporate governance and its significance in the Oil & Gas industry
PC2. Identify the key components of corporate governance policies and practices specific to Oil &
Gas companies.
PC3. Analyze the role of the board of directors and executive management in governance
Role of Ethics in Financial Decision-Making
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Illustrate the ethical considerations in financial decision-making within the Oil & Gas sector
PC5. Evaluate the potential ethical dilemmas that financial professionals may encounter
PC6. Identify the significance of ethics and corporate responsibility in building a successful finance
career in the Oil & Gas industry.
Corporate Governance Challenges and Solutions
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Evaluate the impact of ethical decision-making on an organization's reputation and financial
performance
PC8. Analyze real-world case studies highlighting corporate governance challenges faced by Oil &
Gas companies
PC9. Propose solutions to address corporate governance issues, considering the unique
characteristics of the sector
Ethics and Corporate Responsibility in Finance Careers
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. Establish Present findings and solutions in a clear and structured manner
PC11. Develop a personal code of ethics for finance professionals in the sector.
PC12. Develop a plan for integrating ethical practices into daily financial decision-making

Description
This unit is about financial analysis and reporting
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage communication
Schedule appointments, coordinate meetings /events, record minutes
Maintain records and documentation, drafting and editing documents
Making travel arrangements
Manage office supplies and equipment, provide general administrative support
Reporting
Elements and Performance Criteria
Advanced Financial Reporting in Oil & Gas Companies
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Analyze and interpret complex financial reports and disclosures in Oil & Gas firms
PC2. Make entries in books of accounts, invoice generation and check scrutiny of invoices in Oil &
Gas Companies
PC3. analyze the unique reporting challenges and regulatory requirements in the sector
PC4. Prepare a comprehensive Budget & forecasting report and financial
report(monthly/Quarterly/fortnightly/annually) for an Oil & Gas company

HYC/N9409: Taxation and Regulatory Compliance in Oil & Gas
Description
This unit is about maintaining taxation and regulatory compliance in oil and gas sector.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
In-depth Analysis of Taxation in Oil & Gas Companies
Addressing Regulatory Issues and Compliance Requirements
Strategies for Optimizing Tax Liabilities
Tax Incentives and Exemptions
Elements and Performance Criteria
In-depth Analysis of Taxation in Oil & Gas Companies
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify the various types of taxes applicable to Oil & Gas companies, including income tax,
excise tax, and property tax
PC2. Analyze the tax implications of different stages of the Oil & Gas value chain, from exploration
to production and distribution
PC3. Identify and explain the tax codes and regulations specific to the Oil & Gas sector
PC4. Calculate and interpret tax liabilities for Oil & Gas companies based on real-world scenarios
Addressing Regulatory Issues and Compliance Requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Identify and explain the key regulatory bodies and agencies governing the Oil & Gas industry
PC6. Evaluate the regulatory requirements and reporting obligations for Oil & Gas companies,
ensuring compliance with environmental, safety, and financial regulations
PC7. Develop strategies to navigate complex regulatory environments while maintaining legal and
ethical standards
PC8. Create a compliance checklist for an Oil & Gas company to ensure adherence to industry-
specific regulations
Strategies for Optimizing Tax Liabilities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Evaluate tax planning strategies that can be employed to minimize tax liabilities for Oil &
Gas companies
PC10. Analyze the impact of tax incentives, deductions and credits on the financial performance of
Oil & Gas entities
PC11. Develop a tax optimization plan for a fictional Oil & Gas corporation, considering both short-
term and long-term tax strategies
PC12. Assess the ethical considerations in tax optimization and propose solutions that align with
corporate responsibility.
Tax Incentives and Exemptions
