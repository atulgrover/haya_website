LFS/Q6401: In-Vitro Diagnostic (IVD) Service Engineering and
Maintenance Supervisor
Brief Job Description
This short-term, practical-oriented course will enable learners to understand the fundamentals of medical
diagnostics, the operating principles of In-Vitro Diagnostic (IVD) equipment, and its applications, to take up
Service Engineer/Technician jobs in the IVD segment of the Medical Devices Sub Sector. To equip learners
with industry-relevant skills in mechanical systems, manufacturing processes, automation, regulatory
practices, biomedical engineering concepts, and medical electronics, which are crucial for the efficient
servicing and maintenance professional to follow guidelines set by production, quality assurance, and field
application of In Vitro Diagnostics (IVD).
Personal Attributes
A Service Engineer and maintenance individual in the IVD segment should possess strong analytical and
problem-solving abilities, attention to detail, and a solid technical aptitude to handle biomedical and
electronic equipment effectively. The individual should demonstrate accountability, adaptability, and a
willingness to continuously learn emerging technologies and regulatory practices. Good communication
skills and a customer-centric approach are essential for interacting with healthcare professionals, while
teamwork and time management support efficient service delivery. Additionally, the person must maintain
high ethical standards, ensure compliance with safety and quality guidelines, and exhibit resilience while
working in high-pressure healthcare environments.

Compulsory NOS:
- 1. LFS/N6401: Introduction to IVD, Biomedical Instrumentations and Physiological Measurements

LFS/N6401: Introduction to IVD, Biomedical Instrumentations and
Physiological Measurements
Description
This NOS covers the foundational understanding and practical application of biomedical instrumentation
principles, bioelectric signal measurement, electrode theory, and transducer technology used in In-Vitro
Diagnostics (IVD) and physiological monitoring.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Basic of Biomedical Instrumentation
Physiological Measurements and IVD
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Accurately interpret and measure physiological signals.
PC2. Operate and calibrate biomedical transducers.
PC3. Implement safe man-machine interfacing and signal conditioning circuits.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Biomedical instrumentation fundamentals, origin of action/resting potentials, electrode theory,
transducer classification, signal conditioning.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Ethical responsibility in handling human/animal data. Safety-conscious approach. Teamwork
and reporting skills.

ensure safe and accurate diagnostic outcomes.

LFS/N6403: Analytical Principles & Instruments Used for Clinical
Chemistry
Description
This NOS covers the skills required to perform analytical testing in clinical chemistry laboratories using
various instrumentation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of this NOS is analytical Principles & Instruments Used for Clinical Chemistry
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Attention to detail and analytical thinking.
GS2. Effective communication with laboratory and clinical staff.
GS3. Problem-solving and decision-making during technical issues.

Assessment Guidelines
- 1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by Life Sciences Sector Skill
Development Council (LSSSDC)
- 2. Each Element will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. LSSSDC will also lay down
the proportion of marks for Theory, Practical, Project, and Viva for each Element.
- 3. The assessment for the theory part will be based on the knowledge bank of questions created by the
LSSSDC.
- 4. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected
elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- 5. LSSSDC as an assessment and awarding body will create unique evaluations for each assessment
component i.e. theory, practical, project, and via for every student at each examination/training center
based on this criterion.
- 6. Wherever any assessment component is not applicable/ feasible, the balance assessment components
will be used to assess the candidate, and accordingly the total marks will be calculated only for the applied
