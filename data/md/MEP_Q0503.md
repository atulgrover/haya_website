#### MEP/N0506: Perform Business Value Chain and Operations
Description
This NOS covers the skills required to analyse a business's value chain and operational processes across
functions and industries to identify key activities, dependencies, and potential risk areas impacting overall
performance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Analyze organisational frameworks and value chains
Evaluate impact of operating models on business effectiveness
Drive innovation and enhance customer satisfaction
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Analyze organisational frameworks and value chains
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the key elements and importance of different organizational structures
- PC2. recall the components of the McKinsey 7-S Model and their interrelationships
- PC3. analyze how the Balanced Scorecard framework is used to measure organizational
performance and strategy execution
- PC4. identify various value chain components
- PC5. differentiate between value chains and supply chains
- PC6. recognise the strategic role of value chains in enhancing organization effectiveness
Evaluate impact of operating models on business effectiveness
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. analyze and optimize the procurement process to enhance efficiency
- PC8. improve working capital through efficient order management
- PC9. evaluate the impact of fixed asset management on organizational profitability
- PC10. optimize inventory levels to balance cost and service levels
- PC11. align HR practices with organizational strategy to enhance productivity
- PC12. assess the suitability of different operating models for various business environments
Drive innovation and enhance customer satisfaction
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. implement strategies that encourage innovation and continuous improvement
- PC14. develop strategies to increase customer loyalty and reduce churn
- PC15. enhance organizational responsiveness to dynamic business environments
- PC16. establish a culture that supports learning and adaptability
- PC17. develop and present comprehensive business models tailored to different industries

#### MEP/N0507: Perform Process Mapping  Techniques
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to apply process mapping techniques across industries
to document, analyze, and improve workflows using tools like process maps and value stream mapping for
enhanced operational efficiency.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out process mapping techniques across different industries
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out process mapping techniques across different industries
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the role of process mapping in improving business efficiency
- PC2. analyze various types of process maps and their specific applications
- PC3. develop a process map for a business process using process mapping steps and guidelines
- PC4. apply value stream mapping principles to identify value and wastes in processes
- PC5. conduct process mapping in a retail environment
- PC6. apply process mapping techniques in manufacturing processes
- PC7. create a process map for a service-based process
- PC8. conduct process mapping in a telecom industry environment
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The purpose and benefits of process mapping in improving business efficiency
- KU2. Types of process maps (e.g., flowcharts, swimlane diagrams, SIPOC) and their appropriate applications
- KU3. Principles of value stream mapping and identifying value-added vs. non-value-added activities
- KU4. Industry-specific considerations in process mapping for retail, manufacturing, service, and
telecom sectors
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Use process mapping software or tools to design and document workflows
- GS2. Analyze processes logically to identify inefficiencies and improvement areas

Methodologies
Description
This NOS involves applying process assurance techniques and operational efficiency methodologies -
including analytics, control frameworks, internal audits and process reviews - to evaluate, optimise and
align business processes with organisational goals and risk controls.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Analyse process assurance and operating efficiency methodologies
Apply risk management and control frameworks in business processes
Carryout internal audit and provide assurance on processes
Review activities in various business processes
Optimise processes to drive efficiency
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Analyse process assurance and operating efficiency methodologies

different types of controls

- PC7. analyze the role of internal audits and audit planning in process assurance
- PC8. evaluate internal audit responsibilities and understand stakeholder management
- PC9. develop and implement audit plans focusing on risk management and process optimization
- PC10. evaluate and communicate audit findings to drive continuous improvement

#### MEP/N0509: Manage  Internal Controls Over Financial Reporting
Description
This NOS covers the application of financial and operational control principles, implementation of
compliance frameworks like SOX and ICFR, and assessment of deficiencies to ensure robust internal
controls over financial reporting and regulatory compliance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Apply fundamental principles of financial and operational controls
Implement financial and internal control and compliance frameworks in organisations
Ensure compliance with financial and internal control frameworks
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Apply fundamental principles of financial and operational controls
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. demonstrate basics of accounting
- PC2. interpret financial statements of an organisation
- PC3. identify operating and financial controls and their significance
- PC4. differentiate between operating and financial controls
- PC5. evaluate financial controls
Implement financial and internal control and compliance frameworks in organisations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. identify the role of SOX and its importance in corporate governance
- PC7. identify the role of ICFR and its importance in regulation compliance
- PC8. develop and implement SOX program and its deliverables
- PC9. test and track SOX program using various methods
- PC10. recognize the role of ITGC in ICOFR and its importance in audit
- PC11. apply various methods for improving ITGC framework in an organization
- PC12. review management representation letters
- PC13. identify the various provisions of Companies Act 2013 relating to IFC and ICFR
Ensure compliance with financial and internal control frameworks
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. identify and assess materiality, material weakness and significant deficiency
- PC15. identify the responsibilities of auditors
- PC16. assess the impact of deficiency in financial reporting controls
- PC17. determine remediation for deficiencies.
- PC18. recognize the importance of IFC and various auditing standards

#### MEP/N0510: Carryout Market, Credit and Operational Risk Management
Description
This NOS focuses on applying financial risk management principles to identify, assess, and manage
market, credit, operational, and foreign exchange risks through regulatory frameworks, models, and
instruments such as securitization, derivatives, and asset-liability management.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Apply financial risk management principles to address risk management failures
Carryout credit risk and country risk analysis
Analyse securitization, banking risks, and asset liability management (ALM) principles
Analyse foreign exchange risk and derivatives in financial markets
Evaluate financial failures, regulatory frameworks, and their outcomes
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Apply financial risk management principles to address risk management failures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the building blocks of financial risk management and how firms manage financial
risks
- PC2. analyse the governance around financial risk management, exploring financial disasters and
the causes of financial risk management failures
- PC3. identify the concept of capital asset pricing model (CAPM)
- PC4. recognize the importance of systematic risk (market risk), risk-free rate, Beta, unsystematic
risk, risk and diversification
Carryout credit risk and country risk analysis
