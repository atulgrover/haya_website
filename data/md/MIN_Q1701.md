MIN/Q1701: Mining Rescuer
Brief Job Description
This job role refers to any person who rescues people who have come under accident by fire, gaseous
hazards, work at height, inundation, confined space hazards, etc.
Personal Attributes
Technical knowledge of techniques and tools required for handling hazards like fire, inundation, work at
height, confined spaces. The person should have ability to plan and prioritize, quality consciousness,
sensitivity to problem solving, quick decision making, safety orientation, reading, writing and
communication skills, dexterity and high precision, ability to use internal ERP systems
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MIN/N1706: Undertake actions to prevent the fire situation in a mine
- 2. MIN/N1707: Conduct fire rescue operation
- 3. MIN/N1708: Conduct rescue operation from surface water inundation, underground inundation,
gaseous hazards, roof fall, work at height and confined places
- 4. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Opencast
- 1. MIN/N1703: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental Guidelines for opencast mines
Elective 2: Underground Metal
- 1. MIN/N1702: Follow Health, Safety and Environmental guidelines for Underground Metalliferous
Mines
Elective 3: Underground Coal
- 1. MIN/N1704: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental guidelines for underground coal mines

MIN/N1706: Undertake actions to prevent the fire situation in a mine
Description
This OS unit is about identifying the organizational requirements to ensure safety from fire and
undertaking the actions to prevent the fire situations in a mine
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify the organizational requirements to ensure safety from fire
Undertake actions to prevent the fire situations in a mine
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify the requirements to ensure fire safety in the mines
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the common types of fires i.e. accidental fires and spontaneous fires as well as the
possible sources of the same
PC2. identify the sources of fire i.e. diesel engine, oil spillage, electrical fires, frictional fires,
blasting, explosion etc
PC3. identify the potential location/s of fire i.e. compressors, explosive stores etc
PC4. identify various ways to extinguish the different types of fires and associated equipment
required like water sprinklers, fire extinguishers, etc , degree of gassiness and its permissible
apparatus, prevent and outbreak belowground or the spread to one part to other part of
mine of adjoining mine, all unused workings connected to surface though walkable entrance
to be inspected, periodic checks of sealed off areas due to fire breakouts, in open cast
working all herbaceous plants shall be removed and all dead leaves and dry vegetation
cleared off
PC5. check the availability and workability of fire alarms at the site
PC6. check the adequacy and availability of fire extinguishing equipment at the site
Undertake actions to prevent the fire situations in a mine
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. deal with shortfalls that affect the availability of resources to meet operational readiness
PC8. facilitate the awareness of fire safety and associated safety actions that could be taken at
the time of fire in the mine
PC9. enter inspection, training and emergency medical service calls into the records
PC10. ensure adherence to the fire safety related policies and Guidelines defined for the
organization
PC11. ensure periodic checking and maintenance of the fire extinguishing equipment at the site

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

MIN/N1707: Conduct fire rescue operation
Description
This OS unit is about conducting firefighting operations which comprise of fire control as well as fire rescue
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct the fire fighting and rescue operations
Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct Fire Rescue Operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. equip himself with the fire protection apparatus including breathing apparatus, fire jackets,
hoses etc. and ensure adequate numbers of persons are accompanying
PC2. cut the power supply in the mine
PC3. use various equipment to control fire including ladder trucks, pumper trucks, tanker trucks,
fire hose, fire extinguishers etc
PC4. conduct fire control by depriving a fire of fuel (Reducing Agent), oxygen (Oxidizing Agent),
heat and/or the chemical chain reaction that are necessary to sustain itself or re-kindle
PC5. conduct preliminary salvage operations such as throwing salvage covers, sweeping water
and removing debris
PC6. attack the mine fires through hose streams channelized towards fire
PC7. contact fire brigade/ rescue team immediately for extended support
PC8. search and remove the trapped occupants out of hazardous conditions
PC9. conduct search that is primarily around movement against room walls (to prevent rescuers
from becoming lost or disoriented) and methodical searches of specific areas by designated
teams
PC10. check and mobilize the resources from adjoining areas
PC11. inspect and clean the equipment and apparatus after returning from a fire
PC12. make minor repairs to equipment, perform routine preventive maintenance tasks, and keep
records of such action when unable to locate fire report to senior officials and carry out
intentional flooding with their authorization
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. different types of mines and detail of the mine he is working in- classification of gassy seam
into degree of gassiness; adequate and sufficient arrangements for early detection , control
and extinguishing fire; Measures and precautions with regard to appropriate nature of mine
operation to prevent detect and combat of mine fires as prescribed by mine officials

MIN/N1708: Conduct rescue operation from surface water inundation,
underground inundation, gaseous hazards, roof fall, work at height and
confined places
Description
This OS unit is about conducting rescue operation from surface water inundation, underground inundation,
gaseous hazards, roof fall, work at height and confined places
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Instruct the rescue procedures
Rescue from surface water and underground inundation
Rescue from gaseous hazards
Rescue against roof fall, work at height and confined places
Elements and Performance Criteria
Instruct the rescue procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. establish a fresh air base from where the rescue operation to be conducted
PC2. proceed with self contained breathing apparatus and appropriate rescue equipment
PC3. work as team player or leader of rescue team, as per requirement
PC4. return to the fresh air base (with whole team), if any member experiences trouble due to
physical exhaustion or defect in apparatus
PC5. communicate with hand signals and coded sounds of horns
PC6. mark the route followed by the team (smoky and poor visibility)
PC7. use life line extended from the base as the team proceeds
Rescue from surface water and underground inundation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. identify the source of inundation and inform the workers to withdraw themselves
PC9. identify an escape route which is free from depression, deep drains, hand rails
PC10. identify the places of underground inundation ( seam or section below, water bearing strata )
PC11. wear scuba set, and use it to rescue equipment, personnel, and other purposes.
PC12. dewater the water logged workings in underground/opencast and give immediate strong
support to roof and sides
Rescue from gaseous hazards
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. ensure proper ventilation upto the face and conduct frequent checks for methane, oxygen,
carbon monoxide, hydrogen sulphides, etc.
PC14. use approved flame safety lamp
