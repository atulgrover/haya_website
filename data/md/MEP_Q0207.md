MEP/Q0207: Office Operations Executive
Brief Job Description
The office operations executive is a generalist who checks and prepares documents, writes simple
correspondence, maintains records as well as co-ordinates with various internal and external agencies and
follows up for work completion in line with professional practices of the workplace
Personal Attributes
The individual must be well groomed, attentive, comfortable with multi-tasking and disciplined have good
communication skills and attention to detail respect confidentiality and have a positive attitude and
dependability
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MEP/N0204: Manage routine office activities
- 2. MEP/N0216: Use computers to store, retrieve and communicate information
- 3. MEP/N0224: Check forms and applications for completeness as per norms
- 4. MEP/N0225: Coordinate with internal and external agencies to complete operational requirements
- 5. MEP/N9903: Apply health and safety practices at the workplace
- 6. MEP/N9912: Apply principles of professional practice at the workplace
- 7. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

MEP/N0204: Manage routine office activities
Description
This unit is about managing day to day activities of the organization.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage phone calls and queries
Maintain records and documentation
Organise meeting
Ensure gender mainstreaming and inclusivity
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage phone calls and queries
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Receive and manage communications (calls, chats, emails, or video meetings) from
stakeholders and direct them to the right person using digital tools.
PC2. Greet stakeholders politely across phone, video, email, or chat, following organizational rules
and showing cultural respect.
PC3. Listen carefully, record details manually and digitally (AI transcription or speech-to-text tools)
and ask questions to understand the query.
PC4. Use AI assistants or CRM systems to give quick responses, check past records, and confirm
information with clients/customers.
PC5. Summarize and document queries with the help of note- taking or knowledge management
tools so they can be easily accessed later.
PC6. Resolve queries within your scope using AI knowledge bases and FAQs or pass them to the
right authority if needed.
PC7. Access and update digital knowledge hubs (CRM, intranet, AI systems) to provide accurate
and up-to-date answers to stakeholders.
Maintain records and documentation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Maintain updated contact details of staff, service providers, suppliers, and stakeholders using
manually and digitally (CRM tools, shared directories, or AI-driven contact management
systems)
PC9. Maintain correspondence, contracts, and official documents in cloud-based filing systems
(Google Drive, OneDrive, SharePoint, DMS platforms, etc.) with proper authorization and
access control.
PC10. Maintain a manual or digital catalogue or database of all files and documentation, with
periodic AI-assisted audits and auto-inventory features.
PC11. Use search-enabled digital filing systems or AI document retrieval tools to quickly access,
share, and replace files, ensuring proper digital/physical order is maintained.

Ensure Inclusivity and Gender Mainstreaming
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC18. Show inclusive and respectful behavior in all workplace interactions, ensuring gender-neutral
communication with colleagues and teams (including emails, chats, and meetings).
PC19. Use non-gendered language and respectful phrases in verbal, written, and digital
communication (e.g., phone calls, client interactions, social media, AI-enabled chat tools).
PC20. Leverage digital platforms, AI tools, and workplace apps to promote inclusivity (e.g., using AI
grammar checkers for bias-free language, inclusive templates for presentations, and virtual
meeting guidelines).
PC21. Stay updated with organizational policies, diversity guidelines, and new workplace trends
related to gender equity, inclusivity, and accessibility.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Knowledge of the organisations profile, area of operations, statutes, hierarchy, departments,
reporting structure, and escalation matrix.
KU2. Understanding of access control, data security, confidentiality, file naming conventions,
backups, version control, archiving procedures, file retention periods, and authorization
requirements as per company policy.
KU3. Awareness of management tools, CRM systems, AI-powered chatbots, and knowledge bases
used for recording, categorizing, tracking, and resolving customer queries.
KU4. Familiarity with standard scripts, templates, and communication protocols for handling
customer queries across multiple channels (phone, email, chat, video).
KU5. Importance of keeping customers informed about timescales for resolution, maintaining
transparency, and using digital tools for real-time updates and follow-ups.
KU6. Use of information systems, databases, and software applications for data storage, retrieval,
analysis, and secure communication, including AI-enabled search and auto-summarization.

MEP/N0216: Use computers to store, retrieve and communicate
information
Description
This unit deals in detail with storing, retrieval and communication of information using computers
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Retrieve and Manage Information using Digital Systems and AI Tools
Store information using word processors and spread sheets
Manage digital communication and ensure safe use of technology
Work safely and securely with technology
Elements and Performance Criteria
Retrieve and Manage Information using Digital Systems and AI Tools
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Recognize different operating systems, cloud storage platforms, and AI-driven applications
used for storing and retrieving data.
PC2. Use computer drives, cloud storage, and AI search features to locate files, data, and
documents quickly and accurately
PC3. Use AI tools and automated systems to quickly find, organize, label, and safely store digital
files, while following the organization’s rules for data access and security
Store information using word processors and spreadsheets
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Enter, edit, and save data or information (letters, reports, tables, etc.) using word processors,
spreadsheets, and AI-enabled digital tools.
PC5. Format, design, and present information professionally using features like templates, styles,
references, review tools, and AI-assisted formatting.
PC6. Apply basic and AI-supported formulas, functions, charts, and data visualization techniques
in spreadsheets to simplify data presentation.
PC7. Follow organizational data policies on file naming, backups, privacy, and cyber security while
using digital and AI tools.
Manage digital communication and ensure safe use of technology
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Use computers to prepare and share information through printouts, reports, or digital
formats.
PC9. Communicate using emails, AI-powered writing assistants, or workplace collaboration tools
(e.g., MS Teams, Slack, Google Workspace) while following security rules.
PC10. Ensure compliance with organizational data security and privacy policies while sharing files
or messages.
Work safely and securely with technology
