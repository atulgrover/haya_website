#### HYC/Q3603: Process Safety Supervisor Oil Gas
Brief Job Description
The process safety officer is responsible for conducting regular site inspections to identify potential
hazards and ensure that safety measures are in place and properly maintained, investigate accidents and
incidents to determine the root causes, and recommend corrective actions to prevent future occurrences.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have a good knowledge and communication skills on process envelops including
safety aspects. The person must have supervisory skills. The individual should be proactive, disciplined,
safety-conscious, courageous and work individually and in a team.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HYC/N3606: Develop policies, SOPs and train to ensure safety at workplace

workplace
Description
This unit covers Design and implement workplace safety policies, SOPs, and training programs to ensure a
safe work environment, promoting employee well-being and accident prevention.
Scope

This unit/task covers the following:
stakeholders

#### Elements and Performance Criteria stakeholders
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Develop and implement safety policies and procedures to ensure compliance with industry
standards and regulations
- PC2. Implement policies and procedures that ensure consistent service delivery that is safe and
efficient.
- PC3. Follow Oil Industry Safety Directorate (OISD) standards related to process safety, system,
equipment and appliances in oil and gas industry.

company.
- PC7. Puts safety first in all company transactions following Safety policies and ensuring others
comply to protect people, environment, assets, and the company’s reputation in company operations.

concerns and improve overall safety performance.
- PC9. Convey process information like P&ID, SOP, MOC, SIMOPS
Training & competency development

- PC11. Create a comprehensive matrix outlining initial, mandatory, refresher, and advanced training
needs, along with evaluation techniques

emergency response procedures.

opportunities for various types of safety-related programs.
