RSC/Q3601: Tyre Fitter-Servicing and Maintenance
Brief Job Description
A Tyre Fitter-Servicing and Maintenance is responsible for fitting different types of tyres
(new/used/replacement) to the rim. Tyre fitment can be made as a part of service sector (manufacturing of
automobile /or in the market) or in-house in the tyre manufacturing plant for testing such as plunger and
wheel testing. Also, he is responsible for carrying out proper tyre inflation and maintenance.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to be a good observer and demonstrate spontaneity in resolving the
different repairing issues. He should be careful and strict in adhering to the specified norms. He should be
active and energetic. He should be able to work independently under the guidance of supervisor. As a
good learner, he should be willing to learn efficient ways to perform his work. He should be disciplined,
focused and comfortable in performing laborious work.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N3601: Prepare Material, Tools and Machine and Inspect Tyre for Servicing/Maintenance
- 2. RSC/N3602: Undertake tyre servicing/maintenance using upgraded tools
- 3. RSC/N3608: Perform post tyre servicing/ maintenance activities using automated tools
- 4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

RSC/N3601: Prepare Material, Tools and Machine and Inspect Tyre for
Servicing/Maintenance
Description
This unit covers the competencies required to prepare the workplace, tools, and machines, and to carry
out tyre and rim inspection before undertaking servicing and maintenance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare Tools, Equipment and Work Area
Inspect Tyres and Rims
Document inspection results
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare tools, equipment, and work area
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check and confirm work instructions, job card, and customer request
PC2. collect and arrange required tools, equipment, and consumables
PC3. verify availability of semi-automatic tyre changer, wheel spanner, jack, and safety stands
PC4. ensure tools are calibrated and safe for use
PC5. prepare the vehicle by securing it on jack or lift
PC6. follow safety precautions like PPE, gloves, and eye protection
Inspect tyres and rims
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. identify tyre type, size, and rim specifications as per the vehicle model
PC8. inspect tyres visually for tread wear, cuts, bulges, cracks, or uneven wear patterns
PC9. check tyre pressure levels before removing
PC10. verify wheel nut torque condition before loosening
PC11. inspect rim for bends, cracks, rust, or deformation
PC12. check for valve leakage or damage
Document inspection results
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. segregate tyres requiring servicing, replacement, or disposal
PC14. record tyre condition and observations in job sheet
PC15. ensure compliance with manufacturer guidelines while inspecting tyres

RSC/N3602: Undertake tyre servicing/maintenance using upgraded tools
Description
This unit covers the competencies required to remove, fit, rotate, balance, and repair tyres using upgraded
and semi-automated tools, ensuring quality and safety standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Remove tyre and rim assembly
Fit and mount tyre
Rotate, balance, and repair tyres
Ensure service quality and safety
Elements and Performance Criteria
Remove tyre and rim assembly
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. loosen wheel nuts using a cross spanner or pneumatic wrench
PC2. loosen wheel nuts using a cross spanner or pneumatic wrench
PC3. remove wheel assembly carefully to avoid damage
PC4. deflate tyre before dismantling
PC5. operate bead breaker to separate tyre from rim
Fit and mount tyre
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. clean rim surface before mounting tyre
PC7. apply tyre paste/lubricant to bead area
PC8. mount tyre on rim using semi-automatic tyre changer
PC9. ensure correct tyre orientation (directional/inside-outside type)
PC10. ensure correct tyre orientation (directional/inside-outside type)
PC11. check for proper bead seating on the rim
Rotate, balance, and repair tyres
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. follow recommended tyre rotation patterns (front-to-rear, cross, etc.)
PC13. perform wheel balancing using computerized wheel balancer
PC14. apply weights at appropriate points for balancing correction
PC15. carry out puncture repairs using plug/patch kits
PC16. carry out puncture repairs using plug/patch kits
PC17. test repaired tyre for air leakage
Ensure service quality and safety

RSC/N3608: Perform post tyre servicing/ maintenance activities using
automated tools
Description
This NOS covers the skills and knowledge required to perform post tyre servicing and maintenance
activities using automated tools. It involves carrying out post-servicing checks, ensuring correct finishing
of tyres and wheels, documenting the service performed, and maintaining workplace safety and
cleanliness. The individual is responsible for verifying tyre quality, balancing, alignment, and recording
results to ensure customer satisfaction and compliance with industry standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out post-servicing checks
Complete finishing and documentation tasks
Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out post-servicing checks
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. verify tyre pressure using digital gauges and ensure it matches manufacturer specifications
PC2. check wheel balancing status with automated balancers to confirm smooth rotation
PC3. inspect tread depth and wear patterns using electronic testers to ensure compliance with
safety standards
PC4. perform leak detection tests with automated equipment to identify and address air or fluid
leaks
PC5. ensure correct torque of wheel nuts using calibrated torque wrenches or automated tools
PC6. confirm alignment settings through computerized alignment systems to prevent uneven
wear
PC7. record and report inspection results in digital or manual formats for traceability and
customer assurance
Complete finishing and documentation tasks
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. clean the serviced tyre and wheel assembly using approved tools and materials to ensure
proper finishing
PC9. apply protective coatings or treatments (if required) to extend tyre life and maintain
appearance
PC10. dispose of used materials, waste, and lubricants as per safety and environmental guidelines
PC11. update service records manually or digitally to document completed maintenance activities
PC12. tag or label tyres (if applicable) for identification, warranty, or customer tracking
PC13. communicate final service details to the supervisor or customer clearly and accurately

- 1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
- 2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
- 3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected
elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- 4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
- 5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
- 6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
- 7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
