#### MSU/THC/Q0101: Rural Tourism Entrepreneur
Brief Job Description
On completion of this course, candidates will be equipped to effectively manage and host guests at rural
homestays, ensuring a comfortable and authentic experience. They will learn how to prepare the homestay
environment, provide exceptional hospitality, and engage in cultural exchanges with visitors. Additionally,
they will be able to initiate and lead rural tourism activities, offering guided tours that showcase local
attractions and traditions. The course also covers maintaining guest privacy, ensuring security, promoting
health and safety, and implementing environmentally sustainable practices in rural hospitality operations.
Personal Attributes
A candidate must have strong communication skills, adaptability, and cultural sensitivity to effectively
engage with diverse guests in a rural setting. They should demonstrate attention to detail, problem-solving
abilities, and a hospitable attitude to ensure a positive guest experience. Additionally, the candidate
should possess environmental awareness to promote sustainable tourism, physical stamina to manage the
demands of rural hospitality, and patience and empathy to cater to individual guest needs.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### MSU/THC/N0101: Prepare for hosting the guest at rural homestay
Description
In this OS unit, candidate will learn to register the rural homestay, furnish the hosting space, promote the
establishment and handle online bookings.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Register the rural homestay
Furnish the hosting space
Promote the establishment
Handle online bookings
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Register the rural homestay
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the area or space in the house that can be shared with the guests.
- PC2. identify the type and category of the establishment.
- PC3. identify various state and local rules, regulations, and permissions to be taken to start a rural
homestay.
- PC4. survey the current trends and forecast the profitability of the business.
- PC5. make a list of homestay features and facilities required by guests such as wi-fi, local and
international cuisine, privacy, parking space, television, etc.
- PC6. determine the pricing for the accommodation duly considering the pricing of nearby
homestays, cost of offering a homestay, target population of guests, taxes, etc.
- PC7. get the necessary permissions and register the accommodation for homestay under rural
tourism as per the government policy.
- PC8. register the accommodation on online platforms for the guests to view and book.
- PC9. prepare for classification/reclassification inspection.
Furnish the hosting space
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. renovate the space like plastering, western toilets, whitewashing, etc., if required.
- PC11. rooms should be well-proportioned as per guest accommodation.
- PC12. ensure adequate ventilation and lighting in the rooms.
- PC13. provide air -conditioning & heating depending on climatic conditions.
- PC14. place clean and comfortable bedding in the rooms along with necessary items like dustbins,
curtains, cabinets, etc.
- PC15. make sure bathrooms have all the necessary amenities like running water, geyser, buckets,
mugs, etc.

#### MSU/THC/N0102: Host the guests in a rural homestay
Description
In this OS unit, candidate will learn to assist guests before check-in, welcome the guests, assist guests
during the stay, prepare and serve meals, check-out the guests, and manage finances.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist guests before check-in Welcome the guests
Assist guests during the stay
Prepare and serve meals
Check-out the guests
Manage Finances
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist guests before check-in To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. equip the guest with all the relevant information, including how to reach the accommodation,
recommended items to carry/pack, etc.
- PC2. attend to guests’ queries for any information related to the activities they can take up in the
rural area and facilities in the homestay.
- PC3. coordinate with the guests for check-in time.
Welcome the guests
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. greet and welcome the guests, personally, if possible.
- PC5. make the guests feel at home by incorporating a welcome note, welcome drink, aqua guard /
RO / mineral water, etc.
- PC6. collect mandatory information and documents from the guests.
- PC7. collect advance money from the guests if the booking is not pre-paid.
- PC8. check-in the guests as per the procedure.
- PC9. maintain a register (physical or electronic) for guest check-in and check-out records including
passport details in case of foreign tourists.
- PC10. escort the guests to the room.
- PC11. inform the guests about the usage of various appliances and equipment.
- PC12. explain the home rules and rules of the village to the guest.
- PC13. inform the guests of the menu card, if available, or other meal services available in the
village.
- PC14. show around the space including the kitchen and living area to the guests.

#### MSU/THC/N0103: Initiate tour guide operations for rural tourism.
Description
In this OS unit, candidate will learn to prepare for tour guide operations, engage with tourists, ensure
tourist safety, and prepare invoice and receive payment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for tour guide operations
Engage with tourists
Ensure tourist safety
Prepare invoice and receive payment
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for tour guide operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. collect information on the type of tour to be conducted rural lifestyle, art and culture,
ecotourism, adventure, religion, heritage, etc.
- PC2. obtain the tour plan with dates and duration from the agents and operators.
- PC3. select items and information that are relevant to include in the tour.
- PC4. obtain information on tourist or tour group’s details like the number of elderly and babies,
interests, requirements, special needs, names, contact numbers, emergency contacts, etc.
- PC5. coordinate with the tour operator for the meeting point and time.
- PC6. confirm the time required to complete the tour, accommodation, budget, dietary and access
requirements with the appropriate authority.
- PC7. coordinate with travel agents/tour operators to schedule visits to homestays, local artisans,
adventure guides, religious places, treks, etc.
- PC8. select the route and sequence of the tour to meet tour objectives.
- PC9. determine the pace at which information will be provided to tourists to enable a better
understanding.
- PC10. prepare the narratives and commentary for the tour sites and the village art, history and
culture.
- PC11. ensure that the format, content, and style of presentations are engaging and appropriate.
Engage with tourists
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. arrange the requisite equipment for each tour like GPS units, variometer, radios, etc.
- PC13. greet the tourists on arrival at the meeting point as per standards and local customs.
- PC14. build rapport with tourists through self-introduction and asking their names.
- PC15. assist tourists with transfers, and seating arrangements in buses, vans, etc., as required.
- PC16. inform the tourists about the itinerary.

### Page 61

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

| Electives | Electives are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as contributive to specialization in a job role. There may be multiple electives within a QP for each specialized job role. Trainees must select at least one elective for the successful completion of a QP with Electives. |
| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
