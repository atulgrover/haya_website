ASP/CON/Q0201: Road Construction Engineer
Brief Job Description
Road construction Engineers are professionals responsible for the overseeing, costing, estimating,
planning as well as implementation of construction projects of roads that connect cities, towns, and states.
They play a pivotal role in overseeing all aspects of road construction, from the initial planning and design
stages to construction and project completion. Their work ensures that roads are built safely, efficiently,
and in compliance with the regulations.
Personal Attributes
Attention to Detail ,Problem-Solving Skills ,Project Management Skills, Knowledge of Materials &
Construction Methods, Familiarity with Regulations
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. ASP/CON/N0201: Overview on Road Design and Construction
- 2. ASP/CON/N0202: Overview of Construction Tools, Machinery and Practices

- 4. ASP/CON/N0204: Construction of Pavement Upper Layers and Safety Measures

ASP/CON/N0201: Overview on Road Design and Construction
Description
Road design and construction involve planning, engineering, and building roads to ensure safe, efficient,
and durable transportation infrastructure.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of this module covers the fundamental principles of road design and construction,
including geometric design, material selection, pavement structure, drainage, and safety
considerations. It also explores modern engineering techniques, sustainability practices, and
regulatory standards for efficient road infrastructure development.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Assess the basic understanding of road construction principles
PC2. Evaluate proficiency in conducting topographic surveys using GPS, Total Station, and Auto
Level, including accurate site setting out.
PC3. Assess an overall knowledge of DPR Preparation
PC4. Assess the knowledge in interpretation of Drawings in the DPR
PC5. Evaluate the proficiency in the Geometric and Pavement Design
PC6. Assess the knowledge about Labour Laws, Project Management- CPM, PERT , MS Project,
Primavera, Building Information Modeling (BIM)

ASP/CON/N0202: Overview of Construction Tools, Machinery and Practices
Description
This NOS provides a comprehensive understanding of road construction processes, including the selection,
operation, and maintenance of construction tools and machinery. It equips learners with the skills needed
to manage preparatory works, utility services, and material handling efficiently. The NOS emphasizes best
practices in construction techniques, sustainability, and safety compliance. It also covers quality
management through field laboratory operations, testing, and inspections to ensure adherence to project
specifications. By integrating practical training with industry standards, this NOS prepares professionals to
contribute effectively to road construction projects with a focus on quality, efficiency, and environmental
responsibility.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the fundamental aspects of road construction engineering, focusing on the essential
tools, machinery, and best practices required for efficient project execution. It includes preparatory
works, construction techniques, quality management, utility management, and material selection.
The scope also extends to ensuring safety, sustainability, and compliance with industry standards
through the effective use of construction equipment and quality control measures.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Evaluate the understanding of Quality Management Plan -Setting up of Field laboratory,
Quality Assurance & Quality Control
PC2. Assess the understanding of Site Clearance-Earthwork, Erosion Control and Drainage- Quality
Control
PC3. Assess the knowledge of Construction Tools and Machinery- Types, Applications, Suitability
and Selection. Assess the knowledge on Full Depth Reclamation (FDR), precast pavements,
white topping and surface dressing
PC4. Evaluate the knowledge of Material Selection and Management, utility management

ASP/CON/N0203: Construction of Embankment and Granular Layers
Description
This NOS provides a comprehensive understanding of the methods and techniques required for
constructing and managing embankments, subgrades, and granular layers in road construction. It covers
material specifications, grading, compaction, and stabilization techniques to ensure durability and
performance. The NOS emphasizes quality assurance through laboratory and field testing of soils and
aggregates while integrating environmental sustainability measures. Learners will gain hands-on
experience with surveying, excavation, grading, and material testing equipment. Additionally, this NOS
includes fundamental design principles for earth retaining works, ensuring stability and structural integrity.
Traffic management and worker safety measures are also highlighted to minimize risks during
construction.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the essential principles and practices involved in the construction of subgrade,
embankments, granular layers, and earth retaining structures in road projects. It encompasses
material selection, compaction techniques, quality control measures, environmental considerations,
and traffic management strategies. The scope also includes the use of appropriate construction
equipment, adherence to safety protocols, and the implementation of best practices to ensure
compliance with industry standards and project specifications.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Check the understanding of Embankment and Subgrade - Design Criteria, Construction and
Quality Control
PC2. Assess the knowledge about Field and Laboratory Testing of Soil, Aggregates and Other
Additives/ Stabilizing Agents
PC3. Assess the understanding of Earth Retaining Works- Types, Basic Design Principles and
Construction Practices
PC4. Assess the knowledge about Sub-Bases, Bases (Non-Bituminous) and Shoulder - Types,
Material Specifications and Construction Practices

ASP/CON/N0204: Construction of Pavement Upper Layers and Safety
Measures
Description
This NOS provides a detailed understanding of the principles and techniques involved in constructing and
maintaining the upper layers of pavements. It includes the specifications and requirements for bituminous
and concrete pavements, covering aspects such as design mix, reinforcement, and thickness standards.
The NOS emphasizes the correct use of equipment, such as asphalt pavers, rollers, and concrete mixers, to
ensure the efficient execution of pavement construction. Safety measures are integrated throughout the
process to protect workers and the public, while traffic management strategies ensure smooth operations.
Learners will gain hands-on experience in layer compaction, pavement marking, and installation of road
safety elements to enhance the overall quality and functionality of road infrastructure.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the construction and maintenance of pavement upper layers, including bituminous
and concrete surfaces. It includes best practices for material selection, layer installation,
compaction, and quality control to ensure pavement durability and strength. The scope also
encompasses the proper use of construction equipment, adherence to industry standards, and
implementation of safety measures for workers and road users. Additionally, the NOS highlights the
importance of traffic management and road safety through the correct installation and maintenance
of traffic signs, markings, and other road appurtenances.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. - Assess the knowledge about Bituminous Bases and Surface Courses - Specifications and
Construction Practices. Evaluate Local/New/Alternate/Modified Construction Materials and
Technologies - Use of Waste Plastic, Full Depth Reclamation (FDR), Reclaimed Asphalt
Pavement (RAP) etc; Bitumen testing- and Test on mixes- Marshall stability, etc.
PC2. Evaluate the knowledge of Concrete Pavement - Specification and Construction Practices
PC3. Understanding of Traffic Signs, Markings and Other Road Appurtenances
PC4. Understanding of Construction Equipments and Machinery for base and surface courses

ASP/CON/N0205: Geotechnical Investigations, Construction of Bridges and
Cross Drainage Structures
Description
This NOS provides a comprehensive understanding of geotechnical investigations and their role in
infrastructure development. Learners will gain knowledge of soil and rock sampling techniques, laboratory
and in-situ testing methods, and geotechnical analysis to inform construction decisions. The module also
covers the construction processes for bridges and cross drainage structures, including excavation, piling,
concrete placement, reinforcement installation, and structural erection. Emphasis is placed on ensuring
compliance with industry standards, contractual obligations, and safety regulations. Practical training will
include hands-on experience in geotechnical assessments, foundation design, and the construction of
bridge components such as piers, abutments, girders, and decks. The NOS also includes project completion
procedures, final inspections, and site handover best practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers geotechnical investigations and the construction of bridges and cross drainage
structures.
It includes the study of soil and rock properties, groundwater conditions, and other geotechnical
parameters necessary for designing stable foundations, slopes, and retaining structures.
The scope also encompasses the execution of construction activities for substructures and
superstructures, ensuring compliance with regulatory standards and quality requirements. It
highlights the importance of safety, proper documentation, and final inspections before project
handover.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. • Assess the knowledge of Surface and Sub-Surface Geotechnical Investigations- Principles
• and Practices
PC2. Access knowledge of Foundations - Types, Material Specifications, Testing and Construction
Practices
PC3. Evaluate the understanding of Construction of Substructures and Super Structures of Bridges
and Cross Drainage Structures, Formworks
PC4. Evaluate Steel Reinforcement, Prestressing, Bearings, Expansion Joints, Appurtenances, Repair
and Retrofitting of Structures
PC5. Access the knowledge of the Completion of Work and Site Handing Over

insights into financial management, professional ethics, business planning, and sustainable work practices.

engage in effective teamwork while maintaining workplace hygiene and etiquette.
Scope

This NOS covers essential health and safety practices in road construction and the employability
skills required for career readiness. It includes identifying and mitigating worksite hazards, adhering
to industry safety regulations, and implementing emergency response protocols. Additionally, it
focuses on developing professional skills such as communication, teamwork, financial literacy,
digital proficiency, and entrepreneurship, ensuring learners are equipped for sustainable career
growth in the road construction industry.
