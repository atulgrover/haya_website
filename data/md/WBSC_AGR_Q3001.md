WBSC/AGR/Q3001: Fruits and Vegetables Product Producer
Brief Job Description
Operate machines and perform routine tasks to prepare canned and frozen fruit and vegetables, and make
and package sauces, jams and juices and different product of fruits and vegetables.
Personal Attributes
The individual should possess a keen sense of cleanliness, good observation skills and the ability to follow
food safety standards. They should be disciplined, cooperative in team settings and adaptable to work in
real job scenarios involving food processing.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. WBSC/AGR/N3001: Apply Safe Working Practices.
- 2. WBSC/AGR/N3002: Identify and select fresh fruits and vegetables with the help of checklist.
- 3. WBSC/AGR/N3003: Identify the spoilage in fruits and vegetables and state the reason for the
spoilage.
- 4. WBSC/AGR/N3004: Recognize the different raw materials which will be Identify as spices and food
additives by visual inspection.
- 5. WBSC/AGR/N3005: Prepare and pack perishables for storage with safety precautions.
- 6. WBSC/AGR/N3006: Prepare fruit juices with juice extracting machines with safety precautions and
preserve fruit juices with addition of preservatives and determine the acidity and TSS content.
- 7. WBSC/AGR/N3007: Prepare and package fruit beverages such as Squashes, RTS, Nectar, Cordial,
Crush and Syrup by using appropriate machines such as pulper, juice extractor, autoclave, and
corking machine with safety precautions, determine the acidity and TSS content.
- 8. WBSC/AGR/N3008: Prepare and preserve Tomato products by using appropriate machines such as
pulper, Autoclave, and corking machine with safety precautions, determine acidity and TSS content.
- 9. WBSC/AGR/N3009: Prepare, dry and storage fruits and vegetables with appropriate methods such
as drying, cabinet drying and solar drying with safety precautions and determine the moisture.
- 10. WBSC/AGR/N3010: Prepare, preserve and store jam, jelly and marmalades by using appropriate

WBSC/AGR/N3001: Apply Safe Working Practices.
Description
Apply Safe Working Practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Safe Working Practices.
Recognition of Unsafe situations.
Fire and safety hazards.
Different fire extinguishers and their usage.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Apply and maintain Safe Working Practices.
PC2. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy.
PC3. Identify fire and safety and fire hazards.
PC4. Identify different fire extinguishers and use them as per requirements.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Procedures to achieve a safe working environment in line with occupational health and safety
regulations and requirements according to site policy.
KU2. Any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assess his report accordingly.
KU3. Fire and safety hazards and take necessary precautions against it and report according to site
policy and procedures.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assess his report accordingly.
GS2. Demonstrate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) like: safety helmet, safety glove, and safety
shoe, use the same as per related working environment.
GS3. Demonstrate basic first aid & CPR and use them under different circumstances.
GS4. Identify different fire extinguishers and use the same as per requirement in a mock drill.

WBSC/AGR/N3006: Prepare fruit juices with juice extracting machines
with safety precautions and preserve fruit juices with addition of
preservatives and determine the acidity and TSS content.
Description
Prepare fruit juices with juice extracting machines with safety precautions and preserve fruit juices with
addition of preservatives and determine the acidity and TSS content.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintenance of the perfect hygiene standard.
Selection of perfect fruits.
Preparation and measurement of fruits juice.
Determination of TSS and acidity.
Process of filling, Corking and crowning of the preserved fruit juices in sterilized bottles.
Labelling of the bottles.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Maintain the perfect hygiene standard.
PC2. Select perfect fruits.
PC3. Prepare fruits juice.
PC4. Measure juice.
PC5. Determine TSS and acidity.
PC6. Fill the preserved fruit juices in sterilized bottles.
PC7. Cork and crown the bottles.
PC8. Label the bottles.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Technology of extraction of juices from different types of fruits.
KU2. Definition of Preservatives-types of preservatives commonly used in food industry limits of
usage of preservatives.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Prepare Fruit Juice.
