CUTM/AGR/Q1201: Dairy Processing and Development Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The Individual is able to understand the technologies of raw milk processing operations, get hands on
experience to prepare different dairy products, microbiological analysis and role of dairy starters in
manufacturing fermented dairy products, monitor safety and analyze chemical quality of milk and milk
products.
Personal Attributes
A Dairy Processing and Development Supervisor should be quality-focused, responsible, and attentive to
detail, with strong technical knowledge of dairy operations. They must demonstrate leadership in
managing teams, problem-solving ability in handling processing challenges, and adaptability to modern
dairy technologies. Good communication, planning, hygiene consciousness, and commitment to food
safety and efficiency are essential.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CUTM/AGR/N1201: Understand and Execute Collection, Validation, Processing and Storage of Raw
Milk
- 2. CUTM/AGR/N1202: Apply Dairy Starters and Execute Microbiological Analysis of Milk and Milk
Products

CUTM/AGR/N1201: Understand and Execute Collection, Validation,
Processing and Storage of Raw Milk
Description
The NOS "Understand and execute collection, validation, processing and storage of raw milk" under the
qualification Dairy Processing and Development Supervisor (NSQF Level 5) focuses on equipping learners
with the knowledge and skills to manage hygienic milk collection and processing operations effectively. It
involves implementing clean milk production practices, understanding milk pricing systems, ensuring
quality validation through physical, chemical, and microbiological parameters, and carrying out standard
operating protocols for separation, pasteurization, homogenization, chilling, and storage of milk.
Additionally, the NOS emphasizes thorough documentation and adherence to safety and quality protocols
across all processes.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the end-to-end operations of raw milk handling, starting from hygienic milk
production at the farm level to systematic procurement, quality validation, processing, and
appropriate storage. It includes implementation of milk pricing systems, platform quality tests,
usage of dairy equipment (e.g., separators, pasteurizers, chillers), and adherence to SOPs to
maintain milk quality. It emphasizes hygienic practices, accurate documentation, and safety
protocols to ensure high-quality raw milk is delivered for further processing into dairy products.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Hygienic milk production and milk pricing policies
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Milk collection and pricing system based on fat content, volume, total milk solids, species of
milk animals and cost of milk production
PC2. Clean milk production practices in dairy farm
PC3. Hygienic production of milk by inspecting raw milk for fulfilment to physical, chemical, and
microbiological parameters.
PC4. Milk preservation system.
Milk procurement and processing operations with complete documentation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Sampling and lactometer reading of procured milk to determine quality following standard
operating protocols.
PC6. Platform tests (COB, alcohol, sediment, organoleptic evaluation etc.) related to appearance,
smell, acidity, consistency, color, temperature etc.
PC7. Filtration and checking microbial quality of milk according to standard protocol.
PC8. Process of separation through separator to separate cream from milk according to standard
protocol.

CUTM/AGR/N1202: Apply Dairy Starters and Execute Microbiological
Analysis of Milk and Milk Products
Description
This NOS covers the application and management of dairy starter cultures and the execution of
microbiological analyses to ensure the safety and quality of milk and its products. It includes
understanding the roles and types of dairy starters, ensuring their purity and activity, and propagating
them under hygienic conditions. The NOS also involves preparing culture media, isolating and identifying
microbial colonies, conducting Grams staining, biochemical tests, and microbial enumeration techniques to
detect contamination and maintain microbiological standards in dairy processing.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS focuses on the selection, propagation, and preservation of dairy starters used in
fermentation processes, along with the execution of microbiological analysis to ensure the safety
and quality of milk and dairy products. It covers starter purity checks, activity tests, and sterile
propagation techniques. Additionally, it includes microbial culturing, colony isolation, Gram staining,
biochemical analysis, and enumeration techniques. The scope ensures the supervisor can detect
contamination, identify desirable cultures, and maintain hygienic standards, enabling consistent
production of high-quality fermented dairy products.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Selection and importance of dairy starters
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Concept and role of dairy starters in dairy and food fermentations
PC2. Starter culture purity check: Gram's staining and catalase test
PC3. Starter activity check: Dye reduction tests
PC4. Dairy starter cultures propagation under stern hygienic conditions
PC5. Preservation of dairy starters
Microbiological analysis of milk and milk products
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Preparation and sterilization of agar media/broth (specific (MRS/M17/PDA/Mac-Conkey)/non-
specific (Nutrient agar) with other pre-requisites as per standard operating procedure
PC7. Serial dilutions with the sample as per standard protocol
PC8. Aseptic techniques (streak/spread/pour plate) for diluted sample in prepared media plates to
isolate single colonies
PC9. Identify various morphological characteristics of selected, desirable isolated cultures
PC10. Gram's staining for microscopical examination of the specific culture
PC11. Biochemical analysis of isolated dairy cultures
PC12. Preservation selected pure cultures in appropriate broth/glycerol for long term use

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the fundamental role of dairy starters in fermentation processes, their types, and their

KU2. the key indicators of starter culture purity and activity, including Gram's staining, catalase

KU3. hygienic propagation methods of dairy starters and preservation techniques such as freeze
drying and spray drying, which ensure long-term viability of cultures.
KU4. theoretical understanding of probiotics and related concepts, including their application in
developing functional fermented dairy products for health benefits.
KU5. microbiology related to dairy, including sample preparation, serial dilution, media
preparation, and aseptic techniques like streak/spread/pour plating.
KU6. how to prepare and sterilize both specific (MRS, M17, PDA, MacConkey) and non-specific
(nutrient agar) media, and understand their use for isolating and cultivating microbial
cultures.
KU7. identifying morphological characteristics of microbial colonies, and performing biochemical
tests to differentiate among dairy-relevant microorganisms.
KU8. the function and operation of instruments such as autoclaves, incubators, laminar air flow
#### units, microscopes, and UV-Visible spectrophotometers, essential for microbiological
analysis.
KU9. microbial enumeration techniques and apply appropriate microbial media to quantify
microbial load in milk and milk products.
KU10. SOPs, laboratory safety protocols, proper storage of chemicals/media, and the ability to
document microbial testing results effectively.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. understand the role, types, and application of dairy starters in fermented dairy product
manufacturing, including knowledge of probiotics, prebiotics, synbiotics, and strategies to
mitigate starter culture failures.
GS2. operate essential lab equipment like autoclaves, laminar airflow units, incubators,
microscopes, and use aseptic techniques such as streaking, pouring, and spreading methods
for isolating dairy microbes.
GS3. Perform starter culture purity checks (e.g., Gram staining, catalase test), activity checks (e.g.,
dye reduction tests), and microbial enumeration using standard media (MRS, M17, PDA,
MacConkey, Nutrient Agar).
GS4. Apply preservation methods like freeze-drying or glycerol storage for long-term use of pure
dairy cultures, ensuring microbial viability and quality over time.
GS5. follow safety guidelines, and maintaining sterile and hygienic lab and production
environments, essential for food safety.

CUTM/AGR/N1203: Process Different Types of Dairy Products
Description
The NOS "Process different types of dairy products" (CUTM/AGR/N1202) under the qualification "Dairy
Processing and Development Supervisor" (NSQF Level 5) focuses on the production of a wide variety of
dairy products. This includes preparing Indian dairy products such as khoa, chhana, paneer, fermented
items like dahi and lassi, and fat-rich products like butter and ghee. The NOS emphasizes adherence to
hygiene and standard operating procedures during processing, proper utilization of by-products,
packaging, storage, and documentation. The aim is to ensure the production of high-quality, safe, and
market-ready dairy products through practical application of dairy technologies and quality control
measures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of the NOS Process different types of dairy products under the Dairy Processing and
Development Supervisor qualification (NSQF Level 5) involves the preparation, production, quality
assurance, and documentation of various traditional and value-added dairy products. This includes
khoa and its sweets, chhana-based items, fermented products like dahi and lassi, cereal-based
products, and fat-rich products like butter and ghee. The NOS also encompasses utilization of by-
products, packaging, cold storage, and maintaining product quality through standard operating
procedures. Emphasis is placed on hygiene, process standardization, and compliance with safety
protocols.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Processing and sustaining quality in manufacturing dairy products
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Indian dairy products and their classification
PC2. Preparation and manufacture of khoa
PC3. Preparation and manufacture of khoa based sweets (burfi, peda, kalakand, rabidi, milk cake
etc.)
PC4. Preparation and manufacture of channa
PC5. Preparation and manufacture of channa based sweets (gulab jamun, rasomalai, rasogolla,
rajbhog etc.)
PC6. Preparation of paneer and yield calculations
PC7. Preparation of fermented dairy products (dahi, lassi and mishti dahi)
PC8. Preparation of cereal based dairy products (kheer and payasam)
PC9. Cream and cream standardization
PC10. Production of butter
PC11. Production of ghee from cream and butter
PC12. Utilization of butter milk and ghee residues
PC13. Accomplish packaging and proper storage of prepared dairy products
