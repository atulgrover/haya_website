#### MEP/Q0501: Design Thinking Executive
Brief Job Description
Design Thinking Executive is a professional practicing design thinking can apply a human-centred
approach effectively at workplace for problem solving, generating new ideas, implementing projects
innovatively with a high level of empathy for all involved in the process including users and non-users. The
individual would be more hands-on in their approach towards presenting & implementing ideas, working
with prototypes from initial stages itself and use both analytical and intuitive capabilities for achieving
desired outcomes.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be a good listener, have high level of empathy towards all, be active and energetic
towards work, have an open mind and be a good communicator.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MEP/N0503: Develop a design thinking mindset
2. MEP/N0504: Follow the process of design thinking for managing a project
3. MEP/N0505: Implement design solutions and evaluate impact

#### MEP/N0503: Develop a design thinking mindset
Description
This unit covers the ways to develop a design thinking mindset as a tool and methodology for project
management
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand principles of design thinking
Cultivate a design thinking mindset
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand principles of design thinking
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate understanding of the core principles of design thinking and human centred
approach
- PC2. Explain the importance of empathy in design thinking
- PC3. Identify techniques for fostering creativity and innovation
- PC4. Recognize the value of interdisciplinary collaboration in design thinking
- PC5. Analyse case studies and examples of successful design thinking applications
- PC6. Critically evaluate different design thinking frameworks and methodologies

#### MEP/N0504: Follow the process of design thinking for managing a project
Description
This unit is about following five steps involved in the process of Design Thinking during project
management.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Empathize - research the user needs
Define - state the user needs and problems
Ideate - challenge assumptions and create ideas
Prototype - create potential solutions
Test - try out the identified solutions
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Empathize – research the user needs
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Apply empathy in to design a human-centred process to solve problems.
- PC2. Use appropriate tools (e.g., interviews, mind map, empathy map, observations, etc.) to gain
a deeper, personal understanding of the issues involved in order to capture and synthesize user insights and pain points.
- PC3. Collaborate with diverse experts/ stakeholders to understand their areas of concern, and
gauge their perspectives about the service/ product
- PC4. Validate assumptions through empathy driven research and user feedback loops.
Define – state the user needs and problems
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Gather and organise all project related observations/ findings from empathize stage
- PC6. Analyse the observations/ findings to define the core problem(s) and then formulate the
problem statement(s) in a human centred manner.
- PC7. Bring clarity about the problem(s) that needs to be solved.
Ideate – challenge assumptions and create ideas
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Look at the problem from multiple perspectives and ideate innovative solutions to the
problem statement(s).
- PC9. Generate as many ideas as possible at the start of the ideation stage.
- PC10. Utilise different ideation techniques/ tools (such as Brainstorm, Brainwrite, Worst Possible
Idea, SCAMPER – Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Re- arrange/ Reverse, Timeboxing, Sketching & Dot Voting etc.)
- PC11. Investigate and test ideas to choose the best ones in order to move forward, either to solve
the problem or provide elements required to circumvent it.
Prototype – create potential solutions

#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. Investigate the key solutions and develop prototypes (inexpensive versions or specific
features) of the product/ service
- PC13. Share and run the test of the prototypes within a focus group.
- PC14. Identify the best possible solution for each of the problems identified during the first three
stages of the design thinking process
- PC15. Implement the chosen solutions within the prototypes and then test them one-by-one to
investigate the same.
- PC16. Accept, improve or reject the prototypes based on real users experiences.
Test – try out the identified solutions
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC17. Test the product/ service using the best solutions identified in the prototype stage.
- PC18. Collect feedback from the users in a constructive manner to investigate the conditions of the
use and the behaviours, thoughts and feelings of these users towards the end product.
- PC19. Rule out alternative solutions by proceeding with further iterations, alterations and
refinements.
- PC20. Loop back to an appropriate previous stage in the process in case of one or more further
problems are redefined in this stage.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Meaning of design thinking processes and its tools
- KU2. Five key steps of design thinking process
- KU3. Tools to support empathy walk such as interviews, mind map, empathy map etc.
- KU4. Meaning of a problem statement and how to write the same
- KU5. Concepts of effective business communication
- KU6. Concepts of leadership skills, decision making skills, team work etc.
- KU7. Tools for effective ideation such as Brainstorming, Brainwriting, Timeboxing, Sketching & Dot
Voting etc.
- KU8. Organisation's management tools and systems for recording, categorizing and analysing data.
- KU9. Standard tools, templates, and scripts available for dealing with user's data and feedback.
- KU10. Industrial standards and policies relevant to the organisation/ department
- KU11. Information systems used for recording and managing data and information
- KU12. Importance of maintaining the privacy and confidentiality of documents.
- KU13. Various type of clients/customer organization is dealing with
- KU14. Gender egalitarian communication

#### MEP/N0505: Implement  design solutions and evaluate impact
Description
This unit covers the implementation of design solutions and evaluating their impact to ensure they meet
user needs and project goals, with an emphasis on ethical considerations
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Effective execution and management of design projects
Assessment of design impact and ethical considerations
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Effective execution and management of design projects
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Develop a comprehensive implementation plan detailing tasks, timelines, and resources.
- PC2. Assign clear roles and responsibilities to team members involved in the implementation.
- PC3. Conduct regular team meetings to monitor progress and address issues.
- PC4. Utilize project management tools to track implementation activities.
- PC5. Ensure that the design solution adheres to defined quality standards.
- PC6. Collect feedback from stakeholders throughout the implementation process.
- PC7. Conduct regular quality assurance checks to ensure compliance with design specifications.
- PC8. Develop and follow a risk management plan to mitigate potential issues.
- PC9. Document the implementation process thoroughly for future reference.
- PC10. Promote sustainable practices by selecting eco-friendly materials and processes.
Assessment of design impact and ethical considerations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Define and monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) to evaluate the success of the design
solution.
- PC12. Conduct post-implementation reviews to assess project outcomes.
- PC13. Gather and analyse user feedback to measure satisfaction and usability.
- PC14. Compare pre-implementation and post-implementation data to evaluate impact.
- PC15. Ensure user privacy and data security throughout the implementation and evaluation phases.
- PC16. Obtain informed consent from participants in all user research and testing activities.
- PC17. Address ethical considerations and potential biases in the design solution.
- PC18. Implement design solutions that are inclusive and accessible to all users.
- PC19. Engage with stakeholders transparently, providing regular updates and addressing concerns.
- PC20. Document lessons learned and best practices to guide future projects
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.

| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |

| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
