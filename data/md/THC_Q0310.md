#### THC/Q0310: Restaurant Service (WorldSkills)
Brief Job Description
The restaurant service practitioner provides high quality food and drink service to guests. A food service
practitioner generally works in the commercial sector offering a range of services to customers. There is a
direct relationship between the nature and quality of the service required and the payment made by the
guest. Therefore the practitioner has a continuing responsibility to work professionally and interactively
with the guest in order to give satisfaction and thus maintain and grow the business. The waiter is the
most important person in dealing with guests and creating the right atmosphere in restaurant. It is
therefore necessary to have a complete command of serving rules and to know the preparation of special
dishes and drinks at the guests table or in the bar. Basic requirements are skill and resourcefulness, good
manners. good interaction with guests and practical ability.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be hospitable, patient, polite, and service-oriented with great inter-
personal and communication skills.

#### THC/N0332: Maintain work organization and manage self (Restaurant
Service)
Description
This NOS emphasizes upon professionally engaging with guest while working effectively as a team
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintaining professional etiquettes
Dealing ethically with customers
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Present themselves to guests in a professional manner
- PC2. Demonstrate personal attributes including personal hygiene, smart and professional
appearance, demeanor, and deportment
- PC3. Organize tasks effectively and plan workflows
- PC4. Consistently demonstrate hygienic and safe work practices
- PC5. Minimize waste and any negative impact on the environment
- PC6. Work effectively as part of teams and with other departments
- PC7. Act honestly and ethically in all dealings with customers, colleagues, and the employer
- PC8. Be responsive to unexpected or unplanned situations and effectively solve problems as they
occur
- PC9. Engage with continuous professional development
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Different types of food service establishment and the styles of food service that they will use
- KU2. The importance of the ambiance of the restaurant to the overall meal experience
- KU3. Target markets for various types of food service styles
- KU4. Business and financial imperatives when running a food service establishment
- KU5. Relevant legislative and regulatory requirements, including health, safety, and environment,
food handling and hygiene, and the sale and service of alcoho
- KU6. The importance of working efficiently to minimize wastage and negative impacts on the environment from business activity and to maximize sustainability
- KU7. Ethics linked to the food service industry
- KU8. The importance of effective inter-departmental working

#### THC/N0335: Serve Food
Description
This NOS describes the various styles of service across a range of menus and meals
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare portions and assemble dishes
Serve various dishes
Various styles of service
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. • Prepare, portion, and serve specialist dishes from Gueridons, including
• •Assembly of dishes.
• • Carving of meat, poultry, and wild meat
• • Filleting fish
• • Preparing and carving fruits
• • Creating garnishes for cocktails
• • Using spices in preparing dishes
• • Serving different cheeses
• • Preparing salads and salad dressings
• • Flambé dishes (meat/dessert, seafood, fruit)
• • Preparing main courses, starters, deserts
- PC2. Demonstrate appropriate flare and theatre
- PC3. Clear plates and other items from customers’ tables
- PC4. Crumb down at appropriate times between courses
- PC5. Serve a range of meals including breakfast, lunch, afternoon tea, dinner, casual, a la carte,
bar, banqueting, and fine dining service
- PC6. Provide high quality restaurant service in highly specialized or international restaurants
- PC7. Create own dishes (flambé) from ingredient lists
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. National and international food and beverage service styles and techniques
- KU2. When and in what circumstances various food service techniques would be used
- KU3. Ingredients, method of cookery, presentation, and service for all dishes on the menu, sufficient to advise guests
- KU4. Current and future trends in restaurant service
- KU5. A range of highly specialized and international cuisines and their styles of restaurant service

#### THC/N0336: Serve Beverages
Description
This NOS describes the preparation and service of various beverages following correct service procedure
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Services of various beverages
Use of special equipment to serve beverages
#### Elements and Performance Criteria

boilers, blenders, juicers, ice machines, boilers, blenders, juicers, grinders, beverage carbonating systems etc.
- PC3. Use specialist equipment for serve beverages (sommelier knife, opener, strainer, jiggers etc.)
- PC4. Create own specialty beverages with an accepted range of choice
- PC5. Prepare and serve a range of hot and cold drinks
- PC6. Prepare and serve liqueurs including with beverages
- PC7. Prepare and serve silver served hot drinks and their accompaniments
- PC8. Serve teas and coffees at banquets and functions
- PC9. Serve petit fours or sweetmeats as appropriate
- PC10. Prepare cocktails
- PC11. Prepare garnish cocktails
- PC12. Serve beverages, wines, beers, liquors, spirits, cocktails, waters

- KU1. The range of beverages that may be prepared and served in a restaurant or other outlet

- KU3. The range of glassware in which beverages may be served
- KU4. The range of china and glassware in which beverages may be served
- KU5. The range of china, silver and glassware that may be used such as sugar bowls, milk and cream jugs, spoons, strainers, tongs, etc.
