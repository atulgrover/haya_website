HSS/Q6101: Hospital Front Desk Coordinator
Brief Job Description
Individuals in this job are usually the first contact point in healthcare organizations encountered by
patients and visitors, whether on the phone, online or in person. They assist in billing, scheduling
appointments, entering data in hospital information system (HIS) and performing Front Desk/Help Desk
activities at Healthcare Facility. They should be able to adapt with new advancements/software/apps in
rendering front-office/billing activities. Individuals would perform the billing activities by keeping oneself
abreast with revision of hospital biiling structure, government schemes and policies related to TPA and
insurance services.
Personal Attributes
The job requires individuals to have good communication skills and ability to handle a high level of stress
and activity while managing fastpaced office duties. They must be computer savvy. They must be skilled
to interact with a wide range of personality types in both pleasant and difficult situations . The job requires
individuals to possess key qualities such as patience, confidence, maturity, compassion, patient centricity,
good listeningcircumstances.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HSS/N6101: Accomplish allocated task at hospital front desk
- 2. HSS/N6102: Ensure Customer Service Excellence & Patient Satisfaction
- 3. HSS/N6103: Perform billing activities and assist in TPA services
- 4. HSS/N9620: Comply with infection control and biomedical waste disposal policies
- 5. HSS/N9624: Maintain a safe and secure working environment
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

Description
This OS unit is about the skills required for coordinating activities starting from patient/visitor interaction,
admission to discharge at healthcare facility
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Customer Interaction and Handling
Manage patient flow
Carry out patient registration
Legal & ethical aspects of front desk management
Elements and Performance Criteria
Customer Interaction and Handling
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. handle the queries received over a phone/mail/visiting patients etc. applying relevant
medical words & terms used in hospitals
PC2. coordinate for care needs with other care providers through appropriatecommunication
method

channel

occupancy, approx. discharge timings, cost etc.

patients

PC10. ensure smooth patient flow within the hospital
PC11. ensure hospital environment is comfortable & pleasing to patients, visitors, employees &
others

HSS/N6102: Ensure Customer Service Excellence & Patient Satisfaction
Description
This OS unit is about the tasks involved to address patient / visitor complaints / concerns by taking
appropriate course of action to maintain customer service excellence and arrive at a satisfactory
resolution.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Provide comfort to visitors/customers and patients as per organizational process
Maintain quality care, ethical practices, and effective communication.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Provide comfort to visitors/customers and patients as per organizational process
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. maintain service excellence in healthcare settings keeping empathy, passion andpatience
PC2. meet patients / visitors expectations as much as possible in various hospital areas in
accordance to organizational policies
PC3. coordinate to address complaints related to service provided
PC4. ensure corrective & preventive actions are taken timely for patients / visitors concerns
PC5. know the departmental process and adhere to them while assisting
PC6. indulge in training at regular intervals to learn best practices
PC7. apply process quality tools used in the organization as directed
Maintain quality care, ethical practices, and effective communication.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. to address the patients by name or sir/mam, also not putting any relations.
PC9. understand the key behaviours while interacting with patient/family at all times (eg: eye
contact smile/art of saying no , pleasing body language, positive expression).
PC10. educate the patient family on patient’s rights and responsibilities /consent management.
PC11. know various patient rights and responsibilities applicable to work area
PC12. escalate process to competent authority in case of any deviation or non-conformance as per
organizational policies and procedures

HSS/N6103: Perform billing activities and assist in TPA services
Description
This OS unit is about how to process cash and credit transactions
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Process payments transactions & Reconcile patient accounts
Assist TPA services
Elements and Performance Criteria
Process payments transactions & Reconcile patient accounts
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the services being rendered to the client
PC2. assess accurateness of the invoice generated through various means
PC3. check that payments from patients are valid and accurate
PC4. record payments from patients promptly and accurately as per organizationalpolicies
PC5. record clearly and accurately the reasons if payments are overdue
PC6. identify problems accurately and sort them out promptly
PC7. inform senior or concerned authority promptly about any problems which cant be sorted out
PC8. store collected payments securely and in line with organization policies
PC9. check that charges, credits made to patient accounts are correct
PC10. identify and sort out problems with patient accounts
PC11. inform senior or concerned authority promptly about problems with patientaccounts which
are beyond the limits of competence & authority
Assist TPA services
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. know the process of TPA and insurance services
PC13. understand the hospital billing structure, government schemes and policies related to TPA
and insurance services
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. legislation, standards, policies, and procedures followed in the organization relevant to own
employment and performance conditions
KU2. hospital topography and spectrum of internal & external clients that visit the hospital
KU3. role and importance of the front desk in supporting healthcare operations
KU4. organization pricing, discount policy, documentation & reporting process

Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
Assessment Guidelines
- 1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Either
each element/Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC
will also lay down proportion of marks for Theory, viva and Skills Practical for each element/PC.
- 2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions approved by the SSC.
- 3. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each
candidate/batch at each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
- 4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical & viva for every student
at each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
- 5. In case of successfully passing as per passing percentage of the job role, the trainee is certified for the
Qualification Pack.
- 6. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
Minimum Aggregate Passing % at QP Level : 70
