CPC/CAP/N0701: Basic Engineering
Description
This NOS is machine tool used for shaping or machining parts by rotating a workpiece against a cutting
tool. It is a highly precise and automated tool commonly used in manufacturing processes.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS is about developing the fundamental engineering knowledge and practical skills necessary
for operating, maintaining, and assisting in basic engineering activities in a plastics manufacturing
or processing environment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Apply Basic Engineering Concepts
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understanding of basics of bench working
PC2. Identify various hand tools
PC3. Importance of filling & fitting
PC4. Understanding various tools used for filling & fitting operation
PC5. Follow the Procedure of filling
Perform Basic Maintenance and Housekeeping
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Able to make various fittings like V fit, square fit, dovetail fit
PC7. Able to operate the measuring instruments
PC8. Measurement of outside diameter & inside diameter of shaft without any error
PC9. Able to measure pitch of threads and taper angles
Use Basic Measuring Instruments
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. Do simple calculations to find the weight, angles & tapers
PC11. Understanding of basics of drawing.
PC12. Able to make isometric and orthographic projections
PC13. Understanding drawings for preparation of process planning
PC14. Understanding and interpretation of geometric tolerances
PC15. Able to make & measure the job precisely

Description
This NOS covers the competencies required to operate CNC lathe machines safely and efficiently to
produce precision components as per job specifications
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers includes machine setup, operation, inspection, troubleshooting, and maintenance
under minimal supervision
