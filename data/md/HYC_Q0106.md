HYC/Q0106: Mud mix Assistant-Drilling Operations
Brief Job Description
Individual at this job is responsible to maintain the flow of mud mix fluid during drilling operation,
maintains the mud pumps, tanks, hoses, air compressors, generators, valves to ensure the smoothly flow
of mud during drilling operation. The person at this job checks the quantity and quality of the drilling mud,
sand content viscosity, specific to gravity of drilling mud mix.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have good sense of responsibility, a good organizer and time keeper. The person
should have the ability to perform physical/hard work individually or in a team.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HYC/N0109: Preparation for maintenance of the mud pump and other equipment

operation

HYC/N0109: Preparation for maintenance of the mud pump and other
equipment
Description
This unit is about preparing mud pump and other equipment for drilling operation at oil or gas rig.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for operational and maintenance of mud pump and equipment
Perform repair and maintenance of mud pump and equipment
repairing motor/pump during breakdown, monitor motor/pump to ensure it is functional and
maintain motor/pump to prevent it from any breakdown
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for maintenance of mud pump and equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check the availability of calibrated tools or instrument, spare parts, equipment for repair and
maintenance activity
PC2. wear Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) before entering into the workplace
PC3. carry out visual inspection for leakage, damage, etc. in Mud Tanks, Mud pumps, Standpipe,
Rotary Hose, Shale Shaker, Degasser, Centrifugal Pump, Hopper, Agitator, Suction
Dampener, Mud Pump Discharge line, etc
PC4. clean the workplace Mud Tanks, Mud pumps, Standpipe, Rotary Hose, Shale Shaker,
Degasser, Centrifugal Pump, Hopper, Agitator, Suction Dampener, Mud Pump Discharge line,
etc. before repair or maintenance work
PC5. install maintenance and repair signages at the site
PC6. report malfunctioning of equipment to shift Incharge
Perform repair and maintenance of mud pump and equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. perform preventive, routine and non-routine maintenance and safety check to ensure proper
functioning of internal combustion engines, pumps, compressors and drilling related
equipment of the rig
PC8. lubricate mud pump, compressor and other equipment before use
PC9. coordinate with technician for repair of faulty part of pump, engine, equipment, etc.
PC10. isolate equipment, engine or pump before starting repair activity
PC11. perform disassembly, loading, unloading and assembly of the drilling equipment, engine,
pump, etc. for repair
PC12. Position the equipment, machinery, physical structures, or other objects for assembly or
installation, using hand tools, power tools, and moving equipment
PC13. follow material/equipment lifting and handling procedure

HYC/N0110: Prepare and maintain the property of drilling mud fluid
throughout the drilling operation
Description
This unit is about creating drilling mud fluid for drilling for extraction of oil and gas in a rig.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for operational and maintenance of mud pump and equipment
Perform repair and maintenance of mud pump and equipment
repairing motor/pump during breakdown, monitor motor/pump to ensure it is functional and
maintain motor/pump to prevent it from any breakdown
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare mud mix for drilling operation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. obtain geotechnical information of the field
PC2. design and prepare the mud mix using mud and chemical in the mud tank as per the drilling
procedure and geology of drilling site
PC3. check the fluid to establish a baseline by recording different parameters such as marsh
funnel viscosity, pH, fluid density, fluid loss, yield point, plastic viscosity, etc.
PC4. collect and check mud samples after every one hour with the baseline to detect and monitor
any problem
PC5. make necessary adjustments in the mud viscosity, mud temperature, pH balance, and
chemical components as per the requirement during the drilling process,
PC6. report any deviation in mud mix with the standard baseline to the shift incharge
Maintain mud mix flow cycle
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. store mud mix in a mud tank / suction tank
PC8. maintain the flow of mud mix from mud tank through mud pump, Standpipe and Rotary Hose
to the drill Bit and returned back through Annulus /Return Line to Shaker & Mud Tanks
PC9. remove the cuttings from the mud mix using shale shaker and hydrocyclone/ centrifugal
Pump
PC10. check the property of mud, degasify the mud mix in degasser in case of gas found in the
mud mix
PC11. store the mud mix in the mud tank which is returned back from annulus after removal of
cutting and gas
PC12. use agitator to maintain the consistency of mud mix
PC13. maintain the flow the mud mix cycle during drilling operation
