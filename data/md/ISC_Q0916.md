#### ISC/Q0916: Mechanic High Speed  Rotating Machinery (Steel Plant)
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role performs mechanical maintenance, of fans, compressors, turbo blowers, sinter
fans etc. by following organizational specified procedures and standards.
Personal Attributes
The person should be patient, organised, team-oriented and have the ability to work for long hours in
adverse conditions. They must be able to plan and prioritise tasks effectively and have an eye for detail
and quality.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ISC/N0008: Use basic health and safety practices at the workplace
2. ISC/N0953: Prepare for maintenance of high-speed rotating machinery
3. ISC/N0954: Perform maintenance of high-speed rotating machinery
4. ISC/N0955: Perform high speed rotating machinery post-maintenance activities
5. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### ISC/N0953: Prepare for maintenance of high-speed rotating machinery
Description
This OS unit is about preparing for maintenance of maintenance of high-speed rotating machinery like
fans, compressors, turbo blowers, sinter fans etc.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the following:
. Identify work requirements
. Prepare for maintenance activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify work requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the machine, its manufacturer's specifications and functioning from the user manual
- PC2. identify the work to be done by interpreting the checklist, engineering drawings, work order
and SOPs etc.
- PC3. interpret information from sections, views and scale of measurement used in drawing of the
machine
- PC4. identify correct limits, tolerance and fits of rotating machine by interpreting the drawings
properly
- PC5. identify the tools, equipment, consumables and spare parts required during the task
Prepare for maintenance activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. use appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for safe working in workshop
- PC7. read maintenance schedule and checklist and plan the time and schedule for conducting the
maintenance
- PC8. select and arrange the right tools, equipment, consumables and spare parts as per the SOP
and job requirements
- PC9. check the tools, equipment, consumables and spare parts for any defects and that they are
as per the required quality standards
- PC10. check and calibrate the tools and equipment before use
- PC11. prepare tools, tackles, spares, lifting equipment etc. as per SOP/WI
- PC12. use checklist and coordinate with the superior for confirming maintenance tasks
- PC13. report damaged / defective components of equipment and tools as per the escalation matrix

#### ISC/N0954: Perform maintenance  of high-speed rotating machinery
Description
This OS unit is about performing the maintenance of high-speed rotating machinery as per the SOP.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the following:
Perform maintenance of high-speed rotating machinery
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform maintenance of high-speed rotating machinery
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. enquire with the user/operator about any problems/unusual conditions noticed on the
rotating machinery
- PC2. follow safety practices during maintenance activities as per organisational SOP
- PC3. perform basic health check-up of rotating machinery as specified in the maintenance
checklist
- PC4. conduct visual and dimensional inspections to assess the components
- PC5. check the equipment for any operational deviations, geometric inaccuracies or internal
conditions to test its working status and expected conditions
- PC6. perform various calculations to size and assess rotating equipment performance (head,
discharge temp, inlet capacity, volumetric efficiency etc.)
- PC7. check the machine parts for any wear and tear, damage, improper functioning etc.
- PC8. dismantle the equipment and replace/change the spare parts and consumables as per the
