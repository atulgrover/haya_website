MSU/THC/Q0201: Sustainable Tourism Manager
Brief Job Description
This is a short-term qualification that prepares individuals to effectively manage and promote sustainable
practices within the tourism industry, focusing on environmental, social, and economic impacts.
Personal Attributes
A sustainable tourism manager should have strong environmental awareness, problem-solving skills, and
leadership abilities. Effective communication, cultural sensitivity, and collaboration are key, alongside
analytical thinking and adaptability. Passion for sustainability and a commitment to responsible tourism
are essential for success in this role.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MSU/THC/N0201: Develop sustainable tourism strategies for hospitality and tourism enterprises
- 2. MSU/THC/N0202: Organise staff training and development for sustainable tourism
- 3. MSU/THC/N0204: Implement sustainable tourism activities and operations
- 4. MSU/THC/N0203: Generate awareness and engage guests and tourists

MSU/THC/N0201: Develop sustainable tourism strategies for hospitality
and tourism enterprises
Description
This NOS equips learners with the skills and knowledge required to apply environmental management
system, sustainable procurement and supply chain management and implement sustainable infrastructure
and facilities in tourism sector. This unit of competency could be practically applied in various settings
within the tourism industry like hotels, resorts, lodges, promoters of tourism in specific destinations, NGOs
working in the tourism sector, tourism management and hospitality schools and NGOs working in the
tourism sector.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assess the Environmental Impact
Assess goals, objectives and sustainability needs of the hospitality/tourism enterprise
Ensure Supply chain, infrastructure, and facilities management
Elements and Performance Criteria
Assess the Environmental Impact
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify sustainable tourism processes applicable to the tourist destination
PC2. Collect and analyze data related to environmental performance and sustainability indicators
PC3. Interpret data to identify trends, areas for improvement, and potential environmental risks
PC4. Evaluate the sustainable tourism planning technique being employed
PC5. Develop a set of practical and feasible strategies to achieve a balance between the
organization’s growth and sustainability
PC6. Engage stakeholders in the organization's sustainability agenda and actively solicit their
input on effective sustainability measures in their realm of operation
- 2. Assess goals, objectives and sustainability needs of the hospitality/tourism enterprise
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Describe the Global Sustainable Tourism Council's criteria and guidelines to identify relevant
environmental, social, and economic indicators
PC8. Discuss the principles of responsible tourism, as outlined by World Tourism Organization
(UNWTO)
PC9. List and explain the stakeholder engagement models and sustainable development
frameworks
PC10. Name and explain the various impact assessment methodologies
Ensure Supply chain, infrastructure, and facilities management
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Assist stakeholders in mapping their work areas (like supply chain, construction) to identify
environmental and social risks, areas for improvement, and develop mitigation strategies

MSU/THC/N0202: Organise staff training and development for sustainable
tourism
Description
This NOS equips learners with the skills and knowledge required to effectively plan, implement, and
evaluate staff training and development programs and teach them communication strategies to promote
eco-friendly behaviour.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
conduct sustainable tourism training program
develop staff engagement and communication strategies
Elements and Performance Criteria
conduct sustainable tourism training program
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. effectively educate staff members on the fundamental principles of sustainable tourism
PC2. explain the importance of responsible tourism and its impacts on the environment, local
communities, and cultural heritage
PC3. provide guidance on integrating sustainability into daily operations and guest interactions
develop staff engagement and communication strategies
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. train staff in feasible and practical specific skills and techniques related to sustainable
operations
PC5. enhance staff knowledge in waste management, energy conservation, water efficiency, and
biodiversity conservation
PC6. promote eco-friendly practices and behaviors among staff members
PC7. train staff to provide information, answer queries, and promote sustainable behaviors and
choices to guests
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. sustainable tourism principles, concepts, and best practices
KU2. environmental, social, and cultural impacts of tourism and the importance of responsible
tourism
KU3. sustainable operations and strategies for integrating sustainability into daily operations and
guest interactions
KU4. techniques related to waste management, energy conservation, water efficiency, and
biodiversity conservation

MSU/THC/N0204: Implement sustainable tourism activities and operations
Description
This NOS equips learners with the skills and knowledge required to Plan and organise sustainable tourist
activities and ensure safety of guests, promote natural and cultural heritage, collaboration with local
community members and sustainable practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Implement principles, interpretation techniques and safety precautions
Incorporate environmental initiatives
Elements and Performance Criteria
implement principles, interpretation techniques and safety precautions
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. plan and organise tourist activities that are sustainable in nature including but not limited to
ecotourism, adventure tourism, cultural tourism, rural tourism, culinary tourism, wildlife
tourism, etc.
PC2. organise promotion of the natural and cultural heritage of the destination
PC3. ensure that required safety precautions plans for guests
PC4. arrange training for staff to take appropriate action in emergency situations
incorporate environmental initiatives
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. organise environmental awareness among guests about local ecosystems, biodiversity, and
conservation efforts
PC6. promote responsible behavior and respect for the environment and community during
tourism activities
PC7. develop strong partnerships and collaborations with local communities, ensuring that they
benefit from tourism activities while actively participating in cultural preservation efforts
PC8. organise assessments to identify the needs, skills, and capacity of the local community for
tourism-related employment
PC9. collaborate with local community members to develop community-based tourism initiatives
that showcase their culture, traditions, and natural resources, creating employment
opportunities for the community
PC10. develop and implement sustainable practices and guidelines for the organisation focusing on
waste management, energy and water conservation, responsible use of natural resources,
and biodiversity conservation

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
