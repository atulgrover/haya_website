#### ISC/Q1701: Suraksha Saathi
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role follows health and safety procedures specified by an organization to maintain
safety of self and others at workplace.
Personal Attributes
The person should be patient, organised, team-oriented and have the ability to work for long hours in
adverse conditions. They must be able to plan and priorities tasks effectively and have an eye for detail
and quality.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ISC/N0009: Work effectively with others
2. ISC/N1701: Controlling hazards and accidents at workplace
3. ISC/N1702: Keeping workplace clean and tidy to avoid accidents

#### ISC/N0009: Work effectively with others
Description
This OS unit is about communicating with colleagues/superiors and others, either in own work group or in
other work groups within organisation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communicate effectively with colleagues and others
Interact with supervisor
Follow appropriate behaviour at work place
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Communicate effectively with colleagues and others
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. coordinate with colleagues to share work, as per the workload in order to achieve team goals
- PC2. maintain clear communication with colleagues and others, wherever needed, through all
means i.e. face-to-face, telephonic or written
- PC3. adjust communication styles to reflect gender and persons with disability (PwD) sensitivity
- PC4. respect all colleagues and co-workers
- PC5. resolve conflicts by communicating with colleagues and other departments
Interact with supervisor
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. identify work requirements by receiving instructions from reporting supervisor
- PC7. escalate problems to supervisors that cannot be handled
- PC8. report the completed work
- PC9. interact with the reporting supervisor about any possible hazards and safety concerns
Follow appropriate behaviour at work place
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. extend help to people with Disability (PwD) at workplace, if required
- PC11. empathize with people with disability
- PC12. adopt a gender neutral behavior
- PC13. adopt responsible and disciplined behaviours at the workplace
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the importance of effective communication and establishing good working relationships with
colleagues and supervisor

#### ISC/N1701: Controlling hazards and accidents at workplace
Description
This OS unit is about following safety practices at the workplace specified by the organization to control
and maintain the safety of self and others.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identifying and eliminating hazards at workplace
Emergency and fire safety procedures in case of accidents
First-aid procedures in case of accidents
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identifying and eliminating hazards at workplace
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. inspect the workplace to identify hazardous activities and their possible causes and risks or
accidents associated with them
- PC2. carry out routine check of the tools and equipment for identifying potential hazards.
- PC3. use appropriate protective clothing/equipment while working at workplace.
- PC4. use appropriate machines, tools and equipment for the job and ensure that they are used
properly and safely.
- PC5. follow appropriate safety practices and use appropriate safety equipment i.e. respirator, face
mask etc. while working in and around trenches and confined spaces.
- PC6. follow appropriate safety practices and use appropriate safety equipment i.e. safety harness,
ladder etc. while working at heights.
- PC7. follow COHSS and MSDS while using hazardous substances and chemicals.
- PC8. follow correct procedure and body postures while lifting heavy objects manually or use
appropriate lifting equipment if lifting mechanically.
- PC9. report or communicate about any hazards, breaches in safety and security policies, accidents
and incidents to the designated person.
Emergency and fire safety procedures in case of accidents
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. attend safety training and fire safety drills to obtain information about organization’s
emergency response plan and respond promptly during an emergency.
- PC11. follow emergency procedures such as raising alarm, safe evacuation, use of fire-exit, calling
on emergency number etc. during an emergency or fire accident.
- PC12. respond promptly and appropriately to an accident or medical emergency.
- PC13. use appropriate type of fire extinguisher in case of fire accident.
- PC14. apply appropriate rescue techniques during fire hazard.
Apply first-aid procedures in case of accidents

#### ISC/N1702: Keeping workplace clean and tidy to avoid accidents
Description
This OS unit is about always keeping the workplace clean and maintain housekeeping to avoid accidents
due to waste, clutter, oil spills etc.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Apply 5S for housekeeping
Carry out cleaning of workplace
Follow waste management practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Apply 5S for housekeeping
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. follow the fundamentals of 5S for housekeeping.
- PC2. sort the tools and equipment as per 5S system.
- PC3. return the equipment, materials and personal protective equipment that were used to the
right places making sure they are clean, safe and securely stored.
- PC4. remove unnecessary items from each area.
- PC5. clean and inspect each area regularly.
Carry out cleaning of workplace
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. ensure good housekeeping in order to prevent hazards and accidents.
- PC7. check for spills/leakages in various tasks/activities/processes.
- PC8. display the appropriate signage for the work being conducted.
- PC9. ensure that there is no oily substance, scrap material, waste, clutter etc. are left on the floor
to avoid slips, trips and falls.
- PC10. follow regular cleaning and disinfection practices at workplace using appropriate techniques
and materials.
- PC11. follow health and hygiene practices at workplace using appropriate techniques and
materials.
- PC12. identify and report to the appropriate person any additional cleaning required that is outside
one's responsibility or skill.
Follow waste management practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. segregate waste into different categories
- PC14. identify recyclable, non-recyclable and hazardous waste.
- PC15. dispose non-recyclable, recyclable and reusable waste appropriately at identified location.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |

| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
