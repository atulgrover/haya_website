MEP/Q2901: Instructional Designer
Brief Job Description
The Instructional Designer qualification equips individuals with the skills to design, develop, and evaluate
effective training programs across various learning formats, including classroom and digital environments.
The role involves applying instructional design models, creating learner-centric content, integrating
assessment strategies, and ensuring quality through alignment with legal, ethical, and pedagogical
standards. It prepares professionals to work collaboratively with stakeholders and subject matter experts
to deliver impactful and engaging learning experiences.
Personal Attributes
The individual must have expertise in the technical/vocational domain of instruction. They must have
strong communication, organizational and interpersonal skills. They must be quality focused and
encourage learner engagement. Additionally, they should remain abreast with the latest trends in their
domain and upgrade their facilitation skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MEP/N2910: Apply instructional design principles and models
- 2. MEP/N2911: Design training courses and evaluation plans
- 3. MEP/N2902: Plan and design specific learning and development interventions and programs
- 4. MEP/N2908: Review learning resources as per design document
- 5. MEP/N2912: Design and evaluate e-learning courses
- 6. MEP/N2909: Comply with Intellectual Property Rights (IPR), Rules, Regulations and Ethics
- 7. MEP/N2913: Design and evaluate assessment methods
- 8. MEP/N9912: Apply principles of professional practice at the workplace

MEP/N2910: Apply instructional design principles and models
Description
This NOS focuses on applying instructional design principles to create effective, learner centered training
experiences. It covers analyzing learners, selecting suitable models, and developing structured, outcome-
aligned learning content.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the foundations of instructional design and learning principles
Analyze learning context and audience
Apply instructional models and frameworks
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand the foundations of instructional design and learning principles
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the concept, purpose, and value of instructional design in developing effective
learning experiences.
PC2. Describe essential learning theories (such as behaviorism, cognitivism, and constructivism)
and their implications for instructional design.
PC3. Distinguish between different learning styles and apply key principles for creating learner-
centered environments.
PC4. Identify and describe core guiding principles of accelerated learning for increasing learner
motivation and retention.
Analyze learning context and audience
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Conduct learner analysis to identify audience characteristics, prior knowledge, and learning
needs.
PC6. Perform content and task analysis to structure learning logically and sequentially.
PC7. Analyze contextual factors such as learning environment, delivery mode, and available
resources to guide instructional decisions.
PC8. Apply differentiated learning principles suitable for designing training for children and adults,
based on their developmental and cognitive needs.
Apply instructional models and frameworks
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Identify and apply instructional design models such as ADDIE, Gagné’s Nine Events of
Instruction, and Bloom’s Taxonomy.
PC10. Use Kirkpatrick’s Four Levels of Evaluation to design outcome-aligned assessment strategies.
PC11. Write clear, measurable learning outcomes using action verbs aligned with Bloom’s
taxonomy levels.

MEP/N2911: Design training courses and evaluation plans
Description
This NOS enables professionals to plan and design training programs based on learner needs and
organisational goals. It also guides them in developing evaluation strategies to measure training
effectiveness.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Design the structure and components of a training course
Conduct need analysis to determine course requirements
Design training evaluation plans
Elements and Performance Criteria
Design the structure and components of a training course
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify the purpose and scope of the training course in alignment with organizational or
learner objectives.
PC2. Define the key elements of the training course including course objectives, content topics,
delivery modes, duration, and instructional strategies.
PC3. Organize the course structure into logical modules or units that align with the intended
learning outcomes.
PC4. Document the course design plan with clearly outlined components and alignment to desired
competencies.
Conduct need analysis to determine course requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Select an appropriate need analysis methodology such as surveys, interviews, job/task
analysis, or performance data review.
PC6. Collect and analyze data to determine training needs, performance gaps, learner profiles,
and contextual challenges.
PC7. Use findings from the need analysis to tailor the course content, delivery methods, and level
of complexity.
Design training evaluation plans
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Identify key training outcomes to be evaluated (e.g., learning, behavior change, performance
improvement).
PC9. Design a training evaluation strategy using relevant models such as Kirkpatrick’s Four Levels
or equivalent approaches.
PC10. Select appropriate tools and criteria for evaluating training effectiveness, including pre/post
assessments, feedback forms, observation checklists, or interviews.
PC11. Document the evaluation plan specifying what will be measured, how, when, and by whom.

MEP/N2902: Plan and design specific learning and development
interventions and programs
Description
This unit deals in detail with planning and designing learning and development interventions and programs
based on identified training and development needs.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify the learning outcomes required for learning and development programmes
Prepare a plan for a learning and development intervention to meet learning outcomes
Review learning program and structure
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify the learning outcomes required for learning and development programmes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify the objectives of the learning intervention by referring to the training needs analysis
and engaging with stakeholders.
PC2. Confirm competency standards and training specifications to be addressed in the learning
program.
PC3. Identify characteristics of the target learner group including age, literacy levels, prior
learning, and language preferences.
PC4. Draft learning outcomes aligned to the program objectives and obtain stakeholder feedback
and approvals.
Prepare a plan for a learning and development intervention to meet learning outcomes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Identify available resources including budgets, delivery modes, trainers, materials, and
timeframes for implementation.
PC6. Develop a training structure using instructional principles, segmenting content, sequencing
sessions, and aligning to defined learning outcomes.
PC7. Prepare a session plan that defines methodologies, duration, assessment strategy, and
required materials for each learning segment.
PC8. Review and validate the planned intervention with stakeholders and obtain necessary
approvals.
Review learning program and structure
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Review the draft program and content with stakeholders using appropriate tools and collect
structured feedback.
PC10. Revise the learning program based on feedback and obtain final approvals.
PC11. Document the final learning program in line with organizational and national qualification
frameworks.

MEP/N2913: Design and evaluate assessment methods
Description
This NOS covers the skills and knowledge required to design, implement, and evaluate assessment
strategies using assessment principles, diagnostic processes, competency standards, and Kirkpatricks
evaluation model. It includes developing assessment tools, conducting diagnostic assessments, evaluating
assessor performance, and improving assessment quality based on evidence and feedback.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Design and execute assessment strategy using principles of assessment and Kirkpatrick's evaluation
model
Conduct diagnostic assessment to determine individual training and performance needs
Design and develop competency-based assessment tools and processes
Evaluate assessment effectiveness and assessor performance to ensure quality and consistency in
assessment processes
Elements and Performance Criteria

training context and organizational needs.
