PSS/Q2419: Bijli Mistry
Brief Job Description
The incumbent in the job is responsible for maintenance services on a diverse range of services, including
LV electrical distribution equipment, General & Emergency Lighting, LV power, Motors, Pumps, Domestic
Appliances and Control Systems. The role of the Bijli Mistry is to undertake both planned and reactive
maintenance and repair tasks across the range of electrical building services, plant and equipment across
and to be able to undertake their electrical work effectively.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be physically fit, attentive to detail, and capable of working in both indoor and outdoor
environments. They should demonstrate strong problem-solving skills, technical aptitude, and the ability to
perform tasks safely under minimal supervision. Good communication skills, teamwork, a customer-
oriented approach, and a strong sense of responsibility and discipline are essential for ensuring efficient
maintenance and repair services.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. PSS/N1336: Working effectively with others
- 2. PSS/N2418: Carry out power supply installation and maintenance

and solar water pumps

- 5. PSS/N2421: Operation and Maintenance of Diesel Generator Set
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

PSS/N1336: Working effectively with others
Description
This unit covers basic practices that improve effectiveness of working with others in an organizational set-
up
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Work effectively with others
Respect diversity
Elements and Performance Criteria
Work effectively with others
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. accept information and instructions from the supervisor and fellow workers and seek
clarification when required
PC2. pass the information to the persons to all those concerns within agreed timeframe in a
manner they can understand and confirm its receipt
PC3. help & assist fellow workers in performing their tasks in a positive manner so as to maximize
efficiency
PC4. use appropriate tone, pitch and language while communicating to convey politeness,
assertiveness, care and professionalism
PC5. listen attentively and respond appropriately while interacting with others at work
PC6. act responsibly and demonstrate disciplined behaviour at the workplace (Disciplined
behaviour: Punctuality, completing tasks within agreed timescales, ensuring quality
standards, not gossiping and wasting time, honesty, etc.)
PC7. escalate grievances and problems to the appropriate authority as per the organizational
procedure to resolve them and avoid conflict
Respect diversity
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. respect gender diversity in the workplace
PC9. use respectful verbal, non-verbal and written communication that is gender, disability and
culturally inclusive
PC10. communicate with everyone without any personal bias based on gender, disability, caste,
religion, colour, sexual orientation and culture
PC11. recognize indicators of harassment and discrimination based on gender, disability, caste,
religion, colour, sexual orientation and culture at workplace and follow organisational policy
for reporting the same

PSS/N2418: Carry out power supply installation and maintenance
Description
This module provides the skills and knowledge to install, inspect, and maintain household electrical power
supply systems. It includes basic electrical principles, wiring methods, use of protective devices,
troubleshooting common faults, and ensuring safety and cleanliness in the work environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers installation, inspection, troubleshooting, and maintenance of household electrical
power supply systems while ensuring proper safety practices.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Basic concepts of electricity
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain basic electrical terms such as voltage, current, resistance, power, and energy with
practical relevance to household wiring
PC2. Interpret electrical symbols, wiring diagrams, and single-line diagrams
PC3. Identify types of electrical supply systems (single-phase, three-phase) and their applications
PC4. Explain the significance of earthing, neutral, and phase in an electrical circuit
PC5. Calculate simple electrical parameters using Ohm’s law and basic formulae
Household power supply installation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Identify and arrange devices, tools, and equipment required for installation
PC7. Select appropriate rating and current-carrying capacity wires, cables, fuse, switches, sockets,
MCBs, ELCBs as per requirements
PC8. Lay conduit pipe concealed and open wiring, batten, casing-capping, and temporary cleat
wiring
PC9. Ensure correct selection of protective devices against electric shock and overload
PC10. Implement methods of protection from external influences (switchgear, warning signs,
danger notices)
Maintenance and troubleshooting
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Inspect and locate faults such as blown fuse, Miniature Circuit Breaker/ Residual Current
Device trip, short circuit location
PC12. Check for open circuit, overheated switches, sockets, loose contacts, or overloads
PC13. Ensure equal load distribution in three-phase wiring in large premises
PC14. Check polarity to ensure all switches are connected in phase conductors
PC15. Repair or replace faulty parts using correct procedures
PC16. Ensure work area is clean and safe after job completion

PSS/N2419: Install, repair, and maintenance of household electrical
gadgets including solar panels and solar water pumps
Description
This NOS involves installing, repairing, and maintaining household electrical gadgets, solar panels, and
solar water pumps, including site preparation, wiring, safety checks, preventive maintenance, fault repair,
testing, and basic troubleshooting as per standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Installation, repair, and maintenance of household electrical gadgets ensuring safety, reliability, and
efficiency.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Installation of household electrical gadgets
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify, select, and verify specifications of household gadgets household electrical gadgets
including lights, fans, heaters, mixer grinders, microwave ovens, electric kettles, induction
cooktops, toasters, sandwich makers, washing machines, inverters, coolers, doorbells,
Motors etc. as per customer/site requirement
PC2. Prepare installation site ensuring safety clearances, wiring arrangement, and compliance
with standards
PC3. Install household gadgets using correct tools, mounting techniques, and wiring methods
PC4. Connect household gadgets to supply system following correct wiring diagrams and load
ratings
PC5. Ensure protective devices (MCB, RCCB, fuses) and earthing are installed and tested
PC6. Test the household gadget for safe operation and record installation details
Repair and Maintenance of Electrical Household Gadgets
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Perform preventive maintenance such as cleaning, lubrication, tightening, and inspection of
household gadgets
PC8. Diagnose common faults such as wiring issues, component failure, overheating, or short
circuits
PC9. Repair or replace defective parts like switches, wiring, terminals, motors, heating elements,
control boards
PC10. Test repaired gadgets for functionality, insulation resistance, and safety compliance
PC11. Maintain accurate records of repairs, replacements, and maintenance schedules
Installation & Maintenance of Solar Panels and Solar Water Pumps
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. Identify specifications, type, and capacity of solar panels/solar water pumps for site
requirements

PSS/N1337: Apply Basic Health and Safety Practices for Power Related
Work
Description
This unit covers health, safety and security for power related work. This includes procedures and practices
that candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work environment in a power
plant, power station/substation or on the field while working on power equipment. It covers responsibilities
towards self, others, organizational assets and the environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Follow workplace health and safety practices
Follow fire safety practices
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow workplace health and safety practices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. use protective clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions (Protective clothing:
leather or asbestos gloves, flame proof aprons, flame proof overalls buttoned to neck, cuff
less (without folds) trousers, reinforced footwear, helmets/hard hats, cap and shoulder
covers, ear defenders/plugs, safety boots, knee pads, particle masks, glasses/goggles/visors)
(Equipment: hand and face shields, machine guards, residual current devices, shields, dust
sheets, respirator)
PC2. identify job-site hazards and possible causes of risks/accident at the workplace (Hazards:
electrical hazards, sharp edged and heavy tools, heated metals, oxyfuel and gas cylinders,
welding radiation, hazardous surfaces, hazardous substances, physical hazards. Possible
causes of risk and accident: physical actions, not following instructions, inattention, sickness
and incapacity, health, not taking safety precautions)
PC3. use standard safe working practices when working at heights, confined areas, trenches and
with electrical equipment
PC4. follow warning signs and symbols while working with electrical systems (Warning signs:
Danger plate, high voltage area, out of service, etc.)
PC5. ensure positive isolation of electrical equipment and system as per the given standards
PC6. identify any abnormalities in electrical equipment or system viz temperature, pressure, flow
of current etc.
PC7. use various methods of accident prevention in the work environment (Methods of accident
prevention: use of equipment and working practices, safety notices, advice, instruction from
colleagues and supervisors)
PC8. use proper scaffolds, elevated platforms and ladders for working at height
PC9. inspect various tools and plants (T & P) routinely for any signs of oil, water leakages
PC10. store flammable materials and machine lubricating oil safely and correctly
