#### SGJ/Q4011: EV Charging Installation Technician
Brief Job Description
The EV Charging Installation Technician qualification provides learners with the knowledge and skills to
manage the complete lifecycle of EV charging infrastructure-covering planning, installation, operation, and
maintenance. The program includes training in EV science, relevant policies, digital tools like software-
based charging and GPS mapping, as well as site readiness and safety protocols. Participants will be
equipped to lead teams, ensure efficient station operations, and support the growth of India EV ecosystem.
This Qualification will support EV industries, by providing skilled workers for management of EV charging
infrastructure as Charging Station Supervisors. EV industry has rolled down several EV models in 2-
Wheeler, 3-Wheeler and 4-Wheeler segments and have already put significant charging infrastructure in
many Indian cities.
Personal Attributes
An EV Charging Installation Technician should be technically skilled, safety-conscious, and detail-oriented.
They must possess leadership qualities, be adaptable to digital tools, and committed to supporting
sustainable EV infrastructure.

#### SGJ/N4079: Introduce Basics of EV Science and Impact on Low Carbon
Transport.
Description
This unit outlines the foundational knowledge required to understand electric vehicle (EV) technology and
its role in promoting low-carbon transportation. It includes the basic principles of EV science, components
and functioning of electric vehicles, and the environmental benefits of EV adoption.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The Scope covers the foundational understanding of electric vehicle (EV) technology and its relevance to low-carbon transport systems. It includes the basic science behind EVs, key
components and working principles, environmental and economic advantages, and the role of EVs in
reducing emissions and supporting clean and sustainable mobility solutions.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Basic EV science and Impact of EV to build low carbon transportation; Comparison between ICE and
Electric Vehicles
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand the key components of an electric vehicle
- PC2. Comprehend the types of batteries used in EVs, their characteristics, and their role in energy
storage and delivery.
- PC3. Explore the environmental benefits of EVs
- PC4. Understand the concept of life cycle analysis and how it applies to EVs, comparing it with the
life cycle of ICE vehicles.
- PC5. Evaluate the total cost of ownership for EVs, including factors like purchase price,
fuel/electricity costs, maintenance, and government incentives.
- PC6. Explore the growth and challenges of EV charging infrastructure, including the importance of
public/home charging stations.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Basic principles of electric vehicle (EV) technology.
- KU2. Components of EVs including motors, batteries, and controllers.
- KU3. Types and characteristics of EV batteries (e.g., Li-ion, Lead-acid).
- KU4. Total cost of ownership comparison between EV and ICE vehicles.
- KU5. Charging systems and power requirements for EVs.
- KU6. Role of EVs in achieving sustainable transportation goals.
- KU7. National and global trends in EV adoption.

#### SGJ/N4080: Demonstrate  EV Charging through Computer/Mobile
Applications
Description
This unit outlines the skills and knowledge required to operate electric vehicle (EV) charging stations using
computer and mobile applications. It includes understanding the components and user interface of
software-driven EV charging systems, managing charging sessions, monitoring real-time data, processing
payments, and troubleshooting common issues.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the use of digital platforms computer and mobile applications for operating and
managing EV charging stations. It includes initiating and monitoring charging sessions, handling
user authentication and payments, analyzing charging data, and resolving common software-related
issues. The scope also involves ensuring secure and user-friendly interactions to enhance the efficiency and accessibility of EV charging infrastructure.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Demo of computer/mobile use for software driven EV charging (Master Class)
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand the essentials of document creation, formatting, and editing in Word, including
templates, styles, and collaboration tools.
- PC2. Learn spreadsheet management, data entry, functions, formulas, and data analysis using
Excel, including charts and graphs.
- PC3. Gain insight into the components and architecture of software-driven EV charging systems,
including charging stations, charging management software, and communication protocols.
- PC4. Navigate the user interface of software-driven EV charging systems, monitor charging
progress, and troubleshooting common issues.
- PC5. Explore data collection and management in EV charging, including billing, usage tracking, and
remote monitoring capabilities.
- PC6. Successfully initiate and manage EV charging sessions using software-driven systems,
including user authentication and payment processing.
- PC7. Identify and resolve common issues that may arise during EV charging
- PC8. Use software-driven tools to monitor and collect charging data, including billing and usage
statistics.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Basics of computer and mobile operations.
- KU2. Overview of EV charging software platforms.

#### SGJ/N4081: Introduce Policy and Regulatory Ecosystem for Electric
Vehicle Charging Stations
Description
Gain comprehensive knowledge of the policy and regulatory ecosystem for EV charging stations, including
safety standards, permitting, land use, environmental considerations, government incentives, and
international best practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Covers the legal, procedural, and environmental aspects of planning, establishing, and operating EV
charging stations, along with incentives and global regulatory insights.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Policy & Regulatory Ecosystem of Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging Stations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand the existing policy landscape for EV charging stations, covering areas such as
safety standards, permitting processes, land use regulations, and grid integration.
- PC2. Gain insight into the regulatory framework that governs the siting, design, and operation of EV
charging stations, including legal requirements and codes of practice.
- PC3. Explore government incentives, subsidies, and tax benefits that promote the development of
EV charging infrastructure and encourage EV adoption.
- PC4. Learn about environmental regulations and considerations, including emissions reductions and
sustainability requirements, related to EV charging infrastructure
- PC5. Navigate the permitting and zoning requirements for EV charging stations, including land use
permissions and location-specific regulations.
- PC6. Explore international best practices in EV charging station regulation and policy, enabling you
to adapt and improve local policies and standards.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. EV charging station policies, safety standards, and grid integration.
- KU2. Legal requirements and codes for siting, design, and operation.
- KU3. Government incentives, subsidies, and tax benefits.
- KU4. Environmental and sustainability regulations.
- KU5. Permitting, zoning, and land use rules.
- KU6. International best practices and regulatory models.

#### SGJ/N4084: Conduct Testing and Commissioning  of EV Charging Stations
Description
The individual will be responsible for identifying testing requirements, developing and executing a
commissioning plan, performing accurate testing procedures, and demonstrating proficiency in the end-to-
end commissioning of EV charging stations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
It covers the skills and knowledge required to test, commission, and ensure the proper functioning of
various components of Electric Vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Testing and Commissioning of EV Charging Station
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify testing requirements for various charging station components.
- PC2. Develop a commissioning plan for EV charging infrastructure.
- PC3. Execute testing procedures accurately.
- PC4. Demonstrate proficiency in the commissioning process.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understanding the types, architecture, and components of EV charging stations (AC/DC chargers, connectors, power modules, communication interfaces).
- KU2. Knowledge of relevant national and international testing standards (e.g., IEC, IS, IEEE) applicable to EVSE (Electric Vehicle Supply Equipment).
