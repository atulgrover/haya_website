#### RSC/Q2601: Rubber Extruder Operator
Brief Job Description
The Rubber Extruder operator sets up, operates, and maintains automated rubber extrusion machines to
produce consistent rubber profiles. The Operator controls process parameters, inspects output quality,
troubleshoots operational issues, and collaborates with production teams to ensure efficient and safe
operations.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to work independently or under supervision. He should be motivated and
have a positive attitude. He should be comfortable in performing laborious work and operate machines
efficiently. The individual must be result oriented and be able to work in factory environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N2612: Setup Extrusion Machine
2. RSC/N2613: Operate & Control Automated Rubber Extrusion Machine
3. RSC/N2614: Monitor Production and Perform Maintenance
4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol

#### RSC/N2612: Setup Extrusion Machine
Description
This unit covers the preparation activities required before starting rubber extrusion: material verification,
machine setup, die installation, temperature setting, and pre-operation safety checks.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare work area and safety
Select and inspect material
Prepare extrusion machine
Initial parameter setting and trial
Documentation and handoff
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare work area and safety
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure work area is clean, well-lit, and hazard-free
- PC2. place safety signage and remove unused items
- PC3. wear PPE and check functionality
- PC4. verify emergency stops and interlocks
Select and inspect material
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. verify rubber compound type against job sheet
- PC6. inspect compound for contamination or moisture
- PC7. document material lot number and expiry
- PC8. weigh compound accurately
Prepare extrusion machine
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. clean barrel and screw before loading
- PC10. fit correct die geometry and tightness
- PC11. install hopper and feeding system
- PC12. check heating zones and thermocouple connectivity
- PC13. set temperature zones, screw speed, and feed rate
- PC14. conduct trial run and adjust parameters
- PC15. ensure consistent flow and correct shape
- PC16. fine-tune cooling or cutter positioning
Documentation and handoff
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

#### RSC/N2613: Operate & Control Automated Rubber  Extrusion Machine
Description
This unit teaches safe, efficient operation of automated extrusion machinery, including continuous
monitoring of output, making parameter adjustments, quality inspection, and coordinating with auxiliary
systems.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Continuous monitoring
Quality inspection
Process control and adjustment
Coordination with teams and compliance
Emergency handling
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Continuous monitoring
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. monitor melt pressure, torque, and temperature zones
- PC2. review HMI alarms and resolve minor alerts
- PC3. check conveyor alignment and torque load
- PC4. verify cooling water flow and temperature
- PC5. oversee feeder alignment and material feeding
Quality inspection
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. measure profile dimensions using calipers
- PC7. inspect surface finish and color uniformity
- PC8. check hardness or density if specified

- PC12. fine-tune die height and cutter timing
- PC13. optimize feeder pressure for compound flow
- PC14. coordinate with auxiliary cooling and winding
Coordination with teams and compliance

#### RSC/N2614: Monitor Production and Perform Maintenance
Description
This unit equips candidates with skills to monitor continuous extrusion operations, perform routine
maintenance, detect performance decline, and execute minor troubleshooting or machine care tasks.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Routine monitoring
Preventive maintenance
Calibration & sensor care
Troubleshooting & minor repairs
Waste disposal & Team Coordination
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Routine monitoring
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. check line output vs target speeds
- PC2. record temperature and pressure values periodically
- PC3. inspect cooling and cutting system function
- PC4. log any variation from normal process trends
Preventive Maintenance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. schedule cleaning for die, barrel, and screw
- PC6. lubricate bearings, gearbox, and feeder units
- PC7. replace filters, seals, and worn linings
- PC8. tighten bolts per torque specifications
Calibration & sensor care
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. calibrate temperature and pressure sensors
- PC10. verify calibration with standard references
- PC11. document calibration dates and results
Troubleshooting & minor repairs
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. inspect mechanical components for wear
- PC13. replace gaskets, belts, and screws
- PC14. adjust feeder tension or alignment
- PC15. debug minor electrical faults under guidance
Waste disposal and team coordination

Description
This unit is about maintaining health and safety of self and others at workplace.
Scope

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
