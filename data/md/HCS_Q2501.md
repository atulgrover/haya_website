HCS/Q2501: Decorative Glass Cutter (Glassware)
Brief Job Description
The decorative cutter-glassware makes required decorative shapes on glass articles with the help of
cutting machine. To perform the work competently the person is required to work with precision in
delivering outcomes with the help of appropriate cutting techniques. The individual works under
supervision and follows instructions related to the work. The individual understands the different kinds of
etching machines used in glass manufacturing and how to operate the etching matchines.
Personal Attributes
Needs to be focused, alert and physically active as well as capable of working for long hours in sitting
position. Must understand the importance of quality work. He should have to ability to handle glassware
safely during the cutting process.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HCS/N2501: Prepare for making decorative design on glass articles
- 2. HCS/N2404: Etch the Glass products
- 3. HCS/N9916: Follow basic health and safety practices for glassware crafts
- 4. HCS/N9901: Coordinate with colleagues and work as a team
- 5. HCS/N9902: Maintain Safe and Healthy Work Environment
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

HCS/N2501: Prepare for making decorative design on glass articles
Description
Decorative glass cutter makes required decorative shapes on glass articles with the help of cutting
machine. To perform the work competently the person is required to work with precision in delivering
outcomes with the help of appropriate cutting techniques.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
prepare work area for making decorative design on glass articles
work safely
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare work area for making decorative design on glass articles
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Obtain job instructions from responsible personnel Job instruction: Design, cutting
requirement, dimensions of the product to be cut, area etc.
PC2. Read and confirm that the given instructions and design are complete and correct.
PC3. Obtain appropriate machine and materials suitable for the required work Machine: Basic
cutting machines Raw materials: Glass articles
PC4. Report on any shortage or defect of raw materials to the concerned person before
commencement of work Concerned person: Supervisor, line in charge, store keeper etc.
PC5. Plan out an efficient sequence of work as per the job requirement in accordance with
approved procedure.
PC6. Check that the selected tools and machine are in safe working condition and ready to use.
PC7. Select wheel for cutting machine as per design requirement
PC8. Use approved procedures to inspect glass articles visually for any possible defects as per
required standard parameters prior to work Defects: Unevenness, chipped glass, holes on
surface, rough texture, etc.
PC9. Identify the surfaces and dimensions of the article
PC10. Fix wheel on cutting machine for making decorative design on glass articles
Work safely
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Comply with health and safety requirements and procedures for decorative glass cutting at
all the times.
PC12. Work safely at all times, complying with health and safety legislation, regulations and other
relevant guidelines.
PC13. Maintain correct body postures while seating for long hours in allocated work space.
PC14. Lift, carry or move glass articles from one place to another using safe working practices.

HCS/N2404: Etch the Glass products
Description
This unit is about the etching of raw glass products to ensure that the surface of the product is smooth by
working the same on the etching machine.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
identify the etching requirements of the product
etch the product
achieve quality
take safety measures to be adopted during etching
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify the etching requirements of the product
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand instructions from production supervisor regarding product dimensions for
etching.
PC2. Obtain instructions from production supervisor regarding daily targets for etching.
PC3. Identify design and pattern to be etched on the surface.
PC4. Identify the measure of precision and surface finished to be produced.
PC5. Work on different types of etching wheels used in the process.
PC6. Return products and etching wheel to designated locations.
Etch the product
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Assemble etching wheel correctly to ensure they are properly balanced and fit to use.
PC8. Ensure that the proper method of dressing an etching wheel is followed.
PC9. Handle etching wheel.
PC10. Control speed of etching wheel.
PC11. Control functions of all the components used the etching wheel.
PC12. Make pattern/ design for etching on the product by pencil or sketch.
PC13. Place the surface to be etched on the etching wheel.
PC14. Apply appropriate quantities of water as lubricant during the process.
PC15. Ensure minimum damage during the process of etching.
PC16. Ensure that the machinery is suitable for its intended use and properly maintained.
PC17. Control the stoppage of devices.
PC18. Get specific work related instructions from supervisor, if required.
Achieve quality
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

HCS/N9916: Follow basic health and safety practices for glassware crafts
Description
This unit covers health, safety and security at the workplace. This includes procedures and practices that
candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work environment.
Scope
