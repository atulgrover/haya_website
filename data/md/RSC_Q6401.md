RSC/Q6401: Processing Assistant - TSR (Technically Specified Rubber)
Brief Job Description
The Processing Assistant TSR supports the production of Technically Specified Rubber by assisting in
shredding, washing, drying, sieving, baling, and packing operations. The role ensures process efficiency,
quality compliance, workplace safety, and proper documentation under the supervision of senior
operators.
Personal Attributes
The individual in this job should have good physique to handle heavy machinery and amenable to
discipline and must have good sense of neatness and cleanliness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N1013: Assist in preparation of raw material for TSR Processing
- 2. RSC/N1015: Support in shredding and washing operations
- 3. RSC/N1017: Perform drying, sieving, baling and packing of finished rubber
- 4. RSC/N5629: Follow safety, environmental and hygiene practices
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

RSC/N1013: Assist in preparation of raw material for TSR Processing
Description
This NOS covers the competencies required to support the preparation of raw material for Technically
Specified Rubber (TSR) production. It includes collection, cleaning, sorting, chopping, weighing, and safe
handling of raw rubber to ensure quality feedstock for subsequent processing stages.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Support in raw material preparation for TSR production
Assist in maintaining process readiness
Elements and Performance Criteria
Support in raw material preparation for TSR production
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify and collect field latex, cup lumps, and other raw materials as per standard guidelines
PC2. assist in cleaning, chopping or cutting raw rubber lumps to the required size for processing
PC3. carry out sorting and removal of foreign matter, stones, and wood maintain quality
PC4. follow standard procedures for blending and homogenizing raw material before processing
PC5. ensure uniformity and readiness of raw material for subsequent TSR operations
PC6. apply correct methods for weighing and measuring raw rubber before processing
PC7. demonstrate safe storage practices of collected raw material to prevent contamination and
deterioration
PC8. conduct visual checks to recognize moisture content or spoilage in raw rubber
PC9. assist in documenting batch details, including raw material source, quantity, and preparation
status
PC10. ensure traceability of raw materials by labeling and tagging as per organizational
requirements
Assist in maintaining process readiness
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. prepare workstations, tools, and machinery required for raw material preparation
PC12. assist in safe loading/unloading and transfer of raw materials to designated areas
PC13. maintain the cleanliness or orderliness of the work area in compliance with safety and
environmental norms
PC14. report and raw material inconsistency or equipment malfunction to the supervisor
PC15. follow prescribed health, safety and PPR guidelines during all preparation activities
PC16. perform basic cleaning of work area, tools, and containers before raw material transfer
PC17. arrange required utilities such as water, electricity, and air supply for uninterrupted
processing

RSC/N1015: Support in shredding and washing operations
Description
This NOS covers assisting in the shredding and washing processes of field coagulum or cup lumps for the
production of Technically Specified Rubber (TSR). It includes tasks such as operating and monitoring
shredding and washing equipment, ensuring uniform cleaning, maintaining safety, and supporting waste
management in compliance with environmental standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist in Shredding Operations
Support in Washing Activities
Maintain Process Hygiene and Safety
Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist in Shredding Operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify and arrange new rubber for shredding as per instructions
PC2. assist in feeding raw rubber lumps into the shredding machine safely
PC3. monitor machine functioning during shredding to avoid jamming and overloading
PC4. recognize uneven shredding or irregular sizes and report to the operator
PC5. apply basic adjustments such as aligning material flow to ensure uniform shredding
PC6. collect shredded material and transfer to the designated washing area
PC7. follow proper housekeeping practices around shredding equipment
PC8. ensure adherence to machine safety protocols, including use of PPE
PC9. maintain a log of material fed and shredded as per batch records
PC10. support preventive maintenance by assisting in cleaning and lubrication of the machine
Support in Washing Activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. prepare washing equipment and ensure adequate supply of clean water
PC12. load shredded rubber into washing tanks or conveyors as instructed
PC13. monitor washing process to ensure effective removal of dirt and impurities
PC14. adjust water flow and temperature under supervision for consistent washing
PC15. identify improperly washed material and repeat the process as needed
PC16. collect and drain washed rubber for further processing
PC17. record washing parameters such as water usage, duration, and batch size
PC18. ensure safe disposal or recycling of used water as per environmental guidelines
PC19. follow hygiene and safety practices to prevent slips, contamination, and hazards
Maintain Process Hygiene and Safety

RSC/N1017: Perform drying, sieving, baling and packing of finished
rubber
Description
This NOS covers supporting the drying, sieving, baling, and packing of finished TSR or processed rubber.
The individual is required to monitor and carry out operations, ensure quality and uniformity, maintain
hygiene and safety standards, and document process parameters for efficient handling and storage of
finished rubber.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct rubber finishing operations
Perform quality and safety compliance in rubber finishing
Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct rubber finishing operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify and list out finished rubber batches for drying, sieving, baling, and packing
PC2. demonstrate, perform, and carry out drying operations while ensuring required moisture
content
PC3. apply sieving methods to separate impurities and achieve uniform particle size
PC4. conduct and perform baling operations according to standard specifications
PC5. show, display, and apply correct packing techniques for safe storage and shipment
PC6. recognize deviations in quality or process and report them to the supervisor
PC7. develop good housekeeping and hygiene practices in the work area
PC8. document and record process parameters, batch details, and operational observations
Perform quality and safety compliance in rubber finishing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. understand quality requirements for finished rubber and ensure compliance during drying,
sieving, baling, and packing
PC10. explain, define, and elaborate on standard operating procedures (SOPs) for handling rubber
PC11. identify potential hazards and develop safe working practices
PC12. discuss and summarize process outcomes to maintain quality standards
PC13. point out, state, and mention corrective actions in case of process deviations
PC14. show, display, and demonstrate correct use of PPE and safety equipment
PC15. perform and carry out material handling efficiently while maintaining workplace safety
PC16. conduct inspections to verify that drying, sieving, baling, and packing operations meet
required standards
