#### SGJ/Q1203: Solar Photovoltaic Site Survey Assistant
Brief Job Description
Solar Photovoltaic Site Survey Assistant performs basic solar site survey and assessment for the
installation of small solar system. He/She conducts a physical solar site survey using various tools and
ensures compliance with state and national regulations, guidelines, along with industry and safety
standards for solar PV installations. The job holder must show a high level of attention to while surveying
and have good written and verbal skills to communicate with supervisor, solar consumers and fellow team
members.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it without any accidents so
diligence and hardworking are desired attributes for individuals performing this role. He/She must also
demonstrate strong work ethics, an ability to communicate courteously with co-workers and must be good
with following instructions of the supervisor.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SGJ/N1209: Introduce Solar Energy applications and importance of site survey
2. SGJ/N1210: Introduce Solar Photovoltaic Plant
3. SGJ/N1211: Assess shadow free area at solar project site
4. SGJ/N1212: Perform the use of tools for site survey
5. SGJ/N1213: Perform load analysis for small solar consumers
6. SGJ/N1214: Assess Consumers need for solar power
7. SGJ/N1215: Prepare a site survey report
8. SGJ/N1216: Maintain Personal Health & Safety while performing site survey at solar project site
9. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
