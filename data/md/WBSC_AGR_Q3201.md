#### WBSC/AGR/Q3201: Service Assistant (Agriculture Machineries)
Brief Job Description
Perform repair and servicing of Agriculture machineries like Tillage, Seeding, Harvesting, Threshing
machineries and Tiller, Plant Protection equipment along with Diesel pump set.
Personal Attributes
The individual should possess a keen sense of Agricultural process, Mechanical Aptitude, Attention to
Detail, and Problem-Solving Skills. They should be disciplined, cooperative in team settings and adaptable
to work in real job scenarios involving food processing.

#### WBSC/AGR/N3201: Apply Safe Working Practices (Service Assistant
Agriculture Machineries)
Description
Apply Safe Working Practices
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Safe Working Practices.
Recognition of any unsafe situations according to site policy.
Fire and safety hazards.
Different types and usage of fire extinguishers as per requirements.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Apply and maintain Safe Working Practices.
- PC2. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy.
- PC3. Identify fire and safety and hazards.
- PC4. Identify different fire extinguishers and use them as per requirements.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The procedures to achieve a safe working environment in line with occupational health and safety regulations and requirements according to site policy.
- KU2. Recognition of any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assess his report accordingly.
- KU3. Identification and process of taking necessary precautions on fire and safety hazards and report according to site policy and procedures.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assess his report accordingly.
- GS2. Demonstrate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) like: safety helmet, safety glove, and safety
shoe, use the same as per related working environment.
- GS3. Demonstrate basic first aid & CPR and use them under different circumstances.
- GS4. Identify different fire extinguishers and use the same as per requirement in a mock drill.

#### WBSC/AGR/N3206: Classify IC engines used in agriculture, explain the
working of engine system, fuel system, cooling system, lubricating system, inlet & exhaust system with the help of flow charts
Description
Classify IC engines used in agriculture, explain the working of engine system, fuel system, cooling system,
lubricating system, inlet & exhaust system with the help of flow charts
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Different types of IC engine.
Characteristics and application of two stroke and four stroke engine.
Different parts and function of an IC engine.
Components and function of different systems concerned with engine such as engine system, fuel system, cooling system, lubricating system, and inlet & exhaust system with the help of flow chart.
Dismantlement of the engine, cleanliness of different parts, detection of fault, rectification and refit.
Different parts such as couplings, pipes, glands, gaskets etc and identification of the defects and
repair of the same.
Alignment test for engine and its adjustment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate different types of IC engine.
- PC2. Describe the characteristics, application of two stroke and four stroke engine.
- PC3. Recognize different parts of an IC engine and the function of each.
- PC4. Describe components and function of different systems concerned with engine such as engine
system, fuel system, cooling system, lubricating system, and inlet & exhaust system with the help of flow chart.
- PC5. Dismantle the engine, clean different parts, detect the fault, rectify and refit.
- PC6. Identify different parts such as couplings, pipes, glands, gaskets etc.
- PC7. Identify the defects and perform repairing of the same.
- PC8. Perform alignment test for engine and its adjustment.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Engine classification - Cl and SI Engines, 2S and 4S Engines, working principles of the IC
engines and difference between 2S & 4S, Cl & SI Engines.
- KU2. Brief description of Engine components.
- KU3. Engine Systems- Brief description of engine systems and their necessity.
