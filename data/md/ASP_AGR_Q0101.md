ASP/AGR/Q0101: HYDROPONICS PRODUCER
Brief Job Description
Hydroponics producers are individuals who specialize in growing plants using the hydroponic method.
Hydroponics is a soilless growing technique that involves cultivating plants in a nutrient-rich water
solution.
Personal Attributes
Hydroponics Farm Entrepreneurship and managerial skills
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

ASP/AGR/N0101: BASICS OF HYDROPONICS
Description
The candidate will obtain knowledge on hydroponics, conventional farming and plant growth requirements.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The candidate will obtain knowledge on hydroponics, conventional farming and plant growth
requirements.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Evaluate the basic understanding of hydroponics gardening
PC2. Assess the knowledge on the features and advantages of soilless cultivation
PC3. Evaluate the knowledge of the various environmental factors affecting the plant growth
PC4. Knowledge about the importance of plant nutrition and essential nutrition requirements for
healthy plant growth.
PC5. Assess the ability to differentiate between different hydroponics systems
PC6. Knowledge on concept and design of Hydroponics systems
PC7. Evaluate the awareness on site considerations for setting up Hydroponics systems
PC8. Evaluate knowledge on the growing media
PC9. Assess the basic knowledge on various pumping and electrical systems
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. BASICS OF HYDROPONICS FARMING

ASP/AGR/N0102: NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT
Description
The candidate will obtain knowledge on the requirements and methodology of hydroponic nutrient
management.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding Nutrient Requirements.
Nutrient Solution Formulation.
Troubleshooting and Problem solving regarding nutrient management.
Familiarize with different hydroponic nutrient delivery systems.
Understanding the significance of pH in hydroponic nutrient management.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Basic Understanding of various nutrients and their effect on plants
PC2. Ability to select the right component salts in proper ratio
PC3. Knowledge about TDS/EC and its significance
PC4. Knowledge about Ph and its effect on plant growth
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. BASIC KNOWLEDGE

This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Career Development & Goal Setting
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs

Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
Assessment Guidelines
ASAP Kerala follows an assessment framework which provides weightage for all the activities connected
with skilling in which students get involved during the training program. The components of assessment
include Attendance, Assignments, Internal Assessments and Final Assessment. Of these, Attendance and
Internal Assessment come under Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE). All Assessments with
regard to the academic status of the student should be reflected as marks and overall assessment by
awarding grades.
Attendance Criteria
Skill based sessions and training are delivered through lectures, discussions, demonstrations, and
experiments ensuring student participation during daily learning activities. So those who miss such
sessions will not be able to compensate for the loss. Regular student participation in daily classroom
activities plays a significant role in student’s success. For each course and batch, the student’s attendance
will be marked daily, and marks will be awarded accordingly on course completion before final
assessment. Students whose attendance fall below 70% will not be eligible for final assessment and course
completion certificate.
