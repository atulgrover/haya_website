#### PSC/Q0602: Industrial wastewater Treatment Plant Operator
Brief Job Description
Industrial Wastewater Treatment Plant Operator responsible for operating and maintaining effluent
treatment systems, monitoring water quality parameters, conducting routine tests, adjusting chemical
dosages, troubleshooting equipment issues, and ensuring compliance with environmental regulations.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be able to work independently and be comfortable in performing laborious work. The
person should be good at following instructions and a team player with a positive attitude.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PSC/N0170: Operating Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP)
2. PSC/N0171: Monitoring and managing Effluent Treatment Plant
3. PSC/N0172: Analysis of Effluent
4. PSC/N0603: Maintain Records & Reporting at Site
5. PSC/N0602: Health and Safety Practices at the treatment Plant
6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### PSC/N0170: Operating  Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP)
Description
This unit is about operating the Effluent treatment plant and understanding the various types of unit
operations in effluent treatment plants. This unit is also about different terminologies that are used in ETP.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for effluent treatment
Carry out operations in ETP
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for Effluent treatment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Start the plant by switching on the pump, mixer, controllers, and other equipment.
- PC2. Perform the manual work in the effluent treatment plant.
- PC3. Check, open, or close the valves.
- PC4. Perform the operations at outflow & inflow of the plant by controlling the discharge.
- PC5. Collect the samples and facilitate the calibrations of samples.
Carry out operations in ETP
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Removal of large solids such as rags, sticks, grit, and grease that may damage equipment.
- PC7. Removal of floating and settled materials such as suspended solids and organic matter.
- PC8. Removal of biodegradable organic matter and suspended solids.
- PC9. Perform the Aerobic biological treatment technologies.
- PC10. Perform biotreatment process by Conventional Activated Sludge Process (CASP).
- PC11. Can carry out another biotreatment process by Cyclic Activated Sludge System (CASS).
- PC12. Familiar with how to carry out Integrated Fixed Film Activated Sludge (IFAS) system.
- PC13. Knowledge of the process of Membrane Bioreactor (MBR) for biological degradation.
- PC14. Removal of residual suspended solids/dissolved solids.
- PC15. Complete the documentation work as required.
Operational control Strategies
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC16. Removal of wastewater by mixed liquor suspended solids process.
- PC17. Calculation of Sludge volume index and sludge density index.
- PC18. Sludge age: Mean Cell Residence Time (MCRT).
- PC19. Able to calculate the Food/Mass Ratio.
- PC20. Able to calculate Constant MLSS.

#### PSC/N0171: Monitoring and managing  Effluent Treatment Plant
Description
This unit is based on monitoring and managing the effluent treatment plant and to ensure proper working
of effluent treatment plant and how the problems should be troubleshoot in case of any discrepancy.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Monitoring the ETP
Managing the ETP
Troubleshooting the problems in ETP
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Monitoring the ETP
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Monitor the working of mechanical equipment of ETP
- PC2. proper check of mechanical instruments such as blowers, pumps, centrifugal separators,
valves, etc
- PC3. Monitor the physical quality of water at the intermediate of treatment and at outflow of the
plant.
- PC4. Monitor weather the secondary treatment is carried out as per the standards.
- PC5. Proper check the meter and gauge readings in the plant.
- PC6. Proper check the working of all the treatment units that carries out treatment.
- PC7. Evaluate the performance of biological treatment processes, such as activated sludge,
biofilters, or lagoons
- PC8. Monitor the biomass concentration, sludge age, and microbial activity to ensure proper
treatment. Adjust aeration and nutrient dosing if required.
Managing the ETP
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Measure and monitor the flow rates of wastewater and treatment chemicals throughout the
system
- PC10. Regularly test the influent and effluent water to assess its quality and identify any deviations
from the required standards
- PC11. Clean or repair these components to maintain proper sedimentation and clarification
processes.
- PC12. Check the condition of filters, such as sand filters, activated carbon filters, or membrane
filters
- PC13. Monitor sludge quality, volume, and disposal practices according to regulatory requirements.
Troubleshooting the problems in ETP
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

#### PSC/N0172: Analysis of Effluent
Description
This unit is based on the analysis of physical, chemical, biological quality of effluent.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
identify the commonly used chemicals.
Perform chemical tests according to procedures.
Precautions during testing
#### Elements and Performance Criteria identify the commonly used chemicals
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Familiar with the chemicals used in Coagulation and flocculation like acrylamide copolymers,
aluminium chloride, aluminium sulphate, etc…
- PC2. An individual should be aware of chemicals used in pH adjustment like calcium carbonate,
calcium hydroxide, calcium oxide, carbon dioxide, etc…
- PC3. Chemicals used in disinfection and oxidation products such as anhydrous ammonia,
ammonium hydroxide, calcium hypochlorite, etc…
- PC4. Different chemicals used in softening, Algicide, Taste and odour control, Dechlorinate and
antioxidant, Bleaches, etc…
- PC5. And individual should know the use and applications with some other major chemicals like
Ferric alum or Ammonium iron (III) sulphate, Lime (calcium oxide), Polyelectrolyte
(flocculant) and De-chlorinator
Perform chemical tests according to procedures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Able to perform Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD)
- PC7. Analyse Microbial test
- PC8. An individual must be able to carry out Dissolved Oxygen Test (Winkler Method)
- PC9. An individual should be able to carry out Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)
- PC10. Total Suspended Solids (TSS)
- PC11. Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)
- PC12. Oil & Grease Content
- PC13. Jar Testing
- PC14. Other relevant tests
Precautions during testing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC15. Make sure that all chemicals in the laboratory have proper labels.
- PC16. Perform the use of chemicals, gases, or reagents with labels.

#### PSC/N0603: Maintain Records &  Reporting at Site
Description
This unit focuses on the importance of maintaining accurate records and effectively reporting activities at
a sewage treatment plant. It emphasizes the significance of documentation in ensuring compliance with
regulations, monitoring plant performance, and facilitating continuous improvement. Trainees will learn
how to record data, prepare reports, and communicate relevant information to stakeholders.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand how to maintain records and data
Reporting to the higher officials
Respond to higher officials
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
