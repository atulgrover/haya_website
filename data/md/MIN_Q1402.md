#### MIN/Q1402: Dumper/ Tipper Operator
Brief Job Description
The Dumper/Tipper operator uses the Dumper/Tipper (a heavy specialized truck) for transporting large
volume of loose material such as sand, gravel, crushed rock, coal, over burden, ore etc. to designated
place such as crusher hopper, site, dump yard, washing/handling plant etc. The operator is also
responsible for discharging its load to rear, bottom or on one side efficiently.They also ensure that the
safety systems are working correctly and performing essential maintenance on the Dumper/Tipper.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the task at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should also be physically agile, strong, have good eye sight and not suffer from
colour-blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:

5. MIN/N1703: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental Guidelines for opencast mines

#### MIN/N1405: Prepare  the Dumper/Tipper for operations
Description
This unit is about preparing the Dumper/Tipper for activities that need to be carried out during the shift.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conducting pre-operation checks and routine maintenance of the Dumper/Tipper
Recording and reporting the details of Dumper/Tipper's pre-operation inspection and maintenance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conducting pre-operation checks and routine maintenance of the Dumper/Tipper
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. follow the work related instructions given by the supervisor before the shift starts
- PC2. check oil levels of engine, differential, hydraulic tank etc. and top up as required
- PC3. check water level in the radiator and top up as required
- PC4. confirm availability and functionality of safety features in the Dumper/Tipper like audiovisual
alarm, reverse camera, mirrors, horn, seat belt, Roll-Over Protection Structure (ROPS) and Falling Object Protective Structure (FOPS)
- PC5. check fan belt tension, electrolyte level, and batteries’ terminal tightness
- PC6. clean the Dumper/Tipper and outer element of an air cleaner with compressed air
- PC7. apply grease to all the required parts
- PC8. drain accumulated water from the diesel tank
- PC9. check correct air pressure in tires
- PC10. check and take action as per the entries done by the previous operator in the Dumper/Tipper
logbook
- PC11. check for any spills/leakages in the engine, hydraulic systems, differential etc.
- PC12. plug spill, leakage of oil and lubricants and escalate to appropriate authority if unable to
rectify
- PC13. check functioning of fire extinguishers, automatically operated fire suppression system and
devices including fire detection system in the Dumper/Tipper
- PC14. clean air filter dust bowls
- PC15. ensure the locking bar is in position to prevent the front and rear chassis moving and
creating a crushing zone (articulated machines only)
- PC16. keep footplates and steps clean and free from dirt and oil
- PC17. check main brake and emergency brakes, engine cooling system, lifting jacks
- PC18. check different types of levers provided in the Dumper/Tipper.
- PC19. check and adjust driving position, rear and side mirrors, seat belts and confirm clear
uninterrupted vision all around
Recording and reporting the details of Dumper/Tipper's pre-operation inspection and maintenance

#### MIN/N1406: Perform  Dumper/TIpper  operations
Description
This unit is about performing various Dumper/Tipper operations in the mines during the shift.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Starting and driving the Dumper/Tipper
Loading and hauling the load to the designated destination
Discharging the ore/waste correctly by tipping operation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Starting and driving the Dumper/Tipper
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. use Dumper/Tipper in an optimum way as per the instruction manual
- PC2. start the engine by using pushbutton and key switch together
- PC3. select correct gear range by shifting the quadrant device
- PC4. maintain a safe distance between the two Dumpers/Tippers while driving
- PC5. follow all bye-laws while driving the Dumper/Tipper
- PC6. identify and report any unsafe condition while driving the Dumper/Tipper such as trenches,
potholes and cables etc.
- PC7. use various signalling devices available in the Dumper/Tipper such as turn signal, parking
indicator, air horn etc. as per requirement
- PC8. take proper precautions while reversing the Dumper/Tipper
- PC9. park the Dumper/Tipper at an appropriate place
- PC10. use parking brakes and stopper after parking the Dumper/Tipper
- PC11. drive Dumper/Tipper within the required cycle time in all situations, to achieve production
targets
Loading and hauling the load to the designated destination
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. place the Dumper/Tipper at correct spotting location on the loading site
- PC13. operate Dumper/Tipper while ensuring stipulated load and operating within speed limits, as
specified by the manufacturer
Discharging the ore/waste correctly by tipping operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. follow the shovel operator's signal/instruction regarding unloading location as per the quality
of loaded material
- PC15. ensure that the Dumper/Tipper is on the level ground before discharging the load
- PC16. execute unloading/discharging operation accurately and safely at the site

#### MIN/N1407: Perform  basic maintenance and troubleshooting of the
Dumper/Tipper
Description
This unit is about performing basic maintenance and troubleshooting of the Dumper/Tipper.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Performing preventive maintenance
Performing basic diagnosis and troubleshooting
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Performing preventive maintenance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. track machine operating hours to assess the required service
- PC2. park the Dumper/Tipper on a firm and level ground before attempting to carry out any
maintenance activity
- PC3. check and maintain the tyre rims, air pressure, wheel nuts and treads
- PC4. tighten the loose battery terminals with the help of an auto-electrician or HEMM Electrician
- PC5. ensure that the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) recommended procedure and
checklist is followed for routine servicing
- PC6. replenish coolants, lubricants, and fluids
- PC7. carry out the maintenance with Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM) mechanic or superior
in case of non-routine service or repair
- PC8. assist HEMM Mechanic and auto-electrician or HEMM Electrician as and when required
- PC9. fill the daily, weekly and monthly maintenance/defect sheets as per the format lay down by
the organization
Performing basic diagnosis and troubleshooting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. ensure that no diagnosis on the machine is performed when it is running or is still hot
- PC11. identify fault in the Dumper/Tipper and trace the causes of this fault
- PC12. check the instrument panel and control gauge to identify out of range readings to find the
defect/cause of failure
- PC13. conduct scheduled, routine examination and assessments against equipment specifications
to identify wear, damage, corrosion, inadequate fluid levels, leaks and general condition and serviceability
- PC14. check complaint sheet, logbook and history sheet of the equipment and identify repair
requirements
- PC15. carry out diagnostic procedures as per the troubleshooting checklist prepared by the Original
Equipment Manufacturer (OEM)
