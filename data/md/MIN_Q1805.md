MIN/Q1805: Mining Supervisor (Mineral Sands)
Brief Job Description
A Mining Supervisor (Mineral Sands) supervises mine workers and coordinates various mine activities,
including organizing and monitoring workflow for various processes. The individual is responsible for
ensuring compliance with the applicable regulatory requirements and safety procedures at mining sites.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the task at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should also be physically agile, strong, have good eye sight and not suffer from
colour-blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MIN/N1814: Supervise excavation, accumulation, loading and transportation of raw sand
- 2. MIN/N1815: Supervise the safety of dredging operations at DWUP
- 3. MIN/N1816: Supervise the conveyance of mining output through slurry pipelines
- 4. MIN/N1810: Follow the applicable health, safety and environmental guidelines (Placer Mining)
- 5. MIN/N1811: Work collaboratively and effectively in a team
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

MIN/N1814: Supervise excavation, accumulation, loading and
transportation of raw sand
Description
This occupational standards unit is about supervising/ coordinating different activities involving mineral
production at the point of extraction, in accordance with the applicable requirements. It also includes
supervising the use of Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM)
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Protect personnel working in vicinity of site excavation
Oversee the HEMM operations
Oversee inspection and maintenance of branch road & main haul road to excavation site
Elements and Performance Criteria
Protect personnel working in vicinity of site excavation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify and prevent unauthorized entry at the site of excavation, and report it to the
relevant official
PC2. check for the deployment of inexperienced and unsupervised personnel at the excavation
site and report it to the relevant official
PC3. identify deficiencies in safety equipment and conditions in the mine or section and report to
the relevant official
PC4. supervise hazardous operations to ensure they are carried out safely with the protection of
personnel involved
PC5. ensure effective monitoring of the area under charge until the end of the shift or until
relieved by a duly appointed substitute and acquainting with the matters requiring attention
PC6. supervise the personnel within the area of responsibility
PC7. ensure the personnel are able to identify hazardous occurrences situation and are aware of
the appropriate action to be taken while taking ensuring own and other’s safety
PC8. conduct appropriate checks on the competency of persons employed in mineral extraction
PC9. supervise the workers during work to ensure they do not come in contact with moving plant/
equipment/ machine parts
PC10. ensure the personnel use the appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) while
conducting hazardous activities
PC11. check and ensure the personnel are not exposed to excessive work hazards (e.g. vibration,
hazardous substances)
PC12. instruct the workers to use mechanical lifting devices to minimize manual handling tasks
PC13. inspect and ensure appropriate checks on safety devices are carried out
PC14. identify different problems, such as obstructions, geological movements, mechanical or
service failure, and report to appropriate person for appropriate remedial action

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the duties and responsibilities of a mining mate/supervisor as mentioned in Metalliferous
Mines Regulations, 1961 and updated Directorate General Of Mines Safety (DGMS) circulars
KU2. the safety policy of IREL(India) Limited
KU3. Mining Rules and Regulations
KU4. the role of workmen under the Mines Act
KU5. the basic technology used in and the functioning of various components and equipment in
dredge mining, and supplementary mining plant
KU6. the use of appropriate tools to assess and confirm technical faults in excavation equipment
KU7. the use of pressure indicators, fuel pressure testers, manifold gauge sets, oil pressure
gauges, measuring equipment (Venire calipers, micrometer, feeler gauges, multi-meter, flow
meter, temp gauge, dial gauge etc.), condition monitoring systems for temperature,
vibration, sound etc.
KU8. the appropriate safety measures to be taken in the vicinity of different types of machinery
KU9. the importance of being aware of and following the locally prepared emergency response
plan/ disaster management plan
KU10. the importance of promptly reporting the identified problems/ accidents/ incidents

MIN/N1815: Supervise the safety of dredging operations at DWUP
Description
This unit is about supervising the safety of dredging operations at the Dredge and Wet Upgradation Plant
(DWUP).
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Ensure safety in the dredging operations
Implement the equipment safety measures
Elements and Performance Criteria
Ensure safety in the dredging operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. ensure safe access to the float line pontoon
PC2. check the approach road is fitted with suitable gradient to float line pontoon
PC3. ensure the provision of handrails, chains, flaps on the float line pontoon, and arrange for
their regular maintenance and painting
PC4. ensure appropriate illumination standards on the float line pontoon
PC5. direct the workers to shift material from the shore to the plant deck by using the
maintenance barge
PC6. ensure no material is shifted using a single pontoon
PC7. direct the relevant personnel to conduct regular cleaning of the concentrator deck, head feed
bin deck, and dredge deck
PC8. check for the regular cleaning of the manhole compartment, including its dewatering as per
standard procedure
PC9. ensure regular cleaning of the spirals
PC10. monitor the free board draft of the concentrate deck, head feed bin deck, and dredge deck
PC11. carry out appropriate anchoring of the floating units, i.e. concentrator deck, head feed bin
deck and dredge deck
PC12. check and ensure safe operating parameters of the dredge plant and concentrator plant are
maintained
PC13. dispose of tailing with proper density with timely replacement of tails nozzle
PC14. conduct surface preparation of dredge face appropriately with the help of HEMM
PC15. carry out timely shifting of all the field setup
PC16. ensure the provision of safe and wholesome drinking water to the dredge plant
Implement the equipment safety measures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC17. ensure the use of appropriate safety gear for anchor block shifting, winch wire rope handling,
locking of winches, sandblasting, etc.
