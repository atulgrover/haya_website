#### SGJ/Q4105: Water Hyacinth (Value added Products) Entrepreneur
Brief Job Description
The purpose of this qualification is to increase livelihood opportunities for the unskilled population and
promote entrepreneurial skills through systematic training on the collection, processing, value-addition
and marketing of products made from water hyacinth. By turning a challenging weed into a valuable
resource, not only can environmental harm be mitigated, but local economies can also be bolstered
through the creation of sustainable livelihoods.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be eco-conscious, creative, and self-motivated, with a strong interest in sustainability
and local entrepreneurship. They must be skilled in hands-on work, willing to learn, and adaptable to new
techniques. Good communication skills, attention to detail, problem-solving ability, and a basic
understanding of business and marketing are essential for success in this role. A sense of responsibility
towards the community and the environment is also important.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SGJ/N4118: Introduce course collection, cleaning and processing of water hyacinth.
2. SGJ/N4119: Utilise water Hyacinth for Pulp Processing and Paper making

4. SGJ/N4121: Utilise water Hyacinth for Mushroom Cultivation

6. SGJ/N4123: Utilise water Hyacinth for Biogas production.
7. SGJ/N4124: Finishing and Marketing of Products

#### SGJ/N4118: Introduce course collection, cleaning and processing of water
hyacinth.
Description
The NOS introduces learners to the concept of water hyacinth as an invasive aquatic weed and its potential
as a valuable resource. The learner gains knowledge about identifying water hyacinth in natural water
bodies, understanding its environmental implications, and learning proper techniques for collection,
cleaning, and post-harvest drying.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This scope covers the foundational understanding and basic skills required for identifying, collecting,
cleaning, and preserving water hyacinth as a raw material for various value-added applications. It
includes knowledge about the nature and impact of the water hyacinth plant, safe and sustainable
harvesting methods, and the initial steps involved in preparing the plant for further use in handicrafts, paper, biogas, briquette, or mushroom cultivation processes.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Water hyacinth, source of water hyacinth and its types etc.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Describe about the problems of water hyacinth growing in waterbodies.
- PC2. Discuss how it is a problem for fishing community
- PC3. Discuss how it is economically viable
- PC4. Describe the concept of invasive species
- PC5. Discuss the invasive weed water hyacinth
- PC6. Discuss the management of the invasive weed species
- PC7. Discuss the need for the eradication procedures of invasive weed
- PC8. Demonstrate and show water hyacinth plant in various water bodies, the stem of the plant
and various products made out of it.
- PC9. Demonstrate a range of cognitive and practical skills required to identify polluted areas.
- PC10. Demonstrate polluted water bodies, identify different weed and extraction process.
Raw Material Preparation and Preservation of Water Hyacinth
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Understand the invasive weed water hyacinth.
- PC12. Understand the procedures for collecting water hyacinth.
- PC13. Discuss the techniques that are involved to descend into water body and harvest the plant.
- PC14. Discuss how to cut leaves and root off the stems and laid out to dry in sunlight
- PC15. Discuss post-harvest processing of water hyacinth like cleaning, drying and managing raw
material.

#### SGJ/N4119: Utilise water Hyacinth for Pulp Processing and Paper making
Description
This NOS enables individuals to transform water hyacinth into usable pulp and produce handmade paper or
molded pulp items. The learners are trained in pulping techniques, preparation of moulds and deckles, and
the sheet-forming process. They also learn to carry out quality checks, smooth and trim finished paper,
and apply basic binding and decorative methods.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The Scope covers the skills and knowledge required to process water hyacinth into pulp and utilize it
for handmade paper and pulp-based product creation. It includes pulping techniques, mould and deckle-based papermaking processes, and finishing methods such as trimming, embossing, and
binding.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Pulping and making of pulp products
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate how to chop and prepare water hyacinth for the pulping process.
- PC2. Perform the chemical or mechanical pulping of water hyacinth using appropriate tools.
- PC3. Measure the consistency and quality of water hyacinth pulp through simple tests.
- PC4. Create basic pulp products, such as sheets, packaging materials, or molded items.
- PC5. Demonstrate various steps to make different products
- PC6. Demonstrate how to create Finished Products
Mould and Deckle Handmade paper making process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Demonstrate Collection & processing of water hyacinth.
- PC8. Make mould & deckle using appropriate tools.
- PC9. Demonstrate Hand-made paper making process
- PC10. Create Handmade paper products and work on their finishing
- PC11. Inspect pulp products for defects, such as uneven texture or weak spots.
Product finishing, binding
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. Explain the importance of finishing in enhancing the quality of pulp products made from
water hyacinth.
- PC13. Identify different binding techniques used in paper and pulp product manufacturing.
- PC14. Describe the steps involved in the finishing process, including smoothing, trimming, and
embossing.
- PC15. Discuss the types of adhesives and materials used for binding pulp products.
- PC16. Prepare pulp products from water hyacinth for the finishing process.

#### SGJ/N4120: Utilise water Hyacinth for Weaving and handicraft making.
Description
This NOS enables individuals to convert dried and processed water hyacinth into attractive and functional
handicrafts using traditional and modern weaving techniques. Learners gain practical skills in preparing
raw material, weaving basic and complex patterns, assembling finished products, and applying decorative
elements.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the utilization of processed water hyacinth for creating woven and handcrafted
products. It includes the identification, preparation, and treatment of water hyacinth fibers, understanding weaving techniques, and the creation of various handicraft items such as mats,
baskets, and decorative articles.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Processing of Water Hyacinth for Weaving and handicraft making.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the steps involved in processing water hyacinth for use in weaving and handicraft
making.
- PC2. Identify the different properties of water hyacinth that make it suitable for weaving.
- PC3. Describe the preparation techniques for cleaning and drying water hyacinth before weaving.
- PC4. Discuss the types of tools and equipment commonly used in weaving with water hyacinth.
- PC5. Demonstrate cleaning techniques to remove impurities from water hyacinth fibres.
- PC6. Perform drying methods to prepare water hyacinth for storage and future use.
- PC7. Cut and organize water hyacinth fibres into manageable sizes for weaving.
- PC8. Weave simple patterns using water hyacinth fibres, such as mats, baskets, or mats.
- PC9. Practice basic weaving techniques such as over-under, plain weave, or twill.
- PC10. Assemble multiple strips of water hyacinth into more complex woven products.
- PC11. Inspect finished woven products for quality, ensuring tightness, consistency, and durability.
- PC12. Decorate water hyacinth woven items using additional natural fibres, beads, or other
embellishments.
- PC13. Document the steps and outcomes of processing water hyacinth for weaving, noting areas
for improvement.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Basics of traditional and modern weaving methods.

#### SGJ/N4121: Utilise water Hyacinth for Mushroom Cultivation
Description
This unit enables individuals to utilize water hyacinth an invasive aquatic weed as a sustainable substrate
for cultivating edible mushrooms. Learners are trained in collecting, cleaning, and processing water
hyacinth for bed preparation, selecting appropriate mushroom species, and maintaining ideal growth
conditions.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The Scope covers the use of water hyacinth as a substrate for mushroom cultivation. It includes
knowledge and skills related to the preparation of mushroom beds using processed water hyacinth,
identification of suitable mushroom species, cultivation techniques, and post-harvest practices such
as packaging and value addition.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Processing of Water Hyacinth for bed preparation/substrates for mushroom cultivation. Identify different
species of Mushroom. Packaging of Mushrooms and Value addition
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the importance of using water hyacinth as a substrate for mushroom
cultivation.
- PC2. Describe the processing steps required to prepare water hyacinth for mushroom
bed creation.
- PC3. Discuss the nutritional benefits of water hyacinth as a substrate for mushroom
growth.
- PC4. Identify different types of mushrooms that can be cultivated on water hyacinth
substrates.
- PC5. Explain the factors that influence the success of mushroom cultivation using water
hyacinth.
- PC6. Discuss the packaging techniques that help maintain the freshness and quality of
harvested mushrooms.
- PC7. Collect water hyacinth suitable for use as a substrate for mushroom cultivation.
- PC8. Prepare water hyacinth by cleaning, drying, and shredding it into smaller pieces.
- PC9. Inoculate the substrate with mushroom spawn, ensuring proper spacing and
coverage.
- PC10. Monitor the moisture levels in the water hyacinth substrate to support healthy
mushroom growth.
- PC11. Harvest mushrooms at the appropriate stage of development based on visual and
growth indicators.
- PC12. Package harvested mushrooms using breathable containers to maintain freshness
and prevent spoilage.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |

| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
