SGJ/Q4007: Sewer Entry Professional
Brief Job Description
The Sewer Entry Professional, is an individual, who is responsible for emptying, Cleaning and transport and
disposal of sludge from the sewer line to specified site/Co-treatment plant. He /She has to do manual
sewage cleaning in the areas having space limitations He/She can use pumps, blowers, scrapper, Manila
Rope and Cloth Ball, Sectional Sewer Rods and other tools/equipment to empty the sewage manholes.
Personal Attributes
The Individual is expected to be physically fit and should be able to work across various location/site
conditions. He/ She should be self-motivated and able to produce good quality work. He/ She must have
good communication skill, team spirit, be courteous and polite. He should be efficient in working with
proper planning skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. SGJ/N4045: Introduce Sewer Cleaning management and resources
- 2. SGJ/N4046: Maintain health and safety at Sewer cleaning site
- 3. SGJ/N4047: Describe process to perform Manual sewer cleaning operations
- 4. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

SGJ/N4045: Introduce Sewer Cleaning management and resources
Description
This unit is about introduce Sewer Cleaning management and resources
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Manual Sewerage Cleaning practices and associated schemes
Provision and Entitlements for SSW- Role of ULB
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Manual Sewerage Cleaning practices and associated schemes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. discuss the importance of sewer cleaning management
PC2. explain how to perform sewer cleaning manually while following safety guidelines
PC3. explain the key challenges with manual sewage cleaning and the Government initiatives to
address the issues
PC4. explain the modalities of Government schemes for faecal sludge management like Swachh
Bharat Mission, Swachhta Udyami Yojana (SUY) etc.
PC5. explain benefits of govt. scheme for Sewage and Septic Tank Sanitation Workers(SSW)
PC6. describe Legal provision for punitive actions against hazardous cleaning as per PEMSR Act,
2013
PC7. describe Roles and responsibilities of the Employer, Local Authority in preventing Hazardous
manual cleaning
PC8. explain roles of Emergency Response Sanitation Unit (ERSU) including Responsible Sanitation
Authority (RSA) and Duty Supervisor
PC9. explain roles of Sewer Entry Professionals (SEP) and Private Sanitation Service Organisation
(PSSO)
PC10. identify role of ERSU Authority, ULB in case of manual entry of SSW required
PC11. discuss the powers of the Sanitary Inspector as part of PEMSR Act, 2020
PC12. discuss Roles and responsibilities of Employer, Local Authority in implementing Manual
Sewer Cleaning
PC13. illustrate the importance of sewer cleaning and outline key challenges in manual sewer
cleaning
PC14. outline key aspects of performing sewage cleaning operations manually while ensuring all
safety requirements
PC15. illustrate Roles and responsibilities of all key stakeholders including Employer, Local
Authority in implementing Manual Sewer Cleaning

SGJ/N4046: Maintain health and safety at Sewer cleaning site
Description
This unit is about to explain how to maintain health and safety at sewer cleaning site
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Health & Safety in manual sewage cleaning operations
Emergency Preparedness during manual cleaning of sewers
Elements and Performance Criteria
Health & Safety in manual sewage cleaning operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. explain the importance of safety and discuss how to incorporate safety measures in manual
sewer cleaning
PC2. discuss how to fully adhere to safety requirements and norms for manual sewer cleaning
PC3. explain how to ensure personal cleanliness and hygiene
PC4. discuss the potential hazards and preventive actions to be taken during sewer cleaning
PC5. discuss mitigating occupational hazards like biological hazards, gas hazards, electrical
hazards, fire hazards, chemical drain openers, heavy load lifting, high-pressure water,
sinking and animal/rodent/snake bites etc.
PC6. discuss the common myths about PPE such as comfort and productivity
PC7. discuss the possible injuries from sharp objects, animal and/ or insect bites, management of
bleeding, bruises, and sprain
PC8. discuss how to respond to emergencies and identify the essential items in first aid box with
their uses
PC9. explain first aid intervention during suffocation, asphyxiation, and nausea
PC10. demonstrate how to keep sewer sites clean, ensure personal cleanliness and hygiene and
safety during work
PC11. show how to fully adhere to safety requirements and norms for manual sewer cleaning
PC12. demonstrate how to use appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) while performing
work
PC13. demonstrate correct techniques to move an injured person during an emergency
PC14. show how to provide first aid to a victim in case of injuries form sharp objects, animal and/ or
insect bites, management of bleeding, bruises, and sprain
PC15. demonstrate how to measure temperature of a person
PC16. demonstrate how to dispose hazardous waste as per State/ Local Authority norms
PC17. perform the steps to clean and disinfect material, tools, equipment, and other supplies
before starting work and after completing the job
Emergency Preparedness during manual cleaning of sewers
