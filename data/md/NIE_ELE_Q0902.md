NIE/ELE/Q0902: Solar Panel Installation and Maintenance Technician (O-
Level 'Solar Power Electronics')
Brief Job Description
Nature: The Certificate course is targeted for creating qualified professionals in the field of Solar Panel
Installation. Qualification has been developed in consultation with industry experts in the domain, aiming
at Empowering the future workforce with necessary skills for employment and entrepreneur development
of the qualifier. Purpose: The objective of this O Level (Solar Panel Installation and Maintenance
Technician) course is to equip candidates with the knowledge and practical skills needed to safely and
effectively install and maintain photovoltaic systems for renewable energy generation.
Personal Attributes
This course demands technical expertise in solar technology, hands-on installation skills, and meticulous
attention to detail. Learners must be safety-conscious, physically fit, and adept at troubleshooting and
innovating for efficiency. Adaptability, continuous learning, and effective communication are key for
success.

NIE/ELE/N0910: Solar Technology Fundamentals
Description
Solar Technology Fundamentals equips students with a thorough understanding of solar energy, covering
its historical development, environmental benefits, and its critical role in both global and Indian energy
landscapes. Students will learn the fundamental principles of solar radiation, including measurement
techniques and site assessment methods, to ensure optimal solar panel placement. The course will also
provide expertise in various solar photovoltaic (PV) systems, including different types of panels, cell
technologies, and system configurations such as rooftop, ground mount, and floating panels. Through a
combination of theoretical knowledge and practical skills, students will be prepared to design and
implement effective solar PV systems, select the right panels for different applications, and address factors
that impact efficiency and longevity.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Solar Technology Fundamentals module provides a comprehensive understanding of renewable
energy sources, the historical development of solar technology, and its environmental significance.
It encompasses global and Indian energy scenarios and policies. The module covers the physics of
solar radiation, measurement techniques, site assessment tools, and shade analysis for optimal
panel placement. It also includes various solar PV technologies, such as different module types, cell
technologies, and system configurations like rooftop, ground mount, and floating panels. Practical
skills in designing efficient solar PV systems and selecting appropriate panels for specific
applications are emphasized.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Solar Energy
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify various renewable energy sources, outline the historical development of solar
technology, and explain the role of solar energy in environmental sustainability.
PC2. Describe the global and Indian energy scenarios, and understand relevant energy policies that
promote solar energy usage.
Solar Radiation and Site Assessment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Explain the fundamental physics of solar radiation, differentiate between global beam and
diffuse radiation, and use appropriate tools and methods to measure solar radiation
accurately.
PC4. Apply site assessment techniques and tools to evaluate solar energy potential and perform
shade analysis to determine the optimal placement of solar panels for maximum efficiency.
Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Systems Overview
