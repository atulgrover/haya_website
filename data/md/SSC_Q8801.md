#### SSC/Q8801: AR/VR  Consultant
Brief Job Description
An AR/VR Consultant performs a comprehensive analysis of customer requirements, opportunities, costs,
and impacts while ensuring data privacy is upheld. It requires creating a functional design for the AR/VR
solution that aligns with specified business requirements. Additionally, the consultant must develop project
plans and procedures to create solutions and enhance product effectiveness, ensuring that key performance indicators (KPIs) and service goals are met. Setting technical parameters to monitor and
manage the performance of the AR/VR architecture and preparing statistical reports on product performance is also essential. Lastly, engaging in innovative research and development within the AR/VR
field while staying current with the latest technological trends is crucial to the role. The AR/VR consultant
also acts as a strategic advisor and technical expert, guiding businesses through successfully integrating
AR/VR technology.
Personal Attributes
AR/VR Consultants must be able to communicate effectively. They must have strong interpersonal skills,
analytical mindset and a zeal to learn new things.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SSC/N8801: Conduct a thorough customer requirement analysis, opportunity analysis, cost analysis, and impact analysis and ensure data privacy
2. SSC/N8803: Establish a functional design of the AR/VR solution based on the defined business
requirements
3. SSC/N8804: Define project plans and processes to develop solutions and drive product effectiveness, ensuring essential KPIs/service goals are met
4. SSC/N8809: Define technical parameters to monitor and manage the performance of the AR/VR architecture and develop statistical reports on product performance
5. SSC/N8818: Conduct cutting-edge research and development in AR/VR and other related fields and
be updated with the latest trends in technology
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### SSC/N8801: Conduct  a thorough customer requirement  analysis,
opportunity analysis, cost analysis, and impact analysis and ensure data privacy
Description
This NOS is about understanding the customer requirement and feasibility of the ARVR Solution
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Customer Requirement Analysis,Opportunity Analysis, Product Offering,Cost Analysis,Impact
Analysis
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Evaluate and define the business requirements of the client
- PC2. Utilize available studies (market research, internal) and competitive analysis to refine
customer needs
- PC3. Define a product/solution differentiation strategy that is aligned with customer requirements
- PC4. Coordinate with internal teams (R&D, Design, etc.) to understand user requirements and
define user engagement goals
- PC5. Identify and assess other competitors and providers of similar products/ solutions in the
market
- PC6. Determine new opportunities by highlighting specific areas in the market that are upcoming
or underserved
- PC7. Identify a suitable type of product/solution offering for the customer (or new market
opportunity)
- PC8. Determine if the solution requires the addition of AR/VR features to existing products in the
organization
- PC9. Assess the initialization cost for the AR/VR solution
- PC10. Evaluate execution cost for the AR/VR solution covering various aspects such as licensing
cost, cost of devices/ hardware, content creation cost (including costs for model creation, customization, and outsourcing), resources cost, etc.
- PC11. Calculate Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) including implementation cost, transition cost,
operational cost, and retirement cost
- PC12. Evaluate various business models (subscription, bundling, etc.) to select the best revenue-
generating model
- PC13. Define the margins for profit
- PC14. Outline the impact that can be achieved in terms of quality, scalability, and resources by
AR/VR implementation
- PC15. Evaluate the risks associated with AR/VR implementation

#### SSC/N8809: Define technical parameters to monitor and manage  the
performance  of the AR/VR architecture and develop statistical reports on product performance
Description
This unit is about monitoring the performance of implemented AR/VR architecture using technical
parameters and boundary conditions
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Tools and KPIs,,Analytics,User Feedback,Continuous Improvement,Performance monitoring and evaluationReporting
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Define the possible methods and tools to analyze the technical and operational performance
of the AR/VR solution
- PC2. Define the KPIs for monitoring the performance of the AR/VR solution, and integrate with the
KPIs of existing infrastructure, whenever applicable
- PC3. Define the frequency of measuring KPIs
- PC4. Define methods for monitoring application and hardware resource usage
- PC5. Draw map of possible bottlenecks (related to memory, I/O, etc.) in the AR/VR solution to
evaluate if there is a need for deeper code and design changes
- PC6. Monitor analytics using appropriate tools to obtain technical, operational, and business
insights such as accuracy, success rate, duration, outcomes, etc
- PC7. Define methods for monitoring feedback from the user
- PC8. Define the plan to review technical performance and improve the process efficiency
- PC9. Collaborate with appropriate stakeholders to identify the methods and tools for monitoring
product performance
- PC10. Monitor overall utilization pattern of the product and calculate estimated vs. actual effort
utilized
- PC11. Evaluate KPIs and analyze the product's performance using suitable analytics tools
- PC12. Report the metrics and evaluation results to appropriate stakeholders
- PC13. Perform continuous reviews and obtain feedback on product improvement from appropriate
stakeholders
