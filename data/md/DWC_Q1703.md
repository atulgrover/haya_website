DWC/Q1703: Personal Butler
Brief Job Description
A Personal Butler is responsible for delivering highly personalized and discreet services to high-net-worth
individuals (HNWIs), celebrities, business executives, or royal households. Their role demands exceptional
hospitality, household management, and attention to detail to ensure a seamless and luxurious living
experience. It is a short-term course.
Personal Attributes
The role demands exceptional hospitality, household management, and attention to detail to ensure a
seamless and luxurious living experience.

DWC/N1623: Manage Personal and Household Services
Description
This NOS defines the core responsibilities of a Personal Butler or Household Manager in overseeing and
managing personal and household services to ensure seamless luxury living.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Household Operations and Personalized Lifestyle Management
Elements and Performance Criteria
Household Operations and Personalized Lifestyle Management
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand and record the employer’s preferences, routines, and lifestyle requirements.
PC2. Oversee and coordinate daily household operations efficiently.
PC3. Maintain and manage employer’s schedule, including appointments, social events, and travel
plans.
PC4. Ensure high standards of cleanliness, organization, and hygiene within the residence.
PC5. Supervise and manage domestic staff duties, ensuring smooth workflow.
PC6. Procure and restock household essentials and luxury items.
PC7. Maintain an inventory of high-value household assets, including artwork and antiques.
PC8. Handle emergency situations such as medical incidents, security threats, or domestic crises.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Best practices in luxury household management and hospitality.
KU2. High-level etiquette, cultural sensitivities, and protocol.
KU3. Security measures and risk management for private residences.
KU4. Smart home technologies and automation systems.
KU5. Personal styling, wardrobe care, and luxury goods management.
KU6. Event planning, catering coordination, and guest hospitality.
KU7. Financial management for household budgets and expenditures.
KU8. Ethical and legal considerations in private service.

User/individual on the job needs to know how to:

DWC/N1621: Provide Personalized Hospitality Services
Description
This NOS defines the core responsibilities of a Personal Butler or Household Manager in providing
personalized Hospitality Services
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Provide Personalized Hospitality Services
Elements and Performance Criteria
Provide Personalized Hospitality Services
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. welcome and attend to guests professionally and warmly.
PC2. serve meals, beverages, and refreshments following fine dining protocols.
PC3. cater to dietary restrictions, allergies, and personal food preferences.
PC4. adhere to etiquette and protocols in formal and informal gatherings.
PC5. set up, organize, and decorate tables according to the employer’s preferences.
PC6. supervise kitchen and pantry management for high-standard meal preparation.
PC7. manage entertainment arrangements, including music, lighting, and ambiance.
PC8. handle concierge services, such as arranging theater bookings, restaurant reservations, and
luxury experiences.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. High-end hospitality service standards and luxury lifestyle management.
KU2. International etiquette, protocol, and cultural sensitivities.
KU3. Fine dining, gourmet cuisine, and wine pairing principles.
KU4. Best practices in luxury household management and hospitality.
KU5. High-level etiquette, cultural sensitivities, and protocol.
KU6. Security measures and risk management for private residences.
KU7. Smart home technologies and automation systems.
KU8. Personal styling, wardrobe care, and luxury goods management.
KU9. Event planning, catering coordination, and guest hospitality.
KU10. Financial management for household budgets and expenditures.
KU11. Ethical and legal considerations in private service.
KU12. VIP guest handling and concierge service best practices.

DWC/N1622: Provide wardrobe management and personal styling
Description
This NOS defines the core responsibilities of a Personal Butler or Household Manager in providing wardrobe
management and personal styling services
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Handle Wardrobe Management and Personal Styling
Elements and Performance Criteria
Handle Wardrobe Management and Personal Styling
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. organize and maintain the employer’s wardrobe based on seasons and occasions.
PC2. assist with personal styling, outfit selection, and dressing coordination.
PC3. manage laundry, dry cleaning, and wardrobe maintenance.
PC4. pack and unpack luggage systematically, ensuring wrinkle-free garments.
PC5. recommend and purchase high-end clothing, accessories, and grooming products.
PC6. track fashion trends to provide styling advice based on employer preferences.
PC7. maintain a digital catalog of wardrobe inventory for easy outfit selection.
PC8. ensure proper storage of jewelry, watches, and valuable fashion accessories.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. High-end hospitality service standards and luxury lifestyle management.
KU2. International etiquette, protocol, and cultural sensitivities.
KU3. Fine dining, gourmet cuisine, and wine pairing principles.
KU4. Best practices in luxury household management and hospitality.
KU5. High-level etiquette, cultural sensitivities, and protocol.
KU6. Security measures and risk management for private residences.
KU7. Smart home technologies and automation systems.
KU8. Personal styling, wardrobe care, and luxury goods management.
KU9. Event planning, catering coordination, and guest hospitality.
KU10. Financial management for household budgets and expenditures.
KU11. Ethical and legal considerations in private service.
KU12. VIP guest handling and concierge service best practices.
KU13. Smart home automation and security management.

KU2. International etiquette, protocol, and cultural sensitivities.

KU4. Best practices in luxury household management and hospitality.
