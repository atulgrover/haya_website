#### CUTM/CSC/Q0201: CNC  Turning Programmer
Brief Job Description
CNC Turning Programmer is responsible for understanding the design requirements, material, generate
CAM design for manufacturing of various parts using CNC lathe, inspect the parts to finalize and deliver the
finished product. Also maintain good condition of the machine with regular maintenance.
Personal Attributes
A CNC Turning Programmer should be precise, analytical, and technically skilled. They must possess strong
interpretation abilities for technical drawings and CAM programming. Attention to detail, problem-solving,
and machine-handling expertise are essential. Good time management, commitment to quality, adaptability to new technologies, and the ability to work independently or in teams are crucial for this role.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CUTM/CSC/N0201: Interpretation of designs, drawings and process planning.
2. CUTM/CSC/N0202: Design CAM Programs, metrology, maintenance of machineries
3. CUTM/CSC/N0203: Operate CNC Turning machine
4. CUTM/CSC/N0204: Inspect, Finalize and deliver the parts
5. CUTM/VSQ/N7805: Ensure and Implement Safety standards at Work Place
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### CUTM/CSC/N0201: Interpretation of designs, drawings and process
planning.
Description
This NOS focuses on understanding engineering drawings and technical specifications. It involves
analyzing part requirements, selecting appropriate machining processes, determining tool paths, and
planning operations to ensure precision, efficiency, and compliance with design standards in CNC turning.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS includes analyzing engineering drawings, selecting machining processes, determining tool
paths, planning operations, optimizing cutting parameters, and ensuring precision manufacturing
while adhering to quality standards and production
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Interpret technical drawings and specifications of the product.
- PC2. Identify critical features of the product, surface finish and geometric requirements.
- PC3. Analyze manufacturability with the available resources.
- PC4. Identify and prepare for 3D mental representation of parts.
- PC5. Identify the materials for turning and their availability.
- PC6. Identify critical sequences (with a high possibility of damage or unsafe practice)
- PC7. Prepare for production of parts according to specifications.
- PC8. Imagine solutions with reference to the capacities of the workshop environment and the
required work (size of batch, complexity)
- PC9. Identify the features of work pieces and the appropriate machining and measuring
processes.
- PC10. Identify and prepare the best work holding methods.
- PC11. Identify, prepare, and calibrate the correct measuring tools.
- PC12. Identify and prepare the correct cutting tools.
- PC13. Identify critical sections (with a high possibility of damage or unsafe practice) and consider
alternatives.
- PC14. choose innovative ways of using the environment to solve technical issues.
- PC15. Check if the potential solutions will be reliable until the end of the process.
- PC16. Weigh each solution and choose the best based on the various parameters: speed, safety,
price
- PC17. Plan the sequence of operations and machining strategy.
- PC18. Create awareness actions for critical operations where no alternative is available.

#### CUTM/CSC/N0202: Design  CAM Programs,  metrology, maintenance of
machineries
Description
This NOS focuses on creating efficient CAM programs, applying metrology techniques for precision
measurement, and performing routine maintenance of CNC machines. It ensures accurate machining,
minimizes errors, and enhances machine performance for optimal production efficiency.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS includes developing CAM programs for CNC turning, applying metrology techniques for precision measurement, performing machine maintenance, troubleshooting issues, optimizing
machining processes, and ensuring compliance with quality and safety standards for efficient manufacturing operations.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select the best sequence for machining according to the production type and part
specification.
- PC2. use skill specific software and related hardware.
- PC3. generate programs using CAD/CAM systems.
- PC4. create CNC programs using the provided drawings and software.
- PC5. transfer programs to machines.
- PC6. create or edit programs directly on the control (without CAD/CAM)
- PC7. set work pieces and tools.
- PC8. select appropriate measuring- or gauging instruments.
- PC9. calibrate measuring tools.
- PC10. use selected tools to make measurements on all components on drawings.
- PC11. understand properties, uses, and handling of ferrous and non-ferrous materials.
- PC12. do preventative maintenance to avoid issues arising.
- PC13. troubleshoot minor repairs in the machinery.
- PC14. escalate to the appropriate team if there is a major problem.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Common principles of Computer-Aided Manufacturing (CAM) and its application in CNC turning.
- KU2. Selection and configuration of CAM software for generating CNC programs.

CMM (Coordinate Measuring Machine).

#### CUTM/CSC/N0203: Operate  CNC Turning machine
Description
This NOS focuses on setting up, programming, and operating CNC turning machines. It includes selecting
tools, loading workpieces, executing machining processes, monitoring operations, ensuring dimensional
accuracy, troubleshooting issues, and following safety and quality standards for efficient production.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS includes setting up CNC machines, selecting tools, loading workpieces, executing machining programs, monitoring operations, ensuring dimensional accuracy, troubleshooting issues,
maintaining safety protocols, and optimizing processes to achieve high-quality production in compliance with industry standards.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Follow their selected process strategy.
- PC2. Follow a given process- strategy (external CNC program)
- PC3. Upload generated CNC programs to CNC lathes and perform test runs.
- PC4. Mount and align selected tools.
- PC5. Mount and align selected work holding devices.
- PC6. Mount and align selected accessories (Tailstock, Parts catcher, etc.)
- PC7. Avoid vibration in the machining sequences.
- PC8. Apply burr-removal techniques on work pieces.
- PC9. Optimize machining strategies.
- PC10. Quickly react if anything goes wrong (making emergency stops)
- PC11. obtain dimensions, geometries, surface roughness by interacting with CNC lathes.
- PC12. Make the final parts conform to blueprints.
- PC13. Report health, safety, and environmental issues to the appropriate personnel.
- PC14. Report equipment failure to the appropriate personnel.
- PC15. Note all the production related information on the respective register, log, work sheet.
- PC16. Update all production information on the MIS system.
- PC17. Store the CNC program against the batch number for future reference.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Fundamentals of CNC turning operations, including coordinate systems, tool paths, and machining principles.

#### CUTM/CSC/N0204: Inspect, Finalize and deliver the parts
Description
This NOS focuses on conducting precision inspections using metrology tools, verifying dimensions against
design specifications, identifying defects, performing necessary finishing operations, documenting quality
checks, and ensuring proper packaging and labeling for final delivery while meeting industry standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS includes conducting precision inspections using metrology tools, verifying dimensions against design specifications, identifying defects, performing finishing operations, documenting
quality checks, ensuring proper packaging, and delivering defect-free parts while adhering to industry quality and safety standards..
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. check visual inspection on completion of all the process as per drawing.
- PC2. check the calibration status of various measuring instrument.
- PC3. identify parameter wise measuring instruments for measurement.
- PC4. inspect the machined product with various measurement tools like Vernier caliper and
micrometer.
- PC5. use coordinate Measuring Machine to inspect the profile measurements.
- PC6. use height gauge for appropriate measurements.
- PC7. check the surface finish as per requirements.
- PC8. check the fitness of the product using go or no-go devices.
- PC9. check visual inspection on the damage, burr, excess materials.
- PC10. document all the measured data for various parameters.
- PC11. identify the out of dimension parts.
- PC12. check for rework possibilities and complete the rework.
- PC13. reject the parts not able to rework.
- PC14. Complete the documentation as per ISO Standards.
- PC15. pack the parts in proper way to avoid any damages during transport.
- PC16. complete the delivery documents.
- PC17. deliver the parts, drawings and digital memory devices to the appropriate location and/or
personnel as required by the organization
- PC18. dismount tools, clamping, accessories.
- PC19. Use Statistical Process Control tools to monitor the process.
- PC20. set the environment to its initial state, ready for the next job.
