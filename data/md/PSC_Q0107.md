PSC/Q0107: Pipeline Fitter
Brief Job Description
A Pipeline Fitter specializes in the installation, repair, and maintenance of pipelines used for various
purposes, such as transporting liquids and gases. The individual carries out pipe fitting using the
appropriate tools and equipment as per the applicable blueprints, drawings and specifications.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be able to work independently and be comfortable in performing laborious work. The
person should be able to work as per the applicable requirements and in coordination with others while
maintaining a positive attitude. The individual should have appropriate verbal and written communication
skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. PSC/N0161: Install pipelines for internal and external distribution systems
- 2. PSC/N0162: Carry out Repair and Maintenance of different types of Pipelines
- 3. PSC/N0136: Apply Health and Safety Practices at the Workplace
- 4. PSC/N0137: Work Effectively with Others
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Carry out Industrial Pipe Fitting
This unit deals in detail with laying and installing internal and basic external pipes for both, internal and
external distribution systems and their accessories.
- 1. PSC/N0185: Carry out Industrial Pipe Fitting
Elective 2: Carry out Municipal Pipe Fitting
This unit deals in detail with laying and installing internal and basic external pipes for both, internal and
external distribution systems and their accessories.
- 1. PSC/N0186: Carry out Municipal Pipe Fitting

PSC/N0161: Install pipelines for internal and external distribution
systems
Description
This unit deals in detail with laying and installing internal and basic external pipes for both, internal and
external distribution systems and their accessories.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for installation
Install the pipes
Carry out post-installation activities
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for pipeline installation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. study blueprints, drawings and specifications to determine the type of pipes and tools to be
used
PC2. conduct a route survey
PC3. determine the fitting requirements to install the pipes
PC4. create detailed sketches for pipe and equipment fabrication and installation, as required
PC5. . plan the sequence of tasks
PC6. prepare the area for installation
PC7. transport and stack the pipes, fittings, and other materials at the site
PC8. measure, cut, thread, groove, bend, and assemble metal, plastic and fibreglass pipes, valves
and fittings using couplings, clamps, screws, bolts, etc.
PC9. carry out cutting, caulking and threading appropriately for effective joining of pipes
PC10. establish the sequence of internal and external pipe installations
PC11. plan installation in or around obstructions such as electrical wiring, pavements, etc.
PC12. locate and mark the position of pipe installations, connections, passage holes, and fixtures in
structures, using measuring instruments such as rulers and levels
PC13. . prepare trenches and ditches for housing the pipelines
PC14. cut openings in structures to accommodate pipes and pipe fittings, using hand and power
tools (if required)
PC15. measure, cut, thread, and bend pipe to the required angle, using hand and power tools or
equipment such as cutting, threading and bending equipment
Install the pipeline
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC16. install horizontal and vertical supports to hold pipes in place
PC17. string pipes along the trench

PSC/N0162: Carry out Repair and Maintenance of different types of
Pipelines
Description
This unit is about the activities that involve the repair and maintenance of various types of pipelines.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for pipeline repair work
Repair the pipeline
Carry out post-repair work
Carry out maintenance of pipeline
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for pipeline repair work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Interpret the pipeline layout drawing and identify the pipeline job requirements
PC2. identify the specifications of the pipeline from the layout drawings
PC3. identify the problem/fault in the system and its cause
PC4. diagnose the reason for failure
PC5. establish the sequence of repair/maintenance
PC6. assemble fittings, fixtures and tools required for the task
Repair the pipeline
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. isolate the repair area
PC8. locate and mark the position of the component to be replaced/repaired, using measuring
instruments such as rulers and levels
PC9. cut openings in structures/trenches to remove defected pipes and pipe fittings, using hand
and power tools
PC10. measure, cut, thread, and bend pipes to required angle, using hand and power tools or
equipment such as cutting, threading and bending equipment
PC11. remove and replace worn pipe assemblies, components and fittings, and carry out general
maintenance work
PC12. re-assemble the pipeline with fittings post correcting the defect
PC13. repair internal piping network connections with external service mains such as water and
sewerage lines
Carry out post-repair work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC14. test the pipe joints and fixtures for proper functioning
PC15. use the appropriate testing equipment to check systems for leaks

PSC/N0185: Carry out Industrial Pipe Fitting
Description
This unit deals in detail with laying and installing internal and basic external pipes for both, internal and
external distribution systems and their accessories.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for industrial pipe fitting activities
Install the industrial pipe fittings
Inspect, test, and commission the piping system
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for industrial pipe fitting activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. evaluate the project requirements, including the type of fluid, pressure, temperature, and
environmental conditions
PC2. study the basic drawings and schematics of the piping system, to determine the pipe routes,
materials, and dimensions
PC3. conduct a thorough inspection of the site to identify potential obstacles and ensure the
environment is suitable for installation
PC4. use appropriate materials based on the project requirements, such as steel, copper, PVC, etc.
PC5. measure and cut pipes to the required lengths using tools like pipe cutters, saws, or torches.
PC6. assemble sections of the piping system in a controlled environment before transporting them
to the installation site.
Install the industrial pipe fittings
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. position the pipes correctly according to the schematics, ensuring proper alignment to
prevent leaks and ensure smooth fluid flow
PC8. join pipes using appropriate joining methods, such as welding, threading, and flanging,
depending on the type of pipe and the specific application.
PC9. install supports and anchors to secure the pipes and prevent movement due to pressure or
thermal expansion
Inspect, test and commission the piping system
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. check the piping system visually for any obvious defects, misalignments, or improper joints.
PC11. perform pressure tests to ensure the system can handle the designated pressure without
leaking.
PC12. carry out Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) following appropriate methods, such as Xrays and
ultrasonic testing to inspect welds and joints for internal defects
PC13. carry out appropriate repair and maintenance of the piping system based on test results.

- 1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
- 2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
- 3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected
elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- 4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
- 5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
- 6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
- 7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
