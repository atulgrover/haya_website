MSU/ITS/Q2201: Digital Communication and Collaboration Associate
Brief Job Description
This is a short-term qualification for upskilling of all existing and aspiring managers and team leaders,
communication staff and members of teams involved in collaborative work from different locations, in
digital communication and collaboration tools.
Personal Attributes
A Digital Communication and Collaboration Associate must have a strong proficiency with digital tools and
technology, combined with a creative mindset to develop effective communication strategies. They should
possess excellent teamwork and language skills, ensuring clear, precise messaging that aligns with the
company's brand values. Additionally, they must be adept at managing multiple tasks while maintaining
attention to detail and adapting to evolving communication trends.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MSU/ITS/N2201: Prepare for Digital Communication and Collaboration
- 2. MSU/ITS/N2202: Undertake Corporate Communications using Digital Communication Tools
- 3. MSU/ITS/N2203: Use Digital Collaboration Tools to Work with Teams
- 4. MSU/ITS/N2204: Carry out Remote Online Work and Communication

- 6. MSU/ITS/N2302: Enhance Productivity and Team Collaboration
- 7. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

MSU/ITS/N2201: Prepare for Digital Communication and Collaboration
Description
This OS is about activities involved in preparing for the deployment of digital communication channels and
collaboration tools.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
identify work requirements
confirm the tool and related applications for digital communication and collaboration
Elements and Performance Criteria
identify work requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the type of tasks to be performed and information to be shared by gathering inputs
from the relevant stakeholders
PC2. confirm other parameters that could impact the need and type of communication or
collaboration tool(s) required. Parameters: type of business functions to be catered to; the
number of teams and users, type of communication and collaboration required, resources
available; constraints, analytics required, etc.
PC3. record the information obtained in line with organisational work practices
PC4. carry out research to understand the constraints and existing base of resources and their
scope of utilisation. Constraints: Budgets, data size, security and privacy concerns, user
capabilities, etc. Resources: supporting applications; connected devices; space requirements,
etc.
PC5. establish requirements according to business specifications, legislations and other inputs
confirm the tool and related applications for digital communication and collaboration
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. identify the types of communication and collaboration tools required based on the
requirements identified. Communication Tools: Email, Web conferencing, e-messaging, group
chatting, online forums, internet calling, website, social media, etc. Collaboration tools: co-
authoring, digital workspaces, digital brainstorming, cloud file storage and sharing, etc.
PC7. scout for tool options available in the market that meet the requirement
PC8. seek quotations and demonstrations for their products from the respective vendors and
share with the stakeholders
PC9. assess the feasibility of the communication and collaboration tool to be used and finalise it
as per requirement/s
PC10. seek approvals for obtaining the respective applications for carrying out remote work
PC11. carry out procurement process as per organisations policy and obtain necessary licences
PC12. prepare a plan to deploy the digital communication and collaboration tool effectively
PC13. communicate the plan to relevant stakeholders as per standard operating procedure

MSU/ITS/N2202: Undertake Corporate Communications using Digital
Communication Tools
Description
This OS is about various activities involved in corporate communication using digital tools.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communication strategy implementation
Website communications management
Social media campaigns and posting
Communication analytics
Internal Communications
Elements and Performance Criteria
Communication strategy implementation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. provide inputs and required support while executing a communication strategy
PC2. communicate to promote the brand’s image and visibility using messaging and sharing
quality content during internal and external interactions
PC3. oversee the development of communication collaterals to ensure that it is consistent with the
corporate identity
PC4. collaborate with marketing and design teams to ensure consistent brand messages across all
deployed digital communication channels
PC5. use standard practices (writing, proofreading and editing) to manage content on channels
Website communications management
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. organise and update content for the company website in coordination with the content team
PC7. create a system for regular reviews of website content
PC8. edit information as and when required as per company amendments and updates
Social media campaigns and posting
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. create content for all social media platforms by using information, graphics, video and other
means
PC10. reach out to influencers and broadcast content
PC11. schedule multiple announcement updates for continuous engagement
PC12. respond to relevant posts and queries on social media as per organisational guidelines
PC13. generate daily engagement reports for reviewing efficiency and impact and sharing with
stakeholders
PC14. maintain an editorial calendar to ensure the timely release of content on the websites

MSU/ITS/N2203: Use Digital Collaboration Tools to Work with Teams
Description
This OS is about using digital technology to plan and undertake working and collaboration with other team
members, usually from large teams, remote working teams, outsourced work, and teams with a lot of
collaboration work
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan and manage collaboration activities
Use digital collaboration tools for project management activities
Use software for digital document collaboration and co-authoring
Elements and Performance Criteria
Plan and manage collaboration activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the collaboration activities involved in the work process
PC2. initiate and configure related tools and applications as required in the process
PC3. set up accounts to utilise various digital collaboration tools Collaboration tools: co-authoring,
digital workspaces, digital rainstorming, document management, decision support,
knowledge management, etc.
PC4. test tool for effectiveness and security by conducting a pilot run
PC5. develop an action plan for collaboration
Use digital collaboration tools for project management activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. employ collaboration tools for facilitating team interactions, and business discussions
PC7. prepare workflow in formats like Gantt charts, Kanban boards and project calendars using
digital collaboration tools
PC8. carry out work scheduling and work allocation using digital collaboration tools
PC9. update performance and share work using digital collaboration tools
PC10. track performance of work using analytics for digital collaboration tools
PC11. prepare and share work status reports using the digital collaboration tools
Use software for digital document collaboration and co-authoring
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. set parameters of digital document software and document properties for collaboration and
co-authoring correctly
PC13. apply suitable methods to create, use and adapt templates for different types of documents
PC14. employ functions such as combining and merging information within a document from a
range of sources

| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
