#### HCS/Q4601: Paper Mache Art Promoter (Paper Mache)
Brief Job Description
Paper mache Art Promoter plays major role right from the process beginning with strategic planning for
order booking to final shipment. The span of tasks for Paper mache Art Promoter involves getting the right
merchandise, in the right place, at the right time, in the right quantities at the right price.
Personal Attributes
A Paper mache Art Promoter performs in a fast pace environment full of challenges and unpredictability.
He is thus expected to adapt to changes quickly and respond rationally. He is expected to think critically
and work well under pressure. He/she needs to have excellent communication skills, be a good team
player and have interest and predilection analyzing data.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### HCS/N4601: Analyzing the product
Description
This unit describes inputs that make one understand organization business plan for product designed to
fulfill needs of specified clientele base along with target market segment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
marketing trend analysis defining key elements of design order pre-booking activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Marketing Trend Analysis
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Research on market trends.
- PC2. Review and analysis of past designs and samples developed by the organization.
- PC3. Assess correlation and relevance of current design/samples with the past.
Defining key elements of design
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Identify organization objectives to align with client requirements.
- PC5. Carry out research on target market for products.
- PC6. Carry out research to identify new, novel and promising materials.
- PC7. Identify applicable quality standards relevant to designs.
- PC8. Identify elements of cost, budget and timeline applicable to design.
- PC9. Check the design features/motif received from client and review it with inhouse team of
designers and artisans.
- PC10. Check design features provided is complete with all relevant specifications, if not get it
clarified, amended and consolidated in consultation with client and/or in-house design team and artisans.
Order pre-booking activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Collect designs as approved from buyer.
- PC12. Check consumption of various materials like paper, paints, colour etc.
- PC13. Prepare price quotation and send to and/or discuss and seek approval from buyer.

#### HCS/N4602: Establishing Merchandising Objectives
Description
This unit provides Performance Criteria, Knowledge & Understanding, Skills & Abilities required to set
objectives for monitoring progress for the development of product as per design intended.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
vendor/supplier identification and order booking preparation of bill of materials evaluation of manufacturing processes
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Vendor/Supplier Identification and Order Booking
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify the vendors/suppliers for raw materials, dyes, chemicals and accessories.
- PC2. Evaluate capability and commitment of vendors for supply of raw materials.
- PC3. Update the vendor database.
- PC4. Negotiate for price against bulk requirements of raw materials.
- PC5. Generate BOM.
- PC6. Book order as per BOM.
Preparation of Bill of Materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Prepare a well structured and all-comprehensive bom (bill of material) for each of the
designs containing raw materials, various components along with quantities of each needed to manufacture the end product.
- PC8. Cost of product developed for sampling.
Evaluation of Manufacturing Processes
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Appropriate personnel identified for use of various material components (from raw materials
to accessories) throughout the manufacturing process.
- PC10. Identify nature and degree of involvement required by designer and artisan.
- PC11. Define monitoring procedures and check points all along the processes.
- PC12. Develop TNA (time and action) calendar and gantt chart to define and monitor progress of
the project after receiving confirmation of the sample.

#### HCS/N4603: Developing and Presenting Merchandising Plan
Description
This unit provides Performance Criteria, Knowledge & Understanding, Skills & Abilities required to develop
merchandising plan beginning with development of sample, order confirmation to identifying processes
steps with sequence for bulk production.
Scope

overseeing of sample development finalization of merchandising plan

Overseeing of sample development

- PC1. Check the specification details prepared is comprehensive for all information contents.

development.

approval.

#### HCS/N4604: Organizing and Conducting Pre-production Meeting
Description
This unit provides Performance Criteria, Knowledge & Understanding and Skills & Abilities required to
organize and conduct pre-production meeting with all concerned people.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
preparation of preproduction meeting conducting meeting output of meeting
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparation of Preproduction Meeting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Ensure tna prepared is updated.
- PC2. Check procurement state and status of raw materials, accessories etc. for order placement,
availability target time and so on from warehouse/store with awareness to all concerned departments.
- PC3. Ensure production file is complete for all information like approved sample, design details,
buyers comments etc.
- PC4. Ensure participant members represent sampling, production, procurement, store and quality.
Conducting Meeting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Buyers comment on sample.
- PC6. Share queries asked by buyer regarding production and quality of product.
- PC7. Quality standards expected for the product at various stages of production.
- PC8. Discuss difficulties encountered and ways of overcoming the same while developing product
at sample stage.
- PC9. Critical stages at production and care/precaution needed to overcome.
- PC10. Discuss details of processes, skills, handtools, machines and equipments required for
manufacturing.
- PC11. Discuss dos and donts observed at all stages of manufacturing.
- PC12. Discuss and update tna for materials and accessories availability.
- PC13. Discuss tna with all stages and time for inspection and testing.
Output of Meeting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. Preparation of minutes of the meeting.
- PC15. Handing over production file (as relevant in segments or complete) to all concerned along
with minutes.

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
