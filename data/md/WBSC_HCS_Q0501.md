#### WBSC/HCS/Q0501: Jari and Thread Work Karigar
Brief Job Description
A Jari and Thread Work Karigar is responsible for creating designs using metallic threads, beads with
various fabrics. They meticulously execute traditional patterns, ensuring precision and quality. The artisan
inspects raw materials, maintains tools and upholds quality control standards to produce high-end
decorative items and garments.
Personal Attributes
A Jari and Thread Work Karigar demonstrates exceptional attention to detail, patience and creativity. They
possess strong hand eye coordination and manual dexterity, quality conscious and disciplined nature for
upholding high craftsmanship standards. Adaptability, teamwork and entrepreneurial thinking further
enhance their professional growth.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. WBSC/HCS/N0501: Explain the roles and responsibility of Jari and Thread Work Karigar.

#### WBSC/HCS/N0501: Explain the roles and responsibility of Jari and Thread
Work Karigar.
Description
Explain the roles and responsibility of Jari and Thread Work Karigar.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The history and cultural significance associated with Jari and Thread work.
Different types of Jari and their suitability on different fabrics.
Maintenance of Safe Working Practices.
Durability check including stretching and light washing of sample fabrics.
Different types of fire extinguishers and their use.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Describe the history and cultural significance associated with Jari and Thread work.
- PC2. Identify different types of Jari and their suitability on different fabrics.
- PC3. Apply and maintain Safe Working Practices.
- PC4. Perform durability check including stretching and light washing of sample fabrics.
- PC5. Identify different fire extinguishers and use them as per requirements.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The history and cultural significance of Jari work.
- KU2. The roles and responsibility of Jari worker.
- KU3. Various safety hazards associated with jari work.
- KU4. Different types of jari, categorized on material quality and application (e.g. real jari, imitation
jari(metallic jari), plastic jari).
- KU5. Fire and safety hazards and necessary precautions to ensure a safe working environment.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Identify different types of Jari (e.g., real Zari, imitation Zari, plastic Zari) and their suitability
on different fabrics.
- GS2. Demonstrate the use of Personal Productive Equipment (PPE).

#### WBSC/HCS/N0502: Identify different tools and materials for Jari and
Thread work.
Description
Identify different tools and materials for Jari and Thread work.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Different tools and raw materials required for jari and thread work.
Various types of threads (Real jari, imitation jari, plastic jari).
Stencils and design patterns, tracing paper, design templates.
Different types of needles for Jari work.
Different types of Scissors for Jari work.
Selection of threads & needles according to the texture and fiber of the material.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify different tools and raw materials required for jari and thread work.
- PC2. Differentiate various types of threads (Real jari, imitation jari, plastic jari).
- PC3. Explain Stencils and design patterns, tracing paper, design templates.
- PC4. Identify different needles for Jari work.
- PC5. Identify different Scissors for Jari work.
- PC6. Select threads & needles according to the texture and fiber of the material.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The basic tools and materials used in Jari work, including needles, frames, various threads and
base fabrics.
- KU2. The purpose of essential tools such as tambour needles, frames (adda), scissors, and thimbles.
- KU3. Categorization of basic hand embroidery stitches, demonstration of their techniques, and application of them in appropriate contexts.
- KU4. Various base fabrics like silk, velvet, chiffon, cotton, and organza, and determine their suitability for Jari work.
- KU5. Selection of supporting threads, such as cotton, silk, and polyester, based on the requirements of the design.
- KU6. Usage of stencils, design patterns, tracing paper, and design templates to create accurate and
intricate designs.

#### WBSC/HCS/N0503: Prepare the workplace for Jari and Thread work.
Description
Prepare the workplace for Jari and Thread work.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparation of the workplace for Jari and Thread work.
Setting up large work table.
Usage of cushions or pillows to support hands and arms.
Fixing the fabric on the embroidery frame correctly positioned.
Adjustment of lighting and temperature control for Jari work.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Prepare the workplace for Jari and Thread work.

tools.

- KU5. Adjustment of lighting and temperature to create an optimal environment for Jari work.
- KU6. Recognization of potential hazards, such as sharp tools and handle them safely to prevent injuries.

Description
Apply different techniques with design and color.
