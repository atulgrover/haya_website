#### RSC/Q8701: Parali Brick Fuel Machine Operator
Brief Job Description
Parali Brick Fuel Machine Operator is responsible for assisting in the process of converting agricultural
stubble (parali) into briquettes. The role involves operating and maintaining stubble briquetting machine,
handling chemicals, binders, and nanomaterials (as per requirement) and ensuring the quality of the final
product. The Parali Brick Fuel Machine Operator will work under the supervision of senior operators and
follow safety protocols to ensure a safe and efficient production environment. This role plays a crucial part
in reducing stubble burning and promoting energy practices.
Personal Attributes
The candidate should demonstrate strong attention to detail, a commitment to safety, and the ability to
work collaboratively in a team-oriented environment. They should also possess problem-solving skills and
a proactive attitude towards maintenance and quality assurance in the briquetting process.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N0807: Operate Biomass/ Stubble Briquetting Machinery
2. RSC/N8801: Handle Chemical and Binders Safely
3. RSC/N8302: Perform routine maintenance and quality checks
4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### RSC/N0807: Operate Biomass/ Stubble Briquetting Machinery
Description
This unit is about preparing stubble and machinery for briquetting operation and operating briquetting
machine to produce briquettes.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare Stubble for Briquetting Operation
Handling and Preparation of Stubble/ Biomass
Operate Briquetting Machine
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare Stubble for Briquetting Operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. inspect and select stubble suitable for briquetting, ensuring that the material is free from
unwanted debris and contaminants
- PC2. measure moisture content of stubble using appropriate tools and techniques to ensure it
meets briquetting standards
- PC3. prepare stubble by cutting it to the required size and shape using appropriate cutting tools or
machines
- PC4. set up and calibrate pre-treatment machinery (if applicable) to process stubble effectively
- PC5. follow environmental and safety guidelines during the handling and preparation of stubble to
avoid fire hazards
Handling and Preparation of Stubble Biomass
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. load stubble into storage areas or conveyors for transportation to the briquetting area
- PC7. mix stubble biomass with required binders, chemicals, or nanomaterials (as per the product
specification)
- PC8. check the quality and consistency of the mixture to ensure uniformity for briquette formation
- PC9. operate auxiliary equipment for handling biomass, such as conveyors, balers, or shredders,
as required
- PC10. maintain cleanliness of the work area and handle chemicals or binders following safety
procedures
Operate Briquetting Machine
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. start, operate, and monitor the briquetting machine following standard operating procedures
(SOPs)
- PC12. adjust machine settings to ensure briquettes meet the required dimensions, density, and
quality standards

#### RSC/N8801: Handle Chemical and Binders Safely
Description
This unit is about handling chemicals and binders and applying it on parali before/ during briquetting
process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Handling Binders
Handling Chemicals
Applying Chemicals and Binders on Parali
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Handling Binders
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify and select appropriate binders as per the production requirements and quality
standards
- PC2. measure and prepare binders in the correct proportions, ensuring accuracy according to
product specifications
- PC3. safely store binders in designated areas, ensuring containers are properly sealed and labeled
to avoid contamination
- PC4. handle binders using appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) such as gloves,
masks, and goggles to avoid direct contact
- PC5. maintain cleanliness in the binder handling area to prevent spills or cross-contamination with
other materials
Handling Chemicals
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. identify and select chemicals used in the briquetting process, including additives or
preservatives, as per standard procedures
- PC7. measure chemicals accurately using suitable measuring tools to avoid incorrect dosage and
ensure uniform application
- PC8. store chemicals in a safe and secure location, following the organization’s safety protocols
for labeling, storage temperature, and hazard classification
- PC9. handle chemicals while following all safety regulations, including the use of PPE, proper
ventilation, and safe handling techniques to avoid inhalation or skin contact
- PC10. follow spill management procedures in case of accidental chemical spills and immediately
report to the supervisor for further action
Applying Chemicals and Binders on Parali
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. mix binders and chemicals with the stubble biomass in the correct ratio as per the technical
guidelines for briquetting

#### RSC/N8302: Perform routine maintenance and quality checks
Description
This unit is about regularly inspecting the machinery and carrying out routine maintenance as required.
Also includes performing quality checks on manufactured briquettes.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Regular Inspection of Machinery
Carry Out Maintenance of Machinery
Carry Out Quality Checks on Manufactured Briquettes
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Regular Inspection of Machinery
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. conduct daily visual inspections of the briquetting machine for signs of wear, damage, or
malfunction before starting the operation
- PC2. check all key components, including rollers, press plates, and feeding mechanisms, for any
operational irregularities
- PC3. ensure that safety guards and emergency stops are functional and not tampered with
- PC4. inspect electrical connections and hydraulic systems for leaks, loose wires, or potential
hazards
- PC5. report any irregularities or mechanical issues to senior operators or supervisors for further
action
Carry Out Maintenance of Machinery
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. lubricate moving parts of the briquetting machine as per the manufacturer’s
recommendations to reduce wear and tear
- PC7. tighten loose bolts, nuts, or fittings to maintain machine stability and operational efficiency
- PC8. replace or clean filters, belts, and any worn-out parts of the machine according to the
maintenance schedule
- PC9. maintain a logbook of maintenance activities, recording the date, time, and type of service
performed on the machinery
- PC10. ensure the machine is cleaned and free from stubble residues or debris after each operation
to prevent clogging and maintain efficiency
Carry Out Quality Checks on Manufactured Briquettes
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. inspect briquettes for uniformity in size, shape, and density during and after the production
process
- PC12. measure moisture content in briquettes to ensure it meets the required quality standards for
fuel efficiency
