TNSD/CSC/Q0602: Precision Machinist - Conventional
Brief Job Description
This qualification comprises various modules essential for the Precision Machinist - Conventional job role.
This qualification is designed to enable learners to achieve the following:Operate and maintain
conventional machine tools (such as Lathe, Drilling, Milling, and Grinding machines) to manufacture
precision parts according to engineering drawings and standard specifications.
Personal Attributes
Apply foundational knowledge in manufacturing technology, engineering measurements, technical
mathematics, and applied science relevant to machining operations.Interpret and create engineering
drawings using both manual techniques and AutoCAD.Secure employment across a wide range of sectors,
including general engineering, manufacturing industries, machine tool industries, and the automobile
sector.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. TNSD/CSC/N0610: Perform Basic Manufacturing Operations
- 2. TNSD/CSC/N0611: Conduct Engineering Measurements
- 3. TNSD/CSC/N0612: Interpret and Create Engineering Drawings
- 4. TNSD/CSC/N0613: Create 2D Drawings using AutoCAD
- 5. TNSD/CSC/N0614: Apply Technical Mathematics
- 6. TNSD/CSC/N0615: Apply Principles of Applied Science
- 7. DGT/VSQ/N0104: Employability Skills (120 Hours)
- 8. TNSD/CSC/N0616: Demonstrate Computer Literacy

TNSD/CSC/N0610: Perform Basic Manufacturing Operations
Description
Understand different types of hand tools and machine tools used in tool room/Machine shop. Understand
applications of different machine tools used for manufacturing of mechanical parts / assembly. Understand
different types of welding processes such as SMAW, GMAW and GTAW
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Familiarize with the properties & uses of ferrous and non-ferrous metals.
Knowledge of safety precautions which is to be observed in workshop/ and while working on
machines.
Knowledge of engineering drawing with Limits, fits and GD&T.
Knowledge of different measuring instruments and gauges used in Machine shop.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Learn different types of ferrous and non-ferrous material used in engineering practices
PC2. Understand the Properties and Heat-treatment processes of materials used for engineering
purpose.
PC3. Understand the safety aspects and PPE used in workshop
PC4. Understand the different types of hand tools and bench work like filing, hacksawing,
hammering, marking, letter punching, etc.,
PC5. Learn the Process of drilling and related operations like tapping, reaming, counter sinking
and counter boring.
PC6. Understand the Different parts and its functions of conventional tool room machines-Drilling,
lathe, Milling and Grinding.
PC7. Familiar with Different types of operations like turning,milling,grinding performed on tool
room machines.
PC8. Understand the different types of work holding devices, Tool holding devices of Drilling,
lathe, Milling and grinding.
PC9. Understand the Nomenclature of cutting tool angles and re – sharpening
PC10. Make right angled block and different types of fits by bench work.
PC11. Machine parts as per drawing using different machines and assembling.
PC12. Fabricate different parts of a project and assembling

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

TNSD/CSC/N0611: Conduct Engineering Measurements
Description
Understand the usage of different measuring instruments Familiarization with different checking gauges
used in tool room/manufacturing. Understand the system of limits &fits used in the manufacturing
industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the usage of different measuring tools & familiarization with different checking methods
Understand the importance of limits and fits in engineering practices.
Understand the system of GD& T
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Learn about Co ordinate systems and tools for marking
PC2. Know difference between checking and measuring and relevant tools used
PC3. Understand linear ,angular and bore checking and measuring instruments
PC4. Understand limits and fits and reading of tolerance chart
PC5. Know different types of fits and its applications
PC6. Understand Hole based system and shaft based system
PC7. Learn about Interchangeability and standardization
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Know different types of fits and its applications
KU2. Understand linear ,angular and bore checking and measuring instruments
KU3. Understand limits and fits and reading of tolerance chart
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Learn about Co ordinate systems and tools for marking

TNSD/CSC/N0612: Interpret and Create Engineering Drawings
Description
Read and understand engineering drawing so that items can be manufactured as per dimensions and
specifications mentioned. Draw Board drawings of parts which are required to be manufactured.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Ability to read engineering drawing.
Understand the different types of threads and its representation.
Ability to understand simple mechanical assemblies
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Learn the Basics of engineering drawing and free hand sketching
PC2. Draw Different geometrical constructions
PC3. Draw the types of projection, sectional views with dimensioning
PC4. Draw Orthographic views and isometric views
PC5. Draw the assembly drawing and part drawing
PC6. Draw the Development of different sections
PC7. Learn the basics of GD & T
PC8. Draw the representation of various types of joints and fasteners
PC9. Draw the representation of different threads, weld joints and rivets
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Learn the Basics of engineering drawing and free hand sketching
KU2. Learn the basics of GD & T
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Draw the representation of various types of joints and fasteners

TNSD/CSC/N0614: Apply Technical Mathematics
Description
Understand the concept of basic mathematics used for doing calculations related to engineering
application and general purpose
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Familiar with System of units, conversions, Simple Arithmetic operations of fractions and decimals.
Understanding the basic concepts of Ratio, proportion and mensuration.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Learn Fundamental and derived units, System of units, conversions, Arithmetic operations of
fractions and decimal fractions
PC2. Review of Basic concepts of Arithmetic operations under Whole numbers, Common fraction
and Decimal Fraction.
PC3. Understand the Basic concepts of Square root ,perfect square numbers, Prime factorization
method, Application of Pythagora’s theorem
PC4. Learn to Calculate Ratio, proportion and Percentage related problems
PC5. Learn to Find the Area, Perimeter of circles, triangles, Quadrilaterals & Polygons and related
problems.
PC6. Understand to Evaluate the Volume, lateral surface area, total surface area of 3D shapes.
PC7. Learn the Concepts of trigonometry ratios and identities.
PC8. Learn to Find the unknown sides of triangle by using the sine, cos, tan ratios, Sine bar , taper
ratio related problems.
PC9. Understand to find the Solution of triangles using Sine rule, Cosine rule and projection
formula and related problems.
PC10. Understand the Concepts of Algebra , algebraic expressions , algebraic equations and
Factorization by using Algebraic Identities.
PC11. Learn to Solve the homogeneous equations by algebraic concepts.
PC12. Learn to Perform Addition, subtraction, multiplication and division of matrices.
PC13. Learn to find the determinant of matrices, inverse of Matrices, Form the Euclidean form and
find the rank of matrices.
PC14. Learn to Solve the linear equations by Cramer rule and classification of solution of
homogeneous equations

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
