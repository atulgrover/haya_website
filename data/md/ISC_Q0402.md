#### ISC/Q0402: Locomotive Driver
Brief Job Description
The job is all about operating the locomotive to ensure movement of raw materials, intermediates, finished
products within the plant. It includes maintaining log book for performance parameters and working
condition of locomotive.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the candidate to work independently as well as in teams. Some of the key attributes
includes physical fitness, not having colour blindness , having analytical skills, problem solving attitude,
disciplined, high concentration levels, a sharp reflex and willingness to work in a factory environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ISC/N0008: Use basic health and safety practices at the workplace
2. ISC/N0009: Work effectively with others
3. ISC/N0404: Inspect critical systems of locomotive
4. ISC/N0405: Operate locomotive to carry material

#### ISC/N0008: Use basic health and safety practices at the workplace
Description
This OS unit is about knowledge and practices relating to health, safety and security that candidates need
to use in the workplace. It covers responsibilities towards self, others, assets and the environment. It
includes understanding of risks and hazards in the workplace, along with common techniques to minimize
risk, deal with accidents, emergencies, etc

#### ISC/N0404: Inspect critical systems of locomotive
Description
This unit is about inspecting the locomotive to ensure they are in working condition before operating it for
transfer of material
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Take handover from previous shift operator
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. report on the job as per schedule given by shift in charge
- PC2. take over from the previous shift operator and understand the task to be done safely
- PC3. zero derailment /collision and no accident
Conduct physicalinspections to check working condition of locomotive
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. conducts physical inspections to check coolant level, oil level etc. as per checklist
- PC5. report deviation from desirable levels including rail track , if any to shift in charge to get them
rectified
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. quality and damage checks to be done and importance of the same
- KU2. risk and impact of not following defined procedures/work instructions
- KU3. escalation matrix for reporting identified issues
