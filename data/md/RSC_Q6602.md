RSC/Q6602: Processing Technician - Rubber Sheet
Brief Job Description
The Processing Technician - Rubber Sheet is responsible for carrying out operations involved in the
preparation, coagulation, sheeting, smoking, and drying of ribbed smoked sheets (RSS) from natural latex.
The individual ensures process efficiency, product quality, hygiene, and safety compliance throughout the
rubber processing stages under the supervision of senior technicians or plant operators.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be detail-oriented, safety-conscious, and physically capable of handling rubber
processing operations efficiently. They should demonstrate discipline, teamwork, observational skills, and
a commitment to quality and environmental practices.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N1018: Prepare for RSS Processing
- 2. RSC/N1019: Carry out coagulation and sheeting operations
- 3. RSC/N1020: Perform smoking and drying of rubber sheets
- 4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

RSC/N1018: Prepare for RSS Processing
Description
This NOS covers the preparation of raw rubber for RSS (Ribbed Smoked Sheet) processing, including
sorting, cleaning, blending, and staging of raw material. The individual ensures material uniformity, quality
compliance, workplace safety, and proper documentation under supervision.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparation of Raw Rubber Processing
Conduct Quality and Safety Compliance in RSS Rubber processing
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparation of Raw Rubber Processing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify and list raw latex, coagulum, or field rubber for processing
PC2. demonstrate and perform preliminary cleaning and sorting of raw rubber
PC3. apply standard techniques to break or blend coagulum for uniform consistency
PC4. show and display proper measurement of raw rubber quantities as per batch requirements
PC5. recognize impurities or contaminants and report them to the supervisor
PC6. ensure safe handling of raw rubber and chemicals used in pre-processing
PC7. develop and carry out proper storage and staging of raw rubber for processing
PC8. conduct checks on moisture content and physical quality of rubber before processing
PC9. explain, define, and elaborate on the preparation procedures to team members
PC10. point out, state, and mention deviations from standard quality or preparation practice
PC11. demonstrate correct use of personal protective equipment (PPE) during preparation
PC12. apply housekeeping practices to maintain a clean and safe work area
PC13. summarize batch preparation details and document all observations in logbooks
PC14. perform coordination with downstream processing teams to ensure smooth workflow
PC15. understand and display compliance with environmental and waste disposal norms
Conduct Quality and Safety Compliance in RSS Rubber processing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC16. identify and list out quality parameters and safety standards applicable to RSS rubber
processing
PC17. demonstrate and perform inspections of raw and processed rubber to ensure compliance
with specifications
PC18. apply standard procedures to monitor consistency, moisture content, and physical quality of
rubber sheets

RSC/N1019: Carry out coagulation and sheeting operations
Description
This NOS covers the operations involved in coagulating latex and forming ribbed rubber sheets through
controlled processing. The individual prepares equipment, performs coagulation, operates sheeting rollers,
and ensures quality, safety, and process efficiency.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare and Set Up Equipment for Coagulation and Sheeting
Perform Coagulation of Latex to Form Rubber Sheets
Operate and Maintain Sheeting, Rolling, and Handling Processes
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare and Set Up Equipment for Coagulation and Sheeting
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify and arrange the required raw materials, equipment, and coagulants as per
instructions
PC2. inspect the coagulation tanks, rollers, and sheeting tables to ensure cleanliness and
operational readiness
PC3. prepare and calibrate measuring instruments and mixing tools for latex and coagulant
solutions
PC4. verify the availability and concentration of coagulants (e.g., formic acid) as per standard
ratios
PC5. mix latex and coagulant in specified proportions under supervision to initiate uniform
coagulation
PC6. ensure the work area is clean, dry, and free from contaminants before beginning operations
PC7. follow equipment pre-checks for mechanical integrity, lubrication, and safety compliance
PC8. arrange and label collection trays or molds for sheet formation as per batch plan
PC9. wear appropriate PPE and adhere to safety procedures during setup
PC10. record preparation parameters and confirm readiness with the supervisor before starting
coagulation
Perform Coagulation of Latex to Form Rubber Sheets
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. pour latex into the coagulation tanks or trays in measured quantities as per batch
instructions
PC12. add coagulant solution gradually and mix gently to ensure uniform coagulation without air
entrapment
PC13. observe the coagulation process and identify signs of uneven setting or poor separation
PC14. maintain optimal temperature and resting time for complete coagulation as per SOP

sheeting tables

coagulation
KU5. the appropriate ratios and concentration of coagulant solution required for uniform
coagulation
KU6. factors affecting coagulation efficiency such as temperature, resting time, and pH levels
KU7. the correct procedure to test sheet consistency, thickness, and uniformity during formation
KU8. techniques for adjusting roller speed, pressure, and gap to ensure consistent ribbed sheet
quality
KU9. the importance of removing excess water and impurities during rolling to enhance sheet
strength and finish

sheets
