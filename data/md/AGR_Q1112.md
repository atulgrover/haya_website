#### AGR/Q1112: Custom Hiring Centre In-charge (Agri machinery)
Brief Job Description
A Custom Hiring Centre In-charge (Agri machinery) is responsible for providing hiring services to small and
marginal farmers for various agricultural machineries, tools and equipment used in different farm
operations. For this purpose, the person sets up a centre and makes these available for hiring to farmers
on demand basis.
Personal Attributes
The individual in this role must have managerial skills. The person must be able to read, write and
communicate well. Good computing and accounting skills are other desirable attributes for this job role.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. AGR/N9921: Establish and Manage a Business
2. AGR/N1140: Prepare to set up the custom hiring service centre
3. AGR/N1141: Operationalise the custom hiring service centre
4. AGR/N1139: Use a computer to process payments, communicate and maintain records
5. AGR/N9903: Maintain health and safety at the workplace
6. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

#### AGR/N9921: Establish and Manage a Business
Description
This OS unit is about establishing a business as per a plan after conducting market research and
monitoring its performance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :

#### AGR/N1140: Prepare to set up the custom hiring service centre
Description
This OS unit is about preparing to set up a custom hiring service centre.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct field and online research
Identify the resources required to set up the centre
Select the location of the centre
Apply for financial assistance
Arrange the necessary resources
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct field and online research
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the target area after conducting research on multiple areas
- PC2. collect information about the relevant government schemes and policies that support custom
hiring services
- PC3. co-ordinate with the existing custom hiring service providers to keep abreast with the
business’s viability and latest trends in the field
- PC4. conduct surveys in the target area to get information about the need of different farm
machineries, tools and equipment
Identify the resources and prepare the cost estimates
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. prepare a list of farm machineries, tools and equipment required for the centre
- PC6. estimate the requirement of other resources such as manpower, utilities, etc.
- PC7. prepare the cost estimates for setting up the centre considering all fixed, variable and
recurring costs
Select the location of the centre
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. identify a location for setting up the centre based on factors such as ability to cater a larger
audience and availability of utilities i.e. water, electricity, telephone
- PC9. ascertain that the location is away from any residential area and safe from any significant
hazards
Apply for financial assistance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. identify the financial institution(s) under relevant government schemes to secure financial
assistance
- PC11. secure financial assistance following the application procedure of the identified financial
institution(s)

Establish the centre
Initiate and manage the operations

| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
