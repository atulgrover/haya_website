#### THC/Q0419: Cooking (WorldSkills)
Brief Job Description
Professional chefs need to demonstrate outstanding skills in food preparation (with both speed and
accuracy), in production (using varied cooking methods and techniques) and in the final presentation and
flavor. They are expected to create and adapt dishes that meet the expectations of demanding customers
with varying nutritional and religious dietary requirements. Fashions and trends in cuisine also fluctuate,
so it is important that the professional chef keeps abreast of these trends and adapts their product and
service accordingly The Professional chef needs to be adept at managing food cost and maintaining
environmentally-friendly sustainable food production by making use of locally sourced and seasonal
ingredients, as well as controlling unnecessary waste by creatively re-using trimmings and reducing
ingredient wastage.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have the stamina to work for long hours, attention to details, creativity
and patience.

#### THC/N0340: Maintain work organization and management  (Cooking)
Description
This NOS emphasizes upon efficiently coordinating and overseeing kitchen operations, ensuring a
systematic and organized workflow in culinary activities
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan daily activities of the kitchen
Understand ingredients and their use in recipes
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Maintain quality standards throughout production, even when under pressure
- PC2. Prepare work plans for single dishes and plan schedules for the preparation of a menu
- PC3. Plan daily work for self and others
- PC4. Co-ordinate all preparatory tasks (mise-en place) prior to service
- PC5. Prioritize work to differentiate important issues from less important ones
- PC6. Optimize workflow
- PC7. Function effectively in every area of the kitchen
- PC8. Account for time and staff resources
- PC9. Detect conflicts, address them and find solutions with the team
- PC10. Apply environmentally sustainable energy saving practices when using cooking equipment
- PC11. Operate all commercial kitchen equipment and technology properly and safely
- PC12. Apply legal requirements for the description and sale of menu items
- PC13. Be responsive and flexible to unforeseen situations
- PC14. Adapt working methods to given situations and be an effective member to the team
- PC15. Make suggestions for improvement of operational procedures
- PC16. Create and adapt recipes with own ideas and develop them further
- PC17. Demonstrate a feel and understanding of ingredients and be able to create dishes without
recipes
- PC18. Treat all goods with respect and apply sustainable economic resourcefulness
- PC19. Judge personal performance ability and avoid unnecessary stress
- PC20. Set goals independently and achieve them
- PC21. Take personal responsibility for own continuing professional development

#### THC/N0441: Communicate  and provide customer service (Cooking)
Description
This NOS emphasizes the importance of customer service as a crucial aspect as it ensures customer
satisfaction and proposes solutions to implement meal promotions and timings for service delivery
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan and implement meal and menu promotions
Communicate effectively with customers and team members
Maintain personal and workplace hygiene
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Pay attention to own cleanliness, personal hygiene, and professional appearance (including
dress code) at all times
- PC2. Pay attention to work area cleanliness and appearance at all times
- PC3. Communicate effectively with colleagues, teams, and customers
- PC4. Make use of a menu as a sales and communication tool
- PC5. Check dishes produced match the menu description
- PC6. Apply appropriate food service styles to the situation and clientele
- PC7. Place customer service at the heart of decision-making and activities
- PC8. Provide advice and guidance on culinary matters to management, colleagues, and customers
- PC9. Propose solutions and discuss with goal orientated attitude, arriving at agreed solutions
- PC10. Plan and implement meal promotions
- PC11. Plan and implement timings for service delivery
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The characteristics of different types and styles of food service and when each would be appropriately used
- KU2. The impact on food production of the various types of food service
- KU3. The importance of the menu as a sales and communication tool
- KU4. Legal requirements and restrictions on promotional materials and menu presentation
- KU5. The importance of appearance when on public view and dealing with customers
- KU6. The importance of effective communication within the team, with customers and contractors

#### THC/N0443: Understand Food hygiene  and health, safety, and
environment
Description
This NOS describes the importance for the food handlers and chefs to prevent and respond to risks
ensuring a hygienic environment and food preparation
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Ensuring safe handling of food
Preventing cross contaminations and other food related hazards
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Work hygienically and practise hygienic food tasting procedures.
- PC2. Maintain clean, neat, and tidy workstation, storage, and waste areas.
- PC3. Practice food safety during storage, preparation, cooking, and service (HACCP principles are
applied)
- PC4. Prevent cross-contamination, fridge drip-contamination and time-temperature abuse.
- PC5. Store all food items contained, covered, labelled, and dated.
- PC6. Store all food items contained, covered, labelled, and dated
- PC7. Ensure all equipment and work areas are cleaned to the highest standards
- PC8. Work safely and uphold accident prevention regulations
- PC9. Use all tools and equipment safely and according to the manufacturer’s instructions
- PC10. Promote health, safety, and environment and food hygiene within the working environment
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Legislation and good practice relating to the purchase, storage, preparation, cooking, and
service of food
- KU2. Legislation and good practice for safe working practices in a kitchen and for using commercial
catering equipment
- KU3. The causes of deterioration of food
- KU4. Quality indicators for fresh and preserved foods

#### THC/N0444: Handle Ingredients and Develop Menu
Description
This NOS describes the proper handling of ingredients and application of nutrition principles while
developing menu
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Selecting and handling ingredients properly
Maintaining standards while selecting ingredients
Menu composition
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Advise on procurement of ingredients and equipment
- PC2. Select appropriate product quality standards for menus, budgets, and situations
- PC3. Recognize and comply with ingredient labelling
- PC4. Apply nutritional principles in order to comply with guests’ expectations and requirements
- PC5. Identify the quality state of ingredients and recognize the signs of quality and freshness, e.g.
appearance, aroma, structure, etc.
- PC6. Appraise and reject sub-standard goods
- PC7. Select appropriate products for speciality meals
- PC8. Compose menus for a variety of situations and occasions
- PC9. Be accurate in menu preparation and account for obligatory declarations such as dietary and
allergy information
- PC10. Compile menus for a variety of occasions and settings
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

- KU1. Market prices for ingredients and the correlation between price and quality
- KU2. The nature and types of ingredients used in cookery
- KU3. Product seasonal availability and value
- KU4. The correlation between product quality, menu sophistication, and gastronomic level
- KU5. The nutritional properties of ingredients
- KU6. The physical nutritional impact of cooking methods
- KU7. Import regulations and restrictions that apply to some ingredients
- KU8. Types and styles of menu
- KU9. Balance in menu compilation

#### THC/N0446: Prepare Ingredients
Description
This NOS unit describes preparing various ingredients and appropriate cooking techniques
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparing various dishes from ingredients provided
Preparation of eggs, meats, fish, and poultry
Preparation of seasonal and other vegetables
Preparation of dairy products
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Make environmentally sustainable choices and use locally sourced meat, poultry, game,
offal, seafood, eggs, dairy, fruit, and vegetables currently in season.
- PC2. Order and prepare only what is needed for producing sufficient output for a menu
- PC3. Calculate, measure, and weigh correct amounts of products
- PC4. Apply excellent knife skills and common cutting techniques
- PC5. Debone meat, poultry and game cuts and prepare for further processing, and re-use any
wastage and trimmings
- PC6. Trim and tie meats, poultry, and game
- PC7. Prepare, portion, and fillet meat, poultry, and game cuts
- PC8. Prepare offal
- PC9. Prepare whole fish for further processing - scale, remove entrails, fillet, and re-use any
wastage and trimmings
- PC10. Prepare accepted portions of fish for further processing and cooking
- PC11. Prepare shellfish
- PC12. Prepare and use eggs
- PC13. Prepare and use dairy products
- PC14. Prepare fruits, salad ingredients, and vegetables
- PC15. Prepare stocks, soups, sauces, marinades, accompaniments, and dressings
- PC16. Plan and assemble the mise en place for the menu in its entirety with minimal wastage
- PC17. Re-use and utilize unused wastage, trimmings, and mise en place for other applications
- PC18. Make preparations for the production of pastry, bakery products, and desserts
- PC19. Produce a variety of pasta and other farinaceous items
- PC20. Separate waste for recycling and composting
