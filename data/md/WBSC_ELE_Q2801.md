#### WBSC/ELE/Q2801: Service Assistant (Domestic Electronics Products)
Brief Job Description
Service Assistant (Domestic Electronics Products) will provide service and customer support for the
domestic electronics product during field visits or dispatches. Manage all onsite installation, repair,
maintenance and test tasks. Diagnose errors or technical problems and determine proper solutions.
Produce timely and detailed service reports.
Personal Attributes
A Service Assistant (Domestic Electronics Products) should possess strong technical aptitude combined
with a hands-on, solution-oriented mindset. They are dependable, detail-oriented and capable of working
independently during field visits while maintaining a high level of professionalism. They demonstrate clear
communication skills to explain technical issues. He should be customer-focused, patient and willing to
learn new things.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. WBSC/ELE/N2801: Apply Safe Working Practices (Service Assistant (Domestic Electronics
Products)).
2. WBSC/ELE/N2802: Select and perform electrical/ electronic measurement of single range meters
and record the data.
3. WBSC/ELE/N2803: Plan and execute soldering & de-soldering of various electrical components used in domestic appliances and assemble and test a AM/FM receiver and evaluate performance.
4. WBSC/ELE/N2804: Install, configure, interconnect given computer system(s) and demonstrate its
I/O devices.
5. WBSC/ELE/N2805: Identify, operate various controls, troubleshoot and replace modules of the LCD/LED TV and its remote.
6. WBSC/ELE/N2806: Install a DTH system by proper selection of site, assembling of different parts/
accessories and troubleshoot the system.
7. WBSC/ELE/N2807: Dismantle, identify the parts of microwave oven and washing machine for the testing and repair.
8. WBSC/ELE/N2808: Install a CCTV system and configure the system for surveillance function.
9. WBSC/ELE/N2809: Work in real job situation with special emphasis on basic safety and hazards in
this domain (Service Assistant (Domestic Electronics Products)).

#### WBSC/ELE/N2801: Apply Safe Working  Practices (Service Assistant
(Domestic Electronics Products)).
Description
Apply Safe Working Practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Procedures to achieve a safe working environment in line with occupational health and safety regulations and requirements.
Any unsafe situations according to site policy.
Precautions on fire and safety hazards.
Personal Productive Equipment (PPE).
Basic first aid & CPR and use them under different circumstances.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Maintain procedures to achieve a safe working environment in line with occupational health
and safety regulations and requirements according to site policy.
- PC2. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assess his report accordingly.
- PC3. Identify and take necessary precautions on fire and safety hazards and report according to
site policy and procedures.
- PC4. Demonstrate Personal Productive Equipment (PPE) like: safety helmet, safety glove, safety
shoe use the same as per related working.
- PC5. Demonstrate basic first aid & CPR and use them under different circumstances.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Procedures to achieve a safe working environment in line with occupational health and safety
regulations and requirements according to site policy.
- KU2. Any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assessing his report accordingly.
- KU3. Necessary precautions on fire and safety hazards and reporting according to site policy and
procedures.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assess his report accordingly.

#### WBSC/ELE/N2802: Select and perform electrical/ electronic measurement
of single range meters and record the data.
Description
Select and perform electrical/ electronic measurement of single range meters and record the data.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Work in compliance with standard safety norms.
Type of electronic instruments.
The measurement errors while measuring resistance.
Extension of the range of MC voltmeter and ammeter.
Measurement of the value of resistance, voltage and current using digital multimeter.

- PC1. Plan work in compliance with standard safety norms.
- PC2. Identify the type of electronic instruments.
- PC3. Determine the measurement errors while measuring resistance.
- PC4. Extend the range of MC voltmeter and ammeter.
- PC5. Measure the value of resistance, voltage and current using digital multimeter.

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Introduction to electrical and electronic measuring instruments.

meters.

- KU4. Characteristics of meters and errors in meters.
- KU5. Multi meter, use of meters in different circuits.
- KU6. Care and maintenance of meters.
- KU7. The use of CRO.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Demonstrate various analog measuring instruments.

Description
Plan and execute soldering & de-soldering of various electrical components used in domestic appliances
and assemble and test a AM/FM receiver and evaluate performance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Work in compliance with standard safety norms.
The primary and secondary transformer winding's and test the polarity.
The primary and secondary voltage of different transformers.
Soldering and de-soldering for a given components and circuits.
Avoiding waste, ascertaining unused materials and components for disposal, storing these in an environmentally appropriate manner and preparing for disposal.
Planning and selecting tools to assemble the receiver.
The various components of AM and FM on the trainer kit.
Troubleshooting and replacing the faulty components.
The functionality of AM/ FM receiver.

- PC1. Plan work in compliance with standard safety norms.
- PC2. Identify the primary and secondary transformer winding's and test the polarity.
- PC3. Measure the primary and secondary voltage of different transformers.
- PC4. Solder the given components Identify and test.
- PC5. Practice soldering and de-soldering for a given components and circuits.
- PC6. Avoid waste, ascertain unused materials and components for disposal, store these in an
environmentally appropriate manner and prepare for disposal.
- PC7. Plan and select tools to assemble the receiver.
- PC8. Test the various components of AM and FM on the trainer kit.
- PC9. Troubleshoot and replace the faulty components.
- PC10. Check the functionality of AM/ FM receiver.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Soldering process.
