RSC/Q8201: Assistant Operator - Plastic Waste Recycling and Processing
Brief Job Description
The individual at work assists in setting up and supporting operations of plastic waste recycling processes.
The person is responsible for preparing materials, tools and equipment, supporting pre-processing
activities such as sorting, washing and shredding, and assisting the operator during the recycling process
while ensuring cleanliness, basic safety and adherence to instructions.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be calm, composed, and physically fit along with good observational
skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N8201: Prepare for plastic waste recycling operations
- 2. RSC/N9702: Support in pre-processing activities
- 3. RSC/N9703: Assist in operating recycling equipment
- 4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

RSC/N8201: Prepare for plastic waste recycling operations
Description
This NOS covers the skills and knowledge to assist in preparing the workplace, materials, and equipment
for plastic waste recycling operations. It includes identification of plastic waste categories, checking
machine readiness, arranging tools and PPE, and preparing workstations under supervision.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify job instructions and work requirements
Prepare tools, equipment and workplace
Ensure safety and readiness
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify job instructions and work requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check with the supervisor about daily work allocation, targets and priorities
PC2. read or listen to work instructions related to the recycling job
PC3. identify the type and volume of plastic waste to be processed
PC4. check documentation regarding batch details and process parameters
PC5. report any missing or unclear instructions to the concerned person
Prepare tools, equipment and workplace
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. collect required tools, PPE and basic materials for recycling operations
PC7. clean and organize the work area before beginning operations
PC8. inspect the tools and equipment for usability or damage
PC9. check basic machine settings as per supervisor’s instructions
PC10. assist in performing minor cleaning or lubrication of machines
Ensure safety and readiness
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. wear required PPE such as gloves, aprons, masks, shoes, etc.
PC12. follow standard operating procedures (SOP) and work instructions
PC13. ensure emergency exits and fire safety equipment are accessible
PC14. report any hazards, leaks or unsafe conditions to the supervisor
PC15. ensure availability of raw materials and segregated plastic waste

RSC/N9702: Support in pre-processing activities
Description
This NOS involves sorting, washing, drying, and shredding plastic waste materials according to
specifications. It includes segregating different types of plastic, removing contaminants, and operating
basic pre-processing equipment under the guidance of the operator.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist in plastic waste segregation
Support in washing and drying operations
Assist in shredding and storage
Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist in plastic waste segregation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify recyclable and non-recyclable plastic waste
PC2. segregate plastic based on type, color or grade
PC3. remove contaminants like paper, metal, or organic matter
PC4. place segregated waste in designated containers or areas
PC5. ensure safe handling and movement of plastic waste
Support in washing and drying operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. feed segregated plastic into washing equipment as instructed
PC7. assist in adding water or cleaning agents
PC8. remove cleaned plastic and transfer to drying station
PC9. spread plastic properly to enable complete drying
PC10. ensure no moisture remains before shredding
Assist in shredding and storage
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. feed dried plastic into shredder or grinder
PC12. monitor shredding process and avoid overloading
PC13. collect shredded plastic flakes or granules
PC14. store processed plastic in labelled containers
PC15. clean and maintain the area after pre-processing
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

RSC/N9703: Assist in operating recycling equipment
Description
This NOS covers assisting in operating plastic recycling machinery such as extruders, agglomerators, or
grinders. It includes feeding materials, monitoring operations, checking outputs, and reporting
abnormalities while adhering to quality and safety protocols.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist in machine operation
Maintain quality and safety during operation
Clean and shut down equipment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist in machine operation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. confirm machine settings from operator or supervisor
PC2. feed pre-processed plastic into the designated hopper or feeder
PC3. check input quality and report defects
PC4. monitor basic indicators such as temperature or speed
PC5. remove processed output from the discharge end
Maintain quality and safety during operation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. check processed product for defects or color changes
PC7. stack or store finished output safely
PC8. avoid direct contact with hot or moving parts
PC9. ensure cooling or cutting units are working properly
PC10. immediately inform the operator in case of breakdown or issue
Clean and shut down equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. remove remaining material from hopper or chamber
PC12. assist in basic cleaning and wiping of equipment
PC13. dispose of rejected material as per instructions
PC14. shut down machines under supervision
PC15. record basic production and downtime details
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
Description
This unit is about maintaining health and safety of self and others at workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
Render appropriate emergency procedures
Maintain standard safety procedures at the workplace
Participate in safety awareness campaigns
Understand potential sources of accidents
Use safety gears to avoid accidents
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. undertake basic safety checks before operation of all machinery and equipment and report
hazards to the appropriate supervisor
PC2. work for which protective clothing or equipment is required is identified and the appropriate
protective clothing or equipment is used in performing these duties in accordance with
workplace policy
PC3. read and understand the hazards of use and contamination mentioned on the labels of
chemicals, utilities etc
PC4. prior to performing manual handling jobs, risk is assessed and work is carried out according
to currently recommended safe practices.
PC5. use equipment and materials safely and correctly and return the same to designated storage
when not in use
PC6. dispose off waste safely and correctly in a designated area
PC7. risks to bystanders are recognized and action taken to reduce risk associated with jobs in the
workplace
PC8. perform work in a manner which minimizes environmental damage
PC9. all procedures and work instructions for controlling risk are followed closely
PC10. report any accidents, incidents or problems without delay to an appropriate person and take
immediate necessary action to reduce further danger
Render appropriate emergency procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. follow procedures for dealing with accidents, fires and emergencies, including
communicating location and directions to emergency.
PC12. follow emergency procedures as per company standards and workplace requirements
