#### IAC/ASC/Q0101: Junior Tyre Specialist
Brief Job Description
This qualification pack is designed to equip personnel with the necessary knowledge and skills to excel in
the Tyre Management Industry. This program covers topics such as safe working practices, tyre usage,
maintenance, and repair, vehicle driving dynamics related to tyres, and basic problem-solving techniques.
Personnel will also receive training on Lean principles, productivity improvement, supervisor training, and
soft skills such as team building, task management, and leadership. The program aims to provide
personnel with the technical and soft skills required to excel as Jr. Technical Supervisors/Service Engineers
in the tyre management industry.
Personal Attributes
Perform detailed tyre inspection including tyre pressure, wear and tear, etc. Create an inspection sheet for
tyres. Inspect the wheel system, suspension system, and braking system of a motor vehicle.

#### IAC/ASC/N0112: Introduction to Industry and Job Role & Automotive
Fundamental
Description
Introduction to Industry and Job Role & Automotive Fundamental
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the history and evolution of Tyre Management Industries, including the services offered,
their origin, and current market position

offered, their origin, and current market position

- PC7. Select and apply appropriate tools and measuring equipment.
- PC8. Apply knowledge of petrol and diesel engine operation.

- KU3. Understanding of Safe working practices
- KU4. Understanding the environmental and sustainability best practice

#### IAC/ASC/N0103: Tyre basics
Description
Tyre basics
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the importance of tyre usage in terms of load, shock absorption, and vehicle handling.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand the importance of tyre usage in terms of load, shock absorption, and vehicle
handling.
- PC2. Describe the key features of tyre specifications and how they impact tyre performance.
- PC3. Recognize and address issues related to low NSD, hub mismatch, and flat tyres.
- PC4. Understand the impact of tyre wear on driving force and braking power.
- PC5. Explain the relationship between tyre usage and steering wheel movement.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Introduction to Tyre usage load, Shock absorption, vehicle handling driving force and braking
power, steering wheel movement.
- KU2. Introduction to Tyre Specification.
- KU3. Introduction to Low NSD, Hub Mismatch, Flat tyres.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Tyre usage load, Shock absorption, vehicle handling driving force and braking power, steering
wheel movement.
- GS2. Understanding of Tyre Specification.
- GS3. Demonstration/ practical on Low NSD, Hub Mismatch, Flat tyres.

#### IAC/ASC/N0106: Business Growth
Description
Business Growth
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Principles of Lean and Toyota Production System: Introduction to Lean methodology and Toyota
Production System for process improvement and waste reduction.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Principles of Lean and Toyota Production System: Introduction to Lean methodology and
Toyota Production System for process improvement and waste reduction.
- PC2. Basic Problem-Solving Techniques: Knowledge and application of 7 QC (quality control) and 7
MP (management and planning) tools for continuous improvement.
- PC3. Improving Productivity: Introduction to Standard Work, MOST (Maynard Operation Sequence
Technique), and OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) for productivity improvement.
- PC4. Supervisor training: Training on vendor development, warehouse management, and supply
chain management for effective supervision and leadership.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Principles of Lean and Toyota Production System
- KU2. Concept of Muda, Mura, Muri & Managing Flow
- KU3. Introduction to Value Stream Thinking
- KU4. Understanding Flow and Process Kaizens
