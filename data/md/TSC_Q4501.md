#### TSC/Q4501: Entrepreneur  - Textile Waste Management & Recycling
Brief Job Description
An Entrepreneur - Textile Waste Management & Recycling is responsible to focus on the kay areas of the
textile waste recycling business including business development, operations, compliance, financial
management, marketing, innovation, and team leadership, ensuring that the candidate is well-prepared to
lead a successful and sustainable recycling operation.
Personal Attributes
An Entrepreneur - Textile Waste Management & Recycling should have physicals skills like dexterity,
Ability to work long hours, strength, maintain high energy levels to oversee operations, manage teams,
and handle multiple tasks. An Entrepreneur - Textile Waste Management & Recycling should also have
ability to work in diverse environments, including outdoor sites, warehouses, and recycling facilities, which
may have varying temperatures, noise levels, and other physical conditions
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. TSC/N4502: Gather business overview, market analysis of textile waste recycling operation
2. TSC/N4503: Planning shredding operation in textile waste recycling
3. TSC/N4504: Explore various circular applications of shredded textile waste
4. TSC/N4501: Adhere to legalities, models and strategies in textile waste recycling
5. TSC/N7802: Coordinate with team for finance and administrative activities
6. TSC/N7805: Follow organizational health, safety and security for self and others in the MSME
textile sector
7. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### TSC/N4502: Gather business overview, market analysis of textile waste
recycling operation
Description
This NOS unit about performance criteria, knowledge and understanding and generic skills required for
executing necessary techniques to gather business overview, market analysis of textile waste recycling
operation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Research and collate Textile recycling business-related market data
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Research and collate textile recycling business-related market data
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Assess the current market size for textile recycling and projected growth rate over the next
- PC5. 10 years
- PC2. Identify and analyze different market segments such as by material type (cotton, polyester,
etc.), recycling process (mechanical, chemical), and end-use applications (apparel, home furnishings, industrial materials).
- PC3. Study the textile recycling ecosystem, including key players, their market share, strengths,
and weaknesses
- PC4. Analyze consumer attitudes towards recycled textiles, demand for sustainable products, and
purchasing behaviors
- PC5. Analyse the various business model (e.g., B2B, B2C, circular economy) and articulate a clear
value proposition
- PC6. Identify target markets and customer segments
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. overview of the textile industry and waste generation
- KU2. importance of recycling in the textile sector
- KU3. types of textile waste such as pre-consumer, post-consumer
- KU4. sources and composition of textile wastes
- KU5. various recycling process such as mechanical (shredding, carding, spinning) and chemical recycling process (solvent-based, thermal)
- KU6. contaminated or non-recyclable textiles
- KU7. processes involved in recycling different textiles
- KU8. compliance requirements for handling and disposing of textile waste

#### TSC/N4503: Planning shredding operation in textile waste recycling
Description
This NOS unit about performance criteria, knowledge and understanding and generic skills required for
executing necessary techniques to for planning a shredding operation in textile waste recycling.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Textile waste shredding - Operational Planning
Quality Control and Output Handling
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Textile waste shredding - Operational Planning
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify suitable location for shredding operation, considering proximity to waste sources and
markets
- PC2. Plan the layout of your facility to optimize workflow, including areas for sorting, shredding,
storage, and offices
- PC3. Obtain the necessary permits and approvals for the textile waste shredding facility
- PC4. Choose the appropriate shredding machinery and allied equipment based on the types and
volumes of textile waste
- PC5. Recruit skilled and unskilled workers for various roles in the shredding operation
- PC6. Establish a system for collecting textile waste from various sources including transport
- PC7. Set up sorting processes to separate different types of textiles and remove contaminants
- PC8. Develop a shredding schedule and operating procedures to ensure efficient processing
Quality Control and Output Handling
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Implement quality control measures to ensure the textile shredded materials meet the
required standards
- PC10. Incorporate sustainable practices in the shredding operation, such as energy-efficient
equipment and waste reduction strategies
- PC11. Monitor and report the environmental impact of the shredding operation, including waste
reduction and resource conservation
- PC12. Implement inventory management systems to track incoming waste and outgoing products
- PC13. Identify potential buyers and applications for shredded textile waste
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. different shredding machines and technologies used in textile recycling

#### TSC/N4504: Explore various circular applications of shredded textile
waste
Description
This NOS unit about performance criteria, knowledge and understanding and generic skills required for
executing necessary techniques to explore various circular applications of shredded textile waste.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Circularity applications of textile waste
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Circularity applications of textile shredding waste
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explore various products made from textile waste such as Recycled fibre, Recycled fiber yarn,
woven and knitted fabrics, Recycled non-woven fabrics, Recycled fiber application in technical textiles
- PC2. Expertise in the production process flow of recyled materials such as fibre, yarn, fabric, non-
woven materials, etc.
- PC3. Evaluate the cost of processing shredded textile waste versus the value of the end products
- PC4. Assess the potential revenue and profit margins from each application
- PC5. Assess the feasibility of processing shredded materials using available technologies and
equipment
- PC6. Analyze market trends and demand for products made from recycled materials
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. various textile recycling process methodologies
- KU2. various applications of the recycled materials such as insulation/ fillers, Composite materials,
Absorbant products, etc.
- KU3. properties of the shredded textile waste
- KU4. various certifications such as ISO, sustainability certifications, etc.
- KU5. sustainable production processes for natural and man made fibers
- KU6. non conventional fibers and non textiles from renewable resources, and their applications
- KU7. sustainable production processes of recycled fibers, fiber yarns, woven, non woven, knitted
fabrics
- KU8. properties and advantages and of recycled fibers, fiber yarn, woven, knitted and non woven
fabrics

#### TSC/N4501: Adhere  to legalities, models and strategies in textile waste
recycling
Description
This NOS unit about performance criteria, knowledge and understanding and generic skills required for
executing necessary techniques to adhere to legalities, models and strategies in textile waste recycling
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Follow legal frameworks in textile waste recycling
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow legal frameworks in textile waste recycling
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Comply with national and local laws on waste management, including the handling,
transportation, and disposal of textile waste
- PC2. Adhere to regulations on emissions, effluents, and waste discharge to prevent environmental
pollution
- PC3. Follow regulations on the safe handling and disposal of chemicals used in textile processing
and recycling
- PC4. Obtain relevant certifications to validate the sustainability and safety of recycled materials
such as OEKO-TEX Standard
- PC5. Follow the business model according to the requirements such as collection and sorting,
mechanical/ chemical recycling, Upcycle, Reuse, etc.
- PC6. Adopt advanced recycling technologies to improve efficiency, quality, and sustainability
- PC7. Analyze competitors to identify gaps and opportunities in the market
- PC8. Establish reliable sources of textile waste to ensure a steady supply for recycling
- PC9. Educate consumers about the benefits of recycling and encourage responsible disposal of
textile products
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. details of national and local regulations governing the collection, transportation, and disposal
of textile waste
- KU2. details of regulations related to air, water, and soil pollution, and how recycling operations can
comply with these standards
- KU3. awareness of EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) schemes that hold producers accountable for the end-of-life management of their products

- KU5. safe handling, storage, and disposal protocols for chemicals used in textile recycling

- KU7. strategies for sourcing textile waste from households, businesses, and industrial entities

#### TSC/N7802: Coordinate with team for finance and administrative
activities
Description
This OS unit is about developing and using the necessary skills, knowledge and attributes for managing
finance and personnel in the MSME textile sector.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Build a team for marketing, administration and operations
Manage financial and administrative activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Build a team for marketing, administration and operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. build a strategy to recruit people for the business
- PC2. analyse different hiring and marketing strategies to short-list the most feasible and cost-
effective ones such as posters, newspaper, online, consultants, references, campus placement, etc.

requirement using different channels short-listed

for the required roles in the organization

by tracking key performance parameters
- PC6. follow necessary procedures to ensure timely transfer of salaries and payments to the
employees and business associates
- PC7. calculate and issue monetary and non-monetary incentives to motivate the employees based
on performance
Manage financial and administrative activities

- PC8. organize and safely store record files of members/employees, bills, office documents in
appropriate places
- PC9. maintain and update the attendance of employees /members in a designated register

financial schemes offered by various financial agencies such as nationalized banks, SIDBI, etc. select the schemes suitable for the business interests of the organization

bank officer
