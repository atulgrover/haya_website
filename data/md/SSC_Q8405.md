SSC/Q8405: Mobile Device Management Analyst
Brief Job Description
The qualification in Mobile Device Management focuses on developing and implementing effective
strategies for managing mobile devices within an organization. It encompasses the configuration and
maintenance of compliance policies, remote installation and management of business applications, and
proactive monitoring to secure devices against potential threats. Additionally, this qualification includes
integrating Mobile Device Management systems with existing enterprise systems to ensure seamless
operations and data security. This role is vital in today's digital landscape, where mobile technology is
integral to business success.
Personal Attributes
Individuals in this role must manage and collaborate with various stakeholders involved in the process of
managing mobile devices in the organization. They must lead team members and possess good
communication skills to achieve project success.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. SSC/N8456: Develop the organization's mobile device management strategy
- 2. SSC/N8431: Manage and maintain mobile device configuration, compliance policies in the mobility
solutions of the organisation
- 3. SSC/N8432: Install, configure and manage business applications remotely on mobile devices
- 4. SSC/N8433: Monitor and secure mobile devices in case of security threats
- 5. SSC/N8434: Integrate Mobile Device Management systems with the existing enterprise systems
- 6. SSC/N8457: Strategies device integrity and secure protocols for better consumer experience
- 7. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

SSC/N8456: Develop the organization's mobile device management
strategy
Description
Develop the organization's mobile device management strategy
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develop the organization's mobile device management strategy
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. analyze the organization's work environment, business objectives, obstacles, and needs
PC2. determine the appropriate control measures for mobile devices to establish a suitable device
policy, such as BYOD (Bring Your Own Device), CYOD (Choose Your Own Device), strict kiosk
lockdown mode, COBO (Corporate-Owned Business Only), or COPE (Corporate-Owned,
Personally-Enabled)
PC3. implement MDM solutions to manage and secure a broad range of devices, including
wearables, laptops, and IoT devices, ensuring effective oversight of a diverse ecosystem of
endpoints
PC4. establish criteria for choosing a suitable mobile device management solution for the
organization
PC5. select and deploy an appropriate mobile device management solution within the organization
PC6. implement MDM solutions that effectively balance employee convenience with corporate
security in response to the increasing adoption of Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) and Choose
Your Own Device (CYOD) models
PC7. establish and outline a thorough and adaptable operational strategy for enterprise mobile
devices
PC8. design, develop, and integrate business applications that are fully compatible with Windows-
based mobile devices, focusing on performance, security, and usability
PC9. develop and execute a thorough privacy and security plan utilizing Mobile Device
Management (MDM)
PC10. implement cloud-based MDM solutions that provide scalability and flexibility to support
expanding device fleets and evolving business requirements
PC11. utilize cloud-based platforms with user-friendly interfaces and automated workflows to
minimize the administrative workload on IT teams
PC12. Integrate user data from enterprise systems into the MDM interface to enable seamless user
experiences and provide personalized access to applications and resources based on user
roles, preferences, and historical behaviors
PC13. formulate a strategy for an expense policy regarding corporate-owned devices

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. principles of mobile device management (MDM) and its role in enterprise IT infrastructure
KU2. various mobile device ownership models such as BYOD, CYOD, COBO, COPE, and their

KU3. features, capabilities, and limitations of cloud-based and on-premises MDM solutions.
KU4. criteria and parameters for evaluating and selecting suitable MDM solutions for different
organizational needs.
KU5. privacy, security, and compliance regulations applicable to mobile device usage in enterprise
environments.
KU6. integration of MDM platforms with enterprise systems and user directories for seamless
authentication and role-based access
KU7. considerations for designing and deploying business applications optimized for Windows-
based mobile platforms.
KU8. cost factors, budgeting principles, and expense management strategies associated with
corporate-owned mobile devices
KU9. key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics to measure the effectiveness of MDM
implementation
KU10. latest trends in device ecosystems, including wearables and IoT, and how they affect mobile
device strategy.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. communicate technical information effectively to both IT and non-IT stakeholders
GS2. collaborate with cross-functional teams to align mobile device strategies with business
objectives
GS3. critically analyze organizational needs and translate them into practical MDM policies
GS4. prioritize tasks and make informed decisions under time and resource constraints.
GS5. adapt strategies in response to evolving technologies and organizational dynamics.

SSC/N8431: Manage and maintain mobile device configuration,
compliance policies in the mobility solutions of the organisation
Description
This unit is about managing and maintaining configurations and policies for different mobile devices in the
organisation
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Device configuration and management
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the devices in the organization to be monitored using mobile device management
solutions
PC2. register the devices to monitored in the organization's network and on the mobile device
management solutions
PC3. implement a zero-trust approach by ensuring that devices and users undergo continuous
authentication and authorization without default trust
PC4. set up various policies around devices to secure corporate resources
PC5. implement MDM solutions with enhanced data encryption, loss prevention, and remote wipe
features to protect sensitive corporate information
PC6. implement initial device configurations in compliance with the corporate rules
PC7. implement fingerprint and facial recognition features to enhance secure access to devices
PC8. integrate Mobile Device Management (MDM) with other endpoint management tools,
including PC management and endpoint security solutions
PC9. perform timely installation of patches and updates of solution software and firmware
PC10. regularly scan devices to ensure they are compliant with company policy
PC11. integrate MDM solutions with productivity suites such as Microsoft 365 to enhance the user
experience
PC12. generate periodic reports on the health and security of the mobile devices
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. fundamentals of mobile device management (MDM) architecture and its role in enterprise IT
operations
KU2. principles and components of a zero-trust security model and its application to device and
user authentication

SSC/N8432: Install, configure and manage business applications remotely
on mobile devices
Description
This unit is about installing, configuring and managing business application on the mobile devices of the
organisation remotely
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Application configuration and management
Elements and Performance Criteria
Application configuration and management
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the target devices for installation of business application
PC2. initialize files and tools required for over the air installation and update of applications on the
registered mobile devices
PC3. perform over-the-air installation and update of applications on the registered mobile devices
PC4. perform encryption of files to secure over the air installation and update process
PC5. distribute files to intended target devices
PC6. implement automation for the integration of new devices into the MDM platform
PC7. Install, configure, and update business applications on Windows-based mobile devices to
meet organizational needs and requirements
PC8. employ AI technologies to diagnose and resolve device and application issues efficiently
PC9. utilize AI to predict potential issues and take proactive measures to resolve them
PC10. perform post-update activities such as activating updated code
PC11. follow the requirements of the client to install and configure business applications remotely
on Andorid or iOS devices
PC12. track and resolve issues related to Android or iOS device management and business
application usage
PC13. ensure business applications are compatible with the latest iOS or Android versions and
update them as necessary
PC14. verify that the system is functioning as per specifications
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. process and protocols for remote and over-the-air (OTA) installation and updating of mobile
business applications.
