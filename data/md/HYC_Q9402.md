HYC/Q9402: Executive HR - Oil & Gas
Brief Job Description
The HR Executive maintains employee data for the Human Resource (HR) department and complies all
necessary payroll data, enters it into the organization data system , calculates each employee respective
pay , computing statutory and tax related entitlements and deductions and coordinates with the accounts
department for disbursements and fund transfers.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have good sense of responsibility, a good organizer and time keeper. The person
should have the ability to work in a team and maintain excellent interpersonal relationships.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HYC/N9404: Carry out hiring and recruitment process
- 2. HYC/N9405: Maintain employee records for compensation and benefits calculations.

- 4. HYC/N9301: Working effectively in a team

HYC/N9404: Carry out hiring and recruitment process
Description
This unit is about perform hiring and recruitment process in oil and gas industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform hiring and recruitment process
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform hiring and recruitment process
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Assist in job preparation
PC2. Assist in publishing advertisement in national & local platform
PC3. Assist in searching application of candidates from various source
PC4. Coordinate in sorting of application of candidates
PC5. Send call letters to the shortlisted candidates
PC6. Coordinate for interview exercise
PC7. Send offer letters to finalized candidates
PC8. Assist in joining formalities of candidates
PC9. Collect documents from candidates
PC10. Conduct induction program and training session for candidate
PC11. Inform to the reporting officer about new employee.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the basics of office management
KU2. the importance of reporting and systematic working
KU3. standard operating procedures of organizations while working
KU4. relevant health and safety requirements applicable to the Oil & Gas industry
KU5. the importance of personal protective equipment used in OMCs
KU6. ways of efficiently managing the resources required
KU7. the difference between recyclable/reusable and waste material
KU8. the concept of Mission Karmayogi
KU9. the concept of Swachhata Hi Seva

HYC/N9405: Maintain employee records for compensation and benefits
calculations.
Description
This unit is about maintaining employee records for compensation and benefits calculations
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain employee records
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain employee records
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. prepare records of new employees (Record of new employees: e.g. employee name, ID, team
details, compensation details, date of birth, address, etc.)
PC2. update records of existing employees to reflect changes (Changes: new designation, change
in pay band, salary structure, department, etc.)
PC3. Maintain attendance record
PC4. update leave details of employees into the database.
PC5. calculate monetary impact of leave without pay and overtime details of employees and
update records accordingly
PC6. Maintain performance records
PC7. Maintain conflict and grievance record
PC8. record and update the resignation details of the employees
PC9. submit employee status to various departments as per organizational procedure
PC10. calculate salary and benefits for full and final settlement
PC11. prepare full and final settlement letter and forward to relevant authority for approval
PC12. Calculate tax, PF, ESI etc. as per statutory norms
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the basics of office management
KU2. the importance of reporting and systematic working
KU3. standard operating procedures of organizations while working
KU4. relevant health and safety requirements applicable to the Oil & Gas industry
KU5. the importance of personal protective equipment used in OMCs
KU6. ways of efficiently managing the resources required

HYC/N9406: Process statutory entitlements for finalizing compensation
and benefits.
Description
This unit is about processing statutory entitlements for finalizing compensation and benefits.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Process statutory entitlements
Elements and Performance Criteria
Process statutory entitlements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. issue the correct regulatory documentation where entitlement to statutory payments is
applicable/not applicable or cease
PC2. determine staff’s entitlement to statutory payments and benefits. (Statutory payments:
Provident Fund (PF), Employees State Insurance (ESI), Professional Tax etc.)
PC3. calculate exceptional payments and deductions in accordance with organizational
requirements, within agreed timelines
PC4. apply relevant PF deduction (PF deduction: Employees contribution, employers’ contribution,
minimum and maximum PF deduction allowed, government website through which payment
is to be made, eligibility criteria etc.)
PC5. apply relevant processes regarding ESI deduction (Processes regarding ESI deduction:
Employees contribution, employers’ contribution, minimum and maximum ESI deduction
allowed, government website through which payment is to be made, applicability of ESI
provisions to employees with salary as limited by present rules etc.)
PC6. enter the amount due in respect of redundancy in the payroll system, to ensure timely
payments and appropriate tax treatment
PC7. process PF nomination, PF-withdrawal and PF-transfer documents
PC8. Calculate various salary components as per tax and non-tax components of salary package
PC9. Prepare payroll of the employee
PC10. Maintain MIS report using computer
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the work ethic at workplace
KU2. the importance of confidential information
KU3. standard operating procedures while working in Oil & Gas industry
KU4. Legal and ethical obligations related to confidentiality

HYC/N9302: Maintain health, safety and security procedures
Description
This unit covers maintaining health, safety and security procedures at the workplace. This includes
procedures and practices that candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work
environment
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Practice health and safety measures
Follow fire safety procedures
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow health and safety measures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. use protective clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions
PC2. identify documents, location and people responsible for health and safety in the workplace
PC3. identify possible causes of risk or accident in the workplace
PC4. carry out safe working practices while dealing with hazards to ensure the safety of self and
others
PC5. lift heavy objects safely using correct procedures
PC6. identify common safety signs, displayed in various areas
Follow fire safety procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. use the various appropriate fire extinguishers on different types of fires correctly
PC8. follow rescue techniques applied during fire hazard
PC9. follow good housekeeping practice in order to prevent fire hazards
PC10. list issues concerning the safety in work place
PC11. inform fire safety department about any near-miss incidents in the work place
PC12. follow the applicable laws, regulations and codes as per safety standard
PC13. prepare written accident/incident report and share with the concerned officer/department
