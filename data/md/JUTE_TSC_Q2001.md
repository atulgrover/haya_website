#### JUTE/TSC/Q2001: Junior Quality Control Technician (Jute Production)
Brief Job Description
Quality Control Technician (Jute Production) should be able to carry out Quality control/Quality assurance
processes, understanding product and processes required at various stages of production for meeting the
expectations of final customers.
Personal Attributes
A Quality Assessor carries out quality assurance /quality control procedures in the sampling, pre-
production, during production, and post production stages, reports deviations and prevents quality failures.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
2. JUTE/TSC/N1006: Maintain health, safety and security at work place (L4)
3. JUTE/TSC/N2001: Jute Retting, Selection and Batching
4. JUTE/TSC/N2002: Understanding of product specification, the process & procedure
5. JUTE/TSC/N2004: Testing and Quality Control in Jute Fabric Manufacturing Process
6. JUTE/TSC/N2005: Quality Assurance in spinning and weaving section
7. JUTE/TSC/N2003: Testing and Quality Control in Jute Yarn Manufacturing Processes

#### JUTE/TSC/N2001: Jute Retting, Selection and Batching
Description
Gain knowledge on Quality of Retting of Jute and Grading of Jute fibre
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding of Jute bale quality wise sorting, Identification of defects, moisture compliances, BIS
Gradation / hand & eye gradation system & instrument grading system
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding of Jute bale quality wise sorting, Identification of defects, moisture compliances, BIS
Gradation / hand & eye gradation system & instrument grading system
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Knowledge Conventional retting Process
- PC2. Identify need for improved and efficient method of retting
- PC3. Demonstrate factors affecting the retting process
- PC4. Upgrade of low grade jute due to retting of jute
- PC5. Demonstrate of improved retting with microbial formulation Quality improvement/ piling
technique
- PC6. Knowledge on Dos and Don’ts of fibre upgradation in shop floor
- PC7. Understand Gradation of Jute fibre – BIS std IS271:2020
- PC8. Identify the approximate root portion (approximate weight %) in bundles of raw jute strand
(Reed) in morah
- PC9. Carry out proper cleaning and opening each bundle for proper selection and grading
- PC10. Carry out selection of raw jute bundles in proper manner giving due importance to each
quality parameter and defects etc.
- PC11. Carry out by hand and eye method the following quality parameters of raw jute fibres during
selection, Strength, Root content, Defects, Colour, Fineness, Bulk-Density
- PC12. Carry out that after proper selection of jute bundle, each morah (approx. 1500 ± 200 gms) is
half twisted and folded at the middle before stacking for further processing to keep grade- wise
- PC13. Carry out raw jute after selection is to be kept in dry and clean conditions
- PC14. Carry out that barrows with selected morahs be marked with proper identification mark, to
avoid mixing up of one quality of jute fibre morahs with other quality morahs
- PC15. Identify the Jute bales of a particular grade as required for processing of a specific yarn
count, has to be reported to the next senior. Up-gradation or down-gradation of jute to be noted
- PC16. Carry out up-gradation and down-gradation the jute bundle has been separately stacked with
identification mark

#### JUTE/TSC/N2002: Understanding  of product specification, the process &
procedure
Description
Comply with industry, regulatory and organizational requirements
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Explain various quality wise machine and process parameters
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Explain various quality used in Jute mill like Sacking Warp, Sacking Weft, Hessian warp and weft, export
quality yarn value added other product

- PC1. Prepare flow chart and batch composition for Sacking Warp quality
- PC2. Prepare flow chart and batch composition for Sacking Weft quality
- PC3. Prepare flow chart and batch composition for Hessian Warp & Weft batch quality
- PC4. Prepare flow chart and batch composition for S4 A Warp & Weft batch quality
- PC5. Prepare flow chart and batch composition for Export Sale yarn quality
- PC6. Prepare batch composition for Type ‘A’ and Type ‘B’ - B. Twill jute bags
- PC7. • Carry out frequent checking morah weight for compliance
• Ensure quality management of delivered sliver of spreader and softener and other jute spinning
machineries

Defects in Emulsions
- PC9. Carry out Pile Management, piling methods, pile duration, morah size etc.
- PC10. Calculate Formulation and Mixing of Emulsions with Spreader Production calculations
- PC11. Knowledge of Breaker/Inter / Finisher cards & their basic features and principles of
operations
- PC12. Explain basic idea of lead, draft, draft constant, Draft Change pinion, doubling and its

- PC13. Objectives of carding, drawing, spinning, Principles of Drafting, Twisting & Winding
- PC14. Explain yarn count and twist. Idea of twist constant
- PC15. Explain to assure yarn end breakage rate as per industry standard, Doffing time & release of
full/half/empty bobbin if segregate
- PC16. Explain Sizing- purpose, importance and quality of size mixture. Sizing/beaming machine and

| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
