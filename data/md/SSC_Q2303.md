SSC/Q2303: Specialist Transactional F&A
Brief Job Description
A Specialist Transactional F&A plays a critical role in managing and executing financial transactions within
an organization, ensuring accuracy, compliance, and timely processing of financial documents. An
individual in this role is responsible for maintaining customer accounts, generating and processing invoices
and credit notes, handling payments and claims, and resolving financial queries related to accounts
payable and receivable. The individual also ensures the secure and confidential handling of customer
financial data, upholding data privacy standards.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to follow detailed instructions and procedures with an eye for detail. The
individual should be analytical and result oriented and should demonstrate logical thinking.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. SSC/N2305: Maintain customer accounts
- 2. SSC/N2306: Generate invoices and credit notes

- 8. SSC/N2321: Maintain Data Security and Privacy of customer accounts

SSC/N2305: Maintain customer accounts
Description
This unit is about setting up customer accounts and keeping them up-to-date, as part of an outsourced
finance and accounting process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Check appropriate sources like contracts, supporting documentation, service level agreements
(SLAs), sales team, customers, client, account manager clients, etc.
Perform credit checks using internal sources and external agencies
Interact with appropriate people like line manager, peers, team taxation specialists, etc.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check master database to verify whether records of customers already exist
PC2. ensure accurate setup of customer accounts in the financial system
PC3. utilize financial tools effectively to analyze, track, and report financial data
PC4. update customer account information regularly, including contact details
PC5. record credit terms and limits for each customer as per agreements
PC6. monitor customer account balances and aging reports
PC7. add relevant charts, graphs, and tables where necessary to visually enhance the document
PC8. apply payments received to corresponding customer invoices
PC9. identify and resolve account discrepancies or payment mismatches
PC10. verify customer credit status before processing orders
PC11. use AI-based platforms to ensure compliance with F&A policies, including tax, audit, and
regulatory requirements
PC12. process adjustments for any disputed or incorrect charges
PC13. generate monthly statements for customer accounts
PC14. ensure accurate preparation and review of the balance sheet by recording all financial
transactions correctly and reconciling assets, liabilities, and equity as per accounting
standards
PC15. use AI-driven tools for automating bank reconciliations and the generation of financial
reports
PC16. analyze patterns in transactional data to flag potential fraudulent activities using AI
algorithms
PC17. communicate with supervisors to confirm that shared documents align with organizational
requirements

SSC/N2306: Generate invoices and credit notes
Description
This unit is about generating invoices and credit notes to send to customers, as part of an outsourced
finance and accounting process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Process invoices and claims
Interact with appropriate people like line manager, peers, sales team, etc.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. interpret the research requirements and objectives to ensure relevance and accuracy in
analysis
PC2. obtain requests to generate invoices, credit notes and other supporting documentation from
relevant departments
PC3. use AI-driven tools to automatically capture, validate, and categorize invoices
PC4. verify customer details and account status before generating invoices and credit notes
PC5. review the terms of service or product delivery to determine invoice accuracy
PC6. calculate applicable taxes, discounts, and other charges
PC7. generate invoices as per agreed-upon pricing and terms
PC8. review invoices for completeness and accuracy before dispatch
PC9. send invoices/credit notes to department heads for signature, if required
PC10. issue credit notes for returned goods, service errors, or billing adjustments, etc.
PC11. confirm authorization of credit notes with relevant departments
PC12. ensure timely generation and dispatch of invoices to customers
PC13. ensure timely generation and dispatch of invoices to customers
PC14. ensure compliance with accounting standards in invoice generation
PC15. reconcile monthly totals for invoices and credit notes issued
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. organizations invoicing policies, billing cycles, and approval workflows.
KU2. components of a valid invoice or credit note, including applicable tax structures, discounts,
and other charges.

SSC/N2302: Process invoices, credit notes and claims
Description
This unit is about processing invoices and credit notes from vendors and travel and expenses claims from
personnel/ employees, as part of an outsourced finance and accounting process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Interact with people like line managers, software developers, subject matter experts, etc.
Analyze codes related to cost centre, accounting, tax codes, etc.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. verify invoice details with purchase orders and goods received notes and assign appropriate
codes
PC2. ensure accuracy of invoice information, including vendor details, invoice number, and date
PC3. extract and validate information from invoices and receipts automatically using AI tools
PC4. check for applicable taxes, discounts, and rebates as per company policy
PC5. flag and resolve discrepancies between invoices and purchase orders
PC6. utilize advanced tools like pivot tables to prepare forecasts, scenario analyses, and budget
plans that align with organizational goals
PC7. prepare credit notes for returned goods or services as per sop
PC8. prepare credit notes for returned goods or services as per sop
PC9. validate claims documentation against contractual terms
PC10. classify and prioritize invoices, credit notes, and claims for processing
PC11. enter invoice data into accounting or ERP software accurately
PC12. route invoices, credit notes, and claims for approvals as per workflow
PC13. utilize software like Excel, SAP, or Tally for data processing and analysis
PC14. track invoice status and report discrepancies for timely resolution
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. standard operating procedures (SOPs) and company policies for processing invoices, credit
notes, and claims.
KU2. relationship between purchase orders, goods received notes, and invoices for accurate
verification and coding
KU3. tax structures, discount policies, and rebate mechanisms applicable to vendor transactions.

Maintain Data Security and Privacy of customer accounts
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain Data Security and Privacy of customer accounts
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. develop and adhere to organizational data security policies, ensuring compliance with
regulations like GDPR, CCPA, or equivalent standards
PC2. implement and regularly review role-based access control (RBAC) to safeguard sensitive data
PC3. ensure appropriate authorization levels for employees and revoke access when necessary
PC4. identify and classify PII within organizational systems and apply necessary protections (e.g.,
encryption, anonymization)
PC5. verify and process data access, modification, or deletion requests in compliance with
applicable data protection laws
PC6. securely handle financial transactions, ensuring that payment and customer account data
are encrypted and protected against fraud
PC7. implement an incident response plan to address data breaches or cyberattacks promptly
PC8. conduct root-cause analysis of incidents and ensure lessons learned are incorporated into
improved processes
PC9. periodically audit organizational data handling practices to ensure compliance with data
protection standards
PC10. apply restrictions on data sharing with third parties, ensuring adherence to contractual
agreements and legal standards
PC11. establish automated and secure data backup processes to protect against data loss
PC12. test and refine recovery procedures to minimize downtime and ensure continuity after an
incident
PC13. maintain a helpdesk for responding to internal and external queries on data security and
privacy
PC14. regularly review policies to address evolving ethical and legal considerations in data handling
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. legal and regulatory requirements related to data protection and privacy such as GDPR, CCPA,
and relevant national standards
