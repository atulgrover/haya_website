WBSC/FFS/Q0201: Wooden Furniture Maker
Brief Job Description
Wooden Furniture Maker is responsible for producing, designing, crafting, and testing wooden furniture
products such as cabinets, table, chair, door, windows, and other furniture using specialized tools.
Personal Attributes
A Wooden Furniture Maker should possess strong attention to detail, creativity and a deep appreciation for
craftsmanship to produce high-quality furniture. Patience, precision, physical stamina and manual
dexterity are essential personal attributes. A good furniture maker is also problem-solving oriented, able to
interpret designs, adapt to challenges and make adjustments during production.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. WBSC/FFS/N0201: Apply Safe Working Practices (Wooden Furniture Maker).
- 2. WBSC/FFS/N0202: Identify timber/ wood & apply measuring, marking and testing instrument and
holding & supporting hand tools.
- 3. WBSC/FFS/N0203: Identify and apply various saws and portable power saw machines for ripping,
cross cutting, oblique sawing and curve cutting etc.
- 4. WBSC/FFS/N0204: Analyze the surface finish with exact sizing by planning operation, identify and
apply various shaving tools, pairing tools or portable power planning machine.
- 5. WBSC/FFS/N0205: Identify and classify various types of joints, analyze and prepare correct joint at
correct position, related with strength and appearance.
- 6. WBSC/FFS/N0206: Demonstrate ripping, cross cutting,curve cutting etc. on jig or scroll saw and
circular saw machine.
- 7. WBSC/FFS/N0207: Demonstrate working on pedestal/potable drilling machine, wood turning lathe
in correct location on woodwork.
- 8. WBSC/FFS/N0208: Prepare various type of wooden floor, partition wall, roof truss, door and
windows frame and shutters, assembling &fixing (wooden/ aluminium or PVC).
- 9. WBSC/FFS/N0209: Create a wooden piece following a provided drawing and using specified timber
/plywood /sunmica/block board etc.
- 10. WBSC/FFS/N0210: Work in real job situation with special emphasis on basic safety and hazards in
this domain (Wooden Furniture Maker).

Description
Apply Safe Working Practices
Scope

Procedures to achieve a safe working environment.

Personal Productive Equipment (PPE).

site policy and procedures.

shoe and use the same as per related work.

procedures.

testing instrument and holding & supporting hand tools.
Description
Identify timber/ wood & apply measuring, marking and testing instrument and holding & supporting hand
tools.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Different types of wood/ timber.
The measuring, marking, work holding and testing instruments.
Marking as per drawing and measuring dimensions for checking.
Usage of testing instrument and other usable hand tools.
Identification of wooden sample piece.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify different types of wood/ timber.
PC2. Identify the measuring, marking, work holding and testing instrument.
PC3. Mark as per drawing and measure dimensions for checking.
PC4. Demonstrate use of testing instrument and other usable hand tools.
PC5. Identify wooden sample piece (Annual ring, knots, shakes & chicks etc.).
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Introduction of the trade.
KU2. General discipline, workshop discipline & Housekeeping.
KU3. Safety precaution in the workshop and industrial safety.
KU4. Introduction of timber, growth of timber trees, cross-section of exogenous tree trunk, types of
tree, different part of a tree, Soft & hard wood, their differences.
KU5. Identification and use of different types of the measuring, marking and testing tools & their
applications.
KU6. Identification and use of different types of work holding devices.

WBSC/FFS/N0203: Identify and apply various saws and portable power
saw machines for ripping, cross cutting, oblique sawing and curve cutting
etc.
Description
Identify and apply various saws and portable power saw machines for ripping, cross cutting, oblique
sawing and curve cutting etc.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Selection of material and visual inspection for defects.
Marking of the job as per drawing and check measurements before sawing.
Marking of an angle with the aid of bevel square and miter square for oblique sawing.
The required tools for desired operations and making of the job.
Ripping/cross, cutting/curve, sawing/ cutting operations.
Checking for dimensional accuracy.
Usage and practice of Portable power circular saw.
Different types of saw blade.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Select material and inspect visually for defects.
PC2. Mark the job as per drawing and check measurements before sawing.
PC3. Mark an angle with the aid of bevel square and miter square for oblique sawing.
PC4. Identify and arrange the required tools for desired operations and make the job.
PC5. Perform Ripping/cross, cutting/curve, sawing/ cutting operations according to the marking
following safety norms.
PC6. Check for dimensional accuracy.
PC7. Use and practice Portable power circular saw.
PC8. Sharpen and set different type saw blade.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Type of bench vice and their uses.
KU2. Introduction of different saw and their uses.
KU3. Introduction of power circular saw and its use.
KU4. Type of special saw and its uses i.e. - compass saw, coping saw, bow saw, fret saw.
KU5. Saw sharpening and sharpening tools.

WBSC/FFS/N0204: Analyze the surface finish with exact sizing by planning
operation, identify and apply various shaving tools, pairing tools or
portable power planning machine.
Description
Analyze the surface finish with exact sizing by planning operation, identify and apply various shaving tools,
pairing tools or portable power planning machine.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Selection of material and appropriate planner for required surface finish and size.
Setting of planner and performing required planning operation to obtain required size and finish.
Plane across the grain and end grain.
Checking the size, flatness, squareness and finish of the job as per drawing.
Removal, sharpening and fitting of planner blade.
Arranging of woods with vertical/ horizontal grains and required type of chisel for performing
operation (chiseling across the grain) as per drawing.
Marking of the work as per dimension of the drawing.
Performing of chiseling as per drawing and ensure better finish.
The usage of different types of chisel, chiseling, chiseling along& across the grain.
Checking of the finished job as per drawing.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Select material and appropriate planner for required surface finish and size.
PC2. Set planner with sharpened cutting iron and perform required planning operation to obtain
required size and finish.
PC3. Plane across the grain and end grain.
PC4. Check the size, flatness, squareness and finish of the job as per drawing.
PC5. Demonstrate removal, sharpening and fitting of planner blade observing standard operating
procedures.
PC6. Arrange woods with vertical/ horizontal grains and required type of chisel for performing
operation (chiseling across the grain) as per drawing.
PC7. Mark the work as per dimension of the drawing.
PC8. Perform chiseling as per drawing and ensure better finish.
PC9. Demonstrate the use of different types of chisel, chiseling, chiseling along& across the grain.
PC10. Check the finished job as per drawing.

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
