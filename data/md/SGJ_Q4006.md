#### SGJ/Q4006: Junior Technician - Mechanized Sewer Cleaning
Brief Job Description
Junior Technician- Mechanized Sewar Cleaning , is an individual, who is responsible for emptying, cleaning,
transport and disposal of sludge from the sewer line to a specified site/Co-treatment plant. He /She has to
operate and maintain Mechanized sewage cleaning machine, Sludge Vacuum tank with trucks, pumps,
suction hoses, and other machinery/equipment to empty the manholes.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to be responsible for Mechanized cleaning, transport and disposal of faecal
sludge from the septic tank/sewer line to desludging site / FSTP / Co-treatment plant. He /She has to
operate Sludge Vacuum tank with trucks, pumps, suction hoses, and other machinery/equipment to empty
the septic tank./sewer line. Jr. Technician -Sewer Mechanized cleaning must be active and capable of
safely handling the job role .
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
