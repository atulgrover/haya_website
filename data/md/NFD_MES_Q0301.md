#### NFD/MES/Q0301: Digital Video Editor
Brief Job Description
The Digital Video Editor edits raw digital video and audio footage and syncs soundtrack as per the
specifications of the director of the production to produce a film, documentary or video in line with the
script, genre, aesthetical guidelines and target audience preferences. The editor does this by using video
editing software.
Personal Attributes
A successful digital video editor is a masterful blend of tech whiz and artistic storyteller. They wield editing
software like a brush, crafting compelling narratives with pixel-perfect precision. Their critical eye hunts for
inconsistencies, ensuring polished visuals, while their keen ear sculpts audio to perfection. But beyond
technical prowess, they excel in collaboration, weaving feedback into their tapestry of creativity.
Adaptability fuels their passion, as they embrace new challenges and trends, staying ahead in the ever-
evolving world of video. In essence, a great editor is a problem-solver with an artist's soul, leaving their
mark on every frame they touch.

#### NFD/MES/N0301: Carry out footage Selection and assembly
Description
This NOS is designed to perform footage Selection and assembly
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Footage Selection and assembly
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Footage Selection and assembly
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Select the most relevant and impactful footage while adhering to project timelines and
budgets
- PC2. Assemble footage in a way that aligns with the video's narrative and objectives,
demonstrating strong pacing and flow.
- PC3. Identify potential issues or gaps in footage and utilizes creative solutions to overcome them.
- PC4. Maintain continuity and consistency throughout the edit, ensuring smooth transitions and
adherence to editing guidelines.
- PC5. manage large libraries of footage, prioritizes key shots, and maintains clear organization for
revisions.
- PC6. Collaborate with directors, producers, and other stakeholders to grasp the desired message,
target audience, and tone of the video.
- PC7. Analyze story flow, scene transitions, and shot requirements to plan editing strategies.
- PC8. Label, categorize, and store raw footage effectively for easy retrieval and access during
editing.
- PC9. Assemble preliminary versions of the video based on the script and creative vision, identifying
strong shots and potential editing points.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand non-linear editing (NLE) systems.
- KU2. Apply basic editing techniques (cuts, dissolves, wipes, etc.).
- KU3. Analyze and apply shot composition.
- KU4. Adapt editing for different platforms (YouTube, broadcast, etc.).
- KU5. Develop effective media management skills.
- KU6. Utilize collaboration tools for video editing projects.
- KU7. Understand storytelling fundamentals (narrative structure, character development, pacing,
conflict resolution, tone and mood).
