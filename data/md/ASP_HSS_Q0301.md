ASP/HSS/Q0301: Ayurveda Therapist
Brief Job Description
Ayurvedic Therapists use their expertise in lifestyle intervention and natural Ayurveda therapies to guide
clients on ways to prevent ailments and promote health, anti-aging and healing of body and soul. They
specialize in understanding and guiding on the basic Ayurveda principles, working of yoga, medication,
food and nutrition, rejuvenation therapies, Panchakarma, among others.
Personal Attributes
An Ayurveda therapist demonstrates empathy, patience, and a grounded presence, supporting clients
through personalized, holistic care. With a strong grasp of Ayurvedic principles and keen observation skills,
they tailor treatments and communicate complex practices effectively. Their commitment to a balanced
lifestyle reflects the wellness practices they promote, creating a nurturing environment for client well-
being.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. ASP/HSS/N0301: Understanding the History and basic principles of Ayurveda.
- 2. ASP/HSS/N0302: Familiarizing Sareera Vinjanam - Human Anatomy and physiology
- 3. ASP/HSS/N0303: Basic Understanding of Dravya Vinjanam - Ayurveda Pharmacology and
Pharmaceuticals
- 4. ASP/HSS/N0304: Panchakarma - Classical Ayurvedic Treatment procedures
- 5. ASP/HSS/N0305: Keraleeya Vishesha Chikitsa - Unique Ayurvedic treatment procedures in Kerala
- 6. ASP/HSS/N0306: Understanding the Basics of Yoga Sasthra and Physiotherapy
- 7. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

ASP/HSS/N0301: Understanding the History and basic principles of
Ayurveda.
Description
Understanding of the various Indian philosophical systems that have influenced Ayurveda. Develop a
comprehensive understanding of the core principles of Ayurveda, including the concepts of doshas
(biological humors), dhatus (tissues), malas (waste products), and prakriti (individual constitution)
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Comprehensive understanding of the philosophical foundations and historical development of
Ayurveda, the traditional system of medicine in India. Understanding of the treatment for Vata, Pitta
and Kapha imbalance along with the treatment of Dosh imbalance and Aasma.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Evaluating the basic understanding of philosophy and history of Ayurveda
PC2. Assessing the basic knowledge of Ayushkameeyam
PC3. Evaluating the knowledge of Dinacharya & Rithucharya
PC4. Evaluating the knowledge of vegas and thrayopathambhas
PC5. Assessing the basic Understanding of karmas and vruddhi kshaya lakshanas of Doshas,
Dhatus and Malas etc
PC6. Evaluate the understanding of Dosha sthanas, bhedas, karmas, Rogamargas, types of rogas,
nidana panchaka , dasavidha and Ashtasthaana pareeksha etc
PC7. Evaluate the knowledge of Tridosha Samanya chikitsa, Sakha gata dosha Chikitsa, Sudha
chikitsa, Amam etc
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The core principles of Ayurveda, including the concepts of doshas (biological humors), dhatus
(tissues), malas (waste products), and prakriti (individual constitution).
KU2. The Sareera (Body), Dosha, Dhatu, Mala , Agni , Prakriti, Dravya (Medicine), type of diseases,
types of treatment,etc.
KU3. Daily Regime to be followed which includes time to wake up from sleep, Sanitary measures,
Oral hygiene, Eye care, Nasyam, Gandoosham, Dhumapanam ,Thamboola Charvam
KU4. Concept of Pathya& Apathya(Wholesome diet and regimen)
KU5. Functions of Dosha , Dhatu , Mala, Symptoms by its imbalance in the body, Ojas ( VIGOR /
Radiance)

ASP/HSS/N0302: Familiarizing Sareera Vinjanam - Human Anatomy and
physiology
Description
Comprehensive understanding of the respiratory system, Digestive system, Urinary System, Nervous
System, sense organs, Bones, muscles and membranes. and the concept of Agni and its types, Circulatory
system, endocrine system and Excretory System.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Comprehensive understanding of the respiratory system, Digestive system, Urinary System, Nervous
System, sense organs, Bones, muscles and membranes, circulatory system, endocrine system and
Excretory System.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Basic Understanding of Ayurvedic and relevant modern aspects of the following: - Garbha,
Asthi , Sandhi , Sira , Lasika , Pesi , Koshtangas , Grandhi, Kala, Twak , Masthishka, Hrudaya
Guda , Marma, Indriyas etc
PC2. Basic Understanding of Ayurvedic and relevant modern aspects of the following: Ahara
Pachana, Agni, Dosha, Dhathu, Upadathu, Ojus . General aspects of Nervous system, Hrudaya
Pravruthi, Raktha Chamkramana , malas and mala pravathi (Pureesha,Moothra , sweda) etc
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The respiratory system, Digestive system, Urinary System, Nervous System, sense organs,
Bones, muscles and membranes.
KU2. The concept of Agni and its types, Circulatory system, endocrine system and Excretory
System.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Understand the respiratory system, Digestive system, Urinary System, Nervous System, sense
organs, Bones, muscles and membranes
GS2. Understand the concept of Agni and its types, Circulatory system, endocrine system and
Excretory System.

ASP/HSS/N0303: Basic Understanding of Dravya Vinjanam - Ayurveda
Pharmacology and Pharmaceuticals
Description
Understanding of Dravya -its classifications and functions, concept of Rasa and karma and the details on
medicine administration and preparations of formulations including Swarasa, Kalkam, Kwatham, Hima,
Phandam, Churnam,Gulika , Varthi , Modhaka , Avaleha.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding of Dravya classifications and functions,and preparations of formulations including
Swarasa, Kalkam, Kwatham, Hima, Phandam, Churnam,Gulika , Varthi , Modhaka , Avaleha.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Basic knowledge of Dravya vargeekaranam, rasa guna veerya vipaka, prabhava karmam,
oushadha prayoga margam, pathralekhana vijnanam ( prescription) , oushadha kalam , maana
paribhasha etc
PC2. To assess the knowhow of the preparation and the administration of the following:
Kashayakalpana, Thoyapaaka vidhi, Yoosham, yavagu, Peya, Vilepi, tharpana, mamsarasam,
paanakam, modhakam, varthi, choorna, ksheerapaka, dhanyamla etc
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Dravya and its classifications and functions, concept of Rasa and karma and the details on
medicine administration.
KU2. Preparations of formulations including Swarasa, Kalkam, Kwatham, Hima, Phandam,
Churnam,Gulika , Varthi , Modhaka , Avaleha
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Understand Dravya and its classifications and functions, concept of Rasa and karma and the
details on medicine administration
GS2. Prepare formulations including Swarasa, Kalkam, Kwatham, Hima, Phandam, Churnam,Gulika ,
Varthi , Modhaka , Avaleha.

ASP/HSS/N0304: Panchakarma - Classical Ayurvedic Treatment
procedures
Description
Understanding of the Snehavidhi, other treatments like Udwarthanam and Udkarshanam, five types of
purificatory methods and the different methods of blood letting.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding of the Snehavidhi, Udwarthanam and Udkarshanam, five types of purificatory
methods and the different methods of blood letting.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. To evaluate the expertise in bahyasneha prayoga (Abhyanga, Shirodhara, Shiropichu etc ) ,
abhyanthara sneha prayoga ( achapaanam, vicharana etc ) , Agneya and anagneya sweda.
PC2. To evaulate the understanding of utkarshanam, udwartanam
PC3. - To evaluate the understanding of description, classification, materials and methods,
observations and complications of the following karmas: Vamana, virechana, nasyam,
anuvasana, nirooham
PC4. Undestanding of Types of rakthamosha and its complications
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Snehavidhi (External & Internal Use).
KU2. Other treatments like Udwarthanam and Udkarshanam.
KU3. The five types of purificatory methods
KU4. The different methods of blood letting
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Understand the Snehavidhi (External & Internal Use).
GS2. Understand other treatments like Udwarthanam and Udkarshanam.
GS3. Understand the five types of purificatory methods.
GS4. Understand the different methods of blood letting.

ASP/HSS/N0305: Keraleeya Vishesha Chikitsa - Unique Ayurvedic
treatment procedures in Kerala
Description
Comprehensive understanding of Abhyangam, Thalapothichil, Thalam, different types of Parisheka, Kizhi
and Shastika anna lepam
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Comprehensive understanding of Abhyangam, Thalapothichil, Thalam, different types of Parisheka,
Kizhi and Shastika anna lepam
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. To evaluate the understanding of Abhyangam, Thalam, Thalapothichil and Different types of
Dhara, urovasthi , kadivasthi, greevavasthi, Januvasthi, Ksheeradhumam etc
PC2. Understanding of Different types of Kizhi (sweda) and Shashtika anna lepam
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Abhyangam, Thalapothichil, Thalam, different types of Parisheka.
KU2. Different types of Kizhi and Shastika anna lepam
