#### HYC/Q4006: Green Hydrogen  Fuel Supply Chain Manager
Brief Job Description
Green hydrogen fuel Supply Chain Manager is in charge of every stage of an organizations supply chain,
from purchasing raw materials to production of hydrogen. Individual is responsible for developing and
monitoring of the companys supply chain strategy, keep track of logistics, inventory and ensure that the
right product is available at the right time with a goal to improve productivity & efficiency and reduce
costs, while securing high quality of green hydrogen that meet quality and safety standards.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have an excellent understanding of supply chain processes and good knowledge of
relevant software and ERP systems. Overall, supply chain manager duties require high sense of responsibility, great project management and problem-solving skills awareness about financial transaction
and good written and oral communication skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management Procedures
2. HYC/N4011: Understanding basic concepts of Supply Chain Management
3. HYC/N4012: Managing Green Hydrogen Supply Chain
4. HYC/N4013: Roles and Responsibilities of Green hydrogen Supply Chain Manager
5. HYC/N9301: Working effectively in a team

#### HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management
Procedures
Description
This unit covers maintaining health, safety and security procedures at the workplace where Hydrogen is
produced and handled.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view
Safe handling of Hydrogen
Follow fire safety procedures
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view & Safe handling of Hydrogen
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Ensure that personnel under their supervision have a deep understanding of hydrogen's
properties, including its flammability, explosive limits and potential hazards.
- PC2. Ensure personal and team adherence to the use of appropriate protective
clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions
- PC3. Identify and communicate the relevant documents, responsible individuals, and designated
locations pertaining to health and safety in the workplace, facilitating effective safety management.
- PC4. Oversee and participate in risk assessments specific to hydrogen storage, handling and
production processes.
- PC5. Proficiently identify hydrogen process hazards and assess risks including the analysis of risk
or accident in the workplace due to hydrogen leak/fire
- PC6. capability to accurately read and interpret data from sensing equipment used in hydrogen
production processes
- PC7. apply key safety parameters for handling hydrogen & oxygen
- PC8. Evaluate hydrogen release pressures and understand hydrogen flame detection methods,
emphasizing the colorless nature of hydrogen flames
- PC9. Apply critical safety requirements for electrical equipment used in green hydrogen
production, minimizing electrical hazards
- PC10. competent in the operation and functioning of hydrogen exhaust systems, facilitating the
safe removal of hydrogen from the workplace
- PC11. Execute inertization processes using nitrogen to displace hazardous gases enhancing safety
in hydrogen-related operations
- PC12. Proficiently identify and recognize common safety signs displayed in various areas,
promoting a safe work environment.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |

| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
