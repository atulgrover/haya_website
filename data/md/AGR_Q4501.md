#### AGR/Q4501: Goat and Sheep Farmer
Brief Job Description
A Goat/Sheep Farmer is responsible for establishing the goat/sheep farm, procuring and rearing goats/sheep, and managing their feeding and health. The individual also breeds goats/sheep and markets
them and their produce.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be physically fit to work for long hours. The person should be able to make
independent decisions and solve problems. The individual should have basic written and verbal communication skills and numerical ability.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. AGR/N4507: Establish and maintain the goat/sheep farm
2. AGR/N4508: Procure, rear and market goats/sheep
3. AGR/N4509: Carry out breeding of goats/sheep
4. AGR/N4510: Undertake milking of goats/sheep
5. AGR/N9922: Engage in collective farming/activity
6. AGR/N9903: Maintain health and safety at the workplace
7. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Options(Not mandatory):
Option : Sheep shearing
Specialization in Sheep Shearing
1. AGR/N4511: Undertake shearing of sheep

#### AGR/N4507: Establish and maintain the goat/sheep farm
Description
This OS unit is about establishing and maintaining the goat farm. It also covers growing appropriate crops
to feed goats/sheeps.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Coordinate the construction of goat/sheep sheds
Maintain the goat/sheep sheds
Grow goat/sheep feed
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Coordinate the construction of goat/sheep sheds
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select a suitable area for rearing goats/sheep, ensuring adequate space for growing grass
and plants for goats/sheep
- PC2. arrange the required material, tools and equipment for the construction of goat/sheep sheds
- PC3. select an appropriate area in the goat/sheep farm for the construction of appropriate types of
sheds, e.g. general flock shed (ewe/doe shed), ram/buck shed, lambing/kidding shed, lamb/kid shed, etc.
- PC4. coordinate with the relevant expert for the construction of sheds, ensuring adequate space,
ventilation and protection from varied weather conditions
- PC5. ensure the flooring in the sheds is suitable for goats/sheep
- PC6. install the goat feeder at the recommended height for the convenient feeding of goats/sheep
- PC7. ensure effective drainage arrangements to prevent waterlogging in the sheds
- PC8. build and repair fences to keep animals contained and safe
Maintain the goat/sheep sheds
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. check the goat/sheep sheds regularly to identify the repair and maintenance needs
- PC10. carry out appropriate repair and maintenance or coordinate with an expert, as required
- PC11. carry out fumigation in the goat/sheep sheds
- PC12. remove animal waste from the sheds regularly and disinfect the sheds using the
recommended disinfectants
- PC13. perform regular cleaning of the goat/sheep feeder and water pans
Grow goat/sheep feed
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. select the appropriate legume, grass and tree fodder crops to be grown for goat/sheep feed,
e.g. hedge lucerne, cowpea, fodder maize, neem, agathi, etc.

#### AGR/N4508: Procure, rear and market goats/sheep
Description
This OS unit is about procuring, rearing and marketing goats/sheep, including their feed and health
management.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Procure goats/sheep
Feed the goats/sheep
Maintain the health of goats/sheep
Market goats/sheep and their produce
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Procure goats/sheep
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select the appropriate goat/sheep breeds for rearing based on their adaptability to the
prevailing climatic conditions, the purpose of rearing and the relevant feeding management system
- PC2. procure young goats/sheep of the selected breeds in the required number from a reliable
breeder, ensuring they are pure-bred with high genetic merit
- PC3. check the goats/sheep being procured are vaccinated for the Peste des Petits Ruminants
(PPR) plague
- PC4. tag the procured goats/ sheep for their identification
Feed the goats/sheep
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. use the recommended ingredients, e.g. green leguminous roughage and grasses/tender tree
leaves, in the goat/sheep feed to ensure their nutrient requirements are met
- PC6. supplement the sheep feed with the recommended quantity of sulphur to improve wool and
meat production
- PC7. determine and follow the recommended feed requirements for the fattening of goats/ sheep
for meat production
- PC8. feed colostrum to newborn kids/lambs as per the recommendation
- PC9. monitor the kids/lamb for correct suckling and feeding
- PC10. follow the appropriate colostrum and creep feeding schedule for kids/lambs
- PC11. follow the recommended practices to induce optimum lactation in female goats/sheep
- PC12. feed supplement concentrate ration in pellet or mesh form to goats/sheep as per the
requirement, ensuring it has an appropriate quantity of nutrients
- PC13. follow the semi-intensive feed management system to achieve better goat/sheep production
- PC14. water the goats/sheep according to their age and water requirements
Maintain the health of goats/sheep

#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC15. follow the recommended preventive measures to protect goats/sheep from diseases and
disorders, e.g. regular cleaning of goats/sheep and maintaining dry sheds
- PC16. maintain the stock of necessary vaccines and first aid kit for goats/sheep through
consultation with the veterinarian
- PC17. ensure appropriate vaccinations for goats/sheep at the appropriate stages of their growth
- PC18. carry out deworming in goats/sheep by administering them the recommended anthelmintic
drug in an appropriate dose
- PC19. carry out drenching in goats/sheep using the recommended coccidiosis drug at an
appropriate age to prevent coccidiosis infestation
- PC20. carry out dipping in goats/sheep in the appropriate weather to prevent the ectoparasite
infestation
- PC21. practice drug rotation to prevent drug resistance in goats/sheep
- PC22. identify and segregate goats/sheep to be dehorned
- PC23. carry out dehorning of the identified goats/sheep following the appropriate procedure
- PC24. perform hoof trimming for goats/sheep using the appropriate tools and equipment
- PC25. identify the symptoms of diseases and disorders in goats/sheep, such as reduced feed
intake, abnormal discharge, abnormal behaviour, etc.
- PC26. treat the common diseases and disorders by administering the appropriate treatment to
goats/sheep and coordinate with a veterinarian to treat serious diseases and disorders
- PC27. maintain the kids/lambs and sick goats/sheep in separate sheds to provide them with the
necessary care
- PC28. identify and cull the poor-performing and aged goats/sheep to maintain high productivity at
the goat/sheep farm
- PC29. maintain the appropriate records concerning the identification and treatment of diseases and
disorders in goats/sheep
- PC30. adhere to animal welfare laws, biosecurity measures, and food safety regulations
- PC31. maintain logs for health, breeding, birth, and production (milk/wool/meat)
Market goats/sheep and their produce
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC32. harvest the goat/sheep produce, e.g. milk, wool and hide
- PC33. select mature and healthy goats/sheep for marketing
- PC34. identify the appropriate buyers for goats/sheep and their produce and negotiate with them to
secure a profitable price
- PC35. arrange an appropriate mode of transport to deliver goats/sheep and their produce to the
buyers
- PC36. process the payments using an appropriate payment method, including the e-payment
method
- PC37. budget, track expenses and income, and plan for seasonal fluctuations
- PC38. maintain the manual and/or electronic records concerning the marketing activities

possibility of transmission of bacteria

milking, e.g. applying appropriate and even pressure on udders
