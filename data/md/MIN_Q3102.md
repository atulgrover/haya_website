MIN/Q3102: Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM) Electrician
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role is responsible for the service, maintenance, repair and overhaul of electrical and
electronic aggregates of HEMM available in mine.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should be physically agile, strong, have good eye-sight and not suffer from color-
blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MIN/N3106: Carry out service, repair and overhaul of electrical and electronic aggregates of HEMM
- 2. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Underground Metal
- 1. MIN/N1702: Follow Health, Safety and Environmental guidelines for Underground Metalliferous
Mines
Elective 2: Opencast
- 1. MIN/N1703: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental Guidelines for opencast mines
Elective 3: Underground Coal
- 1. MIN/N1704: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental guidelines for underground coal mines
Options(Not mandatory):
Option : Electric Vehicle

MIN/N3106: Carry out service, repair and overhaul of electrical and
electronic aggregates of HEMM
Description
This OS unit is about carrying out diagnosis of fault, service and repairs of the electrical and electronic
systems of a HEMM.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for service and repair work
Perform service, repair and overhaul of electrical/electronic aggregates of HEMM
Perform post service/repair activities
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for service and repair work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. determine the work requirements such as type of tasks to be performed (repair,
maintenance, etc.), HEMM to be maintained from work instructions, manuals, site visits, etc.
PC2. identify the auto component manufacturer specifications related to the various
components/aggregates in the HEMM
PC3. review complaint sheet, log book and history sheet of the equipment and understand repair
requirements
PC4. identify and arrange tools, consumables, spare parts, etc. required for performing service
and repair work
PC5. inform relevant departments about the service and repair work to be performed and obtain
approval before starting the work
Perform service, repair and overhaul of electrical/electronic aggregates of HEMM
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. shut down the HEMM properly by disconnecting all primary and secondary energy sources
safely
PC7. inspect the HEMM for defects and faulty parts/components by carrying out standard checks
PC8. diagnose direct or indirect faults in HEMM electrical/electronic systems as per OEM SOP
PC9. fix and verify that lockout has been successful and attach a lockout or tagout device to the
HEMM to ensure that it cannot be started without removing the device
PC10. dismantle the HEMM and disconnect the faulty parts from it safely
PC11. check all wirings and electrical connections for any faults, damage, loose connections, etc.
PC12. inspect the HEMM for charge leakage, short circuit in parts, breakage of wires and clamps,
unusual contact of electrical wires with moving parts, erratic/problematic performance or any
other problem as reported in the complaint
PC13. repair all electrical and electronic faults including direct faults in input sensors, output
actuators, wiring harnesses etc. as per SOP/WI

Description
This unit is about the repair and maintenance of Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM) Electric Vehicles
(EVs). It includes routine inspections, diagnosing faults, and performing repairs on electrical and
mechanical components to ensure optimal performance, safety, and longevity of the vehicle.
Scope

Maintain battery and motors
Maintain and repair cooling systems
Maintain charging infrastructure
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain electrical systems
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. conduct a visual and physical inspection of the electrical input and output bus for signs of
corrosion, looseness, or damage
PC2. use thermal imaging or infrared cameras to detect overheating or loose connections
PC3. check for damaged insulation and shielding to prevent electrical short circuits or exposure

PC4. verify that bus bars and connectors are securely fastened and free from external

inefficiencies

high-voltage insulation monitoring sensors, accelerator pedal position sensors, brake
pressure sensors, inverter temperature sensors, etc.) for dust, grease, or contamination that
may interfere with accurate readings

correct feedback on battery health, motor performance, and system temperatures
PC9. test solenoids for proper actuation, responsiveness, and electrical continuity
PC10. replace faulty sensors or solenoids with Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) -approved
components to maintain system reliability
PC11. conduct a thorough inspection of the wiring harness for signs of chafing, cuts, and exposure
to environmental hazards such as water or extreme temperatures
