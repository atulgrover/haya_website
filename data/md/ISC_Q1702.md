ISC/N0946: Manage and implement health and safety practices at the
work place
Description
This OS unit is about managing and implementing health, hygiene, safety and sustainable practices for
optimising use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain safe and secure working environment
Maintain health and hygiene
Manage housekeeping at workplace
Implement material and energy conservation practices
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain safe and secure working environment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify hazardous activities and the possible causes of risks or accidents in the workplace
PC2. implement safe working practices while dealing with hazards to ensure safety of self and
others
PC3. ensure that team is following appropriate safety practices while working in and around
trenches, elevated places and confined areas
PC4. conduct regular checks of the machines with support of the maintenance team to identify
potential hazards
PC5. organise safety drills or training sessions to create awareness amongst others on the
identified risks and safety practices
PC6. fill daily check sheet to report improvements done and risks identified
PC7. ensure that relevant safety boards/signs are placed on the shop floor for the safety of self
and others
PC8. report any identified breaches in health, safety and security policies and procedures to the
designated person
PC9. ensure that team is following appropriate emergency procedures such as raising alarm, safe
evacuation etc.
Maintain health and hygiene
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. ensure team is aware about hygiene and sanitation regulations and following them on the
shop floor
PC11. ensure workplace, equipment, restrooms etc. are cleaned and sanitized regularly
PC12. ensure availability of running water, hand wash and alcohol-based sanitizers at the
workplace
PC13. report advanced hygiene and sanitation issues to appropriate authority

Description
This OS unit is about managing team, colleagues/superiors and others, either in own work group or in other
work groups within organisation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communicate effectively with team members
Interact with supervisor
Manage appropriate behaviour at work place

Communicate effectively with team members
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. manage and co-ordinate with team members to integrate work, as per the workload in order

PC5. carry out commitments made to team members

PC7. guide the team members to follow the organisation’s policies and procedures
PC8. ensure team goals are given preference over individual goals
PC9. encourage team members to rectify errors as per feedback and minimize mistakes in future

To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. report progress on job allocated and team performance to the superiors
PC11. escalate problems to supervisors that cannot be handled
PC12. report the completed work
PC13. train the team members to report completed work and receive feedback on work done

To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC14. help PwD team members to overcome the challenges, if asked
PC15. ensure team shows sensitivity towards all genders and PwD
PC16. adjust communication styles to reflect gender sensitivity and sensitivity towards person with
disability

ISC/N1705: Prepare for Safety aspects for handling of Green Hydrogen
Description
This OS unit is about performing preparatory activities regarding safety aspects for handling of green
hydrogen
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out preparatory activities regarding safety aspects for handling of green hydrogen
Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out preparatory activities regarding safety aspects for handling of green hydrogen
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. obtain the required information and work completed in previous shift from logbook,
discussions etc. and take handover from the previous shift engineer by following
organizational procedures
PC2. ensure availability of permissible quantity of green hydrogen for storage and transportation
PC3. assess variation in availability of green hydrogen for storage and transportation
PC4. control green hydrogen flow and pressure for storage and transportation
PC5. coordinate with supply chain of green hydrogen for consistent supply for storage and
transportation
PC6. ensure proper pressure, temperature and flow of green hydrogen for storage and
transportation
PC7. ensure purity of green hydrogen for storage and transportation as per SOP
PC8. ensure real time monitoring system of incoming green hydrogen for storage systems
PC9. ensure that work area is clean and free of any obstacle or encumbrances
PC10. ensure that all safety measures, interlocks, alarms are working and in conformance with the
safe operation of green hydrogen production system as per SOP
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. relevant standards and procedures followed in the company
KU2. Understand the design parameters of green hydrogen production system
KU3. Basic properties of hydrogen gas
KU4. Green hydrogen production system
KU5. Green hydrogen production technologies
KU6. Various green hydrogen storage systems
KU7. Various green hydrogen transportation systems
