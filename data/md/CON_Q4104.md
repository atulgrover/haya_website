CON/Q4104: Painting and Decorating (WorldSkills)
Brief Job Description
A painter and decorator works in the commercial and public sectors and is responsible for the external and
internal appearance of a building and its protection from water, rust, corrosion, mould, and insect
infestation. There is a direct relationship between the nature and quality of the service required and the
payment made by the client. Therefore, the painter and decorator has a continuing responsibility to work
professionally and interactively with the client in order to give satisfaction and thus maintain and grow the
business.
Personal Attributes
The person should be physically fit to work in varying locations and conditions. The person should be
organized, diligent, methodical, safety-conscious, and a prompt decision- maker. In addition to being a
team player, the individual should have good communication skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CON/N4101: Work Organization, Management and Communication
- 2. CON/N4102: Problem Solving, Innovation and Creativity

CON/N4101: Work Organization, Management and Communication
Description
This NOS deals with ensuring safe and healthy working environment at competition area by proper
utilization of safety gears and effective communication skill with juries and competitors.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Adopt healthy and safe work practices at competition area
Management of workplace
Effective communication at competition area
Elements and Performance Criteria
Adopt healthy and safe work practices at competition area
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain health and safety legislation, obligations, and documentation
PC2. check and ensure the availability of appropriate and usable Personal Protective Equipment
(PPE) at the workplace
PC3. use the appropriate PPE according to the nature of work and ensure other personnel also do
the same
PC4. follow and promote the appropriate measures to ensure hygiene in the workplace,
e.g.regular cleaning and sanitization
PC5. follow the applicable emergency procedures and ensure the personnel also do the same
PC6. Learn precautions for the safe use of power and cordless tools
PC7. Apply standards and laws relating to security, safety, and hygiene in plastering and drywall
systems
Management of workplace
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. establish methods for optimal and economically organized construction site. regarding
construction plan and equipment, organization and procedures, material management,
staffing and timelines
PC9. determine the correct calculations and estimates by using principles and methods
PC10. store plasterboards and related products safely and securely
PC11. be proactive in own continuous professional development in order to keep abreast of
methods of working in the construction industry and changing technologies, for example
acoustics, sustainability and environmental impact
PC12. Work effectively as part of teams
PC13. Work effectively with other trades
PC14. Establish and maintain high quality standards and working processes
PC15. identify problems promptly and manage their resolution

CON/N4102: Problem Solving, Innovation and Creativity
Description
This NOS deals with diagnostic approaches to problem solving by innovations and creativity
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develop the problem solving techniques by new innovations and creativity
Elements and Performance Criteria
Develop the problem solving techniques by new innovations and creativity
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Check work regularly, particularly for accuracy/standard to minimize problems at a later stage
PC2. Recognize and understand problems swiftly and follow a self-managed process for resolving
PC3. Challenge incorrect information to prevent problems
PC4. Develop creative solutions to challenges when working on restoration projects
PC5. Recognize opportunities to contribute ideas to improve the product and overall level of
customer satisfaction
PC6. Demonstrate a willingness to try new methods and embrace change
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The common types of problem which can occur within the work process
KU2. Diagnostic approaches to problem solving
KU3. understand rends and developments in the industry including new materials Methods, and
equipment/technology
KU4. Keep up to date with changes in the industry
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. maintain work-related notes and records
GS2. read the relevant literature to learn about the latest developments in the field of work
GS3. communicate clearly and politely with co-workers and clients
GS4. listen attentively to understand the information/ instructions being shared by the speaker
GS5. plan and prioritize tasks to ensure timely completion
GS6. coordinate with co-workers to achieve work objectives

CON/N4103: Interpretation of Drawings
Description
This NOS deals with understanding of the test project drawings along with its process of measurements.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Interpretation and execution of drawings as per standards
Elements and Performance Criteria
Interpretation and execution of drawings as per standards
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Prepare site measurement drawings
PC2. Accurately interpret all plans, elevations, sections and enlarged details
PC3. Produce basic outline drawings (hand and CAD) including elevations, plans, and sections to
full size
PC4. produce accurate complex drawings on wood to make figure on the wall/floor
PC5. Identify drawing errors or items that require clarification
PC6. Determine and check quantities of materials required
PC7. Calculate a cost and price for the work
PC8. Identify horizontal and vertical key dimensions and all angles
PC9. Identify curved work and mortar joint finishes
PC10. Interpret all project features and their required construction methods
PC11. Establish any features that need special equipment or templates and source these
PC12. Recognize specified bonding patterns and obey bonding rules during construction
PC13. Provide advice and guidance to other professionals such as architects and quantity surveyors
PC14. Keep essential notes on each installation process
PC15. Explain complex specialist and technical information about installations to clients and other
professionals
PC16. Apply mathematic geometry principles to the calculation of angles, areas, perimeters,
curves, arcs, volumes, ratios, etc.
PC17. Prepare the materials requirements, taking into account increased requirements due to
compression, wastage, breakage, etc.
PC18. Calculate formwork surfaces and materials requirements
PC19. Calculate formwork surfaces and materials requirements for face concrete formwork
PC20. Interpret, analyse, and work with construction plans (e.g. design plans, formwork plans,
reinforcement plans, detail drawings, etc.) and material and parts lists

20, the marking needs to be changed). Competitors who finish simultaneously will receive the same
