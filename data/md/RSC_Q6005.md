#### RSC/Q6005: Junior Assistant - Rubber Nursery
Brief Job Description
The Junior Assistant - Rubber Nursery supports in preparing nursery sites, handling planting materials,
maintaining rubber seedlings, and assisting in transplantation and hardening activities. The role involves
labour-oriented tasks such as land preparation, soil filling, potting, irrigation, weeding, fertilization, pest
management, and nursery upkeep. The job holder also follows safe working practices, maintains hygiene,
and adopts environmentally sustainable practices. The role is entry-level and requires the individual to
work under close supervision of nursery supervisors or field officers.
Personal Attributes
The individual on the job should have capacity for undertaking labour oriented activities, should have good
observational skills, attention to detail.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N6010: Prepare the nursery site and materials
2. RSC/N5612: Carry out planting of rubber plants and its maintenance
3. RSC/N6011: Support in transplantation and hardening
4. RSC/N5629: Follow safety, environmental and hygiene practices
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### RSC/N6010: Prepare the nursery site and materials
Description
This NOS covers assisting in nursery site preparation, collection and arrangement of materials, soil mixture
preparation, and maintaining cleanliness of the nursery area.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist in nursery site preparation
Collect and prepare nursery materials
Maintain nursery area hygiene
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist in nursery site preparation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. clear unwanted weeds, debris, and stones from the nursery site as instructed
- PC2. level and prepare seedbeds or polybag filling areas under supervision
- PC3. ensure proper drainage channels are maintained to prevent waterlogging
Collect and prepare nursery materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. collect soil, sand, and compost/manure from identified sources
- PC5. sieve and remove unwanted particles from soil and sand
- PC6. prepare soil mixture in recommended proportions as guided by supervisor
- PC7. fill polythene bags with soil mixture uniformly
- PC8. arrange filled polybags in rows as per instructions
Maintain nursery area hygiene
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. dispose of waste and unusable materials at the designated place
- PC10. maintain basic cleanliness and orderliness in the nursery area
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. basic importance of nursery site preparation in rubber plant raising
- KU2. common materials required for nursery (soil, sand, compost, polybags)
- KU3. correct proportion of soil-sand-compost mixture
- KU4. importance of proper drainage and site cleanliness
- KU5. safe handling of tools and materials used in nursery preparation

#### RSC/N5612: Carry out planting of rubber plants and its maintenance
Description
This NOS involves assisting in planting operations, watering, weeding, mulching, fertilization, pest/disease
management, and maintaining records for healthy growth of rubber seedlings in nurseries.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist in planting operations
Carry out irrigation and upkeep
Support in nutrient and pest management
Maintain nursery records and order
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist in planting operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. collect and arrange healthy seedlings for planting
- PC2. inspect seedlings for signs of damage or disease before planting
- PC3. dig small pits or holes for planting seedlings
- PC4. plant seedlings in polybags or beds at correct depth and spacing
- PC5. press soil gently around the roots to ensure stability
- PC6. provide shade using shade nets, palm fronds, or banana leaves
- PC7. install proper spacing between rows to facilitate growth and maintenance
Carry out irrigation and upkeep
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. water seedlings regularly in required quantity
- PC9. ensure proper drainage after watering to avoid root rot
- PC10. carry out hand weeding around seedlings at regular intervals
- PC11. mulch the seedling base with dry leaves or organic matter to retain soil moisture
- PC12. remove damaged, stunted, or dead seedlings from the nursery
- PC13. clean nursery beds and paths to prevent weed growth
Support in nutrient and pest management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. apply fertilizers or manures in correct quantity as per instructions
- PC15. assist in foliar spraying of micronutrients
- PC16. mix and apply pesticides, fungicides, or biocontrol agents safely under supervision
- PC17. identify common pests such as aphids, mealybugs, and scales
- PC18. identify symptoms of common diseases like powdery mildew, leaf spot, or anthracnose
- PC19. segregate affected plants and report them to supervisors

#### RSC/N6011: Support in transplantation and hardening
Description
This NOS covers assisting in safe transplantation of seedlings into polybags or field sites, supporting the
hardening process to acclimatize seedlings, and ensuring maintenance of transplanted plants for healthy
establishment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist in transplantation
Support in hardening process
Maintain transplanted seedlings
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist in transplantation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select healthy seedlings suitable for transplantation
- PC2. carefully uproot seedlings without damaging roots
- PC3. prune taproots where necessary under guidance
- PC4. transport seedlings safely to new sites using baskets or trays
- PC5. dig pits or prepare larger polybags with appropriate soil mixture
- PC6. transplant seedlings at correct depth with firm soil support
- PC7. water transplanted seedlings immediately after planting
- PC8. provide temporary shade to transplanted seedlings when required
Support in hardening process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. gradually expose seedlings to direct sunlight
- PC10. reduce irrigation frequency step by step for hardening
- PC11. remove shade structures at the recommended stage
- PC12. monitor seedlings for signs of stress or wilting during hardening
- PC13. report wilting or mortality to the supervisor
- PC14. assist in grouping hardened plants ready for field planting
Maintain transplanted seedlings
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC15. weed around transplanted plants regularly
- PC16. mulch base of transplanted seedlings for moisture conservation
- PC17. apply basal dose of fertilizers or manures after transplanting
- PC18. assist in spraying pesticides or fungicides to control pests/diseases
- PC19. replace dead or wilted seedlings with new ones (gap filling)

#### RSC/N5629: Follow safety, environmental and hygiene practices
Description
This NOS involves adopting safety practices, using protective equipment, maintaining personal hygiene,
and following environmentally sustainable practices in the nursery.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Follow health and safety procedures
Maintain hygiene and cleanliness
Practice environmental sustainability
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow health and safety procedures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. wear basic protective equipment such as gloves and boots
- PC2. use tools and materials safely under guidance
- PC3. follow safety instructions while handling fertilizers and pesticides
- PC4. store tools and agrochemicals in designated places
Maintain hygiene and cleanliness
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. wash hands and maintain personal hygiene before and after work
- PC6. keep nursery pathways and surroundings clean
- PC7. dispose of nursery waste safely
Practice environmental sustainability
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. avoid wastage of water during irrigation
- PC9. follow eco-friendly practices such as use of organic manure
- PC10. segregate biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. importance of personal safety and protective gear
- KU2. hazards related to improper handling of tools and agrochemicals
- KU3. hygiene practices to prevent infections and diseases
- KU4. safe methods of disposing waste
- KU5. importance of water conservation and eco-friendly practices

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
