#### HCS/Q1502: Carving Artisan - Stonecraft
Brief Job Description
A stonecraft carving artisan expertly shapes raw stone into beautiful sculptures and functional art,
showcasing a blend of traditional techniques and creative expression.
Personal Attributes
Detail-oriented and patient, a stonecraft carving artisan possesses a keen eye for design and a deep
appreciation for the natural beauty of stone, allowing them to create intricate and lasting works of art.

#### HCS/N1503: Carve stone to create stoneware product
Description
This unit is about producing stoneware products by strategically cutting and shaping the stone as per the
desired product design. The process starts with envisioning and sketching the design as per customer
requirements and ends with the desired product in form of a finished stoneware product
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparing the Workspace and Materials
Carving the Stone
Refining and Polishing the Stone
Finalizing and Packaging the Stoneware Product
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparing the Workspace and Materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Organize the workspace to ensure a clean and safe environment for carving.
- PC2. Select the appropriate stone based on design requirements and product durability.
- PC3. Inspect the stone for cracks or imperfections and remove unsuitable sections.
- PC4. Gather and inspect all necessary tools, such as chisels, hammers, rasps, grinders, and
protective gear.
- PC5. Set up the workstation to ensure tools are within easy reach and properly aligned for efficient
carving.
Carving the Stone
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Mark the stone with a design template or sketch, ensuring accurate layout for carving.
- PC7. Use tools (e.g., hammers and point chisels) to roughly shape the stone according to the
design outline.
- PC8. Refine the shape by using finer chisels and rasps, working in stages to achieve the desired
depth and detail.
- PC9. Continuously check alignment with the design, adjusting as necessary to maintain precision
and symmetry.
- PC10. Use specialized tools for detailed carvings and intricate patterns, ensuring clean and sharp
edges.
Refining and Polishing the Stone
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Smooth the rough surfaces using rasps, sandpaper, or grinding tools to eliminate tool marks.
- PC12. Apply finer abrasives for a polished finish, ensuring an even texture and surface appearance.
- PC13. Perform water or oil polishing to enhance the stone's natural shine and remove surface dust.

Finalizing and Packaging the Stoneware Product

- PC19. Clean and maintain tools after use, storing them properly to prevent wear and damage.
- PC20. Dispose of waste materials, including stone dust and debris, in an environmentally
responsible manner.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand the properties, characteristics, and uses of different stones.
- KU2. Identify stones suitable for specific designs or products.
- KU3. Knowledge of tools like hammers, chisels, rasps, grinders, and polishing wheels.
- KU4. Understanding how to use and maintain tools for precision and longevity.
- KU5. Knowledge of symmetry, proportions, and artistic techniques in stone carving.
- KU6. Understanding roughing techniques for shaping stones.
- KU7. Knowledge of detailing methods for intricate patterns and designs.
- KU8. Awareness of quality standards for stoneware products.
- KU9. Techniques to ensure finished products meet customer specifications.
- KU10. Methods for organizing tools and materials for efficient workflow.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Ability to understand and follow verbal and written instructions.
- GS2. Effective communication with clients, team members, and supervisors to discuss designs, timelines, and expectations.
- GS3. Skill in planning and prioritizing tasks to meet deadlines efficiently.
- GS4. Creative thinking to adapt designs or processes based on unexpected challenges.
- GS5. Basic calculations for measuring dimensions, proportions, and material requirements.
- GS6. Precision in carving intricate designs and achieving symmetrical patterns.
- GS7. Careful inspection to ensure there are no flaws in the final product.
- GS8. Expertise in using carving tools like chisels, hammers, rasps, and grinders.

#### HCS/N8523: Entrepreneurship skills for Stonecraft Carving Artisan
Description
This unit covers the key entrepreneurial skills required by a Stonecraft Carving Artisan to successfully
manage, sustain, and grow their stonecraft business.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Market Research and Opportunity Identification
Innovation and Product Development
Business Planning
Financial Management
Marketing and Sales
Customer Relationship Management
Quality Control and Craftsmanship
Legal and Regulatory Compliance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Market Research and Opportunity Identification
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Analyze current market trends related to stonecraft, cultural designs, and consumer
demands.
- PC2. Identify niche markets and emerging opportunities in stone carving, evaluate customer
demographics and geographic locations where the products are most in demand.
Innovation and Product Development
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. Research and develop innovative designs by blending traditional craftsmanship with
contemporary trends.
- PC4. Stay updated with new carving techniques, tools, and materials to enhance the efficiency
and quality of work.
Business Planning
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Develop a comprehensive business plan that includes vision, mission, objectives, strategies,
and financial projections.
- PC6. Set short-term, mid-term, and long-term business goals that align with market opportunities
and business capabilities.
Financial Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Establish and maintain a budget for stone carving projects, managing costs effectively. Track
expenses related to raw materials, tools, labor, shipping, and marketing.

#### HCS/N9929: Working in a team
Description
This NOS describes the communication and coordination skills and knowledge to work with colleagues and
supervisor to achieve a smooth and hazard-free workflow.

interact with supervisor or superior work as a team by coordinating with colleagues within and outside the department and include

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
