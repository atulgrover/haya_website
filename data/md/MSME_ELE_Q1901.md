Qualification Pack
MSME/ELE/N1904: Perform Mechanical & Chemical Operation to
Manufacture PCB
Description
After completion of course Student should be able to perform Plan sheet cutting.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
After completion of course Student should be able to perform Plan sheet cutting.
Elements and Performance Criteria
MSME/CCPCBF/04 Perform Mechanical & Chemical Operation to Manufacture PCB
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Plan sheet cutting
PC2. Cut sheets by using shearing machine.
PC3. Make mounting holes/slots by using punching machine.
PC4. Final cutting by using angular cutting machine.
PC5. Drill manually using drilling machine.
PC6. CNC programming.
PC7. CNC drilling.
PC8. CNC routing.
PC9. Knows the materials used in HAL machine.
PC10. Understand how to flux before tinning.
PC11. Understand how to tin on single as well as double side PCB.
PC12. • Remove the oxidation layer from copper surface by manual cleaning,
• chemical process.
PC13. Remove the oxidation layer from copper surface by chemical process.
PC14. Remove unwanted copper by using etching process.
PC15. Spray etching.
PC16. Splash etching
PC17. Bubble etching.
PC18. Tank etching.
PC19. Inspect the PCB with the help of BBT technique.
PC20. Inspect open circuit and short circuit.
PC21. Inspect for over etched/under etched
PC22. Inspect hole size.
PC23. Inspect hole for conformity with pad.
PC24. Inspect for missing/extra hole.
