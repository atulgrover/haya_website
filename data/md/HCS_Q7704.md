#### HCS/Q7704: Crochet Lace - Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The Crochet Lace Supervisor oversees the production and quality of intricate lace designs, ensuring
adherence to craftsmanship standards while managing a team of skilled artisans. This role combines
technical expertise with leadership to maintain the artistic integrity and efficiency of lace manufacturing.
Personal Attributes
A good Crochet Lace Supervisor has a sharp eye for detail and a creative flair, along with strong leadership
and communication skills to guide their team and ensure top-quality work. They are also good at solving
problems and staying organised to keep everything running smoothly.

#### HCS/N7721: Role of Supervisor in Crochet lace production
Description
This unit is about oversees the entire process of creating crochet lace products, ensuring efficiency, quality
control, and effective team management.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Planning and Organizing Work
Supervision of Production
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Planning and Organizing work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Analyse production orders and customer requirements to develop a comprehensive
production plan for crochet lace items.
- PC2. Estimate and procure raw materials (yarns, threads, hooks, accessories) needed for
production, based on the design complexity and volume, ensuring optimal inventory levels.
- PC3. Schedule tasks and assign specific production stages to individual artisans or groups,
considering their skill level and expertise in crochet techniques.
- PC4. Ensure that artisans are provided with clear work instructions and pattern specifications,
minimizing errors and maximizing productivity.
- PC5. Develop plans for potential production challenges, such as material shortages, artisan
absenteeism, or equipment malfunction, to avoid disruption.
- PC6. Monitor production progress on a daily basis, ensuring products are being manufactured
correctly and making adjustments if necessary.
- PC7. Coordinate with the design team to ensure that the lace patterns and motifs are feasible
within the production capabilities and timeframe.
Supervision of Production
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Monitor the entire crochet lace production process to ensure that work is being performed
according to the planned schedule and quality standards.
- PC9. Conduct regular inspections at various stages of production to ensure that patterns, motifs,
and designs are being followed accurately by the artisans.
- PC10. Identify and resolve any production issues, such as design inconsistencies, equipment
malfunctions, or delays in workflow, to prevent disruption in the production process.
- PC11. Provide technical guidance and support to artisans on complex crochet techniques and
designs, ensuring consistent skill development and production quality.
- PC12. Ensure that production tools (crochet hooks, frames, blocking tools) and workstations are
properly maintained and set up for efficient operations.

#### HCS/N7722: Inventory and Resource Management
Description
This unit is about the effective planning, tracking, and utilization of materials, tools, and human resources
to ensure seamless production. The supervisor is responsible for maintaining optimal inventory levels,
allocating resources based on production needs, and ensuring the efficient use of materials to minimize
waste while ensuring quality.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Inventory Management
Resource Allocation and Management
Cost and Budget Management
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Inventory Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Accurately forecast the material requirements based on production schedules and expected
demand.
- PC2. Maintain optimal inventory levels of raw materials (e.g., yarns, threads, hooks) and tools to
avoid overstocking or stockouts.
- PC3. Monitor and record material usage on a daily basis to ensure efficient utilization and prevent
wastage.
- PC4. Conduct regular inventory audits to ensure accuracy in stock levels and identify
discrepancies.
- PC5. Implement stock control measures to ensure materials are stored properly to avoid damage
or spoilage.
Resource Allocation and Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Assign tasks to artisans based on their skill level, experience, and the complexity of the
production requirements.
- PC7. Ensure that all artisans are equipped with the necessary tools and materials to carry out
their tasks efficiently.
- PC8. Monitor the usage of tools and equipment, ensuring proper handling and preventing damage
or loss.
- PC9. Track the performance and productivity of artisans, making necessary adjustments in task
allocation to optimize output.
- PC10. Communicate clearly with artisans about production goals, deadlines, and any changes in
resource allocation.
- PC11. Identify any gaps or shortfalls in resource availability (tools, materials, or workforce) and
take prompt corrective action.

#### HCS/N7723: Quality Control and Assurance
Description
This unit is about the Quality Control and Assurance in crochet lace production involves systematic
processes aimed at ensuring that the final products meet established quality standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Establishing Quality Control Standards
Conducting Quality Inspections
Implementing Corrective Actions and Continuous Improvement
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Establishing Quality Control Standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Set quality control standards for materials, production processes, and finished crochet lace
products, ensuring alignment with customer specifications and industry level.
- PC2. Explain quality control standards effectively to all team members, ensuring their
understanding and adherence during the production process.
- PC3. Regularly review and update quality standards based on feedback, production outcomes, and
market trends to ensure continuous improvement.
- PC4. Collaborate with designers and artisans to ensure that quality standards are feasible and
practical within the production context.
Conducting Quality Inspections
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Implement a systematic schedule for conducting quality inspections at various stages of the
crochet lace production process, including raw material evaluation and final product assessment.
- PC6. Perform inspections to identify defects, inconsistencies, and differences from established
quality standards in both in-process and finished items.
- PC7. Maintain accurate records of inspection results, documenting any defects found and actions
taken to address quality issues.
- PC8. Provide immediate feedback to artisans during inspections to promote real-time learning and
quality awareness.
Implementing Corrective Actions and Continuous Improvement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Identify and analyse the causes of defects or quality issues, and develop appropriate
corrective action plans to address them effectively.
- PC10. Communicate with team members about identified deficiencies and necessary improvements
to foster a collaborative approach to quality enhancement.

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.

| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
