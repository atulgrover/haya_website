CUTM/FIC/Q0101: Milk Testing Facility Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The Milk Testing Facility Supervisor should be able to collect market information for business planning and
decision making, set-up paper recycling plant, operate the plant, manage the team, finance and
administrative activities along with marketing, making quality products ensuring organizational health,
safety and security.
Personal Attributes
A Milk Testing Facility Supervisor should be detail-oriented, disciplined, and knowledgeable in dairy quality
standards. They must possess strong analytical skills, integrity, and technical proficiency in using milk
testing equipment. Effective communication, documentation ability, and a commitment to hygiene,
accuracy, and food safety are essential in this supervisory role.

CUTM/FIC/N0101: Incoming milk quality check.
Description
The NOS "Incoming Milk Quality Check" covers the procedures for assessing raw milk quality upon arrival
at the facility. It includes sampling, testing for adulterants, analyzing key parameters like fat, SNF, and
microbial load, ensuring compliance with regulatory standards, documenting results, and making decisions
on acceptance or rejection based on quality standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS "Incoming Milk Quality Check" includes verifying milk quality through sampling, physical
and chemical analysis, and microbiological testing. It covers detecting adulterants, ensuring
compliance with industry standards, maintaining hygiene during handling, documenting test results,
and coordinating with procurement and quality teams for acceptance or rejection of milk
consignments.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. analyzing quality of milk organoleptic ally by its flavor, odour, and appearance.
PC2. discard milk sample in case it is giving any foul, abnormal smell or have abnormal color.
PC3. blend 5 ml milk sample in test tube and add 5 ml ethanol and 4 drops of rosalic acid solution.
Analyze color, rose red color indicates presence of neutralizer in milk, whereas brownish
color indicates pure milk.
PC4. add few drops of 1% iodine solution in 10 ml of milk, to analyze the presence of starch.
Development of blue color indicates presence of starch, whereas pure milk will not change
the color
PC5. add 0.1 g resorcinol powder along with 10 ml HCl in 10 ml of milk, for added sugar detection.
Heat milk in water bath for 5 min, color changes to red color indicates presence of sugar in
milk.
PC6. verify fat, SNF and added water by using milk analyzer instrument.
PC7. ensure to heat the milk to 40oC before testing and then cool to 20oC. After thorough mixing
take 20 ml of sample in the sampler.
PC8. validate fat content by Gerber method or using a milk analyzer.
PC9. verify SNF (Solid not fat) content by using lactometer or milk analyzer.
PC10. check acidity of milk by titrating 10 ml of milk with 0.1 N NaOH using 3 drops of
phenolphthalein as indicator.
PC11. check COB (clot on boiling) test for heat stability of milk.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

testing.

consistent milk quality.

and compliance.

CUTM/FIC/N0102: In-process and outgoing milk quality check.
Description
The NOS "In-Process and Outgoing Milk Quality Check" covers monitoring milk quality during processing
and final product inspection. It includes testing key parameters, detecting deviations, ensuring compliance
with regulatory standards, maintaining hygiene, documenting results, and coordinating with production
and quality teams to ensure consistent, safe, and high-quality milk products.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS "In-Process and Outgoing Milk Quality Check" includes monitoring milk quality at different
processing stages, conducting chemical, physical, and microbiological tests, detecting deviations,
ensuring compliance with industry standards, maintaining hygiene protocols, documenting test
results, and collaborating with production and quality assurance teams to ensure safe and high-
quality milk products.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check presence or absence of alkaline phosphatase enzyme in milk by phosphatase test.
PC2. transfer 5 ml of buffer substrate solution (mixture of disodium p- nitrophenyl phosphate and
carbonate- bicarbonate buffer) in test tube and place in water bath maintained at 37 degrees
C.
PC3. ensure to mix content thoroughly after 5 min by adding 1 ml of milk.
PC4. prepare blank from boiled milk and incubate all tubes in water bath at 37 degrees C for 2 hr.
After 2hr remove tubes and compare colour using comparator and special disk.
PC5. verify tube containing raw or unpasteurized milk to show yellow colour whereas the boiled
milk to remain white in colour
PC6. verify fat, SNF and added water by using milk analyzer instrument after pasteurization or by
manually using Gerber method and using lactometer.
PC7. ensure to pump in the milk sample in sensing system to measure milk temperature. Milk type
is selected by following the required steps. Required steps: Menu > Setting > Select milk type
then arrow mark to select it. Press (*) key.
PC8. ensure to pump out milk sample, once count down timer stops and values of fat, SNF and
added water are shown.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Key quality parameters of in-process and outgoing milk, including fat content, SNF, microbial
load, acidity, and temperature.

CUTM/FIC/N0103: Maintenance of milk testing equipment (milk analyzer)
Description
The NOS "Maintenance of Milk Testing Equipment (Milk Analyzer)" covers routine cleaning, calibration,
troubleshooting, and preventive maintenance of milk analyzers. It includes ensuring accurate test results,
following safety protocols, documenting maintenance activities, identifying malfunctions, and coordinating
with technical teams or service providers for repairs to maintain equipment efficiency and compliance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS "Maintenance of Milk Testing Equipment (Milk Analyzer)" includes routine cleaning,
calibration, troubleshooting, and preventive maintenance of milk analyzers. It covers identifying
technical issues, ensuring accurate test results, following safety and hygiene protocols, maintaining
records, and coordinating with service providers for repairs to ensure optimal equipment
performance and regulatory compliance.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. ensure milk sample should be homogeneous, free from air bubbles and temperature of milk
should be 10 oC to 40oC.
PC2. ensure milk is cooled and some of fats gets separated. Heat the milk to 40 – 42oC in water
bath slowly and cool it down to 20-25oC for dissolving separated fat.
PC3. assure to stir milk while heating and cooling by ultrasonic stirrer to remove air bubbles.
PC4. manage rinsing three times by five cycles of water pumped in and out by selecting
appropriate option. Contaminated water is poured out Option : Menu > Cleaning > Rinse
PC5. manage daily cleaning process by using 2% alkaline solution, prepared by mixing thoroughly
490 ml water in 10 ml daily clean concentrate Option : Menu > Cleaning > Daily Clean
PC6. clean in and outlet pipes with fresh water by using sample container and then by alkaline
solution and rinse thrice.
PC7. ensure to clean the in and out pipes with fresh water after alkaline wash.
PC8. facilitate weekly cleaning by using 10% acid solution, prepared by mixing thoroughly 50 ml
weekly clean concentrate and 450 ml water Option: Menu > Cleaning > Weekly Clean
PC9. cleanse in and outlet pipes with fresh water by using sample container and then by acid
weekly solution and rinse for 5 times.
PC10. ensure to clean in and out pipes with fresh water after the acid wash Option: click the Menu
key to come out of cleaning procedure
PC11. carry out manual cleaning by switching off machine and keeping fresh water in sample
stand. Remove rubber plug and insert syringe plunger instead.
PC12. pull up and down plunger several times in order to get out milk residues out. Repeat process
3 - 4 times.
PC13. keep container on surface and using plunger check that no water is present inside sensor
system.

CUTM/VSQ/N7801: Ensure organizational health, safety, and security
Description
This NOS focuses on maintaining workplace safety, health, and security by identifying hazards, following
safety procedures, using personal protective equipment (PPE), and responding to emergencies. It ensures
compliance with health and safety regulations, proper waste disposal, and adherence to security protocols
to create a safe and productive work environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers workplace hazard identification, adherence to safety regulations, proper use of
personal protective equipment (PPE), emergency response, fire safety, first aid, and waste
management. It also includes implementing security measures, ensuring workplace hygiene, and
promoting a safety-conscious culture to prevent accidents and maintain a secure working
environment.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify different kinds of possible hazards at the workplace hazards: environmental,
personal, ergonomic and chemical.
PC2. recognize other possible security issues existing in the workplace.
PC3. plan safety techniques for self and others.
PC4. recognize different measures to curb the hazards.
PC5. implement programs for health, safety and security of the people at the workplace.
PC6. communicate the safety plans to everyone at the workplace.
PC7. attach disciplinary rules with the implementation at the workplace.
PC8. comply with regulatory health and safety guidelines applicable to the workplace.
PC9. use and maintain protective equipment as per requirement in a production house.
PC10. ensure organizational activities in line with approved guidelines and procedures.
PC11. undertake and advise healthy lifestyle for self and others.
PC12. follow environment management system related procedures.
PC13. ensure safe handling and disposal of waste and debris.
PC14. minimize health and safety risks to self and others due to own actions.
PC15. monitor the workplace and work processes for potential risks and threats to self and others.
PC16. carry out periodic check to keep work area free from hazards and obstructions.
PC17. ensure participation of self and others in mock drills/ evacuation procedures organized at the
workplace.
PC18. organize first aid, firefighting and emergency training sessions for others.
PC19. instruct and guide others to take action in the event of fire, emergencies or accidents.
