#### WBSC/HCS/Q2201: Jute Product Maker
Brief Job Description
A Jute Product Maker is responsible for crafting a diverse range of jute-based handicraft items,
demonstrating to design and assemble products such as bags, home decor items and accessories. They
select appropriate jute materials, employ various crafting techniques, ensure product quality through
inspections, manage inventory and may customize items to meet client requirements.
Personal Attributes
A Jute Product Maker should possess creativity and artistic sense, customer-focused approach, dedication
to craftmanship, attention to detail, teamwork, physical stamina, technical aptitude, communication skills,
numerical ability, organizational skills and safety consciousness to design and craft a diverse range of jute-
based products.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. WBSC/HCS/N2201: Apply safe working Practices (Jute Product Maker).

7. WBSC/HCS/N2207: Set up a small jute product Business unit.
8. WBSC/HCS/N2208: Work in real job situation with emphasis on basic safety and hazards (Jute
Product Maker).

#### WBSC/HCS/N2201: Apply safe working Practices (Jute Product Maker).
Description
Apply safe working Practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Safe working environment in line with occupational health and safety regulations.
Any unsafe situations according to site policy.
Necessary precautions on fire and safety hazards.
Personal Productive Equipment (PPE).
Basic first aid & CPR.
Different types of fire extinguishers and their use.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Maintain procedures to achieve a safe working environment in line with occupational health
and safety regulations and requirements according to site policy.
- PC2. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assess his report accordingly.
- PC3. Identify and take necessary precautions on fire and safety hazards and report according to
site policy and procedures.
- PC4. Demonstrate Personal Productive Equipment (PPE) use the same as per related working
environment.
- PC5. Demonstrate basic first aid & CPR and use them under different circumstances.
- PC6. Identify different fire extinguishers and use the same as per requirement in a mock drill.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Procedure to ensure a safe working environment.
- KU2. Occupational health and safety regulations and requirements in accordance with site policy.
- KU3. Implementation procedure to measure to prevent accidents and ensure the well being of all personnel.
- KU4. Unsafe situations in the workplace.
- KU5. Potential fire and safety hazards.
- KU6. Correct use of personal protective equipment (PPE) in line with the related working environment.
- KU7. Basic first aid and CPR techniques.
- KU8. Different types of fire extinguishers and their applications.

#### WBSC/HCS/N2203: Prepare jute fiber from raw jute & staining of jute
fiber.
Description
Prepare jute fiber from raw jute & staining of jute fiber.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Different system extracting of jute fiber from raw jute.
Different types of post production of jute fiber from raw jute with proper examples.
Various tools and equipment along with its utility for jute fiber from raw jute & staining of jute fiber.
The selection of site and principles of profitable jute fiber from raw jute & staining of jute fiber.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the different system extracting of jute fiber from raw jute.
- PC2. Describe different types of post production of jute fiber from raw jute with proper examples.
- PC3. Explain various tools and equipment along with its utility for jute fiber from raw jute & staining
of jute fiber.
- PC4. Describe the selection of site and principles of profitable jute fiber from raw jute & staining of
jute fiber.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Various systems involved in extracting jute fiber from raw jute.
- KU2. Various post production processes for jute fiber from raw jute.
- KU3. Tools and equipment used in the extraction of jute fiber from jute.
- KU4. Criteria for selecting a suitable site for jute fiber extraction.
- KU5. The significance of each system in obtaining high quality jute fiber.
- KU6. The environmental impact of jute production compared to synthetic materials.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Demonstrate various systems for extracting jute fiber from raw jute.
- GS2. Perform post production processes such as retting, breaking, scutching and hackling.
- GS3. Identify the tools and equipment for jute fiber extraction.
- GS4. Identify a suitable site for jute fiber extraction.
