#### ELE/Q6705: Intelligent Security Technology
Brief Job Description
This training program equips learners with the knowledge and skills required to design, install, optimize,
and maintain modern security and intelligent systems. It combines engineering fundamentals, safety and
regulatory compliance, digital and AI technologies, and practical industry practices to prepare
professionals for end-to-end security system implementation and operations.
Personal Attributes
Detail-oriented, safety-conscious, and technically competent professional with strong analytical thinking,
problem-solving ability, teamwork, and commitment to continuous learning in intelligent security
technologies.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ELE/N6716: Work organization and management (Intelligent Security Technology)

#### ELE/N6716: Work  organization and management  (Intelligent Security
Technology)
Description
This NOS is about to plan, organize, and manage tasks effectively to ensure timely completion of work
while maintaining quality and safety standards. Coordinate resources, communicate efficiently with team
members, and demonstrate responsibility, discipline, and professional work ethics.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Work organization and management
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Comply with applicable health, safety, and environmental regulations, including correct
selection and use of personal protective equipment (PPE), to ensure safe working practices at all times.
- PC2. Apply first aid principles and emergency response procedures effectively to address
workplace incidents and minimize risk to personnel and assets.
- PC3. Interpret and adhere to international, national, and organizational standards, regulations,
and guidelines relevant to the construction and implementation of security and protection systems.
- PC4. Follow official approval and compliance processes required for constructing security and
protection systems, in accordance with system type, purpose, and jurisdictional requirements.
- PC5. Create, read, and interpret engineering drawings, layout plans, and configuration documents
to ensure accurate installation and integration of security and protection systems.
- PC6. Plan and manage project activities by applying principles of cost control, quality assurance,
scheduling, and timely completion to achieve optimal project efficiency.
- PC7. Implement electrical safety measures, including grounding, bonding, and lightning protection
practices, during construction and installation activities.
- PC8. Select, use, maintain, clean, and store tools, equipment, and materials safely and efficiently,
while managing inventory, material flow, and on-site logistics.
- PC9. Maintain accurate records of work progress, design changes, inspections, and test results,
ensuring data integrity, security, and traceability for operation and maintenance purposes.
- PC10. Demonstrate professional development by staying updated with emerging technologies,
regulations, systems, and methods, and proactively sharing knowledge and improvement ideas with stakeholders.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

#### ELE/N6717: Communication  and  interpersonal skills (Intelligent Security
Technology)
Description
This NOS is about to effectively communicate technical and non-technical information with clients, team
members, and stakeholders to ensure clear understanding of security system requirements and operations. Demonstrate professionalism, teamwork, active listening, and problem-solving skills to build
strong working relationships and ensure successful project execution in Intelligent Security Technology.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communication and interpersonal skills
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Listen actively to colleagues and stakeholders, asking relevant questions to enhance
understanding of complex situations and work requirements.
- PC2. Communicate clearly and professionally with colleagues using appropriate verbal and written
communication methods.
- PC3. Adapt communication style and approach to meet the changing needs, roles, and
expectations of colleagues and team members.
- PC4. Establish and maintain effective working relationships with colleagues and managers to
support collaborative and productive work environments.
- PC5. Apply principles and techniques of teamwork to lead and/or support the development of
strong, efficient, and cohesive teams.
- PC6. Communicate effectively with external organizations, authorities, and stakeholders to obtain
required project approvals, permissions, or consents.
- PC7. Share professional knowledge and skills with colleagues and provide guidance, coaching, or
support to enhance team capability and performance.
- PC8. Identify, manage, and resolve misunderstandings, tension, and conflicts professionally to
support positive and constructive outcomes.
- PC9. Apply structured problem-solving processes and techniques to generate options, evaluate
alternatives, and implement appropriate solutions.
- PC10. Maintain concentration, focus, and professional demeanour while working in complex, multi-
system, and high-pressure environments.

#### ELE/N6724: Project initiation and holistic planning. (Intelligent Security
Technology)
Description
This NOS is about to initiate security technology projects by analyzing client requirements, conducting site
assessments, and defining scope, objectives, and technical specifications. Develop comprehensive project
plans covering resources, timelines, risk management, compliance, and system integration to ensure
efficient and successful implementation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Project initiation and holistic planning
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Prepare for participation in the sales process by reviewing organizational sales structures,
procedures, market research, and consulting with relevant colleagues.
- PC2. Identify and understand the range of roles and responsibilities that contribute to the sales
and solution development process.
- PC3. Engage with clients using structured questioning and analysis techniques to accurately
define, interpret, and document client needs and expectations.
- PC4. Operate within the scope and limits of one’s professional role while engaging with clients,
escalating requirements appropriately where necessary.
- PC5. Inspect the asset requiring security solutions (such as a building, infrastructure, or
environment) to validate, refine, or modify initial client requirements.
- PC6. Analyze technical, operational, and financial feasibility of potential security solutions,
including alternative options and their implications.
- PC7. Formulate and present viable solution options in coordination with internal experts,
professionals, and external suppliers.
- PC8. Apply standard or bespoke costing and pricing methods to estimate installation costs,
system components, and alternative solutions with financial comparisons.
- PC9. Create and/or interpret technical specifications and blueprints using appropriate tools and
software for security system infrastructure and components.
- PC10. Liaise with relevant colleagues and continuously review and validate design, costing, and
documentation throughout the solution development process to ensure accuracy and compliance.

Description

conditions, and client requirements to develop technically sound and scalable solutions. Prepare system

Planning and design of security systems

understand and document user and stakeholder requirements for security systems.

security system solutions.
- PC3. Interpret and comply with applicable standards governing infrastructure, auxiliary
components, and system integration.
- PC4. Read, interpret, and analyze technical drawings and specifications in accordance with
recognized industry standards and regulations.
- PC5. Create clear and compliant security management system diagrams and architecture layouts
using appropriate drawing tools.

of security system projects.
- PC7. Design scientifically sound, practical, and scalable security system solutions aligned to
specific technologies and products.
- PC8. Evaluate project environments and site conditions to enhance system safety, reliability, and
performance.
- PC9. Analyze project plans and specifications for intelligent security systems to ensure technical
feasibility and compliance.
- PC10. Develop detailed design plans and documentation in compliance with applicable
international standards and best practices.

#### ELE/N6719: Installation and implementation of security systems
(Intelligent Security Technology)
Description
This NOS is about to install and implement intelligent security systems by following technical specifications, safety standards, and approved design layouts. Ensure proper integration, configuration,
testing, and commissioning of security equipment to achieve reliable and efficient system performance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Installation and implementation of security systems

implementation of security system applications.
- PC2. Evaluate construction and site environments to prepare appropriately for secure, efficient,
and compliant project execution.
- PC3. Select optimal security system design solutions that align with specific application
requirements and operational conditions.
- PC4. Read, interpret, and apply engineering drawings, wiring diagrams, schematics, technical
manuals, and specifications accurately.
- PC5. Assess system functionality and identify key operational and performance parameters for
security system components.
- PC6. Select appropriate components and devices based on technical specifications, application
characteristics, and compatibility
- PC7. Use specialized instruments, tools, and meters to support correct installation, testing, and
verification of security systems.
- PC8. Apply scientifically sound, energy-efficient, and environmentally sustainable installation
practices.
- PC9. Adhere strictly to applicable safety regulations and implement necessary safety measures
throughout installation activities.
- PC10. Complete security system installations accurately and systematically in accordance with
approved construction drawings and specifications.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Knowledge of client requirement analysis techniques and application assessment methods for
effective planning and implementation of security system installations.

#### ELE/N6721: Debugging  and optimization of security systems (Intelligent
Security Technology)
Description
This NOS is about to Identify, troubleshoot, and rectify faults in intelligent security systems using
systematic diagnostic methods and technical tools. Optimize system performance through configuration
adjustments, firmware updates, and performance testing to ensure reliability and efficiency.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
