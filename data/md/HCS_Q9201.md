#### HCS/Q9201: Handcrafted Candle Maker
Brief Job Description
Candle Makers are responsible for using a combination of wax and wicks in a flame to create a product by
using manual dipping methods. They use their artistic and design skills to create custom candle designs
and work to ensure all products conform to manufacturing standards
Personal Attributes
A chandler should have a good eye for color and design to work with molten wax, large machinery and
ability to stand for long periods. And have in-depth knowledge of candle waxes and their properties
including beeswax, paraffin wax and soy wax.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N9201: Preparing Wax for making Candle

#### HCS/N9201: Preparing Wax for making Candle
Description
This unit is about preparing wax by using raw material and performing various steps.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
prepare raw material for preparing wax prepare three different waxes for making candle melting solid form of wax by using double boiler technique
prepare fragrance oil and color check quality standards of melting wax
#### Elements and Performance Criteria prepare raw material for preparing wax
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. collect raw material like beeswax, soy wax, paraffin wax, double boiler, pouring pot, color
and essential oils
- PC2. collect tools like silicone spatula and thermometer
prepare three different waxes for making candle
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. use soy wax, beeswax along with paraffin wax for the better quality.
- PC4. make sure melting Paraffin wax at around 100 degrees
- PC5. melt soy wax at approximately 124 degrees
- PC6. melt bee wax at around 145 degrees
melting solid form of wax by using double boiler technique
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. pour the flakes into a pouring pot and measure the same amount again, because the flakes
melt down so much.
- PC8. melt solid form of wax is in a unique technique called double boiler method and slowly warm
the wax, using spatula
- PC9. follow this method of double boiler to melting the wax as it prevents the wax from catching
flame and also controls the temperature.
- PC10. use double boiler for melting the wax, 0.5/4th of water is poured into the boiler and the
blocks of wax are allowed to melt at 50-55 degree Celsius approximately.
- PC11. make sure the vertically lengthy candles, wax is melted at 59 degrees Celsius as to improve
the long-lasting potency of the candles a hardening agent is added to wax.
prepare fragrance oil and color

#### HCS/N9202: Preparing candle container and wick
Description
This unit provides Performance Criteria, Knowledge & Understanding and Skills & Abilities required to carry
out pouring molten wax and taper candle
Scope
The scope covers the following :
carry out processing of raw material prepare beeswax taper candle pouring molten wax to the silicone baking mold
prepare cotton thread as the wick to light the candle.
check quality of prepared candle
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out processing of raw material
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. collect raw material like Container or silicone baking mold, beeswax, soy wax, paraffin wax
hot glue, hex nut, candle wick, metal holder or wick holder and cleaning cloth
- PC2. collect tools like wick trimmer, butter knives and for measuring like digital scale and
thermometer
Prepare beeswax taper candle
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. cut 2 to4 wick to 25 approx. in length
- PC4. tie each end of the wick with a hex nut
- PC5. fill a tall jar with cold water and set it aside
- PC6. fold the wick in half and knot the nuts at both ends in the center
- PC7. dip the ends slowly into the wax
- PC8. dip the wicks into a jar of cold water
- PC9. return to hot wax and then to the cool water. Repeat for a total of 10 times
- PC10. cut the weight as near to the nut as possible at the bottom
- PC11. dipping the tapered candle back and forth between the wax and the water until the desired
candle diameter is achieved
Pouring molten wax to the silicone baking mold
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. ensure glass container and metal molds (made from aluminium material) needs hot soapy
water to clean.
- PC13. use silicone baking mold to shape the remaining molten beeswax and add soy wax, paraffin
wax to making the candles hard
- PC14. check the container surface is clean of any dust, dirt or residue.

#### HCS/N9203: Preparing design and different layers of colors on candle
Description
This unit is about producing handmade candle products by strategically cutting and shaping the hot candle

#### Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
