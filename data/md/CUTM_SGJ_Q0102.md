CUTM/SGJ/Q0102: Paper Recycling Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The paper recycling Supervisor should be able to collect market information for business planning and
decision making, set-up paper recycling plant, operate the plant, manage the team, finance and
administrative activities along with marketing, making quality products ensuring organizational health,
safety and security.
Personal Attributes
A Paper Recycling Supervisor should be organized, responsible, and environmentally conscious. They must
possess leadership qualities, technical knowledge of recycling processes, and the ability to manage teams
and workflows. Attention to detail, safety awareness, problem-solving skills, and effective communication
are essential for maintaining productivity and quality in recycling operations.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CUTM/SGJ/N0104: Ensure setting up of paper recycling plant
- 2. CUTM/SGJ/N0105: Overseeing of paper recycling operation, monitoring & troubleshoot

CUTM/SGJ/N0104: Ensure setting up of paper recycling plant
Description
This NOS focuses on business planning, resource assessment, legal compliance and operational
groundwork. It covers market research, estimation of plant requirements, identifying funding schemes,
preparing project proposals, selecting equipment, and fulfilling environmental and safety regulations to
establish a functional paper recycling unit efficiently.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of the NOS includes identifying market needs, collecting business data, selecting
appropriate technology, estimating costs, securing finances, and complying with legal and
environmental guidelines to establish a functional, safe, and efficient recycling plant for sustainable
paper production.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. gather market information for collection of various waste paper resources like direct or
indirect
PC2. collect various types of high demanded, recycled paper products available in the market
PC3. • collect information for estimating market demand for products at various levels, Levels: Local,
regional, national,
• and international
PC4. • select type of recycled product by obtaining information on costs of various resources required
for setting up of plant
• resources: land, building, paper waster, power, water, skilled manpower & operational cost, etc.
PC5. Plan, prepare project proposal with financials on capital investment, operational expenses
and profit
PC6. evaluate various options for raising capital options: personal, loans, micro financing, SME
finance, crowd funding, etc
PC7. analyze and choose appropriate scheme from the list provided by the government for
entrepreneurial development in paper recycling project
PC8. identify appropriate legal ownership structure suitable to the nature of the business activity
and convenience of operations
PC9. identify various legal, health and safety regulations and guidelines applicable
PC10. select and register a business name for organization by checking availability and duplicity of
name
PC11. design an appropriate trademark or logo for the organization with aesthetic features
features: appropriate, unique, portray business activities, attractive, simple, etc.
PC12. identify different locations for paper waste collection like recycler and paper merchants
PC13. collect and pack the paper waste together for easy transportation
PC14. sort out the recovered waste paper by its grade, surface treatment and structure

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. how to collect and analyze market data to estimate demand for recycled paper products at
local, regional, and national levels.
KU2. how to assess costs and availability of essential resources like land, water, power, skilled
manpower, and raw materials for establishing a recycling unit.
KU3. applicable legal structures, health and safety standards, business registration processes, and
government schemes that support recycling entrepreneurship.
KU4. how to draft a business plan including capital investment, operational expenses, and
potential profitability.
KU5. about various financial support options such as loans, microfinancing, and crowdfunding for
setting up the recycling plant.
KU6. suitable locations for the plant based on logistics, accessibility, and availability of waste
paper. Know how to organize physical setup and infrastructure.
KU7. the operation and maintenance of recycling equipment like shredders, beater machines, and
pulp processors used during setup and production.
KU8. techniques to identify, sort, and prepare different grades of paper waste for recycling
operations.
KU9. the importance of designing a business name, logo, and trademark that reflect the nature
and uniqueness of the recycling business.
KU10. the strategies for sourcing waste paper from recyclers, merchants, and community
partnerships, ensuring efficient transportation and storage.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. gather market intelligence on waste paper sources, recycled product demand, and customer
preferences at local, regional, and national levels.
GS2. prepare detailed business proposals including capital investment, operational costs,
profitability forecasts, and financial sourcing (e.g., loans, government schemes).
GS3. be proficient in understanding of legal structures, licensing, safety regulations, and
organizational registration requirements for establishing a recycling unit.
GS4. be competent in evaluating land, water, power, manpower, and equipment needs for setting
up the plant and choosing suitable locations.
GS5. be best in organizing and managing collection, segregation, packing, and transportation of
different grades of waste paper efficiently.
GS6. operate recycling processes such as pulping, de-inking, sheet formation, drying, and finishing
especially for planning plant layout and equipment flow.
GS7. identify machinery suppliers, negotiate purchases, and coordinate installation, setup, and
team training for operations.

CUTM/SGJ/N0105: Overseeing of paper recycling operation, monitoring &
troubleshoot
Description
This NOS involves managing and supervising the recycling process, monitoring plant operations, and
ensuring timely maintenance. It includes setting up plant infrastructure, training staff, checking equipment
functionality, handling breakdowns, maintaining safety, and ensuring smooth operation of all machinery to
produce quality recycled paper products. This NOS also covers documentation, preventive maintenance
schedules, and rapid troubleshooting to avoid major disruptions in operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of the NOS includes managing daily operations of the recycling plant. This covers setting
up equipment, supervising processing stages, ensuring quality output, maintaining machinery, and
troubleshooting technical issues to avoid disruptions and maintain optimal efficiency.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Estimate the plant capacity requirement based on the market potential, paper waste
collection capacity and budget of the projects
PC2. Identify the land and building location to set-up recycling plant based on the capacity
PC3. Identify the recycling plant equipment supplier and negotiate the pricing
PC4. Issue purchase order for the plant equipment, set-up, installation and training
PC5. Identify the facilities required for plant set up Facilities: power, water and floor type
PC6. Prepare all the required infra before plant commissioning
PC7. Prepare floor map for proper location of each equipment based on size and working area
required
PC8. • Arrange necessary power supply to each equipment
•
PC9. Assist the plant installation team as per their requirement
PC10. Check the working condition of each equipment
PC11. Train the team on operation and maintenance of the plant
PC12. Check the quality of the product from the installed plant
PC13. • Complete all documentation work for the plant
•
PC14. plan regular preventive and predictive maintenance schedule as per equipment supplier
recommendation
PC15. train the team to do breakdown, preventive and predictive maintenance
PC16. follow of the complete of regular maintenance as per schedule
PC17. complete repair work as soon as issue faced to avoid major breakdown

CUTM/SGJ/N0106: Plan activities for increasing sales of recycled paper
products
Description
This NOS focuses on enhancing product quality, packaging, marketing, and customer engagement. It
emphasizes analyzing market trends, identifying prospective clients, taking feedback, pricing
competitively, and adopting promotion strategies to boost sales and customer satisfaction.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes developing efficient production processes, maintaining product quality, setting
competitive pricing, conducting market research, gathering client feedback, implementing
promotional strategies, and ensuring timely distribution to increase sales and customer satisfaction.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check the working condition of various equipment of recycling plant
PC2. identify the type and grade of paper to be recycled
PC3. set-up the machine parameter as per the input paper type, quantity
PC4. make changes in the process parameter of the equipment
PC5. feed the waste paper on the chopper machine to cut into small pieces. Vary the cutting
parameter to get required size of cut pieces
PC6. transfer cut pieces of paper into beater machine based on the capacity of the machine
PC7. switch on the beater machine and run up to 8 hours to make it into a pulp
PC8. move the pulp into storage tank for further processing
PC9. transfer the required quantity to paper lifting VAT machine to get proper pulp sheet
PC10. send the sheet to screw press machine to dry out 80% of water content in the pulp sheet
PC11. store the sheet up to 20 hours for natural dry
PC12. load the dried sheet to calendar machine with high pressure to get the required surface
finish and thickness of the paper sheet
PC13. cut the sheet to the required size using paper cut machine
PC14. check the quality of the paper as per requirement
PC15. count the required quantity and pack the paper bundle for various application
PC16. dispatch the recycled paper to next stage for further process
PC17. achieve the required productivity to meet the delivery time
PC18. achieve the required quality by ensuring proper process steps followed each stage
PC19. follow the safety standards to avoid any accidents, damage to the machine, product or
personnel
PC20. report problem to the concerned immediately for rectification

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the full paper recycling workflow from waste paper collection and shredding, to pulping,
drying, finishing, and packaging of the final product
KU2. working knowledge of equipment such as chopper machines, beaters, paper lifting VATs,
screw presses, calendar machines, and paper cutters used throughout the production line
KU3. how to achieve desired productivity targets and maintain the quality of paper sheets through
adherence to proper procedures and timely troubleshooting
KU4. how to prepare recycled paper products for market by ensuring their quality, quantity, and
professional packaging to meet customer expectations and market standards
KU5. aware of paper quality benchmarks such as thickness, strength, surface finish, odor control,
and moisture content as part of ensuring customer satisfaction
KU6. to research current market pricing, seasonal demand patterns, and pricing strategies to stay
competitive and relevant in the recycled paper market
KU7. consumer preferences, motivations, and buying habits to effectively tailor marketing and
sales strategies
KU8. how to collect and act on client feedback regarding product quality, delivery timelines, and
responsiveness to enhance customer relationships
KU9. develop familiarity with customer relationship management systems (CRM), cold calling
techniques, email outreach strategies, and customer data tracking
KU10. acquire skills to analyze sales metrics, KPIs, and market response in order to continuously
optimize sales performance and strategic decisions
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. monitor and adjust machinery parameters (e.g., chopper, beater, VAT machines) to ensure the
