#### PSS/Q2416: Smart  Grid Technician
Brief Job Description
The incumbent in the job is responsible for operation and maintenance of smart grid. The role of the
technician is to undertake both planned and reactive operation and maintenance tasks across the range of
smart grid systems.
Personal Attributes
The job holder must have an eye for detail as well as the patience and discipline required to carry out
detailed and repetitive tasks. The candidate should be able to read and understand technical manuals,
instructions, warnings and adhere to applicable safety rules and regulations.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PSS/N2409: Carry Out Maintenance Of Smart Grid

#### PSS/N2409: Carry Out Maintenance  Of Smart Grid
Description
This unit is about performing various tasks for routine and preventive maintenance of smart grid.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for maintenance work
Perform maintenance activities
Troubleshooting and repairing of faulty equipment
Perform post-maintenance activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for maintenance work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Determine the routine maintenance requirements of various components and control system
by interpreting manufacturer manual, maintenance checklist and schedule, work instructions, etc
- PC2. Read maintenance schedule and checklist and plan the time and schedule for conducting the
maintenance activities
- PC3. Identify various systems, sub systems and control systems of the smart grid
- PC4. Arrange tools, consumables, spare parts, etc. required for performing repair and
maintenance
- PC5. Clear the work area thoroughly to ensure no unwanted materials are present before starting
the work
- PC6. Ensure that devices, machines etc. in the smart grid are not connected to any power source
before starting maintenance work
- PC7. Adhere to all safety norms (such as wearing protective gloves and shoes, etc.) in accordance
with the international/ national and organisational standards
- PC8. Inform all affected parties of the intended work plan prior to disconnecting power supply line
Perform maintenance activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Perform basic health check-up of instruments, sensors, controllers etc. as specified in the
manufacturer manual
- PC10. Start and shut down process of smart grid to observe the functioning of power plant and its
systems under the supervision of supervisor/engineer
- PC11. Check the technical and mechanical systems to ensure their operation within the specified
parameters
- PC12. Inspect power stations, transmission lines, distribution lines, automated systems and other
critical equipment for identification of faults, possible wear and tear etc.

#### PSS/N1720: Perform  Operation Of Smart Grid
Description
This unit is about performing activities related to operation, monitoring and controlling of smart grid
components i.e. power stations, transmission lines, and distribution lines under the guidance of
supervisor/engineer and as per the specified standards and procedures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform operation of smart grid
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform operation of smart grid

regulations

other automation system installed in the grid

#### PSS/N1337: Apply Basic Health and Safety Practices for Power Related
Work
Description
This unit covers health, safety and security for power related work. This includes procedures and practices
that candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work environment in a power
plant, power station/substation or on the field while working on power equipment. It covers responsibilities
towards self, others, organizational assets and the environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Follow workplace health and safety practices
Follow fire safety practices
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow workplace health and safety practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. use protective clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions (Protective clothing:
leather or asbestos gloves, flame proof aprons, flame proof overalls buttoned to neck, cuff less (without folds) trousers, reinforced footwear, helmets/hard hats, cap and shoulder
covers, ear defenders/plugs, safety boots, knee pads, particle masks, glasses/goggles/visors)
(Equipment: hand and face shields, machine guards, residual current devices, shields, dust sheets, respirator)
- PC2. identify job-site hazards and possible causes of risks/accident at the workplace (Hazards:
electrical hazards, sharp edged and heavy tools, heated metals, oxyfuel and gas cylinders, welding radiation, hazardous surfaces, hazardous substances, physical hazards. Possible
causes of risk and accident: physical actions, not following instructions, inattention, sickness
and incapacity, health, not taking safety precautions)
- PC3. use standard safe working practices when working at heights, confined areas, trenches and
with electrical equipment
- PC4. follow warning signs and symbols while working with electrical systems (Warning signs:
Danger plate, high voltage area, out of service, etc.)
- PC5. ensure positive isolation of electrical equipment and system as per the given standards
- PC6. identify any abnormalities in electrical equipment or system viz temperature, pressure, flow
of current etc.
- PC7. use various methods of accident prevention in the work environment (Methods of accident
prevention: use of equipment and working practices, safety notices, advice, instruction from colleagues and supervisors)
- PC8. use proper scaffolds, elevated platforms and ladders for working at height
- PC9. inspect various tools and plants (T & P) routinely for any signs of oil, water leakages
- PC10. store flammable materials and machine lubricating oil safely and correctly
