#### RSC/Q8016: Operator-Plastic Products Making (Plastic Toy/ Ball Pen)
Brief Job Description
The individual at work is responsible for the assembling, finishing and inspecting plastic products.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be calm, physically fit, and a keen observer along with an eye for detail.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N8027: Carry out assembling and finishing of plastic products
2. RSC/N8028: Carry out inspection activities of plastic products
3. RSC/N5610: Coordinate and communicate effectively at the workplace
4. RSC/N5001: Carry out housekeeping
5. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
6. RSC/N5603: Follow ethical and sustainable practices at the workplace
7. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Plastic toy finishing and packaging
The individual at work is responsible for finishing and packaging toys.
1. RSC/N8029: Perform toy finishing activities
2. RSC/N8030: Package the toys for shipment
Elective 2: Plastic ball pen assembling and finishing
The individual at work is responsible for assembling and finishing of plastic ball pen.
1. RSC/N8031: Assembling and finishing of plastic ball pen

#### RSC/N8027: Carry out assembling and finishing of plastic products
Description
The OS unit is about preparing and performing assembling and finishing activities for plastic products as
per SOP.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for plastic product assembling and finishing activities
Perform plastic products assembling and finishing activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for plastic product assembling and finishing activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. obtain the job card cum product design features and drawing from the supervisor
- PC2. check the type of assembling requirements like mechanical, welding and solvent bonding, or
adhesive
- PC3. check the specifications like size, shape, colour, material properties, characteristics, etc.
- PC4. identify and collect various components, tools, accessories, and other materials as per work
instructions
- PC5. ensure work area is cleaned as per SOP
- PC6. make sure required tools, equipment, auxiliary tools, machinery are cleaned and hazard free
as per standards
- PC7. perform pre-operational checks on the machine and equipment
- PC8. set up tools and equipment as per the supervisor’s instructions
Perform plastic products assembling and finishing activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. operate machines, equipment, tools to cut, shape, splice and fit plastic material to form parts
and assemblies
- PC10. carry out assembling operations such as adhesive joining, mechanical joining, plastic welding
as required
- PC11. assemble composite materials on patterns to form parts and assemblies using bonding
agents while welding/ adhesive joining
- PC12. apply resin mixtures to metal or wooden mould to form plastic products
- PC13. load and operate autoclave oven to cure and bond plastic and composite parts and sub-
assemblies
- PC14. trim, grind or buff plastic products to form final product
- PC15. clean the area after assembling and finishing to ensure work environment is maintained in a
safe and productive manner
- PC16. ensure safe usage of machines

#### RSC/N8028: Carry out inspection activities of plastic products
Description
The OS unit is about performing inspection and post- inspection activities as per SOP.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform inspection and post- inspection activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform inspection and post- inspection activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. measure the specifications of the finished products as per quality standards
- PC2. carry out inspection of manufactured plastic products for defects using visual methods or
instruments
- PC3. make minor adjustments and repairs to the product
- PC4. affix seals and tags to mark approved plastic product and defective products
- PC5. re-route defective products for repair or recycling process
- PC6. prepare product inspection report and submit them to the appropriate authority
- PC7. assist supervisor in training new workers
- PC8. prepare logs and records as per organizational standards
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. methods to perform inspection of manufactured plastic products
- KU2. types of defects like flow lines, weld lines, burn marks, assembling, finishing, etc.
- KU3. how to make minor adjustments and repairs to the product
- KU4. SOP to affix seals, tags, and mark to the approved products
- KU5. how to fill inspection report format
- KU6. basic training procedure
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. read and interpret job card and other related documents
- GS2. note down the information communicated by the supervisor
- GS3. communicate effectively with supervisor and team members

Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for housekeeping practices
Carry out housekeeping operations
Perform post housekeeping activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for housekeeping activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. inspect the area/s to identify the different types of surfaces that require cleaning

efficiency and type of stain
- PC3. ensure that cleaning equipment is in proper working condition

appropriate equipment and materials are not available

clean areas and surfaces

out

being used
Carry out housekeeping operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. ensure that the cleaning activity is carried out as per SOP
- PC10. manage accidental damage, as per the workplace procedure, caused while carrying out the
work
- PC11. report to the appropriate person regarding difficulties in carrying out the work
- PC12. identify and report to the appropriate person if any additional cleaning required that is
outside one's responsibility or skill
Perform post housekeeping activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. ensure that housekeeping equipment and supplies are stored and maintained as per
company standards
- PC14. ensure that, on completion of the work, the area is left clean and dry as per the requirements

#### RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
Description
This unit is about maintaining health and safety of self and others at workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
Render appropriate emergency procedures
Maintain standard safety procedures at the workplace
Participate in safety awareness campaigns
Understand potential sources of accidents
Use safety gears to avoid accidents
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. undertake basic safety checks before operation of all machinery and equipment and report
hazards to the appropriate supervisor
- PC2. work for which protective clothing or equipment is required is identified and the appropriate
protective clothing or equipment is used in performing these duties in accordance with workplace policy
- PC3. read and understand the hazards of use and contamination mentioned on the labels of
chemicals, utilities etc
