#### ICE/CON/Q1501: MEP  Executive
Brief Job Description
The MEP Executive provides essential support in the installation, maintenance and repair of Mechanical,
Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) systems. This role involves assisting MEP technicians and engineers with
various tasks, including reading blueprints, handling tools, performing basic installations and
troubleshooting minor issues in line with sector-specific standards. The individual collaborates with other
teams on-site or within facilities to ensure MEP systems operate efficiently, safely and in compliance with
industry codes and standards.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be physically fit and mentally alert. The person should have problem-solving,
coordination and appropriate verbal and written communication skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ICE/CON/N1523: Identify Tools, Equipment and Materials Relevant to MEP Work at a Construction
Site
2. ICE/CON/N1524: Read and interpret MEP system Drawings and Specifications
3. ICE/CON/N9901: Implement Safe Work Practices and Environmental Stewardship at Construction
Sites
4. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Mechanical Systems
1. ICE/CON/N1517: Assist in Installation of Mechanical Systems and HVAC Ducts
2. ICE/CON/N1518: Assist in Repair, Maintenance and Testing of Mechanical Systems
Elective 2: Electrical Systems
1. ICE/CON/N1519: Assist in Electrical Wiring Low Voltage (LV) Installations and Earthing systems
2. ICE/CON/N1520: Assist in Installation, Repair and Maintenance of Electrical Systems

#### ICE/CON/N1523: Identify Tools, Equipment and Materials Relevant to MEP
Work at a Construction Site
Description
This NOS defines the skills and knowledge required to identify, verify and handle tools, equipment and
materials used in Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) work at construction sites, ensuring readiness
for installation, operation and safe usage under supervision.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify Tools and Equipment for MEP Task
Identify Materials Used in MEP Works
Follow Safety and Storage Procedures for MEP Tools & Materials
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify Tools and Equipment for MEP Tasks
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify commonly used hand tools for mechanical works such as spanners, pliers, pipe
wrenches, threading tools and cutters.
- PC2. identify electrical tools including testers, multimeters, screwdrivers, crimping tools, cable
cutters, and insulation tools.
- PC3. identify plumbing tools such as pipe wrenches, PVC/CPVC cutters, threaders, pipe benders,
and jointing equipment.
- PC4. identify power tools relevant to MEP works, including drills, grinders, hydraulic benders, and
cable pulling machines.
- PC5. identify lifting and shifting equipment used for MEP material handling such as chain blocks,
trolleys, pulleys and scaffolds.
- PC6. verify the suitability of tools and equipment as per the assigned task and work method
statements.
- PC7. check tools and equipment for damage, defects or missing components before use.
- PC8. ensure that tools and equipment are calibrated, labelled and maintained as per
manufacturer’s guidelines.
Identify Materials Used in MEP Works
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. identify commonly used mechanical materials such as ducts, GI sheets, insulation material,
fasteners and hangers.
- PC10. identify electrical materials including cables, conduits, DBS, switches, sockets, MCCB, and
earthing components.
- PC11. identify plumbing materials such as pipes (PVC, CPVC, PPR, GI, HDPE), fittings, valves,
sealants, and tapes.
- PC12. understand the size, type and specification of MEP materials as per drawings and BOQs.

#### ICE/CON/N1524: Read  and interpret MEP system Drawings and
Specifications
Description
This NOS covers the ability to read, interpret and apply mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) system
drawings, layouts, schematics, legends and technical specifications for installation and site execution
work. The job requires strong understanding of MEP symbols, technical standards and interpretation of
BOQs, shop drawings, GFC (Good for Construction) drawings and other relevant documentation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Interpret Mechanical (HVAC & Fire-Fighting) Drawings
Interpret Electrical Drawings & Specifications
Interpret Plumbing & Public Health Engineering Drawings
Refer to BOQs, Specifications & Standards
Apply Drawing Interpretation in Site Execution
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Interpret Mechanical (HVAC & Fire-Fighting) Drawings
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify symbols, line types and notations used in HVAC, fire-fighting and mechanical
drawings.
- PC2. interpret duct routing, pipe routing, equipment layout and mounting locations.
- PC3. read duct sizes, pipe diameters, insulation details and damper/joint specifications.
- PC4. interpret mechanical schedules such as airflows, pump heads, equipment capacities and
material specifications.
- PC5. interpret coordination drawings including MEP clearance zones and ceiling space planning.
- PC6. verify drawing information such as elevations, section IDs and reference levels.
Interpret Electrical Drawings & Specifications
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. identify symbols for electrical equipment, lighting, wiring, switches, control systems and
earthing.
- PC8. interpret cable routing, conduit layouts, DB (Distribution Board) schedules and panel
diagrams.
- PC9. read cable sizes, circuit ratings, breaker capacities and load details.
- PC10. interpret SLD (Single Line Diagram) and schematics for power distribution.
- PC11. understand electrical layouts such as lighting, power socket, low voltage, CCTV and fire
alarm systems.
- PC12. verify technical compliance with electrical specifications and codes.
Interpret Plumbing & Public Health Engineering Drawings

#### ICE/CON/N9901: Implement  Safe Work  Practices and Environmental
Stewardship at Construction Sites
Description
This NOS focuses on enabling construction workers and supervisors to actively adopt safe work practices,
promote environmental stewardship and ensure a hazard-free workplace through proactive risk management, sustainable resource usage and timely emergency response.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct workplace safety inspections
Apply safe operational practices
Promote health, hygiene, and well-being
Implement sustainability and environmental conservation measures
Respond effectively to onsite emergencies
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct Workplace Safety Inspections
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify unsafe conditions such as improper scaffolding, poor lighting, open edges or
obstructed pathways
- PC2. assess task-specific risks involving tools, machinery, and hazardous materials
- PC3. document safety observations and communicate them to the safety lead or supervisor
- PC4. verify that safety controls such as guard rails, signages and access controls are in place
Apply Safe Operational Practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. use appropriate PPE and ensure correct fit before beginning any activity
- PC6. follow safe operation procedures for cutting, lifting, mixing and power tools
- PC7. implement ergonomic practices to reduce fatigue and prevent musculoskeletal injuries
- PC8. maintain housekeeping standards by ensuring clean and clutter-free work areas
Promote Health, Hygiene and Well-Being
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. follow personal hygiene practices such as handwashing, clean clothing and proper hydration
- PC10. identify signs of health issues such as heat stress, dehydration and respiratory discomfort
- PC11. ensure availability and safe usage of sanitation facilities, drinking water and resting zones
- PC12. report workplace illnesses or physical discomfort to the assigned authority
Implement Sustainability and Environmental Conservation Measures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. follow procedures for safe storage and controlled use of chemicals, fuels and hazardous
substances
