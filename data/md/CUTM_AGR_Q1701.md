#### CUTM/AGR/Q1701: Polyhouse Installation, Monitoring and Service
Supervisor
Brief Job Description
A Polyhouse Installation, Monitoring and Service Supervisor will design the structure and assist in installing
the polyhouse. He shall monitor operation and maintenance of the polyhouse structure. He may use and
control the instruments installed inside the structure. The person may also assist in cultivation practices
such as maintaining nursery, transplanting, inter cultivation, irrigation, micro climate control, harvesting
and post harvesting of vegetable and flower crops.
Personal Attributes
A Polyhouse Installation, Monitoring and Service Supervisor should be technically skilled, proactive, and
detail-oriented. They must possess leadership qualities, problem-solving ability, and a strong understanding of protected cultivation practices. Good communication, teamwork, adaptability, and
commitment to safety and quality are essential for managing installation, crop care, and post-harvest
operations effectively.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CUTM/AGR/N1701: Plan and setup the polyhouse

#### CUTM/AGR/N1702: Preparation of land and installation of irrigation inside
polyhouse
Description
Monitoring and maintaining a polyhouse involves regular checks of temperature, humidity, irrigation
systems, and pest control to ensure optimal conditions for plant growth. It also includes timely cleaning,
repairing structural components, and maintaining proper ventilation for healthy crop production.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS includes managing environmental parameters, ensuring the functioning of irrigation and fertigation systems, and addressing pest and disease management. It also covers routine
maintenance of polyhouse structures and ensuring compliance with safety and quality standards.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Land preparation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Levelling the land.
- PC2. Ploughing and harrowing the land.
- PC3. Soil solarization for pathogen free soil.
- PC4. Raise bed preparation.
Irrigation system installation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Measure the area required to install irrigation system.
- PC6. Calculation of drip materials requirement for polyhouse.
- PC7. Procurement of drip irrigation requirement.
- PC8. Installation of drip irrigation system.
- PC9. Skill to operate and maintain the drip irrigation system.
Fertigation system installation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Calculation of nutrients required.
- PC11. Selection and installation of fertigation system.
- PC12. Operation and maintenance of fertigation system.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Design and components of a polyhouse (frame, cladding, ventilation, etc.).
- KU2. Functionality and maintenance of systems like irrigation, fertigation, and cooling.

#### CUTM/AGR/N1703: Monitor and maintain polyhouse
Description
This NOS involves supervising, monitoring, and maintaining the infrastructure, equipment, and climatic
conditions of a polyhouse to ensure optimal crop growth. It includes managing ventilation, irrigation, pest
control, structural integrity, sensor systems, and hygiene, while ensuring compliance with safety and
agronomic standards for high-value horticultural production.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes monitoring polyhouse structures, microclimate control systems, irrigation lines,
fertigation units, and pest exclusion nets. It also covers managing repairs, preventive maintenance,
calibration of sensors, record-keeping, and workforce coordination to ensure sustainable and uninterrupted operation of polyhouse facilities for commercial crop production.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Polyhouse operation and maintenance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand microclimate changes in polyhouse.
- PC2. Raising curtains, cover shade nets whenever required.
- PC3. Monitor of polyhouse instruments.
- PC4. Washing of Polyfilmate regular interval.
- PC5. Painting of structural members to avoid rusting.
- PC6. Liming of polyfilm during summer to reduce sunlight intensity.
Regular checking of structure
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Periodically checking of structure to identify the damages.
- PC8. Regular monitoring the instruments workability.
Regular checking of structure
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Replacing the broken profiles/ springs.
- PC10. Pasting the damaged polyfilms with UV stabilized tapes.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Structure and components of different types of polyhouses (e.g., naturally ventilated, fan and
pad system).

#### CUTM/AGR/N1704: Suggest suitable Crop production and nursery
management   in polyhouse
Description
This NOS involves recommending appropriate crops, cultivation techniques, and nursery management
practices suited to polyhouse conditions. It includes crop planning, variety selection, seedling raising,
transplanting schedules, and resource optimization, ensuring high productivity and quality. It supports
protected cultivation through scientific nursery establishment and environmentally controlled crop
management strategies.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS involves recommending appropriate crops, cultivation techniques, and nursery management practices suited to polyhouse conditions. It includes crop planning, variety selection,
seedling raising, transplanting schedules, and resource optimization, ensuring high productivity and
quality. It supports protected cultivation through scientific nursery establishment and environmentally controlled crop management strategies.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Raising seedlings
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Suggest suitable seed selection of vegetables and flower for polyhouse
- PC2. Suggest suitable seed selection of vegetables and flower for polyhouse
- PC3. Suggest suitable bed preparation for raising nursery
- PC4. Sowing the seeds of vegetables and flowers
- PC5. Suggest watering the seedlings
- PC6. Suggest providing required nutrition
- PC7. Suggest applying the pesticide if required
Plant transplanting

- PC10. Watering the transplanted plants.
- PC11. Gap filling by replacing the dead plants
- PC12. Learning to give proper support for the crops
- PC13. Staking procedure for crops like cucumber, tomato and capsicum
- PC14. Water the polyhouse crops as per the crop need.
- PC15. Application of water soluble fertilizers in the recommended quantity to promote the healthy
growth of seedlings, vegetables and plants.
- PC16. Application of micronutrients.

| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |
| --- | :---: |
| Organisational Context | Organisational context includes the way the organisation is structured and how it operates, including the extent of operative knowledge managers have of their relevant areas of responsibility. |
| Technical Knowledge | Technical knowledge is the specific knowledge needed to accomplish specific designated responsibilities. |
| Core Skills/ Generic Skills (GS) | Core skills or Generic Skills (GS) are a group of skills that are the key to learning and working in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment. In the context of the OS, these include communication related skills that are applicable to most job roles. |
| Electives | Electives are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as contributive to specialization in a job role. There may be multiple electives within a QP for each specialized job role. Trainees must select at least one elective for the successful completion of a QP with Electives. |
| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
