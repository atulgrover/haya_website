ASP/MES/Q0401: Assistant Offset Printing Operator
Brief Job Description
Individuals will be able to operate single colour or multicolour offset machine and do necessary settings
related with paper, ink and other chemicals. Taking prints to require number of copies with satisfying all
parameter as customer requirements. Printing operator also responsible for the maintenance and cleaning
of cylinders and inking rollers.
Personal Attributes
Attention to detail, Manual dexterity, Willingness to learn, Safety awareness
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:

protocols to ensure smooth and safe functioning of printing machinery. Emphasis is given to identifying

ASP/MES/N0405: Consumables
Description
This section covers the essential consumables used in offset printing, including water, additives, offset
chemistry, and important spares, accessories, and equipment associated with offset presses. It provides
learners with knowledge of how consumables impact the quality and efficiency of the printing process.
Understanding the role of consumables and their proper management is crucial to maintaining consistent
print quality and minimizing production issues.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The section aims to equip learners with practical knowledge of consumables essential for offset
printing. It covers the types and properties of water, additives, and offset chemicals, along with the
maintenance and handling of crucial spares and accessories. By mastering these concepts, learners
will be able to optimize print operations, troubleshoot common issues, and enhance the quality and
durability of print outputs.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Familiarise with the various consumables in offset printing
PC2. Become skilled in using water and additives in offset printing methods
PC3. Gain knowledge about the application of chemistry in offset printing

ASP/MES/N0403: Colour & Quality
Description
This NOS provides an in-depth understanding of color theory, color printing techniques, and quality
management in the printing industry. It covers the fundamentals of human and computer vision, exploring
how colors are perceived and represented digitally. Students will learn about the principles of color theory
and its practical applications in color printing. Additionally, the NOS includes methods of color
measurement and the significance of maintaining international quality standards and certifications. By
mastering these concepts, learners will be equipped to ensure high-quality print outputs while adhering to
global quality benchmarks.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This section aims to equip learners with a comprehensive understanding of color management and
quality control in the printing industry. It covers essential concepts of human and computer vision,
color theory, and practical applications in color printing processes. Learners will gain insights into
accurate color measurement techniques and the implementation of international quality standards
and certifications. Upon completion, learners will be able to apply color management principles
effectively to ensure consistency, accuracy, and high-quality print outputs across various printing
applications.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Human Vision Vs. Computer Vision, Visual process, Fundamentals of colour perception. Colour,
Colour Management
PC2. RGB Vs CMYK, LAB values, Importance of Colour, Consistency
PC3. Process of four colour Printing using Cyan, Magenta, Yellow and Black and more colours.
PC4. Density Vs Colour, Colour measurements, Equipment & tools for density, colour measurements

ASP/MES/N0402: Offset and Digital Printing Machine
Description
This module covers both offset and digital printing techniques, focusing on essential functional units and
modern advancements. It includes feeding, sheet forwarding, inking, dampening, printing, ink drying, and
delivery units in offset printing, along with pre-make ready, makeready, and post-makeready operations.
The module also introduces digital printing techniques, applications, and advanced control systems like
CPC and CIP3/4 for improved precision and efficiency.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This module equips learners with a thorough understanding of both offset and digital printing
techniques, making them proficient in operating and managing modern printing machines. It covers
essential functional units, advanced control systems, and modern printing applications, enabling
learners to adapt to evolving industry demands. The knowledge gained from this module is crucial
for careers in printing technology, production management, and quality control within the printing
and packaging industries.
Elements and Performance Criteria
