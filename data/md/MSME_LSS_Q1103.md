MSME/LSS/Q1103: Jr. Technical Supervisor Sports Shoe Manufacturing
Brief Job Description
Qualified learners who attain this qualification are capable in Shoe Design & Development, Materials
Science Manufacturing Techniques, Industrial Engineering, Quality Assurance & Control, Production
Management, Retailing, Merchandising & Supply Chain, and Sustainability within the fields of Sports
Footwear and Allied sectors. Learner can get a job in Design & Development, Production, Quality,
Productivity, Costing & Cost Control, Marketing, Retailing & Merchandising fields of Sports Footwear sector
and also can able to become an entrepreneur
Personal Attributes
Qualified learners who attain this qualification are capable in Shoe Design & Development, Materials
Science Manufacturing Techniques, Industrial Engineering, Quality Assurance & Control, Production
Management, Retailing, Merchandising & Supply Chain, and Sustainability within the fields of Sports
Footwear and Allied sectors. Learner can get a job in Design & Development, Production, Quality,
Productivity, Costing & Cost Control, Marketing, Retailing & Merchandising fields of Sports Footwear sector
and also can able to become an entrepreneur

MSME/LSS/N1174: Cost Control in Sports Shoe Manufacture
Description
Understand cost estimation, material optimization, waste reduction, and production planning to control
manufacturing costs while maintaining the quality of sports shoes.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand cost estimation, material optimization, waste reduction, and production planning to
control manufacturing costs while maintaining the quality of sports shoes.
Elements and Performance Criteria
MSME/ ACSMM/09
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand cost control importance, overview management principles, and discuss the
relationship with profitability.
PC2. Classify costs, analyze behavior, and conduct cost-volume-profit analysis.
PC3. Apply costing methods, develop budgets, and perform variance analysis.

MSME/LSS/N1172: Advanced Manufacturing Techniques
Description
Understand modern footwear manufacturing processes, advanced machinery, automation, and efficient
production techniques to improve quality and productivity.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand modern footwear manufacturing processes, advanced machinery, automation, and
efficient production techniques to improve quality and productivity.
Elements and Performance Criteria
MSME/ ACSMM/07
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand sports footwear industry, importance of advanced manufacturing, and key
challenges.
PC2. Learn principles, processes (FDM, SLS, SLA), and materials used in 3D printing for sports
footwear.
PC3. Gain proficiency in CAD software, digital prototyping, and CAD/CAM integration
PC4. Understand automation's role, implement automated processes, and evaluate cobots in
footwear production.
PC5. Explore Industry 4.0 concepts, smart factories, IoT sensors, and predictive maintenance in
footwear manufacturing.

Understand the basic principles of footwear design, including ergonomics, aesthetics, material selection,
functionality, comfort, durability, and current fashion trends to create practical and appealing footwear
designs.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the basic principles of footwear design, including ergonomics, aesthetics, material
selection, functionality, comfort, durability, and current fashion trends to create practical and
appealing footwear designs.
Elements and Performance Criteria
MSME/ ACSMM/01
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Describe foot structure and function, identify foot shapes and arch types, explain foot
biomechanics
PC2. Define foot parameters and sizing systems, apply sports shoe size systems, utilize foot
measuring devices
PC3. • Biomechanics of Sports Shoes - Analyze biomechanical aspects of running, evaluate
biomechanical design principles for
• sports shoes
PC4. Design for comfort and performance, address common foot issues, consider customization and
fit
PC5. Define lasts, apply proportional measurements, differentiate foot and last anatomy, identify
last shapes and styles.
