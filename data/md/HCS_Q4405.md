#### HCS/Q4405: Natural Fibre Product Maker
Brief Job Description
A Natural Fibre Maker is responsible for making different products such as grass coasters, mirror frames,
bangles, toys, containers, bowls, bags, baskets, mats and other utility items, by using different grasses like
palm leaves, sheetal patti, reed, sikki grass, kasi grass, cona, jute kenaf, flax, hemp, sisal and palmyra etc.
The Grass Craft Maker shall be able to make different products of different specifications by using different
types of grasses. This job requires the individual to have understanding and knowledge of raw materials to
be used for making specific products and techniques used for the same.
Personal Attributes
A Grass Craft Maker should have good eyesight, hand-eye coordination, motor skills and vision. The person
should have basic mathematical skills, particularly making calculations and measurements. He/she should
be patient and tenacious for long hours of work. He/she should be imaginative, creative and analytic.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### HCS/N8302: Prepare the Raw materials for making grass craft products
Description
The unit discuss in details about the preparation of raw materials before beginning the production
processes. It informs about performance criteria, knowledge and understanding, and skills required to
become a grass craft artisan.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Procurement of raw materials and Arrange tools and Equipment
Processing the raw materials
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Procurement of raw materials and Arrange tools and Equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the raw materials required as per product specifications
- PC2. ensure raw material are as per product requirement both in terms of quality and quantity
- PC3. identify the appropriate amount of munj and khar (another form of grass) based on the job
specifications
- PC4. arrange the suitable dyes and colors as per the product requirement required for coloring the
objects
- PC5. place appropriate quantity of water in a drum based on the job specification
- PC6. arrange needle of appropriate size and dimensions required for shaping the iron object with a
rounded head for grip
- PC7. place Knife of standard size required for slitting the grass to extract excess amount of pieces
- PC8. arrange twine, takua and blade of appropriate sizes required for pierce the needle for
stitching the craft
Processing the raw materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. place the sliver by slicing the grass in the right manner
- PC10. shave the slivers for smoothness and the desired size
- PC11. measure and maintain the uniformity of the sliver to be achieved
- PC12. maintain the sharpness of the equipment used in slicing and shaping of grass objects
- PC13. identify the required tools and equipment for slicing and processing the raw materials as per
product specification
- PC14. perform the standard process required for soaking the slivers in water for a period of time to
make them more pliable
- PC15. place khar and munj provide the basic shape and additional strength to the grass objects
- PC16. identify the colors and shade from shade card based on the job specification

#### HCS/N8303: Identify Requirements of product and design specifications
Description
The unit discuss in details about the identifying the requirements of raw materials, tools, making process,
etc. with respect to the product or design specifications. It informs about performance criteria, knowledge
and understanding, and skills required by a grass craft maker to carry out the activity.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand design specifications and prepare for the production process
Coloring of slivers as per the product and design requirements
Drying and Sorting
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand design specifications and prepare for the production process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the tools to be used for the preparation of the product in accordance to the
specifications provided
- PC2. identify the suitable process to be used for the production of any specific product
- PC3. define the amount of prepared raw materials required with respect to the design
specifications
- PC4. arrange the required tools, equipment and raw materials in the work area
Colouring of slivers as per the product and design requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. select the correct dyeing vessel and chemicals/colors for dyeing the required grass slivers as
per specifications
- PC6. perform the process of mixing the different chemicals/ingredients required for making the
dye bath
- PC7. apply the standard operating procedures in order to maintain proportions while measuring
and mixing chemicals
- PC8. demonstrate the standard operating procedures for heating the dye bath to desired degrees
- PC9. place and dip and grass objects in order to ensure complete immersion of the slivers in the
dye bath correctly
- PC10. estimate the duration dip for the desired results
- PC11. apply standard safety procedures in order to take necessary precautions to avoid uneven
dyeing of materials
- PC12. maintain the concentration of the dye bath
- PC13. apply standard operation procedures for washing and neutralizing the dyed slivers or sticks
appropriately
- PC14. minimise and dispose the leftover chemicals in the approved manner

#### HCS/N8304: Carry out product making Operations and finishing of the
product
Description
This unit discusses in detail the different processes involved in making any grass craft product including
shaping of main frame of product with hard grass, coiling of grass slivers on the hard grass frame and then
finishing of the final product.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out making of hard grass frame as per design and product
Coiling and twining of grass slivers on frame
Carry out finishing, varnishing, drying & storage of final grass craft product
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out making of hard grass frame as per design and product
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select hard grass such as ‘munj’ and ‘Khar’ on the basis of flexibility
- PC2. perform standard procedures for cutting the hard grass in different sizes as per the design
and product requirement
- PC3. apply the standard procedures required for smoothing the surface of grass with shaver to
avoid any cuts on hands
- PC4. demonstrate the process of allowing the object to get the required basic shape of hard grass
to provide additional strength to the grass products
- PC5. take specific type (based on width & length) of Hard Grass slivers suitable for preparing the
two-dimensional base structure
- PC6. plan out the base & fix it to obtain a rigid structure
- PC7. ensure that the base structure is fixed in a proper manner before coiling the grass slivers on
to the base structure
- PC8. make the two dimensional hard grass base according to required design & pattern
Coiling and twining of grass slivers on frame
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. take appropriate grass slivers (based on width & length) for coiling and twining on base
structure frame formed by hard grass according to shape, size, colour & design pattern required in making specific product
- PC10. perform basic coiling of grass slivers on the given structure/frame
- PC11. analyze and maintain uniform gap between the slivers as per the given specification
- PC12. analyze and maintain compactness of the sliver to ensure rigidity of the structure
- PC13. demonstrate the use different colored slivers for different patterns
- PC14. avoid overlapping in coils to obtain uniformity and quality

| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
