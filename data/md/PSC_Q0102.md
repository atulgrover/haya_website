PSC/Q0102: Assistant Plumber - General
Brief Job Description
An Assistant Plumber - General assists the plumber in installation and repair of plumbing fittings and
fixtures. The job role holder organises and hands over tools, clears work area, cuts and bends pipes as per
the specified dimensions. The individual also supports the plumber with other tasks as per instructions
received.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be able to perform laborious work, be good at receiving and following instructions, and
be a good team player. The person must have good listening skills, be result oriented and have an eye for
detail.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. PSC/N0187: Support Plumbing Installation
- 2. PSC/N0140: Maintain Plumbing Tools and Equipment
- 3. PSC/N0188: Perform Repair and Maintenance of Plumbing System Post Installation
- 4. PSC/N0136: Apply Health and Safety Practices at the Workplace
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

PSC/N0187: Support Plumbing Installation
Description
This unit describes how an assistant plumber supports the plumber in carrying out installation, operation,
repair and maintenance work.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Follow instructions for installation, communising and maintenance of the plumbing work
Support senior plumber
Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow instructions for installation, communising and maintenance of the plumbing work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. follow work instructions received from plumber or supervisor
PC2. Receive pipes, fittings and fixtures as per work requirement
PC3. check quantity and quality of pipes, fittings and fixtures, etc as per specification
PC4. report any shortcomings like quantity, quality and standards of supplies against
specifications or requirements
PC5. Arrange and maintain tools, measuring device and consumables.
Support senior plumber
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Assist in mobilizing material requirements for the work.
PC7. Safely carry the materials to the work location.
PC8. Carry out the work as required under supervision.
PC9. use hand and power tools for cutting, bending and assembling pipes and fittings in a safe
manner under guidance of senior plumber.
PC10. perform cutting, bending and forming of pipes as per the dimensions required.
PC11. Install pipes, fittings and fixtures as instructed by senior plumber.
PC12. Measure and check the fixture and/or pipework as per instructions
PC13. Support the pipework or fixture steadily during installation as required.
PC14. clear work area and store tools and other plumbing materials safely and as per the specified
instructions.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. organisational requirements
KU2. organisation's policies on delivery standards, safety and hazards, integrity, dress code, etc.

PSC/N0140: Maintain Plumbing Tools and Equipment
Description
This NOS deals in detail with the preventive and corrective maintenance of various plumbing tools and
equipment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform corrective maintenance work
Identify and perform preventive maintenance requirements
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform corrective maintenance work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Ensure tools are in good working condition, identify the reason for underperformance
PC2. Repair the tools as instructed by expert or manufacturer.
PC3. Follow correct repair procedures
PC4. Post repair, check the tools for correct proper operation/working, if not, repeat the repair
process under expert guidance.
PC5. check the power tools for wear and tear and replace the parts as scheduled.
PC6. Ensure correct operation as per the requirements.
PC7. Ensure proper disposal of worn-out, defective and exhausted parts/materials.
Identify and perform preventive maintenance requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Identify preventive maintenance requirements of different tools.
PC9. Follow tool specific maintenance schedule.
PC10. Check and inspect tools to the operations requirement.
PC11. Analyse potential breakdowns and implement corrective actions under expert guidance.
PC12. Ensure proper disposal of worn-out, defective and exhausted parts/materials.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. organisational requirements
KU2. organisation's policies on delivery standards, safety and hazards, integrity, dress code, etc.
KU3. individual's role and responsibilities in the workflow.
KU4. risk and impact of not following defined procedures/work instructions
KU5. usage of plumbing tools, equipment and materials

PSC/N0188: Perform Repair and Maintenance of Plumbing System Post
Installation
Description
This NOS deals detail with repair and maintenance of water distribution and drainage system.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
repair of minor defects like leakage and clogs in plumbing system
Assisting and applying repair solution identified by senior/expert plumber
Perform routine service under expert/senior plumber as per manufacturers recommendation.
Elements and Performance Criteria
repair of minor defects like leakage and clogs
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Ensure availability and usage of right tools for repair.
PC2. Identify the root cause for the problem.
PC3. Apply repair solutions to rectify plumbing issues.
PC4. Identify and mobilize the space, materials, required.
PC5. Guide user/customer to prevent the recurrence.
PC6. Follow safety guidelines
PC7. Ensure Warranty/Guarantee status of the product to be repaired
Assisting and applying repair solution identified by senior/expert plumber
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Clear and prepare the work area for repair
PC9. Analyse and understand the repair solutions
PC10. Ensure availability and usage of right tools for repair.
PC11. Assist senior plumber in resolving the plumbing issues
PC12. Follow instructions of senior plumbers when working independently
PC13. Ensure spare and other materials required for repair meet the specifications.
PC14. Perform post repair checks and ensure issues are fixed.
PC15. Clear the work area and handover to client.
Perform routine service under expert/senior plumber guidance or as per manufacturers recommendation.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC16. Read and interpret or get guided from experts on brand specific service requirements
PC17. Prepare working area ready for servicing
PC18. Identify/replace parts which requires replacements to prevent failure under guidance or as
per manufacturers specification.
PC19. Check the system for satisfactory functioning.
