GNU/ASC/N0206: Sensors and Actuators
Description
Achieve a comprehensive understanding of control systems, signal processing, sensors, actuators, and
mechatronic systems. Develop the ability to compare and analyse open and closed-loop control systems.
Gain proficiency in signal processing techniques, including analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog
conversions. Understand the selection, operation, and troubleshooting of various sensors and actuators.
Acquire practical skills in designing and implementing mechatronic systems. Be prepared to apply
mechatronic principles to real-world applications and future innovations. Develop problem-solving and
critical thinking skills related to advanced control and automation systems.
