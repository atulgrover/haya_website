NIE/ELE/Q0502: Internet of Things (IoT) Associate
Brief Job Description
The Internet of Things (IoT) Associate Qualification enables the enrolled trainee program, install and do the
required troubleshooting of IoT based products. After course completion, the trained learner will exhibit a
refined posture about the basic programming skills required to program the microcontroller used in IoT
devices. This enhances the working of these devices in interdisciplinary areas without degrading their
performance
Personal Attributes
participants should have the ability to analyze and solve problems, ability to deploy IoT solution as per
requirement
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. NIE/ELE/N0511: Identification and troubleshooting of Basic Electronics components
- 2. NIE/ELE/N0508: Conceptualising IoT Platform - Arduino
- 3. NIE/ELE/N0509: Conceptualising IoT based use cases
- 4. NIE/ELE/N0510: Fundamentals of wireless IoT using NodeMCU

NIE/ELE/N0511: Identification and troubleshooting of Basic Electronics
components
Description
The NOS covers examine and identification of several electronic devices and components
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identification of electronics components
Understanding the Fundamentals of basic electronics
Troubleshooting of electronics components
Understanding the operation of measurement devices
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identification of electronics components
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. able to identify the basic electronics components like Resistor, Inductor, diodes, transistor,
LED, Capacitor etc
Understanding the Fundamentals of basic electronics
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC2. Knowledge of basic electrical and electronic components
PC3. able to learn the operation of diodes, transistors, Zener diodes, rectifiers etc
PC4. able to to assemble the electronics components to make a circuit using Bread-board as well as
veroboard.
Troubleshooting of electronics components
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. able to troubleshot electrical and electronics circuits
Understanding the operation of measurement devices.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. able to understand how to operate the multimeter, ammeter, voltmeter, voltage supply etc
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Knowledge of basic electrical, electronics and instrumentation.
KU2. Possesses specialized operational knowledge and understanding of the work
KU3. handling of electronics components and devices

and computer screens. Proctors authenticate the candidate based on registration details, pre-test image
captured and I- card in possession of the candidate. Proctors can chat with candidates or give warnings to
candidates. Proctors can also take screenshots, terminate a specific user's test session, or re-authenticate
candidates based on video feeds.

examination/ internal assessment / Project/ Presentation/. Major Project (if applicable) would be evaluated
preferably by external/ subject expert including NIELIT officials.
