#### MEP/Q9901: Digital and Sales Marketing Executive
Brief Job Description
The Digital Sales and Marketing Executive course prepares individuals to take on key roles in developing
and managing digital marketing initiatives. It covers essential topics such as SEO, SEM, content marketing,
social media marketing, and data analytics, along with foundational knowledge in management, commerce, and business communication, enabling them to lead and optimize digital marketing efforts.
Personal Attributes
The person should be well groomed, attentive, has the ability to multi-task, good communication skills,
pays attention to detail, respects confidentiality and is dependable.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MEP/N0403: Fundamentals of Marketing Management & Digital Marketing
2. MEP/N0408: Introduction to Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) and Search Engine Marketing (SEM)
3. MEP/N0410: Social Media Marketing
4. MEP/N0406: Business Communication - I
5. MEP/N0407: Computer Skills for Professionals
6. DGT/VSQ/N0104: Employability Skills (120 Hours)
7. MEP/N0409: Email and Content Marketing
8. MEP/N0411: Web Designing with Content Management System (CMS) and Web Analytics
9. MEP/N0412: Mobile Marketing and Online Reputation Management (ORM)
10. MEP/N0413: Business Communication II
11. MEP/N0414: Environment Studies
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Principles of Management
This is Elective 1 for Semester 1 of the qualification. This NOS explains about the principles and theories of
management. The trainee can choose either Elective 1 or 2 in Semester 1.

#### MEP/N0403: Fundamentals  of Marketing Management  &  Digital Marketing
Description
This unit describes about various aspects of marketing
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Basics of Marketing
Product and Pricing Decisions
Marketing Mix Decisions
Emerging Trends in Marketing
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Basics of Marketing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Define and explain the scope of marketing, recognising its importance in business.
- PC2. Identify and describe various marketing philosophies and their implications for business
strategies.
- PC3. Analyse the concept of customer value and its significance in building customer
relationships.
- PC4. Outline the marketing management process and its components, including planning,
implementation, and control.
- PC5. Discuss the elements of the marketing mix (4Ps) and their roles in achieving marketing
objectives.
- PC6. Evaluate the marketing environment factors and their impact on marketing decision-making.
- PC7. Understand consumer buyer behaviour and its implications for market segmentation and
targeting.
- PC8. Apply market segmentation, targeting, and positioning strategies to reach target customers
effectively.
- PC9. Assess competitive marketing strategies and their effectiveness in gaining competitive
advantage.
- PC10. Recognise the importance of marketing in driving business success and growth.
- PC11. Explain the concept of digital marketing and its importance in today’s business environment.
- PC12. Identify various digital marketing channels and platforms used for promoting products and
services.
- PC13. Demonstrate an understanding of the customer journey (awareness, consideration, decision)
in the context of digital marketing.
- PC14. Analyze the key components of a digital marketing strategy.
- PC15. Evaluate the use of basic digital marketing tools for tracking and measuring performance.

#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understanding the basics of marketing, including core concepts such as market segmentation,
targeting, positioning, and consumer behavior.
- KU2. Knowledge of product decisions, including product development, lifecycle management, and strategies for product differentiation.
- KU3. Familiarity with pricing strategies and factors affecting pricing decisions, such as cost,
competition, and customer perceptions of value.
- KU4. Comprehension of the marketing mix (4Ps: Product, Price, Place, Promotion) and its role in
creating an effective marketing strategy.
- KU5. Awareness of emerging trends in marketing, including digital marketing, social media marketing, and data-driven marketing techniques.
- KU6. Knowledge of the importance of consumer research and the role it plays in product and pricing decisions, as well as adapting to market trends.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Analytical skills to evaluate market conditions, understand consumer needs, and apply marketing theories to real-world business scenarios.
- GS2. Creativity and innovation skills for developing unique marketing strategies, product ideas, and
promotional campaigns.
- GS3. Communication skills for effectively presenting marketing strategies and collaborating with
teams across various business functions.
- GS4. Adaptability to keep up with and apply emerging marketing trends and technologies in a rapidly changing business environment.

Description
This unit details search engines, search engine optimisation techniques, trouble shooting, digital
marketing, advertising, and analysing trends and performance.
Scope

Introduction to Digital Marketing
