#### RSC/Q7402: Machine Operator (Non-Leather Footwear  Manufacturing)
Brief Job Description
A Machine Operator (Non- Leather Footwear Manufacturing) is responsible for supporting the manufacturing and assembling of non-leather footwear by assisting in machine operations, material
handling, and quality control inspections. Ensure a safe and organized work environment while contributing to the efficient workflow of the manufacturing process.
Personal Attributes
This job role requires the individual to handle multiple tasks in the manufacturing and assembling
operations with a strong attention to detail, ensuring precision in tasks such as machine operation,
material handling, assembling and quality control inspections. Must demonstrate excellent teamwork and
communication skills to collaborate effectively with colleagues in achieving production goals. The ability to
adapt to a fast-paced environment while maintaining a focus on safety and efficiency is essential.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N7107: Perform Polymer Compounding and Processing Operations
2. RSC/N7108: Carry out Non-Leather Footwear Sole Production
3. RSC/N7109: Perform assisting and assembling activities
4. RSC/N5003: Carry out quality checks
5. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### RSC/N7107: Perform Polymer Compounding   and Processing Operations
Description
This unit is about selecting raw materials and getting the instruments and machineries ready for the
manufacturing operation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Selection of Raw Materials
Readiness of the Instruments
Optimizing the Material Selection
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Selection of Raw Materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select the desired polymers depending on their desired properties
- PC2. choose additives such as plasticizers, stabilizers, flame retardants, colorants, fillers,
reinforcements
- PC3. identify solvents used for dissolution or dispersion of additives (if applicable)
Readiness of the Instruments
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. check single-screw or twin-screw extruders for blending and melting polymers with additives
such as internal mixers, Banbury mixers, or two-roll mills for achieving homogeneous mixing
- PC5. ensure availability of auxiliary equipments, such as extruders, mixers, and pelletizers, are
properly connected, calibrated, and functioning correctly
- PC6. setup pelletizers to form the compounded polymer into pellets for easier handling
- PC7. inspect Molding Machine for processes such as Injection molding, blow molding, compression
molding, or rotational molding
- PC8. use Extrusion lines to produce tubes, films, and sheets
- PC9. produce thin films and sheets with precise thickness using calendaring machines
- PC10. measure the flow properties of molten properties with the help of Rheometers
- PC11. use viscometer for viscosity measurements
- PC12. use spectrophotometers for color analysis
- PC13. handle Mechanical Testing Machines in order to evaluate the mechanical properties like
tensile strength, impact resistance, fracture point
- PC14. use personal Protective Equipment (PPE): such as gloves, goggles, aprons, and respirators
- PC15. utilize ventilation systems to control fumes and dust
- PC16. prevent accidents in case of equipment malfunction using emergency shutdown systems
Optimization of parameters

#### RSC/N7108: Carry out Non-Leather Footwear Sole Production
Description
Execute the production of non-leather footwear materials and soles with precision and adherence to
quality standards, ensuring consistent product performance and durability
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Material Selection and Preparation
Assembling, Stitching & Sole Production
Quality Control and Environmental Sustainability
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Material Selection and Preparation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify suitable non-leather materials for the desired footwear type
- PC2. ensure materials meet required specifications for durability, flexibility, and aesthetic appeal
- PC3. cut and prepare materials according to design specifications and production requirements
- PC4. develop or follow patterns for cutting non-leather materials
- PC5. ensure accurate cutting of materials to minimize waste and optimize material usage
- PC6. verify the correctness of patterns and dimensions to meet design specifications
Assembling, Stitching & Sole Production
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. assemble non-leather materials using appropriate techniques such as stitching, bonding, or
heat sealing
- PC8. ensure precise alignment of components to achieve desired aesthetics and functionality
- PC9. perform stitching or bonding operations efficiently to maintain production flow
- PC10. select appropriate materials for sole production, considering factors like traction, cushioning,
and durability
- PC11. prepare and mold sole materials using suitable techniques such as compression molding or
injection molding
- PC12. ensure consistent quality and uniformity in sole production to meet performance standards
- PC13. apply adhesives accurately and uniformly to bond non-leather materials and soles
- PC14. ensure proper curing and adhesion strength to withstand stress and wear during use
- PC15. follow safety protocols when handling adhesives to prevent health hazards
Quality Control, Performance Testing and Environmental Sustainability
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC16. trim excess material and clean finished footwear to achieve a polished appearance
- PC17. inspect footwear for defects such as stitching irregularities, material flaws, or misalignment
