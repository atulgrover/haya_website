HYC/Q4501: Petrochemical Plant Operator Production
Brief Job Description
Individual at this job is responsible for monitoring and controlling the production processes in a
petrochemical plant. The person at this job is responsible for safely and efficiently operating the plant to
ensure maximum efficiency, cost-effectiveness and quality of the products.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have good sense of responsibility, a good organizer and time keeper. The person
should have the ability to work individually / team in different shift.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HYC/N4501: Preventive maintenance at petrochemical plant
- 2. HYC/N4502: Operate, repair and maintenance activities at petrochemical plant

HYC/N4501: Preventive maintenance at petrochemical plant
Description
This unit is about preventive maintenance to be caried out at petrochemical production plant.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Preventive maintenance of instrument and equipment installed at petrochemical plant
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preventive maintenance of instrument and equipment installed at petrochemical plant
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. perform routine visual inspections of all plant equipment and systems, including piping,
pressure vessels, tanks, pumps, valves, Monitoring of TMT Tube metal temperature etc.
PC2. monitor and adjust process parameters according to recommended intervals, process systems
for corrosion and erosion
PC3. check and adjust operating and safety controls for proper functioning.
PC4. inspect and maintain process safety systems, utility piping, pumps, valves, fire protection
systems, etc.
PC5. maintain plant utilities, such as steam, compressed air, and cooling water systems
PC6. report logs, finding and fault to the supervisor/manager
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the basic principle of petrochemical plant operation
KU2. standard operating procedures of petrochemical product production
KU3. importance of maintaining clean workplace
KU4. relevant health and safety requirements applicable to the work place
KU5. the importance of personal protective equipment used during welding operation
KU6. ways of efficiently managing material and water in the process
KU7. the difference between recyclable/reusable and waste material
KU8. the concept of Mission Karmayogi
KU9. the concept of Swachhata Hi Seva

HYC/N4502: Operate, repair and maintenance activities at petrochemical
plant
Description
This unit is about operating, repair and maintenance activities for proper functioning of petrochemical
production plant.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Operate different component at petrochemical production plant
Analyse equipment and instruments for repairing or maintenance at a petrochemical production
plant
Elements and Performance Criteria
Operate different component at petrochemical production plant
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. operate pumps, valves, reactors, heat exchangers, turbines, compressors, separators,
storage tanks, piping, etc. and follow all safety protocols and procedures
PC2. regulate pumps and compressors as per the instruction of supervisor/manager
PC3. monitor and adjust production process parameters such as pressure, temperature, flow, and
levels
PC4. control the plant operations such as controlling the various processes and operations within
the plant and controlling the temperature, pressure and other parameters for the various
processes.
PC5. read temperature in pressure gauges, flowmeters, vacuum and time, record readings in the
log
PC6. monitor and operate processing unit, cracking unit, separation unit, synthesis unit,
purification unit, product packaging unit, etc. to produce petrochemical product.
PC7. clean processing units’ interior by circulating solvent and chemicals as per SOP
PC8. follow all shift-specific operational procedures and protocols
PC9. report logs, finding and fault to the supervisor/manager
PC10. Assisting in Shutdown activity like preparing blinding list, adhering to safe shutdown
procedure, SOP etc.
PC11. Assisting in Startup activity like leak checking, gasket checking, loop checking etc.
Analyse equipment and instruments for repairing or maintenance at a petrochemical production plant
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. identify the repair and maintenance need in the plant
PC13. schedule and inform repair and maintenance activity at plant

HYC/N9302: Maintain health, safety and security procedures
Description
This unit covers maintaining health, safety and security procedures at the workplace. This includes
procedures and practices that candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work
environment
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Practice health and safety measures
Follow fire safety procedures
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow health and safety measures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. use protective clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions
PC2. identify documents, location and people responsible for health and safety in the workplace
PC3. identify possible causes of risk or accident in the workplace
PC4. carry out safe working practices while dealing with hazards to ensure the safety of self and
others
PC5. lift heavy objects safely using correct procedures
PC6. identify common safety signs, displayed in various areas
Follow fire safety procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. use the various appropriate fire extinguishers on different types of fires correctly
PC8. follow rescue techniques applied during fire hazard
PC9. follow good housekeeping practice in order to prevent fire hazards
PC10. list issues concerning the safety in work place
PC11. inform fire safety department about any near-miss incidents in the work place
PC12. follow the applicable laws, regulations and codes as per safety standard
PC13. prepare written accident/incident report and share with the concerned officer/department
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC14. provide appropriate first aid to victims in emergency situation
PC15. demonstrate basic techniques of bandaging
PC16. respond promptly and appropriately to an accident
