#### MIN/Q1506: Low Profile Dump Truck (LPDT) Operator
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role uses the Low Profile Dump Truck (LPDT) for transporting large volume of loose
material such as sand, gravel, crushed rock, coal, ore etc. to designated place site, dump yard,
washing/handling plant etc. The operator is also responsible for discharging its load to rear, bottom or on
one side efficiently. They also ensure that the safety systems are working correctly and performing
essential maintenance on the LPDT available in a underground mines.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should be physically agile, strong, have good eye-sight and not suffer from color-
blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MIN/N1515: Prepare LPDT for operation
2. MIN/N1516: Perform LPDT operations
3. MIN/N1517: Perform maintenance of the LPDT
4. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Underground Metal
1. MIN/N1702: Follow Health, Safety and Environmental guidelines for Underground Metalliferous
Mines
Elective 2: Underground Coal
1. MIN/N1704: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental guidelines for underground coal mines

#### MIN/N1515: Prepare  LPDT for operation
Description
This unit is about inspecting & preparing the LPDT for activities that need to be carried out.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify work requirements
Inspect and prepare the LPDT for operation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify work requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the work requirements from the instructions received from supervisor and
interpreting information from previous day/shift logbook
- PC2. use personal protective equipment appropriate for work activities
- PC3. select appropriate type of auxiliary equipment for work activities
- PC4. inspect and assess site conditions to determine if scaling is required and take action
according to site requirements
Inspect and prepare the LPDT for operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. ensure the locking bar of vehicle is in position to prevent the unforeseen movement of front
and rear chassis
- PC6. inspect the LPDT visually for any damage on body, low tire pressure, damage mirror and
window, fluid leakage etc.
- PC7. check coolant level and oil levels of engine, differential, hydraulic tank etc. and top up/refill
as required
- PC8. check water level in the radiator and top up as required
- PC9. check various controls, gauges, emergency brake, parking brake, main horn, reverse horn,
head light, warning lamp, other safety devices etc. for proper functioning and any defect
- PC10. check and adjust fan belt tension, driving position, rear and side mirrors, seat belts etc. and
confirm clear uninterrupted vision all around
- PC11. clean dust from the air filter and drain water and sediment from the fuel tank
- PC12. check electrolyte level, terminals, seating arrangements of the battery and take corrective
actions as per SOP, if required
- PC13. check functioning of fire extinguishers, automatically operated fire suppression system and
devices including fire detection system in the LPDT
- PC14. apply grease to all greasing pins and pivot points of the vehicle
- PC15. keep footplates and steps clean and free from mud, dirt and oil

#### MIN/N1516: Perform  LPDT operations
Description
This unit is about performing various LPDT operations in the mines during the shift.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Start and drive the LPDT
Load and haul the load to the designated destination
Discharge the material from LPDT
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Start and drive the LPDT
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. start the engine using the push-button switch and key switch together
- PC2. select correct gear range by shifting the quadrant device
- PC3. follow safety practices such as wear safety belt & helmet, no use of phones etc. while driving
- PC4. look out for people working, stationary objects and hazards such as overhead pipes, power
lines, etc. during driving
- PC5. follow the safety signs and locally developed signals during driving
- PC6. select & use the right type of brake in different situations during driving
- PC7. use various signalling devices available in the LPDT such as turn signal, parking indicator,
etc. as per requirement
- PC8. take proper precautions while reversing the LPDT
- PC9. monitor the LPDT performance using appropriate indicators and ensure efficiency of
operations
Load and haul the load to the designated destination
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. place the LPDT at correct spotting location on the loading site
- PC11. load the material to be transferred safely & correctly, ensuring that it does not slip/fall-off
during loading or journey
- PC12. ensure that LPDT load and operating speed are within limits as specified by the manufacturer
- PC13. move the vehicle along with the material and transfer it to the designated or specified area
Discharge the material from LPDT
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. follow the LHD/SDL operator's signal/instruction regarding unloading location as per the
quality of loaded material
- PC15. discharge the load safely at the position and in the manner as specified in WI/SOP
- PC16. follow the code of practice for tipping at the Over Burden (OB) dump yard or stockyard
framed by the manager

#### MIN/N1517: Perform  maintenance  of the LPDT
Description
This unit is about performing routine maintenance and troubleshooting tasks on the Vehicle / LPDT.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for maintenance work
Repair and maintain the LPDT
Perform post-maintenance activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for maintenance work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the vehicle and its manufacturer's specifications from the user manual and vehicle
drawings
- PC2. read maintenance schedule and checklist and plan the time and schedule for conducting the
maintenance
- PC3. track machine operating hours to assess the right service schedule
- PC4. identify and arrange the tools, consumables and spare parts required during the task
Repair and maintain the LPDT
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. follow safety practices during maintenance activities as per organisational SOP
- PC6. perform basic health check-up of LPDT as specified in the maintenance checklist
- PC7. check and maintain the tyre rims, air pressure, wheel nuts and treads
- PC8. tighten the loose battery terminals with the help of an auto-electrician
- PC9. replace/change the spare parts and consumables of the vehicle as per the schedule
- PC10. replenish coolants, lubricants and fluids as per SOP
- PC11. clean air filter dust bowls, footplates, pedals and steps and ensure that they are free from
mud, or dirt
- PC12. check the internal conditions of LPDT parts i.e. bearings, shafts, battery systems, motors,
wiring harness and connectors and other similar parts etc. to test its working status and expected conditions
- PC13. check the instrument panel and control gauge to identify out of range readings to find the
defect/cause of failure
- PC14. check the systems of the LPDT to find out root cause of the problems like any leakage, short
circuit in parts, breakage of wires and clamps, unusual contact of electrical wires with other parts etc. and discuss the same with senior if required
- PC15. ensure that no diagnosis on the machine is performed when it is running or is still hot
