#### DMR/LSC/Q0501: Junior Engineer -Electrical & Mechanical System (Metro
Rail)
Brief Job Description
A Junior Engineer -Electrical & Mechanical Systems (Metro Rail) is responsible for the operation and
maintenance of Electro-Mechanical (E&M) systems in metro stations, including underground and elevated
setups. The individual ensures energy efficiency, infrastructure management, and compliance with safety
standards for escalators and elevators, contributing to smooth station operations. The person coordinates
with teams to implement preventive maintenance and address technical issues.
Personal Attributes
The individual should possess strong analytical and organizational skills, and attention to detail. A
proactive approach to problem-solving and commitment to safety and efficiency are essential.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. DMR/LSC/N9901: Implement metro operations training and safety procedures
2. DMR/LSC/N0501: Monitor the operations and maintenance of E&M systems in metro railways
3. DMR/LSC/N0502: Monitor and maintain the underground and elevated station systems
4. DMR/LSC/N0503: Monitor energy utilization and infrastructure maintenance in metro stations
5. DMR/LSC/N0504: Monitor and ensure safe escalator and elevator operations in metro systems
6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### DMR/LSC/N0501: Monitor the operations and maintenance  of E&M
systems in metro railways
Description
This unit is about the monitoring, troubleshooting, and optimizing critical Electro-Mechanical (E&M)
systems, including ECS, TVS, fire suppression, and hydraulic systems, for metro operations, ensuring
efficient operations, compliance with codal requirements, and troubleshooting during commissioning.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Overview of E&M Systems
Introduction to Variable Refrigerant Volume (VRV) System
Site Visit to Elevated Station for VRV System
Environmental Control System (ECS)
Tunnel Ventilation System (TVS)
Visit to ECS and TVS Demo Room
Site Visit to Underground Station for ECS System
Fire Detection and Suppression Systems
Visit to Underground Station for TVS and Fire Equipment
Hydraulic Systems
Site Visit to PTM
Station Illumination
Building Management System (BMS)
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
