HYC/Q4402: Compressed Biogas Plant Technician
Brief Job Description
Compressed Biogas Plant technician will be responsible for maintaining the optimum plant conditions for
biogas production, select proper size and design of biogas plant and to construct a biogas plant, perform
testing of biogas plants identify and locate the problems in biogas plants and repair them etc.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have a technical background & aptitude, ability to give attention to detail,
adaptability, teamwork, and a strong commitment & passion towards safety and sustainability. The person
should have the ability to think creatively and work individually and in a team for extended hours in
confined spaces or at height. Experience with operating basic site machinery (e.g., forklift/telehandler,
boom lift, scissor lift) is an advantage.

HYC/N4403: Introduction to Compressed Biogas
Description
This unit provides general introduction about the biogas plants in general and about compressed biogas
(CBG) plants in particular, general process of producing the biogas, feedstocks/raw material for biogas
production, anaerobic decomposition process, significance of optimum Condition for Biogas production,
production of compressed biogas (CBG) from biogas and their applications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Biogas/CBG production process
Raw materials for biogas production
Compressed biogas and applications
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Compressed Biogas
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Define what is biogas
PC2. Know its composition and energy content
PC3. Understand why it is also called Bio-CNG
PC4. Understand the basic process of anaerobic decomposition/digester
PC5. Identify different steps involved in the process like crushing, slurry preparation, anaerobic
digestion (fermentation), Biogas Purification, Bio-NG Compression & Cylinder or Cascade Gas
Filling
PC6. Identify key factors affecting anaerobic biodigester performance
PC7. Appreciate importance of different micro-organisms involved in biogas production
PC8. Know the role of methanogenic microorganisms
PC9. Identify different raw materials which can be used to produce biogas
PC10. Identify by-products produced during the process
PC11. Understand wastes being generated and its handling/disposal
PC12. Know difference between biogas and compressed biogas
PC13. Define benefits of biogas and CBG
PC14. Know potential of CBG in India
PC15. Appreciate importance of raw material availability for CBG production
PC16. Know about different types of bio-digesters and plant configuration
PC17. Identify and understand different units/components in a CBG plant
PC18. Understand the laboratory testing and data recording
PC19. Know the proper sampling procedures

HYC/N4404: CBG Plant and Machinery
Description
This unit covers details about different types of biogas plants, their configuration, different mechanical
systems involved and installation and commissioning activities.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Plant & machinery
Types of biogas plants
Fundamentals of mechanical systems in a digester
Elements and Performance Criteria
CBG Plant and Machinery
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Know different components of a biogas plant
PC2. Understand main anaerobic digester working
PC3. Identify the role of slurry storage tank
PC4. Know the type of slurry and sludge pumps used
PC5. Understand types of organic waste crusher shredder machine
PC6. Know different types /configuration of anaerobic digesters
PC7. Identify classification of biogas plants depending upon plant design and mode of working
PC8. Know about Batch Type Biogas Plant and Continuous Type biogas Plant
PC9. Know about movable Type Drums biogas plant
PC10. Understand fundamental of mechanical systems in a biogas plant
PC11. Identify which type of pumps to be used for a type of feed stock/raw material, Total solid (TS)
concentration
PC12. Know the importance of system redundancy
PC13. Understand piping system design based on flow rates, pressure, Moisture content,
Temperature etc
PC14. Understand importance of regular mixing in a digester
PC15. Know different types of mixers to be used
PC16. Know other important components like feedstock conveyer
PC17. Understand importance of biogas leak detection systems
PC18. Know the working gas detection and alarm systems to ensure safety
PC19. Identify pressure regulators and other valves in the plant
PC20. Know about biogas blowers and compressors

HYC/N4405: Testing and Repairing in a CBG Plant
Description
This unit covers details of various testing related requirements as well as maintenance & repairing
activities in a CBG plant.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Laboratory Testing
System inspection and maintenance
Elements and Performance Criteria
Laboratory Testing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify what different routine testing is to be done in a CBG plant
PC2. Know significance of regular calibration
PC3. Understand how to measure Total Solids (TS) in a slurry
PC4. Test volatile solids (VS) in TS
PC5. Test pH in a digester
PC6. Test other parameters like total alkalinity and temperature etc
PC7. Measure methane and CO2 content in biogas
PC8. Monitor other important parameters like COD, BOD, nutrients, metals, Nitrogen,
Phosphorous, Potassium etc
PC9. Know the working of different online analysers and laboratory equipments
PC10. Follow proper sampling procedure and frequency of testing as per manufacturer protocols
and SOPs
PC11. Report the data as per prescribed format to the operator
System inspection and maintenance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. Appreciate importance of system inspection & maintenance
PC13. Identify routine versus major maintenance
PC14. Know the Impact of Process Conditions on Frequency of maintenance
PC15. Check digester tanks periodically for leaks, cracks, or other evidence of structural problems
PC16. periodically check and operate pressure relief valves (PRVs)
PC17. Check the piping and equipment for leaks daily
PC18. Monitor the corrosion on a regular basis
PC19. Replace any Worn or damaged seals and/or drive belt in a pump
PC20. Check the mechanical mixers for any excessive vibration and rectify the same
