TNSD/CSC/Q0301: Electro - Mechanical Technician
Brief Job Description
Electro-Mechanical Technician job role is designed with various modules to equip learners with the
necessary skills for diverse industrial applications. This qualification ultimately helps learners in the
following ways: To become proficient in the design and manufacturing of control panels, industrial
automation, and machine maintenance.
Personal Attributes
Design and construct a Mechatronics subsystem, write a PLC program to automate the system, and test its
function for prescribed outputs. To secure employment opportunities across a wide range of sectors,
including Engineering/Manufacturing industries, process industries, and the automobile industry. To pursue
entrepreneurial ventures.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. TNSD/CSC/N0312: Apply Sensor Technology Principles.
- 2. TNSD/CSC/N0313: Operate and Troubleshoot Pneumatic and Electro-Pneumatic Systems.
- 3. TNSD/CSC/N0314: Operate and Troubleshoot Hydraulic and Electro-Hydraulic Systems.
- 4. TNSD/CSC/N0315: Program and Interface Microcontrollers with C
- 5. TNSD/CSC/N0316: Program and Troubleshoot Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs).
- 6. TNSD/CSC/N0318: Operate and Program CNC Machines.
- 7. TNSD/CSC/N0317: Implement and Analyze Control Systems & Instrumentation.
- 8. TNSD/CSC/N0319: Design and Optimize Mechatronics Systems.
- 9. TNSD/CSC/N0320: Create 2D and 3D Drawings using SolidWorks.
- 10. TNSD/CSC/N0321: Simulate CNC Programs using Keller Software.
- 11. TNSD/CSC/N0322: Apply Technical Mathematics for Engineering Applications.
- 12. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

TNSD/CSC/N0312: Apply Sensor Technology Principles.
Description
Able to familiarize the sensors and their functionalities. Able to understand their applications in industries.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the basics of different types and working of Sensors & Transducers.
Familiarize with the Classification, characteristics & application of sensors. Familiarize with the
Classification, Application of Proximity Sensors
Understand different types of sensors used for Distance, Displacement, Force Temperature and
Pressure
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Familiarize the basics of different types and working of Sensors & Transducers with the
Classification , characteristics & application
PC2. Familiarize with the Classification , Application of Proximity Sensors
PC3. Familiarize with the Classification , Application of Temperature Sensors
PC4. Familiarize different types of sensors used for Distance & Displacement
PC5. Familiarize different types of sensors used for Force and Pressure
PC6. Familiarize how to select sensors for different Mechatronics application
PC7. Demonstrate functions of different sensors.
PC8. Demonstrate functions of different Transducers
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Familiarize the basics of different types and working of Sensors & Transducers with the
Classification , characteristics & application
KU2. Familiarize with the Classification , Application of Proximity Sensors
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Familiarize different types of sensors used for Force and Pressure
GS2. Familiarize how to select sensors for different Mechatronics application

TNSD/CSC/N0313: Operate and Troubleshoot Pneumatic and Electro-
Pneumatic Systems.
Description
Able to understand the functioning of all the pneumatic components Able to make pneumatic circuits for
industrial applications and troubleshoot.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the Importance and basic concepts of Pneumatic Systems.
Understand the function of Compressor and properties of compressed air.
Familiarize the Symbols and schematic design of Pneumatic Circuits.
Familiarize the function of Direction, Pressure, Flow control Valves.
Understand different types of Actuators and Output devices.
Understand Safety awareness, Testing and Trouble shooting of Pneumatic Systems.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand the Basic concepts of Pneumatic Systems, Properties of compressed air.
PC2. Understand the Function of Pneumatic Components and Compressor.
PC3. Familiarize the Symbols and schematics of Pneumatic Circuits.
PC4. Familiarize the function of Direction control Valves, Pressure control Valves , Flow control
Valves.
PC5. Understand the different types of Actuators and Output devices.
PC6. Understand the Safety awareness, Testing and Trouble shooting of Pneumatic Systems.
PC7. Demonstrate Various components of pneumatics system and construct pneumatic circuit to
check functionality.
PC8. Demonstrate the Construction of an electro-pneumatic circuit and check functionality of a
process.
PC9. Perform Simulation of the Pneumatic circuit on simulation software and detect fault as per
diagnostic procedure for Pneumatics system design.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand the Basic concepts of Pneumatic Systems, Properties of compressed air.
KU2. Understand the Function of Pneumatic Components and Compressor.
KU3. Understand the different types of Actuators and Output devices.
Generic Skills (GS)

TNSD/CSC/N0314: Operate and Troubleshoot Hydraulic and Electro-
Hydraulic Systems.
Description
Able to understand the functioning of all the hydraulic components Able to construct & execute hydraulic
circuits for given industrial applications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the properties of hydraulic fluid and function of Reservoir
Understand the Symbols and schematics and design of Hydraulic Circuits
Familiarize the function of Direction, Pressure, Flow control Valves.
Understand different types of Actuators and Output devices.
Understand the various hydraulic oil types and their selection methods.
Learn about Safety awareness, Testing and Trouble shooting of Hydraulic Systems
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand the properties of hydraulic fluid and function of Reservoir
PC2. Understand Function of Direction, Pressure, Flow control Valves.
PC3. Understand the different types of Actuators and Output devices.
PC4. Understand the types of hydraulic oils and selection
PC5. Understand the simple hydraulic circuits
PC6. Demonstrate installation of accessories in hydraulic system and troubleshoot defects
PC7. Construct hydraulic circuit for verification of various processes to assess functioning of
valves and auxiliaries.
PC8. Demonstrate to Install hydraulic pump, motors and carryout maintenance of these
components
PC9. Perform different hydraulic system such as Clamp control, injection control, reciprocating
screw, oil filtration, hydraulic press control, accumulator control
PC10. Perform Simulation of the Hydraulic circuit on simulation software and detect fault as per
diagnostic procedure for Hydraulics system design.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand the properties of hydraulic fluid and function of Reservoir
KU2. Understand the different types of Actuators and Output devices.
Generic Skills (GS)
