#### PSS/Q3501: Technician - Tower Construction
Brief Job Description
The individual in this job carries out erection, installation and commissioning of power transmission towers
of all types.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be physically and mentally able to perform essential functions of the job safely. Work
is performed mostly outdoors in all weather conditions. He/she will be required to perform manual
activities for long periods of time.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PSS/N1331: Apply basic health and safety practices for power related work
2. PSS/N1336: Working effectively with others
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Erection and Stringing
Specializes in Tower Erection and Stringing of Transmission lines.
1. PSS/N3501: Perform erection and stringing of a transmission tower
Elective 2: Foundation
Specializes in Foundation of Transmission Tower
1. PSS/N3502: Construct the foundation of a transmission tower

#### PSS/N1331: Apply basic health and safety practices for power related
work
Description
This unit covers health, safety and security for power related work. This includes procedures and practices
that candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work environment in a power
plant, power station/substation or on the field while working on power equipment. It covers responsibilities
towards self, others, assets and the environment
Scope
This unit/task covers the following:
Follow workplace health and safety practices
Follow fire safety practices
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures

#### PSS/N3501: Perform  erection and stringing of a transmission tower
Description
This unit covers the erection of a transmission tower and stringing of a transmission line as per
procedures.
Scope
This unit/ task covers the following:
Perform erection of a transmission tower as per specifications and standard operating procedures
Perform stringing of a transmission line as per specifications and standard operating procedures
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform erection of a transmission tower as per specifications and standard operating procedures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. read and interpret the instructions for erection of the transmission tower
- PC2. identify various tools and machineries required for tower erection
- PC3. receive and check the materials, fasteners, members etc. of tower
- PC4. ensure proper loading, unloading and storage of the materials and components at the site
- PC5. check that laying of the foundation including stub setting is as per the layout drawing
- PC6. carry out section-wise ground assembly of the transmission tower
- PC7. carry out proper fitting and arrangement of sections, cross arm and peck assembly as per
instructions given by the concerned authority
- PC8. carry out tightening of fasteners and punching of threads as per requirement
- PC9. perform tack welding of nuts of the transmission tower
- PC10. paint joints of the transmission tower
- PC11. carry out earthing of the tower
Perform stringing of a transmission line as per specifications and standard operating procedures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. mount insulators
- PC13. lay earth wire and conductors
- PC14. check the tension and ground clearance of earth wire and conductors
- PC15. fix other accessories such as anti-climbing device, danger plates, bird guard, dampers etc. as
per requirement
- PC16. run conductor/rope through travelers to reduce friction in case of sagging
- PC17. attach pulling equipment to conductor/ rope
- PC18. perform paving of conductors on the ground along the tower taking into account permissible
span length and sag
- PC19. transfer the conductor from traveler to insulator
- PC20. secure the conductor using clamps and ties

This unit covers the preparation and formation of a transmission tower foundation as per procedures.

- PC12. remove the shuttering after performing the curing process
- PC13. check foundation for any voids or cracks with approved method
- PC14. ensure backfilling of earth following standard procedure
- PC15. inspect the tower foundations and stubs etc. for any damage and perform its rectification/
reinforcement
