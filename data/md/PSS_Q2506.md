PSS/Q2506: Assistant Technician - Street Light Installation & Maintenance
Brief Job Description
The incumbent in the job is responsible for installation, testing, commissioning, operations & maintenance
of a streetlight network including poles, fixtures, cables, junction boxes, feeder pillars and other associated
accessories.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to work outdoors in all weather conditions and requires availability in the
event of emergency situations. The job also requires the ability to lift large heavy objects and perform
strenuous physical labor. The individual requires bending, climbing, pulling, carrying, kneeling, walking,
and standing for significant periods of time.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. PSS/N2510: Perform installation of street lighting system
- 2. PSS/N2511: Perform testing and repairing of street lighting system
- 3. PSS/N1331: Apply basic health and safety practices for power related work
- 4. PSS/N1336: Working effectively with others

PSS/N2510: Perform installation of street lighting system
Description
This OS unit is about performing the installation/erection of street light poles, fixtures, lighting system and
laying underground wiring/cable in accordance with the company and Indian standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Street light installation
Laying the underground cable
Elements and Performance Criteria
Street light installation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. carry out the root survey for planning of the street lighting system installation and cable lay
work
PC2. identify tools and equipment required for street light installation
PC3. select appropriate type of lighting system based on the requirements and parameters i.e.
wattage and lumen level
PC4. test light fixture and drivers, prior to the installation
PC5. install protection devices such as surge protection device, voltage fluctuation, over voltage
protection etc.
PC6. connect the streetlight fixture with the power line meant for street lighting
PC7. set timing on lighting system control switch for automatic switch on and off operation of light
Laying the underground cable
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. select and lay underground cable for street lighting as per the standards
PC9. connect power supply from distribution transformer to the street lighting system
PC10. troubleshoot and rectify all the problems involved in underground electrical wiring
PC11. report to supervisor or engineer any problem if needed in laying down the wiring
PC12. perform primary testing on the cables and connections of street light
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. relevant standards, policies and installation procedures followed in the organization
KU2. common electricity terminology e.g. current, voltage, resistance, kilowatt (kw), kilowatt
hour(kwh)

PSS/N2511: Perform testing and repairing of street lighting system
Description
This OS unit is about performing testing and repairing activities on the street light system
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Inspect and test the street lights
Repair and replace the light components
Elements and Performance Criteria
Inspect and test the street lights
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify testing equipment such as tester, multimeter, wire, bulb etc. required during
maintenance work
PC2. check all the intersections, joints, junction box, street light fixture, light bulb etc. for any
defect as per the checklist
PC3. use lux meter to check the lux level of light fixture
PC4. test the supply across the MCB / ON-OFF switch and all the joints and intersections in the
wiring
PC5. test the cable and lamp head by multimeter or tester or clipon meter for checking the
continuity of supply
PC6. check the fixture and other parts of street light system
Repair and replace the light components
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. check the photoelectric control relay and surge protection devices of lighting system and
repair or replace in case of any defect or problem as per the organizational procedures
PC8. troubleshoot underground electrical wiring and repair it in case of any problem
PC9. check the street light equipment including drivers, heads, poles, controllers, lights, circuitry,
switches, fuses, and cabinet and repair or replace in case of any defect or problem as per the
organizational procedures
PC10. replace and retrofit the existing light fixture with modern light fixture, where required
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. relevant street lighting operational and maintenance standards followed in the organization
KU2. technical specification of lights, associated components and protection devices
KU3. the wiring diagram and cabling system of the street light
KU4. use of lux meter, multimeter and other testing equipment

PSS/N1331: Apply basic health and safety practices for power related
work
Description
This unit covers health, safety and security for power related work. This includes procedures and practices
that candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work environment in a power
plant, power station/substation or on the field while working on power equipment. It covers responsibilities
towards self, others, organizational assets and the environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Follow workplace health and safety practices
Follow fire safety practices
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow workplace health and safety practices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. use protective clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions (Protective clothing:
leather or asbestos gloves, flame proof aprons, flame proof overalls buttoned to neck, cuff
less (without folds) trousers, reinforced footwear, helmets/hard hats, cap and shoulder
covers, ear defenders/plugs, safety boots, knee pads, particle masks, glasses/goggles/visors)
(Equipment: hand and face shields, machine guards, residual current devices, shields, dust
sheets, respirator)
PC2. identify job-site hazards and possible causes of risks/accident at the workplace (Hazards:
electrical hazards, sharp edged and heavy tools, heated metals, oxyfuel and gas cylinders,
welding radiation, hazardous surfaces, hazardous substances, physical hazards. Possible
causes of risk and accident: physical actions, not following instructions, inattention, sickness
and incapacity, health, not taking safety precautions)
PC3. use standard safe working practices when working at heights, confined areas, trenches and
with electrical equipment
PC4. follow warning signs and symbols while working with electrical systems (Warning signs:
Danger plate, high voltage area, out of service, etc.)
PC5. ensure positive isolation of electrical equipment and system as per the given standards
PC6. identify any abnormalities in electrical equipment or system viz temperature, pressure, flow
of current etc.
PC7. use various methods of accident prevention in the work environment (Methods of accident
prevention: use of equipment and working practices, safety notices, advice, instruction from
colleagues and supervisors)
PC8. use proper scaffolds, elevated platforms and ladders for working at height
PC9. inspect various tools and plants (T & P) routinely for any signs of oil, water leakages
PC10. store flammable materials and machine lubricating oil safely and correctly
