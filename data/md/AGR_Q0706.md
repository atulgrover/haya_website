AGR/Q0706: Floristry
Brief Job Description
The individual on the job is responsible for various activities such as designing the floral patterns and
decorating/ arranging the avenues with flowers, foliage, plants and also carry out bridal works and special
floral commissions in a creative way
Personal Attributes
The individual must possess creative thinking to create various floral designs along with basic marketing
and customer service skills. Ability to read, write and communicate well are other important attributes
required in this job role
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. AGR/N0730: Work organization and management (Floristry)
- 2. AGR/N0731: Care of materials and tools
- 3. AGR/N9930: Business and communications

- 6. AGR/N0726: Floral room decorations
- 7. AGR/N0727: Floral works using plants

AGR/N0730: Work organization and management (Floristry)
Description
This OS unit is about organizing and managing the work
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Organize and manage work
Elements and Performance Criteria
Organize and manage work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Be creative, artistic, innovative, and resourceful
PC2. Show initiative
PC3. Be responsive and flexible to changing circumstances
PC4. Apply practical, technical, and theoretical expertise
PC5. Maintain continuing professional development in order to be aware of current trends, new
ideas, styles, and materials
PC6. Show willingness to try new methods and embrace change
PC7. Plan and organize work effectively to make best use of available resources
PC8. Plan projects of varying sizes and complexity
PC9. Work within Health, Safety, and Environment regulations and practice
PC10. Work under time pressures, meeting deadlines and targets
PC11. Work effectively, using initiative and a high degree of autonomy
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The scope and characteristics of the floral industry and how it interacts with other professions
KU2. Principles and elements of floral design
KU3. Health and safety legislation, regulations, documentation, and practices.
KU4. The importance of work organization and prioritization
KU5. The importance of time management
KU6. The ways in which working practices can maximise profit by control of wastage and costs
KU7. Sustainable practices within the floral industry, including recycling and/or re-use of materials

AGR/N0731: Care of materials and tools
Description
This OS unit is about care and maintenance of tools and materials required for work
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain tools and materials
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain tools and materials
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify the origins of botanical materials in relation to the cost of supply and the practices of
their growers
PC2. Treat all botanical materials with appropriate post-harvest care
PC3. Condition and prepare the botanical materials for the design
PC4. Maintain the condition of botanical materials
PC5. Keep botanical materials at the correct temperature
PC6. Take proper care of all materials in relation to the product, including containers, sundry
materials, and other materials used in the design
PC7. Select and use tools and equipment in relation to the product
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The characteristics of flowers and botanical material
KU2. The correct nomenclature of all horticultural materials
KU3. The storage requirements of the materials, including correct temperatures
KU4. Correct handling procedures, and the most appropriate application of materials in relation to
projects
KU5. The care and handling of cut flowers and plants
KU6. The seasonal availability of plants and botanical materials
KU7. Ethical sourcing of plant materials, in relation to transport costs and responsible growers
KU8. Post-harvest care of materials
KU9. The tools and equipment used in floristry
KU10. Appropriate techniques in the use tools
Generic Skills (GS)

AGR/N0724: Floral design concept
Description
This OS unit is about creating floral designs
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carryout floral design concept
Elements and Performance Criteria
Carryout floral design concept
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Interpret commissions
PC2. Apply elements and principles of floral design to floral products
PC3. Ensure that botanical materials predominate in designs
PC4. Select botanical materials, containers, technical aids/materials, and decorative accessories
for planned floral designs
PC5. Create floral designs that interpret particular themes
PC6. Create floral designs appropriate for particular occasions
PC7. Create floral designs that take account of the need for sustainable practice
PC8. Create floral designs to suit the locations and environments where they will be situated
PC9. Create floral designs that are conventional and/or innovative
PC10. Communicate emotions through floral designs
PC11. Use dominance, contrasts, and combinations of colour to create harmony in design
PC12. Use colour theory effectively, for example by building colour, positioning colour, and using
tints, tones, and shades
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Principles and elements of floral design, including composition, colour, design, and technique
KU2. The rationales behind themes
KU3. Current trends and fashions in floral design
KU4. The significance of ethical and sustainable practice on floral design and realisation
KU5. The importance of floral designs and how they relate to their physical environment
KU6. Cultural differences in relation to floristry, for example weddings and other events
Generic Skills (GS)
