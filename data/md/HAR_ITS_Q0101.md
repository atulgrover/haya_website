HAR/ITS/N0101: Introduction to Digital Marketing and Website Creation
Description
Provides knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress and Digital Marketing concepts.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Creation and maintaining websites using html, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress and role of digital
marketing.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the role and importance of digital marketing in a rapidly changing business landscape.
PC2. Define the key elements of a digital marketing strategy
PC3. Differentiate between Traditional marketing and Digital Marketing
PC4. Define latest marketing trends and defining marketing goals
PC5. Know the basics of html, CSS and JavaScript
PC6. Understand the concept of CMS-Wordpress, its features and benefits
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Organization's structure, policies, procedures and guidelines.
KU2. The purpose and scope of the work to be carried out within the Organization.
KU3. WordPress, HTML, and CSS to manage and optimize content
KU4. Dynamic interactivity and DOM manipulation for web pages
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Produce accurate work output as per prescribed format
GS2. Manage tasks and organize content for timely project completion.
GS3. Actively listen to understand client or team feedback.
GS4. Design user-friendly websites with client needs in mind
GS5. Use data to optimize web design and performance

HAR/ITS/N0102: Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
Description
Provides knowledge and skills requires in optimizing websites.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Optimizing websites for higher search engine rankings, better user experience, and measurable
business outcomes.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand the working of Search Engines
PC2. Use the main search engine optimization techniques for business websites
PC3. Describe keywords and its types
PC4. Describe Competition Analysis and steps to perform Competition Analysis
PC5. Collaborate with web developers, content creators, and marketers to implement SEO best
practices across the site.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Organization's structure, policies, procedures and guidelines.
KU2. The purpose and scope of the work to be carried out within the Organization.
KU3. to collaborate with web developers to ensure SEO best practices are integrated into site
design and functionality
KU4. Ensure content is aligned with SEO objectives and regularly updated to maintain relevance
KU5. to communicate SEO results and strategies clearly to management
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Produce accurate work output as per prescribed format
GS2. collaborate with team and listen to client requirements.
GS3. Prioritizing SEO tasks such as keyword research, content optimization, and link building to
meet deadlines
GS4. Troubleshooting SEO challenges and continuously improving SEO strategies.

HAR/ITS/N0103: Search Engine Marketing (SEM)
Description
Provides knowledge of paid search advertising, including Google Ads, keyword bidding, campaign
management, and performance analysis.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Individuals can manage paid search campaigns, optimize ad performance. etc.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain Search Engine Marketing and its Process.
PC2. Define GoogleAds and create Google account to use google Ads.
PC3. Explain the concept of Paid marketing and Pay Per Click (PPC)
PC4. Manage Pay-Per-Click (PPC) campaigns effectively, including keyword selection, ad creation,
and budget management
PC5. Understand landing pages, PPC campaigns, campaign creation and management, ads
placement
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Organization's structure, policies, procedures and guidelines.
KU2. The purpose and scope of the work to be carried out within the Organization.
KU3. Paid Search Advertising, keyword research and selection.
KU4. To setup, organize, and manage SEM campaigns
KU5. Monitoring competitor ads, keywords, and bidding strategies to identify opportunities for
improvement.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Manage multiple SEM campaigns simultaneously, ensuring all deadlines are met without
compromising quality.
GS2. Prioritize tasks like keyword research, bid adjustments, and reporting to ensure smooth
campaign execution
GS3. Communicating SEM results, strategies, and recommendations clearly and effectively to
clients, team members, or management.

HAR/ITS/N0104: Social Media Marketing (SMM)
Description
Provide knowledge and skills to optimize social media platforms to drive and increase traffic to your
website
Scope
The scope covers the following :
leveraging platforms like Facebook, Instagram and others to promote brands, engage with
audiences, increase website traffic, drive sales, and enhance brand visibility through marketing
strategies
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand the meaning and importance of Social media
PC2. Understand to develop a social media marketing strategy
PC3. Know to optimize social media platforms like Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Youtube to drive
and increase traffic to your website
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Organization's structure, policies, procedures and guidelines.
KU2. To ensure that social media activities align with the organization's branding, values, and
marketing goals.
KU3. To work closely with the marketing team, designers, and content creators to produce
cohesive, brand-aligned campaigns and visuals
KU4. To monitor competitor's social media activities to understand industry trends and identify
opportunities for differentiation and improvement
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Write engaging and brand-aligned posts, respond to comments professionally, and clearly
convey the brand's message across platforms.
GS2. Develop unique content ideas and visually appealing posts that attract and resonate with the
target audience.
GS3. to effectively engage audiences, grow the brand's online presence, and achieve meaningful
business results in alignment with organizational goals.

HAR/ITS/N0105: Online Marketing
Description
Provides knowledge of various Digital Marketing Channels and Strategies.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Key digital marketing strategies across email, SMS, video, and e-commerce channels to effectively
reach, engage, and convert target audiences
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Define the concept of online marketing, including its strategies, channels, and tools used to
reach and engage audiences through digital platforms
PC2. Explain various techniques used for online marketing
PC3. Write effective content of SMS marketing
PC4. Describe concept of video marketing and steps to create video marketing strategies.
PC5. Describe E-Commerce marketing and its strategies
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The principles, types, and tools associated with email, SMS, video, and e-commerce marketing
to tailor content for effective audience engagement.
KU2. To use analytics to refine campaigns across all channels, ensuring strategies are aligned with
audience preferences and organizational goals.
KU3. to work effectively with design, content, and analytics teams to create cohesive and impactful
digital campaigns.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Efficiently plan, organize, and manage multiple campaigns, balancing time and resources to
meet deadlines and align with business objectives.
GS2. Write clear and engaging content for emails, SMS, videos, and social media.
GS3. Communicate with team members and clients to ensure strategies align with goals.

HAR/ITS/N0106: Lead Generation and Google Analytics
Description
provides knowledge and practical skills for Lead Generation and Google Analytics.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The process of lead generation, track website performance, analyze traffic, set goals, and optimize
marketing strategies for better conversion rates.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the meaning and process of Lead generation
PC2. To creating and setting landing pages for lead generation
PC3. To convert visitors into leads and leads into sales
PC4. to install Google Analytics in websites to track and analyze traffic and analyze basic Audience,
Acquisition, and Behavior and conversion reports
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

KU1. Organization's structure, policies, procedures and guidelines.
KU2. The purpose and scope of the work to be carried out within the Organization
KU3. The stages of lead generation, including identifying target audiences, creating effective
campaigns, and converting visitors into leads
KU4. How to design and optimize landing pages specifically for lead generation, ensuring they are
compelling, user-friendly, and aligned with conversion goals.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Strong oral communication skills to collaborate with team members and clients
GS2. Apply critical thinking to assess the performance of lead generation campaigns, identify areas
for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to optimize conversion rates
GS3. Organize tasks efficiently, manage multiple projects, and meet deadlines while ensuring
quality output.

Description
Provides knowledge of Google Adsense and Affiliate Marketing as key strategies for monetizing websites

Understanding and utilizing Google Adsense and Affiliate Marketing as effective methods for
