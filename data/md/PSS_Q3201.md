#### PSS/Q3201: Surveyor  -Transmission Line
Brief Job Description
The individual in this job carries out route survey of power transmission line and prepares line drawing of
the proposed line path with survey data.
Personal Attributes
Work is performed in outdoors and requires excessive travel in irregular terrain, ability to read and write
geographical data/drawing and to use GPS map. He/she should be well aware of all the type of tools and
tackles used in route survey of transmission line.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PSS/N1331: Apply basic health and safety practices for power related work
2. PSS/N1336: Working effectively with others
3. PSS/N3201: Conduct transmission line route survey

that candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work environment in a power
plant, power station/substation or on the field while working on power equipment. It covers responsibilities
towards self, others, assets and the environment

#### PSS/N3201: Conduct  transmission line route survey
Description
This unit is about carrying out route survey of power transmission line and to prepare line drawing of
proposed line path with survey data.
Scope
This unit/ task covers the following:
Conduct transmission line route survey on field
Prepare final route drawing and survey report
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct transmission line route survey on field
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify and draw tools and machinery for survey such as total station, global positioning
system (GPS) differential geographical positioning system (DGPS) etc.
- PC2. conduct route profiling
- PC3. read and interpret geographical drawings
- PC4. study hazard profiles of areas wherein transmission lines and towers are to be erected
- PC5. prepare a line drawing of proposed line path with survey data
- PC6. identify and mark the tower location
- PC7. conduct soil test to measure soil resistivity and to find out soil strength parameters at
different locations such as bore log test and standard penetration test etc.
- PC8. identify alternate routes to the tower location and suggest the best route as per
requirements
- PC9. seek relevant permissions and clearances from competent authorities such as railway
authorities, highway authorities etc. to build the tower
- PC10. ensure compliance of statutory clearances such as sag, ground clearance, right of way etc.
while marking the tower location
Prepare final route drawing and survey report
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. prepare final route drawing using necessary software(s)
- PC12. prepare the final survey report and submit to competent authorities for approval
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
1.  legislation, standards, policies, and procedures followed in the organization relevant to employment and performance conditions
2.  relevant health and safety requirements applicable in the work place

| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
