#### HYC/Q9201: Scaffolder -Oil & Gas (Construction services)
Brief Job Description
A Scaffolder - Oil & Gas (Construction Services) is responsible for constructing and dismantling scaffolding
structures in the oil and gas industry. The person at this job does planning/ configuring scaffolding,
erection of scaffolding of various types, establishing platforms and safety measures and dismantling and
removing on completion of job at the onshore and offshore sites. The person will carry out work safely with
a focus on energy conservation, system efficiencies and operational optimization. The Scaffolder is safety
conscious and detail-oriented individual who can contribute to a safe and efficient work environment in the
Oil & Gas industry.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have ability to be safety-focused, detail-oriented, physically fit, adaptable, team
player, problem-solving skills, time management and professional to ensure safety, efficiency and success
in the challenging Oil & Gas environment. The person should have the ability to work individually / team in
different shift.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HYC/N9202: Identification of Scaffolding Equipment and work
2. HYC/N9201: Perform Scaffolding Work Operations
3. HYC/N9301: Working effectively in a team
4. HYC/N9302: Maintain health, safety and security procedures
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### HYC/N9202: Identification of Scaffolding Equipment and work
Description
This unit is about identifying different types of scaffolding equipment and understanding their applications.
The person will learn about safety protocols, proper usage and maintenance of scaffolding equipment,
ensuring a solid foundation for efficient and safe scaffolding work in Oil & Gas industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identification and understanding of scaffolding equipment for scaffolding work
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identification of Scaffolding Equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify different types of scaffolding equipment and their use in construction projects.
- PC2. identify the purpose and specific applications of each type of scaffolding equipment.
- PC3. perform to utilize the components and materials that make up scaffolding structures.
- PC4. interpret the plans, drawings and specifications related to scaffolding equipment
Work Identification in Scaffolding
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. check the correct size, type and quantity of materials/components needed for scaffolding
work.
- PC6. conduct the initial inspection of scaffolding equipment for safety, functionality and
compliance with job requirements before use
- PC7. utilize proper techniques for assembling and dismantling scaffolding structures.
- PC8. apply safe work practices and follow procedures to ensure the stability and integrity of
scaffolding.
- PC9. identify and address potential hazards associated with scaffolding work.
- PC10. coordinate effectively with team members and supervisors regarding scaffolding equipment
and work requirements
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the types and applications of scaffolding equipment
- KU2. Components and materials used in scaffolding structures
- KU3. Safe work practices and procedures for scaffold stability
- KU4. Identifying and addressing potential hazards in scaffolding work
- KU5. Communication skills for conveying equipment and work requirements

#### HYC/N9201: Perform Scaffolding Work Operations
Description
This unit is about safely and efficiently assembling, erecting, altering and dismantling scaffolding
structures according to industry standards, work plans and safety procedures. This includes identifying and
using appropriate equipment, materials and tools, conducting inspections, implementing fall prevention
measures and maintaining clear communication with team members to ensure the successful completion
of scaffolding work.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Plan and prepare for the work
Erect, secure and inspect scaffolding
Dismantle Scaffolding
Work Completion as per site requirements
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Plan and prepare for the work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify work requirements through work orders or as instructed by Supervisor
- PC2. Identify and apply OISD standards, Occupational Health and Safety standards, Industry’s
codes of practice and specifications, environmental requirements throughout the work procedure.
- PC3. Identify, obtain and inspect resources required to meet the job specifications for compliance.
- PC4. select and interpret relevant plans, drawings and text in accordance with the work plan.
- PC5. check the correct size, type and quantity of materials/components for compliance with the
job specifications.
- PC6. coordinate for planning of work in detail, including sequencing, prioritizing and considering
plant security and capacity in accordance with site requirements.
- PC7. identify potential hazards, select prevention/control measures in accordance with the work
plan and site procedures.
- PC8. prepare the work area according to work requirements and site procedures.
Erect, secure and inspect scaffolding
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. check the expected loading on the scaffold/equipment and supporting structure by using
load tables and calculations in line with the work plan- Identify any limitations.
- PC10. Set up the foundations for the scaffold/equipment in accordance with both OISD standards
and the established work plan.
- PC11. Inspect the scaffold/equipment and its components before use for safety and compliance
with job and statutory requirements.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |

| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
