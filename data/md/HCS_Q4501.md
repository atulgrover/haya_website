#### HCS/Q4501: Craft Specialist- Paper Mache
Brief Job Description
The span of job for a paper mache craft specialist involves market study, perceiving trends underline,
predict and forecast trends setting in for the product, identify theme and based on theme and forecast,
develop designs for broad range of paper mache products in relation to the theme.
Personal Attributes
He/she should have flair for art and drawings, have basic knowledge of various geometrical figures, should
possess communication skills. He should have good understanding of colours in relation to national and
international buyer communities. He should be highly imaginative, creative, analytic and man of voracious
readings and keen listener too.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N4501: Preparation of Designs for objects of Paper Mache

#### HCS/N4501: Preparation of Designs for objects of Paper Mache
Description
This unit is about quantification and measurement of skills and competencies enabling one to work
satisfactorily in adding/creating thematic look to paper mache products, either by self-imagination and
creativity or inspired out of motifs supplied by the customer.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
study and adapt to design trends relevant to product class analyze trend and consolidate
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Study and adapt to design trends relevant to product class
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. market study for paper mache trends from various sources of information magazines, trade
fairs, cultural events, directional shopping trips, historical sources, internet etc.
- PC2. identify basic trends and forecasts based on past review of design exploration.
- PC3. determine differences between forecast and actual behaviour with possible reasons for such
behavioural difference
- PC4. review designs of products from other business players working in paper mache
- PC5. identify emerging trend and people preference
- PC6. translate inputs as per company procedure for adoption and adaptability
- PC7. apply statutory and regulatory standards applicable to design
- PC8. quality elements applicable to designs
Analyse trend and consolidate
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. identify theme and style requirements for designs
- PC10. budgeting to define and include elements of cost and time frame
- PC11. identify requirements of various materials along with its suppliers
- PC12. identify manufacturing techniques and processes with steps involved in creating designs
- PC13. design to include elements of client satisfaction for paper mache and its acceptability
- PC14. design to include any statutory and/or regulatory requirements to products
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. organization policies, procedures, guidelines
- KU2. product type, class and materials

satisfactorily in developing design for range of product class belonging to paper mache.

Reviewing Design Details

owners

information related to design

Specifying Designing Processes

merchandiser, painter, rubbing operator etc.

tolerance

- PC9. monitor sample development processes for time and cost defined under budget.
- PC10. problems or inconsistencies are identified and addressed suitably
- PC11. evaluate the prototype sample received
