#### RSC/Q7202: Chemical Safety Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The Chemical Safety Supervisor is responsible for conducting chemical risk assessment followed by
developing and implementing a safety program. The person is also responsible for conducting safety
audits and maintaining the related documents as well as performing accident investigation and suggesting
preventive measures.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to work independently and be comfortable in handling demanding tasks.
The person should be focused and attentive in performing the assigned task diligently. The individual
should be result oriented and positive in attitude. The person must be able to work independently under
the guidance of the engineer. The individual must be willing to work in the factory environment which
involves handling and managing chemicals also.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N7202: Conduct chemical risk assessment
2. RSC/N7203: Develop and implement safety program
3. RSC/N7204: Perform accident investigation and suggest preventive measures
4. RSC/N7205: Conduct safety audits and maintain documents including Global Standards on Chemical Safety
5. RSC/N5601: Maintain health and safety
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### RSC/N7202: Conduct chemical risk assessment
Description
This unit is about categorizing various chemicals and conducting a risk evaluation as per the standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identifying and categorizing chemicals
Assessing chemical exposure and consequences
Perform chemical risk evaluation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identifying and categorizing chemicals
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. create a wide list of all chemicals used, stored, or produced in the workplace
- PC2. refer to Safety Data Sheets (SDS) to understand the physical, chemical, and toxicological
properties of each chemical
- PC3. review and understand local, national, and international regulations pertaining to chemical
safety
- PC4. identify and categorize potential hazards associated with each chemical, including health
hazards (toxicity), physical hazards (flammability, reactivity), and environmental hazards
Assessing chemical exposure and consequences
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. determine potential exposure routes, such as inhalation, ingestion, and skin contact
- PC6. quantify potential exposure levels by considering factors like concentration, duration, and
frequency of exposure
- PC7. determine effect of mixing chemical with other chemicals or exposure to high temperatures
or pressure
- PC8. evaluate the potential health effects resulting from exposure, considering acute and chronic
effects
- PC9. evaluate potential health effects resulting from mixing chemicals or their exposure to high
temperatures or pressure
- PC10. assess the impact of chemical releases on the environment, including air, water, and soil
Perform chemical risk evaluation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. use qualitative methods (e.g., risk matrices) to assess and prioritize risks based on the
likelihood and severity of potential harm which can be caused by different chemicals
- PC12. employ quantitative methods, such as exposure modelling or calculations, to assess risks
more precisely
- PC13. prioritize identified risks based on the results of the risk assessment, focusing on high-
priority hazards

#### RSC/N7203: Develop and implement  safety program
Description
This unit is about developing and implementing safety program after risk evaluation is conducted.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Proposing risk mitigation measures
Implementation of safety program
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Proposing risk mitigation measures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. propose appropriate control measures to mitigate identified chemical risks
- PC2. consider the hierarchy of controls and recommend measures that eliminate or minimize the
risks at the source
- PC3. provide justifications for the selected risk mitigation measures, considering their
effectiveness and feasibility
- PC4. propose control measures to eliminate or reduce identified risks. Follow the hierarchy of
controls: elimination, substitution, engineering controls, administrative controls, and personal protective equipment
- PC5. develop and implement chemical safety policies and procedures to minimize risks and
ensure compliance with regulatory requirements
- PC6. create detailed procedures for the safe handling, storage, transportation, and disposal of
chemicals
Implementation of safety program
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. ensure that the organization complies with all relevant safety standards and regulatory
requirements
- PC8. develop and implement training programs for employees, emphasizing chemical hazards,
emergency response, and proper use of personal protective equipment (PPE)
- PC9. conduct regular training sessions and refresher courses to ensure ongoing awareness and
compliance
- PC10. develop comprehensive emergency response plans specific to chemical incidents
- PC11. coordinate drills and exercises to test the effectiveness of the plans
- PC12. establish a clear communication plan for disseminating chemical safety information to all
relevant stakeholders
- PC13. ensure proper labelling of chemicals and effective communication of hazards through Safety
Data Sheets (SDS)
- PC14. implement systems for reporting and documenting chemical incidents, spills, or exposure

#### RSC/N7204: Perform accident investigation and suggest preventive
measures
Description
This unit is about carrying out accident investigation and suggesting preventive measures to avoid
accidents in future.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Incident Reporting and Investigation
Implementing preventive measures
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Incident reporting and investigation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. implement systems for reporting and documenting chemical incidents, spills, exposures or
any type of chemical hazard
- PC2. implement damage control methods and ensure that employees are safe and sound
- PC3. perform thorough accident investigation and collect requisite data and perform analysis
- PC4. perform root cause identification and suggest preventive measures after successful analysis
- PC5. perform case study of accidents happened in the past
- PC6. implement preventive measures on basis of the analysis done on past accidents
- PC7. communicate investigation findings and preventive measures to management, employees,
and relevant stakeholders
Implementing preventive measures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. conduct group exercises simulating accident scenarios
- PC9. participants practice conducting investigations, identifying root causes, and proposing
preventive measures
- PC10. ensure preventive measures are being implemented
- PC11. ensure safety guidelines are being implemented as per the set rules and regulations
- PC12. update regulations, guidelines, preventive measures to be followed as required

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the importance of incident reporting systems for chemical incidents, spills, exposures, and
hazards

#### Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
#### Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will also lay
down the proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each PC.
- PC2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on the knowledge bank of questions created by the
SSC.
- PC3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- PC4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for the theory part for each
candidate at each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
- PC5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
- PC6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score a minimum of 70% of % aggregate marks to successfully clear the assessment.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
