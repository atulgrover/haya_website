#### CUTM/CSC/Q0101: Reverse  Engineering and Additive Manufacturing QA
Supervisor
Brief Job Description
Reverse Engineering and Additive Manufacturing QA Supervisor, also called Rapid Prototype Specialist is
responsible for understanding the design requirements, material, generate design program for additive
manufacturing of various parts 3D Printer, assemble it and inspect the parts to finalize and deliver the
work piece. Also maintain good condition of the machine with regular maintenance.
Personal Attributes
A Reverse Engineering and Additive Manufacturing QA Supervisor should possess strong analytical skills,
attention to detail, problem-solving ability, and a keen understanding of quality standards. Effective
communication, leadership, and teamwork are essential, along with adaptability to evolving technologies.
A commitment to precision and continuous improvement is crucial in this role.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CUTM/CSC/N0101: 3D digitizing, scan to CAD and interpretation of designs
2. CUTM/CSC/N0102: Programming, Metrology, Maintenance, and repair of 3D machine
3. CUTM/CSC/N0103: Operate 3D Printing machine
4. CUTM/CSC/N0104: Inspect, Finalize, and deliver the product
5. CUTM/VSQ/N7801: Ensure organizational health, safety, and security
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### CUTM/CSC/N0101: 3D  digitizing, scan to CAD and interpretation of
designs
Description
This NOS focuses on 3D digitizing, converting scan data into CAD models, and interpreting designs for
manufacturing and quality assurance. It involves operating 3D scanning equipment, refining scanned data,
and ensuring accuracy in CAD models for reverse engineering applications while maintaining industry
standards and quality control.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes handling 3D scanning technologies, processing scan data for CAD conversion, and verifying design accuracy. It applies to industries such as automotive, aerospace, and medical
device manufacturing, where precise digital modeling, reverse engineering, and quality assurance
are critical for product development and defect analysis.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
3D digitizing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. perform equipment's adjustment and calibration process.
- PC2. make technical decisions regarding the possibility of optical 3D digitizing Technical: Possible
or impossible to perform, the accuracy it is possible to attain for the object, required conditions for digitizing.
- PC3. make decisions regarding pre-process work, Pre-process work: disassembly, washing,
painting
- PC4. perform pre-process actions for applying matted coatings.
- PC5. apply matted coatings and optical marks.
- PC6. coordinate to fix objects for subsequent digitization.
- PC7. perform 3D digitizing for various objects with different materials, surface characteristics,
geometrical complexity.
- PC8. ensure to save results in the required form.
Scan to CAD and optimize
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. create editable CAD models by digitized data (polygonal models)
- PC10. apply mathematical modeling of additive manufacturing technologies to improve the
performance of device, machine, and mechanism parts.
- PC11. • restore missing data of elements of objects to be redesigned, from available data:
• • of polygonal models (for example, the gear wheel has only one preserved tooth, the worm has only one turn, or there is only one-third of the flange)
• • taken from connected parts
• • taken from existing objects by manual measuring (for example, the depth of a blind hole)

#### CUTM/CSC/N0102: Programming,   Metrology, Maintenance, and repair of
3D machine
Description
This NOS covers programming, metrology, maintenance, and repair of 3D machines used in reverse engineering and additive manufacturing. It involves precision measurement, machine calibration,
troubleshooting, and preventive maintenance to ensure accuracy and efficiency in 3D scanning and
printing, supporting quality assurance in manufacturing processes.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes programming 3D machines, implementing metrology techniques for accuracy, performing routine maintenance, and troubleshooting technical issues. It applies to industries such
as aerospace, automotive, and healthcare, where high-precision 3D scanning and additive manufacturing are essential for quality control, component validation, and product development.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Create programming
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select the best sequence for production of parts based on type and part specification.
- PC2. use skill specific software and related hardware.
- PC3. generate programs using software.
- PC4. transfer programs to machines.
- PC5. create or edit programs directly on the control.
Study metrology requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. prepare objects and measuring instruments for measurements.
- PC7. calibrate, adjust, and align measuring instruments.
- PC8. select correct measuring instruments and devices (styluses, probes, etc.), auxiliary and fixing
devices (vices, Vblocks, clamps, etc.), relating to measurement strategy.
- PC9. perform measurements using various control and measuring instruments.
- PC10. read the indications of measuring instruments.
- PC11. identify and estimate the correctness of measurements and the reliability of the data
obtained, to minimize associated human factor errors.
- PC12. find the required information in specialized reference books, tables, or diagrams.
- PC13. carry out routine maintenance of measuring instruments.
Maintenance and repair of machining equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. do preventative maintenance to avoid issues arising.
- PC15. make repairs when problem arise.

#### CUTM/CSC/N0103: Operate  3D Printing machine
Description
This NOS covers the operation of 3D printing machines, including machine setup, material selection, print
optimization, and troubleshooting. It ensures adherence to industry standards and safety guidelines while
producing high-quality printed parts for reverse engineering and additive manufacturing applications
across various industries.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes operating, calibrating, and maintaining 3D printers, optimizing print parameters,
and ensuring quality output. It applies to industries such as aerospace, healthcare, automotive, and
consumer goods manufacturing, where precise 3D printing is essential for prototyping, production,
and quality assurance in additive manufacturing.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Follow their selected process strategy.
- PC2. Follow a given process- strategy (external Additive Manufacturing programe)
- PC3. Upload generated programes to 3D printing.
- PC4. Mount and align base plate.
- PC5. Identify and designate the different processes.
- PC6. Optimize the process strategy.
- PC7. Define and adjust the parameters as a function of the operation sequence, material type,
type of operation
- PC8. Start the process from the raw material.
- PC9. Perform the Additive Manufacturing operations.
- PC10. Avoid vibration in the machining sequences.
- PC11. Record the machine parameter.
- PC12. Optimize machining strategies.
- PC13. Quickly react if anything goes wrong (making emergency stops)
- PC14. Obtain dimensions, geometries, surface roughness.
- PC15. Make the final parts conform to blueprints.
- PC16. Report health, safety, and environmental issues to the appropriate personnel.
- PC17. Report equipment failure to the appropriate personnel.
- PC18. Note all the production related information on the respective register, log, work sheet.
- PC19. Update all production information on the MIS system.
- PC20. Store the approved program against the batch number for future reference.

#### CUTM/CSC/N0104: Inspect, Finalize, and deliver the product
Description
This NOS focuses on inspecting, finalizing, and delivering products manufactured through reverse
engineering and additive manufacturing. It ensures quality control, defect detection, post-processing, and
compliance with industry standards. Professionals in this role validate product accuracy, functionality, and
readiness before dispatching to clients or further production stages.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes product inspection using metrology techniques, quality validation, post- processing, packaging, and final delivery. It applies to industries like aerospace, automotive,
healthcare, and consumer goods, where precision, compliance, and product integrity are critical for
successful reverse engineering and additive manufacturing applications.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Inspect the manufactured products
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. complete the post processing methods.
- PC2. clean the parts for inspection.
- PC3. check visual inspection on completion of all the process as per drawing.
- PC4. check the calibration status of various measuring instrument.
- PC5. identify parameter wise measuring instruments for measurement.
- PC6. inspect the machined product with various measurement tools like vernier caliper,
micrometer.
- PC7. check the surface finish as per requirements.
- PC8. check the fitness of the product using go or no-go devices.
- PC9. check visual inspection on the damage, burr, excess materials.
- PC10. document all the measured data for various parameters.
Finalize and deliver the product
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. clean the parts.
- PC12. pack the parts in proper way to avoid any damages during transport.
- PC13. complete the delivery documents.
- PC14. deliver the parts, drawings, and digital memory devices to the appropriate location and/or
personnel as required by the organization.
- PC15. dismount tools, clamping, accessories.
- PC16. clean the machines.
- PC17. set the environment to its initial state, ready for the next job.
- PC18. evaluate and report on factors and outcomes relevant to requirements and expectations.
