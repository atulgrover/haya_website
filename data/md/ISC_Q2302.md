#### ISC/Q2302: Process Manager- Green  Hydrogen Injection (Blast Furnace)
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role performs Hydrogen injection in Blast Furnace operations and its associated
functions.
Personal Attributes
The person should be patient, organized, team-oriented and have the ability to work for long hours in
adverse conditions. They must be able to plan and prioritise tasks effectively and have an eye for detail
and quality.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ISC/N0946: Manage and implement health and safety practices at the work place
2. ISC/N0947: Manage team and others at workplace

#### ISC/N0946: Manage  and implement  health and safety practices at the

Description
This OS unit is about managing and implementing health, hygiene, safety and sustainable practices for
optimising use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain safe and secure working environment
Maintain health and hygiene
Manage housekeeping at workplace
Implement material and energy conservation practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain safe and secure working environment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify hazardous activities and the possible causes of risks or accidents in the workplace

others

trenches, elevated places and confined areas

potential hazards

identified risks and safety practices

- PC7. ensure that relevant safety boards/signs are placed on the shop floor for the safety of self
and others

designated person

evacuation etc.
Maintain health and hygiene

- PC10. ensure team is aware about hygiene and sanitation regulations and following them on the
shop floor
- PC11. ensure workplace, equipment, restrooms etc. are cleaned and sanitized regularly
- PC12. ensure availability of running water, hand wash and alcohol-based sanitizers at the
workplace

#### ISC/N2303: Prepare for hydrogen injection in Blast Furnace operations
Description
This OS unit is about performing preparatory activities for carrying out various Blast Furnace operations
related to Hydrogen injection
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out preparatory activities for Blast Furnace operations
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
• Carry out preparatory activities for Blast Furnace operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. obtain the required information and work completed in previous shift from logbook,
discussions etc. and take handover from the previous shift operator by following organizational procedures

the requirement w.r.t. hydrogen injection
- PC3. ensure proper functioning of hydrogen injection system
- PC4. ensure hydrogen injection rates permissible as per SOP
- PC5. ensure that work area is clean and free of any obstacle or encumbrances
- PC6. ensure that all safety measures, interlocks, alarms are working and in conformance with the
safe operation of Blast Furnace w.r.t. hydrogen injection as per SOP
- PC7. coordinate with supply chain of Hydrogen for consistent supply including storage facilities and
Hydrogen production source

- KU1. relevant standards and procedures followed in the company

- KU6. Operation and control system of hydrogen injection system
- KU7. Supply chain of hydrogen with storage and transportation of hydrogen

Description
This OS unit is about performing Blast Furnace operations with respect to hydrogen injection and the
standards specified by the organization.

Perform Blast Furnace operations with respect to hydrogen injection

continuous basis

- PC12. Monitor the effect of hydrogen on RAFT
- PC13. Control of RAFT with varying hydrogen flow rates

- PC15. Establish relation of hydrogen injection with Hot Metal quality
- PC16. monitor the top gas analysis on continuous basis
- PC17. Establish relation of hydrogen injection with top gas analysis and flow

- PC19. monitor the heat loss in furnace and take appropriate action to reduce the losses
- PC20. monitor the CO2 emission with varying hydrogen flow rates
- PC21. Establish relation of hydrogen injection with CO2 emission at different flow rates of hydrogen

#### Performance Criteria (PC) (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will also
lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each PC.
- PC2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
- PC3. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
- PC4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
- PC5. In case of successfully passing only certain number of NOSs, the trainee is eligible to take subsequent
assessment on the balance NOS's to pass the Qualification Pack.
- PC6. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack
