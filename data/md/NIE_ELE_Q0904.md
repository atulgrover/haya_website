NIE/ELE/Q0904: Assistant Computer Technician
Brief Job Description
The Assistant Computer Technician certificate course is designed to equip learners with the essential skills
for assembling, troubleshooting, and installing computer hardware, software, and peripheral devices.
Developed in collaboration with industry experts, the course prepares students for both employment and
entrepreneurial opportunities in the IT hardware field.
Personal Attributes
The Assistant Computer Technician course develops key attributes like technical expertise, attention to
detail, and problem-solving abilities. Students learn to identify and configure hardware and software,
install devices, follow safety procedures, and troubleshoot system issues. They also gain skills in managing
operating systems, ensuring security through antivirus software, and performing diagnostic tasks with
precision and analytical thinking.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. NIE/ELE/N0918: Identification of Computer Hardware Components and Peripherals devices
- 2. NIE/ELE/N0920: Installation of Computer Hardware Components and setting up of peripherals
devices
- 3. NIE/ELE/N0919: Installation and Configuration of Computer Software
- 4. NIE/ELE/N0917: Troubleshooting and Maintenance of Computer System
- 5. NIE/ELE/N0921: Comprehensive Computer System Setup, Installation, and Maintenance Project
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

NIE/ELE/N0918: Identification of Computer Hardware Components and
Peripherals devices
Description
This module provides a comprehensive understanding of different types of computers, their working
principles, and the various hardware components that form the core of a computer system. Students will
learn to distinguish between hardware and software components, identify input/output devices, and
recognize different types of motherboards, memory systems (RAM, ROM), storage devices, and computer
ports and connectors. The module combines theoretical knowledge with practical skills, enabling students
to install and configure hardware such as motherboards, memory, storage, and peripherals like printers,
keyboards, and mice. Through hands-on experience with tools and equipment, students will be prepared to
effectively manage, troubleshoot, and support computer hardware systems in real-world settings.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS includes the identification and understanding of various computer types, hardware
components, and peripheral devices. It covers differentiating between hardware and software,
identifying input/output devices, and recognizing components such as motherboards, memory
systems, storage devices, and computer ports. This NOS also focuses on developing practical skills
for installing, configuring, and managing these components, ensuring students are equipped to
handle real-world computer hardware tasks, including troubleshooting and maintenance.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identification of Computer Types and Hardware Components
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Students shall identify different types of computers, including desktop, laptop, and server
systems, and describe their working principles.
PC2. Students shall differentiate between hardware and software components, explaining the
function of key hardware elements such as the CPU, motherboard, and RAM.
Recognition and Installation of Input/Output Devices and Peripherals
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Students shall recognize common input/output devices (e.g., keyboard, mouse, printer,
monitor) and explain their roles in a computer system.
PC4. Students shall demonstrate the ability to install and configure various input/output devices
and peripheral components.
Identification and Configuration of Motherboards, Memory, and Storage Devices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Students shall identify different types of motherboards, memory (RAM, ROM), and storage
devices (HDD, SSD) and explain their functions within the computer system.
PC6. Students shall demonstrate the installation and configuration of motherboards, memory
#### modules, and storage devices.

performance.
KU3. Identify common input (keyboards, mice) and output devices (monitors, printers) and
comprehend the processes involved in their installation and configuration for proper
operation.
KU4. Gain knowledge of different motherboard types, various memory (RAM, ROM), and storage
systems (HDD, SSD), as well as the various computer ports and connectors, understanding
their purposes and specifications for effective connectivity.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Develop the ability to analyze and assess various types of computers and their components,
enabling informed decisions regarding hardware selection and configuration.
GS2. Acquire hands-on skills in installing and configuring computer hardware, including
motherboards, memory systems, and peripherals, enhancing technical competence in real-
world scenarios.
GS3. Cultivate problem-solving abilities to troubleshoot and resolve issues related to hardware
components and connectivity, ensuring efficient system performance.
GS4. Strengthen attention to detail when identifying components, connectors, and installation
processes, promoting accuracy and thoroughness in technical tasks.

NIE/ELE/N0920: Installation of Computer Hardware Components and
setting up of peripherals devices
Description
The NOS focuses on the installation of computer hardware components and the setup of peripheral
devices. Upon completion of this module, students will be proficient in installing all necessary components
within the CPU cabinet, including the case, fan, power supply, heat sink, CPU, motherboard, and various
storage devices such as RAM, hard disk drives, and SSDs. Additionally, students will learn to install and
connect peripherals like printers and scanners to the computer system and execute the corresponding
setup programs. The module emphasizes the importance of following safety procedures while handling and
installing equipment to ensure a secure working environment. Through a combination of theoretical
knowledge and practical experience, students will be well-equipped to manage hardware installation and
setup tasks effectively in real-world scenarios.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the essential training for installing computer hardware components and configuring
peripheral devices. Students will gain hands-on experience in assembling internal CPU components,
including the motherboard and storage devices. They will also learn to set up and connect
peripherals like printers and scanners. Emphasizing safety procedures, this NOS equips students to
handle installations securely and effectively in professional settings.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Installation of CPU Components
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Correctly install all components within the CPU cabinet, including the case, fan, power supply,
heat sink, CPU, and motherboard.
Installation of Storage Devices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC2. Install different types of storage devices, including RAM, hard disk drives, and SSDs, according
to specifications.
PC3. Ensure all storage devices are properly connected to the motherboard and power supply.
Setup of Peripheral Devices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Successfully install and connect peripheral devices such as printers and scanners to the
computer system.
PC5. Execute the necessary setup programs for each peripheral device to ensure functionality.
Adherence to Safety Procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Adhere to established safety procedures while handling and installing computer equipment.

NIE/ELE/N0919: Installation and Configuration of Computer Software
Description
The NOS focuses on the installation and configuration of computer software, equipping students with
essential skills for effective software management. Upon completion of this module, students will
understand the booting processes in both DOS and Windows environments and the functionalities of BIOS
utilities, including its setup. They will learn to install operating systems and relevant application software
while also gaining the ability to reset BIOS and system passwords. This module combines theoretical
concepts with practical experience, ensuring students are well-prepared to handle software installation
and configuration tasks in various computing environments.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS encompasses the essential processes involved in the installation and configuration of
computer software, including operating systems and application software. It covers understanding
the booting processes in DOS and Windows environments, as well as BIOS utility functions and setup
procedures. Students will gain hands-on experience in partitioning hard drives, installing various
operating systems, and managing software configurations. Additionally, the module addresses
security aspects such as resetting BIOS and system passwords. This comprehensive approach
prepares students for real-world scenarios in software management and troubleshooting, ensuring
they can effectively support and maintain computer systems.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Boot Processes and BIOS Configuration
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Describe the booting process in DOS and Windows environments.
PC2. Access and navigate the BIOS setup utility, configuring boot options and settings effectively.
Disk Management and Operating System Installation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Format hard disks and partition drives according to specified requirements.
PC4. Execute the installation of various operating systems, including configuring dual-boot setups
as necessary
Application Software Installation and Configuration
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Identify various types of application software relevant to user needs and install them correctly.
PC6. Install and configure antivirus software to provide optimal protection against threats.
Command Prompt Execution and Security Management
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Execute various Windows command prompt commands effectively to manage software and
system configurations.
PC8. Reset the BIOS password securely while ensuring compliance with security protocols.

NIE/ELE/N0917: Troubleshooting and Maintenance of Computer System
Description
The NOS concentrates on the troubleshooting and maintenance of computer systems. Upon completion of
this module, students will acquire a comprehensive understanding of troubleshooting methodologies and
the significance of data backup protocols. They will gain proficiency in executing system utility tools and
diagnosing issues related to operating systems, audio components, USB interfaces, and LAN connectivity.
Furthermore, students will develop the expertise to identify various computer components, perform
installation and configuration of operating systems and drivers, and effectively resolve technical issues as
they arise. The module also underscores the necessity of cybersecurity by configuring and utilizing
antivirus software to protect against malware and other threats. Through a combination of theoretical
knowledge and hands-on experience, students will be equipped to maintain and support computer systems
in professional environments.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS focuses on the essential skills and knowledge required for troubleshooting and maintaining
computer systems. It encompasses a wide range of topics, including preventive and breakdown
maintenance, system diagnostics, and the execution of utility tools. Students will learn to identify
