MEP/Q0205: Multifunctional Office Executive
Brief Job Description
A Multifunctional Office Executive performs administrative and office support activities. It involves the
upkeep and maintenance of office premises, purchase, and vendor management for office supplies
stationery, and upkeep, ensuring the comfort of staff and visitors by receiving and forwarding calls, co-
ordination of incoming and outgoing mail, and organizing meetings and making travel arrangements for
staff.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be well groomed, attentive, multi-task and handle pressure, have good communication
skills and attention to detail, respect confidentiality, have a positive attitude and dependability.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MEP/N0217: Ensure up-keep of office premises and facilities
- 2. MEP/N0218: Manage vendors for procurement of office supplies and services
- 3. MEP/N0219: Maintain and issue office stationery and supplies
- 4. MEP/N0220: Maintain official records and documentation
- 5. MEP/N0221: Organise for local transport and outstation ticketing for staff in coordination with
travel vendor
- 6. MEP/N0222: Organize for meeting rooms and venues for office meetings and events
- 7. MEP/N0215: Co-ordinate incoming and outgoing mail
- 8. MEP/N0216: Use Computers to store, retrieve and communicate information
- 9. MEP/N9914: Communicating with clients, visitors and colleagues effectively
- 10. MEP/N9903: Apply health and safety practices at the workplace
- 11. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

MEP/N0217: Ensure up-keep of office premises and facilities
Description
This unit is about ensuring up-keep of office premises and smooth operation of office facilities.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Ensure the upkeep of office premises
Ensure smooth operation of office facilities for employees and visitors
Manage the housekeeping staff
Elements and Performance Criteria
Ensure the upkeep of office premises
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. ensure adequate stock of housekeeping supplies through timely restocking
PC2. identify the housekeeping requirements in different parts of the office area
PC3. ensure the cleanliness of the office premises by coordinating with the housekeeping staff
Ensure smooth operation of office facilities for employees and visitors
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. identify various office facilities under own responsibility
PC5. determine the up-time and upkeep requirements for various facilities
PC6. check the entire office premises to identify any facility that is not working as it should as per
the office norms
PC7. organise and commission repair or maintenance works to address requirements in the office
concerning electric works, plumbing, masonry work, carpentry, etc
PC8. record all the maintenance requirements as per organisational procedures
PC9. co-ordinate with the service provider for repair or maintenance work to ensure minimum
downtime
PC10. ensure the availability and replenishment of adequate consumables for various office
facilities as per organisational procedures
Manage the housekeeping staff
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. assist in the verification, onboarding and orientation of the housekeeping staff
PC12. instruct the housekeeping staff concerning the quality of work and acceptable behaviour at
work
PC13. allocate work to the housekeeping staff according to their expertise and profile
PC14. supervise the housekeeping staff and conduct regular checks to ensure housekeeping work
is carried out as per the applicable standards
PC15. identify the areas of improvement in housekeeping and provide feedback to the
housekeeping staff

MEP/N0218: Manage vendors for procurement of office supplies and
services
Description
This unit is about sourcing and managing vendors for procurement of office requirements in terms of
supplies and services.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Select vendors for procuring office supplies and services
Monitor procurement from vendors
Ensure timely payments to vendors
Elements and Performance Criteria
Source vendors for procurement of office supplies and services
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. obtain requisition for goods or services from authorised persons as per organisational
requisitioning process with details of goods or services required including quantity, quality
and delivery schedule related specifications
PC2. identify the appropriate vendors and shortlist the relevant vendors as per the organisational
norms, sourcing information about suppliers from reliable contacts and other sources
PC3. obtain quotations from supplier(s) after providing them quality / brand specifications and
quantity requirements of the desired materials
PC4. follow the organizational norms for vendor evaluation and selection to identify supplier(s)
that best meet the requirements
PC5. carry out a comparative analysis after obtaining the price quotes from the shortlisted
vendors and studying their terms and conditions
PC6. negotiate with the supplier(s) on prices and delivery of materials to get the best deal for the
organisation
PC7. select the relevant vendor(s) for procuring the office supplies based on the quality, price of
supplies, delivery schedule and any other applicable criteria
PC8. carry out appropriate documentation concerning the vendor selection, including the
contracts, internal approvals, etc
PC9. register the vendor in co-ordination with the finance team as per organisational norms after
PC10. sourcing vendor registration information and documentation from the vendor
Monitor procurement from vendors
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. ensure the timely release of purchase orders through coordination with the finance
department for the procurement of goods or services
PC12. follow up with the suppliers for the timely procurement of relevant goods or services
PC13. assess the quality of supplies and services against the applicable standards/specifications
