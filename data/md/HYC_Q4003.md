HYC/Q4003: Green Hydrogen Plant Supervisor
Brief Job Description
A Green Hydrogen Plant Supervisor is responsible for leading the team in operations, maintenance and
safety of a green hydrogen production facility. The person at this job has deep understanding of the
mechanical and electrical principles and processes involved in air separation and hydrogen generation. His
role is to manage the seamless operation of critical equipment like electrolysers, dryers, deoxy reactor,
electrical heater, pump, compressors, and storage systems, ensuring smooth production processes.
Adherence to process Safety and safety protocol during the production process is also one of the primary
responsibilities of the Plant Supervisor
Personal Attributes
The individual should have ability to give attention to detail, technical aptitude, adaptability, leadership
teamwork and management and a strong commitment to safety and sustainability. The person should
have the ability to work individually / team round the clock and must always promote adherence to safety
protocol.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management
Procedures
Description
This unit covers maintaining health, safety and security procedures at the workplace where Hydrogen is
produced and handled.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view
Safe handling of Hydrogen
Follow fire safety procedures
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view & Safe handling of Hydrogen
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Ensure that personnel under their supervision have a deep understanding of hydrogen's
properties, including its flammability, explosive limits and potential hazards.
PC2. Ensure personal and team adherence to the use of appropriate protective
clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions
PC3. Identify and communicate the relevant documents, responsible individuals, and designated
locations pertaining to health and safety in the workplace, facilitating effective safety
management.
PC4. Oversee and participate in risk assessments specific to hydrogen storage, handling and
production processes.
PC5. Proficiently identify hydrogen process hazards and assess risks including the analysis of risk
or accident in the workplace due to hydrogen leak/fire
PC6. capability to accurately read and interpret data from sensing equipment used in hydrogen
production processes
PC7. apply key safety parameters for handling hydrogen & oxygen
PC8. Evaluate hydrogen release pressures and understand hydrogen flame detection methods,
emphasizing the colorless nature of hydrogen flames
PC9. Apply critical safety requirements for electrical equipment used in green hydrogen
production, minimizing electrical hazards
PC10. competent in the operation and functioning of hydrogen exhaust systems, facilitating the
safe removal of hydrogen from the workplace
PC11. Execute inertization processes using nitrogen to displace hazardous gases enhancing safety
in hydrogen-related operations
PC12. Proficiently identify and recognize common safety signs displayed in various areas,
promoting a safe work environment.

HYC/N4006: Hydrogen Technology Overview and Operations Management
Description
This unit is about gaining a thorough understanding of various aspects related to hydrogen technology
e.g., production, storage, transportation and utilization. This knowledge is essential for effective decision-
making, overseeing operations and ensuring the safety and efficiency of the hydrogen production and
storage facility.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manufacturing (Electrolysis Plants)
Transportation (Hazardous Materials Transportation e.g., road transportation and Pipelines
transportation)
Storage Technologies e.g. storage in gaseous and liquid form
Utilization in domestic and industrial set up including Fuel Cell Systems, Gas Engine, and Gas
Turbine Systems
Hydrogen use in vehicles (direct injection and fuel cell)
Hydrogen refilling station design and layout, Hydrogen Re-fuelling Systems parameters like
pressure, temperature at the fuelling station, refilling parameters and equipment. Safety at the
Filling station
Handling leaks in the refilling station and handling general emergency in the refilling stations
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manufacturing (Electrolysis Plants)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Familiarization with Engineering standards and guidelines related to production, storage,
transportation and utilization of Hydrogen
PC2. Familiarization with types of electrolysers and their advantages and disadvantages
PC3. Apply knowledge of production processes in electrolysis plants to optimize production, taking
into consideration quality and safety standards.
PC4. Analyze and assess the production processes in electrolysis plants to identify areas for
process improvement and operational efficiency.
PC5. Develop strategies to manage and ensure the quality of equipment used in electrolysis
plants, adhering to safety regulations and compliance
PC6. check the Importance of Feedwater quality and maintain its quality control
PC7. Handle Feed water (to electrolyser) management
Transportation (Hazardous Materials Transportation and Pipelines)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Familiarization with mode of transportation of Hydrogen e.g. gaseous Hydrogen, Liquid
Hydrogen,
PC9. Familiarization of process of liquification of Hydrogen, comparative advantage and
disadvantages of liquid hydrogen and gaseous hydrogen in transportation
