#### MIN/Q3207: Slurry Pump Operator (Mines)
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role is responsible for transferring material or slurry from one place to another in the
mines or from one location to another location for processing, by operating and maintaining the slurry
pump unit.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should be physically agile, strong, have good eye-sight and not suffer from color-
blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MIN/N3221: Prepare slurry pump unit for operation
2. MIN/N3222: Perform operation of slurry pump unit
3. MIN/N3223: Perform maintenance of the slurry pump
4. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Underground Metal
1. MIN/N1702: Follow Health, Safety and Environmental guidelines for Underground Metalliferous
Mines
Elective 2: Opencast
1. MIN/N1703: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental Guidelines for opencast mines
Elective 3: Underground Coal
1. MIN/N1704: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental guidelines for underground coal mines

#### MIN/N3221: Prepare  slurry pump unit for operation
Description
This OS unit is about inspecting & preparing the slurry pump unit for activities that need to be carried out.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for work
Inspect and prepare the slurry pump unit for operation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the work requirements from the instructions received from supervisor and
interpreting information from previous day/shift logbook
- PC2. use personal protective equipment appropriate for work activities
- PC3. select appropriate type of auxiliary equipment for work activities
Inspect and prepare the slurry pump unit for operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. check various controls, gauges, slurry pump and allied components and accessories for
proper functioning and any defect
- PC5. check the direction of rotation of motor is corresponding to the regulated rotation direction of
pump and ensure that motor should be tested alone and it must not be connected with pump during check
- PC6. ensure that concentricity of motor shaft and slurry pump shaft is correct and elastic cushion
of coupling is correct and complete
- PC7. check the impeller clearance by manually turn the slurry pump (including motor) for
detecting any friction, tightness etc. in it
- PC8. check the bearing oil level in the bearing box and replenish it as per SOP, if requires
- PC9. check the condition of inlet and outlet valves and ensure that they are working properly and
in normal state
- PC10. check the pipe system and other parts such as anchor bolt, flange sealing gasket and other
bolts for proper working and good condition
- PC11. check all foundation bolts and outlet pipework bolts for appropriate tightness and condition
- PC12. ensure all electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, sealant and lubrication systems (as applicable)
are connected and operational
- PC13. add the seal water (cooling water for mechanical seal), before starting the slurry pump
- PC14. perform pump priming by opening the air vents on the inlet and outlet piping until the
pumped fluid flows out and after that closing the air vents
- PC15. ensure the inlet pipe and pump casing are completely full of liquid before starting continuous
duty operation and pump fluid must be above the pump casing

#### MIN/N3222: Perform  operation of slurry pump unit
Description
This OS unit is about performing operation of slurry pump unit in the mines for transferring the slurry from
one place to another place.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Start and operate the slurry pump unit
Stop the slurry pump unit
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Start and operate the slurry pump unit
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure flushing and/or cooling liquid supplies are turned on and all vent connections are
closed before starting the pump
- PC2. open the inlet valve and fully close or partially open the outlet valve before starting the
pump
- PC3. open any recirculation or cooling lines
- PC4. start the motor and slowly open the outlet valve until the pump reaches the desired flow
- PC5. check the pressure gauge to ensure that the pump reaches the required outlet pressure
- PC6. stop the pump, if there is no pressure or low pressure
- PC7. perform the pump priming process again and restart the motor again
- PC8. monitor the pump for bearing temperature, excessive vibration, and noise during the
operation
- PC9. shut down the pump immediately, if the pump exceeds normal levels and take the corrective
action as per SOP
- PC10. repeat steps 6,7,8 and 9 until the pump runs properly
- PC11. check the oil level at regular intervals
- PC12. observe any slurry leakage during the pump operation and stop the pump immediately, if
found
Stop the slurry pump unit
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. close the outlet valve, but ensure that the pump runs in this condition for no more than a few
seconds
- PC14. stop the pump
- PC15. switch off flushing and/or cooling/heating liquid supplies at a time appropriate to the process
- PC16. maintain and complete all required documentation clearly, concisely as per SOP

#### MIN/N3223: Perform  maintenance  of the slurry pump
Description
This OS unit is about performing routine maintenance and troubleshooting tasks on the slurry pump.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for maintenance work
Repair and maintain the slurry pump
Perform post-maintenance activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for maintenance work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the slurry pump manufacturer's specifications from the user manual and drawings
- PC2. read maintenance schedule and checklist and plan the time and schedule for conducting the
maintenance
- PC3. identify and arrange the tools, consumables and spare parts required during the task
- PC4. follow safety practices during maintenance activities as per organisational SOP
Repair and maintain the slurry pump
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. perform basic health check-up of slurry pump unit as specified in the maintenance checklist
- PC6. check the level and condition of the oil through the sight glass on the bearing frame
- PC7. replenish coolants and lubricants as per SOP
- PC8. clean dirt and dust from areas around close clearances, bearing housings and motors
- PC9. check coupling alignment and re-align if necessary
- PC10. check the slurry pump parts i.e. motor, impellers, casing, shaft and bearing assembly, shaft
sleeves, seals, strainer, agitator, auxiliary supplies e.g. heating/cooling (if fitted) wiring harness, connectors etc. for proper functioning to find out root cause of the problems like
any leakage, short circuit in parts, breakage of wires and clamps, etc. and discuss the same with senior if required
- PC11. check for unusual noise vibration and bearing temperatures
- PC12. check the pump and piping, seal chamber and stuffing box etc. for leaks
- PC13. check any are functioning correctly
- PC14. check the instrument panel and control gauge to identify out of range readings to find the
defect/cause of failure
- PC15. replace/change the spare parts and consumables of the slurry pump as per the schedule
- PC16. ensure proper lock out and tag out of slurry pump to avoid any untoward incident triggered
due to unknowingly operation of machine / system under maintenance
