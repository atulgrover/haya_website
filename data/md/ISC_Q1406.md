ISC/Q1406: Stainless Steel Making - Process Control Engineer
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role coordinate and perform stainless steel making process and its associated
functions.
Personal Attributes
The person should be patient, organized, team-oriented and have the ability to work for long hours in
adverse conditions. They must be able to plan and prioritise tasks effectively and have an eye for detail
and quality.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. ISC/N0946: Manage and implement health and safety practices at the work place
- 2. ISC/N0947: Manage team and others at workplace
- 3. ISC/N1409: Prepare for Stainless Steel Making Process
- 4. ISC/N1413: Perform stainless steels making process
- 5. ISC/N1704: Apply Decarbonization practice in plant
- 6. ISC/N1703: Apply statistical process control (SPC) techniques

ISC/N0946: Manage and implement health and safety practices at the
work place
Description
This OS unit is about managing and implementing health, hygiene, safety and sustainable practices for
optimising use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain safe and secure working environment
Maintain health and hygiene
Manage housekeeping at workplace
Implement material and energy conservation practices
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain safe and secure working environment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify hazardous activities and the possible causes of risks or accidents in the workplace
PC2. implement safe working practices while dealing with hazards to ensure safety of self and
others
PC3. ensure that team is following appropriate safety practices while working in and around
trenches, elevated places and confined areas
PC4. conduct regular checks of the machines with support of the maintenance team to identify
potential hazards
PC5. organise safety drills or training sessions to create awareness amongst others on the
identified risks and safety practices
PC6. fill daily check sheet to report improvements done and risks identified
PC7. ensure that relevant safety boards/signs are placed on the shop floor for the safety of self
and others
PC8. report any identified breaches in health, safety and security policies and procedures to the
designated person
PC9. ensure that team is following appropriate emergency procedures such as raising alarm, safe
evacuation etc.
Maintain health and hygiene
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. ensure team is aware about hygiene and sanitation regulations and following them on the
shop floor
PC11. ensure workplace, equipment, restrooms etc. are cleaned and sanitized regularly
PC12. ensure availability of running water, hand wash and alcohol-based sanitizers at the
workplace
PC13. report advanced hygiene and sanitation issues to appropriate authority

ISC/N0947: Manage team and others at workplace
Description
This OS unit is about managing team, colleagues/superiors and others, either in own work group or in other
work groups within organisation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communicate effectively with team members
Interact with supervisor
Manage appropriate behaviour at work place
Elements and Performance Criteria
Communicate effectively with team members
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. manage and co-ordinate with team members to integrate work, as per the workload in order
to achieve team goals
PC2. implement ways to share information with team members in line with organisational
requirements
PC3. ensure that work requirements are clearly communicated to the team members through all
means including face-to-face, telephonic and written
PC4. work in a way that show respect for all team members and customers
PC5. carry out commitments made to team members
PC6. resolve conflicts within the team members at work to achieve smooth workflow
PC7. guide the team members to follow the organisation’s policies and procedures
PC8. ensure team goals are given preference over individual goals
PC9. encourage team members to rectify errors as per feedback and minimize mistakes in future
Interact with supervisor
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. report progress on job allocated and team performance to the superiors
PC11. escalate problems to supervisors that cannot be handled
PC12. report the completed work
PC13. train the team members to report completed work and receive feedback on work done
Manage appropriate behaviour at work place
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC14. help PwD team members to overcome the challenges, if asked
PC15. ensure team shows sensitivity towards all genders and PwD
PC16. adjust communication styles to reflect gender sensitivity and sensitivity towards person with
disability

ISC/N1409: Prepare for Stainless Steel Making Process
Description
This OS unit is about performing preparatory activities for carrying out Argon Oxygen Decarburization
(AOD) process of stainless steel making
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out preparatory activities
Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out preparatory activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. obtain the required information and work completed in previous shift from logbook,
discussions etc. and take handover from the previous shift operator by following
organizational procedures
PC2. read work orders, blueprints etc. to determine setup specifications, work sequences, grade of
stainless steel required
PC3. receive the stainless liquid steel from EAF/IF in a Argon Oxygen Decarburization (AOD) vessel
by following organizational procedure
PC4. check the quality of liquid steel received from EAF/IF
PC5. check chemical composition and temperature of the liquid steel by following SOP
PC6. ensure that work area is clean and free of any obstacle or encumbrance
PC7. ensure that all safety measures, interlocks, alarms are working and in conformance with the
safe operation of steel making plant as per SOP
PC8. coordinate with other related departments such as raw material handling and quality
departments
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. relevant standards and procedures followed in the company
KU2. AOD process of stainless steel making
KU3. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) recommended by OEM for using tools and equipment
required during AOD process of stainless steel making
KU4. basic working process of ladle refining furnace
KU5. material required for AOD process
KU6. SOP as recommended by competent authority of the organisation
KU7. normal running characteristics of AOD equipment
KU8. impact of parameters i.e temperature, O2, chemical composition etc. on the final product
