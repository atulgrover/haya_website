#### SGJ/Q4306: Electrolyzer Manufacturing Plant Technician
Brief Job Description
Electrolyzer Manufacturing Plant technician is responsible for supporting the safe, efficient, and high-
quality production of electrolyzer system. Their work is essential to provide technical support to maintain
the manufacturing process of electrolyzer and meeting production goals while adhering to safety and
environmental standards. He/She plays a vital role in producing and maintaining electrolyzer along with
associated subsystems and is accordingly responsible for various electrical components and systems-
related tasks within the Electrolyzer manufacturing facility.
Personal Attributes
This unit tells about job role of technician in Electrolyzer Manufacturing Plant.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SGJ/N4318: Introduction about the Electrolyzer and its types

#### SGJ/N4318: Introduction about the Electrolyzer and its types
Description
This unit tells about Introduction about the Electrolyzer and its types
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Describe Electrolysis Process.
Explain about Electrolyzer Technology.
Discuss about applications of Electrolyzer.
Introduce different types of Electrolyzer
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Basics of electrolysis, electrolysis process and Electrolyzer technology
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. describe and demonstrate electrolysis process on a small model
- PC2. explain and demonstrate working of electrolytic cell through picture or videos.
- PC3. explain and demonstrate laws related to electrolysis.
- PC4. discuss in brief various terms associated with Electrolysis Process.
- PC5. discuss and illustrate about Electrolyzer technology through pictures and videos
- PC6. discuss key aspects and challenges related to electrolyzer manufacturing in India.
- PC7. discuss about the factors affecting electrolysis.
- PC8. discuss and demonstrate the roles of a technicianat an Electrolyzer Manufacturing facility

specifications of Alkaline electrolyzer.

specifications.

electrolyzer.

#### SGJ/N4320: Perform Manufacturing  Process of Electrolyzer
Description
This unit tells about Fundamental techniques and process for Manufacturing of Electrolyzer.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Electrolyzer cell configuration and its types
Electrolyzer membrane selection and integration
Explain electrode fabrication techniques of Electrolyzer.
Explain step by step process of installation of Electrolyzer
Explain assembling and Disassembling of Electrolyzer.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Fundamental techniques and process for manufacturing Electrolyzer
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify and show different types of materials used in manufacturing electrolyzer.
- PC2. discuss about the electrolyzer cell components and its types and show them the electrolyzer
cell components and its types through pictures and videos.
- PC3. discuss and show the process for membrane selection and Integration and how to select
appropriate membrane for Electrolyzer manufacturing.
- PC4. discuss about the stacking techniques for electrolyzer manufacturing.
- PC5. discuss about Emerging Manufacturing Technologies for Electrolyzers.
- PC6. discuss and show the scaling up and automation in electrolyzer manufacturing through
pictures and videos.
- PC7. discuss about the progress and Challenges in Electrolyzer Manufacturing industry.
Assembling and Disassembling of Electrolyzer
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. explain and show how to read and interpret drawings of electrolyzer manufacturing facility
- PC9. discuss and show how to perform Cleaning and inspection of components of Electrolyzer for
wear and tear.
- PC10. discuss step by step process for assembly/Installation of Electrolyzer- Bipolar plate,
separator(membrane), Porous Transport layer, electrodes etc.
- PC11. discuss and show the process of Membrane electrode assembly (MEA) handling and
installation and process of membrane preparation through pictures and videos.
- PC12. discuss and show how to implement the procedure for Electrolyzer stack assembly.
- PC13. discuss the importance of Manifold and sealing in Electrolyzer Manufacturing
- PC14. identify and showcase the key aspects and learnings of successful cases studies of
Electrolyzer manufacturing plants in India and abroad.
