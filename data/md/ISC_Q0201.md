#### ISC/Q0201: Battery Operator
Brief Job Description
The job is all about carrying out all enabling activities which are integral part of coke making in a slotted
coke oven battery for producing metallurgical coke.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to work independently as well as in teams. He should be physically fit, not
having colour blindness, having analytical skills, problem solving attitude, high concentration levels and
willingness to work in a factory environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ISC/N0008: Use basic health and safety practices at the workplace
2. ISC/N0096: Work effectively with others and manage team members
3. ISC/N0201: Carry out all enabling jobs pertaining to coke production
4. ISC/N0202: Prepare and carry out charging operation
5. ISC/N0203: Prepare and carry out pushing operations

#### ISC/N0008: Use basic health and safety practices at the workplace
Description
This OS unit is about knowledge and practices relating to health, safety and security that candidates need
to use in the workplace. It covers responsibilities towards self, others, assets and the environment. It
includes understanding of risks and hazards in the workplace, along with common techniques to minimize
risk, deal with accidents, emergencies, etc

#### ISC/N0096: Work effectively with others and manage team members
Description
This unit covers basic etiquett and competencies that a candidate is required to possess and demonstrate
in their behaviour and interactions with others at the workplace.These cover areas such as communication
etiquette, discipline, listening, handlingconflict and grievances
Scope
This unit/task covers the following:
Communicate effectively with team members, co-workers and supervisors
Encourage friendly working environment
Use appropriate titles and terms of respect
Follow code of conduct and ethics as applicable
Follow organisation specific policies
Demonstrate effective behaviours for team work
Ensure engagement of team members through on-the job handholding & support

#### ISC/N0201: Carry out all enabling jobs pertaining to coke production
Description
This NOS is about carrying out all enabling jobs pertaining to coke production
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand allenabling jobs inaccordance with jobschedule
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. interpret the job of coke production and understand the job requirements.
- PC2. understand the specified enabling jobs for coke production.
Understand thesequence of activitiesfor execution
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. clean the area specified as per job requirement
- PC4. clean the battery machines as per machine cleaning schedule
- PC5. identify ovens for technological cleaning of gas carrying equipments
- PC6. carry out technological cleaning as per oven identified
- PC7. operate designated cleaning equipment of relevance
- PC8. seal the cleaning surfaces and regulate them as necessary, if required
Seek clarification, ifrequired
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. identify any clarifications that he wants to seek with respect to the given schedule
- PC10. recognize whom to contact for clarifications on the given schedule
- PC11. escalate the concern to the supervisor or shift-in-charge, if needed
Understand advancepreparation requiredfor execution
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
