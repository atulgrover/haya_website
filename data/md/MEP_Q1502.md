MEP/Q1502: Business Analytics Executive
Brief Job Description
The Business Analytics Executive qualification equips learners with essential skills in data analysis,
statistical techniques, and business analysis tools such as Power BI, enabling them to support data-driven
decision-making in organizations. This qualification focuses on practical applications of analytics in various
business domains, preparing individuals for entry-level roles in business analytics.
Personal Attributes
Analytical thinking, problem-solving ability, attention to detail, logical reasoning, strong communication
skills, adaptability, technical proficiency, data-driven mindset.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MEP/N1506: Fundamentals of Business Analytics

MEP/N1506: Fundamentals of Business Analytics
Description
This NOS discusses about the fundamentals of business analytics.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Business Analytics
Databases, Data Warehousing, and Data Mining
Analytics Methodology
Visualization of Data
Time Series Analysis
Linear Regression Analysis
Types of Business Analytics
Business Analytics Future Trends
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Business Analytics
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Define and explain the concept of business analytics and its importance in decision-making
processes.
PC2. Differentiate between various types of analytics, including descriptive, predictive, and
prescriptive analytics, and their applications in business.
PC3. Identify and describe the application fields of business analytics, such as marketing, finance,
HR, and operations analytics.
PC4. Discuss the organization and sources of data, emphasizing the importance of data quality
and strategies for dealing with missing or incomplete data.
PC5. Analyze the role of a data scientist in business and society, highlighting key responsibilities
and the impact on organizational performance.
Databases, Data Warehousing, and Data Mining
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Differentiate between structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data, and explain their
significance in business analytics.
PC7. Compare and contrast data warehouses and databases, highlighting their roles in data
storage and retrieval.
PC8. Explain the differences between relational and non-relational databases and describe RDBMS
and columnar data structures in the context of business analytics.
PC9. Define data mining and explain its importance in discovering patterns and insights from large
datasets.
PC10. Apply data mining techniques such as association rules, clustering, decision trees, and
random forests to real-world business problems.

Analytics Methodology
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Describe the analytics methodology, outlining key steps from preparing objectives to
evaluating outcomes.
PC12. Identify data requirements and apply appropriate data collection methods to gather relevant
data for analysis.
PC13. Perform data preparation tasks, including data cleansing, normalization, data blending, and
data modeling.
PC14. Evaluate the effectiveness of the analytics process by applying feedback mechanisms to
refine and improve models.
Visualization of Data
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC15. Summarize data using various visualization methods, including tables, graphs, charts,
histograms, and frequency distributions.
PC16. Calculate and interpret measures of central tendency and dispersion, using tools such as box
plots to represent data distribution.
PC17. Apply basic probability concepts, including conditional probability, to analyze and visualize
data distributions.
PC18. Differentiate between continuous and discrete probability distributions and apply them in
sequential decision-making processes.
Time Series Analysis
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC19. Conduct trend analysis and identify seasonality and cyclical behaviors in time series data.
PC20. Apply moving average and exponential smoothing methods, including single, double, and
HOLT-WINTERS, to forecast future data points.
PC21. Utilize ARIMA and multiple linear regression-based forecasting techniques to predict trends
and patterns in business data.
Linear Regression Analysis
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC22. Perform simple linear regression analysis, including calculating the coefficient of
determination, conducting significance tests, and performing residual analysis.
PC23. Interpret the results of simple linear regression, including confidence and prediction
intervals.
PC24. Conduct multiple linear regression analysis, interpreting the coefficients, dealing with
categorical variables, and addressing issues like heteroscedasticity, multi-collinearity, and
outliers.
PC25. Apply autoregression techniques and variable transformations to improve model accuracy
and predictive power.
Types of Business Analytics
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC26. Compare the four different types of data analytics- Descriptive analytics , Diagnostic
analytics, Predictive analytics and Prescriptive analytics
PC27. Explain different types of diagnostic analytics
PC28. Analyze which business analytics types do different companies prefer

MEP/N1507: Web & Social Media Analytics
Description
This NOS focuses on analyzing web and social media data to measure performance, track user behavior,
and optimize digital marketing strategies for business growth.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Social Media Analytics (SMA)
Web Metrics and Web Analytics
Facebook Analytics
Data Analysis Language and Tools
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Social Media Analytics (SMA)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the components of the social media landscape, including websites, web apps, mobile
apps, and social media platforms.
PC2. Identify the need for social media analytics (SMA) in both small and large organizations,
highlighting its importance in business decision-making.
PC3. Discuss the application of SMA across different areas, such as marketing, customer service,
and brand management.
PC4. Define and explain key social media KPIs, including reach and engagement, and their
significance in evaluating social media performance.
PC5. Implement a structured approach to performing social media analytics, including setting
business goals, selecting KPIs, gathering data, conducting analysis, and providing feedback.
Web Metrics and Web Analytics
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Conduct clickstream analysis and A/B testing to evaluate user behavior and optimize web
performance.
PC7. Utilize online surveys to gather user feedback and insights for web analytics.
PC8. Apply Google Analytics to monitor and analyze web traffic and user interactions.
PC9. Explain the process of web crawling and indexing and its relevance to search engine
optimization (SEO).
PC10. Analyze PULSE metrics (Page views, Uptime, Latency, Seven-day active users) to assess
business and technical performance issues.
PC11. Evaluate HEART metrics (Happiness, Engagement, Adoption, Retention, and Task success) to
understand user behavior and improve user experience.
PC12. Differentiate between on-site and off-site web analytics and apply the goal-signal-metric
process for comprehensive web performance analysis.

outcomes in business scenarios.

Description
This NOS focuses on using Microsoft Power BI for data visualization, analysis, and reporting to support
data-driven decision-making in business analytics.
Scope

Introduction to Power BI

Advanced Visualization Tools

Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Power BI
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the need, importance, and advantages of Power BI for data visualization and
business intelligence.
PC2. Describe the history and evolution of Power BI, including key components like Power View,
Power Query, and Power Pivot.
PC3. Identify and explain the different data sources supported by Power BI, including data
warehouse files and cloud services.
PC4. Describe Power BI architecture, including data access options for on-premises and cloud data
sources like Microsoft OneDrive.
PC5. Demonstrate the installation of Power BI Desktop and the setup of a Power BI Cloud account.

collaboration options for business analysts using MS Cloud Tools.
Reports & Auto Filters
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Design reports using legacy files, .DAT files, and database tables in Power BI.
PC8. Utilize the Power BI Report Designer, including report canvas and report pages, for creating,
renaming, and organizing multiple visualizations.
PC9. Use the “GET DATA” options to import data, apply report fields, and set up filters for effective
data analysis.
PC10. Experiment with visual interactions, including advantages of using multiple pages and
visualizations within a single report.
PC11. Publish reports to the Power BI Cloud and verify their functionality using different view
options (e.g., full screen, fit page, width scale).
PC12. Design reports using databases and queries, including setting up query settings and data
preloads for optimized report performance.

MEP/N1512: Business Research Methods
Description
This NOS focuses on applying research methodologies to collect, analyze, and interpret business data for
informed decision-making and strategic planning.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Business Research
Research Process and Concepts in Research
Formulation of Research Problem and Hypotheses

Data Analysis

Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Business Research
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Define business research, explaining its objectives and the role it plays in organizational
decision-making.
PC2. Identify and describe the characteristics of research, including the scientific method and
criteria for good research.
PC3. Differentiate between various types of research, such as exploratory, descriptive, and causal,
and discuss their applications in business contexts.
PC4. Discuss the importance of ethics in research, outlining ethical considerations and practices
that ensure integrity and validity in research.
Research Process and Concepts in Research
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Outline the steps involved in the research process, from problem identification to data
analysis and reporting.
PC6. Describe and differentiate between various research designs, including experimental,
exploratory, and explanatory, and discuss their relevance to business research.
PC7. Explain key research concepts such as methodology and methods, and the distinction
between theory, concepts, constructs, and variables.
PC8. Apply deductive and inductive logic in formulating and testing research hypotheses and
theories.
Formulation of Research Problem and Hypotheses
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Identify and formulate research problems by diagnosing symptoms and understanding the
underlying issues.

of Managerial Discretion.

MEP/N1514: Finance Analytics
Description
This NOS focuses on applying financial data analysis techniques to evaluate business performance,
optimize financial decision-making, and support strategic planning.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Financial Analytics
Performance Measures and Holt-Winters Model
Stationarity and ARIMA Model
Modern Portfolio Theory
Introduction to Algorithmic Trading
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Financial Analytics
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Define financial analytics and explain its significance in business forecasting and decision-
making.
PC2. Describe the role of subjective forecasting in financial analytics, emphasizing its application
in real-world scenarios.
PC3. Explain the concepts of business forecasting and time series analysis, highlighting their
importance in predicting financial outcomes.
PC4. Analyze forecasting performance measurements, focusing on distance metrics and their use
in evaluating forecasting accuracy.
Performance Measures and Holt-Winters Model
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Apply various forecasting methods, including the average method, naïve method, and linear
regression, to predict financial trends.
PC6. Use R programming to implement forecasting techniques, such as moving averages and
exponential smoothing, and assess their effectiveness.
PC7. Explain the principles of exponential smoothing, including simple and Holt’s exponential
smoothing methods.
PC8. Apply Holt-Winters forecasting model using R programming, demonstrating how it accounts
for seasonality in financial data.
PC9. Describe the concept of auto-regression and apply it to financial time series data using R
programming for improved forecasting accuracy.
Stationarity and ARIMA Model
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. Define stationarity and explain its significance in time series analysis for financial
forecasting.

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

making.
KU3. Financial modeling techniques and tools used for data-driven financial insights.
KU4. Identify best practices for ensuring financial data accuracy, compliance, and reporting
standards.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Communicate financial insights effectively using data visualization and reporting tools.
GS2. Apply analytical skills to interpret financial data and assess business performance.
GS3. Use problem-solving techniques to optimize financial strategies and risk management.
GS4. Demonstrate proficiency in financial analytics tools such as Excel, Power BI, and financial
modeling software.
