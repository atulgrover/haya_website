#### CUTM/AGR/Q0203: Fisheries Post Harvest Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The Individual is responsible to understand quality parameters of fish and fishery products, postharvest
handling of fish and shellfishes, value addition to fish and shellfish and conversion of fish by-catch and
processing by-products into high-value products.
Personal Attributes
A Fisheries Post-Harvest Supervisor should be detail-oriented, hygienic, and knowledgeable in fish handling
and preservation. They must possess strong organizational and technical skills to ensure quality, safety,
and compliance during processing. Commitment to cleanliness, ability to manage teams, and awareness of
sustainable waste management are essential, along with adaptability to cold environments and evolving
food safety standards.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CUTM/AGR/N0209: Post-Harvest handling of Fish and Shellfish
2. CUTM/AGR/N0210: Value added fisheries product and preservation
3. CUTM/AGR/N0211: Quality Assurance in Fish processing
4. CUTM/AGR/N0212: Waste Management in Fish Processing
5. CUTM/VSQ/N7804: Ensure organizational health, safety and security for self and others
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### CUTM/AGR/N0209: Post-Harvest handling of Fish and Shellfish
Description
This NOS focuses on efficient post-harvest handling of fish and shellfish to maintain freshness and quality.
It includes hygiene practices, cold storage, transportation, sorting, grading, and packaging methods. The
objective is to minimize spoilage, extend shelf life, and ensure food safety compliance in the fisheries
supply chain.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes handling, preserving, and storing fish and shellfish to maintain quality and
prevent spoilage. It covers chilling, freezing, hygiene protocols, food safety compliance, grading, and
packaging techniques. The NOS also focuses on efficient cold chain logistics and reducing post-
harvest losses for both domestic and export markets.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify the best packaging materials for storage of fish and shellfish.
- PC2. Factors contributing to spoilage of fish and shellfish.
- PC3. Pre-processing techniques of fish, prawns, lobster, squid, cuttle fish, crab, depuration of
bivalves, etc.
- PC4. Chemical, physical and sensory freshness analysis of fish and shellfish.
- PC5. Cold chain and refrigerated transport systems.
- PC6. Identify different freezing equipment.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. post-harvest handling techniques to maintain fish and shellfish quality.
- KU2. chilling, freezing, and transportation methods to prevent spoilage.
- KU3. of food safety standards such as HACCP, FSSAI, and FDA regulations.
- KU4. biochemical changes in fish post-harvest and their impact on freshness.
- KU5. sanitation and hygiene practices in fish handling and storage.
- KU6. cold chain logistics and its role in fish preservation.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. handle fish safely while following hygiene protocols.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0210: Value added fisheries product and preservation
Description
This NOS focuses on processing and preserving fishery products to enhance value and shelf life. It includes
filleting, drying, pickling, smoking, marination, and packaging techniques. The objective is to create
market-ready fish products while ensuring food safety, nutritional quality, and compliance with industry
standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes processing techniques, preservation methods, storage, and packaging of fishery
products. It covers nutritional enhancement, food additives, hygiene, quality control, and marketability. The NOS also focuses on utilization of by-products and application of modern
preservation technologies to minimize waste and maximize profitability.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Minced-based fish product.
- PC2. Cured fish product.
- PC3. Fermented fish product
- PC4. Marinated Fish product
- PC5. Understand the end product quality.
- PC6. Knowledge on natural preservative applicable for fish products.
- PC7. Understanding on packaging and storage methods of different value-added fish products.
- PC8. Understand different parameters involved the preservation (time, temperature, humidity,
water activity)
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. processing techniques for value-added fish products like fillets, fish sausages, and surimi.
- KU2. preservation methods, including drying, pickling, smoking, and marination.
- KU3. packaging requirements for extended shelf life and improved marketability.
- KU4. nutritional composition and factors affecting fish product quality.
- KU5. food additives, preservatives, and their regulatory limits.
- KU6. waste utilization techniques for fish by-products.
- KU7. emerging technologies like vacuum packaging and modified atmosphere storage.
#### Generic Skills (GS)

#### CUTM/AGR/N0211: Quality Assurance in Fish processing
Description
This NOS focuses on ensuring quality and safety in fish processing through proper handling, storage,
testing, and compliance with food safety regulations. It includes quality checks, hygiene monitoring, and
laboratory analysis to maintain product integrity and ensure consumer health protection.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes quality inspection, food safety compliance, microbiological testing, traceability,
and documentation in fish processing. It also covers cold chain monitoring, and regulatory audits to
ensure high-quality fishery products meet market and export standards.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding the different quality parameters associated with fish
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Important quality parameters, fish physiology affecting the quality.
- PC2. Factors affecting the quality of fish and fishery products.
Knowledge on various methods of assessment and maintenance of quality
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. Post-harvest technology to maintain the quality of raw material.
- PC4. Microbiological procedures and Equipment used in microbiology lab.
- PC5. Management of quality parameters within prescribed limits.
- PC6. Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point (HACCP)
- PC7. Quality certification standards
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. quality parameters in fish processing, including sensory, microbiological, and chemical aspects.
- KU2. food safety laws and regulatory requirements, such as FSSAI, HACCP, and EU standards.
- KU3. contaminants in fishery products, including heavy metals, biotoxins, and microbial pathogens.
- KU4. Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) in fish processing.
- KU5. sampling and testing methods for evaluating fish freshness and safety.
- KU6. cold chain monitoring and its role in maintaining fish quality.
- KU7. waste disposal practices to ensure hygiene in processing units.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0212: Waste Management   in Fish Processing
Description
This NOS focuses on effective waste management practices in fish processing, ensuring compliance with
environmental regulations. It includes waste segregation, disposal, recycling, and by-product utilization to
minimize environmental impact and promote sustainability in the fisheries industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes waste classification, disposal methods, recycling, and sustainable waste management in fish processing. It covers environmental safety, bio-composting, by-product
utilization, and pollution control measures to ensure responsible waste handling and regulatory
compliance.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Knowledge on biochemistry of fishery by-catch and by-products
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand the difference between fish catch and by-catch.
- PC2. Understand the composition of various parts of fish and shellfish.
- PC3. Clearly understand the role of different chemicals used for conversion.
Information about handling of fish and shellfish waste
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Understand detailed information on parameters differentiating bycatch with catch.
- PC5. Knowledge on different processing practices of fish and shellfish.
- PC6. Information about the different kinds of by-products produced in different processing plants.
- PC7. Know about handling of different fishery by-catch and by-products.
Knowledge on production of high value end product
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Know the biochemistry of different parts of fish and shellfish.
- PC9. Selection and application of appropriate method for production of high value product.
- PC10. Biochemical analysis of high value end products.
- PC11. Functional properties of high value end products.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. types of fish processing waste, including organic and inorganic materials.
- KU2. waste segregation techniques and proper disposal methods.
- KU3. environmental regulations related to fisheries waste management.

#### CUTM/VSQ/N7804: Ensure organizational health, safety and security for
self and others
Description
This NOS focuses on maintaining personal hygiene, workplace safety, and environmental responsibility. It
outlines procedures for disease prevention, safe equipment handling, waste disposal, and emergency
response. The standard ensures that individuals follow preventive measures and safety protocols to create
a clean, hazard-free, and health-conscious work environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers adherence to sanitation and hygiene norms, safe use of tools and chemicals, proper waste management, first aid administration, and compliance with government and workplace
advisories. It aims to prepare individuals to work safely under routine and emergency situations,
reducing risks to themselves, coworkers, and the environment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain personal hygiene
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. wash hands, legs and face with soap/alcohol based sanitizer at reasonable intervals
- PC2. wash the worn clothes with soap and sundry before use next time
- PC3. ensure the face is covered with mask or three layers of cloth-piece
- PC4. follow the workplace sanitization norms including distancing from sick people
Maintain clean and safe workplace
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. carry out basic safety checks before operation of all tools, implements, and machinery and
report identified hazards to the supervisor
- PC6. follow the instructions mentioned on the labels of chemicals/pesticides/fumigants etc to
avoid hazards
- PC7. sanitize equipment, tools and machinery before and after use
- PC8. dispose waste safely and correctly in the designated area
- PC9. work in a manner which minimizes environmental damage, ensuring all procedures and
instructions for controlling risks are followed
- PC10. follow government / workplace advisories in case of outbreak of any disease/disaster
Administer appropriate emergency procedures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. follow procedures for dealing with accidents, fires and emergencies, including
communicating location and directions to the location of emergency, as per the workplace requirements

#### DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Career Development & Goal Setting
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Employability Skills
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify employability skills required for jobs in various industries

| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
