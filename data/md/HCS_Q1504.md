HCS/Q1504: Inlay Artisan - Stonecraft
Brief Job Description
A stonecraft inlay artisan carefully places different stones together to create beautiful patterns and
designs. Their work turns ordinary items into stunning pieces of art.
Personal Attributes
A stonecraft inlay artisan is patient and detail-oriented, with a strong eye for colour and design. Their
creativity and precision enable them to craft beautiful, intricate patterns that bring surfaces to life.

HCS/N1505: Create inlay work on marble
Description
This unit is about understanding customers requirements of a decorative piece, planning of motif designs,
setting its colour scheme and tonality followed by executing the plan to create the pattern on the marble.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understanding customer's requirements
putting customer's design idea / concept on the paper
selecting stones for the motif
shaping semiprecious and precious stones as per the design
engraving the marble
polishing the marble with motif
achieve productivity and quality standards
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding customer's requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. sit with the customer to understand the specific requirements related to the pattern, size and
colour combination
PC2. capture customers idea of colour scheme and tonality of the design
Putting customers design idea / concept on the paper
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. draw the pattern in symmetric sections using traditional geometric tools and create the final
sketch of the design
PC4. trace the final sketch of the design on a brass sheet
PC5. cut out the brass sheet as per the design to make templates of every component of the
design
Selecting stones for the motif
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. select a variety of semiprecious and precious stones for the motif
Select stones for the motif
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. select the base marble as per the required size on which the pattern is to be developed
PC8. choose various shades of these stones to give the right gradation and shading to the motif
Shaping semiprecious and precious stones as per the design
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. operate the hand driven tool known as saan & kamaani having an emery wheel

HCS/N1515: Create Inlay work on Sandstone
Description
Inlay work on sandstone is a meticulous craft that involves embedding pieces of colored stones or
materials into the surface of sandstone to create intricate designs and patterns.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Planning and Cutting on Brass Sheet
Tracing the Design on the Base Stone
Carving the Base Stone
Shaping the Colored Stones
Setting the Shaped Stones
Smoothing the Surface
Inspecting the Final Product
Elements and Performance Criteria
Planning and Cutting on Brass Sheet
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Plan a design and carefully cut out a brass sheet.
PC2. Design is cut out from a thin brass sheet using tools like a jeweler’s saw.
PC3. Ensure that the intricate patterns are accurately replicated onto the stone.
Tracing the Design on the Base Stone
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Place the brass sheet template on the base sandstone.
PC5. Trace the design on the stone surface using a fine pencil or marker.
Carving the Base Stone
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Use chisels, a set-square, and other carving tools, the stone is carved along the traced lines
to create recesses or grooves.
PC7. Depth and width of the grooves must be carefully controlled to match the thickness of the
colored stones that will be inlaid.
Shaping the Colored Stones
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Select different color stones to fit into the carved grooves.
PC9. Cut the stones into small pieces that match the shapes needed for the design.
PC10. Use traditional tools like a diamond-tipped saw or water jet cutter, depending on the
hardness and fragility of the sandstone.
Setting the Shaped Stones
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

PC12. Apply an adhesive, such as epoxy resin or a stone-specific glue to the grooves, and the cut

Smoothing the Surface
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. Polish using abrasive materials, such as sandpaper or diamond pads, to smooth out the

PC15. Check for any imperfections, such as gaps between the inlay pieces and the base stone,
loose stones, or uneven surfaces.
PC16. Correction often by filling gaps or repolishing certain areas.
PC17. Ensure the finished product meets the desired standards and quality of the product.

HCS/N1516: Create Inlay work on Black Marble
Description
The process of inlay work on black marble, often referred to as Pietra Dura, is a traditional and exquisite
craft that involves embedding colorful stones into the surface of black marble to create intricate designs.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Material and Tool Preparation
Design Transfer and Planning
Stone Cutting and Shaping
Engraving and Embedding
Finishing and Quality Check
Elements and Performance Criteria
Material and Tool Preparation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify and source high-quality black marble and semi-precious stones as per design
specifications.
PC2. Inspect and prepare tools such as chisels, grinders, and engraving tools for functionality and
precision.
PC3. Clean and level the marble surface to ensure proper adherence for inlay work.
PC4. Ensure the availability of adhesives and polishing agents suitable for marble inlay.
Design Transfer and Planning
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Interpret the design requirements from sketches, blueprints, or client briefs.
PC6. Accurately transfer the design onto the black marble using stencils, tracing, or hand drawing.
PC7. Verify alignment, proportions, and dimensions of the design on the marble surface.
PC8. Modify the design layout, if necessary, to suit the marble’s surface or client preferences.
Stone Cutting and Shaping
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Use cutting tools to shape semi-precious stones as per the design’s intricate patterns.
PC10. Ensure that cut stones are of the correct size, shape, and smoothness for embedding.
PC11. Polish stones to achieve a glossy finish and enhance their visual appeal.
PC12. Follow safety protocols while cutting and shaping materials to prevent injuries.
Engraving and Embedding
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. Engrave precise grooves into the black marble surface to match the design outline.
PC14. Ensure that the depth and width of engravings allow for a snug fit of inlay pieces.

PC17. Smooth the marble surface and polish the completed inlay to achieve a flawless finish.

KU7. Knowledge of the types of adhesives suitable for securing stones in marble, including their

This unit focuses on the essential entrepreneurship skills required in the inlaying sector of stonecraft. It
covers the technical mastery of stone inlay techniques, material knowledge, market understanding, and
innovation. Additionally, it emphasizes business management, including budgeting, pricing, and marketing
strategies, alongside ethical sourcing and networking opportunities. The unit equips entrepreneurs to
balance craftsmanship with modern business practices to thrive in both traditional and contemporary
markets.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Technical expertise in stone inlaying
Market understanding and innovation
Business and financial management
Networking and partnership development
Customer relationship management

PC1. Use the art and techniques involved in stone inlaying.

and maintaining high-quality standards.
