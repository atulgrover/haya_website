LFS/Q5101: Microentrepreneur - Pharmacy
Brief Job Description
Microentrepreneur is responsible for operating and managing retail pharmacy services, including storage,
dispensing, and sale of pharmaceutical and biological products in compliance with legal and regulatory
guidelines. The job role also involves prescription validation, customer service, inventory control, and the
ability to plan, open, and run a Jan Aushadhi Kendra, while promoting safe medicine use and ensuring
adherence to Good Storage and Pharmacy Practices.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have knowledge of different materials and chemicals along with the storing and
selling appropriate pharma product. He must be able to identify defects, select appropriate storage areas,
and handle inventory/stock. He should have the ability to do quality assurance, inventory control,
manufacturing dispensing.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. LFS/N0674: Introduction to Life Sciences sector and basics of retail pharmacy
- 2. LFS/N5101: Perform Entrepreneurship and Business Planning Activities in Pharmacy Setup
- 3. LFS/N5102: Adhere to Drug Laws, Licensing, and Regulatory Norms in Pharmacy Practice
- 4. LFS/N5103: Collaborate with Regulatory, Supply Chain, and Community Stakeholders in Retail
Pharmacy
- 5. LFS/N5104: Initiate and Operate Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Kendra (PMBJK) as per SOP
- 6. LFS/N0676: Supervise Customer Engagement, Prescription Fulfilment, and Sales Transactions in
Retail Pharmacy
- 7. LFS/N5105: Supervise Manage Drug Storage, Inventory Rotation, and Stock Replenishment in
Retail Pharmacy
- 8. LFS/N0666: Perform Pharmacovigilance, Promote Responsible Antibiotic Use, and Ensure Safe
Handling of Controlled Substances
- 9. LFS/N0675: Comply EHS Rules and sanitization protocol at retail pharmacy in Compliance with
Good Storage Area Practices
- 10. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

LFS/N0674: Introduction to Life Sciences sector and basics of retail
pharmacy
Description
This NOS is about introduction to Life Sciences sector and basics of retail pharmacy
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Life Sciences Sector
Basic of retail Pharmacy
Environment Sustainability
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Life Sciences Sector
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. describe the key sectors and value chain of the Life Sciences industry in Indian and global
context.
PC2. list applicable regulations and good practices such as GMP, GLP, and GDP relevant to
pharmacy operations.
PC3. list key responsibilities and duties of a Chemist Retail Pharmacist.
Basic of retail Pharmacy
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Explain the difference between OTC, prescription, controlled, and Schedule H/X drugs.
PC5. identify brand name, generic name, expiry date, and batch number from packaging
PC6. interpret basic components of a prescription including dosage form, frequency, and route of
administration.
PC7. demonstrate correct storage of drugs as per label instructions and regulatory norms.
PC8. respond appropriately to scenarios such as prescription denial or customer confusion
Environment Sustainability
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. list energy conservation practices relevant to pharmacy operations.
PC10. identify actions that optimize electricity and equipment usage during daily operations
PC11. follow practices that reduce water wastage, chemical spills, and air pollution
PC12. segregate waste into recyclable, non-recyclable, and hazardous categories
PC13. use appropriate color-coded containers for waste collection and disposal
PC14. prepare and follow a waste management checklist for daily pharmacy operations

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

Pharmacy Setup
Description
This NOS equips individuals with essential entrepreneurial and financial competencies to establish and run
a pharmacy business.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This job function covers the following job elements
Formulate Business and Financial Plans
Evaluate Financing Options and Government Schemes
Conduct Location and Market Feasibility Analysis
Business Innovation and Break-even Analysis
Present and Promote Business Concept
Elements and Performance Criteria
Formulate Business and Financial Plans
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Draft a detailed business plan outlining the vision, objectives, and operational strategies of a
pharmacy.
PC2. Prepare a pharmacy-specific Detailed Project Report (DPR) using structured formats.
PC3. Estimate startup capital and prepare a 6-month projected budget and cash flow sheet.

sources.

pharmacy.

LFS/N5102: Adhere to Drug Laws, Licensing, and Regulatory Norms in
Pharmacy Practice
Description
This Job function is about the Microentrepreneur (Pharmacy) complying to legal, regulatory, and licensing
requirements governing retail pharmacy operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This job function covers the following job elements
Interpret Legal Provisions for Retail Pharmacy Operations
Identify Licensing and Registration Requirements
Ensure Compliance with Operational and Storage Norms
Prepare and Maintain Regulatory Documentation
Verify and Review Regulatory Applications and Compliance
Elements and Performance Criteria
Interpret Legal Provisions for Retail Pharmacy Operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Interpret relevant sections of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Rules, 1945 applicable
to retail pharmacy.
PC2. Identify legal responsibilities of a pharmacist and pharmacy owner under national
regulations.
PC3. Explain permissible drug categories for retail sales under Schedule H, H1, X, and OTC.
Identify Licensing and Registration Requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. List the various licenses required to operate a retail pharmacy.
PC5. Identify authorities responsible for issuing each license or registration.
PC6. Differentiate between requirements for Retail Drug License and Wholesale License.
Ensure Compliance with Operational and Storage Norms
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Describe correct storage conditions for temperature-sensitive and schedule-specific drugs.
PC8. Demonstrate compliance standards for maintaining segregation of expired or recalled
medicines.
PC9. Ensure availability of registered pharmacist during all working hours.
PC10. Maintain sale records and prescription logs as per statutory guidelines.
