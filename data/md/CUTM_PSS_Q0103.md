CUTM/PSS/Q0103: Transformer Manufacturing Engineer
Brief Job Description
The Individual is responsible to review the customer requirement, transformer design documents,
verification of procured material, and supporting the manufacturing team for quality transformer
fabrication along with testing transformers to meet customer requirements.
Personal Attributes
A Transformer Manufacturing Engineer should be detail-oriented, safety-conscious, and technically
proficient. They must possess strong analytical thinking, problem-solving ability, and effective
communication skills. A commitment to quality, teamwork, time management, and adaptability to
emerging technologies is essential. Integrity and a proactive mindset are key to success in this role.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CUTM/PSS/N0105: Review transformer manufacturing requirements
- 2. CUTM/PSS/N0106: Perform transformer manufacturing Process
- 3. CUTM/PSS/N0107: Review Transformer Testing requirements

CUTM/PSS/N0105: Review transformer manufacturing requirements
Description
It covers the skills and knowledge required to interpret and review customer specifications, technical
documents, and applicable standards for transformer manufacturing. It involves assessing manufacturing
feasibility, preparing documentation, and coordinating with relevant teams to ensure alignment with
product design, quality requirements, and organizational capabilities.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
It covers the competencies required to evaluate customer and project-specific requirements related
to transformer manufacturing. It includes interpreting technical documentation, standards, and
specifications to identify design, raw material, and process needs, ensuring that all manufacturing
requirements align with regulatory, safety, and quality standards. The scope also involves
coordinating with internal teams and stakeholders to facilitate efficient planning and execution of
transformer production.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Review the customer requirement, engineering drawing and standards
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. interact with the head of transformer manufacturing on customer requirement, engineering
drawings and standards to be followed for transformer manufacturing process.
PC2. Review tools, raw materials and consumables required for production in sufficient quantity as
per production plan/operators’ instructions.
PC3. clearly check the application of transformer and standards to be followed for manufacturing
and testing process.
Reading of detailed assembly drawing, dimensional tolerances, and assembly process
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Review detailed assembly drawing, material grade, dimensional tolerances, and assembly
process for the specific order to be executed.
PC5. distribute the assembly drawing, standards, and process steps to be followed for each stage.
PC6. distribute the work order details required for each process, batch details to be followed for
the manufacturing stage.
PC7. get necessary clarification from the appropriate authorities in case of any doubts.
Review all bill of materials
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Review all bill of materials along with quantity required transformer manufacturing process
with the expected quality requirements.
PC9. ensure that required raw material/tools is procured from stores /vendor before starting work.
PC10. check incoming quality Inspection reports on meeting the quality and quantity requirements.

CUTM/PSS/N0106: Perform transformer manufacturing Process
Description
This NOS involves supervising and performing the complete transformer manufacturing process, including
core assembly, winding, and final assembly. It ensures adherence to safety, quality standards, and
technical specifications while coordinating with other departments. It also emphasizes resource planning,
process inspection, troubleshooting, and maintaining proper documentation for quality assurance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Overseeing and executing the complete transformer manufacturing workflow, including coil winding,
core assembly, tank fabrication, insulation, oil processing, and testing, while ensuring quality
standards, safety compliance, use of tools and materials, process optimization, and coordination
with design and production teams.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Confirm availability of required quantity as per transformer design
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check quantity available at each process stage as per transformer design.
PC2. report the concern in case of any quality / quantity discrepancy.
PC3. arrange for discrepant quantity/quality of materials.
Core Assembly
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. understand core size, material, and grain orientation.
PC5. cut core shape as per design.
PC6. understand different core stacking methods.
PC7. use of temporary bolt guides for stacking.
PC8. stake core as per assembly drawing requirement with appropriate quantity.
PC9. tighten bolt to avoid gap between cores.
PC10. insulate assembled core.
Coil Assembly
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. understand coil size, material and number of coils as per design.
PC12. keep winding room separate from other manufacturing areas with positive pressure and
stake horizontal/vertical winding mandrels.
PC13. check conductor is either copper magnetic wire or continuously transposed conductor.
PC14. check conductor purchased are pre-wrapped with thermally upgraded paper or nomex.
PC15. check high strength wire or epoxy bonded CTC used when high short circuit forces required
as per design requirement.
