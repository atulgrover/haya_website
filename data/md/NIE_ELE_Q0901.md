#### NIE/ELE/Q0901: Product Assembly  Assistant(Solar-LED)
Brief Job Description
The qualification spans diverse subjects concerning electrical and electronic components and devices,
emphasizing the design, assembly, and utilization of solar-LED lighting products. Enrollees will acquire
crucial skills and knowledge encompassing the conceptualization of electronic components and devices,
LED characteristics and specifications, power conversion from AC-DC to DC-DC, LED driver circuit design,
assembly of LED lighting products, characteristics of solar PV cells, batteries suitable for solar-LED lighting
products, and the design, assembly, and installation of solar-LED lighting products.
Personal Attributes
People who finish the training learn a wide range of skills, including how to work with electronic parts,
LEDs, and power conversion. They are skilled at designing and putting together Solar-LED lighting goods
and have a lot of experience putting together LEDs. Specialized knowledge includes solar PV cells,
batteries, and the ability to develop, put together, and install products. The school focuses on providing
students with skills that will help them get jobs. Overall, graduates meet the National Occupational
Standards and are well-equipped to do well in Solar-LED technology. This makes them skilled workers in
solar energy applications, electronic design, and assembly.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. NIE/ELE/N0909: Conceptualizing Electronic Components and Devices
2. NIE/ELE/N0902: LED characteristics & specifications
3. NIE/ELE/N0904: AC-DC and DC-DC power conversion
4. NIE/ELE/N0905: Design of LED Driver circuit
5. NIE/ELE/N0906: LED lighting products Assembly
6. NIE/ELE/N0907: Solar PV cell: Characteristics, parameter and specification
7. NIE/ELE/N0908: Batteries for Solar-LED lighting Products
8. NIE/ELE/N0903: Design, assembly and installation of Solar-LED Lighting Products.
9. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### NIE/ELE/N0909: Conceptualizing Electronic Components and Devices
Description
This unit covers the basics of Electronic Components & Devices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Basics of Electronic Components and Devices, and their testing.
Study of different types of rectifiers and their performance parameter.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identification of Electronic Components .
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identification of Electronic Components.
- PC2. Resistor types and colour coding.
- PC3. Various type of capacitors and their identification.
Testing of Electronic components.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Testing of passive components using multimeter.
- PC5. Testing of diode and transisters using DMM.
Realising and testing rectifier
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Realising half-wave rectifier for a given O/P voltage, and viewing output waveform.
- PC7. Realising full-wave rectifier for a given O/P voltage, and viewing output waveform.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. • Organizational Context:
• Organizational policies, procedures and guidelines related for development of Solar-LED Lighting
Products
• Testing methodology of Electronic components
- KU2. • Technical Knowledge:
• Broad knowledge of various types of passive and active components and devices, and their testing.
• Specification of various types of electronic components.
• Knowledge of different types of rectifiers and their features.

#### NIE/ELE/N0904: AC-DC and DC-DC  power conversion
Description
This unit is dedicated to AC-DC and DC-DC power conversion techniques required for design and development of LED Driver circuit and charger circuit for Solar-LED Lighting Products.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Fundamentals of AC to DC Power conversion techniques.
Study of Buck converter, Boost converter and Buck-Boost converter for design/analyzing the driver
circuit of Solar-LED Lighting Products.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
AC to DC Power conversion techniques.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Design of Unregulated Power Supply.
- PC2. Design of Regulated Power Supply using 3-Terminal voltage regulator ICs
Fundamentals of DC- DC converter.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. To be familiar with the concept of PWM used in switching regulation.
- PC4. Realize various DC-DC converter topology.
- PC5. Familiarity with the working of Buck converter, Boost converter and Buck-Boost converter.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. • Organizational Context :
• Organizational policies, procedures and guidelines related for design of efficient Driver circuit for
Solar-LED Lighting Products.
• The range of standard tools available and how to use them.
- KU2. • Technical Knowledge:
• Fundamental of charger circuit of LED lighting product.
• Development of driver circuit for Solar- LED lighting products.
