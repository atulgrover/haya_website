Brief Job Description
The qualification equips individuals with foundational skills and knowledge required for food and beverage
service in the hospitality industry. It covers essential competencies such as service techniques, beverage
knowledge, menu understanding, and customer interaction. Candidates will learn practical skills like table
setups, beverage preparation, and sales processes, along with soft skills for communication and teamwork.
This qualification prepares candidates for entry-level roles in hotels, restaurants, and cafes, ensuring
compliance with industry standards and customer satisfaction.(EHL)
Personal Attributes
Positive attitude and willingness to learn Strong communication and interpersonal skills Attention to detail
and hygiene awareness Teamwork and collaboration mindset Discipline and time management

#### CII/THC/N1101: Basics of F&B Service Operations
Description
This NOS covers the skills required to deliver efficient and professional food and beverage service across
dining formats such as breakfast, brunch, and buffet. It includes guest handling, table setup, service
techniques, order taking, and maintaining service standards. It also focuses on communication, upselling,
POS operations, billing, and payment handling to ensure customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and
a seamless dining experience while maintaining hygiene and quality standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The learner will be able to set up dining areas, perform basic food and beverage service, take and
process orders using POS systems, handle billing and payments, communicate effectively with guests, apply upselling techniques, and maintain hygiene and service standards to ensure a smooth
dining experience.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
F&B Service Techniques
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Ensure that guests are welcomed and served as per basic service rules, considering
breakfast, buffet, and brunch service SOPs.
- PC2. Plan and prepare the menu, taking into account various meal times such as breakfast,
brunch, and buffet.
- PC3. Carry a tray with crockery while maintaining a professional demeanor and balance during
service.
- PC4. Handle wine glasses and cutlery properly according to the in formal F&B service standards.
- PC5. Fold napkins and display tablecloths following SOPs, ensuring attention to detail in
presentation.
- PC6. Set up tables for different types of meals, including basic and in formal settings for breakfast,
brunch, and buffet.
- PC7. Perform plate service techniques, effectively clearing tables in a professional manner.
- PC8. Take orders efficiently using various order-taking methods while addressing customer
queries about the menu.
- PC9. Serve cheese and dessert following established processes, including portion control and
guest preferences.
- PC10. Execute billing functions using correct procedures to ensure accuracy in billing
- PC11. Identify and respond to verbal and non-verbal cues from guests to enhance service quality.
- PC12. Execute a sales approach by recommending additional menu items and upselling where
appropriate
- PC13. Complete the dining experience by following farewell techniques, ensuring a memorable
guest experience

- KU6. Basics of upselling and sales techniques in F&B service.
- KU7. Hygiene, cleanliness, and safety standards in service areas.

User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Communicate clearly and professionally with guests and team members.
- GS2. Demonstrate customer service orientation and positive attitude.

#### CII/THC/N1102: Beverage Knowledge  and Service Techniques
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to prepare, present, and serve a variety of beverages,
including tea, coffee, alcoholic, and non-alcoholic drinks. It includes understanding beverage types, origins,
production processes, and food pairings, along with applying standard service techniques and maintaining
appropriate serving conditions. The NOS also emphasizes handling guest queries professionally, using
correct glassware, and complying with legal regulations. Additionally, it covers the history, preparation
methods, and quality control practices related to tea and coffee service to ensure a consistent and high-
quality guest experience.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The learner will be able to prepare and serve a range of beverages including tea, coffee, alcoholic
and non-alcoholic drinks, following standard procedures. The scope includes understanding beverage types, origins, brewing and production processes, and applying appropriate service
techniques, glassware, and food pairings. The learner will also handle guest queries effectively,
maintain service quality and hygiene standards, and comply with relevant regulations while ensuring
a professional and satisfying guest experience.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Beverage Knowledge
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Prepare and serve tea and coffee products as per the service guidelines.
- PC2. Introduce beverage to guests, explaining its varieties and origins.
- PC3. Understand the ingredients and the malting process involved in beer production.
- PC4. Demonstrate knowledge of the brewing process
- PC5. Identify and differentiate between the various types of beverage.
- PC6. Apply food-pairing techniques with alcoholic beverages, explaining pairings to guests.
- PC7. Understand and explain non-alcoholic cocktails, including their ingredients and
characteristics.
- PC8. Handle guest enquiries regarding alcoholic beverages and non-alcoholic beverages in a
professional manner.
- PC9. Use appropriate glassware for different types of alcoholic beverages and non-alcoholic
beverages.
- PC10. Work in accordance with local legislation regulating the service of alcoholic and non-alcoholic
beverages.
Techniques of serving beverages
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Serve in a restaurant as per SOPs.
- PC12. Serve soft beverages in a restaurant following SOPs.

#### CII/THC/N1103: Hospitality Operations & Customer Service - F&B
Description
This NOS covers the foundational knowledge and skills required to understand hospitality operations and
deliver effective customer service. It includes an overview of the hospitality and tourism industry, types of
establishments, basic terminology, and operational coordination across departments. The NOS also
focuses on guest handling, communication, complaint resolution, and personalized service to ensure guest
satisfaction. Additionally, it includes event planning basics, reservation systems, and awareness of industry
trends to support efficient operations and enhanced guest experiences.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The learner will be able to understand the hospitality industry structure, its key sectors, and
operational functions, and apply basic industry terminology in workplace communication. The scope
includes coordinating with departments, assisting in event planning, and using reservation systems
to support operations. The learner will also develop customer service skills such as guest handling,
personalized service, effective communication, and complaint resolution. Additionally, the learner
will maintain service standards, handle guest feedback, and ensure a professional and welcoming
environment to enhance overall guest satisfaction.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Hospitality Operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate an understanding of the hospitality and tourism industry, including its scope,
key sectors, and major players.
- PC2. Apply effective customer service skills relevant to the hospitality and tourism industry,
focusing on professionalism and guest satisfaction
- PC3. Plan and organize travel and assembly events, including coordinating logistics,
accommodations, and activities
- PC4. Create detailed event plans, incorporating timelines, budgets, and resource allocation to
ensure successful event execution
- PC5. Implement standard operating procedures (SOPs) to enhance guest services and operational
workflows, ensuring consistency and efficiency
- PC6. Understand and describe the various types of hospitality establishments (e.g., hotels,
resorts, restaurants) and their operational functions
- PC7. Utilize basic industry terminology and concepts to communicate effectively within the
hospitality sector
- PC8. Assist in the development and execution of marketing strategies to promote hospitality
services and attract guests
- PC9. Demonstrate knowledge of booking and reservation systems used in the hospitality industry
to manage guest accommodations
