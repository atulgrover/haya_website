#### CUTM/PSS/Q0101: Transformer  Testing Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The individual is responsible to understand the testing requirement, transformer specification, verification
of assembled transformer, testing functional and quality of transformer fabrication along with necessary
safety norms.
Personal Attributes
A Transformer Testing Supervisor must have strong technical expertise in electrical testing, analytical
thinking for fault diagnosis, and problem-solving skills for troubleshooting. They should possess leadership
abilities to manage teams, attention to detail for quality assurance, safety awareness, effective
communication, and time management to ensure transformers meet industry standards and customer requirements.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CUTM/PSS/N0101: Understand transformer testing requirements.
2. CUTM/PSS/N0102: Testing and quality check of assembled transformer
3. CUTM/VSQ/N7801: Ensure organizational health, safety, and security
4. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### CUTM/PSS/N0101: Understand  transformer testing requirements.
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to understand transformer testing requirements for
ensuring quality, performance, and compliance with standards. It includes interpreting technical
specifications, understanding different types of transformers, and identifying key testing parameters such
as insulation resistance, turns ratio, impedance, and load loss. The individual should be able to follow
safety procedures, read testing reports, and comply with national and international standards. This
competency ensures that transformer testing supervisors can effectively assess transformers' operational
efficiency, diagnose faults, and maintain optimal performance in power distribution and industrial
applications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers transformer testing requirements, including interpreting specifications, conducting
insulation, impedance, and load loss tests, ensuring compliance with safety and industry standards,
diagnosing faults, and analyzing test reports. It ensures transformers meet operational efficiency
and quality standards in power distribution and industrial applications.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify Testing Parameters and Prepare Test Equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Interact with Transformer Testing Engineer on customer requirement, engineering drawings
and standards to be followed.
- PC2. Understand tools, raw materials and consumables used for manufacturing.
- PC3. Clearly understand the application of transformer and standards to be followed for testing
process.
- PC4. Understand detailed assembly drawing, material grade, dimensional tolerances, and
assembly process for the specific batch to be tested.
- PC5. Understand the process steps to be followed during inspection.
- PC6. Verify the work sheet steps on each stage of process followed matching to Client
requirement.
- PC7. Get necessary clarification from the appropriate authorities in case of any doubts.
- PC8. Verify the working condition of all Testing equipment.
- PC9. Check the calibration validity of the measuring instruments to be used for Testing.
- PC10. Check incoming quality inspection reports on meeting the quality and quantity requirements.
- PC11. Ensure tools required are placed in respective stages of transformer testing.
- PC12. If tool is unavailable collect tools from stores, vendor site and place it in respective stages.
- PC13. Update all testing details in appropriate logs, template, MIS.
- PC14. Maintain test file, log, template, MIS and update as per requirement.

#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The basics of electricity, magnetism, and transformer working principles.
- KU2. Knowledge of different transformer types such as power, distribution, instrument, and special-
purpose transformers.
- KU3. Transformer parts, including windings, core, insulation, oil, bushings, and cooling systems.
- KU4. Knowledge of testing techniques like insulation resistance, turns ratio, short circuit, open
circuit, and impulse voltage tests.
- KU5. Common transformer faults (e.g., overheating, insulation failure, oil contamination) and their
detection methods.
- KU6. Awareness of electrical safety protocols, PPE requirements, and risk management during transformer testing.
- KU7. Ability to document test results, prepare reports, and maintain compliance records.
- KU8. Leadership, work delegation, and quality assurance in transformer testing environments.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Communicate clearly with team members, engineers, and clients regarding transformer testing procedures and results.
- GS2. Supervising and guiding the testing team to ensure efficiency and accuracy in operations.
- GS3. Identify faults, analyzing issues, and making data-driven decisions for troubleshooting.
- GS4. Efficiently planning and utilizing manpower, testing equipment, and other resources.
- GS5. Follow workplace safety standards, electrical safety norms, and environmental regulations.
- GS6. Expertise in various testing methods like insulation resistance, short circuit, open circuit, and
oil testing.
- GS7. Analyz test results to ensure compliance with industry standards and customer requirements.
- GS8. Handle testing tools such as megger, TTR meter, high-voltage testers, and thermal cameras.
- GS9. Maintain accurate records, preparing technical reports, and ensuring quality control in transformer testing processes.

#### CUTM/PSS/N0102: Testing and quality check of assembled transformer
Description
This NOS covers testing and quality checks of assembled transformers to ensure performance, safety, and
compliance with standards. It includes conducting electrical tests, verifying insulation, assessing load
performance, identifying defects, and analyzing test reports to certify transformer reliability in power
distribution and industrial applications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS includes conducting electrical tests, insulation checks, load performance assessments, and
verifying compliance with standards. It covers fault detection, quality assurance, report analysis, and
ensuring transformer reliability. It ensures assembled transformers meet operational, safety, and
industry standards for efficient power distribution and industrial applications.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Various testing methods as per transformer design and applications
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. understand various testing methods to be followed as per design and application.
- PC2. prepare check list of parameters for consideration during testing and inspection.
- PC3. verify connections of HT/LT side and inspect bolt/lugs and solder of electrical connections.
- PC4. perform routine test, loss measurement and temperature rise test, dielectric test.
- PC5. carry out insulation-resistance test to identify grounding and shorting faults in the
connections.
- PC6. • check for any damage, flash, and hair crack with respect to specified parameters.
• Parameters: condition of OLTC, leakage of oil in bushing collar, porcelain insulator bushing,
gasket joints and flanges
- PC7. inspect excess humming noise in transformer and identify loose fitting of silicon mixed steel
alloys laminated core joints.
Quality check and confirmation of proper assembly
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. • identify faults by visually inspecting general appearance, leakage of oil, and physical condition
• Physical condition: rust on body and radiators
- PC9. check silica gel breather for the oil level in oil cap on specific parameters.
- PC10. • identify faults
• Faults: faults due to primary winding, braze /solder of LT winding joints melted, overheat, open
circuit in internal wiring etc.
- PC11. to maintain records of transformer Basic information: serial number, diagram, rating plate
etc. and defects/repair showing diagnostic records for assessing the transformer performance history.

- KU1. The transformer principles, electromagnetic induction, and types of transformers.

- GS5. test insulation resistance, turns ratio, short circuit, and oil breakdown voltage tests.

- GS7. handle megger, TTR meter, high-voltage testers, and infrared thermography tools.
- GS8. Prepare quality check reports, maintaining records, and documenting non-conformities for corrective action.

#### CUTM/VSQ/N7801: Ensure organizational health, safety, and security
Description
This NOS focuses on maintaining workplace safety, health, and security by identifying hazards, following
safety procedures, using personal protective equipment (PPE), and responding to emergencies. It ensures
compliance with health and safety regulations, proper waste disposal, and adherence to security protocols
to create a safe and productive work environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers workplace hazard identification, adherence to safety regulations, proper use of
personal protective equipment (PPE), emergency response, fire safety, first aid, and waste management. It also includes implementing security measures, ensuring workplace hygiene, and
promoting a safety-conscious culture to prevent accidents and maintain a secure working environment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify different kinds of possible hazards at the workplace hazards: environmental,
personal, ergonomic and chemical.
- PC2. recognize other possible security issues existing in the workplace.
- PC3. plan safety techniques for self and others.
- PC4. recognize different measures to curb the hazards.
- PC5. implement programs for health, safety and security of the people at the workplace.
- PC6. communicate the safety plans to everyone at the workplace.
- PC7. attach disciplinary rules with the implementation at the workplace.
- PC8. comply with regulatory health and safety guidelines applicable to the workplace.
- PC9. use and maintain protective equipment as per requirement in a production house.
- PC10. ensure organizational activities in line with approved guidelines and procedures.
- PC11. undertake and advise healthy lifestyle for self and others.
- PC12. follow environment management system related procedures.
- PC13. ensure safe handling and disposal of waste and debris.
- PC14. minimize health and safety risks to self and others due to own actions.
- PC15. monitor the workplace and work processes for potential risks and threats to self and others.
- PC16. carry out periodic check to keep work area free from hazards and obstructions.
- PC17. ensure participation of self and others in mock drills/ evacuation procedures organized at the
workplace.
- PC18. organize first aid, firefighting and emergency training sessions for others.
- PC19. instruct and guide others to take action in the event of fire, emergencies or accidents.
