DWC/Q0111: Pest Control Technician (Household & Small Establishment)
Brief Job Description
A Pest Control Technician plays a pivotal role in managing pest-related issues for households and small
establishments. This operational position involves implementing pest control measures and maintaining a
pest-free environment. The technician is responsible for assessing, identifying, and addressing pest
infestations through various methods and treatments.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to take full responsibility of the domestic and commercial pest control
services. The individual should be attentive to details, and have a methodical approach. The individual
should also have problem solving attitude with basic communication skills, and good personal hygiene
along with physically fitness and able to work in a variety of environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. DWC/N0127: Post treatment activity and after sales service
- 2. DWC/N0126: Conduct pest control activity as per treatment plan
- 3. DWC/N0125: Develop customised treatment plan based on identified pests
- 4. DWC/N0124: Conduct inspection of the infested area
- 5. DWC/N9902: Maintain service standards and communicate effectively
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
- 7. DWC/N9903: Maintain health, hygiene and safety standards

DWC/N0127: Post treatment activity and after sales service
Description
This unit is about conducting post treatment activities and providing after sales service to the clients.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct post treatment inspection
Provide advice and recommendations on preventive measures
Maintain detailed records of inspections, treatments, and follow-up activities
Collect payment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct post treatment inspection
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. conduct a thorough inspection of the treated area within the designated timeframe after
treatment
PC2. utilise appropriate tools and techniques to ensure a comprehensive evaluation of the treated
environment
PC3. assess the effectiveness of the treatment based on the observed reduction in pest activity
and client feedback
Provide advice and recommendations on preventive measures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. offer detailed advice to clients on potential pest attractants and recommend effective
preventive measures
PC5. recommend practical and sustainable preventive measures based on the identified pest type
and property type (e.g., sealing cracks, storing food properly, eliminating clutter)
PC6. assess the presence of structural damage or deficiencies contributing to pest access or
harbourage (e.g., leaky pipes, gaps in walls, damaged flooring)
PC7. recommend necessary repairs or modifications to address structural weaknesses and
prevent future pest entry
PC8. provide clients with basic information about common pests and their identification signs
(e.g., appearance, behaviour, damage caused)
Maintain detailed records of inspections, treatments, and follow-up activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. accurately document all findings and observations during inspections, treatments, and
follow-up activities
PC10. maintain detailed records of applied treatments, including dates, methods, pesticides used,
and specific locations of application
PC11. use a standardised format for records to ensure consistency, clarity, and easy retrieval of
information

DWC/N0126: Conduct pest control activity as per treatment plan
Description
This unit involves carrying out pest control activities as per the customised treatment plan, while adhering
to all the relavent safety protocols.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare the area for pesticide application
Apply selected pesticides using specialised equipment
Adhere to safety precautions
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare the Area for Pesticide Application
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. assess the need for temporary removal of obstacles to access pest harbourages and ensure
thorough treatment
PC2. safely and carefully disassemble and relocate furniture, fittings, or false ceiling panels,
minimising damage and ensuring their safe reinstallation
PC3. clearly communicate the need for relocation to the client and obtain their consent before
proceeding
PC4. reinstall furniture, fittings, and false ceiling panels properly and securely, minimising
disruption to the client's environment
PC5. ensure proper removal or protection of food items, utensils, and personal belongings from
the treatment area
PC6. secure the treatment area and restrict access to unauthorised personnel and pets during
application and for the designated re-entry period
Apply selected pesticides using specialised equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. select and assemble appropriate equipment based on the chosen treatment method (e.g.,
sprayers, bait stations, fumigation equipment, traps)
PC8. calibrate and adjust equipment settings as needed to deliver the correct dosage and
application pattern
PC9. follow the prescribed application method for each pesticide in the treatment plan,
considering factors like target pest, property type, and environmental conditions
PC10. mix chemical/botanical pesticides accurately, adhering to specified ratios and dilution
instructions to avoid under- or over-application
PC11. select the appropriate application technique (spraying, baiting, fumigation, trapping) based
on the chosen pesticide, target pest, and property type
PC12. apply pesticides precisely and evenly, focusing on identified harbourages, entry points, and
areas of pest activity

DWC/N0125: Develop customised treatment plan based on identified
pests
Description
This unit involves carrying out pest identification and developing customised treatment plan for addressing
identified pest.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify types of pest and extent of infestation
Develop customised treatment plan
Provide cost estimation to client
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify types of pest and extent of infestation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. analyse inspection findings to accurately identify the specific pest species present based on
physical characteristics, behaviour, and activity signs

DWC/N0124: Conduct inspection of the infested area
Description
This unit encompasses the knowledge, understanding, and skills needed to conduct a thorough inspection
of the infested areas, encompassing detailed examination of both indoor and outdoor locations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct site visit
Inspect both interior and exterior of the property
Detect presence of pest
Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct site visit
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. schedule and organise site visits in a timely manner in accordance with client preference /
scheduled appointment times
PC2. establish effective communication with the property owner or designated contact person to
gather relevant information
PC3. obtain necessary background information about the pest problem, including type of property
(e.g., residential, office building, food establishment), suspected pest type(s), severity of the
infestation and history of previous pest control attempts
PC4. briefly explain the inspection process to the concerned person and address any immediate
safety concerns
Inspect both interior and exterior of the property
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. gather relevant safety equipment (e.g., gloves, mask, flashlight) and ensure proper attire for
the inspection
PC6. visually examine the interior spaces of the property, including rooms, kitchens, storage
areas, and other accessible spaces
PC7. inspect areas with high moisture content, potential food sources, and harbourage sites
conducive to pest activity
PC8. thoroughly inspect the exterior of the property, covering potential pest entry points, and
hiding spots such as cracks, crevices, drains, and around windows/doors
PC9. utilise appropriate tools and equipment to aid in the inspection, such as flashlights, mirrors,
and probes
Detect presence of pest
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. identify pest presence through sound and visual cues, including direct sightings of live or
dead pests and/or eggs
PC11. search for droppings, nests, gnaw marks, burrows or any other indications of pest activity

Description
This unit is about the following personal and workplace hygiene, following workplace practices to promote
wellbeing and managing waste at workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Follow personal and workplace hygiene
Follow workplace practices to promote wellbeing
Manage waste at workplace
Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow personal and workplace hygiene
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. wash and sanitize hands at regular intervals with hand soap and alcohol-based sanitizers
PC2. keep the home or workplace free of germs, insects, and bugs by utilizing the proper
environmentally friendly solutions or cleaning chemicals
PC3. wash the dishes and other items in accordance with the set requirements
PC4. sanitize all tools, equipment, and appliances with touch points on a regular basis
PC5. make sure that the garbage cans are frequently cleared in accordance with the timetable for
cleanliness and maintenance
PC6. place trash in designated bins or the proper trash container
PC7. wear appropriate PPE like hair net, protective aprons, footwear, respirators, masks, etc. at
work
PC8. maintain personal hygiene by brushing teeth frequently, bathing daily, dressing well, eating
healthfully, etc.
PC9. avoid consumption of tobacco, paan, alcohol, smoking cigarettes, etc. at the workplace
Follow workplace practices to promote wellbeing

PC10. adhere to safety protocols when using materials, tools, and equipment
PC11. follow guidelines and safety protocol while using electrical household gadgets
PC12. follow first aid instructions appropriately
PC13. recognize workplace risks and promptly inform anybody who should know about them
PC14. report to various emergency circumstances with prompt response and reporting
PC15. attend regular health check-ups
PC16. place medicines and hazardous chemicals away
PC17. keep sharp objects out of reach of child
Manage waste at workplace
