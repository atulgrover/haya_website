#### MSU/SSC/Q0101: Digital Content Developer
Brief Job Description
This is a short-term qualification to meet the growing need for digital content developers in the country.
Personal Attributes
Candidates should be adept at computer systems and creative bent of mind. They should be able to work
on computers for a long period of time. Good team team playing skills and language skills are also
desirable.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MSU/ITS/N0101: Prepare digital textual content
2. MSU/ITS/N0102: Develop audio and visual content using appropriate tools
3. MSU/ITS/N0103: Review digital content
4. MSU/ITS/N0104: Publish digital content
5. MSU/ITS/N0105: Work as per plan
6. MSU/ITS/N9901: Maintain health and safety in a digital workspace-
7. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### MSU/ITS/N0101: Prepare digital textual content
Description
This NOS includes the skills and knowledge for developing textual content using appropriate digital tools
based on identified digital textual content requirements.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify digital textual content requirements
Develop textual content using appropriate digital tools
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify digital textual content requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Gather the content production requirements from the authorized personnel as per
organizational norms.
- PC2. Determine the objective of the content and elements required in the content
- PC3. Determine the needs and viewpoints of the target users and audience using various user
data.
- PC4. Obtain organizational templates and style manuals to maintain content uniformity and help
with content production.
- PC5. Identify digital media-related compliances and regulations to be observed
- PC6. Evaluate platform capabilities and constraints to guide content production.
- PC7. Analyze how the material is to be used on internal and external platforms to guide content
creation and facilitate cross-platform usability.
- PC8. Get the organization and product information needed for content production.
Develop textual content using appropriate digital tools
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Schedule content creation to coincide with organizational branding and marketing initiatives
- PC10. Source text-based content materials that may be incorporated and referenced
- PC11. Fill in the perceived information gaps in text-based materials using various supplementary
sources.
- PC12. Evaluate and choose the most suitable content ideas and writing styles in consultation with
the right personnel.
- PC13. Come up with various ideas relevant to the objective of the content to be created.
- PC14. Organize content by category, order, and structure to make it simple to read and navigate.
- PC15. Write content using principles and best practices of effective communication.

starting the content development process

the website, create brand and target market appeal, achieve consistency and alignment with organizational branding and promotion activity
- KU5. Types of data that may provide information on users and audience

- KU12. Identification of different sources to be used as supplementary material and information
- KU13. Writing styles and their purpose

#### MSU/ITS/N0102: Develop audio and visual content using appropriate tools
Description
This NOS includes the skills and knowledge for developing audio and visual content using appropriate
digital tools based on identified digital audio and visual content requirements.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify audio and visual digital content requirements
Develop audio and visual digital content
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify audio and visual digital content requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Gather audio-visual digital content requirements for the organization, target audience
profiling, resources available and constraints.
- PC2. Obtain organizational templates and style manuals to maintain content uniformity and help
with content production.
- PC3. Determine platform capabilities and constraints to guide content production.
- PC4. Analyze how the material is used on internal and external platforms to guide content
creation and facilitate cross-platform usability.
- PC5. Identify digital media-related compliances and regulations to be observed
Develop audio and visual digital content
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Source audio and visual content that appeals to the target audience and fits the
requirement, organization's logo, style guide, and marketing strategy received from authorized sources. Authorized sources: organization-approved graphics; audio video from
the production team; free online sources of images, graphics, audio and video; etc.
- PC7. Edit audio and visual material to produce attractive and high-quality material using media
editing tools like Photoshop, Canva, and Video Editors like Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro,
AVID media composer, etc.
- PC8. Provide captions, sub-titles or descriptions ensuring relevancy, accuracy and SEO
optimization
- PC9. Embed the audio and visual content in the overall content seeking maximum impact and
effective utilization of space and size.
- PC10. Ensure that audio and visual representations are error-free as per the requirements.
- PC11. Send the updated audio and visual content to stakeholders for review and consideration.
- PC12. Consider the feedback from relevant parties and make changes where necessary.
- PC13. Send information by the deadline established by organizational policies.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

#### MSU/ITS/N0103: Review digital content
Description
This NOS includes the skills and knowledge for reviewing the digital content based on identified digital
content requirements.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify requirements
Prepare for review
Conduct review
Share review
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Gather information about review requirements
- PC2. Define stakeholders to be consulted with
- PC3. Obtain the scope of work and review guidelines from stakeholders
- PC4. Establish review goals, processes and timelines
- PC5. Identify digital media-related compliances and regulations to be observed
- PC6. Identify content objectives, outline and sequence
- PC7. Obtain guidelines with respect to technical and creative quality standards including
appearance, language, format and templates, etc.
- PC8. Identify limitations of the software and personnel as well as the organizational constraints
with respect to the content development and editing
- PC9. Create a checklist for reviewing the content that covers all aspects to be reviewed and get it
approved by authorised personnel
Prepare for review
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Receive content from authorised sources
- PC11. Ensure currency of version and receipt of complete content
- PC12. Ensure availability of standard web browser, applications or software required to view the
content in the form it will be visible to the target audience
- PC13. Agree with the content writer/editor on the mode, format and template in which the review
findings and feedback are to be shared
- PC14. Create a review strategy to ensure adherence to all review objectives, guidelines, timelines
and standards
- PC15. Take a backup of the entire content before initiating the review
Conduct review

#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC16. Review online content using an industry-standard web browser or application to check
layout, appearance and accessibility requirements are met
- PC17. Proof-read digital content to identify any errors with respect to language, format and

- PC18. Check adherence to branding and messaging guidelines across the content
- PC19. Identify gaps and inadequacies in the content by evaluating the content based on the

- PC20. Follow protocols for ensuring the accuracy and authenticity of information
- PC21. Check for superseded and inaccurate information and suggest current information and

- PC22. Check and identify plagiarized content using digital tools
- PC23. Analyse the efficiency of content in achieving its purpose using digital tools for analytics
- PC24. Seek clarifications when in doubt from authorised personnel and document it with the review
guidelines
Share review
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC25. Compile and organise the findings and feedback in the template and format agreed upon
- PC26. Share the compiled report of the findings and feedback to the authorised personnel within
the agreed timelines
- PC27. Provide clarifications, where required, to the content writer/editor and other stakeholders in a
manner and format that they will find easy to understand
- PC28. Archive the materials as per organisational standard operating procedures

- KU1. Importance of content review before and after publishing the content
- KU2. Various types of digital content, their objectives, target audience and stakeholders
- KU3. General review goals and process
- KU4. Information on technical and creative quality standards
- KU5. Common limitations and concerns regarding the development and replacement of original material, review and editing strategies or other technical errors
- KU6. Common organisational constraints like time, budgets, access to materials, etc.
- KU7. Digital media-related compliances, regulations, codes of practice and relevant aspects of
privacy and copyright laws
- KU8. Documentation of instructions and specifications for digital content review
- KU9. Evaluation of stakeholder feedback and incorporation to meet production standards
- KU10. Importance of documenting all requirements and creating a checklist for review
- KU11. Importance of ensuring content received is from authorised sources, of correct version and
complete
- KU12. Difference in content review process due to browser, application or software used

### Page 51

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
