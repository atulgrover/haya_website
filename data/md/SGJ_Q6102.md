#### SGJ/Q6102: Safai Mitra
Brief Job Description
Safai Mitra sweeps, cleans and removes garbage from public areas and buildings. In public areas and
roads, Safai Mitra sweeps with a broom and / or other suitable equipment to remove dust, debris and
garbage. In buildings, he/she sweeps the floor, scrubs the floor using appropriate cleaning solution to
remove the fine dust. He/She removes the garbage and aggregates the garbage in the designated areas.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate and undertake the work regularly and with dedication.
He/She must communicate courteously with public and co-workers.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SGJ/N6105: Cleaning of Roads, Pavements, Public Area etc.
2. SGJ/N6106: Cleaning of floor(s) of buildings
3. SGJ/N6107: Maintain Personal Health & Safety while Cleaning
4. SGJ/N6112: Cleaning of toilets
5. SGJ/N6113: Cleaning with Mechanized cleaning tools

#### SGJ/N6105: Cleaning of Roads, Pavements, Public Area etc.
Description
This unit is about properly cleaning roads, pavements, streets, lanes, parks and public areas
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Sweeping/cleaning of Roads, Pavements etc.
Sweeping/cleaning of Parks and Public Areas
Collect and segregate garbage
Transportation of garbage/waste materials to designated area
Separate solid waste, plastics and recyclable waste from biodegradable waste
Effective waste management/recycling practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to role of Safai Mitra
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. provide an overview of the waste management sector and highlight its importance.
- PC2. explain the importance of the role of Safai Mitra in waste management, by cleaning of roads,
pavements, buildings and other public areas and demonstrate the role of Safai Mitra in cleaning of roads, pavements, buildings and other public areas

- PC4. understand basic skills possessed by individuals working as Safai Mitra.

waste management and demonstrate how to read instructions, identify colour codes, signs and symbols used in waste management.
Cleaning of Roads, Pavements, Public Area etc.

techniques for cleaning/sweeping.
