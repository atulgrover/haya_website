#### TNSD/CSC/Q0103: Die & Mould Maker
Brief Job Description
A Die & Mould Maker is an expert in process planning, manufacturing, and design of various dies and
moulds, including press tools, jigs, fixtures, and die-casting dies. This role requires advanced skills in CNC
programming and operation, CAD/CAM software, material analysis, and economic principles.
Personal Attributes
Upon successful completion, learners will be qualified to become experts in the process planning,
manufacturing, and design of dies & moulds, jigs & fixtures, press tools, and die casting dies. They will
secure employment in engineering, manufacturing, machine tools, automobile, and all types of machinery
industries, and also be equipped for entrepreneurship.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. TNSD/CSC/N0128: Manufacture and Try-out Press Tools, Jigs & Fixtures.
2. TNSD/CSC/N0120: Design and Manufacture Press Tools for Sheet Metal Components
3. TNSD/CSC/N0121: Design and Utilize Jigs & Fixtures for Mass Production
4. TNSD/CSC/N0122: Program and Operate Advanced Multi-Axis CNC Machines
5. TNSD/CSC/N0123: Generate Basic 2D/3D Models and Toolpaths using Master cam
6. TNSD/CSC/N6912: Create Basic 2D/3D Models using SolidWorks
7. TNSD/CSC/N0124: Design Complex Press Tools, Jigs & Fixtures using Advanced SolidWorks
8. TNSD/CSC/N0125: Analyze Material Stress, Strain, and Thermal Effects
9. TNSD/CSC/N0126: Apply Core Principles of Organizational Management
10. TNSD/CSC/N0127: Perform Tool & Die Cost Estimation and Economic Analysis

#### TNSD/CSC/N0128: Manufacture  and Try-out Press Tools, Jigs & Fixtures.
Description
Understand the manufacturing of press tools, jigs &Fixtures by using Conventional, CNC machines and
different soft wares. Try-out of press tools, Jigs and fixtures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the manufacturing of press tools, jigs &Fixtures by using Conventional, CNC machines
and different soft wares
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Learn the standard grades of sheet metal used for processing in Press Tools and general
Press Tool operation ,construction and features
- PC2. Understand the concept of theory of shearing ,cutting force and cutting clearance calculation
for different operations ,Strip layout and economy factor
- PC3. • Understand cutting elements- Punch, Die block. Its types and importance.
• Effect of land and angular clearance. Effect of force in Buckling of punches.
- PC4. Understand the functions of punch holder, Stripper and pilot and its types.
- PC5. Learn to Select the right type of gauges, stoppers and fasteners
- PC6. Understand the usage of side cutter and location of shank in a press tool
- PC7. Perform Design and drawing of open die and guide plate press tools .
- PC8. Demonstrate Design and drawing of progressive type of press tools with cutting and Non-
cutting operations.
- PC9. Perform Design and drawing of compound and combination press tool.
- PC10. Demonstrate design and drawing of fine blanking tool
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand the concept of theory of shearing ,cutting force and cutting clearance calculation
for different operations ,Strip layout and economy factor
- KU2. • Understand cutting elements- Punch, Die block. Its types and importance.
• Effect of land and angular clearance. Effect of force in Buckling of punches.
- KU3. Understand the functions of punch holder, Stripper and pilot and its types.

User/individual on the job needs to know how to:

#### TNSD/CSC/N0120: Design and Manufacture  Press Tools for Sheet Metal
Components
Description
Able to understand sheet metal processing, Able to design and draw different types of press tools used for
manufacturing sheet metal Components.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the sheet metal processing by press working operations.
Understand the different parts of press tool like die set, punch, die block, stripper, pilot, ejector etc.
Familiar with different types of press tools like progressive tool, compound tool and combination
tools.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria

- PC1. • Understand the degrees of freedom, location of job and planes of movements in a
• jig, fixture.
- PC2. Understand the difference between jig and fixture and its parts
- PC3. Learn to Select correct types of jigs and fixtures for a specific operation
- PC4. Understand tool location with respect to machine in a jig and fixture
- PC5. Understand mounting and location of a fixture on a machine tool
- PC6. Learn to Calculate cutting forces acting on jig, fixture
- PC7. Demonstrate Design and drawing of different types of jigs.
- PC8. Demonstrate Design and drawing of different types of fixtures.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. • Understand the degrees of freedom, location of job and planes of movements in a
• jig, fixture.
- KU2. Understand the difference between jig and fixture and its parts.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Learn to Calculate cutting forces acting on jig, fixture
- GS2. Demonstrate Design and drawing of different types of jigs.
