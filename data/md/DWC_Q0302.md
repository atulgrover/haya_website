#### DWC/Q0302: Manager  Care Homes
Brief Job Description
The individual at work manages the day-to-day operations of the Day care/ Old Age Home/ PwD Home. The
individual participates in the development, recommendation, and administration of policies, procedures
and processes in support of Centre operations.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be sensitive to the cause of the organization and its resident,
empathetic, courageous, compassionate, good at presentation, PR and interpersonal, leadership and
supervisory skills, good at both written and oral communication skills. The individual should be responsible,
computer savvy and should be willing to take initiatives and should have attention to safety.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. DWC/N0810: Supervise technical/support staff
2. DWC/N0809: Plan and implement activities for the day-to-day operation of the centre
3. DWC/N0811: Carry out basic administrative and documentation duties
4. DWC/N9902: Display standards of hygiene and work etiquettes
5. DWC/N9903: Maintain a clean and secure working environment
6. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)
7. DWC/N0228: Communicate effectively with individuals at all levels and practice inclusivity

#### DWC/N0810: Supervise technical/support staff
Description
This OS unit talks about building and overlooking staff at the centre
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage staff related activities at the centre
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage staff related activities at the centre
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. assist in the recruitment and hiring of the most suitable candidates, with both skills and
temperament, for the position
- PC2. provide orientation, supervision, coaching and encouragement to staff
- PC3. evaluate and provide feedback on staff performance through a review process
- PC4. take corrective action when necessary
- PC5. recommends dismissal of staff as necessary
- PC6. assess training needs
- PC7. provide opportunities for training and development
- PC8. Ensure all staff receive training on emerging trends in caregiving and technology.
- PC9. manage staff working schedules and time off
- PC10. prepare weekly roster of the staff members
- PC11. ensure staff adhere to the Codes of Practice and rules, policies and procedures contained in
the Staff Handbook
- PC12. ensure good quality working relationships are built and maintained between staff and the
individuals they are supporting
- PC13. ensure a range of regular meetings take place between the staff
- PC14. review and assist in weekly program planning
- PC15. help staff members with their professional development
- PC16. Provide a confidential mechanism for staff to raise concerns or report workplace issues
- PC17. supervise volunteers
- PC18. Facilitate partnerships with government agencies and NGOs to access additional resources or
funding for the centre.
- PC19. Promote the centre's services through community engagement events and public awareness
campaigns.
- PC20. Create a grievance redressal mechanism for all stakeholders, including families, staff, and
residents.

Description
This OS unit is about the knowledge and skills required to behave appropriately in a work environment

| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
