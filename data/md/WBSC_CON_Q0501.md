WBSC/CON/Q0501: Assistant House Wireman and Motor Winder
Brief Job Description
Install, maintain and repair electrical wiring, and lighting systems. Read technical diagrams and blueprints.
Perform general electrical maintenance. Also rewind and produce coils, replacing and preparing
components, such as the stator core for winding.
Personal Attributes
An Assistant House Wireman and Motor Winder should have attention to detail, teamwork, physical
stamina, time management, communication skills, numerical ability, organizational skills and safety
consciousness to perform tasks effectively and accurately.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. WBSC/CON/N0501: Apply Safe Working Practices (Assistant House Wireman and Motor Winder)
- 2. WBSC/CON/N0502: Make electrical wire joints & soldering
- 3. WBSC/CON/N0503: Analyse, demonstrate and test basic electrical connection
- 4. WBSC/CON/N0504: Plan and prepare Earthing installation
- 5. WBSC/CON/N0505: Assemble, install and test wiring system
- 6. WBSC/CON/N0506: Plan and execute electrical illumination system
- 7. WBSC/CON/N0507: Perform winding for armature of a ceiling fan / table fan
- 8. WBSC/CON/N0508: Work in real job situation with special emphasis on basic safety and hazards in
this domain.
- 9. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

WBSC/CON/N0501: Apply Safe Working Practices (Assistant House
Wireman and Motor Winder)
Description
Apply Safe Working Practices
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Application and maintenance of Safe Working Practices.
Recognition of any unsafe situations according to site policy.
Identification of fire and safety and fire hazards.
Identification of different fire extinguishers and use them as per requirements.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Apply and maintain Safe Working Practices
PC2. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy.
PC3. Identify fire and safety and fire hazards
PC4. Identify different fire extinguishers and use them as per requirements.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Maintenance of procedures to achieve a safe working environment in line with occupational
health and safety regulations and requirements according to site policy.
KU2. Recognition of any unsafe situations according to site policy and assess his report accordingly.
KU3. Identification and taking necessary precautions on fire and safety hazards and report
according to site policy and procedures.
KU4. Concepts of standard operation of electrical mains.
KU5. The process of responding to emergencies seg. Power failure,fire and system failure.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assess his report accordingly.
GS2. Demonstrate Personal Productive Equipment (PPE) like: safety helmet, safety glove, safety
shoe, use the same as per related working environment.
GS3. Demonstrate basic first aid & CPR and use them under different circumstances
GS4. Identify different fire extinguishers and use the same as per requirement in a mock drill.

WBSC/CON/N0502: Make electrical wire joints & soldering
Description
Make electrical wire joints & soldering
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Making of simple straight twist and rat-tail joints in single strand conductors / married and T (Tee)
joint in stranded conductors.
Soldering and de-soldering of the finished copper conductor joints with precaution.
Safety/precaution taken during joints & soldering.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Make simple straight twist and rat-tail joints in single strand conductors / married and ‘T’ (Tee)
joint in stranded conductors.
PC2. Solder and de-solder the finished copper conductor joints with precaution.
PC3. Follow the safety/ precaution during joints & soldering.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Types of wires,cables and verify their specifications.
KU2. Simple straight twist and rat-tail joints in single strand conductors / married and T (Tee) joint
in stranded conductors.
KU3. Procedure for soldering and de-soldering.
KU4. Safety precautions taken during joints and soldering.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Make simple straight twist and rat-tail joints in single strand conductors / married and T (Tee)
joint in stranded conductors.
GS2. Solder and de-solder the finished copper conductor joints with precaution.
GS3. Demonstrate & identify Trade hand tools
GS4. Use, care & maintain various hand tools
GS5. Practice on using cutting pliers, screwdrivers etc. skinning the cables
GS6. Practice on joining a single strand.
GS7. Practice on bare conductors joints Viz Britannia, straight, Tee, Western union.

WBSC/CON/N0503: Analyse, demonstrate and test basic electrical
connection
Description
Analyse, demonstrate and test basic electrical connection
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Types of wires, cables and verify their specifications.
The characteristics of series, parallel and its combination circuit.
Measure Current, Voltage and Resistance in a single phase supply / load.
Identification of the phase, neutral and earth in a single phase supply.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify types of wires, cables and verify their specifications.
PC2. Verify the characteristics of series, parallel and its combination circuit.
PC3. Measure Current, Voltage and Resistance in a single phase supply / load.
PC4. Identify the phase, neutral and earth in a single phase supply.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The characteristics of series, parallel and its combination circuit.
KU2. Testing of single phase voltage using test lamp.
KU3. Measurement of Voltage, current and resistance.
KU4. Testing of domestic wiring installation by using Megger
KU5. Draw wiring of house.
KU6. Practice of common domestic wiring.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Test single phase voltage using test lamp.
GS2. Measure current, voltage in a single phase system.
GS3. Demonstrate the types of fuses, their ratings and applications and also to identify the parts of
a MCB, ELCB and RCCB.
GS4. Check installation of earthing system and test of earthing system.
