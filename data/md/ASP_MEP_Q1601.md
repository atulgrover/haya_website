#### ASP/MEP/Q1601: Communicative  English Trainer
Brief Job Description
Individuals at this job are responsible for training young learners spread over diverse professional
environments to develop their language skills by leveraging their training & domain knowledge in English.
They should be able to render training in English in a structured, deliberate manner inorder to inform,
influence, and educate the learners.
Personal Attributes
Good Communication skills, Confidence, Patience, Adaptability, A passion for teaching, and the ability to
motivate and engage learners form diverse backgrounds.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### ASP/MEP/N1601: Personal Development  and Social Interaction
Description
This NOS focuses on enhancing an individuals self awareness, confidence and communication ability. It
aims to develop essential personal traits and social interaction skill required for effective engagement in
professional and social environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS builds self-awareness, improves communication and body language, develops creative thinking and mindfulness, enhances skills for group discussions, digital etiquette, media
understanding, and active listening with feedback techniques.

#### ASP/MEP/N1603: Training for Language Proficiency Tests
Description
This NOS focuses on building the language skills required to perform well in English proficiency tests like
the Aptis Test, through practice in grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, speaking, and listening.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS covers the development of integrated language skills, pronunciation, sentence construction, reading comprehension, and writing fluency, along with test strategies to improve
overall performance in language proficiency assessments.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Aptitude for Language Proficiency tests
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. • Demonstrate understanding of basic grammar concepts (parts of speech,
• sentence structure).
- PC2. Apply correct verb forms and tenses in spoken and written tasks.
- PC3. • Construct meaningful sentences by mastering sentence and word
• formation techniques.
- PC4. • Identify and correct common grammatical errors through practice
• exercises.
- PC5. Decode unfamiliar words using phonics strategies accurately
- PC6. • Demonstrate reading comprehension by identifying main ideas, details,
• and making inferences.
- PC7. Build well-structured sentences demonstrating cohesion and coherence.
- PC8. Recognize and produce correct phonological and phonemic sounds.
