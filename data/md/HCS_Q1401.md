#### HCS/Q1401: Stone Preparation Artisan
Brief Job Description
A stone preparation artisan expertly shapes and finishes stone materials for use in construction, sculpture,
or decorative projects. Their skilled craftsmanship ensures that each piece is precisely cut and polished,
highlighting the stone's natural beauty and durability.
Personal Attributes
A stone preparation artisan is meticulous and detail-oriented, with a deep appreciation for the natural
qualities of stone. They possess strong manual skills and a creative mindset, enabling them to transform
raw materials into stunning finished products.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N1517: Finishing Touches and Tool Maintenance
2. HCS/N8502: Entrepreneurship skills for Stone preparation artisan
3. HCS/N9952: Working together as a team
4. HCS/N9946: Managing workplace safety
5. HCS/N9937: Maintain Cleanliness
6. HCS/N9941: Understanding Business Operations
7. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Stone chiseller operation
A stone chiseller operates specialised tools to carve and shape stone, creating intricate designs and
structures for architectural or artistic purposes. Their expertise in manipulating various types of stone
ensures precision and quality in every project.
1. HCS/N1510: Receive raw slate stone and Work Instructions
2. HCS/N1402: Split slate stone into layers
Elective 2: Drill machine operation

#### HCS/N1517: Finishing Touches and Tool Maintenance
Description
In this unit, the artisan focuses on applying finishing touches by smoothing, polishing, and sealing the
stone surface for a refined and durable result. Tool maintenance involving regular sharpening, cleaning,
lubrication, and proper storage. Together, these practices ensure high-quality craftsmanship and the
longevity of both tools and finished works.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
finishing touches in stone preparation tool maintenance in stone preparation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Finishing Touches in Stone Preparation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. using fine abrasives (e.g., sandpaper, water stones) for the final smoothing of the stone
surface
- PC2. using polishing compounds such as diamond paste or polishing powder to achieve a glossy
finish
- PC3. use smaller chisels or fine engraving tools to add intricate details or texture
- PC4. apply a sealant to protect against water damage or staining
- PC5. check for any imperfections like cracks and make necessary repairs
Tool Maintenance in Stone Preparation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. regular sharpen chisels, hammers, and other cutting tools to maintain precision
- PC7. need lubrication for the moving parts of power tools to prevent wear and tear
- PC8. check for any signs of wear or damage (like bent chisels, loose hammerheads) and repair or
replace as needed
- PC9. clean tools after use to remove stone dust and debris, which can dull edges and clog
machinery
- PC10. store tools in a dry environment to prevent rust or corrosion
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. familiar with techniques such as polishing, honing, sandblasting, and bush hammering to achieve the desired texture and aesthetic for the stone
- KU2. different types of stones have unique physical characteristics such as hardness, grain, and
porosity

#### HCS/N8502: Entrepreneurship skills for Stone preparation artisan
Description
This unit aims to equip learners with the essential entrepreneurial skills required to succeed in the stone
preparation industry. Participants will gain a holistic understanding of both the technical and business
aspects of stone preparation, emphasizing the integration of innovative practices and sustainability.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
industry knowledge business management skills entrepreneurial mindset customer relationship management
sustainability practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Industry Knowledge
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. understanding different types of stones (e.g., granite, marble, limestone) and their properties
- PC2. proficiency in using stone-cutting and finishing equipment (e.g., saws, polishers)
- PC3. ability to assess stone quality and ensure products meet industry standards
- PC4. awareness of trends in stone usage, design preferences, and sustainability practices
Business Management Skills
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. skills in budgeting, pricing strategies, and cost control to ensure profitability
- PC6. ability to create business plans, set goals, and develop strategies for growth and
sustainability
Entrepreneurial Mindset
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. ability to develop new products or processes that meet customer needs or improve efficiency
- PC8. identifying potential risks and developing strategies to mitigate them
- PC9. being flexible in response to market changes, customer preferences, and technological
advancements
Customer Relationship Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. understanding how to effectively communicate the value of products to potential customers
- PC11. building relationships within the industry, including suppliers, customers, and other
stakeholders
Sustainability Practices
