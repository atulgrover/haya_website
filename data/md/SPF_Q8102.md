#### SPF/Q8102: Sports Bat and Racquet Craftsperson (Junior)
Brief Job Description
Sports bat and racquet craftsperson are involved in production, assembling, and finishing sports bats and
racquets such as cricket bats, baseball bats, table tennis, lawn tennis, badminton racquets, etc. Their tasks
range from operating machines to fine handcrafting of equipment
Personal Attributes
Individuals in this role should be creative, details-oriented, self-motivated and energetic. Individuals in this
role must demonstrate a very good listening and comprehension skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SPF/N8103: Prepare tools and materials to make bats and racquets
2. SPF/N8104: Craft bats and racquets
3. SPF/N8105: Carry out final steps of crafting the bats and racquets
4. SPF/N1169: Improve workplace resource usage
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### SPF/N8103: Prepare tools and materials to make bats and racquets
Description
This unit is about preparing equipment and materials for production of sports goods.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Select equipment for the production of bats and racquet
Prepare materials for the production bats and racquet
Maintain hygiene and sanitation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Select tools and machinery for crafting sports bats
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure the availability of all required equipment and tools for the production
- PC2. use the correct tools and equipment
- PC3. check that equipment is safe and set up in readiness for use
- PC4. select the correct component parts for the style being worked on
- PC5. conform to company quality standards
- PC6. report any damaged equipment to the responsible person
- PC7. follow company reporting procedures about defective tools and machines which affect work
and report risks/ problems
- PC8. sort and place work to assist the next stage of production and minimize the risk of damage
Prepare materials for the production
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. identify the properties of wood
- PC10. select type of wood based on the product to be manufactured
- PC11. select the wood that can be used in making of cricket bats, baseball bats, table tennis
racquets, etc.
- PC12. identify the process of grading wood
- PC13. select types of tools and equipment that should be used for shaping the wood
- PC14. ensure correct wood is selected before it is put in use
- PC15. check quality of wood and maintain its standards
- PC16. identify the process of drying of wood
Maintain hygiene and sanitation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC17. ensure personal hygiene
- PC18. ensure equipment, workshop, etc. are sanitized before and after the usage
- PC19. guide others about hygiene and sanitation workplace requirements

#### SPF/N8104: Craft bats and racquets
Description
This unit is about performing cutting operations using hand and machine.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Craft cricket bats
Craft table tennis racquets
Assemble lawn tennis racquets
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Craft cricket bats
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. calculate the number of components needed for production
- PC2. select appropriate willow to craft bats
- PC3. identify the properties of Kashmir willow, English willow, and White willow
- PC4. ensure the willows are free from moisture before using them
- PC5. cut or match the willow to create clefts
- PC6. use waxing technique to smoothen the edges
- PC7. grade clefts as per four levels based on straightness, width, blemishes
- PC8. ensure the standard size of the bats are 38inches and width as 4.25 inches
- PC9. use pressing machine to compress the willow to a solid compact state
- PC10. cut/slice the willow or the blade to create space for the handle
- PC11. assemble handle with the blades using wood adhesive
- PC12. use drawknife to shape the shoulders of the blades to the cricket bat
- PC13. ensure rounding off the tow and filling away unnecessary pieces on the bat
- PC14. use sanding process to smoothen the surface of the bat
- PC15. operate foot treadle to bind the handle of the bats with twine
- PC16. transfer bats for the quality check and next stage of production
Craft table tennis racquets
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC17. identify the types of laminated woods to be used to craft table tennis racquet
- PC18. dry the wood using sun drying techniques or an incubator
- PC19. measure the dryness of the wood using appropriate tool
- PC20. identify the properties of plywood cypress, wood balsa, soft ash, walnut, etc.
- PC21. select appropriate laminated wood to craft table tennis racquet
- PC22. slice the wood into core blades

#### SPF/N8105: Carry out final steps of crafting the bats and racquets
Description
This unit is about performing final assembly to produce sports goods
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assemble different components of bats
String and apply finishing touch to racquets
Perform quality and standards check of bats and racquets
Work effectively with others
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Assemble different components of bats
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the points of inspection of the assembling goods
- PC2. rasp the handles using appropriate tools
- PC3. ensure the handles are rasped to provide comfort and efficiency to the user
- PC4. bind the handle with traditional linen thread which is applied on a custom-made binding
lather
- PC5. operate foot treadle to bind the handle
- PC6. apply appropriate glue to the binding and leave it for drying
- PC7. polish the cricket bat once the bindings dry
- PC8. use compression machine to ensure rubber, foam and plywood stuck together on a table
tennis racquet
String and apply finishing touch to racquets
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. use stringing machine to force the strings through the holes using threader
- PC10. stress test the racquets to determine proper stiffness and hardness
- PC11. inspect weight of racquets, usually before and after stringing, to make sure they meet
specifications
- PC12. check the balance of the racquets to ensure it is not too heavy at the head or at the handle
- PC13. wound the grip smoothly ensuring no nicks or scratches
- PC14. ensure the length-wise strings are threaded through forcefully first
- PC15. use stringing machine to adjust the tension of the strings
- PC16. ensure cross strings are threaded through afterward and the tension is adjusted
- PC17. wrap handle in double sided tape and apply vinyl grip tap
Perform final quality and standards checks of bats and racquets
