#### HCS/Q5206: Namda  Craft Artisan
Brief Job Description
In making a patterned Namda, the craftsmen need to felt in the design into the Namda fibre itself. This
involves a dual process of felting. First, a pattern is laid on the grass or jute mat which is then surfaced
with wool spread evenly with the help of a broom locally called manzyen. Then the same process of
sprinkling soap water and rolling of the mat is again carried out. On untying, it reveals a beautiful Namda
drawn over with colorful patterns.
Personal Attributes
A Namda Craft Maker should have good eyesight, hand-eye coordination, motor skills and vision. The
person should have basic mathematical skills, particularly making calculations and measurement. He/she
should be patient and tenacious for long hours of work. He/she should be imaginatinative, creative and
analytic.

#### HCS/N5209: Carry out the process involved in making Cut Work Namda
Description
This unit discuss in details about the Cut Work Namda is a type of Namda where designs are created by
cutting out shapes from felted wool. These cut-out pieces are then either layered on top of the base wool
or stitched to create patterns.
Scope

preparing the base felt cutting and creating the cut work design dyeing the wool and layering the pattern
sprinkling soap solution and felting together

Preparing the Base Felt

layering, felting, and drying wool)
Cutting and creating the cut work design
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC2. transfer the design onto the wool felt base using a fabric marker or chalk
- PC3. carefully cut out the areas of the design that will be removed by using sharp blade and scissor
Dyeing the wool and layering the pattern
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. dye the felted wool by using natural dyes

out

using hand rolling or machine pressing.

#### HCS/N9030: Carry out the process of making Namda products
Description
This unit focuses on the traditional craft of making Namda products, specifically cushion covers and dining
mats. Participants will learn the complete process, from selecting and preparing wool to designing, felting,
and finishing the products. Emphasizing both creativity and technical skills, this unit aims to preserve
cultural heritage while equipping learners with practical abilities in textile arts. The hands-on approach will
enhance understanding of material properties, design principles, and the importance of craftsmanship.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Process of making Namda Cushion Covers
Process of Making Namda Dining Mats
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Process of Making Namda Cushion Covers
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select high-quality wool in various colors for design and texture.
- PC2. use a sturdy cotton or felt fabric as the base for the cushion cover.
- PC3. draw unique patterns or motifs for the cushion cover, drawing inspiration from nature or
traditional designs
- PC4. choose colors that complement each other and align with the intended aesthetic
- PC5. cut wool into strips or pieces according to the design
- PC6. dye the wool strips using natural dyes to achieve vibrant colors
- PC7. cut the base fabric to the desired size of the cushion cover
- PC8. sew the edges of the base fabric to prevent fraying
- PC9. lay wool strips in a layered fashion on the base fabric, ensuring they cover the entire area
- PC10. use moisture and pressure (rolling or beating) to mat the wool fibers together, creating a
cohesive felt layer
- PC11. trim excess wool and shape the cover as needed
- PC12. add zippers or buttons for easy removal and washing
Process of Making Namda Dining Mats
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. choose durable wool and cotton fabric for the dining mats
- PC14. gather natural dyes for the coloring wool
- PC15. choose geometric or floral patterns that can be repeated across the mat
- PC16. use a strong base fabric, such as canvas or felt, to support the wool
- PC17. cut the base fabric into desired sizes for dining mats
- PC18. lay wool strips on the base fabric, ensuring an even distribution
