#### SGJ/Q2601: Solar Enterprise Assistant Manager
Brief Job Description
According to the Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency Limited (IREDA), India is endowed with
abundant solar energy capable of producing 5,000 trillion kilowatts (kW) of clean energy. Moreover, India
gets 300 sunny days a year in most parts of the country and solar insulation of 4-7 kWh per square meter
per day. Strengthen domestic manufacturing of various technology components like solar cells, modules,
batteries to generate indirect employment opportunities in these allied sectors. This course would help
them to support industry players for implementation of projects across the country. To enhance their
knowledge in the field of Solar Energy Technologies and its applications. The participants would be able to
learn the knowledge on theory and practical session for quality aspect as qualitative and quantitative
measurement. Course will cover both Theoretical as well as Practical sessions which help participants to
understand the importance of solar energy applications.
Personal Attributes
This unit tells about Solar Enterprise

#### SGJ/N4066: Introduction to Renewable (solar)energy, climate change,
tools used in solar energy, Electromagnetism
Description
This unit tells about Introduction to Renewable (solar)energy, climate change, tools used in solar energy,
Electromagnetism
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Providing overview of Renewable energy and current information on renewable energy and related technologies.
Explain Concept of Climate Change and about Solar Energy and its impacts on Climate Change .
Learn about the use of tools and common myths about PPEs.
Concepts of Electromagnetism and benefit from electromagnetic principles in the conversion of sunlight into heat energy.

energy.

#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. understanding the principles of solar energy, including how sunlight is converted into electricity using photovoltaic cells, is fundamental to grasping the purpose and function of the
components in a solar PV system.
- KU2. knowledge of the different types of photovoltaic cells (e.g., monocrystalline, polycrystalline,
thin-film) and how they are assembled into solar panels/modules is essential.
- KU3. understanding the types of inverters (string, micro, central), their efficiency, and their role in
system performance is important.
- KU4. inverters are crucial for converting the direct current (DC) electricity generated by solar
panels into alternating current (AC) electricity that can be used by household appliances and the electric grid.
- KU5. understanding the electrical wiring, connectors, junction boxes, combiner boxes, and other
electrical components is necessary for designing and installing a safe and efficient solar PV system.
- KU6. knowledge of local, national, and international regulations and safety standards related to
solar PV systems is important for compliance and ensuring the safety and reliability of the system.
- KU7. understanding the principles of system design, including site assessment, sizing, shading analysis, and orientation, is important for designing an efficient and effective solar PV system.
- KU8. knowledge of installation techniques, best practices, and quality assurance is also essential.
- KU9. knowledge of the economic and environmental benefits of solar PV systems, including cost savings, energy independence, and reduced carbon emissions, is important for decision-
making and advocacy.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. solar PV systems are complex, so it is important to be able to read and understand technical
drawings and specifications.
- GS2. understanding how solar cells work and how they convert sunlight into electricity.
- GS3. staying informed about the latest advancements in solar PV technology, such as bifacial panels, smart inverters, and energy storage solutions, is important for staying competitive
and offering innovative solutions to customers.
- GS4. knowledge of direct current (DC) and alternating current (AC) electricity, voltage, amperage,
resistance, and power.
- GS5. solar panels are often installed on roofs, so workers need to be able to climb ladders and work
at heights.
- GS6. ability to work safety protocols such as wearing fall protection, using electrical safety equipment, and being aware of potential hazards.
- GS7. solar PV systems are complex, so it is important to be able to read and understand technical
drawings and specifications.

| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |

| Organisational Context | Organisational context includes the way the organisation is structured and how it operates, including the extent of operative knowledge managers have of their relevant areas of responsibility. |
| Technical Knowledge | Technical knowledge is the specific knowledge needed to accomplish specific designated responsibilities. |
| Core Skills/ Generic Skills (GS) | Core skills or Generic Skills (GS) are a group of skills that are the key to learning and working in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment. In the context of the OS, these include communication related skills that are applicable to most job roles. |
| Electives | Electives are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as contributive to specialization in a job role. There may be multiple electives within a QP for each specialized job role. Trainees must select at least one elective for the successful completion of a QP with Electives. |
| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
