#### HCS/Q5407: Finisher - Carpets
Brief Job Description
A carpet finisher is responsible for the final touches on carpets, including trimming, binding, and inspecting
for quality, ensuring a polished and durable product that meets design specifications and customer
expectations.
Personal Attributes
A carpet finisher should possess strong attention to detail, excellent hand-eye coordination, and a
commitment to quality, along with the ability to work independently and efficiently to meet production
deadlines.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N5416: Understanding of Basic Activities involved in final finishing of carpet
2. HCS/N5417: Entrepreneurship skills for Carpet finisher
3. HCS/N9901: Coordinate with colleagues and work as a team
4. HCS/N9913: Maintain health, safety and security at workplace

#### HCS/N5416: Understanding of Basic Activities involved in final finishing of
carpet
Description
This OS unit is about the understanding of basic activities involved in finishing of the carpet.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
application of third backing cloth and tape to strengthen and decorate the edges ensure 100% compliance and adherence to quality standards
coordinate with other team members
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Application of Third backing cloth and tape to strengthen third backing cloth
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. assemble the necessary raw material in appropriate quantity
- PC2. applies third backing cloth at the back of the tufted carpet
- PC3. ensure that third backing cloth is properly stretched from all ends
- PC4. ensure that third backing cloth sticks to latex which is applied on the back side of the
carpet., from all ends
- PC5. ensure that third backing cloth is not torn from anywhere
- PC6. ensure the cloth is of prescribed quality to ensure strength and durability
- PC7. use backing cloth of different colors as per the buyers requirements
- PC8. ensures the edges are adequate and cleanly latex and fasten to prevent from fraying
- PC9. fix the strip from all ends properly
- PC10. ensure strip covers all the four ends on the third backing cloth
- PC11. cut the ends properly for proper finishing
- PC12. set aside the raw material for further usage
- PC13. return the raw materials back to store on time
- PC14. ensure third backing and carpet is free from any stains
Ensure quality and productivity standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC15. visually examine the raw materials
- PC16. achieve targets as set by the supervisors
- PC17. ensure no quality flaws as this is the final stage in tufted carpet manufacturing
- PC18. inform in advance in case of any anticipated faults
- PC19. ensure availability of raw materials and coordinate with stores on inventory update
Coordination with other team members

#### HCS/N5417: Entrepreneurship skills for Carpet finisher
Description
This unit is specializes in performing the final stages of carpet production, ensuring quality, finishing, and
preparing the product for sale. The role requires a combination of technical expertise, artistic skills, and
entrepreneurial capabilities to manage the business aspects of carpet finishing.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Business Planning and Strategy
Financial Management
Quality Assurance and Innovation
Marketing and Sales Strategy
Legal and Regulatory Compliance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Business Planning and Strategy
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Develop a detailed business plan, outlining objectives, market analysis, product offerings,
target customers, pricing strategy, and operational goals.
- PC2. Create short-term and long-term strategies for business growth, including market expansion
and product diversification.
- PC3. Identify and exploit market opportunities for custom-designed or niche carpets, including
eco-friendly or luxury carpets.
- PC4. Develop contingency plans and maintain financial reserves to manage potential risks and
operational challenges.
Financial Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Set up effective budgeting and financial control systems to monitor production costs,
overheads, and revenue.
- PC6. Establish pricing strategies that balance affordability, quality, and profitability, taking into
account production costs and market competition.
Quality Assurance and Innovation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Ensure that the carpet finishing process maintains high-quality standards by paying attention
to design precision, material selection, and final detailing.
- PC8. Innovate in finishing techniques to differentiate products from competitors and appeal to
evolving consumer tastes.
Marketing and Sales Strategy:
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

#### HCS/N9913: Maintain health, safety and security at workplace
Description
This unit provides Performance Criteria, Knowledge & Understanding and Skills & Abilities required to
comply with health, safety and security requirements at the workplace and covers procedures to prevent,
control and minimise risk to self and others.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Comply with health, safety and security requirements at work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Comply with health and safety related instructions applicable to the workplace.
- PC2. Use and maintain personal protective equipment as per protocol.
- PC3. Carry out own activities in line with approved guidelines and procedures.
- PC4. Maintain a healthy lifestyle and guard against dependency on intoxicants.
- PC5. Follow environment management system related procedures.
- PC6. Store materials and tools in line with manufacturers and organisational requirements.

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.

each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
