#### SPF/Q1124: Golf Instructor
Brief Job Description
Golf instructors are responsible for teaching people how to play golf. They may also be responsible for
developing practice plans, providing feedback on proper form and technique, and helping learners improve
their game over time.
Personal Attributes
Individuals in this role should have good communication and comprehension skills. They should have the
ability to motivate learners. They should be hard working, creative, self-motivated, and energetic. The role
requires them to have a good eyesight and a good fitness level.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SPF/N1176: Plan golf coaching sessions
2. SPF/N1177: Organise the golf coaching environment
3. SPF/N1178: Conduct effective golf coaching sessions
4. SPF/N1179: Evaluate golf coaching
5. SPF/N1169: Improve workplace resource usage
6. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

#### SPF/N1176: Plan golf coaching sessions
Description
This unit is about planning for golf coaching sessions.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Collect and analyse information about participants
Select and plan activities for the golf coaching session
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Collect and analyse information about participants
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. collect relevant information using appropriate methods of initial assessment
- PC2. undertake performance analysis of participants
- PC3. analyse the information and identify participants’ needs and implications for planning the
coaching sessions
- PC4. identify the skills of golf to be developed in participants
- PC5. identify physical, psychological and social factors which may affect the acquisition of the
skills of golf
Select and plan activities for the golf coaching session
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. access and interpret the key rules and etiquette requirements of golf
- PC7. identify the type of golf facility and the resources needed for sessions
- PC8. brief participant on the rules, equipment, types of golf course and other relevant information
about the game of golf
- PC9. identify and agree goals for coaching sessions with the participant
- PC10. ensure session goals are consistent with the overall aims of the coaching programme
- PC11. identify activities for each session that will motivate the participant and achieve the planned
goals
- PC12. plan for fun and enjoyment in the sessions
- PC13. plan for a balance of golf instruction, activity and discussion within the sessions
- PC14. select activities to improve the skills of golf - putting, chipping, pitching, bunker play, full
swing, and on course situations
- PC15. identify appropriate teaching methods and coaching styles to develop the skills of golf
- PC16. carry out a risk assessment for planned activities
- PC17. identify the information which should be collected to evaluate sessions

#### SPF/N1177: Organise the golf coaching environment
Description
This unit is about preparing the golf coaching environment for safe and effective learning.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare resources and facilities for golf coaching sessions
Establish and maintain a safe environment for golf coaching
Work effectively with others
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare resources and facilities for golf coaching sessions
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. organise the golf facility and resources in a way that is appropriate for planned coaching
sessions
- PC2. ensure the coaching environment is safe, appropriate and conducive to learning for all
participants
- PC3. ensure other people involved in the session have the information they need
- PC4. assist with the development of the facility in relation to improving the coaching environment
Establish and maintain a safe environment for golf coaching
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. ensure participants and others have the necessary information about health and safety
requirements for the session and relevant emergency procedures
- PC6. ensure all golf equipment and facilities meet health, safety and other legal requirements
- PC7. identify possible hazards and assess the likelihood of causing harm
- PC8. implement planned procedures for managing risk during the sessions
- PC9. follow organisational health and safety requirements
Work effectively with others
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. interact (verbal, non-verbal and written) in a gender, disability, and culturally sensitive
manner
- PC11. promote a safe and interactive environment
- PC12. identify and report inappropriate behaviour (e.g. sexual harassment) to appropriate authority

#### SPF/N1178: Conduct  effective golf coaching sessions
Description
This unit is about coaching different techniques of golf to participants
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare participants for golf coaching
Analyse the skills and techniques of golf
Conduct drills, activities and/or games to teach or develop the skills of golf
Conclude golf coaching sessions
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare participants for golf coaching
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure proper dress code required for the game of golf - participant’s and self
- PC2. identify and assess participant’s need and characteristics
- PC3. provide useful information in regards to club selection pertaining the approach shot
- PC4. identify the difference between male clubs, female clubs and senior citizen clubs
- PC5. identify the pace of the game
Analyse the skills of golf
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. provide participants with the information required to play the shot
- PC7. identify swing fundamentals in terms of their effect on the ball flight laws, (along with swing
principles and preferences)
- PC8. identify and document golf swing movement from a bio-mechanical perspective and linked to
ball flight laws
- PC9. identify cause and effect relationships and document their relationship to error identification
and correction
- PC10. identify the golf swing and its purpose and break the swing into components, of pre-swing
(set-up), backswing and forward swing fundamentals
- PC11. identify observable body movements for each stage of the swing in priority of importance
- PC12. identify pre-swing and in-swing fundamentals
- PC13. identify variances in swing mechanics for an extensive variety of shot types
- PC14. evaluate a variety of techniques of the skills of golf
- PC15. use appropriate technological aids to assist the assessment of technique e.g. video
assessment, computer enhanced assessment systems, etc.
- PC16. detect patterns of errors, by use of round analysis charts and statistical results
Conduct drills, activities to teach or develop participant’s skill
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |

| Organisational Context | Organisational context includes the way the organisation is structured and how it operates, including the extent of operative knowledge managers have of their relevant areas of responsibility. |
| Technical Knowledge | Technical knowledge is the specific knowledge needed to accomplish specific designated responsibilities. |
| Core Skills/ Generic Skills (GS) | Core skills or Generic Skills (GS) are a group of skills that are the key to learning and working in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment. In the context of the OS, these include communication related skills that are applicable to most job roles. |
| Electives | Electives are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as contributive to specialization in a job role. There may be multiple electives within a QP for each specialized job role. Trainees must select at least one elective for the successful completion of a QP with Electives. |
| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
