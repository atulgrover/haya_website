#### MEP/Q0204: Receptionist
Brief Job Description
The Receptionist qualification will equip the individual with advanced skills in AI-powered tools and modern
technologies essential for dynamic office environments. It will provide comprehensive training in digital
communication, visitor management, and administrative tasks, enabling the individual to efficiently
manage responsibilities while leveraging the latest technological advancements. The qualification will
ensure the individual is adept at using AI and digital tools to enhance productivity, streamline operations,
and deliver superior service.
Personal Attributes
The individual must have excellent communication and interpersonal skills, a customer-centric approach,
strong organizational abilities, and adaptability to learn and use AI-powered tools. They must demonstrate
attention to detail, problem-solving abilities, professionalism, and a commitment to maintaining
confidentiality and data privacy.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MEP/N0213: Receive visitors and direct them as per requirement
2. MEP/N0252: Manage office correspondence and inventory
3. MEP/N0253: Event coordination and office services

6. MEP/N9914: Communicating with clients, visitors and colleagues effectively
7. MEP/N9912: Apply principles of professional practice at the workplace
8. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### MEP/N0213: Receive visitors and direct them as per requirement
Description
This unit focuses on effectively managing visitor interactions by welcoming, identifying their needs, and
directing them appropriately, ensuring a professional and seamless visitor experience.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Visitor management
Handling communications
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Visitor Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Greet visitors warmly and address their inquiries using automated response systems
- PC2. Use Visitor Management System (VMS for streamlined visitor check-in and check-out.
- PC3. Ensure visitor badges/ cards are issued and collected using the visitor management system.
- PC4. Verify the purpose of the appointment, duration, and any special requirements.
- PC5. Provide visitors with necessary information and assistance during their visit.
- PC6. Assist visitors in filling out forms, if any, accurately and efficiently by providing clear
instructions and necessary assistance
- PC7. Record visitor complaints and escalate them using CRM (Customer Relationship
Management) tools
- PC8. Maintain a log of visitor details and ensure data privacy compliance.
- PC9. Manage appointment request received via phone call, email, in-person request, or online
booking system.
- PC10. Schedule and manage visitor appointments via calendar management apps/ software
- PC11. Ensure that there are no conflicting appointments.
- PC12. Send automated confirmations and reminders to relevant stakeholders.
- PC13. Identify the appropriate department and personnel to be contacted to address the
requirement of the visitor
- PC14. Inform department and personnel on the nature of business of the visitor
- PC15. Provide access to the visitor to that area after obtaining necessary approvals, as per
organisational process
- PC16. Direct visitors to the appropriate areas or individuals in the office.
- PC17. Monitor and manage visitor flow to ensure a smooth and orderly process.
- PC18. Handle any changes or cancellations of appointments promptly and update relevant
stakeholders.
- PC19. Coordinate with security personnel to ensure visitor safety and compliance with protocols.

#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC20. Utilize telecommunication systems to answer and forward calls promptly.
- PC21. Greet the customer and speak clearly and slowly
- PC22. Listen carefully when collecting information from the caller
- PC23. Use effective and assertive questions to clarify caller requests
- PC24. Give clear and concise information to caller in response to questions or requests in line with

- PC25. Use purposeful questions and answers to control the length of the conversation
- PC26. Take permission from caller to put their call on hold for necessary action during a call
- PC27. Summarize the outcome of the call and any actions that are required to be taken
- PC28. Complete any follow up actions agreed during the call
- PC29. Take a clear message for passing on to the appropriate staff
- PC30. Maintain a log of all incoming and outgoing calls using telecommunication software.
- PC31. Provide callers with accurate information and resolve inquiries using a knowledge base.
- PC32. Ensure all telecommunications equipment are functioning properly and report any issues.
- PC33. Respond to emails promptly using email management tools as used by the organisation
- PC34. Handle queries received through the official company website using live chat tools.
- PC35. Communicate effectively with visitors in person, providing assistance and information as
needed.
- PC36. Maintain records of all communications, including phone calls, emails, and website queries,
for future reference and follow-up.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand the use of Visitor Management Systems (VMS) and appointment scheduling tools.
- KU2. Be aware of data protection and privacy compliance when handling visitor information.
- KU3. Know how to use CRM tools, telecommunication systems, and email management software.
- KU4. Understand methods for handling complaints and escalating issues appropriately.
- KU5. Be familiar with security protocols for ensuring visitor safety and access compliance.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Communicate clearly and professionally with visitors and callers.
- GS2. Provide excellent customer service and address inquiries effectively.
- GS3. Manage appointments efficiently and respond promptly to changes.
- GS4. Resolve unforeseen issues and problems effectively.
- GS5. Maintain accurate logs and records of visitor details and communications.

#### MEP/N0252: Manage  office correspondence and inventory
Description
This unit involves handling incoming and outgoing communication, maintaining accurate records, and
managing office supplies and inventory to ensure smooth administrative operations and efficient resource
utilization.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Handling office correspondence
Managing office inventory and expenses
Handling travel and booking arrangements
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Handling office correspondence
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Manage and organize office correspondence using document management tools.
- PC2. Use automated/ manual systems as used by the organisation for sorting and distributing
mails/ packages.
- PC3. Ensure receipt of incoming mail by completing the necessary procedure
- PC4. Segregate incoming mail and dispose of unwanted ‘junk’ mail after taking necessary
approvals
- PC5. Organise for the distribution or handover of the incoming mail or packages
- PC6. Track mail movement and confirm receipt
- PC7. Follow the correct procedures of escalation when there are problems with incoming mail
- PC8. Collect and sort outgoing mail or packages as per urgency, local/outstation, weight and type
or packet, etc.
- PC9. Ensure timely delivery and dispatch of all office correspondence.
- PC10. Arrange for courier service to collect outgoing mail or packages where requested
- PC11. Maintain a record of all incoming and outgoing correspondence.
- PC12. Use secure methods for handling confidential documents and information.
- PC13. Handle transcription, printing, photocopying, and faxing tasks efficiently using tools.
Managing office inventory and expenses
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. Utilize tools to maintain an accurate record of office supplies.
- PC15. Implement automated reordering alerts to ensure continuous availability of office supplies.
- PC16. Record and manage petty office expenses related to reception with tools
- PC17. Download detailed expense reports for record keeping.
- PC18. Conduct regular audits of reception/ office inventory and reconcile with records using
relevant tools.

Handling travel and booking arrangements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

- PC24. Utilize automated systems to send notifications well in advance to relevant stakeholders and
prior to the scheduled time.
- PC25. Use online booking platforms to arrange travel and accommodation.
- PC26. Book conference halls or venues for any external official meeting
- PC27. Ensure all travel and lodging arrangements comply with company policies and budget.
- PC28. Maintain a log of travel bookings and related expenses.
- PC29. Provide travelers with itineraries and necessary travel documentation.
- PC30. Handle any changes or cancellations of travel arrangements and update relevant parties.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Know company policies for correspondence, inventory, and travel.
- KU2. Understand tools for document management and inventory tracking.
- KU3. Know how to handle mail securely and manage confidential documents.
- KU4. Be aware of courier services and mail classification methods.
- KU5. Understand procedures for travel bookings and expense reporting.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Use digital tools for correspondence and inventory tasks.
- GS2. Organize and manage time effectively.
- GS3. Communicate clearly with stakeholders.
- GS4. Solve problems related to mail, inventory, or bookings.
- GS5. Pay attention to details in records and reports.

#### MEP/N0253: Event coordination and office services
Description
This unit focuses on planning, organizing, and executing office events and meetings, ensuring seamless
coordination of logistics, resources, and communication, while managing office services to maintain
operational efficiency and a productive work environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Supporting office events
Managing meeting room availability
Overseeing basic office services and safety and hygiene standards
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Supporting office events
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Coordinate internal and external events using relevant tools
- PC2. Coordinate processes like recruitment, onboarding, and termination to support the HR team
- PC3. Coordinate with event vendors and service providers to ensure smooth event execution.
- PC4. Manage event logistics, including venue setup, catering, and equipment.
- PC5. Collect feedback from event participants and generate reports for future improvements.
Managing meeting room availability
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Maintain a booking log or calendar for conference/meeting rooms.
- PC7. Ensure that meeting rooms are prepared and equipped for scheduled meetings.
- PC8. Coordinate with staff to resolve any conflicts or overlapping bookings.
- PC9. Provide support during meetings, such as setting up equipment and providing refreshments
if needed.
- PC10. Monitor the usage of meeting rooms to optimize availability and efficiency.
Overseeing basic office services and safety and hygiene standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Communicate effectively with the cleaning and maintenance staff using tools
- PC12. Schedule routine maintenance and cleaning to ensure the office environment remains clean
and operational.
- PC13. Ensure the reception area and common areas are clean and presentable at all times.
- PC14. Use simple checklists to conduct daily checks
- PC15. Report any safety or hygiene issues to the appropriate personnel or service providers
promptly.
- PC16. Keep a log of cleaning schedules and maintenance activities using relevant tools.
