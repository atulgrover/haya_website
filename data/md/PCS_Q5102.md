#### PCS/Q5102: Powder  Coater
Brief Job Description
The Powder Coater coats metal surfaces like home appliances, auto parts, control panels, furniture, heavy
machinery, hospital equipment, office furniture, etc. The individual at work assesses the component to be
coated, understands colour, powder chemistry and finish required, prepares the part for powder application and coats it using appropriate tools and equipment to achieve the desired finish as per
company standards and requirement.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to undertake physical labour, have good eye-sight with no colour blindness
and work in all types of weather conditions.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PCS/N5109: Plan, prepare, pre-treat and coat the component with powder coating
2. PCS/N9901: Co-ordinate with colleagues and customers
3. PCS/N9902: Maintain standards of product/ service quality
4. PCS/N9903: Maintain OH&S standards and follow environmental norms

#### PCS/N5109: Plan, prepare, pre-treat and coat the component with powder
coating
Description
This OS unit is planning, preparing, pre-treating and powder coating the components/ parts.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan the work
Prepare and pre-treat the components
Apply powder coating
Maintain the equipment and jigs/ hangers
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Plan the work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. receive work instructions, review and plan to meet the quality requirements
- PC2. inspect the work area, assess hazards and risks and apply controls
- PC3. check the work site for adequate ventilation and implement steps to prevent the formation of
powder cloud, explosions and fires
- PC4. check that the equipment and jigs/ hangers are suitable and ready for use
Prepare and pre-treat the components
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. inspect the component condition visually
- PC6. prepare and pre-treat the components as per SOP and instructions received
- PC7. prepare components for re-coating by removing the defects and pre-treatment as per SOP
- PC8. mask the areas not required to be coated with masking tapes, paper, plugs
- PC9. implement measures to maintain coating area dust free
- PC10. prepare the powder coating material for application by fluidizing the powder as per SOP
- PC11. ensure that the powder is fluidized throughout the application process
- PC12. set up the application and powder recovery system as per SOP and conduct pre-operational
checks before actual coating
Apply powder coating
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. set up the recommended application parameters for the type of powder coating and
application equipment
- PC14. operate the application equipment as recommended by the equipment manufacturer and
powder coating supplier
- PC15. apply the coating to achieve the desired dry film thickness (DFT), colour, opacity and finish

Description
This OS unit is about communicating effectively with superiors, colleagues and customers, for achieving a
smooth workflow.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Coordinate with supervisors and colleagues
Address customer complaints and grievances
Follow inclusive practices at work

Coordinate with supervisors and colleagues
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. collect job order from the supervisor
- PC2. establish the work output requirements, quality standards, delivery schedule, targets,
performance indicators and incentive policies
- PC3. ensure prompt and quality work as per agreed timeline
- PC4. report challenges at work and reasons for delay to the supervisor
- PC5. incorporate feedback received from the supervisor and colleagues
- PC6. provide colleagues with adequate and accurate information and knowledge

improve

- PC11. provide customers with adequate information on potential costs and hazards

organisational policies

have a hazard-free environment and avoid downtime. It is also about following government laid norms for
waste treatment and disposal.
Scope

Prevent accidents at work place and damage to environment
Follow standard health, safety, and environmental policies and procedures
Use safety tools and personal protective equipment (PPE)
Achieve health, safety and environmental standards

Prevent accidents at workplace and damage to environment

pollution

- PC10. ensure that material and water conservation processes are closely monitored at work

accidents from recurring

#### Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Practical Skills for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for practical skill for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
