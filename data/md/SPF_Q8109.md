#### SPF/Q8109: Gym  Equipment  Service Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The Gym Equipment Service Supervisor is responsible for overseeing the maintenance and repair of gym
equipment, ensuring that it is in optimal condition for use by gym members. This role involves supervising
a team of technicians, coordinating equipment inspections, scheduling repairs, and ensuring efficient
service delivery.
Personal Attributes
Individuals in this role should have an excellent interpersonal skill, communication skill, and the ability to
work with and lead team members. Their excellent communication skills enable effective coordination with
team members and stakeholders, while adaptability allows them to handle dynamic situations with ease.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SPF/N8123: Prepare for the service of gym equipment
2. SPF/N8124: Supervise the service of gym equipment
3. SPF/N8125: Manage service technicians and clients
4. SPF/N1169: Improve workplace resource usage
5. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

#### SPF/N8123: Prepare for the service of gym equipment
Description
This unit is about planning for the service of gym equipment
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan for gym equipment service
Manage vendor and inventory
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Plan for gym equipment service
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. develop and implement a preventive maintenance schedule for gym equipment
- PC2. create a plan for routine inspections and identify maintenance needs
- PC3. schedule and coordinate equipment repairs with minimal disruption
- PC4. prioritize service tasks based on urgency and member needs
- PC5. collaborate with the service team to establish efficient work schedules
- PC6. assess equipment performance data and service records for improvement
- PC7. conduct meetings with the team to address challenges and provide guidance
- PC8. coordinate warranty services and procure necessary parts from vendors
- PC9. analyze data for equipment upgrades or replacements
- PC10. optimize the service planning process
Manage vendor and inventory
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. establish relationships with vendors and suppliers for equipment parts and tools
- PC12. negotiate pricing and contracts for equipment repairs and replacements
- PC13. coordinate with vendors to ensure timely delivery of necessary materials
- PC14. manage inventory levels of spare parts and tools for efficient equipment repairs
- PC15. conduct regular inventory audits to track stock and identify replenishment needs
- PC16. maintain accurate records of inventory, purchases, and service history
- PC17. develop and implement inventory control procedures to minimize waste and loss
- PC18. collaborate with vendors to stay updated on new equipment models and technology
- PC19. evaluate vendor performance and seek alternative suppliers when necessary
- PC20. monitor equipment warranty periods and coordinate warranty services with vendors
- PC21. optimize procurement processes to streamline vendor management and inventory control
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

#### SPF/N8124: Supervise the service of gym equipment
Description
This unit is about the management of gym equipment service processes, systems and methodologies
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Resolve, diagnose service-related issues
Supervise service quality
Recommend improvement for service process
Work effectively with others
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Resolve, diagnose service related issues
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. assist service technicians in troubleshooting and resolving equipment service-related issues
- PC2. provide technical guidance and expertise to ensure efficient and effective service delivery
- PC3. collaborate with the service team to allocate resources and prioritize tasks
- PC4. coordinate with other departments, such as facilities or operations, to ensure smooth service
activities
- PC5. utilize problem-solving skills to identify root causes of equipment issues
- PC6. implement appropriate solutions to resolve equipment malfunctions or failures
- PC7. diagnose complex service problems and recommend corrective actions
- PC8. collaborate with manufacturers or suppliers to obtain technical support for complex issues
Supervise service quality
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. ensure service tasks are performed to high-quality standards and within specified timelines
- PC10. conduct inspections and quality checks to verify the effectiveness of service activities such
as equipment
- PC11. functionality, wear and tear, proper alignment, calibration, loose or damaged parts
- PC12. monitor and maintain compliance with safety protocols and regulations
- PC13. provide feedback and guidance to service technicians to improve their work quality
- PC14. implement initiatives to enhance service outcomes
Recommend improvement for service process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC15. evaluate service processes to identify areas for enhancement
- PC16. implement improvements to streamline service operations and increase efficiency
- PC17. collaborate with the team to develop and implement preventive maintenance programs
Work effectively with others

#### SPF/N8125: Manage  service technicians and clients
Description
This unit is about managing service technicians and clients.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist with staffing and training of technicians
Perform administrative duties
Mange client relationship
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist with staffing and training of technicians
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. assess the skills and knowledge gaps of technicians and provide training and development
opportunities
- PC2. provide training on specific gym equipment models, their functionality, and service
procedures
- PC3. conduct hands-on training sessions to familiarize technicians with tools, diagnostic
equipment, and software used in gym equipment service
- PC4. offer ongoing training and development opportunities to enhance technicians' technical skills
and knowledge
- PC5. provide training on proper documentation practices to ensure accurate recording of service
activities, repairs, and maintenance
- PC6. mentor and coach technicians to enhance their performance and professional growth
- PC7. provide constructive feedback and conduct performance evaluation
- PC8. evaluate the effectiveness of training programs and make adjustments to improve their
quality and impact
- PC9. provide effective leadership and guidance to a team of technicians
- PC10. delegate tasks, set priorities, and manage workloads
- PC11. motivate and inspire technicians to achieve high-quality work and meet service goals
Perform administrative duties
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. approve leave requests (e.g., vacations, personal leave, family leave)
- PC13. prepare timesheets
- PC14. communicate with clients or stakeholders regarding service updates, scheduling, and any
administrative matters
- PC15. assist in budgeting and financial tracking related to equipment service and maintenance
Manage client relationship

energy, and trash, in workplace operations.

conservation procedures for materials power conservation methods waste management/recycling procedures

#### DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)
Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Career Development & Goal Setting
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Employability Skills
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. understand the significance of employability skills in meeting the current job market
requirement and future of work
- PC2. identify and explore learning and employability relevant portals
- PC3. research about the different industries, job market trends, latest skills required and the
available opportunities
Constitutional values – Citizenship
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. recognize the significance of constitutional values, including civic rights and duties,
citizenship, responsibility towards society etc. and personal values and ethics such as honesty, integrity, caring and respecting others, etc.
- PC5. follow environmentally sustainable practices
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. recognize the significance of 21st Century Skills for employment

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
#### Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will also lay
down the proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each PC.
- PC2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on the knowledge bank of questions created by the
SSC.
- PC3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- PC4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for the theory part for each
candidate at each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
- PC5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
- PC6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score a minimum of 70% of aggregate
marks to successfully clear the assessment.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

| Electives | Electives are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as contributive to specialization in a job role. There may be multiple electives within a QP for each specialized job role. Trainees must select at least one elective for the successful completion of a QP with Electives. |
| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
