RSC/Q8003: Jr. Designer - Plastic Products
Brief Job Description
The Jr. Designer - Plastic Products supports the design and development of plastic components by assisting
in conceptualization, CAD modeling, and prototyping. The role involves creating and modifying 2D/3D
drawings, collaborating with senior designers and engineers, and participating in product validation and
testing. The designer ensures functional, manufacturable, and customer-oriented product designs in
alignment with industry standards.
Personal Attributes
The individual at work should be creative and innovative with an eye to detail.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N8003: Assist in product design & development
- 2. RSC/N4409: Create and Modify 2D/3D Drawings using CAD Software
- 3. RSC/N4410: Assist in Prototyping and Product Testing
- 4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol

RSC/N8003: Assist in product design & development
Description
This unit covers the process of supporting the design and development of plastic products, from
understanding design briefs to preparing basic concept sketches and assisting in feasibility analysis.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand design brief and specifications
Conduct product benchmarking and research
Create design concepts
Assist in creating functional mock-ups
Support design feasibility assessment
Maintain project records
Maintain records and follow-up
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand design brief and specifications
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify product requirements based on client/supervisor inputs
PC2. identify the functionality and aesthetic needs of the product
PC3. assess the plastic material constraints related to the design
PC4. consider environmental, economic, and regulatory factors influencing design
Conduct research and benchmarking
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. collect reference data and analyze similar existing products
PC6. study functional, aesthetic, and ergonomic parameters
PC7. document benchmark metrics and identify innovation opportunities
Prepare basic concept sketches
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. generate freehand sketches or concept diagrams
PC9. propose variations and alternatives in initial design
PC10. incorporate supervisor feedback into revised concepts
Assist in product detailing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. support the preparation of dimensional sketches
PC12. help identify tolerances and draft angles based on process constraints
PC13. suggest material thickness based on load and stress considerations
Support feasibility analysis

RSC/N4409: Create and Modify 2D/3D Drawings using CAD Software
Description
This unit covers creating, editing, and managing 2D/3D CAD models of plastic parts and assemblies,
including dimensioning, tolerancing, and rendering using standard CAD software tools.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare 2D Drawings
Model 3D Parts
Assemble Parts Virtually
Modify CAD Models
Prepare files for sharing and documentation
Ensure Quality and Compliance of Drawings
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare 2D Drawings
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. create orthographic, sectional, and auxiliary views
PC2. add dimensional tolerances, surface finish, and material details
PC3. follow BIS/ISO standards while creating drawing templates
PC4. generate exploded views and part lists for assemblies
Model 3D Parts
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. use solid modeling tools to create parts using extrude, revolve, and loft features
PC6. apply fillets, chamfers, ribs, and cuts as needed for strength and design intent
PC7. use parametric constraints to ensure model accuracy
Assemble parts virtually
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. apply assembly mates, joints, and alignments
PC9. check and resolve part interferences and motion constraints
PC10. animate assemblies to visualize movement and alignment
Modify CAD Models
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. update parts and assemblies based on feedback
PC12. revise dimensions, features, or constraints as required
PC13. maintain version control of design files
Prepare files for sharing and documentation

RSC/N4410: Assist in Prototyping and Product Testing
Description
This unit deals with assisting in the creation of prototypes using 3D printing, CNC, or other methods and
participating in the testing and evaluation of plastic products.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare Files and Materials for Prototyping
Assist in Prototype Fabrication and Validation
Assist in Functional Testing
Support Iteration and Documentation
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare Files and Materials for Prototyping
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. convert CAD models into STL/STEP formats
PC2. verify model geometry and orientation for prototyping
PC3. coordinate with service providers or in-house labs
Assist in Prototype Fabrication and Validation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. support 3D printing, CNC machining, or vacuum casting processes
PC5. inspect physical output for surface quality and dimensional accuracy
PC6. perform basic post-processing like trimming or assembly
PC7. participate in fitment trials or ergonomic checks
PC8. report observed deviations and provide input for design iteration
Assist in Functional Testing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. measure strength, stability, or deformation under stress
PC10. collect and organize testing data
PC11. identify visible or functional defects in prototypes
Support Iteration and Documentation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. incorporate design changes based on test results
PC13. track design revisions and create test summaries
PC14. follow documentation formats for prototype evaluation
PC15. assist in preparing technical review reports
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
