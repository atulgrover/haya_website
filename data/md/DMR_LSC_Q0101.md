#### DMR/LSC/Q0101: Junior Engineer -Traction (Metro Rail)
Brief Job Description
The Junior Engineer - Traction oversees the installation, operation, and maintenance of Power Supply
Installation (PSI) and SCADA systems, ensuring reliable power supply within the traction network. This role
includes coordinating Overhead Electrification (OHE) maintenance, coordinating with technical teams, and
ensuring adherence to safety protocols for efficient traction system operations.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and effective communication to
oversee complex traction systems. A proactive approach, technical aptitude, and the ability to lead teams
under high-pressure situations are essential for smooth operations and maintenance.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. DMR/LSC/N9901: Implement metro operations training and safety procedures
2. DMR/LSC/N0101: Oversee Power Supply Installation (PSI) and maintenance in the traction system
3. DMR/LSC/N0102: Oversee power system protection and control
4. DMR/LSC/N0103: Oversee the SCADA system operations and maintenance
5. DMR/LSC/N0104: Oversee the maintenance of Overhead Electrification (OHE) systems
6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### DMR/LSC/N0101: Oversee  Power Supply Installation (PSI) and
maintenance  in the traction system
Description
This unit is about managing power supply arrangements, including metering systems, energy meters, and
adherence to regulatory guidelines. It covers site assessments, workforce planning, and safe operational
practices for power blocks and feed extensions. Key areas include earthing fundamentals, TPC roles,
interlocking principles, cable jointing, and familiarization with PSI tools and plants, aligned with Indian
electricity rules and tariff regulations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Power supply arrangement
Metering systems
Energy meters
DERC, HERC, and UPPCL guidelines
Site visit to RSS for supply system arrangement
Types of power block and Permit to Work (PTW)
Workforce planning and staff coordination
Fundamentals, types, requirements, and benefits of earthing
Equipment earthing and mock drill for power block availing and cancellation
Role and duties and importance of TPC
Feed extension safety, emergency feeding conditions, and interlocking principles
Tools and Plants used in PSI
Power cables, cable jointing procedures, and testing
PSI store and project site visit for tools and cables familiarization
Indian electricity rules and acts related to tariff

downtimes

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
#### Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will also lay
down the proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each PC.
- PC2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on a knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
- PC3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- PC4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for the theory part for each
candidate at each examination/training centre (as per the assessment criteria below).
- PC5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practicals for every student at
each examination/training centre based on this criterion.
- PC6. To pass the Qualification Pack, every trainee should score a minimum of 60% of aggregate marks to
successfully clear the assessment.
- PC7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
