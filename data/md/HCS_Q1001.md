#### HCS/Q1001: Quality Check Technician - Ceramics
Brief Job Description
A Quality Check Technician in ceramics is responsible for inspecting and testing ceramic products to
ensure they meet established quality standards.
Personal Attributes
The Quality Check Technician in ceramics is meticulous and detail-oriented, with a strong analytical
mindset to identify defects and inconsistencies. They possess excellent observational skills and a
commitment to quality, ensuring that every product meets the highest standards before reaching the
customer.

#### HCS/N1007: Quality parameters for Raw Materials in Ceramics
Manufacturing
Description
This unit focuses on understanding, inspecting, and ensuring compliance with quality standards for raw
materials and processes in ceramics manufacturing. It provides a comprehensive approach to the inspection and quality control of raw materials and production processes, ensuring the final ceramic
products meet industry standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
know the quality standards inspection of raw materials quality inspection during production
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Know the Quality Standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. learn about the international and national standards such as ISO 9001, ASTM, or BIS for
ceramics manufacturing
- PC2. discuss the specific quality requirements for raw materials like clay, feldspar, quartz, and
additives
- PC3. identify the raw materials meet the functional and aesthetic requirements of the final
product (e.g., tiles, pottery, sanitary ware)
Inspection of Raw Materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. verify the source and specifications of raw materials using supplier documentation
- PC5. check for consistency in color, texture, and form
- PC6. identify visible impurities like stones, debris, or organic matter
- PC7. measure using moisture meters to ensure optimal levels for processing
- PC8. conduct a plasticity test (e.g., rolling test) to evaluate workability
- PC9. use sieve analysis or laser diffraction to ensure uniformity in particle size distribution
- PC10. identify the presence of oxides and unwanted compounds through techniques like X-ray
Fluorescence (XRF)
- PC11. use a moisture meter to ensure raw materials like clay have optimal moisture levels for
processing
- PC12. verify supplier certifications and conduct batch inspections to ensure consistency
Quality Inspection during Production
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. perform small-scale tests of the material mix before large-scale production

#### HCS/N1008: Quality Inspection and Problem Resolution in Ceramics
Description
This unit involves inspecting the finished ceramics, identifying any problems or defects, resolving issues
effectively, and documenting the entire process for accountability and continuous improvement.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
inspection of final product problem identification and resolution documentation and reporting
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Inspection of Final Product
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. technician performs detailed visual checks to detect surface defects like cracks, chips,
discoloration, glaze imperfections, or inconsistencies in finish
- PC2. check that the product conforms to design specifications, including size, shape, and
alignment
- PC3. use of tools like calipers, micrometers, or gauges to verify the dimensions of the product
- PC4. test the product for strength (resistance to breaking or chipping) and other functional
properties (e.g., water resistance, fireproofing for specific types of ceramics)
- PC5. verify the weight of products to ensure that there are no significant variations in batch
production that may indicate quality issues
- PC6. measure the residual moisture content in the product to avoid issues like cracking during the
drying or firing stages
- PC7. identify the consistency of the ceramic’s thickness to prevent uneven firing or brittleness
- PC8. check for any defects like streaks, bubbles, or uneven coverage in the glaze
Problem Identification and Resolution
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. identify the nature of common ceramic defects, such as cracks, warping, pinholes, uneven
glaze, or surface imperfections
- PC10. identify the root cause of these issues, whether they are due to raw material defects,
improper firing, moisture imbalance, or handling errors
- PC11. categorize defects as cosmetic or functional to determine the severity of the issue and the
appropriate response
- PC12. investigate the underlying cause of defects (e.g., insufficient drying time, improper kiln
settings, poor raw material quality)
- PC13. provide guidance to production staff or operators on correct procedures to avoid recurring
issues, like controlling moisture levels or ensuring correct glazing techniques

- KU1. understand the physical, aesthetic, and functional properties required for the final ceramic
product. This includes factors like color, shape, texture, smoothness, and uniformity
- KU2. familiarity with customer specifications and industry standards for finished products (e.g.,
resistance to wear, thermal conductivity, and strength)
- KU3. using measurement tools like micrometers, calipers, or automated machines to ensure the final product conforms to the required dimensions
- KU4. recognize the signs of issues from firing (e.g., uneven firing leading to warped shapes or
discoloration)
- KU5. differences between the desired and actual size of the product due to issues during molding,
shaping, or firing
- KU6. technician needs to understand the root causes of defects, which may arise from raw material

#### HCS/N8520: Entrepreneurship Skills for Ceramics Quality Check
Technician
Description
In the role of a Quality Check Technician (Ceramics), entrepreneurial skills are essential for identifying
opportunities for improvement, driving process efficiencies, and contributing to the overall success of the
business. A technician's ability to manage quality control with an entrepreneurial mindset helps in building
a competitive advantage for the organization, ensuring consistent product quality, and fostering innovation
within the ceramics industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understanding of business and market needs problem identification and continuous improvement cost management and resource optimization
effective communication and stakeholder management leadership and teamwork risk management and decision-making
entrepreneurial thinking in quality control process legal and regulatory awareness
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding of Business and Market Needs
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. discuss the role of a Quality Check Technician as both a quality control expert and an
integral part of the business's entrepreneurial ecosystem
- PC2. aware of the broader market trends in ceramics, customer expectations, and the demand for
specific ceramic products (e.g., tiles, sanitary ware, or decorative items)
- PC3. recognize that the quality of products directly impacts customer satisfaction and brand
reputation
- PC4. identify how quality control aligns with customer preferences, and how defects or
inconsistencies affect market competitiveness
- PC5. identify the ways to differentiate products by ensuring superior quality through meticulous
inspection and problem resolution
Problem Identification and Continuous Improvement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. identify and analyze problems within the production process (e.g., defects in raw materials,
processing, or firing) and understand their potential impact on the business
- PC7. being proactive in adopting new technologies, equipment, or methods for inspection and
quality assurance, which may lead to better efficiency or more accurate defect detection
Cost Management and Resource Optimization

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
