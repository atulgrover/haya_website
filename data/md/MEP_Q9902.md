#### MEP/Q9902: Advance Course  in Digital Sales and Marketing
Brief Job Description
The Advance Course in Digital Sales and Marketing equips learners with comprehensive skills in digital
marketing strategies, including SEO, SEM, email marketing, social media marketing, and the application of
generative AI. This course also covers e-commerce marketing, branding, and analytics, preparing
individuals to effectively plan and execute digital marketing campaigns and drive online sales.
Personal Attributes
Advanced Digital Marketing Skills E-commerce and Branding Expertise Generative AI Application Data-
Driven Decision Making
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MEP/N0415: Digital Marketing Planning and Display Advertising
2. MEP/N0416: E-commerce Marketing
3. MEP/N0418: Introduction to Affiliate Marketing
4. MEP/N0420: Introduction to Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI)
5. MEP/N0421: Marketing Analytics
6. MEP/N0422: Entrepreneurship Development & Innovation (EDI)
7. MEP/N0423: OJT Mandatory - Digital Sales and Marketing
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Elective 1: Branding and Marketing Communications
This elective is for the trainees who wants to take up branding and marketing communications as subject
of interest. The trainee has to be choose either Elective 1 or Elective 2 while pursuing this course.
1. MEP/N0417: Branding and Marketing Communications
Elective 2: Elective 2: Basics of Advertising
This elective is for the trainees who want to take up advertising as a subject of interest. The trainee has to
choose either Elective 1 or Elective 2 while pursuing this course.

#### MEP/N0415: Digital Marketing Planning and Display Advertising
Description
This unit details planning for digital marketing,market research, tools for display advertising platforms,
analysing advertising performance and future trends.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Digital Marketing Planning
Market Research and Analysis
Digital Marketing Channels
Display Advertising Fundamentals
Display Advertising Campaign Planning
Display Advertising Platforms and Tools
Measuring and Analysing Display Advertising Performance
Future Trends in Display Advertising and Digital Marketing
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Digital Marketing Planning
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the key steps involved in the digital marketing planning process
- PC2. Explain the significance of strategic planning in achieving digital marketing objectives
- PC3. Describe the importance of understanding the target audience and market segmentation in
digital marketing
- PC4. Develop SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound) objectives for
a digital marketing campaign based on a given scenario
- PC5. Apply digital marketing planning concepts to real-world case studies
- PC6. Analyse the effectiveness of different digital marketing strategies in achieving business goals
- PC7. Discuss the strengths and weaknesses of various digital marketing planning approaches
- PC8. Design a digital marketing plan for a specific product or service, incorporating elements of
strategic planning
Market Research and Analysis
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Explain various methods and techniques used in market research for digital marketing
- PC10. Explain the importance of competitor analysis in shaping digital marketing plan
- PC11. Describe the role of market analysis tools such as SWOT (Strength, Weakness, Opportunity
and Threat) and PESTEL (Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, and Legal) in digital marketing planning
- PC12. Conduct market research using online tools and databases to gather relevant data
- PC13. Apply competitor analysis frameworks to assess the competitive landscape in a given market
- PC14. Analyse market trends and consumer behaviour patterns to identify opportunities

Introduction to E-commerce Marketing

E-commerce Website Optimization
E-commerce Content Marketing

business objectives

strategies

campaigns

marketing

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
