#### RSC/Q7103: Technician: Battery (Alkaline) Maintenance, Processing and
Recycling
Brief Job Description
The individual is involved in performing the production and maintenance of alkaline batteries as per the
organizational standards and procedures. He/she also responsible for performing process of recycling of
alkaline batteries.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be attentive, focused, disciplined and able to plan and priorities tasks effectively. The
individual must possess manual dexterity and capable of performing routine works accurately.

#### RSC/N7103: Carry out processing of alkaline batteries
Description
This unit is about performing various tasks for manufacturing alkaline batteries by following organizational
standards and procedures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for manufacturing process
Perform production of alkaline batteries
Perform post-production activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for production process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. obtain the information and instructions from supervisor about the production schedule
- PC2. ensure that required quantity of raw material i.e., nickel plated steel sheet (container),
manganese dioxide powder and graphite/coal dust (cathode), zinc powder (anode), potassium hydroxide solution (electrolyte), gelling agent, brass pins, paper or fibrous fabric
(separator), sealing plug, negative cap, sealant etc. are available as per the production plan and job requirements
- PC3. ensure that raw material is as per the required and approved quality
- PC4. prepare the assembly line for the alkaline battery manufacturing process
- PC5. check that assembly line is functioning properly before starting the manufacturing process
- PC6. adhere to all safety norms (such as wearing protective gloves and shoes, etc.) in accordance
with the international/ national and organisational standards
Perform production of alkaline batteries
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. start the assembly line and perform battery manufacturing process by following SOP
- PC8. set the mixers and their parameters as per the SOP
- PC9. fill the manganese dioxide powder, graphite/coal dust, electrode, sealing agent etc. in mixers
to prepare cathode tablets/rings/powder as per the organisational standards and procedures
- PC10. fill zinc powder with potassium hydroxide solution and gelling agent in mixers to prepare
anode gel as per the organisational standards and procedures
- PC11. ensure that cathode and anode are free from any foreign articles, dust, other metals
- PC12. feed the nickel-plated steel sheet through the cutters, coaters and rollers for performing
cutting, trimming and carbon coating operation and preparing the steel container of the battery by following SOP
- PC13. move the steel cans to next section of assembly line for inserting and pressing the required
quantity of cathode tablets/rings/powder in the steel can by following SOP

- PC14. move the steel containers through to next section where a paper strip is rolled into a small
tube and sealed on the bottom, formed separator and then it is inserted into the middle of the cathode rings
- PC15. add electrolyte inside the battery container after the separator as per SOP
- PC16. move the steel container to next section, where the zinc gel (anode) is injected into the inner

- PC17. move the steel container for last section where a brass nail, plastic seal ring and metal

- PC18. follow shutdown procedures in case of any emergency/ normal process as per the SOP
- PC19. report any issues faced (if any) during the production process to the supervisor
Perform post-production activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC20. collect the manufactured batteries into bins or plastic trays as per organisational guidelines
and procedures
- PC21. check the batteries for any defect, charge leakage, appropriate voltage and amperage
ratings by following SOP
- PC22. segregate the defective and correct batteries into different bins as per organisational
guidelines and procedures
- PC23. carry out labelling and packaging of batteries as per organisational guidelines and
procedures
- PC24. send the final packaged batteries to the store room as per SOP
- PC25. clean the equipment properly as per SOP after completion of work
- PC26. dispose waste material into the disposal area in accordance with the company's policies and
environmental regulations
- PC27. update and maintain the log book with details of production output, material usage, lot
number, batch number etc. to facilitate the traceability

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. organisational and government standards, guidelines and procedures related to alkaline battery production work
- KU2. safety requirements to be followed during production work
- KU3. SOP recommended by the manufacturer for using machines for alkaline battery production
- KU4. raw materials required for production of an alkaline battery
- KU5. different parts of an alkaline battery
- KU6. procedure of an alkaline battery production
- KU7. safe handling of different parts of an alkaline battery
- KU8. various types of defects in an alkaline battery
- KU9. quality check procedure of an alkaline battery
- KU10. labelling and packaging requirements of an alkaline battery

#### RSC/N7104: Carry out recycling of alkaline batteries
Description
This unit is about performing various tasks for recycling of alkaline batteries.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for recycling process
Perform recycling of alkaline batteries
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for recycling process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. obtain the information and instructions from supervisor and manufacturer manual related to
battery type, specifications and recycling procedure
- PC2. arrange tools and equipment such as battery recycler or a hammermill required for
performing alkaline battery recycling process
- PC3. check the recycler for proper functioning or any defects before starting the recycling process
- PC4. ensure that batteries are discharged completely before starting the recycling process
- PC5. adhere to all safety norms (such as wearing protective gloves and shoes, etc.) in accordance
with the international/ national and organisational standards
Perform recycling of alkaline batteries
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. fill the batteries into recycler/ hammermill by following SOP
- PC7. start the recycler/ hammermill and perform battery recycling process by following SOP
- PC8. dismantle the different parts (i.e., steel, plastic and zinc/manganese) of alkaline batteries
and then segregate them properly into recycler/ hammermill by following SOP
- PC9. collect all the parts separately into different bins/boxes as per organisational guidelines and
procedures
- PC10. send the segregated bins/boxes to their designated places for next process as per
organisational guidelines and procedures
- PC11. prepare and maintain all the records and documents related to recycling work as per
organisational guidelines
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. organisational and government standards, guidelines and procedures related to alkaline battery recycling work
- KU2. safety requirements to be followed during recycling work

#### RSC/N7105: Carry out maintenance of alkaline batteries
Description
This unit is about performing various tasks for routine and preventive maintenance of alkaline batteries.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for maintenance work
Perform maintenance activities of the battery
Perform post-maintenance activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for maintenance work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. determine the routine maintenance requirements of alkaline batteries by interpreting
manufacturer manual, maintenance checklist, work instructions, etc.
- PC2. track battery usage to assess the need of battery service
- PC3. arrange tools, consumables, spare parts, etc. required for performing repair and
maintenance
- PC4. clear the work area thoroughly to ensure no unwanted materials are present before starting
the work
- PC5. ensure that battery is not connected with the equipment or any power source before starting
maintenance work
- PC6. adhere to all safety norms (such as wearing protective gloves and shoes, etc.) in accordance
with the international/ national and organisational standards
Perform maintenance activities of the battery
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. perform basic health check-up of battery as specified in the manufacturer manual
- PC8. check that the battery is clean, dry, free of dirt and grime
- PC9. inspect the battery case, terminals, screws, clamps and cables for breakage, damage or
loose connections
- PC10. ensure that battery terminals, screws, clamps and cables clean, tight and free of corrosion
- PC11. check battery for any sign of electrolyte leakage, overheating or overcharging
- PC12. check that battery has sufficient electrolyte covering the battery plates in case of
maintainable battery
- PC13. use distilled or demineralised water for top up the electrolyte in battery, do not over fill as
the fluid levels will rise when the battery is fully charged and may overflow
- PC14. check the battery's state of charge by checking State of Charge Indicator on top of the
battery that will give an on-the-spot diagnosis of the battery condition

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |
| --- | :---: |
| Organisational Context | Organisational context includes the way the organisation is structured and how it operates, including the extent of operative knowledge managers have of their relevant areas of responsibility. |
| Technical Knowledge | Technical knowledge is the specific knowledge needed to accomplish specific designated responsibilities. |
| Core Skills/ Generic Skills (GS) | Core skills or Generic Skills (GS) are a group of skills that are the key to learning and working in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment. In the context of the OS, these include communication related skills that are applicable to most job roles. |
| Electives | Electives are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as contributive to specialization in a job role. There may be multiple electives within a QP for each specialized job role. Trainees must select at least one elective for the successful completion of a QP with Electives. |
| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
