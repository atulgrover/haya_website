CUTM/AGR/Q0101: Seed Production Supervisor
Brief Job Description
To acquaint an individual with conventional and modern breeding methods and its viable application in
varietal development, to impart comprehensive knowledge to get seed production in vegetable and cereal
crops with adequate practical training, to apprise with the legislative provisions and processes as well as
the mechanisms of quality control and seed certification.
Personal Attributes
A Seed Production Supervisor should be detail-oriented, methodical, and knowledgeable in agronomy and
crop science. They must possess strong organizational and observation skills, along with the ability to
manage field teams and maintain quality standards. Adaptability, record-keeping accuracy, and
communication skills are essential for ensuring efficient seed production and certification.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CUTM/AGR/N0101: Identification of Seed production method, set-up storage and monitor for
Vegetable types
- 2. CUTM/AGR/N0102: Identification of Seed production method, set-up storage and monitor for Cereal
Crops
- 3. CUTM/AGR/N0103: Identification of Seed production method, set-up storage and monitor for Fruit
types
- 4. CUTM/AGR/N0104: Seed Certification and Seed Quality Testing
- 5. CUTM/VSQ/N7801: Ensure organizational health, safety, and security
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

CUTM/AGR/N0101: Identification of Seed production method, set-up
storage and monitor for Vegetable types
Description
This NOS outlines the process of identifying appropriate seed production methods for various vegetable
crops, establishing seed plots, and monitoring crop growth and seed development. It includes post-harvest
activities such as seed extraction, cleaning, grading, drying, and storage, ensuring the production of high-
quality, viable, and disease-free vegetable seeds.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers seed production planning, isolation, pest control, harvesting, drying, cleaning,
grading, and storage of vegetable seeds. It also includes monitoring germination and ensuring
adherence to seed quality standards for various vegetable crops.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Floral biology in self- and cross-pollinated species.
PC2. Emasculation and hybridization techniques in different crops like Paddy, Tomato, Brinjal etc.
PC3. Selection methods in segregating populations and evaluation of breeding material.
PC4. Growing different genotypes of a crop using appropriate experimental design.
PC5. Harvesting of F1 seeds and check the hybrid (F1) fidelity using molecular markers.
PC6. DNA isolation, DNA purity and quantification tests.
PC7. DNA finger printing using PCR-based DNA markers.
PC8. Gel electrophoresis and scoring and data analysis for tagging and phylogenetic relationship.
PC9. Estimation of heterosis, inbreeding depression and heritability.
PC10. Learning techniques in hybrid seed production using male-sterility in field crops.
PC11. Male sterile line creation in crop plants, problems in creation of CGMS system, ways of
overcoming them.
PC12. Use of standard MAS procedures- screening crops for resistant to different biotic and abiotic
stress traits.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Different seed production techniques for vegetable crops (open-pollinated, hybrid, etc.).
KU2. Climatic and soil requirements for vegetable seed production.
KU3. Isolation distance and pollination control practices.
KU4. Standards for genetic and physical purity in vegetables.

CUTM/AGR/N0102: Identification of Seed production method, set-up
storage and monitor for Cereal Crops
Description
This NOS focuses on identifying appropriate seed production techniques for cereal crops and managing
their field-to-storage lifecycle. It includes monitoring purity, crop health, harvesting, processing, and
storage to ensure high-quality cereal seed production.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Includes field-level planning and execution of seed production for cereals, post-harvest seed
processing, and storage. Emphasis is placed on maintaining seed purity, documenting inspections,
and ensuring storage conditions support long-term viability and certification compliance.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Selection of suitable areas/locations and seed/planting material for quality seed production.
PC2. Determination of planting ratios and practice on seed production of varieties for hybrid seed
production of cereals and vegetables.
PC3. Study of floral biology of vegetable crops like Tomato, Brinjal and Okra.
PC4. Seed extraction methods and their effect on quality of vegetables.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Seed production techniques for cereals like wheat, rice, and maize.
KU2. Land preparation and sowing methods for cereals.
KU3. Genetic and physical purity standards for cereal seeds.
KU4. Foundation and certified seed production processes.
KU5. Crop monitoring during vegetative and reproductive stages.
KU6. Pest and disease management in cereal seed production.
KU7. Maturity indicators and harvesting methods.
KU8. Post-harvest processing techniques for cereal seeds.
KU9. Seed storage conditions (temperature, moisture).
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:

CUTM/AGR/N0103: Identification of Seed production method, set-up
storage and monitor for Fruit types
Description
This NOS covers the techniques and processes involved in seed production of fruit crops, including seed
extraction, post-harvest processing, quality control, and safe storage. It enables the production of viable,
healthy, and true-to-type fruit seeds for propagation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS includes identification of fruit-specific seed production methods, setting up extraction and
drying processes, and ensuring seed health during storage. It includes handling post-harvest
challenges and monitoring quality to meet industry or certification standards.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Selection of suitable areas/locations and seed/planting material for quality seed production.
PC2. Determination of planting ratios and practice on seed production of varieties for hybrid Fruits.
PC3. Study of floral biology of fruit crops like Mango, Gua, and Avagoda.
PC4. Seed extraction methods and their effect on quality of Fruits.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Reproductive biology and pollination of fruit crops.
KU2. Seed extraction techniques for fleshy and dry fruits.
KU3. Isolation distance and plant spacing norms.
KU4. Climatic requirements and seasonality.
KU5. Harvesting indicators for different fruit seeds.
KU6. Extraction, fermentation, and drying procedures.
KU7. Pest, disease, and physiological disorder management.
KU8. Packaging and labeling of fruit seeds.
KU9. Seed treatment and storage techniques.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Select suitable fruit varieties for seed production.

CUTM/AGR/N0104: Seed Certification and Seed Quality Testing
Description
This NOS details the certification process and seed quality testing required for legal and marketable seed
distribution. It ensures the seed meets the prescribed quality standards through systematic inspection,
sampling, and documentation procedures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Includes managing certification requirements from field to lab, ensuring seeds undergo proper
testing and quality checks, and maintaining documentation for traceability and regulatory
compliance. The role supports both seed producers and certification authorities in ensuring market-
ready seeds.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identification of weed and other crop seeds as per specific crops.
PC2. Field inspection at different stages of a crop.
PC3. Inspection and sampling at harvesting/threshing.
PC4. Inspection and sampling at processing and after processing.
PC5. Specifications for tags and labels to be used for certification purpose.
PC6. Varietal Identification through Grow Out Test.
PC7. Varietal Identification through Electrophoresis.
PC8. Molecular test for pre- and post-harvest quality control.
PC9. Biochemical test for pre- and post-harvest quality control.
PC10. Learn the seed sampling methods.
PC11. Physical Purity test.
PC12. Moisture test
PC13. Germination and Vigour test
PC14. Viability test
PC15. Control measures for stored grain disease and pest.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Seed certification stages (field inspection to final tagging).
KU2. Eligibility criteria and seed class definitions.
KU3. Testing protocols (germination, purity, moisture, seed health).
KU4. Field inspection procedures and documentation.

KU6. Tagging, labeling, and bagging as per standards.

to create a safe and productive work environment.
Scope

personal protective equipment (PPE), emergency response, fire safety, first aid, and waste
management. It also includes implementing security measures, ensuring workplace hygiene, and
promoting a safety-conscious culture to prevent accidents and maintain a secure working
environment.
