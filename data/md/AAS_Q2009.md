#### AAS/Q2009: Aircraft Maintenance Technician
Brief Job Description
The Aircraft Maintenance Technician performs inspection, servicing, troubleshooting, and repair of aircraft
systems, structures, and components following regulatory and manufacturer standards. The role includes
line maintenance, airframe/powerplant checks, avionics diagnostics, and metal/composite structural
repairs using approved procedures. The individual ensures safety, documentation accuracy, and airworthiness while coordinating effectively within a team environment.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be safety-focused, disciplined, and detail-oriented with strong technical accuracy and
problem-solving ability. They should demonstrate professionalism, teamwork, clear communication, and
the ability to work under operational pressure. Adaptability, willingness to learn new technologies, and
maintaining high-quality standards are essential for this role
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### AAS/N2025: Work Organization and Managements
Description
This NOS covers the ability to organize, plan, and carry out aircraft maintenance work safely, efficiently,
and in accordance with approved procedures. It includes compliance with manuals, safety standards, tool
usage, documentation practices, and maintaining airworthiness through disciplined work habits.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Application of approved procedures, manuals, and safety standards in all maintenance tasks.
Proper handling, use, and storage of tools, equipment, and materials.
Effective planning, workflow management, and maintenance of a clean, safe, and organized work environment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow Safety Standards and Approved Procedures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Follow the Company Maintenance Policy Manual (MPM) and relevant ATA chapters.
- PC2. Use approved manuals, manufacturer data, and regulatory documents.
- PC3. Comply with health, safety, and environmental legislation during maintenance tasks.
- PC4. Use PPE appropriately including safety footwear, gloves, respirators, and eye/ear protection.
- PC5. Apply electrostatic discharge (ESD) precautions when handling sensitive systems.
- PC6. Maintain tool control and ensure proper accountability before and after tasks.
Use Tools, Equipment, and Materials Safely
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Select correct hand tools, power tools, and equipment for required maintenance tasks.
- PC8. Clean, maintain, and store tools properly to prevent damage or unsafe operation.
- PC9. Handle materials safely including chemicals, lubricants, sealants, and composites.
- PC10. Store materials as per organizational and regulatory procedures.
- PC11. Identify unserviceable tools or equipment and report for repair or replacement.
Plan and Organize Work Effectively
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. Plan maintenance work to maximize efficiency and minimize delays.
- PC13. Prepare the work area and ensure availability of tools and materials prior to tasks.
- PC14. Maintain a clean, tidy, and FOD-free workspace at all times.
- PC15. Prioritize tasks based on aircraft schedule, safety requirements, and operational urgency.
- PC16. Recognize personal authority limits and escalate concerns appropriately.
Maintain Quality, Accuracy, and Airworthiness

#### AAS/N2026: Communication  & Interpersonal Skills
Description
This NOS covers the communication, teamwork, and interpersonal abilities required in an aircraft
maintenance environment. It includes accurate technical documentation, professional interactions,
teamwork, conflict resolution, and maintaining customer confidence while supporting safe and efficient
operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Accurate and clear communication with engineering teams, flight crew, and support departments.
Effective teamwork, collaboration, and conflict resolution in high-pressure aviation environments.
Professional behavior that supports safety, customer confidence, and organizational standards.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Communicate Accurately and Professionally
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Complete technical documentation accurately and legibly.
- PC2. Use corrects technical terminology in reports, logbooks, and defect entries.
- PC3. Communicate clearly with supervisors, engineers, and supporting staff.
- PC4. Provide accurate updates on task status, delays, or defects found during maintenance.
- PC5. Follow communication protocols during aircraft operations and safety-critical tasks.
Maintain Positive Interpersonal Relationships
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Establish and maintain productive working relationships with colleagues and other
departments.
- PC7. Show respect, professionalism, and a positive attitude in all interactions.
- PC8. Understand the roles and responsibilities of related departments (logistics, stores,
engineering).
- PC9. Support effective coordination during shift handovers and team briefings.
- PC10. Build customer confidence by demonstrating competence and disciplined behavior.
Demonstrate Effective Teamwork and Collaboration
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Work cooperatively within a team to complete tasks efficiently and safely.
- PC12. Follow teamwork principles including mutual support, shared responsibility, and clear
communication.
- PC13. Participate actively in team tasks, briefings, and aircraft turnaround operations.
- PC14. Identify team priorities and adjust work practices to support task completion.
- PC15. Promote team safety culture by encouraging proper procedures and hazard reporting.

#### AAS/N2027: Problem Solving, Innovation, & Creativity
Description
This NOS focuses on the ability to identify, analyze, and resolve technical problems in aircraft maintenance
using structured diagnostic approaches. It includes following updated manuals, preventing errors, applying
innovative thinking, and ensuring solutions meet airworthiness standards. The individual must demonstrate adaptability, continuous improvement, and accuracy in problem-solving.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identifying and resolving maintenance problems using approved diagnostic methods.
Applying updated manuals, data revisions, and technical references during troubleshooting.
Contributing innovative ideas and embracing new technologies within approved practices.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify and Analyze Problems
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify common maintenance problems such as leaks, electrical faults, corrosion, or system
malfunctions.
- PC2. Gather relevant information from manuals, task cards, and system history.
- PC3. Distinguish between normal system behavior and abnormal conditions.
- PC4. Follow step-by-step fault isolation procedures as per the FIM or troubleshooting guide.
- PC5. Recognize incorrect or conflicting information and report it.
Apply Diagnostic and Troubleshooting Techniques
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Use approved diagnostic tools and equipment to verify system performance.
- PC7. Interpret readings, measurements, and test results accurately.
- PC8. Follow the latest revision of the manufacturer’s maintenance manuals and documentation.
- PC9. Perform systematic checks to identify root causes of faults.
- PC10. Persist in resolving complex or recurring problems.
Prevent Errors and Ensure Work Quality
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Check and verify own work regularly to minimize rework.
- PC12. Support verification of co-workers’ tasks during team operations.
- PC13. Identify potential issues early and recommend preventive actions.
- PC14. Ensure all solutions meet international airworthiness standards.
- PC15. Document problem analysis and corrective actions clearly.
Encourage Innovation and Continuous Improvement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

#### AAS/N2028: Sustainable Practices
Description
This NOS focuses on adopting environmentally responsible and socially inclusive practices within aircraft
maintenance. It includes reducing waste, using eco-friendly materials, adopting digital documentation,
minimizing fuel and energy consumption, understanding emerging green technologies, and promoting
diversity and inclusivity in the workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Application of environmentally sustainable maintenance practices.
Use of digital systems and optimized ground operations to reduce emissions.
Promotion of inclusivity, diversity, and responsible workplace behavior.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow Environmental and Regulatory Standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Comply with environmental regulations and organizational sustainability policies.
- PC2. Use environmentally friendly materials wherever approved and applicable.
- PC3. Dispose of hazardous waste, chemicals, and consumables safely.
- PC4. Segregate recyclable items such as metals, plastics, and composite waste
- PC5. Minimize the use of non-recyclable or harmful materials during maintenance.
Reduce Waste and Promote Sustainable Use of Resources

#### Assessment Guidelines
1.Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2.The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3.Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4.Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5.Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at each
examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6.To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass % aggregate for the QP.
7.In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack

| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
