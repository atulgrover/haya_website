CUTM/AGR/Q1101: Advanced Farm Equipment Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The supervisor is responsible to supervise advanced farm machinery operation based on the standard
operating procedure, supervise GPS-guided machinery and automated systems. He also monitor crop
health and environmental conditions using sensors, adjust farming practices based on data analytics and
remote sensing and conduct routine maintenance and ensure equipment safety.
Personal Attributes
An Advanced Farm Equipment Supervisor should be technically skilled, safety-conscious, and detail-
oriented with strong problem-solving abilities. They must demonstrate leadership in managing teams,
adaptability to modern mechanized farming practices, and responsibility in equipment maintenance. Good
communication, planning, and decision-making skills, along with a commitment to efficiency,
sustainability, and precision farming, are essential.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CUTM/AGR/N1101: Supervise advanced machining seedbed preparation
- 2. CUTM/AGR/N1102: Supervise Advanced Machining Sowing/Transplanting

CUTM/AGR/N1101: Supervise advanced machining seedbed preparation
Description
The Supervise advanced machining seedbed preparation NOS under the Advanced Farm Equipment
Supervisor qualification (NSQF Level 5) focuses on equipping learners with the skills to manage precision
tillage operations using advanced technologies. Here trainees learn to handle, calibrate, and operate
precision tillage equipment, implement variable depth and width adjustments, and apply advanced soil
engagement techniques. The goal is to ensure uniform soil conditioning, optimal seedbed preparation, and
efficient field operations using GPS-guided systems and smart machinery. The module emphasizes safety,
calibration accuracy, and effective use of technology for modern, sustainable farming practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS enables supervisors to manage and execute advanced seedbed preparation using
precision tillage equipment. The scope includes calibrating and operating modern machinery,
implementing variable depth and width adjustments, and applying advanced soil engagement
techniques to achieve optimal soil tilth. It emphasizes uniform soil conditioning, soil health
enhancement, and maximizing crop yield. The role also includes ensuring operational safety and
troubleshooting equipment issues, aligning with the integration of smart agriculture practices and
sustainable land preparation.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Comprehensive Farm Machinery Operation and Crop Field Preparation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Field type for different crop farming operation.
PC2. Cropping season (Kharif and Rabi crops) & Knowledge on Climatic condition for farming.
PC3. Soil type; Different crops need different type of soil for growth.
PC4. Levelling of seed bed for sowing or transplanting by laser land levelers.
PC5. Skill on operation of primary tillage equipment (M.B Plough, Disc plough, Indigenous plough,
rotavator, Power tiller & Tractor).
PC6. Skill on operation of secondary tillage equipment (Cultivator, Ridger, Harrow).
PC7. Advanced farm machinery operation based on artificial intelligence.
PC8. Operation of driverless tractor, self-operated machinery.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. different soil types and their suitability for various crops. This includes knowledge of Kharif
and Rabi seasons, climate factors, and how they influence seedbed preparation.

CUTM/AGR/N1102: Supervise Advanced Machining Sowing/Transplanting
Description
This NOS focuses on supervising the operation, calibration, and maintenance of advanced sowing and
transplanting machinery such as precision planters, pneumatic seed drills, and transplanters. The
supervisor ensures optimal seed placement, depth, spacing, and integration of variable rate technology
(VRT) to maximize crop emergence, yield, and resource efficiency. It also includes managing IoT-enabled
equipment, troubleshooting technical issues, and performing pre/post-operational maintenance. The role
combines technical expertise with data-driven decision-making to enhance productivity, sustainability, and
precision in modern sowing and transplanting operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of this NOS includes supervising and operating advanced sowing and transplanting
machinery to ensure precision in seed placement and crop establishment. It encompasses managing
the calibration and maintenance of modern equipment such as precision planters, pneumatic seed
drills, and transplanters. The role also involves integrating variable rate technology (VRT), GPS
systems, and IoT-based machinery for enhanced accuracy and efficiency. The supervisor ensures
proper handling of automated systems to optimize planting density, reduce seed wastage, and
improve overall farm productivity while adhering to safety and maintenance protocols.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Operation and Maintenance of Advanced Sowing Machinery and Equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand the importance of preparing appropriate germinated seeds and seedlings for
sowing through advanced machinery/equipment.
PC2. Understand the working and operation of each component of advanced machinery.
PC3. Learn the operation of all the advanced sowing machineries i.e., Precision planters, multi crop
pneumatic seed drills, multi crop planters and advanced trans planters.
PC4. Learn to maintain the IOT based and advanced machinery before use, after use and during un
seasons by proper greasing and lubricating.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the significance of using appropriately germinated seeds and healthy seedlings suitable for
advanced sowing/transplanting machinery to ensure high germination and crop
establishment rates
KU2. detailed knowledge of the structure, function, and operation of components of advanced
sowing equipment like precision planters, pneumatic seed drills, and transplanting machines

CUTM/AGR/N1103: Supervise an Advanced Intercultural Equipment
Description
The Supervise an advanced intercultural equipment NOS equips learners to manage and operate smart
intercultural machinery such as GPS-guided sprayers and drone-based application systems. It focuses on
integrating IoT-enabled devices, ensuring equipment maintenance, calibrating sprayers, preparing spray
solutions, and following safety and environmental guidelines. This module trains supervisors to optimize
input application through Variable Rate Technology (VRT), improve water and nutrient management, and
ensure safe and precise intercultural operations in modern, data-driven agriculture.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of this NOS includes supervising the operation, maintenance, and safety of smart
intercultural farm equipment such as automated sprayers, drones, and IoT-integrated systems. The
role encompasses precision tasks like GPS-guided machinery navigation, variable rate technology
(VRT) utilization, drone battery management, and calibration of sprayers. It also involves ensuring
equipment safety, reporting workplace emergencies, and promoting sustainable practices like waste
disposal and PPE usage. This NOS equips the supervisor to optimize resource use, enhance
operational efficiency, and contribute to sustainable precision farming practices.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Operation, Maintenance, and Safety Practices for Smart Spraying Equipment and Drone-Based
Applications
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Connect and operate machines with mobile applications (Bluetooth or GSM) and IOTs.
PC2. To change the drone battery and perform post operation maintenance of drone.
PC3. To inspect the electrostatic unit of the sprayer and clean the nozzles for any blockages.
PC4. Inspect the delivery hose joints and clean the tank using clean water after removing the
nozzle as per the SOP.
PC5. Clean the strainers and delivery hoses using clean water.
PC6. Test the tank for leakage or lose hose connections.
PC7. To examine the by-pass and cut-off valve in engine-controlled sprayer for wear and
tear/damage.
PC8. To prepare the spray solution and mixture as per the guidelines.
PC9. To calibrate the sprayer and make necessary adjustments for field operation.
PC10. To check the nozzles and replace them after certain period of time.
PC11. Wear protective equipment and clothing such as face mask, respirators, hand gloves, boots
while field operation.
PC12. Report workplace emergencies/ accidents as per the organizational policy.
PC13. Dispose non-recyclable waste appropriately.
