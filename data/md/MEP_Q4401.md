#### MEP/Q4401: Career and Education Counsellor
Brief Job Description
A Career and Education Counsellor is responsible for providing guidance and support to students and job
seekers on career and educational selection by determining their interest and aptitude. Not only do they
give one on one support, but also arrange information sessions, orientation programs and other services
as required. They keep abreast with developments in the education and labor market to link candidates to
career and education opportunities in India and abroad. They also provide advice and information to
employer clients regarding employment issues and human resources.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be well-groomed, attentive, comfortable with multi-tasking, disciplined and attentive
to details. This person must have a positive attitude, be dependable and have the ability to work in a team.
The person should be capable of interacting with individuals from different age groups, educational
background and job strata.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### MEP/N4401: Manage  information for career and education counselling
Description
This unit details performance requirements for gathering information on career and education opportunities and demand. It also describes gathering information about job and education opportunities
in India and other countries/ abroad.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Gather market information on career and education in India/abroad
Organise data and information for effective use
Promote the counselling service
Material and energy conservation practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Gather market information on career and education in India/abroad
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify demand in the market for various careers and education
- PC2. gather information regarding educational and work opportunities in different parts of India
and relevant information in these geographies
- PC3. assemble information on labour market, job content, eligibility, progression paths, salary and
compensation and application deadlines
- PC4. identify specialist agencies and other professionals for meeting client needs not serviceable
by self or own organisation
- PC5. gather information regarding skilling, higher education, and work opportunities in specific
countries, including eligibility and application processes, based on the international scope catered by the organisation
- PC6. Track emerging career domains including AI, sustainability, remote work, and gig economy
using reliable data sources
- PC7. Access and use government portals (e.g., National Career Service, UGC, NSDC) and verified
global sources to gather updated data
Organise data and information for effective use
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. extract quantitative and qualitative information from documents and reports relevant to
career and education counselling
- PC9. organise and file information gathered from different sources using manual and/or electronic
filing systems
- PC10. record and maintain a database of previous client experiences, challenges, successes for
future reference
- PC11. prepare quantitative presentations for using with students, job seekers and employer clients

#### MEP/N4402: Elicit information from career and education counselling
consumers  and employer clients
Description
This unit deals in detail with eliciting information from persons seeking career and education counselling
services and employer clients, that enable effective provision of counselling on education and career
choices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develop profile of career and education counselling consumers
Organise information from employer clients
Ensure gender mainstreaming and inclusivity
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Develop profile of career and education counselling consumers
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. use aptitude and achievement assessments to help consumers evaluate their interests,
skills, and abilities
- PC2. elicit information from consumers related to their needs, aspirations, preferences and
priorities in relation to education and work
- PC3. evaluate consumer clients’ background, education, and training as well as work experiences
- PC4. prepare education/ career profile of the consumer client
- PC5. match consumer profile with market requirements and job profile
- PC6. evaluate global education and employment pathways by aligning Indian qualifications, skills,
and work experience with country-specific and hybrid program requirements
- PC7. Use validated digital tools and career exploration platforms for profiling and mapping client
readiness
- PC8. Consider NSQF alignment and skilling certifications while preparing client profiles for
vocational and non-traditional career tracks
- PC9. Design and conduct age-appropriate career guidance sessions for school students in
alignment with NEP 2020 recommendations and academic pathways
Organise information from employer clients
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. use appropriate channels to connect with client
- PC11. gather required information from employers on their needs and expectations
- PC12. evaluate employers’ requirements and deadlines and organise job profile
- PC13. distinguish Indian and foreign employers and education/career trajectories
- PC14. Identify industry-specific hiring patterns, remote work opportunities, and emerging roles to
inform client recommendations

#### MEP/N4403: Provide career and education counselling
Description
This unit deals with providing career and education counselling to client, by providing information and
supporting them to find clarity for decision making
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for the counselling session (group/face to face/ online/tele-counselling)
Provide one-on-one counselling
Conduct group counselling
Assist laid-off workers
Ensure the effectiveness of counselling
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for the counselling session (group/face to face/ online/tele-counselling
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. organise the workspace/ interaction platform to communicate effectively with the client(s)
and make them comfortable
- PC2. arrange required information, tools, equipment and resources for conduct of the counselling
session
- PC3. decide on communication content, time management, and other relevant parameters
viz.a.viz. group/face to face/ online/tele-counselling
- PC4. analyse diversity of individuals and target group and probable numbers, when planning
group counselling session
- PC5. assess constraints and risks to delivery and achievement of objectives
- PC6. contextualise existing program materials to meet the needs of the group
- PC7. Explore and prepare AI-enabled or digital career exploration tools and psychometric
platforms to be used during the session, if applicable
Provide one-on-one counselling
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. communicate the role of the counsellor and the client’s rights and responsibilities
- PC9. assist clients in locating and applying for jobs
- PC10. advise clients on resolving problems that may occur at the workplace
- PC11. furnish information on educational requirements for various jobs and available educational
avenues
- PC12. enable individuals to select and apply educational programs, degrees and credentials
- PC13. facilitate the process of building a professional and personal portfolio
- PC14. prepare individuals for professional interactions such as interviews, networking events, job
fairs, discussions and dialogues
