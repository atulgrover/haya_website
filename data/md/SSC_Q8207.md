#### SSC/Q8207: IoT Security Analyst
Brief Job Description
An IoT Security Analyst is a professional specializing in protecting Internet of Things (IoT) devices,
networks, and systems from threats and vulnerabilities. They design, implement, manage, and monitor
security measures tailored to IoT environments, ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of
data and operations. Their role involves understanding the unique security challenges of IoT devices such
as limited processing power, diverse protocols, and widespread deployment and applying best practices,
standards, and open-source tools to safeguard these assets. They also conduct vulnerability assessments,
develop incident response plans, and ensure compliance with relevant security standards. Overall, an IoT
Security Specialist helps organizations maintain resilient and secure IoT ecosystems critical for modern
businesses and infrastructure.
Personal Attributes
IoT Security Specialists must have good interpersonal and communication skills. They must have a zeal to
develop their knowledge and analytical abilities.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SSC/N8231: Design processes to manage security threats and incidents across multiple technology
layers

4. SSC/N8238: Create technical documents and manuals
5. SSC/N8249: Operational Technology Security

#### SSC/N8231: Design  processes to manage security threats and incidents
across multiple technology layers
Description
This unit is about designing processes to mitigate security risk at the network, hardware, cloud, application
and platform layers
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage access to IoT assets, Ensure data security, Ensure network and hardware security, Design
network security, Define security architecture, Ensure cloud, application and platform security,
Define fallback mechanisms
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Manage access to IoT assets and systems based on identity access and management tools
and frameworks
- PC2. Evaluate security threat levels and criticality of IoT assets and systems to prioritize security
measures
- PC3. Define security parameters and develop encryption standards for data stored in cloud
platforms and databases
- PC4. Maintain logs of data security performance metrics and analyze variances to identify issues
and take action
- PC5. Conduct security assessments including penetration testing and vulnerability scans using
automated and manual techniques
- PC6. Protect the IoT network and devices from unauthorized access by configuring and
troubleshooting security hardware and software
- PC7. Secure the perimeter of the IoT network and classify components based on function and data
sensitivity
- PC8. Employ network monitoring tools and design logging systems to detect and respond to
security threats in real time
- PC9. Ensure all security policies and standards are consistently followed and security processes
are managed daily
- PC10. Regularly apply security patches and employ digital certificates along with disaster recovery
plans to ensure resilience
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. organizational policies, procedures, and guidelines that relate to maintaining solution security

#### SSC/N8232: Detect occurrences of security incidents to IoT solutions
Description
This unit is about detecting threats and anomalies, employing continuous monitoring processes, maintaining detection processes and performing audits
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Review past incidents, Drive audit operations, Perform security analysis, Drive continuous monitoring and threat assessment
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. record, classify and prioritize information security incidents using standard templates and
tools
- PC2. access their organization’s knowledge base for information on previous information security
incidents and how these were managed
- PC3. participate in audit reviews
- PC4. identify the requirements of audits and prepare for audits in advance
- PC5. liaise with appropriate people to gather data/information required for audits
- PC6. provide immediate support to auditors to carry out audit tasks
- PC7. carry out required audit tasks using standard tools and following established
procedures/guidelines/checklists
- PC8. evaluate the impact of potential anomalies
- PC9. ensure both signature-based and anomaly-based intrusions are detected in a timely manner
- PC10. perform regular maintenance of detection processes
- PC11. ensure readiness of maintenance processes to reliable detect incidents
- PC12. apply security controls to IoT solutions in line with data security policies, procedures and
guidelines
- PC13. perform security assessments of gateway / edge devices systems using automated security
assessment tools
- PC14. design and develop dashboards/KPIs for monitoring the incidents, the root cause and
detection to resolution corrective actions using standard Commercial off-the-shelf based
Investigation Management tool
- PC15. identify critical security threats and vulnerabilities across all layers of the IoT solution
- PC16. predict and extrapolate attack trends ahead of their occurrence
- PC17. carry out backups of security devices and applications in line with security policies,
procedures and guidelines

#### SSC/N8233: Respond  to detected security incidents and restore affected
capabilities
Description
This unit is about responding to security incidents, preventing their expansion and ensuring timely
restoration and recovery of affected capabilities
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Drive response planning, Manage communications, Prepare preliminary reports, Drive restoration and recovery

#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. plan timely response to detected security incidents
- PC2. execute post-incident processes and procedures in line with security policies, procedures,
and guidelines
- PC3. automate responses to detected security threats and incidents
- PC4. assign information security incidents promptly to appropriate people for investigation/action

appropriate people where progress does not comply with standards or service level agreements (SLAs)
- PC6. liaise with stakeholders to gather, validate and provide information related to information
security incidents, where required
- PC7. report to law enforcement agencies, if required
- PC8. prepare accurate preliminary reports on information security incidents using standard
templates and tools
- PC9. submit preliminary reports promptly to appropriate people for action
- PC10. neutralize the effects of the security incident or mitigate it
- PC11. prevent further expansion of the security incident
- PC12. ensure timely restoration of IoT assets and systems affected by the security incident
- PC13. incorporate lessons learned to prevent future security incidents
- PC14. update the organization’s knowledge base promptly and accurately with information security
incidents and how they were managed

#### SSC/N8249: Operational Technology  Security
Description
Operational Technology Security
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operational technology security
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand OT fundamentals and the importance of security in industrial environments.
- PC2. Elaborate the difference between IT and OT environments
- PC3. Discuss OT network topology components
- PC4. Familiarize with international standards and compliance requirements.
- PC5. Learn how to implement segmentation and zone-based security and ensure OT networks are
segmented from IT networks to reduce attack surface and prevent lateral movement of threats.
- PC6. Maintain an inventory of all OT devices and systems, including legacy equipment and
proprietary systems not on standard networks.
- PC7. Evaluate the Common vulnerabilities in OT devices legacy hardware, firmware & conduct
regular assessments tailored for industrial control systems, including firmware and hardware vulnerabilities unique to OT devices.
- PC8. Implement strict controls over modifications to OT systems to prevent accidental or
malicious changes that could impact safety or operations
- PC9. Deploy OT-optimized intrusion detection systems IDS and continuous monitoring solutions
capable of handling real-time data flows and specialized protocols (e.g., Modbus, DNP3,
Proprietary protocols and detect and respond to OT security incidents.
- PC10. Balance security with operational safety.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. organizational policies, procedures, and guidelines that relate to designing, assembling, and
manufacturing solution components
- KU2. organizational policies and procedures for sharing data
- KU3. organizational policies and procedures for documenting component designs and specifications
