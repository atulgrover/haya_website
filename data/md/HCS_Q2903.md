HCS/Q2903: Inlay Artisan - Metalware
Brief Job Description
A metalware Inlay Artisan creates unique handcrafted pieces by combining intricate inlay techniques with
various metals. Their work showcases a blend of traditional craftsmanship and modern design, resulting in
elegant and distinctive items.
Personal Attributes
A metalware Inlay Artisan is detail-oriented and creative, demonstrating exceptional craftsmanship and
patience in their intricate designs. They are passionate about their craft, adaptable to client needs, and
dedicated to producing high-quality, unique pieces.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

- 1. HCS/N2904: Set silver/gold wire in the metal craft

HCS/N2904: Set silver/gold wire in the metal craft
Description
This OS unit is about preparing the pre engraving requirements to engrave design on a metal surface
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understand work requirement
assemble the required raw materials
prepare the metal surface for inlaying
inlay the metal strips / wire into the craft
achieve quality and design standards
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding work requirement
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. make note of the inlay requirement on aspects such as shape, style, materials, etc.
PC2. interpret the design requirements
PC3. make note of the areas to be engraved and inlayed
PC4. plan the target on number of pieces to be completed for the day
Assembling the required raw materials
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. select appropriate tool such as hammer, chisel, knife, etc. to set the metal
PC6. collect appropriate inlaying material such as gold /silver (usually softer metals)
PC7. sharpen tools periodically
PC8. collect and arrange materials required to begin the process
PC9. check metals and ensure they are appropriate for the design requirement (edge of chisel,
hammer size, width of inlay metal string)
PC10. report on any shortage or defect of raw materials to the concerned person
PC11. ensure to stock required materials in advance
Preparing the metal surface for inlaying
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. receive the metal product from engraver to be inlayed (usually the product is made of harder
metal)
PC13. inspect the metal craft and ensure grooves are as per standards to hold the metal string /
wire / strip for inlay process
PC14. work on the grooves to ensure appropriate setting width / depth by chiseling in them (if
required)
PC15. clean the surface of the metal before starting the inlaying process
Inlaying the metal strips / wire into the craft

HCS/N2911: Carry out the process of making Bidri craft
Description
This OS unit is about performing the Bidri crafts on a metal surface.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the following :
understand work requirement
assemble the required raw materials
prepare the metal surface for inlaying
inlay the metal strips / wire into the craft
achieve quality and design standards
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparing the Base Metal
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Ensure proper composition of zinc and copper metals in the alloy.
PC2. Melt the alloy at the appropriate temperature and pour into molds.
PC3. Ensure the casting is free of defects such as air bubbles or incomplete forms.
PC4. Smooth and refine the base metal surface using filing and sanding techniques.
Design transfer on Base Metal
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Accurately transfer the design onto the metal using traditional tools or stencils.
PC6. Follow design outlines meticulously, ensuring symmetry and precision.
PC7. Use chisels to etch or engrave the design without causing cracks or unwanted marks on the
metal surface.
PC8. Ensure consistent depth of the etching for better inlaying later.
Inlay process on Base Metal
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Cut metal (silver, gold, brass and copper) sheets or wires to match the intricate design
patterns.
PC10. Embed metal into the etched grooves with precision, ensuring tight fitting without gaps.
PC11. Smooth the Metal inlay so it is flush with the surface of the base metal.
PC12. Ensure there is no over-spilling or misalignment of the inlay.
Smoothing and Polishing the object
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. Smoothen the inlaid object with files, sandpaper, or buffing tools without damaging the silver
or base metal.

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Historical and cultural significance of Bidri ware, including its origins in Bidar, Karnataka.
KU2. Traditional techniques used in Bidri ware, including the use of zinc and copper alloys,
inlaying, and blackening processes.
KU3. Understanding of different types of metals and alloys used in Bidri craft (e.g., zinc, copper,
lead), their properties, and the purpose they serve in the casting process.
KU4. Detailed knowledge of design elements commonly used in Bidri ware, including geometric,
floral, and traditional motifs.
KU5. Techniques for transferring designs onto metal surfaces, including tracing, engraving, and
etching methods.
KU6. Methods for polishing and smoothing the final product, ensuring a high-quality finish.
KU7. The traditional blackening process, including the use of copper sulfate and soil from Bidar
Fort for creating contrast in the final product.
KU8. Knowledge of various tools used in Bidri ware (e.g., chisels, hammers, engraving tools,
polishing tools) and their specific purposes.
KU9. Understanding how to maintain and handle tools to ensure precision and safety.
KU10. Knowledge of materials required for casting, inlaying, and blackening, including the correct
composition of zinc alloy, silver, and blackening solutions.
KU11. Safety procedures for handling molten metals and sharp tools during the crafting process.
KU12. Techniques for achieving symmetry and precision in design transfer, engraving, and inlay
work.
KU13. Knowledge of common defects in Bidri ware (e.g., cracks, uneven inlay, incorrect blackening)
and how to avoid them.
KU14. Understanding of quality standards for Bidri products, ensuring durability, aesthetic appeal,
and functional use.
