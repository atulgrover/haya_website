IAC/ASC/Q0201: Supervisor Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facility (RVSF)
Brief Job Description
This course provides participants with the necessary knowledge and skills for safe and responsible end-of-
life vehicle (ELV) de-pollution and dismantling. It covers topics such as safety, hazards and risks associated
with vehicle dismantling, ELV guidance for waste handling, personal protective equipment training,
personnel safety, Tools, and equipment. The course aims to ensure compliance with regulations and
promote environmental responsibility in the automotive industry.
Personal Attributes
Knowledge of health and safety risks in ELV operations and apply appropriate risk assessment and
management techniques. Understand the environmental considerations associated with ELV management
and implement strategies to minimize pollution and negative impacts
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. IAC/ASC/N0201: Introduction to ELV and Environmental Impact

Scrapping Facility (RVSF)

IAC/ASC/N0201: Introduction to ELV and Environmental Impact
Description
Introduction to ELV and Environmental Impact
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Learners will comprehend the definition and characteristics of ELVs, including the factors that
contribute to their end-of-life status.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Learners will comprehend the definition and characteristics of ELVs, including the factors that
contribute to their end-of-life status.
PC2. Learners will gain an awareness of the environmental challenges associated with ELVs, such
as pollution, resource depletion, and waste generation.
PC3. Learners will recognize and analyze the key stakeholders involved in the ELV industry,
including customers, suppliers, and competitors.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Concept of End-of-Life Vehicles (ELVs) and their significance in the automotive industry.
KU2. Legislative, regulatory and policy aspects.
KU3. Environmental impact of ELVs and the need for proper management and disposal practices.
KU4. Key responsibilities of a Various roles in the RVSF.
KU5. Different segments in Automotive
KU6. Various stakeholders involved in the ELV industry, including customers, suppliers, and
competitors.

Description
Comprehensive Management of End-of-Life Vehicles: Understanding Processes, Ensuring Implementation,
and Suggesting Improvements
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Learner will acquire the complete understating of vehicle components, assemblies, and
subassemblies, vehicle dismantling process, proper usage of tools and equipment, handling of
hazardous material during the dismantling, the risk associated with the dismantling process, record
keeping, identification of ok/not ok material, proper handling of material, etc.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Demonstrate the usage of each of the tools and equipment.
PC2. Demonstrate how to conduct scheduled maintenance checks on tools and equipment to
identify any issues or malfunctions.
PC3. Demonstration of Plasma Arc Cutting, Gas Cutting and Hand Grinder
PC4. Practical Task of document completion of Various Vehicles.
PC5. Creating and maintaining records.
PC6. Creating a unique stock number for an end-of-life vehicle
PC7. How to deal with vehicles where the vehicle registration mark (VRM) and chassis number
(VIN) do not match data sources.
PC8. Removal of batteries and liquefied gas tanks
PC9. Removal or neutralization of potential explosive components (e.g., air bags)
PC10. Removal and collection of all types of fluids
PC11. Removal of wheels and tires
PC12. Removal of hazardous components (e.g., lead balancing weights or mercury switches)
PC13. Demonstration of components and subassemblies of Vehicle
PC14. Removing body parts, engine, transmission, suspension system, and other parts, using
appropriate tools and techniques.
PC15. Practical Dismantling of Vehicle (Various Segments)
PC16. Demonstration of operating Shredder & Baler
PC17. Categorize and organize materials based on size, type, or other relevant factors.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

IAC/ASC/N0204: Managing the team on the line/ shift on a day-to-day
basis
Description
Managing the team on the line/ shift on a day-to-day basis.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Learners will acquire the competence to efficiently oversee operations during shifts or on production
lines, mastering essential skills and strategies for effective management.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Generate daily and monthly Management Information System (MIS) reports for production,
meticulously comparing actual performance against set targets. Subsequently, communicating
these findings to the Production In charge within the context of End-of-Life Vehicles (ELV)
operations.
PC2. Conduct thorough quality inspections on incoming materials for scrap vehicles and provide
pertinent feedback to the store department.
PC3. Perform comprehensive quality inspections on the dismantling process, ensuring adherence to
specified parameters and guidelines. Provide necessary feedback to line leaders.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand strategically assigning manpower and effectively managing shifts to ensure the
appropriate placement of personnel at designated workstations.
KU2. Support Shift In Charge/ Process head/ Shop head is finalizing the shift rosters for the week
and month based on the production plan available.
KU3. Initiate the submission of inventory requisitions to both the Stores and Purchase departments,
and subsequently, maintain active communication with these departments to ensure the
punctual receipt of materials, including spares and consumables, aligned with the context of
End-of-Life Vehicles (ELV) operations.
KU4. Secure the alignment of material movement on the shop floor with the designated Time-At-
Task (TAT) durations outlined in Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) or Work Plans.
KU5. Optimal resource utilization (man, machine, and material) and streamlining of activities within
the shift.
KU6. Identify & implement action steps to reduce losses and wastages during shift operation.
KU7. Support the in charge/ Engineer/ Shop Head in analyzing the various data sheets related to
production, maintenance, manpower deployment etc.
KU8. Support the maintenance function to ensure execution of the maintenance schedules.

Premises
Description
Execution of 5S Initiatives in the Shop Floor and Office Premises
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Learners will acquire the skills to ensure the effective execution of sorting, streamlining, organizing,
storage, and detailed documentation practices.
