#### MEP/Q1206: Senior Internal Auditor
Brief Job Description
Senior Internal Auditor assesses management systems, internal controls, and processes to ensure they
align with organizational goals and compliance standards. Once areas for improvement are identified, this
person recommends solutions, and contributes to enhancing overall organizational performance. The
individual also assesses risk management practices, financial controls, and governance structures,
providing valuable insights to support informed decision-making of management.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be well-groomed, attentive, comfortable with multi-tasking, disciplined and attentive
to details. This person must have a positive attitude, be dependable and can work in a team. The
individual should be comfortable working on computers and mobile phones.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MEP/N1225: Plan and Prepare for Management Audit
2. MEP/N1226: Evaluate Internal Controls and Risk Management
3. MEP/N1227: Audit Financial Policies, Procedures, and Performance
4. MEP/N1228: Document Audit Findings and Prepare Reports
5. MEP/N9903: Apply health and safety practices at the workplace
6. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

#### MEP/N1225: Plan and Prepare for Management  Audit
Description
This NOS involves understanding the organizational context, identifying key business processes, and
planning the audit by defining scope, objectives, and necessary documentation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand organizational context
Prepare for the audit engagement
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand organizational context
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Research about the industry, business domain, and internal control mechanisms of the
organization.
- PC2. Analyse key business processes and their relevance to audit objectives.
- PC3. Evaluate organizational auditability based on the availability of data and stakeholder
cooperation.
- PC4. Determine the scope, objectives, and limitations of the audit.
Prepare for the audit engagement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Prepare a comprehensive checklist of financial and operational documents to be audited.
- PC6. Assess potential control risks based on preliminary information.
- PC7. List audit techniques and tools to be employed, such as sampling or analytical reviews.
- PC8. Draft a letter of engagement outlining the scope, nature, and timeline of the audit.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand the industry, business domain, and internal control mechanisms relevant to audit
planning.
- KU2. Know the principles of audit engagement, including scope definition, risk assessment, and documentation requirements.
- KU3. Learn the methods for identifying financial and operational records required for a comprehensive audit.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:

#### MEP/N1226: Evaluate Internal Controls and Risk Management
Description
This unit details auditing different non-financial procedures in the organization or company like Human
Resources, legal compliance and industry standards, management strategies and resources, lines of
reporting and the like.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Inspect and assess internal controls
Evaluate operational risks and processes
Review human resource and stakeholder processes
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Inspect and assess internal controls
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Document components of the organization’s internal control system.
- PC2. Examine information and communication systems used for decision-making.
- PC3. Analyze control activities for adequacy and effectiveness.
- PC4. Develop and implement a test of control to validate system reliability.
Evaluate operational risks and processes
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Assess risks and existing risk management measures for adequacy.
- PC6. Analyze operational cycles, including production, revenue, and conversion.
- PC7. Verify compliance in investing and financing activities.
- PC8. Track the effectiveness of resource allocation across departments.
- PC9. Document irregularities, errors, and other process gaps in risk management.
Review human resource and stakeholder processes
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Examine the organization’s personnel training, recruitment, and promotion processes.
- PC11. Analyze stakeholder and shareholder engagement and its alignment with growth strategies.
- PC12. Evaluate decision-making strategies for operational and strategic goals.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand the components of internal control systems and their role in organizational governance.

#### MEP/N1227: Audit Financial Policies, Procedures, and Performance
Description
This NOS involves verifying audit strategies, overseeing financial functions, and analyzing financial data to
ensure accuracy, compliance, and efficiency in financial reporting.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Verify audit strategy
Oversee financial functions
Analyze and interpret the financial data
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Verify audit strategy
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Check the availability and reliability of financial documents and records.
- PC2. Define the nature, extent, and timing of audit activities.
- PC3. Conduct vouching and tracing to ensure transaction accuracy.
- PC4. Identify and report material misstatements and inconsistencies.
Oversee financial functions
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Assess financial statements’ adherence to relevant accounting standards.
- PC6. Examine cost control and project management mechanisms.
- PC7. Verify compliance with statutory and regulatory financial requirements.
Analyze and interpret the financial data
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Prepare analytical procedures to compare financial metrics.
- PC9. Verify income accounts for completeness and accuracy.
- PC10. Scrutinize expense accounts for proper allocation and authorization.
- PC11. Validate assets and liabilities against records and physical verification.
- PC12. Document financial insights and discrepancies.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand financial auditing principles, including vouching, tracing, and material misstatement detection.
- KU2. Learn the application of accounting standards, cost control mechanisms, and statutory compliance in financial audits.

#### MEP/N1228: Document  Audit Findings and Prepare Reports
Description
This NOS involves collecting and validating audit evidence, preparing structured documentation, and
drafting comprehensive audit reports with key findings, risks, and recommendations for stakeholders.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Collect and validate audit evidence
Prepare audit documentation
Draft and present audit reports
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Collect and validate audit evidence
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify sources of audit evidence, including financial and operational data.
- PC2. Obtain sufficient, relevant, and reliable audit evidence for key findings.
- PC3. Perform detailed inspections and inquiries to confirm evidence validity.
- PC4. Reconcile discrepancies between financial data and operational outputs.
Prepare audit documentation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Verify data and validate its authenticity for documentation.
- PC6. Define the form, content, and extent of audit documentation to meet standards.
- PC7. Ensure documentation reflects findings, issues, and recommendations clearly.
Draft and present audit reports
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Prepare a structured audit report with executive summaries and actionable insights.
- PC9. Highlight critical risks, irregularities, and improvement areas.
- PC10. Present findings to stakeholders using clear, concise, and visual communication.
- PC11. Provide recommendations to address gaps and enhance compliance and efficiency.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand the principles of audit evidence, including types, sources, and reliability assessment.
- KU2. Learn the standards for audit documentation, including format, content, and regulatory compliance.

#### MEP/N9903: Apply health and safety practices at the workplace
Description
This unit deals in detail with application of health and safety practices in the workplace
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Apply relevant health and safety practices at the workplace
Maintain a healthy and hygienic environment
Deal with emergency situations
Follow fire safety requirements
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Apply relevant health and safety practices at the workplace
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify, control and report health and safety issues relating to immediate work environment
according to procedures
- PC2. follow procedures and instructions for dealing with hazards, within the scope of
responsibilities and competencies
- PC3. document and report all hazards, accidents and near-miss incidents as per set process
- PC4. document safety records according to organisational policies
Maintain a healthy and hygienic environment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. maintain the work area in a clean and tidy condition
- PC6. ensure that the work area is sanitised as and when required
- PC7. maintain personal hygiene
- PC8. use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) where required
- PC9. wash hands using soap and water or alcohol based sanitiser
- PC10. report hygiene related concerns promptly to the relevant authority
Emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. administer appropriate first aid to victims wherever required e.g. in case of bleeding, burns,
choking, electric shock, poisoning etc.
- PC12. respond promptly and appropriately to an accident situation or medical emergency in real or
simulated environments
- PC13. perform rescue activity during an accident if applicable (e.g. if moving victim is advisable)
Follow fire safety requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. follow fire safety practices
- PC15. identify the type of fire and its stage

#### DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)
Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Career Development & Goal Setting
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs
#### Elements and Performance Criteria

#### Acronyms

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
