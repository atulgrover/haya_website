#### HCS/Q7301: Traditional Hand Embroiderer
Brief Job Description
A Traditional Hand Embroiderer is skilled in the art of hand-stitching intricate designs onto fabric using
various embroidery techniques. They have a deep appreciation for craftsmanship and cultural heritage,
often creating unique, personalised pieces that reflect traditional patterns and styles.
Personal Attributes
Traditional Hand Embroiderer is patient and meticulous, with a strong eye for detail and a passion for
preserving cultural artistry. They possess creativity and a steady hand, allowing them to produce beautiful,
intricate designs with precision.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N7376: Quality and Packaging of Hand Embroidered Textiles
2. HCS/N8518: Entrepreneurship Skills for Traditional Hand Embroiderer
3. HCS/N9901: Coordinate with colleagues and work as a team
4. HCS/N9912: Maintain work area and tools
5. HCS/N9913: Maintain health, safety and security at workplace
6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Phulkari Embroidery
Phulkari embroidery is a traditional Punjabi craft characterised by vibrant, floral patterns stitched onto
fabric using bright threads.

Elective 2: Chikankari Embroidery
Chikankari embroidery is a delicate and intricate hand-embroidery technique originating from Lucknow,
India. Known for its fine white thread work on lightweight fabrics, it features floral and geometric patterns,
creating an elegant and timeless aesthetic that enhances traditional garments.

#### HCS/N7376: Quality and Packaging of Hand Embroidered Textiles
Description
The quality and packaging of hand-crafted textiles are essential in preserving their unique artistry and
ensuring customer satisfaction.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Inspect finished hand embroidered textiles for quality
Carry out final finishing processes
Perform quality checks as per industry standards
Package the product for safe storage and transportation
Follow health, safety and environmental practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Inspect finished hand embroidered textiles for quality
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Check the finished textile for consistency in embroidery patterns and stitch quality.
- PC2. Ensure there are no loose threads, skipped stitches, or uneven tension.
- PC3. Examine the fabric for stains, dirt, or physical damage.
- PC4. Match the finished product with the original design specifications or sample.
- PC5. Verify colour fastness and uniformity of dyeing if applicable.
- PC6. Document any defects found and segregate faulty items for correction.
Carry out final finishing processes
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Trim any excess threads or fabric as per quality standards.
- PC8. Gently iron or press the textile using suitable temperature settings.
- PC9. Apply appropriate finishing techniques (e.g., stiffening, lining) if required.
- PC10. Ensure that embellishments and accessories (if any) are securely fixed.
- PC11. Fold or roll the fabric neatly to avoid creases or damage.
Perform quality checks as per industry standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. Conduct visual inspection using standardised lighting and background.
- PC13. Use measuring tools to verify dimensions and layout accuracy.
- PC14. Cross-check labels, tags, and branding information for correctness.
- PC15. Adhere to quality assurance procedures specific to hand embroidery.
- PC16. Maintain a checklist or quality log for record-keeping.
Package the product for safe storage and transportation

#### HCS/N8518: Entrepreneurship Skills for Traditional Hand Embroiderer
Description
This unit focuses on developing the knowledge and competencies needed to run a sustainable and profitable embroidery venture while maintaining traditional craft standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understanding the embroidery business product development pricing and costing marketing and branding
sales and customer relations financial management supply chain and inventory management legal and regulatory knowledge
sustainability practices networking and partnerships
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding the Embroidery Business
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. learn about the traditional hand embroidery industry, including key market trends, customer
demands, and potential growth opportunities
- PC2. develop an understanding of how traditional hand embroidery can be adapted to modern
trends while preserving craftsmanship
Product Development
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. create unique embroidery designs that stand out in the market by blending traditional
techniques with innovative ideas
- PC4. focus on product quality, ensuring each piece is well-executed and meets customer
expectations
- PC5. learn how to produce embroidery items in various categories such as clothing, accessories,
home décor, and gifts
Pricing and Costing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. learn how to calculate the cost of materials (fabric, thread, tools) and labor involved in
creating hand-embroidered products
- PC7. identify the pricing strategies to ensure profitability, considering market competition and
customer willingness to pay for handcrafted products
