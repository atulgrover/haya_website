#### NIE/SSC/Q1801: Multiomics Data Analyst
Brief Job Description
This Short-term Certificate Course is targeted for creating qualified professionals in the field of Multiomics
Data, which will help in employment or Entrepreneurship development of the qualifier in the areas of
Health Informatics/ Medical Informatics/ Life Sciences, etc. The individual performs research and
designing/reshaping of algorithmic models for identification, troubleshooting, and implementation of
improvements to existing pipelines. The person also provides lead scientific, technical and knowledge
support for revealing hidden patterns and information in biological big data. S/he is a key contributor in
building the drug discovery platform for the future.
Personal Attributes
The purpose of this qualification is to train the students of Life Sciences with advanced-level Bioinformatics
and Multiomics Data to upskill them and increase their employability in the field of Life Sciences and allied
disciplines.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. NIE/SSC/N1805: Use of R Programming in Biological Data Analysis

#### NIE/SSC/N1805: Use of R Programming  in Biological Data Analysis
Description
Module introduces R Programming's application in biological data analysis, emphasizing comprehension,
link establishment, and practical usage. Students learn R's fundamentals, its role in deciphering biological
phenomena, and how to analyze biological data through interactive graphical plots, fostering proficiency in
data interpretation. By mastering R, students gain a powerful toolset for extracting insights and visualizing
trends, enhancing their capabilities in biological research.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Module covers the foundational understanding and practical application of R Programming in biological data analysis. Students will learn R's fundamentals and its relevance in biological
research, gaining skills to analyze datasets and create interactive graphical plots. The module aims
to equip students with proficiency in using R for insightful data interpretation and unraveling
complex biological phenomena.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to R in Biological Data Analysis & • Bioinformatics Applications in R
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. • Overview of R programming language, Installing R, Choosing and IDE; Application of R in
biological data.
•
- PC2. Sequence analysis using Bioconductor packages; Genomic data analysis and visualization
Advanced Topics in Bioinformatics with R & • Emerging Trends and Technologies
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. Functional enrichment analysis; Pathway analysis and systems biology
- PC4. Application of R in single-cell RNA sequencing; Use of R for analyzing multi-omics data.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Mastery of R Programming fundamentals, encompassing data structures, syntax, functions, and libraries.
- KU2. Recognition of the intrinsic link between R programming and biological data analysis, understanding how R facilitates the manipulation and interpretation of biological datasets.
- KU3. Proficiency in utilizing R for the analysis and interpretation of biological data, with a focus on
generating interactive graphical plots to visualize trends and patterns effectively.

#### NIE/SSC/N1807: Mathematical  Modeling of Biological Networks using
Systems Biology Framework
Description
The NOS encompasses a comprehensive study of Systems Biology Framework focusing on mathematical
modeling of Biological Networks. It includes an introduction to Systems Biology, various types of Biological
Networks, and Mathematical Representation of Biological Components using differential equations and
logical modeling.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Systems Biology and Network Modeling: Fundamentals and applications in biological
network analysis, emphasizing mathematical modeling.
Types of Biological Networks: Study of gene regulatory, protein-protein interaction, metabolic, and
signaling networks for structure-function understanding.
Mathematical Representation and Analysis: Using differential equations, Boolean modeling, and kinetic approaches for network dynamics and topology analysis.
Applications and Tools: Applying models for drug targets, disease mechanisms, and synthetic biology, with hands-on experience using software like COPASI and CellDesigner.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Fundamentals of Systems Biology and Network Modeling
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Ability to explain the key principles of systems biology and their relevance in biological
research.
- PC2. Demonstrated knowledge of mathematical modeling techniques used in systems biology, such
as differential equations and Boolean modeling.
- PC3. Capability to identify and describe different types of biological networks and their functions in
cellular processes.
Analysis of Biological Networks:
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Proficiency in constructing mathematical models for gene regulatory, protein-protein
interaction, and metabolic networks.
- PC5. Competence in applying network analysis methods to study network topology, dynamics, and
stability
- PC6. Skill in integrating experimental data into network models and evaluating model accuracy
through validation techniques
Application and Tools
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Ability to apply mathematical models to simulate dynamic behaviors and predict system
responses.

#### NIE/SSC/N1808: Healthcare Informatics for Medical Data Management
Description
The NOS encompasses a wide array of topics crucial to modern healthcare systems. It starts with an
introduction to the field, highlighting its scope and importance in managing medical data efficiently. The
focus then shifts to Electronic Health Records (EHR) Systems, Health Information Exchange (HIE), and
Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSS), emphasizing seamless data exchange, privacy, security, and
decision-making support.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Fundamentals of Healthcare Informatics: Understanding the scope and significance of healthcare informatics in managing medical data efficiently.
Electronic Health Records (EHR) Systems: Exploring the design, implementation, and interoperability
standards of electronic health record systems for seamless data exchange.
Health Information Exchange (HIE): Learning about connecting disparate healthcare systems, focusing on information sharing protocols and addressing privacy and security concerns.
Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSS): Integrating informatics into clinical decision-making
processes, emphasizing the applications and advantages of CDSS in healthcare settings.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Healthcare Informatics Fundamentals
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate an understanding of healthcare informatics by defining key terms and explaining
their relevance in healthcare.
- PC2. Identify and discuss the challenges and benefits associated with effective medical data
management in healthcare settings
- PC3. Apply knowledge of healthcare informatics fundamentals to analyze real-world scenarios and
propose solutions for improving data management processes.
Exploring Electronic Health Records (EHR) Systems
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Design and develop a basic electronic health record system prototype, including data storage
and retrieval functionalities
- PC5. Evaluate interoperability standards and protocols to ensure seamless data exchange between
different EHR systems.
- PC6. Assess the impact of EHR systems on patient care and healthcare workflows through case
studies and simulations.
Health Information Exchange (HIE) and Clinical Decision Support Systems (CDSS)
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Implement a secure health information exchange mechanism using encryption and access
control measures.

#### NIE/SSC/N1804: Data security law
Description
The course on Data Security Law covers a comprehensive range of topics essential for understanding and
implementing effective data protection measures. Students will gain insights into the evolution of data
security laws, including key concepts and terminology.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Foundations of Data Security Law: Covers fundamental concepts, historical evolution, and the importance of data protection.
Legal Frameworks and Compliance: Focuses on major data protection laws, comparative analysis, and compliance challenges.
Managing Data Breaches: Addresses understanding breaches, legal responses, and developing incident plans.
Risk Management and Emerging Trends: Includes risk assessment, compliance strategies, and future
developments in data security laws.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conceptual Understanding
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate comprehension of key data security concepts and terminology by accurately
defining and explaining terms like encryption, authentication, and data breach.
- PC2. Identify and describe the historical evolution of data security laws, including landmark
legislation and significant events that shaped data protection practices.
- PC3. Evaluate the importance of data protection in modern society by analyzing case studies,
industry reports, and real-world examples to understand the impact of data breaches on businesses and individuals.
Legal Framework Exploration
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Compare and contrast major data protection laws such as GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, and analyze
their scope, requirements, and implications for businesses and consumers.
- PC5. Critically examine international data security regulations to understand the global landscape
of data protection and privacy laws, including differences in approaches and enforcement mechanisms.
- PC6. Identify and explain jurisdictional differences and compliance challenges related to data
security laws, considering factors like cross-border data transfers, data residency requirements, and regulatory oversight.
