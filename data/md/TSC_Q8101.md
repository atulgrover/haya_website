#### TSC/Q8101: Machine  Operator - Sanitary Napkin
Brief Job Description
A Machine Operator-Sanitary Napkin- gets work allotted by his supervisor and is responsible for operating
the machine efficiently so as to get maximum output with minimum defect and carrying out all activities
on the machine, giving due importance to safety and environmental aspects.
Personal Attributes
A Sanitary Napkin Machine operator should have physicals skills like dexterity, coordination, motor skills,
strength, stamina and handle heavy equipment. A sanitary napkin machine operator should also have
sensory skills like hearing ability and vision (normal distance vision, colour vision, night vision, peripheral
vision, depth perception and ability to change focus).
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. TSC/N8101: Taking charge of shift and handing over shift to machine operator - Sanitary Napkin
2. TSC/N8102: Identification of Sanitary Napkin Machine parts and Understanding Process flow
3. TSC/N8103: Operating of Sanitary Napkin Making Machine
4. TSC/N8104: Maintaining the Sanitary Napkin Making Machine
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
6. TSC/N8105: Soft Skills to improve behaviour, hygiene, attitude, etc. and safety precautions

#### TSC/N8101: Taking charge of shift and handing over shift to machine
operator - Sanitary Napkin
Description
This unit is about taking charge of shift from previous shift sanitary napkin machine and relieving the
responsibilities to the next shift sanitary napkin machine operator
Scope
The scope covers the following :
taking charge of shift from Machine operator - Sanitary napkin handing over shift to sanitary Machine operator - Sanitary napkin
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. reach the department at least 15-20 minutes earlier
- PC2. take a round around the Machine
- PC3. check the cleanliness of the Sanitary Napkin machine
- PC4. check the ongoing material/lot on machine
- PC5. check the availability of sufficient raw materials and auxiliaries required during the shift
- PC6. discuss with previous shift machine operator regarding working of the machines. If any issue
then report to Supervisor
- PC7. check that the wastes collection is properly carried-out
- PC8. take a charge of machine from the outgoing operator in a proper manner
- PC9. take information regarding the machine stoppages or breakdown of machines, production
and quality issues. If any report to the Supervisor
- PC10. check the machine has to be properly handed over to the incoming machine operator
- PC11. report to the Supervisor before leaving the department
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. potential hazards associated with sanitary napkin machines and important safety precautions
- KU2. protocol and standard procedures and formats to obtain information on work-related tasks in
the production area
- KU3. details of the various job roles and responsibilities in the production department
- KU4. quality particulars pertaining to the sanitary napkin pad
- KU5. production defects and causes
- KU6. importance of quality napkin production
- KU7. reporting methods and log book formats

#### TSC/N8103: Operating of Sanitary Napkin Making Machine
Description
This NOS unit is about the machine operation in the sanitary napkin machine.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operating the Sanitary Napkin Machine
Monitoring the Sanitary Napkin Machine Operation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. check the quality and quantity of raw material to be used on machine before running the
machine
- PC2. check that control switches for starting, running and stopping of machine are functioning
properly
- PC3. operate the sanitary napkin machine
- PC4. check that the flow of the Continuous process divides into four steps - wood pulveriser, core
forming unit, wing making, packing unit
- PC5. check the proper functioning of compressed sheet form of wood pulp passed into Pulverizer
unit to open the sheet into individual fibres
- PC6. check the proper functioning of Super Absorbent Polymer is laying over wood fluff/fibres and
pressed in SAP feeding unit
- PC7. check the functioning of sheet passing to bottom film feeding unit where glue film placed
over core material.
- PC8. check the proper functioning of the material psses over to cutting unit where required shape
is given
- PC9. check the proper working condition of Packing cum sealing unit
- PC10. inspect the produced sanitary napkin is free from faults
- PC11. identify the damaged caused in sanitary napkin produced in the shift
- PC12. leave the work area safe and secure after work done
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. potential hazards associated with machine operation
- KU2. quality requirements of sanitary pad
- KU3. various defects in the sanitary napkin machine
- KU4. time limits for attending various types of stoppages

#### TSC/N8104: Maintaining the Sanitary Napkin Making Machine
Description
This unit is about maintaining the sanitary napkin machine, and other safety work responsibilities
associated to the sanitary napkin machine.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintaining the Sanitary Napkin Machine
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. check that all the stop motions are functions as per the standards
- PC2. check the material is properly passes over to next section in the sanitary napkin machine
- PC3. follow the preventive maintenance schedule as per the instructions
- PC4. check that any tool/ spare/raw material/any other waste are not thrown under/near the
machine
- PC5. follow the prescribed precautionary measures in the work place

- PC11. follow the prescribed waste collection procedure inside the department
- PC12. use the personal protective equipment such as “ear plug”, “face mask“, “head cap” etc., as
per protocol
- PC13. carry out cleaning according to the scheduled and limits of responsibility

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
