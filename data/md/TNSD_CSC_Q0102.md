#### TNSD/CSC/Q0102: Mould  Maker
Brief Job Description
A Mould Maker is responsible for the design, manufacture, and assembly of various moulds and dies,
including injection moulds, compression moulds, and die-casting dies. This role requires expertise in
advanced machining processes, CAD/CAM software, quality control, and the ability to troubleshoot and
repair.
Personal Attributes
Upon successful completion, learners will be qualified to become Die Makers for manufacturing dies,
moulds, special purpose tools, and die-casting dies. They will gain employment opportunities in
engineering, manufacturing, machine tools, automobile, and general machinery industries. This qualification also supports pathways to entrepreneurship.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. TNSD/CSC/N0109: Perform Non-Conventional Machining and Mould Fabrication Operations
2. TNSD/CSC/N0110: Design and Draw Multi-day Light and Compression Moulds
3. TNSD/CSC/N0111: Design Die-Casting Dies and Analyze Defects

5. TNSD/CSC/N0113: Program and Machine Components using CNC Lathe and Milling Machines
6. TNSD/CSC/N0114: Perform Basic 2D and 3D Modeling using CREO Software
7. TNSD/CSC/N0115: Design Complex Assemblies and Moulds using Advanced CREO Features
8. TNSD/CSC/N0116: Simulate CNC Programs using KELLER Software

#### TNSD/CSC/N0109: Perform Non-Conventional  Machining and Mould
Fabrication Operations
Description
Understand the Non-conventional machining processes used for mould making. Understand the manufacturing of multi-day light injection moulds, compression moulds and simple die-casting dies.
Understand the precision matching of mould with die spotting machine.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the non-conventional machining processes
Understand the manufacturing of Multi-day light injection mould by using CNC and EDM Machine.
Understand the manufacturing concept of compression mould.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand Non-conventional machining practices for advanced mould making process.
- PC2. Familiar with CNC Machining for the mould making process.
- PC3. Manufacture a 2 plate injection mould by using conventional machines, CNC machines and
Bench working. Try-out samples.
- PC4. Manufacture a moulds with ejection system by using conventional machines, CNC machines
and Bench working. Try-out samples
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand Non-conventional machining practices for advanced mould making process.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Familiar with CNC Machining for the mould making process.
- GS2. Manufacture a moulds with ejection system by using conventional machines, CNC machines and Bench working. Try-out samples
