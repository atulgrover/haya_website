MEP/Q1205: Documentation Executive (Import-Export)
Brief Job Description
Documentation Executives (Import – Export) are those professionals who provide information and
assistance to those individuals who wish to start their own goods exportation. Assistance involves helping
with planning for the beginning of the exporting and importing process, setting up and forwarding the legal
proceedings, acquiring approval assistance for export and import proceedings including transportation
assistance, consultation regarding trade agreements between different countries, providing advice
regarding the exportation and importing of goods to beneficial countries and such other matters.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be passionate about the work, should be well-groomed, attentive, comfortable with
flexible work, must possess versatile personality and well disciplined. This individual must be well versed
with computer and digital skills along with an optimistic attitude shown towards the work.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MEP/N1222: Plan for Import-Export applications

MEP/N1222: Plan for Import-Export applications
Description
This unit covers tasks involved in making a systematic planning strategy for import and export
proceedings.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Examine the Import and Export Procedure
Assist with Registration and applications
Elements and Performance Criteria
Examine the Import and Export Procedure
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify import and export procedures applicable to the company
PC2. determine licenses and applications required
PC3. examine applicable laws for entity
PC4. identify the registration requirements and required documents
PC5. prepare document list
PC6. verify documents
Assist with Registration and applications
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. organize requisite documents
PC8. fill all the requisite forms as per the guidelines
PC9. submit the application
PC10. follow up applications of licensees, registration and other applications
PC11. review and resubmit as required
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. types of Business
KU2. laws applicable for import and export business
KU3. characteristic features of import and exports
KU4. detailed evaluation of documents for online application
KU5. procedures and timeline of formation
KU6. steps in preparing activity plan
KU7. difference in procedures in manual and e-form

MEP/N1223: Apply for Certificates through online process
Description
This unit covers the process and other requirements to apply for various certificates.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Apply for Importer- Exporter Code (IEC)
Register for Registration cum Membership Certificate (RCMC)
Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India (ECGC) and Food Safety and Standards Authority of
India (FSSAI) applications
Elements and Performance Criteria
Apply for Importer- Exporter Code (IEC)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. submit the application with requested documents for IEC Code
PC2. pay charges mentioned in the portal as per the guidelines
PC3. check the application status as mentioned in the application
PC4. generate or obtain IEC License
Register for Registration cum Membership Certificate (RCMC)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. register on Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) portal
PC6. submit the application and attach the documents as per the norms
PC7. sign the application and make payment
PC8. generate or obtain certificate
Export Credit Guarantee Corporation of India (ECGC) and Food Safety and Standards Authority of India
(FSSAI) applications
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. register and login to ECGC portal
PC10. identify valid policy or Export Credit Insurance for Banks Whole Turnover Packaging Credit
and Post Shipment (ECIB- WTPC & PS) coverage
PC11. submit forms as per applicability
PC12. examine applicability of FSSAI
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. digital skills
KU2. online application process and its various steps

Description

MEP/N1218: Online Application of Customs
Description
This unit covers detailed description and methodologies of customs. It also includes essential points of
preparation of import and export documents, and provides guidelines to customs.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare and verify the documents
File customs returns
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare and verify the documents
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Maintain and verify PAN and other documents
PC2. Apply IEC code through directorate of foreign trade portal
PC3. Prepare documents for ICGATE and eSanchit registration
PC4. Create login credential in ICEGATE portal
PC5. Maintain import and export documentation
File customs returns
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Login to ICEGATE portal with necessary credentials
PC7. Fill the forms as per the guidelines
PC8. Verify and check the return for payment
PC9. Submit the forms
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. books of Accounts
KU2. indirect Tax laws and filing indirect tax returns
KU3. Tax Deducted at Source and its filings
KU4. accounting concepts and techniques for recording transactions
KU5. financial concepts such as calculation of tax liabilities of Customs, GST, and other indirect
taxes
KU6. Tax rates and computation of TDS, TCS, and net value of taxable goods / supplies under GST
KU7. stipulated timelines for depositing taxes, including GST, to government or tax authority
KU8. timeline for issue of TDS certificate by deduct or to deducted and details there in under GST
and penalties

DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Career Development & Goal Setting
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Employability Skills
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify employability skills required for jobs in various industries
PC2. identify and explore learning and employability portals
Constitutional values – Citizenship
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. recognize the significance of constitutional values, including civic rights and duties,
citizenship, responsibility towards society etc. and personal values and ethics such as
honesty, integrity, caring and respecting others, etc.
PC4. follow environmentally sustainable practices
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
