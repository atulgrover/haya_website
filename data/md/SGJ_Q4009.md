SGJ/Q4009: Water Technology
Brief Job Description
The individual on the qualification would be specialized to observe, identify, report, maintain, repair
equipment and control processes at the water treatment plant and across the networks. The individual
would work within sewer networks, for wastewater and sludge treatment in local or industrial wastewater
treatment plants. He/She would carry out their work independently based on technical documents,
regulations, and legal requirements. They acquire information, plan, and coordinate their work.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it without any accidents so
diligence and hardworking are desired attributes for individuals performing this role. He/She must also
demonstrate strong work ethics, an ability to communicate courteously with co-workers and must be good
with following instructions of the supervisor. He/She also has to monitor the activities of Wastewater
Treatment Plant and troubleshoot accordingly, so he should be a quick decision maker and good in
analysis

SGJ/N4060: Introduce water treatment fundamentals
Description
This unit is about introduction to water treatment fundamentals
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Basics of water treatment processes
Sources of Waste water
Elements and Performance Criteria
Basics of water treatment processes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. explain Overview of the water cycle and sources of wastewater and depict water cycle and
sources of wastewater through pictures or videos
PC2. discuss basics of Water Chemistry
PC3. explain Objectives of water treatment
PC4. explain parameters for water quality assessment
PC5. discuss chemical properties of water
PC6. discuss demineralization flow diagram, cation and anion exchangers mixed bed, regeneration
of cation and anion exchangers and degasser and illustrate them through pictures or videos
PC7. explain basic principles of electricity and illustrate with electrical control of machines and
actuators
PC8. discuss roles and responsibilities of a water treatment plant technician and prepare a list of
roles and responsibilities
PC9. discuss functions and objectives of a water treatment plant and showcase the stages of
treatment along with flow diagram, schematic and layout of wastewater treatment plant
PC10. discuss importance of wastewater treatment for environmental and public health protection
PC11. give an overview of global water challenges
PC12. discuss historical perspective and evolution of water treatment
Sources of Waste water
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. provide an overview of domestic wastewater generation
PC14. discuss industrial wastewater generation and its diversity
PC15. discuss types of industries producing wastewater (chemical, food processing, textile, etc.)
and document the types and volumes of wastewater generated from these locations
PC16. discuss characteristics of industrial wastewater: pollutants, toxic substances, pH variations,
and temperature
PC17. discuss industrial processes contributing to wastewater generation and show how residential
wastewater is collected, treated, and disposed of in on-site septic systems

Description
This unit is about Wastewater treatment plant and its components
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduce Wastewater Treatment Plant Components
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduce Wastewater Treatment Plant Components
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. discuss water cycle and its components and create scaled-down small models or diagrams
representing various treatment plant components (e.g., primary clarifiers, aeration tanks,
filtration systems)
PC2. discuss groundwater and surface water interactions
PC3. discuss and show materials required as metals, composites, plastics, etc. required for
wastewater treatment
PC4. discuss the basics of mechanical engineering (mechanics, sealing methods, gear technology,
etc.)
PC5. explain the primary treatment components and demonstrate the operation of primary and
secondary clarifiers
PC6. discuss types of filtration equipment and their application
PC7. explain the Biological Treatment Systems and depict through pictures or Videos
PC8. explain Tertiary Treatment Components
PC9. discuss chemical addition processes like coagulation and disinfection
PC10. discuss the Control and Automation Systems required in wastewater treatment
PC11. set up a model demonstrating the aeration process
PC12. show working of various Control and Automation Systems required in waste water treatment

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. principles and applications of safe working in general and for water and wastewater treatment
and operation in the networks and in solid waste management

together with their safety implications

environment

SGJ/N4062: Assess water quality
Description
This unit is about Water Quality Assessment
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Sampling techniques and testing methods for water treatment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Sampling techniques and testing Methods for water treatment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. explain grab sampling and demonstrate techniques using sample bottles or containers
PC2. discuss principles and procedures of grab sampling and show how to mix and create a
composite sample for analysis
PC3. discuss types of grab samples
PC4. discuss Parameters for assessing water quality
PC5. discuss drinking water treatment methods like filtration, disinfection and purification
PC6. discuss water quality standards and Regulations
PC7. discuss the process of membrane preparation
PC8. explain sample container selection and preparation and Set up small automated sampling
equipment (if available) and showcase its operation.
PC9. discuss the importance of proper labeling and documentation
PC10. explain physical testing methods
PC11. discuss methods and instruments for physical parameter analysis and demonstrate different
methods for sample preservation, such as adding preservatives or controlling temperature,
to maintain sample integrity during transportation and storage
PC12. explain laboratory techniques and equipment for chemical parameter analysis
PC13. discuss Advanced Water Treatment Technologies like Membrane filtration (Reverse Osmosis,
Ultrafiltration), Advanced oxidation processes, Desalination technologies
PC14. discuss microbiological testing methods: membrane filtration, multiple tube fermentation,
and other techniques
PC15. discuss metals and toxic substances testing
PC16. explain variations in sampling techniques at different stages of treatment
PC17. perform tests for physical parameters like temperature, turbidity, and color using appropriate
meters or instruments
PC18. conduct chemical tests for parameters like pH, dissolved oxygen, and chemical oxygen
demand (COD)

and techniques to which WorldSkills assessment and marking must conform. Expert assessment practice
