LSS/Q5701: Quality Control Inspector - Leather Accessories & Garments
Brief Job Description
Quality Control Inspector inspects, monitors, and ensures that all the products manufactured comply with
the customer and organizational standards.
Personal Attributes
A Quality Control inspector needs to be able to manage a team and effectively utilize them to maintain
quality standards and targets. He needs to be observant, alert, and have attention to detail. Good team
management and effective communication skills are helpful.

LSS/N5702: Carry out the quality control checks at leather goods and
accessories
Description
This unit of competency describes the performance criteria required to carry out the quality control in
leather goods and accessories.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out the quality control in leather goods and accessories.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. inspect leather goods and accessories according to established quality standards and
specifications.
PC2. identify and document any defects or irregularities in materials, stitching, finishing, or overall
construction.
PC3. utilize appropriate measurement tools and techniques to assess dimensions, thickness, and
other quality parameters of leather materials and components.
PC4. evaluate the color consistency, texture, and surface appearance of leather goods, ensuring
adherence to desired aesthetic standards.
PC5. verify the functionality and durability of hardware components such as zippers, buttons,
buckles, and straps.
PC6. perform tests and assessments to evaluate the strength, flexibility, and tear resistance of
leather materials.
PC7. assess the stitching quality, seam strength, and stitch consistency of assembled leather
products.
PC8. compare finished products against approved samples or prototypes to ensure consistency
and compliance with design specifications.
PC9. record inspection findings accurately and comprehensively using standardized
documentation or quality control forms.
PC10. communicate any quality issues or deviations from standards to relevant personnel,
including supervisors, production staff, or suppliers.
PC11. collaborate with production teams to implement corrective actions and preventive measures
to address quality issues and improve processes.
PC12. monitor and track quality metrics and performance indicators to identify trends, patterns,
and areas for continuous improvement.
PC13. adhere to safety protocols and quality control procedures to ensure a safe and healthy
working environment for all personnel involved in the manufacturing process.
PC14. maintain cleanliness and organization in the quality control area to facilitate efficient
inspection processes and prevent contamination or damage to products.

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the leather types, grades, and characteristics.

KU5. quality control methods and techniques.
KU6. common defects and issues that may arise in leather goods production.
KU7. the importance of quality control in maintaining brand reputation and satisfaction.
KU8. the relevant laws and regulations related to product quality and safety.
KU9. inspection equipment and tools used in quality control processes.
KU10. corrective actions and measures to address quality issues.
KU11. documentation and record-keeping procedures related to quality control inspections.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. read the manuals, health and safety instructions, memos, reports, job cards, etc.,
GS2. communicate effectively with operators, supervisors, managers, etc.,
GS3. listen the issues and comments given by the co-workers, supervisors, and managers.
GS4. plan and organize the work in order.
GS5. fill up appropriate technical forms, process charts, and activity logs in the prescribed format
of the company.
GS6. analyze the defects and the procedure for dealing with it.
GS7. complete the tasks efficiently and accurately within the stipulated time.
GS8. organize the material requirements sequentially.
GS9. select the optimal process and appropriate tools & equipment to complete the work
efficiently.
GS10. use reasoning skills to make proper decisions and troubleshoot concerns related to own
responsibilities.
GS11. use problem-solving skills to solve the problems that occurred during relevant operation.

LSS/N5703: Carry out quality control checks on leather and non-leather
garments
Description
This unit of competency describes the performance criteria required to Carry out quality control checks on
leather and non-leather garments.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out quality control checks on leather and non-leather garments
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. recognize the defects in both leather and non-leather garments during the quality control
process.
PC2. assess the quality of materials used in garment production, including leather, synthetic
materials, and accessories such as zippers and buttons.
PC3. demonstrate the visual inspections of garments to ensure they meet quality standards in
terms of stitching, seams, alignment, and overall appearance.
PC4. ensure the garment measurements against specified dimensions to ensure accurate sizing
and fit according to design requirements.
PC5. inspect finishing techniques such as dyeing, printing, embossing, and coating to ensure
consistency, colorfastness, and uniformity.
PC6. carry out the tests to assess the durability and strength of seams, fastenings, and
reinforcements to ensure garments withstand wear and tear.
PC7. check the functional features of garments, including pockets, closures, and adjustable
elements, to ensure they operate smoothly and securely.
PC8. evaluate garment comfort and wearability factors such as fabric breathability, flexibility, and
comfort against the intended use and customer expectations.
PC9. adhere to brand/customer quality standards and specifications throughout the quality control
process to maintain consistency and compliance with industry regulations.
PC10. document the inspection findings accurately, including any defects or deviations from quality
standards, to facilitate corrective actions and ensure traceability.
PC11. communicate the quality control findings effectively to relevant stakeholders, such as
production teams or supervisors, to facilitate timely resolution of issues.
PC12. ensure the cleanliness and organization of the inspection area and equipment to prevent
contamination and maintain a conducive working environment.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

LSS/N5704: Carry out quality checks on bags and luggage
Description
This unit of competency describes the performance criteria required to Carry out quality checks on bags
and luggage.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out quality checks on bags and luggage
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. conduct visual inspections of bags and luggage to identify any defects or irregularities in
materials, stitching, and construction.
PC2. utilize appropriate measuring tools and techniques to ensure that dimensions and
proportions of bags and luggage meet specifications.
PC3. perform functional tests on zippers, handles, wheels (if applicable), and other components to
ensure proper operation and durability.
PC4. inspect hardware such as buckles, clasps, and straps for functionality, proper alignment, and
secure attachment.
PC5. verify the accuracy and completeness of labeling, including brand labels, care labels, and
product information tags.
PC6. check for color consistency and uniformity across different parts of the bag or luggage item.
PC7. assess the overall finish and appearance of the product, including smoothness of surfaces,
absence of scratches or marks, and alignment of seams.
PC8. evaluate the strength and resilience of materials used, particularly in high-stress areas such
as handles and straps.
PC9. document and record any quality issues or deviations from standards identified during the
inspection process.
PC10. communicate findings effectively to relevant stakeholders, such as production teams or
quality assurance managers, and collaborate on corrective actions as needed.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the non leather materials, fabrics and its properties.
KU2. manufacturing processes for bags and luggage.
KU3. quality control methods and techniques.
KU4. common defects and issues that may arise in bags and luggage production.
KU5. the importance of quality control in maintaining brand reputation and satisfaction.
