THC/Q4201: Transport Coordinator-Tourism and Hospitality
Brief Job Description
The individual at work takes bookings for customer travel, arranges for vehicle, monitors the trip and
collects payment.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have customer centric approach, strong interpersonal and
communication skills, and ability to prioritize and handle pressure at the workplace.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. THC/N4216: Arrange Transportation for Customers
- 2. THC/N4217: Supervise Administrative Duties
- 3. THC/N9901: Communicate effectively and maintain service standards
- 4. THC/N9903: Maintain organisational confidentiality and respect guests’ privacy
- 5. THC/N9906: Follow Health, Hygiene and Safety practices
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

THC/N4216: Arrange transportation for customers
Description
This OS unit is about interacting with the customers, assessing their travel needs and taking booking,
scheduling and arranging for transportation, monitoring the transportation activities, and providing
assistance to customers in case of any issues.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assess customer needs and take booking
Make arrangements for transportation
Monitor transportation activities and provide assistance
Coordinate for payment details
Elements and Performance Criteria
Assess customer needs and take booking
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. obtain travel plan from customers, with travel dates, destination, duration, and number of
people travelling
PC2. obtain customers’ vehicle preference and special request, if any
PC3. review all the transport schedules to accommodate the travel plan
PC4. suggest available vehicle options to the customer
PC5. brief the customer on the fare and other applicable charges like night shift, etc.
PC6. confirm booking, pick-up and drop schedule with customer
PC7. update the travel schedule
PC8. send booking confirmation with vehicle and driver details to the customer as per SOP
PC9. update the payment records after collecting advance or partial payment from the customer
as per organizational standards
Make arrangements for transportation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. brief the driver about booking details like pick up location, time, preferred route and any
special request from the customer
PC11. ensure vehicle is in proper working condition and meets the required safety standards
PC12. make sure safety gears are in place, like first aid, fire extinguishers, etc.
PC13. check for cleanliness and sanitization of the vehicle
PC14. verify that the vehicle papers are in order
PC15. ensure the driver carries his valid driving license
PC16. arrange maps, communication devices, etc. for drivers, if required
Monitor transportation activities and provide assistance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

THC/N4217: Perform administrative duties
Description
This OS unit is about performing administrative duties like monitoring vehicle maintenance and upkeep,
maintaining documentation like attendance sheet, log sheets, etc. and reporting any issues to the
management workflow.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Monitor vehicle maintenance
Maintain documentation and report to management
Elements and Performance Criteria
Monitor vehicle maintenance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. ensure timely maintenance of vehicles
PC2. check that all vehicles meet security and operational standards
PC3. ensure all vehicle papers and documents meets basic legal and compliance e. g. rule books
of State Road Transport Undertaking (STU)s, Central Motor Vehicles Rule (CMVR)/other
guidelines from Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MORTH) and other guidelines
issued by Road Transport Authorities like RTOs (including fast tag facility) or any other
safety, security and environmental guidelines
PC4. inspect vehicle service record for any history of technical defects or immediate need for
servicing like oil/filter change
PC5. examine records of any technical/compliance issues observed during the previous trip
PC6. report actual or potential defects to Transport Manager in detail to determine road
worthiness of the vehicle
PC7. inform Transport Manager about any requirements such as fuel refill or repair
Maintain documentation and report to management
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. maintain attendance sheet of the drivers
PC9. review and arrange all transportation paperwork inclusive of driver logs, trip reports and
invoices
PC10. maintain vehicle logbook with vehicle numbers for tracking
PC11. verify the transport documentation and the trip sheets submitted by the drivers
PC12. maintain records of the driving license, Aadhar card, etc. and other necessary documents of
the drivers
PC13. report driver issues such as accidents, safety concerns, or licensing issues to the Transport
Manager
