WBSC/HCS/Q2101: Water Hyacinth Handicrafts Maker
Brief Job Description
A Water Hyacinth Handicrafts Maker is responsible for crafting a variety of handmade products using dried
water hyacinth stems. This role involves the entire process from harvesting and preparing raw materials to
designing and assembling finished items. They will use traditional techniques to create eco-friendly
products such as ropes, coasters, baskets, trays and hand purses.
Personal Attributes
A Water Hyacinth Handicrafts Maker should be creative, patient, and detail-oriented, with a strong
appreciation for traditional craftsmanship and eco-friendly practices. They need to have good hand-eye
coordination, manual dexterity and need to be hardworking and disciplined.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. WBSC/HCS/N2101: Describe water hyacinth potential application.
- 2. WBSC/HCS/N2102: Demonstrate the process of harvesting and drying.
- 3. WBSC/HCS/N2103: Apply dye on water hyacinth stem.
- 4. WBSC/HCS/N2104: Make basic craft items like ropes, coasters etc.
- 5. WBSC/HCS/N2105: Make intricate items such as Baskets, Trays and Hand purses etc.
- 6. WBSC/HCS/N2106: Set up a small unit for water hyacinth handicraft products.
- 7. WBSC/HCS/N2107: Work in real job situation with emphasis on basic safety and hazards (Water
Hyacinth Handicrafts Maker).
- 8. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

WBSC/HCS/N2101: Describe water hyacinth potential application.
Description
Describe water hyacinth potential application.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Various parts of the water hyacinth plant.
The problems caused by water hyacinth for fishing communities.
The environmental impacts of water hyacinth.
Basic handicraft items made from water hyacinth.
The economic benefits of using water hyacinth for crafts.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify various parts of the water hyacinth plant.
PC2. Highlight the problems caused by water hyacinth for fishing communities.
PC3. Describe the environmental impacts of water hyacinth.
PC4. Demonstrate basic handicraft items made from water hyacinth.
PC5. Illustrate the economic benefits of using water hyacinth for crafts.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Water Hyacinth and its components.
KU2. How water hyacinth grows and spreads in water bodies.
KU3. Fishing communities problems such as water way blocking, aquatic habitats, entanglement of
fishing nets, difficulty navigating boats etc.
KU4. Its environmental impact such as oxygen level reduction for aquatic life, sunlight blocking
from reaching submerged plants, fish movement hindrance etc.
KU5. Safety precaution at work place.
KU6. The potential for turning water hyacinth into profitable products.
KU7. The benefits to local economies and job creation.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Visit to a water body to observe water bodies and water hyacinth plant.
GS2. Identify the plants key components like stems, leaves and roots.

WBSC/HCS/N2102: Demonstrate the process of harvesting and drying.
Description
Demonstrate the process of harvesting and drying.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Usage of the right tools for harvesting water hyacinth.
Separating stems from leaves and roots.
Arranging stems properly for drying.
Turning stems at the right time for even drying.
Checking moisture to prevent spoilage.
Signs of good and bad drying.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Use the right tools for harvesting water hyacinth.
PC2. Separate stems from leaves and roots.
PC3. Arrange stems properly for drying.
PC4. Turn stems at the right time for even drying.
PC5. Check moisture to prevent spoilage.
PC6. Identify signs of good and bad drying.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Safe harvesting techniques and tool usage.
KU2. The process of separating stems from leaves and roots.
KU3. The method of drying the stems.
KU4. The importance of turning stems periodically during drying.
KU5. The effect of drying time on quality of the stems.
KU6. Moisture effect on product durability.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Demonstrate the process of harvesting the plant.
GS2. Collect the plant from the water and remove mud, debris from it.
GS3. Cut leave and roots off the stems.

WBSC/HCS/N2103: Apply dye on water hyacinth stem.
Description
Apply dye on water hyacinth stem.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Setting up of the boiling apparatus correctly.
Measuring the right amount of dye to the water.
Submerging water hyacinth stems evenly in the dye bath.
Maintenance of proper boiling time for uniform color.
Wash stems properly to remove excess dye.
Drying of the dyed stems correctly for best results.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Set up the boiling apparatus correctly.
PC2. Measure the right amount of dye to the water.
PC3. Submerge water hyacinth stems evenly in the dye bath.
PC4. Maintain proper boiling time for uniform color.
PC5. Wash stems properly to remove excess dye.
PC6. Dry the dyed stems correctly for best results.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The steps involved in dyeing: boiling water, measuring dye, adding stems, removing excess
color.
KU2. Dye ratio and timing for quality results.
KU3. How boiling time affects color depth and evenness.
KU4. The steps for washing stems post- dye to remove excess color.
KU5. The methods for achieving even color distribution.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Set up boiling apparatus and boil the water.
GS2. Add color into the hot water.
GS3. Maintain correct dye-to-water ratios for desired color.

WBSC/HCS/N2104: Make basic craft items like ropes, coasters etc.
Description
Make basic craft items like ropes, coasters etc.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Making of ropes by twisting & braiding water hyacinth stems.
Creating chain designs using loops and knots.
Weaving a flat surface for placemats or table mats.
Usage of coil weaving to make a small basket.
Forming circular mats for coasters with a flat base.
Braiding and stitching of stems to create a simple bag or purse.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Make ropes by twisting & braiding water hyacinth stems.
PC2. Create chain designs using loops and knots.
PC3. Weave a flat surface for placemats or table mats.
PC4. Use coil weaving to make a small basket.
PC5. Form circular mats for coasters with a flat base.
PC6. Braid and stitch stems to create a simple bag or purse.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The process involved in rope and chain making.
KU2. Versatility in product design using these techniques.
KU3. Weaving and braiding methods for varied shapes.
KU4. The steps for making items like coasters, mats, hand bags etc.
KU5. Design options including color combinations and patterns.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Practice on making ropes by twisting and braiding.
GS2. Make chain designs using loops and knots.
GS3. Create small items of different colors and designs.
GS4. Create a flat woven surface for placemats or table mats.

| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |
