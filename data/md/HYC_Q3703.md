HYC/Q3703: Marketing Manager (Oil and Gas)
Brief Job Description
The Sales and Marketing Manager in the Oil & Gas sector is responsible for conducting market research
and analysis to identify opportunities and trends, developing and implementing effective sales strategies,
and managing customer relationships to ensure satisfaction. This role involves gathering and interpreting
data related to customer preferences and competitor activities, formulating sales plans to meet
organizational goals, and collaborating with marketing teams to align promotional activities. The manager
also ensures customer satisfaction through effective communication, problem resolution, and feedback
collection, working closely with various departments to optimize sales processes and outcomes.
Personal Attributes
The individual should possess strong analytical and strategic thinking skills, with a keen understanding of
market dynamics. Excellent communication and persuasion abilities are essential, along with the capability
to build and maintain customer relationships. The person should be a proactive problem solver, able to
work both independently and as part of a team. Good organizational skills, time management, and a
customer-centric approach are crucial for success in this role. Adaptability and resilience in a fast-paced
environment are also important attributes.

HYC/N3704: Conduct Market Research and Analysis
Description
This unit involves conducting market research and analysis specifically tailored to the sales and marketing
needs within the oil and gas industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conducting comprehensive market research and analysis relevant to sales and marketing strategies.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify market trends and opportunities for sales and marketing initiatives.
PC2. Gather data from various sources to analyze market dynamics and customer preferences.
PC3. Analyse competitor activities and their impact on the market.
PC4. Develop insights and recommendations based on market analysis findings.
PC5. Present findings and recommendations to relevant stakeholders.
PC6. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to align market research with sales and marketing
strategies.
PC7. Utilize qualitative and quantitative research methods to gather comprehensive data on
consumer behaviour and market trends.
PC8. Interpret statistical data and trends to identify key insights and opportunities for strategic
decision-making.
PC9. Conduct SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis to assess market
positioning and competitive advantage.
PC10. Utilize market segmentation techniques to identify target customer segments and tailor
marketing strategies accordingly.
PC11. Stay updated on industry developments, emerging technologies, and regulatory changes
affecting the market landscape.
PC12. Develop and maintain databases or CRM systems to organize and analyse market data
effectively.
PC13. Evaluate the effectiveness of marketing campaigns and initiatives through data analysis and
performance metrics.
PC14. Identify potential risks and challenges in the market and propose mitigation strategies.
PC15. Collaborate with product development teams to incorporate market insights into product
innovation and development processes.
PC16. Monitor and assess customer feedback, reviews, and sentiment to gauge brand perception
and identify areas for improvement.
PC17. Conduct pricing analysis and competitive benchmarking to optimize pricing strategies and
maximize profitability.

HYC/N3705: Maintain Records for Sales and Marketing Activities
Description
This unit involves maintaining records related to sales and marketing activities for effective tracking and
analysis.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintaining records of sales and marketing activities.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Prepare records of new clients or customers, including relevant details such as contact
information, preferences, and interaction history.
PC2. Update records of existing clients or customers to reflect any changes, such as updated
contact information or purchasing preferences.
PC3. Maintain records of sales transactions, including details of products or services sold,
quantities, prices, and payment terms.
PC4. Update records of marketing campaigns and initiatives, including details of target audience,
channels used, and outcomes achieved.
PC5. Calculate and maintain records of sales performance metrics, such as conversion rates,
revenue generated, and return on investment.
PC6. Maintain records of customer feedback and complaints, including actions taken to address
them and outcomes.
PC7. Record and update the status of ongoing sales and marketing projects, including timelines,
milestones, and key deliverables.
PC8. Submit reports on sales and marketing activities to relevant stakeholders, including
management and other departments as required.
PC9. Ensure compliance with data protection regulations and organizational policies regarding the
handling and storage of customer information.
PC10. Monitor inventory levels and update records of product availability, backorders, and
replenishment schedules to support sales efforts.
PC11. Track and record customer interactions across various communication channels, such as
phone calls, emails, and social media messages.
PC12. Document competitive analysis findings, including pricing strategies, product features, and
market positioning, to inform sales and marketing strategies
PC13. Maintain records of lead generation efforts, including sources of leads, lead qualification
criteria, and follow-up activities.
PC14. Capture and analyze demographic data and market segmentation information to tailor
marketing messages and campaigns effectively.

HYC/N3706: Process Market Data and Finalize Sales and Marketing
Strategies
Description
This unit is about processing market data and finalizing sales and marketing strategies.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Process market data and develop sales and marketing strategies.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Collect and organize relevant market data, including customer demographics, purchasing
behavior, and industry trends.
PC2. Analyze market data to determine potential opportunities and threats.
PC3. Develop sales and marketing strategies based on the analyzed data, aligning with
organizational goals.
PC4. Prepare detailed reports on market analysis findings and proposed strategies.
PC5. Present market analysis and strategy recommendations to key stakeholders.
PC6. Implement approved sales and marketing strategies, ensuring alignment with organizational
objectives.
PC7. Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of implemented strategies, making adjustments as
necessary.
PC8. Maintain a database of market research, sales data, and customer feedback for ongoing
analysis.
PC9. Ensure compliance with relevant legal and regulatory requirements in all sales and
marketing activities.
PC10. Utilize computer software to manage and analyze market data, track performance, and
generate reports.
PC11. Conduct competitor analysis to identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats
(SWOT) in the market landscape.
PC12. Develop pricing strategies based on market demand, competitive positioning, and product
value proposition.
PC13. Create targeted marketing campaigns tailored to specific customer segments identified
through market analysis.
PC14. Collaborate with product development teams to align product offerings with market needs
and preferences.
PC15. Implement feedback mechanisms to gather insights from sales representatives, customers,
and other stakeholders for continuous improvement.
PC16. Establish key performance indicators (KPIs) and benchmarks to measure the success of sales
and marketing strategies over time.
