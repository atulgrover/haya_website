CII/THC/Q1301: Fundamental Course in Rooms Division
Brief Job Description
Individuals in this role will be responsible for efficient housekeeping and laundry operations within the
hospitality industry. This qualification, of EHL (Ecole hoteliere de Lausanne) hospitality standards, is
designed to equip individuals with comprehensive skills and knowledge required to perform housekeeping
operations at an optimum level. It emphasizes the importance of maintaining high standards of
cleanliness, operational efficiency, and guest satisfaction.
Personal Attributes
Attention to detail and accuracy in service and inventory handling Good communication skills with a
customer-focused approach Professional grooming, hygiene, and ethical conduct Ability to work in a team
with responsibility and discipline Time management, adaptability, and problem-solving skills

CII/THC/N1301: Introduction to Laundry Operations
Description
This NOS describes the knowledge, skills, and abilities required to assist in laundry operations within a
professional setting. It covers maintaining workflow efficiency, managing supplies, following quality and
safety standards, operating equipment, and ensuring proper handling of fabrics. The role emphasizes
hygiene, attention to detail, and effective communication to support smooth laundry processes.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This learner will be able to assist in end-to-end laundry operations, including handling and cleaning
of linen, operating laundry equipment, and applying appropriate cleaning techniques. It involves
managing supply inventory, supporting quality control, ensuring safe use of chemicals, and
maintaining hygiene and safety standards.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand and participate in laundry operations, assisting in maintaining smooth processes
and gaining familiarity with the overall workflow.
PC2. Assist in managing the inventory of laundry supplies by learning to track stock levels and
initiating the ordering process when replacements are necessary
PC3. Support the implementation and monitoring of quality control procedures, ensuring that
laundry outputs meet established standards
PC4. Follow protocols for replacing, storing, and rotating linen, with attention to maintaining
hygiene and correct inventory levels
PC5. Apply appropriate cleaning techniques tailored to various types of linen, ensuring effective
stain removal and preservation of fabric quality
PC6. Operate laundry equipment, such as washing machines, dryers, and ironing machines,
following operational guidelines to develop proficiency
PC7. Adhere to health and safety protocols during the cleaning process to prevent contamination
and ensure the safety of oneself and colleagues
PC8. Measure and use cleaning agents accurately, according to manufacturer instructions, to
achieve effective cleaning while maintaining fabric integrity
PC9. Accurately read and interpret care labels on various fabrics, applying the appropriate
washing, drying, and ironing techniques as indicated
PC10. Follow established procedures for the safe mixing and dilution of chemicals, ensuring
compliance with safety standards and avoiding hazardous reactions
PC11. Manage and dispose of laundry waste properly, following guidelines for the safe disposal of
chemicals and non-recyclable materials
PC12. Develop effective communication skills to report any issues with laundry operations,
equipment, or supplies to supervisors and team members

CII/THC/N1302: Introduction to Front Office Operations
Description
This NOS outlines the knowledge and skills required to assist in front office operations. It covers handling
guest interactions, managing reservations, check-in and check-out processes, maintaining records, and
providing accurate information and services.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS ensures smooth coordination with guests and staff, proper record-keeping, and friendly,
professional customer service for a positive guest experience.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the role and significance of the front office within the overall hotel operations.
PC2. Understand and describe the history and classification of hotels, including their various types
and categories.
PC3. Utilize hotel management software (such as Opera) and systems accurately to manage
reservations, billing, and guest information
PC4. Analyze the target markets for different types of hotels and understand their specific needs
and preferences
PC5. Apply front office procedures appropriately for different types of hotels, ensuring consistency
and accuracy
PC6. Demonstrate the ability to communicate clearly and effectively with guests, colleagues, and
management
PC7. Exhibit active listening skills to accurately understand and address guest needs and
preferences
PC8. Respond appropriately to guest inquiries, requests, and complaints, ensuring they feel heard
and valued
PC9. Utilize classification information to enhance guest satisfaction by aligning their expectations
with the hotel’s offerings
PC10. Identify and categorize various types of guests, such as business travelers, tourists, families,
and VIPs
PC11. Accurately identify and describe various types of hotel rooms, including their features and
amenities
PC12. Listen, understand, and address guest complaints promptly and professionally
PC13. Accurately document guest complaints and the steps taken to resolve them
PC14. Report complaints to the appropriate department or supervisor for further action if necessary
PC15. Clearly describe the various positions within the Rooms Division, including their
responsibilities and hierarchical relationships

CII/THC/N1303: Introduction to Housekeeping Operations
Description
This NOS describes the knowledge and skills needed to support housekeeping operations. It includes
cleaning and maintaining guest rooms and public areas, handling linen and supplies.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS ensures proper hygiene, safe use of cleaning agents and equipment, and delivery of neat,
well-maintained spaces for a pleasant guest experience.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Coordinate daily housekeeping activities to ensure timely and high-quality service delivery.
PC2. Allocate resources effectively to maintain cleanliness and order throughout the hotel,
including managing staff assignments and supplies
PC3. Assist in providing training and development opportunities for housekeeping staff to improve
their skills and efficiency
PC4. Conduct regular inspections of guest rooms and public areas to maintain consistency and
high standards in housekeeping quality
PC5. Implement cost-effective measures for the use and replenishment of housekeeping materials
to optimize resource utilization
PC6. Optimize housekeeping processes to enhance productivity and service quality, including the
use of efficient cleaning techniques and time management
PC7. Set up all guest rooms according to standard content guidelines, ensuring that all amenities
and furnishings are arranged correctly
PC8. Manage the inventory of guest room components, including linens, toiletries, and amenities,
to ensure timely replacement and replenishment
PC9. Implement and maintain security measures to protect both staff and guests, including
procedures for safeguarding personal belongings and hotel property
PC10. Implement proper waste disposal methods to prevent contamination and ensure adherence
to health and safety regulations
PC11. Demonstrate correct usage techniques for various cleaning equipment, including vacuum
cleaners, mops, and disinfectants
PC12. Follow established protocols for reporting and addressing maintenance issues or damage
observed during housekeeping duties
PC13. Maintain high standards of personal hygiene, including regular handwashing and the correct
use of personal protective equipment (PPE)
PC14. Perform cleaning and sanitization tasks following established hygiene protocols to ensure a
clean and safe environment

Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to manage front office operations in hospitality,
interdepartmental coordination, and use of Property Management Systems (PMS) like Opera. The role
emphasizes professionalism, effective communication, personalized guest service, and operational
efficiency.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS includes handling guest inquiries and reservations, managing accounts and payments,
coordinating with hotel departments, ensuring security and service standards, and maintaining front
office records and systems for a seamless guest experience.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify and describe different functions of Front Office Operations, including the three
modes of guest contact, and ensure effective communication throughout all interactions.
PC2. Answer telephone calls within 3 rings and 10 seconds, greet the guest using their name, and
route calls to the appropriate person, maintaining professional and friendly communication.

channels, and maintain reservation records, ensuring all details are accurately captured and
organized.
PC4. Prepare and follow a comprehensive checklist for Reservations Agents, including taking room
reservations as per SOP, determining room rates, and preparing confirmation letters.
PC5. Operate according to the hotel’s policy on guaranteed reservations and no-shows, and
manage room availability, revenue forecasting, and occupancy forecasting accurately.
PC6. Monitor advance deposit requirements, handle daily correspondence, and respond to guest
inquiries promptly to ensure a seamless reservation experience.
PC7. Maintain a clean and neat appearance and work area, and promote goodwill by being
courteous, friendly, and helpful to guests, managers, and fellow employees.
PC8. Perform room reservations using the Property Management System (PMS) and Opera,
including creating new company profiles, handling various types of reservations, and
maintaining accurate data.
PC9. Follow the 7-steps of the registration process, provide a warm welcome to guests, and
perform check-in functions in Opera, ensuring a personalized and efficient service.
PC10. Escort guests to their rooms, offering personalized service by pointing out hotel facilities,
assisting with belongings, explaining room features, and performing cross-sell opportunities.
PC11. Ensure effective communication and coordination with interdepartmental teams, including
Bellhop, Concierge, Housekeeping, Engineering & Maintenance, and Food & Beverage, to
maximize guest satisfaction during occupancy.

Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to effectively manage goods, inventory, and
environmental practices within a hospitality organization while ensuring compliance with sustainability
standards and legal requirements. It also includes the ability to use office tools and digital applications to
create, manage, and communicate operational information efficiently. The role focuses on waste
management, energy conservation, eco-friendly operations, inventory control, preventive maintenance,
and professional documentation using standard office software.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The learner will be able to implement effective waste management practices including segregation,
disposal, and compliance with environmental regulations, while applying sustainability principles
such as zero waste, energy conservation, and use of eco-friendly products. They will monitor and
manage inventory through stock-taking, maintaining par levels, and classifying goods, along with
ensuring proper handling, storage, and disposal of cleaning chemicals and hazardous materials. The

goods and environmental practices, and utilize office tools such as Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and

documentation practices effectively within hospitality operations.

tasks while maintaining safety, hygiene, and service quality standards.

departments, including guestroom cleaning, laundry processing, and basic guest handling. The role
involves maintaining professional grooming and conduct, assisting in inventory and record-keeping,
and supporting interdepartmental coordination. The learner will also follow safety, hygiene, and
environmental practices while participating in briefings and training sessions, with a focus on
developing competence and gradually handling responsibilities independently.
