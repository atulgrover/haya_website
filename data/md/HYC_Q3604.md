HYC/Q3604: Industrial Safety Associate (Oil and Gas)
Brief Job Description
Individual at this job is responsible for undertaking appropriate safety and emergency management
measures in the industry, through maintaining ergonomic principles identification of occupational and
environmental hazards, safety measures developing skills regarding posture assessment, subjective
quantification of body part discomfort, analysis of accidents ensuring precautionary measures to prevent
transmission of pathogens supporting medical professionals in assessing physiological status of individual
in industry performing first aid for all levels of injury/illness including those injuries sustained at various
workplaces.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have comprehensive knowledge and skills regarding integrated aspects of industrial
safety and emergency management. The person should be digitally literate. The individual should have the
ability to communicate effectively with others. The person should have the ability to work in a team,
maintain excellent interpersonal relationships and professional behaviour.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HYC/N3610: Identification of occupational and environmental hazards, risk factors for accident and
injury, occupational safety measures
- 2. HYC/N3611: Maintain ergonomic principles in the industry to improve efficiency and productivity
- 3. HYC/N3612: Follow infection prevention and control measures safety measures and emergency
preparedness in industry
- 4. HYC/N3613: Support medical professionals in measurement of vital parameters and undertaking
examination of individual in industry
- 5. HYC/N3614: Perform cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) and first aid for trauma and medical
emergency
- 6. HYC/N3615: Undertake activities related to computer, tablet, software, internet usage
- 7. HYC/N9301: Working effectively in a team
- 8. HYC/N9302: Maintain health, safety and security procedures
- 9. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

HYC/N3610: Identification of occupational and environmental hazards,
risk factors for accident and injury, occupational safety measures
Description
This unit covers identification of chemical, mechanical, biological, electrical and toxic material related
occupational hazards and various environmental hazards such as noise, heat and cold stress, vibration,
illumination etc, which may lead to accidents and injury evaluate accident implement appropriate remedial
measures to maintain healthy, safe and secure work environment
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identification of occupational and environment hazards in work place
Identification of postural stressors posture analysis assessment of body part discomfort
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identification of occupational and environment hazards in work place; occupational safety measures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify occupational hazards with special emphasis on hazardous chemicals (HAZCHEM
code)
PC2. Identify sources of chemical disasters and implement chemical disaster management
techniques
PC3. understand medical preparedness and emergency medical responses in disasters
PC4. identify environmental hazards in industry
PC5. understand the adverse impact of various occupational and environmental stressors
PC6. support in implementation of remedial measures
Identification of postural stressors; posture analysis; assessment of body part discomfort
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Identify risk factors for postural stress in industry
PC8. operate relevant software for analysis of working posture and evaluate the risk for
developing musculoskeletal symptoms
PC9. assess body part discomfort using standardized method
PC10. use techniques for subjective quantification of perceived exertion and stress
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. concept of occupational safety principles
KU2. various occupational and environmental hazards
KU3. chemical disaster management (Five Employer Requirements)

HYC/N3611: Maintain ergonomic principles in the industry to improve
efficiency and productivity
Description
This unit covers the aspects of preservation of precious resource of human health in a safe environment
through application of ergonomic principles in industry to improve efficiency of individual and enhance
productivity of the system and reducing postural stress and musculoskeletal disorders.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Application of ergonomic principles in industry
Identification of hazards associated with manual material handling and remedial measures
Perform various anthropometric measurements to support in designing of workplace and
assessment of nutritional status of individual
Elements and Performance Criteria
Application of ergonomic principles in industry
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. understand the concept of human factors
PC2. provide support in ergonomic assessment of the workplace
PC3. follow ergonomic principles in the workplace
PC4. identify ergonomic risk factors for developing musculoskeletal disorders
PC5. understand various cognitive abilities of the individual and their relationship with
efficiency and productivity
Identification of the ergonomic hazards associated with manual material handling and remedial measures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. identify the ergonomic hazards associated with manual material handling (MMH)
PC7. support in assessment of physiological workload due to MMH
PC8. conduct awareness session on recommended techniques for performing tasks
associated with MMH
Perform various anthropometric measurements to support in designing of workplace and assessment of
nutritional status of individual
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. undertake anthropometric measurements
PC10. operate various equipment used for measurement of anthropometric parameters
PC11. derive anthropometric indices based on measurements
PC12. develop the concept of ergonomic design of workplace
PC13. assess nutritional status of the individual
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

HYC/N3612: Follow infection prevention and control measures safety
measures and emergency preparedness in industry
Description
This unit is about various measures for ensuring standard precautions to prevent the transmission of
pathogen, safety of the personnel in industry and preparedness for rescue, relief and remedial measures in
emergency conditions.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding of various types of pathogens
Practice standard precautions in industry
Practice safety measures in industry
Understanding of categorization of victims during mass casualty incident
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding of various types of pathogens

PC1. understand route of transmission of pathogens
PC2. understand the concept of incubation period, infectivity, pathogenicity and virulence
PC3. understand example of various diseases developed due to various types of pathogens
Practice infection prevention and control measures in industry
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. perform hand washing and hand rubbing as per WHO guidelines
PC5. perform wearing and removing of PPE in an appropriate order
PC6. ensure appropriate disposal, transport and treatment of biomedical waste
PC7. perform safe handling of needle and syringe
PC8. undertake management of needle stick injury
PC9. undertake spillage management
Practice safety and emergency preparedness in industry
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. undertake mass evacuation
PC11. perform triage
PC12. Implement fire safety principles
PC13. support in resuscitation and safe transportation
PC14. use of PPE at the incident site

HYC/N3613: Support medical professionals in measurement of vital
parameters and undertaking examination of individual in industry
Description
This unit is about understanding the significance of vital signs and physical examination parameters
techniques required to check the parameters and possible interpretations of the results.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding of vital signs and skill-based techniques to undertake general examination of
personnel
Undertake appropriate steps for primary survey
Ensure supporting role in secondary assessment of personnel
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding of vital signs and skill-based techniques to undertake general examination
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. assist medical professional in measurement of vital signs and understand their normal range
PC2. identify abnormality in vital parameters and report to appropriate authority
PC3. operate medical devices used to measure vital signs
PC4. support medical professional in general examination
Undertake appropriate steps for primary survey
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. assess safety of the scene
PC6. employ AVPU scale to check the responsiveness
PC7. able to activate emergency response system
PC8. assess airway, breathing and circulation
Ensure supporting role in secondary assessment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. assist medical professional in taking history of the individual
PC10. assist medical professional in various methods associated with physical examination
PC11. assist medical professional in head-to-toe trauma assessment
PC12. identify possible reasons for unconsciousness or altered level of consciousness and support
medical professional
PC13. identify possible symptoms for poison ingestion and support medical professional
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Career Development & Goal Setting
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs
