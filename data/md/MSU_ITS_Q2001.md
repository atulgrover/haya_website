#### MSU/ITS/Q2001: AI Ethics Specialist
Brief Job Description
The qualification is intended for individuals aspiring to work as AI ethics specialists or those engaged in AI
system development and deployment. It covers stakeholder engagement, collaboration with technical
experts, ethical risk assessment, the application of AI ethics frameworks, and conducting ethical impact
assessments. This qualification equips professionals, including AI developers, data scientists, and
interdisciplinary groups, with the practical skills to navigate ethical considerations in AI projects. It fosters
ethical awareness, communication, and responsible AI practices in a rapidly evolving landscape.
Personal Attributes
To excel as an AI Ethics Specialist, one must possess a strong sense of ethical integrity, critical thinking,
and effective communication skills to navigate complex ethical considerations in AI. A collaborative
mindset, adaptability, and empathy are crucial for engaging with diverse stakeholders and ensuring
responsible AI practices. The ability to assess ethical risks and apply AI ethics frameworks with
transparency and foresight is essential in this rapidly evolving field.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MSU/ITS/N2003: Identify and Assess the Ethical Risks of AI Systems
2. MSU/ITS/N2002: Develop and Implement Ethical Guidelines and Policies for AI Development and Use
3. MSU/ITS/N1001: Facilitate Ethical Communication and Educational Outreach with Stakeholders
4. MSU/ITS/N2001: Collaborate with Cross-Functional Teams for Ethical AI Development
5. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

#### MSU/ITS/N2003: Identify and Assess the Ethical Risks of AI Systems
Description
This OS unit is about engaging stakeholder to obtain ethical insights, assess ethical risks, analyse ethical
impact and to recommend mitigation strategies.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Engage Stakeholders and Obtain Ethical Insights
Assess Ethical Risks
Analyse Ethical Impact and Recommend Mitigation Strategies
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Engage Stakeholders and Obtain Ethical Insights
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify and map key stakeholders in AI development, deployment, and regulation,
illustrating their roles, ethical concerns, and perspective
- PC2. Collaborate with various stakeholders, including AI developers, legal teams, and technical
experts, to gain insights into the technical aspects of AI systems and identify potential ethical risks
- PC3. Forge robust relationships with senior executives and CxOs, leveraging Data Science
background to connect on technical and strategic levels
- PC4. Collaborate with the interdisciplinary team to identify synergies between Data Science and
Responsible AI for client strategies
- PC5. Conduct thorough consultations to comprehend client needs, leveraging Data Science
acumen to decipher complex requirements
- PC6. Provide expert guidance on embedding ethical considerations in AI development and
governance strategies
- PC7. Deliver tailor-made solutions that integrate Data Science and AI ethics, aligning them with
client's unique objectives
Assess Ethical Risks
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Research and analyse the potential ethical risks associated with AI systems
- PC9. Assess the data privacy and security aspects of AI systems to ensure that they respect
privacy rights, protect data, and have robust security measures in place
- PC10. Examine the data used to train AI algorithms and evaluate potential biases and fairness
issues that may arise in AI systems
- PC11. Evaluate the social and societal impacts of AI systems by assessing the potential
consequences for employment, inequality, human rights, and environmental sustainability

#### MSU/ITS/N2002: Develop and Implement  Ethical Guidelines and Policies
for AI Development and Use
Description
This OS unit is about staying updated in AI ethics, crafting and communicating ethical standards and
guidelines and ensuring ethical integration and compliance in AI Development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Stay Updated in AI Ethics
Craft and Communicate Ethical Standards and Guidelines for AI
Ensure Ethical Integration and Compliance in AI Development
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Stay Updated in AI Ethics
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Monitor and analyse the latest developments in AI ethics, encompassing emerging trends
and controversies
- PC2. Engage with AI ethics forums, conferences, and online communities to exchange insights and
perspectives
- PC3. Participate in continuous learning through webinars, podcasts, and reading materials on AI
ethics
- PC4. Collaboratively map out the ethical implications of evolving AI technologies with fellow
professionals
Craft and Communicate Ethical Standards and Guidelines for AI
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Define and communicate ethical standards for responsible AI development, including fairness
and transparency
- PC6. Formulate clear and comprehensive ethical guidelines for responsible AI development and
deployment
- PC7. Align ethical guidelines with international standards, legal frameworks, cultural values, and
social, political, and environmental impact
- PC8. Ensure adherence to ethical principles in AI projects and applications
- PC9. Communicate ethical guidelines effectively to stakeholders involved in AI projects
Ensure Ethical Integration and Compliance in AI Development
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Collaborate with technical teams to ensure ethical considerations are seamlessly integrated
into Data Science and AI projects
- PC11. Design and implement AI systems with a strong emphasis on transparency, fairness,
accountability, and privacy, utilizing your Data Science expertise
