SVO/FIC/Q0101: Basic Sweets Maker
Brief Job Description
Odisha has a very rich culture of sweets. The state has indigenously prepared sweets and has claimed for
GI tag. There is a good market of sweets and good number of Sweets making Industries are running. In the
absence of skilled manpower or technician the entire work is managed by unskilled personnel. This course
has been designed to generate Skilled man power in the field of Sweets making with all associated
knowledge and skill of handling of tools and equipment, hygiene and safety. The employment opportunity
in this domain is higher. It would be a long-term course of one-year duration.
Personal Attributes
Basic Sweets Maker
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. SVO/FIC/N0101: Sweets Production Preparation
- 2. SVO/FIC/N0102: Sweets Making

SVO/FIC/N0101: Sweets Production Preparation
Description
Sweets production preparation, often referred to as confectionery, involves a variety of processes
depending on the specific sweet being made. Generally, it includes ingredient selection and preparation,
mixing and cooking, shaping and molding, and finally, cooling, packaging, and storage.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The "Sweets Production Preparation" NOS (National Occupational Standard) is the primary stage of
Sweet making in which the entire production line is prepared.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Summarize the key role and responsibilities of “Sweets Maker”.
PC2. Explain the layout and arrangement of Sweets Workshop
PC3. Explain milk-based sweets, flours base sweets, vegetables, fruits base sweets and their
characteristics
PC4. Summarize the sweeteners both natural and artificial.
PC5. Explain about canning, packaging, labelling, storage,
PC6. Demonstrate Maintenance of equipment in Sweet industry
PC7. Discuss about personal hygiene and safety.
PC8. Identify the markets of Sweets and career opportunities
PC9. Demonstrate the arrangements of machinery
PC10. Identify/familiarize Tools and equipments and their usage
PC11. Identify/familiarize commonly used food additives used in sweets
PC12. Demonstrate grooming and personal hygiene
PC13. Do’s and Don’ts in Sweets industry workshop
PC14. Perform Elementary First Aid
PC15. Maintain cleanliness and hygiene
PC16. Practice of Fire fighting and use of Fire Extinguisher
PC17. Identify different raw materials for different sweet products
PC18. Demonstrate ratio and proportions of quantities of ingredients.
PC19. Demonstrate product preparation flow chart
PC20. Demonstrate product pre-preparation preservation required for some ingredients
PC21. Determine physical and chemical properties of milk and other materials
PC22. Demonstrate different types of oil and fats used for sweet making
PC23. Identify different raw materials for different sweet products

SVO/FIC/N0102: Sweets Making
Description
A sweets making describes the skills and knowledge required for various tasks related to sweet production
in the food processing industry. This includes planning production, maintaining work areas and equipment,
and organizing for the manufacturing process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope for sweet-making is vast, encompassing diverse cultural traditions, technological
advancements, and evolving consumer preferences. It's a global industry with strong roots in both
traditional practices and modern innovations. The rise of vegan, gluten-free, and allergy-friendly
options has created new markets for specialized sweets.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Test the properties and quality of milk.
PC2. Arrange Milk based products and other ingredients required inappropriate proportions.
PC3. Identify different Flavoring agents
PC4. Identify different Rising agents
PC5. Practice and perform milk based sweets
PC6. Practice and perform Flour based sweets
PC7. Practice and perform vegetable and Fruit based sweets
PC8. Demonstrate how to cool the products
PC9. Demonstrate leak detecting method with instruments
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Hygiene requirements and standards relevant to food processing unit.
KU2. Standards and procedures followed in the organisation for cleaning process and to disinfect
equipment/ tools.
KU3. Job responsibilities/duties to maintain hygiene standards.
KU4. Know how to properly clean and sanitise equipment and utensils.
KU5. Knowledge of food safety standards and regulations (as per FSSAI)
KU6. Determine the use of different raw materials required in making sweet
KU7. Prepare raw materials required in making sweet
KU8. Demonstrate modern and traditional methods of Sweet making

SVO/FIC/N0103: Sweets preservation, Packing and Labeling
Description
The "Sweets preservation, Packing and Labeling" NOS (National Occupational Standard) is an important
part of the sweet manufacturing process. It protects food products from physical, chemical, and biological
damage along with enhancing the outlook. Many reasons are considered for Preservation, Packing and
Labeling of a given product such as display, convenience, handling, sale, security concerns, barrier
protection, physical protection, information transmission, agglomeration or containment, marketing and
finally portion control.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the following :
Ensure food safety and personal hygiene
Manage infection control
Provides information details regarding the Sweets.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify Commonly used packaging materials
PC2. Perform how to sterilize the canned products.
PC3. Perform how to pack using appropriate methods and machines appropriately.
PC4. Demonstrate canning of wet and dry products
PC5. Demonstrate the use of preservatives in different types of sweets
PC6. Demonstrate the standard practice followed in Labeling
PC7. Demonstrate how to do the maintenance of machines and equipments
PC8. Determine Acidity and ph.
PC9. Determine reducing sugar, sucrose, starch
PC10. Identify food components with respect to proteins, minerals, enzyme, carbohydrates, fatty
acid etc.
PC11. Identify the different Microorganism in Sweets
PC12. Demonstrate the culture of Microorganism
PC13. Demonstrate food toxification effects
PC14. Demonstrate how to control microorganism
PC15. Demonstrate segregation of waste into different categories
PC16. Perform how to dispose recyclable and non recyclable waste
PC17. Perform how to dispose hazardous waste safely
PC18. Determine Acidity and ph.
PC19. Determine Adulteration of Milk and other ingredients
