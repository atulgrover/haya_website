#### CUTM/AGR/Q0601: Pollinator Habitat Maker
Brief Job Description
A Pollinator Habitat Maker will be responsible for fabricating and maintaining natural habitat for supporting
and sustaining biological species causing pollination. Broadly, the job of the designer will include designing
the landscape of the habitat using appropriate methods, selection of plant species, selection of pollinating
species and strategies for the conservation of habitat. The work will further include engaging the public to
raise awareness about managing pollination, pollinators and pollinator habitat.
Personal Attributes
A Pollinator Habitat Maker should be environmentally conscious, patient, and attentive to ecological
details. They must possess a strong interest in biodiversity, plant-pollinator interactions, and sustainable
habitat design. Good observational skills, creativity in planting layout, and a commitment to conservation
are essential. Communication abilities are important for spreading awareness and engaging communities
in pollinator-friendly practices.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CUTM/AGR/N0601: Pollination, Pollinators, habitat designing

5. CUTM/AGR/N0605: Health and Hygiene in pollinator habitat

#### CUTM/AGR/N0601: Pollination, Pollinators, habitat designing
Description
This NOS focuses on focuses on understanding the role of pollinators in agriculture and biodiversity. It
covers the study of different pollinators, their habitat requirements, and the impact of environmental
factors on their survival. The standard includes designing and maintaining pollinator-friendly habitats,
selecting native plants, and implementing conservation strategies. It also emphasizes sustainable farming
practices, ecological balance, and pollination management techniques to enhance crop productivity and
biodiversity conservation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS focuses the study and management of pollinators essential for biodiversity and agricultural
productivity. It includes designing and maintaining pollinator-friendly habitats, selecting native plant
species, and implementing conservation strategies. The scope extends to ecological balance, sustainable farming practices, and mitigating threats like habitat loss and pesticides. It also involves
monitoring pollinator populations, enhancing natural pollination, and ensuring compliance with environmental regulations for long-term sustainability.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Definition of pollination, importance of pollination.
- PC2. Types of pollination, self and cross pollination
- PC3. Mechanism of pollination.
- PC4. Basic requirement of design of pollinator paradise
- PC5. Aspects required for site selection for the new habitat
- PC6. Methods of planting new habitat.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understanding the role of pollination in plant reproduction.
- KU2. Types of pollination: self-pollination and cross-pollination and Factors affecting pollination
efficiency.
- KU3. Different types of pollinators (bees, butterflies, birds, bats, etc.).
- KU4. Principles of designing a pollinator-friendly habitat.
- KU5. Sustainable landscaping techniques to support pollinators.
- KU6. Importance of maintaining ecological balance and biodiversity.
- KU7. Organic and eco-friendly farming practices that support pollinators.
- KU8. Conservation laws and policies related to pollinator protection.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0602: Plant species for pollination
Description
This NOS focuses on understanding the role of specific plant species in attracting and supporting
pollinators like bees, butterflies, and birds. It covers the identification, selection, and cultivation of native
plants that enhance pollination efficiency. The standard also includes sustainable habitat management,
conservation strategies, and the impact of environmental factors on plant-pollinator interactions. This NOS
ensures the development of pollinator-friendly ecosystems to improve biodiversity, agricultural
productivity, and ecological balance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS covers the selection, cultivation, and management of plant species that support pollinators
like bees, butterflies, and birds. It includes identifying native plants, enhancing biodiversity, and
designing pollinator-friendly habitats. The scope extends to sustainable agricultural practices,
conservation strategies, and ecological balance. It also focuses on mitigating threats like habitat loss
and pesticide use while promoting pollination for improved crop yield and environmental sustainability.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Specific features of plants attracting pollinators.
- PC2. Suitable nesting sites for pollination.
- PC3. Specific criteria for plant selection
- PC4. Recognition of flowers required for pollination
- PC5. Evaluation of plant shape and size required for selection of plants
- PC6. Role of plant density in effective pollination
- PC7. Native plants and seed sources needed for pollination
- PC8. Methods of planting a pollinator habitat.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Identification of key plant species that attract pollinators.
- KU2. Floral traits that influence pollinator attraction (color, scent, nectar production).
- KU3. Importance of plant diversity in supporting pollinators.
- KU4. Impact of climate, soil, and environmental conditions on plant-pollinator interactions.
- KU5. Effects of pesticides and chemicals on pollination.
- KU6. Sustainable farming practices that support pollination.
- KU7. Selection and cultivation of pollinator-friendly plants.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0603: Pollinator habitat maintenance and conservation
Description
This NOS focuses on the knowledge and skills required to maintain and conserve pollinator habitats. It
includes monitoring habitat health, managing floral diversity, removing invasive species, applying eco-
friendly practices, and ensuring the long-term sustainability of environments that support pollinators such
as bees, butterflies, and other beneficial insects.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS outlines the scope of work involved in maintaining and conserving pollinator habitats. It
includes assessing habitat conditions, preserving native plant species, managing seasonal floral
resources, preventing habitat degradation, and protecting nesting and foraging areas. The scope
emphasizes sustainable practices to enhance biodiversity and ensure the continued presence of essential pollinators in natural and managed landscapes.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Significance of pollinator habitat conservation
- PC2. Maintenance of Flowering forbs, woody species and successional shrubs.
- PC3. Conservation practice for site management of pollinators
- PC4. Practice for pesticide protection
- PC5. Practice for appropriate nesting sites.
- PC6. Practice for forages
- PC7. Government and Environmental organizations associated with conservation of pollinator
habitat.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Methods for controlling invasive species without harming pollinators.
- KU2. Best practices for conserving natural pollinator habitats.
- KU3. Importance of native plant species for long-term sustainability.
- KU4. Techniques to enhance habitat resilience against climate change.
- KU5. Identifying signs of habitat degradation.
- KU6. Understanding policies and laws related to pollinator habitat conservation.
- KU7. Compliance with environmental protection guidelines.
- KU8. Key plant species that support pollinators.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0604: Awareness  on importance of pollinator habitat
Description
This NOS focuses on building awareness about the vital role of pollinators in ecosystems and agriculture. It
involves understanding pollinator species, their interactions with plants, threats to their survival, and
sustainable practices to conserve their habitats. The aim is to promote ecological balance and biodiversity
through informed actions and community engagement.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the scope of understanding the significance of pollinators, recognizing their role in
plant reproduction and food production, and identifying threats to their survival. It includes creating
awareness about pollinator-friendly practices, promoting habitat conservation, and encouraging community participation to protect and enhance pollinator populations for ecological and agricultural
sustainability.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Self-understanding on pollination, pollinators and pollinator habitat.
- PC2. Educational programme at school level for awareness about pollination and pollinators.
- PC3. Use of educative and publicity materials for awareness about pollination, pollinators
- PC4. Corner meeting, workshops and conferences for increasing awareness.
- PC5. Essay and debates at different educational levels and community levels for increasing
awareness about importance of pollination, pollinators and pollinator habitat.
- PC6. Organizing training camps for awareness.
- PC7. Different NGO forums across the world promoting the importance of pollinators, pollination
and making habitat.
- PC8. Debate, and discussion in workplace of employees on general awareness on pollination and
making habitat.
- PC9. Creating digital social media (facebook, twitter etc..) for spreading awareness among public
- PC10. Making short motivational movies for awareness about making and conservation of pollinator
habitat
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand the importance of native and diverse plant species in supporting pollinator populations.
- KU2. Know the consequences of habitat destruction, pesticide use, and climate change on pollinators.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0605: Health and Hygiene in pollinator habitat
Description
This NOS involves the maintenance of proper health and hygiene standards in pollinator habitats to ensure
a safe, clean, and sustainable environment for bees, butterflies, and other beneficial species. It includes
daily cleaning practices, contamination control, use of eco-friendly materials, and awareness of pollinator
behavior to prevent disease and stress.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers cleaning of pollinator enclosures, organic waste disposal, regular inspection of
water and food sources, and maintaining sanitary conditions in both natural and man-made pollinator spaces. It also includes identifying risks and taking proactive steps to ensure optimal living
conditions for pollinators year-round.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Habitat assessment procedure
- PC2. Pollinator population sustainability
- PC3. Criteria for plant species selection
- PC4. General idea on designing the landscape
- PC5. Creating awareness programme on making pollinator habitat
- PC6. Building relationship with Govt and NGOs on making pollinator habitat
- PC7. Threat to pollinator habitat
- PC8. Criteria for restoration of habitat
- PC9. Policies for sustainability of pollinator habitat
- PC10. Conservation strategies to be adopted.
- PC11. Maintenance of sanitation and hygiene
- PC12. Research programme on importance of pollinator habitat
- PC13. Environmental factors affecting the habitat.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Importance of cleanliness and hygiene in pollinator habitats.
- KU2. Basic understanding of pollinators' behavior and their sensitivity to pollutants.
- KU3. Types of harmful chemicals and contaminants that affect pollinators.
- KU4. Techniques for safe removal of waste from habitats.
- KU5. Proper maintenance of bee boxes, hives, and nesting sites.

| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
