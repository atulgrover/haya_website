TPCL/SGJ/Q0301: GREEN ENERGY SUPERVISOR - SOLAR
Brief Job Description
A Green Energy Supervisor Solar specializes in technology, installation, and maintenance &
troubleshooting of photovoltaic (PV) systems to ensure efficient solar energy generation. They are
responsible for implementing system design, installing panels, troubleshooting issues, and performing
routine maintenance to enhance performance and safety. Their role also involves adhering to industry
standards, optimizing energy output, and contributing to the growth of renewable energy solutions.
Personal Attributes
Leadership, Team Management, Attention to Safety & Compliance, Problem-Solving Mindset and Technical
Aptitude
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. TPCL/SGJ/N0303: Understand and implement Safety and Health Practices in Solar PV Projects
- 2. TPCL/SGJ/N0301: Interpret and Apply Electrical Concepts and Wiring
- 3. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
- 4. TPCL/SGJ/N0302: Demonstrate knowledge of Renewable Energy and Execute the Solar PV Plant

TPCL/SGJ/N0303: Understand and implement Safety and Health Practices
in Solar PV Projects
Description
Covers safety practices including electrical safety, working at heights, material handling, fire safety, first
aid, and housekeeping.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Applies to supervising safety and health practices during solar PV installation, O&M, and project
execution.
Elements and Performance Criteria
General Safety
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain general workplace safety guidelines and best practices.
Electrical Safety
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC2. Describe the hazards associated with electrical work.
Safety While Working at Height
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Explain the risks and precautions while working at heights.
Material Handling Safety
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Describe the proper techniques for lifting and transporting materials.
Behavioural Safety
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Explain the importance of a proactive safety culture in the workplace.
Fire Safety
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Explain fire hazards, fire classifications, and firefighting techniques.
First Aid
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Explain the basic principles of first aid and emergency response.
Housekeeping and 5S
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Explain the principles of 5S methodology and its benefits.

TPCL/SGJ/N0301: Interpret and Apply Electrical Concepts and Wiring
Description
Covers fundamentals of electricity, circuit diagrams, wiring practices, and application of electrical concepts
in solar PV systems.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Applies to supervising and implementing basic and advanced electrical wiring for solar PV
installation and maintenance.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Fundamentals of Electricity
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify basic electrical terms: voltage, current, and resistance.
PC2. Explain the use of Ohm’s Law in simple circuits.
PC3. Demonstrate the measurement of voltage, current, and resistance using a multimeter.
Circuit Diagrams and Wiring
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Identify common electrical symbols in circuit diagrams.
PC5. Explain the difference between series and parallel circuits.
PC6. Demonstrate basic electrical wiring for a simple circuit.
PC7. Demonstrate how to check continuity in a circuit using a multimeter.
PC8. Identify safety precautions for electrical wiring.
PC9. Demonstrate proper wire connections and insulation techniques.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand electrical concepts, conductors, insulation, earthing, fuses, circuit diagrams,
electromagnetism.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Ability to perform wiring, use measuring instruments, ensure safety compliance, and
supervise electrical work.

Execute the Solar PV Plant Lifecycle
Description
Focuses on renewable energy basics, solar PV design, installation, commissioning, O&M, troubleshooting,
and performance monitoring.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Applies to supervising and executing end-to-end solar PV project lifecycle from site survey to O&M.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Fundamental Concepts of Renewable Energy
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the different types of renewable energy sources and their significance.
PC2. Describe the advantages and limitations of renewable energy sources.
Fundamentals of Solar Photovoltaic
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Explain the basic working principle of solar photovoltaic technology.
PC4. Describe the different types of solar panels and their efficiency factors.
Tools and Components for Solar PV Systems
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Identify the essential tools required for solar PV installation.
PC6. Explain the function of key electrical and mechanical components in a solar PV system.
Site Survey and Assessment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Explain the importance of conducting a site survey before solar PV installation.
PC8. Demonstrate the use of site survey tools and techniques for solar PV assessment.
Installation and Commissioning of Solar PV Sys
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Explain the step-by-step process of installing a solar PV system.
PC10. Describe the commissioning process and performance verification of a solar PV system.
Operation and Maintenance of Solar PV Systems
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Explain the key maintenance practices for solar PV systems.
PC12. Describe fault detection techniques for solar PV systems.

To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
