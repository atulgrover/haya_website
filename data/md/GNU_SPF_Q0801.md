GNU/SPF/Q0801: Water Sports Assistant Trainer
Brief Job Description
On completion of training candidates shall be able to provide hands on instruction and training to
individual or groups in various water sports activities
Personal Attributes
He or she should qualify in the swimming proficiency test held by the institute.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. GNU/SPF/N0806: Water Safety and Rescue Technique
- 2. GNU/SPF/N0805: Water Sports Equipment Principle and its Operation
- 3. GNU/SPF/N0803: Water sports Ethics and Environmental Awareness
- 4. GNU/SPF/N0802: Swimming Skills
- 5. GNU/SPF/N0801: Introduction to Water Sports and its relevance in present times
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
- 7. GNU/SPF/N0807: Water Sports Teaching Methodologies and significance of safety aspect
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Snorkeling
- 1. GNU/SPF/N0709: Snorkeling
Elective 2: Canoeing and Kayaking
- 1. GNU/SPF/N0710: Kayaking & Canoeing
Elective 3: Windsurfing and Kiteboarding
- 1. GNU/SPF/N0711: Windsurfing & Kiteboarding

GNU/SPF/N0806: Water Safety and Rescue Technique
Description
Water safety and rescue techniques are essential skills for anyone participating in water sports. Training in
water safety and rescue techniques leads to a multitude of positive outcomes, including enhanced safety
awareness, skill development, increased confidence, physical fitness, and community safety. Familiarize
yourself with rescue equipment and basic techniques to operate safely
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Water safety and rescue techniques are essential skills for anyone participating in water sports.
Training in water safety and rescue techniques leads to a multitude of positive outcomes, including
enhanced safety awareness, skill development, increased confidence, physical fitness, and
community safety.
Familiarize yourself with rescue equipment and basic techniques to operate safely

This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs

PC6. work with others in a team

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| :--- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
