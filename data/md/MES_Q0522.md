#### MES/Q0522: Industrial Design Technology - Indiaskills Worldskills
Brief Job Description
Industrial design technology is the creation of a product designed for mass consumption. It must succeed
in both form (appearance) and function, and promote efficient manufacture. It must be technically feasible
to produce, and meet a genuine need in the marketplace, at an acceptable price.
Personal Attributes
The individual in this role must be creative with high degree of professional responsibility and timeliness
with deadlines. He/she in this role has to be open-minded, willing to try new things and comfortable taking
advice from unexpected sources. Individual must not be colour blind at any stage.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### MES/N0571: Work organization and management-   Worldskills
Description
Work organization and management" involves overseeing tasks, processes, and resources within an
organization to ensure efficiency and productivity. Responsibilities include planning, organizing, delegating
tasks, and monitoring progress to achieve organizational goals effectively
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Work organization and management offers a broad scope for individuals to streamline processes, optimize resources, and drive productivity within organizations. Responsibilities include planning,
organizing, delegating tasks, and monitoring progress to ensure efficiency and goal attainment
#### Elements and Performance Criteria

- PC1. Apply safe working methods personally and for others
- PC2. Select and keep to efficient and effective work methods and habits
- PC3. Estimate time requirements for each phase of the design process, and create timelines
- PC4. Select and use appropriate planning and management tools
- PC5. Maintain orderly and secure work areas
- PC6. Maintain work records as required and helpful
- PC7. Minimize distractions that impact on own effectiveness and efficiency
- PC8. Respond positively to formal and informal opportunities to learn and update knowledge and
expertise

#### MES/N0572: Market research and ideas formation-Worldskills
Description
Market Research and Ideas Formation in short involves analyzing market data to understand trends and
consumer behavior, then using this insight to develop innovative strategies for business success.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Market Research and Ideas Formation involves gathering and analyzing data to understand market trends and consumer behavior. It includes generating innovative ideas and strategies based on
research findings to address business challenges and seize opportunities. Professionals in this field
contribute to informed decision-making and creative problem-solving to drive organizational success.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Receive and mentally process information and requests
- PC2. Participate in new market research and product planning
- PC3. Participate in new market research and product planning
- PC4. Review the relationship of potential new products to the organization’s product range and
plans
- PC5. Investigate the potential need and benefit of new products and product lines using suitable
research methods
- PC6. Draw conclusions from the market research
- PC7. Maintain records of the market research and thinking process

#### MES/N0573: The design process- Worldskills
Description
The design process involves creating visual solutions to meet specific needs or solve problems through a
systematic approach.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of the design process includes identifying objectives, conducting research, generating
ideas, developing concepts, refining designs, and delivering final solutions across various mediums
such as print, digital, and multimedia.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Conduct research into trends in design
- PC2. Conceive or receive a design idea
- PC3. Through market research and consultation, create, realize, and evaluate design concepts for
manufacturing
- PC4. Evaluate the feasibility of design ideas, relative to • Appearance • Safety • Function, •
Serviceability • Budget • Production methods and costs • Market characteristics
- PC5. Modify and refine design ideas, based on the above factors
- PC6. Complete the design process within the parameters of the business or commission

#### MES/N0574: Drawing, illustration, and graphic- Worldskills
Description
Drawing, illustration, and graphic design involve the creation of visual content using various artistic
techniques and digital tools to convey ideas, messages, or concepts.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of drawing, illustration, and graphic design includes creating illustrations for print and
digital media, designing logos, layouts, and branding materials, and producing artwork for advertising, marketing, publishing, and entertainment industries. These occupations require
creativity, technical skills, and an understanding of visual communication principles to effectively
communicate messages and engage audiences.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explore ways of articulating design ideas visually
- PC2. Prepare sketches, iteratively, exploring options and results
- PC3. implement decisions regarding colour, visual materials’ properties, and composition
- PC4. Create • detailed drawings • illustrations • artwork or blueprints
- PC5. use drafting instruments and tools
- PC6. use CAD software
- PC7. Draft, lay out, and specify technical devices, plants and equipment
- PC8. Update sketches, drawings, and documentation as development proceeds
- PC9. maintain document control throughout the design process.

#### MES/N0575: Materials science and engineering- Worldskills
Description
Materials science and engineering involves studying the properties, structure, and behavior of materials to
develop new materials or improve existing ones for various applications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of materials science and engineering includes researching, designing, testing, and manufacturing materials such as metals, polymers, ceramics, and composites for industries such as
aerospace, automotive, electronics, healthcare, and construction. This field encompasses a wide
range of disciplines, including metallurgy, polymer science, biomaterials, and nanotechnology, and
plays a crucial role in advancing technology and addressing global challenges such as sustainability
and renewable energy.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Consult with engineers and/or other knowledge sources to plan tests and prototypes
- PC2. Select and specify machines and tools for prototyping
- PC3. Identify and apply relevant procedures and regulations to the testing and prototyping process
- PC4. Determine the purposes, range and scope of tests and prototypes
- PC5. Put in place measures to ensure the validity of information and data collected
- PC6. Conduct tests and prototyping
- PC7. Collect planned information and data for analysis
- PC8. Review the implications of the analysis for • The manufacturing process, and outcomes, and •
the selection and use of materials.

#### MES/N0576: The development  process- WorldSkills
Description
The development process involves the systematic creation, refinement, and implementation of products,
services, or systems to meet specific goals or objectives.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of the development process encompasses various stages, including conceptualization, design, prototyping, testing, production, and deployment. It applies to a wide range of fields, such as
product development, software development, project management, and business process improvement. The process involves interdisciplinary collaboration, critical thinking, problem-solving,
and iterative refinement to ensure the successful delivery of high-quality and innovative solutions.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Research production specifications, costs, production materials, and manufacturing methods
- PC2. Provide cost estimates and itemized production requirements
- PC3. Build models, patterns, or templates
- PC4. Fabricate models or samples in a range of materials, using hand and power tools
- PC5. Monitor processes, materials, and surroundings to detect or assess problems
- PC6. Collect and process information by compiling, categorizing, calculating, and verifying
information and data
- PC7. Select and use suitable and robust testing equipment, tools, methods, and techniques
- PC8. Analyse and evaluate information to determine compliance with standards
- PC9. Determine time, costs, resources, or materials needed for production
- PC10. Present designs and reports to clients or managers for approva
- PC11. Raise and discuss the needs for and benefits of modification
- PC12. Techniques for optimizing manufacture and distribution
- PC13. Review, adapt, and provide documentation, detailed instructions/specifications, or drawings,
for fabrication, construction, assembly, modification, maintenance and use

#### MES/N0577: Implementation- Worldskills
Description
Implementation refers to the process of putting plans, strategies, or projects into action to achieve specific
goals or objectives.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of implementation includes translating ideas or concepts into tangible actions, deploying
resources, executing plans, and monitoring progress to ensure successful outcomes. It applies to
various domains such as project management, business strategy execution, software development, policy implementation, and organizational change management. Implementation involves
coordinating activities, managing stakeholders, overcoming challenges, and adapting strategies as
needed to effectively execute plans and achieve desired results.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Develop industrial standards and regulatory guidelines
- PC2. Check the relationship of the product to the organization’s business strategy and plan
- PC3. Develop promotional strategies or plans for the product
- PC4. Develop artistic or design concepts for decoration, exhibition, or commercial purposes
- PC5. Design graphic material for use as ornamentation, illustration, advertising, and packaging
- PC6. Present evaluation reports, including • Handling and safety • Market appeal • Production
efficiency • Distribution. • Use • Maintenance.
