SSC/Q2217: IT Customer Care Executive
Brief Job Description
The IT Customer Care Executive is responsible for addressing customer queries through voice and non-
voice channels. They use basic generative artificial intelligence (Gen AI)-based tools and BPM platforms in
both voice and non-voice processes. The individual answers customer inquiries, resolves problems, records
complaints and collects customer feedback.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to work independently and interact with customers. The individual should
be result-oriented and possess logical thinking and interpersonal skills. The person should know how to
prioritize workload and be willing to work at a desk-based job.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. SSC/N2201: Build and maintain positive relationship with customer
- 2. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Voice
Make Outbound Calls to Customers
- 1. SSC/N3020: Make Outbound Calls to Customers
- 2. SSC/N2202: Use Basic Gen AI and BPM Platforms in Voice Customer Services
Elective 2: Non-Voice
Deal remotely with customer queries – Domestic Non-Voice
- 1. SSC/N3021: Deal remotely with customer queries Domestic Non-Voice
- 2. SSC/N2203: Usage of basic Gen AI and BPM Platforms in Non-Voice Customer Support

SSC/N2201: Build and maintain positive relationship with customer
Description
This unit focuses on the skills required for a IT Customer Care Executive to build and maintain positive
relationships with customers, ensuring high-quality service and customer satisfaction. It emphasizes
creating a rapport, understanding customer needs, and providing consistent and empathetic support
across all touchpoints.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Establish and Build Customer Rapport
Provide Consistent and Empathetic Customer Service
Resolve Customer Complaints and Queries
Maintain Ongoing Customer Relationships
Elements and Performance Criteria
Establish and Build Customer Rapport
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. greet customers warmly, using polite language to establish rapport
PC2. actively listen to customers’ needs and concerns, displaying understanding and empathy
PC3. use clear, professional, and friendly communication to create a welcoming atmosphere
PC4. identify customer needs and offer relevant support, information, or solutions
Provide Consistent and Empathetic Customer Service
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. maintain a positive tone and attitude throughout all customer interactions
PC6. respond promptly and effectively to customer inquiries, ensuring customer satisfaction
PC7. recognize and address potential customer issues before they escalate, showing proactive
support
PC8. demonstrate patience and understanding when dealing with challenging situations or
complaints
Resolve Customer Complaints and Queries
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. investigate complaints or issues thoroughly, seeking relevant information to resolve them
PC10. provide clear, accurate, and timely resolutions to customer problems or concerns
PC11. escalate unresolved or complex issues to the appropriate authority while keeping the
customer informed
PC12. ensure that customers are satisfied with the resolution and follow up when necessary
Maintain Ongoing Customer Relationships

SSC/N3020: Make Outbound Calls to Customers
Description
This unit is about making telephone calls to existing and prospective customers concerning the marketing
of relevant products/services.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Undertake outbound calling
Log and escalate complains
Collect customer feedback
Elements and Performance Criteria
Undertake outbound calling
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. develop a good understanding of the company’s products or services to provide accurate
information to customers
PC2. establish contact with customers, following the organization’s procedures
PC3. communicate clearly and professionally with customers using appropriate language and tone
PC4. provide an appropriate introduction to the customer and explain the purpose of the call,
following standard scripts
PC5. listen actively and obtain relevant information from customers to identify their product or
service requirements
PC6. provide accurate and up-to-date information to customers about relevant products, services,
policies, and procedures
PC7. handling customer calls to address their queries, objections, and concerns with empathetic
tone while adhering to standard scripts.
PC8. adapt the approach and style to customer preferences, within the limits of competence and
authority
PC9. identify and act on opportunities to up-sell or cross-sell other products/services to customers
PC10. close sales with confirmation from customers, ensuring they have all the necessary
information about the products/services being marketed to them
PC11. obtain required financial information from customers, following organizational procedures
PC12. complete the organization’s post-sales procedures to finalize sales
Manage Customer Interaction
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. ensure high-quality customer service in every interaction.
PC14. maintain accurate and detailed records concerning customer interactions and transactions
PC15. comply with the relevant standards, policies, procedures, and guidelines when making
outbound telesales calls.
