MIN/Q1803: Dredge and Ore Processing Operator
Brief Job Description
A Dredge and Ore Processing Operator is responsible for extracting ores from dredge, sending the slurry to
wet concentrator unit, supplementary mining plant and spiral operation, tailing management and dumping.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should be physically agile, strong, have good eye-sight and not suffer from
colorblindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MIN/N4107: Carry out the dredging operation
- 2. MIN/N4108: Carry out the upgradation unit operations

MIN/N4107: Carry out the dredging operation
Description
This occupational standards unit is about carrying out the dredging operation
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Check the dredge operation
Elements and Performance Criteria
Check the dredge operation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. select the appropriate type of dredge for use in the plant
PC2. ensure the dredger unit is in complete floating condition and there is no grounding of the
#### Unit at any point
PC3. check the dredger unit is appropriately positioned and anchored at all corners using winch
ropes and spuds
PC4. transport the materials in the maintenance barge
PC5. assess the weight of the material and keep the load in the designated place with appropriate
anchorage
PC6. check the slurry pump for the correct functioning
PC7. ensure the dredger pump is appropriately primed and the suction pipe is completely
submerged inside water before starting the dredger pump
PC8. check and ensure all the operating parameters of the dredger unit, e.g. main motor
temperature, mass flow meter reading, main motor ammeter reading, head feed bin level
indicator reading, etc., are within the permissible limits
PC9. maintain the appropriate flow rate and pulp density of the dredger to match with the
requirements for the operation of the up-gradation unit
PC10. maximize the dredge cutting depth to six meters below the surface of the water over the
entire area by monitoring the depth indicator and by swinging mechanism to get the uniform
pond bottom profile for the ease of plant shifting
PC11. maintain the required suction vacuum and discharge pressure of the dredger pump by using
the pressure indicators provided on the unit
PC12. convey the slurry to the wet up-gradation unit maintaining parameters, such as feed rate,
pulp density, head feed bin indicator, mass flow meter reading, etc
PC13. check the layout and condition of the components of the hydraulic system, such as the
hydraulic power pack, accumulator, actuators, check valve, pressure control valve, flow
control and direction control valves, hoses and their coupling
PC14. conduct operational checks on the areas of potential issues, e.g. unusual noises/ vibrations,
blockages and obstruction, lubrication of drive head
PC15. ensure the surge bin level is within the safe operating range

MIN/N4108: Carry out the upgradation unit operations
Description
This occupational standards unit is about the upgradation unit operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operate the trommel
Operate the static and vibrating screens
Perform the preventive maintenance of vibrating screen
Perform spiral separation
Transfer concentrate minerals to MSP
Manage the tailing
Elements and Performance Criteria
Operate the trommel
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check the layout and components of the trommel System, including drive mechanism,
rollers, guards, screen, trommel tyres, feed end and discharge chute, roller bearings,
controls such as fusible plug-in fluid coupling, etc
PC2. check the operations of mechanical and electrical driving units including safety devices
PC3. ensure the roller bearings, feed/discharge ends, drive mechanisms, lubrication systems, and
controls drive belts, etc., of the trommel are appropriately maintained
PC4. feed sand slurry maintaining the appropriate parameters, such as feed-rate, ampere meter
reading of drive motor, suction and discharge pressure of main pump, head feed bin level
indicator, mass flow meter reading, etc.
PC5. conduct the screening of foreign material
PC6. convey the sand slurry through the trommel underflow chute to the surge bin below the
trommel, and thereafter pump the same to the spirals
PC7. maintain appropriate parameters, e.g. feed/ material rate, alignment and inclination of the
trommel, RPM, screen jamming, etc., while conveying sand slurry
PC8. maintain the cleanliness of the screens in the trommel and apertures to prevent
blockages/jamming and maintain the required surge in the surge bin for the spiral operation
PC9. follow the safe code of practice for the erection, installation, operation, repairs, maintenance,
and dismantling of the screens and the drive mechanism to prevent accidents
PC10. follow appropriate safety measures concerning the trommel operations
Operate the static and vibrating screens
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. check the process of screening in supplementary mining plant
PC12. check the layout of components and power supply unit of vibrating screen, drive mechanism,
bearing, feed arrangements, etc

PC1. check different types of HEMM used in OSCOM mining areas, such as dozer, loader,

inadequate fluid levels, leaks, and wear

requirements
PC4. use the onboard digital diagnostic tools to assess the problem
PC5. use the equipment data obtained from pressure gauges, filler gauges, callipers, condition
monitoring devices and compare it with the standards to detect faults in the system
PC6. check the lubricant levels and refill as required, ensuring to use the lubricant of the correct
code and grade
PC7. follow the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM)-recommended procedure and checklist for
routine servicing
PC8. calibrate, align and adjust the settings, alignment, pressures, tension, speeds and levels of
the engine, transmission system, load bearing arms and structure, electrical and electronic
components, valves, ignition, fuel and emissions, brakes, lights, tires, steering and body
fittings
PC9. identify wear and tear in components, such as oil and air filters, drive belts, braking system
due to continuous usage and replace them with new ones
PC10. follow the appropriate measures to avoid damage to the vehicle and its components during
repair and maintenance
PC11. carry out breakdown maintenance at the worksite or workshop, as required
PC12. follow appropriate safety measures while operating and maintaining the HEMM, and
hydraulic machine
