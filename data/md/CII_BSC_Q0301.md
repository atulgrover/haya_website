CII/BSC/Q0301: Sales Executive (Banking & Finance)
Brief Job Description
Individuals in this role will be responsible for promoting and selling the financial products (Banking,
Insurance etc.) and services to individual and corporate customers. He/she plays a crucial role in achieving
sales targets, credit cards selling, new account opening, building customer relationships, and contributing
to the overall growth and business development of the bank.
Personal Attributes
Individuals in this job must be able to communicate with the client and be customer service oriented.
He/she will be attentive to analyze, integrity and trustworthy able to work in team and collaboration with
problem solving skill. Sales Executives are well-organized multitaskers who have a habit of accomplishing
all the responsibilities that face them.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CII/BSC/N0305: Prepare MIS/reports and maintain data integrity
- 2. CII/BSC/N0304: Conduct Pre & Post Sales activities

CII/BSC/N0305: Prepare MIS/reports and maintain data integrity
Description
Individuals in this role will be liable to prepare Management Information System (MIS) reports and ensure
the maintenance of data integrity within the banking sales department. This role requires a keen eye for
detail, strong analytical skills, and a commitment to delivering accurate and timely reports.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers all the Elements and Performance Criteria
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify the need of Banking solution
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. • Establish clear data management policies and procedures that outline how customer-related
• information should be collected, stored, and updated. Ensure compliance with data protection
• regulations, such as GDPR or CCPA, as applicable.
PC2. • Implement a Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system to centralize customer data.
• A CRM system helps automate record-keeping, tracks interactions, and allows for easyaccess
• to customer information by authorized personnel.
PC3. • Ensure that CRM system captures comprehensive customer data, including contact
• information, purchase history, service requests, communication history, and any other
• relevant details specific to the financial institutions.
PC4. • Implement a process for regularly updating customer records. This may include scheduled
• reviews of customer accounts, verifying contact information, and updating purchase or service
• history.
PC5. • Develop, generate, and distribute regular MIS reports as per the specified schedule and
• customize reports to meet the diverse informational needs of different stakeholders
• within the banking sales team.
PC6. • Use the CRM system to segment customer data based on various criteria, such as industry,
• location, purchasing behavior, or engagement level. This segmentation allows for targeted
• communication and marketing efforts.
PC7. • Create standardized reports for management that provide insights into customer relations,
• such as customer satisfaction levels, sales trends, service request patterns, and account
• growth. These reports should be generated on a regular interval.
PC8. • Present data in a visual and understandable format using charts, graphs, and dashboards.
• Visualization tools can help management quickly grasp key insights.
PC9. • Offer actionable insights and recommendations based on the findings. Suggest strategies for
• improving customer relations or capitalizing on opportunities in the banking industry.
PC10. • Maintain strict data security measures to protect customer information and ensure compliance
• with data protection regulations. Regularly update security protocols as needed.
PC11. • Collect relevant data from various sources within the department and analyze data to identify
• trends, patterns, and areas for improvement in sales performance.

CII/BSC/N0304: Conduct Pre & Post Sales activities
Description
Individuals in this role will conduct various activities starting from lead generation to post uses. He/she will
manage aligned services its sale and post-sales support including record management.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Provide pre & post service support.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify the need of Banking solution
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. • Analyze the demographics and needs of potential customers and competitors to identify
• unique selling points.
PC2. • Categorize potential customers based on their financial needs and preferences and
• create detailed profiles of typical customers.
PC3. • Gain in-depth knowledge of banking products and services and be updated on industry
• trends and changes.
PC4. • Highlight the key benefits and value propositions of available financial solution sync with client
• requirement. Clearly explain how it addresses the client's pain points and improves their
• operations.
PC5. • Use online channels for lead generation and attend events, engage in community activities,
• and build a network.
PC6. • Initiating contact through calls, emails, or in-person visits creates a concise and compelling
• pitch introducing your banking products.
PC7. • Analyze the customer's financial goals and challenges and pay attention to the customer's
• needs and concerns.
PC8. • Create personalized presentations based on the customer's profile and be prepared to
• handle objections and concerns effectively.
PC9. Verify the KYC with relevant documents.
PC10. • Close the solution offered like credit cards, account opening, loan support or other
• available financial solutions.
PC11. • Ensure all necessary documentation is completed accurately and assist customers in navigating
• through the onboarding process.
PC12. Express Gratitude and ask for the feedback to improve sales process.
PC13. • Explore other banking products that align with the customer's financial objectives and propose
• complementary services to enhance the customer's banking experience.
PC14. • Schedule follow-up meetings to maintain a connection, use customer data to personalize
• interactions and offers.
PC15. • Resolve any post-sales issues promptly and efficiently, turn customer problems into
• opportunities to showcase excellent service.

CII/BSC/N0303: Presentation And professional appearance
Description
Presentation and professional appearance are vital aspects of being a successful sales professional in the
banking services. Individual in this role will mark his/her appearance, demeanor, and presentation skills
which significantly impact how potential customers perceive individual and service providing company.
Ultimately building trust with clients and contributing to the success of team and organization. These
documents serve as essential tools to communicate in offerings and demonstrating the value addition in
terms of offered financial solutions to potential clients.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develop proposal documents and present in professional manner - Demonstrate the applicable
financial solution to the client.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify the need of Banking solution
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. • Analyze client's needs, pain points, and priorities. Tailor the presentation to address theirspecific
• challenges and interests with available financial solutions.
PC2. • Know the available solution / products and services thoroughly. Anticipate common questions
and
• be ready with informed responses. Organize the presentation/proposal with a clear structure:
• introduction, main points, supporting details,and conclusion. Use headings and bullet points to
• make it easy to follow.
PC3. • Highlight the key benefits and value propositions of available banking solution sync with client
• requirement. Clearly explain how it present the client's pain points and improves theirtransaction
