MIN/Q3211: Mechanic -Fitter (Mineral Sands)
Brief Job Description
A Mechanic Fitter (Mineral Sands) is responsible for the maintenance and repair of different types of
processing and material handling equipment used in dredging and SMPs unit, mineral separation plant,
and rare earth extraction plant.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have attention to detail, organizational and problem-solving skills. The person should
also have appropriate verbal and written communication skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MIN/N3224: Maintain and repair the dredging and SMPs unit equipment
- 2. MIN/N3225: Maintain and repair the mineral separation plant equipment
- 3. MIN/N3226: Maintain and repair the rare earth extraction plant equipment
- 4. MIN/N1810: Follow the applicable health, safety and environmental guidelines (Placer Mining)
- 5. MIN/N1811: Work collaboratively and effectively in a team
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

MIN/N3224: Maintain and repair the dredging and SMPs unit equipment
Description
This occupational standards unit is about carrying out the dredging operation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain the dredge
Maintain the trommel
Maintain the spiral
Maintain the static and vibrating screen
Maintain the HEMM
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain the dredge
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. follow the manufacturer’s instructions for the safe handling and maintenance of the dredge
PC2. carry out maintenance of hydraulic system, including power pack, actuators, direction control
valves, pressure control valve, check valve, hoses, coupling, etc.
PC3. check the operating parameters of the dredger unit, e.g. main motor temperature, mass flow
meter reading, main motor ammeter reading, head feed bin level indicator reading etc., are
within the permissible limit
PC4. check for the correct functioning of the slurry pump
PC5. monitor the pumps for abnormal noise, vibration, overheating of bearing, loose belt, etc
PC6. carry out maintenance of the pumps, including belt tension, foundation bolt, pulley
replacement, alignment of belt, greasing and lubrication
PC7. assemble and dismantle pumps and pipelines
PC8. use the correct components/ spares and consumable for the maintenance
Maintain the trommel
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. check for the correct functioning of trommel components, e.g. drive mechanism, rollers,
guards, screen, trommel tires, feed end, discharge chute, roller bearings, fusible plug in fluid
coupling, etc
PC10. maintain the cleanliness of the screen in the trommel to prevent any blockage/ jamming
PC11. check different parameters, such as ampere meter reading of drive motor, feed rate,
discharge pressure, head feed bin level indicator, and mass flow meter reading
PC12. check the condition of support roller, screens, gearbox, inlet chute, pulley alignment, belt
drive, and roller bearing carry out greasing and lubrication of the moving parts
PC13. identify issues such as vibration and unusual noises in the trommel and take appropriate
corrective measures
Maintain the Spiral

Maintain the HEMM
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC23. follow the appropriate methods to assess the vehicle to identify damage, corrosion, fluid
levels, leaks, etc.
PC24. conduct routine examinations as per the maintenance schedule recommended by the
manufacturer
PC25. check the lubricant level and refill as required, using the lubricant of appropriate grades
PC26. follow the OEM-recommended procedure and checklist for routine servicing of HEMM
PC27. identify wear and tear of oil and air filters, drive belts, and braking system, and replace them
as required
PC28. carry out breakdown maintenance in the workshop or work site when required
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. different dredging equipment used in the beach sand mining industry
KU2. the working principle of the slurry pump
KU3. the functions of different components of the slurry pump and the pump characteristic/
performance curves
KU4. different types of Heavy Earth Moving Machinery (HEMM)
KU5. the basic technology used in and functioning of various components and equipment in the
dredging process
KU6. the use of appropriate tools to assess and confirm technical faults
KU7. the use of instruments like pressure indicators, fuel pressure testers, manifold gauge sets, oil
pressure gauges, measuring equipment (Vernier callipers, micrometer, feeler gauges, multi-
meter, flow meter, temp gauge, dial gauge etc.), condition monitoring systems for
temperature, vibration, sound, etc.

MIN/N3225: Maintain and repair the mineral separation plant equipment
Description
This occupational standards unit is about maintaining and repairing different types of equipment used in
an earth extraction plant.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain the mineral separation plant equipment
Conduct preventive maintenance of processing equipment components
Track the preventive maintenance activities
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain the mineral separation plant equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. follow the manufacturer’s instructions for the safe use and maintenance of the mineral
separation plant equipment
PC2. check for the correct functioning of different types of separators, e.g. High Tension
Separator, Electric plate separator, Magnetic Separator, Induced Roll Magnetic Separator, Lift
Roll Magnetic Separator, etc
PC3. check for the correct functioning of material handling equipment, e.g. bucket elevator,
bucket belt conveyor, belt conveyor, screw feeder, and different c, e.g. rotary drier, fluidized
bed drier, and shaft drier
PC4. check for the correct functioning of different components of the power transmission systems,
e.g. gear and gearboxes, couplings, V belts, V pulleys, etc
PC5. follow the appropriate procedure of mounting, assembling, and disassembling different types
of bearings
PC6. check for the correct functioning of water pumps, slurry pumps, oil pumps, vacuum pumps,
and carry out appropriate troubleshooting as required
PC7. assist in the installation, repair and maintenance of different types of machinery
PC8. use the relevant mechanical tools following the applicable blueprints and manuals
Conduct preventive maintenance of processing equipment components
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. create maintenance plans as per the schedule recommended by the equipment
manufacturer
PC10. perform routine inspections of the manufacturing equipment
PC11. inspect valves, linkages, bearings, hydraulic and pneumatic systems for smooth operation
PC12. monitor the performance of different plant machinery, detect malfunctions and take
appropriate corrective measures
PC13. resolve issues with the equipment by applying equipment knowledge and standard
procedures
