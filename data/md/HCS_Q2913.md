#### HCS/Q2913: Usta Craft Artisan
Brief Job Description
An Usta craft artisan from Bikaner is skilled in decorating walls, wood, camel leather, etc. using colourful
painting and gold metal embossing. Their work is known for raised designs, fine detail, and a rich golden
finish.
Personal Attributes
Usta artisans are known for their patience, attention to detail, and artistic flair. They possess a steady
hand, a strong sense of colour and design, and a deep dedication to preserving their traditional craft with
care and precision.

#### HCS/N2915: Introduction to Usta or Gesso Painting
Description
This unit introduces the traditional art of Usta painting, focusing on its rich heritage, characteristic use of
gold embossing, and the selection and preparation of materials to create intricate decorative designs.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the Essence of Usta or Gesso Painting
Explore the Historical and Cultural Relevance
Understand the Evolution of Usta Painting Over Time
Assess the Geographical and Economic Context of Usta Painting
Analyse the Current State and Need for Revival
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand the Essence of Usta or Gesso Painting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Define Usta or Gesso painting and its key characteristics (gold embossing, floral motifs,
naqashi technique, etc.).
- PC2. Identify traditional materials used (natural pigments, gold foil, handmade brushes).
- PC3. Recognise the aesthetic and symbolic value of the art.
- PC4. Explain the philosophical, cultural, geographical and other influences in the motifs.
- PC5. Distinguish Usta Painting from other Indian traditional art forms.
Explore the Historical and Cultural Relevance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Describe the origins and development of Usta Painting in the Bikaner region.
- PC7. Explain how the art form evolved through a blend of regional and external artistic influences.
- PC8. Understand the role of cultural exchange, travel, and trade relations in shaping and
sustaining the tradition.
- PC9. Discuss the importance of Usta Painting in architectural embellishment and heritage sites.
- PC10. Examine how Usta Painting came to be associated with refined aesthetic values and cultural
significance across different regions.
Understand the Evolution of Usta Painting Over Time
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Trace the migration and settlement of Usta Painting artisans in Bikaner.
- PC12. Discuss how materials, tools, and techniques have changed over time.
- PC13. Analyse the impact of modernity and colonial periods on Usta Painting.
- PC14. Compare historical and contemporary applications (walls vs. wooden/metal. artefacts)
- PC15. Understand the intergenerational transfer of knowledge within artisan families.
Assess the Geographical and Economic Context of Usta Painting

#### HCS/N2914: Tools, Materials & Base Surfaces in Usta Painting
Description
This unit covers identification and use of traditional tools and materials in Usta Painting, including natural
pigments and gold leaf. It explains preparation and selection of traditional and modern base surfaces like
camel leather, wood, metal, and glass. Safe handling, storage, workspace cleanliness, eco-friendly
disposal, and inventory management are also emphasized for effective and responsible practice.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identification of traditional tools and materials
Understanding Base Surfaces for Usta Work
Safe Handling and Storage
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identification of traditional tools and materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. List and describe traditional tools used in Usta Painting, such as handmade brushes, Pencils,
design transfer papers/ tracing papers, embossing tools, sanding papers and laveling tools.
- PC2. Identify key materials including natural pigments, gold leaf, adhesive (arabic gum), and
animal glue.
- PC3. Distinguish between natural and synthetic alternatives currently used by artisans.
- PC4. Demonstrate knowledge of mixing and preparing pigments for different surfaces.
Understanding Base Surfaces for Usta Work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Identify traditional surfaces used for Usta painting such as walls, wooden panels, and camel
leather.
- PC6. Describe preparation techniques for different base materials before applying art.
- PC7. Understand the suitability of modern surfaces like metal, glass, MDF, and ceramics.
- PC8. Compare the aesthetic and durability outcomes on different base types.
- PC9. Select appropriate base surfaces based on the artwork’s purpose and design.
Safe Handling and Storage
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Follow safety precautions while handling sharp tools and hazardous materials.
- PC11. Demonstrate proper ventilation and workspace cleanliness during material use.
- PC12. Ensure safe storage of gold foil, natural pigments, and tools to avoid damage or
contamination.
- PC13. Understand the role of temperature and humidity in affecting materials during application.
- PC14. Adhere to eco-friendly practices for disposal of waste materials.
- PC15. Maintain inventory and labelling of tools and supplies for easy access and organisation.

#### HCS/N2917: Identify and Understand Design Motifs and Patterns in Usta
Painting
Description

emphasis on Mughal architectural elements like paisleys, arches, and medallions. It explores cultural
symbolism, Persian and Rajasthani influences, and the evolution of motifs through different architectural
periods. It includes floral, geometric, and natural inspirations, sketching and design planning techniques,
and adapting motifs for various surfaces and purposes while maintaining traditional authenticity and visual
harmony.
Scope

Understanding Mughal architect Motifs
Floral, Geometric, and other Inspirations
Drawing and Planning Designs

- PC4. Recognise the historical connection between royal Interest and Artistic demand resulted in

techniques, local sourcing, and long-term maintenance strategies to reduce waste, extend the life of tools
and materials, and promote environmentally responsible craftsmanship.
