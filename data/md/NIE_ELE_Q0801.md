#### NIE/ELE/Q0801: Installation and Repair Assistant (Consumer Electronics)
Brief Job Description
This qualification delves into various aspects of electronic appliances and devices, covering topics such as
LCD-LED TV and Monitor technology, Cable TV and DTH Services, CCTV Camera and Home Theatre System
installation, Washing Machine operation and maintenance, and the utilization of Induction Stove and
Microwave Oven. Participants will gain practical insights into the functionality, servicing, and
troubleshooting of these electronic devices, enhancing their proficiency in managing and maintaining a
range of household and entertainment appliances. The course provides a well-rounded understanding of
contemporary electronic technologies.
Personal Attributes
Upon completion of this comprehensive course, candidates exhibit a diverse skill set contributing to their
professional competence. They develop technical proficiency through hands-on experience with LCD-LED
TVs, monitors, cable TV, DTH services, CCTV cameras, home theatre systems, washing machines, induction stoves, and microwave ovens. Analytical skills are honed for precise issue diagnosis, while
attention to detail becomes ingrained in installation and maintenance tasks. Problem-solving abilities
adapt to dynamic challenges, and effective communication skills are cultivated. A customer service
orientation emphasizes quality support, safety awareness ensures secure practices, and adaptability
readies candidates for evolving technologies. Teamwork and efficient time management skills further
solidify their versatility in electronic appliance servicing.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. NIE/ELE/N0801: LCD-LED TV and Monitor
2. NIE/ELE/N0802: Cable TV and DTH Services.
3. NIE/ELE/N0806: CCTV Camera and Home Theatre System.
4. NIE/ELE/N0804: Washing Machine and Hair Dryer
5. NIE/ELE/N0805: Induction Stove and Microwave Oven.
6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

and computer screens. Proctors authenticate the candidate based on registration details, pre-test image
captured and I- card in possession of the candidate. Proctors can chat with candidates or give warnings to
candidates. Proctors can also take screenshots, terminate a specific user's test session, or re-authenticate
candidates based on video feeds.

examination/ internal assessment / Project/ Presentation/. Major Project (if applicable) would be evaluated
preferably by external/ subject expert including NIELIT officials.
