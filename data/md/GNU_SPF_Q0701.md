#### GNU/SPF/Q0701: Water Sports Trainer
Brief Job Description
Understand the concept of water adventure sports Classify the water sports Understand type of water for
watersports activities
Personal Attributes
He or she should qualify in the swimming proficiency test held by the institute This test will consist of
Continuous swimming without stopping for 100 meters Underwater swimming for 5 minutes Floating for
minimum 5 minutes Jumping from the height of 7.5 meters in the deep end
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. GNU/SPF/N0708: Navigation & Seamanship Skill

#### GNU/SPF/N0709: Snorkeling
Description
Explore the historical and cultural significance of snorkelling and freediving in coastal communities.
Develop awareness about reducing personal impact on the marine environment while snorkelling. Gain
knowledge about the specific marine species and habitats in the local area where snorkelling is practiced.
Focus on improving physical fitness to enhance snorkelling performance and endurance. Opportunities to
collaborate or learn from experts to deepen understanding of marine life.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Explore the historical and cultural significance of snorkelling and freediving in coastal communities.
