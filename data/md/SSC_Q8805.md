#### SSC/Q8805: AR/VR  Test Engineer
Brief Job Description
An AR/VR Test Engineer ensures the quality and functionality of augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality
(VR) applications and hardware. They design and execute tests, identify and track defects, and collaborate
with developers to improve the user experience. This role requires a strong understanding of software
testing methodologies, AR/VR technologies, and relevant development tools.
Personal Attributes
AR/VR test engineer must be able to communicate effectively, build relationships with others and
collaborate with various stakeholders. They must have strong analytical abilities and a zeal to learn new
things.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SSC/N8815: Define test strategy and test plan for the solution in collaboration with appropriate
stakeholders
2. SSC/N8817: Run the simulated test cases, identify automation opportunities and evaluate the outcomes
3. SSC/N8824: Identify, document, and track defects and issues found during testing and communicating them to the development team for resolution.
4. SSC/N8822: Provide support in maintaining and troubleshooting the AR/VR solution
5. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### SSC/N8815: Define test strategy and test plan for the solution in
collaboration with appropriate stakeholders
Description
This unit is about strategizing and planning functional and non-functional requirements of test methods for
the solution in collaboration with relevant stakeholders
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Scope,Test Strategy,Test Plan,Test Automation Framework,Integration Testing,Performance testing,Usability testing,Immersive testing,Hardware testing,Best practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Gather information on the scope of the product by collaborating with other relevant
stakeholders.
- PC2. Define the objectives of testing the solution
- PC3. Provide support to the concerned team (e.g., strategy) in developing test strategy
- PC4. Outline an overall plan for test automation strategy for device/platform solution
- PC5. Prepare a list of functional/non-functional requirements and real-world experiences for
testing
- PC6. Identify any issues with the requirements for testing and clarify these with appropriate
people
- PC7. Create the test plan to cover all the requirements and prepare a requirement traceability
matrix
- PC8. Identify the interactions between various components of the AR/VR solution
- PC9. Collaborate with relevant teams (strategy, development, etc.) to develop a set of processes
and standards for test automation
- PC10. Test the integration of the AR VR product/solution fully into the client's existing SDLC
cadence
- PC11. Define tests to measure performance, gameplay, camera tracking, and latency by emulating
the real-world environment
- PC12. Define tests for preproduction testing with testers that fit the product's target demographic
- PC13. Determine whether the use case selected for AR VR makes sense for the target audience
- PC14. Test the product for unwarranted experiences such as motion sickness, lack of immersion,
eye strain, etc.
- PC15. Determine optimum physical environment attributes for better immersion (e.g., how much
open space is needed around an individual using VR
- PC16. Design tests to ensure that the hardware is working properly

#### SSC/N8824: Identify, document, and track defects and issues found
during testing and communicating them  to the development team for resolution.
Description
This NOS is about to tracking defects
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Defect Identification,Defect Documentation,Issue Tracking,Clear Communication,Collabrating for resolution, Verfying Fixes,Closure and Reporting,
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Systematically test the application through various techniques, including functional testing,
usability testing, and performance testing. Identify issues such as bugs, glitches, user interface inconsistencies, and performance bottlenecks.
- PC2. Employ testing tools and frameworks suitable for AR/VR applications (such as Unity Test
Framework or software testing suites) to aid in identifying defects. Automated testing scripts may also be used for regression testing to ensure that previously functioning features remain
stable after updates
- PC3. For each identified defect, document detailed information
- PC4. Utilize issue tracking tools (such as Jira, Bugzilla, or Trello) to log defects and manage their
lifecycle. This allows for organized tracking of reported issues, making it easier for the team
to view the status and history of each defect
- PC5. Assign defects to appropriate team members (developers, product managers) based on
expertise and responsibility, ensuring accountability for resolution
- PC6. Maintain open lines of communication with the development team. Provide regular updates
on discovered defects, especially if new critical issues arise during ongoing testing.
- PC7. Arrange meetings or forums for testers and developers to discuss defects in more detail,
seeking clarifications on the issues identified and potential solutions
- PC8. Be available to assist developers in understanding the context of the defects. A collaborative
approach can lead to quicker identification of the root causes and more effective solutions.
- PC9. After developers implement fixes, conduct retesting to verify that the issues have been
resolved. This includes checking that the defect no longer occurs and that no new issues have been introduced as a result of the changes
- PC10. Perform regression testing to confirm that other functionalities remain unaffected by the
fixes, ensuring overall stability and performance of the application.
- PC11. Update defect documentation with the status of the defects once resolved, including the
solutions implemented. This serves as a historical record that can be useful for future reference

Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
