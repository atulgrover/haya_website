THC/Q0420: Patisserie and Confectionery (WorldSkills)
Brief Job Description
The Patisserie and Confectionery trades have highly skilled professionals who produce a wide range of
intricate and predominantly sweet items. They produce various confectionery products such as hand
finished chocolates, candies, and petits fours for service in hotels and restaurants or for retail in specialist
shops and outlets. Patissiers/Confectioners produce a full range of hot and cold desserts, cakes, biscuits,
and iced products for service in luxury/boutique hotels, restaurants, and pastry shops for retail sale. They
may also produce elaborate display pieces using chocolate, sugar, ice, marzipan, or other decorative
materials and ingredients. Some may specialize in producing decorated and themed cakes for special
events.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be hospitable, patient, polite, and service-oriented with great inter-
personal and communication skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. THC/N0454: Maintain and Organize Work (Patisserie and Confectionery)
- 2. THC/N0455: Food hygiene and health and safety
- 3. THC/N0456: Prepare Cakes, gateaux, and entremets
- 4. THC/N0457: Hot, cold, iced and plated desserts
- 5. THC/N0458: Prepare Confectionery and chocolate
- 6. THC/N0459: Prepare miniatures, individual cakes, and petits fours
- 7. THC/N0460: Present Creative Designs
- 8. THC/N0461: Practice techniques of modelling, moulding, coloring, and presenting

THC/N0454: Maintain and Organize Work (Patisserie and Confectionery)
Description
This NOS describes efficiently maintaining and organizing work in the patisserie and confectionery
#### unit/workstation , ensuring a seamless production flow, meticulous presentation, and adherence to quality
standards
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Planning production schedules, coordinating tasks
Ensuring timely delivery of high-quality pastries and confections
Optimizes resource utilization
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Check and prepare tools and equipment to maximize workflow and efficiency
PC2. Prioritize and plan work effectively to work within a given timeframe
PC3. Show respect for raw materials and finished goods
PC4. Use ingredients cost effectively and to minimize waste
PC5. Pre-order goods and materials accurately for planned work
PC6. Demonstrate inspiration, gastronomic flair and innovation in design and work techniques
PC7. Work within given themes
PC8. Follow detailed written and verbal instructions and copy from images
PC9. Produce portfolios for clients that include images of products and methods of making and
presentation, along with visionary statements if requested
PC10. React professionally and effectively to unexpected situations and requests
PC11. Formulate and substitute other ingredients to overcome unforeseen shortages
PC12. Communicate effectively with colleagues, teams, and clients
PC13. Produce a varied range of products within given timeframes and present at stipulated times
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Basic principles involved in accurately combining ingredients to achieve optimum results and
troubleshooting when results are not as planned.
KU2. The manipulation of raw materials through production techniques
KU3. Range of ingredients used in pastry work and confectionery including seasons, availability,
costs, storage, and use
KU4. Colour applications, taste combinations and texture co-ordination

THC/N0455: Food hygiene and health and safety
Description
This NOS lays down the importance of food hygiene and upholding rigorous health and safety standards in
the culinary unit, safeguarding the well-being of customers and staff through meticulous adherence to
sanitation protocols and safety regulations
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Implementing and monitoring rigorous cleanliness and hygiene protocols in food handling and
preparation
Adherence to compliance with health and safety regulations
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Work hygienically, taking responsibility for regulations for food storage, preparation, cooking,
and service
PC2. Respond effectively to overcome adverse environmental conditions which may not be as
expected
PC3. Pay attention to own cleanliness and appearance at all times
PC4. Observe all safety processes and requirements in relation to dietary and allergy information
PC5. Prepare accurate menus and account for obligatory declarations such as dietary and allergy
information
PC6. Ensure all work areas and equipment are cleaned to the highest standards and equipment is
replaced
PC7. Work safely and uphold accident prevention regulations
PC8. Use tools and equipment safely and within manufacturers’ instructions
PC9. Store all commodities and made products safely and hygienically
PC10. Observe service temperatures of all products during production and service, to maintain
health and safety
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Requirements for health including dietary and allergens, safety, and environment, food
hygiene and legislation relating to the production, display, and sale of product
KU2. Legislation and good practice relating to the use and care of speciality tools and equipment
and safe working methods
KU3. The causes of deterioration of food
KU4. Quality indicators for fresh, preserved, and dry goods

THC/N0456: Prepare Cakes, gateaux, and entremets
Description
This NOS describes the craft for an array of cakes, gateaux, and entremets, showcasing culinary artistry
through precision baking, creative flavor combinations, and presentation
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Demonstrates baking techniques for a variety of cakes, gateaux, and entremets
Innovate and experiment with diverse flavor profiles
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Produce a comprehensive range of cakes using various techniques, types of cake and
decorations
PC2. Construct and finish a comprehensive range of gateaux demonstrating flair and innovation
PC3. Produce a comprehensive range of entremets effectively incorporating such layers as sponge,
biscuit, creams, custards, ganache, jellies, mousses, fruits etc.
PC4. Produce a comprehensive range of entremets effectively incorporating such layers as sponge,
biscuit, creams, custards, ganache, jellies, mousses, fruits etc.
PC5. Produce cakes, gateaux, and entremets to a high quality for taste with appropriate
combinations, texture, presentation, and decoration
PC6. Ensure yield products are of consistent and accurate size, weight, quality, and appearance,
taking account of portion control and costs and minimizing waste
PC7. Combine tastes, textures, and colours effectively
PC8. • Present cakes, gateaux, and entremets to maximize appeal and to be appropriate for the
occasion, environment, and style of service and within set times
•
PC9. Serve all cakes at safe and appealing temperatures for consumption
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. A comprehensive range of cakes, gateaux, and entremets, classical and contemporary
KU2. Methods of production, storage, and presentation of cakes, gateaux, and entremets
KU3. Specialist tools used in the production of cakes, gateaux, and entremets
KU4. The range of ingredients used to produce and decorate cakes, gateaux, and entremets
KU5. Variances in international customs and practices and dietary considerations
KU6. Different expectations and definitions relating to cakes, gateaux, and entremets
KU7. Optimum temperatures for consumption

THC/N0457: Hot, cold, iced and plated desserts
Description
This NOS describes the preparation and presentation of Hot, cold, iced, and plated desserts
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparation and presentation of various desserts
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Produce a comprehensive range of hot, cold, and iced desserts to a consistently high
standard with or without allergens and other dietary considerations
PC2. Use appropriate cooking methods and ensure all products are safe and desirable to eat
PC3. • Present plated desserts that are tidy, appropriately flavoured, and textured, well-co-ordinated,
innovative, and balanced
•
PC4. • Present desserts for service for a range of settings and occasions such as street-food concepts,
buffets, banquets and for fine dining
•
PC5. Produce hot, cold, iced, and plated desserts within time constraints and to a consistently
high standard
PC6. • Follow instructions, recipes, and dish specifications in order to produce desserts spontaneously
by applying prior experience & knowledge
•
PC7. React to ingredient shortages or changes and supplement appropriately
PC8. Safeguard against over-production
PC9. Utilize excess with other products
PC10. Use methods which are feasible to the equipment available
PC11. Present at optimum safe temperatures
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. A comprehensive range of classical and contemporary hot, cold, and iced desserts including
methods of production, ingredients, alternative presentations, and costs of production
KU2. Allergies and dietary restrictions
KU3. The range of ingredients used to produce hot, cold, iced, and plated desserts, storage
seasons, availability, and costs
KU4. How hot, cold, iced, and plated desserts can be presented and safely served in a range of
settings and occasions to reflect traditional/classical and contemporary trends
