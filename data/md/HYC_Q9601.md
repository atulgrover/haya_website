HYC/Q9601: Industrial Cleaner - Oil & Gas
Brief Job Description
An Industrial Cleaner in the oil and gas industry is responsible for maintaining the cleanliness and hygiene
of various facility areas, including offices, control rooms, laboratories, restrooms, common areas, and
industrial spaces. This role involves performing a range of cleaning tasks such as sweeping, mopping,
dusting, sanitizing surfaces, and disposing of waste. Housekeepers must adhere to strict safety and
sanitation protocols, ensuring compliance with industry regulations. They are required to use industrial
cleaning equipment and chemicals safely and effectively.
Personal Attributes
Working as an Industrial Cleaner in the oil and gas industry requires specific personal attributes to
maintain high standards of cleanliness and safety. Attention to detail is crucial for ensuring a spotless and
hazard-free environment. A strong safety consciousness is essential, involving strict adherence to the oil &
gas industry protocols. Technical skills, such as using industrial cleaning equipment and understanding
cleaning agents, are fundamental. The job also demands attention to detail, physical stamina, and the
ability to work independently or as part of a team.

HYC/N9601: Prepare for housekeeping activities
Description
This unit covers the preparations required for performing housekeeping operations. It includes selecting
the most appropriate tools and materials for the job and preparing the assigned area for housekeeping.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify housekeeping requirements, procedures and resources as per areas to be cleaned
Prepare for housekeeping activities
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify housekeeping requirements, procedures and resources as per areas to be cleaned
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Check assigned area as per duty roster.
PC2. Inspect the area for cleaning.
PC3. Assess the frequency of cleaning for each area based on usage levels, and industry
standards.
PC4. Assess areas for potential safety hazards, such as chemical/oil spills, tripping hazards, or
biohazards.
PC5. Create detailed cleaning procedures.
PC6. Identify guidelines for handling hazardous materials and waste.
PC7. Implement safety protocols to protect housekeeping staff.
PC8. Arrange personal protective equipment (PPE) and adherence to safety data sheets (SDS) for
all cleaning chemicals.
PC9. Identify necessary cleaning equipment, tools, and materials needed for each area.
PC10. Track the usage of cleaning supplies and equipment to prevent shortages.
PC11. Perform regular inspections of cleaned areas to ensure compliance with established
procedures and standards.
PC12. Collect regular feedback from staff and other personnel on their specific requirements and
efficiency of housekeeping practices.
Prepare for housekeeping activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. Identify specific housekeeping requirements for different areas of the facility in particular
control rooms and laboratories.
PC14. Create a detailed plan outlining the tasks to be performed, including timelines and priorities.
PC15. Communicate with other departments to schedule housekeeping activities at convenient
times to minimize disruption.
PC16. List down environmental regulations and guidelines for collection of waste material and later
disposal.

HYC/N9602: Clean Premises, Buildings & Laboratories
Description
This unit covers cleaning of floors as well as toilets and rest-rooms in different areas of an oil & gas
industry. This unit also includes housekeeping activities to be undertaken in the laboratories, control
rooms, pantry and kitchen area as per standard protocols.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Clean floors, toilets and washrooms
Clean control rooms, laboratories, canteen and kitchen area
Perform assigned housekeeping, responsibilities and reports.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Clean floors, toilets and washrooms
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Select appropriate cleaning tools and chemicals based on the floor type and soil content.
PC2. Choose the appropriate technique for eliminating dust and debris based on the floor and
amount involved.
PC3. Remove any large pieces of debris by hand with cautious
PC4. Apply proper cleaning solutions to remove unrecognised stains.
PC5. Use suitable cleaning agents and methods as per the floor type e.g., mopping for hard floors,
vacuum cleaning for carpets etc
PC6. Ensure that the floor and its surroundings are dry and free of stains after cleaning.
PC7. Remove any dirt, soil etc without causing any damage to the surface.
PC8. Put up appropriate warning signs, such as "Wet Floor," to alert personnel of the cleaning
activities.
PC9. Ensure the floor is dry and safe to walk on before removing warning signs.
PC10. Inform the supervisor of any stains that are impossible to remove.
PC11. Gather all required cleaning materials, including disinfectants, scrub brushes, mops, and
protective gear required for toilets & washrooms.
PC12. Display appropriate signage to indicate that the restroom is temporarily closed for cleaning.
PC13. Clean and disinfect all surfaces, including sinks, countertops, mirrors, and fixtures.
PC14. Pay special attention to high-touch areas such as door handles, faucet handles, and
dispensers.
PC15. Thoroughly clean and disinfect toilets and urinals, including bowls, seats, and flush handles.
PC16. Use appropriate cleaning agents to remove stains, buildup, and odors.
PC17. Sweep and mop the restroom floor with a suitable disinfectant solution.
PC18. Ensure the floor is thoroughly dried to prevent slips and falls.
PC19. Verify that the toilets are emptying and flushing freely.
