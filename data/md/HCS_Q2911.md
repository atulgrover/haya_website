#### HCS/Q2911: Cutting and Threading Operator (Metalware)
Brief Job Description
The job holder typically works in a team and carries out job duties based on job instructions received. The
key job responsibility is to accurately measure and cut metal sheets into desired shape and dimensions
within agreed timelines. The worker works under supervision. The individual at work is responsible to drill
and create screw threads in the metal craft as per specification.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have: attention to details, good eyesight, steady hands, good hand eye
coordination, ability to sit / stand in same position for longer hours, patience and creativity.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N2906: Perform threading on the metal craft
2. HCS/N2910: Manually cut metal sheets into required shapes using appropriate tools and press tools
3. HCS/N9901: Coordinate with colleagues and work as a team
4. HCS/N9902: Maintain Safe and Healthy Work Environment
5. HCS/N9903: Maintain Personal Hygiene
6. HCS/N9904: Basic Business Management

#### HCS/N2906: Perform threading on the metal craft
Description
This OS unit is about creating screw threads in the metal craft by threading process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understanding work requirement preparing for threading operation threading the metal craft using machine
achieving quality and productivity
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding work requirement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. interpret the design requirement
- PC2. understand the drilling requirement for the product (if required)
- PC3. plan the target based on daily requirements
- PC4. interact with supervisor on any measurement related clarification
Preparing for threading operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. make note of internal and external threading requirement
- PC6. make note of the threading requirement on size, number of turns, pitch etc.
- PC7. interpret and select for right hand thread / left hand thread
- PC8. collect and arrange the materials to begin the process
- PC9. check the metal craft and clean the surface for threading
- PC10. report on any shortage or defect of raw materials to the concerned person
Threading the metal craft using machine
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC21. call for maintenance and repair activity whenever required
- PC11. make note of controls and operations of the threading machine (automatic/semi-automatic)
- PC12. select appropriate insert feed type as per size, pitch
- PC13. place the craft accurately on the pointer of the machine where the threading needs to be
done
- PC14. apply appropriate pressure while performing threading operation and ensure no damage to
the craft
- PC15. control the cutting speed
- PC16. must be able to thread as per specification and avoid any angle deviation / error
- PC17. remove chips while threading and ensure no damage to the machine due to chips

#### HCS/N2910: Manually cut metal sheets into required shapes using
appropriate tools and press tools
Description
This OS unit is designed to prepare learners to carry out manual cutting of metal sheets based on received
work instructions. Learners are expected to comply with required quality specifications such as correct
dimensions, shapes, edges, surface requirements, etc. of the work piece.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
work safely prepare work area for metal cutting use approved procedures to cut metal sheets carry out post metal cutting activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Work safely
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. work safely at all times, complying with health and safety legislation, regulations and other
relevant guidelines.
- PC2. maintain correct body posture while seating in an allocated work space for long hours.
- PC3. select and use appropriate personal protective equipment compatible to the work and
compliant to relevant occupational health and safety guidelines.
- PC4. lift, carry or move heavy objects from one place to another using approved safe working
practices.
- PC5. follow electrical safety measures while handling electrically powered tools.
- PC6. ensure that the work area is free of clutters and the environment favorable to carry out
metal cutting work.
Prepare work area for metal cutting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. obtain job specification document or instructions from responsible personnel.
- PC8. confirm that the given information or instructions are complete and correct.
- PC9. interpret job requirements accurately and plan out sequence of work in accordance with job
specifications.
- PC10. obtain appropriate tools, materials and accessories suitable for the required work.
- PC11. confirm that the selected tools and accessories are in safe working condition and ready for
use.

required standard parameters prior to work.
