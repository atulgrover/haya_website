RSC/Q0626: Operator - Triplex & Gluer Operator (Corrugated Boards)
Brief Job Description
The Triplex and Glue Operator is responsible for operating machines that laminate three-ply corrugated
boards and apply adhesives in the corrugated board production line. The role ensures the production of
high-strength boards by proper machine calibration, glue application, and quality assurance.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be attentive, safety conscious, and skilled in teamwork to ensure efficient machine
operation and high product quality.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N0616: Conduct Machine Operation & Setup
- 2. RSC/N0617: Preparation of Adhesive and its application
- 3. RSC/N0618: Inspecting Quality and Basic Troubleshooting
- 4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol

RSC/N0616: Conduct Machine Operation & Setup
Description
This NOS covers performing machine setup and operation as per standard operating procedures to ensure
optimal performance, product quality, and safety compliance during production.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Set up and calibrate the Triplex laminator and glue application machines
Operate the machines efficiently during production run
Perform routine machine maintenance and minor troubleshooting
Elements and Performance Criteria
Set up and calibrate the Triplex laminator and glue application machines
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. inspect the Triplex laminator and glue application machines to ensure they are clean,
functional, and free from defects before starting setup
PC2. select and load the correct liner and fluted paper grades according to production
specifications
PC3. calibrate machine settings such as roller pressure, speed, and temperature based on the
type and GSM of the board to be laminated
PC4. aligned feed rollers, glue pans, and delivery belts accurately to ensure proper sheet flow and
uniform glue application
PC5. adjust glue viscosity and roller speed to achieve optimal adhesive spread and bonding
strength
PC6. perform trial runs and inspect the initial output for defects like misalignment, bubbling, or
glue skips, making necessary adjustments
PC7. record and document machine settings and calibration parameters for reference and quality
control
Operate the machines efficiently during production run
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. monitor machine parameters such as speed, pressure, and temperature continuously to
ensure consistent production quality
PC9. load and feed raw materials (liner and fluted boards) into the machine smoothly without
causing jams or misfeeds
PC10. detect and respond promptly to any irregularities in the machine operation, such as unusual
noises, vibrations, or product defects
PC11. adjust machine controls in real-time to correct issues like glue inconsistencies, sheet
misalignment, or uneven lamination
PC12. maintain uninterrupted workflow by coordinating with upstream and downstream operators
to manage material flow and avoid bottlenecks

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

components and their functions

machine settings and product quality
KU3. standard operating procedures (SOPs) for machine setup, calibration, and operation
KU4. techniques for adjusting roller pressure, speed, temperature, and glue viscosity for optimal
bonding
KU5. common defects such as misalignment, bubbling, or glue skips, and corrective actions to
address them
KU6. methods for conducting trial runs and inspecting laminated boards for quality compliance
KU7. safety practices and lockout/tagout procedures to prevent machine-related accidents
KU8. routine maintenance requirements, including lubrication schedules, cleaning methods, and
preventive checks
KU9. use of basic hand tools, measuring instruments, and cleaning agents safely and effectively
KU10. process documentation standards, including recording machine settings, maintenance logs,
and production data
KU11. communication and coordination protocols with upstream and downstream operators for
smooth production flow

RSC/N0617: Preparation of Adhesive and its application
Description
This NOS covers preparing adhesive mixtures as per formulation requirements and applying them
uniformly on substrates to ensure strong bonding, quality output, and compliance with safety standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare starch-based or synthetic adhesives
Apply adhesive uniformly on corrugated sheets
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare starch-based or synthetic adhesives
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the type of adhesive required (starch-based or synthetic) based on the job card,
board grade, and customer specifications
PC2. measure and mix raw materials (starch, water, resins, additives) in the correct ratio using
manual or semi-automatic mixing equipment
PC3. monitor and adjust adhesive viscosity, pH, and temperature during preparation to achieve
desired bonding properties
PC4. follow safe handling procedures for chemicals and additives used in adhesive preparation,
including the use of gloves, goggles, and aprons
PC5. ensure the mixing container and tools are clean and free from residue before and after use
to prevent contamination
PC6. store prepared adhesive in suitable containers, properly labeled, and transferred to glue
pans or feed systems as per production schedule
PC7. dispose of unused or expired adhesives as per environmental and workplace safety
guidelines
PC8. record batch details, ingredients, preparation time, and operator initials in the adhesive
logbook for traceability and quality assurance
Apply adhesive uniformly on corrugated sheets
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. load the prepared adhesive into glue pans or feed systems while ensuring proper level and
flow consistency
PC10. adjust glue roller gap, speed, and pan position to achieve uniform adhesive application
across the sheet surface
PC11. monitor glue spread during operation to ensure there are no dry spots, over-gluing, or glue
skips that could affect bonding
PC12. control adhesive temperature and viscosity during application to suit environmental
conditions and material types
