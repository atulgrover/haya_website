#### LSS/Q4101: Shoe  Maker
Brief Job Description
Makes entire footwear using hand tools and machine depending on the requirements from the end customers or as required by the footwear manufacturing units.
Personal Attributes
The individual should possess good hand-eye coordination and judgment in positioning and cutting. Also
should have basic estimation and numerical skills along with ability to choose the correct leather/non-
leather parts for different components. He/She should be inclined towards creativity and interested in
mixing materials, textures and threads to create quality products.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. LSS/N4101: Carry out complex repairs on the footwear and other goods
2. LSS/N4102: Carry out footwear making operations using hand tools, equipment, and basic machines
3. LSS/N4103: Contribute to achieving Product Quality in leather repair works and footwear making
4. LSS/N4104: Coordinate with vendors and footwear manufacturing unit for work
5. LSS/N4105: Start and manage enterprise for footwear repair and assembling
6. LSS/N8601: Maintain health, safety & security, a clean work area & equipment, use optimized resources at the workplace, and comply with industry & organizational Requirements
7. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### LSS/N4101: Carry out complex repairs on the footwear and other goods
Description
This unit is about doing complex repairs in footwear, leather goods and bags & luggage
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out complex repairs on the footwear and other goods
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. describe leather, artificial leather & materials
- PC2. understand the materials used in making footwear or goods
- PC3. describe hand tools for repairing of footwear
- PC4. explain stitching and pasting methods
- PC5. explain complex repairing procedures
- PC6. identify different types of stitching
- PC7. stitch the torn part using appropriate tools and materials
- PC8. use hand tools and perform different types of stitching
- PC9. perform heel attaching/ heel replacing/ heel trimming as per repair requirements
- PC10. fix various types of accessories of the footwear
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

- KU1. expectations and responsibilities of the job role
- KU2. organization's rules, codes, guidelines and standards

- KU6. importance of team work and harmonious working relationships

doing so
- KU9. knowledge of the pattern cutting process

#### LSS/N4102: Carry out footwear making operations using hand tools,

Description
The person is required to make complete footwear using tools, equipment and basic machines
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out footwear making operations using hand tools, equipment, and basic machines
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Cutting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. make sure the work area is free from hazards
- PC2. calculate the number of components needed for production
- PC3. select the tools and materials for the work
- PC4. set up the equipment
- PC5. check the quality and characteristics of the material match the required standards before
starting to cut
- PC6. use appropriate tools and equipment for hand or machine cutting
- PC7. make sure that when cutting the material avoid damaging self and others avoid damage to
the knife and other equipment avoid any imperfections in the material when cutting calculate the components required cut appropriately to avoid wastage
- PC8. meet company usage tolerances for efficient pattern interlocking
- PC9. identify and respond to imperfections, defects and damage due to mishandling
- PC10. make sure that the cut material is correctly sorted to assist the next stage of production
Skiving
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. ensure if the Rampi/Khurpi is clean, if not, clean the Rampi/Khurpi
- PC12. ensure if the Rampi/Khurpi is sharp, if not, sharpen the Rampi/Khurpi using the sharpening
stone
- PC13. adjust the width and thickness as per the specifications to perform skiving of the material
(marked leather sheets)
- PC14. carry out skiving operation as per the specification
- PC15. carry out folding post skiving based on the net pattern or base pattern, if required
- PC16. carry out visual inspection to ensure the accuracy of the skiving machine

#### LSS/N4103: Contribute to achieving Product Quality in leather repair
works and footwear making
Description
The person is required to ensure that the quality is achieved while making footwear
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Contribute to achieving Product Quality in leather repair works and footwear making
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Contribute to achieving the product quality
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. analyse the footwear/ leather goods to determine quality issues
- PC2. identify faults in finished products and trace their causes
- PC3. ensure the optimum condition of the hand tools used in footwear making
- PC4. identify faults in materials and products
- PC5. ensure that the stitching is done appropriately and neatly
- PC6. check for any loose threads after the stitching is complete and trim if required
- PC7. ensure that both the pair of the footwear look similar after completion of the process
- PC8. ensure that there is no spillage of glue in any part of the footwear after pasting different parts
- PC9. ensure that enough pressure is put by hand/ hammer after glue application to avoid opening
of any part

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.

| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
