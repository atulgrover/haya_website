#### RSC/Q8101: Assistant Operator - Printing and Packaging (Consumer &
Industrial Products)
Brief Job Description
The individual assists in preparing and operating printing and packaging equipment, supports automated
printing processes, and helps in packaging operations for consumer and industrial plastic products. The
person ensures material flow, maintains equipment cleanliness, follows safety and quality standards, and
coordinates with the team for smooth production.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have good eyesight, and physical fitness to stand for long hours along
with good observational skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N0310: Prepare for Printing and Packaging Operations
2. RSC/N8101: Assist in Operating Automated Printing Machines
3. RSC/N8102: Support in packaging processes
4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### RSC/N0310: Prepare for Printing and Packaging Operations
Description
This NOS covers the skills and knowledge required to prepare raw materials, tools, and equipment for
printing and packaging operations, ensuring readiness of the machine and materials.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Collect and check raw materials
Prepare machines, tools, and consumables
Follow SOPs and documentation requirements
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Collect and check raw materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. receive raw materials (films, foils, labels, cartons) as per job sheet
- PC2. check physical condition of raw materials for defects or damage
- PC3. verify batch number, specifications, and supplier details
- PC4. ensure proper handling of materials during transfer
Prepare machines, tools, and consumables
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. clean rollers, trays, and machine parts before loading materials
- PC6. prepare inks, coatings, and adhesives as per instructions
- PC7. set up tools and consumables required for printing and packaging
- PC8. check availability of safety gear and protective clothing
- PC9. lubricate moving parts of the machine under operator’s supervision
Follow SOPs and documentation requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. fill material requisition and issue slips correctly
- PC11. display material identification tags at workplace
- PC12. segregate defective or rejected materials as per SOPs
- PC13. follow instructions of operator regarding machine readiness
- PC14. perform housekeeping of work area before starting operation
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. basic types of printing substrates and packaging materials

#### RSC/N8101: Assist in Operating Automated Printing Machines
Description
This NOS involves assisting in operating automated printing machines, maintaining smooth production,
performing quality checks, and ensuring safety and efficiency in the printing process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Support in machine operation
Monitor printing quality
Assist in troubleshooting and adjustments
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Support in machine operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. load materials into automated printing machine under supervision
- PC2. align materials properly on feeder trays
- PC3. assist operator in setting machine speed and feed
- PC4. ensure ink trays and rollers are filled as per requirement
- PC5. carry out trial runs as instructed by operator
- PC6. remove printed sheets/rolls and stack properly
- PC7. assist in reel changeover without machine stoppage
- PC8. handle waste collection and disposal during operations
Monitor printing quality
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. observe print clarity, registration marks, and color consistency
- PC10. identify smudges, ink bleeding, or misalignment
- PC11. compare output with sample standards
- PC12. segregate defective prints for reprocessing
- PC13. record production counts and defects
Assist in troubleshooting and adjustments
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. report machine malfunctions immediately to operator
- PC15. help in replacing worn-out rollers or trays
- PC16. check ink viscosity and report deviations
- PC17. carry out minor adjustments like tightening clamps
- PC18. clean printing heads periodically during shifts
- PC19. assist in preventive maintenance checks
- PC20. ensure safe shutdown of machine in emergencies

#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. working principle of automated printing machines
- KU2. major parts of printing press (feeders, rollers, dryers)
- KU3. common printing processes in packaging (flexo, gravure, offset)
- KU4. role of color theory and ink mixing in printing
- KU5. importance of registration marks and alignment
- KU6. quality standards in printed packaging materials
- KU7. common defects in printing and their causes
- KU8. basics of machine troubleshooting and adjustments
- KU9. preventive maintenance schedules for printing machines
- KU10. safety and emergency procedures during printing
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. read simple diagrams, markings, and labels on printing machines and printed material
- GS2. record machine parameters and quality checks in prescribed formats
- GS3. communicate politely with the operator and quality inspector while following instructions
- GS4. apply basic mathematical calculations such as measuring sheet sizes, roll lengths, and counting printed outputs
- GS5. recognize routine printing defects (misalignment, smudging, color fade) and inform the operator
- GS6. use digital or manual tools (counters, gauges) for assisting machine operations
- GS7. follow sequence of tasks and maintain discipline while handling automated equipment
- GS8. report abnormalities such as machine vibration, overheating, or jammed material to the operator

#### RSC/N8102: Support in packaging processes
Description
This NOS covers supporting activities in packaging of consumer and industrial products, including material
handling, assisting in automated packaging operations, and maintaining workplace hygiene and safety.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Handle packaging materials
Support packaging operations
Maintain packaging area and equipment
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Handle packaging materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. bring required cartons, films, or foils to the machine
- PC2. check packaging materials for damage before use
- PC3. cut or prepare materials to required size
- PC4. load packaging materials into machine
Support packaging operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. assist in machine-based sealing, cutting, or wrapping
- PC6. stack packed goods as per batch size
- PC7. apply labels or barcodes under guidance
- PC8. segregate defective packs
- PC9. record packaging counts
Maintain packaging area and equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. clean sealing jaws, rollers, or conveyors
- PC11. carry out basic lubrication of packaging machine
- PC12. keep packaging area organized and free of clutter
- PC13. follow waste segregation norms in packaging area
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. types of packaging materials for consumer & industrial products
- KU2. basic functions of packaging: protection, identification, branding

#### RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
Description
This unit is about maintaining health and safety of self and others at workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
Render appropriate emergency procedures
Maintain standard safety procedures at the workplace
Participate in safety awareness campaigns
Understand potential sources of accidents
Use safety gears to avoid accidents
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. undertake basic safety checks before operation of all machinery and equipment and report
hazards to the appropriate supervisor
- PC2. work for which protective clothing or equipment is required is identified and the appropriate
protective clothing or equipment is used in performing these duties in accordance with workplace policy
- PC3. read and understand the hazards of use and contamination mentioned on the labels of
chemicals, utilities etc
- PC4. prior to performing manual handling jobs, risk is assessed and work is carried out according
to currently recommended safe practices.
- PC5. use equipment and materials safely and correctly and return the same to designated storage
when not in use
- PC6. dispose off waste safely and correctly in a designated area
- PC7. risks to bystanders are recognized and action taken to reduce risk associated with jobs in the
workplace
- PC8. perform work in a manner which minimizes environmental damage
- PC9. all procedures and work instructions for controlling risk are followed closely
- PC10. report any accidents, incidents or problems without delay to an appropriate person and take
immediate necessary action to reduce further danger
Render appropriate emergency procedures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. follow procedures for dealing with accidents, fires and emergencies, including
communicating location and directions to emergency.
