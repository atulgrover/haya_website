#### WBSC/HCS/Q2501: Toy Maker (Soft Toys),V2
Brief Job Description
Soft toy maker creates soft toy by using various fabrics, threads, decorative materials, etc. The artisan
performs various tasks like cutting, stitching to make the end product.
Personal Attributes
A soft toy maker should be creative, patient and detail-oriented with a strong sense of craftsmanship and
design. They should have good hand-eye coordination and fine motor skills allowing them to cut, stitch and
assemble materials with precision. They should be imaginative and artistic often visualizing the final
product before it is made and they show care and dedication in their work to ensure quality and safety.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. WBSC/HCS/N2501: Apply Safe Working Practices (Toy Maker (Soft Toys),V2).
2. WBSC/HCS/N2502: List out the classification of soft toys and its utility.
3. WBSC/HCS/N2503: Identify and procure the raw materials and tools needed for making the soft toys as per design.
4. WBSC/HCS/N2504: Select the design and position each parts as per drawing.
5. WBSC/HCS/N2505: Recognize the manufacturing steps for each type of doll till its finish in expected shape.
6. WBSC/HCS/N2506: Make a bear/elephant with proper design and complete it with stitching and stuffing and complete it in expected shape.
7. WBSC/HCS/N2507: Display different types of soft toy for livelihood. Make proper marketing strategy to sell the product.
8. WBSC/HCS/N2508: Work in real job situation with special emphasis on basic safety and hazards in
this domain (Toy Maker (Soft Toys),V2).
9. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### WBSC/HCS/N2501: Apply Safe Working Practices (Toy Maker (Soft
Toys),V2).
Description
Apply Safe Working Practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Safe Working Practices.
Unsafe situations according to site policy.
Fire and safety and fire hazards.
Different types of fire extinguishers and use them as per requirements.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Apply and maintain Safe Working Practices.
- PC2. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy.
- PC3. Identify fire and safety and fire hazards.
- PC4. Identify different fire extinguishers and use them as per requirements.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Procedures to achieve a safe working environment in line with occupational health and safety
regulations and requirements according to site policy.
- KU2. Any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assess his report accordingly.
- KU3. Necessary precautions on fire and safety hazards and report according to site policy and procedures.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Recognize any unsafe situations according to site policy, and assess his report accordingly.
- GS2. Demonstrate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) like: safety helmet, safety glove, and safety
shoe, use the same as per related working environment.
- GS3. Demonstrate basic first aid & CPR and use them under different circumstances.
- GS4. Identify different fire extinguishers and use the same as per requirement in a mock drill.

Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
