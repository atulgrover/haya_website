#### ITG/ELE/Q0101: Industrial IoT and Edge AI Analyst
Brief Job Description
Learners will understand the prevailing difficulties for Industries to adopt IoT, Learner will be able to design
and develop an intelligent and sustainable digital transformation solution under Industry 4.0/Industry 5.0
with cloud server and all needed securities.
Personal Attributes
Knowledge of programing (preferably C/C++, python) and Basic Electronics and Embedded System
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ITG/ELE/N0101: Advance Embedded C
2. ITG/ELE/N0102: ARM based Multicore Architecture
3. ITG/ELE/N0103: Embedded Linux
4. ITG/ELE/N0104: IoT Architecture and Deployment
5. ITG/ELE/N0105: Industrial IoT
6. ITG/ELE/N0107: Edge AI
7. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)
8. ITG/ELE/N0106: Cloud Management for IoT

#### DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)
Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Career Development & Goal Setting
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Employability Skills

- PC1. understand the significance of employability skills in meeting the current job market
requirement and future of work
