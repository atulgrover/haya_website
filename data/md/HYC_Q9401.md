HYC/Q9401: Secretarial Office Assistant - Oil & Gas
Brief Job Description
Individual at this job is responsible for collecting information from different offices/plants and compiling
those for the preparation of daily reports, MIS reporting, interpretation of data and making presentations,
managing communication, coordinating meetings & events, schedule appointments, maintain records &
documentation, taking minutes, making travel arrangements, assisting with project coordination,
managing office supplies & equipment, Drafting and editing documents, handling confidential information,
providing general administrative support to the reporting officer in an oil & gas industry. The person is
responsible for operating digital tools used for modern office management. The role of Secretarial
Assistants is to work closely with senior management, providing one-on-one administrative support,
organizing schedules and maintaining official records for companies or organizations in Oil & Gas sector.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have good sense of responsibility, a good organizer and time keeper. The person
should have the ability to work in a team and maintain excellent interpersonal relationships.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HYC/N9401: Preparation for secretarial office administrative task in oil & gas industry
- 2. HYC/N9402: Perform secretarial office administrative task in oil & gas industry

- 5. HYC/N9302: Maintain health, safety and security procedures
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

HYC/N9401: Preparation for secretarial office administrative task in oil &
gas industry
Description
This unit is about preparation for office administration task, agenda and schedule of the day, collecting
information, interpreting the data for preparation of report specifically tailored to the oil & gas industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare agenda and schedule meeting for the day
Prepare reports and update MIS
Check readiness of general office administrative tools
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare agenda and schedule meetings for the day
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. prepare and update agenda of the day for reporting officer using digital tools
PC2. prioritize the important meetings or tasks
PC3. consider logistical details such as reserving a meeting room, setting up audiovisual
equipment or arranging for any necessary refreshments.
PC4. send meeting invitations to all participants, including the date, time, location and agenda for
each meeting and assisting in these tasks
PC5. Assist in coordination with participants well in advance
Prepare reports and update MIS
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. • collect updated information/data from departments/field/plants/offices subject to hydrocarbon
value chain operation and administration such as
• Upstream - Drilling Report, Mud Logging Report, Well Testing Report, Geophysical Survey
Report etc.; Midstream - Inventory Report, Logistics Report, Supply Chain Report, Pipeline Report
etc.;
• Downstream - Production Summary Report, Maintenance Report, Safety Report, Energy
Consumption Report, Environmental Compliance Report, Safety Incident Report,
PC7. Take dictation, transcript it and all types of correspondence, prepare presentations, report,
documents as per the standard formats defined in oil & gas companies or PSUs
Check readiness of general office administrative tools
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. arrange stationary, related documents, refreshment, etc. for day-to-day office activity
PC9. check the readiness of equipment and tools required for general safety of office
PC10. arrange preventive maintenance of digital devices used in office

HYC/N9402: Perform secretarial office administrative task in oil & gas
industry
Description
This unit is about performing secretarial and administrative task in oil & gas corporate or divisional office,
coordinate and communicate with stakeholders, schedule meetings, organize files and documents, record
minutes, manage travels, drafting and editing documents and letters, etc.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage communication
Schedule appointments, coordinate meetings / events, record minutes
Maintain records and documentation, drafting and editing documents
Making travel arrangements
Manage office supplies and equipment, provide general administrative support
Reporting
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage communication
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. communicate and respond to the telephone calls from internal and external stakeholder
PC2. connect / transfer the calls related to the reporting officer
PC3. record and communicate the information to the reporting officer
PC4. forward digital / physical mails or correspondence to the reporting officer
PC5. communicate important notices issued by ministry, regulatory body, corporate office, etc. to
the respective stakeholders and assist in this activity
PC6. liaison / follow-up with regulatory agencies, government bodies, stakeholders as per the
instruction from reporting officer
Schedule appointments, coordinate meetings / events, record minutes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. schedule and coordinate meetings, conferences, and events with various internal and
external stakeholders’ such as executives, employees, clients, suppliers, etc
PC8. prepare meeting agenda, relevant documents and communicate it with respective
stakeholder
PC9. check the readiness of event / meeting venue and coordinate for the event / meeting
PC10. record Minutes of the Meeting (MoM), prepare actionable and circulate it with the respective
stakeholders after approval
Maintain records and documentation, draft and edit documents
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. prepare and maintain digital and physical copy of files

HYC/N9403: Maintain confidentiality and work ethic
Description
This unit is about maintaining confidentiality and work ethic in the office, building trust and ensure a
professional environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain confidentiality and work ethic
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain confidentiality and work ethic
