#### HCS/Q6902: Assembly Machine  Operator (Woodware)
Brief Job Description
The individual is responsible for all the assembly operations by putting all discrete wood pieces together as
per fit to replicate the sample.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be fit, confident with machines, have an eye for detail and be willing to
work in a physically demanding environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N6902: Plan assembly operations
2. HCS/N6904: Carry out undisrupted operations as per requirements organization standards
3. HCS/N6903: Assemble wooden pieces as per design
4. HCS/N6608: Use basic health and safety practices in a wood working environment
5. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### HCS/N6902: Plan assembly operations
Description
This unit covers planning all kinds of assembly operations for producing wooden products, considering the
work flow and dispatch schedules.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
interpret the work requirement plan a suitable activity schedule achieve productivity, quality and safety standards as per company's policy
work safely
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Interpret the work requirement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. interact with the supervisor in order to accurately interpret and calculate the day's
production requirement
- PC2. interact with the supervisor to accurately interpret the delivery schedules of the batch being
prepared
- PC3. ensure that all machines and equipment used in the production process are in safe working
condition
Plan a suitable activity schedule
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. plan the day's production activities based on the feedback obtained from supervisor
- PC5. use appropriate job instructions or work manuals to scope the work accurately
- PC6. ensure smooth assembly operations to enable timely delivery of assembled pieces to other
departments for further processing
- PC7. ensure that the activity plan is in accordance of the urgency of delivery of any batch for
further processing
- PC8. obtain the right amount of supplies, machinery, tools and equipment required for carrying
out assembly operations
Achieve productivity, quality and safety standards as per company's policy
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. work, plan and achieve 100% target of daily production
- PC10. work, plan and achieve 100% of planned work scheduled on or before time
- PC11. work, plan and achieve zero errors as per the company's standards
- PC12. record any defects/inadequacies noted during the assembly process
- PC13. maintain safety standards as per company policy
Work safely

#### HCS/N6904: Carry out undisrupted operations as per requirements
organization standards
Description
This unit involves ensuring that the assembled final product is as per the design specification. It is also
involved in maintaining productivity of the process and machine.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
check and ensure that the work after every assembly cycle is complete undertake corrective and preventive measures on the machine
achieve productivity and quality standards
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Check and ensure that the work after every assembly cycle is complete
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. carry out work tasks to achieve the specification requirement of the final product as provided
through supervisor instructions, product sample or design documents
- PC2. check the output of every cycle to make sure it is in accordance with the set standards
- PC3. diagnose and reject the discrepancies, if any, in the final output
- PC4. maintain a uniformity in the final output of every cycle
Undertake corrective and preventive measures on the machine
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. maintain machine to avoid downtime by regular cleaning, usage as per guidelines and
following preventive maintenance schedule
- PC6. perform regular cleaning and maintenance operations to enable smooth flow of activities
- PC7. diagnose any loopholes in the assembly operations while on the machine
- PC8. take corrective measures immediately to avoid any damages or losses
Achieve productivity and quality standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. plan and achieve 100% target of daily production
- PC10. plan and achieve achieve 100% of planned work scheduled on or before time
- PC11. plan and achieve achieve zero errors as per the company's standards
- PC12. record any defects/inadequacies noted during the assembly process
- PC13. maintain safety standards as per company policy

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

#### HCS/N6903: Assemble wooden  pieces as per design
Description
This unit deals with the assembly of discrete wooden pieces (cut as per design ) into the proper to make
the finished product as per sample or design.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
prepare the assembly machine feed the work pieces to assembly machine manual assembly final assembly
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare the assembly machine
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. assess and quantify the assembly requirements of the day through production plans,
supervisor instructions, etc.
- PC2. compare the assembly requirements to capabilities and capacities of each part of the
assembly machine to arrive at a feasible work plan that meets production requirements
- PC3. plan the assembly tasks in a sequential manner taking into account the activity undertaken
by the assembly machine at each stage
- PC4. obtain raw materials, supplies and components based on production plan requirement and
feed rate of the machine
- PC5. ensure the machine is clean, safe and approved for usage
Feed the work pieces to assembly machine
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. switch on and off the machine safely and as per work requirements
- PC7. identify the time taken at each stage and the required flow of wood pieces to assembly
machine for optimal productivity
- PC8. maintain a desired flow of wooden pieces as per requirement of the machine
- PC9. achieve the right balance between the wooden pieces and machine to avoid any
damage/loss of material from overflow of material supply to the machine
- PC10. use appropriate job instructions or work manuals to carry out the work
- PC11. ensure the smooth assembly operations to enable timely delivery of assembled pieces to the
other department for further processing
- PC12. ensure that the activity plan is in accordance of the urgency of delivery of any batch for
further processing
Manual Assembly
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
