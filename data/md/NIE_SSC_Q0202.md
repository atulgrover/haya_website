#### NIE/SSC/Q0202: Office Automation and IT Assistant
Brief Job Description
This course provides learners with practical knowledge and hands-on skills in computer fundamentals,
office productivity tools, internet applications, desktop publishing, and cybersecurity. It enables learners to
use MS Office applications (Word, Excel, and PowerPoint), email, web browsers, digital payment systems,
and cloud-based collaboration tools effectively. The course also introduces desktop publishing software
such as PageMaker, Photoshop, CorelDRAW, and Canva for designing professional publications.
Additionally, it emphasizes cybersecurity awareness, safe digital practices, protection of personal devices,
and the secure use of e-Governance and e-Commerce services. The curriculum is further strengthened
through employability skills training and project-based learning supported by On-the-Job Training (OJT).
Personal Attributes
The individual should possess logical and analytical thinking, attention to detail, willingness to learn new
technologies, effective communication and teamwork skills, problem-solving ability, time management,
adaptability, creativity, and a responsible attitude towards cybersecurity, data privacy, and professional
ethics. They should be capable of working independently as well as collaboratively while maintaining
accuracy, quality, and customer-focused service.

#### NIE/SSC/N0211: Fundamentals  of Computers  and Operating Systems*
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to understand computer fundamentals, hardware and
software components, memory and storage devices, input/output devices, data representation, operating
systems, file and folder management, cyber hygiene, and the effective use of ICT, mobile, and smart
technologies for personal and professional applications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the knowledge and skills required to identify computer components, understand hardware and software concepts, manage files and folders in Windows and Linux operating systems,
apply cyber hygiene practices, and use ICT, mobile, and smart technologies effectively for everyday
computing tasks.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify the basic components of a computer, including hardware, software, memory, storage,
and input/output devices.
- PC2. Operate Windows (GUI-based) and Linux (CLI-based) operating systems, and manage files and
folders efficiently.
- PC3. Apply cyber hygiene practices to protect systems from viruses and other cyber threats.
- PC4. Use ICT applications, mobile devices, and smart technologies for everyday personal and
professional tasks.
- PC5. Demonstrate an understanding of data representation and the effective use of computer
systems for digital operations.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Explain the fundamentals of computers, including their classification and components
- KU2. Describe memory, storage devices, input/output devices, and data representation concepts.
- KU3. Understand the functions of hardware, software, Windows (GUI), and Linux (CLI) operating systems.
- KU4. Explain file and folder management, cyber hygiene practices, and protection against computer viruses.
- KU5. Understand the applications of ICT, mobile devices, and smart technologies in personal and
professional environments.

#### NIE/SSC/N0215: Office Productivity Tools*
Description
This NOS equips learners with the knowledge and practical skills required to use modern office productivity
applications for creating, managing, analyzing, and presenting information. It covers word processing,
spreadsheets, presentations, cloud collaboration, database fundamentals, and AI-enabled productivity
tools to improve workplace efficiency, communication, and digital document management.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes creating and formatting professional documents, developing spreadsheets with
formulas and charts, designing presentations, using cloud-based collaboration tools, applying AI
features in office applications, managing files through cloud storage, understanding basic database
concepts, and integrating office productivity tools for efficient workplace communication and information management.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Create, edit, format, review, and print professional documents using word processing
software, including templates, styles, tables, and mail merge.
- PC2. Develop and manage spreadsheets by entering data, applying formulas and functions,
creating charts, using conditional formatting, and generating PivotTables for data analysis.
- PC3. Design, customize, and deliver professional presentations using presentation software by
incorporating themes, multimedia, animations, transitions, and presentation tools.
- PC4. Use cloud-based collaboration tools to create, store, share, synchronize, and co-author
documents while maintaining version control and data security.
- PC5. Apply AI-enabled productivity features in office applications to generate content, improve
document quality, analyze data, and enhance workplace productivity using appropriate digital tools.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The features, functions, and applications of office productivity software, including word processing, spreadsheets, presentation software, cloud storage, and database tools.
- KU2. Document creation and formatting techniques, including styles, templates, tables, mail merge, page layout, printing options, and document review features.
- KU3. Spreadsheet concepts, including data entry, cell referencing, formulas, functions, charts,
sorting, filtering, conditional formatting, and PivotTables for data analysis.
- KU4. Principles of presentation design, multimedia integration, animation, transitions, presentation
delivery techniques, and the use of AI-assisted presentation tools.

networking fundamentals, internet architecture, online services, search techniques, cybersecurity best
practices, email etiquette, and digital collaboration tools essential for personal and professional use.

The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the knowledge and skills required to use the internet, web browsers, email, and
digital collaboration tools, perform secure online transactions, practice internet safety, and protect
digital accounts using password management and Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).

- PC4. Perform secure digital financial transactions using internet banking and digital payment
systems.
- PC5. Secure digital accounts using strong passwords, password managers, and Two-Factor
Authentication (2FA).
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

- KU1. Explain the fundamentals of networking, internet architecture, and internet services.
- KU2. Describe the functions of web browsers, search engines, and email services.

- KU4. Explain the principles of digital financial literacy, internet banking, and digital payment
systems.

(2FA) in securing digital accounts.

#### NIE/SSC/N0212: Concept  of Desktop Publishing Software-
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to create, edit, format, and publish professional
documents using Desktop Publishing (DTP) software. It includes page layout, text and image editing,
graphic design, print and web publishing, and the use of AI-powered design tools for efficient content
creation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the knowledge and skills required to use DTP applications for page design, text
formatting, graphics creation and editing, image handling, layout management, print and web publishing, and AI-assisted design tools to produce professional-quality publications.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Create and format professional documents using DTP software, including page setup, text
formatting, styles, and templates.
- PC2. Design and edit graphics using drawing tools, shapes, images, layers, filters, and text effects.
- PC3. Manage page layouts by importing, arranging, aligning, grouping, and organizing text and
graphic elements.
- PC4. Prepare publications for print and web by applying appropriate print settings, export options,
and web publishing features.
- PC5. Utilize modern AI-powered design tools and platforms (e.g., Canva) to create visually
appealing and professional publication materials efficiently.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Explain the fundamentals of Desktop Publishing (DTP) and its applications.
- KU2. Understand page layout, text formatting, styles, templates, and document design principles.
- KU3. Understand print and web publishing options, including file import, export, and optimization.
- KU4. Explain the use of modern AI-powered design tools (e.g., Canva) for creating professional publications.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Create and format professional publications using DTP tools with accuracy and creativity.
- GS2. Organize and manage text, images, and graphics effectively for print and digital media.

1. The question papers for the theory and practical exams are set by the Examination wing (assessor) of NIELIT HQS.

Proctors authenticate the candidate based on registration details, pre-test image captured and I- card in possession of the candidate. Proctors can chat with candidates

7. For re-examination prescribed examination fee is required to be paid by the candidate only for
the assessment component in which the candidate wants to reappear.
8. There would be no exemption for any paper/module for candidates having similar qualifications or skills.
