TSC/Q4101: Knitting Machine Operator Circular Knitting
Brief Job Description
A Circular knitting machine operator is responsible for operating industrial Circular knitting machines,
repair yarn related faults at the knitting head and fabric press-offs, operate process machines ancillary to
the knitting production, identify and sort wastes, and demonstrate knowledge of yarn-related faults in
knitted fabrics. This job requires the individual to have thorough knowledge in process flow and material
flow in a knitting machine for fabric production and should know the important functions and operations of
knitting machines.
Personal Attributes
A Knitting operator should have good eyesight, hand-eye-leg coordination, motor skills and vision. He/she
should also have good interpersonal skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. TSC/N4101: Taking charge of shift and handing over shift to Knitting Machine Operator -Circular
Knitting
- 2. TSC/N4102: Operate the Circular Knitting machine
- 3. TSC/N4103: Knotting the yarn and taking off the fabric roll from the circular knitting machine
- 4. TSC/N4104: Repair yarn related faults in Circular Knitting

- 6. TSC/N9002: Working in a team

- 8. TSC/N9004: Comply with industry and organizational requirement

TSC/N4101: Taking charge of shift and handing over shift to Knitting
Machine Operator -Circular Knitting
Description
This unit is about taking charge of shift from previous shift Knitting Machine operator circular Knitting and
relieving the responsibilities to the next shift Knitting Machine operator-circular Knitting
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Taking charge of shift from Knitting Machine operator-circular Knitting
Handing over shift to Knitting Machine operator-circular Knitting
Elements and Performance Criteria
Taking charge of shift from Knitting Machine Operator-circular Knitting
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- 1. reach atleast 10 - 15 minutes early to the work place
- 2. bring the necessary operational tools to the department
- 3. meet the previous shift operator and collect the information regarding the yarn count, process,
issues faced in quality, and current beam production followed in the knitting department
- 4. be updated on information regarding the current order such, as it is for which company and
other details
- 5. ensure proper functioning of machine and problems if any should be reported to the supervisor
and maintenance in- charge
- 6. understand and be informed about the current order quantity and balance quantity
- 7. be updated on the new order fabric details and quantity
- 8. contribute in over all department cleanliness
Handing Over of Shift to Knitting Machine operator-circular Knitting
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- 9. clean the machine and department before handing over the shift
- 10. hand over the necessary operational tools if any
- 11. meet the next shift operator and give the information regarding the count, GSM, loop length,
process, issues faced in quality, and current fabric production followed in the knitting
department
- 12. note the production details for the current shift
- 13. Ensure proper functioning of the machine and problems if any, should be reported to the
supervisor and maintenance incharge
- 14. give details to the next shift operator about the current order quality, quantity and balance
quantity
- 15. convey information regarding new order fabric details and quantity

TSC/N4102: Operate the Circular Knitting machine
Description
This unit is about carrying out the procedure for operating the circular knitting machine
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operate the circular knitting machine
Repair yarn related faults at the knitting head and fabric press-offs
Carry out maintenance activities
Material handling and safety at workplace
Other tenting responsibilities
Elements and Performance Criteria
Operate the Circular knitting machine
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. start the machine
PC2. operate the control switches for starting and stopping the knitting machine
PC3. follow the signal lamps used in machines
PC4. ensure proper functioning of the knitting machine by verifying in the display panel
PC5. ensure the machines are operated in accordance with workplace procedures.
PC6. ensure the yarns are run through correct yarn-paths at operating tensions according to
machine-builders instructions
PC7. ensure the yarns are creeled and ends joined in accordance with workplace procedures.
PC8. ensure the production is monitored for faults, and variations notified
PC9. ensure the machines are monitored for continuous functioning of all systems, and variations
notified
PC10. ensure the doff is removed from machine as soon as it is full
PC11. ensure the documentation of production is completed in accordance with workplace
procedures.
PC12. ensure the machines are lubricated as directed in machine manual.
PC13. ensure the yarn paths; eyelets, knitting heads, machine parts and working environment is
clean and free of contamination.
PC14. check whether the yarns are properly fed in the knitting machine
PC15. knot the yarn during breakage
PC16. view the display panel or signal and identify the reasons for machine stoppages if any
PC17. ensure the knitting machine is running in the set speed by viewing the display panel
PC18. ensure the working area is clean
