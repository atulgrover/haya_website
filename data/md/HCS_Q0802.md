#### HCS/Q0802: Sketching and painting artisan - Ceramics
Brief Job Description
A Sketching and Painting Artisan in ceramics specialises in creating intricate designs and vibrant colours
on ceramic pieces. They combine artistic talent with technical skills to enhance the aesthetic appeal of
each item, ensuring that every piece is a unique work of art.
Personal Attributes
The Sketching and Painting Artisan in ceramics is highly creative, with a keen eye for detail and a passion
for artistic expression. They possess strong technical skills in painting and sketching, along with patience
and precision to bring their designs to life on ceramic surfaces.

#### HCS/N0820: Perform Sketching
Description
This OS unit is about sketching the designs on the ceramic mould with plain dark shades using appropriate
brushes
Scope
The scope covers the following :
create/assimilate designs sketch on the ceramic product ensure quality and production standards
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Create / assimilate designs
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. create unique, intricate and appealing designs, in the absence of model
- PC2. create and design market relevant designs
- PC3. create appropriate designs depending on the product shape, in the absence of model
- PC4. structure the dimension of the designs according to the product shape, in case of absence of
model
- PC5. select the design depending on the end usage of the product, in case of absence of model
- PC6. pay close attention to the various aspects of the design
- PC7. integrate design based on model
- PC8. decide colour, type of brush and color consumables to be used based on the model
- PC9. use recommended tools and colours for sketching
Sketch on the ceramic products
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. recognize the appropriate and relevant colours for sketching
- PC11. carefully sketch the design from the model according to the product dimension using various
tools
- PC12. sketch using the right stroke of brush in the right thickness and angle
- PC13. check visually if the patterns match to the model
- PC14. set aside the sketched product
- PC15. set aside the colours and tools in appropriate locations
Ensure quality and productivity standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC16. visually examine the designs/models for conformance with the product
- PC17. ensure that there is no wastage of the materials
- PC18. create unique designs which is difficult to replicate

#### HCS/N0205: Perform Glazing
Description
This unit focuses on the glazing process, which is a critical step in ceramic production. It includes the
knowledge and skills required to select, prepare, and apply glazes to enhance the durability, appearance,
and functionality of ceramic pieces.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
preparing the surface glaze preparation application techniques layering and detailing firing the glazed piece
final touches maintaining tools and work area
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparing the Surface
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. clean and smooth bisque-fired ceramics to remove dust or imperfections
- PC2. dry completely the painted design before glazing
- PC3. choose glazes that complement the painted designs, such as transparent, opaque, or matte
finishes
Glaze Preparation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. mix powdered glaze with water to achieve the desired consistency
- PC5. sieve the glaze to remove lumps for smooth application
- PC6. test glaze consistency (thickness, viscosity) for proper application
Application Techniques
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. use wax resist or masking tape to protect intricate hand-painted areas if a transparent glaze
is not being used
- PC8. use soft brushes to apply glaze in layers, suitable for detailed painted designs
- PC9. dip large items to achieve consistent coverage without drips
- PC10. pour glaze over large or irregular surfaces
- PC11. spray by using an airbrush for a fine and even coat, suitable for intricate designs
Layering and Detailing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. apply multiple glaze layers to achieve depth or special effects

#### HCS/N8519: Entrepreneurship Skills for Sketching and Painting Artisan
(ceramics)
Description
This module provides artisans with the tools and knowledge to manage and grow a ceramics-based business, particularly for those specializing in sketching and painting ceramic items. It covers key aspects
such as product design and development, market research, branding, and financial management all aimed
at helping artisans navigate the competitive market while preserving their unique artistic vision.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understanding business fundamentals product design and development branding and promotion sales and negotiation
marketing execution customer relationship management financial management sustainability practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Business Fundamentals
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. learn the basics of starting and running a business, including legal formalities, registration,
and licenses
- PC2. understand market dynamics specific to ceramic art, such as demand for custom-painted
ceramics and seasonal trends
Product Design and Development
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. sketch unique designs for ceramic products that cater to specific markets
