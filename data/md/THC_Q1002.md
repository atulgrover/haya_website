THC/Q1002: Advance Diploma in Hospitality Operations
Brief Job Description
The Advance Diploma in Hospitality operations is a qualification designed to equip learners with
comprehensive skills and knowledge in the hospitality industry. it covers key areas such as food
production, f & b service, accommodation operations, business communication and hospitality professional
skills. The program also includes elective modules like food styling and photography or digital sales &
marketing, allowing students to enhance manage their learning experience. This also has a very important
component as Industrial training which provides hands on experience in real world hospitality settings,
enhancing employability.
Personal Attributes
Diverse career opportunities, Specialized job roles, Industry exposure and growth, Higher education and
entrepreneurship opportunities
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. THC/N0464: Intermediate Course in Food Production
- 2. THC/N0347: Intermediate Course in Food & Beverage Service
- 3. THC/N0155: Intermediate Course in Accommodation Operations
- 4. THC/N2916: Business Communication- III
- 5. THC/N1001: Hospitality Professional Skills
- 6. THC/N9922: Emotional Intelligence
- 7. THC/N9923: Entrepreneurship & Innovation
- 8. THC/N1002: Industrial Training
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Food Styling and Photography
This NOS focuses on the artistic presentation of food, combining culinary aesthetics with professional
photography techniques to create visually appealing food images. It covers plating, lighting, composition,
and editing to enhance food branding, marketing, and social media presence.
- 1. THC/N0465: Food Styling and Photography

THC/N0464: Intermediate Course in Food Production
Description
This NOS covers essential culinary techniques, kitchen operations, and food safety standards, equipping
learners with practical skills for professional food preparation. It focuses on advanced cooking methods,
ingredient handling, and menu planning to enhance employability in the hospitality industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develops skills in food preparation, cooking techniques, and kitchen safety
Enhances knowledge of menu planning, ingredient selection, and cost control
Efficient kitchen management.
Provides a foundation for specialization in bakery, confectionery, or international cuisines.
Elements and Performance Criteria
European Cuisine
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain History and Regions of European Cuisine (French, German, Italian, Spanish)
PC2. Describe Popular ingredients used in French cooking.
PC3. Explain Special equipment used in European cuisine.
PC4. Write about popular European dishes.
PC5. • Prepare various types of Menus:
• a. French
• b. Italian
• c. Spanish
• d. German
Asian Cuisine

PC9. Describe various Tools and equipment's of Asian cuisine.
PC10. Explain Cooking Methods and Techniques used in Asian Cuisine
PC11. • Prepare various types of Menus:

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

KU2. Popular ingredients used in French cooking such as herbs, butter, wine, and seafood.
KU3. Special equipment used in European cuisine like mandolins, ravioli cutters, and souffle
dishes.
KU4. Popular European dishes such as Coq au Vin (French), Spaghetti Carbonara (Italian), and
Paella (Spanish).
KU5. Common ingredients used in Asian cuisine like soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and chili
paste.
KU6. Specialty dishes in Asian cuisine such as Sushi (Japanese), Pad Thai (Thai), and Kimchi
(Korean).
KU7. Tools and equipment used in Asian cuisine including woks, bamboo steamers, and sushi
mats.
KU8. Cooking methods and techniques in Asian cuisine like stir-frying, steaming, and grilling.
KU9. History and regions of Mediterranean cuisine including Lebanese, Turkish, and Greek.
KU10. Uses of wine in Mediterranean cooking such as deglazing, marinades, and reductions.
KU11. Various herbs used in cooking such as thyme, rosemary, basil, and oregano.
KU12. Menu preparation for Mediterranean cuisine including Lebanese, Greek, and Turkish dishes.

THC/N0347: Intermediate Course in Food & Beverage Service
Description
This NOS equips learners with essential skills in restaurant operations, guest service, and beverage
handling. It focuses on professional table service, order management, and customer interaction to
enhance the dining experience.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develops expertise in table setting, order taking, serving etiquette, and handling beverages.
Enhances knowledge of food pairing, menu explanation, and guest engagement.
Improve service standards.
Career opportunities in fine dining, cruise ships, banquets, and airline catering.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Alcoholic Beverages
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Introduce & define Alcoholic beverages.
PC2. Classify Fermented Alcoholic Beverages
PC3. Define and explain fermentation & distillation
PC4. Identify Bar equipment and Glassware
PC5. Demonstrate Bar setup and operations
PC6. Practice on Menu - matching wines with food
Introducing Wines
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Explain different terminologies associated with wine
PC8. Name and explain wine producing countries and regions
PC9. Explain different types of Grapes.
PC10. Classify different types of Wines.
PC11. Describe the production process of Wines and types (Table/Still/Natural, Sparkling, Fortified,
Aromatized, Fruit Wines and Bitters)
PC12. Explain the storage of Wines
PC13. • Demonstrate –
• a. Service of White Wines
• b. Service of Red Wines

• e. Service of Fortified Wines

THC/N0465: Food Styling and Photography
Description
This NOS focuses on the artistic presentation of food, combining culinary aesthetics with professional
photography techniques to create visually appealing food images. It covers plating, lighting, composition,
and editing to enhance food branding, marketing, and social media presence.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Enhances skills in visual storytelling, branding, and digital marketing for restaurants, food bloggers,
and culinary businesses.
Provides opportunities in editorial photography, cookbook publishing, and social media content
creation.
Equips culinary professionals with the ability to collaborate with other chefs, marketers, and media
houses to create high-quality food visuals.
