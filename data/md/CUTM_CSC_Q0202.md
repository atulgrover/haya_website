#### CUTM/CSC/Q0202: CNC  Milling Programmer
Brief Job Description
CNC Milling Programmer is responsible for understanding the design requirements, material, generate CAM
design for manufacturing of various parts using CNC lathe, inspect the parts to finalize and deliver the
finished product. Also maintain good condition of the machine with regular maintenance
Personal Attributes
Personal attributes for a CNC Milling Programmer include strong attention to detail for interpreting designs
and process planning, technical proficiency in CAM programming and machine setup, troubleshooting skills
for preventive maintenance, and the ability to operate CNC machines efficiently. Additionally, a keen eye
for quality control, inspection, and finalizing products ensures precision in delivering high-quality, finished
components.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CUTM/CSC/N0205: Interpretation of designs, drawings and process planning
2. CUTM/CSC/N0206: CAM Programming, setting equipment Machining & Preventive maintenance of machines
3. CUTM/CSC/N0207: Operate CNC Milling machine and complete the machining process
4. CUTM/CSC/N0208: Inspect, finalize and deliver the product
5. CUTM/VSQ/N7805: Ensure and Implement Safety standards at Work Place

#### CUTM/CSC/N0205: Interpretation of designs, drawings and process
planning
Description
This NOS covers the skills and knowledge required to read and interpret mechanical engineering drawings
and translate them into an actionable machining plan. It includes understanding CAD models, manufacturing tolerances, materials, and sequencing required for CNC milling operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS includes interpretation of technical drawings, selection of materials and tools, and detailed
process planning for CNC milling operations. It helps ensure that parts are manufactured to specification, reducing errors and improving production efficiency.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Interpret technical drawings and specifications of the product.
- PC2. Identify critical features of the product.
- PC3. Analyze manufacturability with the available resources.
- PC4. Identify and prepare for potential assembly issues if any.
- PC5. Identify and prepare for any maintenance issues that may arise during production.
- PC6. Specify stock to be kept for different operations.
- PC7. Identify critical sequences (with a high possibility of damage or unsafe practice).
- PC8. Plan the production of parts according to specifications.
- PC9. The importance of planning to improve efficiency.
- PC10. Procedures to manufacture molds with the available resources.
- PC11. Machining operations and their sequences.
- PC12. Method of clamping work pieces.
- PC13. Choices of cutting tools and cutting parameters.
- PC14. Machine and work piece setting.
- PC15. Measuring tools and equipment.
- PC16. Bench work and assembly techniques.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. engineering drawings and geometric tolerances.
- KU2. symbols, surface finish, and dimensional standards used in blueprints.

#### CUTM/CSC/N0206: CAM  Programming,  setting equipment Machining  &
Preventive maintenance of machines
Description
This NOS covers the use of CAM software for programming CNC milling machines, setting up tools and
workpieces, and conducting preventive maintenance. It ensures that machines are configured correctly for
optimal performance and longevity.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Includes programming and simulation in CAM, transferring programs to machines, physical setup, tool preparation, and routine maintenance. It ensures efficient and safe operation of CNC milling
machines in a production environment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Select the best sequence for machining each specific workpiece.
- PC2. Program manually and in CAM software.
- PC3. Transfer programs to machines.
- PC4. Set work pieces and tools.
- PC5. Apply the principles and processes of Computer Aided Manufacturing (CAM)
- PC6. Set up and use machine centre input data into CNC machine controllers (tool offset, work
offset, etc.)
- PC7. Create machining programs in software, and transfer to machine controllers.
- PC8. Test the finished products and assess them for accuracy in accordance with the specified
drawings.
- PC9. Machine each part of the dies, taking account of each plastic product’s requirements.
- PC10. measure pieces of work accurately.
- PC11. set offsets according to measured size.
- PC12. achieve the required geometry and finish.
- PC13. Fabricate all parts to commercial standards using: Bench grinders.
- PC14. Do preventative maintenance to avoid issues arising.
- PC15. Make repairs when problem arise.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. CAM software interfaces and post-processing.
- KU2. how to define machining operations in CAM.

Description

parameters when needed, and ensuring that the final product meets specifications. It includes real-time
monitoring and problem resolution during machining.

and efficient handling of operational challenges. This ensures continuous production flow and quality
maintenance on CNC milling machines.

- PC6. Optimize the machining strategy.

type, type of operation, and CNC machine tool.

#### CUTM/VSQ/N7805: Ensure and Implement  Safety standards at Work Place
Description
This NOS focuses on developing the ability to operate CNC machining centers safely and efficiently while
adhering to health and safety standards. It includes creating CAM programs, selecting tools and cutting
parameters, maintaining machinery, communicating effectively with teams and clients, and continuously
upgrading skills to ensure excellence in manufacturing processes.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope encompasses CNC machine operation, safety compliance, program development, tool selection, maintenance, and effective collaboration with team members and customers. It promotes
high professional standards through ongoing skill enhancement, problem-solving, and customer engagement while maintaining health and safety protocols throughout the manufacturing lifecycle.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. adhere to health and safety regulations in the workplace
- PC2. proactively promote best practice in health and safety in the working environment
- PC3. work independently on CNC machining centres
