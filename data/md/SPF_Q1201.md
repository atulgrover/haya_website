#### SPF/Q1201: Camping  & Trekking Guide
Brief Job Description
A camping and trekking guide offers insights, recommendations, and guidance for outdoor enthusiasts
engaging in activities like camping and trekking. They possess extensive knowledge in various aspects of
the outdoors, including gear selection, campsite selection, trail navigation, and safety precautions while
enjoying outdoor adventures.
Personal Attributes
A camping, and trekking guide should possess certain personal attributes to effectively guide and assist
others in outdoor adventures. They should have a strong sense of adventure and enthusiasm for the
outdoors, as well as excellent communication skills to convey information and instructions clearly.
Additionally, they should be patient and adaptable, able to handle unexpected situations and provide
solutions calmly.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SPF/N1201: Plan for camping and trekking activities
2. SPF/N1202: Guide and monitor trekkers
3. SPF/N1203: Conclude camping and trekking activities
4. SPF/N1204: Maintain health & safety standards during the trekking activities
5. SPF/N1169: Improve workplace resource usage
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### SPF/N1201: Plan for camping and trekking activities
Description
This unit is about planning for outdoor, camping and trekking activities.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare logistics for the trek
Set fitness expectations for the trek
Prepare navigation and orientation
Select gear and equipment for the trek
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare logistics for the trek
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. assess trekkers' preferences, skill levels, and physical capabilities for outdoor activities
- PC2. create detailed itineraries, including transportation arrangements and accommodation
options
- PC3. assist in obtaining necessary permits and ensuring compliance with local regulations to
ensure a smooth and legal outdoor experience
- PC4. consider factors like weather conditions, seasonal variations, and terrain challenges
- PC5. identify suitable routes, estimating distances, rest stops and water sources
Set fitness expectations for the trek
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. assess trekkers' fitness levels and provide guidance on physical preparation
- PC7. recommend appropriate clothing and footwear for trekking
- PC8. discuss pacing, rest stops, and managing fatigue during long treks
- PC9. educate on the importance of hydration and nutrition during trekking activities
Prepare navigation and orientation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. utilize topographic maps, compasses, GPS devices, and trail markers for navigation
- PC11. interpret trail maps, understanding elevation changes, and identifying landmarks
- PC12. teach navigation skills to trekkers, including route planning and map reading
- PC13. adapt navigation strategies in response to unexpected circumstances or changes in trail
conditions
Select gear and equipment for the trek
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. recommend appropriate gear based on camping activity, climate, and duration
- PC15. assist trekkers in selecting camping gear and clothing

#### SPF/N1202: Guide and  monitor trekkers
Description
This unit is about guiding and monitoring trekkers
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Educate trekker on basics of hiking and trekking
Guide trekkers on nature observation
Manage waste during camping and trekking
Work effectively with others
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Educate trekkers on basics of hiking and trekking
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. guide trekkers to explore trek through diverse terrains
- PC2. guide trekkers to watch their foot placement on uneven terrain, including rocks, roots, or
slippery surfaces, to prevent slips, trips, or falls.
- PC3. guide trekkers to maintain good balance by keeping the center of gravity aligned and using
trekking poles if necessary
- PC4. guide trekkers to cultivate a positive mindset and maintain mental resilience during
challenging sections of the hike or trek
- PC5. guide trekkers in practicing mindfulness and deep breathing exercises to stay present and
engaged in the experience
- PC6. ensure that trekkers drink water regularly, even before feeling thirsty to prevent dehydration
- PC7. ensure that trekkers consume nutritious snacks or meals, such as energy bars, fruits, and
trail mix, provides sustained energy throughout the outdoor, camping and trekking
- PC8. ensure that trekkers are mindful of dietary restrictions or allergies and carrying suitable food
options is essential for maintaining energy levels and overall well-being
- PC9. guide trekkers to set up tents and create temporary campsites to spend the night immersed
in the outdoor environment
- PC10. conduct activities to provide opportunities for trekkers to appreciate starry nights, and enjoy
the serenity of nature
Guide trekkers on nature observation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. guide trekkers to engage in observing and appreciating the diverse flora and fauna found in
the outdoor environment
- PC12. help trekkers in spotting various species of birds, animals, and plants and learn about their
behaviors and habitats
- PC13. guide trekkers, if required, to capture images of landscapes, wildlife, and natural wonders
encountered during the activities

#### SPF/N1203: Conclude  camping and trekking activities
Description
This unit is about performing post camping and trekking activities
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Evaluate the effectiveness of the camping and trekking program
Maintain camping and trekking equipment post usage
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Evaluate the effectiveness of the camping and trekking program
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. debrief with the trekkers to review their achievements and reflect on the challenges faced
- PC2. organize a post-trip celebration or gathering to commemorate the adventure
- PC3. compile and curate photos and videos to create a memorable trip album or slideshow
- PC4. write a trip report summarizing the highlights, challenges, and lessons learned
- PC5. share the trip experiences and recommendations on social media or a personal blog
- PC6. send personalized thank-you notes or emails to trekkers for their participation
- PC7. follow up with trekkers to offer additional resources or answer any lingering questions
- PC8. update personal knowledge and skills by researching new routes, camping spots, or trekking
techniques
- PC9. engage in professional development activities, such as attending workshops or certifications
related to outdoor guiding
- PC10. conduct a comprehensive post-trip analysis to identify areas for improvement in future
outings
- PC11. share trip stories and insights with fellow guides or colleagues in the outdoor industry
- PC12. collaborate with local organizations or communities to support conservation efforts in the
area visited
- PC13. evaluate and refine safety protocols and risk management strategies based on the
experience
- PC14. seek opportunities for continued learning and growth in outdoor leadership and guiding skills
- PC15. coordinate with local authorities or land management agencies to ensure compliance with
regulations and permits
- PC16. research and recommend local businesses or services that align with sustainable and eco-
friendly practices
- PC17. reflect on personal growth and achievements gained through guiding the outdoor, camping,
and trekking experience
Maintain camping and trekking equipment post usage
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

#### SPF/N1204: Maintain health & safety standards during the trekking
activities
Description
This unit is about maintaining health & safety standards during the trekking activities
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct risk assessment
Maintain equipment
Prevent and attend to injuries
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct risk assessment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. conduct a thorough inspection of the trek, camping site to identify any potential hazards or
risks that could pose a danger to trekkers
- PC2. assess the severity and likelihood of identified hazards to determine the level of risk they
pose
- PC3. identify risks based on their severity and develop a plan to mitigate or eliminate them
effectively
- PC4. carry out proactive measures to minimize risks, such as implementing safety signage, setting
up protective barriers, and providing necessary safety equipment
- PC5. monitor and review the effectiveness of risk mitigation measures, making necessary
adjustments and updates to maintain a safe environment
Maintain equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. conduct routine inspections of equipment to identify any signs of damage, wear, or
malfunction
- PC7. get the equipment cleaned and sanitized to maintain cleanliness, hygiene, and prevent the
