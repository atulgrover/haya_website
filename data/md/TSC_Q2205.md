#### TSC/Q2205: Knotting Machine  Operator
Brief Job Description
A Knotting Machine Operator is a job-role in the weaving preparatory department. The responsibility of a
Knotting Machine Operator is to Knot the warp ends in the run out beams with that of the fresh beams.
efficiently so as to get quality output with minimum defects giving due importance to safety and
environmental aspects.
Personal Attributes
A Knotting Machine Operator should have good eyesight, eye-hand coordination, motor skills and vision
(including near vision, distance vision, colour vision, peripheral vision, depth perception and ability to
change focus).
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. TSC/N2209: Taking charge of shift and handing over shift to Knotting Machine Operator
2. TSC/N2210: Operate the Knotting Machine
3. TSC/N9001: Maintain work area, tools, and machines
4. TSC/N9002: Working in a team
5. TSC/N9003: Maintain health, safety and security at workplace
6. TSC/N9004: Comply with industry and organizational requirement

#### TSC/N2209: Taking charge of shift and handing over shift to Knotting
Machine Operator
Description
This unit is about taking charge of shift from previous shift knotting machine operator and relieving the
responsibilities to the next shift knotting machine operator
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Taking charge of the shift from Knotting Machine operator
Handing over the shift to Knotting Machine operator
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Take Charge of the Shift from knotting machine operator
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. come at least 10 - 15 minutes earlier to the work place
- PC2. check for the necessary items like gum tape, knotting comb and knotting brush, knife etc.
- PC3. meet the previous shift knotting machine operator& discuss with him/ her regarding the issues
faced by them with respect to the quality, production, machine, safety or any other specific instruction etc.
- PC4. check the run outs for his/ her shift.
- PC5. check whether any sort change program is there for the said run outs
- PC6. check the availability of the beams and accordingly inform the same to the higher authority
- PC7. check the availability of the knotting needles required for the running counts of the running
beams
- PC8. check the condition of the knotting stand, knotting machine, knotting needles etc.
- PC9. take over the shift from the outgoing operator in a proper manner
Handing over the Shift to knotting machine operator
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. hand over the shift to the incoming knotting machine operator in a proper manner & get clearance from the incoming counterpart before leaving the work spot
- PC11. report to his/ her shift superiors as well as that of the incoming shift, in case his/ her counterpart doesn't report for work, in such a case, the shift has to be properly handed over to
the incoming shift superior and get clearance sought from him/ her, before leaving the work spot
- PC12. report to his/ her shift superior about the quality / production / safety issues or any other issue
faced in his/ her shift, and should leave the department only after getting concurrence for the
same from his/ her superiors
- PC13. ensure the work place is clean

#### TSC/N2210: Operate  the Knotting Machine
Description
This unit provides performance criteria, knowledge & understanding and skills & abilities required to
operate a knotting machine, so as to get quality output & minimum defects, without entertaining any
damage to the people, the machine etc., & without spoiling the environmental aspects.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Mounting of Beams on the Looms
Dressing of Beams
Knotting
Running of knotted looms
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Mounting of Beams on the Looms
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure that the loom is stopped with warp just enough (around 2-3 meters) for knotting
- PC2. report to the higher authority if the loom is stopped with excess warp
- PC3. when the knotting has to be carried out in the looms with twin beams (in most of the cases),
ensure that there is no difference in the left out warp meters
- PC4. correct the dropper crossing (if any)
- PC5. check whether any ends are left out in the run out beams, particularly near the selvedges and
bring the same to the knowledge of the higher authority and act as per his/ her instructions
- PC6. check the condition of the reed on the loom in the selvedge sides for any deviation, the same
has to be brought to the knowledge of the higher authority and act as per his/ her advice
- PC7. check whether any bead formation is there in the droppers. if so, the same has to be removed/cleaned
- PC8. check the condition of the beams for the beam fault like soft size, size patches, shade variation
etc. the same has to be brought to the knowledge of the higher authority and act as per his/ her
advice
- PC9. cut the old warp leaving only 2-3 meters of warp length required for knotting
- PC10. cover the left warp with card board or polythene sheet or with hope sack if the qty left out due
to beam complaint is more as per the advice of the higher authority
- PC11. remove the cut/ empty beams from the loom shed and transport to the place where they have to be stored
- PC12. clean the loom and all the places thoroughly, as advised by the supervisor
- PC13. check whether the fresh beams are properly covered with paper, card board or polyethene sheet to avoid any damage during handling

- PC14. check the loom card for following to ascertain the fresh beams to be loaded: warp count, total
ends, set no., beam no.s ( for the opposite sides), beam meters ( there should not be any difference in meters).
- PC15. bring the fresh beams from the place they are stored to the loom, where knotting has to be carried out
- PC16. check the condition of the fresh beams after removing the beam wrappers, for transit damages;
in case of the same it has to be brought to the notice of the higher authorities. the fresh beam
has to be checked faults like sunken ends, soft size, size patches, etc.; further action can be
carried out as per the advice of the higher authority only.
- PC17. mount the fresh beams on the loom carefully without causing damage to the raw material, beams, machine, or for any individual, including himself or herself
- PC18. ensure beam alignment in case of twin beams
Dressing of Beams
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC19. ensure no cross ends in the old warp. a group of not more than 100 ends can be taken & brushed properly
- PC20. check for the sizing tapes in the fresh beams
- PC21. cut the fresh warp in the second tape
- PC22. bring the fresh warp to the knotting stand without any cross ends using knotting comb
- PC23. correct the cross ends in the old warp also using knotting comb, before bringing the same to the
knotting stand
- PC24. dress the warp sheet carefully without giving room for cross ends or cut ends etc
- PC25. weigh the knotting waste & the same has to be recorded in the knotting entry note
- PC26. tie the thrums (sufficient enough till the run out) on the loom neatly.
- PC27. collect the knotting waste & store in the place earmarked
Knotting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC28. use the needle that is earmarked for the beams mounted
- PC29. check the machine & the needle by doing few knots. for any deviation, bring the same to the
notice of the higher authority. do the knotting of the beams in the speed as advised by the higher authority
- PC30. check for double end, knot slippage etc. through close monitoring of the performance of the
knotting
Running of Knotted Looms
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC31. remove the knotting machine from the stand
- PC32. remove the knotting stand from the knotted loom, collect the waste and to get the same stored
in the place earmarked
- PC33. apply the beam locks (if any) & to see that the knotted beams are in tight condition so as to run
the machine without any problem till the knots reach the droppers zone
- PC34. bring the knots out of the droppers, heald wires, reed etc., without any breakage of warp ends
- PC35. draw the broken warp ends
- PC36. ensure proper functioning of the warp stop motion

#### TSC/N9001: Maintain work area, tools, and machines
Description
This unit provides performance criteria, knowledge & understanding and skills & abilities required to
organise/ maintain work areas and activities to ensure tools and machines are maintained as per norms
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintaining the work area, tools and machines
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintaining the work area, tools and machines
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. handle materials, machinery, equipment and tools with care and use them in the correct way
- PC2. use correct lifting and handling procedures
- PC3. use materials to minimize waste
- PC4. maintain a clean and hazard free working area
- PC5. maintain tools and equipment
- PC6. carry out running maintenance within agreed schedules
- PC7. carry out maintenance and/or cleaning within ones responsibility
- PC8. report unsafe equipment and other dangerous occurrences

| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |

| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
