#### SPF/Q8101: Sports Goods  Manufacturing Helper
Brief Job Description
Sports goods manufacturing helper are responsible for assisting with the production process in factories.
Moves carts and dollies with items on them, unloads trucks and boxes, and loads pieces onto conveyor
belts, assists in cleanup, start up and stop equipment, etc.
Personal Attributes
Individuals in this role should have the ability to follow instructions, self-motivated and energetic.
Individuals in this role must demonstrate a very good listening and comprehension skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SPF/N8101: Assist in Production of Sports Goods
2. SPF/N8102: Maintain Waste and Scrap Control
3. SPF/N1169: Improve workplace resource usage
4. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### SPF/N8101: Assist in Production of Sports Goods
Description
This unit is about preparing equipment and materials for production of sports goods.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist in preparing for the production of sports goods
Handle production materials
Assist with equipment handling
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist in preparing for the production of sports goods
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the work order and is able to meet the timelines as specified by the supervisor
- PC2. prioritizes and schedules the work in the most efficient order for the day, accounting for
inventory, maintenance schedules, etc.
- PC3. help with machine setup by ensuring that materials and tools are correct, available, and
ready to use.
- PC4. load materials and/or removes product to prevent bottlenecks from forming.
- PC5. assist in the monitoring of production equipment, under direction, to ensure machine
operation is within acceptable limits.
- PC6. provide relevant information (e.g., inventory levels, production numbers, build records, etc.)
in order to enable decision- makers to have reliable information for review.
Handle production materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. ensure self and others use appropriate lifting and handling techniques according to
Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) standards and SOPs
- PC8. assess safest and most efficient way to move materials to appropriate destination(s)
- PC9. select appropriate equipment to aid in material handling, as required.
- PC10. ensure the availability of the material to run the order
- PC11. follow quality processes to identify nonconforming materials
- PC12. mix ingredients, according to procedure
- PC13. load and unloads processing equipment or conveyance used to receive raw materials or to
ship finished products
Assist with equipment handling
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. clean and lubricates equipment
- PC15. replace damaged or worm equipment parts

#### SPF/N8102: Maintain Waste  and Scrap Control
Description
This unit is about performing cutting operations using hand and machine.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Handle waste and recyclable materials
Follow safe housekeeping practices
Work effectively with others
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Handle waste and recyclable materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. report of materials ordered, materials used, and waste/scrap generated in order to ultimately
reduce waste/scrap
- PC2. categorizes waste according to SOPs and determines if waste is hazardous and/or regulated
by legislation to ensure compliance
- PC3. store the waste according to the waste characteristics and storage requirements
- PC4. ensure all hazardous waste is labelled, stored, and/or sent to the correct location as per
internal and external requirements to minimize harm to people and the environment
- PC5. keep records to provide evidence that the waste is stored according to all requirements
- PC6. remove product, machine attachments, and waste material from machine
- PC7. identify recyclable and non-recyclable, and hazardous waste generated
- PC8. segregate waste into different categories
- PC9. dispose non-recyclable waste appropriately
- PC10. deposit recyclable and reusable material at identified location
- PC11. follow processes specified for disposal of hazardous waste
Follow safe housekeeping practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. practice good housekeeping in own area to reduce risk of injury to self and others.
- PC13. use lockout/tag-out procedures when repairing/servicing tools and equipment.
- PC14. ensures self and others use personal protective equipment (e.g., face masks, gloves, safety
glasses, steel-toed boots, hearing protection, etc.).
- PC15. inspect tools, materials, equipment, and machines prior to use for visible defects and informs
appropriate personnel if damage found
- PC16. report health and safety hazards, injuries, or concerns, including any unsafe machine
practices, damaged equipment, and workplace harassment to supervisors.
Work effectively with others

| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
