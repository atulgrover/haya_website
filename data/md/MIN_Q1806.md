#### MIN/Q1806: Rare Earths Extraction Plant Operator
Brief Job Description
A Rare Earths Extraction Plant Operator is responsible for conducting the extraction of rare earth using
appropriate equipment and following the applicable processes including health and safety at the worksite.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the task at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should also be physically agile, strong, have good eye sight and not suffer from
colour-blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MIN/N1817: Use different material handling equipment in rare earth extraction plant
2. MIN/N1818: Undertake different unit operations in rare earth extraction plant
3. MIN/N1819: Undertake the treatment of rare earth extraction plant effluents
4. MIN/N1820: Follow the Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) for chemicals
5. MIN/N1821: Carry out corrective and preventive maintenance of RE extraction plant equipment
6. MIN/N1810: Follow the applicable health, safety and environmental guidelines (Placer Mining)
7. MIN/N1811: Work collaboratively and effectively in a team

#### MIN/N1817: Use  different material handling equipment in rare earth
extraction plant
Description
This unit is about conveying the monazite sand to the processing plant through pipeline systems and
conveyors
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operation of belt conveyors and bucket elevator
Operation of screw conveyors
Operation of pumps and pipelines
Operation of valves
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Operation of belt conveyors and bucket elevator
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. check the material handling equipment, such as bucket elevator, bucket belt conveyor, belt
conveyor, screw conveyors etc., for the correct functioning
- PC2. check the layout and components of the belt conveyor system including its principal
components, i.e. conveyor belt, clips, roller chains, guards, tensioners, feed and discharge chutes, head, tail and take-up pulleys, bearings, braking system, controls such as switches,
trip cords, interlocks, alarms, etc.
- PC3. check the mechanical and electrical driving units including safety devices for effective
functioning
- PC4. convey the monazite sand to the processing unit considering appropriate parameters, such
as feed/ material rate, alignment of conveyor belts, spillage amount and rate, conveyor jams, etc.
- PC5. inspect the equipment for potential issues, e.g. unusual noises, blockages and obstruction,
and the requirement for lubricating motors, conveyor and electromagnet
- PC6. check the hoppers and bins, chutes, and guardrails deployed to control stockpile levels
Operation of screw conveyors
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. check the layout and components of screw conveyors including conveyor screw, trough,
hanger bearings, couplings, internal collar, end lugs, shafts, feed/discharge chute, drive mechanisms, lubrication systems, controls, drive belts, hangers/cables, etc., for wear and
tear or damage
- PC8. feed material to the various processing equipment, maintaining the recommended
parameters, e.g. feed rate, voltage, Revolution per Minute (RPM), etc.
- PC9. identify safety hazards, such as spillage, leaks, abnormal noises and odour, and take
appropriate preventive and corrective measures
Operation of pumps and pipelines

couplings, internal collar, end lugs, shafts, feed/discharge chute, drive mechanisms, lubrication systems, controls, drive belts, hangers/cables, etc.
- KU6. the duties of workmen at a rare earth extraction plant
- KU7. the provision of wages, working hours and accident compensation as per the Mines act
- KU8. the specific safety instructions for conveying systems (pull chord, label switch, fire alarm
etc.)
- KU9. the belt mechanisms/systems (belt fastening, belt jointing etc.)

#### MIN/N1818: Undertake  different unit operations in rare earth extraction
plant
Description
This unit is about undertaking different rare earth extraction plant unit operations, e.g. grinding, attacking,
leaching, crystallizing, centrifuging, etc
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Grinding operation
Attack operation
Leaching process
Utilities
Crystallization process
Centrifuging operation
TSP flash drying operation
Evaporation process
RE extraction process
Deactivation process
RE evaporation process
Filtration
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Grinding operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. follow the working principle of the ball mill, considering parameters like feed/ material rate,
residence time
- PC2. follow the standard procedure for the startup and shut down of the ball mill unit
- PC3. using rope/barriers, signboard, etc., to minimize the relevant hazards and report the
condition to the supervisor
- PC4. identify spillage, blockages, obstruction etc., and take appropriate corrective measures
Attack operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. follow the standard procedure of agitator unit startup and shutdown
- PC6. maintain the relevant process parameters during the addition of caustic lye and steam
- PC7. ensure the availability of guards and fences against potential hazards like spills, leaks and
overflows
Leaching process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. follow the leaching process appropriately
- PC9. monitor the working of the pedal type of agitator, and decantation operation

#### MIN/N1819: Undertake  the treatment of rare earth extraction plant
effluents
Description
This unit is about treating and disposing of rare earth extraction plant effluents.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Treat and dispose of the RE extraction plant effluents
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Treat and dispose of the RE extraction plant effluents
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure the effluent from the RE processing plant is sent to the effluent treatment area
- PC2. treat the effluent using the required chemicals
- PC3. check the pH level of effluent to ensure it is fit for discharge within the limits set by the
Pollution Control Board (PCB)
- PC4. decant the clear solution to another tank, after the settling process
- PC5. carry out the filtration of the slurry and discharge the solid waste, as per the discharge norms
- PC6. check the sample of water being discharged and monitor the level of water in ponds
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. different types of chemical plants and their operations
- KU2. the importance of time-keeping, discipline and punctuality
- KU3. various chemical processes carried out in a rare earth extraction plant
- KU4. the in-force standing orders relevant to effluent management at the company/plant
- KU5. the impact of violation of applicable safety procedures
- KU6. the relevant quality norms
- KU7. different types of unit operations involved in rare earth extraction, chemical processes, and
associated equipment
- KU8. different types of effluent treatment processes and the use of associated equipment
- KU9. the use of different water purifying and waste treatment agents
- KU10. the hazards and the appropriate safety measures in acid/ alkali handling activities and usage
of relevant PPE

#### Assessment Guidelines

Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.

4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
