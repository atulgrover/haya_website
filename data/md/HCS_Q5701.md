#### HCS/Q5701: Carpet Weaver
Brief Job Description
A carpet weaver is a skilled artisan who intricately crafts carpets by hand or using looms, blending
traditional techniques with creative designs to produce unique, high-quality textiles that enhance interior
spaces.
Personal Attributes
A carpet weaver needs to be careful and creative, with plenty of patience and skill. This allows them to
create detailed designs while ensuring their work is high-quality and reflects cultural traditions.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N8522: Entrepreneurship skills for Carpet weaver
2. HCS/N9929: Working in a team
3. HCS/N9931: Maintain health and safety at workplace
4. HCS/N9934: Managing Personal Sanitation
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Carry out the process of knotted carpet weaving
Knotted carpet weaving is the process of tying individual yarn knots onto a loom to create intricate designs
and textures, resulting in durable carpets that often reflect cultural heritage.
1. HCS/N5708: Install loom for knotted carpet weaving
2. HCS/N5709: Prepare warp structure for knotted carpet weaving and fix into the loom
3. HCS/N5710: Perform advanced knotted carpet weaving
Elective 2: Carry out the process of tufted carpet weaving
Tufted carpet weaving involves inserting yarn into a backing material using a tufting gun or hand tools to
create loops or cut piles, resulting in soft, durable carpets with intricate designs.
1. HCS/N5705: Perform tufted carpet weaving as per given design

#### HCS/N8522: Entrepreneurship skills for Carpet weaver
Description
This NOS outlines the competencies required to develop entrepreneurial skills for individuals involved in
the carpet weaving industry. It focuses on practical knowledge, business planning, financial management,
and market-driven strategies necessary to operate a successful carpet weaving enterprise.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develop a Business Plan
Manage Resources and Production
Financial Management
Marketing and Sales Strategies
Innovation and Sustainability
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Develop a Business Plan
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify business goals and objectives for carpet weaving, considering market demand and
personal skillset.
- PC2. Research target markets, customer preferences, and competitor activities in the carpet
industry.
- PC3. Establish a pricing model based on raw material costs, labor, and competitive market rates.
Manage Resources and Production
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Select appropriate raw materials (wool, silk, cotton) that meet design and quality standards.
- PC5. Plan and organize production processes to ensure timely completion of carpet orders.
- PC6. Maintain and operate weaving tools and equipment efficiently to minimize downtime.
Financial Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Keep accurate financial records, including costs, sales, and profits, for the carpet weaving
business.
- PC8. Prepare a budget and monitor expenses to ensure financial sustainability of the business.
- PC9. Analyze financial statements to evaluate business performance and make necessary
adjustments.
Marketing and Sales Strategies
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Develop a brand identity that reflects the uniqueness and craftsmanship of the woven
carpets.
- PC11. Create a marketing strategy that includes online and offline promotion, leveraging e-
commerce platforms and social media.

#### HCS/N9934: Managing  Personal Sanitation
Description
This NOS provides the abilities required for taking responsibility for their own health at the workplace and
is about using the correct procedures to prevent, control and minimize risk to them and others at the
workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
adopt healthy work practices achieve work productivity while maintaining health
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Adopt healthy work practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. always cover the mouth and nose with a dust mask while working and keep on changing when
it gets blocked with dust
- PC2. follow work instructions strictly to reduce the amount of pollution at the work place e.g. wet
the rock / craft material before working on it
- PC3. wear protective goggles over eyes and replace them when scratches on it obscure the vision
- PC4. wear gloves as per the materials used for making handicraft to avoid blisters; scratches and
cuts
- PC5. undergo preventive health checkups at regular intervals
- PC6. take prompt treatment from the doctor in case of illness
Achieve work productivity while maintaining health
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. follow SOPs for dealing with blisters; scratches; accidental fires or any other type of
