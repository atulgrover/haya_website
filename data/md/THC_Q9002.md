#### THC/Q9002: Ground Staff (Paratrike, Paramotoring, Paragliding)
Brief Job Description
The ground staff is responsible for completing all pre and post flight ground duties of the concerned aero
activity. This includes equipment clean up, assembling and repair work under close supervision of the
ground crew chief/equipment in charge.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to withstand long working hours in open areas under the sun. The ground
staff should have an aptitude to grasp and execute repairs for equipment as well as be attentive and
efficient.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. THC/N9014: Prepare for the Paragliding/Paramotoring/Paratrike Flight as Ground Staff

#### THC/N9014: Prepare for the Paragliding/Paramotoring/Paratrike Flight as
Ground Staff
Description
This unit is about assessing pre-flight information and completing equipment checks
Scope
The scope covers the following :
complete pre-flight requirements manage persons with disabilities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Complete pre-flight requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. access daily work instructions and activity schedule from the
Paragliding/Paramotoring/Paratrike ground crew chief/equipment-in-charge/organizer.
- PC2. assist the ground crew chief/equipment-in-charge in performing a visual inspection of all
components of the wing such as panels, ribs, structural diagonals and straps, plastic rod sleeves, tabs, lines, splices, risers, seams, etc.
- PC3. ensure to check the condition and numbers of all personal protective equipment like harness,
carabiners and helmets carefully and have them ready before the students/clients arrive for the activity.
- PC4. ensure to maintain a service record and log of any damaged equipment and hand over daily
records to ground crew chief/equipment-in-charge/organizer.
- PC5. assist the ground crew chief/equipment-in-charge to check all the connections and fittings
before a flight.
- PC6. use two-way radios and follow essential communication terms/signals to coordinate with the
ground crew chief/equipment-in-charge, when needed.
- PC7. assist the Tandem Pilot in queuing and anchoring the students/clients based on their weight
and weather conditions.
- PC8. document photos and videos of the activity on action cameras provided by the company.
Manage persons with disabilities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. offer help to persons with disability, when asked for.
- PC10. empathize with and respect persons with disabilities.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the company’s reporting structure and documentation procedures.

#### THC/N9015: Complete Post Flight Requirements as Ground Staff
Description
The unit is about completing post flight requirements including equipment clean up, packing and
submitting activity report.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
pack up equipment and submit activity report
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Pack up equipment and submit activity report
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure to check the condition of all equipment after the activity.
- PC2. ensure to carefully fold and pack parachute/canopy as well as other equipment and return to
the equipment-in-charge/ground crew chief.
- PC3. maintain a record of the total duration the canopy and equipment was used for.
- PC4. record loss or damaged equipment in the logbook and submit it to the equipment-in-
charge/ground crew chief.
- PC5. ensure to collect written performance feedback from the equipment-in-charge/ground crew
chief/organizer daily.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the manufacturer's recommended equipment maintenance policy.
- KU2. proper methods of post activity equipment check and pack up.
- KU3. company's documentation procedures including post trip equipment record logbook entries.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. write and report information.

#### THC/N9016: Assess and Mitigate Risks as Ground Staff
Description
This unit is about conducting risk/hazard evaluation and taking measures to mitigate them.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
support in taking safety measures and responding to emergency situations.
disaster management.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Support in taking safety measures and responding to emergency situations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure to keep the operational area protected, safe and hazard free and inform the ground
crew chief/equipment-in-charge about any turbulence sources or hazards observed.
- PC2. ensure to keep the students/clients under observation and away from the operations area.
- PC3. ensure that Personal Floatation Devices (PFD) are attached to the flying equipment when
operating near water.
- PC4. ensure that first-aid kits, stretchers (spinal board) are easily accessible on the site and provide
basic first aid/CPR, if required.
- PC5. ensure to follow the prescribed emergency action plan and have all emergency equipment at
hand in case of an emergency.
- PC6. ensure to have the radio communication devices ready before/during/after the flight and
follow instructions from the ground crew chief/tandem pilot.
- PC7. maintain calm and be composed in emergency situations.
Disaster management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. assist the ground crew chief/equipment-in-charge with establishing communication and
coordinating with the tandem pilot/organizer and all third party vendors to provide land and air rescue, in case of an emergency.
- PC9. support in evacuating students/clients from the danger zone and provide shelter till rescue
arrives, in case of disasters.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. organisational policies and procedures to enable safe response to an emergency situation according to one's own work role and level of responsibility.
- KU2. contingency management techniques.
- KU3. call-out procedures to access emergency services personnel.
