#### MES/Q2511: Digital 3D Game Art- Indiaskills Worldskills
Brief Job Description
Individuals at this job are responsible for creating computer generated models (characters, machines,
props, objects etc.) for animation under close supervision of a senior.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to create various types of models using modelling software and tools such
as Maya, 3D Studio Max etc. The individual must also have a good understanding of the human anatomy,
skeleton structure, joints, facial muscles, expressions etc. The individual must be well-versed with the
principles and techniques of 3D modelling and animation.

#### MES/N2546: Work organization and management   - Worldskills
Description
Work organization and management involves structuring tasks, resources, and processes to optimize
efficiency, productivity, and performance within an organization.
Scope

#### MES/N2548: Concept Art- Worldskills
Description
Concept art involves creating visual representations of ideas, characters, environments, or objects for
various creative projects such as films, video games, animations, or illustrations. It serves as a preliminary
stage in the design process, helping to convey the overall look, feel, and style of the final product. Concept
artists use various techniques, including sketches, digital painting, and 3D modeling, to bring concepts to
life and provide visual direction for the project's development team.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of concept art involves creating visual representations of ideas, characters, environments, or objects for creative projects like films, video games, animations, or illustrations. It
serves as a preliminary stage in the design process, helping to convey the overall look, feel, and
style of the final product, using techniques such as sketches, digital painting, and 3D modeling.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Use imagination and inspiration to generate original visuals
- PC2. Conform to the defined art style, colours, and themes
- PC3. Digitally paint to demonstrate form, line, shading, perspective, proportion, light, and shadow
- PC4. Use digital techniques to produce appropriate effects and make efficient use of time
- PC5. Choose appropriate software to paint concept art pieces in with maximum production in the
swiftest time
- PC6. Review and select each piece of concept art to inform the look of finished 3D models

#### MES/N2549: 3D Modelling- Worldskills
Description
3D modeling entails crafting digital representations of objects, characters, environments, or scenes using
software. It's widely used across industries like animation, gaming, architecture, and product design to
visualize concepts or create final products. Modelers manipulate virtual objects to achieve desired shapes,
textures, and animations for realistic and immersive experiences.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of 3D modeling involves creating digital representations of objects, characters, environments, or scenes using specialized software. It is used in various industries, including
animation, gaming, architecture, product design, and visual effects, to visualize concepts, prototypes, or final products. 3D modelers manipulate virtual objects to achieve desired shapes,
textures, and animations, providing a realistic and immersive experience for viewers or users.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Use innovative and optimization techniques on the 3D game models to ensure the scalability
and sustainability of the game production
- PC2. Select appropriate 3D modelling software to begin models, e.g. 3DS Max or Maya for hard
surface modelling, or a sculpting tool for organic sculpts
- PC3. Utilize skills in sculpting, edge modelling, or box modelling to produce the basic form of
models
- PC4. Use tools and modifiers to create further details on models
- PC5. Constantly review models from all angles to determine refinements, improvements, and
additional detail

#### MES/N2550: UV Unwrapping-  Worldskills
Description
UV unwrapping is the process of flattening a 3D model's surface into a 2D representation, like unwrapping
the surface of a globe into a map. This step is crucial for applying textures or images accurately onto the
model in computer graphics, animation, and game development. It involves creating a UV map, which
serves as a guide for texture placement, ensuring that textures adhere correctly to the model's surface
when rendered.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of UV unwrapping involves flattening the surface of a 3D model into a 2D representation,
known as a UV map. This process is essential for accurately applying textures or images onto the
model in computer graphics, animation, and game development, ensuring that textures adhere correctly to the model's surface when rendered.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Use innovative and optimization techniques on the UVs to increase productivity of the game
production
- PC2. Use UV unwrapping tools to project maps on to all the surfaces of 3D assets
- PC3. Separate surfaces into appropriate shells to flatten over the UV space
- PC4. Organize shells to make the most of space
- PC5. Group shells with similar colours together
- PC6. Export UV coordinates to texture tools or painting software
- PC7. Bake UV from 3D assets

#### MES/N2551: Texturing- Worldskills
Description exturing is the process of adding surface detail, color, and texture to 3D models to enhance their realism
and visual appeal. It involves applying images or procedural techniques to simulate various materials like
wood, metal, or fabric. Texturing is essential in industries such as animation, gaming, architecture, and
product design to create lifelike and immersive environments.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of texturing involves applying surface detail, color, and texture to 3D models to enhance
realism and visual appeal. This process is essential in various industries, including animation,
gaming, architecture, and product design, to create lifelike and immersive environments. Texturing
techniques include painting directly onto models, applying pre-made textures, or using procedural
methods to generate textures programmatically.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Use innovative and optimisation techniques on the game textures to ensure the scalability and
sustainability of the game production
- PC2. Select appropriate software to produce textures and materials
- PC3. Paint a variety of physical materials and adapt to the art style set out in the brief (e.g. hand-
painted and/or PBR)
- PC4. Paint or engineer specular maps for controlling shine and glossiness of surfaces
- PC5. Paint opacity maps, as required, to handle complex objects or sections of assets
- PC6. Export a variety of maps (normal, specular, ambient occlusion etc) from appropriate pieces of
software and import into the preferred 3D software

#### MES/N2552: Rigging and Animation- Worldskills
Description
Rigging involves creating a digital skeleton for a 3D model, enabling realistic movement, while animation
brings the model to life by creating movement sequences. These processes are essential for creating
dynamic visual content in industries like film, gaming, and advertising.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Rigging involves creating a digital skeleton within a 3D model, enabling it to move realistically.
Animation involves bringing these rigged models to life through movement sequences. Together, they form the backbone of creating dynamic visual content in industries such as film, gaming, and
advertising.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Create an appropriate bone structure to form a working rig for the in-game asset
- PC2. Set up a parent child structure for FK or IK chain
- PC3. Skin the mesh and paint how the bones influence the 3D model
- PC4. Set simple animation keys to test the motion of the asset in an engine

#### MES/N2553: Technical Art and Game Engine- Worldskills
Description
Technical art involves optimizing performance and implementing visual effects in game development,
bridging the gap between art and technology. Game engines are software frameworks used to build and
run games, providing tools for rendering graphics, physics, audio, and scripting. They work together to
create immersive gaming experiences, balancing artistic vision with technical constraints.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Technical art involves bridging the gap between art and technology in game development, focusing
on optimizing performance, creating tools, and implementing visual effects. Game engines are software frameworks used to build and run games, providing tools for rendering graphics, physics,
audio, and scripting. Together, they form the foundation for creating immersive gaming experiences,
balancing artistic vision with technical constraints.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Conduct performance testing and performance tuning of game assets
- PC2. Choose and use a renderer, pose the object, and select appropriate lighting and settings to
highlight the best qualities of the asset
- PC3. Export 3D models and rig/animation into a game engine
- PC4. Select an appropriate game engine and stress test the asset for model, UV, and deformation
errors

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |
