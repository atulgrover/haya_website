#### RSC/Q6601: Grader(Ribbed Smoked  Sheet Trading)
Brief Job Description
The Grader - Ribbed Smoked Sheet (RSS) Trading is responsible for inspecting, classifying, and grading
smoked rubber sheets based on BIS/ISO standards, ensuring proper documentation, labeling, and storage
to maintain quality and traceability. The role involves visual inspection, identification of defects, applying
grading criteria, maintaining records, labeling batches, and adhering to sustainable, safe handling, and
storage practices as per trading and export norms. The individual works under supervision, follows industry
standards, and ensures consistent product quality to meet customer and export specifications.
Personal Attributes
The individual in this job must have good eye sight and vision. The individual should also be a team player
with good ability to understand and implement instructions.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N6601: Inspect and Sort Rubber Sheets for Grading
2. RSC/N1014: Ensure Proper Documentation and Labeling of Grading
3. RSC/N1016: Follow Handling and Storage Practices

#### RSC/N6601: Inspect and Sort Rubber Sheets for Grading
Description
This unit covers the competencies required to inspect, assess, and grade ribbed smoked sheets based on
BIS/ISO grading standards, physical parameters, and visual inspection techniques.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for grading operations
Inspect and identify defects
Sort and grade sheets
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for grading operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the grading standards applicable for ribbed smoked sheet (RSS)
- PC2. check the lot and batch numbers for traceability before inspection
- PC3. ensure the workplace, tools, and illumination meet inspection requirements
- PC4. wear appropriate PPE such as gloves, apron, and safety shoes
- PC5. ensure that the sheets are properly cured and dried before grading
- PC6. segregate sheets that show signs of contamination, wetness, or fungal growth
Inspect and identify defects
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. examine each sheet visually for air bubbles, blisters, and tackiness
- PC8. check color uniformity, translucency, and cleanness of the sheets
- PC9. assess thickness and uniformity using manual or mechanical gauges
- PC10. identify physical defects such as cuts, tears, or foreign matter
- PC11. detect foul odor, uneven drying, or burnt appearance
- PC12. classify sheets with multiple minor defects as per grading norms
Sort and grade sheets
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. separate sheets based on grades RSS1 to RSS5 (or relevant BIS grades)
- PC14. ensure uniform stacking of sheets within each grade category
- PC15. cross-check sample sheets against standard grade reference panels
- PC16. tag and label each stack with grade, lot, and date
- PC17. record the grading outcome in the inspection register
- PC18. report any deviations or abnormal defects to the supervisor
- PC19. ensure no cross-contamination between different grade stacks
- PC20. maintain cleanliness and organization in the grading area

#### RSC/N1014: Ensure Proper Documentation  and Labeling of Grading
Description
This unit involves maintaining proper documentation, labeling, and traceability for each graded batch of
RSS, ensuring compliance with quality, trading, and export norms.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Record grading details
Label and tag graded lots
Maintain traceability and data management
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Record grading details
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. collect grading sheets and inspection records from the grading area
- PC2. enter batch details in the grading register or digital log
- PC3. verify that recorded grades match physical stacks
- PC4. maintain data accuracy and legibility in all entries
- PC5. report inconsistencies in records to quality control personnel
- PC6. ensure documentation follows company and BIS formats
Label and tag graded lots
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. prepare labels with grade, lot, and date details
- PC8. affix tags securely to stacks without damaging the sheets
- PC9. use waterproof and durable label materials
- PC10. ensure label information matches recorded data
- PC11. cross-check labels for legibility and placement consistency
- PC12. segregate and color-code labels for different grade categories
Maintain traceability and data management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. update daily grading summary reports
- PC14. file and archive completed records for future audits
- PC15. assist supervisors in preparing export or dispatch documents
- PC16. handle digital data entry on ERP or spreadsheet formats
- PC17. maintain confidentiality and accuracy of all documentation
- PC18. follow workplace record-keeping norms and timelines

#### RSC/N1016: Follow Handling and Storage Practices
Description
This unit involves ensuring safe and proper handling, stacking, and storage of graded RSS sheets to
prevent deterioration, contamination, or damage before dispatch.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Handle graded sheets safely
Store and preserve quality
Follow environmental and safety practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Handle graded sheets safely
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. use clean gloves while handling rubber sheets
- PC2. avoid folding, dragging, or pressing the sheets
- PC3. stack sheets of the same grade in uniform piles
- PC4. maintain gap between stacks for air circulation
- PC5. avoid contact of sheets with oil, dirt, or chemicals
- PC6. follow ergonomic lifting and carrying methods
Store and preserve quality
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. maintain proper ventilation and humidity in storage areas
- PC8. monitor temperature and moisture conditions periodically
- PC9. identify signs of fungal or bacterial growth
- PC10. apply preventive measures for pest control
- PC11. ensure FIFO (First In First Out) rotation of stocks
- PC12. maintain cleanliness and 5S in storage areas
- PC13. handle damaged or downgraded sheets as per SOP
Follow environmental and safety practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. use PPE and safety signage during handling operations
- PC15. dispose of waste rubber pieces responsibly
- PC16. follow company norms for fire safety and housekeeping
- PC17. report any safety or environmental hazards promptly
- PC18. participate in safety drills and mock exercises
- PC19. maintain compliance with sustainable and ethical practices

#### RSC/N5603: Follow ethical and sustainable practices at the workplace
Description
This unit is about Greening of Jobs, PwD, and Behavioural Skills
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Adopt resource conservation practices (Greening)
Follow effective waste management practices
Display behavioural Skills at workplace
Adopt workplace practices and policies respecting gender and ability differences
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Adopt resource conservation practices (Greening)
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. follow organizational policies for usage of alternate energy source, such as solar energy, for
the site
- PC2. ensure proper usage of fuels (such as diesel) to minimise pollution and conserve energy
- PC3. use resources in a responsible manner
- PC4. ensure water conservation practices at the workplace
- PC5. carry out processes to prevent soil erosion during plantation and other related activities
Follow effective waste management practices

hazardous waste generated

categorisation
- PC8. undertake disposal of non-recyclable waste appropriately as per the prescribed procedure
- PC9. organise storage of recyclable and reusable material at identified location
- PC10. ensure proper disposal of hazardous waste as per specified processes
Display behavioural Skills at workplace

- PC11. ensure timely execution of the assigned tasks.

#### Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
