HCS/Q7201: Hand Block Printing Artisan
Brief Job Description
A hand block printing artisan meticulously carves intricate designs into wooden blocks, skillfully applying
vibrant colours to create unique, handcrafted textiles that embody both tradition and artistry.
Personal Attributes
Detail-oriented and patient, with a strong appreciation for artistry and tradition. Creative and innovative,
they possess a deep passion for hand block printing, coupled with a commitment to excellence and
sustainability in their craft.

- 1. HCS/N7212: Preparation of Tools, Materials, and Work Area for Hand Block Printing

HCS/N7212: Preparation of Tools, Materials, and Work Area for Hand
Block Printing
Description
This unit covers the skills and knowledge required to prepare tools, materials, and the work area for hand
block printing.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Fabric Selection and Preparation
Dye and Mordant Preparation
Tool Maintenance and Preparation
Work Area Organization
Elements and Performance Criteria
Fabric Selection and Preparation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify and select suitable fabrics for printing (e.g., cotton, silk, linen).
PC2. Pre-wash and dry the fabric to remove impurities and enhance dye absorption.
Dye and Mordant Preparation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Procure required dyes, chemicals, and mordants.
PC4. Mix dyes to achieve desired shades and consistencies.
PC5. Perform small-scale testing on sample fabric for shade accuracy.
Tool Maintenance and Preparation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Clean and inspect wooden blocks for damage or wear.
PC7. Ensure printing tools such as trays, brushes, and spatulas are in working order.
PC8. Organize blocks for sequential use based on the design layout.
Work Area Organization
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Set up a clean, spacious workstation with adequate lighting.
PC10. Arrange tools and materials in an easily accessible manner.
PC11. Ensure proper ventilation and safety measures for dye and chemical handling.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Guidelines for maintaining quality in hand block printing.

HCS/N7213: Execution of Hand Block Printing Process
Description
This unit covers arranging prepared tools and materials, performing the printing operation, and ensuring
consistency, precision, and quality in printed fabrics while adhering to safety and organizational standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Fabric Alignment and Stabilization
Dye Application to Blocks
Block Placement and Printing
Design Customization and Multi-Color Patterns
Elements and Performance Criteria
Fabric Alignment and Stabilization
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Spread the fabric on the printing table evenly, ensuring no wrinkles.
PC2. Secure the fabric with pins, clamps, or adhesives to avoid movement during printing.
Dye Application to Blocks
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Dip wooden blocks into prepared dye evenly to avoid excess or uneven application.
PC4. Use a sponge or brush for precise and controlled dye application when required.
Block Placement and Printing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Align blocks accurately with design outlines or previous prints.
PC6. Press blocks firmly onto the fabric to achieve even pressure and sharp prints.
PC7. Repeat stamping process with precision to create continuous or repeating patterns.
Design Customization and Multi-Color Patterns
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Apply additional blocks for overlapping, borders, or multi-color designs.
PC9. Monitor color consistency and adjust dye application as needed.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Guidelines for maintaining quality in hand block printing.
KU2. Workplace safety rules and regulations, including handling chemicals and tools.
KU3. Understanding of production schedules and deadlines.
KU4. Reporting structure and communication protocols.

HCS/N7214: Post-Processing, Quality Check, and Finishing
Description
This unit covers inspecting the printed fabric for defects, ensuring the quality of the prints, performing
post-processing techniques such as color fixing, and ensuring the fabric is ready for delivery or display.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Drying the Printed Fabric
Color Fixation
Quality Check
Finishing and Packaging
Elements and Performance Criteria
Drying the Printed Fabric
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Allow the fabric to air dry in a dust-free environment.
PC2. Use mechanical drying techniques if necessary, ensuring no damage to the prints.
Color Fixation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Treat the fabric using steaming, washing, or chemical processes to fix colors.
PC4. Ensure proper handling of chemicals to maintain safety and fabric quality.
Quality Check
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Inspect fabrics for defects such as smudges, misalignment, or faded prints.
PC6. Compare the output to the original design for accuracy and consistency.
PC7. Document identified defects and necessary corrections.
Finishing and Packaging
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Conduct touch-ups for minor defects using small brushes or dyes.
PC9. Iron and fold fabrics neatly for storage or packaging.
PC10. Label and pack finished products per customer or market requirements.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Guidelines for maintaining quality in hand block printing.
KU2. Workplace safety rules and regulations, including handling chemicals and tools.
KU3. Understanding of production schedules and deadlines.
