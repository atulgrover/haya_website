#### JUTE/TSC/Q3001: Jute Machine Maintenance  Technician (Elective 1:
Spinning machinery, Elective 2: Weaving Machinery)
Brief Job Description
A Jute Machine Maintenance Technician should able to carry out Practical hands-on experience, in addition
to theoretical knowledge, is crucial for achieving consistent and high-machine efficiency in yarn/fabric
production in the jute industry
Personal Attributes
The courses helps to develop career on Jute Machinery and its Maintenance and able to keep /bring
mechanical, electrical and hydraulics system used in jute mill machinery in satisfactory operation
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. JUTE/TSC/N3001: Workshop Calculation, Power Transmission and accessories
2. JUTE/TSC/N3002: Basic maintenance, machine audit and Spare Parts Inventory
3. JUTE/TSC/N3003: Electrical Appliance and retrofit Automation / Automated system with Basic
Computer Skill
4. JUTE/TSC/N3004: Lubrication and its schedule
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
6. JUTE/TSC/N1006: Maintain health, safety and security at work place (L4)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Elective NOS/s:1– Spinning Machinery
Jute Spinning Machinery operation and its maintenance requirements
1. JUTE/TSC/N3005: Jute Spinning Machinery operation and its maintenance requirements
Elective 2: Elective NOS/s: 2– Weaving Machinery
Jute Weaving Machinery operation and its maintenance requirements
1. JUTE/TSC/N3006: Jute Weaving Machinery operation and its maintenance requirements

#### JUTE/TSC/N3001: Workshop  Calculation, Power Transmission and
accessories
Description
Elucidate various workshop machinery and maintenance tools, power transmission devices in Jute mill
machinery
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform basic workshop operations using suitable tools for fitting, riveting, milling, lathe, grinding,
files, drilling etc
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Workshop Calculation, Power Transmission and accessories
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Describe the working principles of workshop machineries viz. lathe, shaping machine, drilling
machine, milling machine and grinding machine
- PC2. Identify, troubleshoot and rectify different faults in the machine and machine parts
- PC3. Plan work in compliance with standard safety norms
- PC4. Repair, overhaul and assemble the parts in the machine with the help of blueprint
- PC5. Check for functionality of part and ascertain faults of the part/ machine in case of improper
function
- PC6. Ascertain and select tools and materials for the job and make this available for use in a
timely manner
- PC7. Produce component by observing standard procedure
- PC8. Check the dimensions of the produced components to ensure dimensions are within
prescribed limit
- PC9. Measure all dimensions in accordance with standard specifications and tolerances
- PC10. Identify Hand Tools for different fitting operations and make the available for use in a timely
manner
- PC11. Prepare the job for Hacksawing, chiselling, filing, drilling, tapping, grinding
- PC12. Perform basic fitting operations viz., Hacksawing, filing, drilling, tapping and grinding to close
tolerance as per specificationto make the job.
- PC13. Check for dimensional accuracy as per standard procedure
- PC14. Identify various types of gears- spur gear, bevel gear, helical gear, worm and worm wheel,
rack and pinion and its calculations
- PC15. Identify different types of gear used in jute machinery and calculate power transmission
takes place through gear
- PC16. Explain different types of belt used in jute machinery and their speed transmission takes
place

#### JUTE/TSC/N3002: Basic maintenance, machine  audit and Spare Parts
Inventory
Description
Standard maintenance of machineries, machine audit and spare parts Inventory
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan for standard maintenance of machineries as recommended by machine manufacturer
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Basic maintenance, machine audit and Spare Parts Inventory
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Plan maintenance activities based the work flow and deliverables
- PC2. Follow the maintenance policy of the organization and time available for daily or annual
maintenance
- PC3. Interact with the supervisor in order to understand the work schedules, shifts and delivery
dates
- PC4. Plan the work including time, manpower and material requirement
- PC5. Co-ordinate with stores and arrange for any consumables
- PC6. Cheek the tools and equipment are adequate and are in good condition
- PC7. Arrange for safety gears and tools to be used while carrying out maintenance
- PC8. Refer to and use relevant product manuals, job instructions, wiring diagrams and schematics
- PC9. Maintain electrical equipment such as single, three-phase and direct current power supplies,
motors, starters, switch gears, control systems and wiring enclosures
- PC10. Adopt Proper Inventory Control Measures of Effective parts
- PC11. Analyse all the VED [viz. vital (V), essential (E), and desirable (D)] in focusing the attention of
the management on vital items and ensuring their availability by frequent review and reporting
