RAS/Q0403: Visual Merchandising for IndiaSkills/ WorldSkills Competition
Brief Job Description
A visual merchandiser creates window and interior displays in shops and department stores thus is
essentially responsible for the “look” of the retail outlet. The chief aim of a visual merchandiser is to
maximize sales by communicating with the target audience and creating a positive atmosphere.
Personal Attributes
The visual merchandiser communicates directly with the target audience through their designs and creates
a positive impression which in turn impacts on the sales revenue of the business. Whilst not possible to
precisely measure the impact of the visual merchandiser’s displays it is accepted that they are an
increasingly important element of the marketing and sales mix of a successful retail business.

RAS/N0407: To manage work organization and management for
IndiaSkills/ WorldSkills.
Description
This OS describes the skills and knowledge required to manage work organisation and management for
IndiaSkills/ WorldSkills
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Work organization and management
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Follow health and safety standards, rules, and regulations
PC2. Identify and use the appropriate personal protective clothing including safe footwear as
required
PC3. Select, use, clean, maintain, and store all tools, paint brushes, and equipment safely/securely
PC4. Select, use materials including mixing paints, effectively/efficiently, and store safely/securely
PC5. Plan, schedule and re-prioritize work as the need arises to meet deadlines
PC6. Work well under pressure to meet the demands of the business
PC7. Maintain safe and ergonomic working practices
PC8. Maintain sustainable working practices
PC9. Keep the working area clean and tidy
PC10. Discard waste ecologically
PC11. Keep up-to-date with fashion, trends, and developments in the industry and society
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Health and safety legislation, obligations, regulations, and documentation
KU2. The situations when personal protective clothing including footwear must be used
KU3. The purposes, uses, care, maintenance, and safe/secure storage of hand/electric tools and
equipment
KU4. The purposes, uses, care and safe/secure storage of materials
KU5. IT programmes for 2D and 3D design
KU6. Techniques of time management to support productivity
KU7. The principles of ergonomics to support efficient workflow
KU8. The significance of sustainability and environmentally friendly work practices

RAS/N0408: Communication and Interpersonal Skill
Description
To manage Communication and interpersonal skills
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communication and interpersonal skills
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Accurately determine the needs and aspirations of target audiences: internal and external
customers
PC2. Communicate visually through storytelling and design with the customer
PC3. Build and maintain trust of target audiences: internal and external customers
PC4. Consistently manage effective verbal, and written communications with colleagues
PC5. Listen, reflect, and respond positively and constructively to feedback from colleagues and
external customers
PC6. Professionally present ideas, rationale, and designs/displays to colleagues and related
departments/professionals
PC7. Reflect on and respond appropriately to questions and ideas from colleagues
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The roles and requirements of colleagues and professionals related to visual merchandising,
e.g. brand managers
KU2. The importance of building and maintaining productive working relationships with colleagues
across the organization e.g. marketing and sales
KU3. Techniques for effective teamwork
KU4. The educational/social responsibility role of visual merchandising
KU5. Negotiation methods e.g. purchasing materials

RAS/N0409: To effectively showcase Problem solving techniques,
innovation, and creativity thinking.
Description
This OS describes the skills and knowledge required to effectively showcase creative thinking, innovation
and problem solving techniques
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Problem solving, innovation, and creativity
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Recognize problems swiftly and follow a self-managed process for resolving
PC2. Pro-actively re-prioritize internal customers’ problems as the situation demands
PC3. Recognize the boundaries of own expertise/responsibility and refer to the appropriate
colleagues
PC4. Gain inspiration e.g. through reviewing books/magazines/the internet, city/shop visits, films,
exhibitions, and professional literature
PC5. Maintain an “open mind” whilst accurately determining the needs of the target audience,
internal, and external customer
PC6. Use imagination and inspiration to generate original ideas
PC7. Develop and maintain the confidence “to be different”
PC8. Keep up to date with IT programmes for 2D and 3D design
PC9. Lead changes in design for an organization
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The common types of problem which can occur within visual merchandising work e.g.
improvisation of props
KU2. Diagnostic approaches to problem solving
KU3. Boundaries for resolving problems safely e.g. referring to an electrician
KU4. Creative thinking techniques to generate unique designs
KU5. Latest/future trends and fashions

RAS/N0410: Interpret and research brief
Description
This OS describes the skills and knowledge required to research and develop display ideas to meet
client/organizational goals/target market needs and aspirations
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct research based on lifestyle concepts, trends, and store attributes and develop original
concepts which clearly meet client/organizational goals/target market needs and aspirations
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Conduct research based on lifestyle concepts, trends, and store attributes
PC2. Identify and effectively use research tools e.g. internet
PC3. Research briefs, themes, and products/brands to design unique displays
PC4. Research and develop display ideas to meet client/organizational goals/target market needs
and aspirations
PC5. Evaluate current trends in display design, equipment, and materials
PC6. Develop original concepts which clearly meet client/organizational goals/target market needs
and aspirations
PC7. Create and professionally present clean, professional mood boards to colleagues, managers,
and related departments/professionals to include sketches, images, and colour fabric charts
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The concept of visual merchandising: communicating with the target audience by attracting,
connecting, and engaging with customers to maximize sales
KU2. Roles of related departments/professionals and their focus/needs e.g. marketing and sales
targets
KU3. The floor plans and sizes of areas to be used for display purposes
KU4. Target market consumer behaviour and aspirations
KU5. The common factors which determine themes to include seasons of the year, current
fashions/trends, special events, and promotions
KU6. Composition of images

RAS/N0411: Design
Description
This OS describes the skills and knowledge required create 2D and 3D designs, e.g. displays and windows
with props and text.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Utilise IT programms or softwares including Photoshop and Illustrator, for various design applications
such as rendering, graphic design, 2D design, and 3D design.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Make 2D and 3D designs, e.g. displays and windows with props and text
PC2. Visualize and accurately ‘translate’ ideas and designs
PC3. Demonstrate brand empathy
PC4. Produce scale drawings by hand and by computer using Photoshop and Illustrator
PC5. Work within the available budgets
PC6. Professionally present visuals to colleagues, managers and related departments/professionals
and gain “sign off”
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Full range of applications of IT programs e.g. Photoshop and Illustrator
KU2. Technological developments and how to exploit them
KU3. Rendering
KU4. Graphic design/2D design
KU5. Design of props and spaces/3D design
