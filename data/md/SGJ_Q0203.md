#### SGJ/Q0203: Solar PV Cell Manufacturing Technician
Brief Job Description
Solar PV Cell Manufacturing Technician performs the solar cell manufacturing tasks in a production line,
cleans silicon wafer, undertake chemical and thermal processing in making solar cells. The job holder
operates various stations including metallization processes and is also responsible for testing and
packaging of solar cells.
Personal Attributes
The candidate must be diligent, hardworking, organised and a team player to be successfully able to
complete the job safely and timely. She/He must also demonstrate strong work ethics, communicate
courteously with co-workers and must be good with following instructions of the supervisor.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SGJ/N3105: Describe the basics of PV Cell, prepare and identify the bill of material and its
specification
2. SGJ/N3104: Perform Solar cell manufacturing- Chemical Process
3. SGJ/N3103: Perform Solar cell manufacturing-Thermal Process
4. SGJ/N3102: Perform Solar cell manufacturing- Metallization Process
5. SGJ/N3101: Perform packing of solar PV Cell
6. SGJ/N0147: Maintain personal health & safety in a manufacturing facility
7. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### SGJ/N3105: Describe the basics of PV Cell, prepare and identify the bill of
material and its specification
Description
This NOS covers the fundamentals of solar PV cells, including basic concepts of solar energy, semiconductor properties, solar cell technology, and identification of bill of materials (BOM) along with
their specifications used in solar cell manufacturing.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Basics of solar PV cells and solar energy concepts.
Identification of BOM and material specifications.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Basic of Solar Energy and PV Cell
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the role of Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Cell Manufacturing technician, its importance in
the sector and the advantages of doing the course.
- PC2. Definition of properties of light, Global Horizontal Irradiation (GHI) and Direct Normal
Irradiance
- PC3. Discuss the property of Semiconductor and P-N Junction.
- PC4. Concept of Solar energy, Working of solar cell, Electrical Properties of Solar Cell, Solar PV
Applications
- PC5. Discuss mono and Poly crystalline Silicon Solar cell technology and recent trends.
- PC6. Discuss the basic aspects of solar energy and power generation from solar energy
- PC7. Demonstrate how crystal(mono and Poly crystalline) influence performance of solar cell.
- PC8. Demonstrate how to interpret signs, notices and/or cautions of shop floor.
- PC9. Show Solar Cell Manufacturing Process Flow Chart
- PC10. Show how to provide an overview of Machine Parts, Raw Material to Front Grid (FG) Cell.
Prepare and identify the BOM and its Specification for Solar PV Cell
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Discuss various Materials used in solar cell manufacturing
- PC12. Silicon Wafer, Chemicals, PPEs, Carrier & Trolley, Gases, Quartz Boat, Graphite Boat Spare,
Tweezer, Tube cleaning rod, Printing Screen, Squeegee, Paste, Squeegee holder, Paper Roll, etc.
- PC13. Technical Specifications & uses, role /importance in cell performance.
- PC14. Demonstrate and show each component and explain their application in Solar cell
- PC15. Prepare the BOM of Solar cell
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and practical skills required to perform chemical processes in solar cell
manufacturing, including use of tools, etching techniques, process control, and defect identification in PV
cells.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Covers chemical processes, the role of chemicals, and wetline techniques used in solar cell manufacturing.

Solar Cell Manufacturing – Chemical Process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain Chemical process general requirement and types
- PC2. Discuss tools used for Chemical process
- PC3. Discuss purposes of Chemical Process
- PC4. Explain Operation of the tools
- PC5. Discuss how to Avoid wrong practices during Chemical Process.
- PC6. Discuss the Statistical Process Control
- PC7. Explain Identification of Defects & their criticality in PV Cell.
- PC8. Demonstrate how to perform etching.
- PC9. Demonstrate the bath change, make-up. techniques with hands on experience
- PC10. Demonstrate use of Chemical tools during the process

- KU1. Chemical process requirements, types, and purposes involved in solar cell manufacturing.
- KU2. Tools and equipment used in chemical processes and their proper operation.
- KU3. Safe practices and precautions to avoid wrong practices during chemical processing.
- KU4. Application of Statistical Process Control (SPC) to maintain process efficiency and quality.
- KU5. Identification of defects and quality checking in PV cells during the chemical processing stage.

#### SGJ/N3103: Perform Solar cell manufacturing-Thermal Process
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and practical skills required to perform thermal processes in solar cell
manufacturing, including diffusion and PECVD operations, PN junction formation, ARC deposition, machine
handling, and defect identification.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Diffusion and PECVD thermal processing in solar cell manufacturing.
Equipment handling and defect inspection during the process.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Solar Cell Manufacturing – Thermal Process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Discuss the flow chart of Diffusion and PECVD manufacturing process.
- PC2. Explain Machine Overview.
- PC3. Discuss types of Chemical/Gas using for the process.
- PC4. Explain bubbler change method.
- PC5. Explain removal of defective broken cells from boat/tubes
- PC6. Explain Solar cells PN Junction
- PC7. Explain ARC Layer formation and deposition method
- PC8. Discuss the Quality parameter and defects
- PC9. Demonstrate solar cell PN Junction
- PC10. Demonstrate ARC Thickness & Refractive Index
- PC11. Demonstrate how to perform bubbler change.
- PC12. Demonstrate graphite boat repair or assembly process.
- PC13. Demonstrate to check defects.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Flow of diffusion and PECVD processes in solar cell manufacturing.
- KU2. Machine overview and operation used in thermal processes.
- KU3. Types of chemicals and gases used during the process.
- KU4. PN junction formation and ARC layer deposition in solar cells.
- KU5. Quality parameters, defect identification, and basic maintenance practices (bubbler change,
boat handling).

Description
This NOS covers the metallization process in solar cell manufacturing, including screen printing, firing in

- PC11. Demonstrate how to operate Halm.
- PC12. Demonstrate how to perform IV/ EL test

- PC14. Demonstrate how to generate efficiency report

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Metallization process flow in solar cell manufacturing.
- KU2. Screen printing process and machine operation.
- KU3. Use of paste chemicals in metallization.

#### SGJ/N0147: Maintain personal health & safety in a manufacturing facility
Description
This unit is about maintaining health & safety in a manufacturing facility by following established safe work
procedure, maintaining personal protective equipment as well as proper use of required tools and
equipment
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Establish and Follow safe work procedure
Use and maintain personal protective equipment
Identify and mitigate safety hazards
Demonstrate safe and proper use of required tools and equipment
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain personal health & safety in a manufacturing facility
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the requirements for safe work area.
- PC2. discuss how to Administer first aid.
- PC3. discuss how to identify the personal protective equipment used for the specific purpose.
- PC4. discuss how to identify the electrical and chemical hazards associated with photovoltaic
installations.
- PC5. discuss how to identify work safety procedures and instructions to Handle Sharp and heavy
component in a solar PV manufacturing.
- PC6. discuss occupational health & safety standards and regulations for installation of Solar PV
system.
- PC7. show how to Administer first aid.
