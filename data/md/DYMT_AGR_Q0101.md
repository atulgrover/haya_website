DYMT/AGR/N0101: Understanding Soil and its Physical Characteristics
Description
This unit is about the understanding of soil and its physical characteristics
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Explain the role of soil, soil types, and soil profile
Demonstrate proper soil sampling techniques
Explain and demonstrate analysis of soil's physical characters
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Role of soil for plant; types of soil found in India; soil profile; procedure and guidelines for
collection of soil samples
PC2. Soil physical characteristics- composition, soil texture, soil color, water holding capacity, soil
bulk density, soil porosity, soil moisture; Other soil properties- soil pH, electrical conductivity,
presence of sulphates and chlorides.
PC3. Soil sample collection techniques, Analysis of soil physical characteristics
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Importance of soil quality for enhancing farm productivity
KU2. Different types of soil found in India such as clay, loam, and sandy soils
KU3. Soil profile and their formation processes
KU4. Roles and procedures to operate basic equipment used for the soil analysis
KU5. Procedures and guidelines for collecting soil samples to assess their quality and properties
effectively
KU6. Different types of soils
KU7. Identify chemicals/reagents required for conducting various soil physical and chemical tests
KU8. • Demonstrate the analysis of soil physical characteristics like composition, texture, color, water
holding capacity, bulk density, porosity,
• and moisture content
KU9. Demonstrate the analysis of soil pH level, electrical conductivity, and the presence of sulfates
and chlorides

User/individual on the job needs to know how to:

DYMT/AGR/N0102: Understanding Nutrient Detection in Soil and Fertilizer
Recommendation
Description
This Unit is about Understanding Nutrient Detection in Soil and Fertilizer Recommendation
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Explain soil nutrient functions, deficiency symptoms, and common fertilizers
Explain nutrient testing technologies available in the market.
Explain the need for optimal fertilizer use and implementation of fertilizer recommendations.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Functions of different soil nutrients for the plants, deficiency symptoms of nutrients in plants;
introduction to common fertilizers in use; Problems associated with overuse of fertilizers
(Health, environment)
PC2. Available soil testing kits in market, applications and limitations
PC3. Available fertilizer recommendation apps and their limitations
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Explain the essential roles of nutrients such as nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, and
micronutrients in plant growth
KU2. Explain nutrient deficiency symptoms in plants
KU3. Explain major fertilizers used by farmers
KU4. Explain environmental and health risks associated with the overuse of fertilizers
KU5. Explain about the available soil testing kits
KU6. Explain applications and limitations of available fertilizer recommendation apps.
KU7. Demonstration of application methods of fertilizers
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Manage soil nutrient, functions, deficiency symptoms, and common fertilizers
GS2. Know about the nutrient testing technologies available in the market
GS3. Need for optimal fertilizer use and implementation of fertilizer recommendations

Description
This unit is about Analyzing soil parameters using Dharti ka Doctor Technology by Patanjali
Scope

Demonstrate use of Dharti Ka Doctor technology for soil analysis and interpret soil test reports

KU1. Explain the basics and applicability of Dharti ka Doctor kit
KU2. Explain the basics and applicability of Dharti ka Doctor machine
KU3. Demonstrate soil nutrient analysis by Dharti ka Doctor (DKD) soil testing kit
KU4. Demonstrate soil nutrient analysis by Dharti ka Doctor (DKD) soil testing machine
KU5. Interpret soil testing report
KU6. Suggest and implement fertilizer recommendation proved by the Dharti ka Doctor (DKD) Lab
app
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Use of Dharti Ka Doctor technology for soil analysis and interpret soil test reports
GS2. Suggest fertilizer recommendation for optimal use of fertilizers

Description
This unit is about Organic farming and Nutrient Management
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Explain the importance of organic management of fields
Explain the criteria and processes involved in organic certification
Demonstrate comparison of organic with normal soil based on appearance and characters
Explain and demonstrate organic methods of nutrient management in the field

crop rotation)
KU4. Comparison of organic soil with normal soil based on appearance and physical characters
KU5. Comparison of organic soil with normal soil based on nutrient composition
KU6. Organic methods in agricultural settings

DYMT/AGR/N0105: Maintaining health and safety guidelines
Description
This unit is about how to maintain personal hygiene and administer emergency procedures
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Discuss how to maintain personal hygiene and administer emergency procedures
Demonstrate the tasks to be performed for conducting the hygiene practices and maintain clean and
safe workplace
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Maintenance of personal hygiene, sanitation and safety guidelines of the lab
PC2. Knowledge and Understanding of rules and regulations
PC3. General SOP for working in Lab
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Detail the relevant legislation, standards, policies, and procedures at work
KU2. Explain the importance of personal hygiene in laboratory settings
KU3. Explain safety measures during working in the field including physical strain, chemical
exposure, extreme temperature and biological hazards (bacterial infection, fungal infection
and insect stings)
KU4. Explain sanitation protocols to prevent contamination and ensure experiment reliability
KU5. Explain rules and regulations governing laboratory safety.
KU6. Explain safety regulations to mitigate risks and maintain a safe working environment
conducive to scientific research and experimentation.
KU7. Detail the instructions mentioned on the labels of any reagent bottle or instrument.
KU8. List the types of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) required at the workplace and their
importance
KU9. Demonstrate hygiene practices such as handwashing and proper lab attire
KU10. Demonstrate sanitation procedures for equipment and workspaces
KU11. Demonstrate mitigation strategies for any risks in the working environment
KU12. Demonstrate how to carry out basic safety checks before the operation of all tools,
implements, and machinery and report identified hazards to the supervisor
KU13. Demonstrate general SOP for working in Lab
