#### CUTM/AGR/Q0301: Agriculture Value Addition Consultant
Brief Job Description
The individual possesses a perspective of the Agriculture Value Addition Consultant sector i.e agri-inputs,
marketing, value-addition, and price analysis of the Agri output. Has requisite knowledge and skills for
managerial and entrepreneurial decision making. The person can contribute to improving the livelihoods of
the small producers, processors, and traders at the community level who plan to develop or expand their
businesses.
Personal Attributes
An Agriculture Value Addition Consultant should be analytical, proactive, and detail-oriented with strong
communication and advisory skills. They must possess a deep understanding of agri-markets, sustainability practices, and value chains. A collaborative mindset, problem-solving ability, and adaptability
to rural fieldwork and digital platforms are essential for effective stakeholder engagement.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CUTM/AGR/N0301: Suggest Farmers for Sustainable Seeds, Pesticides based on the Market
Conditions
2. CUTM/AGR/N0302: Value Chain Developer and Analyst
3. CUTM/AGR/N0303: Study of Agri-Warehouse Operations
4. CUTM/AGR/N0304: Study and Analysis of the Formation and Operations of the Farmer Producer
Organizations
5. CUTM/VSQ/N7802: Apply Health and Safety at Workplace
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### CUTM/AGR/N0301: Suggest Farmers for Sustainable Seeds, Pesticides
based on the Market Conditions
Description
This NOS focuses on helping farmers choose the right seeds and pesticides based on market demand, soil
quality, and environmental impact. An expert in this role guides farmers in using sustainable and eco-
friendly farming methods to improve productivity and income.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS focuses on helping farmers make smart choices about seeds and pesticides that are good
for their crops, the environment, and the market. It ensures that farmers use sustainable, high-
quality, and eco-friendly agricultural inputs to improve productivity and income.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. • Understand the concepts of marketing mix (Product, Price, Place, and
• Promotion)
- PC2. • Understand the features, quality, and the use of the product (Product
• component of marketing mix)
- PC3. • Study the pricing and payment strategies of the product (Price component of
• marketing mix
- PC4. • Study the locations, channels and the availability of the input (Place
• component of marketing mix)
- PC5. • Review the promotion strategies employed to know the discounting strategy
• (Promotion component of marketing mix)
- PC6. Study the different seed testing parameters – germination %, purity%
- PC7. • Study the different pesticide formulations (powder, granular, liquid), and
• applications
- PC8. Understand the different classes of seeds – foundation, certified
- PC9. • Estimate the demand and supply and price of the inputs – based on the
• market trends
- PC10. • Understand the government policies – laws, acts – seed acts, fertilizer and
• pesticide laws
- PC11. • Study the secondary data (Govt portals, articles, and company websites) and
• prepare a market report for the inputs
- PC12. Develop the strategy for the use of inputs based on the market trends
- PC13. Estimate the cost of cultivation based on the crop grown, use of inputs

#### CUTM/AGR/N0302: Value Chain Developer and Analyst
Description
This NOs focuses on analyzing agricultural value chains to improve efficiency, sustainability, and market
competitiveness. It involves identifying value addition opportunities, optimizing post-harvest processes,
enhancing supply chain logistics, and conducting market research. Professionals in this role help farmers,
agribusinesses, and stakeholders maximize profitability by implementing innovative strategies, financial
planning, and sustainable practices while ensuring compliance with industry regulations and quality
standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS includes analyzing market trends, improving post-harvest management, enhancing supply chain efficiency, and identifying value addition opportunities. The scope also involves integrating
technology, ensuring sustainability, and complying with regulatory standards. Professionals in this
role work with farmers, agribusinesses, and stakeholders to strengthen market linkages, reduce losses, and maximize profitability in the agricultural sector.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Study the characteristics of agri-food markets and impact on small farmers
- PC2. Understand the Porter’s model of value chain and competitive advantage in the context of
the value chain
- PC3. Study the value chain implications to producers (farmers), traders, processors, wholesalers,
and retailers
- PC4. Study and analyze the credit sources for small and marginal farmers
- PC5. Understand the credit barriers for the small and marginal farmers
- PC6. Review the characteristics and challenges of the Indian Agri food value chains for various
commodities/products
- PC7. Understand the role of consumers, local bodies, NGO’s, in the food value chain – community
involvement for market linkages
- PC8. Review the national and international policies – laws, acts affecting the value chains
- PC9. Study the trends in the agricultural value chains – consolidation, vertical integration – review
examples of industry consolidation in various sectors – agri inputs, processing, and retailing
- PC10. Prepare a SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis report of the value
chains (commodities, processed products)
- PC11. Develop a sustainable food value chain framework for the community
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

#### CUTM/AGR/N0303: Study of Agri-Warehouse Operations
Description
This nos focuses on the efficient management of agricultural warehouses. It covers key aspects such as
storage techniques, inventory management, quality control, logistics, and regulatory compliance.
Professionals in this role ensure the safe handling, preservation, and distribution of agricultural
commodities, reducing post-harvest losses. They also implement modern technologies, risk management
strategies, and financial planning to enhance warehouse efficiency and support a sustainable agricultural
supply chain.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This nos covers the management of agricultural storage facilities to ensure efficient handling,
preservation, and distribution of commodities. The scope includes inventory control, quality assurance, warehouse logistics, risk management, and regulatory compliance. It also involves the
adoption of modern storage technologies, sustainable practices, and financial planning for warehouse operations. Professionals in this field play a crucial role in minimizing post-harvest losses,
improving supply chain efficiency, and supporting farmers with better market access.
