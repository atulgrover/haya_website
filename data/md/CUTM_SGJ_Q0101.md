CUTM/SGJ/Q0101: Paper Recycling Operator cum Technician
Brief Job Description
The Paper Recycling Operator cum Technician should be able to prepare the recycling operation, operate
the paper recycling plant, maintain and repair the machine to produce quality products by practicing
occupational health, safety and security as per the organizational standard.
Personal Attributes
A Paper Recycling Operator cum Technician should be environmentally conscious, physically fit, and detail-
oriented. They must have good mechanical aptitude, the ability to follow safety protocols, and strong
observational skills. Responsibility, teamwork, and commitment to quality are essential, along with a
willingness to learn and adapt to evolving recycling technologies.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

- 1. CUTM/SGJ/N0101: Prepare for paper recycling operation based on Client requirement
- 2. CUTM/SGJ/N0102: Perform paper recycling operation

requirement

CUTM/SGJ/N0101: Prepare for paper recycling operation based on Client
requirement
Description
This NOS covers the preparation process for paper recycling based on client requirements. It includes
identifying suitable wastepaper, understanding recycling procedures, selecting appropriate chemicals and
equipment, ensuring compliance with quality standards, and organizing raw materials efficiently to
optimize recycling operations while maintaining environmental and safety regulations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of this NOS includes assessing client requirements, selecting appropriate wastepaper for
recycling, preparing equipment and chemicals, and ensuring adherence to quality and
environmental standards. It also involves organizing raw materials, maintaining records, and
coordinating with team members to optimize efficiency in the paper recycling process.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the sources for collecting papers.
PC2. obtain the papers from identified places/persons.
PC3. obtain the Work plan from authorized person.
PC4. identify the Information and data for the end product.
PC5. prepare the raw material as per the work plan.
PC6. check the availability of the raw materials: type of wastepaper, relevant chemicals, dyes,
various natural fiber materials.
PC7. check the working of the tools, equipment and machinery.
PC8. use the Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) as per the safety guidelines.
PC9. receive the raw materials and remove non fiber materials non-fiber materials: paper clips,
polythene and plastics, metals and other trash.
PC10. clean the wastepaper to get dust free papers for maintaining the quality.
PC11. sort the waste papers as per color, thickness and sizes.
PC12. weigh and label the sorted papers.
PC13. store the Sorted papers separately for further processing.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Different types of wastepaper, their classification, sources, and suitability for recycling based
on client requirements.

CUTM/SGJ/N0102: Perform paper recycling operation
Description
This NOS covers executing the paper recycling process, including pulping, cleaning, deinking, refining, and
sheet formation. It involves operating and monitoring recycling machinery, maintaining process
parameters, ensuring quality standards, minimizing waste, and following environmental and safety
regulations to produce high-quality recycled paper as per client specifications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of this NOS includes carrying out the complete paper recycling process, operating and
monitoring machinery, controlling process parameters, and ensuring efficient pulp recovery. It also
involves maintaining quality standards, optimizing resource utilization, troubleshooting process
issues, minimizing waste, and adhering to environmental, safety, and compliance regulations.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check the working condition of various equipment of recycling plant.
PC2. identify the type and grade of paper to be recycled.
PC3. set-up the machine parameter as per the input paper type, quantity.
PC4. make changes in the process parameter of the equipment.
PC5. feed the wastepaper on the chopper machine to cut into small pieces. Vary the cutting
parameter to get required size of cut pieces.
PC6. transfer cut pieces of paper into beater machine based on the capacity of the machine.
PC7. switch on the beater machine and run up to 8 hours to make it into a pulp.
PC8. move the pulp into storage tank for further processing.
PC9. transfer the required quantity to paper lifting VAT machine to get proper pulp sheet.
PC10. send the sheet to screw press machine to dry out 80% of water content in the pulp sheet.
PC11. store the sheet up to 20 hours for natural dry.
PC12. load the dried sheet to calendar machine with high pressure to get the required surface
finish and thickness of the paper sheet.
PC13. cut the sheet to the required size using paper cut machine.
PC14. check the quality of the paper as per requirement.
PC15. count the required quantity and pack the paper bundle for various application.
PC16. dispatch the recycled paper to next stage for further process.
PC17. achieve the required productivity to meet the delivery time.
PC18. achieve the required quality by ensuring proper process steps followed each stage.
PC19. follow the safety standards to avoid any accidents, damage to the machine, product or
personnel.
PC20. report problem to the concerned immediately for rectification.

GS3. Maintain accurate records of raw material usage, process parameters, and production output

GS4. Plan and organize recycling tasks efficiently, ensuring optimal use of time, machinery, and
resources.
GS5. Identify and troubleshoot process issues such as poor fiber recovery, excessive ink residues,

based on operational requirements.
GS7. Apply problem-solving skills to minimize waste, improve efficiency, and ensure consistent

CUTM/SGJ/N0103: Inspect finished products quality, quantity, and
package as per client requirement
Description
This NOS covers inspecting the quality, quantity, and packaging of recycled paper products as per client
requirements. It includes conducting quality checks, identifying defects, ensuring proper packaging,
maintaining records, and coordinating with production and logistics teams to ensure that finished products
meet specified standards before dispatch.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of this NOS includes inspecting recycled paper products for quality, verifying quantity as
per client requirements, and ensuring proper packaging. It involves conducting tests, identifying
defects, maintaining inspection records, adhering to quality standards, and coordinating with
production and logistics teams to ensure timely delivery of compliant products.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Check paper texture, color, surface finish, and etc. with the given approved product.
PC2. Segregate and reject the paper material that are not meeting the quality requirement and
process, Recycle it again.
PC3. Write the observations of the process parameter, inspection parameter and maintain batch
wise information.
PC4. Maintain records of each batch per the batch etc.
PC5. Report to Supervisor on the regular basis as per organizational requirement. Report the issues
immediately in case of emergency.
PC6. Collect a sample of first and last output from each batch for future reference.
PC7. Get approval from the Supervisor to ship the material to next stage.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Quality parameters of recycled paper, including strength, thickness, brightness, moisture
content, and surface finish.
KU2. Standard testing methods for inspecting recycled paper, such as tensile strength testing, GSM
measurement, and moisture analysis.
KU3. Different types of packaging materials and techniques used to protect finished paper products
during storage and transportation.
KU4. Client specifications for product quality, dimensions, and packaging standards based on end-
use requirements.

KU6. Compliance requirements for labeling, barcoding, and record-keeping to ensure traceability

CUTM/VSQ/N7801: Ensure organizational health, safety, and security
Description
This NOS focuses on maintaining workplace safety, health, and security by identifying hazards, following
safety procedures, using personal protective equipment (PPE), and responding to emergencies. It ensures
compliance with health and safety regulations, proper waste disposal, and adherence to security protocols
to create a safe and productive work environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers workplace hazard identification, adherence to safety regulations, proper use of
personal protective equipment (PPE), emergency response, fire safety, first aid, and waste
management. It also includes implementing security measures, ensuring workplace hygiene, and
promoting a safety-conscious culture to prevent accidents and maintain a secure working
environment.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify different kinds of possible hazards at the workplace hazards: environmental,
personal, ergonomic and chemical.
PC2. recognize other possible security issues existing in the workplace.
PC3. plan safety techniques for self and others.
PC4. recognize different measures to curb the hazards.
PC5. implement programs for health, safety and security of the people at the workplace.
PC6. communicate the safety plans to everyone at the workplace.
PC7. attach disciplinary rules with the implementation at the workplace.
PC8. comply with regulatory health and safety guidelines applicable to the workplace.
PC9. use and maintain protective equipment as per requirement in a production house.
PC10. ensure organizational activities in line with approved guidelines and procedures.
PC11. undertake and advise healthy lifestyle for self and others.
PC12. follow environment management system related procedures.
PC13. ensure safe handling and disposal of waste and debris.
PC14. minimize health and safety risks to self and others due to own actions.
PC15. monitor the workplace and work processes for potential risks and threats to self and others.
PC16. carry out periodic check to keep work area free from hazards and obstructions.
PC17. ensure participation of self and others in mock drills/ evacuation procedures organized at the
workplace.
PC18. organize first aid, firefighting and emergency training sessions for others.
PC19. instruct and guide others to take action in the event of fire, emergencies or accidents.

| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently.
