RSC/Q8801: Jr. Technician - Rubberwood Seasoning and Chemical
Treatment (Lightwood Furniture)
Brief Job Description
The individual working will be preparing the rubberwood for its use in making lightwood furniture. The
person will be responsible for pre-seasoning, chemical treatment, and post-seasoning of rubberwood
followed by proper handling and packing process using suitable methods and techniques.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to work independently and be comfortable in performing laborious work.
The person should be focused and attentive in performing the assigned task diligently. The individual
should be result oriented and positive in attitude. The person must be able to work independently under
the guidance of the supervisor.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N6204: Perform pre-seasoning of rubberwood
- 2. RSC/N4408: Prepare boron solution and perform chemical treatment on rubber wood
- 3. RSC/N6205: Carry out post-seasoning of rubberwood
- 4. RSC/N6206: Manage the treated sawn timber and pack them safely
- 5. RSC/N5626: Follow Environmental and Legal Compliance
- 6. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
- 7. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

RSC/N6204: Perform pre-seasoning of rubberwood
Description
This unit is about performing pre-seasoning on rubberwood sawn timber to reduce its moisture content so
that it is suitable for chemical treatment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparing rubberwood sawn timber for pre-seasoning
Perform pre-seasoning of rubberwood sawn timber
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparing rubberwood sawn timber for pre-seasoning
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check the quality of sawn and sectioned rubberwood sawn timber
PC2. categorize the received rubberwood sawn timber on the basis of its size and its final
requirement to make furnitures
PC3. measure the moisture content of received rubberwood sawn timber and segregate them
accordingly
PC4. identify green, partially dried and air dried rubberwood sawn timber
PC5. ensure the sorting is done as per the SOP
Perform pre-seasoning of rubberwood sawn timber
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. select a suitable process for pre-seasoning
PC7. setup the area and equipments as per the method of pre-seasoning chosen
PC8. perform pre-seasoning on partially dried and green rubberwood sawn timber using the
chosen method
PC9. check the moisture content of air dried rubberwood sawn timber
PC10. ensure the moisture content of air dried rubberwood sawn timber is in the range of 12-18%
PC11. analyse the moisture content of pre-seasoned partially dried and green rubberwood sawn
timber
PC12. ensure the moisture content of pre-seasoned rubber wood sawn timber is in the range of
12-18%
PC13. move the pre-seasoned and air dried rubberwood sawn timber for chemical treatment
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. various grades of rubberwood sawn timber

RSC/N4408: Prepare boron solution and perform chemical treatment on
rubber wood
Description
This unit is about preparation of boron solution followed by chemically treating the rubber wood sawn
timber using boron solution as well as a suitable technique of impregnation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparing boron solution
Preparing rubber wood sawn timber for chemical treatment
Performing chemical treatment on rubber wood
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparing boron solution
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. select the process of boron treatment to be opted for chemical treatment of rubber wood
PC2. opt out the concentration of boron solution required for chemical treatment of rubber wood
as per the process chosen
PC3. measure the weight of boric acid and borax accurately as per the required proportion
PC4. prepare the boron solution as per the required solution strength
PC5. add 0.5% of suitable fungicide (like Sodium Pentachlorophenate) if required
PC6. ensure the prepared solution is as per the requirement of the tank
PC7. confirm the solution is mixed well and meets the requirements
Preparing rubber wood for chemical treatment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. make sure that the rubber wood are in sizes as per the size of the tank
PC9. ensure rubber woods have undergone pre-seasoning
PC10. measure the moisture content of the rubber wood using moisture meter
PC11. verify that the moisture content of rubber wood picked for chemical treatment is in the range
of 12-18%
PC12. perform stacking of rubber wood pieces into the fixture if required
Performing chemical treatment on rubber wood
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. prepare the equipments required for chemical processing of rubber wood as per the chosen
method
PC14. operate the equipments as per the standard operating procedure
PC15. maintain the process factors like time, pressure, temperature as per the selected method

RSC/N6205: Carry out post-seasoning of rubberwood
Description
This unit is about performing post-seasoning on chemically treated rubberwood to reduce its moisture
content so that it can be used to make lightwood furnitures.
