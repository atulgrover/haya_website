#### HCS/Q3101: Painter - Metal Handicrafts
Brief Job Description
A metal handicrafts painter specialises in transforming metal surfaces with vibrant colours and intricate
designs, bringing life and character to each piece. Their artistry combines traditional techniques with
contemporary flair, resulting in unique, eye-catching creations.
Personal Attributes
A metal handicrafts painter is an artistic and detail-oriented individual, skilled in vibrant colour application
and intricate design. They are passionate about their craft, adaptable to client needs, and dedicated to
creating unique, high-quality pieces.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N3104: Perform pre Painting requirements

#### HCS/N3104: Perform pre Painting requirements
Description
This OS unit is about preparing the pre painting requirements to color paint on the jewel surface
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understand work requirement assemble the required raw materials prepare the paint prepare the metal surface
#### Elements and Performance Criteria understand work requirement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. receive instructions on work requirement from senior for design, colour combinations, type of
surface, areas to paint, etc.
- PC2. plan the target on number of pieces to be completed
assemble the required raw materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. ensure all the raw materials required such as brush, acids, handling materials and other
consumables are bought and arranged
- PC4. report on any shortage or defect of raw materials to the concerned person
- PC5. collect the metal product to be painted
prepare the paint
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. mix the necessary components such as pigments, binder and solvents
- PC7. blend the components to form the paint for primer coats, intermediate coats and finish coats
prepare the metal surface
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. inspect the metal surface completely to identify the areas of rust, damage, stains, etc.
- PC9. ensure these areas are taken special care during the painting process
- PC10. ensure the surface of the metal is clean and dry, before applying the paint
- PC11. ensure to wear safety goggles and mask while preparing the surface
- PC12. remove any traces of oil, dust and grease on the surface so that it does not interrupt the
paint to stay on the surface
- PC13. degrease using solvents or acid solution
- PC14. ensure to de-rust using appropriate acid or solvents at appropriate room temperature
- PC15. perform phosphate coating at room temperature

#### HCS/N3105: Apply Paint on the metal surface
Description
This OS unit is about painting the metal, setting various colors and patterns on the finished jewel. This is
part of finishing stage in the jewel making process. These paints harden and give a long working life to the
color on the jewel, preventing rusting and wear out of metal with time.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
paint the metal surface achieve quality standards
#### Elements and Performance Criteria paint the metal surface
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. decide on the appropriate application process and the suitable type of paint to be used
- PC2. ensure to select the suitable primer paint based on the type of metal being painted
- PC3. cover the work area with some cloth or paper to avoid spilling of paint
- PC4. apply the color to the prepared surface using brush, roller, spray or other similar equipment
based on the requirement and surface of the metal
- PC5. perform the primer coat, intermediate coat, stripe coats and finish coats as per requirements
with the specified prepared paint
- PC6. ensure to perform painting in a well ventilated and well lightened place
- PC7. ensure to avoid humid conditions while painting as it can affect the drying of paint
- PC8. ensure the appropriate room temperature is maintained in the room where painting is
carried out
- PC9. ensure to wear appropriate attire and take necessary eye precautions while painting
- PC10. shake the paint can if using a sprayer can to paint the metal
- PC11. apply the paint by dipping or spraying onto the surface
- PC12. use jigs to hold the metal while painting and ensure not to drop the metal product
- PC13. ensure the paint doesn’t spill over
- PC14. ensure to use the colors one by one and separate brushes for every color
- PC15. ensure not to thick coat the paint as it becomes difficult to dry
- PC16. ensure the various compositions of paints used are compatible with one another
- PC17. use thinner if specified in the job sheet as per requirement
- PC18. dry the paint before beginning the second coat
- PC19. ensure the paint is spread evenly across the metal surface
- PC20. perform sanding if smoother finish is required
- PC21. dry the painted surface for the appropriate required time

#### HCS/N8524: Entrepreneurship skills for Metalware painter
Description
This unit covers essential entrepreneurship skills required for a Metalware Painter to establish, manage,
and grow a successful business.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding market trends and customer preferences
Business planning and strategy development
Financial management and Budgeting
Marketing and Branding strategies
Quality control and customer satisfaction
Scaling Up and business growth
Legal compliance and ethical practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding market trends and Customer preferences
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify current market demand for different styles, colors, and finishes in metalware
painting.
- PC2. Conduct research on customer preferences through surveys, competitor analysis, and trend
studies.
- PC3. Stay updated on emerging trends in metalware painting, including sustainable and
innovative techniques.
- PC4. Identify niche markets, such as customized, antique, or premium painted metalware.
Business planning and Strategy development
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Develop a structured business plan, including vision, mission, and unique selling proposition
(USP).
- PC6. Set realistic short-term and long-term business goals based on market analysis.
- PC7. Identify potential business challenges and create risk mitigation strategies.
- PC8. Establish a pricing strategy considering cost, competition, and perceived value.
Financial management and Budgeting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Estimate costs for raw materials, tools, labor, and overhead expenses.
- PC10. Maintain financial records, track expenses, and manage cash flow efficiently.
- PC11. Determine appropriate pricing for painted metalware products to ensure profitability.
- PC12. Explore funding options such as bank loans, government schemes, and crowdfunding.
- PC13. Develop financial forecasts and budget planning to ensure business sustainability.
