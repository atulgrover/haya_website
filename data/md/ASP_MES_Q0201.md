#### ASP/MES/N0203: Personal Skills
Description
The candidate will obtain knowledge on various industry sectors, their career prospects and acquire
knowledge and hands on experience regarding various recruitment processes. This module aids candidate
to acquire desirable qualities for a successful career.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Stay up to date with current trends, innovations, and emerging technologies within specific sectors.
Familiarization with job application process, stages involved in recruitment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Develop Knowledge of various Industry sectors.
- PC2. Develop an awareness of the career prospects and placements.
- PC3. Design your resume and draft applications for professional positions.
- PC4. Display aptitude for different job roles and associated job skills.
- PC5. Apply Interview ,GD skills for specific job requirements.
- PC6. Showcase Professional values-integrity, perseverance, fairness, justice, impartiality and
positivity.
- PC7. Display humanitarian concerns- Empathy, tolerance, accommodation, understanding and
Kindness.
- PC8. Demonstrate an understanding of ethical standards and code of conduct.
- PC9. Prepare a case study on professional values and ethics in Industry.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Develop Knowledge of various Industry sectors

#### Assessment Guidelines
ASAP Kerala follows an assessment framework which provides weightage for all the activities connected
with skilling in which students get involved during the training program. The components of assessment
include Attendance, Assignments, Internal Assessments and Final Assessment. Of these, Attendance and
Internal Assessment come under Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE). All Assessments with
regard to the academic status of the student should be reflected as marks and overall assessment by
awarding grades.
Attendance Criteria
Skill based sessions and training are delivered through lectures, discussions, demonstrations, and
experiments ensuring student participation during daily learning activities. So those who miss such
sessions will not be able to compensate for the loss. Regular student participation in daily classroom
activities plays a significant role in student’s success. For each course and batch, the student’s attendance
will be marked daily, and marks will be awarded accordingly on course completion before final assessment. Students whose attendance fall below 70% will not be eligible for final assessment and course
completion certificate.
