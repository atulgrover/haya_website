#### ISC/Q0915: Stainless Steel Heavy Structure Fabricator
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role coordinate and perform stainless steel heavy fabrication process and its
associated functions.
Personal Attributes
The person should be patient, organized, team-oriented and have the ability to work for long hours in
adverse conditions. They must be able to plan and prioritise tasks effectively and have an eye for detail
and quality.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ISC/N0946: Manage and implement health and safety practices at the work place
2. ISC/N0947: Manage team and others at workplace
3. ISC/N0948: Understanding Stainless Steel Metallurgy
4. ISC/N0949: Influence of Elements in Weld Environment
5. ISC/N0950: Welding Preparation for SS Heavy Fabrication
6. ISC/N0951: Welding Process of Stainless Steel for Heavy Fabrication as per their Applications - Part
I
7. ISC/N0952: Welding Process for SS for Heavy Fabrication - Part II
8. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

#### ISC/N0946: Manage  and implement  health and safety practices at the
work place
Description
This OS unit is about managing and implementing health, hygiene, safety and sustainable practices for
optimising use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain safe and secure working environment
Maintain health and hygiene
Manage housekeeping at workplace
Implement material and energy conservation practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain safe and secure working environment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify hazardous activities and the possible causes of risks or accidents in the workplace
- PC2. implement safe working practices while dealing with hazards to ensure safety of self and
others
- PC3. ensure that team is following appropriate safety practices while working in and around
trenches, elevated places and confined areas
- PC4. conduct regular checks of the machines with support of the maintenance team to identify
potential hazards
- PC5. organise safety drills or training sessions to create awareness amongst others on the
identified risks and safety practices
- PC6. fill daily check sheet to report improvements done and risks identified
- PC7. ensure that relevant safety boards/signs are placed on the shop floor for the safety of self
and others
- PC8. report any identified breaches in health, safety and security policies and procedures to the
designated person
- PC9. ensure that team is following appropriate emergency procedures such as raising alarm, safe
evacuation etc.
Maintain health and hygiene
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. ensure team is aware about hygiene and sanitation regulations and following them on the
shop floor
- PC11. ensure workplace, equipment, restrooms etc. are cleaned and sanitized regularly
- PC12. ensure availability of running water, hand wash and alcohol-based sanitizers at the
workplace
- PC13. report advanced hygiene and sanitation issues to appropriate authority

#### ISC/N0947: Manage  team and others at workplace
Description
This OS unit is about managing team, colleagues/superiors and others, either in own work group or in other
work groups within organisation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communicate effectively with team members
Interact with supervisor
Manage appropriate behaviour at work place
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Communicate effectively with team members
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. manage and co-ordinate with team members to integrate work, as per the workload in order
to achieve team goals

requirements

#### ISC/N0948: Understanding Stainless Steel Metallurgy
Description
This OS unit is about understanding the basic metallurgy that goes into the making of stainless steel
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding stainless steel making process
Learn about different grades of stainless steel and their identification methods
Learn the welding characteristics of stainless steel
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding stainless steel making process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Carry out practical application of different stainless steel production methods (e.g., electric
arc furnace, basic oxygen furnace).
- PC2. Engage in simulations or visual aids illustrating the sequential steps of stainless-steel making
- PC3. Understand temperature and timing effects on steel quality.
- PC4. Highlight quality control measures in stainless steel manufacturing
- PC5. Handle equipment or tools used in stainless steel refining processes for practical insight
Learn about different grades of stainless steel and their identification methods
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Participating in simulations or visual aids to understand the microstructural make-up of the
different grades of SS
- PC7. Practice in pre-weld and post-weld treatments to understand their influence on stainless
steel joint integrity

#### ISC/N0949: Influence of Elements in Weld Environment
Description
This OS unit is about understanding how elements influence the integrity of a weld.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Influence of Elements in Weld Environment
Influence of Alloying on Weldability & Weld Structure
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Influence of Elements in Weld Environment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Engaging in hands-on exercises showcasing hydrogen-induced cracking and sulphur’s
influence on hot cracking and corrosion in welds.
- PC2. Analysing practical scenarios highlighting carbon's effects on weld strength and heat-affected
zones, and phosphorus's implications for weld quality
- PC3. Practicing pre-weld and post-weld treatments to understand their influence on stainless steel
joint integrity.
- PC4. Examining case studies illustrating trace elements' alteration of weld characteristics and
practicing controlled environments to prevent element-induced weld defects.
Influence of Alloying on Weldability & Weld Structure
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Engage in hands-on exercises to observe the impact of alloy composition on weld heat-
affected zones.
- PC6. Examine samples to identify the influence of alloying elements on weld solidification and
defect formation.
- PC7. Analyse real-life case studies highlighting the trade-offs between alloying for properties and
weldability challenges.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand the impact of oxygen on weld quality and its role in oxidation and porosity formation.
- KU2. Understand the effects of nitrogen on weld properties and its contribution to brittleness or
strength.
- KU3. Recognize the significance of hydrogen in welds and its potential for causing cracking.
- KU4. Identify the role of sulphur in welds and its influence on hot cracking and corrosion resistance.
