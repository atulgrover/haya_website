MIN/Q1804: Mineral Processing Technician
Brief Job Description
A Mineral Processing Technician efficiently processes mineral ores and employs specialized techniques,
including magnetic and electrostatic separation, to extract valuable minerals while adhering to quality and
safety standards in the industry.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the task at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should also be physically agile, strong, have good eye sight and not suffer from
colour-blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MIN/N4109: Carry out rare earth material ore processing
- 2. MIN/N4110: Convey the material from mine for processing
- 3. MIN/N4111: Operate the spiral separator and density separator
- 4. MIN/N4112: Operate the drier and blower
- 5. MIN/N4113: Operate the electrostatic separators, magnetic separators, screens, air table
- 6. MIN/N4114: Carry out wet table separation, flotation separation, dewatering and screening
- 7. MIN/N4115: Carry out general maintenance of ore processing equipment
- 8. MIN/N4116: Perform water treatment and management of subgrade ore
- 9. MIN/N1810: Follow the applicable health, safety and environmental guidelines (Placer Mining)
- 10. MIN/N1811: Work collaboratively and effectively in a team
- 11. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

MIN/N4109: Carry out rare earth material ore processing
Description
This unit is about understanding the job requirement, equipment and processes for ore processing as per
the specified standards
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Ore processing procedure, related equipment and parameters
Elements and Performance Criteria
Ore processing procedure, related equipment and parameters
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. carry out ore processing activity following the process manuals/ work instructions and
Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
PC2. use the appropriate reagents in the recommended quantities in ore processing, applying
effective reagents handling techniques
PC3. monitor and record the process parameters during ore processing as per the standard
procedure
PC4. use the appropriate hand and power tools, such as pneumatic and hydraulic tools at different
stages of ore processing
PC5. use the appropriate equipment as per the manufacturer’s instructions, such as conveyor,
compressor, mobile equipment and lifting equipment, at various stages of ore processing
PC6. prepare for the tools and equipment-related emergencies, such as malfunction and breakdown
PC7. follow the recommended emergency procedure during the malfunctioning or breakdown of the
tools and equipment
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. different types of minerals extracted from ore mines
KU2. different ore processing processes and appropriate equipment/machinery used in the mine
KU3. the statutory requirements and safety regulations for ore processing operations
KU4. the importance of discipline and punctuality in ore processing operations
KU5. the benefits of following 5s principles in ore processing
KU6. the appropriate safety precautions for working in in the vicinity of ore processing machinery
KU7. the duties of workmen and applicable working hours in the ore processing plant
KU8. the provision of wages, working hours and accident compensation as per the Mines act
KU9. the impact of violation of safety procedures

MIN/N4110: Convey the material from mine for processing
Description
This unit is about conveying the ore to the processing plant through slurry pipeline systems and
conveyors.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operation of the conveyors
Operation of the feeders
Operation of the pumps and pipelines
Elements and Performance Criteria
Operation of the conveyors
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check the slurry pumping system, layout of the pipeline for the correct functioning
PC2. check the appropriate parameters, such as feed rate, pulp density etc., for components like
pumps and motors
PC3. check the layout and components of belt conveyor system including its principal
componentse.g., through scrapers, conveyor belt, roller chains and tensioners for the correct
functioning
PC4. test the operations of mechanical and electrical driving units including the safety devices
PC5. convey the ore to the processing unit, maintaining appropriate parameters, such as
feed/material rate, alignment of conveyor belts, spillage amount and rate, conveyor jams,
etc
PC6. inspect for potential issues, such as unusual noises, blockages, obstruction, etc
PC7. undertake the lubrication of pumps, motors, conveyors and electromagnet
PC8. inspect the hoppers and bins, chutes, guardrails deployed to control stockpile levels, stability
of stockpiles, clearing of the obstruction for the correct installation
Operation of the feeders
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. check the layout, functioning and components of feeders, including chains, pans, bearings,
rollers, shafts, feed/discharge chute, drive mechanisms, lubrication systems, etc. controls,
drive belts, hangers/cables, etc
PC10. feed ore to the various processing equipment maintaining the relevant parameters, e.g. feed
rate, voltage, Revolution per Minute (RPM) etc., as per the applicable standards and
recommendations
PC11. follow the appropriate preventive or corrective measures for safety hazards, such as spillage,
abnormal noises/ odour, lubrication, leaks, etc
Operation of the pumps and pipelines
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

MIN/N4111: Operate the spiral separator and density separator
Description
This unit is about spiral and density separation process and operation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Spiral separator operation
Density separator operation
Elements and Performance Criteria
Spiral separator operation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check different operational parameters, such as tank level, alarms, pumps, monitor-pulp
density, feed rate, splitter position, etc., at appropriate intervals following the applicable
checklists and given instructions
PC2. check for the recommended feed rate for spiral ore separation process
PC3. check the pulp density is as per the process requirement
PC4. conduct the separation process following the applicable standards to extract heavy and lighter
minerals
PC5. check and monitor the threshold limit values to ensure they are as recommended
PC6. assist the supervisor in identifying relevant hazards by checking and reporting the work site
conditions
PC7. mitigate the identified hazards, such as spillage, blockages and obstructions by using
ropes/barriers, signboards, etc.
Density separator operation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. check the functioning of different types of density separators, e.g. hydro-sizer and flotex
density separator
PC9. monitor the threshold limit values of the density separator
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. different types of density separators, such as hydro-sizer, flotex density separator and its
working principle
KU2. different types of heavy minerals separation processes
KU3. the functioning of various alarms, pumps, screens, valve, conveyor etc., relevant to ore
separation equipment
KU4. the use of reagents as per the process requirements
