#### NIE/ELE/Q0903: Printed Circuit Board Technician
Brief Job Description
This course provides an overview of Printed Circuit Board (PCB) technology, starting with an exploration of
basic concepts such as electronic component packages, types of PCBs, and the history of PCB development. Participants gain insight into Electronic Design Automation (EDA) tools, with a focus on
customizing Autodesk Eagle for in-depth analysis and design. The curriculum delves into component types,
including through-hole packages, and emphasizes adherence to design rules. Participants learn the CAD
workflow and the importance of manufacturing documentation. Additionally, they develop proficiency in
creating PCB flowcharts, understanding layering and materials, and simulating electronic circuits to
analyze waveform outputs. Furthermore, the course covers Film Master generation methods, material
selection, and assembly techniques for both leaded and surface-mounted devices, along with familiarization with assembly tools.
Personal Attributes
A Printed Circuit Board (PCB) Technician needs strong attention to detail and technical aptitude to handle
intricate designs and electronic components. They should possess good problem-solving skills for
troubleshooting circuit issues and have manual dexterity for precise soldering and component handling.
Patience and focus are essential, as the work often involves complex and time-consuming tasks. Strong
analytical thinking is required to test and diagnose circuit functionality effectively. Additionally, good
communication and adaptability are important for working with teams and keeping up with technological
advances.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. NIE/ELE/N0914: Fundamentals of PCB Design

3. NIE/ELE/N0916: PCB Manufacturing and Assembly Techniques

#### NIE/ELE/N0914: Fundamentals  of PCB Design
Description
This module focuses on the design and analysis of Printed Circuit Boards (PCBs), emphasizing their role as
vital components in electronic systems. It differentiates PCBs from PWBs, highlighting PCB's complexity
and integration with integrated circuits. Learners will explore various types of PCBs, such as Single-Sided
and Multi-Layer, and materials like FR-4, understanding their impact on performance and cost. The module
covers Electronic Design Automation (EDA), including the history and current trends, and introduces EDA
tools for design, analysis, and optimization. Key tools include SPICE, PSPICE for simulation, and Autodesk
Eagle for efficient PCB design. Additionally, learners will understand different PCB components and
package types, including through-hole packages, and their role in PCB development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS for Printed Circuit Board Design and Analysis Techniques encompasses understanding
PCBs' critical role in electronic systems, distinguishing them from PWBs, and exploring various types
and materials like FR-4. It includes learning about Electronic Design Automation (EDA), its history,
current trends, and tools such as SPICE, PSPICE, and Autodesk Eagle for PCB design and simulation.
The scope also covers identifying different PCB components and package types, including through-
hole packages. Key outcomes include proficiency in PCB design using Autodesk Eagle, differentiation
between PCB and PWB, and effective use of EDA tools for circuit design and analysis.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Printed Circuit Board
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand PCB as a vital electronic component for interconnecting electronic parts.
- PC2. Differentiate between PCB and PWB, noting PCB's complexity and integration with integrated
circuits.
- PC3. Recognize types of PCBs (Single Sided, Multi-Layer) and materials used (e.g., FR-4), and
understand their impact on performance and cost.
Electronic design Automation (EDA)
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Learn about the history of EDA and current market trends, emphasizing its importance in
modern electronics development.
- PC5. Explore various EDA tools and their functionalities for design, analysis, and optimization of
electronic circuits.
- PC6. Get introduced to SPICE and PSPICE for circuit simulation, and understand their role in design
validation and optimization.
- PC7. Familiarize with AUTODESK Eagle for PCB design, learning its interface, tools, and workflow for
efficient project implementation.

Description

and the various layers and materials used in PCB construction. Students will also gain practical skills in
Autodesk Eagle, learning to simulate both basic and analog electronic circuits, analyze power supplies for
efficient power delivery, and master simulation techniques for sensor modules and digital circuits.

The NOS for Printed Circuit Board Design and Analysis Techniques covers essential aspects of PCB
design, including footprint design and the importance of adhering to design rules and CAD workflows. It involves understanding the PCB design flow chart, various layers, and materials used in
PCB construction. Learners will gain practical skills in Autodesk Eagle, including simulating basic and
analog circuits, analyzing power supplies for efficient delivery, and mastering simulation techniques
for sensor modules and digital circuits. The scope emphasizes creating accurate manufacturing documents and implementing effective design strategies to ensure successful PCB development.

#### NIE/ELE/N0916: PCB Manufacturing  and Assembly Techniques
Description
This module focuses on the key techniques used in PCB manufacturing and assembly. Learners will explore
both traditional film-based and modern digital techniques for translating electronic designs into physical
layouts, with a strong emphasis on accurate layout creation. Critical manufacturing steps such as plating,
etching, and cleaning will be covered to ensure reliable board production. The module emphasizes real-
world applications, including quality control in manufacturing. In PCB assembly, learners will gain
foundational knowledge of assembly techniques for both leaded and surface-mounted devices, as well as
the tools used in the process. Additionally, the module includes a detailed study of soldering techniques,
exploring the materials and methods involved in creating secure and efficient connections on PCBs.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of this module covers the key techniques in PCB manufacturing and assembly. It includes
learning traditional and modern methods for converting electronic designs into physical layouts,
focusing on critical manufacturing steps like plating, etching, and cleaning to ensure accuracy and
reliability. The module also introduces assembly techniques for leaded and surface-mounted devices, emphasizing the tools used during the process. Additionally, students will gain knowledge
of soldering methods and materials to ensure proper connections, with a focus on applying these
skills in real-world manufacturing and quality control scenarios.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
PCB Manufacturing Techniques
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Learn both traditional film and modern digital techniques for PCB design, focusing on
converting electronic designs into physical layouts and understanding precise layout creation methods.
- PC2. Gain insight into critical steps of PCB manufacturing, including plating, etching, and cleaning
techniques to ensure accurate and reliable board production.
- PC3. Apply acquired knowledge to real-world manufacturing scenarios, emphasizing quality control
for consistent and reliable results.
PCB Assembly Techniques
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Understand the basic level knowledge required to understand assembly techniques for leaded
and Surface mounted devices.
- PC5. Acquire the basic level knowledge of use of various tools during assembly.
Study Soldering Techniques

#### NIE/ELE/N0913: Implementation  of PCB Design Project
Description
This National Occupational Standard (NOS) outlines the essential skills and knowledge required for
successful design and fabrication. It emphasizes proficiency in using PCB design software, adhering to
industry standards, and ensuring quality control throughout the process. Key competencies include the
ability to analyze project requirements, create schematic diagrams, and develop PCB layouts. Effective
collaboration with cross-functional teams and troubleshooting design issues are also critical components.
Lastly, the NOS highlights the importance of staying updated with technological advancements and best
practices in PCB design.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of the National Occupational Standard (NOS) for PCB Design implementation encompasses the complete design process, from initial concept to final layout, ensuring adherence
to specifications. It includes proficiency in using industry-standard PCB design software for schematic capture and layout creation. Quality assurance methodologies are emphasized to validate
PCB designs for reliability and performance. Effective collaboration with cross-functional teams is
essential for ensuring manufacturability and project success.

Design and Implementation of PCB

material used for manufacturing, cleaning methods of base material.

Understand the mechanical method in manufacturing.
- PC3. Understand the methods of soldering of PCBs. material used in soldering process. Understand
Methods of soldering.
- PC4. Understand the basic concept of SMD Soldering and understand repair and rework methods.

- KU2. Acquire the basic level knowledge required to understand assembly techniques for leaded and
SMDs. Acquire the basic level knowledge of use of various tools during assembly.

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. The question papers for the theory and practical exams are set by the Examination wing (assessor) of
NIELIT HQS.
2. The assessor assigns roll number
3. The assessor carries out theory online assessments through remote proctoring methodology. Theory
examination would be conducted online and the paper comprise of MCQ. Conduct of assessment are through trained proctors. Once the test begins, remote proctors have full access to candidate's video feeds
and computer screens. Proctors authenticate the candidate based on registration details, pre-test image
captured and I- card in possession of the candidate. Proctors can chat with candidates or give warnings to
candidates. Proctors can also take screenshots, terminate a specific user's test session, or re-authenticate
candidates based on video feeds.
4. An External Examiner/ Observer may be deployed including NIELIT officials for evaluation of Practical
examination/ internal assessment / Project/ Presentation/. Major Project (if applicable) would be evaluated
preferably by external/ subject expert including NIELIT officials.
5. Pass percentage would be 50% marks in each component.
