#### MSU/ITS/Q1901: Web Video Production and Editing
Brief Job Description
On completion of this course, candidates will be equipped to handle all aspects of web video production,
from pre-production tasks like planning and scripting, to producing and editing high-quality videos. They
will be able to publish and distribute web videos across digital platforms while adopting strategies to
enhance user engagement and maximize the impact of their content. This course prepares individuals for
key roles in the growing field of digital media and web video creation.
Personal Attributes
A candidate in web video production and editing should be creative, tech-savvy, and detail-oriented, with
strong organizational skills to manage multiple tasks. They must be effective communicators, able to
collaborate and convey ideas clearly, and adaptable to new trends and technologies. Problem-solving
abilities and strong time management are essential to meet deadlines, while a focus on audience
engagement ensures content resonates with viewers and enhances user interaction.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MSU/ITS/N1901: Pre-production tasks for web video production and editing
2. MSU/ITS/N1902: Produce web videos
3. MSU/ITS/N1903: Edit the web videos
4. MSU/ITS/N1904: Publish and distribute web videos
5. MSU/ITS/N1905: Adopt practices to enhance user engagement
6. MSU/ITS/N9901: Maintain health and safety in a digital workspace-
7. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### MSU/ITS/N1901: Pre-production tasks for web video production and
editing
Description
This OS unit includes identifying the requirements, creating storyboard and script and organising for
location, team and resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify requirements
Prepare a plan
Create a storyboard and script
Organise for location, team and resources
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. determine the purpose of the video as per client inputs, marketing goals, organizational
vision and business objectives Purpose: increasing web traffic, customer engagement, raising brand awareness, etc.
- PC2. record the information received to determine the tasks to be performed for video production
- PC3. define your target audience, core messages, and tone for web video production Target
Audience: with respect to Who are they? Where are they? What do they like? What do they value? Core Message: visiting social media, purchasing a product, training, etc.
Prepare a plan
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. select a video type that covers the topics and business objectives to be presented Video
type: Animated, Live-action, Screen recording, Whiteboard, Motion graphics Typography,
Combination
- PC5. develop a plan to accomplish the goals through animation and editing Plan: goals and
expectations; competitor’s tactics; audience needs; best topics to be discussed; best- performing video topic; important news and trends, etc.
- PC6. set parameters for accomplishing essential goals for video production Parameters: view
count, engagement, conversions, comments
- PC7. identify resources required and where they can be sourced from
- PC8. identify tools, equipment and software to be used during video production Resources:
camera, tripod, lighting kit, backdrop, an external recorder, lapel microphone, shotgun microphone, computing system with adequate specifications, video editing software (s), etc.
- PC9. identify constraints and risks that could impact the plan and prepare a backup plan for the
same
- PC10. plan the budget requirements to be shared with the investors/corporate unit heads

#### MSU/ITS/N1902: Produce web videos
Description
This OS unit is about performing the requisite tasks like preparation for production and conducting
photoshoot.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for production
Conduct photoshoot
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for production
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. unpack the shooting equipment and place them in the designated location
- PC2. set up the camera considering the time of the shoot, the number of shots, the type of
footage to be captured and the video script.
- PC3. set up the lighting depending upon setup time, number of locations required for shooting and
types of shots Lighting: soft light, hard light, natural lighting, studio lighting, lighting equipment (key light, rim light, triggers and flashes; softbox or shapers; reflectors)
- PC4. select the background for shooting the video Background: solid-coloured, white, wall,
bedsheet, backdrop paper, etc.
Conduct a photoshoot
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. focus the camera by making required adjustments in the settings and taking sample shots
- PC6. direct the talent for delivering the script and examine their performance for the video’s
speed, dynamics, style and audience appeal
- PC7. capture shots from different angles keeping in mind the theme of the video
- PC8. adjust the screen resolution and image quality for desired appropriateness
- PC9. conduct interviews as per the video script and ensure adequate coverage of content
- PC10. record voice-overs and audio (such as narration) as per the video type
- PC11. balance the audio and visual quality using microphones and camera adjustments
respectively Microphones: condenser microphones, dynamic microphones, lavalier microphones (or, lapel mics)
- PC12. add graphics and animation to illustrate complex topics and business objectives
- PC13. organise the sequence of videos shot accurately to prepare for editing
- PC14. confirm if the quality of shots covers the content and is ready for editing

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

#### MSU/ITS/N1903: Edit the web videos
Description
This OS unit is about performing the tasks like adding graphs, special effects, soundtracks and voiceovers.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Add graphics and special effects
Add soundtracks and voiceoverst
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Add graphics and special effects
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. organise all the videos and make them readily available for editing
- PC2. compile the best takes and import them using video editing software
- PC3. use the software to cut the video clips and obtain the essential parts
- PC4. create a timeline to understand the sequence and relevance of the message to be conveyed
- PC5. add graphics, animations and special effects as required to make the video appealing
Animations: animated graph to illustrate a key figure; animated mock-up of a product being explained; screen recording of you scrolling through your product’s website while it’s being

audience Voiceover: as per style, tone, and accent.

importance

#### MSU/ITS/N1904: Publish and distribute web videos
Description
This OS unit is includes tasks like publishing web videos and distributing web videos.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Publish web videos
Distribute web videos
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Publish web videos
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. share the requirements with the marketing team to publish and distribute the video content
- PC2. finalise the format for rendering the video (such as mp4 on YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn and
Twitter; for Snapchat or an Instagram Story, it will need to be in vertical video aspect ratio)
Format: MP4, MOV, WMV, AVI, FLV, etc
- PC3. identify the platforms wherein the videos would be hosted after evaluating the demographics
of each social media platform Social Media Platform: Reddit, Quora, Facebook, Twitter,
Instagram or YouTube.
- PC4. seek inputs from the marketing team to identify groups and forums wherein the videos
would generate maximum impact
- PC5. publish the video on finalised social media platforms strategically Strategically: right-sizing,
video duration, and engaging thumbnails, including a call to action, customising GIF thumbnails, etc.
- PC6. implement a system that redirects customers towards organisational products and services.
Distribute web videos
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. identify the distribution platforms to share the videos whilst evaluating audience types and
as per organisational policies Distribution platforms: free/paid ads, websites, blogs, landing pages, news, podcast platforms, webinars, native advertising, sponsored content, influencer
marketing, etc
- PC8. prepare a plan for distributing the videos keeping in mind the way customers interact with
your business, leading to more customer satisfaction and repeat target actions Plan: should be diversified and perfectly fit the goals, as well as alternate channels
- PC9. incorporate the video in daily communication channels such as emails, weekly or monthly
newsletters, onsite presentations, client meetings, etc.
- PC10. run the videos in routine meetings with external stakeholders and business partners
- PC11. coordinate with the marketing team for presenting videos at key events and conferences

| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
