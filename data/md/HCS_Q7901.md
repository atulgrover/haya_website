HCS/Q7901: Agarbatti Maker
Brief Job Description
An Agarbatti Maker is a skilled worker who prepares incense sticks by mixing fragrant materials, rolling
them onto bamboo sticks, and drying them for packaging. This traditional craft combines precision, scent
blending, and manual dexterity.
Personal Attributes
An Agarbatti Maker should have good hand-eye coordination, attention to detail, and patience for
repetitive tasks. A strong sense of smell and cleanliness are also important to maintain quality and
consistency in fragrance and appearance.

HCS/N7911: Raw material preparation using discarded flowers
Description
This unit is about efficiently collecting, sorting, and preparing raw materials for the production of Agarbatti
using discarded flowers.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Raw material Selection
Use of Discarded flowers for making Agarbatti
Mixing of ingredients
Elements and Performance Criteria
Raw Material Selection
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Ensure proper sourcing of discarded flowers from temples, ensuring that they are free from
any toxic materials or pollutants.
PC2. Inspect flowers for freshness and separate usable flowers from any decayed or contaminated
ones.
PC3. Choose high-quality bamboo sticks, charcoal powder, and natural adhesives to complement
the flower powder.
PC4. Ensure that the selected materials meet industry standards for fragrance retention and
quality.
Use of Discarded Flowers for Making Agarbatti
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Dry the selected flowers in sunlight or controlled drying equipment to remove all moisture
content without affecting the aroma.
PC6. Grind the dried flowers into a fine powder suitable for mixing with other Agarbatti
ingredients.
PC7. Ensure that the flower powder is uniform in texture and free from large particles or
impurities.
PC8. Properly dispose of any non-usable flower waste in an environmentally responsible manner.
Mixing of Ingredients
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Accurately measure and mix the flower powder with other ingredients like charcoal powder,
jigat (adhesive), and fragrance oils.
PC10. Maintain a proper ratio of flower powder to other materials to ensure a balanced aroma in
the final product.
PC11. Adjust the mixture as needed to create a dough that adheres well to the bamboo sticks.
PC12. Continuously check the consistency of the mixture to ensure it is smooth and easy to apply
to the sticks without being too dry or too sticky.

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand the different types of flowers (e.g., roses, marigolds, jasmine) and their potential
for use in Agarbatti making.
KU2. Knowledge of how to identify flowers that are free from contamination, harmful chemicals, or
decay, and the impact of these on the final product quality.
KU3. Understand proper procedures for handling and inspecting flowers, including checking for
freshness and quality before processing.
KU4. Know the correct storage conditions to maintain the quality of flowers and prevent mold or
spoilage.
KU5. Know other essential raw materials such as bamboo sticks, charcoal powder, natural
adhesives (e.g., jigat), and essential oils, and their role in Agarbatti production.
KU6. Knowledge of appropriate drying techniques to remove moisture from discarded flowers
without affecting their fragrance or quality.
KU7. Know the process of grinding dried flowers into fine powder, and how the particle size affects
the quality and consistency of the Agarbatti.
KU8. Knowledge of eco-friendly methods for disposing of unusable flower waste, ensuring minimal
environmental impact.
KU9. Understanding the correct ratio to create a balanced mixture for Agarbatti making.
KU10. Know the effect that different proportions of ingredients have on the fragrance, burning time,
and quality of the final product.
KU11. Knowledge of proper mixing techniques to ensure a uniform and consistent dough that
adheres well to the bamboo sticks.
KU12. Awareness of troubleshooting common issues in the mixing process, such as dryness,
excessive stickiness, or poor adherence to bamboo sticks, and how to adjust the mixture
accordingly.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Ability to effectively communicate with suppliers and workers regarding the sourcing and
quality of discarded flowers and other raw materials.
GS2. Skill in performing basic arithmetic to measure and proportion ingredients accurately during
the mixing process (e.g., calculating ratios of flower powder to charcoal or adhesive).
GS3. Ability to identify and resolve issues during the Agarbatti-making process, such as
inconsistent ingredient mixing or subpar quality of raw materials.
GS4. Inspecting flowers thoroughly for freshness and contamination to ensure high-quality raw
material selection.
GS5. Maintaining the proper texture and consistency of the mixture for uniform Agarbatti rolling.
GS6. Skill in efficiently managing time during the flower drying and material preparation
processes to ensure timely production of Agarbatti.

HCS/N7912: Carry out the process of Rolling and Shaping of Agarbatti
Description
This unit covers the competencies required to efficiently roll and shape agarbatti using prepared dough.
The process involves hand-rolling or using a machine to achieve the desired shape, size, and consistency
of incense sticks, ensuring uniformity and quality standards are met.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Hand Rolling or Machine Rolling
Size and Weight consistency
Proper drying techniques
Elements and Performance Criteria
Hand Rolling or Machine Rolling
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Apply the prepared incense dough or paste evenly onto bamboo sticks by hand, ensuring the
mixture adheres smoothly along the stick without lumps.
PC2. Ensure uniformity in the thickness and even distribution of the paste along the entire length
of each stick.
PC3. Operate Agarbatti rolling machines correctly, adjusting settings to ensure uniform coating of
the dough on bamboo sticks.
PC4. Monitor machine operations for consistent rolling, ensuring the machine doesn’t cause
excessive breakage or uneven application of the paste.
Size and Weight Consistency
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Ensure that the length of each rolled Agarbatti is consistent, following specified product
standards (e.g., 8-inch or 9-inch sticks).
PC6. Regularly measure the thickness of the incense sticks to ensure that they fall within
acceptable tolerance levels.
PC7. Check the weight of the incense sticks after rolling, ensuring uniformity across batches.
Adjust the dough application if weight inconsistencies are detected.
PC8. Make adjustments during the rolling process to maintain consistent weight and size across all
sticks in a batch.
Proper Drying Techniques
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Arrange rolled Agarbatti sticks on drying racks in a well-ventilated space, ensuring even
airflow around each stick for uniform drying.
PC10. Monitor temperature and humidity levels in the drying area to prevent moisture retention or
cracking due to rapid drying.

quality, product appearance, and customer satisfaction.

HCS/N7914: Process of making Fragrance
Description
This NOS describes the skills, knowledge, and understanding required to create and apply fragrances
specifically for Agarbatti.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Selection and Preparation of Raw Materials
Formulation, Mixing and Aging
Filtration, Quality control and Testing
Elements and Performance Criteria
Selection and Preparation of Raw Materials
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Select appropriate natural and synthetic ingredients such as essential oils, Rose,
Sandalwood, Jasmine and Lavender for the desired scent profile (Musk, Amber, Citrus, Ocean
Breeze, Lavender and Peach Blossom).
PC2. Ensure all materials meet quality standards and comply with safety and regulatory
guidelines.
PC3. Measure and prepare ingredients according to the formulation recipe.
Formulation, Mixing, and Aging
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Accurately blend ingredients in correct ratios, ensuring uniform mixing.
PC5. Ensure the mixture is prepared in a contamination-free environment.
PC6. Store the scent mixture in dark, airtight containers for aging, allowing the fragrance to
mature.
PC7. Monitor the aging process to ensure the fragrance’s balance and harmony.
Filtration, Quality Control, and Testing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Filter the scent solution to remove impurities and achieve a clear product.
PC9. Conduct quality control checks to ensure the scent matches the intended profile and
chemical properties.
PC10. Ensure compliance with safety standards and conduct necessary allergen and dermatological
tests.
PC11. Ensure even and consistent application of the fragrance solution to the Agarbatti sticks,
either through dipping or spraying, to achieve uniform scent distribution.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
