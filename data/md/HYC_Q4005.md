#### HYC/Q4005: Green Hydrogen  Storage Assistant technician
Brief Job Description
A Green Hydrogen Storage Assistant technician is responsible for the safe and efficient operation,
maintenance and management of hydrogen storage systems, ensuring the safe and reliable storage for
utilization of green hydrogen.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have ability to give attention to detail, technical aptitude, adaptability, safety and
team leadership, site management and a strong commitment to safety and reliability. The person should
have the ability to work individually / team round the clock and must always promote adherence to safety
protocol.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management Procedures
2. HYC/N4010: Hydrogen Storage Technologies and Methods
3. HYC/N9301: Working effectively in a team
4. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management
Procedures
Description
This unit covers maintaining health, safety and security procedures at the workplace where Hydrogen is
produced and handled.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view
Safe handling of Hydrogen
Follow fire safety procedures
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view & Safe handling of Hydrogen
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Ensure that personnel under their supervision have a deep understanding of hydrogen's
properties, including its flammability, explosive limits and potential hazards.
- PC2. Ensure personal and team adherence to the use of appropriate protective
clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions
- PC3. Identify and communicate the relevant documents, responsible individuals, and designated
locations pertaining to health and safety in the workplace, facilitating effective safety management.
- PC4. Oversee and participate in risk assessments specific to hydrogen storage, handling and
production processes.
- PC5. Proficiently identify hydrogen process hazards and assess risks including the analysis of risk
or accident in the workplace due to hydrogen leak/fire
- PC6. capability to accurately read and interpret data from sensing equipment used in hydrogen
production processes
- PC7. apply key safety parameters for handling hydrogen & oxygen
- PC8. Evaluate hydrogen release pressures and understand hydrogen flame detection methods,
emphasizing the colorless nature of hydrogen flames
- PC9. Apply critical safety requirements for electrical equipment used in green hydrogen
production, minimizing electrical hazards
- PC10. competent in the operation and functioning of hydrogen exhaust systems, facilitating the
safe removal of hydrogen from the workplace
- PC11. Execute inertization processes using nitrogen to displace hazardous gases enhancing safety
in hydrogen-related operations
- PC12. Proficiently identify and recognize common safety signs displayed in various areas,
promoting a safe work environment.

#### HYC/N4010: Hydrogen Storage Technologies and  Methods
Description
This unit is about various hydrogen storage methods like gaseous storage and liquid storage. The unit also
covers compressors, tanks, cylinders, liquefication, and absorption-based methods. It emphasizes safety,
guidelines, hazard classification and regulatory compliance for hydrogen storage. The person will gain the
knowledge and skills required to operate, maintain and troubleshoot hydrogen storage systems safely and
efficiently, making it an essential component for professionals in green energy and hydrogen storage.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Hydrogen storage methods: Storage of hydrogen in gaseous and liquid form, compressors, tanks, cylinders, liquefaction and Technologies
Safety, compliance, guidelines, hazard classification and regulations of Hydrogen Storage handling
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Hydrogen storage methods: compressors, tanks, cylinders, liquefaction and Technologies e.g. storage in
gaseous and liquid form
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Apply various methods of storing Hydrogen
- PC2. Check the storing of hydrogen in gaseous form, liquid form
- PC3. Apply other methods of hydrogen storage like adsorption in metal hydrides and bonding with
liquid organic hydrogen carriers (LOHC)
- PC4. Check the advantages and disadvantages of each method of storing hydrogen
- PC5. Check the storing of Hydrogen in Cylinders, bullets and salt caverns
- PC6. Understand various types of hydrogen storing cylinders (steel, aluminum, or composite
materials), their storage capacity and utilization
- PC7. Efficiently Manage compressed hydrogen storage systems
- PC8. Manage Liquified hydrogen storage methods and liquid hydrogen storage tanks
- PC9. Apply hydrogen liquefaction process and safe handling of liquid hydrogen
- PC10. Apply LOHC method and its utility.
- PC11. Apply the fundamentals of storage in adsorption-based materials and their applications.
- PC12. Comprehend the thermodynamics and design principles of absorption-based hydrogen
storage, including metal hydrides.
- PC13. Manage Hydrogen storage yards including safety of the storage site
- PC14. Manage Storage of hydrogen in refilling stations
#### Element 2 - Safety, compliance, guidelines, hazard classification and regulations of Hydrogen Storage
handling
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC15. Implement safety protocols diligently in all hydrogen storage operations.
