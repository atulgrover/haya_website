#### WBSC/CSC/Q0801: Welder (Manual Metal Arc Welding and Gas Cutting)
Brief Job Description
A Welder (Manual Metal Arc Welding and Gas Cutting) welds and assembles metal pieces and products by
using handheld welding equipment. He will also be responsible for setting up parts for welding using hand
tools, clamps, jigs and fixtures and ensures weld quality through visual inspection. The role involves
interpreting drawings and work instructions and performing welding and cutting tasks in accordance with
specified procedures and safety standards.
Personal Attributes
A Welder (Manual Metal Arc Welding and Gas Cutting) should be safety-conscious, disciplined, and able to
follow procedures and instructions carefully. Physical stamina, the ability to work in different positions and
environments and a responsible attitude toward tools and equipment are essential. The role also requires
basic problem-solving skills, the ability to read and understand technical drawings, teamwork and a
willingness to learn and adapt to new techniques or workplace requirements.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. WBSC/CSC/N0801: Apply Safe Working Practices (Welder (Manual Metal Arc Welding and Gas
Cutting).
2. WBSC/CSC/N0802: Recognize the application of electricity in welding.
3. WBSC/CSC/N0803: Illustrate the formation of arc and principle of arc welding with parameters and
related terms.
4. WBSC/CSC/N0804: Demonstrate different types of electrodes, their symbols, welding symbols, edge preparation.
5. WBSC/CSC/N0805: Demonstrate different types of weld joins with neat and appropriate sketch for
welding different types of materials.
6. WBSC/CSC/N0806: Perform arc welding in different joint of two plates having different thicknesses.
7. WBSC/CSC/N0807: Set up gas welding plant and Perform gas welding & gas cutting following safety precautions.
8. WBSC/CSC/N0808: Recognize various defect of welding for plates and pipes.
9. WBSC/CSC/N0809: Work in real job situation with special emphasis on basic safety and hazards in
this domain (Welder (Manual Metal Arc Welding and Gas Cutting)).
10. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### WBSC/CSC/N0802: Recognize the application of electricity in welding.
Description
Recognize the application of electricity in welding.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Different basic terms used in electricity.
Various use of ammeter and voltmeter.
The use of electricity as applied to both AC and DC welding machine.
Types of electric welding.
Power source required for arc welding: transformer, inverter.
Flow diagram for electric arc welding process.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify different basic terms used in electricity.
- PC2. Identify and demonstrate the various use of ammeter and voltmeter.
- PC3. Demonstrate the use of electricity as applied to both AC and DC welding machine.
- PC4. Demonstrate the types of electric welding.
- PC5. Identify Power source required for arc welding: transformer, inverter (types, function only).
- PC6. Draw Flow diagram for electric arc welding process.

- KU1. Basic Electricity - Current, Voltage, Conductor, Insulator D.C., A.C., Resistance, Ohms Law,
Voltmeter, Ammeter power & heating effect or current.
- KU2. Use of Electricity as applied to welding - A.C. - D.C. types of electric welding and application.
- KU3. Types of power sources along with applications required in arc welding.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Demonstrate Ohms Law.
- GS2. Identify the different electrical instruments used.
- GS3. Demonstrate the uses of Electricity as applied to welding - A.C. - D.C. types of Electric welding
and application.
- GS4. Draw flow diagram of arc welding process along with all components.

#### WBSC/CSC/N0803: Illustrate the formation of arc and principle of arc
welding with parameters and related terms.
Description
Illustrate the formation of arc and principle of arc welding with parameters and related terms.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The principles of arc, characteristic of arc, effect of arc length.
The effect of polarity on arc and their use.
The defects such as arc blow, distortion in welding caused by arc along with the method of minimizing its effect.
The arc formation.
The application of welding fixture.
Different Process parameters: voltage, current, travel speed, heat input, cooling rate (definition and
overall influence on arc welding in tabular form only), Weldability, metallurgy (concept only).
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate the principles of arc, characteristic of arc, effect of arc length.
- PC2. Illustrate the effect of polarity on arc and their use.
- PC3. Identify the defects such as arc blow, distortion in welding caused by arc along with the
method of minimizing its effect.
- PC4. Demonstrate the arc formation.
- PC5. Describe the application of welding fixture.
- PC6. Identify different Process parameters: voltage, current, travel speed, heat input, cooling rate
(definition and overall influence on arc welding in tabular form only).
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The principles of arc welding - necessity of welding machines types of machines - care and
maintenance.
- KU2. Arc - its formation & characteristics, arc length its effect and uses, advantages & dis- advantages.
- KU3. Polarity-types, uses.
- KU4. Principles of arc welding - Use of welding fixture.
- KU5. Arc blow definition, distortion in arc welding, causes &effects, methods of minimizing its
effects.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |
