MIN/Q1408: Shovel Operator
Brief Job Description
A Shovel Operator is responsible for operating large hydraulic or electric-powered rope mining shovels
used in mining operations. The individual plays a crucial role in excavating and loading materials such as
ore, overburden, or minerals onto haul trucks or conveyor systems. The person performs appropriate pre-
operation checks on the shovel and its basic repair and maintenance.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should be physically agile, strong, have good eye sight and not suffer from
colour-blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MIN/N1419: Perform the pre-operation checks on the shovel
- 2. MIN/N1420: Operate the shovel for mining operations
- 3. MIN/N1421: Perform the post-shovel operation activities
- 4. MIN/N1422: Carry out appropriate activities before and after the shovel maintenance
- 5. MIN/N1425: Follow the mining plan and instructions for material handling
- 6. MIN/N1426: Carry out basic repair and maintenance of the mining shovel
- 7. MIN/N1703: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental Guidelines for opencast mines
- 8. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Electric Rope Shovel
- 1. MIN/N1423: Operate and maintain the electric rope shovel
Elective 2: Hydraulic Shovel
- 1. MIN/N1424: Operate and maintain the hydraulic shovel

MIN/N1419: Perform the pre-operation checks on the shovel
Description
This unit is about performing the appropriate pre-operation checks on the shovel to ensure its readiness
for use in mining operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform routine checks on the shovel
Check the emergency equipment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform the pre-operation checks on the shovel
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check the shovel components for signs of wear and tear or damage, and repair or replace
them as appropriate
PC2. check for loose components and tighten them appropriately using the recommended tools
PC3. check for fluid leaks and take appropriate corrective measures to seal the leaks
PC4. examine the safety devices, such as seat belts and emergency shut-off switches for the
correct functioning
PC5. check the hydraulic fluid, engine oil, coolant, and fuel levels and refill them as required to
maintain the recommended levels
PC6. inspect the tires or tracks for wear, damage, or punctures, and replace or repair them as
appropriate
PC7. check the controls and instruments, such as levers and pedals for the correct functioning
PC8. follow the manufacturer-provided checklist for performing a thorough safety inspection of the
shovel
PC9. maintain the record of pre-operation checks
Check the emergency equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. test the fire extinguishers for the correct functioning
PC11. check the first-aid kit is updated with the necessary items
PC12. ensure the emergency equipment is readily accessible to deal with emergencies promptly
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the importance of conducting pre-operation checks on a mining shovel
KU2. the major components of the mining shovel, including the boom, dipper, bucket, hydraulic
systems, tracks or tires, engine, electrical systems, and controls

MIN/N1420: Operate the shovel for mining operations
Description
This unit is about operating the shovel during mining operations for effective mining.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for the shovelling operation
Excavate the material
Dump the material
Maintain effective communication with other personnel
Follow the safety protocols
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for the shoveling operation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. determine the location of the material to be excavated, the haul trucks' routes, and the
dumping points
PC2. follow the appropriate safety practices to enter and exit the operator's cabin to prevent slips
or falls
PC3. follow the manufacturer's startup procedure, including turning on the ignition, engaging the
parking brake, and starting the engine
PC4. ensure appropriate adjustment of the operator's seat and controls for optimal visibility and
control
PC5. follow the appropriate practices for effective engine performance, such as allowing the
engine to warm up for the appropriate duration
PC6. use the controls to maneuver the shovel into position for excavation and material handling
tasks
PC7. ensure the shovel is on stable ground with the surrounding area clear of obstacles and
personnel
Excavate the material
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. use the controls to operate the shovel's boom, dipper, and bucket (or other attachment) to
dig into the material
PC9. use the bucket or attachment safely, engaging it steadily to pick up and release material
PC10. follow the recommended loading techniques to ensure a full and evenly distributed load
PC11. adhere to the shovel's load capacity to maintain its stability and prevent tipping
PC12. use the appropriate digging technique according to the type of material being excavated
PC13. maintain control of the shovel for its safe operation, avoiding sudden movements
PC14. use mirrors and cameras (if available) to be aware of the surroundings and the movement of
other equipment and personnel in the mining area
