#### AAS/Q1301: Aerospace Composite  Technician
Brief Job Description
Aerospace Composite Technician is responsible for performing composite operation on aerospace parts
and understanding the part drawings and its process sheets, and understanding the safety points and
precautions for manufacturing composite parts.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have basic communication ability and should be responsible in order to maintain
procedures. He should understand work instructions, possess knowledge of layup of composite parts and
vacuum bagging, and possess knowledge on the safety and handling of composite materials and parts.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. AAS/N1305: Understand different types of composite materials
2. AAS/N1301: Understand the composite parts fabrication process for aircraft components/structures
3. AAS/N1302: Perform manual fabrication process of the composites for aircraft components/structures
4. AAS/N1303: Perform automatic fabrication process of the composites for aircraft components/structures
5. ASC/N0021: Maintain 5S at the work premises
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### AAS/N1305: Understand different types of composite materials
Description
This module introduces learners to the types, structures, and applications of composite materials used in
the aerospace industry. It covers fiber types, matrix materials, lay-up techniques, and the benefits and
limitations of composites in aircraft structures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the types, properties, and structures of composite materials used in aerospace.
Learn manufacturing methods and processing techniques such as lay-up, curing, and defect identification.
Identify applications, benefits, and limitations of composites in aerospace components and structures.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify Types and Characteristics of Composite Materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Define composite materials and understand their role in aerospace engineering.
- PC2. Identify different types of fibers (e.g., carbon, glass, aramid) used in aerospace composites.
- PC3. Describe various resin/matrix materials (e.g., epoxy, polyester, phenolic) used in composite
structures.
- PC4. Distinguish between thermoset and thermoplastic composites and their applications.
- PC5. Understand the concept of fiber orientation and lay-up patterns.
Understand Manufacturing and Processing Methods
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Identify common manufacturing methods (e.g., hand lay-up, vacuum bagging, RTM, filament
winding).
- PC7. Understand the curing process and the importance of temperature, pressure, and time
control.
- PC8. Recognize defects in composite manufacturing such as delamination, porosity, or fiber
misalignment.
Know Applications and Limitations of Composites
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Identify key aerospace components made from composite materials (e.g., fuselage panels,
fairings, control surfaces).
- PC10. Understand the advantages of composites (e.g., high strength-to-weight ratio, corrosion
resistance).
- PC11. Understand the limitations and challenges, including repair complexity and material
sensitivity.

#### AAS/N1301: Understand the composite parts fabrication process for
aircraft components/structures
Description
This unit is about hand layup and bagging of composite parts
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand and interpret composite part drawings and fabrication tolerances.
Plan and execute the lay-up process, ensuring all tools, materials, and safety measures are in place.
Coordinate with supervisors/designers to finalize manufacturing processes and ensure compliance
with procedures and safety standards.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding composite parts for fabrication process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. study and understand the component drawings for fabricating the composite parts mentioned
in the drawings.
- PC2. ensure availability of route book and plan lay-up sequence, procedure to manufacture a part
- PC3. ensure understanding of required fabrication tolerances mentioned in the drawings
- PC4. ensure process is finalised and required accessories are available to cut the material
- PC5. check with shop supervisor/ designer to finalise the manufacturing process for open mould
and closed mould composite parts
- PC6. ensure that the forming tool is calibrated before start of tool cleaning; refer to the calibration
sticker attached to the tool for the calibration date check
- PC7. clean the tool layup surface using right cleaning agent as mentioned in the work instructions/
route book
- PC8. ensure appropriate safety factors have been considered and precautions taken to avoid
accidents
- PC9. follow the various safety instructions mentioned in the work instructions/ safety sign boards /
process sheets
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. relevant standards & procedures followed in the company
- KU2. different types of products manufactured by the company
- KU3. different bagging process for different parts
- KU4. process flow/ routing of various components in the composite shop/ organisation
- KU5. aerospace manufacturing standard and procedures such as ISO 9001, AS9100, etc

#### AAS/N1302: Perform manual fabrication process of the composites for
aircraft components/structures
Description
This unit is about Hand Layup and bagging of composite parts
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare tools and materials as per work instructions and ensure calibration and cleanliness of lay-up
tools.
Carry out hand layup and bagging of composite parts following drawings, lay-up sequences, and process sheets.
Ensure correct material handling, vacuum bagging, leak checks, and safe trimming of parts, maintaining a clean and compliant work environment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Tools and materials preparation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure the right tool are available for the part layup, refer work instructions and the route
book/ process sheet for the tool number and the part number
- PC2. ensure the tool is calibrated before start of tool cleaning; refer to the calibration sticker
attached to the tool for the calibration date check
- PC3. clean the tool layup surface using right cleaning agent as mentioned in the work instructions/
route book
Hand layup of composite parts and bagging
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. interpret and gather components drawings, route book, lay up sequence/ sketch for
establishing the process control and development controls
- PC5. ensure the prepeg material should be kept for the required hours at room temperature
before use
- PC6. ensure availability of required materials like prepregs, adhesives, cores, sheet metal details
etc. required to start part/assembly fabrication
- PC7. ensure start lay-up of part as per the layup sequence/ sketch, refer to drawings where
required and ensures correctness of material and orientation. Lay-up of travel coupon panels
(TC panels) as required and the route book
- PC8. lay-up (monolithic or sandwich) as per approved drawings/ process sheet
- PC9. place the thermocouple between the tool surface after completion of lay-up, and lay-up to
monitor the temperature during curing
- PC10. ensure the right bagging film, release film, breather and the sealant are used for bagging;
check the shelf life of these materials with the supervisor
- PC11. prepare vacuum bag and seal the lay-up with vacuum bag, provide the vacuum valves

#### AAS/N1303: Perform automatic fabrication process of the composites for
aircraft components/structures
Description
This unit is about the knowledge, understanding and skills required of anindividual to carry out composite
fabrication using automatic laying upmachine
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand and follow layup sequences and process instructions for automated composite fabrication.
Prepare tools, materials, and machine settings to carry out precise and continuous layup operations.
Perform trimming, edge finishing, and test specimen preparation while ensuring cleanliness and quality compliance.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Tools and material preparation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. interpret and understand the layup sequence mentioned in the drawing/ route card/ process
sheet
- PC2. collect all the required tools from stores
- PC3. fill up required mixture of composite fibres and resins as mentioned in the process sheet in
the hooper.
- PC4. prepare the prepregs material for moulding and store in the laying up machine
Layup using machine
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. ensure that the layup sequence is adhered as per drawings/ process sheet
- PC6. maintain the required gap between layup rollers and the bench
- PC7. roll the rollers such that there is continuity in laying up the prepreg material or fix up the
ends of the fibre on the machine and fix up on the surface. Ensure procedure/ laying instructions as per the laying up machine are followed
- PC8. apply adhesives or appropriate bonding on the surface using another sets of roller on the
machine
- PC9. ensure sufficient thickness is been achieved on the surface as per given process sheet/ work
instruction
- PC10. trim the excess material using the cutters on the machine
- PC11. finish the edges as per procedures provided in the drawing
- PC12. maintain a clean and safe working environment near the machine to avoid contaminated oil/
moisture absorption
