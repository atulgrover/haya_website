TSC/Q7902: Twister cum Doubler - Handloom
Brief Job Description
The person is responsible for twisting and doubling the yarn to improve yarn uniformity, abrasion
resistance, tenacity and flexural endurance by ensuring health, safety and security, and complying with
the workplace requirements. The person can either work as a freelancer or seek employment in a
cooperative/organization.
Personal Attributes
Needs to have basic knowledge of fibers,precision, an eye for detail, and an alert mind.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. TSC/N7903: Carry out twisting or doubling for multi-fold yarn
- 2. TSC/N9005: Maintain the work area, tools and machines

TSC/N7903: Carry out twisting or doubling for multi-fold yarn
Description
The unit deals with necessary skills, knowledge and attributes required by the machine operator to
undertake doubling or twisting for multi-fold yarns efficiently
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Undertake twisting or doubling for multi-fold yarn
Elements and Performance Criteria
Undertake twisting or doubling for multifold yarn
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- 1. check that the doubling machine have yarn tensioning device for even tension
- 2. mount, position or place roll of yarn or bobbin of thread on machine and threads through guides
and rollers
- 3. start the machine and observe operation to detect malfunctions or defects, such as breaking of
material or thread
- 4. adjusts machine controls, such as width or tension guides, to keep operations within
specifications
- 5. set control on machine according to width, length, or other specifications
- 6. ensure the production of long length of knot free yarns which facilitates better performance in
the subsequent processes
- 7. examine and inspects yarns for defects and size specifications
- 8. unite two or more yarn ends on a package
- 9. ensure the ply yarns are free from corkscrew defect due to equalized tensioning of the single
yarns
- 10. carry out doubling in both the systems efficiently, Systems: Single process and two process
- 11. unite two or more doubled yarn/ compact yarn ends by twisting, , in order to obtain a stronger
yarn
- 12. carry out twisting in parallel state to contribute fully to the yarn strength and yarn lustre
- 13. remove full packages or rolls from machine manually, or using hoist
- 14. tie breaks in yarn
- 15. record production information, such as lot, number of defects and machine stops, and types of
defects
- 16. notify supervisor of defects or machine malfunctions
- 17. stack or wrap and pack product for further processing or shipping
- 18. clean and lubricate machines
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

Description
This unit provides performance criteria, knowledge, understanding, skills and abilities required to
organise/maintain work areas and activities to ensure tools used for warping are maintained as per norms
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain the work area, tools and machines
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain the work area, tools and machines
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- 1. Handle materials and tools safely and correctly
- 2. Use correct lifting and handling procedures
- 3. Use materials in a manner to minimize waste
- 4. Maintain a clean and hazard free working area
- 5. Maintain the tools and equipment used for warping
- 6. Carry out maintenance and/or cleaning within ones responsibility
- 7. Identify damaged tools and materials and take action according to the standards followed
- 8. Ensure that the correct tools and yarn required are in place
- 9. Work with the correct posture
- 10. Use cleaning equipment and methods appropriate for the work to be carried out
- 11. Dispose of waste safely in the designated location
- 12. Store cleaning equipment safely after use
- 13. Carry out cleaning according to schedule and limits of responsibility
