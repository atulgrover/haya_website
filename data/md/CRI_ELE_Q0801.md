#### CRI/ELE/Q0801: SENIOR  AUTOMATION   TECHNICIAN
Brief Job Description
A Senior Automation Technician is a professional responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining
automation systems in industrial settings. Their primary goal is to optimize processes, enhance efficiency,
and ensure the smooth operation of manufacturing or production facilities. The job description may vary
depending on the industry and specific company needs but generally includes the following responsibilities
and Technical & Managerial skills.
Personal Attributes
Honesty, tech savvy, technically sound, self motivated, upgraded with updated technology, adaptive,
efficient and should have sound knwoledge about the recent updations in the automation segment.

#### CRI/ELE/N0801: Applications of PLC Programming- Architecture and
Design
Description
The primary guiding force driving automated industrial plants is a "Programmable Logic Controller,"
commonly referred to as a PLC. A PLC serves as a computing system strategically programmed to oversee
electromechanical processes within industries. This unit provides an understanding of both conceptual and
practical fundamentals of automation, encompassing PLC hardware and programming. The critical attributes of PLC hardware and programming play pivotal roles in the realm of industrial automation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the concept of Automation in Industry
Understand the basics of PLC.
Familiar with Hardware of PLC
Use of Totally Integrated Automation Portal (TIA) for PLC Programming.
Check out the basic hardware connection of PLC.

#### CRI/ELE/N0802: Control Technologies in Automation- Man, Machine
Interface (SCADA)
Description
As industries continue to evolve towards increased automation, the integration of a Supervisory Control
and Data Acquisition (SCADA) system becomes imperative. The SCADA system acts as the vital link
between human operators and the PLC, facilitating real-time monitoring, control, and optimization of
industrial processes. This rationale explores the key attributes and significance of the Man-Machine
Interface (MMI) in the form of SCADA within automated industrial plants.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Receive an overview of the scope of performance of the engineering framework.
Understand the concept of SCADA.
Design multiple screens for the visualization of automation process.
Use of Totally Integrated Automation Portal (TIA) for SCADA designing.
Familiar with the system requirements.

#### CRI/ELE/N0803: System Design  and Integration
Description
The system design ensures compliance with industry regulations and safety standards. PLC programming
includes safety interlocks and emergency shutdown features to mitigate risks. and also includes
diagnostics and error-handling features, enhancing the overall reliability and minimizing downtime. The
well-thought-out design and integration strategies contribute to the efficiency and success of the
automated industrial processes. This unit is about analyzing and understanding user requirements,
designing the system and testing it to conform to the user/client specifications.

The scope covers the following :
The system comprises various components of diverse types, encompassing electrical components, electronic assemblies, instrumentation and sensors, electro-mechanical devices, pneumatic and
hydraulic components, wiring, control panels, PLCs, SCADA, communication components, and associated
software.
This unit/task encompasses the following:
Analyzing and understanding client/user requirements
Design and Develop control Systems
Testing the system develop
