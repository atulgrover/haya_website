NFD/MES/Q0101: Chief Camera Assistant
Brief Job Description
The Chief Camera Assistant is responsible for the setup of the camera, Supervising the power and cabling,
rigging any Ergonomic Camera Equipment as well and arranging the lighting and other related equipment
to suit the needs of a particular sequence/scene to be shot, as per the specifications given by the director
of photography.
Personal Attributes
In the heart of a bustling film set, the aspiring Chief Camera Assistant orchestrates each shot with
precision, attuned to the director's vision and the subtle interplay of light. With technical expertise and
calm agility, they navigate challenges seamlessly, ensuring every frame captures the essence of the story.
Their silent symphony of teamwork and dedication culminates in cinematic moments that resonate long
after the final take, a testament to their unwavering pursuit of visual excellence.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. NFD/MES/N0101: Set up and maintain camera equipment
- 2. NFD/MES/N0102: Perform shot planning and operate the camera

- 5. NFD/MES/N0304: Follow Workplace Health & Safety Protocols
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

NFD/MES/N0101: Set up and maintain camera equipment
Description
This NOS is designed to set up and maintain camera equipment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Oversee the setup, calibration, and maintenance of all camera equipment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Oversee the setup, calibration, and maintenance of all camera equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Collaborate with the Director of Photography to select and prepare appropriate cameras,
lenses, and accessories for each shot.
PC2. Lead and supervise the camera department, including camera operators and camera
assistants.
PC3. Collaborate with the Director of Photography and other department heads to ensure
seamless communication and coordination on set.
PC4. Collaborate with the Director of Photography (DP) and camera team to set up and prepare
the camera equipment for shooting.
PC5. Ensure that cameras, lenses, and accessories are in proper working condition.
PC6. Execute precise and smooth focus pulls during shots, maintaining a sharp focus on the
subject as required by the Director and DP.
PC7. Monitor and adjust the focus during moving shots or changes in the scene.
PC8. Work closely with the camera operator to achieve the desired visual effect.
PC9. Perform routine maintenance on cameras and lenses to ensure optimal performance.
PC10. Clean lenses and sensors to eliminate dust or debris that could affect image quality.
PC11. Exhibit a keen attention to detail by inspecting all camera components, verifying proper
connections, and ensuring no issues are overlooked.
PC12. Troubleshoot and address technical issues with the camera equipment.
PC13. Adhere to safety protocols during equipment setup and maintenance.
PC14. Maintain accurate and organised documentation of camera settings and issues.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Analyze the principles of optics and their application in camera lenses.
KU2. Differentiate between different camera formats, sensors, and their strengths and
weaknesses.

NFD/MES/N0102: Perform shot planning and operate the camera
Description
This NOS is designed to perform shot planning and operate the camera
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform shot planning and operate the camera
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform shot planning and operate the camera
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Assist in shot planning and framing, considering the director's vision and the technical
requirements of each scene.
PC2. Coordinate with the camera operators to achieve precise and dynamic shots.
PC3. Monitor and control focus, aperture, and exposure during filming to ensure consistent and
high-quality imagery.
PC4. Implement creative depth-of-field and lighting techniques as directed by the Director of
Photography.
PC5. Oversee the setup and operation of camera movement equipment, such as dollies, cranes,
and stabilisers.
PC6. Ensure the safe and effective use of rigging systems for specialised shots.
PC7. Supervise the organisation and backup of digital footage, ensuring the integrity and security
of all media.
PC8. Work closely with the post-production team to facilitate a smooth transition of media assets.
PC9. Quickly address technical issues related to camera equipment and resolve any challenges
during shooting.
PC10. Collaborate with the production team to solve logistical and technical challenges creatively.
PC11. Mentor and train junior camera assistants, fostering skill development and a collaborative
team environment.
PC12. Stay updated on industry trends and emerging technologies to continually enhance the
team's capabilities.
PC13. Conduct regular checks on equipment to ensure optimal performance and adherence to
industry standards.
PC14. Review footage to ensure it meets the technical and creative requirements of the project.
PC15. Enforce and adhere to safety protocols on set, especially concerning the handling and
operation of camera equipment.
PC16. Conduct regular safety briefings with the camera department.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
