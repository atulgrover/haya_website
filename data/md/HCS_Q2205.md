#### HCS/Q2205: Glass Blowing Operator (Glassware)
Brief Job Description
The individual at work is responsible for blowing the glass gob through a hollow pipe to give shape to the
product. The individual understands the shapes produced through blowing, blow moulding and quality of
gob required to produce different kinds of products. The individual is also responsible for cooling of the ball
of gob and ensures that the orifice of the ball is not closed
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have: ability to work in high temperature environment near furnace, work
in high decibel noise environment, work in standing position for long time, physically fit and ability to
continuously blow through a hollow pipe
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N2206: Blow the gob to make glass artifacts
2. HCS/N2204: Handle the gob from furnace and form a hollow ball of liquid glass

#### HCS/N2206: Blow the gob to make glass artifacts
Description
This unit is about moulding of glass artefacts through controlled blowing of air through the hollow pipe. It
also involves moulding of the ball of glass inside a metal mould.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
deduce the product design and properties requirement blow the glass gob through a hollow pipe give shape to the glass gob
understand the furnace conditions and requirement achieve quality and productivity
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Deduce the product design and properties requirement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Confirm the shapes and designs that can be produced through blow moulding.
- PC2. Infer the weight, dimension and colour specifications for the product to be produced.
- PC3. Understand the temperature sensitivity of product during blowing.
- PC4. Estimate the time required to form different kinds of products/designs for optimum
efficiency.
- PC5. Understand the optimum temperature of the gob for blow moulding.
Blow the glass gob through a hollow pipe
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Coordinate with the ball maker and moulding helper in the blowing process.
- PC7. Perform the process of blowing through the hollow pipe as per the standards procedure.
- PC8. Blow the pipe with appropriate air and control breath as per the shape requirement.
- PC9. Ensure that the orifice of the ball does not get closed during the process.
- PC10. Follow recommended safety standards while handling the glass gob and blowing.
Give shape to the glass gob
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Coordinate with ball maker to form adequate size of ball.
- PC12. Estimate the air pressure required to form different kinds of shapes through blowing.
- PC13. Evaluate the expected weight of the product for different shapes of glass gob.
- PC14. Deduce the possibility of formation of a particular design through blowing.
- PC15. Carry out different kinds of blows like round blow and straight blow for different kinds of
products.
- PC16. Evaluate the shape of the product visually while blowing and stop blowing when it attains the
required shape.

#### HCS/N2204: Handle the gob from furnace and form a hollow ball of liquid
glass
Description
This unit is about handling the gob from furnace and making a hollow ball of liquid glass and the controling
of the quantity of glass through a hollow pipe.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
handle the glass gob from furnace make ball of liquid glass control Quantity achieve Quality ensure safety measure during gob handling
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Handle the glass gob from furnace
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Coordinate with production supervisor for making balls of requisite size.
- PC2. Take the glass gob from the furnace using a hollow pipe.
- PC3. Control the glass temperature on the hollow pipe, outside the furnace.
- PC4. Ensure appropriate quantity of glass gob is taken for ball making.
Make ball of liquid glass
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Coordinate with production supervisor and blower for making balls of required sizes.
- PC6. Work on different types of furnaces used for glass melting.
- PC7. Identify optimum furnace temperature for ball making.
- PC8. Identify optimum glass temperature within furnace for ball making.
- PC9. Identify hot spots and flow of glass within the furnace during melting.
- PC10. Make ball of gob in accordance with product specifications and dimensions.
- PC11. Ensure minimum damage during the process of ball making.
- PC12. Ensure that the daily targets for different products are achieved.
- PC13. Get specific work related instructions from supervisor, if required.
Control Quantity
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. Get specific instructions from production supervisor regarding product characteristics and
dimensions.
- PC15. Work on furnace used for glass melting.
- PC16. Identify optimum furnace temperature to make ball of required size.
