MEP/Q7204: CCTV Video Footage Auditor
Brief Job Description
A CCTV Video Footage Auditor audits, reviews, examines, and minutely inspects CCTV footage daily, at
periodic intervals, as per set guidelines, SOPs, and situational awareness. Using specialized CCTV video
footage auditing and investigation tools, the auditor looks out for exceptions, process violations,
abnormalities, performance lapses, behavioral patterns, potential threats, risks, and so on.
Personal Attributes
Basic proficiency in using a computer (Windows based) and MS Office and good communication skills is
required. The person must be alert, observant, and possess visual faculties which entail object or entity
recognition and differentiation, tracking, and detection of movement/anomalies. Further, the person should
possess Information handling skills which entail pattern recognition, co-relating events/occurrences
(joining the dots), discerning exceptions, and suspicious activity/behavior.

MEP/N7225: Audit CCTV video footage
Description
This unit covers the knowledge and skills required to audit CCTV video footage as a standard operating
procedure in order to detect exceptions, process violations, abnormalities, performance lapses, behavioral
patterns, potential threats, risks and so on, to tag audit findings/incidents, and to create a well categorized
institutional library for future reference.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Analyze CCTV video footage
Elements and Performance Criteria
Analyze CCTV video footage
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. operate the requisite software/ AI-powered video analytics software for CCTV video footage
auditing, in operation at the said establishment
PC2. carry out forensic analysis/ Machine Learning-based anomaly detection systems of the CCTV
video footage using the requisite software
PC3. Analyze footage from multiple camera angles using synchronized playback systems to ensure
context and accuracy.
PC4. detect threats, risks, hazards, emergencies, and other kinds of exceptions, process
violations/standard operating procedures being flouted/compliance issues,
abnormal/suspicious behavior/activity, behavioral patterns/body language, health and safety
issues, issues with camera/video feed, issues pertaining to specific scenarios, from the CCTV
footage being audited
PC5. Tag relevant clips with digital identifiers (incident code, timestamp, camera ID) for ease of
reference.
PC6. Capture and export critical frames or video segments for reporting, ensuring tamper-proof
export format (hash-verified).
PC7. Record all observations and anomalies using structured digital templates within the
organization’s Audit Management System (AMS).
PC8. Maintain objectivity, confidentiality, and adherence to ethical AI surveillance practices
throughout the audit.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The layout of the premises and the areas of responsibility
KU2. routine activities in the premises
KU3. possible threats/risks in the premises

MEP/N7226: Report and Document Audit Findings
Description
This unit covers the knowledge and skills required to report and document audit findings, incidents in a
standardized format.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Report and document audit findings and incidents
Elements and Performance Criteria
Report and document audit findings/incidents
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Summarize findings with supporting snapshots, event timestamps, and data logs using digital
audit tools (Excel, Power BI, or VMS dashboards) daily basis
PC2. Highlight critical or recurring deviations using visual analytics (heat maps, frequency charts, or
incident trend summaries).
PC3. Store, back up, and version-control all reports and footage securely in encrypted digital
repositories.
PC4. Operate the requisite software to document reports of the audit findings/incidents covering
the 5 Ws and 1 H (what, when, where, why, who and how), in a standardized format
PC5. Ensure reports adhere to organizational formatting, escalation timelines, and data compliance
norms (DPDP Act 2023).

KU1. reporting/recording procedure and formats
KU2. the 5 Ws and 1 H (what, when, where, why, who and how) that are essential for information
gathering
KU3. sources of evidence related to safety/security incidents and the importance of preserving
them
KU4. signages and notices

MEP/N7227: Back up CCTV video footage
Description
This unit covers the knowledge and skills required to back up CCTV video footage at multiple locations for
the purpose of disaster recovery.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Store and retrieve the CCTV video footage
Material and energy conservation practices
Elements and Performance Criteria
Store and retrieve CCTV video footage
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. operate advanced Video Management and AI-enabled backup systems with automated
scheduling.
PC2. Use multi-location, cloud-based and hybrid storage solutions for redundancy and disaster
recovery.
PC3. retrieve and share requested data from the backup with authorized person(s).
PC4. Implement digital approval workflows and access logs for traceable data authorization.
PC5. Strengthen cybersecurity skills- encryption, secure transmission, and privacy compliance
awareness.
Material and energy conservation practices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Promote Green ICT awareness for optimizing digital storage and resource use.
PC7. Learn automated data deduplication and archival management tools to reclaim storage
efficiently.
PC8. Adopt smart power management and energy-efficient equipment usage practices.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. the establishment's backup policies, procedures and priorities
KU2. the importance of confidentiality and data protection
KU3. sources of evidence related to safety/security incidents and the importance of preserving
them
KU4. compliance with data protection laws (DPDP Act 2023, ISO 27001, GDPR) during storage,
access, and transmission.
Generic Skills (GS)
