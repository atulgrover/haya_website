#### TEL/Q7201: Assembly  Process Technician - Wafer Testing
Brief Job Description
The individual for this job role is responsible for performing various tasks associated with the testing and
quality control of semiconductor wafers used in telecom applications. They are also responsible to work
with sophisticated testing equipment and follow established procedures to ensure the integrity and
performance of the wafers. In addition, the individual also document test results, identify any defects or
issues, and collaborate with engineering teams to implement corrective actions. The individual for this job
role requires a keen attention to detail and a strong commitment to maintaining high standards of quality.
Personal Attributes
The individual must demonstrate attention to detail, analytical and problem-solving abilities, and effective
communication skills, both written and verbal. The individual should be adept at working collaboratively
with others.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. TEL/N7201: Assemble Wafer Test Equipment

3. TEL/N7203: Analyze Wafer Test Data

#### TEL/N7201: Assemble  Wafer Test Equipment
Description
This NOS unit focuses on preparing the wafer test environment, setting up equipment, and loading wafers
for testing as per defined procedures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare Test Environment according to SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures)
Set up & Calibrate Wafer Test Equipment
Load Wafers into the Test Equipment Following Handling Protocols
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare Test Environment according to SOPs
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. review SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) for specific test requirements.
- PC2. identify and gather all necessary materials and tools listed in the SOPs for wafer testing
- PC3. verify the functionality and proper calibration of any required measuring tools.
- PC4. consult equipment recommendations or SOPs for the ideal temperature and humidity range
for optimal test performance
- PC5. utilize thermometers and hygrometers to monitor the test environment conditions
- PC6. adjust environmental controls (e.g., air conditioning, dehumidifiers) as needed to maintain
the specified temperature and humidity levels.
- PC7. verify that essential utilities like compressed air and power supply are available and
functioning properly
- PC8. conduct a visual inspection of utility lines and connections for any signs of damage or leaks
- PC9. test the functionality of compressed air pressure and power supply voltage according to
established procedures
- PC10. document the test environment conditions (temperature, humidity, etc.) and any
adjustments made according to SOPs
Set up & Calibrate Wafer Test Equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. analyze the type of telecom wafers to be tested
- PC12. review test program requirements for specific functionalities to be evaluated
- PC13. identify the appropriate test equipment model from available options based on wafer type &
test needs and consult SOPs for the selected test equipment model
- PC14. identify and gather required test accessories like test heads and probes suitable for wafer
testing
- PC15. install the accessories onto the designated ports or fixtures on the test equipment, following
established procedures, ensuring proper connection and secure positioning of the test heads and probes for optimal test performance

#### TEL/N7202: Perform Wafer  Test Procedures
Description
This OS unit covers executing test programs, monitoring progress, and documenting results to evaluate
wafer functionality.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Select & Execute Appropriate Test Program
Monitor Test Progress & Document Test Results
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Select & Execute Appropriate Test Program
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. analyze the traveler sheets or batch records to determine the type of wafers being tested
- PC2. review the specifications of the wafers, considering factors like their intended functionalities
within a telecom system
- PC3. consult the traveler sheets or batch records to understand the specific test requirements for
the wafers
- PC4. access the library of available test programs for the test equipment being used
- PC5. select the appropriate test program that aligns with the specified test requirements, based
on the identified wafer type and functionalities
- PC6. review the documentation associated with the selected test program to understand its
objectives
- PC7. identify the scope of the test program, considering the specific functionalities it will assess
within the wafer
- PC8. initiate the process of loading the selected test program by following the SOPs (Standard
Operating Procedures) for the test equipment
- PC9. access the program settings within the loaded test program
- PC10. configure relevant program parameters based on the specific test requirements, to ensure
the test aligns with the intended evaluation
- PC11. initiate the test program execution on the test equipment
- PC12. monitor the test progress through the equipment interface, observing any real-time data
displays or progress indicators
- PC13. prepare to respond to prompts or error messages that might appear on the equipment
interface during test execution
- PC14. follow established procedures for handling prompts (e.g., user confirmation for specific test
steps) and troubleshooting error messages (e.g., consulting troubleshooting guides or notifying maintenance personnel)
Monitor Test Progress & Document Test Results

#### TEL/N7203: Analyze Wafer Test Data
Description
This OS unit focuses on analyzing test data to identify trends, failing devices, and classifying wafers for
further action.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Analyze & Evaluate Test Results
Document, Report & Communicate Results
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Analyze & Evaluate Test Results
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. utilize statistical process control (SPC) techniques to analyze the collected test data for
wafers
- PC2. select appropriate SPC charts based on the specific test parameters and wafer functionalities
- PC3. interpret control chart data to identify trends, shifts, or outliers that might indicate potential
process issues
- PC4. calculate statistical metrics to assess process capability and identify opportunities for
improvement
- PC5. compare the test data for each wafer against established criteria or historical data,
considering tolerances and expected variations
- PC6. identify any outliers, unexpected dips or spikes in parameters, or deviations from normal test
results, categorizing them by severity (critical, minor)
- PC7. utilize wafer maps or reference markers to correlate specific test data points with their
corresponding locations on the wafer
- PC8. analyze spatial patterns of defects to identify potential causes related to specific locations
within the wafer fabrication process
- PC9. apply knowledge of circuit design principles and Semiconductor Manufacturing Processes to
understand the potential causes of the identified defects based on the type of test performed
- PC10. consult with design engineers or process specialists to gain further insights into potential
root causes based on their expertise
- PC11. classify failing devices based on the type of defect (e.g., electrical, physical) and its severity
level (critical, moderate, marginal)
- PC12. evaluate the impact of failing devices on overall wafer functionality, considering redundancy
or isolation mechanisms within the circuit design
- PC13. recommend appropriate next steps for failing devices, including retesting, scrapping, or
further investigation using specialized equipment
- PC14. analyze data from multiple wafers and test runs to distinguish between random defects
(isolated incidents) and systematic problems (affecting multiple devices or wafers)
