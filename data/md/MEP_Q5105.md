MEP/Q5105: Udyami Mitra
Brief Job Description
Udyami Mitra is a community-based mentor who will assist women to become entrepreneurs through
conducting entrepreneurship awareness programs, entrepreneurship development programs and
individually assisting women with information and link to resources to start and run an enterprise.
Personal Attributes
The person should have basic technical aptitude and people skills. The person must be alert and
observant, able to work alone without supervision as well as part of a team, is reliable, disciplined and
attentive to details.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MEP/N5132: Discover the entrepreneur in self
- 2. MEP/N5133: Understand financial and digital literacy skills

- 5. MEP/N5136: Promote entrepreneurial activities of women

MEP/N5132: Discover the entrepreneur in self
Description
The unit focuses on behaviour analysis and skills assessment in determining mentoring and
entrepreneurial skills in oneself and mentees. Included are techniques of self-reflection and evaluation of
available skills, identification of areas requiring further development, and utilization of resources available
to develop needed skills for entrepreneurship.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Enhance entrepreneurial skills of self
Assist mentees to evaluate entrepreneurial skills
Elements and Performance Criteria
Enhance entrepreneurial skills of self
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. undertake behavioural analysis to identify own strengths and weaknesses for mentoring
PC2. undertake upskilling through individual or group training or mentor support
PC3. identify limiting beliefs causing diffidence and low self-esteem.
PC4. reprogram self-talk to overwrite limiting beliefs with strengths
PC5. imitate successful women entrepreneur to strengthen one’s weak skill sets
PC6. identify leadership skills and ways to motivate women
PC7. convert own strengths to be leader and communicate effectively
PC8. use verbal and non-verbal communication techniques effectively during explanation and
presentation
Assist mentee to evaluate entrepreneurial skills
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. evaluate social and psychological empowerment of the individual
PC10. assist in carrying out behaviour analysis and identifying strong qualities and areas that need
strengthening
PC11. carry out skills scan to identify development needed in the knowledge, behaviours and skills
of mentees for running a business
PC12. use successes of women to showcase ways to learn skills
PC13. prepare a development to identify methods for developing skills identified
PC14. link with resources to learn and strengthen weak skill sets
PC15. discuss with persons who can list risks and problems in the implementation plan
PC16. evaluate risks in the business plan and prepare mitigation strategies
PC17. evaluate past failures and determine ways to convert it to success
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

MEP/N5133: Understand financial and digital literacy skills
Description
This unit is about assisting mentees to develop financial and digital literacy skills and using digital devices
and applications securely.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out savings and budgeting
Comply with laws and regulations
Carry out book-keeping and financial management
Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out savings and budgeting
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. evaluate numeracy skills and develop the weak skills
PC2. identify resources to develop numeracy skills
PC3. use digital applications to send and receive money
PC4. ensure secure use while using digital and financial tools
PC5. prepare financial plan and budgets
PC6. list probable debt traps and solutions
PC7. list moneylenders in the neighbourhood and alternate options like micro finance schemes
available
PC8. prepare payback/ repayment plan and alternate plans for borrowings
PC9. calculate interest on borrowings and money spent as interest over the borrowed amount
PC10. work-out start-up capital, return on investments and possible barriers to achievement
Comply with laws and regulations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. examine funding opportunities
PC12. identify micro-credit and micro-finance options
PC13. explore angel investors and angel groups
PC14. navigate banking, insurance and other systems
PC15. recognise possible scams and distinguish scamster and bank messages and communication
PC16. examine GST and tax compliances
Carry out book-keeping and financial management
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC17. recognise basic accounting and book-keeping terminologies
PC18. record daily income and expenses in a designated book
PC19. analyse cash flow and cash requirements

MEP/N5134: Develop life skills
Description
This unit is about assisting mentees to develop various essential life skills to become a successful
entrepreneur.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Problem solving and conflict management
Negotiation and effective communication
Leadership and self confidence
Elements and Performance Criteria
Problem solving and conflict management
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. recognise discrimination and gendered beliefs and act to change those beliefs and
behaviours
PC2. plan work-life balance schedules
PC3. use caselets, case studies and role plays to learn necessary skills.
PC4. assist women to describe issues and identify solutions.
PC5. reinforce confidence by pointing to the value of the identified solutions
PC6. evaluate gender based behaviours and challenges as a result
PC7. examine socio-cultural, familial and other barriers to entrepreneurship .
PC8. reflect on gender inequality interplay and assist on identifying solutions
PC9. apply stress management strategies and conflict resolution techniques
PC10. use affirmations for reinforcement and moral boosting.
PC11. identify and manage burnout
Negotiation and effective communication
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. engage in assertive communication to boost self-confidence
PC13. modify communication to get the outcome you want
PC14. manage written, oral and social media communication effectively
PC15. have the flexibility to change your communication based on situations
PC16. become aware of other’s views and intentions and modify communication
PC17. develop business negotiation skills
PC18. manage emotions by focusing on and discussing facts and not feelings
PC19. evaluate opinions and differences by dissociating from friendships and likes/dislikes of
person or their behaviour
Leadership and self confidence
