#### MSU/MEP/Q0101: Vedic Mathematics Trainer
Brief Job Description
A Vedic Mathematics Trainer applies ancient mathematical principles and techniques, enabling precise
mental calculations and problem-solving. They adeptly handle arithmetic operations, advanced calculations, fractions, and algebra using Vedic methods. They foster inclusivity by integrating diverse
cultural perspectives, encourage technological proficiency, ensure effective online teaching, and promote
active learning through interactive tools and virtual experiences.
Personal Attributes
A Vedic Maths trainer should possess a strong understanding of mathematical concepts, problem-solving
abilities, and an aptitude for teaching. Excellent communication skills, patience, and the ability to simplify
complex ideas are crucial. A passion for mental math and enhancing students' numerical abilities is
essential. Adaptability, creativity in teaching methods, and a commitment to continuous learning are vital
for success in this role, along with a deep respect for traditional Vedic knowledge.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MSU/MEP/N0102: Apply Vedic mathematic techniques to enhance mathematical calculation abilities
2. MSU/MEP/N0103: Teach Vedic mathematics principles and techniques to learners
3. MSU/MEP/N0101: Implement effective teaching practices
4. MSU/MEP/N0104: Assess learning outcomes and provide feedback
5. MSU/MEP/N0105: Integrate digital technology in classrooms
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

Apply specialised Vedic mathematics fundamental techniques

enhance calculation efficiency

accessible

world problems

calculations
- PC13. Apply advanced cubing techniques to higher-level in mathematical challenges

#### MSU/MEP/N0103: Teach Vedic mathematics principles and techniques to
learners
Description
This OS unit is about evaluating learners' needs and preparing for Vedic Mathematics instruction to foster
inclusive and engaging learning environments. It emphasizes tailoring teaching strategies to accommodate
diverse needs, ensuring effective participation and engagement in Vedic Math lessons for all students.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Evaluate Learner's Needs
Prepare for Vedic Mathematics Instruction
Promote Inclusive and Engaging Vedic Math Learning Environments
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Evaluate Learner's Needs
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Assess learners' preferred styles (visual, auditory, kinesthetic) for Vedic math instruction to
tailor teaching methods
- PC2. Gather insights on preferred engagement methods via discussions and surveys for effective
Vedic math learning
- PC3. Encourage learners to share their past experiences with Vedic mathematics, aiding
personalized instruction
- PC4. Conduct surveys or interviews to gather information about learners' cultural backgrounds
and preferences in Vedic math
Prepare for Vedic Mathematics Instruction
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Share the historical and cultural significance of Vedic mathematics to deepen learners'
appreciation and understanding
- PC6. Invite experts or practitioners of Vedic mathematics from diverse cultural backgrounds to
share their experiences and insights
- PC7. Assign Vedic math projects that explore mathematical concepts within different cultural
contexts, encouraging a broader perspective
- PC8. Discuss cultural interests and experiences to integrate culturally relevant Vedic math
examples into instruction
- PC9. Provide Vedic math instructional materials in multiple languages to accommodate learners
from diverse linguistic backgrounds
- PC10. Include Vedic math problems inspired by different cultures, showcasing their unique
mathematical contributions
- PC11. Organize workshops or sessions where learners explore mathematical concepts through
traditional cultural practices

#### MSU/MEP/N0104: Assess learning outcomes and provide feedback
Description
This OS unit is about designing and administering assessments and implementing a work plan. It emphasizes creating effective evaluations and integrating them into a structured plan for smooth and
organized assessment management.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Design and administer assessments
Implement a work plan
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Design and administer assessments
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify the learning objectives and outcomes that need to be assessed for the selected topic
- PC2. Select and design appropriate formative assessment types that effectively evaluate student
learning and provide ongoing feedback (Examples: Quizzes, projects, or presentations)
- PC3. Develop rubrics and clear marking criteria as specified by the board, examination-conducting
agency/body
- PC4. Design summative assessments to evaluate and measure the learning outcomes and learner
achievement at the end of each instructional period or unit
- PC5. Ensure that the assessments align with the learning objectives, Bloom's taxonomy, syllabus,
and examination patterns by seeking feedback
- PC6. Administer assessments regularly, as planned in the academic calendar
Implement a Work Plan
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Assess different types of formative and summative assessments in accordance with the
assessment rules and procedures
- PC8. Mark the assessment using rubrics and marking criteria while ensuring accuracy and fairness
- PC9. Analyse and interpret assessment data to identify areas of strength and improvement. and
provide constructive feedback
- PC10. Provide timely and constructive feedback to support student's growth and learning
- PC11. Integrate technology to communicate assessment results and progress clearly to learners
and parents
- PC12. Ensure ethical considerations while providing feedback
- PC13. Facilitate reflective practises encouraging students' self-assessment and metacognition
- PC14. Record learners' progress in their respective learners' profiles and store the assessment
records safely
- PC15. Recognise and celebrate learners' achievements to motivate and reinforce learning
