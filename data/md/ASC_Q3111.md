ASC/Q3111: Welding
Brief Job Description
A welder prepares and joins a range of metals and metallic alloys using mainly processes where an electric
arc is the heat source. Electric arc processes utilize a gas shield or a flux to protect the molten weld area
from contamination by the surrounding atmosphere. A welder needs to be able to interpret engineering
drawings, standards and symbols and correctly translate these requirements into accurate structures and
fabrications.
Personal Attributes
The person should be patient, organised, team-oriented and have the ability to work for long hours in
adverse conditions. They should be keen observers and have an eye for detail and quality.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. ASC/N9819: Work organization and management (Welding)
- 2. ASC/N3120: Preparation and assembly techniques
- 3. ASC/N3121: Welding materials
- 4. ASC/N3122: SMAW (111) and GMAW (135) Process

- 6. ASC/N3124: GTAW (141) Process

ASC/N9819: Work organization and management (Welding)
Description
This NOS unit is about implementing safety, planning work, adopting sustainable practices for optimising
the use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage work and resources
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage work and resources
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Work safely with regard to themselves and others
PC2. Select, wear, and maintain PPE as required
PC3. Recognize hazardous situations and take appropriate actions with regard to their own and
others’ safety
PC4. Follow correct procedural processes when working in hazardous environments
PC5. Locate and identify dimensions and weld symbols
PC6. Adhere to manufacturers’ safety data sheets
PC7. Maintain a clean working environment
PC8. Complete work within agreed timescales
PC9. Make essential connections for specific welding procedures
PC10. Problem solve efficiently
PC11. Take account of the physical and mental demand of the tasks
PC12. Use hand and eye coordination with precision
PC13. Utilize fume extraction systems efficiently to remove emissions
PC14. Create efficient work practices to eliminate waste
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Standards and legislation relating to the health, safety, security, and hygiene in the welding
industry
KU2. Range, use and maintenance of personal protective equipment used in the industry for any
given circumstances
KU3. Selection and use of safety equipment related to specific or hazardous tasks
KU4. ISO A and/or E (American and European) drawing representation
KU5. Technical terms and symbols used in drawings and plans

ASC/N3120: Preparation and assembly techniques
Description
This NOS unit is about preparing for welding activities
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparing for welding activities
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparing for welding activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Set up welding equipment to manufacturers’ specifications including (but not limited to):
Welding polarity, Welding amperage, Welding voltage, Wire feed speed, Travel speed,
Travel/electrode angles, Mode of metal transfer
PC2. Prepare material edges in line with specifications and drawing requirements
PC3. Set up and operate appropriate controls to minimize and correct distortion
PC4. Carry out appropriate procedures to control heat input
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Interpretation of fabrication or engineering drawings and weld symbols
KU2. Classification and specific uses of welding consumables including: Coding and designation of
welding rods, Diameters and specific use of welding wire, Choice and preparation of welding
electrodes
KU3. How surface contamination can influence the finished weld characteristics
KU4. Correct machine settings to be aligned to: Welding polarity, Welding position, Material,
Material thickness, Filler material and Feed speed
KU5. Any fine adjustments needed to machine hardware, TIG electrode shape, wire type and
diameter etc.
KU6. Methods of edge preparation to align with joint profile, strength, and material
KU7. Methods of distortion control in steels, alloys, and aluminium

ASC/N3121: Welding materials
Description
This NOS is about preparing raw material for welding work.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparing raw material
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparing raw material
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Use materials with consideration to their mechanical and physical properties
PC2. Store welding consumables correctly with reference to type, use and safety considerations
PC3. Select and prepare materials with reference to drawing material list
PC4. Select methods used in shielding the weld area from contamination
PC5. Select gases used for shielding and purging
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Mechanical and physical properties of: Carbon steels, Aluminium and its alloys, Stainless
steels
KU2. How to correct the alignment of process with the material being used
KU3. Selection of welding consumables
KU4. Correct storage and handling of welding consumables
KU5. Terminology, characteristics, and safe use of welding and purging gases
KU6. Effects of welding on the structure of the material
