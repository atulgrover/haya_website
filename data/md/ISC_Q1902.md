#### ISC/Q1902: Hot Rolling Mill (Long Products) - Process Control Engineer
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role performs hot rolling mill operations and its associated functions.
Personal Attributes
The person should be patient, organized, team-oriented and have the ability to work for long hours in
adverse conditions. They must be able to plan and prioritise tasks effectively and have an eye for detail
and quality.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ISC/N0946: Manage and implement health and safety practices at the work place
2. ISC/N0947: Manage team and others at workplace
3. ISC/N1904: Prepare for hot rolling mill operation
4. ISC/N1908: Perform hot rolling mills operations
5. ISC/N1704: Apply Decarbonization practice in plant
6. ISC/N1703: Apply statistical process control (SPC) techniques
7. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)

#### ISC/N0946: Manage  and implement  health and safety practices at the
work place
Description
This OS unit is about managing and implementing health, hygiene, safety and sustainable practices for
optimising use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain safe and secure working environment
Maintain health and hygiene
Manage housekeeping at workplace
Implement material and energy conservation practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain safe and secure working environment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify hazardous activities and the possible causes of risks or accidents in the workplace
- PC2. implement safe working practices while dealing with hazards to ensure safety of self and
others
- PC3. ensure that team is following appropriate safety practices while working in and around
trenches, elevated places and confined areas
- PC4. conduct regular checks of the machines with support of the maintenance team to identify
potential hazards
- PC5. organise safety drills or training sessions to create awareness amongst others on the
identified risks and safety practices
- PC6. fill daily check sheet to report improvements done and risks identified
- PC7. ensure that relevant safety boards/signs are placed on the shop floor for the safety of self
and others
- PC8. report any identified breaches in health, safety and security policies and procedures to the
designated person
- PC9. ensure that team is following appropriate emergency procedures such as raising alarm, safe
evacuation etc.
Maintain health and hygiene
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. ensure team is aware about hygiene and sanitation regulations and following them on the
shop floor
- PC11. ensure workplace, equipment, restrooms etc. are cleaned and sanitized regularly
- PC12. ensure availability of running water, hand wash and alcohol-based sanitizers at the
workplace
- PC13. report advanced hygiene and sanitation issues to appropriate authority

Description
This OS unit is about managing team, colleagues/superiors and others, either in own work group or in other

#### ISC/N1904: Prepare for hot rolling mill operation
Description
This OS unit is about performing preparatory activities for various hot rolling mill operations
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out preparatory activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out preparatory activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. obtain the required information and work completed in previous shift from logbook,
discussions etc. and take handover from the previous shift operator by following organizational procedures
- PC2. read rolling orders, blueprints, and mill schedules to determine setup specifications, work
sequences, product type (i.e. bar, rod, wire rod, angles, channels, rails etc.) product dimensions, and installation procedures
- PC3. receive the hot metal slabs from the reheating furnace through conveyors in the hot rolling
mills by following SOP/WI
- PC4. ensure that team is checking the quality of incoming material and it is heated at required
temperature before reaching to rolling mill
- PC5. ensure that work area is clean and free of any obstacle or encumbrance
- PC6. install equipment such as guides, guards, gears, cooling equipment, and rolls, using hand
tools
- PC7. ensure that all safety measures, interlocks, alarms are working and in conformance with the
safe operation of pellet plant as per SOP
- PC8. fill oil cups, adjust valves, and observe gauges to control flow of metal coolants and
lubricants onto workpieces
- PC9. calculate draft space, roll gap, pass profile and roll speed for each mill stand to plan rolling
sequences and specified dimensions and tempers
- PC10. set distance points between rolls, guides, meters, and stops, according to specifications
- PC11. coordinate with other related departments such as raw material handling and quality
departments
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. relevant standards and procedures followed in the company
- KU2. different types of rolling mills

#### ISC/N1908: Perform hot rolling mills operations
Description
This OS unit is about performing various hot rolling mill operations as per the final output specifications
and the standards specified by the organization
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform hot rolling mills operations
Perform post-operation activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform hot rolling mills operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. use appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) for safe working in work area
- PC2. check and measure the temperature of heated slab before passing it through the rolls
- PC3. pass the heated slab between the rolls and start operation of rolling and milling machines to
flatten, temper, form, and reduce metal sections and to produce required type of product
- PC4. adjust and correct machine set-ups to reduce thicknesses, elongation, reshape products, and
eliminate product defects
- PC5. manipulate controls and observe dial indicators to monitor, adjust, and regulate speeds of
machine mechanisms
- PC6. monitor machine cycles and mill operation to detect jamming and to ensure that products
conform to specifications
- PC7. use water at high pressure for cleansing the roll and to remove the mill scale
- PC8. ensure rolls cooling at the end of the process
- PC9. control the cooling rate to allow for the correct microstructure and crystallization of the roll
- PC10. collect the cooled rolls and store it by following organizational procedures
Perform post-operation activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. examine, inspect, and measure the finished products to verify conformance to specifications
- PC12. send the samples for quality check to the concerned department
- PC13. adjust the process parameters on the basis of quality check report
- PC14. repeat the process till required size is attained
- PC15. update and maintain shift log of production and prepare production and other reports
- PC16. record all instructions received from higher authorities in the instruction book and all
incidents in the occurrence book
- PC17. inform concerned authority in case of any abnormalities observed during execution of work

### Page 45

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
