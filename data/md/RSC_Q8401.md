RSC/Q8401: Machine Operator - CNC Milling of Plastic
Brief Job Description
The Machine Operator - CNC Milling of Plastic is responsible for interpreting CNC programs and engineering
drawings, setting up CNC milling machines, performing milling operations on plastic workpieces, and
ensuring precision and quality of the final products. The role involves selecting appropriate tools, adjusting
machine parameters, conducting inspection checks, and performing routine maintenance. Operators must
adhere to workplace safety standards, follow sustainable practices, and maintain the work area in optimal
condition.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be calm, composed and physically fit along with good observational
skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N9901: Interpret CNC programs and drawings and set up CNC Milling Machine
- 2. RSC/N9902: Perform milling operations on plastic workpieces using CNC Machine
- 3. RSC/N9903: Maintain machine, tools, and work area
- 4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

RSC/N9901: Interpret CNC programs and drawings and set up CNC Milling
Machine
Description
This NOS covers the skills and knowledge required to read and interpret CNC programs and engineering
drawings, select appropriate cutting tools and workholding devices, set up CNC milling machines for plastic
machining, and ensure that machine parameters are configured according to the job specifications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Interpret job requirements
Prepare machine setup
Configure machine parameters
Conduct trial run and verification
Elements and Performance Criteria
Interpret job requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. obtain job order and specifications from the authorized person
PC2. read and interpret CNC programs for the specified plastic component
PC3. read engineering drawings and identify dimensions, tolerances, and surface finish
requirements
PC4. identify material type and properties of the plastic workpiece to be machined.
Prepare machine setup
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. select and verify the cutting tools as per job requirements
PC6. inspect workholding devices and fixtures for suitability
PC7. mount and secure workpiece on the machine table using approved clamping methods
PC8. install and align the selected cutting tools in the machine spindle
PC9. set workpiece datum and zero points according to the program reference
Configure machine parameters
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. load and verify the CNC program in the machine controller
PC11. adjust spindle speed, feed rate, and depth of cut as per plastic machining requirements
PC12. simulate the CNC program and check for errors
PC13. ensure coolant or air blast is set appropriately for the plastic material
Conduct trial run and verification
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC14. perform a dry run without cutting to verify tool paths

RSC/N9902: Perform milling operations on plastic workpieces using CNC
Machine
Description
This NOS covers executing CNC milling operations on plastic workpieces, monitoring the machining
process, ensuring product quality, and making necessary adjustments to achieve dimensional accuracy
and surface finish.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for machining operations
Execute CNC milling operations
Conduct in-process inspection and quality control
Complete machining operations
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for machining operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. verify the CNC milling program against the work order and technical drawings
PC2. load the correct workpiece and secure it on the machine fixture or vice
PC3. check the availability and condition of required cutting tools and inserts
PC4. set tool offsets and work coordinates using precision measuring instruments
PC5. ensure coolant and lubrication systems are functioning as per the requirement
Execute CNC milling operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. start the CNC program and monitor the initial machining pass
PC7. adjust feed rate, spindle speed, and depth of cut as per the material’s behavior
PC8. monitor chip formation, tool wear, and surface finish during operations
PC9. apply emergency stop procedures in case of abnormalities
PC10. maintain consistent production cycle time while meeting quality parameters
Conduct in-process inspection and quality control
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. measure dimensions using vernier calipers, micrometers, gauges, or CMM
PC12. compare measurements with specifications in the job order or drawing
PC13. make necessary offsets in the program to correct deviations
PC14. record in-process inspection data as per organisational procedures
Complete machining operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC15. deburr and clean the machined workpiece

RSC/N9903: Maintain machine, tools, and work area
Description
This NOS covers routine and preventive maintenance of CNC milling machines, upkeep of tools, and
ensuring a clean, safe, and organized work area to enhance productivity and machine life.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform routine maintenance of CNC milling machine
Maintain cutting tools and accessories
Maintain cleanliness and safety of the work area
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform routine maintenance of CNC milling machine
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. clean the machine bed, fixtures, and tool holders after operations
PC2. check and top up coolant and lubrication levels as per maintenance schedule
PC3. inspect belts, filters, and hoses for wear and tear
PC4. verify spindle alignment and machine accuracy using test indicators
PC5. lubricate moving parts following manufacturer’s recommendations
Maintain cutting tools and accessories
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. inspect cutting tools for wear, chipping, or deformation
PC7. replace worn-out tools and update tool inventory records
PC8. store tools in a clean, dry, and organized manner to prevent damage
PC9. calibrate and store measuring instruments securely
Maintain cleanliness and safety of the work area
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. remove chips, dust, and debris from the work area regularly
PC11. segregate and dispose of plastic waste and scrap as per guidelines
PC12. ensure all safety guards, covers, and emergency stops are functional
PC13. store hazardous materials in compliance with safety norms
PC14. maintain proper lighting and ventilation in the work area
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. preventive and routine maintenance procedures for CNC milling machines

RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
Description
This unit is about maintaining health and safety of self and others at workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
Render appropriate emergency procedures
Maintain standard safety procedures at the workplace
Participate in safety awareness campaigns
Understand potential sources of accidents
Use safety gears to avoid accidents
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. undertake basic safety checks before operation of all machinery and equipment and report
hazards to the appropriate supervisor
PC2. work for which protective clothing or equipment is required is identified and the appropriate
protective clothing or equipment is used in performing these duties in accordance with
workplace policy
PC3. read and understand the hazards of use and contamination mentioned on the labels of
chemicals, utilities etc
PC4. prior to performing manual handling jobs, risk is assessed and work is carried out according
to currently recommended safe practices.
PC5. use equipment and materials safely and correctly and return the same to designated storage
when not in use
PC6. dispose off waste safely and correctly in a designated area
PC7. risks to bystanders are recognized and action taken to reduce risk associated with jobs in the
workplace
PC8. perform work in a manner which minimizes environmental damage
PC9. all procedures and work instructions for controlling risk are followed closely
PC10. report any accidents, incidents or problems without delay to an appropriate person and take
immediate necessary action to reduce further danger
Render appropriate emergency procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. follow procedures for dealing with accidents, fires and emergencies, including
communicating location and directions to emergency.
PC12. follow emergency procedures as per company standards and workplace requirements
