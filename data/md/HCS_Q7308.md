HCS/Q7308: Traditional Soft Toy Artisan
Brief Job Description
A Traditional Soft Toy Artisan creates charming, handcrafted soft toys using natural or fabric materials.
Their work showcases creativity and precision, producing unique toys that reflect cultural heritage and
artistic skill.
Personal Attributes
A Traditional Soft Toy Artisan is creative and detail-oriented, skilled in sewing and assembling soft
materials with precision. They are patient, imaginative, and dedicated to delivering high-quality, safe, and
unique handcrafted toys.

HCS/N7356: Carry out the process of making Traditional soft toy
Description
The Traditional Soft Toy Maker is responsible for crafting soft toys using traditional techniques, materials,
and designs that reflect cultural heritage.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Material Selection and Preparation
Pattern Cutting and Stitching
Stuffing and Shaping
Detailing and Decoration
Quality Check and Finishing
Tool Maintenance and Work Area Management
Elements and Performance Criteria
Material Selection and Preparation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify appropriate fabrics, stuffing materials, and decorative elements based on the toy
design.
PC2. Ensure materials are safe, non-toxic, and suitable for children, adhering to quality standards.
PC3. Measure and cut fabric accurately according to design templates or patterns.
PC4. Provide tools such as scissors, needles, sewing machines, and stuffing equipment for the
process.
Pattern Cutting and Stitching
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Using patterns or stencils, create the design onto the fabric.
PC6. Cut fabric pieces neatly, ensuring uniformity and precision.
PC7. Stitch fabric pieces together, maintaining consistent seam allowance and secure stitching.
PC8. Provide appropriate openings for stuffing and ensure seams are reinforced for durability.
Stuffing and Shaping
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Select suitable stuffing materials such as cotton, wool, or synthetic fibres.
PC10. Ensure that the toy is properly shaped and soft, without lumps or empty spaces.
PC11. Adjust the quantity of stuffing to maintain the toy’s structural integrity and comfort.
PC12. Close the stuffing opening neatly, ensuring stitches for a polished finish.
Detailing and Decoration
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. Attach decorative elements such as buttons, beads, or embroidery with secure stitching.
PC14. Using embroidery or fabric paint, create facial features and other design details.

Quality Check and Finishing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC21. Clean and maintain tools like needles, scissors, and sewing machines after each use.
PC22. Store tools safely and organise the workspace for efficiency.
PC23. Provide proper disposal of waste materials and maintain a healthy working environment.
PC24. Keep records of material usage and maintain inventory for effective production.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understanding traditional soft toy designs, motifs, and patterns unique to cultural heritage.
KU2. Familiarity with various types of fabrics, threads, and stuffing materials used in soft toy
making.
KU3. Knowledge of sewing techniques, including hand-stitching and machine sewing methods.
KU4. Awareness of quality standards and finishing techniques to ensure durability and
consistency.
KU5. Insight into market trends, customer preferences, and opportunities for product innovation.
KU6. Understanding safety protocols for handling sharp tools and maintaining a clean, organised
workspace.
KU7. Knowledge of basic inventory management and documentation practices to track materials
and designs.
KU8. Knowledge of embellishment techniques such as embroidery, beadwork, fabric painting, etc.
KU9. Insight into packaging and labelling requirements for retail and export markets.
KU10. Awareness of pricing strategies and cost calculation to ensure profitability while maintaining
affordability.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Ability to design unique and culturally inspired soft toys.
GS2. Ensuring precision in cutting, stitching, and embellishing.
GS3. Prioritising tasks to meet production deadlines efficiently.
GS4. Responding to changing market trends and customer preferences.

HCS/N8525: Entrepreneurship Skills for Traditional Soft Toy Artisan
Description
This unit focuses on entrepreneurship skills for traditional soft toy artisans, balancing cultural heritage with
market demands. It covers product innovation, financial management, marketing, and sustainability to
support business growth.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding Market Trends and Opportunities
Business Planning and Financial Management
Product Development and Innovation
Branding, Marketing, and Sales Strategies
Production and Quality Control
Legal and Ethical Considerations
Scaling and Sustainability
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Market Trends and Opportunities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify developing trends in soft toy design, materials, and consumer preferences.
PC2. Analyse market demand, target customers, and pricing strategies.
PC3. Identify competition and create a unique selling proposition (USP) for soft toys.
Business Planning and Financial Management
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Develop a basic business plan highlighting vision, goals, and strategies.
PC5. Estimate the cost of raw materials, labour, packaging, and distribution.
PC6. Identify funding sources, such as self-financing, loans, or government schemes.
PC7. Maintain financial records, including sales, expenses, and profits.
Product Development and Innovation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Select the appropriate materials and techniques for making soft toys.
PC9. Develop prototypes and test designs for durability and safety compatibility.
PC10. Include innovative elements such as eco-friendly fabrics or interactive features.
Branding, Marketing, and Sales Strategies
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Design attractive packaging and branding items to enhance product appeal.
PC12. Promote soft toys through online and offline advertising channels.
PC13. Identify and collaborate with retailers, e-commerce platforms, and local markets.
PC14. Utilise social media and digital platforms for customer engagement and sales.

PC17. Improve production efficiency while maintaining product quality.
Legal and Ethical Considerations

PC19. Follow ethical sourcing methods for materials and fair labour policies.
PC20. Ensure compliance with child safety and eco-friendly manufacturing standards.

PC22. Implement sustainable methods, including waste reduction and recycling.
PC23. Develop partnerships with NGOs, schools, and corporate customers for bulk orders.

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understanding market trends, customer preferences, and identifying target audiences.
KU2. Knowledge of blending traditional designs with modern trends to create unique products.
KU3. Understanding the steps involved in developing a business plan, setting goals, and defining
strategies.
KU4. Awareness of budgeting, pricing, cost control, and profit calculation to ensure financial
sustainability.
KU5. Understanding branding techniques, promotional strategies, and leveraging digital platforms
for product visibility.
KU6. Knowledge of sales channels, both offline and online, to expand market reach and increase
sales.
KU7. Understanding the importance of building and maintaining strong customer relationships to
ensure repeat business.
KU8. Awareness of effective time management techniques and efficient use of resources to
optimise production.
KU9. Understanding eco-friendly methods, sustainable material sourcing, and waste reduction
techniques.
KU10. Awareness of potential business risks and strategies to mitigate them.
KU11. Knowledge of creating partnerships, participating in artisan communities, and accessing
support systems.
KU12. Understanding basic legal requirements, intellectual property rights, and compliance with
safety standards.
