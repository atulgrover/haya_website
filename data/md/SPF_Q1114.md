#### SPF/Q1114: Rescue  Operation Head
Brief Job Description
A Rescue Operation Head is an individual who plans, supervises and leads the rescue operations during
the aquatic emergencies. The individual is responsible for deploying the first responders of rescue unit,
assess the scale of the emergency and coordinate with authorities for further course of action.
Personal Attributes
Individual must be physically fit with good eyesight and hearing.He/She should be calm, possess a very
good temperament to work under extreme pressure, and can take quick decisions.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SPF/N1144: Plan for aquatic emergencies
2. SPF/N1145: Develop rescue strategy
3. SPF/N1146: Manage search and rescue operations
4. SPF/N1122: Maintain health and safety standards
5. SGJ/N1702: Optimize resource utilization at workplace
6. DGT/VSQ/N0104: Employability Skills (120 Hours)

#### SPF/N1144: Plan for aquatic emergencies
Description
This unit is about preparing for the aquatic emergency
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develop and lead team of first responders
Prepare, maintain and test rescue equipment
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Develop and lead team of first responders
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. specify the team members required to respond for aquatic rescue operation
- PC2. ensure all the crew members have required and updated certifications as life savers
- PC3. ensure that the crew members' physical fitness program is adequate and effective
- PC4. identify training needs of the crew members based on their strengths and areas of
improvement
- PC5. encourage equality and non-discrimination in terms of gender, abilities, religion and
background
- PC6. conduct mock-drills of rescue operation in a simulated environment
Prepare, maintain and test rescue equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. maintain adequate number of equipment for immediate rescue response
- PC8. conduct regular safety checks of the equipment and machines
- PC9. isolate any unsafe equipment, machinery and report to maintenance team
- PC10. record the maintenance schedule of equipment and machinery as per the safety standards
- PC11. ensure navigation equipment are in working condition (inland flood boats)
- PC12. ensure adequate medical equipment like artificial resuscitation, oxygen mask, stretcher, etc.
are readily available
- PC13. ensure public address equipment such as loud speakers, mics, whistle are within reach
- PC14. ensure communication devices such as radio, GPS units, mobile phones, are adequate for the
team
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. qualification, skills, fitness levels required to be a rescue personnel
- KU2. training and assessments of rescue personals

#### SPF/N1145: Develop  rescue strategy
Description
This unit is about developing rescue strategies.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for rescue operation
Develop secondary rescue plan
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for rescue operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. assess the search and rescue area, gather information on the scale of operation required
- PC2. develop search and rescue strategy and techniques based on information available
- PC3. identify rescue team members in accordance with strategy and nature of emergency
- PC4. deploy navigation and rescue equipment as per the condition and the environment of the
rescue area
- PC5. ensure team members are briefed on the strategy and their specific role in the rescue
operation
- PC6. allocate tasks to team members as per their level of competence and experience
- PC7. establish communication system with each unit of rescue team
- PC8. monitor performance of team members and strategy, as the scenario unfolds, to determine
further requirements, if any
Develop secondary rescue plans
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. identify deployment of secondary rescue plan in conjunction with the primary plan (aerial
rescue support, temporary mobility constructions, etc.)
- PC10. apprise external agencies, government authorities and prepare for stand-by reinforcement
- PC11. prepare evacuation plans based on weather forecast and risk assessment
- PC12. establish mobile/temporary medical facility for rescued individuals till further help arrives
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. principles of planning
- KU2. cause and types of flood and other aquatic emergencies
- KU3. aquatic rescue guidelines
- KU4. damage assessment methods

#### SPF/N1146: Manage  search and rescue operations
Description
This unit is about understanding how to manage search and rescue operations
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Navigate in an aquatic environment
Undertake inland flood-boat rescue operations
Support aerial rescue operation
Complete rescue operation
Work effectively with others
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Navigate in an aquatic environment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure the first unit to enter aquatic environment uses personal protective gears and
tethered to a point
- PC2. undertake hazard identification drill such as sink holes, drains, deep gutters, etc. and mark
them as unsafe zones
- PC3. inspect and identify any floating or submerged hazards in the water like sharp objects, logs,
etc.
- PC4. identify submerged powerlines or current leaks and initiate immediate disconnection from its
source
- PC5. identify water contamination and exposure to any toxic chemicals
- PC6. apply sandbagging, water diversion techniques to facilitate on foot navigation
- PC7. create nearby ramps or launching points
Undertake inland flood-boat operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. conduct flood-boat pre-launch checks in accordance with the operational standards
- PC9. ensure adequate rescue equipment like life jackets, throw-bags, inflatable tubes, poles, rope,
etc. are loaded on the boat
- PC10. ensure other essential equipment like public address equipment, binocular, torch, etc. are
within reach
- PC11. guide helmsman to follow planned route or course
- PC12. use GPS equipment to monitor the positions of deployed flood-boats
- PC13. coordinate with deployed flood-boats and call for withdraw/ change of course in case of no
sightings of victims
- PC14. report and prepare medical team for en-route and arrival of survivors or casualties, if any
- PC15. ensure there are no signs of any harassment during the rescue operations

#### SPF/N1122: Maintain health and safety standards
Description
This unit is about understanding how to observe and supervise the clients during workout sessions
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain hygiene and sanitation
Maintain safety
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain hygiene and sanitation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure personal hygiene
- PC2. ensure equipment, gym area, restrooms etc. are sanitized before and after the usage
- PC3. guide others about hygiene and sanitation workplace requirements
- PC4. check availability of running water, hand wash and alcohol-based sanitizers
- PC5. ensure everyone (self, clients, assistants etc.) clean hands with soap or alcohol-based
sanitizer, before and after the workout
- PC6. ensure that clients who are ill do not attend the workout session
- PC7. conduct routine hygiene and sanitation checks of gym area and equipment
- PC8. report advanced hygiene and sanitation issues to appropriate authority
Maintain safety
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. advise clients of the facility's emergency procedures
- PC10. ensure clients adhere to safety guidelines
- PC11. provide first aid for minor injuries and refer severe injuries to qualified medical practitioner
- PC12. perform Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR) when required
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. emergency response procedures
- KU2. how to assess physical injuries
- KU3. administration of basic first aid including CPR
- KU4. anatomy and physiology
- KU5. the effect of various exercises according to physical characteristics of a person
