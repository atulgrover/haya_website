MEP/Q5102: Consultant - Chartered Tax Practitioner
Brief Job Description
A Consultant - Chartered Tax Practitioner - is the person who prepares clients tax returns in accordance
direct & indirect tax regulations for the time being. The consultant also helps in tax planning, fulfills other
legal compliances of MSMEs and assists in maintaining books of accounts and records and documents. This
Professional qualification leads to the membership of the Institute of Chartered Tax Practitioners India.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be well organized and possess strong interpersonal skills with the ability to work in
coordination with different stakeholders. The person should also have excellent verbal and written
communication skills. Must act ethically and should possess skills such as decision making, problem
solving, critical thinking, working in teams or leading teams.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

MEP/N5122: Manage Payroll Processing and Statutory Compliance for
MSMEs
Description
This unit defines the skills and knowledge required for an accountant or compliance advisor to manage
payroll, compute deductions (PF, ESI, TDS, PT), and ensure routine statutory compliance for various
business entities, particularly MSMEs.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Payroll Calculation and Record Management
Core Statutory Filings (PF, ESI, TDS)
Multi-State Professional Tax (PT) Compliance
General Compliance, Dispute Resolution & Technology Usage
Elements and Performance Criteria
Payroll Processing and filling of returns
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Prepare a monthly salary sheet showing all earnings and accurate deductions for PF, ESI,
TDS, and PT.
PC2. Pass necessary salary journals (expenses and liabilities) in the books of accounts at month-
end.
PC3. Generate monthly salary slips to all employees.
PC4. Verify new joining and leaving employee data to ensure correct enrolment/removal from
statutory schemes.
PC5. Maintain the statutory Salary Muster/Register in the legally required format
Core Statutory Filings (PF, ESI, TDS)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Compute the correct employee and employer contributions for PF and ESI based on current
legal rates and wage ceilings.
PC7. Deposit the collected statutory amounts using the correct challans (PF, ESI, TDS) before the
monthly due dates.
PC8. File the monthly Electronic Challan cum Return (ECR) for PF on the EPFO portal.
PC9. File the half-yearly ESIC returns on the ESIC portal as per schedule.
PC10. File the quarterly TDS statements (Form 24Q) and generate annual Form 16 for employees.
Multi-State Professional Tax (PT) Compliance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Explain the constitutional backing (Article 276) and identify state-wise differences in PT
liability and rules.

returns (ECR, Form 24Q, PT Returns) using e-governance portals.
KU4. Understanding how to identify and map the complete statutory compliance list applicable to
different business entities (e.g., Company, LLP, Proprietorship).
KU5. Knowledge of maintaining prescribed statutory registers (Muster, PT Register) and preparing
documentation for audit/review by legal professionals.

MEP/N5121: Handle GST Compliance, Litigation & Consultancy
Description
This unit defines the skills and knowledge required for a Consultant / GST Tax Practitioner to handle Goods
and Services Tax (GST) compliance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
GST Registration and Periodic Return Preparation
Return Filing, Reconciliation, and Annual Compliance
Advanced GST Provisions (Imports, Exports, and Exemptions)
GST Litigation and Advisory Services
Elements and Performance Criteria
GST Registration and Periodic Return Preparation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify the correct category (e.g., normal, composite, non-resident) and legal threshold for
compulsory GST registration.
PC2. Collect and prepare all necessary documents and file the appropriate GST registration form
(REG-01, etc.).
PC3. Identify the correct return form (GSTR-1/3B/4/9) and filing frequency based on the client's
business type and turnover.
PC4. Gather and categorize all periodic financial data, including RCM, Exempt, Inter/Intra-State
sales, and Purchases.
PC5. Prepare the final outward and inward supply statements for filing the monthly/quarterly
returns.
Return Filing, Reconciliation, and Annual Compliance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Verify sales data against E-Invoices and E-Way bills to ensure 100% accuracy in the return
data.
PC7. Reconcile the Computed Input Tax Credit (ITC) with the auto-populated data on the GST
portal (GSTR-2B) to find mismatches.
PC8. Post the final data using the GST portal or offline utility, pay the final tax liability, and submit
the return.
PC9. Complete the annual reconciliation and file the GSTR-9 (Annual Return) and GSTR-9C
(Reconciliation Statement) correctly.
Advanced GST Provisions (Imports, Exports, and Exemptions)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. Differentiate between the two types of zero-rated supplies (Export with IGST/Refund vs.
Export under LUT without IGST).

MEP/N5140: Manage Customs and Foreign Trade Compliance and
Documentation
Description
This unit covers the skills and knowledge required to manage end-to-end documentation and compliance
for import/export activities.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare application for IEC code under customs
Manage Export/Import Documentation and Duties
Prepare Submissions and Responses to Notices
File Appeal, Revision, and Advanced Ruling Applications
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare application for IEC code under customs
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Gather all required entity and ownership data necessary for filing the Import Export Code
(IEC) application.
PC2. Navigate the DGFT portal and accurately fill out the online IEC application form.
PC3. Execute the payment of required statutory fees and submit the completed IEC form on the
portal.
Manage Export/Import Documentation and Duties
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Collect and verify all necessary export data and supporting documentation.
PC5. Compute the export duty amount eligible for drawbacks or refund under applicable customs
and Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) schemes.
PC6. File the drawback or refund application with the customs authority, attaching verified
evidence of export.
PC7. Gather detailed import order data, including product classification and value.
PC8. Calculate the total Import Duty payable, applying relevant customs tariffs, including duties
on declared baggage.
Prepare Submissions and Responses to Notices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Accept the assignment from the client and file the necessary authority letter with the
regulatory body concerned.
PC10. Analyze the statutory notice or order to identify the core issues, allegations, and specific
requirements for response.
PC11. Extract and reconcile all required financial, transactional, and computation data from
company records to support the submission.

MEP/N5141: International Tax Compliance, Transfer Pricing, and DTAA
Application
Description
This unit covers the skills and knowledge required to manage compliance under International Taxation,
Transfer Pricing (TP), and Non-Resident Indian (NRI) provisions of the Income-tax Act
Scope
The scope covers the following :
International Taxation, Transfer Pricing and NRI Compliance
Elements and Performance Criteria
International Taxation, Transfer Pricing and NRI Compliance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Analyze residential status to determine the global income taxability under Sections 5 and 6
and identify the taxability of non-resident income earned by non-residents and foreign
companies including interest, royalty, FTS, capital gains.
PC2. Interpret and apply Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) provisions (Sec 90/90A),
distinguishing between exemption and tax credit methods.
PC3. Explain the concept and tax implications of Permanent Establishment (PE) as per OECD/UN
Models.
PC4. Apply TDS provisions (Sec 195) on non-resident payments, including Forms 15CA/15CB.
PC5. Compute the Arm’s Length Price (ALP) using prescribed methods and prepare required
documentation for Transfer Pricing (TP) compliance (Sec 92C/92D).
PC6. Identify international transactions and Associated Enterprises (AEs) under Sec 92A/92B, and
prepare advanced transfer Pricing (TP) reports, including Country-by-Country Reporting
(CbCR) for international transactions and specified domestic transactions
PC7. Evaluate scenarios to identify impermissible avoidance arrangements under GAAR.
PC8. Explain the impact of BEPS/MLI, and the functioning and purpose of Advance Pricing
Agreements (APA), Safe Harbour Rules, and Mutual Agreement Procedures (MAP) under
Indian law.
PC9. Assess the tax treatment, return filing obligations, and DTAA relief for NRIs, including foreign
asset disclosure requirements.
PC10. Analyze and resolve complex, real-life cross-border tax scenarios by integrating treaty
interpretation, compliance norms, and anti-abuse measures.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Know the statutory provisions (Sec 5, 6, 9, 195) for taxability of non-residents and the
procedural compliance for TDS (Forms 15CA/15CB).
