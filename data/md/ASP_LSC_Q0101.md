ASP/LSC/Q0101: Supply Chain and Logistics Analyst
Brief Job Description
This course prepares individuals to efficiently manage logistics operations, focusing on inventory control,
transportation, and distribution. It covers essential concepts like supply chain workflows, performance
metrics, and compliance requirements, combining theoretical and practical knowledge. Learners will
acquire mid-level skills, including problem-solving, process optimization, and use of technology in logistics.
The program aligns with NSQF Level 5, enabling participants to pursue careers in warehousing,
procurement, and distribution management.
Personal Attributes
The course requires learners to have strong analytical ability, effective communication skills, problem-
solving mindset, attention to detail, teamwork capability, adaptability, and a proactive approach to
handling operational challenges.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. ASP/LSC/N0101: Foundations of Digital Supply Chain Operations
- 2. ASP/LSC/N0102: Integrated Storage Solutions and Inventory Dynamics

ASP/LSC/N0101: Foundations of Digital Supply Chain Operations
Description
Introduces learners to the fundamental principles of logistics and supply chain management, emphasizing
their critical role in global trade and economic development. Participants will explore key components of
the supply chain, including sourcing, production, warehousing, distribution, and customer delivery, gaining
a comprehensive understanding of how each element contributes to supply chain efficiency.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Learners will be able to describe the foundational concepts of logistics and supply chains, including
their significance and role in supporting global trade and economic development.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Ability to describe key logistics and supply chain concepts.
PC2. • Proficiency in identifying supply chain components and their
• interdependencies.
PC3. • Demonstration of material, information, and financial flow
• within supply chains.
PC4. • Application of supply chain models (lean, agile, hybrid) to
• scenarios.
PC5. • Calculation of KPIs like lead time, order accuracy, and
• inventory turnover

ASP/LSC/N0102: Integrated Storage Solutions and Inventory Dynamics
Description
Warehouse management and logistics define the key competencies required for efficient warehouse
operations, inventory control, and material handling.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
These standards cover warehouse layout design, inventory categorization (ABC analysis, FIFO, LIFO,
JIT), and the use of Warehouse Management Systems (WMS) for real-time inventory tracking.
Additionally, NOS includes advanced material handling techniques involving forklifts, conveyors, and
automation tools like RFID and IoT. Cold chain logistics is also addressed, ensuring compliance with
temperature-controlled storage and transportation protocols for perishable goods.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Design and layout optimization for warehouses.
PC2. Application of inventory control techniques (ABC analysis, JIT, FIFO, LIFO).
PC3. Operation and maintenance of material handling equipment.
PC4. Proficiency in using Warehouse Management Systems (WMS) for tracking and inventory
updates

ASP/LSC/N0103: Optimized Transport Networks and Delivery Systems
Description
A Digital Supply Chain and Logistics Analyst ensures the efficient movement of goods by selecting optimal
transportation modes, designing effective distribution networks, optimizing last-mile delivery, ensuring
compliance with global trade regulations, and leveraging digital technologies for real-time logistics
management. The role requires expertise in supply chain analytics, cost optimization, and regulatory
adherence while integrating advanced digital tools to improve logistics operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS defines the knowledge and skills required for professionals managing modern supply chain
logistics. It covers transportation planning, distribution network design, last-mile and reverse
logistics, regulatory compliance, quick commerce, and emerging technologies like AI, IoT, and
blockchain to enhance efficiency and transparency.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Selection of appropriate transportation modes based on cost and demand.
PC2. Route optimization and planning using software tools.
PC3. Design of efficient distribution networks and last-mile delivery strategies.
PC4. Compliance with transportation regulations and logistics standards

ASP/LSC/N0106: Logistics client relations and service excellence
Description
This NOS focuses on equipping professionals with CRM competencies tailored for logistics operations. It
provides insights into customer engagement, expectation alignment, and service delivery improvements
using CRM tools. Learners will develop skills in handling customer interactions, analyzing feedback, and
leveraging CRM software to track service performance. Additionally, it covers retention strategies and
loyalty programs to strengthen customer relationships and drive business success in logistics.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the principles and application of Customer Relationship Management (CRM) in
logistics, emphasizing customer satisfaction, expectation management, CRM software tools, and
customer retention strategies. It includes foundational CRM concepts, the use of technology in
customer interactions, service quality measurement, and designing loyalty programs to enhance
long-term business growth.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understanding and managing CRM tools.
PC2. Ensuring customer satisfaction through efficient problemsolving and conflict resolution
PC3. Implementation of customer retention and loyalty programs.
PC4. Data management and analytics in CRM to improve service quality

Description
This NOS equips professionals with the skills and knowledge necessary to manage e-commerce logistics
efficiently while implementing sustainable logistics practices. It covers key topics such as fulfillment
strategies, last-mile delivery optimization, customer satisfaction, and the role of technology in streamlining
logistics operations. Learners will develop expertise in leveraging route optimization tools, warehouse
management systems, and real-time tracking technologies to enhance service quality. Additionally, they
will explore green logistics initiatives, including carbon footprint reduction, eco-friendly transportation, and
sustainable packaging solutions, to align logistics operations with environmental sustainability goals.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS focuses on the specialized logistics functions required for e-commerce and sustainable
logistics practices. It covers order fulfillment, last-mile delivery strategies, customer experience
management, and the integration of technology in logistics operations. Additionally, it emphasizes
the principles of green logistics, ensuring environmentally responsible and efficient supply chain
management
Elements and Performance Criteria

PC2. Use of technology in logistics, such as drones and autonomous vehicles.

Description
This NOS equips professionals with the knowledge and skills required to implement sustainable logistics
and supply chain practices. It covers key areas such as circular economy principles, waste reduction
strategies, ethical sourcing, and green transportation initiatives. Learners will gain expertise in using
sustainability metrics, optimizing warehouse energy efficiency, and complying with environmental
regulations like ISO 14001. Through practical applications, including carbon footprint analysis, route
optimization, and sustainable packaging solutions, this NOS enables logistics professionals to drive
environmental responsibility while maintaining cost-effectiveness and service quality in supply chain
operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS focuses on integrating sustainability into supply chain and logistics operations by adopting
eco-friendly practices, regulatory compliance, and technological advancements. It covers
sustainable supply chain management, green warehousing, eco-friendly transportation, and
measurement of sustainability metrics to minimize environmental impact while enhancing
operational efficiency.

| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
