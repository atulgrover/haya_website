#### PCS/Q5108: General Industrial (Liquid) Painter
Brief Job Description
The individual at work assesses the substrate to be coated, understands colour, chemistry and finish
required, prepares the substrate for liquid paint application and coats it using recommended tools and
equipment, to achieve the desired finish as per company standards and requirement.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to undertake physical labour, work in a paint heavy environment, have
good/ corrected eye sight with no colour blindness, and work in all types of weather conditions.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PCS/N5105: Prepare for coating
2. PCS/N5106: Pre-treat the component to be coated
3. PCS/N5107: Apply topcoat and finish the surface.
4. PCS/N5108: Maintain jigs and parts
5. PCS/N9901: Co-ordinate with colleagues and customers
6. PCS/N9902: Maintain standards of product/ service quality
7. PCS/N9903: Maintain OH&S standards and follow environmental norms
8. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### PCS/N5105: Prepare  for coating
Description
This OS unit is about planning and preparing the component for pre-treatment and coating
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan the work
Prepare the surface and materials for coating
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Plan the work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. receive work instructions, review and plan to meet the quality requirements
- PC2. inspect the work area, assess hazards and risks and apply controls
- PC3. check the work site for adequate ventilation and implement steps to prevent fires and
explosions
- PC4. check the tools, equipment and jigs/ hangers are suitable and ready for use
Prepare the surface and materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. inspect the condition of the substrate surface visually
- PC6. mask the areas not required to be coated with masking tapes/ plugs
- PC7. implement measures to maintain the painting area dust free
- PC8. mix the paint/ coating materials, diluents as per instructions, to achieve desired quality
- PC9. stir the paint/ coating thoroughly and maintain under constant stirring using suitable
equipment
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. company's policy and work instructions on quality standards
- KU2. importance of the individual's role in the workflow
- KU3. reporting structure of the organization
- KU4. occupational health and safety standards
- KU5. various paints/ coatings for different substrates
- KU6. masking of areas not required to be painted/ coated
- KU7. significance of following the SOP for surface preparation, paint/ coating preparation and
application
- KU8. personal protection including the use of safety gears and equipment

#### PCS/N5106: Pre-treat the component to be coated
Description
This OS unit is about pre-treatment of the components before coating.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Clean the surface
Pre-treat for coating
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Clean the surface
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. inspect and assess the nature and quantity of contaminants to be removed before painting
- PC2. decide on the method to be adopted for cleaning the substrate e.g.-mechanical or chemical
cleaning.
Pretreat for coating
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. prepare and pretreat the substrate as per SOP and instructions received
- PC4. prepare and pretreat the previously coated substrate by removing unwanted coating and
loose films
- PC5. set up the mechanical pretreatment tools and equipment as per SOP and manufacturer's
instruction manual
- PC6. set up the chemical pretreat process and parameters as per SOP and suppliers
recommendation
- PC7. check the quality of the surface preparation as per SOP
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. company's policy and work instructions on quality standards
- KU2. importance of the individual's role in the workflow
- KU3. reporting structure of the organization
- KU4. occupational health and safety standards
- KU5. pretreatment processes for different substrates
- KU6. masking of areas not required to be coated
- KU7. SOP for pretreatment
- KU8. common defects after pretreatment
- KU9. significance of following SOP/ manufacturer's instructions for pretreatment

#### PCS/N5107: Apply topcoat and finish the surface.
Description
This OS unit is about preparation of the liquid paint, application and finishing the surface.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare the equipment and paint for application
Apply the paint, dry/ bake/ cure and finish the surface
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare the equipment and paint for application
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. set up the application system, as recommended by the manufacturer, for the type of paint and
finish quality required
- PC2. conduct pre-operational tests before coating to achieve the desired quality
Apply the paint, dry/ bake/ cure and finish the surface
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. operate the application equipment as recommended by the equipment supplier
- PC4. apply the paint/ coating to achieve the desired colour and opacity
- PC5. dry/ cure/ bake the applied coating as per SOP
- PC6. identify defects in the coating, in an inline quality check
- PC7. take corrective measures to meet the quality requirement, either by rubbing/ polishing or re-
painting
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. company's policy and work instructions on quality standards
- KU2. importance of the individual's role in the workflow
- KU3. reporting structure of the organization
- KU4. occupational health and safety standards
- KU5. preparation of the paint for application as recommended by the manufacturer
- KU6. application methods for different products and/ or surfaces
- KU7. masking of areas not required to be coated
- KU8. SOP for paint application
- KU9. common application defects
- KU10. significance of following SOP/ manufacturer's recommendations for paint/ coating application

#### PCS/N9901: Co-ordinate with colleagues and customers
Description
This OS unit is about communicating effectively with superiors, colleagues and customers, for achieving a
smooth workflow.

The scope covers the following :
Coordinate with supervisors and colleagues
Address customer complaints and grievances
Follow inclusive practices at work
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Coordinate with supervisors and colleagues
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. collect job order from the supervisor
- PC2. establish the work output requirements, quality standards, delivery schedule, targets,
performance indicators and incentive policies
