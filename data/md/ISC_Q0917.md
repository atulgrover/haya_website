#### ISC/Q0917: Scaffolder - (Metal Sector)
Brief Job Description
The job is all about erecting of scaffolding structures safely according to needs to fitter, welder etc. for
working at heights or depths as required.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to work independently as well as in teams. He should be physically fit, not
having colour blindness, having analytical skills, problem solving attitude, high concentration levels and
willingness to work in a factory environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ISC/N0008: Use basic health and safety practices at the workplace
2. ISC/N0009: Work effectively with others
3. ISC/N0956: Understands the given job of Scaffolder
4. ISC/N0957: Carry out scaffolding operations
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### ISC/N0008: Use basic health and safety practices at the workplace
Description
This OS unit is about following safety and adopting sustainable practices for optimising use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain safe and secure working environment
Emergencies, rescue and first aid procedures
Health and hygiene
Housekeeping and waste management
Material and energy conservation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain safe and secure working environment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify hazardous activities and the possible causes of risks or accidents in the workplace
- PC2. follow safe working practices while dealing with hazards to ensure safety of self and others
- PC3. use appropriate protective clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work
- PC4. follow appropriate safety practices while working in and around trenches, elevated places
and confined areas
- PC5. lift heavy objects safely using correct procedures
- PC6. carry out routine check of the machine for identifying potential hazards
- PC7. report any identified breaches in health, safety and security policies and procedures to the
designated person
Emergencies, rescue and first aid procedures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. use appropriate type of fire extinguisher
- PC9. apply appropriate rescue techniques during fire hazard
- PC10. provide appropriate first aid procedure to victims wherever required eg.in case of bleeding,
burns, choking, electric shock etc.
- PC11. follow emergency procedures such as raising alarm, safe evacuation etc.
- PC12. attend safety training and fire drills to respond promptly during an emergency
Health and hygiene
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. follow regular cleaning and disinfection practices at work place using appropriate techniques
and materials
- PC14. follow hand hygiene practices at work place using appropriate techniques and materials
- PC15. report regarding the contagious illness of self or people in close contact
- PC16. avoid contact with ill people and self-isolate in a similar situation

#### ISC/N0009: Work effectively with others
Description
This OS unit is about communicating with colleagues/superiors and others, either in own work group or in
other work groups within organisation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communicate effectively with colleagues and others
Interact with supervisor
Follow appropriate behaviour at work place
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Communicate effectively with colleagues and others
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. coordinate with colleagues to share work, as per the workload in order to achieve team goals
- PC2. maintain clear communication with colleagues and others, wherever needed, through all
means i.e. face-to-face, telephonic or written
- PC3. adjust communication styles to reflect gender and persons with disability (PwD) sensitivity
- PC4. respect all colleagues and co-workers
- PC5. resolve conflicts by communicating with colleagues and other departments
Interact with supervisor
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. identify work requirements by receiving instructions from reporting supervisor
- PC7. escalate problems to supervisors that cannot be handled
- PC8. report the completed work
- PC9. interact with the reporting supervisor about any possible hazards and safety concerns
Follow appropriate behaviour at work place
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. extend help to people with Disability (PwD) at workplace, if required
- PC11. empathize with people with disability
- PC12. adopt a gender neutral behavior
- PC13. adopt responsible and disciplined behaviours at the workplace
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the importance of effective communication and establishing good working relationships with
colleagues and supervisor

#### ISC/N0956: Understands the given job of Scaffolder
Description
This unit is about understanding the requirements of the scaffolding job after the receiving the checklist
from the supervisor, seek any clarifications on the requirements of the job and identify the tools and
tackles that would be needed to carry out the scaffolding job.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding the assigned scaffolding job in accordance with the instructions / checklist
Identify the tools and tackles that are required to carry out the assigned scaffolding job
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding the assigned scaffolding job in accordance with the instructions / checklist

- PC1. Identify if there is a need to setup a scaffold
- PC2. Interpret the checklist and understand the scaffolding job requirements

- PC7. Identify various planks as per set standard and requirement

spanner, podger hammer, Adjustable wrenches, Spirit level, Tape measures or rules,

#### ISC/N0957: Carry out scaffolding operations
Description
This unit is about carrying out the operations that are specific to a Scaffolder.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify positions for setting up the scaffold
Checks before and during erection of scaffolding
Erect scaffolding structures
Checks during the use of scaffold
Dismantling the scaffold structure post completion of job
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify positions for setting up the scaffold
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify where to erect structures for scaffolding according to the needs of the welder, fitter
or other team member
- PC2. Identify which position will be appropriate to set up scaffold
- PC3. Check power lines or conductors in close proximity to the position of the intended scaffold
- PC4. Ensure safety measures are maintained
- PC5. Identify any defects and carry out emergency damage control
Checks before and during erection of scaffolding
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. • Check the following points before and during erection of scaffolding:
• • Ensure that all necessary permits and instructions or information relating to the job prior to
commencing erection are available
• • Inform concerned authority on site before commencing erection
• • Examine the building or structure against which scaffolding is carried out and in case of doubt
about any aspect of the job.
Erect scaffolding structures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Identify and select the required scaffolding equipment and place it near the work area
- PC8. Place suitable sills and ensure there are no holes and foreign objects under the sills and the
ground is level
- PC9. Ensure cross-bracing to prevent the staging swaying back and forth
- PC10. Ensure next level frames are tightly locked in the lower-level staging
- PC11. Plan for the weight that the planks will have to carry
- PC12. Identify and use appropriate clamp pipes and other tools to erect structures for scaffolds
- PC13. Ensure safety measures are followed
- PC14. Identify any deviations and inform supervisor if the problem needs to be escalated
NSQC Approved || Indian Iron and Steel Sector Skill Council         21

Checks during the use of scaffolding
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC15. • Check the following during the use of scaffolds:
• • The frames are correctly aligned and properly supported at their bases
• • There is no undue deflection in ledgers and transoms or putlogs
• • No essential member of the structure has been removed
• • All ties and braces are in place and are effective in stabilising the structure
• • All couplers are tightened properly
• • All scaffold planks are sound and are properly supported
Dismantling the scaffold structure post completion of job
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC16. Examine and check before dismantling the scaffold that all ties and bracings are effectively
in position
- PC17. Suitable warning notices must be displayed for safety at the work site
- PC18. Ensure dismantling progressively from the top level downwards
- PC19. Remove ties, braces, ledgers, transoms, planks and guardrails lift by lift with standards
following as joint positions are reached
- PC20. Avoid mishandling of scaffold materials and stack properly
- PC21. Ensure batch wise tag marking of the scaffold materials for storing purpose
- PC22. Ensure that all scaffolding materials are removed from the work site and no loose materials
are left over
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Quality and damage checks to be done and importance of the same
- KU2. Risk and impact of not following defined procedures/work instructions
- KU3. Escalation matrix for reporting identified issues
- KU4. Understand scaffolding job in order to identify associated hazards
- KU5. Design the scaffold based on the nature of job and load estimation
- KU6. Understand building or dismantling the scaffolding
- KU7. Understand the quantity of material required for the scaffolding
- KU8. Understand the quality of scaffolding as per certification standard
- KU9. Conversant with fastening practices
- KU10. Knowledge of proper specifications of jacks, de shackles, clits, pulleys, chain block, lifting
