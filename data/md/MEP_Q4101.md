#### MEP/Q4101: Pre School and Day Care Facilitator
Brief Job Description
Pre School and Day Care Facilitator is responsible for the education ,care and holistic development of
children 3-6 years old. This is done in a variety of settings, including pre-schools and day care institutions.
They use various early child care and education methodologies and techniques for the purpose. This
person should also maintain necessary records and registers.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be well groomed, attentive, be able to multi-task, exhibit attention to detail, respect
confidentiality, have a positive attitude and should be dependable. The person should have strong
interpersonal skills and ability to communicate to all stakeholders at the pace and knowledge level of the
other person
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MEP/N4101: Ensure child welfare and safety
2. MEP/N4102: Provide learning experiences for holistic development in children
3. MEP/N4103: Collaborate with parents/family in childs learning

5. MEP/N9914: Communicating with clients, visitors and colleagues effectively
6. MEP/N9903: Apply health and safety practices at the workplace
7. MEP/N9912: Apply principles of professional practice at the workplace

#### MEP/N4101: Ensure child welfare and safety
Description
This unit is about the competencies required to ensure health, hygiene, nutrition, security and safety of
children at the centre during normal conditions and in any emergency
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Ensure health, hygiene, safety and security of children,
Ensure gender mainstreaming and inclusivity,
Protect child rights
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Ensure health, hygiene, safety and security of children
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. obtain information of children’ medical history, allergies and specific ailments
- PC2. maintain health and vaccination records of children
- PC3. ensure that the centre environment meets health standards at all times
- PC4. report to authorities if sanitation facilities are not in place
- PC5. oversee personal care factors that affect children’s health and wellbeing
- PC6. ensure that nutrition and hydration need of children are met
- PC7. plan balanced meals, snacks and drinks for children
- PC8. follow hygiene practices like hand washing and clean eating area
- PC9. release children only to parents/guardians or duly authorized persons
- PC10. ensure that centre is secure, safe and properly monitored/guarded
- PC11. allow only authorized persons to enter the centre
- PC12. keep all children audible and within sight
- PC13. work with children to ensure they have strategies to protect themselves and make decisions
about safety
- PC14. support an evacuation of premises during emergency, ensuring the safety of children, adults
and visitors
- PC15. follow procedures for recording and reporting accidents, incidents, injuries, signs of illness
and other emergencies
- PC16. keep children engaged till the authorized persons are available for picking them up
- PC17. support children to explore and manage risk and challenges
- PC18. follow procedures for infection control in an early year setting
- PC19. teach children about hygiene practices and disposal of waste efficiently
Ensure gender mainstreaming and inclusivity

#### MEP/N4102: Provide learning experiences for holistic development in
children
Description
This unit is about fostering development of children by providing a balanced mix of developmentally
appropriate activities in the program, using contextual tools, providing experiences and opportunities to
explore and learn
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develop respectful relationship with children,
Enhance physical, social and emotional development in children
Organize and manage special days and outings
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Develop respectful relationship with children
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. respond to the varying abilities and confidence of children in a sensitive and appropriate
manner
- PC2. model positive behaviours expected of children
- PC3. identify potential triggers or factors that could lead to changes in behaviour
- PC4. use safeguards while responding to negative or disruptive behaviour
- PC5. use strategies to support children to recognise expectations about their behaviour
- PC6. support children’s creativity, creative experiences and imaginative play
- PC7. refer children with special needs to appropriate services
Enhance physical, social and emotional development in children
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. encourage opportunities to express feelings and emotions appropriately and interact with the
child in a calm and reassuring manner whenever required
- PC9. encourage children to talk and share their stories and ideas
- PC10. encourage children to reinforce positive behaviour
- PC11. record instances of behavior in line with work setting requirements
- PC12. use strategies that empower children to make positive and informed choices that support
their behaviour and well-being
- PC13. promote group learning and socialization
- PC14. observe families and children for signs of stress/distress
- PC15. use consistent support and responses to incidences of unacceptable behavior
- PC16. organize and facilitate open-ended and child-initiated activity to encourage independence
and self-direction
- PC17. facilitate activities to teach about choice and decision making

#### MEP/N4104: Assess learning progress of children
Description
This unit is about using different means of assessing and evaluating children in early year settings and
tracking their development
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct and interpret assessment,
Maintain records
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct and interpret assessment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the purpose of the observation and assessment being undertaken
- PC2. select observation and assessment methods or tools for purpose identified
- PC3. use observation for assessment of children in early years sector
- PC4. assess child’s interactions with other children and interactions with teachers and elders
- PC5. gauge physical and mental development of children using appropriate methods
