#### SGJ/Q4901: Solar Manufacturing Junior Technician
Brief Job Description
Junior Technician Solar Manufacturing is responsible for basic preparation, handling and supply of
materials for solar manufacturing. The job holder must adhere to all safety procedures and practice good
housekeeping, safe operation of specific equipment, material or processes. This is an entry level position
for solar manufacturing
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it without any accidents so
diligence and hardworking are desired attributes for individuals performing this role. He/She must also
demonstrate strong work ethics, an ability to communicate courteously with co-workers and must be good
with following instructions of the supervisor
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SGJ/N4019: Explain and identify key components of Solar PV system
2. SGJ/N4020: Identify Different semi-conductor materials and types of solar cell
3. SGJ/N4021: Describe the Basics functions of Solar rooftop plant
4. SGJ/N4022: Maintain Personal Health & Safety for solar manufacturing
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### SGJ/N4019: Explain and identify key components of Solar PV system
Description
This unit is about explain and identify different Renewable Energy Sources and its application
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify key components of various renewable energy system
Analyze important parameters of sunlight for Solar PV applications
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify key components of various renewable energy system
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. discuss the Properties of renewable energy resources and its significance in power
generation and illustrate the working of various renewable energy technology various charts and models
- PC2. discuss the various Renewable energy system and its components and show the key
components of the renewable energy system and outline their functions through plant schematic
- PC3. discuss the selection of Renewable energy resources as per the suitable site locations
- PC4. discuss the key components of wind turbine generator
- PC5. discuss the key components of small hydro plant and bio gasifier
- PC6. illustrate renewable energy technology through quiz and puzzles
Analyze important parameters of sunlight for Solar PV applications.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. discuss the properties of Sun light
- PC8. discuss the Air Mass, Motion of the Sun
- PC9. discuss the declination Angle, Elevation Angle Azimuth Angle and show the formation
azimuth angle, declination angle
- PC10. discuss about the sun's position
- PC11. discuss about the Solar Radiation on a Tilted Surface
- PC12. discuss the impact of temperature, GHI and DNI on power generation from solar PV and
demonstrate the effect of temperature on Solar PV output
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

#### SGJ/N4020: Identify Different semi-conductor materials and types of solar
cell
Description
This unit is about to Identify different semi-conductor materials used in making solar cell.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify different semi-conductor materials used in making solar cells
Identify different type of Silicon solar cells and their properties for solar manufacturing
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify different semi-conductor materials used in making solar cells
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. discuss the brief introduction to semiconductor material and its properties
- PC2. discuss the formation of p-n junction and demonstrate p-n junction formation by various
means
- PC3. discuss the various types of semiconductor material used in making solar cell
- PC4. discuss the use of Gallium arsenide (GaAs), amorphous silicon and Cadmium Telluride (CdTe)
Solar cell
- PC5. discuss the properties of, GaAs, a-Si, CdTe, etc
- PC6. show how to identify various elements used in solar cells from the periodic table
- PC7. show the fabrication structure of solar cell and solar module
- PC8. show generation of electricity from a solar cell by exposing it to light
- PC9. show the effect of shadow on electricity generation from solar cell
Identify different type of Silicon solar cells and their properties for solar manufacturing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. discuss the various silicon based solar cell and show the working of solar cell through model
and chart
- PC11. discuss the type of silicon solar cell
- PC12. discuss the multicrystaline and monocrystalline solar cell
- PC13. discuss the properties of silicon
- PC14. discuss in detail the working of silicon based solar module and show them silicon solar cell
components and solar panel
- PC15. discuss the advantage of silicon based solar cell
- PC16. discuss the efficiency of silicon solar cell
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each Element/
#### Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will also lay
down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
- PC2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
- PC3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- PC4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
- PC5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
- PC6. To pass the assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass % aggregate for the Qualification.
- PC7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification.

### Page 31

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |

In the context of the OS, these include communication related skills that are applicable to most job roles. |
| Electives | Electives are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as contributive to specialization in a job role. There may be multiple electives within a QP for each specialized job role. Trainees must select at least one elective for the successful completion of a QP with Electives. |
| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
