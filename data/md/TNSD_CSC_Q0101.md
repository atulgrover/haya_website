TNSD/CSC/Q0101: Tool & Die Bench Fitter
Brief Job Description
A Tool & Die Bench Fitter assists in the manufacturing, assembly, and maintenance of dies and moulds,
ensuring precision and adherence to specifications. This role involves practical workshop skills, basic
engineering drawing, measurement, and an understanding of mould technology.
Personal Attributes
Upon successful completion, learners will be qualified for employment in engineering, manufacturing,
machine tools, automobile, and general machinery industries, primarily assisting in the production and
maintenance of tools and dies.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. TNSD/CSC/N0101: Perform Basic Manufacturing Processes
- 2. TNSD/CSC/N0102: Execute Engineering Measurements
- 3. TNSD/CSC/N0103: Interpret and Create Basic Engineering Drawings
- 4. TNSD/CSC/N0104: Comprehend Basic Mould Technology
- 5. TNSD/CSC/N0105: Create 2D Drawings using AutoCAD.
- 6. TNSD/CSC/N0106: Apply Basic Technical Mathematics
- 7. TNSD/CSC/N0107: Apply Principles of Applied Science in Manufacturing
- 8. DGT/VSQ/N0104: Employability Skills (120 Hours)
- 9. TNSD/CSC/N0108: Demonstrate Computer Literacy.

TNSD/CSC/N0101: Perform Basic Manufacturing Processes
Description
Understand the different types of hand tools and machine tools used in tool room and their applications
Manufacturing of mechanical parts / simple injection mould and assembly. Understand the different types
of welding processes such as SMAW, GMAW and GTAW.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Familiarize with the properties & uses of ferrous and non-ferrous metals.
Knowledge of safety precautions which is to be observed in workshop/ and while working on
machines.
Knowledge of engineering drawing with Limits, fits and GD&T.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand different types of ferrous and non-ferrous material used in engineering practices.
PC2. Learn about properties and heat-treatment processes of materials used for engineering
purpose.
PC3. Understanding the safety aspects and PPE used in workshop
PC4. Understand different types of hand tools and bench work like filing, hacksawing, hammering,
marking, letter punching, etc.,
PC5. Understand the process of drilling and related operations like tapping, reaming, counter
sinking and counter boring.
PC6. Learn about different parts and its functions of conventional tool room machines-Drilling,
lathe, Milling and grinding .
PC7. Understand different types of operations like turning, milling, grinding performed on tool
room machines.
PC8. Understand different types of work holding devices, Tool holding devices of Drilling, lathe,
Milling and grinding.
PC9. Aware about nomenclature of cutting tool angles and re – sharpening
PC10. Perform the sequence of making right angled block and different types of fits by bench work.
PC11. Machine parts as per drawing using different machines and do assembly.
PC12. Fabricate a hand injection mould and do trial/Project
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand different types of ferrous and non-ferrous material used in engineering practices.

TNSD/CSC/N0102: Execute Engineering Measurements
Description
Understand the usage of different measuring instruments Familiarization with different checking gauges
used in tool room/manufacturing. Understand the system of limits &fits used in the manufacturing
industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the usage of different measuring tools & familiarization with different checking methods
Understand the importance of limits and fits in engineering practices.
Understand the system of GD& T
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Learn about Coordinate systems and tools for marking
PC2. Know difference between checking and measuring and relevant tools used
PC3. Understand linear ,angular and bore checking and measuring instruments
PC4. Understand limits and fits and reading of tolerance chart
PC5. Know different types of fits and its applications
PC6. Understand Hole based system and shaft based system
PC7. Learn about Interchangeability and standardization
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Know difference between checking and measuring and relevant tools used
KU2. Understand linear ,angular and bore checking and measuring instruments
KU3. Understand limits and fits and reading of tolerance chart
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Learn about Interchangeability and standardisation
GS2. Learn about Coordinate systems and tools for marking

TNSD/CSC/N0103: Interpret and Create Basic Engineering Drawings
Description
Read and understand engineering drawing so that items can be manufactured as per dimensions and
specifications mentioned. Draw Board drawings of parts which are required to be manufactured.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Ability to read engineering drawing.
Understand the different types of threads and its representation.
Ability to understand simple mechanical assemblies
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Learn the Basics of engineering drawing and free hand sketching
PC2. Draw Different geometrical constructions
PC3. Draw the types of projection, sectional views with dimensioning
PC4. Draw Orthographic views and isometric views
PC5. Draw the assembly drawing and part drawing
PC6. Draw the Development of different sections
PC7. Learn the basics of GD & T
PC8. Draw the representation of various types of joints and fasteners
PC9. Draw the representation of different threads, weld joints and rivets
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Learn the Basics of engineering drawing and free hand sketching
KU2. Learn the basics of GD & T
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Draw the types of projection, sectional views with dimensioning
GS2. Draw the Development of different sections

TNSD/CSC/N0104: Comprehend Basic Mould Technology
Description
Understand different types of plastics used for different applications like engineering, chemical, household,
defense, Automobile and medical. Familiarize the process of design and manufacture of injection moulds
to make components.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding of different plastics used in Moulding.
Familiarization of the working of Injection Mould and Moulding Machine.
Awareness of basic terminology of Injection Moulds and mould materials.
Understanding the design concept of two plate moulds.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Learn about plastics and different types of plastic materials
PC2. Understand basic injection mould and different parts
PC3. Understand feed system including runner, gate in an injection mould
PC4. Understand shrinkage, cooling and different ejection systems in moulds
PC5. Learn about different considerations for selecting the type of mould
PC6. Understand injection moulding machines and its functioning
PC7. Design and draft two plate moulds with different feed system
PC8. Design and draft two plate moulds with different injection system
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand shrinkage, cooling and different ejection systems in moulds
KU2. Learn about plastics and different types of plastic materials
KU3. Understand basic injection mould and different parts
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Design and draft two plate moulds with different feed system
GS2. Design and draft two plate moulds with different injection system

TNSD/CSC/N0106: Apply Basic Technical Mathematics
Description
Understand the concept of basic mathematics used for doing calculations related to engineering
application and general purpose.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Familiar with System of units, conversions, Simple Arithmetic operations of fractions and decimals.
Understanding the basic concepts of Ratio, proportion and mensuration.
