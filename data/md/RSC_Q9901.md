#### RSC/Q9901: Machine Operator - CNC Turning of Plastic (Lathe)
Brief Job Description
A Machine Operator CNC Turning of Plastic sets up and operates CNC turning machines to produce precision plastic components as per engineering drawings and specifications. The role involves interpreting
CNC programs, selecting tools, preparing raw materials, conducting machining operations, inspecting
output, and maintaining machines, tools, and work areas. The operator ensures safety, quality, and
productivity in line with organizational and NSQF standards.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have good hand eye coordination, attention to detail, and problem-solving ability to
perform precise machining operations. They must demonstrate discipline, responsibility, and commitment
to safety while handling machines and plastic materials. Basic numerical ability, spatial visualization, and
the capacity to follow instructions and work as part of a team are essential. Physical stamina, patience,
and willingness to learn new machining practices are also important attributes.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N9904: Interpret CNC Programs and drawings and set up CNC Turning Machine
2. RSC/N9905: Perform turning operations on Plastic Workpieces using CNC Machine
3. RSC/N9906: Maintain CNC Turning machine, tools and work area
4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### RSC/N9904: Interpret CNC Programs and drawings and set up CNC
Turning Machine
Description
This NOS unit covers interpreting CNC programs and engineering drawings and setting up CNC turning
machines for plastic components. It includes reading drawings, verifying codes, setting tools and work
offsets, conducting trial runs, and ensuring safety, quality, and machine readiness.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Interpret CNC Programs and Drawings
Set Up CNC Turning Machine to the Job
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Interpret CNC Programs and Drawings
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. read and interpret engineering drawings, job sheets, and process documents for turning
plastics
- PC2. identify dimensions, tolerances, fits, and surface finish symbols accurately
- PC3. interpret sectional views, auxiliary views, and geometric dimensioning details relevant to
machining
- PC4. extract relevant information from 2D sketches and 3D CAD models of the component
- PC5. recognize machining allowances, reference points, and datum surfaces from
drawings/models
- PC6. read ISO codes, G-codes, and M-codes used in CNC turning programs
- PC7. understand tool path sequence, cycle codes, and program structure specific to plastics
machining
- PC8. cross-check program codes against drawing dimensions and process sheet requirements
- PC9. ensure correct tool number, offsets, spindle speed, and feed values are used for plastic
components
- PC10. consider material properties of plastics (softening point, chip formation, thermal expansion)
during machining planning
- PC11. identify suitable cutting parameters to minimize deformation, melting, or surface damage
- PC12. identify errors in CNC program (e.g., missing codes, incorrect tool paths, speed/feed
mismatch)
- PC13. detect inconsistencies between drawing, program, and process sheet and escalate/report to
supervisor
- PC14. carry out minor program edits within defined scope (e.g., tool offset correction, feed/speed
adjustment)
- PC15. verify revised code through dry run or simulation before machining

#### RSC/N9905: Perform turning operations on Plastic Workpieces using CNC
Machine
Description
This NOS unit covers performing CNC turning operations on plastic workpieces, including facing, turning,
grooving, threading, drilling, boring, and parting-off while applying correct machining parameters. It
emphasizes quality, safety, and monitoring to achieve accurate dimensions and surface finish without
damaging the plastic material.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out CNC Turning Operations on Plastic Workpieces
Inspect and Ensure Quality of Turned Plastic Components
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out CNC Turning Operations on Plastic Workpieces
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. carry out facing to achieve smooth, flat surfaces on plastic workpieces
- PC2. perform straight turning and taper turning as per specified dimensions
- PC3. cut external and internal grooves accurately to drawing specifications
- PC4. perform threading, boring, drilling, and parting-off operations on plastic rods and billets
- PC5. select and apply spindle speed, feed rate, and depth of cut appropriate for plastic machining
- PC6. adjust machining parameters to prevent melting, deformation, or surface burns on plastics
- PC7. continuously observe chip formation, surface finish, and dimensional accuracy during
operations
- PC8. monitor tool wear, vibration, and machine sounds to identify abnormal conditions
- PC9. apply dry machining, compressed air, or coolant/lubricant as per plastic material
requirements
- PC10. ensure coolant/lubricant does not chemically affect the plastic workpiece
- PC11. load, clamp, and handle plastic workpieces carefully to avoid scratches, dents, or distortion
- PC12. follow safety protocols while machining, including PPE use and machine guarding
- PC13. achieve required tolerances, dimensions, and surface quality as per job sheet and drawing
- PC14. take corrective actions (offset adjustments, tool replacement) when deviations are observed
- PC15. adhere to SOPs, process sheets, and quality norms while carrying out CNC turning operations
- PC16. Maintain documentation of operations performed, adjustments made, and output produced
Inspect and Ensure Quality of Turned Plastic Components
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC17. measure dimensions of turned plastic components using calipers, micrometers, height
gauges, and bore gauges

#### RSC/N9906: Maintain CNC Turning machine, tools and work area
Description
This NOS unit covers maintaining CNC turning machines, cutting tools, and the work area to ensure safe,
efficient, and uninterrupted operations. It includes cleaning, lubrication, preventive checks, tool care, and
adherence to workplace safety and housekeeping standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain CNC Turning Machine and Tools
Maintain Work Area and Safety Compliance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain CNC Turning Machine and Tools
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. remove plastic chips, dust, and debris from machine surfaces and enclosures
- PC2. clean tool posts, work-holding fixtures, and guards to maintain accuracy and safety
- PC3. dispose of plastic waste and scrap in designated bins following organizational procedure
- PC4. check and top up lubrication oil, coolant, or compressed air systems as specified by the
manufacturer
- PC5. Perform routine preventive checks on belts, spindles, slides, and coolant pumps
- PC6. record maintenance activities in machine logbooks as per SOP
- PC7. inspect cutting tools and inserts for wear, chipping, or deformation
- PC8. replace or re-sharpen tools as per tool life standards for machining plastics
- PC9. clean and store tools, tool holders, and fixtures in designated storage to prevent damage
- PC10. check for abnormal vibrations, sounds, or heating during machine operation
- PC11. verify coolant/lubricant levels, leakage, and flow during machining
- PC12. identify and report machine faults, tool breakages, or alignment issues to the supervisor
- PC13. set tools and accessories in proper working order before production runs
- PC14. maintain a record of tool usage, replacements, and calibration status

#### Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
#### Assessment Guidelines
1.Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.

#### Acronyms

### Page 42

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

#### Glossary

### Page 43

| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |
| --- | :---: |
| Organisational Context | Organisational context includes the way the organisation is structured and how it operates, including the extent of operative knowledge managers have of their relevant areas of responsibility. |
| Technical Knowledge | Technical knowledge is the specific knowledge needed to accomplish specific designated responsibilities. |
| Core Skills/ Generic Skills (GS) | Core skills or Generic Skills (GS) are a group of skills that are the key to learning and working in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment. In the context of the OS, these include communication related skills that are applicable to most job roles. |
| Electives | Electives are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as contributive to specialization in a job role. There may be multiple electives within a QP for each specialized job role. Trainees must select at least one elective for the successful completion of a QP with Electives. |
| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
