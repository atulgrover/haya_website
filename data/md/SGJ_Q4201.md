SGJ/Q4201: Biomass Pellet Manufacturing Junior Technician
Brief Job Description
Biomass Pellet Manufacturing Junior Technician is responsible for production, storage, and management of
biomass pellets at the plant. He/she looks after activities such as biomass receiving, pre-
processing/densification and storage in a manner to maintain its quality and safety as per standards.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it without any accidents so
diligence and hardworking are desired attributes for individuals performing this role. He/She must also
demonstrate strong work ethics, an ability to communicate courteously with co-workers and must be good
with following instructions of the supervisor
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. SGJ/N4013: Basics of Biomass availability and utilization
- 2. SGJ/N4014: Preparation of Raw material and quality assessment
- 3. SGJ/N4015: Equipment and machineries of Biomass pellet plant
- 4. SGJ/N4016: Manufacturing of Biomass pellet
- 5. SGJ/N4017: Operation and maintenance of various components of biomass pellet plant
- 6. SGJ/N4018: Package and Storage of biomass pellet
- 7. SGJ/N4051: Maintain Personal Health & Safety in biomass pellet manufacturing facility
- 8. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

SGJ/N4013: Basics of Biomass availability and utilization
Description
This unit outlines the basics of biomass and how the resource is to be utilised for upstream process
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to biomass sector Opportunities
understand the importance of agriculture waste as renewable source of energy
Elements and Performance Criteria
Basics of Biomass availability and utilization
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Provide introduction to the domestic Biomass sector
PC2. Provide an overview of biomass potential in India
PC3. Describe different types of farm waste and its source, supply chain and storage methodology.
and show different types of farm waste and its source, supply chain and storage methodology
through pictures and videos.
PC4. Explain the importance of agriculture residue waste as a sustainable energy resource and its
management and end use applications
PC5. Explain about biomass supply chain and its challenges
PC6. Discuss biomass energy conversion technologies and role of biomass pellets as green fuel and
illustrate biomass energy conversion technologies and role of biomass pellets as green fuel.
PC7. Explain the terminologies used in pellet manufacturing plant sites
PC8. identify the role and responsibilities of Biomass Pellet Manufacturing Junior Technician.
PC9. discuss advantage of doing this course, future prospect, and career opportunities

SGJ/N4014: Preparation of Raw material and quality assessment
Description
This unit outlines how to prepare raw material and perform quality assessment for biomass pellet
prouduction
Scope
The scope covers the following :
various features of raw material in use for biomass pellet production
different parameters of feedstock for biomass pellet manufacturing
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparation of Raw material and quality assessment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. List the raw materials to be used and show samples of different types of raw materials and
binders in use
PC2. Describe the process of receiving, handling and storage of raw materials and demonstrate the
storage procedures for raw materials such as saw dust, wheat straw, etc
PC3. Assess different parameters of feedstock for biomass pellet
PC4. Explain how to pre-process feedstock and prepare the raw material for feeding
PC5. Demonstrate how to measure moisture content of raw material
PC6. Show how to check the raw material quality and grade
PC7. show how to calculate batch size and prioritize urgent orders based on the production
schedule and machine capacity
PC8. Show how to inspect the conformance of raw material quality to standards
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. policies and procedures that impact the business process
KU2. legal formalities applicable for the particular business
KU3. benefits to the company due to practice of these procedures
KU4. the importance of having an ethical and value based approach to governance
KU5. knowledge of processes involved in the business
KU6. country specific regulations for manufacturing and its importance

SGJ/N4016: Manufacturing of Biomass pellet
Description
This unit outlines all the process involved in manufacturing of biomass pellets
Scope
The scope covers the following :
ALL Process involve in manufacturing of biomass pellets
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manufacturing of biomass pellets
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain about placing of biomass on dyes and demonstrate placing of biomass on dyes
PC2. Explain extrusion process
PC3. Explain about operation of various machines in plant such as dryer, hammer mill, pellet mill,
bomb calorimeter, etc and demonstrate use of die, dryer, chipper, hammer mill, pellet mill,
bomb calorimeter, etc
PC4. Show how to Set the control parameters during manufacturing
PC5. Explain about speed, temperature and moisture control in pelleting process and show how to
Adjust pellet producing machineries at dye of 3mm, specific speed, temperature and
moisture control
PC6. Explain the process of cooling of biomass pellet and discuss what is backpressure
PC7. Explain about standard protocol for testing of pellets and important parameters are Gross
Calorific Value, Ash and Moisture.
PC8. Explain Do’s and Don’t
PC9. Demonstrate use of moisture meter to measure water content in different agriculture waste.
PC10. Perform the post-production cleaning and maintenance process followed in the industry.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. local corporations / authorities that have a bearing on the particular business process
KU2. policies and procedures that impact the business process
KU3. legal formalities applicable for the particular business
KU4. benefits to the company due to practice of these procedures
KU5. the importance of having an ethical and value based approach to governance
KU6. knowledge of processes involved in the business
KU7. country specific regulations for manufacturing and its importance
KU8. reporting procedure in case of deviations

| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
