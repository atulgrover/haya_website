#### ASC/Q8319: Industrial Control
Brief Job Description
Industrial Control contains elements of both electrical installations and automation installations, with
greater emphasis on automation installation. The industrial control practitioner requires a wide range of
technical skills, such as installing conduits, cables, instruments, I/O devices and Programmable Logic
Controllers. The industrial control practitioner also designs electrical circuits, programs Programmable
Logic Controllers, parametrizes bus systems and configures Human Machine Interfaces.
Personal Attributes
The person should be result oriented with good technical and analytical skills, should have Excellent
Interpersonal Skills, communication and presentation skills and a good team player. They should have
ability to manage projects, prioritizing of work and mentoring the budding engineers.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ASC/N9830: Work organization and management (Industrial Control)
2. ASC/N8368: Circuit design and modification
3. ASC/N8369: Making of automation control panels/centres
4. ASC/N8370: Field Installation (electrical and automation)
5. ASC/N8371: Programming (Industrial control)
6. ASC/N8372: Fault-finding

#### ASC/N9830: Work organization and management   (Industrial Control)
Description
This NOS unit is about implementing safety, planning work, adopting sustainable practices for optimising
the use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage work and resources
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage work and resources
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Consistently promote and comply with health and safety regulations and industry best
practices in all working environments
- PC2. Correctly use all safety equipment and personal protection equipment (PPE), lock off systems,
and warning indicators
- PC3. Recognize hazards and potentially hazardous situations and take appropriate actions to
minimize risk to self and others
- PC4. Work effectively as part of a team
- PC5. Communicate effectively with other professionals including work-shop supervisors and other
staff where installations are being carried out
- PC6. Explain complex mechanical and engineering projects to colleagues who may not have
specialist knowledge
- PC7. Provide expert advice and guidance regarding on-going use, care, and maintenance of
equipment
- PC8. Think logically and work systematically
- PC9. Reduce waste as much as possible and apply sorting
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Health and safety regulations and best practice, especially in relation to hazardous working
environments and the variety of locations and industrial settings where the work may be conducted
- KU2. Safety requirements relating to plant and equipment
- KU3. SIL levels of safety and the application to relevant industries
- KU4. The importance of site safety inductions
- KU5. The range of safety equipment used to protect self and others and the application relating to
various industries

#### ASC/N8369: Making of automation control panels/centres
Description
This is about making automation control panels/centres of the system.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Making of automation control panels/centres
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Making of automation control panels/centres
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Read, understand, and interpret complex technical drawing, circuit diagrams, layouts, function
descriptions, and terminal drawings
- PC2. Apply information from technical specifications to effective work planning and solutions to
engineering and operational problems
- PC3. Install ducts, terminals, components, and wiring of the control panel according to the drawings
and given tolerances
- PC4. Complete appropriate panel building operations according to specifications
- PC5. Interpret operations manuals and follow guidelines and instructions
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Terminology and symbols used in technical specifications and diagrams
- KU2. Principles of technical drawing, circuit diagrams, layouts, function descriptions, and terminal
drawings
- KU3. Uses and layout of operation manuals
- KU4. Electrical and mechanical tools used in panel building activities, such as drilling and cutting
- KU5. Lean manufacturing processes (wastes etc.)
- KU6. Responsibility/liability to the customer (extra holes, dirt, damages)

#### ASC/N8371: Programming  (Industrial control)
Description
This NOS is about carrying about programming of the installed system.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Programming of system
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Programming of system
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Create programmes according to written specifications and diagrams
- PC2. Configure the HMI-screens according to written specifications and diagrams
- PC3. Configure HMI-screens to switch automatically to customised start screens
- PC4. Configure the VSD or Servodrive as required in the function descriptions
- PC5. Configure VSDs or Servodrives to startup without error messages
- PC6. Test functions thoroughly and safely
- PC7. Demonstrate functions to users and provide expert advice and guidance
- PC8. Conform to IEC sequence programming specifications
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Principles of technical specifications and diagrams
- KU2. Processes of controlling motors, valves, and other devices used in industrial control
- KU3. HMI and PC-based HMI/Visualization to communicate with the PLC code
- KU4. Setting of input limits
- KU5. Uses of industry accepted equipment such as PLC, HMI, VFD/VSD, and distributed IO
- KU6. Distributed IO based and industrial Bus Technologies
- KU7. Industry 4.0 technology
- KU8. IEC sequence-programming methods (IEC 61131-3)

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |

| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
