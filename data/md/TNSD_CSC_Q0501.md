#### TNSD/CSC/Q0501: Advanced  CNC  Programmer
Brief Job Description
An Advanced CNC Programmer designs, develops, and optimizes complex CNC programs for various tool
room machines, ensuring the precise manufacture of parts according to advanced specifications. This role
involves in-depth knowledge of machining processes, CAD/CAM software, quality control, and production
planning. Upon successful completion, learners will be qualified for advanced programming roles in
engineering, manufacturing, machine tools, and automobile industries, enabling them to lead complex
machining projects and contribute to strategic production goals.
Personal Attributes
Upon successful completion, learners will be qualified for advanced programming roles in engineering,
manufacturing, machine tools, and automobile industries, enabling them to lead complex machining
projects and contribute to strategic production goals.

#### TNSD/CSC/N6901: Perform Advanced  Workshop  Operations
Description
Understand the programming and manufacturing of complex parts by using CNC Turn Mill and CAM software. Understand the programming and manufacturing of complex parts by using 5-Axis CNC Milling
Machine and CAM software.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the manufacturing of complex parts by using CNC Turn mill, 5-Axis CNC Milling.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate the safety aspects and Special modules on CNC Turn Mill and CNC 5-Axis Milling.
- PC2. Practice on Different parts and its functions of CNC Turn Mill and CNC 5-Axis Milling.
- PC3. Practice on Exercises with proper cutting terminology on CNC Turn Mill.
- PC4. Practice on Exercises with proper cutting terminology on CNC 5-Axis Milling.
- PC5. Demonstrate Part programming and Machining of parts on CNC Turn mill.
- PC6. Demonstrate Part programming and Machining of parts on CNC 5-Axis Milling
- PC7. Machine different parts of a project on CNC machines and assembling/OJT
- PC8. Practice measuring training on CMM
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Practice on Different parts and its functions of CNC Turn Mill and CNC 5-Axis Milling.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Demonstrate Part programming and Machining of parts on CNC Turn mill.
