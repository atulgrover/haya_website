#### MSU/ELE/Q0101: Semiconductor  IC Design Associate
Brief Job Description
On completion of this qualification the candidate will be able to design and verify basic digital logic gates
and combinational circuits. This training imparts knowledge on semiconductor IC design, covering
electronics fundamentals, the entire microchip development cycle from conceptualization to packaging,
and hands-on experience with EDA tools for design rule check and layout versus schematic verification.
Participants will also learn about fabrication steps, design for manufacturability, and optical proximity
correction.
Personal Attributes
To excel in Semiconductor IC Design, individuals must possess a strong foundation in electronics and a
keen attention to detail for accurate circuit design and verification. A systematic approach, problem-
solving abilities, and a curious mindset are essential for navigating the complexities of the microchip
development cycle. Proficiency in EDA tools and a strong grasp of fabrication processes are crucial for
efficient design and manufacturing. Additionally, a collaborative spirit is valuable for working within design
teams and a continuous drive to learn is necessary to stay updated with the evolving semiconductor
industry.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MSU/ELE/N0101: Foundation of Semiconductor Design - Understanding the Basics
2. MSU/ELE/N0102: Layout Design Development Process- From Theory to Practice
3. MSU/ELE/N0103: Schematic Design Development Process- From Theory to Practice
4. MSU/ELE/N0104: Design Rule Check (DRC), LVS (Layout Vs Schematic) and Reliability Checks
5. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
