#### SSC/Q8108: AI Solution developer
Brief Job Description
The AI Solution Developer qualification is designed to equip individuals with the necessary skills and
knowledge to design, implement, maintain, and validate AI-based solutions in various organizational
contexts. The core competencies include understanding business requirements, evaluating platforms and
technologies, implementing data governance, and ensuring compliance with ethical standards in AI.
Candidates will learn to apply enterprise architecture principles, effectively manage data, and utilize
various tools and methodologies to support the design and deployment of AI solutions. In addition, the
qualification emphasizes the importance of monitoring and evaluating solution performance, ensuring
ongoing compliance, and adapting to evolving industry standards. It also encompasses modules on
employability skills, professional development, communication, and customer service, thereby preparing
candidates for success in the workplace.
Personal Attributes
A solution architect needs significant people and process skills. They often interact with the top
management and can articulate complex scenarios and problems to various other stakeholders using
simpler terminology.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SSC/N8113: Design new solution architectures as per specifications
2. SSC/N8114: Maintain existing architectures for solutions
3. SSC/N8159: Ensure compliance and ethical standards
4. SSC/N8160: Monitor and Evaluate AI-Solution Performance
5. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### SSC/N8113: Design  new solution architectures as per specifications
Description
This unit is about defining and describing an architecture of a system delivered in context of a specific
solution.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Define requirements
Define platform and other component specifications
Monitor performance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify and prioritize business requirements through stakeholder engagement and analysis.
- PC2. Evaluate the underlying platform, considering scalability, performance, and compatibility
with existing systems
- PC3. Assess data governance, privacy regulations and security requirements in a global context.
- PC4. Select design standards, methodologies, and tools that align with best practices and project
objectives
- PC5. Define system level architecture for the solution
- PC6. Design data feeds and implement data quality checks to ensure reliable data integration
- PC7. Define detailed component specifications
- PC8. Translate component specifications into detailed designs for implementation
- PC9. Conduct impact analysis to assess the implications of major design choices on the overall
architecture
- PC10. Develop a technical capabilities roadmap for new solution architectures, ensuring alignment
with organizational strategy and technological advancements
- PC11. Document designed solution architectures for relevant stakeholders, ensuring clarity and
accessibility.
- PC12. Collaborate with stakeholders to validate and refine solution architectures
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand organizational policies for designing solution architectures sharing and documenting them while involving key stakeholders
- KU2. Learn about business requirements industry standards trends and governance issues related to data storage security and privacy

#### SSC/N8114: Maintain existing architectures for solutions
Description
This unit is about maintaining existing architectures for solutions for internal and external clients.
Scope

Review and optimize the architecture
Monitor standards

utilization.

governance standards for security and data privacy

#### SSC/N8160: Monitor and Evaluate AI-Solution Performance
Description
This NOS is about monitring AI silution performance
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Define Performance Metrics,Data analysis,Reporting and Recommendations,User Feedback
Incorporation,Ongoing Improvement
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Establish key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure the effectiveness of AI solutions
- PC2. Align metrics with business objectives to ensure relevance.
- PC3. Conduct regular data analyses to evaluate the performance of AI systems
- PC4. Utilize analytics tools to track and visualize performance trends over time
- PC5. Compile reports detailing the performance of AI solutions, including successes and areas for
improvement
- PC6. Provide actionable recommendations based on performance evaluations to enhance system
effectiveness
- PC7. Gather feedback from end-users to assess the practical impact of AI solutions.
- PC8. Incorporate user insights into future iterations or enhancements of AI architectures.
- PC9. Identify opportunities for optimization based on performance data and feedback
- PC10. Implement updates and enhancements to AI solutions to improve overall efficiency and
effectiveness
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Follow organizational policies and guidelines for designing and documenting AI solution architectures involving relevant stakeholders and using standard templates and tools
- KU2. Understand business requirements and industry standards to develop AI solutions that adhere
to governance norms for data security privacy and monitoring
- KU3. Identify the key elements of solution architecture including business information application
and technology layers and define components to ensure performance and scalability
- KU4. Utilize various platforms cloud services and hardware systems while ensuring ethical practices
compliance and optimal performance of AI solutions
- KU5. Monitor AI solution performance using quality metrics and continuously scale and improve architectures to meet organizational goals and performance standards

#### Acronyms

### Page 32

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
