#### AAS/Q0614: Airport Terminal Operations Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The Airport Terminal Operations Supervisor is responsible for Supervision of daily operations and ensure
that they are safe and secure. He/she also is responsible for supervising the handling of contingencies,
emergencies and enhance customer experience through process improvements. Additionally, they are
expected to manage people and stakeholders.
Personal Attributes
The Airport Terminal Operations Supervisor should possess reasonably good communication skills both
written and verbal. He/she should have a good customer orientation and must be able to make sound
decisions using logical means of thinking. He/she should have good conflict management skills and must
be able to provide attention to detail. Overall He/she should possess good leadership skills and possess the
ability to coach/mentor people in their development process.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. AAS/N0625: Introduction to Airport Operations
2. AAS/N0635: Supervise Daily Operations , Provide Security and Safety Oversight
3. AAS/N0634: Supervise handling of incidents, contingencies , emergency situations and drive customer experience through process improvements

5. AAS/N0629: Digital and AI initiatives at Airport
6. AAS/N0502: Follow safety and security procedures

#### AAS/N0635: Supervise Daily Operations , Provide Security and Safety
Oversight
Description
This unit describes the actions to be performed with reference to supervision of daily operations and
provide an oversight of the security & safety functions in accordance with organisational and regulatory
procedures
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Supervision of daily operations
Provide security and safety oversight
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Supervise daily operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Plan the manpower and resources that are required for the shift
- PC2. Plan and coordinate the activities of the terminal including landside, and parking operations.
- PC3. Plan and coordinate the activities of the airside
- PC4. Direct supervision of all shift personnel
- PC5. Act on escalations from executives on the non-availability of resources such as check-in
counters, parking bays, baggage belts or any resource that hampers operations
- PC6. Cross check the completion of any pending repairs/breakdowns of systems as reported to
ensure a seamless operational environment
- PC7. Obtain an action taken report on the lost and found items during the shift. This should
include decision on assistance after hours, intake/release of items and resolution of inquiries.
- PC8. Complete and review all reports, forms and logs pertaining to operational activities during
assigned shifts for accuracy and completeness
Provide security and safety oversight
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Oversee and supervise daily operations to ensure safe work environment.
- PC10. Identify, prevent, and respond to any reports from the field staff on hazards on the airside
such as FOD, Wildlife incursions, unauthorized vehicles and pedestrian entry, and disabled aircraft.
- PC11. Monitor maintenance and/or construction projects and programs, which could create any
potential security or safety hazards
- PC12. Monitor and manage terminal Safety Management Systems
- PC13. Manage compliance of the airport tenants with the security policy as established and
outlined.

#### AAS/N0634: Supervise handling of incidents, contingencies , emergency
situations and drive customer experience through process improvements
Description
This unit describes the actions to be performed with reference to supervision of incidents, contingency
situations as well as drive enhancement of customer experience through process improvements in accordance with organisational and regulatory procedures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Supervise Handling of Incidents, Contingencies & Emergency Situations
Drive Customer Experience through process improvements
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Supervise handling of incidents, contingencies & emergency situations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Support the emergency management and incident command centre during an aircraft
accident
- PC2. Co-ordinate the activities mandated as a part of the emergency response during aircraft
accidents/incidents
- PC3. Coordinate public safety response to emergencies including Police, Fire and Emergency
Medical Services during emergency situations
- PC4. Respond to all terminal emergencies and incidents as reported by the executive level staff
- PC5. Coordinate and plan with the support of AOCC, the redrawing of check-in counters, parking
bays, baggage belts availability & allocation where a major disruption is anticipated
- PC6. Plan and advise the strategy to be adopted due to major disruption to the ground handlers,
airlines, and other stakeholders
- PC7. Participate in briefing, debriefing sessions post an accident, incident, emergency irrespective
of it being real or simulated
Drive customer experience through process improvements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Actively review the key operational & customer processes with the management and suggest
corrections where necessary
- PC9. Evaluate the Terminal’s operation to improve customer service, productivity, and efficiency
through benchmarking of similar airports in the region/country
- PC10. Organise and participate in quality audits to measure the efficacy of the processes against
benchmarked KPI
- PC11. Prepare initial reports on deviations from the KPI and submit them to the management
- PC12. Conduct review of audit findings and effectiveness of corrective measures implemented
- PC13. Conduct inspection of terminal facilities, floors, restrooms, garbage bins and windows to
ensure a clean and professional airport facility.

each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
