#### PWD/Q9501: Operator - Computer  Application and Assistive Technology
Brief Job Description
Individuals will get proficiency in the computer application and assistive technologies available for persons
with visual impairment to perform various functions efficiently to the maximum extent possible at par with
sighted individuals. After completion of training, they can independently work in different capacities in
office. They can also participate actively in the education, training and in social life.
Personal Attributes
The job requires an individual well-versed with the tactile skills, computer skills, communication skills,
independent orientation and mobility skills. The individual must exhibit a pleasant personality, excellent
interpersonal and communication skills and be able to follow instructions provided by the supervisor.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PWD/N9501: Apply Computer fundamental and various applications of MS Office
2. PWD/N9502: Use of Assistive Technologies for Persons with Visual Impairment.
3. PWD/N9503: Proficiency in reading and writing Braille (Grade I and Grade II) in English
4. PWD/N9504: Proficiency in reading and writing Braille Hindi/Local Language in Grade I.
5. PWD/N9505: Development of Language Skills in English
6. PWD/N0501: Employability Skills for Persons with Disabilities (70 hours)

#### PWD/N9501: Apply Computer  fundamental  and various applications of MS
Office
Description
This unit is about operating computer and its peripheral devices with screen reading software, creating
files in various applications of MS Office, organizing data, performing web searching, email handling, and
managing the communication and ensuring the accuracy and confidentiality of information recorded.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Apply Computer Fundamentals
Apply Fundamentals of MS Office
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Apply Computer Fundamentals
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain computer hardware & software along with various peripheral devices
- PC2. Understand Computer Network and its components, Local Area Network (LAN), Wide Area
Network (WAN) etc
- PC3. Identify network cables, configure wireless network, overview of various network protocols and
network topology.
Apply Fundamentals of MS Office
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Create, format and edit document using MS word processing applications. (e.g. inserting.
table, book marks, hyperlink, header footer, page number, page layout setting, auto correct tool, spelling and grammar checker).
- PC5. Create, format, edit and develop a work book by using MS Excel Spreadsheets application
software. (e.g using formula and features like formatting, sorting, filtering, validating data &
conditional formatting).
- PC6. Create and customized slides for Power Point presentation (various slide layouts, slide design,
animation, transitions).
- PC7. Use the Web Browsers, Search Engines, cloud management, e-mail handling and various
applications for Text, Voice, and Video Chatting
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Basics of computer and its terminology.
- KU2. How to Read & write in English, Hindi, and regional language with screen reading software.
- KU3. Components of a computer and its peripheral devices.

#### PWD/N9502: Use of Assistive Technologies for Persons with Visual
Impairment.
Description
The unit is about the use of assistive technologies which help the Persons with VI to perform their day-to-
day activities independently in an efficient manner. The Individual will also learn to type the text in
Hindi/Regional Languages by using UNICODE based font.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Gain knowledge on use of assistive technology for Persons with Visual Impairment
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Gain knowledge on use of assistive technology for Persons with Visual Impairment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. describe the concept of Assistive Technology and its usability for persons with visual
impairment following the concept of universal design.
- PC2. install and use various screen reader software based on various OS like Window, Android, IOS
with their features.
- PC3. configure and demonstrate various computer and mobile based technology such as -Camera
based Scanner; OCR Technology; Refreshable Braille Display; Audio Voice Labeler; Magnifying
Technology, DAISY and e-pub
- PC4. use various magnifiers, e-pub and daisy book reading devices.
- PC5. select the UNICODE based font for Hindi /Regional Language in Window. Type in-script layout
by using Unicode font with accuracy and speed of 15 words per minute.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the concept of sistive Technology and its usability for persons with visual impairment.
- KU2. importance of universal design for persons with visual impairment.
- KU3. troubleshooting during and after installation of various screen readers..
- KU4. how to configure and demonstrate various computer and mobile based technology
- KU5. how to use various e-pub and daisy book reading devices.
- KU6. how to configure and type in Hindi/ Regional language by using UNICODE based font.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
