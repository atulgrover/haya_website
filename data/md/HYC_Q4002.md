HYC/Q4002: Green Hydrogen Assistant Plant Supervisor
Brief Job Description
A Green Hydrogen Assistant Plant Supervisor is responsible for assist in supervision of efficiently operating
and maintaining a green hydrogen production facility. The person at this job assist in overseesthe
operation of equipment like electrolysers, dryers, deoxy reactor, electrical heater, pump, compressors and
storage systems ensuring smooth production processes. The person will assist in monitor water purification
process, Hydrogen, production rates, energy consumption and system efficiencies and make necessary
adjustments to optimize output and minimize waste prioritizing the Safety and compliance. S/He
collaborates with team members, maintain operational records and contribute to process improvement
initiatives at the Green Hydrogen production Plant
Personal Attributes
The individual should have ability to give attention to detail, technical aptitude, adaptability, teamwork
and a strong commitment to safety and sustainability. The person should have the ability to work
individually / team round the clock and must always promote safety at workplace.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management Procedures
- 2. HYC/N4004: Supervise the operation and maintenance of hydrogen production equipment
- 3. HYC/N4005: Supervise green hydrogen production operations
- 4. HYC/N9301: Working effectively in a team

HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management
Procedures
Description
This unit covers maintaining health, safety and security procedures at the workplace where Hydrogen is
produced and handled.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view
Safe handling of Hydrogen
Follow fire safety procedures
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view & Safe handling of Hydrogen
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Ensure that personnel under their supervision have a deep understanding of hydrogen's
properties, including its flammability, explosive limits and potential hazards.
PC2. Ensure personal and team adherence to the use of appropriate protective
clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions
PC3. Identify and communicate the relevant documents, responsible individuals, and designated
locations pertaining to health and safety in the workplace, facilitating effective safety
management.
PC4. Oversee and participate in risk assessments specific to hydrogen storage, handling and
production processes.
PC5. Proficiently identify hydrogen process hazards and assess risks including the analysis of risk
or accident in the workplace due to hydrogen leak/fire
PC6. capability to accurately read and interpret data from sensing equipment used in hydrogen
production processes
PC7. apply key safety parameters for handling hydrogen & oxygen
PC8. Evaluate hydrogen release pressures and understand hydrogen flame detection methods,
emphasizing the colorless nature of hydrogen flames
PC9. Apply critical safety requirements for electrical equipment used in green hydrogen
production, minimizing electrical hazards
PC10. competent in the operation and functioning of hydrogen exhaust systems, facilitating the
safe removal of hydrogen from the workplace
PC11. Execute inertization processes using nitrogen to displace hazardous gases enhancing safety
in hydrogen-related operations
PC12. Proficiently identify and recognize common safety signs displayed in various areas,
promoting a safe work environment.

HYC/N4004: Supervise the operation and maintenance of hydrogen
production equipment
Description
This unit is about supervise the operation and maintenance of the equipment used in green hydrogen
production, including electrolysers, compressors, and storage systems. Oversee in performing routine
maintenance, inspections, and troubleshooting to ensure optimal operation and reliability.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Supervision of working of water purification, hydrogen production methods and storage and
compression equipment
Supervision of maintenance of hydrogen production and storage equipment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Supervision of working of Water purification, hydrogen production, storage and compression equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify various hydrogen production equipment types
PC2. Understand types of electrolyzers, functions and principles
PC3. Recognize various compressors and their roles
PC4. Familiarize with purification systems and operating procedures
PC5. Comprehend water purification processes for hydrogen production
PC6. Determine user interfaces and instrumentation for equipment
PC7. Apply safety protocols during equipment operation
Supervision of maintenance for hydrogen production and storage equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Conduct routine inspections to ensure equipment functionality
PC9. Perform routine maintenance tasks, including cleaning and lubrication
PC10. Handle component replacements when necessary
PC11. Troubleshoot common issues related to hydrogen production equipment
PC12. Report equipment malfunctions to supervisors
PC13. Coordinate with technicians for equipment repairs
PC14. Demonstrate troubleshooting skills and safe equipment handover for maintenance
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. importance of calibration of tools and instruments used in Green Hydrogen production
KU2. organizational procedures for safe handling of equipment and machine operations
