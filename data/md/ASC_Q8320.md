ASC/Q8320: Manufacturing Team Challenge
Brief Job Description
A Manufacturing Team Challenge is responsible for designing, manufacturing, fitting, assembling, testing
and commissioning components by following the standards of the organization.
Personal Attributes
The person should be result oriented with good technical and analytical skills, should have Excellent
Interpersonal Skills, communication and presentation skills and a good team player. They should have
ability to manage projects, prioritizing of work and mentoring the budding engineers.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. ASC/N9826: Work organization and management (Manufacturing Team Challenge)
- 2. ASC/N9827: Communication and interpersonal skills (Manufacturing Team Challenge)
- 3. ASC/N8362: Design and realization
- 4. ASC/N8363: Drawing
- 5. ASC/N8364: Component manufacture using workshop machinery and equipment
- 6. ASC/N8365: Fitting and assembly
- 7. ASC/N8366: Testing and commissioning
- 8. ASC/N8367: Additive Manufacturing

ASC/N9826: Work organization and management (Manufacturing Team
Challenge)
Description
This NOS unit is about implementing safety, planning work, adopting sustainable practices for optimising
the use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage work and resources
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage work and resources
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. prepare and maintain a safe, tidy, and efficient work area
PC2. prepare self for the tasks in hand, including full regard to health and safety
PC3. schedule work to maximize efficiency and minimize disruption
PC4. select and use all equipment and materials safely and in compliance with manufacturers’
instructions
PC5. apply or exceed the health and safety standards applying to the environment, equipment,
and materials
PC6. restore the work area to an appropriate state and condition
PC7. contribute to team performance both broadly and specifically
PC8. give and take feedback and support
PC9. manufacture components and assembly to meet cost constraints and record manufacturing
costs and budgets
PC10. maximize material utilization in order to reduce waste
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The purposes, uses, care, and maintenance of all equipment and materials, together with their
safety implications
KU2. Environmental and safety principles and their application to good housekeeping in the work
environment
KU3. Principles of team working and their applications
KU4. Personal skills, strengths and needs relative the roles, responsibilities, and duties of others
individually and collectively
KU5. The parameters within which activities need to be scheduled

ASC/N9827: Communication and interpersonal skills (Manufacturing Team
Challenge)
Description
This unit is about communicating with team members, superior and others.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communicate effectively with others
Elements and Performance Criteria
Communicate effectively with others
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. read, interpret, and extract technical data and instructions from documentation in any
available format
PC2. communicate by oral, written and electronic means to ensure clarity, effectiveness, and
efficiency
PC3. use a standard range of communication technologies
PC4. explain complex technical principles and applications to non-experts
PC5. complete reports and respond to issues and questions arising
PC6. respond to clients’ needs face to face and indirectly
PC7. arrange to gather information and prepare documentation as required by the client
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The range and purposes of documentation in both paper based and electronic forms
KU2. The technical language associated with the skill and technology
KU3. The standards required for routine and exception reporting in oral, written, and electronic
form
KU4. The required standards for communicating with clients, team members and others
KU5. The purposes and techniques for maintaining and presenting records, including financial
records

ASC/N8362: Design and realization
Description
This NOS is about planning and preparing design for vehicle manufacturing.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Planning and preparing design
Elements and Performance Criteria
Planning and preparing design
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. read and interrogate briefs or specifications for manufactured items
PC2. identify and resolve areas of uncertainty within the briefs or specifications
PC3. generate designs for the manufacture of a functioning item within given timescales
PC4. generate innovative solutions to design challenges
PC5. prepare and implement documentation for work management and control
PC6. complete the design stage within the required limits of cost and time
PC7. use of engineering measurement tools including rules, verniers, micrometres and digital
measuring tools
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The principles and applications of project design
KU2. The nature and formats of project specifications
KU3. The bases on which the manufactured item will be appraised
KU4. Design parameters including: Options appraisal, Selection of materials and work processes,
Prototype development, Manufacture, Refinement, Commissioning.
KU5. Principles and methods for work organization, control, and management

ASC/N8363: Drawing
Description
This NOS is about creating drawings of the vehicle/parts.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Creating drawing of vehicle/parts
Elements and Performance Criteria
Creating drawing of vehicle/parts
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. create drawings to ISO standards
PC2. create and modify 2D and 3D models
PC3. interpret, construct, and modify engineering CAD drawings to work with 3D modelling and to
convert both to CAM
PC4. complete drawing activities within the planned timetable and to suit the project’s overall
requirements
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. How to interpret drawings that conform to ISO standards
KU2. How to create drawings that conform to ISO standards
KU3. The principles and uses of 2D and 3D modelling software

ASC/N8364: Component manufacture using workshop machinery and
equipment
Description
This NOS unit is about manufacturing vehicle components by using machinery and equipment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manufacturing vehicle components
Elements and Performance Criteria
manufacturing vehicle components
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. safely operate workshop equipment such as lathes, mills, drill presses, sheet metal working
equipment, welding equipment and welding holding equipment, pipe bending equipment and
electronics bench equipment, such as a soldering iron and PSU
PC2. apply safe working practices to all workshop equipment and processes
PC3. address the issues caused by temperature during machining, including the use of coolants
PC4. manufacture components to industry finishes and tolerances and adjust manufacturing
process to meet specifications
PC5. bend and cut sheet metal components in accordance with drawings
PC6. fit sheet metal components to an assembly
PC7. weld a variety of materials
PC8. use a range of welding types
PC9. assemble and commission electronics from drawings
PC10. design control circuits
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The specific safety principles and practices to be used with a range of workshop
machinery/equipment and materials
KU2. The principles and applications of machining
KU3. The use of general machining equipment used in activities such as centre lathing and
manual milling
KU4. The relationship between drawings and machining, including modifying the machining to
meet specifications
KU5. The characteristics of metals and the potential impact on them of cutting tools and
processes

ASC/N8365: Fitting and assembly
Description
This NSO is about fitting and assembly of vehicle components.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Fitting and assembly of components
Elements and Performance Criteria
Fitting and assembly of components
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Design a range of jigs, fixtures, and accessories
PC2. Manufacture jigs, fixtures, and accessories in accordance with drawings and specifications
PC3. Assemble and commission items in accordance with drawings and specifications
PC4. Assemble items using fasteners such as glues, screws, bolts, etc.
PC5. Complete the fitting activity within the planned timetable and to suit the project’s overall
requirements
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Principles and methods for manufacturing parts such as jigs, fixtures, adaptors and process
attachments
KU2. Principles and methods for assembly and fastening of manufactured parts such as jigs,
fixtures, adaptors, and process attachments

ASC/N8366: Testing and commissioning
Description
This NOS is about testing and commissioning of the completed vehicle.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Testing and commissioning
Elements and Performance Criteria
Testing and commissioning
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. test run the assembled item
PC2. review each part of the manufacturing and assembly process against established criteria,
including quality, functionality, time, and cost
PC3. modify, test, and appraise each part of the process, including:, design, tool paths, assembly
procedures, jigs, fixtures, machining
PC4. undertake a final test run to commission the item
PC5. present the item to the client with explanations and responses to questions
PC6. generate and present a portfolio including all essential documentation such as: 2D mechanical
drawings, electronic solid models, electrical drawings, manufacturing plans, design
calculations ,manufacturing costs
PC7. generate support documents such as: the operating manual, the maintenance manual
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The criteria and methods for operating test runs
KU2. The scope and limits of the technologies and methods employed
KU3. Strategies for thinking creatively and generating innovation
KU4. The possibilities and options for making incremental and/or radical changes

Description

Scope

Applying additive manufacturing methodology
Elements and Performance Criteria
Applying additive manufacturing methodology
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. design parts in the special software (www.autodesk.com/solutions/generative-design)
PC2. calculate the variety of materials used
PC3. postprocess jobs of 3D printers

PC5. operate a 3D printer
PC6. adjust and set up parameters for 3D printing; interpret drawings
PC7. respond to engineering problems with a rapid prototype design
PC8. design solutions given a variety of parameters; size, weight, durability, function and finish
PC9. design assemblies for parts larger than the print area of the given machine, or for
interconnecting, moving, interlocking or interacting pieces
