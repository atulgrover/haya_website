SGJ/Q6403: Desludging Assistant
Brief Job Description
The Desludging assistant, also known as Sludge Emptier is an individual, who is responsible for emptying,
transport and disposal of faecal sludge from the septic tank to desludging site / Faecal Sludge Treatment
Plant. He/She has to operate pumps, suction hoses and other machinery/equipment to empty the septic
tank and appropriately dispose the sludge. Desludging assistant will further specializes as an
entrepreneur, he/she would be trained to venture into desludging services for managing Faecal Sludge
Personal Attributes
The Individual is expected to be physically fit and should be able to work across various location/site
conditions. He/She should be self-motivated and able to produce good quality work. He/She must have
good communication skill, team spirit, be courteous and polite. He should be efficient in working with
proper planning skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. SGJ/N6411: Emptying of sewer and septic tank
- 2. SGJ/N6412: Preventive maintenance, transportation and safe disposal of sludge
- 3. SGJ/N6410: Maintain Personal Health & Safety in FSSM
- 4. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

SGJ/N6411: Emptying of sewer and septic tank
Description
This unit is about to emptying the manholes and septic tanks in residential, commercial and institutional
areas
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Initial checks before pumping out the septage from the sewer/septic tank
Pumping out septage from the sewer/septic Tank
Elements and Performance Criteria
Initial checks before pumping out the septage from the sewer/septic tank
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify types of sanitation system
PC2. identify the location and size of septic tank and manholes
PC3. ensure proper opening of septic tank cover/manholes
PC4. visual inspect the consistency of sludge and breakdown the solid sludge with water in case it
is solid in consistency
Pumping out septage from the sewer/septic Tank
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. install pipe in the manholes/septic tank connecting with sludge tank
PC6. ensure health and safety while installing the pipe in the septic tank for pumping out the
sludge
PC7. schedule meter check before pumping out the sludge
PC8. demonstrate use of rodding and jetting tools
PC9. maintain logbook of desludging procedure
PC10. ensure that deep sludge should be left in the bottom of sewer/septic tank as per the industry
standard and government norms
PC11. uninstall the suction pipes
PC12. ensure that the manhole/septic tank is properly closed
PC13. clean their surroundings and pipes before leaving the site
PC14. ensure that sludge vehicle is not leaking during the transit process
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. organizational culture and setup
KU2. various policies and regulations

SGJ/N6412: Preventive maintenance, transportation and safe disposal of
sludge
Description
This unit is about preventive maintenance of desludging vacuum tank/truck and transporting sludge from
source to identified desludging site
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preventive maintenance of desludging vacuum tank/truck
Pumping out septage from the septic tank /sewer
Transportation of sludge
Safe disposal of sludge at dedicated sludge disposal site
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preventive maintenance of desludging vacuum tank/truck
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1.. check the oil levels in the vacuum pump, oil-cooling tank, hydraulic tank and tanker engine
PC2.. check the tanker fuel level, water levels in the tanker engine, windscreen water bottle, wash
tank and water tank for the vacuum pump
PC3.. check the cooling radiator for the hydraulic oil and pump oil
Pumping out septage from the septic tank /sewer
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4.. check if rear door closed and secured
PC5.. check tyre pressures, lights, indicators, horns
PC6.. prevent the tank from being overfilled
PC7.. check contacts between gaskets and seats and performance steel balls
PC8.. check leaks in the hydraulic system and power take-off shafts
Transportation of sludge
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9.. identify the dedicated route to the disposal site
PC10.. transport the sludge from source of sludge to disposal site
Safe disposal of sludge at dedicated sludge disposal site
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11.. maintain the log book of disposal site visit
PC12.. ensure safe disposal of sludge at the disposal site
PC13.. carry out cleaning of sludge vacuum tank after disposal
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Employability Skills
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. understand the significance of employability skills in meeting the job requirements
Constitutional values – Citizenship
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC2. identify constitutional values, civic rights, duties, personal values and ethics and
environmentally sustainable practices
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. explain 21st Century Skills such as Self-Awareness, Behavior Skills, Positive attitude, self-
motivation, problem-solving, creative thinking, time management, social and cultural
awareness, emotional awareness, continuous learning mindset etc.
Basic English Skills
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. speak with others using some basic English phrases or sentences
Communication Skills
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. follow good manners while communicating with others
PC6. work with others in a team

Assessment Guidelines
- 1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification will be created by the Sector Skill Council/Awarding Body.
Each Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS.
SSC/AB will also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.

SSC/AB.
- 3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected
elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- 4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
- 5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
- 6. To pass the assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass % aggregate for the
Qualification.
