#### WBSC/ASC/Q1301: Two/Three-Wheeler  Mechanic (Including LPG & CNG)
Brief Job Description
The Two/Three-Wheeler Mechanic (Including LPG & CNG) will be responsible for diagnosing, troubleshooting and repairing a variety of two and three-wheeler vehicles, including those with LPG and
CNG fuel systems. The role involves working with engines, fuel systems, transmission systems, brakes,
suspension, steering and electrical components. Adherence to safety protocols and quality standards is
essential.
Personal Attributes
A Two/Three-Wheeler Mechanic (Including LPG & CNG) should be technically skilled, detail-oriented and
safety conscious with strong problem-solving abilities. They must be patient and methodical when
troubleshooting complex systems and remain calm under pressure in a busy workshop environment. Good
hand-eye coordination, physical stamina and the ability to follow technical manuals are essential along
with a responsible attitude toward safety procedures and quality standards.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. WBSC/ASC/N1301: Apply Safe Working Practices (Two/Three-Wheeler Mechanic (Including LPG &
CNG)).
2. WBSC/ASC/N1302: Identify different types of Two/Three wheeler and their configuration.
3. WBSC/ASC/N1303: Identify and explain the key features of two and three-wheeler engines.
4. WBSC/ASC/N1304: Identify hand tools, measuring tools and their uses.
5. WBSC/ASC/N1305: Troubleshoot any fault of engine system, fuel feed system and transmission system.
6. WBSC/ASC/N1306: Troubleshoot and repair the fault of brake and wheel, ignition system, electric
system.
7. WBSC/ASC/N1307: Repair parts like fork, suspension system, steering column, mud guard, lubrication system.
8. WBSC/ASC/N1308: Work in real job situation with special emphasis on basic safety and hazards in
this domain (Two/Three-Wheeler Mechanic (Including LPG & CNG)).
9. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### WBSC/ASC/N1303: Identify and explain the key features of two and three-
wheeler engines.
Description
Identify and explain the key features of two and three-wheeler engines.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identification of the four stroke and working principle of four stroke engine.
Differentiation between two stroke and four stroke engines.
Different engine components like cylinders, pistons, crankshafts and their role in engine functionality.
The difference between four stroke LPG and four stroke NG and four stroke petrol engines

#### WBSC/ASC/N1304: Identify hand tools, measuring tools and their uses.
Description
Identify hand tools, measuring tools and their uses.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Hand tools used for repairing Two /Three wheeler.
The purpose and use of hand tools.
The working principles of measuring instruments and special tools used in auto workshop.
Appropriate tools for lifting a three-wheeler.
The measuring instruments with working principle of vernier caliper, caliper, micrometer, scale,
feeler gauge etc.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Recognize the hand tools used for repairing Two /Three wheeler, like. Hammer mallet, torque
wrench, screw driver, file, etc.
- PC2. Describe the purpose and use of hand tools.
- PC3. Plan the working principles of measuring instruments and special tools used in auto workshop.
- PC4. Apply appropriate tools for lifting a three-wheeler.
- PC5. Recognize the measuring instruments with working principle of vernier caliper, caliper,
micrometer, scale, feeler gauge etc.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Introduction to Hand tools: Overview of the purpose and importance of hand tools in the maintenance and repair of 2/3 wheelers.
- KU2. The uses of different measuring instruments (Vernier Caliper, Micrometer, Feeler gauge,
Multimeter, Tachometer).
- KU3. The general-purpose hand tools used in Automobile workshop with the principle.
- KU4. Importance of using right tools for each task to prevent injury and component damage.
- KU5. Selection of tools as per task.
- KU6. Overview of Tyre pressure gauge (Analog / digital).
- KU7. Overview of air compressor.
- KU8. Procedure for compare with recommended pressure.
- KU9. Maintenance of tools.
