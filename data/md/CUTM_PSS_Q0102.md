CUTM/PSS/Q0102: Transformer Manufacturing Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The Transformer Manufacturing Supervisor course trains individuals in supervising transformer production,
quality control, and assembly processes. It covers manufacturing techniques, material selection, safety
protocols, and compliance with industry standards. The course equips learners with technical expertise,
leadership skills, and process optimization knowledge to ensure efficient transformer manufacturing and
workplace safety.
Personal Attributes
A Transformer Manufacturing Supervisor must possess technical expertise in transformer production,
leadership skills to manage teams, and problem-solving abilities to address manufacturing challenges.
Strong attention to detail, communication, and organizational skills are essential. They should also ensure
quality compliance, safety adherence, and efficient workflow management to optimize transformer
manufacturing processes.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CUTM/PSS/N0103: Understand transformer manufacturing requirements
- 2. CUTM/PSS/N0104: Perform transformer manufacturing process.
- 3. CUTM/VSQ/N7801: Ensure organizational health, safety, and security
- 4. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

CUTM/PSS/N0103: Understand transformer manufacturing requirements
Description
The Individual is responsible to understand the design specification, verification of procured material, and
manufacturing transformer for quality transformer fabrication along with following necessary safety norms.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS "Understand Transformer Manufacturing Requirements" includes analyzing customer
specifications, interpreting engineering drawings, identifying required tools and materials,
understanding the bill of materials (BoM), ensuring compliance with manufacturing standards,
maintaining documentation, and coordinating with procurement and production teams to facilitate
efficient transformer fabrication while adhering to quality and safety norms.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding the customer requirement, engineering drawing and standards.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. interact with the head of transformer manufacturing on customer requirement, engineering
drawings and standards to be followed for transformer manufacturing process.
PC2. understand tools, raw materials and consumables required for production in sufficient
quantity as per production plan/operators’ instructions.
PC3. clearly understand the application of transformer and standards to be followed for
manufacturing and testing process.
Reading of detailed assembly drawing, dimensional tolerances and assembly process.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. understand detailed assembly drawing, material grade, dimensional tolerances, and
assembly process for the specific order to be executed.
PC5. distribute the assembly drawing, standards, and process steps to be followed for each stage.
PC6. distribute the work order details required for each process, batch details to be followed for
the manufacturing stage.
PC7. get necessary clarification from the appropriate authorities in case of any doubts.
Study of bill of materials.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. understand bill of materials along with quantity required transformer manufacturing process
with the expected quality requirements.
PC9. ensure that required raw material/tools is procured from stores /vendor before starting work.
PC10. check incoming quality Inspection reports on meeting the quality and quantity requirements.
PC11. ensure tools required are placed in the respective stages of transformer manufacturing
process.
PC12. if tool is not available collect the tools from stores / vendor site and place it in the respective
stages.

GS1. Read and interpret engineering drawings, standards, and customer specifications accurately.
GS2. Communicate effectively with team members, supervisors, and procurement personnel
regarding material and process requirements.
GS3. Document and maintain records such as bill of materials (BoM), production logs, and quality
reports for compliance and traceability.
GS4. Plan and organize transformer manufacturing tasks, ensuring proper sequencing of activities
for efficient workflow.
GS5. Analyze and evaluate raw material requirements, ensuring the availability of required
components before starting production.
GS6. Apply problem-solving skills to address discrepancies in material specifications, design
requirements, or assembly processes.
GS7. Make informed decisions regarding process adjustments based on transformer design and
manufacturing feasibility.
GS8. Ensure customer-centricity by aligning production processes with client requirements and
quality expectations.
GS9. Think critically and analytically to identify potential process improvements and optimize
manufacturing efficiency.

CUTM/PSS/N0104: Perform transformer manufacturing process.
Description
The NOS "Perform Transformer Manufacturing Process" covers executing core assembly, coil winding,
insulation, vapor phase drying, tank fabrication, and final transformer assembly. It includes adhering to
design specifications, ensuring quality checks, following safety protocols, maintaining documentation, and
coordinating with production teams to achieve efficient and standardized transformer manufacturing.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The NOS "Perform Transformer Manufacturing Process" includes executing core and coil assembly,
insulation, vapor phase drying, oil impregnation, and tank fabrication. It covers ensuring material
availability, maintaining quality standards, following safety protocols, conducting testing procedures,
documenting production activities, and coordinating with teams to achieve efficient and high-quality
transformer manufacturing.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Confirm availability of required quantity as per transformer design.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check quantity available at each process stage as per transformer design.
PC2. report the concern in case of any quality or quantity discrepancy.
PC3. arrange for discrepant quantity or quality of materials
Core Assembly
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. understand core size, material, and grain orientation.
PC5. cut core shape as per design.
PC6. understand different core stacking methods.
PC7. use of temporary bolt guides for stacking.
PC8. stake core as per assembly drawing requirement with appropriate quantity.
PC9. tighten bolt to avoid gap between cores.
PC10. insulate assembled core.
Coil Assembly
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. understand coil size, material and number of coils as per design.
PC12. keep winding room separate from other manufacturing areas with positive pressure and
stake horizontal/vertical winding mandrels.
PC13. check conductor is either copper magnetic wire or continuously transposed conductor.
PC14. check conductor purchased are pre-wrapped with thermally upgraded paper or nomex.
PC15. check high strength wire or epoxy bonded CTC used when high short circuit forces required
as per design requirement.
