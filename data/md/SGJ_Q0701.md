#### SGJ/Q0701: Junior Parataxonomist
Brief Job Description
The Junior Parataxonomist supports biodiversity conservation through field-based identification,
classification, and documentation of flora and fauna. They contribute to People's Biodiversity Registers,
implement the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, support Biodiversity Management Committees, and use GIS
tools for conservation planning. With knowledge of national and international biodiversity frameworks, they
connect scientific data with local conservation efforts to ensure sustainable bioresource management.
Personal Attributes
The supports biodiversity conservation through field-based identification, classification, and documentation
of flora and fauna. They contribute to People's Biodiversity Registers, implement the Biological Diversity
Act, 2002, support Biodiversity Management Committees, and use GIS tools for conservation planning.
With knowledge of national and international biodiversity frameworks, they connect scientific data with
local conservation efforts to ensure sustainable bioresource management.

#### SGJ/N7001: Conduct Field Surveys, Collect, Preserve, and Identify Flora
Description
This NOS provides step-by-step techniques for field collection, proper specimen handling, drying, and long-
term preservation for botanical research.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Learn practical methods for collecting, pressing, mounting, and preserving plant specimens.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Field surveys, collections, preservation and identification of flora
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Discuss about the morphological characters of Flowering Plants
- PC2. Explain the identification Techniques Method of Dissecting Floral Parts and Observation
- PC3. Explain the identification Techniques of local plant species using field guides and
identification keys
- PC4. Explain to understand and use scientific names
- PC5. Discuss about Collection of Plants and Field Data; Method of Pressing, Mounting, and
Preservation of Plant Specimens
- PC6. Explain the procedure of Incorporation and Curation of Specimens (Herbarium/ Museum)
- PC7. Develop a deep understanding of plant morphology and terminology.
- PC8. Gain hands-on experience in dissecting, collecting, preserving, and curating plant specimens
- PC9. Enhance skills in identifying plants using various botanical resources and keys.
- PC10. Build practical experience in field data Collection and specimen documentation.
- PC11. Understand best practices in plant preservation methods
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understanding the external features of flowering plants, including root, stem, leaf, inflorescence, flower, fruit, and seed structures.
- KU2. Knowledge of dissecting floral parts and observing reproductive structures to identify plant
families and species.
- KU3. Familiarity with using field guides, dichotomous keys, floras, and botanical resources for
accurate species identification.
- KU4. Understanding the principles of binomial nomenclature, taxonomic hierarchy, and correct usage of scientific names.
- KU5. Knowledge of techniques for collecting, pressing, mounting, labeling, and preserving plant
specimens for herbarium use.

#### SGJ/N7002: Understand  Biodiversity in National and International
Contexts
Description
Covers floral and faunal diversity, classification, agro-climatic and bio-geographic zones, wetlands (Amrit
Dharohar, Ramsar sites), threats to biodiversity, and sustainable restoration measures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
To understand biodiversity, ecological zones, wetlands, and conservation efforts in India and globally.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Biodiversity- An overview (national and international context
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Describe the overview of floral and faunal diversity in India as well as in the World.
- PC2. Explain about the general classification scheme and the introduction with floral and faunal
groups found in India
- PC3. Explain about the Concept of different agro-climatic zones as well as bio-geographic zones and
their contribution to the biodiversity richness.
- PC4. Explain about the Concept of Wetlands (Amrit Dharohar) and their types.
- PC5. Discuss about the Ramsar sites and its faunal diversity in India.
- PC6. Explain about the various threats to our biodiversity and its impact on our ecosystem.
- PC7. Explain about the sustainable measures towards the habitat restoration.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Basic concepts of floral and faunal diversity in India and the world.
- KU2. Classification of major plant and animal groups in India.
- KU3. Role of agro-climatic and bio-geographic zones in biodiversity.
- KU4. Types and importance of wetlands (Amrit Dharohar).
- KU5. Significance of Ramsar sites and their faunal diversity.
- KU6. Key threats to biodiversity and sustainable restoration practices.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:

#### SGJ/N7003: Study and Classify Invertebrates and Vertebrates Using
Taxonomy
Description
Covers classification, collection, preservation, slide preparation, cataloguing, and field-based identification
of zoological specimens, including species of economic, medical, and traditional importance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
To provide integrated knowledge and practical skills in animal taxonomy, preservation, anatomical
structures, and biodiversity documentation across invertebrates and vertebrates.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Taxonomy: Invertebrate and Vertebrate
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Describe characteristics, collection and preservation techniques of different invertebrates.
- PC2. Explain key concept of different major and minor invertebrate phylums and general
classification scheme (Protista, Mesozoa, Porifera, Cnidaria, Ctenophora, Platyhelminthes,
Rotifera, Gastrotricha, Kinorhyncha, Nematoda, Acanthocephala, Sipuncula, Annelida,
Onychophora, Arthropoda, Phoronida, Bryozoa, Entoprocta, Brachiopoda, Chaetognatha,
Tardigrada, Mollusca, Nemertea, Echinodermata and Hemichordata)
- PC3. Describe key concept of different lower chordates.
- PC4. Discuss characteristics and preservation techniques of different vertebrates.
- PC5. Introduce concept of different taxonomic classes of vertebrates (Pisces, Amphibia, Reptilia,
Aves, and Mammals)
- PC6. Introduce concept of exoskeletal structures like hair/ fur, feathers, horn/antlers.
- PC7. Explain concept of bones
- PC8. Introduce cataloguing and Database preparation
- PC9. Explain maintenance of collections/ museum specimens
- PC10. Introduce preparation of slides/ pinning technique.
- PC11. Identify Common species for economic/ medical importance/ traditional knowledge.
- PC12. Illustrate trichotaxaonomy technique.
- PC13. Visit to Nature trail/ Visit to zoological garden
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Basic classification of invertebrates, lower chordates, and vertebrates.
- KU2. Key anatomical features like bones, feathers, hair, and horns.

#### SGJ/N7004: Perform Faunal Surveys, Assess Biodiversity, and Apply
Conservation Practices
Description
This NOS covers wildlife survey methods, conservation practices, biodiversity assessment, and legal
aspects, with hands-on fieldwork and data analysis.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS offers practical knowledge of faunal surveys, conservation strategies, and wildlife laws,
with emphasis on fieldwork and ecological analysis.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Faunal survey, assessment, interpretation and conservation practices. Important Acts and Treaties
towards Protection of Wildlife in India
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Discuss conceptual idea of different sampling technique and survey/ census methods
like transect, quadrate etc.
- PC2. Introduce protected Areas and corridors
- PC3. Discuss different biodiversity indices
- PC4. Introduce in-situ and ex-situ conservation theoretical concept.
- PC5. Analyze the ways to protect, conserve and preserve biodiversity
- PC6. Inspect ecological requirements and maintaining factors for priority species and
ecosystems
- PC7. Visit to Nature trail for Survey
- PC8. Observe behavior of animals in zoological garden
- PC9. Introduction to data documentation and analysis in spread sheet
- PC10. Inspect wildlife related judgment in creating new understanding
- PC11. Build and apply new ideas, methods, and ways of thinking
- PC12. Introduced to field study procedures
- PC13. Describe managing Human – Wildlife conflict
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand various wildlife sampling and census methods like transects and quadrats.
- KU2. Gain knowledge of protected areas, wildlife corridors, and conservation strategies (in-situ &
ex-situ).
- KU3. Learn to assess biodiversity using standard indices and ecological parameters.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
