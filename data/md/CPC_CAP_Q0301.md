CPC/CAP/Q0301: Machine Operator - Plastics Extrusion
Brief Job Description
The Machine operator helps in handling plastics granules (raw material), operates the Plastic Extrusion
Moulding machines, set process parameter, carryout basic quality check of finished products & stores in
the desired place
Personal Attributes
Technical aptitude, Safety-conscious, Reliability and punctuality, Organizational skills, Problem-solving
ability, Teamwork and communication, Quality-focused mindset, Willingness to learn
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CPC/CAP/N0301: Understand the Basics of Plastics Raw Material & concept of Extrusion Techniques
- 2. CPC/CAP/N0302: Understand the basics & importance of Plastics Compounding /Mixing
- 3. CPC/CAP/N0303: Perform HDPE /PVC Pipe Extruder Machine Operation
- 4. CPC/CAP/N0304: Perform - Plastic Film Extruder Machine Operation
- 5. CPC/CAP/N0306: To - carry out reporting and documentation
- 6. CPC/CAP/N0307: To carryout basic quality check of finished products with reference to approved
product
- 7. CPC/CAP/N0401: Basic health and safety practices at the workplace, 5S
- 8. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
- 9. CPC/CAP/N0305: To Carryout House Keeping
Qualification Pack (QP) Parameters

Description
This NOS is about providing a basic knowledge of Plastics Raw Materials Used in Extrusion Process and The
