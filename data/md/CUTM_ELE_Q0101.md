CUTM/ELE/Q0101: PCB NPI Fabrication and Verification Specialist
Brief Job Description
PCB New Product Implementation (NPI) Fabrication and Verification Specialist responsible for
understanding the design requirements, material, generate design program for PCB manufacturing of
various electronic circuit and inspect parts to finalize and deliver the work piece. She/he is also responsible
for regular maintenance of machines.
Personal Attributes
A PCB NPI Fabrication and Verification Specialist should be detail-oriented, analytical, and quality focused.
They must have strong problem-solving skills, technical aptitude, and the ability to interpret complex
designs. Precision, teamwork, adaptability to new technologies, and a commitment to meeting deadlines
are essential traits for this advanced electronics role.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CUTM/ELE/N0101: Interpretation of designs and drawings and process planning
- 2. CUTM/ELE/N0102: Copper CAM Programming, Metrology, Maintenance, and repair of machine.
- 3. CUTM/ELE/N0103: Operate PCB prototype machine and complete the machining process
- 4. CUTM/ELE/N0104: Inspect, Finalize, and deliver the parts.
- 5. CUTM/ELE/N0105: Implement organizational health, safety and security for self and others
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

CUTM/ELE/N0101: Interpretation of designs and drawings and process
planning
Description
Fabrication and Verification Specialist focuses on analyzing PCB schematics, Gerber files, and CAD
drawings. It includes process planning, material selection, tool path optimization, and ensuring
manufacturability while adhering to industry standards and quality requirements.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Fabrication and Verification Specialist focuses on analyzing PCB schematics, Gerber files, and CAD
drawings. It includes process planning, material selection, tool path optimization, and ensuring
manufacturability while adhering to industry standards and quality requirements.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Interpretation of designs and drawings
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. interpret technical drawings and specifications of product.
PC2. identify critical features of product, connections, and geometric requirements.
PC3. analyze manufacturability with available resources.
PC4. identify and prepare for mental representation of parts.
PC5. determine materials for PCB prototype.
PC6. ascertain stock to be kept for different operations.
PC7. identify critical sequences with different tools.
PC8. plan the production of parts according to specifications.
Process Planning
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. ascertain solutions with reference to capacities of working environment and required work
(size of batch, complexity)
PC10. determine features of PCB board and appropriate machining and measuring processes.
PC11. validate and prepare best work holding methods.
PC12. identify, prepare, and calibrate correct measuring tools
PC13. identify and prepare correct cutting tools.
PC14. identify critical sections (with high possibility of damage or unsafe practice) and consider
alternatives.
PC15. devise innovative ways of using environment to solve technical issues.
PC16. ensure if potential solutions will be reliable until end of process.
PC17. weigh each solution and choose the best (considering speed, safety, and price)
PC18. make a final choice and lock strategy.

Description
Fabrication and Verification Specialist covers programming PCB machining using Copper CAM, precision
metrology for quality checks, preventive maintenance, and troubleshooting machine issues. It ensures
efficient PCB fabrication, adherence to specifications, and optimal machine performance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Fabrication and Verification Specialist covers programming PCB machining using Copper CAM,
conducting precision metrology for quality checks, performing preventive maintenance, and
troubleshooting machine issues. It ensures accurate PCB fabrication, optimal machine performance,
and adherence to industry standards.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Create programming
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. understand design requirements, technical specification, and application.
PC2. select best sequence for production of parts based on type and part specification.
PC3. use skill to specific software and related hardware.

prototypes.

PC6. transfer programs to machines.
PC7. create or edit programs directly on the control.

PC13. identify and estimate the correctness of measurements and the reliability of the data
obtained, to minimize associated human factor errors.

PC15. carry out routine maintenance of measuring instruments.

CUTM/ELE/N0103: Operate PCB prototype machine and complete the
machining process
Description
Fabrication and Verification Specialist focuses on setting up, calibrating, and operating PCB prototype
machines. It includes tool path optimization, precision machining, quality inspection, troubleshooting, and
ensuring compliance with safety and industry standards for efficient PCB fabrication.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Fabrication and Verification Specialist focuses on setting up, calibrating, and operating PCB
prototype machines. It includes tool path optimization, precision machining, quality inspection,
troubleshooting, and ensuring compliance with safety and industry standards for efficient PCB
fabrication.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Operate PCB prototype machining
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. follow selected process strategy.
PC2. • follow a given process strategy (external CAM programme)
• • opening Gerber file containing one circuit layer
• • opening next layers (maximum 4), if any
• • detecting or plotting card cut-out contour
• • opening Excellon file for drillings, if any
• • aligning layers (automatic or manual)
• • plotting tracks that are centerline texts
• • calculating isolation contours
PC3. upload generated CAM programme to PCB prototype machine and perform test runs.
PC4. load G-Code for Top Layer PCB prototype machine and print the circuit.
PC5. stick two-layer FR4 Material (Copper board) on Machine bed using 2way tape or any other
clamp.
PC6. mount and align selected bare PCB board.
PC7. plug engraving bit in spindle motor.
PC8. connect one terminal of leveler probe in engraving bit and another one terminal of leveler
probe stick on FR4 material.
PC9. using the Hand-Remote adjusts the X, Y and Z axis and set origin point.
PC10. select X-axis and press “=0” button and set X = 0, Select Y-axis and press “=0” button and
set Y = 0, select Z-axis and rotate hand-wheel in Hand-Remote and move to 2mm.Now the Z
= 2
PC11. press the mode button and select PC mode.
PC12. press the “cycle start” button in PCB prototype machine and start the auto surface detection
