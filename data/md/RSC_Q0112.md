#### RSC/Q0112: Rubber Internal Mixer Operator
Brief Job Description
The Rubber Internal Mixer Operator is responsible for preparing, operating, and monitoring internal mixing
equipment used in the processing of rubber compounds. The individual ensures accurate raw material
loading, optimal machine operation, and adherence to process parameters to achieve consistent product
quality. The operator also monitors the quality of the output mix, performs routine maintenance, and
ensures workplace safety and compliance.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to work independently and be comfortable in performing laborious work. He
should be result oriented and positive in attitude. The individual must be willing to work in the factory
environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N9501: Prepare for Rubber Mixing Operations
2. RSC/N9502: Load Raw Materials, Operate and Control Automated Internal Mixer
3. RSC/N9503: Monitor Product and Process Quality During Mixing
4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### RSC/N9501: Prepare for Rubber Mixing Operations
Description
This NOS covers the competencies required to prepare the work area, tools, equipment, and raw materials
before initiating internal rubber mixing operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan and Prepare Work Area
Arrange Materials and Tools
Prepare documents and specification
Conduct pre-checks on mixer
Follow safety and housekeeping protocols
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Plan and prepare work area
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. inspect the cleanliness and safety of the work area before beginning operations
- PC2. ensure availability of safety gear and personal protective equipment (PPE)
- PC3. follow safety protocols while entering mixing area
- PC4. remove any leftover materials or obstructions from the mixing area
Arrange materials and tools
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. check the availability of raw materials as per mixing plan
- PC6. identify and label materials appropriately
- PC7. ensure tools and containers are clean and in good condition
- PC8. verify weighing balance calibration
Prepare documents and specifications
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. read batch specifications and production plans
- PC10. record and verify raw material batch numbers
- PC11. maintain checklists and pre-operation logbook entries
- PC12. confirm mixing schedule and shift-wise production targets
Conduct pre-checks on mixer
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. inspect machine for any signs of damage or wear
- PC14. ensure emergency stop buttons are functional
- PC15. verify electrical supply and lubrication levels
- PC16. check the temperature control systems and timer settings

#### RSC/N9502: Load Raw Materials, Operate and Control Automated Internal
Mixer
Description
This NOS covers the loading of raw materials into the mixer, operating the internal mixing machine, and
controlling process parameters like temperature, pressure, and rotor speed. It includes monitoring
automation systems and addressing in-process deviations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Load raw materials and verify batch data
Set and monitor machine parameters
Handle machine interruptions and alarms
Ensure product discharge and transfer
Maintain process log and documentation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Load raw materials and verify batch data
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. verify material identity and batch number before loading
- PC2. sequence raw material addition as per job specification
- PC3. ensure feeding port is clean and unobstructed
- PC4. load rubber, fillers, and oils using safe material handling practices
- PC5. use hoist or conveyor system where applicable
- PC6. ensure correct dosing of accelerators and other chemicals
Set and monitor machine parameters
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. input temperature, rotor RPM, and mixing time on control panel
- PC8. start mixer and monitor control panel display
- PC9. check torque value and power consumption indicators
- PC10. monitor ram pressure and chamber temperature
- PC11. adjust water cooling cycle if overheating is observed
- PC12. track real-time data on SCADA system or PLC panel
Handle machine interruptions and alarms
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. recognize fault alarms and take corrective actions
- PC14. pause operations in case of material overload or jamming
- PC15. restart machine after resolving error as per SOP
- PC16. inform maintenance team for recurring issues

#### RSC/N9503: Monitor Product and Process Quality During Mixing
Description
This NOS deals with inspecting rubber batches for conformity, conducting basic in-process quality tests,
documenting results, and coordinating with the QC department. It includes identifying non-conformities
and initiating corrective measures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct in-process inspection
Identify deviations and take action
Maintain quality documentation
Ensure workplace cleanliness and safety
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct in-process inspection
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. collect sample from every batch post-discharge
- PC2. check for color, texture, and lump formation
- PC3. measure Mooney viscosity using testing equipment
- PC4. perform specific gravity or density tests
- PC5. test cure time and tensile strength where required
Identify deviations and take action
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. compare sample properties with formulation specs
- PC7. report off-spec batches to supervisor or QC
- PC8. reject batch if contamination or improper mixing observed
- PC9. adjust mixer parameters for the next batch accordingly
Maintain quality documentation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. record test results in batch inspection sheet
- PC11. label sample containers with batch info and date
- PC12. update process deviation records with corrective actions
- PC13. maintain inspection logs and communicate with QC
Ensure workplace cleanliness and safety
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. clean sampling tools after use
- PC15. dispose of waste sample material as per SOP
- PC16. follow safety norms while handling heated or uncured rubber

#### RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
Description
This unit is about maintaining health and safety of self and others at workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
Render appropriate emergency procedures
Maintain standard safety procedures at the workplace
Participate in safety awareness campaigns
Understand potential sources of accidents
Use safety gears to avoid accidents

Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. undertake basic safety checks before operation of all machinery and equipment and report
hazards to the appropriate supervisor

chemicals, utilities etc

workplace

- PC9. all procedures and work instructions for controlling risk are followed closely

immediate necessary action to reduce further danger

#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.

each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
