#### HCS/Q2401: Silver Coating-Junior Technician (Glassware)
Brief Job Description
The individual at work is responsible for mixing of raw materials for the glass production as per the
prescribed formulations. The individual understands the different kinds of mixers used in glass
manufacturing and how to operate the mixing machine. He mixes various chemicals including tin fluoride,
acids, and glucose in appropriate amount as per prescribed procedure for coating of glass.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have: hand eye coordination, ability to manage and make the chemical
mixture, ability to judge requisite amounts of chemicals to be mixed, judge if the prepared mixture
composition is efficient, ability to sort the mixes and ability to work for long hours in sitting and standing
position.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N2001: Prepare batch and mix the material
2. HCS/N2401: Antique/Silver coating of the glassware product
3. HCS/N9901: Coordinate with colleagues and work as a team

5. HCS/N9903: Maintain Personal Hygiene

#### HCS/N2001: Prepare batch and mix the material
Description
This unit is about preparing the material for blowing process by mixing the raw materials and chemicals in
the mixer machine as per the prescribed chemical composition of the glass to be produced.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
store the raw material understand the mixing formulation measure the raw material mix the raw material
deliver the raw material to furnace achieve quality and productivity standards
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Store the raw material
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Store the raw materials and chemicals as per the safety guidelines.
- PC2. Ensure that the raw materials are properly dried.
- PC3. Sort and arrange the different raw material as per the supervisor guidelines.
- PC4. Ensure the raw material are properly stored and is not affected by external environment.
- PC5. Ensure no material mix up in the storage area.
Understand the mixing formulation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Coordinate with production team / supervisor regarding the requisite compositions of raw
materials as per product specifications.
- PC7. Seek glass technologists approval regarding the formulations/combinations of raw materials.
- PC8. Identify raw material and chemical formulations for different quality of glasses.
- PC9. Understand different chemical combinations for different kinds of products.
- PC10. Make note of chemical composition and basic characteristics/qualities of different kinds of
raw materials and chemicals
Measure the raw material
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Seek glass technologists approval regarding the appropriate method of measurement of raw
material.
- PC12. Clarify for any product identification, weight and other details with the supervisor.
- PC13. Measure the raw materials using weighing scale (manual / digital).
- PC14. Load/unload the raw materials for measurement.
- PC15. Ensure that each raw material is weighed and accurate quantity is taken for mixing process.

#### HCS/N2401: Antique/Silver coating of the glassware product
Description
This unit is about coating the glass product with a reflective substance through a chemical process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
prepare silver coating coat silver product through chemical process achieve productivity and quality standards
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare silver coating
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify the glassware products for silver coating.
- PC2. Identify the type of silver coating to be done, i.e. outside silvering or inside silvering or both.
- PC3. Identify and collect the requisite quantity of chemicals to be used in the process of silver
coating.
- PC4. Wash the product with HF acid.
- PC5. Dry the product.
Coat silver product through chemical process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Apply tin fluoride on the surface to be silver coated (applicable for silver coating).
- PC7. Apply plastic paste on the surface (applicable for antique coating).
- PC8. Apply silver nitrate on the surface.
- PC9. Apply glucose on the surface.
- PC10. Shake the product with chemicals for inside coating.
- PC11. Put the products coated on the tray.
- PC12. Wash the coated product with water.
- PC13. Dry the product.
Achieve productivity and quality standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. Ensure that the surface is uniformly silver coated.
- PC15. Ensure that the product has been silver coated as per prescribed design.
- PC16. Ensure zero production loss during the process.

#### Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |

| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |

| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
