HCS/Q1201: Quality Checker - Fashion Jewellery
Brief Job Description
A fashion jewellery quality checker is responsible for inspecting finished pieces for defects, ensuring that
each item meets quality standards and design specifications, and maintaining the overall integrity and
aesthetic appeal of the jewelry collection.
Personal Attributes
A fashion jewellery quality checker should possess a keen eye for detail, strong analytical skills, and a
commitment to quality, along with excellent communication abilities to provide constructive feedback and
collaborate effectively with the production team.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

HCS/N1201: Perform quality check on the jewellery
Description
This OS unit is about checking on the quality aspects of the jewel and ensuring that the jewellery crafted is
defect free and meets the requirements.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Receive the jewellery from the concerned person
Understand job sheet, design and the customer requirement
Check for defects
Check if the jewellery matches the market requirement
Document the results periodically
Report problems to the supervisor
Packaging and Labelling of Fashion Jewellery
Achieve quality standards
Elements and Performance Criteria
Receive the jewellery from the concerned person
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Coordinate with the concerned department to collect the jewel.
PC2. Identify the procedures of jewel making and the raw materials used.
PC3. Receive the record sheet along with the jewel to note the defects during the specific process.
Understand job sheet, design and the customer requirement
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Receive instructions on the required work from superior.
PC5. Receive the design and the job sheet from concerned person.
PC6. Interpret the job sheet and the design requirement keeping in mind the different quality
parameters of the jewellery and raw materials used to make the same.
PC7. Plan the target number of pieces to be checked.
Check for defects
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Identify design and specific process related defects with respect to design defects such as
mismatch of design, product processing defects such as - rough edges and surfaces,
polishing standards, colouring, shaping, and finishing defects such as - alignment, secure
stone setting, etc.
PC9. Report to the concerned department and return the jewellery, in case rework is required.
PC10. Ensure no functional and specific checks are skipped, for the respective raw material-based
jewel.
PC11. Ensure to take any safety precautions before checking on any jewel.

HCS/N1203: Entrepreneurship skills for Fashion Jewellery Quality Checker
Description
This OS unit is about An Entrepreneurship-Skilled Fashion Jewellery Quality Checker combines the technical
expertise of quality assurance with the entrepreneurial mindset required to manage or grow a fashion
jewellery quality-checking business.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Quality Control Expertise
Market Awareness and Trend Adaptation
Client Relations and Service Delivery
Business Development and Marketing
Financial Management
Legal Standards
Sustainability Practices
Elements and Performance Criteria
Quality Control Expertise
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Master the techniques of inspecting fashion jewellery for defects, including stone setting,
polishing, metal finishes, and structural integrity.
PC2. Apply industry standards to ensure that all jewellery pieces meet customer expectations for
design and durability.
Market Awareness and Trend Adaptation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Understand current fashion jewellery trends, materials, and customer preferences.
PC4. Adapt quality-checking processes and standards to ensure products align with evolving
market demands.
PC5. Stay competitive in a dynamic market by maintaining product relevance and quality.
Client Relations and Service Delivery
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Build and maintain strong relationships with clients by delivering timely, high-quality
services.
PC7. Understand client expectations and tailor quality-checking processes to meet specific
requirements.
PC8. Enhance customer satisfaction and loyalty, leading to repeat business and positive word-of-
mouth.

To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
