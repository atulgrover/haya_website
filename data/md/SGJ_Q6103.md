#### SGJ/Q6103: Waste  Picker
Brief Job Description
Waste picker collects, segregates and safely handles biodegradable, recyclable, nonrecyclable and
hazardous waste from households, streets and waste management facilities. The individual supports door-
to-door collection, ensures proper segregation using basic tools and PPE and prepares recyclable materials
(sorting, cleaning, weighing and storing) for sale to recyclers or aggregators to earn a livelihood. The
qualification also educates learners about the benefits of the NAMASTE Scheme and other relevant
government welfare and social security schemes to promote occupational safety, financial inclusion and
improved livelihood opportunities.
Personal Attributes
A waste picker should have physical stamina, a safety-conscious attitude, and good hygiene practices to
handle waste effectively. They should also have attention to detail for proper segregation, basic
communication skills, and a sense of responsibility. Additionally, environmental awareness and willingness
to learn help improve their work and livelihood opportunities.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

1. SGJ/N3301: Introduce Roles and Responsibilities of a Waste Picker

5. SGJ/N3304: Prepare Reusable and Recyclable Waste for Sale

#### SGJ/N3301: Introduce Roles and Responsibilities of a Waste Picker
Description
This NOS covers the role of a waste picker and basics of waste segregation, including identifying,
collecting, and sorting recyclable waste safely.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
It covers understanding the waste pickers role and work context, along with identification, collection,
and safe segregation of reusable and recyclable waste.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to the Role of Waste Picker
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain why waste collection is important for a clean environment, good health and earning a
livelihood.
- PC2. Describe the role and main duties of a Waste Picker.
- PC3. Explain the importance of discipline, punctuality, honesty and good behaviour at work.
- PC4. Identify simple communication skills needed to talk politely with households, supervisors,
and team members
- PC5. Explain basic points of the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2016 and latest amendments
that a Waste Picker must follow.
- PC6. Explain what the NAMASTE Scheme is and why it is implemented for sanitation and waste
workers
- PC7. Describe the basic steps of municipal solid waste management such as waste generation,
collection, transport, and disposal
- PC8. Explain the importance of equal participation of women and men in waste collection work.
- PC9. Describe simple examples of organised waste collection models from cities like Pune
(SWaCH) and Bengaluru (Hasiru Dala).
- PC10. Show polite and respectful behaviour while collecting waste from households
- PC11. Speak clearly and politely with households during door-to-door waste collection.
- PC12. Work together with team members during waste collection activities
- PC13. Follow instructions given by the supervisor during daily work
- PC14. Take part in a simple group presentation on a successful waste collection model from a city.
- PC15. Share key learnings from stories of organised waste collection models like SWaCH–Pune and
Hasiru Dala–Bengaluru
- PC16. Use pictures, charts or videos to identify benefits and support services under the NAMASTE
Scheme.

#### SGJ/N3302: Execute Door-to-Door Segregated  Waste Collection
Description
This NOS covers door-to-door collection of segregated waste, ensuring proper handling, segregation, and
transportation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
It covers door-to-door collection of segregated waste, including use of tools, route planning, safe
handling, and transportation to collection points.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Practice Door to Door collection of Segregated waste
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the concept and importance of door-to-door segregated waste collection.
- PC2. Describe collection routes, schedules and coordination with households and supervisors.
- PC3. Identify primary collection tools such as bins, bags, handcarts and wheelbarrows.
- PC4. Explain procedures for collecting segregated waste from waste generators.
- PC5. Describe containerization and temporary storage of segregated waste
- PC6. Explain basic transportation of waste to secondary collection points while maintaining
segregation
- PC7. Demonstrate use of handcarts, bins and bags for door-to-door collection
- PC8. Demonstrate how to collect segregated waste from households following route plans
- PC9. Show how to maintain segregation during loading and unloading activities
- PC10. Sort collected waste in appropriate containers.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understanding door-to-door segregated waste collection.
- KU2. Knowledge of routes, schedules, and coordination.
- KU3. Awareness of collection tools and equipment.
- KU4. Understanding collection and transportation procedures.
- KU5. Knowledge of maintaining waste segregation.

#### SGJ/N0803: Follow Personal Health, Hygiene and Safety Practices at Work
Description
This NOS focuses on maintaining personal health, hygiene, and safety at the workplace, including use of
PPE, hazard identification, and basic first-aid practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
It covers personal hygiene, use of PPE, identification of workplace hazards, and following safety and
first-aid procedures during waste management activities.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Workplace Health, Hygiene and Safety Practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain importance of personal cleanliness, hygiene and infection control

- PC4. Describe safety SOPs for collection, segregation, storage and transportation activities
- PC5. Explain basic first-aid requirements and emergency reporting procedures.
- PC6. Demonstrate how wear and remove PPE correctly before and after work
- PC7. Demonstrate the proper use of gloves, masks and other PPE
- PC8. Demonstrate good housekeeping practices and infection control guidelines
- PC9. Demonstrate how to identify the location of first aid materials and administer first-aid.
- PC10. Demonstrate how to follow processes specified for disposal of hazardous waste
- PC11. Follow safety procedures during simulated waste management tasks.

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understanding the importance of personal hygiene and infection control.
- KU2. Knowledge of workplace hazards in waste management activities.
