SGJ/Q1802: Solar Cold Storage Entrepreneur
Brief Job Description
Solar Cold Storage Entrepreneur sets up an enterprise with an aim to provide a service (to Farmers/FPOs
etc) for storing perishable agricultural and horticultural produce for increasing their shelf life and realise
better price of the produce. This entrepreneur shall use an innovative solar based cold storage solution
that can also be powered through the Grid and has thermal energy storage for providing backup during
non-sunny hours.
Personal Attributes
The individual shall have an ability to take initiative, handle pressure and shall be motivated with good
interpersonal and problem solving skills. The individual shall also have leadership skills and organized with
their work and act with integrity while performing multiple tasks for the customers with quality and timely
deliverables.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. SGJ/N1802: Introduction to Solar cold storage system and the evolving business opportunities it
offers
- 2. SGJ/N1804: Elements of Solar Cold Storage system

- 4. SGJ/N1806: Assess lifecycle cost for installing Solar cold storage system
- 5. SGJ/N1807: Key technical and entrepreneurial aspects for supporting growth and business
development in solar cold storage
- 6. SGJ/N1809: Oversee the Assembly, Installation and O&M of Solar cold storage System
- 7. SGJ/N1808: Discuss various case studies showcasing best practices in system installation, working
and business models of solar cold storage system
- 8. SGJ/N0801: Maintain Health and Safety at Solar cold storage project site
- 9. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

SGJ/N1802: Introduction to Solar cold storage system and the evolving
business opportunities it offers
Description
This unit provides an overview of solar cold storage system, the skills required to start and operate an
enterprise along with the business opportunities in solar cold storage space.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to solar cold storage system
Evolving business opportunities
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Solar cold storage system
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Discuss solar energy and its various applications, including in solar cold storage system.
PC2. Discuss the requirement for storing agricultural/horticultural produce by farmers/Farmers
producers’ organisations (FPOs), etc at farm/Mandi level.
PC3. Discuss how cold storage solutions are useful for storing various agricultural/horticultural
produce to extend their shelf life and so useful for enhancing the incomes of
farmers/entrepreneurs/ other users, etc.
PC4. Discuss the overall concept of solar cold storage system illustrate its schematic and explain
the main functions of main components
PC5. Briefly explain the prospects, process, and practice of solar/ cold storage system in India
PC6. Discuss key drivers, sectoral- trends, market potential and overall business opportunities it
offers
PC7. Discuss advantages of solar cold storage and identify its usage for storing a range of produce
by various users
PC8. Discuss the importance of this job role and the basic skills, knowledge and responsibility of
an entrepreneur in cold storage market space
PC9. Discuss the enabling environment including key policy and regulatory provisions both at
centre and states level, key schemes, standards and guidelines etc which are critical to
accelerate the adoption of solar cold storage system.
PC10. Illustrate the significance of solar energy and its applications across various technologies
including in solar cold storage system.
PC11. Illustrate schematic of solar cold storage system and explain the functions of all key
components
PC12. Show how to analyse key drivers, sectoral trends, market potential and overall business
opportunities solar cold storage business offers
PC13. Demonstrate how solar cold storage solutions are useful for storing and extending the shelf
life of agricultural and horticultural produce

SGJ/N1804: Elements of Solar Cold Storage system
Description
This unit specifies the functions of all the key elements and components of a solar cold storage system
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Functions of the key components of a solar cold storage system
Elements and Performance Criteria
Functions of the key components of a solar cold storage system
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the detailed prospects, concept and practice of utilisation of solar cold storage
system in India
PC2. Discuss how to perform planning for installation of solar cold storage solution.
PC3. Discuss how “plug and play” modular, solar-powered cold room is designed for 24/7 storage,
and how it addresses the problem of post- harvest losses.
PC4. Discuss key information on storage/ cooling requirements for different types of produce
including expected shelf life, temperature range, humidity etc
PC5. Discuss key elements of solar cold storage system, and analyse the characteristics and
specifications of those including Solar PV system, Refrigeration system, Insulation
type/Thermal Energy Storage system, Remote monitoring system, etc.
PC6. Discuss how to ensure regular supply of various produce from farmers and accordingly how
to manage the demand from markets.
PC7. Demonstrate the functions of all key components of the solar cold storage system and
showcase its successful operation for storing a range of agricultural/horticultural produce
PC8. Show how linkages from both supply (e.g. farmers) and demand side (e.g. market) have to
be created to ensure the utilisation of solar cold storage system and subsequent viability of
such business
PC9. Show how parameters/info like shelf life, temperature requirement etc are relevant for
storing various produce
PC10. • Analyse various specifications of the key components of solar cold storage system,
including Solar PV system, Refrigeration system, Insulation type/Thermal Energy Storage
system, Remote monitoring system, etc.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. mechanical and electrical features necessary for the long life of the PV system under a wide
range of operating conditions

Cold Storage system
Description
The unit outlines all key functions to be performed for undertaking site survey and implementing all
activities for installation of solar cold storage system

Analysing various parameters for solar cold storage system installation
Elements and Performance Criteria
Survey site and discuss prerequisites for installation of Solar Cold Storage system

PC1. Discuss various parameters for system installation and explain how to identify a suitable
location where solar cold storage system shall be installed.
PC2. Discuss how to analyse key information (e.g. quantity/type of produce, distance from mandi
etc) for identifying the sites for installation of solar cold storage solution.

bankable DPR for installing solar cold storage solutions.

storage system.

installing solar cold storage
PC7. Demonstrate how to perform calculations for estimating demand/potential for solar cold
storage in a given area
PC8. Illustrate all key aspects of a Detailed Project Report (DPR) and show how to develop a
bankable DPR for installing solar cold storage solutions

KU1. company's installation, customer support and other relevant policy

KU3. general terminologies associated with solar power plant and solar cold storage system
KU4. simple calculations to derive the power and energy received from solar radiation in a given
area
KU5. simple calculations to derive the load demand
KU6. mechanical and electrical features necessary for the long life of the solar cold storage unit

Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
Assessment Guidelines
- 1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each Element/
Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will also lay
down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
- 2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
- 3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected
elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- 4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
- 5. Individual assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass % aggregate for the
Qualification.
- 7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification.
Minimum Aggregate Passing % at QP Level : 70

| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |

| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
