#### SGJ/Q4902: Solar Domestic Product Assembler
Brief Job Description
Solar Domestic Product Assembler assembles fabricated parts of small DC light sub assembly units by
operating required tools. He/She also test and calibrate the assembled units to ensure the small DC based
solar products meet the required quality and safety specifications. The job holder also repairs, refits and
handles warranty claims for the concerned assembled units.
Personal Attributes
Solar Domestic Product Assembler is responsible for basic preparation, handling and supply of materials
for Solar Domestic Products. The job holder must adhere to all safety procedures and practice good
housekeeping, safe operation of specific equipment, material or processes. This is an entry level position
for solar manufacturing.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SGJ/N4901: Introduce Renewable Energy resources
2. SGJ/N4902: Introduce Solar Energy
3. SGJ/N4903: Explain Application of Solar PV in small DC system
4. SGJ/N4904: Identify and Explain components for small solar lighting system

6. SGJ/N4906: Maintain Personal Health & Safety while handling various DC based solar products

#### SGJ/N4901: Introduce Renewable  Energy resources
Description
This unit is about introduce Renewable Energy resources
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to various renewable energy Sources
Introduction to Renewable energy Technology key components of the renewable energy system and outline their functions
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduce Renewable Energy resources
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. discuss the various forms of Energy
- PC2. discuss the various Renewable energy source and its applications and illustrate the working of
various renewable energy technology with various charts and models.
- PC3. discuss the advantage and limitations of renewable energy technologies
- PC4. discuss the role and status of renewable energy in India
- PC5. discuss the principle of renewable energy technologies with a focus on solar PV
- PC6. illustrate key components of the renewable energy system and outline their functions through
plant schematic
- PC7. illustrate renewable energy technology through quiz and puzzles

- KU1. company's installation policy
- KU2. company's customer support policy
- KU3. company's documentation policy

- KU9. relevant standards and regulations to be followed in the manufacturing facility

#### SGJ/N4902: Introduce Solar Energy
Description
This unit is about basics of Solar Energy
Scope
The scope covers the following :
basics of solar energy along with various fundamental concepts in electrical energy.
the movement of sun and sun path diagram
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Basics of solar energy
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. explain the basics of electricity and solar energy, generation of electrical energy, thermal
energy along with electrical concepts and perform simple calculations to illustrate the fundamental concepts of power and energy
- PC2. discuss the photoelectric effect
- PC3. explain the importance of Diffused Normal Irradiance (DNI) and Global Horizontal Irradiance
(GHI) along with differences in Irradiance & Irradiation

solar concentrator, solar lamp, solar home lighting system, solar pump etc

sun affects the solar energy generation through model illustrations, chart and videos

energy generation.
