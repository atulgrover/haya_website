#### CUTM/AGR/Q0501: Remote  Sensing Junior Analyst (Agriculture)
Brief Job Description
This advanced course transfers complex information into actionable insights. These analysts use their
expertise in earth observation data to bring valuable insights to farmers and the agricultural industry as a
whole which includes monitoring crop health and growth, soil and water management and weather forecasting that empowers farmers and ultimately contribute to a more sustainable and productive
agricultural sector.
Personal Attributes
A Remote Sensing Junior Analyst (Agriculture) should be detail-oriented, analytical, and tech-savvy with a
strong grasp of geospatial tools and agricultural practices. They must possess good observation skills,
critical thinking, and data interpretation abilities. Curiosity, precision, and the ability to work independently
or in teams are essential for success.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CUTM/AGR/N0501: Fundamentals of Remote Sensing
2. CUTM/AGR/N0502: Spatial analysis techniques and statistical methods for remote sensing data
3. CUTM/AGR/N0503: Applications of remote sensing in crop monitoring, and water management
4. CUTM/AGR/N0504: Understanding of crop physiology, growth stages, and modeling techniques with remote sensing data
5. CUTM/AGR/N0505: Introduction to machine learning algorithms for classification, regression, and
prediction tasks in agriculture
6. CUTM/VSQ/N7802: Apply Health and Safety at Workplace
7. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### CUTM/AGR/N0501: Fundamentals  of Remote Sensing
Description
This NOS provides foundational knowledge of remote sensing, covering the principles, types of sensors,
data acquisition, and image interpretation. It equips learners with the skills to process and analyze satellite
imagery for various applications such as agriculture, forestry, and urban planning, enabling informed
decision-making through geospatial data.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the basic scope of understanding remote sensing principles, electromagnetic spectrum, sensor types, and data interpretation techniques. It enables learners to apply satellite
imagery and geospatial data in fields like agriculture, environmental monitoring, disaster management, and urban development, laying the groundwork for advanced remote sensing analysis
and applications
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Comprehensively articulate principles, techniques and applications of remote sensing in agriculture
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understanding of basic principles of remote sensing and its operation
- PC2. • Introduction to electromagnetic spectrum, sensors, platforms and its interaction
• with the surface of Earth
Interpret remote sensing data in agricultural practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. Digital Image acquisition and processing techniques to analyze and interpret remote sensing
data using software.
- PC4. Learn to address remote sensing technique in multiple areas of agricultural challenges which
includes monitoring crop health and yield and managing pests and diseases
- PC5. Determining soil status and nutrient content
- PC6. Collecting and identifying accurate location data for field-based studies
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the basic principles and definitions of remote sensing.
- KU2. the electromagnetic spectrum and its role in remote sensing.
- KU3. different types of sensors and platforms (e.g., satellite, aerial, UAV).
- KU4. the concepts of spatial, spectral, temporal, and radiometric resolution.
- KU5. various remote sensing data types such as optical, thermal, and radar.
- KU6. the process of data acquisition, pre-processing, and interpretation.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0502: Spatial analysis techniques and statistical methods for
remote sensing data
Description
This NOS focuses on applying spatial analysis techniques and statistical methods to interpret remote
sensing data. It covers the use of GIS tools, statistical analysis, georeferencing, spatial interpolation, and
classification accuracy assessment. The knowledge and skills developed enable effective extraction of
meaningful insights from spatial data for various applications in environmental monitoring, urban planning,
and agriculture.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the application of spatial and statistical techniques for analyzing remote sensing
data. It includes working with GIS tools, spatial layers, georeferencing, interpolation, and accuracy
assessment methods. The scope extends to practical use in land-use planning, environmental assessment, agriculture monitoring, and decision-making based on geospatial intelligence.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand core principles of smart agriculture
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Exploring the various technologies used in precision agriculture including remote sensing,
drones etc.
- PC2. Collecting data from soil, plant, weather and crop yield
Analyze geospatial data to determine and visualize the application of fertilizer, water irrigation and
pesticides
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. Analyzing and interpreting agricultural data and to make informed decisions
- PC4. Utilizing data to determine variable rate applications of fertilizers, pesticides, and water
irrigation in agricultural field.
- PC5. Evaluating the economic and environmental benefits
- PC6. Utilization of robots and drones for different activities in field like planting, weeding, and
harvesting
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the basic concepts of spatial analysis and its role in remote sensing.
- KU2. GIS tools and techniques for spatial data handling.
- KU3. the importance of georeferencing and coordinate systems.
- KU4. basic statistical methods used in image analysis, such as mean, median, standard deviation.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0503: Applications of remote sensing in crop monitoring, and
water management
Description
This NOS focuses on using remote sensing techniques for monitoring crop health, growth stages, and
water resource management. It covers interpretation of vegetation indices, analysis of satellite imagery,
and integration of spatial and field data. The skills developed help support precision agriculture, irrigation
planning, and environmental sustainability through effective geospatial analysis.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS encompasses the use of remote sensing data to assess crop conditions, detect stress, and
plan irrigation. It includes analyzing satellite imagery, applying vegetation indices, and interpreting
temporal patterns. The scope extends to improving agricultural productivity, managing water resources, and supporting data-driven decisions for sustainable farming and resource conservation.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Implement Real-Time Crop Health Monitoring for Precision Agriculture
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understanding the basic principles of soil and water resources, their physical, chemical, and
biological properties.
- PC2. Gaining knowledge regarding the fundamentals and practices of sustainable soil and water
management.
- PC3. Ability to assess and monitor quality and implementing strategies for conservation of soil and
water
- PC4. Problem-solving skills for addressing soil and water resource management challenges.
- PC5. Know about the importance of soil and water resources for food security, ecosystem health,
and human well-being
- PC6. Proper land use planning and watershed management
Analyze soil moisture for Optimization of Soil and Water Resource Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Evaluate appropriate techniques for land and water conservation to prevent soil erosion,
improve fertility of soil and enhance quality of water
- PC8. Know the environmental impact of agricultural practices and water resource management,
and propose sustainable solutions to mitigate negative effects.
- PC9. Effective interaction and recommendations to stakeholders, including farmers, policymakers,
and community members, and engage in collaborative efforts for sustainable resource management.
- PC10. Communication and collaboration skills in resource management
- PC11. Problem-solving skills for addressing different challenges in soil and water resource
management

#### CUTM/AGR/N0504: Understanding of crop physiology, growth stages, and
modeling techniques with remote sensing data
Description
This NOS focuses on understanding crop physiology and growth stages through remote sensing. It includes
using satellite data and modeling tools to simulate crop development, monitor phenological stages, and
assess crop health. The aim is to support precision agriculture and decision-making by integrating field
observations with remote sensing-based crop modeling techniques
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the application of remote sensing in studying crop physiology and identifying growth stages. It includes the use of crop simulation models and satellite data for predicting yield
and monitoring health. The scope involves integrating temporal imagery with agronomic knowledge
to enhance decision-making in agriculture, optimize resource use, and improve farm productivity.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Integrate Crop Physiology with Remote Sensing Analytics
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understanding of agricultural systems, including like crop growth, soil status, weather, and
different farm management practices.
- PC2. Developing a strong foundation in the principles and techniques of machine learning and
artificial intelligence, with an aim on applications in agriculture sector
- PC3. Acquire skills in collecting and preprocessing diverse data from different sectors of
agriculture, such as satellite imagery, sensor data, weather data, soil data and crop yield information
Analyze images and create predictive models for crop monitoring and management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Image processing and sensor based crop monitoring and management
- PC5. Predicting models for disease and pest management in agriculture
- PC6. Automation and robotic systems for different agricultural practices such as planting,
harvesting, and maintenance
- PC7. Proper documentation for agricultural data
- PC8. Implications of AI technologies to solve agricultural problems

- PC10. Ability to apply different learning algorithms
- PC11. Knowledge of emerging technologies and understanding future challenges and opportunities
in agricultural sector

#### CUTM/AGR/N0505: Introduction to machine learning algorithms for
classification, regression, and prediction tasks in agriculture
Description
This NOS introduces fundamental machine learning techniques used in agriculture for classification,
regression, and prediction tasks. It covers key algorithms, data preprocessing, and model evaluation
methods. The focus is on applying ML tools to analyze agricultural data, support crop monitoring, yield
prediction, and decision-making, thereby enhancing the efficiency and accuracy of precision farming
practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS focuses on applying machine learning techniques to solve agricultural challenges. It includes classification for crop type and disease detection, regression for yield and resource
prediction, and forecasting for decision-making. The scope extends to handling diverse agricultural
datasets, integrating remote sensing with ML, and using data-driven insights to enhance productivity
and sustainability in modern farming.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Machine learning and its application in crop management

regression and prediction

normalization.
- PC3. Practice building models for crop classification using labeled data to identify crop types, soil
types, or pest presence.
- PC4. Apply regression techniques to predict crop yields based on variables like weather data, soil
conditions, and crop growth stages
- PC5. Create predictive models for irrigation, fertilization, and pest control scheduling. Evaluate
model effectiveness
- PC6. Implement cross-validation techniques to assess model robustness and use hyper parameter
tuning to optimize model performance for agricultural applications.
Apply Machine learning for monitoring and crop yield prediction, pest management or optimal irrigation
schedules
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. ML algorithms to analyze drone images to detect crop health, pest infestations, and nutrient
deficiencies
- PC8. Models predict crop yields based on weather, soil, and historical data, aiding planning and
supply chain management.
- PC9. Optimization of water and nutrient application, minimizing waste and maximizing crop
growth by analyzing soil and environmental conditions.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
