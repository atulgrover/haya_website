#### RSC/Q2401: Rubber Product-Quality Assurance Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The Rubber Product - Quality Assurance Supervisor is responsible for developing, implementing, and
supervising quality assurance systems across all stages of rubber product manufacturing. The role involves
preparing QA plans, supervising inspection and testing, monitoring compliance with product specifications
and process standards, and ensuring calibration and upkeep of quality instruments. The supervisor acts as
a link between production teams, testing labs, and management to ensure that the finished products meet
customer and regulatory requirements.
Personal Attributes
The role requires strong technical knowledge, problem-solving skills, and leadership ability in a dynamic
manufacturing environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N2402: Develop and implement quality assurance plans
2. RSC/N5019: Supervise inspection and testing activities
3. RSC/N5020: Monitor product and process compliance
4. RSC/N5021: Ensure calibration and maintenance of QA instruments
5. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### RSC/N2402: Develop and implement  quality assurance plans
Description
This NOS is about preparing, developing, and implementing quality assurance (QA) plans for rubber
products, ensuring alignment with customer requirements, standards, and organizational objectives.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand customer and standard requirements
Prepare QA plans
Implement QA plans
Ensure compliance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand customer and standard requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify customer specifications for rubber products from job sheets or purchase orders
- PC2. interpret relevant ISI, ASTM, BIS, and ISO standards for rubber products
- PC3. compare customer requirements with organizational quality policies
- PC4. list out statutory and regulatory compliances relevant to rubber QA
- PC5. discuss past customer complaints and feedback for corrective planning
Prepare QA plans
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. develop a QA checklist for raw material, in-process, and final product inspection
- PC7. define sampling plans for quality inspection as per standards
- PC8. select suitable test methods for physical, mechanical, and chemical parameters
- PC9. plan documentation formats for recording inspection results
- PC10. schedule QA activities in line with production timelines
Implement QA plans
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. demonstrate setting up QA schedules in coordination with production
- PC12. apply QA checklists during production and testing
- PC13. ensure usage of standard reference materials for calibration
- PC14. carry out root cause analysis planning for potential defects
- PC15. revise QA plans as per feedback from higher management or auditors
Ensure compliance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC16. verify whether QA plans adhere to ISO 9001 and other QMS standards
- PC17. conduct internal audits on QA processes

#### RSC/N5019: Supervise inspection and testing activities
Description
This NOS is about supervising the inspection and testing of rubber products to ensure compliance with
specifications at all stages of production.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan inspection and testing
Supervise testing activities
Review inspection results
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Plan inspection and testing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify inspection stages (raw material, in-process, final)
- PC2. allocate manpower for inspection activities
- PC3. decide test frequency based on product and standards
- PC4. set up inspection checkpoints in production line
Supervise testing activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. demonstrate the use of tensile testing machines for rubber samples
- PC6. show hardness measurement using durometer
- PC7. carry out density and specific gravity checks
- PC8. conduct visual and dimensional checks
- PC9. perform aging and weathering tests
- PC10. monitor curing cycle records
- PC11. oversee sampling methods during production
- PC12. check records of physical and chemical testing
- PC13. ensure safety procedures during testing
Review inspection results
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. collect test results and compare with standards
- PC15. identify deviations from specifications
- PC16. classify products as accepted, rework, or rejected
- PC17. escalate deviations to production supervisor
- PC18. prepare inspection summary reports

#### RSC/N5020: Monitor product and process compliance
Description
This NOS is about monitoring both product quality and process parameters to ensure compliance with QA
plans and industry standards.

Monitor process compliance
Monitor product compliance

Monitor process compliance

- PC14. verify labelling and batch coding

- PC16. ensure rejection is documented and segregated

- PC18. support external audit inspections
- PC19. prepare compliance reports
- PC20. discuss compliance issues in QA meetings

| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
