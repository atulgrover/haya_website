#### TNSD/LSC/N0106: Compliance  to health, safety and security norms of
Junior Supervisor - IoT Application (Supply Chain)
Description
This unit is about ensuring compliance with health, safety and security procedures at the workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit covers health, safety and security procedures.
Ensure compliance to health, safety and security.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrates the ability to implement safety protocols and procedures when working with
IoT devices and systems.
- PC2. Ensures strict adherence to health, safety, and security regulations relevant to IoT
applications.
- PC3. Conducts comprehensive risk assessments to identify potential hazards and security
vulnerabilities.
- PC4. Demonstrates proficiency in responding to health, safety, and security emergencies related
to IoT technology.
- PC5. Provides safety training and awareness programs for IoT users and personnel.
- PC6. Encourages the reporting of safety incidents and security breaches and ensures they are
addressed promptly.
- PC7. Implements robust data security measures to protect sensitive information within IoT
applications.
- PC8. Promotes the efficient use of resources, including energy and materials, to reduce
environmental impact.
- PC9. Conducts security audits and assessments regularly to ensure compliance and identify areas
for improvement.
- PC10. Fosters a culture of safety and security awareness within the organization and among
stakeholders.

- KU1. Safety Culture: Fostering a Culture of safety throughout the Organization by prioritizing the
well- being of employees and stakeholders.

#### TNSD/LSC/N0104: Basic skills and Best Security Practices for Junior
Supervisor - IoT Application (Supply Chain)
Description
The unit, "Basic Skills and Best Security Practices for Junior Supervisor - IoT Application (Supply Chain),"
equips professionals with fundamental knowledge and techniques for securing IoT solutions in the logistics
and supply chain domain. Participants will earn essential cyber security principles and strategies to
safeguard sensitive data, devices, and operations while implementing IoT technologies effectively.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit covers foundational security concepts, including encryption, authentication, and access
control, tailored to the specific needs of IoT applications in logistics and supply chain management.
Participants will gain a practical understanding of threat detection and mitigation, enabling them to
proactively address security challenges and minimize risks associated with IoT deployments in the
industry.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrates a strong awareness of IoT security threats and risks.
- PC2. Adheres to security policies, protocols, and best practices in IoT applications.
- PC3. Identifies potential security vulnerabilities and assesses associated risks.
- PC4. • Implements effective access control mechanisms to protect
• IoT devices and data.
- PC5. • Ensures data transmitted and stored by IoT devices is encrypted to protect against
unauthorized
• access.
- PC6. Implements robust user authentication methods to ensure only authorized individual scan
access IoT systems.
- PC7. Demonstrates the ability to respond effectively to security incidents and breaches.
- PC8. Keeps IoT systems and devices up-to-date with security patches and updates.
- PC9. Provides security training and awareness programs for IoT users and staff.
- PC10. Ensures compliance with data privacy regulations and respects user data privacy rights.
- PC11. Maintains audit trails and logs for monitoring and analyzing security events.
- PC12. Evaluates and selects IoT vendors based on their security practices and track record.
- PC13. Maintains comprehensive security documentation, including policies and procedures.
- PC14. Conducts regular security testing and assessments of IoT systems and devices.
- PC15. Actively seeks opportunities for security enhancement and follows a continuous
improvement approach.

#### TNSD/LSC/N0103: IoT user equipment for supply chain gadgets -
documentation
Description
The unit, "Operations of IoT User Equipment for Supply Chain Gadgets - Documentation, "focuses on the
essential aspect of maintaining detailed records and documentation related to the deployment, maintenance, and performance of IoT devices in supply chain operations. Students will learn to create
comprehensive documentation that ensures transparency, troubleshooting efficiency, and regulatory
compliance in this critical field.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit emphasizes the development of structured documentation practices, including device specifications, maintenance logs, and troubleshooting guides for IoT equipment in supply chain
settings.
Students will gain the skills necessary to create comprehensive records that not only facilitate
efficient device management but also support quality assurance, compliance, and process optimization within supply chain
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. The documentation is comprehensive, covering all aspects of IoT user equipment and supply
chain gadget operations.
- PC2. Instructions and procedures are Presented clearly, facilitating easy Understanding for end-
users.
- PC3. The Documentation accurately reflects the technical specifications and configurations of IoT

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
