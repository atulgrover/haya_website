MSME/LSS/Q1201: Jr. Technician- Footwear Design & Production
Brief Job Description
To create multi skilled workers/ operators for pattern cutting and various shoe manufacturing operations
and to develop supervisory capacity for quality and timely production of different types of Footwear
Personal Attributes
To create multi skilled workers/ operators for pattern cutting and various shoe manufacturing operations
and to develop supervisory capacity for quality and timely production of different types of Footwear

MSME/LSS/N1206: Quality Control
Description
After completion of course Student should be able to Describe the concept of quality in footwear
technology
Scope
The scope covers the following :
After completion of course Student should be able to
Describe the concept of quality in footwear technology
Elements and Performance Criteria
Quality Control To understand the concept of Quality control and various check points in different section
of footwear production.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Meaning of Quality , Different Point of Views of Quality
PC2. Total Quality Control of Footwear , Specification,
PC3. • Quality Control in clicking Section, Various Check Points Checking of Dies with the
pattern supplies
• Clicking to inspects & Mark the size, lot no, on each clicked
• components.
• Clicked components are finally inspected by quality
• controllers/ supervise
PC4. • Quality Control in Closing Section
• Sample of each operation is hanged on the machines.
• Strictly follow buyer’s customer’s specification.
• In process inspection by the supervisor and group leader.
• Final inspection by the quality controller.
PC5. • Quality control in bottom and finishing section.
• In process inspection by supervisors.
• Proper handling of lasted upper in various process/
• Operation.
• If any fault found in the initial stage, sent back for rework.
• Final inspection by quality control.

MSME/LSS/N1205: Basic Lasting & Making Technology
Description
After completion of course Student should be able to Explain the concept of lasting
Scope
The scope covers the following :
After completion of course Student should be able to
Explain the concept of lasting
Elements and Performance Criteria
Basic Lasting & Making Technology To supervise the production process of different construction in
lasting/bottom department using latest technology.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. • Types and uses of toe-puffs and stiffeners. Identification of methods of
• attachments
PC2. Methods of conditioning uppers and components.
PC3. • Apply Lasting principles and methods of application. Plan Lasting and bottoming
• systems for different types of construction in general use
PC4. • Perform Heeling processes, including heel building and heel finishing, covering
• Systems and methods of heel attachment.
PC5. • Various Systems of transportation and track management. Discuss combined
• lasting system.
PC6. • Discuss theory and practice of head setting - moist and dry head effects on.
• Materials and adhesive
PC7. • Use of hot-melt adhesive in lasting and bottoming. Discuss Lasting faults and
• effects upon subsequent operations.
PC8. • List correct techniques for sole attachment. Elaborate Composition,
• characteristics and uses of insole and soling materials for different constructions.
PC9. • Discuss Machine cutting direct/caster and planet, rounding operations. Plan
• preparation of cut stock and bottom components including pre finishing and
• assembly of pre-fabricated and Louis heel bottom units
PC10. • Assembly and storage of lasts and components. Discuss Standardization of
• components.
PC11. Practice Multiple thicknesses cutting of components
PC12. Identify Pre-molded shank, insole assemblies. Use confirming equipment's.
PC13. • Practice Departmental management. Plan Control of components and raw
• materials, Fitting up to ticket requirements.
PC14. • Procedure and processes for various soling and heating materials and units.
• Practice methods of attaching Top- piecing and types and characteristics of material
• available.

MSME/LSS/N1204: Basic Closing Technology To supervise the production
process in closing department and knowledge about different
constructions and latest machines.
Description
After completion of course Student should be able to Forecast the design and marketing trends.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
After completion of course Student should be able to
Forecast the design and marketing trends.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Basic Closing Technology To supervise the production process in closing department and knowledge
about different constructions and latest machines.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Discuss basic types and specialist stitching machines
PC2. • Elaborate operational sequence for Skiving, backing, stitch marking, perforation,
• folding and ancillary machinery like for basic styles.
PC3. • Different methods of upper reinforcements, edge and decorative treatments.
• Various types of seams. Welding treatment for upper assembly, decoration and
• ornaments.
PC4. • Classification of Needles and threads: Types, sizes, selection, classification,
• relationship, application to Work.
PC5. Identify Automatic and semi-automatic equipment for upper processing.
PC6. Discuss Upper shaping by forming equipment/blocking machine
PC7. • Plan departmental management of work loading like types and advantages of
• transporter systems, Random and final inspection.
PC8. • Closing Practical:
• Discuss different types of stitching and Non-stitching machine available.
• Various techniques of handcrafts and the use of Machines for similar
• operations, use and in the use of adjustments necessary to functional
• efficiency.
• Practice making of “basic styles of men's and women's footwear"
