GCP/HSS/Q0101: Geriatric Care Provider
Brief Job Description
This program aims to develop Geriatric Care Providers dedicated to providing high quality care and support
to senior citizens. It offers comprehensive, evidence-based training in geriatric care, equipping caregivers
with sub-nursing skills, taking care of an older person who has a chronic illness or disease, elderly in doing
their activities of daily living ,going for physiotherapy, Helping in medications or talking to doctors and
nurses on behalf of client/patient, Helping to bathe or dress someone who is frail or disabled and a deep
understanding of the critical role empathy plays in caregiving. Additionally, the course emphasizes the
importance of self-care to ensure the well-being of caregivers themselves. With a strong focus on practical
skills, the training prepares caregivers to deliver effective interventions in family and community settings,
promoting the welfare and quality of life of older adults. Building Geriatric Caregivers as Digital Health
Assistance.
Personal Attributes
Patience, Good Communication Skills, Respect, Compassion ,Empathy, Observation, Alertness, Teamwork,
Positive Attitude, Adaptability and Flexibility

Compulsory NOS:
- 1. GCP/HSS/N0101: Introduction to Geriatric/Elderly Care/ Government policies and programs
- 2. GCP/HSS/N0102: Body System, functions & related problems in Elderly

- 4. GCP/HSS/N0108: Caring Procedures and therapies
