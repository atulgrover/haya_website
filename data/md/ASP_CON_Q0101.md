ASP/CON/Q0101: Marine Structural Fitter and Fabricator
Brief Job Description
The Marine Structural Fitter and Fabricator course equips learners with skills to assemble, install, and
repair marine structures using tools and techniques like cutting, welding, and shaping materials. It covers
blueprint reading, safety protocols, quality control, and industry standards, preparing candidates for roles
in shipbuilding, repair, and offshore platforms.
Personal Attributes
A **Marine Structural Fitter & Fabricator** needs technical skills, physical strength, attention to detail, and
safety awareness to ensure precise fabrication and compliance with standards. Effective teamwork,
adaptability to new technologies, and a strong work ethic are essential for success in this challenging and
dynamic role.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. ASP/CON/N0101: Introduction to Marine Industry and Professional Skills

ASP/CON/N0104: Joining Operations
Description
To provide knowledge and skill on different types of joints in sheet metals and pipes .
Scope
The scope covers the following :
To provide knowledge and skill on sheet metal and pipe joining process.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. • Demonstrate different joining operations observing standard procedure.
• Practice on sheet metal seams.  Perform brazing operations on sheet metal.
• Perform Cutting operations like Gas cutting, Mechanical cutting, Plasma cutting, Jigsaw cutting,
etc.
• Perform CO2 Welding, Metal Arc welding, Gas welding, TIG welding & MIG welding and spot
welding/TACK welding, FCA Welding on sheet metal plates.
PC2. • Make sheet metal articles according to drawing or sample following safety precaution.
• Plan & work in different sheet metals such as tin, copper, brass.
• Undertake Aluminum frame works.  Make ducts, cabins & panels.
• Perform Pipe welding operations like edge preparation, cutting, fit up, bending, welding, checking
etc.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. To provide knowledge and skill on different types of joints and sheet metal seams
KU2. To provide knowledge and skill on fastening techniques like rivetting and screwing
KU3. To provide knowledge and skill on different types of cutting, welding processes in plates
KU4. To provide knowledge and skill on Brazing process in sheet metal.
KU5. To provide knowledge and skill on pipe joining process
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. exercise skill on different types of joints and sheet metal seams
GS2. Perform fastening techniques like rivetting and screwing
GS3. Perform different types of cutting, welding processes in plates
GS4. Perform Brazing process in sheet metal.
GS5. Perform pipe joining process

ASP/CON/N0105: Ship Building Materials and process
Description
This NOS provides comprehensive training in the selection, processing, and fabrication of materials used in
marine and structural engineering. Learners will gain knowledge of different material types, including
metals, polymers, ceramics, and composites, along with their physical and mechanical properties. The
course covers the structural components of shipbuilding, including hulls, decks, bulkheads, and beams,
with a focus on material selection, design principles, and fabrication techniques.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Covers material selection, structural assembly, surface quality, marking, cutting, forming, and
fabrication in marine structural fitting.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. • Understand the properties of different materials used for ship structure building.
• Understand the structural members of ship building.
• Understand the defects and quality of the plate surface.
• Understand the preservation methods of marine structure.
• Understand marking of plates and profiles. (Manual marking of plates using sketches, Manual
marking of profiles using stiffener list and sketches)
PC2. • Understand cutting of plates and profiles. (Plate cutting using machine/ manual method, Profile
cutting using machine, Marine Structural Fitter & Fabricator manual method, Gases used for
cutting – LPG, Acetylene, Oxygen, CNG, Plasma etc.)
• Understand forming of plates and profiles. (Forming of plates and profiles using mechanical
press, Types of bending – Cold ben
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Materials used for ship building and their grades
KU2. Different kinds of structural members used for ship building.
KU3. Different kind of defects on surface of the plate and quality of surface
KU4. Blasting, Priming and their standards.
KU5. Marking of plates and profiles using stiffener list and sketches
KU6. Plate and profile cutting using machine/ manual methods.
KU7. Forming of plates and profiles using mechanical press and cold and spot heating pressing
methods
KU8. • a) Production drawings
• b) Fit-up Technique
• c) Welding process in ship building
