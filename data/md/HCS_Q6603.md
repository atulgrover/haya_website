HCS/Q6603: Leaf Utility Products Artisan
Brief Job Description
A leaf artisan uses appropriate tools and techniques to manually prepare leaf craft products and form a
given range of existing traditional designs on leaf craft products or based on clients specifications.
Personal Attributes
Needs to be precisive and possess an eye for details. Must be alert and possess a physically active body.
Should also have good eye-sight. Should have commendable dexterity and a strong hand-arm
coordination. The candidate is also expected to be capable of working for long hours in sitting position.
Must be creative, team player and open to learning
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HCS/N0106: Preparing areca leaf craft
- 2. HCS/N0105: Preparing palm leaf craft

HCS/N0106: Preparing areca leaf craft
Description
This OS unit is about preparing areca leaf craft by using raw material and performing various steps.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
prepare raw material for Areca leaf craft
prepare Areca leaf for craft making
preparing areca leaf plates/craft
check quality of prepared Areca leaf craft/product
Elements and Performance Criteria
prepare raw material for Areca leaf craft
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. collect raw material like areca nut sheaths, cleaning brush/ scrub, sandpaper etc.
PC2. collect tools like Scissors, moulding machine and for measuring like marker pen and scale
prepare Areca leaf for craft making
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. collect the fallen sheaths from the areca tress
PC4. soak the areca nut sheaths in freshwater tank for 2 days approx. to soften the material.
PC5. scrub the leaf to stretch little to broaden and to remove the sand particles and dust.
PC6. keep the sheaths in the open sun for drying at least for 2 days to remove the moisture from
the sheaths.
preparing areca leaf plates/craft
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. place an Areca leaf moulding machine at the designated place to make desired craft
PC8. control the heat of two pressing heads of the moulding machine by thermostat
PC9. place the areca leaf in the moulding machine
PC10. set the temperature of moulding machine between 170-180 degree centigrade as to bake
the leaf
PC11. place the sheath between the hot-pressing heads and press them tightly to give appropriate
shape
PC12. press against each side to get the desire shape
PC13. cut the sheath into a proper shape by the help of cutting blades.
PC14. grade the edges of areca leaf plate or bowl, etc. with the help of sandpaper to smoothen the
edges
PC15. cure the plate or bowl, etc. using UV lights to give a beautiful finish and a clean surface
Check quality of prepared Areca leaf craft/product

HCS/N0105: Preparing palm leaf craft
Description
This OS unit is about preparing raw material and Palm leaf craft to an end product.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Collect required raw materials and tools for making palm leaf craft
prepare Palm leaf for craft making
preparing Palm leaf basket/craft
check quality standards of palm leaf craft
Elements and Performance Criteria
Collect required raw materials and tools for making palm leaf craft
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. collect raw material like palm leaf in a length of 12inches, basin, color powder for dying.
PC2. collect tools like cutter/knife, measuring tape, wooden slab etc.
prepare palm leaf for craft making
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. collect the fallen dry palm leaf in a length of 12 inches
PC4. remove the midrib from the dry leaf and split the palm leaf into two halves
PC5. cut the dry palm leaf into more thin strips by the help of cutter/scissors
PC6. palm strips are added to the vat of boiled color water and left for the dye to settle on the
strips to give respective color.
PC7. strips are remove after few minutes and keep for cooling
PC8. take dye strips and further weaving process is begin.
preparing palm leaf basket/craft
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. arrange the palm strips vertically and horizontally interlocking each other according to the
size of basket
PC10. place wooden slabs while arranging the strips with hands and feet for support
PC11. weave two pairs of palm leaf of an inch’s width and place adjacent to each other with half-
centimeter’s gap
PC12. keep the two pairs of an adjacent palm leaf in the same position
PC13. thin strip is brought over front till the half-centimeter’s gap and insert it is run across
horizontally
PC14. interlace the sidewalls of the basket and weave till the required length
PC15. pass the strips through the small space it is brought on top locking the two pair of palm leafs
together, kind of knotting the strips

HCS/N0104: Preparing coconut leaf craft
Description
This OS unit is about preparing Coconut leaf craft by using raw material and performing various steps.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
prepare required raw material to make coconut leaf craft
prepare coconut leaf for craft making
preparing coconut leaf basket
check quality and productivity standards of coconut leaf craft
Elements and Performance Criteria
prepare required raw material to make coconut leaf craft
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. collect raw material like coconut leaf, tape etc.
PC2. collect tools like cutter, thread or knife
PC3. arrange raw materials carefully for further usage in making appropriate coconut basket
prepare coconut leaf for craft making
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. take a long coconut leaf and cut it in a half into two parts
PC5. shave the spine thin so it helps to bend or roll it later
PC6. cut the small triangle notches on both ends for tying together the rim/ the outer edge
PC7. bend the leaf in a circle to help in making the basket
preparing coconut leaf basket
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. weave the strips of coconut leaves next to each other so that the lines on each strips are
face up and from one continuous line to make base of the basket.
PC9. take the one strip of the coconut leaf and skip next 3 and weave 4 as over under
PC10. pull all the weaving strips out to the outer side/ other side.
PC11. make the section of up and down like there is 20 strips and leaving 10 up and 10 down
PC12. take the first one down and weave through the last one up and repeat moving down
PC13. make sure to do it in an order and tighten them all.
PC14. push all the strips to the other side as same as first and weave all the gaps
PC15. cut the tips to make the last weave easier
PC16. cut off all the extra strips and polish the basket.
check quality and productivity standards of coconut leaf craft

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| :--- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
