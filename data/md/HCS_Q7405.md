HCS/Q7405: Jute Products Artisan
Brief Job Description
A jute products artisan crafts eco-friendly items from natural jute fibres, creating unique bags, home decor,
accessories, etc. Their work blends traditional techniques with modern designs, highlighting the beauty
and sustainability of jute.
Personal Attributes
A jute products artisan is creative and resourceful, with a strong appreciation for sustainability. They are
skilled in handcrafting and have a keen eye for detail, ensuring each piece is both functional and
aesthetically pleasing.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

- 1. HCS/N7418: Jute Processing and Preparation

HCS/N7418: Jute Processing and Preparation
Description
This unit involves the extraction, retting, and preparation of jute fibers for production. The process begins
with harvesting the jute plant, followed by retting (soaking) to loosen the fibers. Afterward, fibers are
stripped, washed, dried, and spun into threads. The prepared jute is then ready for weaving or further use
in making products like bags, ropes, and textiles.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
cultivation and harvesting
retting and stripping
washing and drying
sorting and grading
weaving or twisting
finishing
Elements and Performance Criteria
Cultivation and Harvesting
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. jute is primarily grown in warm, humid climates, with India and Bangladesh being major
producers
PC2. jute plants are harvested when they are 4-6 months old, usually before the seedpods fully
mature
PC3. sickle is commonly used to cut the jute stalks
PC4. plants are cut close to the ground or pulled out by hand
PC5. jute stalks are bundled together and prepared for the retting process, which separates the
fibers from the woody stem
Retting and Stripping
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. retting is the process of separating the jute fibers from the woody core of the plant. The
harvested jute stalks are submerged in water (rivers, ponds, or canals) for 10 to 30 days,
depending on water temperature and conditions
PC7. soft water is ideal for retting, and the quality of water impacts the fiber quality
PC8. after retting, the jute fibers become loosened and can be easily extracted
PC9. workers strip the outer layer of the jute stalk by hand, peeling the fibers away from the
woody stem
PC10. use water to wash off any remaining plant material to get clean fibers
Washing and Drying
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC19. weave jute yarn into fabric, which can be used for sacks, bags or textile
PC20. twist jute yarn into thicker strands like ropes or twines
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. understand the ideal growing conditions (climate, soil type) for jute
KU2. importance of sustainable farming practices to ensure soil health and fiber quality
KU3. different retting methods (water retting, dew retting, etc.) that separate fibers from the stem
KU4. techniques for stripping the retted jute to remove the outer bark and obtain the inner fibers
KU5. importance of careful handling to avoid damaging the fibers
KU6. procedures for washing the stripped fibers to remove impurities
KU7. awareness of drying techniques to prevent mold and decay (sun-drying is common)
KU8. role of proper drying in maintaining fiber strength and durability
KU9. criteria for sorting fibers based on length, color, and quality
KU10. importance of grading in determining the market value and potential uses of the fibers
KU11. spinning methods to convert jute fibers into yarn (hand-spinning vs. machine-spinning)
KU12. techniques for weaving or knitting jute yarn into fabric
KU13. applications of jute fabric in various products (bags, rugs, etc.) and its eco-friendly attributes
KU14. finishing processes such as dyeing, bleaching, or treating to enhance the fabric properties
KU15. significance of finishing in determining the aesthetic and functional qualities of jute products
KU16. standards for evaluating jute products, including tensile strength, colorfastness, and
durability
KU17. role of quality control in maintaining product standards and consumer satisfaction
KU18. market demand for jute products and consumer trends toward sustainability
KU19. importance of promoting jute as an eco-friendly alternative to synthetic fibers and its role in
supporting local economies

Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. careful observation is required during harvesting, retting, and processing to ensure quality
control and to prevent damage to the fibers
GS2. steps, like stripping and spinning, require good hand-eye coordination and fine motor skills to
handle the fibers effectively
GS3. jute processing can be labor-intensive, requiring physical stamina to perform tasks such as
harvesting and washing
GS4. identifying and addressing issues during the processing stages, such as retting times or drying
conditions, is essential for producing high-quality fibers
GS5. properties of jute fibers and how they react during processing helps in making informed
decisions to improve quality
GS6. processing steps are done in groups, so good communication and collaboration skills are vital
for a smooth workflow
GS7. environmental impact of jute processing and adopting eco-friendly practices can enhance
product appeal and marketability

HCS/N7419: Product Design and Development
Description
The unit on Product Design and Development focuses on equipping jute artisans with the necessary
knowledge, skills, and techniques to design and develop marketable jute products. This unit covers all
stages of the design and development process, from ideation and material selection to crafting, and final
presentation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understanding jute as a material
perform research and ideation
design jute products
product development process
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Jute as a Material
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. use jute fibers to make sturdy products like bags, rugs, coasters, dolls and home decor items
PC2. learn about the properties, benefits, and limitations of jute fibers, including its durability,
texture, eco-friendliness, and versatility in product design
PC3. select appropriate jute materials for different products
PC4. create how jute’s natural qualities affect the design process
Perform Research and Ideation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. identify trends, consumer needs, and design opportunities by doing market research
PC6. develop creative product ideas by sketching and conceptualizing designs that meet both
aesthetic and functional requirements
PC7. look at what other artisans and manufacturers are producing helps identify gaps or areas for
innovation
Design Jute Products
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. start by sketching initial ideas, deciding on the shape, size, style, and function of the product
PC9. select the appropriate type of jute fiber (coarser for rugs, finer for fashion accessories)
PC10. blend jute with other materials like cotton or leather can also enhance functionality or
aesthetics
PC11. translate design concepts into physical prototypes using appropriate jute processing and
crafting techniques
PC12. test and refine prototypes to ensure functionality, durability, and market readiness

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. knowing the characteristics of jute fibers, such as strength, durability, biodegradability, and
texture, helps artisans decide how to best utilize the material
KU2. different qualities of jute, from coarse to fine, and how these influence the final product (e.g.,
bags, rugs, textiles)
KU3. familiarity with how to treat, dye, weave, and finish jute fibers to ensure durability and
aesthetic appeal
KU4. ability to design visually appealing products by combining colors, patterns, and textures that
suit customer preferences and market trends
KU5. ensure that products, such as bags, rugs, or home decor, are practical, user-friendly, and
meet consumer needs in terms of size, strength, and usability
KU6. design products that are comfortable and easy to use, such as handles on bags or soft-
textured jute items
KU7. keep up with current design trends, as well as sustainable product trends, helps artisans
develop contemporary, marketable products
KU8. create new products by experimenting with jute, combining it with other materials, or using
different techniques (e.g., blending jute with cotton or adding embellishments)
KU9. techniques for dyeing jute to create vibrant colors while maintaining fiber integrity
KU10. various jute weaving techniques, such as plain, twill, or satin weave, and knowing which is
best suited for specific products (e.g., twill weave for strength in bags)
KU11. familiarity with the tools and machinery used for cutting, stitching, and weaving jute
products, as well as safety procedures in handling these tools
KU12. use sustainable methods in jute production, such as using natural dyes and reducing water
consumption during processing
KU13. develop prototypes of new jute products for testing and refinement before mass production
KU14. ensure that jute products undergo testing for strength, durability, and functionality,
particularly for items like bags and home textiles that require long-lasting performance
KU15. understand how to translate design sketches into tangible models

HCS/N7420: Weaving and Crafting techniques
Description
This unit focuses on the essential weaving and crafting techniques employed by jute products artisans to
create high-quality, functional, and aesthetically pleasing items. It covers traditional and contemporary
methods used in the production of various jute products, including bags, rugs, and more jute products.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
introduction to jute fibers
basic weaving techniques
advanced weaving techniques
crafting techniques
finishing techniques
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Jute Fibers
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. obtain jute fiber from the stem of the jute plant
PC2. learn about the properties of jute, including its strength, texture, and environmental benefits.
PC3. learn about the different types of jute fibers and their uses in various products
PC4. process of jute involves several stages, including harvesting, retting, stripping, washing, and
drying
Basic Weaving Techniques
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. learn about the fundamental method of weaving (plain weave), where the warp and weft
threads alternate in a simple pattern
PC6. explore diagonal patterns (twill weave) that enhance texture and durability, ideal for bags
and heavy-duty items
PC7. learn the techniques used for creating sturdy and textured surfaces
Advanced Weaving Techniques
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. introduction to (Jacquard weaving) which is more complex patterns and designs that add
visual interest to jute products
PC9. learn about the knotting techniques to create decorative and functional elements for jute
products, enhancing their aesthetic appeal
PC10. create leno weave a strong and airy fabric by twisting pairs of warp threads around the weft
PC11. float the weft threads over several warp threads before going under one (Satin Weave),
resulting in a smooth, glossy surface
PC12. weave two layers of fabric simultaneously (Double Weave), allowing for the creation of
complex structures and designs

HCS/N7421: Packaging and Quality control
Description
This unit focuses on the essential processes involved in packaging and quality control within the jute
products artisan role. It covers the methods and techniques used to protect jute products during
transportation and presentation while ensuring that the final products meet established quality standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understand packaging principles
select appropriate packaging
implement quality control procedures
conduct quality inspections
document and report quality control findings
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand Packaging Principles
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify different types of packaging materials suitable for jute products, including eco-
friendly options
PC2. describe the role of packaging in protecting products and enhancing visual appeal
Select Appropriate Packaging
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Choose materials that provide adequate protection while being compatible with jute products
PC4. evaluate packaging designs based on product type, size, and target market
PC5. demonstrate the ability to create effective packaging solutions that reflect brand identity
PC6. use colors, graphics, and typography that align with the product’s style and appeal to
consumer preferences
Implement Quality Control Procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. develop and apply quality control checklists to assess finished jute products for defects in
craftsmanship, design, and functionality
PC8. identify and rectify common quality issues in jute products
Conduct Quality Inspections
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. perform routine inspections at various stages of production, including after material
preparation, during weaving or crafting, and before packaging
PC10. use measurement tools and techniques to verify product specifications
PC11. record the results of each inspection, noting any defects or areas for improvement
Document and Report Quality Control Findings
