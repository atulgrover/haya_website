#### DWC/Q0110: House  Manager
Brief Job Description
The individual at work is solely responsible for overseeing and governing the ins and outs of a household in
order to assist family in accomplishing daily activities and to ensure that the family is able to operate as
smoothly as possible. The individual will manage every detail of the household, from upkeep and
maintenance to day to day task lists. He or she may live on-site in the employers home, have a set number
of hours per day to come in, or be on call to take on tasks as needed, depending on the needs of the
employer and home.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to possess magnificent interpersonal competencies and should have
outstanding supervisory and organizational abilities. The individual must be responsible, efficient, discreet,
organized and strong problem solver.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. DWC/N0120: Manage and supervise day-to-day operational activities happening around the household
2. DWC/N0122: Oversee household staff and other stakeholders
3. DWC/N0123: Perform household related administrative and documentation duties
4. DWC/N9902: Display standards of hygiene and work etiquettes
5. DWC/N9903: Maintain a clean and secure working environment
6. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)
7. DWC/N0228: Communicate effectively with individuals at all levels and practice inclusivity

#### DWC/N0120: Manage  and supervise day-to-day operational activities
happening around  the household
Description
This OS unit talks about managing and overseeing activities that take place in the household on a daily
basis along with managing special events or activities.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Oversee routine tasks of the household
Manage infrequent activities or tasks that occur on some special days in household
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Oversee routine tasks of the household
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure that the interior of the house is clean and well- maintained at all times in an orderly
fashion
- PC2. organise and manage daily cleaning of the kitchen and other important areas of the
household
- PC3. supervise daily accomplishment of cleaning requirements of the household
- PC4. maintain cleaning log including normal performance time values
- PC5. oversee procurement of household goods such as grocery, supplies and other requested
items
- PC6. assure that all routine household supplies are always on hand and nothing that is
foreseeable is overlooked
- PC7. perform shopping and errands in accordance with employer’s guidelines such as shoe
repairs/shine, pick up prescriptions, etc
- PC8. prepare inventory of the household and restock things as needed
- PC9. inspect the household on a daily basis for things that need repair such as appliances, cook
ware etc
- PC10. recommend and obtain appropriate equipment to perform everyday tasks of the household
- PC11. perform or see that the laundry is accomplished
- PC12. perform laundering and putting away clothing, or changing linens
- PC13. oversee that the foot wear is being taken care off
- PC14. maintain cars and other personal vehicles
- PC15. handle simple house maintenance like changing air filters, light bulbs, diffusers, etc
- PC16. perform pet care based on the requirement
- PC17. prepare healthy and nutritious meals based on requirement and family’s acceptable eating
standards and guidelines
- PC18. note and bring to the employer about any exceptional conditions, issues and problems

| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |
