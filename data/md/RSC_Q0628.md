#### RSC/Q0628: Corrugator Dry End Machine Operator
Brief Job Description
The Dry End Operator is responsible for operating and monitoring the dry end of the corrugator line,
including the slitter scorer, cutoff, and double facer. They perform setup of production jobs via control
panels, monitor line speed, board warp, delamination, calliper, and scoring accuracy. The role involves
coordinating with the wet end team to maintain board quality and moisture, adjusting board temperature
using hot plates, and monitoring steam pressure. Operators must also maintain cleanliness and ensure
safe working conditions.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be physically fit to work for long hours attentive with good observational
skills and able to work safely and collaboratively in a fast paced production environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N0624: Set up and Operate Dry End Corrugator Machines
2. RSC/N0625: Monitor and Maintain Board Quality at Dry End
3. RSC/N0626: Perform Preventive Checks and Minor Maintenance of Dry End Machinery
4. RSC/N0627: Maintain Workplace Safety and Cleanliness
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### RSC/N0624: Set up and Operate Dry End Corrugator Machines
Description
This NOS covers the skills and knowledge required to set up and operate dry end corrugator machines,
ensuring correct machine settings, smooth production flow, and product quality. It also involves
maintaining safety standards, minimizing downtime, and coordinating with team members for continuous
line efficiency.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare and read job lineups
Set up machines as per job
Operate and monitor machine
Maintain productivity and safety
Conduct shift-end shutdown and handover
Ensure quality control during operation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare and read job lineups
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. read and interpret production job lineup sheet
- PC2. identify job parameters like cut length, scoring dimensions, number of ups, board type
- PC3. verify raw materials required for the job
Set up machines as per job
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. select correct job from HMI/control panel
- PC5. input cut length, scoring dimensions, and job name on the control interface
- PC6. position slitter blades and scorer wheels manually or using auto control
- PC7. test-run first sheet and verify alignment and accuracy
Operate and monitor machine
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. start machines following SOP
- PC9. monitor running speed and sheet count
- PC10. observe output for board defects like misaligned cuts, wrong score positions
- PC11. adjust machine parameters in case of deviation
Maintain productivity and safety
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. ensure machines run at optimal speed for each job
- PC13. coordinate with stacking area for smooth transition
- PC14. follow lockout-tagout for minor cleaning or jam removal

#### RSC/N0625: Monitor and Maintain Board Quality at Dry End
Description
This NOS covers the skills and knowledge required to set up, operate, and monitor corrugator dry end
machines to achieve accurate cutting, scoring, and board stacking as per job specifications. It includes
maintaining product quality, ensuring safe machine handling, and coordinating with the production team
for smooth line operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Monitor board parameters
Communicate with wet end team
Control board temperature
Control warp and quality deviations
Record and report
Inspect edge quality and scoring
Prevent production delays
Maintain cleanliness
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Monitor board parameters
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. monitor calliper (thickness) of the board at regular intervals
- PC2. inspect board for warping, delamination, glue separation
- PC3. measure board length consistency across output sheets
Communicate with wet end team
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. inform wet end operator about excess/low moisture
- PC5. report signs of poor glue bonding or wet board
- PC6. coordinate steam pressure concerns with boiler section
Control board temperature
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. monitor board temperature near the hot plate
- PC8. adjust hot plate zone temperature as required
- PC9. verify temperature feedback from control panel
Control warp and quality deviations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. identify upward/downward warp patterns
- PC11. change tension or apply warp control settings
- PC12. ensure straightness of board through double facer output

#### RSC/N0626: Perform Preventive Checks and Minor Maintenance  of Dry
End Machinery
Description
This NOS covers skills and knowledge required to carry out routine preventive checks, minor adjustments,
cleaning, and basic maintenance of dry-end corrugator machinery to ensure smooth and efficient production. It also includes timely reporting of abnormalities to minimize breakdowns and maintain safety
standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform routine preventive checks
Identify abnormalities
Perform basic adjustments
Conduct tool changes and cleaning
Record and escalate
Support emergency stoppage
Maintain safety and tools
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform routine preventive checks
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. inspect blades, scorers and rollers before job
- PC2. check lubrication points on slitter scorer/ cutoff
- PC3. inspect sensor alignment and condition
- PC4. observe belts, drives, and motors for wear
Identify abnormalities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. identify loose belts, misaligned scorers or worn parts
- PC6. detect strange noises, jerks, or heating issues
- PC7. isolate and report critical issues to technician
Perform basic adjustments
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. realign scorer blades or cutoff blades if required
- PC9. adjust roll pressure for scoring or slitting
- PC10. re-tension belts under supervision
Conduct tool changes and cleaning
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. replace dull blades with new ones
- PC12. clean the dust chute and jammed boards

#### RSC/N0627: Maintain Workplace Safety and Cleanliness
Description
This NOS covers maintaining safe working conditions by following safety protocols, using PPE, and
identifying hazards in the workplace. It also includes ensuring machine zones are kept clean, organized,
and compliant with 5S and housekeeping standards to support efficient and hazard-free operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Follow safety protocols
Maintain cleanliness
Conduct basic 5S
