#### ASC/Q3307: Car Painting
Brief Job Description
Car painters (refinishers) are responsible for reinstating the pre-accident paint finish to cars after the
structure and/or the panels have been repaired or replaced. They may also be asked to completely repaint
a whole vehicle either to change its colour or reinstate its newness. Car painters may also become
involved in matching colours to an original colour no longer available or to colours that prove difficult to
match. A car painter must match the colour, shade, and texture of the adjoining panels that are not being
painted.
Personal Attributes
The person should be patient, organised, team-oriented and have the ability to work for long hours in
adverse conditions. They should be keen observers and have an eye for detail and quality.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ASC/N9824: Work organization and management (Car painting)
2. ASC/N9825: Communication and interpersonal skills (Car painting)

7. ASC/N3319: Colour evaluation and colour adjustment
8. ASC/N3320: Design, layout, and measurement
9. ASC/N3321: Remove minor damage and defects from painted and non-painted surfaces

#### ASC/N9824: Work organization and management   (Car painting)
Description
This NOS unit is about implementing safety, planning work, adopting sustainable practices for optimising
the use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage work and resources
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage work and resources
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. apply occupational Health, Safety, and Environmental regulations and best practice related
to the car painting industry
- PC2. use correctly and maintain personal protective clothing and equipment
- PC3. set-up, use, adjust, and maintain all specialist application equipment
- PC4. set up, adjust, and use all specialist preparation and drying equipment
- PC5. promote health and safety in the workplace
- PC6. apply all recommendations and guidance provided by suppliers and manufacturers of
equipment or products
- PC7. adhere to MSDS (Manufacturers Safety Data Sheets)
- PC8. adopt correct procedures for handling and disposal of environmentally harmful products
- PC9. only use products that are Volatile Organic Compound (VOC) compliant
- PC10. adapt materials to take account of the impact of the environment and climate on paints and
products
- PC11. maintain clean spraying environment within paint areas
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Current occupational Health, Safety, and Environmental regulations related to the car painting
industry
- KU2. Correct use, storage and maintenance of personal protective equipment and clothing
- KU3. All recommendations and information published by the supplier or manufacturer of products and equipment
- KU4. The procedures and process for maintaining and using specialist equipment
- KU5. Terminology that relates to paint materials, processes, and applications
- KU6. The importance of the correct handling and disposal of environmentally harmful products

#### ASC/N3316: Application of adhesion promoters and primers
Description
This NOS is about applying adhesion promoters and primers on the vehicle body/panels.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Applying adhesion promoters and primers
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Applying adhesion promoters and primers
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. apply suitable primers and or fillers to the substrate and appropriate for the process being
used: Etch primers; Primer surfaces; Primer fillers; Plastic primers; UV primers
- PC2. apply the correct procedures for sanding (flatting) primer fillers
- PC3. reinstate the corrosion protection of the panels being painted
- PC4. apply seam sealers
- PC5. follow the Original Engineering Manufacturer (OEM) or paint manufacturer’s (Technical Data
Sheet – TDS) recommendations
- PC6. measure out materials carefully to minimize environmental effects and cost implications
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The range of adhesion promoters and primers available
- KU2. The purpose of adhesion promoters and primers
- KU3. The context of where and when various adhesion promoters and primers are used
- KU4. Mixing and application techniques for each of the adhesion promoters and primers
- KU5. The preparation and application process for the full range of seam sealers, e.g. gap sealers,
weld sealers, seam sealers, etc.
- KU6. Which materials to select for a particular application
- KU7. The drying characteristics of each adhesion promoter or primer

Applying base coat and ground coat

- KU1. How to access information related to colour and application
- KU2. Types and specifications of car paints and their uses
- KU3. Warranty procedures applied to particular vehicles
- KU4. The correct use of equipment used in applying base coats and ground coats
- KU5. Specialist paint finishes
- KU6. The impact upon cost and environment of over mixing materials
- KU7. Wateborne basecoats

#### ASC/N3318: Clear coat application
Description
This NOS is about applying clear coat of paint on the vehicle body/panels.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Applying clear coat of paint
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Applying clear coat of paint
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify, mix, and apply clear coats correctly
- PC2. adjust spray gun to achieve the correct outlet pressure, fan width and fan shape
- PC3. apply clear coats to match existing finishes (matt clear coat, ceramic, and soft healing clear
coat)
- PC4. apply clear coats as per paint manufacturer’s instructions to avoid defects such as runs,
excessive orange peel, etc.
- PC5. follow the Original Equipment Engineering Manufacturer (OEM) or paint manufacturer’s
(Technical Data Sheet – TDS) recommendations
- PC6. dispose of unused clear coat in an environmentally safe manor
- PC7. maintain organized and clean work areas
- PC8. prevent over-use of products
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The purpose of a clear coat
- KU2. The process for identifying, mixing, and applying clear coats
- KU3. The importance of following manufacturer instructions
- KU4. The need for flexible additives as required
- KU5. The spray gun set up and adjustment for clear coat materials
- KU6. The application of UV clearcoats
- KU7. The correct gun pressure, speed, distance, and overlap required to produce an excellent finish
with clear coats and achieve high gloss levels

#### ASC/N3321: Remove  minor damage  and defects from painted and non-

Description
This NOS is about removing minor damage and defects from painted and non-painted surfaces of the
vehicle,
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Removing minor paint damage and defects
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Removing minor paint damage and defects
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the types of defects that may occur on a painted surface such as dirt nibs, pin holes,
runs, environmental effects, etc.
- PC2. apply correct repair procedures to remove or repair paint defects
- PC3. carry out ‘smart’ repairs to small areas of damage
- PC4. evaluate the extend of minor panel damage and plan work accordingly to rectify this damage
- PC5. wet and dry sand clear coat to remove and rectify defects
- PC6. apply polyester and epoxy fillers to repair small stone chip damage to panel surfaces
- PC7. prepare and blend invisibly scratch and scuff damage
- PC8. reinstate original gloss levels using polishing techniques and materials
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Types of paint-related defects
- KU2. The correct repair procedures to rectify minor paint damage or defects
- KU3. The procedures and materials required to rectify minor panel damage
- KU4. Techniques and materials for removal and repair of minor paint defects
- KU5. Techniques to invisibly perform a spot repair or blend paint in confined areas
