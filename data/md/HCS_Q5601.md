HCS/Q5601: Carpet Quality Checker
Brief Job Description
A Carpet Final Inspector performs physical checks, ensures complainces to buyers requirements, ensure
that carpet is free from any technical defects and carries out finished products inspection
Personal Attributes
The job individual to have excellent understanding of each process in the carpet manufacturing, high
concentration, excellent vision, eye for detail and understanding of critical check-points for possible
defects identification etc.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HCS/N5601: Understanding of quality prameters and perform quality checks
- 2. HCS/N5602: Understanding of the value chain involved in carpet manufacturing
- 3. HCS/N9901: Coordinate with colleagues and work as a team
- 4. HCS/N9913: Maintain health, safety and security at workplace

HCS/N5601: Understanding of quality prameters and perform quality
checks
Description
This OS unit is about understanding of necessary quality parameters that are defined for quality checking.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
discuss on quality check parameters with management
perform quality checks and suggest solution
report issues to the management
present the work done by workers and provide feedback
develop and maintain quality procedures and checklist and share with the production managers and
field supervisors
Elements and Performance Criteria
Discuss on quality check parameters with management / QC Head
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. communicate with management / qc head on various quality parameters.
PC2. understand the rationale behind the defined parameters.
PC3. relate the stage of production which is associated with the parameter.
PC4. discuss the parameters with the quality team.
PC5. clarify any doubts with management / qc head.
PC6. provide suggestions on various quality criteria wherever required.
PC7. highlight any discrepancy if observed due to changes in the process.
PC8. identify what aids must be used for checking.
PC9. know the precise manner of examining the carpet.
PC10. communicate within the team and workers.
Perform quality checks and suggest solutions
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. perform quality checks keeping the various stages of carpet manufacturing.

PC12. conduct checks on critical areas which could hamper the final output. some of these quality
checks are as follows:a. dyeing: checks if there are any uneven or patchy stains on the
carpet, wool not dyed as per design map etc.b. washing: checks whether the carpet is
washed properly with appropriate chemicals, color fade etc.c. weaving: ensure that there is
no uneven pile cutting, no broken threads, wrong weaving design, holes other than weaving
pattern etc.d. latexing and third-backing: ensure that latex is dried properly, tufted
withdrawal force is adequate, there are no defects in third backing, proper pasting of nevaar
etc.e. binding: ensure the carpet is stitched properly from all ends.f. embossing: ensure that
design is appearing over the yarn pile and is visible properly; yarn is not overlapping the
design etc.g. finishing: ensure that carpet is sheared and trimmed properly, there are no
dusts or any metal or spools on the carpet, it is not torn from anywhere, colors are matching
in terms of their finish, the final product is matching with the buyers requirements.h.
packing: ensure that labelling is done, color codes are marked, bar codes are there, carpet is
cleaned and properly rolled etc.
PC13. suggest solutions at every stage where the defects are found.
PC14. ensures that end product is free from any defect.
PC15. provides final approval and sign on the clearance receipt.
Report issues to the management
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC16. informs management on some anticipated delays on account of quality.
PC17. explain clearly the reason for delays and corrective action taken to expedite.
PC18. communicate tentative timelines by when the defect shall be addressed.
PC19. seek management views and advise.
PC20. demonstrate ownership and responsibility.
Present the work done by workers and provide feedback
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC21. identify the area of concern at particular stage(s).
PC22. discuss with the worker engaged for undertaking that specific activity.
PC23. give feedback on the quality of work.
PC24. discuss on the corrective measures with the worker.
PC25. discuss with the field supervisor (if applicable).
PC26. discuss timelines by when the corrective measures can be implemented.
PC27. understand technical gaps with the worker for performing a particular task.
Develop and maintain quality procedures and checklist and share with the production managers and field
supervisors
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC28. determine standards operating procedures, checklists etc and discuss with management /
quality head.
PC29. share the checklists / manuals with the production managers, field supervisors and workers.
PC30. suggest process improvements methods and techniques based on past learning or
experiences.
PC31. document each and every check-point minutely and comprehensively.
PC32. ensure complete adherence to the process.

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. organizational policies related to quality compliance.
KU2. organization structure and escalation matrix.
KU3. policies and procedures for conducting quality checks.
KU4. quality assurance methods approved by the company.
KU5. format of presenting the information captured during quality checks.
KU6. importance of inspection.
KU7. techniques of carpet inspection.
KU8. understanding of value chain in the carpet manufacturing process.
KU9. various stages of production.
KU10. quality checklists.
KU11. analysis of repeated issues during quality checking.
KU12. tools and techniques used in the quality process.
KU13. good vision and experience for colors and asthetics.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. prepare status and progress reports.
GS2. write memos and e-mail to customers, co-workers, and vendors to provide them with work
updates and to request appropriate information without english language errors regarding
grammar or sentence construct.
GS3. keep abreast with the latest knowledge by reading brochures, pamphlets, and product
information sheets.
GS4. discuss task lists, schedules, and work-loads with co-workers.
GS5. keep co-workers and supervisors informed about progress.
GS6. make decisions on a suitable course of action.
GS7. plan and work in an effective and balanced manner.
GS8. plan work assigned on a daily basis and provide estimates of time required for each piece of
work.
GS9. build customer relationships and use customer centric approach.
GS10. think through the problem, evaluate the possible solution(s) and suggest an optimum /best
possible solution(s).
GS11. use effective problem solution techniques depending on the rating and context of the
problem.
GS12. use the existing data to arrive at specific data points.
GS13. use the existing data points for improving current systems and processes.
GS14. use the existing data points to make revisions and improvisations in the quality data
checklists.

HCS/N5602: Understanding of the value chain involved in carpet
manufacturing
Description
This OS unit is about understanding of value chain involved and basic business management of the carpet
manufacturing sector.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
basic understanding of value chain
coordination with other team members
Elements and Performance Criteria
Basic understanding of value chain
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. basic understanding about the value chain right from procurement of raw materials till the
final inspection and dispatch.
PC2. understanding the importance of role of quality inspection in the carpet manufacturing
process.
PC3. understanding the impact and importance of various processes involved in the carpet
manufacturing process (put this in qualification also).
PC4. manage the work pressure in case of high volumes.
PC5. create a back-up and train other individual to meet contingencies.
PC6. resolve queries of supervisor and manage expectations.
Coordination with other team members
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. receive orders and instructions from supervisors.
PC8. manage and train other people in the assembly line.
PC9. interact and clarify doubts.
PC10. adhere to instructions given by supervisor.
PC11. help other people and provide active support in their tasks.
PC12. obtain regular feedback from supervisors.
PC13. communicate clearly and effectively with other team members, supervisors for effective
delivery of work.
PC14. maintain etiquettes, politeness and demonstrate responsible and disciplined behaviour to
other team members.
PC15. resolve conflicts for smooth workflow.
PC16. work harmoniously with other colleagues, coordinate and cooperate and support each other
on their performance goals.

Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will

elective/option NOS/set of NOS.

aggregate for the QP.
