#### TSC/Q0901: Ginning Helper
Brief Job Description
The ginning helper is responsible for carrying out various labour intensive jobs in the ginning sector under
supervision by maintaining health, safety and security in the ginning sector. The person can seek
employment in a ginning factory.
Personal Attributes
Needs to have basic knowledge of cleanliness at workplace, sound health, ability to work diligently.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### TSC/N0901: Undertake  loading, unloading and heaping in the ginning
sector
Description
The unit deals with necessary skills, knowledge and attributes required for a ginningworker to load, unload
and heap materials in the ginning sector
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Ensure proper loading, unloading and heaping of materials under supervision
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Ensure proper loading, unloading and heaping of materials under supervision
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. get count sheet, daily targets of unloading and loading of cotton from the ginning supervisor
- PC2. unload the kapas/boras from truck based on shade marked on them, mentioning where the cotton is picked and store it to the respective area by using proper material handling tools like
bale hooks, bale trolleys, mechanical conveyors etc.
- PC3. report to the supervisor regarding any damage/ discrepancies while unloading of kapas/ boras
- PC4. ensure the heaping area is clean before unloading the cotton
- PC5. carry out work in a hygienic condition by cleaning frequently the fiber dust deposits and wear
headwear/caps, masks, gloves, etc. to avoid fiber dust inhalation
- PC6. load the pressed bales to truck by mechanical conveyor
- PC7. ensure trucks/carts/tractors are compactly loaded with cotton bales and make it free from external contacts while transportation
- PC8. carry out all unloading of kapas/ boras based on color tag provided with them at their respective heaping areas
- PC9. heap layer by layer of cotton as per instructions
- PC10. dispense the kapas/ boras to the dispensing machine
- PC11. ensure that heap is covered with tarpaulin to protect from rain, sun and airborne impurities
- PC12. open cotton bags by un-sewing instead of cutting twine into small piece
- PC13. store bales on clean, paved and preferably roofed platforms or in well ventilated, covered godown
- PC14. remove all immature fibres and contaminants like metal wires, bolts, machine parts, leather
pieces, spilt oil cleaning cloths, etc.
- PC15. collect immature fibers and contaminations in separate bags
- PC16. handle all materials such as kapas, seed and lint with care to avoid mixing with foreign matter
- PC17. ensure that the premises are protected from any harmful chemical, plastics and flammable materials

#### TSC/N0902: Assist production department in ginning sector
Description
The unit deals with necessary skills, knowledge and attributes to assist productiondepartment in ginning
sector.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist production department
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist production department
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. transport the heaped cotton to the ginning department in case of manual feed
- PC2. place the heaped cotton near the ginning machine
- PC3. feed the heaped material to auto feeder in case of chute feed
- PC4. assist bale press operators for feeding the lint to traditional bale pressing machine
- PC5. transport the lint/ raw cotton where ever necessary as directed by ginning supervisor
- PC6. assist the ginning machine operators by supplying or holding materials or tools, etc
- PC7. carry out heaping process near the ginning machine for processing small quantity directed by
the ginning supervisor
- PC8. collect the cotton seed from the ginning machine and store in their respective area
- PC9. pack the seeds in desired quantity in sacks as directed by the supervisor
- PC10. mark and tag information provided by the supervisor on cotton seed sacks
- PC11. transport and store the seed bags at their respective area
