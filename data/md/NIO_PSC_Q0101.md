NIO/PSC/Q0101: Drinking Water Purifier Technician
Brief Job Description
The individual should have the knowledge about water purifier installation and repair process. This course
will train in different areas of water purifier repair and maintenance. This course comprises of modules on
different parts of water purifier system, water quality testing devices, repairing the common faults,
replacing faulty components, dealing with customer etc. and it has wide potential to provide employment
to both urban and rural youth of the country.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to possess strong technical aptitude, good mechanical skill, proficiency in
using hand and power tools along with the ability to work with various plumbing and electrical equipments.
They should be dedicated, responsible, trustworthy, attentive, honest and ethical.

NIO/PSC/N0101: Install water purifier system (Domestic and Commercial)
Description
This unit talks about Installation of water purifier system in domestic and commercial setup.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Pre-installation activities
Site Preparation,
Electrical Connections,
Installing Pre-Filters and Post-Filters
Elements and Performance Criteria
Pre-installation activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Evaluate the quality of the water source intended for purification
PC2. select the most suitable water purifier system for specific needs
PC3. Select the appropriate purification technology and system capacity
PC4. Consider factors such as the type of contaminants to be removed, the desired level of
purification, water consumption rate, and available space for installation. Properly sizing the
system ensures optimal performance and efficiency.
PC5. Evaluate the available options and select the most suitable water purifier for your needs..
PC6. Determine if any permits or approvals are required for installing a water purifier system in
your area
PC7. Follow regulations or codes that need to be followed
PC8. Contact the appropriate local authorities or agencies to understand the legal requirements
and ensure compliance if any
PC9. Identify the best location for installing the water purifier system.
PC10. Explain factors such as proximity to the water source, access to plumbing connections,
availability of electrical outlets (if required), drainage options, and convenient access for
maintenance and filter replacements
PC11. Ensure that there is adequate space around the installation location for easy access during
installation and future maintenance activities
PC12. Allow for sufficient clearance to connect plumbing lines, electrical connections, and access
the system for servicing and heat dissipation.
PC13. Ensure that these utilities are functioning properly and meet the requirements of the water
purifier system being installed.
PC14. Understand the installation instructions and guidelines provided by the manufacturer of the
water purifier system. These instructions may include specific requirements,
recommendations, or precautions that need to be followed during installation

NIO/PSC/N0102: Perform service and maintenance of Water purifier
system (Domestic and Commercial)
Description
This OS unit talks about maintenance and troubleshooting of water purifier system
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following: Routine Maintenance of Water purifier system and fault finding
and Troubleshooting of Water purifier system
Elements and Performance Criteria
Routine Maintenance of Water purifier system and fault finding
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Monitor the system's pressure to ensure it is within the recommended range.
PC2. check the electrical connections, cables, and plugs for any signs of wear, damage, or loose
connections.
PC3. Identify and check codes or alarms, and address and resolve any identified issues
accordingly
PC4. Maintain proper documentation of the maintenance activities
PC5. Obtain feedback from the users regarding the water quality, system reliability, and overall
performance should be considered to gauge the effectiveness of the maintenance process.
PC6. Utilize diagnostic tools, if available, to assess the system's performance and identify specific
faults or malfunctions.
PC7. Follow appropriate steps to repair or replace the faulty component or system part.
PC8. Follow the manufacturer's guidelines or consult a professional if necessary.
Troubleshooting of Water purifier system
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Carry out thorough cleaning of water purifier to remove any dirt, sediment, or debris that
may accumulate over time.
PC10. Evaluate water quality by testing the water for parameters such as pH, total dissolved solids
(TDS), microbial contamination, and chemical impurities.
PC11. Ensure that the flow rate of the purified water should be within the acceptable range
specified by the manufacturer.
PC12. Check the water purifier for any leaks or drips during maintenance.
PC13. identify faults and repair promptly to prevent water wastage and system damage.
PC14. conduct tests to ensure that the system is functioning properly and meets the required
performance criteria mentioned earlier.
PC15. conduct functional tests on the system to identify any abnormal behavior or malfunctions.
