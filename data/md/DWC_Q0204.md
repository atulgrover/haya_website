DWC/Q0204: Care Homes Supervisor
Brief Job Description
A Care Homes Supervisor is responsible for assisting in the management of Day Care Centers, Old Age
Homes, PwD Homes, or a combination of these facilities. Their duties include organizing and overseeing
various operations and activities within the care homes, such as maintaining cleanliness, hygiene, and
sanitation, ensuring proper Activities of Daily Living (ADL), providing adequate nutrition, and ensuring the
safety and security of residents. Additionally, they must ensure the care homes remain well-equipped and
fully functional while supervising caregiving services, fostering growth and rehabilitation, and facilitating
recreational activities as required.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual be physically adequate, energetic, cheerful, patient and composed, to be
able to be care for the children, PwD or the Senior Citizens. This person should be sensitive, empathetic,
dedicated and attitude to serve. They should be able to multitask, and good at supervisory skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. DWC/N0222: Assist in management of the care homes
- 2. DWC/N0226: Maintain a good relationship with clients and their families/attendants/guardians
- 3. DWC/N0225: Carry out administrative tasks
- 4. DWC/N9902: Maintain service standards and communicate effectively
- 5. DWC/N9903: Maintain health, hygiene and safety standards
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: DayCare for Children
This OS unit deals with knowledge and skills required to extend support with laws and rights, monitor and
supervise the various activities performed in day care for children such as recreation of children,
preparation and distribution of wholesome diet and supervise the care givers for ADL.
- 1. DWC/N0249: Day care for Children

DWC/N0222: Assist in management of the care homes
Description
This unit covers knowledge and skills for a supervisor to assist the management of Day Care/ Old Age
Homes/ PwD Homes.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify the requirements of care homes
Perform administrative duties in care homes
Supervise cleanliness and maintenance of the care homes
Provide support in recruitment and training of caregivers/other staff and report for further evaluation
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify the requirements of care homes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. categorise different types of care homes (day care/old age home/PwD homes)
PC2. assist in preparation of care plans and implementation of the same
PC3. manage supplies/equipment and maintain accounts as well as record of admission
Perform administrative duties in care homes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. prepare the list of duties of staff of the care homes
PC5. prepare the day care’s plans and allocate responsibilities
PC6. • supervise the processes, such as cleaning, caring and feeding to be followed in the care
• home
PC7. carry out regular inspection of the care home to ensure smooth functioning
PC8. ensure maintenance of day-to-day record for staff, people under care, stocks and visitors
PC9. obtain feedback from the staff and people under care for procuring shortages or addressing
the aspects of rendering care
Supervise cleanliness and maintenance of the care homes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. identify the materials such as detergent, mops and brooms required for cleaning and ensure
timely replenishment
PC11. inspect and supervise daily/periodic cleanliness and maintenance of the premises
PC12. ensure that all equipment and appliances are properly checked for correct adjustments and
regularly maintained
PC13. ensure that care homes are appropriately equipped and functional
PC14. ensure materials in care homes are stacked/arranged neatly for easy access and use
PC15. identify any damages or issue in working of appliances or equipment and report to manager
in case replacement or major repair is required

DWC/N0226: Maintain a good relationship with clients and their
families/attendants/guardians
Description
This OS unit deals with knowledge and skills required for maintenance of good relationship with people
under care and their families/ attendants/guardians
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the following:
Analyse the behaviour of people under care
Maintain good relations and communication with the parents/guardians of the people under care
Resolve conflicts
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding the behaviour of people under care
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. observe and record the achievements of growth milestones of people under care to share the
observations with the parents/guardians.
PC2. study/ observe and record mood swings and change behaviour mood swings and changes in
behaviour of the people under care.
PC3. train the attendants/staff/care givers to deal with the individuals in distress/overwhelming
situations
Maintain good relations and communication with the parents/guardians of the people under care
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. identify the concerns of the parents/guardians
PC5. discuss the conditions and welfare of the children/wards with their parents/guardians
PC6. update parents/guardians on the condition of the people under care, including during
emergencies
PC7. display compassion and understanding towards the concerns of parents/guardians of the
people under care when interacting with them
PC8. attend calls and reply to emails from parents/guardians promptly
Resolve conflicts
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. identify the root cause of conflicts that take place at the care homes
PC10. encourage the caregivers and attendants to be patient and forbearing
PC11. address conflicts as soon as they occur
PC12. assure the caregivers and people under care about their welfare and unassailability
PC13. ensure the safety and protection of the caregiving staff from the adverse effects of the
conflicts and odd behaviours

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. different types of disabilities/life conditions and their implications
KU2. behaviour of the PwD with different challenges/deficiencies, essentially intellectual
deficiencies, and those of the elderly at different stages of their advanced age
KU3. stimuli for odd behaviours of PwD and elderly and how to address them
KU4. various life conditions of the PwD and elderly in different stages and ways to deal with them

staff
GS3. identify the conflicts that can arise with/between the people under care, parents/guardians
and caregivers
GS4. study the behaviour of the people under care
GS5. identify the problem, and find solutions based on consideration of the factors having bearing
on the problem
GS6. maintain warm relationships with employers, staff and vendors
GS7. analyse working environment and learn from such an analysis
GS8. seek and respond to queries from superiors, visitors, staff and people under care
GS9. communicate with all stakeholders politely and patiently, using gender sensitive language
GS10. plan and prioritise periodic work and implement legal compliances, policies/rules/orders and
instructions
GS11. ensure efficient time management and optimum resource utilisation
GS12. follow basic work ethics such as punctuality, discipline and regularity
GS13. follow the process of organising and analysing information relevant to work
GS14. work out methods to make the caring process for individuals, both young and old, more
effective

Description
This OS unit deals with knowledge and skills required to manage supplies/ equipment, maintain accounts,

Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage supplies and equipment
Maintain accounts of the care homes
Maintain record of the admissions and attendances
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage supplies and equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. list the equipment and other supplies needed in a care home
PC2. classify materials and equipment for office/care home and pantry
PC3. prepare the budget category wise
PC4. procure the materials as per requirement
PC5. maintain the registers/ledgers of the equipment/supplies/materials as required in the care
homes
PC6. carry out daily/periodic inspection to ensure that the equipment is serviceable/functional
PC7. identify the damages and make arrangements for the repairs/replacements
PC8. carry out periodic stock taking and report the damages/losses to the management of the
care homes
PC9. investigate the reasons for damages/loss and report the same to the superiors at the care
home
PC10. train fellow staff/caregivers on various aspects of the equipment/supply/appliances correctly
PC11. ensure economic and optimum utilisation of the materials, supplies and essential services
Maintain accounts of the care homes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. prepare a rough but consolidated budget and define the flow of money in terms of receipts
and expenses
PC13. maintain daily/periodic record of expenses as per the accounting process and different heads
and keep the seniors and accounts staff informed
PC14. collect the fees, where tasked to do so, and update the account record
PC15. maintain receipts/issue vouchers and invoices, etc. for different expenses as well as services
Maintain the record of the admissions and attendances
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC16. follow the process of admissions
