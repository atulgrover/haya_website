NIE/ELE/Q0802: Biomedical Equipment Maintenance Assistant
Brief Job Description
The course covers tools and techniques for servicing medical equipment, including soldering and test
instruments, while exploring electrical concepts like current, voltage, and power, elucidating passive
component principles such as resistors, capacitors, and inductors. Detailed study of active components like
semiconductors and integrated circuits offers insights into design, testing, and applications in medical
instrumentation. Biomedical instrumentation topics encompass the man-instrument system, physiological
systems, bio-electric potentials, and microscope usage, alongside thorough examination of ECG machines,
pH meters, patient monitors, and diagnostic equipment, including their principles, testing, and calibration.
The course culminates with a focus on employability skills to prepare learners for success in the medical
equipment and devices industry.
Personal Attributes
A proficient biomedical equipment maintenance assistant should possess sharp observational skills and a
solid understanding of electronics fundamentals, including voltage and current waveforms. They must
demonstrate practical expertise in using tools such as multimeters, CROs, and soldering on PCBs.
Additionally, a strong foundation in biomedical engineering principles and safety protocols is essential for
ensuring patient and equipment safety. Effective communication and collaborative abilities are necessary
for analyzing and troubleshooting a range of biomedical instruments, from ECG machines to microscopes.
Attention to detail and a methodical approach are crucial for performing maintenance tasks with precision
and efficiency.

NIE/ELE/N0808: Introduction to Healthcare Delivery Systems and Role of
Medical Equipment and Devices
Description
This course delves into healthcare delivery systems and the indispensable role of medical equipment and
devices. It explores various models, stakeholders, and regulatory factors shaping healthcare services
globally. Students will examine the lifecycle of medical equipment, from design to disposal, and its
integration into healthcare systems. By course completion, students will be equipped to navigate
complexities and contribute to advancements in healthcare delivery practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The course encompasses developing students' abilities to analyze and understand the fundamental
structure and operations of healthcare delivery systems, with a specific focus on India. Students will
learn to compile comprehensive reports summarizing their observations regarding the basic
structure and functions of India's healthcare delivery system. Additionally, they will gain proficiency
in identifying basic medical devices and equipment crucial for healthcare delivery.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Healthcare Delivery System and Medical Equipment Overview

PC1. Describe the basic structure and function of the healthcare delivery system in India and
Identify the basic medical equipment and devices.
Understanding Healthcare Delivery Systems and Services

PC2. Distinguish between private, public, and non-profit healthcare delivery systems.

operations of healthcare delivery systems, particularly focusing on India.
KU2. Students will learn to compile comprehensive reports summarizing their observations
regarding the basic structure and functions of India's healthcare delivery system.
KU3. Students will gain expertise in identifying basic medical devices and equipment essential for
effective healthcare delivery.

NIE/ELE/N0809: Electronics Fundamentals and Laboratory Techniques
Description
Electronics fundamentals and laboratory techniques encompass the basic principles, theories, and
practical skills essential for understanding and working with electronic circuits and devices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This course providing students with a strong foundation in electronics theory and practical skills.
students will have a thorough understanding of voltage and current waveforms, along with
proficiency in using essential tools such as multimeters and cathode ray oscilloscopes (CRO). They
will be able to identify and calculate values for passive components, comprehend magnetism and
transformer principles, and effectively utilize various active components in electronic circuits.
Additionally, students will acquire hands-on experience in soldering and de-soldering techniques on
printed circuit boards (PCBs). This comprehensive scope prepares students for successful careers in
electronics engineering, maintenance, and technical support roles, equipped with the knowledge
and skills necessary to troubleshoot, analyze, and repair electronic systems and devices effectively.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Electrical Fundamentals and Maintenance Tools
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Introduction to the fundamental concepts of electricity and essential tools used in servicing
and maintenance tasks
PC2. Understand voltage, current, power, and AC waveform principles, along with the tools required
for effective maintenance operations
Practical Applications of Test Equipment and Resistance Fundamentals
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Usage of essential test equipment such as multimeters, cathode ray oscilloscopes (CRO),
signal generators, and LCR meters.
PC4. Understand fundamental concepts related to resistance, including value calculation, types,
working principles, and testing methodologies.
Capacitors, Inductors, and Transformers: Theory and Practical Applications
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Knowledge of capacitors, inductors and introduction to transformers. student will delve into
the theory, types, value calculation methods, working principles, and testing procedures of
capacitors and inductors.
PC6. Learn about magnetic materials, self and mutual inductance, and the operation of
transformers, including step-up and step-down configurations.
Semiconductor Theory, Active Components, ICs, and Soldering Techniques

NIE/ELE/N0807: Biomedical Diagnostics: Instrumentation and Analysis
Description
It encompasses a comprehensive curriculum focusing on the development and utilization of biomedical
instrumentation to understand human physiological systems.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Biomedical Instrumentation Development
Exploration of Biomedical Techniques
Integration of Computing in Biomedical Domain
Safety Protocols and Instrument Operation
Maintenance and Troubleshooting
Elements and Performance Criteria
Biomedical Instrumentation Development
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Student demonstrates an understanding of the fundamental principles underlying the design
and development of biomedical instrumentation, including signal processing, sensor
technologies, and system integration.
PC2. Student applies theoretical knowledge to practical scenarios by designing and prototyping
biomedical instruments, incorporating appropriate sensors, amplifiers, and signal conditioning
circuits to achieve accurate and reliable measurements.
PC3. Student evaluates the performance of biomedical instruments through experimental testing,
identifies areas for improvement, and optimizes design parameters to enhance accuracy,
sensitivity, and usability.
Exploration of Biomedical Techniques
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Student demonstrates proficiency in utilizing a variety of biomedical techniques such as bio-
electric potentials, cardiovascular analysis, microscopic examination, and pH measurement,
accurately interpreting experimental data and results.
PC5. Student designs and executes experiments effectively, selecting appropriate techniques and
instrumentation to address specific biomedical research questions or diagnostic challenges,
while considering factors such as sample preparation, data acquisition, and analysis.
PC6. Student analyzes experimental data using statistical methods, graphical visualization, and
qualitative analysis techniques, interpreting results to draw meaningful conclusions and make
informed decisions in biomedical diagnostics and research.
Integration of Computing in Biomedical Domain
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Student understands the principles of digital computing and microprocessor-based systems,
including programming languages, data structures, algorithms, and interfacing techniques
relevant to biomedical applications.

NIE/ELE/N0816: Implementation of Biomedical Equipment project
Description
The course provides training in servicing and maintaining medical equipment, covering essential skills like
soldering, de-soldering, and using test instruments. It teaches basic electrical concepts and the function of
both passive and active components. It also explores biomedical instrumentation, including physiological
systems, bio-electric potentials, and medical devices such as ECG machines, pH meters, and patient
monitors. The course ends with employability skills to prepare students for careers in the medical device
industry.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The course covers key National Occupational Standards (NOS) for Biomedical Equipment
Maintenance Assistants. It includes an introduction to healthcare systems and the role of medical
devices, along with fundamentals of electronics and laboratory techniques such as soldering,
measuring, and testing components. Learners gain hands-on experience with biomedical diagnostic
equipment like ECG machines, pH meters, and microscopes, focusing on maintenance and safety.
The course also emphasizes employability skills including communication, digital literacy, and
workplace readiness. These NOS units collectively prepare learners for entry-level roles in the
medical device industry.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform basic maintenance and testing of biomedical equipment and devices.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify and explain the working principles of electronic components and diagnostic medical
equipment.
