#### MIN/Q1206: Drill Operator (Down-the-hole/Long Hole (DTH/LH))
Brief Job Description
The DTH/LH (Down-the-Hole/Long Hole) Drill Operator is responsible for drilling long holes and down holes
in the mines by operating DTH/LH drill rig. They also ensure that the safety systems are working correctly
and performing essential maintenance on the DTH/LH drill rig available in underground mines.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should be physically agile, strong, have good eye-sight and not suffer from color-
blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MIN/N1218: Prepare DTH/LH drill rig for operation
2. MIN/N1219: Perform DTH/LH drilling operations
3. MIN/N1220: Perform maintenance of the DTH/LH drill rig
4. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Underground Metal
1. MIN/N1702: Follow Health, Safety and Environmental guidelines for Underground Metalliferous
Mines
Elective 2: Underground Coal
1. MIN/N1704: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental guidelines for underground coal mines

#### MIN/N1218: Prepare  DTH/LH drill rig for operation
Description
This OS unit is about inspecting & preparing the DTH/LH drill rig for activities that need to be carried out.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for drilling work
Inspect and prepare the DTH/LH drill rig for operation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for drilling work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the work requirements from the instructions received from supervisor and
interpreting information from previous day/shift logbook
- PC2. use personal protective equipment appropriate for work activities
- PC3. select appropriate type of auxiliary equipment for work activities
- PC4. inspect and assess site conditions to determine if scaling is required and take action
according to site requirements
- PC5. place visible orientation marks to drill plan according to site procedures
Inspect and prepare the DTH/LH drill rig for operation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. check various controls, gauges, warning lamp, Roll-Over Protection Structure (ROPS) and
Falling Object Protective Structure (FOPS), hydraulic boom, front and rear mounted stingers and other safety devices of the DTH/LH drill rig for proper functioning and any defect
- PC7. perform visual checks to detect cracks, wear and tear or any damage that could result in
structural weakness
- PC8. check coolant level and oil levels of engine, differential, hydraulic tank etc. and top up/refill
as required
- PC9. check electrolyte level, terminals, seating arrangements of the battery and take corrective
actions as per SOP, if required
- PC10. check functioning of fire extinguishers, automatically operated fire suppression system and
devices including fire detection system in the drill rig
- PC11. check under carriage and superstructure of the drill rig
- PC12. apply grease to all greasing pins and pivot points of the drill rig
- PC13. keep footplates and steps clean and free from mud, dirt and oil
- PC14. ensure that drilling site is clear of other mine workers to avoid any accidents
- PC15. maintain the operation and maintenance logbook to detail all activities conducted before
starting the drill rig
- PC16. report defects precisely to the supervisor if beyond scope of his/her role

#### MIN/N1219: Perform  DTH/LH drilling operations
Description
This OS unit is about performing various DTH/LH drilling operations in the mines during the shift.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Move and position the DTH/LH drill rig at site
Perform drilling activities
Perform post-drilling activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Move and position the DTH/LH drill rig at site
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. start the engine using the push-button switch and key switch together
- PC2. follow safety practices such as wear safety belt & helmet, no use of phones etc. while driving
- PC3. raise drill guide to 1.5m above ground level and make it horizontal to prevent any damage in
transit
- PC4. look out for people working, stationary objects and hazards such as overhead pipes, power
lines, etc. during driving
- PC5. follow the safety signs and locally developed signals during driving
- PC6. move, position and stabilise the drill rig at site according to ground conditions
Perform drilling activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. connect auxiliary devices and computer control system with the drill rig
- PC8. align drill rod with the orientation marks
- PC9. feed the drilling parameters i.e. angle of drill rod, drilling speed etc. in the computer control
system as per the requirement and drill plan
- PC10. operate the DTH/LH drill rig safely within working environment limitations and face/ground
conditions
- PC11. drills holes in the surface as per the drill plan
- PC12. realign equipment according to drill design and ground conditions
- PC13. monitor the ground conditions and adjust drilling techniques and components to maintain
drilling operations
- PC14. install collar pipes according to site procedures
- PC15. remove drill cuttings and sludge to ensure drill hole remains clean
- PC16. complete all required documentation clearly, concisely and mark misdirected holes according
to site procedures
Perform post-drilling activities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

#### MIN/N1220: Perform  maintenance  of the DTH/LH drill rig
Description
This OS unit is about performing routine maintenance and troubleshooting tasks on the DTH/LH drill rig.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for maintenance work
Repair and maintain the DTH/LH drill rig
Perform post-maintenance activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for maintenance work
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the drill rig and its manufacturer's specifications from the user manual and drawings
- PC2. read maintenance schedule and checklist and plan the time and schedule for conducting the
maintenance
- PC3. track machine operating hours to assess the right service schedule
- PC4. identify and arrange the tools, consumables and spare parts required during the task
Repair and maintain the DTH/LH drill rig
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. follow safety practices during maintenance activities as per organisational SOP
- PC6. perform basic health check-up of DTH/LH drill rig as specified in the maintenance checklist
- PC7. check and maintain the tyre rims, air pressure, wheel nuts and treads
- PC8. check battery levels and condition of the terminals and carrying out minor adjustments if
required
- PC9. replace/change the spare parts and consumables of the DTH/LH drill rig as per the schedule
- PC10. replenish coolants, lubricants and fluids as per SOP
- PC11. clean air filter dust bowls, footplates, pedals and steps and ensure that they are free from
mud, or dirt
- PC12. check the condition of DTH/LH drill rig parts i.e. hydraulic boom, front and rear mounted
stingers, bearings, shafts, battery systems, motors, wiring harness and connectors and other similar parts etc. to test its working status and expected conditions
- PC13. check the instrument panel and control gauge to identify out of range readings to find the
defect/cause of failure
- PC14. check the systems of the DTH/LH drill rig to find out root cause of the problems like any
leakage, short circuit in parts, breakage of wires and clamps, etc. and discuss the same with senior if required
- PC15. ensure that no diagnosis on the machine is performed when it is running or is still hot
