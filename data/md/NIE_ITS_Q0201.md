#### NIE/ITS/Q0201: ITeS BPO Executive Voice
Brief Job Description
The purpose of this qualification is to make the trainees understand and improve their Soft Skills or Inter
Personal Skills, along with a focus to improve their communicating ability in English and also Computer
skills for office automation with IT tools, and Cybersecurity and Overview of emerging future skills such as
AI, Cloud Computing, IoT, Data Analytics, etc. so as to enhance their opportunities towards meaningful
employability.
Personal Attributes
The qualification requires candidates to possess strong verbal communication skills, active listening, and a
customer-centric approach. They should demonstrate professionalism, ethical conduct, and the ability to
manage stress in a fast-paced BPO environment. Adaptability to different communication styles and
teamwork is essential for effective collaboration. A proactive mindset, problem-solving abilities, and a keen
understanding of business etiquette are crucial for success.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:

#### NIE/ITS/N0204: Office automation with IT Tools-
Description
This module provides a foundational understanding of computer fundamentals, operating systems, and
office automation tools. Students will gain hands-on experience with word processing, spreadsheets, and
presentation software to create and manage professional documents. The module also covers efficient
internet navigation, online research, e-governance services, and cyber security principles to ensure data
protection. Additionally, students will explore emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Cloud
Computing, IoT, and Data Analytics. Practical training includes using digital financial tools like online
banking, UPI, and Aadhaar-enabled payment services, ensuring secure financial transactions in a digital
environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
s module develops essential IT skills for office automation, including computer fundamentals, operating systems, and productivity software. It enhances digital literacy through internet
navigation, online research, and e-governance services while emphasizing cyber security and data
protection. Students also explore emerging technologies like AI, Cloud Computing, and IoT and gain
hands-on experience with digital financial tools for secure transactions.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Computer Operations and Office Productivity

- PC1. Understand computer components, functions, and operating systems.
- PC2. Navigate and operate different operating system environments.

management.
Internet Usage, Cyber Security, and Digital Awareness
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Use the internet effectively for research, communication, and e-governance services.
- PC5. Implement basic cyber security measures to protect data and privacy.
- PC6. Gain awareness of emerging technologies such as AI, Cloud Computing, and IoT.
Digital Financial Transactions and Security

#### NIE/ITS/N0205: Communicate  in English language with the ethics of BPO-
Description
This module focuses on enhancing English communication skills essential for a BPO environment. It covers
effective verbal and written communication, pronunciation, phonetics, and voice modulation to minimize
first-language interference. Students will develop business communication skills, including email writing,
report drafting, and professional etiquette. The module also emphasizes public speaking, presentation
skills, and voice and accent training to improve clarity and confidence in client interactions. Additionally,
ethical communication practices in a BPO setting are reinforced to ensure professionalism and adherence
to industry standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This module equips learners with essential English communication skills for a BPO environment, focusing on verbal fluency, pronunciation, and phonetics to enhance clarity and professionalism. It
covers business communication, including email writing, report drafting, and professional etiquette.
The module also develops public speaking and presentation skills while incorporating voice and accent training for effective client interactions. Emphasis is placed on ethical communication, active
listening, and adherence to industry standards to ensure professionalism in workplace communication.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Effective Communication in a BPO Setting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate proficiency in verbal and written communication by using appropriate
vocabulary, tone, and clarity in BPO interactions.
- PC2. Apply active listening techniques to understand customer queries and respond accurately with
professionalism.
- PC3. Engage in role-plays and real-life scenarios to enhance conversational skills and handle
customer interactions effectively
Pronunciation, Phonetics, and Accent Neutralization
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Apply phonetic rules and accent training techniques to minimize first language interference
and improve pronunciation.
- PC5. Participate in voice modulation exercises to enhance clarity and professionalism in speech.
- PC6. Practice neutral or desired accent adaptation to ensure better interaction with international
clients.
Business Communication and Ethics in BPO
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Develop proficiency in writing professional emails, reports, and business documents following
BPO industry standards.

#### NIE/ITS/N0202: Use of soft skills for BPO
Description
This module focuses on developing essential soft skills required for a successful career in the BPO industry.
It enhances communication, teamwork, and leadership abilities while emphasizing professionalism in
customer interactions. Learners will gain expertise in handling customer calls, managing stress, and
applying emotional intelligence to resolve queries effectively. The module also covers time management,
corporate culture, and customer relationship management (CRM) principles. Through practical exercises,
students will improve their problem-solving skills, confidence in public speaking, and ability to handle
workplace challenges efficiently.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This module covers key soft skills necessary for success in the BPO industry, including effective
communication, emotional intelligence, teamwork, and leadership. It focuses on enhancing verbal
and listening skills for better customer interactions, stress management, and time management to
improve workplace productivity. The scope also includes developing skills in call handling, conflict
resolution, and customer relationship management (CRM), along with building confidence for public
speaking and interviews. Overall, the module prepares learners to navigate professional scenarios
with confidence and professionalism in a BPO environment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Effective Communication and Emotional Intelligence in BPO
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate strong verbal and listening skills to engage professionally with customers and
colleagues.
- PC2. Apply emotional intelligence techniques to handle customer interactions with empathy and
patience.
- PC3. Utilize problem-solving and conflict resolution strategies to manage and resolve customer
complaints effectively.
Stress Management and Professional Etiquette
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Implement stress and time management techniques to enhance workplace productivity and
maintain self-motivation.
- PC5. Exhibit professional etiquette in handling customer calls, maintaining data security, and
following ethical communication standards.
- PC6. Adapt to workplace challenges with confidence, using self-esteem-building techniques to
maintain a positive attitude.
Leadership, Teamwork, and Corporate Culture
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Participate actively in team-building activities and collaborative projects to develop leadership
and teamwork skills.

#### NIE/ITS/N0203: Project-Customer Interaction & Professional
Communication  in ITES BPO (Voice)
Description
This project focuses on enhancing the communication, business ethics, and soft skills of ITES BPO
executives (Voice). The project aims to improve verbal communication proficiency, customer handling
skills, ethical practices, and emotional intelligence in real-world BPO scenarios. Through this project,
learners will gain hands-on experience in handling customer queries, resolving complaints, and delivering
high-quality service while adhering to BPO industry standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of this project includes:
Effective communication in English, including pronunciation, voice modulation, and accent training.
Business communication, including email writing, report drafting, and ethical standards in the BPO
industry.
Soft skills development, including leadership, teamwork, stress management, and customer handling techniques.
Practical implementation through mock call scenarios, role plays, and real-time client interaction
simulations.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Effective Business Communication & Ethical Practices in BPO
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate clarity and professionalism in verbal communication by using appropriate tone,
pronunciation, and grammar during customer interactions.
- PC2. Apply active listening techniques to accurately understand customer needs, respond
effectively, and ensure a seamless conversation flow.
- PC3. Utilize business communication skills to draft clear and professional emails, reports, and other
essential documents in compliance with BPO ethics.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understanding the fundamentals of effective communication and the communication process in BPO.
- KU2. Knowledge of BPO business ethics, data security, and compliance standards.
- KU3. Awareness of emotional intelligence, customer handling techniques, and stress management strategies.

#### Assessment Guidelines
• Assessment of the qualification evaluates candidates to ascertain that they can integrate knowledge,
skills and values for carrying out relevant tasks as per the defined learning outcomes and assessment
criteria.
• The underlying principle of assessment is fairness and transparency. The evidence of the outcomes and
assessment criteria. competence acquired by the candidate
• can be obtained by conducting Theory (Online), Practical assessment, Internal assessment,
Project/Presentation/ Assignment, Major Project. The emphasis is on the • practical demonstration of skills & knowledge gained by the candidate through the training. Each
OUTCOME is assessed & marked separately.
A candidate is required to pass all OUTCOMES individually based on the passing criteria.
