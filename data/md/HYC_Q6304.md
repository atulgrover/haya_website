HYC/Q6304: Excavator - Pipeline
Brief Job Description
The individual in this job is responsible for excavation work involved in laying oil and gas pipeline and
earth backfilling in accordance with safe working practices, plans and approvals. The individual will also
check and identify hazard prone areas before starting excavation work for minimising the possible hazards
to people and the surrounding environment due to excavation work.
Personal Attributes
The individual needs to be physically fit, mentally robust, and committed.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HYC/N6307: Perform excavation work for laying pipeline
- 2. HYC/N9301: Working Effectively in a team
- 3. HYC/N9302: Maintain health, safety and security procedures
- 4. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

HYC/N6307: Perform excavation work for laying pipeline
Description
This unit is about working with various equipment required to excavate for laying oil and gas pipeline
systematically in accordance with safe working practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for excavation work
Carry out excavation work
Follow safety procedures during excavation
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for excavation work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check working status of equipment
PC2. check location/site of excavation before starting work
Carry out excavation work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. carryout excavation work for pipeline laying with recommended method
PC4. excavate the trench as per required depth and bedding for laying of pipeline
PC5. maintain quality work during excavation process
PC6. carryout the rigging of pipes, manhole barrels, etc. for laying of pipeline
PC7. carryout trench refilling to restore the land to its original state after completion of required
pipeline checks by the concerned team
PC8. perform routine maintenance of equipment used in excavation work to ensure smooth
operation
Follow safety procedures during excavation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. identify the risks in handling various hand tools and equipment used for excavation work
PC10. use proper PPEs during excavation operation
PC11. check movement of vehicles near excavated area and place barricades
PC12. follow safe work procedures during rigging of materials into the excavated area
PC13. prevent unauthorized access into the excavated area
PC14. follow safety procedures to prevent cave-ins & loose rock/soil from rolling into the excavated
area
PC15. place the required safety boards on display before and during excavation work at site
PC16. use proper safety methods while handling electrical equipment during excavation

Description

towards self, others, assets and the environment.

PC7. maintain hands hygiene by washing hand frequently and thoroughly with soap and water or
alcohol-based hand rub
