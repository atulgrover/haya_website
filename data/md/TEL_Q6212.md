TEL/Q6212: Telecom Rigger - 5G and Legacy Networks
Brief Job Description
A Telecom Rigger - 5G and Legacy Networks works under supervision to assist in the installation,
maintenance, upgrading, and dismantling of telecom equipment on towers and other structures. The role
involves using rigging techniques and installation skills to ensure the safe and efficient handling of telecom
infrastructure, including cabling and related components.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be physically fit to climb towers and work at heights for extended periods. Strong
problem-solving skills, teamwork, and the ability to coordinate effectively with others are essential.
Additionally, the person should possess clear verbal and written communication skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. TEL/N6310: Assist in the Installation of Telecom Equipment
- 2. TEL/N6323: Assist in the Maintenance, Upgrade and Decommissioning of Telecom Equipment and
Sites
- 3. TEL/N6246: Follow the Occupational Health and Safety Instructions during Tower Climbing
- 4. TEL/N9105: Follow sustainable practices in telecom infrastructure installation

TEL/N6310: Assist in the Installation of Telecom Equipment
Description
This unit covers the installation of telecom equipment on various tower structures such as Ground-Based
Towers (GBT), Roof-Top Towers (RTT), and monopoles. It includes rigging, cabling, alignment, testing, and
ensuring safety compliance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Determine the scope of work
Prepare for the installation of telecom equipment
Assist in the installation of tower equipment
Assist in performing Line-of-Sight (LOS) check
Assist in the shelter room installations
Assist in completing documentation
Elements and Performance Criteria
Determine the scope of work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. determine the scope of installation work by coordinating with the supervisor and
commissioning engineers
PC2. analyse the relevant blueprints, schematics and as-built site plan to determine the work
requirements
PC3. assist the supervisor/field manager by providing appropriate inputs and feedback regarding
site conditions and installation as required, to help achieve the scope of work
Prepare for the installation of telecom equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. check the availability and condition of required installation material, tools and equipment,
and Personal Protection Equipment (PPE)
PC5. report and replace damaged or faulty tools, materials, or safety equipment through co-
ordination with the supervisor to get them replaced
PC6. assist in preparing Radio Frequency (RF) connector and jumper and assembling the relevant
telecom equipment for installation, following the supervisor's instructions
Assist in the installation of tower equipment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. assemble antennas, waveguides, and coaxial connectors as per supervisor’s instructions
PC8. assist in erecting and securing telecom structures (e.g. towers, monopoles, masts, and cable
trays) for equipment installation
PC9. assist in the installation of antennas, feeders, microwave dishes, mast head amplifiers, and
ancillary equipment following appropriate safety measures

them

schematics, and as-built site plans

TEL/N6323: Assist in the Maintenance, Upgrade and Decommissioning of
Telecom Equipment and Sites
Description
This unit covers the maintenance, upgradation, and decommissioning of telecom infrastructure, including
telecom towers, antennas, cabling, and network equipment for 5G and legacy networks.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist in carrying out preventive maintenance
Assist in carrying out corrective maintenance and site upgrade
Assist in decommissioning and recovery of telecom equipment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist in carrying out preventive maintenance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check telecom towers, structures, and components for corrosion, wind-induced vibrations, or
mechanical damage
PC2. check and secure bolts, brackets, and structural fasteners to ensure equipment stability
PC3. verify the protective casing of telecom equipment and identify signs of wear, cracks, or
damage
PC4. replace defective casings, connectors, or mounts as per standard operating procedures
PC5. identify and correct misalignment of antennas, microwave dishes, and feeder cables
PC6. follow the standard preventive maintenance checklist to ensure compliance with
maintenance schedules
Assist in carrying out corrective maintenance and site upgrade
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. assist in conducting visual and physical audits of rigging installations, antennas, and support
structures
PC8. check the operational status of telecom transmission lines, RRUs, power units, and backup
systems
PC9. use appropriate safety gear and tools, such as tower ladders, boom lifts, and harnesses, to
access telecom structures
PC10. identify and report damage to fiber-optic cables, hybriflex cables, and waveguides
PC11. assist in measuring and troubleshooting signal loss or interference using Distance-To-Fault
(DTF) analyzers
PC12. assist in installing or replacing 3G/4G/5G network components, as per site requirements
PC13. carry out regular maintenance of telecom equipment and structures using the appropriate
maintenance tools and equipment
PC14. handle and organize cables, jumpers, and accessories for structured and safe installations
