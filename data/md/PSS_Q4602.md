#### PSS/Q4602: Hydrogen  Plant Installation and Maintenance Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The individual in this job role is responsible for supervising and monitoring installation, operation,
maintenance, testing and commissioning of the equipment, systems and sub-systems of a Green hydrogen
production plant. The individual is also responsible for ensuring quality, material handling, mobilizing
resources and coordinating with seniors.
Personal Attributes
The job holder must have an eye for detail as well as the patience and discipline required to carry out
detailed and repetitive tasks. The candidate should be able to read and understand technical manuals,
instructions and warnings.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
2. PSS/N4604: Supervise Installation, Testing and Commissioning of Green Hydrogen Production Plant
3. PSS/N4606: Supervise Operation and Maintenance of Green Hydrogen Production Plant
4. PSS/N1336: Working effectively with others
5. PSS/N1337: Apply Basic Health and Safety Practices for Power Related Work

#### PSS/N4604: Supervise Installation, Testing and Commissioning of Green
Hydrogen  Production Plant
Description
This NOS is about planning and organizing resources, supervising the green hydrogen production plant
systems installation and testing activities and also maintaining the records and documents related to the
same.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan & organize resources
Supervise installation, testing and commissioning of green hydrogen plant
Reporting and documentation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Plan & organize resources
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Obtain schematic diagrams, engineering drawings, diagrams, lay out plans etc. and
instructions from engineer/manager
- PC2. Interpret the Plant Layout including various equipment and material used in a Green
hydrogen production facility
- PC3. Plan manpower and resources on the basis of provided plant layout
- PC4. Prepare purchase orders for the procurement of tools, machines, raw material etc.
- PC5. Coordinate with vendors and/or procurement team for timely receipt of tools, machines and
raw material required for green hydrogen production system installation
- PC6. Prepare project execution plan with timelines and milestones for each activity
- PC7. Assign tasks to team members across the production plant
Supervise installation and commissioning of green hydrogen plant
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Coordinate with vendors and engineer/manager for determining technical specifications and
requirements of the green hydrogen plant
- PC9. Conduct safety checks for gas filling system, electrolysers, compressors, renewable power
supply system, feed water supply and demineralization system, gas separator, transformer and rectifier, instrumentation systems and other equipment as per health, safety and
environment (HSE) standards
- PC10. Supervise installation, commissioning and testing activities of green hydrogen plant to
ensure it is conducted as per plan, required standards and quality
- PC11. Ensure that sparking equipment or devices e.g. MCBs, switches to be installed isolated from
the hydrogen production area
- PC12. Follow key material and safety codes, technology protocols and standards as applicable in
Green hydrogen industry

#### PSS/N4606: Supervise Operation and  Maintenance of Green Hydrogen
Production Plant
Description
This unit is about planning and supervising various tasks for operation and maintenance of hydrogen plant
equipment and systems.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage manpower and implement improvement practices
Plan and supervise plant operations
Plan and supervise maintenance activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage manpower and implement improvement practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Support Shift In Charge/Process head in finalizing the shift rosters of the manpower based on
the production plan
- PC2. Maintain the information on leaves/in-out time and shift/line overtime of the team and share
the information with the concerned authorities as per the organisational procedures
- PC3. Send inventory requirements to stores and purchase department and follow up with them to
ensure the timely receipt of materials (Spares, Consumables, etc.)
- PC4. Work collaboratively with maintenance, engineering, and production teams to minimize
downtime and ensure seamless operations
- PC5. Ensure optimal resource utilization (man, machine and material) and streamlining of
activities within the shift
- PC6. Identify opportunities for process improvement and implement innovative solutions to
enhance plant performance
- PC7. Conduct regular audits and inspections to ensure compliance with safety and environmental
regulations
- PC8. Track and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure process performance and
identify optimization opportunities
- PC9. Conduct daily floor meeting/morning meetings/staff meetings to communicate the
information such as production targets, new guidelines, new processes etc. to team
- PC10. Organize training sessions for the operators and technicians to improve their skills and
knowledge on new techniques and methods
Plan and supervise plant operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Obtain instructions from the manager regarding production targets and planning

| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |
| --- | :---: |
| Organisational Context | Organisational context includes the way the organisation is structured and how it operates, including the extent of operative knowledge managers have of their relevant areas of responsibility. |
| Technical Knowledge | Technical knowledge is the specific knowledge needed to accomplish specific designated responsibilities. |
| Core Skills/ Generic Skills (GS) | Core skills or Generic Skills (GS) are a group of skills that are the key to learning and working in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment. In the context of the OS, these include communication related skills that are applicable to most job roles. |
| Electives | Electives are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as contributive to specialization in a job role. There may be multiple electives within a QP for each specialized job role. Trainees must select at least one elective for the successful completion of a QP with Electives. |
| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
