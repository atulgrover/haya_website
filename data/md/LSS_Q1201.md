LSS/Q1201: Footwear and Fashion Accessories Retail Associate
Brief Job Description
Individuals in this role typically supervise a set of salespeople employed by the channel partners. Achieve
sales target and volume objectives for the channel in the assigned geographical area. The individual is
responsible for direct sales, increasing market share, and training, coaching, and mentoring the retail
salespeople.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the person to have: a customer-centric approach, the ability to multitask, interact with
customers of diverse lifestyles and convince them, and integrity. The individual should also be presentable
and target-oriented.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. LSS/N1201: Develop Sales and Customer Strategy
- 2. LSS/N1202: Demonstrate the product and interact with the customers
- 3. LSS/N1203: Carry out the sales and post-sales support
- 4. LSS/N1204: Create sales and quality reports and analyze
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Leather and Non - Leather Footwear Retail Sales Advisor
Technical knowledge of footwear sales both (Leather and non-leather footwear)
- 1. LSS/N1205: Carry out sales by explaining to the customers about the footwear products
Elective 2: Leather and non-leather Goods and Garments Retail Sales Advisor
Technical knowledge of leather goods and garments
- 1. LSS/N1206: Carry out sales by explaining to the customers about the goods and garments
products
Elective 3: Bags, Soft Bags and Hard Luggage Retail Sales Advisor

LSS/N1201: Develop Sales and Customer Strategy
Description
This unit describes the performance outcomes required to develop the sales and customer strategy
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Create a Positive Image of Self and Organization
Create Sales and Customer Strategy
Elements and Performance Criteria
Create positive image of self and organization
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. meet the organisations standards of appearance and behavior
PC2. greet customers respectfully and in a friendly manner
PC3. communicate with customers in a way that makes them feel valued and respected
PC4. treat customers courteously and helpfully at all times
PC5. keep customers informed and reassured
PC6. respond promptly to a customer seeking assistance
PC7. check with customers to ensure complete understanding of their expectations
PC8. allow customers time to consider his/her response and give further explanation when
appropriate
Create sales and customer strategy
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. find out which product features and benefits interest individual customers and focus on these
when discussing products
PC10. describe and explain clearly and accurately relevant product features and benefits to
customers
PC11. compare and contrast products in ways that help customers choose the product that best
meets their needs
PC12. check customers responses to his/her explanations, and confirm their interest in the products
PC13. identify suitable opportunities to tell the customer about associated or additional products
and do so in a way that promotes sales and goodwill
PC14. identify the need for additional and associated products and take the opportunity to increase
sales
PC15. identify promotional opportunities and estimate their potential to increase sales
PC16. report promotional opportunities to the right person
PC17. tell customers about promotions clearly and in a persuasive way
PC18. identify and take the most effective actions for converting promotional sales into regular
future sales

LSS/N1203: Carry out the sales and post-sales support
Description
This unit describes performance outcomes required to carry out sales and post slaes support
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Sales and post sales support
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. use available information in the client records to help prepare for consultations
PC2. quickly create a rapport with the client at the start of the consultation
PC3. talk and behave towards the client in ways that project the company image effectively
PC4. tactfully check, where appropriate, how much the client wants to spend
PC5. explain clearly to the client the features and benefits of the recommended products or
services and relate these to the clients individual needs
PC6. pace client consultations to make good use of the selling time while maintaining good
relations with the client
PC7. explain the reasons to customers sensitively and positively when their expectations cannot
be met
PC8. identify alternative solutions for customers either within or outside the organization
PC9. identify the costs and benefits of these solutions to the organisation and to customers
PC10. negotiate and agree solutions with customers which satisfy them and are acceptable to the
organization
PC11. recognise opportunities to exceed customers expectations
PC12. gain the help and support of others to exceed customers expectations
PC13. respond positively to customer service problems following organisational guidelines
PC14. keep customers informed of the actions being taken
PC15. solve problems with service systems and procedures that might affect customers before they
become aware of them
PC16. identify the options for dealing with a repeated customer service problem and consider the
advantages and disadvantages of each option
PC17. work with others to select best options for solving repeated customer service problems,
balancing customer expectations with the needs of the organization
PC18. collect and record feedback on the services
PC19. analyse and interpret feedback and share the findings on the effects of changes with others
PC20. meet the company's customer service standards while dealing with the client
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
