#### SGJ/Q3101: Micro-Entrepreneur NTFP  (Non Timber Forest Produce) Plant
Origin
Brief Job Description
NTFP includes all the Non- Timber Forest products that are harvested from trees, shrubs and other plants
in the forests. The course on (I) Coconut Shells & Fibre Handicrafts covers utilization of the freely and
locally available materials such as coconut leaves, coconut fibre for handicraft production and their
marketing. The course on (II) Bamboo Crafts broadly covers aspects such as species and products range of
bamboo, operation of processing machine, conversion of bamboo, product making & product design,
Bamboo boards preparation/Mud reinforced wall preparation/Roof design/Furniture making.
Personal Attributes
This unit tells about Micro-Entrepreneur NTFP (Non Timber Forest Produce) Plant Origin

#### SGJ/N1829: Importance of Non-Timber Forest Produce and  Handicrafts
and livelihood generation
Description
This unit tell about Importance of Non-Timber Forest Produce and Handicrafts and livelihood generation
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding the Importance of Non-Timber Forest Produce (NTFPs) and locally available NTFPs lies
in recognizing their significant contributions to livelihoods, biodiversity conservation, cultural
heritage preservation, and sustainable development.
Understanding the Importance of Handicrafts and livelihood generation and facilitating community
development through the artisanal production and sale of handmade goods.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Importance of NTFPs and locally available NTFPs
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. explain to the learners comprehend the Importance of NTFPs.
- PC2. explain to the learners internalize the NTFPs and its socio environmental relevance.
- PC3. show how to the learner identifies NTFPs and value add for its economization.
Importance of Handicrafts and livelihood generation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. discuss how to the learner understands the sustainable socio economic development patterns.
- PC5. explain how to the learner comprehends the requirement of human resources on different
handicraft trade.
- PC6. discuss how the learner acquires the skills of working in partnership for entrepreneurship
development and empowerment.
- PC7. show how to the learner analyses and understands the ways to protect, conserve and
preserve biodiversity.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Identification and sustainable harvesting: recognizing different plant species, knowing their
ecological roles, and harvesting methods that don't harm the forest ecosystem.
- KU2. Processing and value addition: techniques for drying, storing, and processing NTFPs to enhance their value and shelf life.
- KU3. Product development: creating innovative products like jams, soaps, or herbal teas from NTFPs.

- KU4. Marketing and sales: reaching customers, understanding market demands, and negotiating fair prices.
- KU5. Business management: Budgeting, record-keeping, and financial planning for their micro- enterprises.
- KU6. Handicrafts often use traditional skills and techniques, preserving cultural heritage and identity.
- KU7. Understanding proper harvesting techniques ensures both resource sustainability and income

partnerships with communities, NGOs, and government agencies.

challenges related to NTFP and handicraft businesses.
- GS5. ability to stay updated on new knowledge and innovations in the field.
- GS6. participate in workshops, trainings, and conferences related to NTFPs, handicrafts, and livelihoods.
- GS7. understanding and respecting diverse cultural values and practices related to NTFPs and handicrafts.

#### SGJ/N1830: Perform collection and processing of raw materials of Coconut
Shells & Fibre Handicrafts industry and concept of zero cost production
Description
This unit tells about Perform collection and processing of raw materials of Coconut Shells & Fibre
Handicrafts industry and concept of zero cost production
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Exploring innovative uses of coconut byproducts, livelihood generation, and cultural heritage preservation.
Perform Collection and Processing of raw material encompasses efficient gathering methods, quality
control measures and fostering economic viability within various industries.
Analyse and expected to practice zero cost production of handicrafts from coconut shells and fibres .
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Coconut Shells & Fibre Handicrafts industry
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. explain to the trainees will be able to understand the locally available Coconut shells and
fibre and their importance.
- PC2. discuss how they will get an advanced knowledge of coconut shells & fibre handicrafts.
- PC3. show how to the trainees will acquire advanced knowledge of Coconut shells and fibre and
their importance.
- PC4. show how to the trainees will be able to familiarize themselves on executing standard
operating procedures pertaining to NTFPs processing.
Collection and Processing of raw material
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. discuss how to the trainees will learn and internalize the availability and collection of
Coconut shell and fibres.
- PC6. explain the trainees equip themselves in the production of handicrafts from coconut shells
and fibres.
- PC7. show how the learner acquire the skill of identifying the availability and collection of Coconut
shell and fibres.
- PC8. show how the skill learning happens among the learners on production of handicrafts from
coconut shells and fibres.
Zero cost production
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. explain how to the trainees will understand ecofriendly practices and sustainable waste
management plans.
- PC10. explain how to the trainees will be able to comprehend conservation and management of the
environment.

- PC11. discuss how to the trainees will learn the zero cost production of handicrafts from coconut
shells and fibres.
- PC12. ensure the trainees demonstrate ecofriendly practices and sustainable waste management
plans.
- PC13. ensure the trainees analyse the conservation and management of the environment.
- PC14. ensure the trainees will analyse and expected to practice zero cost production of handicrafts

#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. identify reliable sources for discarded coconut shells like plantations, processing units, local
markets. Understand seasonal variations in availability.
- KU2. learn to differentiate between mature and immature shells, thickness, and absence of cracks
for optimal crafting.
- KU3. master different techniques like soaking, scraping, drying, and sanding to remove flesh and
impurities without damaging the shell.
- KU4. understand methods like bleaching, polishing, and applying natural oils to enhance appearance and durability.
- KU5. learn manual or mechanical methods for extracting fibre from husks without damaging its strength.
- KU6. develop a unique design aesthetic that reflects cultural heritage, sustainability, and caters to
market trends
- KU7. understand basic business principles like cost analysis, pricing, marketing, and record-keeping
for financial sustainability.
- KU8. master various techniques like carving, etching, weaving, dyeing, and embellishments to create diverse and marketable products.
- KU9. utilize recycled materials like discarded wood, fabric scraps, or natural elements.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. understanding their properties, processing techniques, and potential uses.
- GS2. safe and efficient operation of hand tools, power tools, and potentially CNC machines.
- GS3. maintaining a safe and clean work environment, especially when handling natural materials.
- GS4. identifying and reaching target markets effectively with minimal spend.
- GS5. continuously improve your skills and learn new techniques.
- GS6. identifying quality materials, spotting imperfections, and ensuring precise crafting requires
keen observation.

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification will be created by the Sector Skill Council/Awarding Body.
Each Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS.
SSC/AB will also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the
SSC/AB.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass % aggregate for the Qualification.

#### Acronyms

### Page 49

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |

| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |
| --- | :---: |
| Organisational Context | Organisational context includes the way the organisation is structured and how it operates, including the extent of operative knowledge managers have of their relevant areas of responsibility. |
| Technical Knowledge | Technical knowledge is the specific knowledge needed to accomplish specific designated responsibilities. |
| Core Skills/ Generic Skills (GS) | Core skills or Generic Skills (GS) are a group of skills that are the key to learning and working in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment in today’s world. These skills are typically needed in any work environment. In the context of the OS, these include communication related skills that are applicable to most job roles. |
| Electives | Electives are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as contributive to specialization in a job role. There may be multiple electives within a QP for each specialized job role. Trainees must select at least one elective for the successful completion of a QP with Electives. |
| Options | Options are NOS/set of NOS that are identified by the sector as additional skills. There may be multiple options within a QP. It is not mandatory to select any of the options to complete a QP with Options. |
