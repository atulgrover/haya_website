Description

electronics components and fault finding.

#### MSME/ELE/N2203: Assembly  of PC Architecture & their Troubleshooting
Description
After completion of course Student should be able to Understand the function of hardware peripherals of
computer system, Laptop and procedure to troubleshoot the common issues.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
After completion of course Student should be able to Understand the function of hardware peripherals of computer system, Laptop and procedure to troubleshoot the common issues.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
MSME/ADCHNM/02 Assembly of PC Architecture & their Troubleshooting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the Types of microprocessor and their technology
- PC2. Explain about the Different types of motherboard
- PC3. Explain about the Different types of add- on cards.
- PC4. Explain Different types of port in motherboard
- PC5. Explain Different types of slot in motherboard
- PC6. Explain Different Types of memory of computer.
- PC7. Understand about ROM and it’s used in computer system.
- PC8. Identify Different the type of HDD & Size of HDD
- PC9. • Explain the physical and logical technology used to read and write the data in
• HDD
- PC10. Discuss about the types of CDD & the types of DVD
- PC11. • Explain the physical and logical technology used to read and write the data in
• Optical Drive
- PC12. Demonstrate about the types Keyboard & types Of Mouse
- PC13. Explain the Problems in Keyboard and mouse.
- PC14. • Explain the proper use of Joy Stick, Light pen, Track Ball, Scanner, Graphic Tablet,
• Microphone, Bar Code Reader
- PC15. Explain about different types of Monitor like: - CRT, LCD, LED etc
- PC16. Explain the common issues and troubleshooting method.
- PC17. Explain about the Types of printer
- PC18. Explain the Printing technology of printer
- PC19. Demonstrate about Role and function of different printers
- PC20. Explain common errors of printer and troubleshooting process
