HYC/Q4001: Green Hydrogen Plant Operator
Brief Job Description
A Green Hydrogen Plant Operator is responsible for efficiently operating and maintaining a green hydrogen
production facility. The person at this job oversees the operation of equipment like electrolysers, dryers,
deoxy reactor, electrical heater, pump, compressors, and storage systems, ensuring smooth production
processes. The person will monitor water purification process, Hydrogen, production rates, energy
consumption, and system efficiencies, and make necessary adjustments to optimize output and minimize
waste prioritizing the Safety and compliance. They collaborate with team members, maintain operational
records, and contribute to process improvement initiatives.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have ability to give attention to detail, technical aptitude, adaptability, teamwork,
and a strong commitment to safety and sustainability. The person should have the ability to work
individually / team round the clock and must always promote safety.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management Procedures

HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management
Procedures
Description
This unit covers maintaining health, safety and security procedures at the workplace where Hydrogen is
produced and handled.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view
Safe handling of Hydrogen
Follow fire safety procedures
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view & Safe handling of Hydrogen
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. use protective clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions
PC2. identify documents, location and people responsible for health and safety in the workplace
PC3. identify possible causes of risk or accident in the workplace due to hydrogen leak/fire
PC4. capability to accurately read and interpret data from sensing equipment used in hydrogen
production processes
PC5. apply key safety parameters for handling hydrogen & oxygen
PC6. assess hydrogen release pressure, hydrogen flame detection methods (h2 flame is colour
less)
PC7. apply key safety requirements for electrical equipment’s in green hydrogen
PC8. work as per functioning of hydrogen exhaust system
PC9. inertization of system with nitrogen
PC10. identify hydrogen process hazard and assess risk
PC11. identify common safety signs, displayed in various areas
PC12. safe handling of lye solution (koh solution)
PC13. carry out safe working practices while dealing with hazards to ensure the safety of self and
others
PC14. handle hydrogen ignition source management
PC15. standard practice for safe venting of hydrogen & oxygen, reference cga 5.5 & nfpa 2.0 for h2
venting and aiga021-19 for o2 vent
Follow fire safety procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC16. Familiarization with Hydrogen Leak and flame detection system, Identification of Hydrogen
Fire; use the various appropriate fire extinguishers on different types of fires correctly

equipment

HYC/N4003: Carry out green hydrogen production operations
Description
This unit is about performing green hydrogen production operations which includes monitoring parameters
and production rates, and ensure efficient resource utilization to obtain pure hydrogen gas. The individual
has to assist during routine maintenance, inspections, and should be able to troubleshoot to ensure
optimal operation and reliability.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform activities related to Green Hydrogen production process
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform activities related to Green Hydrogen production process
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. determine the start, stop, adjust parameters, and monitor equipment performance to
achieve optimal electrolysis
PC2. start and monitor the power supply system, which include renewable energy sources
PC3. monitor and regulate the water supply to the electrolyzer
PC4. control and monitor the water purification system, which involve filters, deionization units, or
reverse osmosis systems, to maintain the required water quality
PC5. operate gas purification systems to remove impurities from the hydrogen gas
PC6. determine gas separation processes, such as pressure swing adsorption (psa) or membrane
separation, used to obtain pure hydrogen gas
PC7. monitor storage tanks or cylinders to ensure proper storage conditions for the produced
hydrogen gas.
PC8. report logs, finding and fault to the supervisor/manager
PC9. ensuring compliance with safety and quality standards during production of green hydrogen
PC10. report logs daily, report near miss, incidents, findings to the supervisor/manager.
PC11. ensuring compliance with safety and quality standards during production of green hydrogen
PC12. maintain & record of important parameters of electrolyzer like electrolyte flow, temp,
current, h2 flow rate, pressure parameters.
PC13. monitor proper periodic cip record also need to maintain & proper maintenance the rejected
water from ro either through zero liquid discharge scheme.
PC14. apply generic standard operating procedures
PC15. apply basic knowledge of msds used for koh, h2, o2
PC16. handle safe collection of laboratory samples
PC17. handle emergency actions, troubleshooting

| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently.
