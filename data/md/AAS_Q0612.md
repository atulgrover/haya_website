AAS/Q0612: Airport Terminal Operations Executive
Brief Job Description
The Airport Terminal Operations Executive is tasked with the responsibility of performing tasks that cover
the Terminal and Airside areas of an Airport. They are also responsible for safety and security aspects of
the operations and manage customer & stakeholder expectations.
Personal Attributes
The Airport Terminal Operations Executive should possess good communications skills and customer
orientation. He/she should also have good interpersonal, listening skills along with logical thinking. He/she
must possess decision making skills, manage conflicts along with attention to detail. He/she should have a
technology bent of mind along with the physical abilities to manage long hours of standing.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. AAS/N0625: Introduction to Airport Operations

AAS/N0626: Perform Daily Operations and ensure readiness
Description
This unit describes the actions to be performed with reference to performance of daily operations in the
terminal and airside and ensure readiness of operational personnel and equipment accordance with
organisational and regulatory procedures
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Performance of Daily Operations
Ensure Operational Readiness
Elements and Performance Criteria
Performance of Daily Operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Oversee and coordinate the effective flow of passengers into, out of and throughout the
terminal building and landside facilities.
PC2. Coordinate with AOCC and obtain latest updates on flight arrivals, departures, gate changes
PC3. Appraise the passenger with the latest information on flight arrivals, departures, gate
changes
PC4. • Organize all information relating to the following facilities at the airport for dissemination to the
passenger namely
• Airport operator offices
• Airline offices
• VIP lounges
• International Departure/Arrival terminal & gates
• Domestic Departure/Arrival terminal & gates
• Remote gates
• Duty Free /Retail shops, prayer rooms & baby care
• Airline lounges & restaurants
• Fast food joints, promotional areas, internet café, book stalls & smokers’ room
• Buggy services & inter terminal transfers
PC5. Monitor and report deficiencies if any in the FIDS/signages to the IT/appropriate team
PC6. Monitor the queue flow and inform supervisor for any additional resource requirement
PC7. Proactively guide senior citizens and ladies with infants, passengers with reduced mobility in
the respective queues
PC8. Coordinate special movements in the terminal and concourses for VIPs, dignitaries, and
special events.
PC9. Manage the retrieval of tubs from the arrival so that it can be circulated back to departures
PC10. Report discrepancies relating to the damage/pilferage of the tubs to the facilities Manager
PC11. Prepare electronic/manual logbook entries of daily events, maintain databases, and prepare
reports as needed.

Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Prioritize and complete daily tasks efficiently to maintain smooth terminal operations.
GS2. Stay alert to the terminal environment, anticipating and reacting to potential issues quickly.
GS3. Deliver prompt, courteous support to passengers and handle inquiries or concerns
professionally.
GS4. Respond effectively to emergencies, delays, or unexpected disruptions in operations.
GS5. Coordinate clearly with airlines, security, ground staff, and passengers via verbal and written
communication.
GS6. Work closely with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless daily operations.
GS7. Monitor schedules, signage, flight information, and service standards with precision.
GS8. Resolve operational challenges promptly, minimizing impact on passenger experience.
GS9. Adjust to changes in schedules, weather conditions, and passenger flow without disruption.
GS10. Use airport management systems (e.g. FIDS, BRS, RMS) and communication tools
confidently.
GS11. Ensure daily operations comply with aviation regulations and safety protocols.
GS12. Handle disputes calmly and professionally, ensuring issues are de-escalated quickly.
GS13. Keep records, logs, and reports accurate and well-structured.
GS14. Conduct pre-shift inspections and readiness assessments to prevent operational delays.
GS15. Juggle multiple tasks under pressure while maintaining high performance and accuracy.

AAS/N0627: Incidents, Accidents, Security, and Emergency Handling &
Reporting
Description
This unit describes the actions to be performed with reference to reporting of Incidents, Accidents, Security
Breaches, Emergencies & Safety Violations as well as their handling in accordance with organisational and
regulatory procedures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Incidents, Accidents, Security, Emergency, Safety Reporting & Handling
Elements and Performance Criteria
Incidents, Accidents, Security & Emergency Handling & Reporting
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Assist the terminal operations supervisor by overseeing and resolving operational problems,
emergencies, and other unusual situations in the terminal, airport facilities to ensure the
safety of travellers
PC2. Report to the respective security team on unidentified baggage in the passenger terminal
building
PC3. Report to the respective security team on altercation/violence observed in the passenger
terminal building
PC4. Report to the respective security team on receipt of any bomb threat calls
PC5. Report to the respective security team on issues pertaining to diplomatic mail
PC6. Report to the respective security team on suspicious items found in the level-4 screening of
baggage
PC7. Coordinate the investigation/response to security violations in accordance with the airport’s
security plan.
PC8. Report to the safety team & supervisor on fuel spillage from the aircraft during
refuelling/defueling
PC9. Report to the safety team & supervisor on fuel spillage from ground service equipment
PC10. Report to the safety team & supervisor on incidents involving collision between ground
service equipment and aircraft
PC11. Report to the safety team & supervisor on damage to aerobridge due to collision with
incoming aircraft
PC12. Report to the safety team & supervisor on any vehicle violations on the apron in terms of
over speeding etc.
PC13. Report to the safety team & supervisor on any other incidents/accidents that would have a
bearing on operational flow
PC14. Record & report any suspicious calls received where allocation has been done for manning
telephone lines in accordance with established procedure
