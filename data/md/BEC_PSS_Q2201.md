#### BEC/PSS/Q2201: EV Charging Station Attendant
Brief Job Description
EV Charging Station Attendant is responsible for effectively maintaining the service station, swapping the
customers discharged battery with charged battery, keeping the record of batteries, charging the electric
vehicle in a safe and secure environment, managing the payment and keeping the Charging Station neat
and clean condition.
Personal Attributes
An individual in this job must have good communication and interpersonal skills. The person should be
patient, organised, team-oriented, customer centric and have the ability to work for long hours in adverse
conditions. The individual should be a keen observer and have an eye for detail and quality.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. BEC/PSS/N2201: Cleaning of EV Charger, Battery & operational area
2. BEC/PSS/N2202: Performing Battery Swapping activity
3. BEC/PSS/N2203: Charging of Electric Vehicle (4W) at charging station

Description
Cleaning of EV Charger, Battery & operational area
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Cleaning of EV Charger, Battery & operational area
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Cleaning of EV Charger, Battery & operational area
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Choose a clean and proper cloth to sweep the EV chargers and batteries
- PC2. Sweep/ Clean Operational area properly
- PC3. Aggregate the Collected garbage/ waste at the designated place

- PC8. Clean the electric panel from outside to remove the dust
- PC9. Remove the dust from Canopy used to cover the EV chargers at the Station
- PC10. Clean the dusting cloths and keep all the cloths at designated place

- PC10. Maintain record of Swapping batteries

Charging of Electric Vehicle (4W) at charging station

Charging of Electric Vehicle (4W) at charging station
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

connector

- PC9. Check the display screen
- PC10. Confirm the customer’s order
- PC11. Confirm the payment options from Customer
- PC12. Ask the customer to vacate the vehicle
- PC13. keep the charging gun at a safe distance from the vehicle
- PC14. Avoid dragging the charging gun to Make it reach the vehicle connector
- PC15. Ensure that the charging gun fitted properly into the vehicle connector
- PC16. Select the socket in case of dual Charging gun in EV charger
- PC17. Keep on checking the charging at Regular internal
- PC18. Stop the EV charger and remove the charging gun from connector of the vehicle
- PC19. Obtain feedback from the customer for improvement in service
- PC20. Thank the customer and request them to visit again

| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
