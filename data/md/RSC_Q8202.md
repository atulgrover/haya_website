#### RSC/Q8202: Plastic Waste Segregator
Brief Job Description
The Plastic Waste Segregator is responsible for identifying, classifying, and sorting various types of plastic
waste, followed by cleaning, pre-processing, and ensuring compliance with safety, environmental, and
waste management norms. The role demands basic operational skills, awareness of plastic categories, and
the ability to follow routine instructions under supervision.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be physically fit to work for long hours with good observational and
learning skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N8202: Identify and Sort Plastic Waste
2. RSC/N9701: Perform Cleaning and Pre-Processing Activities
3. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
4. RSC/N5628: Adhere to Environmental, Safety and Regulatory Guidelines
5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### RSC/N8202: Identify and Sort Plastic Waste
Description
This unit is about identifying different types of plastic waste based on resin codes, appearance, or material
type and sorting them as per processing or recycling category.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify and classify plastic waste
Segregate and organize plastic waste
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify and classify plastic waste
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. recognize common types of plastic waste such as PET, HDPE, LDPE, PVC, PP, and PS
- PC2. distinguish plastic materials based on texture, flexibility, and transparency
- PC3. identify plastic items by shape, size, and usage category (e.g., bottles, films, containers)
- PC4. interpret plastic resin identification symbols
- PC5. group waste based on compatibility for recycling
Segregate and organize plastic waste
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. separate plastics by resin code or recyclability
- PC7. use color-coded bins or containers for sorting
- PC8. ensure correct placement to avoid contamination
- PC9. stack, bundle, or label sorted plastic waste
- PC10. follow schedule for moving waste to pre-processing area
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. types and sources of plastic waste
- KU2. plastic identification codes and their meanings
- KU3. basic characteristics of PET, HDPE, LDPE, PP, PS, etc.
- KU4. differences between recyclable and non-recyclable plastics
- KU5. common contamination risks in plastic segregation
- KU6. sorting methods: manual, semi-automatic
- KU7. use of color-coded bins and identification tags
- KU8. health and environmental impact of improper plastic disposal

#### RSC/N9701: Perform Cleaning and Pre-Processing Activities
Description
This unit is about cleaning plastic waste, removing contaminants, drying, and conducting basic shredding
or size reduction before the recycling process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct cleaning of plastic waste
Perform basic pre-processing
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct cleaning of plastic waste
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. remove labels, caps, and residues from plastic waste
- PC2. clean plastic using water, brushes, or detergent solution
- PC3. handle waste that may contain food, dirt, or chemicals
- PC4. rinse and dry plastic before processing
- PC5. wear gloves and safety gear during cleaning
Perform basic pre-processing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. inspect cleaned plastic for visible contamination
- PC7. sort again if contamination is detected
- PC8. operate hand-operated or basic shredders
- PC9. feed cleaned plastic into shredding units safely
- PC10. collect shredded material and transfer to designated containers
- PC11. follow SOPs for equipment use and cleaning
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. need for cleaning before recycling
- KU2. types of contamination: food, oil, chemicals
- KU3. tools used for cleaning (brushes, tanks, gloves)
- KU4. detergents and cleaning agents permitted
- KU5. drying methods for plastic materials
- KU6. use of manual, semi-automatic shredders
- KU7. operating procedure of shredding equipment

#### RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
Description
This unit is about maintaining health and safety of self and others at workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
Render appropriate emergency procedures
Maintain standard safety procedures at the workplace
Participate in safety awareness campaigns
Understand potential sources of accidents
Use safety gears to avoid accidents

Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

hazards to the appropriate supervisor

chemicals, utilities etc

workplace

immediate necessary action to reduce further danger

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
