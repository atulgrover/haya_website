#### AMH/Q1231: Fashion Technology(IndiaSkills / WorldSkills)
Brief Job Description
The Fashion Technician is a highly skilled professional with extensive knowledge of fashion design,
garment construction, and industry trends. They specialize in creating a diverse range of clothing using
various materials and techniques. Employing a combination of technical expertise and creative flair,
fashion technician produces garments through processes such as pattern making, sewing, and innovative
fabric manipulation. Fashion technician also demonstrate artistic proficiency in creating visually appealing
and trend-setting displays, showcasing their understanding of design principles and consumer preferences.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to be artistic, creative and innovative to visualize and create the design by
hand or using illustration software (Corel Draw, Illustrator, Photoshop etc.). A designer should be up to
date with emerging fashion trends, as well as general trends relating to fabrics, colours and shapes.
He/she should also have good communication skills, good sense of aesthetics, sound knowledge of sewing
and pattern making. He/ She must be aware of the target market and its current trends
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### AMH/N1231: Work  organization and management  (Fashion Technology)
Description
This unit covers the ways to organize and manage work place, tools and equipment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Organize and manage workplace, tools and equipment
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Organize and manage workplace, tools and equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Proactively develop own knowledge and skills
- PC2. Demonstrate an awareness of current trends and fashions in clothing design, accessories,
colours, fabrics, etc
- PC3. Take account of the properties of different fabrics including the limitations of certain
fabrics/fibres
- PC4. Fully comply with and promote health and safety practices in the workplace to maintain a
safe and healthy working environment
- PC5. Use all equipment safely and according to manufacturers’ instructions
- PC6. Use and care for all specialist tools and equipment used in the fashion industry
- PC7. Select the correct tool or piece of equipment for each task and design
- PC8. Plan and prioritize work to maximize efficiency in the workplace and to meet deadlines
- PC9. Work cleanly and safely in all work areas so as to protect materials and finished products
throughout
- PC10. Keep all work areas clean to facilitate efficiency and protect materials and tools
- PC11. Source support for business development
- PC12. Purchase materials and fabrics cost effectively, ethically, and applying principles of
sustainability, including, for example, production methods, zero waste and recycling
- PC13. Amend any areas of the process or product that do not meet quality control standards
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Materials/fabrics, their characteristics, properties, and uses
- KU2. The fashion industry processes across the world
- KU3. Processes for mass produced, small collection, bespoke, and couture fashion
- KU4. Industry jargon and terminology

#### AMH/N1237: Problem  Solving, Innovation, and Creativity (Fashion

Description
This unit is about problem solving, innovation and creativity skills
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develop problem solving and creativity skills to innovate in design
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Develop problem solving and creativity skills to innovate in design
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Demonstrate innovation and creativity in design
- PC2. Think creatively to devise innovative solutions
- PC3. Use creative solutions to resolve design and/or production challenges

- KU2. Basic machine care, fault finding, and resolution
- KU3. Creativity and its relevance and importance to the fashion industry
- KU4. All technical aspects of the production process
- KU5. Fabric properties and characteristics
- KU6. The purposes, properties, and characteristics of specialty fabrics
