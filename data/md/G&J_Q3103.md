#### G&J/Q3103: Metal Setter (Basic)
Brief Job Description
A metal setter is a skilled artisan or technician who sets diamonds and gemstones into jewelry using both
traditional and modern tools, ensuring secure and aesthetically pleasing placement. They are responsible
for maintaining the natural brilliance, symmetry, and alignment of each stone while working with precision
and patience. Their tasks include inspecting the setting area, carefully cleaning stones, and performing
quality checks after setting to ensure the jewelry piece meets the required standards of value, durability,
and visual appeal
Personal Attributes
The role necessitates a skilled individual with attention to detail, technical mindset, and hand-eye
coordination to handle delicate metal components in jewelry making. They must work independently, be
safety-conscious, and follow standard procedures. They must be adaptable, communicate effectively, and
open to learning and refining techniques to meet quality standards.

#### G&J/N3101: Set diamond  and gemstones  in precious metal jewellery piece
Description
This unit is about securely setting diamonds and gemstones in the precious metal jewellery frames in a
way that enhances brilliance of the stones
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers the following:
Stone Placement and Security
Surface Finishing and Wearability
Material Conservation and Sustainability
Productivity and Workflow Efficiency
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Stone Placement and Security
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. utilize precision tools and digital measurement techniques to position and secure stones as
per modern design specifications.
- PC2. implement advanced stone-setting techniques, such as micro-pave and tension setting, to
enhance brilliance and durability.
- PC3. minimize pressure during setting to prevent microfractures or structural weaknesses in
gemstones.
Surface Finishing and Wearability
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. employ high-precision finishing tools to smooth edges, ensuring optimal comfort for the
wearer.
- PC5. integrate laser or ultrasonic cleaning methods to remove impurities and enhance the final
finish.
- PC6. adapt setting techniques to accommodate contemporary jewellery trends, such as
minimalistic and invisible settings.
Material Conservation and Sustainability
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. implement technology-assisted tracking systems to monitor and minimize metal and stone
loss beyond permissible limits.
- PC8. optimize resource utilization by incorporating eco-friendly practices and sustainable
alternatives in metal settings.
Productivity and Workflow Efficiency
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. utilize automated setting techniques, where applicable, to meet production targets without
compromising quality.

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.

4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
