JUTE/TSC/Q1002: Jute Loom Weaving Operator (S4 loom)
Brief Job Description
Jute Loom Weaving Operator (S4 loom) is responsible for efficiently operating modern looms (Rapier, S-4A
& one mack etc.). As weaver, S/he makes necessary arrangements including checking of quality of jute
warp beam and jute weft package availability including its quality. To run efficiently and ensure
sustainability of production process
Personal Attributes
S/he is required skill on mechanism of loom, spare parts identification, knowledge of different motions of
loom, tension control mechanism, lubrication schedule, maintenance, detection of fabric faults and Testing
and Quality Control of Jute yarn, fabric on quality of jute packaging materials.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. JUTE/TSC/N1001: Jute beaming, sizing quality and weaving efficiency
- 2. JUTE/TSC/N1002: Testing and Quality Control (S4)
- 3. JUTE/TSC/N1003: Lubricate weaving machines.
- 4. JUTE/TSC/N1004: Operating Shuttleless Jute Weaving machine & its motions, tuning aspects
- 5. JUTE/TSC/N1005: Fire and industrial safety, implementation of production plan as per quality,
Housekeeping, handling of tools & equipment and working
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

Description
Supervise production operation in weaving section
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Taking charge of loom sets and handing loom sets shift to Operator- Weaving in the following shift

Jute beaming, sizing quality and weaving efficiency

PC2. Illustrate Knowledge of various Jute fabric products, its market size and customer base in
state, national and international level
PC3. Overview of Jute process
PC4. Explain Jute Beaming machine and identification of parts
PC5. Explain Jute Sizing and its necessity
PC6. Importance and quality of size mixture.
PC7. Explain Loom Motions and its classification, Identification and function of required tools,
equipment, and consumables needed for the Production
Take Charge of the Shift from loom operator

PC8. come at least 10 - 15 minutes earlier to the work spot
PC9. Ensure whether oil can, grease gun etc. is in order
PC10. Check the cleanliness of the machines & other work areas
PC11. Check whether any spare/raw material/ tool / fabric/ any other material are thrown under the
machines or in the other work areas
PC12. Report to his superior in case next shift reliever doesn’t report for duty. In that case hand
over the shift to next shift supervisor

JUTE/TSC/N1002: Testing and Quality Control (S4)
Description
Supervise production operation in weaving section
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Taking charge of loom sets and handing loom sets shift to Operator- Weaving in the following shift
Elements and Performance Criteria
Testing and Quality Control
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Check quality of feed material (beam and weft package)
PC2. Ensure that quality-wise identification mark for jute yarn (sacking warp, sacking weft, hessian
warp, hessian weft and any other qualities) is proper
PC3. Ensure that operators join the broken ends by proper weavers’ knots using mechanical knotter
hook
PC4. Ensure that defective packages are kept separately
PC5. Minimise unnecessary wastage of yarn while removing faults or during beam change
PC6. Identification of various types of fabric faults
PC7. Quality standards about final finished product to meet the defined requirements
PC8. Explain Importance of proper communication with associated departments and teams in
Production process.
PC9. Report immediately to the supervisor or concern superiors, if any technological or mechanical
problem occurs in machine

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand quality of feed material (beam and weft package)
KU2. Knowledge on quality-wise identification mark for jute yarn (sacking warp, sacking weft,
hessian warp, hessian weft and any other qualities) is proper
KU3. Understand joining of the broken ends by proper weavers knots using mechanical knotter
hook
KU4. Knowledge of defective packages and it should be kept separately
KU5. Knowledge on wastage of yarn while removing faults or during beam change
KU6. Knowledge on various types of fabric faults

JUTE/TSC/N1004: Operating Shuttleless Jute Weaving machine & its
motions, tuning aspects
Description
Machine operation for overall efficiency of loom shed
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Working in a team and machine operation for overall efficiency of loom shed
Elements and Performance Criteria
Working in a team and machine operation for overall efficiency of loom shed
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Ensure the weavers knot is of minimum size to pass easily through the heald wires and reed
PC2. Check the indicating lamp and find out the broken warp end
PC3. Should find out the location of the broken end, by bringing the hands under the dropper bars
, with mechanical droppers
PC4. Use electrical warp stop motion , to detect the location using the indication lamp and by
bringing the hands over the droppers
PC5. Be able to mend the broken warp end in the sized beams with the thrums of the same count
of the sized beams, using " weavers ' knots"
PC6. Be able to draw the mended warp yarn through the healds properly , as per the drawing
order prescribed
PC7. Start the loom without inching/ in one stroke
PC8. Find out the breakage place ( whether it is before accumulator or in the accumulator or in
the weft feeder or in the rapier )
PC9. Be able to find out the last pick inserted in the produced cloth, by ensuring proper pick
finding
PC10. Start the loom without inching/ in one stroke
PC11. Correct the fabric defects like wrong drawing, wrong denting , end out , double end etc.,
immediately and also ensure that the other fabric defects too are corrected at the earliest,
before continuing further production
PC12. Clean the machines and work area, so as to ensure good working atmosphere, without
damaging the fabrics in the looms where the cleaning work is carried out as well as in the
adjacent & opposite looms.
PC13. In case of any floats, should be able to " unweave " the same & should be able to run the
machine without " starting mark or crack"
PC14. Weft yarn reserve packages have to be tied with tail ends of the running weft yarn package
& ensure proper transfer
PC15. Patrol the machines and do mending so as to minimize the stoppages

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| :--- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
