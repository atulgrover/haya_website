RSC/Q0420: Technician - TQC for Plastic Materials & Products
Brief Job Description
The Technician - TQC for Plastic Materials & Products ensures quality compliance by conducting physical,
mechanical, and non-destructive tests on plastic raw materials and finished goods. The role involves
operating and calibrating testing equipment, identifying defects, and maintaining quality records as per
BIS/ISO standards. The technician supports production and QA teams in ensuring product reliability and
customer satisfaction.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have good observational skills, with an eye to detail, and positive
attitude.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N0408: Conduct Testing of Plastic Raw Materials and Finished Products
- 2. RSC/N0409: Ensure Compliance with Quality Standards
- 3. RSC/N0411: Calibrate and Maintain Testing Equipment
- 4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol

RSC/N0408: Conduct Testing of Plastic Raw Materials and Finished
Products
Description
This NOS covers the skills and knowledge required to carry out a wide range of tests on plastic raw
materials and finished products using destructive and non-destructive techniques. It includes specimen
preparation, test execution, analysis of results, and proper documentation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for Testing
Conduct Physical and Mechanical Tests
Perform Non-Destructive Testing
Analyze and Report Results
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for Testing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify material type and testing requirements from work order
PC2. select suitable test methods based on product and standards
PC3. collect and label raw material/product samples properly
PC4. prepare specimens using standard cutting/molding tools
PC5. check testing instruments for functionality and cleanliness
PC6. set up environmental conditions as per test requirements
Conduct Physical and Mechanical Tests
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. perform MFI (Melt Flow Index) tests
PC8. conduct tensile, elongation, and modulus testing
PC9. carry out impact testing (Izod/Charpy)
PC10. execute hardness tests (Shore A/D, Rockwell)
PC11. measure HDT/Vicat softening point
PC12. conduct specific gravity/density tests
PC13. test for opacity, gloss, haze, and color (if applicable)
PC14. log all test parameters and results systematically
Perform Non-Destructive Testing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC15. identify components requiring NDT based on specifications
PC16. perform visual inspection for surface defects
PC17. use ultrasonic testing (UT) to detect internal flaws (where applicable)

RSC/N0409: Ensure Compliance with Quality Standards
Description
This OS unit is about monitoring and ensuring quality compliance during all stages - raw material
inspection, in-process checks, and finished product verification. It includes defect identification, data
analysis, documentation, and audit readiness.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Inspect Incoming Raw Materials
Monitor In-Process Quality
Verify Finished Product Conformance
Support Quality Documentation and Audits
Elements and Performance Criteria
Inspect Incoming Raw Materials
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. review Certificate of Analysis (CoA) and match with specifications
PC2. conduct visual and basic physical tests
PC3. document acceptance or rejection with reasons
Monitor In-Process Quality
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. perform hourly sampling and testing from the line
PC5. check process parameters and match with control plan
PC6. identify visual and dimensional defects during production
PC7. escalate non-conformances to supervisors
PC8. record production quality data
Verify Finished Product Conformance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. perform final Quality Control (QC) tests (strength, dimensions, aesthetics)
PC10. tag and segregate accepted and rejected lots
PC11. conduct NDT where required on finished products
PC12. re-inspect post-repair items using NDT
Support Quality Documentation and Audits
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. maintain inspection checklists and batch records
PC14. help prepare Corrective And Preventive Actions (CAPA) documents
PC15. support internal/external audit teams

RSC/N0411: Calibrate and Maintain Testing Equipment
Description
This OS unit is about periodic calibration, troubleshooting, and maintenance of lab and testing equipment,
including both destructive and non-destructive test instruments. It ensures reliability and accuracy in test
results.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan Calibration Activities
Perform Calibration
Maintain Equipment and Records
Elements and Performance Criteria
Plan Calibration Activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify instruments needing calibration
PC2. refer to SOPs for schedule and frequency
PC3. coordinate with external vendors if applicable
Perform Calibration
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. use certified standard tools and materials
PC5. compare readings against reference standards
PC6. adjust/calibrate instruments as needed
PC7. tag calibrated instruments with updated info
PC8. calibrate NDT tools (e.g., infrared camera, ultrasonic testers)
Maintain Equipment and Records
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. clean and store tools properly
PC10. troubleshoot common equipment faults
PC11. replace worn-out components
PC12. maintain calibration history sheets
PC13. schedule preventive maintenance tasks
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. importance of equipment calibration in quality systems
KU2. calibration techniques for MFI, tensile testers, UTM, etc.

RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
Description
This unit is about maintaining health and safety of self and others at workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
Render appropriate emergency procedures
Maintain standard safety procedures at the workplace
Participate in safety awareness campaigns
Understand potential sources of accidents
Use safety gears to avoid accidents
Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain a clean and efficient workplace
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. undertake basic safety checks before operation of all machinery and equipment and report
hazards to the appropriate supervisor
PC2. work for which protective clothing or equipment is required is identified and the appropriate
protective clothing or equipment is used in performing these duties in accordance with
workplace policy

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| :--- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
