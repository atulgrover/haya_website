#### WBSC/AGR/Q3401: Vermi Composter
Brief Job Description
A Vermi composter farmer produces enough manure for organic farming. He or she produces compost
through aerobic microorganisms. A Vermi-composter analyses the nutrition value of compost to retain the
fertility in the soil.
Personal Attributes
A Vermi-composter farmer is hardworking, environmentally conscious with a strong sense of responsibility
toward sustainable agriculture. He or she is knowledgeable about organic farming practices and understands the role of aerobic microorganisms in producing high-quality compost. Commitment to eco-
friendly methods, attention to detail and a passion for improving soil health productive and sustainable
farming.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. WBSC/AGR/N3401: Identify the species of earthworm capable for producing vermi compost from agriculture waste.
2. WBSC/AGR/N3402: Construct the compost pit and bed with Pit method.
3. WBSC/AGR/N3403: Prepare vermi and other compost.
4. WBSC/AGR/N3404: Collect the vermi wash, analyse the composition and apply to the field.
5. WBSC/AGR/N3405: Pack vermi compost and other compost for transportation.
6. WBSC/AGR/N3406: Apply the compost fertilizer in agricultural field according to the composition of
the soil.
7. WBSC/AGR/N3407: Work in real job situation with special emphasis on basic safety and hazards in
this domain (Vermi Composter).
8. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### WBSC/AGR/N3401: Identify the species of earthworm capable for
producing vermi compost  from agriculture waste.
Description
Identify the species of earthworm capable for producing vermi compost from agriculture waste.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Types of earthworm species.
The role of earthworm in soil.
Identification of earthworm through morphological and physiological examination.
Selection of the agriculture waste for producing vermi compost.
Preparation of vermi compost by using commercial Earthworms.
