#### MIN/Q1207: Mine Foreman/Overman
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role is responsible for supervising and monitoring the various mine activities and
ensures organizing and monitoring of work flow for various processes. The person is also responsible of
managing team and ensure they are following all compliances and regulations as specified by organization.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should be physically agile, strong, have good eye-sight and not suffer from color-
blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MIN/N1221: Manage mining operations and team
2. MIN/N1222: Manage and control mine district
3. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Underground Metal
1. MIN/N1715: Follow Health, Safety and Environmental guidelines for Underground Metalliferous
Mines
Elective 2: Opencast
1. MIN/N1716: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental Guidelines for opencast mines
Elective 3: Underground Coal
1. MIN/N1717: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental guidelines for underground coal mines

#### MIN/N1221: Manage   mining operations and team
Description
This NOS is about managing manpower and availability of material, tools and equipment in the mines. It is
also about supervising production operations and implementing process and team improvement practices
for achieving the targets.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage manpower and material in the mines
Supervise production operations
Implement team improvement practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage manpower and material in the mines
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. prepare shift rosters and allocate requisite manpower based on skills to achieve production
targets
- PC2. monitor overtime list for compliance with overtime distribution
- PC3. maintain time sheets for mine employees along with absence reports and submit weekly to
payroll or concerned authorities as per the organisational procedures
- PC4. maintain the availability of material, tools and equipment in the mines as per the SOP/Work
Plans
- PC5. ensure that the team have all the required material, tools and equipment all the time during
production process
- PC6. ensure optimal resource utilization (man, machine and material) and streamlining of
activities within the shift
Supervise production operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. co-ordinate with other departments for resolution of inter-related problems and achieving
required production target and quality standards
- PC8. analyse possible areas of improvements in production and identify corrective measures to
address the gaps
- PC9. implement corrective actions to reduce losses and wastages
- PC10. prepare and analyse various data sheets and reports related to production, maintenance,
manpower deployment etc.
- PC11. prepare and review daily and monthly production MIS reports to analyse the production
performance and report the same to concerned authorities
- PC12. analyse machine breakdown trends and current maintenance process to identify areas of
improvement and corrective actions for improving the same

#### MIN/N1222: Manage   and control mine district
Description
This OS unit is about managing and controlling all mining operations in a mine district, in accordance with
statutory and company requirements. This includes monitoring and inspection of health and safety
regulations, employees working and equipment operations in accordance with manufacturers/organisational specifications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage and promote safe working on site
Manage and control mining operations
Monitor and control the employees working in the mine district
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage and promote safe working on site
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. implement company requirements and legal regulations, paying specific attention to HSE
legislation and quality
- PC2. give directions as may be necessary to ensure compliance with safety of the district and the
safety and proper discipline of the persons employed therein
- PC3. ensure proper use and maintenance of personal protective equipment
- PC4. carry out and record site inspections
- PC5. identify problems (e.g. obstructions, geological movements, mechanical or service failure)
and report to appropriate person/s for advice or repair/remedial action
- PC6. prepare reports on accident/incident that happen during the shift
- PC7. conduct daily pre-shift meetings to ensure employees are informed of safety and production
issues
- PC8. check all pre-shift reports and note any dangerous or hazardous conditions included in the
pre-shift report
- PC9. give prompt attention to the removal of any danger observed or reported to him, and shall
see that dangerous places are adequately fenced off
- PC10. trace locations and identify emergency signals sent through the use of modern
communication systems like Fleet Management System (FMS), Leaky feeder, etc.
- PC11. conduct incident investigations, hazard analysis, and identify root-causes
- PC12. implement Safety Management Plan (SMP)
Manage and control mining operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. oversee end to end mining operations i.e. stripping, drilling, blasting, loading etc.
- PC14. supervise all operations connected with the transport of explosives and blasting in the mine

#### DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)
Description

digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
