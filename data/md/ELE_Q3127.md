ELE/Q3127: Refrigeration and Air Conditioning
Brief Job Description
A Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician operates in diverse environments including commercial,
residential, industrial, and transportation sectors ensuring the efficient functioning of systems that
regulate temperature and humidity. Their role encompasses planning, designing, installing, testing,
commissioning, and maintaining refrigeration and air conditioning systems. They handle a broad range of
equipment and services, which are essential for preserving quality in areas such as food storage and
ensuring comfort in buildings.
Personal Attributes
The person should be result oriented with good technical and analytical skills, should have Excellent
Interpersonal Skills, communication and presentation skills and a good team player. They should have
ability to manage projects, prioritizing of work and mentoring the budding engineers.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. ELE/N3176: Work organization and management (Refrigeration and Air Conditioning)
- 2. ELE/N3177: Communication and interpersonal skills (Refrigeration and Air Conditioning)
- 3. ELE/N3178: Design refrigeration and air conditioning systems (Refrigeration and Air Conditioning )
- 4. ELE/N3179: Installation and maintenance of refrigeration and air conditioning (Refrigeration and
Air Conditioning)
- 5. ELE/N3180: Commission refrigeration and air conditioning systems (Refrigeration and Air
Conditioning)
- 6. ELE/N3181: Fault finding refrigeration and air conditioning systems (Refrigeration and Air
Conditioning)

ELE/N3176: Work organization and management (Refrigeration and Air
Conditioning)
Description
This NOS unit is about proper training, the correct use of equipment, and adherence to safety protocols in
manufacturing settings.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Principles and Applications of Safe Working
Purposes, Uses, Care, and Maintenance of Equipment and Materials
Environmental and Safety Principles in Good Housekeeping
Principles of Team Working and Their Applications
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand and apply industry health and safety standards.
PC2. Identify hazards and implement control measures for workforce and public safety.
PC3. Perform risk assessments and prepare method statements.
PC4. Apply safe manual handling techniques, including mechanical lifting aids.
PC5. Select and correctly use personal protective equipment (PPE) for all activities.
PC6. Safely use access equipment, including ladders and mobile towers.
PC7. Apply electrical safety procedures while using tools and equipment.
PC8. Handle refrigerant and heat-producing equipment safely.
PC9. Maintain tidy and safe work areas, applying good housekeeping principles.
PC10. Respond appropriately to accidents and emergencies, including first aid for minor and major
injuries.
PC11. Plan work efficiently within time and energy constraints, minimizing environmental impact.
PC12. Apply environmental protection, waste reduction, and safe disposal practices.
PC13. Incorporate energy conservation and smart technology practices in system design and
maintenance.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Knowledge of preparing technical drawings and specifications as per engineering instructions.
KU2. Understanding of schematic capture and PCB layout design tools.
KU3. Understanding of schematic capture and PCB layout design tools.
KU4. Knowledge of CAD tools for electronic drawings across various systems.

ELE/N3178: Design refrigeration and air conditioning systems
(Refrigeration and Air Conditioning )
Description
This NOS unit is about evaluating system purposes, feasibility, and material properties while applying
specialized knowledge in refrigeration, air conditioning, and related calculations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Appraise the Purposes of Required Systems
Appraise the Feasibility of Locating Systems Within Designated Areas
Calculate Relative Densities of Refrigerants to Air and Water
Apply Specialist Knowledge of the Principal Applications and Detailed Properties of Solid Materials
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand standard units of measurement, properties of materials and fluids, and relevant
directives applicable to HVAC&R systems.
PC2. Understand the principles and interactions of energy, heat, power, force, and pressure as
applied to HVAC&R systems.
PC3. Understand electricity, control circuits, and the types and uses of electrical cables and
devices for HVAC&R applications.
PC4. Understand refrigeration and heat pump cycles, condensate drainage, and secondary
refrigerant circuits.
PC5. Understand the principles, conventions, and range of specifications and drawings used in
HVAC&R system design.
PC6. Appraise the purposes and feasibility of HVAC&R systems within designated spaces,
considering environmental and operational constraints.
PC7. Apply specialist knowledge to calculate heat, energy, power, force, pressure, electrical
parameters, and relative densities of refrigerants, fluids, and materials.
PC8. Design efficient refrigeration and air conditioning systems, selecting appropriate refrigerants,
components, joining methods, and heat exchangers.
PC9. Produce technical drawings, specifications, and system layouts using standard conventions,
symbols, and available drawing tools.
PC10. Estimate costs and budgets, select equipment and materials based on performance,
environmental, and financial criteria, and adjust designs to maintain budget compliance.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Knowledge of standard measurement units, material properties, and HVAC&R directives.

ELE/N3179: Installation and maintenance of refrigeration and air
conditioning (Refrigeration and Air Conditioning)
Description
This NOS unit is about interpreting plans for piping and electrical routes, safely handling materials and
equipment in the RAC industry, and performing installations, jointing, and testing of refrigeration and air
conditioning systems.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Interpret Diagrams, Plans, and Specifications to Determine Appropriate Piping and Electricity Routes
Work Safely with Hot Gas Heating Equipment in the RAC Industry
Prepare and, If Needed, Improve the Area and Surfaces Which the Installation Depends Upon
Itemize the Required Tools, Components, and Materials for Installations
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand and apply specific health and safety requirements for fitting, fixing, servicing,
maintaining, and decommissioning HVAC&R systems.
PC2. Understand the working principles, layouts, and operational characteristics of HVAC&R and
heat pump systems.
PC3. Interpret diagrams, plans, and specifications to determine appropriate piping and electrical
routes.
PC4. Apply procedures for fitting, fixing, and testing materials, equipment, and components safely
and accurately.
PC5. Prepare and, if necessary, improve installation areas and surfaces to meet operational
requirements.
PC6. Itemize and organize required tools, components, and materials for installation tasks.
PC7. Take and transfer measurements and angles from drawings to surfaces and piping materials
accurately.
PC8. Identify, check, and safely use various types of gases and equipment used for joining
materials in the HVAC&R industry.
PC9. Safely drain oil and recover refrigerants, including HC, HFC, HFO, and natural refrigerants.
PC10. Join similar and dissimilar materials using permanent (brazed and compression/crimped) and
accessible (flared) jointing methods according to industry standards.
PC11. Fabricate and install mechanical materials and components according to drawings and
specifications.
PC12. Install electrical materials, components, and control devices as per drawings and
specifications.
PC13. Install ancillary components and systems such as condensate drainage and leak detection
systems.
