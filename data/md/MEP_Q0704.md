MEP/Q0704: Deputy Human Resources Manager
Brief Job Description
The Deputy Human Resource Manager is designed to deepen students' expertise in strategic HR practices
and prepare them for high-level operational roles. It focuses on advanced topics such as HR analytics,
labour relations, and strategic manpower planning, equipping students with the skills to manage complex
HR challenges. Students engage exclusively in industrial training and a comprehensive project report,
applying their acquired knowledge in real-world settings to gain practical experience and insights into the
HR industry.
Personal Attributes
Strategic Thinking, Leadership Skills, Analytical Mindset, Problem-Solving Ability, Decision-Making Skills,
Strong Communication, Attention to Detail, Adaptability
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MEP/N0722: Human Resource Information System (HRIS)
- 2. MEP/N0723: Labour and Corporate Laws
- 3. MEP/N0724: HR Analytics
- 4. MEP/N0725: Advance Manpower Planning
- 5. MEP/N0727: Performance Management
- 6. MEP/N0256: Personality Development
- 7. MEP/N0257: Advanced MS Excel
- 8. MEP/N0728: OJT Mandatory - Human Resource Operations
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Management Information System (MIS)
The student/ trainee will either select Elective 1 - Management Information System (MIS) or Elective 2 - HR
Audit and Accounting in semester 3
- 1. MEP/N0258: Management Information System (MIS)
Elective 2: HR Audit and Accounting

MEP/N0722: Human Resource Information System (HRIS)
Description
This NOS focuses on the implementation, management, and utilization of Human Resource Information
Systems (HRIS) to streamline HR operations, improve data management, and enhance decision-making
processes.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Organizations and Information Systems
Concept of Human Resource Information System (HRIS)
Application of HRIS in Employee Engagement
Decision Support System (DSS)
Introduction to HRIS software
Elements and Performance Criteria
Organizations and Information Systems
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Analyze the requirements for HR system development.
PC2. Design and develop an HR system aligned with organizational goals.
PC3. Apply information systems to support managerial decision-making processes.
PC4. Evaluate the impact of information systems on strategic business decisions.
PC5. Integrate information systems with business strategies.
PC6. Assess the role of information systems in achieving competitive advantage.
Concept of Human Resource Information System (HRIS)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Plan and design HRIS to meet organizational needs.
PC8. Develop a project plan for HRIS implementation.
PC9. Identify and select appropriate hardware and software for HRIS.
PC10. Oversee the installation and configuration of HRIS components.
PC11. Develop protocols for maintaining HRIS.
PC12. Implement ongoing HRIS development and upgrades.
Application of HRIS in Employee Engagement
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. Utilize HRIS to manage and analyze compensation data.
PC14. Implement compensation strategies using HRIS.
PC15. Track and manage employee training programs through HRIS.
PC16. Analyze training effectiveness using HRIS data.
PC17. Manage payroll processes using HRIS.

Decision Support System (DSS)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

performance management.
KU2. Recognize the role of HRIS in automating HR processes and improving workforce
management.
KU3. Explain data security, compliance, and privacy considerations in HRIS usage.
KU4. Identify key HR analytics and reporting tools used for strategic HR decision-making.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Communicate HRIS-related insights effectively with HR teams and stakeholders.
GS2. Apply analytical skills to interpret HR data and generate meaningful reports.
GS3. Use problem-solving skills to troubleshoot HRIS issues and optimize system functionality.
GS4. Demonstrate proficiency in operating and managing HRIS platforms and digital HR tools.

MEP/N0723: Labour and Corporate Laws
Description
This NOS focuses on understanding and applying key labour and corporate laws to ensure legal
compliance, protect employee rights, and support business governance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Factories Act 1948
Employees State Insurance Act 1948
Minimum Wages Act 1948
Employee Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952
Payment of Bonus Act 1965
Company basics
Understanding memorandum
Company management and administration
Winding up of companies
Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)
Elements and Performance Criteria
Factories Act, 1948
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Define the requirements and process for licensing and registration of factories.
PC2. Identify the roles and responsibilities of the manager and occupier under the Act.
PC3. Describe the powers of authorities under the Factories Act, 1948.
PC4. Explain the penalty provisions for non-compliance with the Act.
Employee’s State Insurance Act,1948
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Outline the process for the registration of factories and establishments under the Act.
PC6. Describe the functions and duties of the Employees State Insurance Corporation and
inspectors.
PC7. Explain the miscellaneous provisions of the Employee’s State Insurance Act, 1948, including
benefits and compliance requirements.
Minimum Wages Act,1948
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Explain the appointment process and role of the Advisory Board under the Act.
PC9. Discuss the provisions for payment of minimum wages and maintenance of registers and
records.
PC10. Identify the powers of the appropriate government in enforcing the Act.
PC11. Describe the offenses and penalties for non-compliance with the Minimum Wages Act, 1948.

Description
This NOS focuses on assessing HR policies, procedures, and financial aspects to ensure compliance,
efficiency, and alignment with organizational goals.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Human Resource Accounting
Methods and Human Resource Accounting Practices in India
Human Resource Audit
HR Audit for Legal Compliance and Safe Business Practices
HR Audit as Intervention
Elements and Performance Criteria
Human Resource Accounting
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Define the meaning, need, and objectives of Human Resource Accounting.
PC2. Outline the historical development of Human Resource Accounting.
PC3. Identify and explain the different costs involved in Human Resource Accounting: acquisition
cost, training and development cost, and additional cost.
PC4. Assess the benefits and limitations of Human Resource Accounting.
PC5. Discuss the reporting of Human Resource Accounting at national levels.
PC6. Examine disclosures of Human Resource Accounting at the international level.
Methods and Human Resource Accounting Practices in India
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Describe the Cost of Production Approach, including Historical Cost Model, Replacement Cost
Model, and Opportunity Cost, along with their advantages and limitations.
PC8. Explain the Capitalized Earnings Approach, including the Economic Value Model and
Capitalization of Salary, with their respective advantages and limitations.
PC9. Analyze the application of various Human Resource Accounting methods in India.
PC10. Evaluate the effectiveness and challenges of implementing these practices in the Indian
context.
Human Resource Audit
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Define the meaning, features, and objectives of HR Audit.
PC12. Outline the benefits, limitations, and significance of HR Audit in an organization.
PC13. Detail the process of conducting an HR Audit.
PC14. Discuss the different approaches of HR Audit and the principles of effective HR auditing.
