WBVT/ELE/Q0101: ASSISTANT TECHNICIAN OF CREATIVE & DYNAMIC
LIGHTING SYSTEM
Brief Job Description
Assistant Technician of Creative & Dynamic Lighting System is designed to equip learners with
foundational knowledge and practical skills in the setup, programming, and maintenance of creative and
dynamic lighting systems used in events, entertainment, architectural installations, and theatrical
productions. Participants will gain hands-on experience with lighting control software and hardware,
understand DMX and networked lighting protocols, and learn techniques for designing and operating
synchronized lighting shows. The qualification prepares individuals for entry-level roles supporting lighting
assistant technicians, and production teams in a variety of creative industries
Personal Attributes
Personal attribute required for Assistant Technician of Creative and Dynamic Lighting system are - (i)
Technical Aptitude, (ii) Creative & Visual sense, (iii) Attention to detail, (iv) Safety awareness, (v)
Teamwork & Coordination, (vi) Time management & Reliability, (vii) Physical Fitness, (viii) Problem solving
ability, (ix) Learning attitude and (x) Professional behaviour

WBVT/ELE/N0102: Describe the Electrical Principles and Wiring System
Testing
Description
In this NOS, trainees will learn to identify different types of cables, Skinning and jointing of single/multi
strand wires, crimping thimbles, Lugs. Series and Parallel Circuits, Measuring Unknown Resistance using
various meters. Installation of Switches, Holder, Plugs, etc, use of wiring accessories, different types of
Fuses,Wiring, Switching and Domestic electrical Circuits.Earthing, testing of different Contactors relays,
Single-Phase transformer LV & HV winding, measure input and output voltage of Single-Phase transformer
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The individual is expected to -
- 1. Explain Ohms law,Resistors and Laws of Resistance.
- 2. Demonstrate the Series and Parallel Circuits and measurement of unknown resistance.
- 3. Select and install electrical material and components, for lighting arrangement.
- 4. Identify and Use of Wiring Accessories and earthing practice
Elements and Performance Criteria
Electrical Principles and Wiring System Testing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain Ohm’s law , Resistors and Laws of Resistance
PC2. Demonstrate the Series and Parallel Circuits and measurement of unknown resistance
PC3. Select and install electrical material and components, for lighting arrangement
PC4. Identify and Use of Wiring Accessories and earthing practice
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Explanation Properties of Conductors, Semi-Conductors, Insulators, Wires/Cables
KU2. Different types of wires , cables and their specification
KU3. Introduction to the Basic Terms, Definitions, Units and Effects of Electric Current
KU4. Ohms Law - Simple Electrical Circuits and Working Principles
KU5. Resistors - Laws of Resistance - Series and Parallel Circuits
KU6. Introduction to Common Electrical Accessories used in electrical wiring, & their Specifications
KU7. Explanation of the Working Principles and specification of different types of Switches, Lamp
Holders, Plugs and Sockets
KU8. Design Domestic wiring Circuits and Working Principles of Alarms & Switches, MCB,
RCCB,ELCB, MCCB

WBVT/ELE/N0104: Engineering Drawing and Lighting Design
Description
This NOS defines the competencies reqired to design right lighting for each room (kitchen, bedroom,
bathroom), Design shops lighting that makes look attractive and products stand out, stage lights and
spotlights for performers, Control brightness to create the right mood or ambiance, design principles like
balance, focus, and contrast using light. Use engineering drawings to create basic lighting layouts for real-
world applications like theaters, event spaces or homes. Read the layout diagram of different electrical &
electronics circuit
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Explain Lighting fundamentals and different types of light .
Apply lighting principles to various spaces,.
Create different colors and lighting on mood, ambiance, and overall aesthetics.
Read and apply engineering drawing in the field of decorative lighting

Engineering Drawing and Lighting Design

KU4. Light Distribution

KU6. Energy Efficiency lights (LED, CFL)
KU7. Direction and Spread of lights
