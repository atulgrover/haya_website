MIN/Q3302: Instrumentation Technician (Mineral Sands)
Brief Job Description
An Instrumentation Technician (Mineral Sands) is responsible for monitoring, maintaining and repairing the
processing tools and equipment used in rare earth mining.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be physically fit to work for long durations with concentration. The person should
have attention to detail, problem-solving and organizational skills. The individual should also have
appropriate verbal and written communication skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MIN/N3303: Perform inspection, testing, calibration and troubleshooting of measuring and control
equipment
- 2. MIN/N3304: Carry out preventive, breakdown, scheduled and predictive maintenance of mining
equipment
- 3. MIN/N1810: Follow the applicable health, safety and environmental guidelines (Placer Mining)
- 4. MIN/N1811: Work collaboratively and effectively in a team
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

MIN/N3303: Perform inspection, testing, calibration and troubleshooting
of measuring and control equipment
Description
This occupational standards unit is about the inspection, testing, calibration and troubleshooting of
measuring and control equipment
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare and use the testing tools for fault elimination
Test the measuring tools
Analyze the process equipment/ system
Calibrate the measuring tools
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare and use the testing tools for fault elimination
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. select the appropriate testing tool for use, such as multimeter, clamp meter, Piezo meter,
sensor etc., to measure parameters like Voltage, Current, Resistance, Pressure, Flow, Level,
Depth, Temperature etc
PC2. check the testing tools for faults, wear and tear or damage before use
PC3. use the testing equipment as per the Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) manual/
instructions
PC4. validate the calibration and traceability of testing tools against the applicable national
standards
PC5. validate the calibration and traceability of testing tools against the applicable national
standards
PC6. validate the performance of electrical and electronics devices using the testing tools in the
circuit/functional block diagram (FBD) - Centrifuge, Filter Press, Crystallizer, Auto Sampler,
Mass Flow Meter, Magnetic Flow Meter, Water Flow Meter, Air Flow Meter, Oil Flow meter,
Density meter, Load cells etc
PC7. diagnose faults to assess the operation of instrumentation system
PC8. undertake appropriate corrective measures to eliminate the fault to restore the normal
performance of the system
Test the measuring tools
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. check the measuring tools, such as flow meter. level indicator, pH meter, density meter,
pressure gauge/ temperature gauge, pinch control valve, butterfly valve etc., for faults, wear
and tear or damage
PC10. check the zero, span and range on indicators/controllers using the recommended
configuration/ OEM manual

MIN/N3304: Carry out preventive, breakdown, scheduled and predictive
maintenance of mining equipment
Description
This occupational standards unit is about carrying out the preventive, breakdown, scheduled and
predictive maintenance of rare earth mining equipment
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct preventive maintenance
Conduct breakdown maintenance
Conduct condition monitoring
Conduct scheduled maintenance
Conduct predictive maintenance
Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct preventive maintenance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. schedule and perform regular preventive maintenance of instrumentation systems/
equipment/ components, e.g. connection to terminal blocks, cable lugs, relays, Modules,
Switches and Conductor, etc.
PC2. carry out cleaning to ensure a dust and moisture free environment for the instrumentation
system
PC3. lubricate the moving machines parts of reduce wear and tear
PC4. adjust the process parameters for optimal equipment effectiveness
PC5. repair or replace the defective parts identified during preventive maintenance
PC6. carry out appropriate documentation concerning the preventive maintenance activities
Conduct breakdown maintenance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. perform breakdown for the equipment parts that experience breakdown, following the
maintenance plan
PC8. use the relevant testing tools to measure and control pressure, temperature, flow, level,
motion, force, etc.
PC9. use the appropriate tools to diagnose faults in the circuit
PC10. coordinate with the appropriate person concerning the breakdown maintenance emergencies
PC11. adjust the system components and replace the defective parts
PC12. troubleshoot for test failure to determine root causes and develop process plan to avoid
faults
PC13. carry out appropriate documentation concerning the breakdown maintenance activities
Conduct condition monitoring

PC14. use the appropriate critical equipment for condition monitoring, e.g. mass flow meter,

PC25. monitor the critical process parameters through the data acquired from field sensors
PC26. analyze the key trends concerning the faults and update the deviations
PC27. coordinate with the concerned authority or expert to avoid the deviation and optimize
maintenance strategy
PC28. perform maintenance activities as per predictive maintenance fault identification
