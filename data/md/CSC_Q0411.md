CSC/Q0411: Digital Twin Engineer
Brief Job Description
A Digital Twin engineer will develop and maintain a digital twin solution for the manufacturing process. The
CG Digital Twin Engineer will collaborate with cross-functional teams to create virtual replicas that
enhance understanding, simulation, and optimization of physical assets and systems.
Personal Attributes
A digital twin engineer requires a combination of technical skills, creativity, and an understanding of user
experience.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CSC/N0517: Develop, and implement digital twin solution based on the behavior of physical object

- 3. CSC/N1339: Collaboratively coordinate with the team

CSC/N0517: Develop, and implement digital twin solution based on the
behavior of physical object
Description
This NOS is regarding development and implementation of digital solution in the industries
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Implementation and development of digital twin solution
Elements and Performance Criteria
Develop, and implement digital twin solution based on the behavior of physical object
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Develop and integrate digital twin models that accurately represent the physical system.
PC2. Ensure the fidelity of digital twin models to real-world counterparts.
PC3. Establish effective data synchronization between the physical system and its digital twin.
PC4. Minimize latency in data updates to maintain real-time representation.
PC5. Successfully integrate IoT sensor data into the digital twin for comprehensive monitoring.
PC6. Ensure seamless communication between the digital twin and connected sensors.
PC7. Optimize the performance of digital twin models for efficiency and responsiveness.
PC8. Implement strategies to handle large datasets without compromising system performance.
PC9. Ensure interoperability with existing systems and platforms for seamless integration.
PC10. Collaborate with relevant stakeholders to address compatibility issues.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand the fundamental concept of digital twins, including their definition, purpose, and
benefits in various industries.
KU2. Knowledge of integrating data from various sources (sensors, IoT devices, simulations) to
create and maintain a digital twin.
KU3. Understanding of modeling techniques and simulation tools used to create and validate
digital twins.
KU4. Knowledge of IoT devices and sensor technology used to collect real-time data for digital
twins.
KU5. Understanding of data analytics methods and tools used to analyze data from digital twins
and derive insights.
KU6. Knowledge of cloud computing infrastructure and platforms used to store and process data
for digital twins.

KU7. Understanding of cybersecurity principles and practices to ensure the security and integrity
of digital twin data.
KU8. Familiarity with specific industry requirements and standards for digital twins, such as
manufacturing, healthcare, or smart cities
KU9. Knowledge of collaborative platforms and tools used to share and collaborate on digital twin
data and models.
KU10. Understanding of the lifecycle management of digital twins, including updates, maintenance,
and decommissioning.
KU11. Awareness of regulatory requirements and standards related to digital twins, such as data
privacy and security regulations.
KU12. Knowledge of emerging technologies (e.g., AI, machine learning, blockchain) and their
potential impact on digital twin development and implementation.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Ability to analyze complex data sets and derive meaningful insights to improve digital twin
performance and reliability.
GS2. Capacity to identify issues with digital twin models or data and develop effective solutions to
address them.
GS3. Ability to communicate technical concepts and findings to non-technical stakeholders,
facilitating understanding and collaboration.
GS4. Thoroughness in creating and maintaining digital twin models to ensure accuracy and
reliability.
GS5. Flexibility to adapt to changing project requirements and technologies in the field of digital
twins.
GS6. Collaboration with cross-functional teams to develop and implement digital twin solutions
effectively.
GS7. Efficient management of time and resources to meet project deadlines and deliverables.
GS8. Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, R, or MATLAB for data analysis and
model development in digital twin projects.
GS9. Experience with simulation software (e.g., Simulink, ANSYS) for creating and validating
digital twin models.
GS10. Ability to manage large datasets for digital twin applications, including data collection,
storage, and processing.
GS11. Skills in project planning, execution, and monitoring to ensure successful implementation of
digital twin projects.
GS12. Willingness to stay updated with the latest trends and technologies in digital twin
development through self-learning and professional development opportunities.

CSC/N0518: Manage Data, Software, and Technical Support.
Description
This NOS is regarding management ,software and technical aspects of the industrial systems
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Management of data, software and technical aspects
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage Data, Software, and Technical Support.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Adhere to coding standards and best practices in software development.
PC2. Produce high-quality, maintainable, and scalable code for digital twin applications.
PC3. Conduct rigorous testing of developed software to identify and resolve bugs and errors.
PC4. Implement unit testing, integration testing, and system testing as part of the development
process.
PC5. Utilize version control systems to manage code changes systematically.
PC6. Maintain comprehensive documentation for developed software, including code comments
and user manuals.
PC7. Embrace agile development methodologies for flexibility and adaptability.
PC8. Collaborate with cross-functional teams and stakeholders in iterative development cycles.
PC9. Implement robust security measures in software development to protect against cyber
threats.
PC10. Adhere to cybersecurity best practices and address vulnerabilities promptly.
PC11. Meet project deadlines and milestones for software development.
PC12. Communicate effectively with project managers and team members regarding progress and
potential delays.
PC13. Gather and incorporate user feedback into the software development process.
PC14. Iterate on software features based on user experience and requirements.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand the fundamental concept of digital twins, including their definition, purpose, and
benefits in various industries.
KU2. Knowledge of integrating data from various sources (sensors, IoT devices, simulations) to
create and maintain a digital twin.

Description
This OS unit is about building relationships and working with people and groups inside and outside the
organization, using skills and habits, to achieve the team goals and objectives
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Creating team environment
Communicating - giving and receiving
Working cooperatively
Participating in team decision making
Demonstrating Sense of Responsibility
Showing respect for opinions, customs, and preferences

organisation
