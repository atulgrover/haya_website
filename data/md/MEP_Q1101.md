#### MEP/Q1101: CSR and Sustainability Specialist
Brief Job Description
A CSR and Sustainability Specialist perform developmental activities aimed at increasing diversity and
inclusivity. The work involves developing CSR and Sustainability programs for the company, increasing
internal engagement, and ensuring a positive impact on all stakeholders.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be well-groomed, attentive, multi-tasker, good at handling pressure, have good
communication skills, provide attention to details, respect confidentiality, and have a positive attitude.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### MEP/N1126: Integration of CSR and Sustainability
Description
Introduction to CSR and Sustainability is a foundation NOS to learn the basics of CSR and Sustainability. It
gives you a knowledge of different facets of CSR and Sustainability. It prepares you to the next ladder of
theory and practical experiences of CSR and Sustainability
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Align CSR and Sustainability in business
Implement global CSR Laws
Apply SDGs, NVGs, MDGs, BRSR
Design projects on CSR and Sustainability
Implement CSR and Sustainability
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Align CSR and Sustainability in business
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Link CSR and sustainability principles with day-to-day business practices and strategic
decision-making
- PC2. Embed sustainability values into company mission, vision, and operational frameworks
Implement global CSR Laws
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. Apply relevant national and international CSR regulations, including Companies Act, SEBI
mandates, and global CSR disclosure standards
- PC4. Develop CSR policies and form governance structures such as CSR committees in compliance
with national legal frameworks
- PC5. Integrate international CSR and sustainability guidelines, such as those issued by the UN,
OECD, and Global Compact
Apply SDGs, NVGs, MDGs, BRSR
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Align CSR and sustainability initiatives with frameworks such as the Sustainable
Development Goals (SDGs), National Voluntary Guidelines (NVGs), and Millennium
Development Goals (MDGs)
- PC7. Prepare and submit sustainability reports in formats such as BRSR and any other frameworks
mandated by Indian regulators or global platforms
Design projects on CSR and Sustainability
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Design CSR and sustainability projects that are socially impactful, scalable, and aligned with
company values

#### MEP/N1119: Project Management  in CSR
Description
The NOS is designed to train people on project management basics, implementation, and reporting. This
NOS deals with the intricacies of project management and enables the learners to foresee challenges and
find prior solutions for the smooth functioning of the project. It also trains them with tools such as
Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA) methodology to prepare them to communicate with the rural, urban
slums, and other stakeholders to prepare comprehensive studies and find solutions.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
CSR and Sustainability policy framing
Project planning and designing
Project Budgeting
Building roadmap and benchmarks
Reporting and Communication
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
CSR and Sustainability Policy framing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Apply national CSR laws and align them with the organization’s CSR and sustainability
policies
- PC2. Conduct organization-wide and community-level need assessments to inform policy and
project priorities
- PC3. Align applicable sections of CSR law to project selection based on board or director-level
guidance
Project planning and designing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Design projects based on ground-level requirements, national development goals, or
company CSR mandates
- PC5. Develop achievable goals and measurable outcomes aligned with project scope and
stakeholder expectations
- PC6. Map key internal and external stakeholders to be involved in project design and execution
Project Budgeting
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Develop project budgets covering implementation, vendor management, and stakeholder
costs
- PC8. Allocate budget appropriately across different geographies or project sites to ensure equity
and effectiveness
- PC9. Integrate sustainability principles in budgeting to ensure long-term impact and resource
efficiency

#### MEP/N1105: Strategic CSR communication
Description
Strategic CSR communications for CSR manager will give an insight into using communication skills to
create greater impact, contribute towards enhancing organization's image, professional skills, and creating
better stakeholder engagement.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Importance of CSR Communications
Research and data-based approach for communications
Platform for social impact communications for CSR projects
CSR report as per the compliance requirements
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Importance of CSR Communications
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Map appropriate communication materials for different stakeholder groups involved in CSR
initiatives
- PC2. Develop a communication strategy that aligns with the CSR goals and the organizational
brand image
Research and data-based approach for communications
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. Prepare customized CSR communication materials based on the needs and interests of
respective stakeholder groups
- PC4. Conduct data collection and analysis to create credible and evidence-based CSR
communication content
- PC5. Develop a robust communication plan that incorporates facts, stories, and impact indicators
from CSR activities
Platform for social impact communications for CSR projects
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Evaluate external platforms such as social media, public relations outlets, and CSR networks
for communicating project outcomes
- PC7. Prepare and finalize communication content suitable for dissemination through social media,
PR campaigns, and beneficiary outreach
CSR report as per the compliance requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Identify and decide on appropriate CSR and sustainability reporting methodologies applicable
in India and internationally

#### MEP/N1111: Sustainability Indices and Benchmarking
Description
This subject enables the learner to know different measurement tools for the work done and value the
impact It gives a rundown of the Social Stock Exchange Frameworks implementation rating and benchmarking
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Build indicators and reporting systems for sustainable business
Applying national and international sustainability guidelines
Aligning business with sustainability indices and investment norms
Benchmark projects and align with sustainability indices
Formulate measuring and reporting strategy
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Build indicators and reporting systems for sustainable business
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify relevant tools and frameworks (e.g., GRI, B Impact Assessment, SASB) for measuring
sustainable business performance
- PC2. Engage with internal and external stakeholders to identify material sustainability issues and
align performance indicators
- PC3. Build structured internal and external reporting cycles using tools such as dashboards, ESG
reports, and BRSR disclosures
Applying national and international sustainability guidelines
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Apply Indian and international sustainability guidelines and standards such as ISO 26000,
BRSR, GRI, and SDG frameworks
- PC5. Integrate these sustainability guidelines into business operations, decision-making, and
strategic reporting processes
Aligning business with sustainability indices and investment norms
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Backtrack and analyze the listing norms, scoring criteria, and benchmarks used in leading
sustainability indices (e.g., Dow Jones Sustainability Index, NIFTY100 ESG)
- PC7. Integrate sustainability practices in business operations to enhance eligibility for listing on
relevant sustainability indices
- PC8. Promote or invest in sustainability-driven projects that meet long-term ESG goals and offer
inclusion in green investment frameworks
Benchmark projects and align with sustainability indices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down a proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on a knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.

| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
