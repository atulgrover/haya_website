MEP/Q7106: Guard - Cash Logistics
Brief Job Description
Guard - Cash Logistics is required to identify the threats and risks on the given routes. The person handles
cash in transit, protects and manages the operations of the cash van as part of the security services in
terms of the cash in transit. Guard - Cash Logistics also should have the knowledge of the road.
Personal Attributes
Security, Armed guards, Strict protocol, Armoured vehicles, Client trust, Emergency response.

MEP/N7233: Cash handling and cash transportation activities
Description
Carefully handles the cash in transit and takes care of all the necessary activities required to safely handle
the cash in transit.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out assigned tasks in relation to cash/ valuables handling and transportation.
Conform to regulatory and legal requirements.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out assigned tasks in relation to cash/ valuables handling and transportation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Obtain the assignment in relation to transportation of cash/ valuables and coordinate with
other armed security guard
PC2. Have a clear knowledge of the route, and destination
PC3. Plan the movement of the cash van, keeping the threats and route to the destination.
PC4. Brief the cash van driver with respect to the task, route and destination for the assigned task
of cash transportation; and arrange the same to be placed for pick up as required as per
organisational protocol
PC5. Collect/ check the cash/ valuables in sealed boxes as per organisation protocol and secure
the same in the cash van.
PC6. Arrange the cash van to be driven to the destination and maintain its security during the
transit
PC7. Maintain observation of the route during the movement of the cash van to the assigned
destination.
PC8. Arrange to hand over the sealed boxes of cash/ valuables to the authorities at the
destination following the proper security protocol
PC9. Maintain communication with the control through the cash in transit operation to include
reporting the completion of the task
PC10. Inform the control, police and appropriate authority of threats as per the organisational
procedure
Conform to regulatory and legal requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Carry out tasks relevant to the role while complying with applicable regulatory and legal
provisions as per PSAR Act 2005 and Model Rules thereof.
PC12. Work within various Acts and Rules as per clause 13 (1) (j) of PASR Act 2005 read with Clause
4 (3) (iii) of PSAR Act Model Rules-2020, and their implications in the security tasks.
PC13. Keep the law enforcement forces (Police) of the relevant activities with respect to cash van
movement

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Explain organizational protocols for cash transport assignments and armed personnel

van.

KU6. Implement emergency procedures, legal compliance, and communication methods throughout
transit operations.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:

GS2. Document tasks and incidents accurately
GS3. Make informed decision-making by analytically assessing risks
GS4. Response promptly to security threats following protocols
GS5. Plan strategically considering threats and security protocols.
GS6. Adapt to address unexpected challenges.
GS7. Adhere to PSAR Act 2005 & Model Rules for compliance.
GS8. Explain implications of relevant Acts and Rules in security tasks.

MEP/N7234: Identify threats and risks with a view to minimize them
Description
Identifies threats and risks associated with carrying on the task of safeguarding the cash in transit and
delivering it safely
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify threats and risks during cash van movement.
Minimize the threats and risks.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify threats and risks during cash van movement
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify the layout of the cash/ valuable pick up point, route and destination
PC2. Identify the threats and risks focused on the movement pick up point, route and destination.
PC3. Guard pick up point, route and destination, based on the threats and risks
PC4. Carry out guarding and observation tasks at all times
PC5. Patrol the required premises required areas to identify risks and address them
PC6. Report and respond to threats and risks as per organisational procedures, promptly
PC7. Provide adequate briefing during shift change and to superiors
PC8. Maintain basic security registers and records
Minimise the threats and risks
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. carry out required searches of premises and properties as per instructions
PC10. report risks, threats and hazards observed during the search
PC11. liaise with other authorised search parties in the premises
PC12. use the weapon as per the organisational procedures and legality when threat appears
PC13. maintain personal safety and weapon security and safety at all times.
PC14. maintain constant communication during search with relevant authorities
PC15. report incident details to superiors in an accurate and timely manner
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Understand the layout of cash/valuable pick-up points, routes, and destinations.
KU2. Identify threats and risks associated with movement at pick-up points, routes, and
destinations.

MEP/N7235: Safety and Security of Cash Van
Description
Maintaining the security and safety of the cash van while in loading, transit and unloading the cash.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Safety of cash van
Security of the cash van
Elements and Performance Criteria
Safety of Cash Van
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. check the inbuilt safety features of the cash van carrying cash/ valuables
PC2. check the locking and security systems of the cash van
PC3. examine availability of equipment required for safety ( such as fire safety equipment,
distress alarm, first aid kit and communication equipment)
PC4. Ensure the completeness, safety and functional aspects of the weapon being carried
PC5. Oversee safety of the team members, cash van and the cash/ valuables
PC6. Use weapon as per the organisational and legal protocol to deter/ resist criminals
PC7. inform appropriate authority of the threats and risky incidents
PC8. Check the personal and vehicular communication equipment are complete and functional
Security of the cash Van
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. observe the premises for pick up and its surroundings for any unwanted activities
PC10. interpret signs of threat at the pick up point, route and destination and take appropriate
action, report functioning/ malfunctioning of access control equipment to relevant authority
PC11. Examine security and maintenance of weapon and ammunition required to be carried during
assigned task
PC12. Use weapon to deter or resist the criminals, keeping in view the organisational and legal
procedures
PC13. Report to the concerned personnel of the relevant events/ accidents/ threats experienced
during the assigned task
PC14. Report irregularities and maintenance requirements timely to appropriate authority
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Identify safety features and examine locking systems of the cash van.

Assessment Guidelines
Assessment system Overview
Assessment will be carried out by assessment partners with no link to training partners. Based on the
results of assessment, MEPSC will certify the learners. Assessor has to pass online assessment of
theoretical knowledge of the job role and approved by MEPSC. The assessment will have both theory and
practical components in 40:60 ratio.
Testing Environment
Training partner has to share the batch start date and end date, number of trainees and the job role.
Assessment will be fixed for a day after the end date of training. It could be next day or later. Assessment
will be conducted at the training venue.

Qualification Pack
Assessor has to go through orientation program organized by Assessment Agency. The training will give an
overview to the assessors on the overall framework of QP evaluation. Assessor will also be given a NOS

the board in the lab or shared with the candidates verbally.
Methods of Validation
Unless the trainee is registered, the person cannot undergo assessment. To further ensure that the person
registered is the person appearing for assessment, id verification will be carried out. Aadhar card number
is part of registering the candidate for training. This will form the basis of further verification during the
assessment. Assessor conducts the assessment in accordance with the assessment guidelines and
question bank as per the job role. The assessor carries tablet with the loaded questions. This tablet is
geotagged and so it is monitored to check their arrival and completion of assessment. The training partner
will also intimate the time of arrival of the assessor and time of leaving the venue. The assessment will be
video recorded and submitted to MEPSC. Video of the practical session is prepared and submitted to
MEPSC. Random spot checks/audit is conducted by MEPSC assigned persons to check the quality of
assessment. Assessment agency will be responsible to put details in SID. MEPSC will also validate the data
and result received from the assessment agency.
Method of assessment documentation and access
The assessment agency will upload the result of assessment in the portal. The data will not be accessible
for change by the assessment agency after the upload. The assessment data will be validated by MEPSC
assessment team. After upload, only MEPSC can access this data.
MEPSC approves the results within a week and uploads on SID.
