MSME/CSC/Q3101: SR. TECHNICIAN -MECHATRONICS
Brief Job Description
The qualification contains different modules which are required for the job role of Sr. Technician
(Mechatronics) This qualified learner shall be able to Install, operate the mechatronics system.
Personal Attributes
The qualification contains different modules which are required for the job role of Sr. Technician
(Mechatronics) This qualified learner shall be able to Install, operate the mechatronics system.
