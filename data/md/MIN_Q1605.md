MIN/Q1605: Coal Bed Methane (CBM) Extraction Operator
Brief Job Description
The individual in this role is responsible for operating and maintaining methane extraction unit to extract
methane from the coal bed.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it efficiently and
effectively. The individual should be physically agile, strong, have good eye-sight and not suffer from color-
blindness.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MIN/N1613: Prepare methane extraction unit for operation
- 2. MIN/N1614: Perform extraction of methane from coal bed
- 3. MIN/N1615: Perform maintenance of the methane extraction unit
- 4. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Opencast
- 1. MIN/N1703: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental Guidelines for opencast mines
Elective 2: Underground Coal
- 1. MIN/N1704: Follow Health, Safety, and Environmental guidelines for underground coal mines

MIN/N1613: Prepare methane extraction unit for operation
Description
This unit is about inspecting & preparing the equipment for extracting methane from coal bed.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for work
Inspect and prepare the methane extraction unit for operation
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the work requirements from the instructions received from supervisor and
interpreting information from previous day/shift logbook
PC2. use personal protective equipment appropriate for work activities
PC3. select appropriate type of auxiliary equipment for work activities
Inspect and prepare the methane extraction unit for operation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. check various controls, pressure gauges, pump and allied components, hydraulic fracture,
compressor, gas separator and water separator, back pressure regulator, piping system etc.
for proper functioning and any defect
PC5. ensure that all meters, gauges etc. are calibrated
PC6. check the direction of rotation of motor is corresponding to the regulated rotation direction of
pump
PC7. check the condition of inlet and outlet valves and ensure that they are working properly and
in normal state
PC8. check the pipe system and other parts such as anchor bolt, flange sealing gasket and other
bolts for proper working and good condition
PC9. check all foundation bolts and outlet pipework bolts for appropriate tightness and condition
PC10. ensure all electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, sealant and lubrication systems (as applicable)
are connected and operational
PC11. maintain the operation and maintenance logbook to detail all activities conducted before
starting the operation
PC12. report defects precisely to the supervisor if beyond scope of his/her role
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. relevant standards and procedures followed in the company

MIN/N1614: Perform extraction of methane from coal bed
Description
This unit is about performing processes for extracting methane gas from coal bed.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Start and operate the methane extraction unit
Stop the methane extraction unit
Elements and Performance Criteria
Start and operate the methane extraction unit
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. open the inlet valve and fully close the outlet valve before starting the pump
PC2. start the extraction unit (pump and separators) and slowly open the outlet valve until the
pump reaches the desired flow
PC3. check the pressure gauge to ensure that the pump reaches the required outlet pressure
PC4. stop the pump, and take the corrective actions as per SOP
PC5. monitor the gauges for bearing temperature, excessive vibration, and noise during the
operation
PC6. shut down the pump immediately, if there is no pressure or low pressure or pump exceeds
normal levels and take the corrective action as per SOP
PC7. monitor the operation of gas separator and ensure that it is extracting the methane and
water contaminants properly and transferring them through their respective pipes
PC8. monitor the process parameters by reading the various gauges and correct them if not within
standards
PC9. check the oil level at regular intervals
PC10. observe any gas leakage during the operation and stop the extraction unit immediately, if
found
PC11. monitor the operation of complete extraction unit and record the operational data as per the
frequency in the control plan
Stop the methane extraction unit
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. close the outlet valve, but ensure that the pump runs in this condition for no more than a few
seconds
PC13. stop the complete extraction unit
PC14. maintain and complete all required documentation clearly, concisely as per SOP

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

MIN/N1615: Perform maintenance of the methane extraction unit
Description
This unit is about performing routine maintenance and troubleshooting tasks on the methane extraction
#### unit.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for maintenance work
Repair and maintain the methane extraction unit
Perform post-maintenance activities
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for maintenance work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the extraction unit manufacturer's specifications from the user manual and drawings
PC2. read maintenance schedule and checklist and plan the time and schedule for conducting the
maintenance
PC3. identify and arrange the tools, consumables and spare parts required during the task
PC4. follow safety practices during maintenance activities as per organisational SOP
Repair and maintain the methane extraction unit
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. perform basic health check-up of methane extraction unit as specified in the maintenance
checklist
PC6. replenish coolants and lubricants as per SOP
PC7. clean dirt and dust from areas around close clearances, bearing housings, separators and
motors
PC8. check coupling alignment and re-align if necessary
PC9. check the methane extraction unit parts i.e. motor, pump, gas separator, water separator,
back pressure regulator, seals, auxiliary supplies e.g. heating/cooling (if fitted), wiring
harness, connectors etc. for proper functioning to find out root cause of the problems like
any leakage, short circuit in parts, breakage of wires and clamps, etc. and discuss the same
with senior if required
PC10. check for unusual noise vibration and bearing temperatures in the extraction unit
PC11. check the separators, pump and piping, seal chamber and stuffing box etc. for leaks
PC12. check the instrument panel and control gauge to identify out of range readings to find the
defect/cause of failure
PC13. replace/change the spare parts and consumables of the extraction unit as per the schedule
PC14. ensure proper lock out and tag out of methane extraction unit to avoid any untoward
incident triggered due to unknowingly operation of machine / system under maintenance

PC11. disinfect the machine/tools before and after work/task
