ASP/ITS/Q0301: Business Analyst
Brief Job Description
This comprehensive, application-oriented program is designed to equip learners with the critical
knowledge and technical skills required to drive data-driven decision-making in modern businesses. The
course blends statistical foundations, data management, machine learning, and storytelling into one
integrated learning journey. Through core modules in R, Python, Excel, and modern business tools,
students master techniques in data wrangling, visualization, statistical inference, predictive modelling, and
cloud-based big data analytics. Learners are also introduced to emerging technologies like Generative AI
for business insights and explore real-time analytics using cloud and big data platforms.
Personal Attributes
The course will help in bridging the gap between IT and the business using data analytics to assess
processes, determine requirements and deliver data-driven recommendations and reports to executives
and stakeholders.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. ASP/ITS/N0301: Fundamentals of Business Analytics
- 2. ASP/ITS/N0306: Advanced Business Analytics and Applications
- 3. ASP/ITS/N0304: Analytics in Action: Sector Insights and Capstone Delivery
- 4. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Banking and Finance
The Banking and Finance elective in Business Analytics trains learners to apply data analytics for financial
decision-making, risk assessment, fraud detection, and performance analysis in banks and financial
institutions.
- 1. ASP/ITS/N0307: Banking and Finance
Elective 2: Supply Chain and Logistics
This elective teaches how to use data analytics to improve supply chain efficiency, optimize inventory,
forecast demand, and streamline logistics operations for better business performance.

ASP/ITS/N0301: Fundamentals of Business Analytics
Description
This NOS covers the foundational skills and knowledge required to understand, interpret, and apply basic
concepts of business analytics. It enables learners to use data-driven insights to support decision-making,
performance tracking, and strategic planning in a business environment. The standard focuses on
analytical thinking, data literacy, and the practical use of analytical tools and techniques to derive
meaningful business insights.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS defines the scope of skills and knowledge required to understand the core principles,
functions, and environment of modern businesses. It covers the fundamental concepts that form the
foundation for any business professional, including organizational structures, management
functions, business operations, marketing, finance, and ethical practices.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Demonstrates understanding of data science foundations
PC2. Applies data thinking to solve business problems
PC3. Uses Excel functions for data analysis and modeling
PC4. Creates interactive dashboards for business insights
PC5. Visualizes and communicates data insights effectively
PC6. Understands relational database concepts and structures
PC7. Executes SQL queries for data extraction and analysis
PC8. Uses R for data manipulation and exploratory analysis
PC9. Builds data visualizations and interprets results in R
PC10. Implements basic analytics workflows in R
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Curiosity and Problem-Solving
GS2. Critical Thinking
GS3. Communication Skills
GS4. Programming
GS5. Mathematics and Statistics

ASP/ITS/N0306: Advanced Business Analytics and Applications
Description
This NOS covers the competencies required to apply advanced business analytics techniques and software
tools to drive strategic decision-making, performance improvement, and innovation across business
functions. It focuses on leveraging data-driven insights using predictive, prescriptive, and cognitive
analytics to optimize operations, marketing, sales, and customer engagement.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the skills, knowledge, and performance criteria required for professionals to apply
advanced analytical techniques and data-driven applications to solve complex business problems
and enhance decision-making.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Applies statistical concepts for business decision-making
PC2. Demonstrates proficiency in Python for data analysis
PC3. Builds and evaluates machine learning models using business tools
PC4. Understands and applies GenAI in business analytics contexts
PC5. Utilizes big data tools and cloud platforms for scalable analytics
PC6. Manages project workflows and stakeholder communication
PC7. Designs and delivers compelling business presentations
PC8. Collaborates effectively across business and tech
PC9. Applies cross-functional analytics to solve sector-specific problems
PC10. Synthesizes knowledge through end-to-end capstone execution

ASP/ITS/N0304: Analytics in Action: Sector Insights and Capstone Delivery
Description
This NOS covers applying analytical skills to sector-specific data, generating actionable insights, and
delivering a capstone project. It emphasizes interpreting data, identifying trends, making informed
decisions, and effectively communicating findings in real-world business contexts.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS is designed to equip learners with the ability to apply analytical tools, frameworks, and
methodologies to real-world business and sector-specific scenarios. It emphasizes hands-on
application of data analytics, sector insight generation, and the execution of a capstone project to
demonstrate end-to-end problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Demonstrates domain-specific understanding of business challenges
PC2. Applies appropriate analytical tools and techniques to sectoral datasets
PC3. Interprets and derives actionable insights from sector-specific data
PC4. Builds and visualizes relevant KPIs through dashboards or reports
PC5. Designs and executes a complete analytics workflow in the capstone project
PC6. Employs storytelling to present insights effectively
PC7. Collaborates effectively within a project team setting
PC8. Adheres to data ethics and governance in applied work
PC9. Evaluates model performance and aligns outcomes to business goals
PC10. Delivers a comprehensive, well-documented capstone project aligned with business
objectives
