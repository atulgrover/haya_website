HCS/Q2204: Bangle maker
Brief Job Description
A skilled bangle maker specializes in crafting a diverse range of bangles, including wood bangles, conch
shell bangles, glass bangles, lac bangles, and metal bangles. Using traditional techniques and a variety of
materials, the bangle maker demonstrates expertise in shaping, designing, and finishing each type of
bangle to create unique and intricately crafted pieces.
Personal Attributes
The individual must have attention to detail, creative mindset, logical thinking, hand-eye coordination,
good analytical skills, problem solving, reliability, good decision-making skills, basic mathematical skills
and should not have colour blindness. The individual should be good at following instructions.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HCS/N1307: Introduction and different types of Bangle making in India
- 2. HCS/N9908: Working in a Team
- 3. HCS/N9945: Maintaining workplace health and safety
- 4. HCS/N9935: Maintain Personal Cleanliness
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Conch Shell Bangles
This OS unit is about preparing raw materials of making conch shell bangles and ensuring the quality of
the prepared bangles.
- 1. HCS/N1303: Carry out the process of making conch shell bangles
Elective 2: Glass Bangles
This OS unit is about preparing raw materials, making glass bangles, and ensuring the quality of the
prepared bangle.
- 1. HCS/N2207: Carry out the process of making glass bangles

HCS/N1307: Introduction and different types of Bangle making in India
Description
Bangle Maker introduces the core competencies and skills required for individuals engaged in the craft of
bangle making. The knowledge, abilities, and practices necessary to excel in this esteemed occupation,
blending traditional techniques with contemporary innovation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
introduction of Bangle making
different types of Bangle making
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction of bangle making
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. introduce the historical roots of bangle making in India, highlighting its centuries-old tradition
and its role as a symbol of cultural identity and craftsmanship
PC2. in Indian society, bangles symbolize marital status, prosperity, and well-being. Married women
often wear them as a sign of their union, while single women and girls also adorn them for
cultural and aesthetic reasons
PC3. bangles have evolved from being solely traditional accessories to fashion statements, with
contemporary designs catering to modern tastes
Different types of bangles making
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. discuss how bangle making is practiced across different regions of India, each with its own
distinct style, materials, and techniques.
PC5. traditional materials for making bangles include glass, metal, lac, and wood, showcasing the
diversity of resources and skills across different regions.
PC6. bangle making involves skilled artisans shaping and crafting each bangle individually using
traditional tools and techniques.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. understand various materials used in bangle making, including glass, metal, lac, and modern
alternatives
KU2. learn about material properties, suitability for designs, and market availability
KU3. develop understanding of bangle design principles, aesthetics, and cultural motifs
KU4. learn to conceptualize and sketch designs, incorporating traditional and contemporary
elements

HCS/N1304: Carry out the process of making metal bangles
Description
This OS unit is about preparing raw materials for making metal bangles and ensuring the quality of the
prepared bangles.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
collect tools and raw material for making metal bangle
perform the standard process of making metal bangle
check bangle quality and productivity standards
Elements and Performance Criteria
collect tools and raw material for making metal bangle
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. collect raw material such as raw wire (brass or any as per requirement), Sulphuric acid &
nitric acid solution, etc.
PC2. place required material at workplace in a safe manner
PC3. collect tools such as marker, measuring scale, tapper (wooden hammer or light weight
wooden tool), wire cutter, shaping rod, etc.
PC4. arrange machinery like shaping and alignment pressing machine, designing machine, and
electric oven/furnace based on the properties of the raw metal used, etc.
perform the standard process of making metal bangle
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. mark the wire to be cut into the required size using a marker and cut the wire with the help
of a cutter in a safe manner
PC6. bend the wire in round shape using shaping rod and tapper
PC7. join the ends of the round-shaped wire by soldering, and put the soldered bangle in a
solution of nitric acid and sulphate to derust and clean the soldered round shaped bangle
PC8. provide the required shape to the cleaned bangle using a shaping & alignment pressing
machine or manually by using hand tools
PC9. design the bangle using designing machines or manually create different designs using hand
tools, etc. in a careful manner
PC10. prepare a solution of sulphuric acid and nitric acid (solution may differ for different types of
metal bangle) and soak the designed bangle for approx. two to five seconds to make them
lustrous and rust free
PC11. place the coated bangles in an electric oven or furnace as per the properties of metal to dry
them
PC12. pack the bangle using paper and cardboard boxes, etc. safely and in a delicate manner.
check bangle quality and productivity standards
