#### PSS/Q1704: Water  Treatment Operator - Thermal Power Plant
Brief Job Description
The individual in this job role operates a variety of plant equipment in a waste water/raw water treatment
plant and also assists the maintenance team. The individual is responsible for ensuring timely upkeep of
systems and equipment that remove pollutants from wastewater and performing related work, as required.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be physically and mentally fit to perform various activities at a water treatment plant.
Patience, ability to work in coordination with co-workers and inspecting the work in detail are other
important attributes required for this job role.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PSS/N1331: Apply basic health and safety practices for power related work
2. PSS/N1336: Working effectively with others
3. PSS/N1705: Perform operations of water treatment plant

#### PSS/N1705: Perform  operations of water treatment plant
Description
This unit is about various activities performed by an operator at a water treatment plant to ensure
effective water treatment.
Scope
This unit/ task covers the following:
Operate all equipment of water treatment plant
Perform post-operation and inspection activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform operations of water treatmentplant
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. operate all equipment in the water treatment plant viz. valves, flash mixer, clariflocculator,
filter bed, sump, demineralized water plant etc.
- PC2. carry out operations of filtering and chemical treatment equipment to purify and clarify
water, dispose of sewage etc., for production of demineralized water (dm)
- PC3. monitor operating conditions, record instruments and gauges reading /operation data in the
daily log sheets and maintain records
- PC4. • carry out timely maintenance of the water treatment plant systems and its equipments
• filters, tanks, gauges, pump, and valves etc.
- PC5. monitor process and equipment for removal of dissolved undissolved, physical and organic
impurities causing temporary hardness and permanent hardness
- PC6. ensure pre-treatment of water through various type of equipment installed viz. peripheral
clariflocculator and associated equipment filter bed viz. gravity filter beds, pressure filter beds etc.
- PC7. operate the ion exchangers viz. anion exchanger, cation exchanger and mixed bed
exchanger
- PC8. ensure availability of chemicals used in water treatment plant and for neutralization of waste
water
- PC9. operate pumps, valves etc. for implying/ cleaning process of sludge tank
- PC10. identify/ notice sounds, in leakages in pumps, faults in valves, filter beds etc.
- PC11. take necessary precaution about hazards of chemicals used in water treatment plant and
maintain the work area as per procedure and operation specification
- PC12. conduct regular inspections and record reading and findings accurately/ clearly in required
ledgers, forms and formats, log books as per required and approved procedures as per instructions
- PC13. ensure the work area is in a safe and tidy condition on completion of the job/ inspection and
testing activities
