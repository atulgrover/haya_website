#### PCS/Q0102: Technical Sales Representative
Brief Job Description
The individual at work must: interact with customers to understand their paint performance requirements,
help select the most appropriate paint/ paint system, train on the application process. collect feedback on
previously sold paint. offer the most suitable paint solution for a purpose, resolve complaints.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to: have pleasing personality, ability to express technical information in
common language and willingness to travel out of town.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PCS/N0101: Provide technical sales support to industrial paint customers
2. PCS/N0102: Provide technical support to dealers and institutional customers
3. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)
4. PCS/N9901: Co-ordinate with colleagues and customers
5. PCS/N9902: Maintain standards of product/ service quality
6. PCS/N9903: Maintain OH&S standards and follow environmental norms

#### PCS/N0101: Provide technical sales support to industrial paint customers
Description
This OS unit is about interacting with existing and potential industrial paint customers to understand their
special requirements on paint performance and provide technical support in selecting the most suitable
paint for application and closing the sale.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Address technical queries on paint performance, various surfaces and surface related issues
Provide solution for most suited paint system and/or application method
Gather feedback on specific paint or coating product
Document for future reference
Help close the sale
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Address technical queries on paint performance, various surface and surface related issues
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. check with customer about their specific and special performance requirement of the
industrial paint to be used
- PC2. understand the regular and new demand of the customer
- PC3. distinguish whether the requirement pertains to selection of paints,specific performance or
its application process
- PC4. address queries with most suitable technical explanation for the performance or application
requirement
- PC5. provide accurate information from previous experiences which required similar outcomes
- PC6. provide existing customer's references for validation by new or potential customer
- PC7. visit customers site for a thorough understanding of specific requirements for the given
surface or substrate or ambient conditions, etc.
- PC8. seek the support of the R&D team for higher-level queries that may require more detailed
analysis and solution
- PC9. explain about new products or product combinations in the market or of the company to help
resolve specific queries
Provide solution for most suited paint system and/or application method
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. provide logical explanation on why the paint or undercoat is most suited for the performance
desired by the customer, e.g., chemical properties and reactions of material composition of paint
- PC11. provide advice or solution in terms of best form of pre-treatment of surface or substrate for
the desired result

#### PCS/N0102: Provide technical support to dealers and institutional
customers
Description
This OS unit is about advising and/or providing training to dealers and educating institutional customers of
decorative paints on new paint products launched and their distinguishing features.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Advise and/or train dealers and their associates
Educate institutional customers
Provide technical support to customers of existing paint products and services
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Advise and/or train dealers and their associates
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. coordinate with paint dealer for technical support training
- PC2. document and resolve queries of dealers and their associates with respect to paint
performance
- PC3. educate dealer or associates on paint application process, coats and tools/ equipment to be
used
- PC4. coordinate with painters or painting contractors for conducting their training on new products
and application process
- PC5. make the dealer and/or associate aware of best practices and emerging trends

painting contractors
- PC7. advise on best possible paint to be used for the required purpose

- PC9. educate customer on most appropriate application process and undercoat to be applied for
new or existing paint products
- PC10. educate on the combination of tools and machines to be used at different stages of
preparation and painting for the desired finish

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Practical Skills for each Element/ PC.

3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for practical skill for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
