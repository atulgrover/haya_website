HYC/Q4004: Assistant Lab Technician - Green Hydrogen
Brief Job Description
An Assistant Lab Technician - Green Hydrogen is responsible for receiving, labelling and analysing
samples. The individual is responsible for designing and executing laboratory testing according standard
procedures. Conducting experiments under defined conditions to verify/reject various types of hypotheses
using refined scientific methods.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have ability to give attention to detail, technical aptitude, adaptability, teamwork
and a strong commitment to safety and sustainability. The person should have the ability to work
individually / team round the clock and must always promote safety.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management Procedures
- 2. HYC/N4008: Green Hydrogen Industry and Laboratory regulations
- 3. HYC/N4009: Experimentation and Analysis in Green Hydrogen
- 4. HYC/N9301: Working effectively in a team

HYC/N4001: Hydrogen Safety, Security and Health Management
Procedures
Description
This unit covers maintaining health, safety and security procedures at the workplace where Hydrogen is
produced and handled.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view
Safe handling of Hydrogen
Follow fire safety procedures
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Properties of Hydrogen from safety point of view & Safe handling of Hydrogen
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Ensure that personnel under their supervision have a deep understanding of hydrogen's
properties, including its flammability, explosive limits and potential hazards.
PC2. Ensure personal and team adherence to the use of appropriate protective
clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions
PC3. Identify and communicate the relevant documents, responsible individuals, and designated
locations pertaining to health and safety in the workplace, facilitating effective safety
management.
PC4. Oversee and participate in risk assessments specific to hydrogen storage, handling and
production processes.
PC5. Proficiently identify hydrogen process hazards and assess risks including the analysis of risk
or accident in the workplace due to hydrogen leak/fire
PC6. capability to accurately read and interpret data from sensing equipment used in hydrogen
production processes
PC7. apply key safety parameters for handling hydrogen & oxygen
PC8. Evaluate hydrogen release pressures and understand hydrogen flame detection methods,
emphasizing the colorless nature of hydrogen flames
PC9. Apply critical safety requirements for electrical equipment used in green hydrogen
production, minimizing electrical hazards
PC10. competent in the operation and functioning of hydrogen exhaust systems, facilitating the
safe removal of hydrogen from the workplace
PC11. Execute inertization processes using nitrogen to displace hazardous gases enhancing safety
in hydrogen-related operations
PC12. Proficiently identify and recognize common safety signs displayed in various areas,
promoting a safe work environment.

HYC/N4008: Green Hydrogen Industry and Laboratory regulations
Description
This unit covers standard testing procedures and protocols for all the raw material and final products in a
Green Hydrogen Quality Control laboratory. This includes procedures and practices that candidates need
to follow to ensure quality control and quality assurance in the production of green hydrogen in a healthy,
safe and secure work environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understanding Green Hydrogen Industry and Laboratory regulations.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding Green Hydrogen Industry and Laboratory regulations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand the fundamental concept of green hydrogen, which is hydrogen produced using
renewable energy sources and its importance in the context of sustainable energy.
PC2. Understand various methods or pathways for producing green hydrogen, such as electrolysis,
biomass conversion and solar-driven processes.
PC3. Recognize the critical role of raw materials, particularly water and renewable energy sources
in the production of green hydrogen.
PC4. Have awareness of the quality standards and criteria that govern the characteristics of green
hydrogen, including purity levels, impurity limits and environmental considerations.
PC5. Have knowledge of the specific rules and regulations governing quality control laboratories,
encompassing safety protocols, quality assurance procedures and compliance with industry
standards.
PC6. Understand the regulatory requirements specific to research and development labs,
including considerations related to intellectual property, data management and innovation
protection.
PC7. Understand the duties and responsibilities of a lab technician, which may include sample
analysis, instrument maintenance, data recording, adherence to safety protocols and
collaboration with other teams.
PC8. Carry knowledge of the essential functions that laboratories serve within a green hydrogen
production plant, such as quality control, process optimization, troubleshooting and
supporting research and development efforts.
PC9. Understand implementation of principles and procedures that ensure the integrity, reliability
and reproducibility of laboratory data.
PC10. Have knowledge of the quality management and control measures applied in manufacturing
processes to ensure product safety and consistency.
PC11. Carry awareness of the importance of accurate and organized documentation of laboratory
procedures, data and results.
