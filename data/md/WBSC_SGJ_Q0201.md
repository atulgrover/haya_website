WBSC/SGJ/Q0201: Bio Gas Plant & Bio Slurry Technician
Brief Job Description
A Bio Gas Plant & Bio Slurry Technician is responsible for the construction, operation and maintenance of
bio gas plants and the efficient management of bio-slurry for agricultural applications. The role involves
applying professional technical knowledge and practical skills to produce clean energy, manage organic
waste and promote sustainable farming practices. Technicians support farmers by reducing input costs,
improving soil fertility and enhancing productivity through the use of bio-slurry as an organic fertilizer.
Personal Attributes
A Bio Gas Plant & Bio Slurry Technician should possess a strong interest in sustainable agriculture, clean
energy and environmental protection along with a practical and problem-solving mindset. The individual
should be technically inclined, safety-conscious and capable of working both independently and in rural
field conditions. Good communication skills, teamwork and a sense of social responsibility are important as
the technician often works closely with farmers, rural youth and local institutions to promote eco-friendly
practices, enhance livelihoods and contribute to national sustainable development goals.

- 5. WBSC/SGJ/N0205: Construct a Bio Gas Plant with proper care and safety.
- 6. WBSC/SGJ/N0206: Install Cooking Apparatus.
- 7. WBSC/SGJ/N0207: Carry out post BGP construction activities.
- 8. WBSC/SGJ/N0208: Demonstrate the procedure of Bio slurry manure making and Marketing of
Organically grown crops.
- 9. WBSC/SGJ/N0209: Work in real job situation with special emphasis on basic safety and hazards in
this domain (Bio Gas Plant & Bio Slurry Technician).

WBSC/SGJ/N0201: Identify Biomass resource and Bio-energy potential.
Description
Identify Biomass resource and Bio-energy potential.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Classification of biomass.
Bio Energy Resources, World Bio Energy Potential, Bio Energy Potential of India.
Properties and characteristics of biomass.
Different energy conversion methods.
Biomass Combustion methods.
Utilization of bioenergy in Biomass based power generation and domestic cooking.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Classify biomass.
PC2. Identify Bio Energy Resources, World Bio Energy Potential, India’s Bio Energy Potential.
PC3. Identify properties and characteristics of biomass.
PC4. Identify different energy conversion methods.
PC5. Identify Biomass Combustion methods.
PC6. Utilize bioenergy in Biomass based power generation and domestic cooking.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Source biomass and bioenergy.
KU2. Usage of appropriate biomass for the generation of bioenergy.
KU3. Physio-chemical characteristics of biomass.
KU4. Different energy conversion methods.
KU5. Biomass Combustion methods.
KU6. Usage of bioenergy in Biomass based power generation and domestic cooking.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Identify Bio Energy Resources.
GS2. Select suitable biomass for generation of bio energy.
GS3. Check the availability of biomass.

WBSC/SGJ/N0202: Illustrate mechanism of Biogas Systems.
Description
Illustrate mechanism of Biogas Systems.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The Technology of Biogas production, and types of Digester.
Bio methanation process and Chemical reaction of bio methanation process.
Design of Digester, Biogas plant design, operation, fault checking, maintenance.
The use of Dung, Vegetable Waste and Municipal Waste based Biogas plants.
The procedure of Biogas Bottling Plant Technology.
Utilizalization of Biogas as fuel for transportation, Lighting, Running Dual Fuel Engines, Electricity
generation.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Describe the Technology of Biogas production, and Digester types.
PC2. Exhibit Bio methanation process and Chemical reaction of bio methanation process.
PC3. Demonstrate design of Digester, Biogas plant design, operation, fault checking, maintenance.
PC4. Explain the use of Dung, Vegetable Waste and Municipal Waste based Biogas plants.
PC5. Demonstrate the procedure of Biogas Bottling Plant Technology.
PC6. Utilize Biogas as fuel for transportation, Lighting, Running Dual Fuel Engines, Electricity
generation.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Introduction to bio gas.
KU2. Difference between Biogas and LPG/CNG.
KU3. Advantages of Biogas.
KU4. Bio methanation process and Chemical reaction of bio methanation process.
KU5. Criteria for design of Biogas plant.
KU6. Dung, Vegetable Waste and Municipal Waste based Biogas plants.
KU7. Availability of water in the vicinity for making a water solution of dung to be given in the input
inlet.
KU8. Procedure of bottling of Biogas.
KU9. Methods of use of Biogas as fuel for transportation, Lighting, Running Dual Fuel Engines,
Electricity generation.

WBSC/SGJ/N0203: Explain the process of Biomass Gasifiers.
Description
Explain the process of Biomass Gasifiers.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The Principle of Bio mass Gasifiers.
Design of Bio mass Gasifiers, updraft gasifier, down draft gasifier.
Zero carbon biomass gasification plants.
Gasification of plastic-rich waste.
Biomass integrated gasification/combined cycles systems, gasification, pyrolysis, liquefaction
process.
Biomass pre- treatment and processing.
Electricity generation from biomass gasifier, bio-gasoline, bio-diesel and duel fuel engine.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand the Principle of Bio mass Gasifiers.
PC2. Interpret Design of Bio mass Gasifiers, updraft gasifier, down draft gasifier.
PC3. Demonstrate Zero carbon biomass gasification plants.
PC4. Demonstrate Gasification of plastic-rich waste.
PC5. Understand Biomass integrated gasification/combined cycles systems, gasification, pyrolysis,
liquefaction process.
PC6. Demonstrate Biomass pre- treatment and processing.
PC7. Demonstrate Electricity generation from biomass gasifier, bio-gasoline, bio-diesel and duel
fuel engine.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The Design of Bio mass Gasifiers, updraft gasifier, down draft gasifier.
KU2. Construction of Zero carbon biomass gasification plants.
KU3. Gasification of plastic- rich waste.
KU4. Biomass integrated gasification, pyrolysis, liquification process.
KU5. Biomass pre- treatment and processing.
KU6. Electricity generation from biomass gasifier, bio-gasoline, bio- diesel and duel fuel engine.

WBSC/SGJ/N0204: Identify appropriate site for installing the BGP.
Description
Identify appropriate site for installing the BGP.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Selection of suitable location.
Availability of water in the vicinity for making a water solution of dung to be given in the input inlet.
Availability of labour for giving daily input.
Mixing sufficient water to prepare input and collect bio slurry output at regular interval.
Construction of plastic/straw shed over bio slurry collection chamber.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Select suitable location based on factors such as avoidance of water inundation/marshy
land/tall tree, availability of cow dung in nearby areas, maximum 200 ft. distance from kitchen
point and a minimum 40 ft. distance from pond/river/ tube well etc.
PC2. Verify that water is available in the vicinity for making a water solution of dung to be given in
the input inlet.
PC3. Confirm the availability of labour for giving daily input.
PC4. Mix sufficient water to prepare input and also collect bio slurry output at regular interval.
PC5. Construct plastic/straw shed over bio slurry collection chamber and keep sufficient space to
uplift & dry the collected bio slurry.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Introduction to bio gas.
KU2. Difference between Biogas and LPG/CNG.
KU3. Advantages of Biogas.
KU4. Criteria for selection of site or suitable location Based on factors such as avoidance of water
inundation/marshy land/tall tree, availability of cow dung in nearby areas, maximum 200 ft.
distance from kitchen point and a minimum 40 ft. distance from pond/river/ tube well etc.
KU5. Availability of water in the vicinity for making a water solution of dung to be given in the input
inlet.
KU6. Availability of labour for giving daily input.
KU7. Procedure of mixing sufficient water to prepare input.
KU8. Procedure for collection of bio slurry output at regular interval.
KU9. Methods of construction of shed over bio slurry collection chamber.

WBSC/SGJ/N0205: Construct a Bio Gas Plant with proper care and safety.
Description
Construct a Bio Gas Plant with proper care and safety.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Fixed dome Dinabandhu Model family size Bio Gas Plant construction.
The construction technology and materials as per soil and climatic condition of the State.
Help of proper supervisory technical expert for BGP and trained masonry support.
