HAR/ITS/N0501: Fundamentals of Computer & Internet
Description
Provides comprehensive introduction to essential computing concepts and internet skills.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Learners aquire with basic computer operations, essential software, and internet usage, including
browsing, email, and online safety. It aims to build digital literacy for personal and professional
applications.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Define computer,its components and applications
PC2. Define and differentiate between types of memories
PC3. Explain the use of internet for various purpose like searching, downloading, uploading, email
etc.
PC4. Recognize the various security and privacy issues on internet and safety measures
PC5. Explain various e-Governance and digital financial services provided through internet
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Objective of the organization and Scope of work assigned.
KU2. Basics of computers ,its component and troubleshooting
KU3. The working in Windows environment and using essential softwares
KU4. Internet browsing and searching techniques
KU5. To apply digital tools effectively and responsibly in organizational and personal settings.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Perform basic computer operations and software tools.
GS2. To read and interpret technical instructions effectively.
GS3. Write and manage professional communications via email in a clear and effective manner.
GS4. Ensuring accuracy in tasks like data entry or troubleshooting.
GS5. Clear and effective exchange of ideas or instruction with team members

HAR/ITS/N0503: Basic Electronics (45 hrs)
Description
Provides foundational knowledge and skills to perform basic tasks involving electronic circuits and
components of computer
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare learners to pursue careers in electronics repair and manufacturing of computer devices.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Define current, voltage & Resistance and explain the relationship between them
PC2. Describe the functions of resistors, capacitors, and inductors in an electronic circuit
PC3. Identify and describe various active and passive components.
PC4. Describe Integrated Circuits(IC) and Semiconductor memory
PC5. Explain various types of power supplies used in electronics
PC6. Define various Number system and perform conversions between decimal and other number
systems, and vice versa.
PC7. Draw and explain various locic gates and combinational logic circuits such as half-adder and
half-subtractor with their truth tables.
PC8. Define and differentiate between encoders and decoders, Multiplexers and De-multiplexers.
Explain with diagrams and truth tables for each.
PC9. Draw and explain various types of Flip Flops, Latches, Register and Counters.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The objective and goals of organization and scope of work assigned
KU2. The basic principles of electricity, including current, voltage, and resistance.
KU3. The working principles of active and passive electronic components.
KU4. The use of tools like multimeters, oscilloscopes, and soldering irons for designing, testing, and
troubleshooting analog and digital circuits.
KU5. Workplace safety practices, precautions and equipment handling.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Ability to convey technical concepts clearly and effectively, both orally and in writing

HAR/ITS/N0506: Computer Hardware & Peripheral (45 hrs)
Description
Equips learners with the skills required for computer hardware maintenance, assembly, and
troubleshooting.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Provides essential knowledge and practical skills in assembling, disassembling, and troubleshooting
computer systems and peripherals and prepares learners for careers in IT support, hardware
maintenance, and system management.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Define and explain the functions of various internal and external computer components.
PC2. Define process for Assembling and Disassembling the computer.
PC3. Recognize and describe the use of different internal ports, cables, and connectors used for
system integration and communication.
PC4. Explain the use of Modem, Digital Camera, Pen drives, SCSI interface device, Web Camera,
CCTV Camera, Projector
PC5. Describe various types of monitors and their differences in terms of display technology and
application.
PC6. Describe the different types of printers and scanners, and explain their functions in the
computer ecosystem.
PC7. Identify and troubleshoot common hardware and software issues
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The objective & goals of organization and scope of work assigned.
KU2. The components of a computer system and how they interact with each other to form a
functional unit.
KU3. The assembly and disassembly processes, including the installation of components and
internal wiring and configuring a computer system.
KU4. How to diagnose and resolve hardware-related issues, including malfunctioning devices, slow
performance, and connectivity problems.
KU5. The role and importance of computer hardware in different organizational environments,
KU6. How to apply workplace safety standards during the assembly, disassembly, and
troubleshooting of computer hardware.

HAR/ITS/N0505: Operating System (30 hrs)
Description
Provides essential knowledge of operating system concepts, installation, and maintenance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Provides knowledge and skills in installing, configuring, and troubleshooting operating systems and
virus protection techniques, ensuring system security and maintenance in professional
environments.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the role of an operating system and its types
PC2. Define and differentiate System files(FAT and NTFS) and explain the relation between System
File and operating System
PC3. Install and set operating systems with essential system settings and required application
softwares.
PC4. Effectively troubleshoot operating system issues, including system crashes, software
malfunctions, and security threats, by using appropriate tools
PC5. Convert FAT Partition to NTFS
PC6. Explain Computer viruses and its types (Malware, Trojan, Worm, Spyware, Adware).
PC7. Identify common signs of virus infections, such as window popups, self-executing programs,
unexpected logouts, and device crashes.
PC8. Apply virus protection measures, including the use of antivirus software, updating the
operating system, and using privacy settings in browsers.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The objective & goals of Organization and scope of work assigned.
KU2. Fundamental concepts of operating systems, its functions and system files
KU3. The principles of virus protection, types of virus and their impact on system security.
KU4. The practical skills in installing and configuring operating systems, application software, and
system files on different platforms
KU5. how to troubleshoot and resolve common operating system issues, from installation errors to
system crashes.
KU6. The role of operating systems in the day-to-day functioning of an organization's IT
infrastructure, from personal computers to server environments.
