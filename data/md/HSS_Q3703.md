HSS/Q3703: Quality Control and Assurance Executive(Ayurveda Siddha
Unani)
Brief Job Description
The individuals conducts testing of raw materials such as herbs, minerals, etc., as per the ASU
Pharmacopoeia. They also monitor and test samples during various stages of manufacturing and finished
formulations like powders, tablets, syrups, oils, etc. They maintain essential records and documents
related to the Quality Assurance and Control Process.
Personal Attributes
They should exhibit good coordination, self-discipline, dedication, persistence, ethical behaviour and the
ability to deal empathetically with clients and their families. They should have good communication skills
in Hindi, English or any other local language.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. HSS/N3708: Conduct activities to maintain Quality control and Assurance in the Ayurveda , Siddha,
Unani manufacturing unit.

- 6. HSS/N9618: Follow infection control policies & procedures including biomedical waste disposal
protocols

HSS/N3708: Conduct activities to maintain Quality control and Assurance
in the Ayurveda , Siddha, Unani manufacturing unit.
Description
This Occupational Standard outlines the systematic approach to building quality into every stage of
production, from raw materials to finished goods, ensuring safety, efficacy, and market compliance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers quality control , regulatory authority and regulations and quality management
system.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Quality control
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Overview of the Manufacturing Industry in India.
PC2. Wear Personal Protective Equipment (PPE).
PC3. Maintain a conducive ambience, environment and cleanliness in the setup.
PC4. Check the pharmacopeial standards and ensure the safety and efficacy of ASU products.
PC5. Identify the regulatory authorities, regulations, legislation, and good practices.
Regulatory authority and regulations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Maintain drug licensing provisions under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940 and Rules 1945.
PC7. Identify rules for Ayurvedic, Siddha, and Unani drug manufacturing licenses.
PC8. Demonstrate compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices.
PC9. Identify various Pharmacopoeia/Formularies.
PC10. Maintain Sampling and testing of raw drug, intermediate, and finished products.
Quality Management System
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Maintain budget adherence and cost control.
PC12. Coordinate for equipment calibration and maintenance.
PC13. Check for secure and accessible equipment records.
PC14. Check production method as per system-specific pharmacopoeia.
PC15. Demonstrate the Standard Operating Procedure (SOP).
PC16. Identify GMP guidelines.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

HSS/N3709: Conduct Regulatory Compliance in Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani
set up.
Description
This unit describes the systematic and ongoing process of ensuring that all unit operations, products, and
practices consistently meet every applicable law, regulation, and guidelines.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers quality control and assurance , drug and cosmetic act and
pharmacopeia/formularies in the unit.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Quality control and Assurance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Maintain accurate and consistent Identification of raw materials.
PC2. Coordinate tracing of raw materials from source to production.
PC3. Check quality control testing and results.
PC4. Identify calibration and measurement procedures for the machines.
Drug and Cosmetic
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Maintain regulations and standards for the manufacturing industry.
PC6. Maintain rule for Ayurveda, Siddha, and Unani drug manufacturing licenses.
PC7. Check drugs and cosmetics Rules, 1945.
PC8. Develop standard operating procedure (SOP), GMP guidelines for performing pre-analysis
checks.
PC9. Maintain quality control of finished products.
Pharmacopeia/Formularies in the unit.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. Check Physicochemical characteristics such as loss on drying, ash values, extractive values,
pH value etc.
PC11. Monitor macro and microscopic analysis
PC12. Undertake Proactive Risk Mitigation and Problem Identification.
PC13. Identify safety parameters (heavy metals, pesticides, microbial contamination (bacteria,
fungi), and aflatoxins.
PC14. Coordinate for regular audits and inspections of the unit.
PC15. Monitor effective procedures, training programs, and documentation.

HSS/N3710: Carry out Quality Analysis in compliance with current
Practices in the ASU unit.
Description
This unit covers all critical activities performed by relevant personnel across all departments that impact
product quality, safety, and regulatory compliance, ensuring that operations are conducted securely and
ethically.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers standard operating procedures , quality management system and product lifecycle
in the unit.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Standard Operating Procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Coordinate safety procedures for routine and non-routine operations.
PC2. Identify all applicable local, national, and industry-specific laws, regulations, and standards
(e.g., factory acts, environmental protection laws, labor laws, quality standards, product-
specific
PC3. Utilize personal protective equipment (PPE) correctly and ensure its proper maintenance
PC4. Identify and follow emergency response procedures, including evacuation routes and first aid
protocols.
Quality management system
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Identify and resolve quality issues in the unit.
PC6. Identify potential hazards and risks associated with specific tasks, equipment, and the work
environment.
PC7. Provide ASU products and services, ensuring quality assurance throughout the product’s
lifecycle.
PC8. Check the steps in the production process, including equipment settings, processing and
quality control checks.
Product lifecycle in the unit
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Identify Shelf-life studies and storage conditions.
PC10. Identify corrective actions for the safety of the finished product.
PC11. Coordinate for first-expiry-first-out (FEFO) practices.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
