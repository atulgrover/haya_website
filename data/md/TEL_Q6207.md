TEL/Q6207: Optical Network Terminal Technician
Brief Job Description
An Optical Network Terminal Technician is responsible for ensuring the continuous 24x7 operation of
Optical Network Terminal (ONT) sites. The role involves performing routine maintenance, troubleshooting
and repairing basic faults at ONT sites, promoting digital adoption among the local population, and
delivering telecom services efficiently.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be self-disciplined, proactive, and a strong team player with a problem-solving
mindset. The person should possess analytical skills, effective communication abilities, and the ability to
work efficiently under pressure.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. TEL/N6226: Maintain Site Security and Hygiene
- 2. TEL/N6227: Perform Preventive Maintenance of Optical Network Terminal (ONT) Components
- 3. TEL/N6228: Promote use of Devices and Provide Services
- 4. TEL/N9109: Follow sustainable practices in telecom infrastructure management
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

TEL/N6226: Maintain Site Security and Hygiene
Description
This unit covers maintaining the security, hygiene, and sustainability of an Optical Network Terminal (ONT)
site, including renewable energy integration and eco-friendly deployment practices
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Coordinate activities for site security, hygiene, and environmental compliance
Monitor equipment and ensure renewable energy integration
Elements and Performance Criteria
Coordinate activities for site security, hygiene, and environmental compliance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. perform custodial functions for an Optical Network Terminal (ONT) site, ensuring security,
hygiene, and proper functioning
PC2. communicate with Optical Line Terminal (OLT) and Network Operation Center (NOC)
supervisors to report issues or request technological upgrades
PC3. coordinate with Gram Panchayat officials and other local authorities for the smooth operation
and security of the ONT site
PC4. follow eco-friendly site management practices, to ensure adherence to environmental
compliance standards
Monitor equipment and ensure renewable energy integration
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. check the functionality of Charge Controller Unit (CCU), Solar Photovoltaic (SPV) system,
Telephone Junction Box (TJB), battery bank, and other components
PC6. maintain hygiene and operational standards for ONT site equipment, including renewable
energy solutions
PC7. check all cable connectors for secure connections and detect any signs of damage
PC8. perform necessary steps for the repair or replacement of damaged cables, ensuring
compliance with safety protocols
PC9. monitor solar-powered energy sources and battery banks to optimize performance and
ensure uninterrupted power supply
PC10. report faults that cannot be rectified to the supervisor for further action
PC11. use fire extinguishers and follow emergency protocols in case of fire or other hazards
PC12. record faults, maintenance activities, and energy efficiency parameters in the prescribed
format

TEL/N6227: Perform Preventive Maintenance of Optical Network Terminal
(ONT) Components
Description
This unit covers the execution of preventive maintenance activities and real-time monitoring at ONT sites
to ensure 24/7 operational efficiency and fault resolution
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform preventive maintenance and maintain records
Monitor equipment, use advanced testing tools, and report issues
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform preventive maintenance and keep record
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. read and follow preventive maintenance guidelines for Charge Controller Unit (CCU), Solar
Photo Voltaic (SPV) System, Telephone Junction Box (TJB), battery bank, and fire extinguisher
PC2. conduct scheduled preventive maintenance of CCU, SPV, TJB, and battery bank using the
prescribed checklists
PC3. ensure the availability of required equipment, including spectrum analyzers, OTDRs, and
network testers
PC4. perform routine diagnostic checks using real-time remote monitoring tools to detect faults in
ONT components
PC5. document maintenance activities in the preventive maintenance report form in the specified
format
Monitor equipment, use advanced testing tools, and report issues
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. observe the CCU panel, interpret alarms, and take corrective actions as per standard
operating procedures
PC7. inspect battery bank cells and SPV panels for premature ageing and replace defective units
as per manufacturer guidelines
PC8. examine and test patch cords, pigtails, and electrical wiring for damage and replace faulty
components
PC9. conduct interference analysis using spectrum analyzers and OTDRs to identify signal loss and
network degradation issues
PC10. report major equipment faults and alarm triggers to the OLT/NOC supervisor through the
designated escalation matrix
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

TEL/N6228: Promote use of Devices and Provide Services
Description
This unit covers enabling the local population to adopt and efficiently use digital devices (smartphones,
#### tablets, e-terminals) by providing hands-on support, configuring devices, troubleshooting technical issues,
and managing billing and revenue collection
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Facilitate Adoption of Digital Devices
Install, configure, and troubleshoot devices
Manage revenue collection and site payments
Elements and Performance Criteria
Facilitate adoption of digital devices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify and engage target groups to introduce digital devices and their benefits
PC2. promote and recommend relevant devices based on user requirements and digital literacy
levels
PC3. conduct on-site demonstrations of device functionalities to enhance user confidence and
adoption
PC4. address concerns and misconceptions related to device usability, security, and affordability
Install, configure, and troubleshoot devices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. set up and install necessary equipment such as routers, modems, UPS, and cables at
designated locations
PC6. configure smartphones, tablets, and e-terminals as per service provider guidelines
PC7. assist users in setting up accounts, applications, and network connectivity on digital devices
PC8. perform basic troubleshooting for common issues related to hardware, software, and network
connectivity
PC9. guide users on security measures, software updates, and device maintenance to ensure
long-term usability
Manage revenue collection and site payments
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. distribute bills for service usage as per billing cycles
PC11. collect payments and issue receipts and maintain transaction records
PC12. maintain and update rent agreements and electricity bill records for ONT sites
PC13. coordinate rent, electricity, or service fees payments with the supervisor to avoid disruptions
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
