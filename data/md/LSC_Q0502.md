#### LSC/Q0502: Functional Analyst - TMS
Brief Job Description
The individual is responsible for working on the Functional and Technical Design of the Transportation
Management System. The individual is also responsible for working with Transportation Planners to define
the operation process and enabling users to consume Software. The individual should be able to define
different user guides used by end users of Transport Management System end users.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have good communication skills, analytical skills and professional values. She/he
should be able to help operations team to solve problems and aid management in decision making. The
role may require working additional hours from time to time and some physical effort.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. LSC/N0505: Operational Direction and Enablement of Strategic Vision - TMS
2. LSC/N0506: Identify optimization opportunities for industry/domain - TMS
3. LSC/N0507: Lead the design and implementation of TMS in existing and new transportation network
4. LSC/N0508: Prepare documentations of various Transportation Business Processes
5. LSC/N9908: Maintain and monitor integrity and ethics in operations
6. LSC/N9902: Communicate effectively with colleagues and clients
7. LSC/N9910: Follow health, safety and security procedures at workplace

#### LSC/N0505: Operational Direction and Enablement of Strategic Vision -
TMS
Description
This unit is about understanding the Organization's vision and direction for Implementation of Transportation systems in Existing Paper based system or replacing legacy-based transportation
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Existing Master Data Collection
Carrier discussions on the tariff and model the rates
Future Transportation Routes
Design the Software to match transportation requirement
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Existing Master Data Collection
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Collect Master data such as Division and Logistics Group.
- PC2. Obtain information on various Commodities and Freight Class.
- PC3. Obtain information on Shipment Types and Equipment Types.
- PC4. Obtain information on different Shipping Locations
Carrier discussions on the tariff and model the rates
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Collect information on various Routes and zones of transportation
- PC6. Collect information on Carriers and the Zones they cater
- PC7. Obtain information on Carrier tariff
- PC8. Obtain information on Lane Constraints
Future Transportation Routes
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Provide the data analysis for available Carriers and their feasibility to serve the routes
respecting Lane Constraints
- PC10. Compare the Tariffs of several Carriers and rank them based on the Cost
- PC11. Collect data such as traffic information to decide the future routes
- PC12. Provide the re-aligned routes
Design the Software to match transportation requirement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. Enter different divisions as per new Organization structures
- PC14. Define Shipment Types, Shipment Versions, Item commodities.
- PC15. Define Shipment/Load Planning and Shipment/Load Execution

#### LSC/N0506: Identify optimization opportunities for industry/domain - TMS
Description
This unit is about understanding the existing transport operations, areas of improvement and impact of
change operations in overall Business Perspective
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Transportation Operations Familiarization
Order planning and processing against Shipments
Load Execution for Shipments including Tendering process
Areas of improvement in the overall process which matches with implemented Software
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Transportation Operations Familiarization
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Prepare process flow chart after understanding the operations
- PC2. Prepare process flow document after understanding the operations
- PC3. Prepare user guides for transport operations.
Order planning and processing against Shipments
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Observe the different fields required to visualize in Shipments and design/propose the
missing fields
- PC5. Define different Plan IDs against which Transportation Planners will be assigned the
Shipments
- PC6. Design Shipments date, commodity field to ensure shipments routing is possible within that
window
- PC7. Design process to reduce the time taken to plan the shipment.
Load Execution for Shipments including Tendering process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Calculate the time required in Shipment Planning and Shipment Optimization, find the
process to reduce the time taken.
- PC9. Design the process for Load Rating to find the cost required to transport the Load.
- PC10. Design the process to define Load tendering to Carriers
- PC11. Design Report/Email/Notification sent to Carriers for Tender notification
Carrier Sequential Tendering and Dock Scheduling
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. Observe the sequence in which Carriers will be tendered and which Carriers are eligible for
Auto Tender accept, depending on Carrier performance and agreement
- PC13. Calculate the time taken by each carrier to respond to Load Tender request.

#### LSC/N0507: Lead the design and implementation  of TMS in existing and
new transportation network
Description
This unit is about design and implementation of TMS software in existing and new transportation network
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Designing TMS Processes in Implementation cycle
Designing integration touch points with upstream and downstream systems
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Designing TMS Processes in Implementation cycle.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Collect the requirement from Business users after discussing the different Business
processes.
- PC2. Perform As-Is analysis and document the processes.
- PC3. Assist Business Users in explaining the document and revise the document with Review
comments.
- PC4. Prepare Business Requirement Document and get a sign off from Business
- PC5. Prepare Gap analysis document after analyzing the several functionalities of TMS software
being implemented.
- PC6. Prepare Functional Design document and mention the gaps identified from Software
- PC7. Work with business users to amend the transportation processes to minimize the Gaps, that
involves processes while shipment planning, plan ID authorization, load execution, financial process and several report requirements.
- PC8. There can be scenarios that Transportation processes cannot be amended, and GAPs remain
in the processes. In that case work with Transportation Management System software developer to develop the feature.
- PC9. Deploy the feature in TMS and proceed with rest of the design.
- PC10. Configure the system as per the design document and perform Unit testing to ensure all the
Business requirements are met.
- PC11. Assist business users for UAT testing
Designing integration touchpoints with upstream and downstream systems
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. Collect the integration touch points from upstream and downstream data view
- PC13. Perform the data mapping after working with technical Team.
- PC14. Deliver Technical Design document and work with technical team for a sign off
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
