#### SVO/ELE/N0201: Fundamental  for IoT for Home Automation
Description
This unit is about performing installation, onsite troubleshooting and repairing of the home automation
systems as per the specifications of the manufacturer and consultation with the customer addressing
his/her needs.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparatory activities for installation of home automation system
Installation of smart devices and sensors at home
Troubleshooting and repairing of defects in the smart devices
