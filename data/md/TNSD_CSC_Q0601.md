#### TNSD/CSC/Q0601: Precision Machinist- CNC
Brief Job Description
A Precision Machinist - CNC operates, sets up, and maintains Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machine
tools to manufacture precision components according to technical drawings and quality specifications.
Personal Attributes
Upon successful completion, learners will be qualified for employment as a Precision Machinist - CNC or
similar roles in engineering, manufacturing, machine tools, and automobile industries. They will contribute
to quality production and adapt to evolving CNC technologies.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. TNSD/CSC/N0601: Perform Workshop ManufacturingOperations.
2. TNSD/CSC/N0602: Operate and Implement CNC Programming Techniques
3. TNSD/CSC/N0603: Simulate CNC Programs using Keller software
4. TNSD/CSC/N0604: Operate and trouble shoot basic hydraulics and Pneumatics system
5. TNSD/CSC/N0605: Create 3D Technical Drawings Using AutoCAD
6. TNSD/CSC/N0606: Create 2D Technical Drawings Using Solid works
7. TNSD/CSC/N0607: Generate simple CNC Toolpaths using Master cam
8. TNSD/CSC/N0608: Apply Technical Mathematics for CNC Applications
9. TNSD/CSC/N0609: Apply basic electrical and sensor in a Manufacturing Environment
10. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### TNSD/CSC/N0601: Perform Workshop   ManufacturingOperations.
Description
Understand the manufacturing of complex parts by using conventional and CNC lathe, Understand the
manufacturing of complex parts by using conventional and CNC Milling Machines. Understand the calculations related to machining
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the manufacturing of complex parts by using conventional and CNC Machines.
Understand the different non- conventional machining processes.
Understand the workshop related calculations.
Familiarize with machine maintenance process
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand the safety aspects and PPE used in CNC Machines
- PC2. Understand and Demonstrate Special operations – Indexing, Gear cutting, slotting, Sphere
milling, Radius milling and Non-conventional Machining process.
- PC3. Learn different parts and its functions of CNC tool room machines- lathe, Milling.
- PC4. Understand different modules used in CNC machines
- PC5. Learn the different types of work holding devices, Tool holding devices of CNC- lathe, Milling.
- PC6. Learn insert selection as per the raw material.
- PC7. Demostrate Part programming and Machining different Exercises on CNC lathe
- PC8. Demonstrate Part programming and Machining different Exercises on CNC Milling
- PC9. Understand and Practice the measuring technique by using CMM.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Understand the safety aspects and PPE used in CNC Machines.
- KU2. Understand and Demonstrate Special operations Indexing, Gear cutting, slotting, Sphere milling, Radius milling and Non-conventional Machining process.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Demostrate Part programming and Machining different Exercises on CNC lathe

#### TNSD/CSC/N0602: Operate and  Implement CNC  Programming  Techniques
Description
Able to do the programming and manufacturing of components on CNC lathe. Able to do the programming
and manufacturing of components on CNC Milling machine.

Understand the concepts and CNC systems
Familiar with G-codes and M-codes used in programming

- PC3. Learn the concept of offset and different modes of operation
- PC4. Understand the different types of tool holders and inserts used in CNC machines.
- PC5. Demonstrate Program and do machining a part on CNC lathe.

- PC7. Fabricate different parts of an assembly/project on CNC machines and assembling

#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Demonstrate Program and do machining a part on CNC lathe.

#### TNSD/CSC/N0608: Apply Technical Mathematics for CNC Applications
Description
Understand the concept of mathematics used for doing calculations related to engineering application and
general purpose.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the applications of Trigonometrical functions in engineering field.
Understand the basic concept & application of calculus.
Understand the concept of Analytical Geometry.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Learn the Concepts of fundamental Analytical geometry
- PC2. Find the slope of line , parallel and perpendicular lines
- PC3. Find the distance between the vertices Transversal of line
- PC4. Calculate the area of triangles and quadrilaterals , mid points, Equation of lines and related
problems.
- PC5. Calculate the Centroid , in center, Ortho center of a triangles and related problems.
- PC6. Understand the basic concept & application of calculus
- PC7. Solve the linear and non-linear equations , Ordinary differential & partial differential
equations.
- PC8. Find the order and degree of the differential equations, solution of ODE& PDE.
- PC9. Learn the Differential rule , Chain rule, Bernoulli rule and related problems
- PC10. Learn Basic concept of principle of Trigonometry and its applications
- PC11. Solve the problems using trigonometry application
- PC12. Find the Solution of acute , obtuse angled triangles and related problems
- PC13. Find the values of angles by using trigonometric table values, Angle value for the special
angles, Solution of circumscribed triangles by sine rule and practice problems.

- KU1. Learn the Differential rule , Chain rule, Bernoulli rule and related problems
