HCS/Q2912: Planishing Artisan (Metalware)
Brief Job Description
The individual at work is responsible to hammer holes and designs on the surface of the metal as per the
design and requirement .The individual at work is responsible to remove excess metal from the product.
The person reshapes, resizes and smoothens the metal product as per the design and requirement.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have: attention to details, good eyesight, steady hands, ability to sit in
same position for longer hours and patience
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

HCS/N2908: Hammer the metal surface
Description
This unit is about hammering the metal surface as per requirement.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understanding work requirement
assembling the required materials
hammering the metal surface
ensuring quality standards
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding work requirement
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. receive instructions on work requirement from superior
PC2. plan the target on number of pieces to be completed
Assembling the required materials
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. ensure to receive the job sheet and hammer from the concerned department
PC4. report any defect of the hammer used to the concerned person
PC5. collect the metal to be hammered
Hammering the metal surface
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. tape the ends of the metal surface to avoid slipping while hammering
PC7. ensure to perform hammering in a bright lightened place
PC8. take care of the working height between the body and the metal to be hammered to avoid
causing injury and stress during work
PC9. ensure to hold the hammer in a straight position with a relaxed yet firm grip to avoid choke
up and pain in the hands for holding it for a long time
PC10. ensure to put thumb and forefinger straight along the handle
PC11. ensure uniform marks and textures are formed by hammering every blow with equal force
PC12. refine the surface of the metal surface and smoothen the hammer marks
PC13. ensure to wear safety accessories such as eardrums for hammer noise, leather gloves, etc.
Ensuring quality standards
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC14. ensure a proper finish as per requirement
PC15. ensure the target number of pieces are hammered
PC16. ensure the output delivered is defect free and hazard free

HCS/N2907: File the metal surface
Description
This unit is about removing excess metal from the metalware product, reshaping, resizing and
smoothening as per requirement.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understanding work requirement
assembling the required materials
filing the metal product
ensuring quality standards
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding work requirement
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. receive instructions on work requirement from superior.
PC2. understand the filing method and read the job sheet to understand design requirement.
PC3. plan the target number of pieces to be completed.
Assembling the required materials
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. select the appropriate tools to file such as hand files, needle files, knife files, etc.
PC5. collect and arrange the materials to begin the process.
PC6. report on any shortage or defect of raw materials to the concerned person.
PC7. ensure to stock the required materials in advance.
PC8. collect the metal product to be filed from the concerned department.
Filing the metal product
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. ensure the product to be filed is smoother than the file and hardened metal is not used.
PC10. inspect the received metal product for any casting defects and send to the concerned
department for correction.
PC11. report to the supervisor if the defected piece cannot be rectified.
PC12. clean the received metal piece.
PC13. use appropriate lubricants for the cleaning process.
PC14. cut and remove the excess metal from the casted metal surface.
PC15. file the edges, grooves and irregular surfaces of the metal according to design specifications.
PC16. check and rectify the size and shape of the metal piece matching with the design
requirements.
PC17. ensure to use the appropriate tools for the filing process.
