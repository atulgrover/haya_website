#### MEP/Q1104: Environmental Social Governance (ESG) Specialist
Brief Job Description
Environmental Sustainability Governance (ESG) Specialist helps the professional oversee the development
and implementation of strategies to integrate sustainability principles into the organization's operations
and decision-making processes. Responsibilities include establishing ESG goals, tracking performance
metrics, engaging with stakeholders, ensuring regulatory compliance, and fostering a culture of corporate
responsibility. They also collaborate with senior management to align ESG initiatives with overall business
objectives.
Personal Attributes
Analytical Thinking Attention to Detail Strong Communication Skills Problem-Solving Ability
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MEP/N1137: Integrate ESG in business
2. MEP/N1138: Develop ESG frameworks, disclosures and ratings
3. MEP/N1139: Manage sustainable finance
4. MEP/N1140: Manage GHG accounting and governance
5. MEP/N1141: Mitigate risk and manage climate related disclosures
6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### MEP/N1137: Integrate ESG in business
Description
Oversee the systematic embedding of environmental, social, and governance principles across organizational structures and processes to align with corporate strategies and goals.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct ESG assessment and research
Develop benchmarking and compliance
Manage risk and opportunity
Develop strategic plan and monitor performance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct ESG assessment and research
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Assess the scope and relevance of ESG factors in different sectors of the economy to
understand their impact on the organization.
- PC2. Conduct sector-specific research to identify key ESG issues affecting each sector, providing
insights for strategic planning.
- PC3. Conduct an audit of current business operations to identify gaps in ESG compliance and
performance.
- PC4. Map identified ESG issues against their impact on the organization and its stakeholders to
prioritize focus areas.
- PC5. Research and compile a list of applicable local, national, and international ESG regulations.
- PC6. Analyze the current organizational structure to identify potential barriers to effective ESG
integration.
Develop benchmarking and compliance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Benchmark ESG practices of leading companies within the industry to identify best practices
and areas for improvement.
- PC8. Compare the organization's current ESG practices against industry standards and best
practices to ensure competitiveness and compliance.
- PC9. Assess the organization's current compliance status with these regulations, identifying areas
of risk.
- PC10. Develop a compliance plan to address any deficiencies and ensure ongoing regulatory
adherence.
Manage risk and opportunity
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. Evaluate the impact of ESG factors on the organization's financial performance and risk
management, providing data-driven recommendations to the executive team.

- PC29. Prepare and disseminate comprehensive reports showcasing the successes, lessons learned,
and areas for improvement in ESG initiatives.

- KU1. Knowledge of relevant ESG frameworks, standards, and regulations applicable to the business

ESG into strategic decision-making.

#### MEP/N1138: Develop ESG frameworks,  disclosures and ratings
Description
Establish and implement standardized frameworks, disclosure practices, and rating methodologies to
accurately measure, compare, and communicate the organization's ESG performance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Outline frameworks and standards
Determine ESG indicators and scorecards
Discuss reporting and transparency
Determine tools and integration
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Outline frameworks and standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify key principles and criteria for both the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) and
Integrated Reporting (IR).
- PC2. Prepare a comparative table highlighting the focus areas, reporting standards, and
methodologies of GRI vs. IR.
- PC3. Analyze the applicability and adoption rates of both frameworks in different industries.
- PC4. List the subgroups or categories covered by the Business Responsibility and Sustainability
Report (BRSR).
- PC5. Outline the specific metrics within the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB)
framework.
- PC6. Compare the approaches of BRSR and SASB in measuring and reporting sustainability.
- PC7. Identify the ESG categories used by MSCI in their rating system.
- PC8. Outline the assessment categories utilized by Sustainalytics for ESG ratings.
- PC9. Compare and contrast the focus of MSCI and Sustainalytics in their ESG assessments.
Determine ESG indicators and scorecards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Identify a comprehensive set of ESG indicators used across different industries.
- PC11. Categorize the indicators into environmental, social, and governance groups.
- PC12. Assess the relevance of each indicator to various stakeholders.
- PC13. Develop an ESG scorecard template based on course learnings.
- PC14. Determine the criteria and weightings for each ESG component within the scorecard.
- PC15. Create a mock assessment using the scorecard for a hypothetical company.
- PC16. Define the qualitative and quantitative factors contributing to a strong ESG score.
- PC17. Discuss the importance of transparency and accuracy in reporting for a good ESG score.
- PC18. Highlight the role of stakeholder engagement and materiality in achieving a high ESG score.

Discuss reporting and transparency
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC19. Evaluate the effectiveness of various ESG reporting frameworks in ensuring transparency.
- PC20. Discuss the standardization of ESG reporting and its impact on stakeholder trust.
- PC21. Examine the role of third-party audits and certifications in validating ESG disclosures.
- PC22. Outline a process for regular evaluation of ESG performance against set standards.
- PC23. Develop a continuous improvement plan for maintaining and updating ESG practices.
- PC24. Discuss the integration of new regulations and stakeholder expectations into existing ESG
standards.
Determine tools and integration
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC25. Identify ESG-integrated business intelligence tools available in the market.
- PC26. Analyze how these tools can be used to identify and mitigate ESG-related risks.
- PC27. Discuss case studies where ESG tools have successfully aided in risk mitigation.
- PC28. Define business intelligence in the context of decision-making and strategic planning.
- PC29. Discuss the integration of ESG metrics into business intelligence platforms.
- PC30. Evaluate the impact of ESG tools on business transformation and performance over recent
years.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Familiarity with key sustainability reporting standards (e.g., GRI, SASB, TCFD) and their application.
- KU2. Understanding of different ESG rating methodologies and their relevance to stakeholder expectations.
- KU3. Awareness of regulatory requirements, industry benchmarks, and trends influencing ESG disclosures.
- KU4. Knowledge of data collection, validation, and reporting techniques for transparent ESG communication.
#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. Analytical skills to interpret complex ESG data and derive meaningful insights.
- GS2. Communication skills to effectively convey ESG performance and disclosure requirements to stakeholders.
- GS3. Project management skills to coordinate timelines, resources, and cross-functional collaboration for ESG reporting.
- GS4. Collaboration and negotiation abilities to align internal teams and external partners on disclosure goals and standards.

#### MEP/N1139: Manage  sustainable finance
Description
Oversee the development and implementation of financial strategies and instruments that align with ESG
objectives, ensuring responsible resource allocation and ethical investment practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Outline ESG reporting frameworks and standards for sustainable finance
Evaluate ESG risk management and financial performance
Evaluate ESG in Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A)
Develop ESG investing and sustainable development for sustainable finance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Outline ESG reporting frameworks and standards for sustainable finance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Prepare a table to distinguish between ESG reporting frameworks - GRI and IR.
- PC2. Define the scope and reporting principles for both the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) and
Integrated Reporting (IR).
- PC3. Create a side-by-side comparison table highlighting the key differences in reporting
standards between GRI and IR.
- PC4. Discuss the suitability of each framework for different types of organizations.
- PC5. List the key financial and non-financial metrics commonly used in ESG assessment.
- PC6. Determine the most reliable data sources for gathering ESG information.
- PC7. Track the latest trends in ESG practices and reporting.
- PC8. Analyze the drivers behind these trends.
- PC9. Predict future developments in ESG based on current trends.
Evaluate ESG risk management and financial performance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Describe methodologies for quantifying and integrating ESG outcomes into business analysis.
- PC11. Identify the range of ESG risks that can impact financial performance.
- PC12. Quantify potential financial losses from ESG risks.
- PC13. Develop strategic recommendations for managing and mitigating ESG risks.
- PC14. Track stock price movements following ESG news announcements.
- PC15. Analyze the correlation between ESG news and stock performance.
- PC16. Report on case studies where ESG developments have had a notable impact on shareholder
value.
- PC17. Identify companies with recent ESG controversies.
- PC18. Examine the financial impact of these controversies on the companies.

#### Acronyms
NSQC Approved || Management & Entrepreneurship and Professional Skills Council 45

### Page 46

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
