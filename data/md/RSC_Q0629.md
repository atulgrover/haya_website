#### RSC/Q0629: Corrugated Process Quality Control Inspector
Brief Job Description
The Corrugator Process Quality Control Inspector ensures the quality of corrugated board by conducting
paper and board testing, approving first samples, monitoring production parameters, identifying defects,
and maintaining inspection records. The role also involves coordinating with production for corrective
actions and ensuring proper stacking, palletization, and workplace safety.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be physically fit to work on the shop floor for long hours, with strong observation
skills and attention to detail. They must be disciplined, safety-conscious, and possess good communication
and problem-solving abilities to coordinate effectively with cross-functional teams.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. RSC/N0628: Perform Quality Inspection and Testing of Corrugated Board
2. RSC/N0629: Monitor and Control Corrugated Board Line Process Parameters
3. RSC/N0630: Conduct Quality Control Documentation, Material Disposition & RCA

5. RSC/N0632: Maintain Workplace Safety and 5S in Quality Inspection Areas

#### RSC/N0628: Perform Quality Inspection and Testing of Corrugated Board
Description
This NOS covers performing in-process and final quality inspection of corrugated boards by using
measurement tools and standard test methods. It also includes identifying defects, recording results, and
ensuring compliance with customer specifications and quality standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for quality inspection
Perform basic paper testing
Perform corrugated board testing
Conduct in-process inspection and first part approval
Record deviations and communicate
Ensure safety and good housekeeping during inspection
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for quality inspection
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the job specification sheet and quality control checklist for each corrugation job
- PC2. interpret customer or internal specifications related to board properties
- PC3. verify the availability and calibration status of quality testing instruments
- PC4. prepare and label the required number of board samples from the lot for testing
Perform basic paper testing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. measure GSM (grammage) of paper using GSM tester
- PC6. evaluate moisture content of paper using moisture meter
- PC7. inspect visual parameters such as shade, cleanliness, and consistency of paper
- PC8. document paper test values and compare them with permissible limits
Perform corrugated board testing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. assess board GSM and thickness using appropriate gauges
- PC10. check board compression strength using BCT/RCT test setup
- PC11. evaluate board moisture content as per standard procedures
- PC12. perform bursting strength or puncture resistance test on board samples
- PC13. inspect bonding between liners and flutes for delamination and uniformity
- PC14. record board test results accurately in QC logbook
Conduct in-process inspection and first part approval

#### RSC/N0629: Monitor and Control Corrugated Board Line Process
Parameters
Description
This NOS covers monitoring and controlling key process parameters of the corrugated board production
line to ensure consistent board quality and smooth operations. It includes making necessary adjustments
to moisture, temperature, speed, and tension settings to maintain product standards and minimize
defects.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Monitor key board line parameters
Implementation of safety program
Perform corrective actions and process adjustments
Conduct stage-wise inspection on board line
Communicate process issues and support operators
Document process monitoring results
Ensure safe and standard operating practices
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Monitor key board line parameters
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. track and record real-time process parameters such as glue temperature, board
temperature, and moisture
- PC2. observe liner and flute alignment visually and through instruments
- PC3. monitor glue application for uniformity and proper bonding
- PC4. check that flute formation is consistent and not crushed or misaligned
Implementation of safety program
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. analyze parameter trends using process charts and past data
- PC6. identify variations beyond tolerance limits and their impact on board quality
- PC7. determine likely root causes for deviations (e.g., pressure, tension, glue issues)
- PC8. categorize the deviations as process-related or material-related
Perform corrective actions and process adjustments
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. coordinate with machine operators to adjust heat, pressure, or glue settings
- PC10. initiate flushing or cleaning cycles if glue flow is inconsistent
- PC11. assist in adjusting roll settings or pre-heaters to stabilize board properties
- PC12. verify impact of corrective actions on output quality
Conduct stage-wise inspection on board line

#### RSC/N0630: Conduct Quality Control Documentation, Material Disposition
& RCA
Description
This NOS involves documenting quality control results, managing non-conforming materials, and conducting root cause analysis (RCA) to prevent recurring quality issues. It focuses on defect classification,
corrective actions, and adherence to quality standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage inspection documentation
Handle defect identification and segregation
Conduct Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
Coordinate for resolution and release
Maintain quality dashboards and review data
Contribute to continuous improvement
Follow safety and compliance during documentation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage inspection documentation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. record QC data in designated formats, registers, or ERP
- PC2. ensure test reports are complete, legible, and properly stored
- PC3. label inspected lots with identification tags and inspection status
- PC4. generate daily inspection summary reports for review
Handle defect identification and segregation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. inspect finished board/pallets for visual or measured non-conformities
- PC6. segregate defective material and mark with appropriate defect tags
- PC7. record nature of defect, lot number, and shift details on hold tags
- PC8. communicate defective lot details to concerned department for decision
Conduct Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. identify repetitive defects and initiate RCA process
- PC10. collect and present evidence (samples, parameters, records) for RCA meetings
- PC11. use 5 Why, Fishbone, or Pareto methods to analyze root cause
- PC12. suggest possible corrective and preventive actions (CAPA)
Coordinate for resolution and release
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. follow up with production and maintenance teams for timely defect resolution
