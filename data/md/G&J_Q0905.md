G&J/Q0905: Jewellery
Brief Job Description
Jeweler (the jewellery maker) fabricate and repair various jewellery articles. They create models from wax
or metal and they may undertake the casting process (place wax model in casting ring, create moulds,
pour molten metal into mould, or operate centrifugal casting machine to cast articles). Jeweler’s also cut,
saw, file, and solder pieces of jewellery together, using a soldering torch, carving tools and hand tools and
polish the article. The individual works with machine and hand tools to create jewellery components such
as wire, strips etc. and frames from precious metal as per the design of jewellery pieces and articles.
Individual can make exclusive jewellery pieces for use, pieces ready to be set with precious gemstones.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have precision and excellent craftsmanship, attention to details, good
eyesight, visualization, manual and finger dexterity, arm-hand steadiness. The individual is expected to
have integrity.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. G&J/N0907: Work organization and management for Jewellery making
- 2. G&J/N0908: Design jewellery components
- 3. G&J/N0910: Manufacture of precious metal alloys
- 4. G&J/N0909: Preparation of precious metal alloys for the manufacture of jewellery components
- 5. G&J/N0911: Manufacture of both simple and complex jewellery components
- 6. G&J/N0906: Surface finish

Description
This OS unit is about organizing and managing work activity using different planning tools. Also, learn and
follow safety guidelines
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Plan for the work activity
Follow policies, SOPs, work instructions, etc.
Health, hygiene and safety in work area
Use of personal protective equipment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Organizing and managing work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Follow environmental policies, identify potential breaches of environmental regulations, and
suggest improvements within the limit of own authority
PC2. Manage the environmental hazards/risks, resource use and inefficiencies associated with
own workplace, at an appropriate level

Design 2D sketches based on the concepts

Designing a jewellery component

PC1. Apply product knowledge to address customer requirements

G&J/N0910: Manufacture of precious metal alloys
Description
This OS unit is about manufacturing precious metal alloys by casting process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Calculate the quantities of fine precious metals and base metals required for making alloys
Cast precious metal alloy ingots and bars
Elements and Performance Criteria
Make precious metal alloys
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Recognize authenticity and quality signs for precious metals
PC2. Source precious metals of the correct price and quality for jewellery manufacture
PC3. Calculate the proportions and quantities of fine precious metals and base metals required for
any predetermined amount of any recognized precious metal alloy
PC4. Cast precious metal alloy ingots and bars of any predetermined weight, with a minimum of
residual impurities, ready to be milled or rolled in preparation for the manufacture of jewellery
components
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The content of precious metal alloys and the impact that additives have on the precious metal
in terms of colour, pliability, and durability
KU2. How alloys react to various processes used by the jewellery maker
KU3. The properties of precious metal alloys and their solders
KU4. Laws and regulations relating to precious metal content for sale and export
KU5. Assaying processes and procedures for the country of operation, purchase, and sale of
jewellery products
KU6. Forms in which precious metals are sold, such as sheet/wire/ granules etc.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. read job sheets, technical drawings, etc.
GS2. organize required toolkit at the work bench for better time management
GS3. communicate effectively with coordinators
