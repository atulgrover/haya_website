RSC/Q0625: Operator - Wet End Single Facer (Corrugated Boards)
Brief Job Description
Operate and monitor the wet end and single facer machine to produce corrugated boards as per specified
quality standards. Ensure continuous material feed, proper bonding, and in-process quality checks to
maintain consistent board strength and flute formation.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be attentive, disciplined, and safety-conscious while operating machinery. They
should have mechanical aptitude, good observation skills, and the ability to work collaboratively and
responsibly in a production environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N0614: Perform Machine Setup and Material Loading for Wet-End Operation
- 2. RSC/N0615: Conduct Single Facer Machine Operation and In-process Quality Monitoring
- 3. RSC/N5004: Carry out problem identification and escalation
- 4. RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol

RSC/N0614: Perform Machine Setup and Material Loading for Wet-End
Operation
Description
Prepare and set up wet-end machinery according to operational specifications. Load raw materials
accurately to ensure smooth production and maintain process quality.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare and align the liner and medium (fluting) paper reels
Set and calibrate machine parameters
Conduct pre-start machine inspection and dry run
Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare and align the liner and medium (fluting) paper reels
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify the correct type, size, and GSM of liner and fluting paper reels as per the job order or
production plan
PC2. use mechanical or hydraulic reel handling equipment to safely lift and load the paper reels
onto the unwind stand
PC3. check for physical defects in the paper reels such as tears, creases, or moisture patches
before loading
PC4. align the liner and medium paper accurately over the guide rollers and feed pathways to
ensure smooth entry into the single facer unit
PC5. adjust reel brakers and tension controls to maintain consistent web tension during unwinding
and feeding
PC6. ensure correct paper orientation and threading to match flute direction and bonding
alignment
PC7. verify that paper reels are locked and secured properly to avoid misfeed or displacement
during operation
PC8. record reel usage details (paper type, roll number, and weight) in the shift log or production
register
Set and calibrate machine parameters
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. interpret job sheet or production schedule to identify required board grade, flute type, and
glue specifications for the production run
PC10. set the flute roll and preheater temperature based on the paper GSM, moisture content, and
bonding requirements
PC11. adjust nip roller and pressure roll settings to ensure proper contact between the flute and
liner without crushing the board

specifications
KU3. safe handling procedures for lifting and loading paper reels using mechanical or hydraulic
equipment

before use
KU5. techniques for accurate alignment of liner and medium paper over guide rollers and feed
pathways
KU6. correct paper orientation and threading to match flute direction and ensure proper bonding
KU7. machine parameters including flute roll temperature, preheater settings, nip and pressure
roller adjustments, and their effect on board quality
KU8. calibration of glue applicator rollers, control of glue viscosity, temperature, and uniform
application across fluting tips

RSC/N0615: Conduct Single Facer Machine Operation and In-process
Quality Monitoring
Description
Operate and control the single facer machine as per defined production parameters. Monitor and maintain
in-process quality standards to ensure proper flute formation, bonding, and board consistency
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operate the single facer machine during live production
Monitor and control key process parameters
Perform in-process quality checks
Elements and Performance Criteria
Operate the single facer machine during live production
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. ensure continuous and smooth feeding of fluting and liner paper into the single facer unit
without reel displacement or paper misalignment
PC2. operate the machine at the prescribed speed and pressure, maintaining optimal flute
formation and consistent bonding quality
PC3. regulate adhesive flow and roller engagement to ensure even glue application on fluted tips
during production
PC4. monitor flute profile and bonding visually and physically at intervals to confirm proper
adhesion and avoid delamination
PC5. detect signs of improper bonding, glue skips, or liner shifting, and make immediate
adjustments or alert the supervisor
PC6. maintain coordination with adhesive preparation and corrugation control teams to adjust
inputs based on machine response or job changeovers
Monitor and control key process parameters
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. monitor steam pressure and flute roll temperature to ensure proper heat transfer for
effective corrugation of medium paper
PC8. adjust machine speed based on paper type, moisture content, and bonding behavior to
ensure consistent flute formation
PC9. observe glue application consistency and regulate adhesive flow to avoid over-gluing, skips,
or uneven bonding
PC10. check nip pressure settings regularly to ensure sufficient contact between the liner and
fluted paper without crushing
PC11. use control panel indicators or gauges to track real-time process values and make timely
adjustments as required

Perform in-process quality checks
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC15. visually inspect the flute profile for proper shape, height, and uniformity during machine

PC16. check bonding between liner and fluting using manual methods (e.g., peel test) at periodic

PC17. identify common quality defects such as delamination, warping, glue skips, and liner

PC18. monitor paper tracking and ensure that liners and medium are correctly aligned throughout
the width of the board
PC19. take immediate corrective actions (e.g., adjust glue flow, roller pressure, or paper tension) or
inform the supervisor when defects are detected
PC20. record quality check observations and corrective actions taken in the in-process quality
checklist or production log
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. working principles and components of the single facer machine, including flute rolls,
preheaters, and pressure rolls
KU2. types and characteristics of liner and fluting paper used in corrugation and their effect on
bonding quality
KU3. correct procedures for continuous feeding and alignment of liner and medium paper to avoid
misfeed or displacement
KU4. relationship between machine speed, steam pressure, flute roll temperature, and flute
formation quality
KU5. adhesive application process, including control of glue viscosity, temperature, and flow for
uniform bonding
KU6. common in-process defects such as delamination, glue skips, flute crushing, and liner
shifting, and methods to identify and correct them
KU7. use of control panel indicators, gauges, and manual checks to monitor process parameters
such as temperature, pressure, and speed
KU8. standard methods for in-process quality inspection, including visual checks and manual peel
or bonding strength tests
KU9. recording and documentation of process parameters and quality observations in production
logs or checklist
KU10. coordination requirements with adhesive preparation and corrugation control teams for
maintaining production quality
