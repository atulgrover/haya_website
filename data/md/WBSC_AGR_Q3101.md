#### WBSC/AGR/Q3101: Mushroom  Cultivator
Brief Job Description
A mushroom cultivator primarily carries out ex-situ cultivation of mushrooms. Perform routine care
including planting, irrigating and harvesting of mushrooms. Examine quality of mushroom produce and
prepare items for market/sale purposes. Monitor mushroom growing conditions including set water lines,
air flow and barn temperature. Ensure the cleanness of the farm working.
Personal Attributes
A mushroom cultivator is hard working, focused and safety oriented. They observe the crop closely to
notice growth changes, solve problems early and keep the work area clean and hygienic. They have the
physical strength to carry out tasks like planting, watering and harvesting. They are responsible and
disciplined and follow proper cultivation methods and can adjust to changes in the environment.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. WBSC/AGR/N3101: Identify the Scope & importance of Mushroom cultivation in India.
2. WBSC/AGR/N3102: Prepare & pasteurize the compost necessary to cultivate mushrooms.
3. WBSC/AGR/N3103: Select commercially important species of mushroom and design appropriate site to cultivate mushrooms.
4. WBSC/AGR/N3104: Undertake disease control and pest management activities, casing and pinning
for mushroom cultivation.
5. WBSC/AGR/N3105: Demonstrate harvest & post-harvest procedures of mushrooms.
6. WBSC/AGR/N3106: Work in real job situation with special emphasis on basic safety and hazards in
this domain (Mushroom Cultivator).
7. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### WBSC/AGR/N3104: Undertake disease control and pest management
activities, casing and pinning for mushroom cultivation.
Description
Undertake disease control and pest management activities, casing and pinning for mushroom cultivation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Inspection of mushroom bags or beds.
Identification of the diseases.
Controlling diseases and exercising preventive care.
Pasteurization of the mushroom farm to remove nematode in mushroom cultivation.
Preparation for casing soil to hold moisture.
Promotion of the formation of primordia, or mushroom pins by supplying water to the mycelium.
Detection of the earliest formation of recognizable mushrooms from mycelium.
Usage of sterilized casing to control nematodes.
Spraying fungicide after casing to check dry bubble.
Spraying insecticide for control of mites.
Application caustic chemicals top keep rodents away.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Inspect mushroom bags or beds carefully for early detection of pests and diseases.
- PC2. Identify the diseases.
- PC3. Control diseases and exercise preventive care- spray pesticides/ fungicides etc.
- PC4. Pasteurize the mushroom farm to remove nematode in mushroom cultivation.
- PC5. Prepare casing soil to hold moisture.
- PC6. Promote the formation of primordia, or mushroom pins by supplying water to the mycelium.
- PC7. Detect the earliest formation of recognizable mushrooms from mycelium.
- PC8. Use sterilized casing to control nematodes.
- PC9. Spray fungicide after casing to check dry bubble.
- PC10. Spray insecticide for control of mites.
- PC11. Apply caustic chemicals top keep rodents away.

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. Inspection of mushroom bags or beds carefully for early detection of pests and diseases.
- KU2. Controlling of diseases and exercising preventive care- spraying pesticides/ fungicides etc.

#### WBSC/AGR/N3105: Demonstrate  harvest & post-harvest procedures of
mushrooms.
Description
Demonstrate harvest & post-harvest procedures of mushrooms.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assessment of the maturity of mushroom and harvest periods.
Good harvesting practices.
Cutting, cleaning and drying harvested mushroom using approved procedures.
Sorting and grading the harvests as per required quality specifications.
Storing, packaging, labelling and transporting produce.
Recording the information in appropriate registers.
Recording book and logs.
Utilization of spent mushroom substrate in organic farming, vermi composting, bioremediation of
contaminated soil etc.
Sorting of the Mushrooms on the size and quality.
Packaging Mushrooms with labels.
Usage of spent Mushroom in vermi-composting and in organic farming.

appropriate registers, record book and logs.

contaminated soil etc.

Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Career Development & Goal Setting
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Employability Skills
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify employability skills required for jobs in various industries
- PC2. identify and explore learning and employability portals
Constitutional values – Citizenship
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. recognize the significance of constitutional values, including civic rights and duties,
citizenship, responsibility towards society etc. and personal values and ethics such as honesty, integrity, caring and respecting others, etc.
- PC4. follow environmentally sustainable practices
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. recognize the significance of 21st Century Skills for employment
- PC6. practice the 21st Century Skills such as Self-Awareness, Behaviour Skills, time management,
critical and adaptive thinking, problem-solving, creative thinking, social and cultural awareness, emotional awareness, learning to learn for continuous learning etc. in personal
and professional life

#### Assessment Guidelines
Assessment will be based on the concept of Independent Assessors empaneled with West Bengal State
Council of Technical & Vocational Education & Skill Development (WBSCT&VE&SD), identified, selected,
trained and certified on Assessment techniques. These Assessors would be aligned to assess as per the
laid down criteria.
WBSCT&VE&SD would conduct assessment only at the training centers or designated testing centers
authorized by WBSCT&VE&SD. Ideally, the assessment will be a continuous process comprising of two
distinct steps:
A. Continuous assessment by Trainers
B. Term end /Final Assessment by WBSCT&VE&SD
Each National Occupational Standard (NOS) in the respective QPs will be assigned weightage. Each
#### Performance Criteria in the NOS will be assigned marks for theory and/or practical based on relative
importance and criticality of function.This will facilitate preparation of question bank / paper sets for each
of the QPs. Each of these papers sets/question banks created by subject matter experts through
WBSCT&VE&SD, especially with regard to the practical test and the defined tolerances, finish, accuracy

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |
| --- | :---: |
| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
| Knowledge and Understanding (KU) | Knowledge and Understanding (KU) are statements which together specify the technical, generic, professional and organisational specific knowledge that an individual needs in order to perform to the required standard. |
