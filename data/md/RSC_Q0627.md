RSC/Q0627: Corrugator Line Incharge
Brief Job Description
The Corrugator Line Incharge oversees daily operations of the corrugator line ensuring efficient and safe
machine performance, meeting production targets, maintaining product quality, leading shift operators,
implementing quality controls, planning schedules, and keeping accurate production records.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be physically fit to work for long hours, have good observational skills,
maintain team coordination, and ensure quality and safety on the production line.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N0619: Plan, Schedule and Supervise Corrugator Line Operations
- 2. RSC/N0620: Implement Quality and Safety Control on Line
- 3. RSC/N0621: Maintain Line Efficiency and Manage Process Improvement
- 4. RSC/N0622: Ensure Effective Communication and Team Coordination
- 5. RSC/N0623: Manage Documentation and Assist in Audits
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

RSC/N0619: Plan, Schedule and Supervise Corrugator Line Operations
Description
This NOS covers planning and scheduling corrugator line activities, allocating resources, and supervising
operations to ensure optimal productivity, quality output, and adherence to safety and production
standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Interpret production requirements and plan operations
Allocate manpower and ensure setup readiness
Monitor production and performance
Coordinate and report operations
Enforce standards and process compliance
Elements and Performance Criteria
Interpret production requirements and plan operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. interpret daily production schedules, job cards, and customer delivery timelines
PC2. assess material availability including paper reels, adhesives, and additives
PC3. prepare a production plan aligning with shift capacity and deadlines
PC4. coordinate with stores and logistics for just-in-time material supply
PC5. evaluate job changeover requirements and downtime impact
Allocate manpower and ensure setup readiness
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. assign machine operators and helpers based on skill levels and job complexity
PC7. verify machine readiness including alignment, temperature, and glue settings
PC8. cross-check that the job loaded matches approved board specifications
PC9. ensure sample board is produced and approved before full run
PC10. inspect previous job clean-up and shift handover records
Monitor production and performance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. monitor corrugator line speed and evaluate efficiency during the shift
PC12. identify and respond to production delays or unplanned stoppages
PC13. maintain a record of hourly output and compare against targets
PC14. guide team members during troubleshooting of line imbalances
PC15. ensure WIP and finished board movement is smooth and timely
Coordinate and report operations

effectively

downstream (finishing/packing) operations for smooth workflow

RSC/N0620: Implement Quality and Safety Control on Line
Description
This NOS covers implementing quality and safety control measures on the corrugator line to ensure
products meet defined standards while maintaining a safe, compliant, and efficient working environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Ensure quality standards are enforced
Support quality troubleshooting and improvement
Monitor safety and enforce compliance
Record and report quality/safety performance
Elements and Performance Criteria
Ensure quality standards are enforced
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. understand customer specifications and define critical quality parameters
PC2. ensure QC checkpoints are active at wet-end, dry-end, and exit
PC3. verify first board sample against job specs (GSM, calliper, flute integrity)
PC4. ensure regular inspection of board warp, delamination, glue bonding
PC5. guide operators to respond to real-time quality alerts
PC6. review in-process inspection records and sampling reports
Support quality troubleshooting and improvement
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. identify repeat quality issues and initiate preliminary root cause analysis
PC8. document quality non-conformances and coordinate with QC for resolution
PC9. review machine parameters for potential quality deviations
PC10. recommend changes in run conditions to improve board quality
PC11. provide inputs to quality team for improving test plans or specifications
PC12. conduct follow-up reviews on previous quality issues
Monitor safety and enforce compliance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. check that all team members are using required PPE and safety tools
PC14. verify interlocks, guards, alarms, and emergency stop systems before production
PC15. conduct safety toolbox talks and pre-shift safety briefings
PC16. intervene in case of unsafe practices or behavior on shop floor
PC17. monitor hot plate and steam pressure zones for overheating risks
PC18. ensure chemicals (glue/additives) are stored and handled safely
Record and report quality/safety performance

root causes

analysis

quality management systems
KU7. safe handling and storage practices for adhesives, chemicals, and materials used on the
production line
KU8. importance and functioning of machine interlocks, guards, emergency stops, and alarm
systems
KU9. workplace safety regulations, PPE requirements, and procedures for preventing accidents
and injuries
KU10. protocols for conducting safety briefings, toolbox talks, and enforcing compliance on the
shop floor
KU11. record-keeping standards for maintaining QC reports, safety checklists, and deviation logs
KU12. coordination and communication processes with quality control, maintenance, and EHS
teams for effective issue resolution and continuous improvement

GS1. read and interpret customer specifications, job sheets, and quality standards accurately
GS2. conduct visual and physical quality inspections at different stages of the corrugator line
GS3. identify defects such as warp, delamination, or bonding issues and take prompt corrective
actions
GS4. monitor and record key process parameters affecting board quality and product consistency

RSC/N0621: Maintain Line Efficiency and Manage Process Improvement
Description
This NOS covers monitoring and optimizing corrugator line efficiency through process analysis, waste
reduction, and continuous improvement initiatives to achieve higher productivity and consistent product
quality.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Monitor efficiency and analyze performance
Implement process improvement projects
Lead standardization and capability building
Coordinate reviews and continuous improvement
Support change management and team coaching
Elements and Performance Criteria
Monitor efficiency and analyze performance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. track overall equipment effectiveness (OEE), uptime, and losses
PC2. study trends of repeated breakdowns or quality failures
PC3. collect data from MIS, downtime logs, and production reports
PC4. coordinate with maintenance to understand recurring issues
