NFD/MES/Q3501: 3D Animator (TV and Film Making)
Brief Job Description
This course is a short-term skill training program for freshers and amateurs to learn the craft of 3D
animation filmmaking and earn a livelihood through employment or freelance assignments. The objective
of the program is to prepare individuals for a job as 3D Animation Designer where they create visual
concepts, using computer software or by hand, to communicate ideas that inspire, inform, and captivate
consumers and enable them to develop the overall process and production design for various applications
such as Film, Game & advertisements, TV series with high visual impact.
Personal Attributes
This job requires individuals with a creative flair and technical proficiency in 3D animation, capable of
using computer software to craft compelling visual concepts. They should exhibit a keen eye for detail, an
innovative approach to storytelling, and the ability to conceptualize and execute designs that resonate
with audiences. These professionals must be adept at developing the overall process and production
design for diverse applications like films, games, advertisements, and TV series, delivering high-impact
visual experiences that engage and captivate viewers.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. NFD/MES/N3505: Carry out pre-production activities for 3D animation for TV and Film making.
- 2. NFD/MES/N3501: Carry out modeling, texturing and rigging for 3D animation.
- 3. NFD/MES/N3504: Carry out 3D animation.
- 4. NFD/MES/N3503: Carryout post-production for 3D animation
- 5. NFD/MES/N3502: Specialization in Pre-Production/3D Asset Creation/Animation/Post Production
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

NFD/MES/N3501: Carry out modeling, texturing and rigging for 3D
animation.
Description
This NOS is designed to perform modeling, texturing and rigging for 3D animation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Foundation of props & background modeling
Character modeling Art
UV wrapping and texturing
Foundation of 3D Rigging
Elements and Performance Criteria
Foundation of props & background modeling
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Use the interface of maya modeling.
PC2. Create 3D flower Vase model in Maya
PC3. demonstrate maya tools & settings for 3D modeling.
PC4. create props model in 3d software
PC5. Students needs to show proper topology and low poly modeling process
PC6. Create a 3D background like exterior /interior . The 3D model must be low poly with proper
topology
Character modeling Art
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. create detailing of 3D topology for character modeling
PC8. create complete low poly character 3D model
UV wrapping and texturing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. cloth 3D models
PC10. carry out UV wrapping
PC11. carry out texturing and apply colors as per color theory
PC12. to submit one Props model & character model with texture
Foundation of 3D Rigging
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. Demonstrate basic tools and settings required in Maya for rigging
PC14. Design setup for given 3D model for rigging
PC15. Implement proper technique on the assigned model to create rigging
PC16. Complete rigging for mechanical 3D props

Description
This NOS is designed to perform specialization in Pre-Production/3D Asset Creation/Animation/Post
Production.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Specialization in Pre-Production/3D Asset Creation/Animation/Post Production
Elements and Performance Criteria
Pre-Production: Crafting the visual blueprint via design elements and storyboarding, laying the
groundwork for compelling visual narratives in media production.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Develop original character concepts based on storyboards and the director's input.
PC2. Create character sheets with detailed anatomy, personality, and costume design
specifications.
PC3. Adapt existing character designs for different media formats (e.g., film, TV, video games).
PC4. Design visually engaging and story-supporting backgrounds for various scenes.
PC5. Utilise perspective, colour theory, and lighting to create believable and immersive
environments.
PC6. Adapt existing background designs for different environments and camera angles.
PC7. Design functional and visually appealing props that fit the narrative and style of the
production.
PC8. Create functional and visually appealing props that effectively contribute to storytelling,
demonstrating a comprehensive understanding of material selection, size constraints,
production limitations, and overall narrative impact.
PC9. Creatively morph versatile props into scene-specific storytellers, breathing life into diverse
characters through transformation
PC10. Bring stories to life! Masterfully weave scripts and narratives into captivating visual
sequences through the magic of storyboarding.
PC11. Master the language of storytelling! Express camera angles, character movement, and key
actions through crystal-clear visuals.
PC12. Bridge the gap between vision and execution! Collaborate with directors and writers to sculpt
storyboards into masterful storytelling tools.
PC13. Bring static narratives to life! Breathe movement into storyboards by crafting simple
animations that capture timing and flow.
PC14. Unleash the magic of animation! Utilize basic rigs and techniques to showcase character
movement and interaction with dynamic flair.
