#### DWC/Q0301: Caregiver Persons with Disabilities (Non Clinical)
Brief Job Description
Caregiver Persons with Disabilities (Non Clinical) provides daily living support to the persons with
disabilities. These Caregivers can attend to different categories of people with disabilities such as child
(between the age of 2 and 10 years), adolescents (from 10 to 18 years), and adults. Caregivers are
required to provide physical, social and emotional support. Their ability to provide basic education shall be
an added advantage.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be compassionate and caring with a capacity for extreme patience and tolerance. He
or she must be compassionate and empathetic enough to understand special condition of persons with
disabilities, and provide succor required. Association/experience persons with disabilities facilitate
compassion and useful attribute. The person should be reliable, trustworthy and sensitive.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. DWC/N0301: Provide support with the daily activities and basic needs of the child
2. DWC/N0302: Facilitate mental, social and emotional well-being of the child
3. DWC/N0303: Provide support with the daily activities and basic needs of an adolescent/adult
4. DWC/N0304: Facilitate mental, social and emotional well-being of the adolescent/adult
5. DWC/N0305: Extend support with laws and rights of people with disabilities
6. DWC/N9901: Prepare basic food as per dietary requirements
7. DWC/N9902: Display standards of hygiene and work etiquette
8. DWC/N9903: Maintain a clean and secure working environment

#### DWC/N0301: Provide support with the daily activities and basic needs of
the child
Description
This OS unit details the knowledge and skills required by Caregiver to attend to basic needs of the child
with disabilities.
Scope
This unit/task covers the following:
Evaluate characteristics of PwD (Persons with Disabilities) and support them
Provide routine care as per the disabilities
Provide attention to wellbeing/ ailments
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Evaluate characteristics of PwD (Persons with Disabilities) and support them
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. categorize and assess different types of disabilities/ life conditions for physical,
developmental, behavioural, intellectual and sensory impairment
- PC2. assist the child with physical disabilities in carrying out their daily activities
- PC3. maintain a close watch on development progress/ disability and keep the parents/ if required
medical personnel of such observations.
- PC4. predict and manage the behavioural pattern of the child given to such disorders
- PC5. infer the gaps in the sensory impairment of the child and assist him/ her to function
effectively
Provide routine care as per the disabilities
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. develop a daily activity plan based on the requirements of the child as discussed with
parents
- PC7. feed the child as per schedule by following the dietary requirements
- PC8. help the child with his or her bath
- PC9. assist the child with putting on clothes and grooming (for e.g. brushing, combing, nails
cutting, ear cleansing, etc.)
- PC10. assist the child with mobility such as transferring from bed to wheelchair or going to the
washroom
- PC11. clean the child after eating or a visit to the washroom
- PC12. maintain a safe and comfortable environment around the child
- PC13. put the child to sleep as per schedule ensuring his or her comfort
Provide attention to wellbeing/ ailments
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC14. provide right dosage of medicines to the child on time, as instructed by parents/doctor

#### DWC/N0302: Facilitate mental, social and emotional well-being of the
child
Description
This OS details the knowledge and skills required to facilitate mental and emotional well-being of the child
with disabilities.
Scope
This unit/task covers the following:
Engage child in various activities as per requirement
Provide reports to parents
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Engage child in various activities as per requirement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. seek information about the child's developmental needs from parents and the child, when
applicable
- PC2. develop personalized plans to meet child's needs
- PC3. plan activities that encourage the child to be active in their own care, learning and
development
- PC4. identify barriers to participation and choose alternative activities when appropriate
- PC5. promote active participation, to enable the child to play, taking account of their expressed
wishes and preferences
- PC6. encourage the child for his/her good deeds
- PC7. educate the children about the boundaries of acceptable and unacceptable behavior in
themselves and others
- PC8. implement adaptations that can be made with or without the use of special aids or
equipment, depending upon the context
- PC9. refer to parents, promptly, about any concerns about the child, according to the procedures
of the setting
- PC10. make the environment child friendly by rearranging layout of furniture and accessibility of
equipment
Provide reports to parents
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. record children's progress according to agreed methods
- PC12. encourage key people such as parents or siblings to participate in activities with children, as
required
- PC13. give feedback about a child's progress to the child and other adults as required
- PC14. seek help from others when you require information or support
