#### SGJ/Q0119: Solar PV Module Manufacturing  Technician
Brief Job Description
Solar PV Module Manufacturing Technician checks the front glass cover for the PV module, monitors the
process of soldering solar cells to the strings to make interconnect, lamination of modules, framing of solar
PV module, module testing and packaging for transit.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to concentrate on the job at hand and complete it without any accidents so
diligence and hardworking are desired attributes for individuals performing this role. He must also
demonstrate strong work ethics, an ability to communicate courteously with co-workers and must be good
with following instructions of the supervisor
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. SGJ/N0145: Carry out manufacturing of Solar PV Modules
2. SGJ/N0147: Maintain personal health & safety in a manufacturing facility
3. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### SGJ/N0145: Carry out manufacturing of Solar PV Modules
Description
This unit is about manufacturing of Solar PV Modules which provides a broader overview of Solar PV
Technology, Bill of Material of Solar Panel, soldering technology in module manufacturing, Solar Module
Manufacturing process, Visual inspection and Electroluminescence (EL) Test as well as Packing of Solar PV
Modules
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Role and Responsibilities of a Manufacturing Technician
BOM of Solar Module
Soldering process
Manufacturing process
Testing of PV Module
Packing of Solar Module
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Solar PV Technology
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the role of Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Manufacturing technician, its importance in the
sector and the advantages of doing the course.
- PC2. show how irradiation influence performance of solar module.
- PC3. show how to the use of Hand-held irradiation meter.
- PC4. show Solar Module Construction & Manufacturing Process flow Chart.
- PC5. identify mono and poly crystalline silicon solar cell technology and recent trends.
- PC6. identify the concept of solar energy, working of solar cell, Electrical Properties of Solar Cell,
Solar PV Applications
- PC7. identify the property of semiconductor material and P-N Junction.
- PC8. discuss the basic aspects of solar energy and power generation.
- PC9. definition of properties of light, global horizontal Irradiation (GHI) and Direct Normal
irradiance.
- PC10. discuss the various configurations of Solar PV Power plants. (Grid connected, Off grid etc).
- PC11. show how to interpret signs, notices and/or cautions at project site.
Prepare and identify the BOM and its specification for solar module
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

- PC13. show how to prepare the BOM of Solar Module.
- PC14. Identify various Materials used in solar module manufacturing as Solar Glass, Cell, EVA Back
sheet, Aluminium Frame, Junction Box, Interconnect Ribbon, Silicon Sealant

#### DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.

Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills

Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
