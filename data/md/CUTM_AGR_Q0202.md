CUTM/AGR/Q0202: Seaweed Grower and Processor
Brief Job Description
The Individual is responsible to understand about Seaweed Grower and Processor, check water quality,
nutrition, and health benefits and indirectly evaluate the quality parameters of fish and fishery products.
Personal Attributes
A Seaweed Grower and Processor should be environmentally responsible, physically fit, and attentive to
detail. They must have a strong understanding of marine ecosystems and sustainable practices. Patience,
precision, and a commitment to hygiene and quality are essential for post-harvest handling and
processing. Creativity in developing value-added products, along with problem-solving skills, adaptability
to coastal conditions, and dedication to eco-friendly methods, are key traits for success in this role.

CUTM/AGR/N0201: Seaweed Ecology and its environmental importance
Description
This NOS covers the ecological importance of seaweed, its role in marine ecosystems, and sustainable
cultivation methods. It includes species identification, environmental monitoring, and conservation
strategies to promote biodiversity.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes identifying seaweed species, monitoring water quality, understanding ecological
roles, and implementing sustainable cultivation methods to enhance biodiversity while ensuring
environmental protection.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Various types of seaweed, their economic and environmental importance, and the global
seaweed industry.
PC2. Identification and classification of seaweed species through field visits.
PC3. Understanding seaweed anatomy and physiology.
PC4. Growth conditions: light, temperature, salinity, and nutrients.
PC5. Seaweed samples and testing water quality parameters.
PC6. Role of seaweed in marine ecosystems.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. different seaweed species and their ecological significance.
KU2. seaweed's role in carbon sequestration and climate change mitigation.
KU3. about marine biodiversity and seaweed's contribution to habitat formation.
KU4. nutrient cycling and water quality improvement by seaweed.
KU5. environmental parameters affecting seaweed growth.
KU6. sustainable seaweed cultivation methods.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Conduct field surveys to analyze seaweed populations.
GS2. Perform water quality tests for salinity, temperature, and nutrients.
GS3. Identify and classify various seaweed species.

CUTM/AGR/N0202: Post-Harvest handling of Seaweed
Description
This NOS focuses on post-harvest handling of seaweed, covering cleaning, sorting, drying, preservation,
and storage to maintain product quality and extend shelf life while ensuring food safety compliance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes best practices for seaweed harvesting, handling, processing, and packaging,
ensuring minimal spoilage and adherence to safety and quality standards for market distribution.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Cleaning of seaweed as they may contain many impurities such as sand and debris.
PC2. It should be sorted and graded according to their size, color, species and quality.
PC3. One of the pivotal factors in seaweed post harvesting is drying process.
PC4. Proper packaging is important for maintaining quality and freshness.
PC5. Gain insights into the global seaweed market, its economic potential and current trends in
supply and demand.
PC6. Develop entrepreneurial skills.
PC7. Quality control measures are implemented to meet the regulatory standards and customer
requirements.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. best practices for harvesting seaweed to maintain quality.
KU2. techniques for cleaning and sorting seaweed post-harvest.
KU3. proper drying and preservation methods.
KU4. storage conditions to prevent spoilage.
KU5. logistics and transportation of seaweed products.
KU6. quality grading and sorting techniques.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Perform proper post-harvest seaweed cleaning techniques.
GS2. Apply sorting and grading methods to ensure quality.

CUTM/AGR/N0203: Value added seaweed product and preservation
Description
This NOS covers the processing and preservation of seaweed into value-added products such as food,
pharmaceuticals, and cosmetics while ensuring safety and quality compliance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes extraction, preservation, packaging, and marketing of value-added seaweed
products while maintaining high standards of quality and sustainability.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Extraction process for obtaining secondary metabolites such as proteins, polyphenols and
polysaccharides.
PC2. Extract from seaweeds can be used as an ingredient in food products.
PC3. The extracts are commonly used in skincare, hair care and personal care products.
PC4. Seaweed-based bioplastics offer a sustainable alternative to petroleum-based plastics for
packaging and disposable products.
PC5. Seaweed is used as a nutritious feed ingredient for livestock, aquaculture, and pet food due to
its high protein, mineral, and vitamin content.
PC6. Seaweed supplements for pets are also available to promote overall health and vitality.
PC7. Preservation methods such as drying, freezing, pickling, fermentation, and irradiation are
employed to extend the shelf life of seaweed products while retaining their quality, flavor, and
nutritional value.
PC8. Seaweed extracts are utilized as organic fertilizers and soil conditioners in agricultural
practices.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. methods for producing value-added seaweed products.
KU2. preservation techniques like drying, freezing, and fermentation.
KU3. bioactive compounds extracted from seaweed for medicinal uses.
KU4. about seaweed-based food additives and ingredients.
KU5. sustainable packaging for processed seaweed.
KU6. regulatory guidelines for processed seaweed products.
Generic Skills (GS)

CUTM/AGR/N0204: Quality Assurance in seaweed processing
Description
This NOS focuses on ensuring the quality and safety of processed seaweed products through testing,
regulatory compliance, and adherence to global food safety standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes conducting quality checks, maintaining hygiene standards, ensuring safety
compliance, and implementing quality control measures in seaweed processing.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Understanding the different quality parameters associated with seaweed harvesting
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Sustainable harvesting practices and regulations to ensure long-term health of the seaweed.
PC2. Effective methods for cleaning, sorting, and storing harvested seaweed to maintain quality
and prevent spoilage.
Knowledge on various methods of assessment and maintenance of quality
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. Exploring various drying techniques like sun dry, oven dry and their impact on seaweed
quality, nutritional value, and shelf life.
PC4. Learning about different methods for extracting valuable compounds from seaweed, such as
alginates, carrageenan, agar, and fucoid an, for various research and commercial applications.
PC5. Discover processes for converting seaweed into products such as food products, cosmetics,
fertilizers, biofuels, and other marketable goods.
PC6. Understanding the importance of quality control measures in the entire processing method
and gaining knowledge on relevant regulations for food safety and product labeling.
PC7. Regular visit to commercial seaweed processing plants can provide valuable real-world
insights into the industrial and commercial sector.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. quality parameters for seaweed processing.
KU2. microbial testing for seaweed products.
KU3. global certification standards for seaweed processing.
KU4. techniques for maintaining nutritional integrity.
KU5. packaging quality control measures.
KU6. environmental safety in seaweed processing plants.

CUTM/AGR/N0205: Minimizing the use of synthetic pesticides and
fertilizers and practicing the use of organic and sustainable principles
Description
This NOS emphasizes reducing synthetic pesticide use and adopting organic, sustainable seaweed farming
practices for environmental conservation and healthier product yields.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes implementing organic farming methods, minimizing synthetic inputs, ensuring
soil and water conservation, and maintaining sustainable seaweed cultivation practices.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Importance of sustainability in seaweed industry
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. The process of seaweed cultivation is an ecofriendly as compared to some field crops.
Seaweed farming that employs sustainable methods contributes to the preservation of
biodiversity, marine ecosystems, and the health of coastal regions.
PC2. Seaweeds play a vital role in carbon sequestration by absorbing CO2. Large-scale cultivation
of seaweed helps in mitigating climatic change
PC3. Sustainable seaweed cultivation can be a source for reliable and resilient food.
PC4. Seaweeds contain photoactive compounds which has applications in various industries such
as pharmaceuticals and biotechnology.
Environmental impact assessment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Evaluating the impact of seaweed cultivation on marine biodiversity which includes assessing
of effect flora and fauna of local habitat. Examining the water quality, nutrient levels and O2
concentrations and their effects.
PC6. Assessing any physical changes to the seabed or coastal regions which results from
cultivation. The overall environmental can be affected by changes in geological,
transparency, and structure of the habitat which needs to be studied.
PC7. Evaluating the risk of cultivation by introducing invasive species, by ensuring the cultivation
does not cause growth of any non-native seaweed which may create an imbalance in the
ecosystem.
PC8. Evaluating the capacity for nutrient loading in the water during the cultivation. As excessive
nutrient is released during cultivation or processing may lead to eutrophication.
PC9. Evaluating the various technique used for pest and disease management. Chemical used and
other methods should undergo an assessment on non-targeted species before the use.
PC10. Seaweed cultivation will lead to growth of indigenous species rather than invasive
PC11. Seaweed can act as a source of bioenergy.

CUTM/AGR/N0213: Implementation of Seaweed safety standards
Description
This NOS focuses on ensuring health and safety in seaweed processing facilities through proactive risk
identification, regular hygiene practices, employee training, emergency preparedness, and wellness
initiatives. It aims to reduce accidents, occupational illnesses, and promote a healthy work culture by
implementing safety standards and monitoring mechanisms for a secure and productive workplace.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes implementing health and safety protocols, identifying hazards, ensuring personal
and workplace hygiene, conducting emergency response activities, and promoting employee well-
being. It applies to all workers involved in seaweed processing and ensures compliance with
occupational health and safety standards to maintain a safe and efficient work environment.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Implementing safety measures like proper signage, training programs, and the provision of
personal protective equipment (PPE) helps in preventing injuries, accidents, and occupational
hazards.
PC2. Monitoring regularly the risk factors which will help in identifying potential hazards associated
with seaweed processing activities including trips, slips, cuts, falls, burns, chemical exposures,
and ergonomic strains. Strategies are then developed to mitigate these risks effectively.
PC3. Providing comprehensive training and education programs ensuring employees are well
equipped with the knowledge and skills necessary for performing their duties safely. Training
topics may include proper handling of equipment, safety use of chemical, emergency
procedures, and ergonomic practices.
PC4. Maintaining high standards of regular hygiene and sanitation is to prevent contamination and
the spread of foodborne diseases in seaweed processing facilities.
PC5. Establishing a vigilant monitoring for any emergency response to enable swift and effective
actions to incidents like fires, burns, chemical spills, medical emergencies, and natural
disasters.
PC6. Implementing health monitoring programs regularly for the detection and prevention of
occupational illnesses or injuries among workers.
PC7. Promoting employee wellness initiatives, like health and wellness workshops, fitness
programs, and mental health support services, fosters a positive work culture and enhances
overall employee well-being.
