#### HCS/Q7705: Crochet Lace Tailor
Brief Job Description
A Crochet Lace Tailor is responsible for developing/ tailoring crochet lace products as per the given
specifications/ instructions. A crochet lace tailor should be able to perform basic cutting and stitching
operations for developing crochet products of different designs as per quality standards. This job requires
the individual to have knowledge of measurements, sewing and finishing for making quality crochet lace
products.
Personal Attributes
A Crochet Lace Tailor should have good eyesight, hand-eye coordination and, vision (which includes near
vision, distance vision, colour vision, peripheral vision, depth perception and ability to change focus).
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N7724: Introduction to Crochet Industry in India
2. HCS/N8510: Entrepreneurship skills for Crochet Lace Tailor
3. HCS/N9929: Working in a team
4. HCS/N9931: Maintain health and safety at workplace
5. HCS/N9934: Managing Personal Sanitation
6. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Handmade Crochet
Handmade crochet is a traditional craft involving the intricate looping of yarn with a hook to create
decorative or functional items. Each piece reflects skilled artistry and careful attention to detail.
1. HCS/N7725: Prepare for Hand crochet products making
2. HCS/N7730: Construct Hand Crochet products
3. HCS/N7731: Finishing and Packaging of Hand crochet products
Elective 2: Machine-made Crochet

#### HCS/N7724: Introduction to Crochet Industry in India
Description
The crochet industry in India combines traditional hand techniques with modern machine crochet,
showcasing a rich heritage and innovative craftsmanship.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the History and Evolution of crochet work in India
Identify Types of Crochet work - Hand and Machine-made
Understand the production process in Crochet Industry
Explore Market trends and Employment opportunities
Appreciate Sustainability and Cultural relevance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand the History and Evolution of crochet work in India
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Describe the origin and traditional significance of crochet in Indian households and
communities.
- PC2. Identify regional styles and traditional patterns unique to Indian crochet craftsmanship.
- PC3. Explain the historical transition from traditional hand crochet to mechanised production.
- PC4. List key milestones in the development of the crochet industry in India.
Identify Types of Crochet work – Hand and Machine-made
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Differentiate between hand crochet and machine-made crochet based on process and
appearance.
- PC6. Describe the tools and materials used in hand crochet (hooks, threads, needles, patterns).
- PC7. Explain how machine-made crochet is produced using automated or semi-automated
processes.
- PC8. Recognise product categories made by hand and machine crochet (e.g., garments, home
décor, accessories).
Understand the production process in Crochet Industry
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. Outline the step-by-step process involved in hand crochet creation.
- PC10. Explain the workflow in machine-made crochet production, including pre-production and
finishing.
- PC11. Identify common raw materials used in both methods, such as cotton, wool, synthetic yarns,
etc.
- PC12. Discuss the role of quality checks and finishing techniques in crochet work.
Explore Market trends and Employment opportunities

#### HCS/N8510: Entrepreneurship skills for Crochet Lace Tailor
Description
Entrepreneurship skills for a crochet lace tailor involve being creative with designs, managing money well,
and knowing how to market products. Good communication and networking help build customer relationships and promote the brand. Being flexible and open to new ideas is also important for success in
a competitive market.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Develop a business idea for crochet products
Create a business plan for crochet product manufacturing
Set up production and manufacturing process
Manage Inventory and Materials
Develop Marketing and Branding strategies for crochet products
Sales and Customer service management
Financial management for crochet product business
Build business relationships and network
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Develop a business idea for crochet products
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify niche markets for both hand-made and machine-made crochet products, such as
custom lace garments, home décor items, and accessories.
- PC2. Assess consumer demand for crochet products based on current trends, customer feedback,
and market research.
- PC3. Explore different crochet product categories, including fashion items (e.g., scarves, shawls),
home décor (e.g., tablecloths, doilies), and accessories (e.g., bags, headbands).
- PC4. Consider sustainable practices in sourcing yarn and materials, such as using organic cotton
or recycled fibres.
- PC5. Analyse competitors in the crochet industry, assessing their pricing, product variety, and
marketing strategies.
Create a business plan for crochet product manufacturing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Define clear business goals, such as increasing sales, expanding the product range, or
entering new markets.
- PC7. Develop a product portfolio that includes both hand-made and machine-made crochet items,
identifying potential profit margins for each.
- PC8. Identify the costs of production, including raw materials, tools, machine maintenance (if
applicable), labour costs, and packaging.
- PC9. Create a pricing strategy that ensures competitive pricing while covering production costs
and generating profit.

#### HCS/N7727: Operate and Monitor crochet machine during production
Description
Operate and monitor the crochet machine during production by ensuring it runs smoothly and efficiently.
This includes regularly checking the machine settings, observing the stitching process for consistency, and
addressing any issues such as thread breaks or tension problems. Maintain a clean workspace and keep an
eye on the yarn supply to prevent interruptions, ensuring high-quality output throughout the production
run.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the types of crochet in machine making
Start and monitor the machine

- PC1. Differentiate between various types of machine-made crochet, including flat-bed and circular

- PC4. Explain the role of computerised crochet machines in modern manufacturing, including

Description
Conduct in-line and post-production quality checks to ensure the highest standards are met. In-line checks
involve monitoring the crochet process for consistency in stitch quality, tension, and design accuracy

measurements, and ensuring that items meet design specifications. Document any issues and implement
corrective actions as needed to maintain product quality.

Conduct Post-production inspection

#### HCS/N7729: Perform Finishing and Packaging of Machine-made crochet
products
Description
Perform finishing and packaging of machine-made crochet products by inspecting each item for quality,
trimming any loose threads, and folding or rolling them neatly. Package the products in eco-friendly
materials, ensuring labels highlight their machine-made nature. Make sure the packaging is secure and
visually appealing, ready for distribution.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform product finishing
Execute appropriate packaging
Store and dispatch finished goods
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform product finishing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Carefully trim excess yarn threads and uneven edges, ensuring the product is neat and
ready for finishing.
- PC2. Use steam finishers or heat presses to treat the product, setting the shape and reducing
shrinkage.
- PC3. Reinspect the fabric after steaming or pressing to confirm no colour bleeding, shrinkage, or
distortion has occurred.
- PC4. Perform any necessary quality checks, such as testing for fastness in colour and overall
durability.
Execute appropriate packaging
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Roll or fold the finished lace or crochet products according to buyer requirements, ensuring
that the product is not damaged during handling.
- PC6. Use eco-friendly materials to pack the products, such as biodegradable plastic or cotton
bags, to enhance the product's market appeal.
- PC7. Label each package with essential details such as the batch number, fabric type, colour, and
manufacturing date.
Store and dispatch finished goods
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Store the finished products in clean, dry areas to avoid contamination, shrinkage, or mildew.
- PC9. Follow FIFO (First In, First Out) dispatch methods to ensure that older stock is shipped first to
maintain product freshness.
- PC10. Ensure that dispatch records are maintained for every batch, detailing shipment details and
destination.
