#### CUTM/AGR/Q0401: Crop and Plant Supervisor
Brief Job Description
Crop and Plant Supervisor is responsible for understanding the market demand and market price to plan
and development of appropriate land location, preparation of land, plantation, water management,
intercultural operations, pest and disease management, harvesting and processing to deliver to the
customer for Lemon Grass and other integrated plants.
Personal Attributes
A Crop and Plant Supervisor must be observant, practical, and knowledgeable in horticultural practices.
They should possess strong planning and problem-solving abilities, along with a scientific mindset for
integrated nutrient, pest, and irrigation management. Physical stamina, leadership, communication skills,
and a commitment to sustainable farming and quality post-harvest practices are essential for success in
this role.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CUTM/AGR/N0409: Implement Health and Safety standards at Work Place
2. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Dragon Fruit Supervisor
A Dragon Fruit Supervisor is responsible for overseeing the cultivation, care, and post-harvest handling of
dragon fruit crops. The role includes planning land preparation, deploying planting materials, managing
nutrient and irrigation systems, and implementing integrated pest and disease control measures. The
supervisor ensures optimal growth conditions, monitors crop health, and guides labor for field activities.
Additionally, they are involved in harvesting, grading, and coordinating post-harvest storage or marketing.
The position requires practical knowledge of sustainable horticultural practices, record-keeping, and the
ability to train or guide farmers and workers in modern dragon fruit farming techniques.
1. CUTM/AGR/N0401: Plan and Deploy planting materials
2. CUTM/AGR/N0402: Suggest optimal cultivation practices with integrated farming
3. CUTM/AGR/N0403: Suggest integrated nutrient management in Dragon fruit cultivation
4. CUTM/AGR/N0404: Suggest integrated disease and pest management in Dragon fruit cultivation

Elective 2: Lemon Grass Supervisor
A Lemongrass Supervisor is responsible for managing the cultivation, maintenance, and post-harvest
processes of lemongrass crops. The role includes planning land preparation, suggesting suitable nutrient
and irrigation management, implementing weed and pest control measures, and monitoring crop health.
The supervisor oversees harvesting operations and guides the extraction and storage of lemongrass oil.
They ensure efficient use of resources, maintain quality standards, and may assist in marketing value-
added products. The position requires practical knowledge of aromatic plant cultivation, sustainable
farming practices, and the ability to lead field operations effectively.
1. CUTM/AGR/N0405: Plan and Suggest land Preparation and Planting
2. CUTM/AGR/N0406: Suggest Nutrient and Irrigation Management
3. CUTM/AGR/N0407: Suggest Weed Control and Plant Protection

#### CUTM/AGR/N0409: Implement  Health and Safety standards at Work Place
Description
This NOS focuses on maintaining personal hygiene, workplace safety, and environmental responsibility. It
outlines procedures for disease prevention, safe equipment handling, waste disposal, and emergency
response. The standard ensures that individuals follow preventive measures and safety protocols to create
a clean, hazard-free, and health-conscious work environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope covers adherence to sanitation and hygiene norms, safe use of tools and chemicals, proper waste management, first aid administration, and compliance with government and workplace
advisories. It aims to prepare individuals to work safely under routine and emergency situations,
reducing risks to themselves, coworkers, and the environment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Maintain personal hygiene

- PC1. wash hands, legs and face with soap/alcohol based sanitizer at reasonable intervals
- PC2. wash the worn clothes with soap and sundry before use next time
- PC3. ensure the face is covered with mask or three layers of cloth-piece
- PC4. follow the workplace sanitization norms including distancing from sick people

report identified hazards to the supervisor

avoid hazards

instructions for controlling risks are followed

Administer appropriate emergency procedures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. follow procedures for dealing with accidents, fires and emergencies, including
communicating location and directions to the location of emergency, as per the workplace requirements

#### CUTM/AGR/N0401: Plan and Deploy planting materials
Description
This NOS involves selecting appropriate land, testing and preparing soil, laying out fields using standard
systems (square, rectangle, contour), and deploying required tools, equipment, and support structures to
ensure optimal dragon fruit cultivation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of the NOS includes preparing suitable land, testing soil for nutrient levels, selecting the
appropriate crop variety, planning field layout, organizing required tools and equipment, and deploying planting materials effectively to ensure optimal growth and productivity of crops like
dragon fruit.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Leveling of the land appropriately for cultivation
- PC2. Creation of proper drainage systems
- PC3. Selection of the appropriate field layout (square system, rectangle system, triangle system,
quincunxial system, contour system) based on the variety, yield expected and canopy arrangement
- PC4. Placement of the markers at the site at appropriate distances
- PC5. Creating the pits at appropriate depths
- PC6. Irrigating the pits at right amounts and allow dryness
- PC7. Identification of the Agro-climatic zone in which cultivation is taken up
- PC8. Enlisting the varieties that are suitable to the zone
- PC9. Selection of economically attractive variety
- PC10. Identification of various vendors / suppliers (including government nurseries / Universities) of
the planting material, if the farmer is not preparing the planting material
- PC11. Assuring the quality of planting material from each source in terms of ‘free from pests and
diseases’, ‘survival rate’ etc.
- PC12. Determining the prevailing market rates for the planting material
- PC13. Procuring the planting material
- PC14. Identification of an appropriate storage space (free of infestation and having congenial
climatic conditions for the planting material)
- PC15. Storage of the planting material (if there is time lag between procurement and planting)
- PC16. Preparation of the planting material appropriately through practices suitable to variety
- PC17. Usage of vegetative propagation to get true to type planting material
- PC18. Treatment of the planting material appropriately
- PC19. Storage of planting material hygienically

- KU3. theoretical understanding of steam distillation process, the most common method used for extracting lemongrass essential oil.
- KU4. the familiarity with distillation units, including condensers, separators, and boilers used in

- KU5. the quality indicators of lemongrass oil such as color, aroma, density, and chemical composition (e.g., citral content).

#### Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
- GS1. assess the maturity of lemongrass based on standard indicators like leaf size, aroma, and growth duration before initiating harvesting.
- GS2. handle lemongrass post-harvest to avoid contamination, ensure moisture control, and prepare
it properly for oil extraction.
- GS3. be proficient in operating steam distillation units, including monitoring temperature, pressure,
and time to maximize oil yield and maintain quality.
- GS4. store lemongrass oil in appropriate containers (preferably stainless steel or aluminum) to
prevent degradation due to light, air, or temperature.
- GS5. testing the extracted oil for purity, aroma profile, and other quality parameters using both
sensory and basic lab techniques.
- GS6. the practical skills in cleaning, maintaining, and troubleshooting extraction and storage equipment to ensure safety and operational efficiency.
- GS7. do hygienic packaging standards and proper labelling practices, including batch numbers, production dates, and quality grades.
- GS8. maintain detailed records of raw material input, oil yield, processing time, and storage logs to
ensure traceability and compliance.
- GS9. handle and dispose, residual biomass post-extraction in an eco-friendly manner, possibly for
compost or fuel.
