#### ASC/Q8318: Robot Systems Integration
Brief Job Description
The robot system integrator must provide technical solutions to the robotization of all or part of a system
by incorporating a multi-articulating arm, together with the associated handling tools or special processes
(such as handling, machining, painting, and welding), to increase competitiveness and supporting the
ergonomics, health and safety of the users and people around them.
Personal Attributes
The person should be result oriented with good technical and analytical skills, should have Excellent
Interpersonal Skills, communication and presentation skills and a good team player. They should have
ability to manage projects, prioritizing of work and mentoring the budding engineers.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. ASC/N8356: Work organization and management (Robot Systems Integration)
2. ASC/N9820: Communication and interpersonal skills (Robot Systems Integration)
3. ASC/N8357: Layout and design
4. ASC/N8358: Installation and connectivity
5. ASC/N8359: Automation and programming
6. ASC/N8360: Commissioning, maintenance, and troubleshooting
7. ASC/N8361: Documentation, briefing, and reporting

#### ASC/N8356: Work organization and management   (Robot Systems
Integration)
Description
This NOS unit is about implementing safety, planning work, adopting sustainable practices for optimising
the use of resources.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage work and resources
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage work and resources
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. prepare and maintain a safe, tidy, and efficient work area
- PC2. prepare self for the tasks in hand, including full regard to health, safety, and environment
- PC3. schedule work to maximize efficiency and minimize disruption
- PC4. select and use all equipment and materials safely and in compliance with manufacturers’
instructions
- PC5. apply or exceed the health and safety standards applying to the environment, equipment, and
materials
- PC6. restore the work area to an appropriate state and condition
- PC7. contribute to team performance both broadly and specifically
- PC8. give and take feedback and support
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. principles and methods of safe work execution
- KU2. purposes, uses, care and maintenance of all equipment together with their safety implications
- KU3. environmental and safety principles and applications with regard to good housekeeping in the
work area
- KU4. principles of effective communication
- KU5. principles of effective collaboration
- KU6. scope and limits of ones own and others roles, responsibilities, and duties, both individually
and collectively
- KU7. parameters within which activities must be planned
- KU8. principles and techniques for time management

#### ASC/N8357: Layout and design
Description
This NOS is about finalizing layout and design of the system.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare layout and design of the system
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare layout and design of the system

- PC1. acquire and check instructions and guidance for given assignments
- PC2. identify and resolve areas of uncertainty within the parameters of the brief

activators

- KU1. the principles and relevant applications of computing and electronics
- KU2. the relevant practical applications of engineering science and technology
- KU3. the relevant practical implications of physical principles and interrelationships
- KU4. the principles and relevant applications of electrical engineering and pneumatics
- KU5. the design, uses, repair and maintenance needs of relevant machinery and tools
- KU6. the principles and applications of robots, robotic tools and equipment mounted on robots and
in robotic cells

#### ASC/N8358: Installation and connectivity
Description
This NOS is about installing and connecting the components of system.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Installation and connection of the system components
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Installation and connection of the system components
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. check that all items have been delivered according to specification, and follow up as required
- PC2. organize the safe storage of all items, together with arrangements for their checking in and
out
- PC3. check that the pre-manufactured robot has been delivered ready to run, and follow up as
required
- PC4. connect robot system components according to instructions and documentation
- PC5. assemble, position, and fix robotic tools and equipment according to instructions and
documentation
- PC6. align, fit, or assemble components, using hand tools, power tools, fixtures, or templates,
according to specification
- PC7. liaise with specialists for the correct electrical, pneumatic, and mechanical installation of
robots and peripheral equipment
- PC8. connect Input/Output (I/O) control signals between robot and peripheral equipment, either
low voltage (24V) or Ethernet/Bus systems
- PC9. perform tests during the installation process to ensure functionality
- PC10. identify installation issues, consider alternative solutions, and implement selected solution(s)
to resolve the issues
- PC11. respect and take account of the receiving sites’ requirements and characteristics, within the
bounds of safe working, active risk management, and professionalism
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the norms and cultures of the receiving industrial sites
- KU2. principles and methods for the safe receipt and ongoing management of equipment, tools, and materials
- KU3. principles underlying the physical installation of robotics into production systems
- KU4. principles and methods for assembling pre-manufactured robots in their positions for use

#### ASC/N8359: Automation and programming
Description
This NOS unit is about automation and programming of the system.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Automation and programming of the system
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Automation and programming of system
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. consult with client/personnel to clarify program intent
- PC2. develop diagrams or flow charts of systems operations
- PC3. write, analyse, review and rewrite programs, using flow charts and diagrams
- PC4. create application software programs that are easy to document, understand and maintain
- PC5. conduct trial runs of programs and software applications to ensure they will produce the
desired robot and cell performance
- PC6. write, update, and maintain computer programs or software packages to handle specific jobs
- PC7. optimize robot motion performance and I/O handling to minimize cycle time/maximize
throughput while retaining reliable operation
- PC8. correct errors by making appropriate changes and rechecking the program to ensure that the
desired results are produced
- PC9. consult with other personnel to identify problems and suggest changes
- PC10. implement new additional software and hardware options based on standard functionality
- PC11. integrate simple and advanced sensors
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. computer capabilities and symbolic logic
- KU2. principles governing the purposes and functions of computer hardware and software
- KU3. principles and options for: manipulating robot coordinate frames, for robot, cell, and tooling,
controlling robot motion, controlling robot input/output (I/O)
- KU4. the principles, reasons or facts that provide the basis for breaking down information or data
into separate parts
- KU5. methods for obtaining information and data from all relevant sources
- KU6. principles and methods for processing information and data
- KU7. the software in use

Description
This NOS is about commissioning, maintenance, and troubleshooting of the system

Commissioning, maintenance, and troubleshooting of the system
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Commissioning, maintenance, and troubleshooting of the system

- KU1. the formal requirements for successful site acceptance tests
- KU2. the scope and limits of the technologies, methods operational environment
- KU3. criteria and methods for testing equipment and systems
- KU4. strategies for fault finding, problem solving and optimization
- KU5. techniques and options for replacements and repairs
- KU6. principles and techniques for generating creative and innovative solutions
- KU7. principles and options for establishing and maintaining production maintenance regimes
