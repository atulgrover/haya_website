#### AGR/Q6101: Bamboo  Cultivator
Brief Job Description
A Bamboo cultivator is responsible for propagating bamboo saplings in a nursery, transplanting them into
the field, and maintaining and harvesting bamboo culms. The individual is also responsible for marketing
the harvested bamboos culms.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be physically fit to work for long durations. The person must have good eyesight along
with problem-solving and coordination skills. The abilities to read and write are the other important
attributes required in this job role.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. AGR/N6101: Propagate bamboo saplings in the nursery
2. AGR/N6103: Select the bamboo variety and site, and prepare the field
3. AGR/N6104: Transplant and maintain the bamboo saplings
4. AGR/N6105: Carry out pruning and weeding in bamboo crop
5. AGR/N6106: Carry out irrigation, nutrient management and intercropping
6. AGR/N6107: Carry out integrated pest and disease management for bamboo crop
7. AGR/N6108: Carry out harvesting, post-harvest processing and marketing of bamboo culms
8. AGR/N9903: Maintain health and safety at the workplace
9. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

#### AGR/N6101: Propagate bamboo  saplings in the nursery
Description
This OS unit is about propagating bamboo saplings in the nursery for being transplanted into the field.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for the propagation of bamboo sapling in the nursery
Plant and maintain bamboo saplings
Harvest and transport the bamboo saplings
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for the propagation of bamboo sapling in the nursery
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select a site for the propagation of bamboo saplings in the nursery, ensuring proximity to the
bamboo field with the recommended climatic conditions, adequate exposure to sunlight and access to quality water in the required quantity
- PC2. select an appropriate method for the cultivation of bamboo saplings such as planting culms,
cutting, tillering, layering, etc.
- PC3. select an approved vendor and procure the required planting material and other inputs such
as fertilisers, insecticide/ pesticides/ fungicide, tools and implements, ensuring the quality of
inputs
- PC4. arrange for safe and hygienic transportation and storage of the procured planting material
and other resources
- PC5. treat the planting material appropriately for the common pests and disease before use, to
prevent pest and disease during propagation and cultivation
- PC6. prepare the sunken, raised or level nursery bed as per the requirement, arranging for
efficient drainage of water
- PC7. analyse the crop calender
Plant and maintain bamboo saplings
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. plant seeds, cuttings or culms at the recommended depth and planting density and provide
them immediate care such as water and fertiliser
- PC9. protect the saplings from pests and disease by applying the recommended pesticides,
insecticides or fungicides in the prescribed quantity
- PC10. check the saplings regularly for healthy growth and apply the recommended organic or
inorganic fertilisers in an appropriate quantity
- PC11. water saplings with the recommended quantity at regular intervals, ensuring no damage to
them
- PC12. protect the saplings from strong winds, overexposure to sunlight and fluctuations in
temperature

- PC14. clear any waterlogging from the nursery bed

#### AGR/N6103: Select the bamboo variety and site, and prepare the field
Description
This OS unit is about selecting the bamboo variety and site for cultivation along with preparing the field.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Select the bamboo variety and site
Prepare the field for bamboo cultivation
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Select the bamboo variety and site
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select a high-yielding, pest and disease-resistant variety of bamboo to be grown with cost-
effective cultivation practices
- PC2. select a site with the appropriate temperature, precipitation and relative humidity
recommended for the optimum growth of selected bamboo variety, ensuring the site does not experience waterlogging
- PC3. ensure the site has access to a reliable source of water for irrigation, affordable labour and
relevant markets for marketing the produce
- PC4. ensure the soil at the site is well-drained and rich in organic matters
Prepare the field for bamboo cultivation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. remove all waste materials from the site
- PC6. carry out deep ploughing to remove shrubs, weeds, rocks, tree roots and stumps from the
site
- PC7. carry out soil fumigation using the recommended chemicals to disinfect the soil and water it
thoroughly with the recommended quantity to wash away the disinfectants
- PC8. sample the field soil and coordinate with an approved lab to determine its macro and
micronutrients needs
- PC9. apply the prescribed organic and inorganic fertilisers such as compost, peat moss or Farm
Yard Manure (FYM) to the soil in the dose recommended by the lab to improve its fertility and loosen it
- PC10. apply the recommended treatment such as lime or gypsum as per the lab’s recommendation
to adjust the soil’s pH and salinity levels
- PC11. prepare holes of the recommended depth and dimensions at appropriate spacing for planting
bamboo saplings of the selected variety
- PC12. create drains in the field for the effective drainage of water
- PC13. install an appropriate irrigation or fertigation system in the field
- PC14. erect fences around the field to protect it from stray animals and other external threats

#### AGR/N6104: Transplant and maintain the bamboo saplings
Description
This OS unit is about transplanting and maintaining bamboo saplings in the field.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Transplant the saplings
Maintain the saplings
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Transplant the saplings
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. acclimatise the bamboo saplings before being transplanted in the field
- PC2. select an appropriate time of the day with the recommended temperature for transplanting
saplings to ensure their survival
- PC3. transplant the bamboo saplings in the field using the appropriate tools and implements,
maintaining the recommended planting depth and density
- PC4. water the saplings with the recommended quantity and apply the recommended fertiliser in
an appropriate quantity to provide immediate care after transplantation
Maintain the saplings
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. protect saplings from strong winds and direct sunlight during the early stages of their growth
- PC6. irrigate the saplings according to the stages of their growth, avoiding over-irrigation to
prevent root rot
- PC7. monitor the saplings for signs of pest and disease and apply the recommended treatment in
an prescribed quantity
- PC8. replace the unhealthy or dead saplings with the healthy ones
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the importance of acclimatising and transplanting bamboo saplings at an appropriate temperature to ensure their survival
- KU2. use of the relevant tools and implements for transplanting bamboo saplings
- KU3. recommended planting depth and density to be maintained while transplanting bamboo saplings
- KU4. the importance of watering the bamboo sapling with the recommended quantity and applying the recommended fertilisers in an appropriate quantity soon after transplanting them

#### AGR/N6105: Carry out pruning and weeding in bamboo crop
Description
This OS unit is about carrying out pruning and weeding in the bamboo crop.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Carry out pruning
Carry out weeding
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out pruning
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. select the appropriate tools and implements such as pruner and lopper to prune the bamboo
trees
- PC2. carry out pruning to remove unwanted or dead culms, branches or leaves from bamboo
trees, following the recommended method according to the stages of growth of bamboo trees
- PC3. ensure no damage to the bamboo trees during pruning
- PC4. apply the recommended chemicals to the pruned areas on the bamboo trees
- PC5. remove the pruned tree parts and use them to prepare compost
- PC6. remove unwanted, old and rotting clumps
- PC7. prune the clumps at appropriate intervals and as per the requirement to prevent clump
congestion
- PC8. carry out thinning of clumps using the appropriate implements to provide space for new
shoots to emerge
Carry out weeding
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. select the appropriate tools and implements for weeding out unwanted plants growing in the
bamboo field
- PC10. carry out weeding manually or mechanically, as appropriate, without leaving weed roots in
the field
- PC11. apply mulch or recommended herbicides in the prescribed quantity to prevent the growth of
weeds
- PC12. dispose the weeds eliminated from the field safely

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
