#### HCS/Q7202: Block Print Supervisor
Brief Job Description
A Block Print Supervisor supervises the entire process of block printing, both for sampling as well as
production, as per requirements. Block Print Supervisor should be able to supervise the matching of colour
of the printing paste, preparation of fabric and blocks, block printing and post printing processes. The job
requires the Block Print Supervisor to have the skill to carry out and supervise block printing processes
along with an understanding of printing designs/ layouts to produce suitable outcomes.
Personal Attributes
A Block Print Supervisor should have good eyesight, handeye coordination and vision (including near
vision, distance vision, colour vision, peripheral vision, depth perception and ability to change focus).
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N7204: Carry out and supervise the preparatory process of hand block printing

#### HCS/N7204: Carry out and supervise the preparatory process of hand
block printing
Description
This unit is about supervising the preparatory processes to be undertaken for block printing as per given
requirements.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
understand design specifications carry out making and matching of printing paste carry out preparation of fabric for hand block printing
carry out preparation of other tools and equipments
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand design specifications
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. analyse the given design specification or design artwork or design sample
- PC2. choose the appropriate type/technique of printing to be used
- PC3. identify the colours to be used in the given design
Carry out making and matching of printing paste
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. identify the different ingredients for making the required colours
- PC5. make the printing paste as per requirement
- PC6. ensure that the shade of the colour matches the sample or reference
- PC7. ensure that the proportion of colours and gum are appropriate
Carry out preparation of fabric for hand block printing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. check the fabric and ensure that it meets the requirements provided
- PC9. ensure that the fabric is washed and pre-processed appropriately
- PC10. ensure that the fabric is dyed properly as per requirement
Carry out preparation of other tools and equipments
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. identify and select the appropriate blocks
- PC12. ensure that the blocks are prepared appropriately
- PC13. ensure that the printing paste tray is prepared appropriately
- PC14. estimate the time required to complete the given work
- PC15. minimise and dispose the waste materials in the approved manner
- PC16. carry out operations at a rate which maintains workflow

#### HCS/N7205: Carry out hand block printing as per given specifications
Description
This unit provides Performance Criteria, Knowledge & Understanding and Skills & Abilities required to
carrying out hand block printing as per given specifications.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
carry out hand block printing of samples as per the given specifications
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out hand block printing of samples as per the given specifications
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure that the fabric is pinned appropriately on the printing table
- PC2. mark the layout, if required
- PC3. dip the block in the printing paste on the tray appropriately
- PC4. stamp the block on the marked area/ position/ appropriate position
- PC5. test the colour on waste cloth and judge if the colour is appropriate
- PC6. apply optimum pressure on the handle of the block
- PC7. print the outline block ( often called rekh)
- PC8. print the filling blocks appropriately on the registered outline, if required
- PC9. appropriately place succeeding block impressions as per design layout
- PC10. ensure that the fabric is dried appropriately
- PC11. ensure that the print is even
- PC12. avoid damage to fabric/ product while carrying out printing
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. the organisations policies and procedures
- KU2. guidelines for storage and disposal of waste materials
- KU3. potential hazards associated with the machines and the safety precautions that must be taken
- KU4. protocol to obtain more information on work related tasks
- KU5. contact person in case of queries on procedure or products and for resolving issues related
to defective machines, tools and/or equipment
- KU6. details of the job role and responsibilities
- KU7. work target and review mechanism with your supervisor

#### HCS/N7206: Supervise the printing and post printing processes
Description
This unit is about carrying out supervision of printing and the post production processes to be undertaken
for block printing as per given requirements.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Supervise post printing processes
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure that the block printing has been done as per the specifications provided
- PC2. understand the post printing reqirements of the client
- PC3. identify the post-printing processes to be undertaken for the given lot
- PC4. ensure that the curing is done appropriately (if required)
- PC5. ensure that the steaming is done appropriately (if required)
- PC6. ensure that the fabric is washed appropriately
- PC7. ensure that the fabric is ironed appropriately
- PC8. supervise appropriate folding of the given lot
- PC9. ensure that the packaging is done appropriately
Assess production quality
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. segregate the rejects
- PC11. carry out periodic or random checks during production
- PC12. carry out replenishment of raw materials, if needed
- PC13. ensure that the processes are carried in given timelines
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. safe working practices and organisational procedures
- KU2. the organisation's procedures and guidelines
- KU3. quality systems and processes practiced in the organization
- KU4. equipment operating procedures / manufacturers instructions
- KU5. types of problems with quality and how to report them to appropriate people
- KU6. methods to present any ideas for improvement to supervisor
- KU7. the importance of complying with written instructions
- KU8. limits of personal responsibility
- KU9. reporting procedure in case of faults in own/ other processes
- KU10. different quality aspects
- KU11. basic of different post-printing processes

#### HCS/N7203: Contribute to achieve quality in hand block printing
Description
This unit provides Performance Criteria, Knowledge & Understanding and Skills & Abilities required for
maintaining the quality while carrying out hand block printing and related activities to ensure that the
embroidered product meet specifications and quality.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Contribute to achieving the quality in hand block printing and related operations
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Contribute to achieving the quality in hand block printing and related operations
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify and use materials required based on the specifications provided
- PC2. ensure that the fabric is pre-treated as per requirement
- PC3. ensure that uniform tension is maintained while pinning the fabric on the printing table
- PC4. ensure that the dye paste is mixed appropriately
- PC5. ensure that the correct set of blocks are used for outline and filling prints
- PC6. ensure appropriate registering of blocks
- PC7. ensure proper handling of fabric while printing and post- printing to avoid transfer of colour
to other areas
- PC8. ensure that the shape/ size/ pattern/ design is not distorted while printing
- PC9. ensure that there is no shade difference in the colours used
- PC10. store the block printed fabric/ product appropriately
- PC11. ensure uniformity in raw materials, design and techniquea. within a productb. between pair/
set of products
- PC12. apply allowed tolerances
- PC13. take the necessary action when materials do not conform to quality standards
- PC14. maintain the required productivity and quality levels
- PC15. report to the responsible person when the work flow of other production areas disrupts work

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. safe working practices and organisational procedures
- KU2. the organisation's procedures and guidelines

#### HCS/N9908: Working in a Team
Description
This unit is about working as part of a team in the process.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Commitment and trust

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.
