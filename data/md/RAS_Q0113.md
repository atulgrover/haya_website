RAS/Q0113: Retail Sales for IndiaSkills/ WorldSkills Competition
Brief Job Description
The qualifications equip learners to work confidently in the retail industry, which connects producers with
customers and supports business growth. With shopping now happening both in stores and online, retail
professionals need to serve customers well in both settings. Learners will develop good communication
and customer service skills, understand products and promotions, and learn how to handle different
customer needs. As technology and eco-friendly practices become more important, this qualification also
helps learners create value, build customer trust, and ensure a pleasant shopping experience.
Personal Attributes
Learners should demonstrate a customer-focused attitude, effective communication, professionalism, and
a willingness to learn. They should be courteous, honest, adaptable to changing retail technologies, and
capable of working collaboratively in a team. They should also be responsible, empathetic, and committed
to delivering quality service while promoting sustainable practices and creating a positive shopping
experience for customers.

RAS/N0187: Work organization and management (Retail Sales IndiaSkills/
WorldSkills)
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to organise and manage work effectively in a retail
environment while maintaining high standards of professionalism, customer service, health and safety,
and business continuity.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for work and follow organisational policies and brand standards.
Plan, organise, and prioritise work activities.
Maintain health, safety, security, and workplace hygiene.
Deliver customer-focused service and support business continuity.
Maintain store presentation and stock availability.
Update product knowledge and adopt sustainable retail practices
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Follow health and safety standards, rules, and regulations
PC2. Expect briefing and guidance from supervisors and other personnel to ensure business
continuity
PC3. Organize, plan and prioritize work, individually and within teams and groups
PC4. Maintain constant alertness to the health, safety and security of staff and customers
PC5. Continuously prioritize customer service, helping to avoid or lessen delays and dissatisfaction
PC6. Use appropriate times to maintain stock levels, orderliness and the store's appearance
PC7. Take opportunities to update and extend knowledge of existing and new products and
services
PC8. Brief other personnel when leaving work to ensure business continuity
PC9. Minimise waste and environmental impact from commercial practices
PC10. Engage customers with products’ health and environmental features, and their sustainability
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The legal basis of retailing
KU2. The range and nature of companies' policies and procedures
KU3. The brand and brand values that underpin each retailer
KU4. The shop, store or parent organization's expectation of an employee

RAS/N0188: Market research and business development (Retail Sales
IndiaSkills/ WorldSkills)
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to understand the retail business environment, conduct
basic market research, and support business development activities. It enables learners to understand
customer needs, market trends, branding, marketing strategies, customer relationship management, and
the effective use of digital tools and retail technologies to enhance customer experience, promote
products and services, and contribute to business growth.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the retail business environment and market trends.
Support marketing and business development activities.
Use ICT tools and retail technologies for business operations.
Manage customer information and customer relationship programmes.
Promote products and services to enhance customer experience and business growth.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Represent positively the required brand and culture publicly and internally
PC2. • Actively maintain and update knowledge of
• • products and services
• • sales and promotions,
• • policies regarding payment, exchanges and refunds
• • security practices

PC7. Use ICT to support and enhance efficiency and effectiveness
PC8. Monitor processes, materials and surroundings
PC9. Judge the qualities of products, services and people
PC10. Undertake physical tasks, including handling and moving objects with care
PC11. Keep up with laws and regulations to maintain and apply as appropriate

KU1. The areas and aspects of retailing that the store or service fits within or relates to

RAS/N0189: Communication and interpersonal skills including customer
care (Retail Sales IndiaSkills/ WorldSkills)
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required to communicate effectively with customers and
colleagues, deliver high-quality customer service, understand customer needs, promote products and
services, handle enquiries and complaints, support sales activities, and build positive customer
relationships while ensuring a seamless shopping experience.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communicate effectively with customers and team members.
Identify customer needs and provide appropriate product and service solutions.
Support sales, merchandising, and after-sales services.
Handle customer enquiries, complaints, returns, and exchanges professionally.
Promote customer loyalty programmes and maintain customer relationships.
Use digital tools and retail technologies to support customer service and sales.
Train and guide team members, where applicable.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Greet customers and ascertain each customer's wants or needs, taking personal, cultural,
and other relevant factors into account
PC2. Answer questions regarding the store and its merchandise
PC3. Gather customer or product information to determine a customer's needs
PC4. Identify and assist customers with particular needs
PC5. Recommend, select or locate and obtain merchandise based on customers' needs and wants
PC6. Describe merchandise and explain its uses, operation and care
PC7. Explain and interpret the meaning of specific or technical information considering the
environmental and sustainable impact of the merchandise
PC8. Use hand-held devices to support the sales process
PC9. • Carry out the technical processes required for particular merchandise or services, such as
• • dimensions and measurements
• • codes and symbols
• • alternative international information and signs
PC10. Use enquiries data to support sales and/or call other stores, departments or suppliers to find
desired items
PC11. Help customers to try on or fit merchandise
PC12. Prepare merchandise for purchase or rental
PC13. Bag or package purchases, including gift wrapping
PC14. Undertake or arrange for specialist packing of merchandise

| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
