LSS/Q0801: Buffing Operator
Brief Job Description
A Buffing Operator performs the process of removing the grain on the leather by abrasion. More
particularly he/she removes the flesh side of the leather by mechanical abrasion to produce a suede effect
or to reduce the substance. The process is also known as snuffing, if removal is on the grain side of the
leather. The aim of the buffing/snuffing operation is to improve the uniformity of the grain appearance.
Personal Attributes
Buffing Operator should have good hand-eye coordination, judgment in positioning and buffing, motor
skills and vision (including near vision, distance vision, colour vision, peripheral vision), depth perception
and ability to change focus. Also should have basic estimation and numerical skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. LSS/N0801: Carry out buffing and snuffing operations
- 2. LSS/N0802: Carry out dusting operations
- 3. LSS/N0803: Contribute to achieving product quality in buffing operations
- 4. LSS/N8501: Maintain the work area, tools and machines
- 5. LSS/N8601: Maintain health, safety and security at workplace
- 6. LSS/N8701: Comply with industry, regulatory and organizational requirements

Description
This unit provides Performance Criteria, Knowledge & Understanding and Skills & Abilities for carrying out
buffing and snuffing operations in preparing finished leather.
Scope
This unit/task covers the following:
Preparatory work
Buffing / snuffing operation
Reporting & Documentation
Sorting & Placing
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparatory Work
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. make sure the work area is free from hazards
PC2. obtain and check the data on the work ticket or job card
PC3. calculate the number of components needed for production
PC4. select and sort the materials for the work
PC5. set up the equipment
PC6. make sure that tools are safe, sharp enough and clean to use on the material
PC7. ensure that the materials to be used are free from faults
PC8. work as per legal requirements, standards and regulations, policies, procedures and
professional codes
PC9. ask questions to obtain more information on tasks when the instructions you have are
unclear

PC10. agree and review agreed upon work targets with your supervisor
PC11. set up the emery paper in the buffing machine whenever required
PC12. feed hide/skin into the buffing machine without any folds
PC13. ensure no emery marks are left on the substance
PC14. repeat process as per requirement
PC15. leave work area safe and secure when work is complete
PC16. help the mechanic repairing faults in the machines
PC17. process component parts to the quality standard required
