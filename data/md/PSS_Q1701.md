#### PSS/Q1701: Technical Helper - Thermal Power Generation
Brief Job Description
The individual in this job helps technical supervisor/operator/maintenance team member by performing
supporting activities such as material handling, supplying or holding tools, lifting and carrying objects, oil &
clean machinery and equipment etc.
Personal Attributes
The individual must be physically and mentally able to perform essential functions of the job safely. The
candidate should be able to climb ladders, scaffolds and poles of various heights. The candidate should be
able to read, listen carefully to and understand instructions and warnings.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PSS/N1331: Apply basic health and safety practices for power related work
2. PSS/N1336: Working effectively with others
3. PSS/N1701: Perform basic tasks for repair and maintenance of power generation system and components (Electrical)
4. PSS/N1702: Perform basic tasks for repair and maintenance of equipment and components
(Mechanical)

#### PSS/N1331: Apply basic health and safety practices for power related
work
Description
This unit covers health, safety and security for power related work. This includes procedures and practices
that candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work environment in a power
plant, power station/substation or on the field while working on power equipment. It covers responsibilities
towards self, others, assets and the environment
Scope
This unit/task covers the following:
Follow workplace health and safety practices
Follow fire safety practices
Follow emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures

#### PSS/N1701: Perform  basic tasks for repair and maintenance of power
generation system and components  (Electrical)
Description
This unit is about the competencies required by the Technical Helper to perform basic tasks to assist plant
operator/supervisor/maintenance team member in plant operations, inspection, repair and maintenance of
electrical equipment.
Scope
This unit/ task covers the following:
Handling of tools and equipment
Carry out basic tasks related to repair and maintenance activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Handle tools and equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. identify the tools and equipment required for performing various repair and maintenance
activities
- PC2. obtain the required tools and equipment following organizational procedure
- PC3. ensure the tools and equipment are oiled, cleaned, well maintained, calibrated and ready for
use
Carry out basic tasks related to electrical repair and maintenance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. prepare and maintain the work area as per instructions, procedure or operation specification
- PC5. perform preparatory and post activity tasks when supporting in installation, commissioning,
operations and maintenance activities
- PC6. perform various activities to assist supervisor/operator/maintenance team member in
carrying out Operation and Maintenance (O and M) tasks Activities: Holding, handing over components, measuring, greasing, transportation, loading and unloading, etc.
- PC7. perform cable pulling, cable jointing and clamping
- PC8. measure, cut, and bend wire and conduit using measuring instruments and hand tools
- PC9. tighten loose connections and repair broken insulation using hand tools and as per the
guidance provided
- PC10. drill holes and pull or push wiring through openings
- PC11. perform various activities for preparation and adjustment of tools and equipment for use
Preparation and adjustment activities: Raise, lower, or position tools, equipment and materials, using hoist, hand line, or block and tackle
- PC12. paint a variety of objects related to electrical functions
- PC13. perform various repair and maintenance activities using tools and equipment Repair and
maintenance activities: Hammering, tightening, digging, cleaning measuring, pipe cutting etc.

Description
This unit is about the competencies required for technical helpers (mechanical) involved in performing
basic tasks to assist mechanics or mechanical operators in operations, repair and maintenance of
mechanical equipment
Scope
This unit/ task covers the following:
Perform basic tasks in maintenance and repair activities
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Carry out basic tasks related to mechanical maintenance and repair
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. prepare and maintain the work area as per procedures or operational specification
- PC2. identify the tools and equipment required for operation and maintenance of mechanical
system in thermal power plant
- PC3. obtain the tools and equipment identified following the organizational procedure
- PC4. perform various activities to help the mechanic or engineer Activities: holding, handing over
components, measuring, loading and unloading, etc. while assisting mechanics or mechanical operators in carrying out replacement of components such as radiators, hoses,
fan belts, batteries, spark plugs, ignition cables, and other parts
- PC5. assemble and disassemble valves, pumps, motors and other components to ensure proper
functioning of the overall system
- PC6. perform lubrication of various tools and related activities in accordance with the established
procedures and charts Activities: Check and change oil, oil filters and air filters; fuel and service buses and other vehicles; replace hoses, belts, water pumps, batteries and similar
equipment and parts
- PC7. perform a regular condition check of various components and equipment Components and
equipment: Fan belts, hoses, wiper blades and safety equipment; radiators and batteries
- PC8. prepare and adjust various tools Prepare and adjust: Raise, lower, or position tools,
equipment and materials using hoist or hand line
- PC9. paint various tools related to electrical functions, as instructed
- PC10. detect various signs of fault in the equipment Signs of fault: Vibrations, excessive sound, oil,
air, gas, water and steam leakage or any similar abnormality
- PC11. leave the work area in a safe and tidy condition on completion of the repair and maintenance
activities
