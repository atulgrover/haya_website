#### BEC/ELE/Q0101: Integrated Command   Control Center Operator
Brief Job Description
The Integrated Command Control Center Operator monitors all the activities taking place in the city/premises from a centrally located, technology enabled and responsive location. The Integrated
Command Control Centers are placed at smart cities and sensitive locations like government establishments, power plants, factories and nuclear plants.
Personal Attributes
Physical fitness/strength, Alertness/vigilance/mind on the job, Communication skills, Honesty/integrity
Judgement, Leading and teamwork, Attitude/motivation, Training
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. BEC/ELE/N0101: Operate the Access Control, Monitoring and Surveillance system
2. BEC/ELE/N0102: Monitor the Operation of Access control, Monitoring and Surveillance System
3. BEC/ELE/N0103: Maintaining and sharing of back up of recorded data
4. BEC/ELE/N0104: Perform security tasks in accordance with basic security practice
5. BEC/ELE/N0105: Apply health and safety practice at the workplace
6. BEC/ELE/N9901: Apply principles of professional practices at the workplace
7. BEC/ELE/N9902: Communicate with clients and colleague effectively

#### BEC/ELE/N0101: Operate  the Access Control, Monitoring and Surveillance
system
Description
Operating the Access Control, Monitoring and Surveillance system
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operating the Access Control, Monitoring and Surveillance system
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Operate the Access Control , Monitoring and Surveillance System
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Getting information of utilities such as water, waste management, energy, mobility, the built
environment, education, healthcare and safety
- PC2. Decision making basis the input and to respond to the real time events by consuming data
feeds from different data sources and by processing information out of the data sets
- PC3. Standardizing response protocol at city level through institutionalization of standard
processes for recurring events, issues and exigency scenarios
- PC4. Checking the effectiveness of managing civic functions/utilities through ICCC platform
- PC5. How effectively are we managing safety and security operations /utilities through ICCC
platform
- PC6. Read the operating manuals of the security and surveillance system
- PC7. Read the instructions mentioned in manual of the security and surveillance system
- PC8. Learn how to operate the different type of security and surveillance systems
- PC9. Take all the precautions that need to be taken while operating these systems
- PC10. Read the guidelines to use these systems
- PC11. How to identify the risks and threats as per organizational and legal protocols
- PC12. How to identify unauthorized entry and trespass, aggressive and drunken behaviour,
loitering and littering, eve teasing and molestation, robbery; theft
- PC13. How to respond to hazards and emergencies as per organizational policies and procedures
- PC14. Carry out security duties in commercial and industrial Deployments
- PC15. Carry out tasks relevant to the role while complying with applicable regulatory and legal
Provisions

#### DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)
Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Employability Skills
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. understand the significance of employability skills in meeting the job requirements
Constitutional values – Citizenship
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC2. identify constitutional values, civic rights, duties, personal values and ethics and
environmentally sustainable practices
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC3. explain 21st Century Skills such as Self-Awareness, Behavior Skills, Positive attitude, self-
motivation, problem-solving, creative thinking, time management, social and cultural awareness, emotional awareness, continuous learning mindset etc.
Basic English Skills
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. speak with others using some basic English phrases or sentences
Communication Skills
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
