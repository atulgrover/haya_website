MEP/Q7107: General Duty Guard cum Watchman
Brief Job Description
General Duty Guard cum Watchman is entry level staff for assets, which have limited/ no threat. This
person should be able to maintain a high degree of alertness and vigilance and report any untoward
incident/ observation/ emergency to his/ her superior/ owner. In some cases, he/ she should also be able to
intervene/ prevent an untoward incident/ emergency.
Personal Attributes
Vigilance, Reliability, Physical Fitness, Honesty and Integrity, Communication skills, Problem solving skills,
Law abidance
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MEP/N7237: Perform security tasks in accordance with basic security practices under supervision
- 2. MEP/N7130: Provide watch and ward services to people, property and premises
- 3. MEP/N7131: Access control of premises for people and vehicles
- 4. MEP/N9903: Apply health and safety practices at the workplace
- 5. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

MEP/N7237: Perform security tasks in accordance with basic security
practices under supervision
Description
The General Duty Guard cum Watchman needs to perform some basic security tasks under supervision of
a senior.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform allotted security tasks and duties
Adhere to regulatory and legal mandates
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform allotted security tasks and duties
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. carry out watch and ward in line with procedures and instructions
PC2. respond to risks and threats as per organisational and legal protocols preventing security
related incidents, such as unauthorized entry and trespass, aggressive and drunken
behaviour, and preventing/ reporting threats to property and personnel)
PC3. Take first action against emergency situations and raise alarm/ report to superiors
PC4. provide information and access to premises, records and other resources to the police as per
organisation protocol
PC5. identify rank by recognising the badge of rank of police and military personnel
PC6. identify various arms commonly used by the police and perpetrators
PC7. identify improvised explosive devices and take action as per established protocol
PC8. carry out watch and ward in commercial and industrial deployments
Adhere to regulatory and legal mandates
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. carry out tasks relevant to the role while complying with PSAR Act and Rules thereof.
PC10. work within relevant provisions of the Code on Social Security for the employee, such as EPF
ad MP Act, Employee State Insurance, provisions for wages. Working hours, and leave
entitlements
PC11. Cooperate with law enforcement agencies as per the provisions in the PSAR Act
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. basic security procedures, protocols, and industry standards to effectively carry out assigned
tasks while adhering to established security practices.

MEP/N7130: Provide watch and ward services to people, property and
premises
Description
Offer vigilant watch and ward services, safeguarding individuals, property, and premises by maintaining a
continuous and attentive presence, ensuring their protection and safety.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Security industry in India - Security Patroller, Premises Monitor, Entry/Exit Surveillance Officer,
Property Safety Guard, Facility Protection Personnel
Elements and Performance Criteria
Provide watch and ward duties for people and property
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify layout of the premises, assets, movable or immovable property, entry and exit
points, CCTV coverage and boundary walls
PC2. classify categories of people in the premises to be watched such as staff, residents; workers;
visitors; officials; vendors; service providers; general public)
PC3. Provide watch and ward duties at the given post as per instructions
PC4. Maintain surveillance and report threats to people and property, and their safety to the
supervisor
PC5. Search of vehicles for materials, and personnel travelling with a view to authorization and
security
PC6. Patrol/ search designated premises/ route effectively as per instructions
PC7. report risks, threats and hazards observed during the search/ patrolling
PC8. use basic security equipment for search, screening, communication and fire safety
PC9. maintain basic security registers and records
PC10. Carry out proper handing/ taking over at shift change and report the relevant points to the
supervisor.
PC11. maintain personal safety at all times when at work
PC12. follow incident reporting formats and protocols under the guidance of the supervisor
Ensure gender mainstreaming and inclusivity
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. handle people with respect and empathy irrespective of the gender/ caste/ age and use non-
gendered statements in communication
PC14. ensure smooth movement of persons with disability and senior citizens
PC15. treat people without discrimination irrespective of the gender, culture, religion, language and
colour differences
Material and energy conservation practices

MEP/N7131: Access control of premises for people and vehicles
Description
Enforce strict control measures regulating entry and exit of individuals and vehicles into premises,
ensuring authorized access and heightened security.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Security industry in India - Security Patroller, Premises Monitor, Entry/Exit Surveillance Officer,
Property Safety Guard, Facility Protection Personnel
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage entry and exit from premises
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. carry out access control procedures in the premises with or without use of equipment as per
organisational procedures
PC2. establish identity, purpose and authorization of different categories of people/ vehicles/
material seeking to enter or exit from the premises prior to access or egress
PC3. prevent entry and exit of people/vehicles/ material without valid authorization and report
unauthorized access/ exit to the supervisor/ department concerned
PC4. inform concerned department/ supervisor on arrival of relevant consignments
PC5. Check the vehicles for their authorization, purpose and identity during their access and exit
from the premises under watch
PC6. Report to supervisor in case of any irregularity observed during passage of vehicle in the
premises
PC7. assist seniors in maintaining the registers at the duty post
Use security equipment to control access
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. Operate basic security equipment during access/ exit such as metal detector, torch, and
attendance recorders
PC9. Check and ensure housekeeping of the place of duty, taking care to ensure that all safety
precautions are in place
PC10. carry out maintenance of security equipment as instructed
Collect mail and courier after office hours as per organizational procedure
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. receive mail and couriers after office hours as per organisational procedures
PC12. deliver letters and packages to the designated person as per organisational procedures
PC13. identify and report suspicious packages or mail, and stack securely under supervision
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
