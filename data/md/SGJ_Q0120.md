#### SGJ/Q0120: Green Hydrogen  Plant Technician
Brief Job Description
Green Hydrogen Plant Technician shall perform testing, installation and facility integration of electrolyser,
repairs, troubleshooting, upkeep and maintenance of electrical control systems, protection systems, and
other auxiliary equipment and associated tools in Green hydrogen generation facilities. The job holder will
be responsible for the continuous supply of renewable power for its integration with the electrolysers and
the other process equipment for generating Green Hydrogen. This role works closely with the power supply
project, testing, plant engineering, process operation, control & operation across the green hydrogen
generation plant.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to be sincere, diligent, hardworking and attentive to effectively perform the
work timely in a quality manner while following all the safety practices at Green Hydrogen Manufacturing
facility.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### SGJ/N0150: Introduce green hydrogen for clean energy transition
Description
This unit outlines the basics of Green Hydrogen technology and the evolving hydrogen ecosystem as a key
pathway for energy transition
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Fundamental concepts of Green Hydrogen
Skills Required for the Green Hydrogen Economy
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Fundamental concepts of Green Hydrogen
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the necessity of green hydrogen in sustainable energy transition
- PC2. Discuss the properties and characteristics of Hydrogen
- PC3. Describe basic concepts of Hydrogen as an energy carrier
- PC4. Demonstrate with chart colour code nomenclature of Hydrogen
- PC5. Perform an activity for matching the process and source of production as applicable for
different colour codes of hydrogen
- PC6. Discuss the value chain for Green Hydrogen
- PC7. Discuss briefly various technology options for production of green hydrogen and outline
benefits and drawbacks of existing methods of Hydrogen production
- PC8. Briefly discuss the end use applications of Green hydrogen in industry, transport and power
production.
- PC9. Draw a flow diagram of green hydrogen production, conversion and end uses across the
energy system
- PC10. Discuss key aspects and challenges related to production, storage, transportation,
distribution of Green Hydrogen.
- PC11. Discuss various renewable energy sources for large scale production of green hydrogen
through electrolysis of water
Skills Required for the Green Hydrogen Economy
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. Discuss Green hydrogen economy in Indian context along with the key aspects of Green
Hydrogen Policy and Mission of Government of India
- PC13. Show how green hydrogen economy is critical for energy transition and the key policy
measures announced by the Government of India
- PC14. Illustrate key announcements made by the leading private and public sector companies
across the value chain of Green hydrogen and outline how those would create new opportunities for technicians.
- PC15. Discuss the role and responsibilities of the job holder

#### SGJ/N4012: Identify components of Green Hydrogen  Plant and interpret
its layout
Description
This unit outlines aspects on key components of a Green Hydrogen generation system
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Key Green Hydrogen system components
Plant Layout
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Key Green Hydrogen system components
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify all key components of the Green Hydrogen plant including electrical, mechanical and
civil components
- PC2. Illustrate the schematic of Green hydrogen production plant
- PC3. Discuss functions of the key components including of electrolyzer stacks, renewable power
supply system, , feed water supply and demineralisation system, gas separator, transformer and rectifier, gas compression unit, etc
- PC4. Illustrate key components of the plant and outline their functions through plant schematic
- PC5. Discuss fundamental principles of main components on which they operate (e.g. electrolyzer
stack, gas collector, power source, etc.)
- PC6. Discuss briefly on types of electrolysers
- PC7. Illustrate possible combination of various renewable power sources for generating green
hydrogen
Plant Layout
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Explain overall layout of the plant
- PC9. Illustrate how to interpret the Plant Layout including various equipment and material used in
a Green hydrogen production facility.
- PC10. Discuss Sparking items e.g. MCBs, switches to be installed isolated from the hydrogen
production area
- PC11. Explain how to read and interpret electrical and other applicable codes, standards and
protocols relevant to the plant.
- PC12. Explain key material and safety codes, Technology protocols and standards as applicable in
Green hydrogen industry
- PC13. Demonstrate how to interpret signs, notices and/or cautions at project site.
- PC14. Identify processes where energy/electricity and material utilization can be optimized

components of Green Hydrogen generation system

Flexible system operation

Flexible System Operation
