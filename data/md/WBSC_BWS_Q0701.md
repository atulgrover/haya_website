WBSC/BWS/Q0701: Assistant Beautician
Brief Job Description
Assistant Beauticians provide a wide range of services that include the care of the hair, skin and nails. A
beautician primary focus is on improving their clients outward appearance and helping them to feel better
about themselves in general.
Personal Attributes
Assistant beauticians should be friendly, approachable and professional at all times to help clients feel
comfortable and confident. They need good communication and listening skills to understand client needs
and provide appropriate treatments. Assistant beauticians should be reliable, punctual and well-organised
with a positive attitude and willingness to learn new skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. WBSC/BWS/N0701: Prepare work area and assist in client consultation.
- 2. WBSC/BWS/N0702: Perform basic manicure and pedicure services.
- 3. WBSC/BWS/N0703: Carry out temporary removal of Superfluous hair, Threading, Tweezing and
Bleaching.
- 4. WBSC/BWS/N0704: Carry out facial services using different facial packs.
- 5. WBSC/BWS/N0705: Demonstrate day, evening, party, basic makeup and explain effects of light on
makeup.
- 6. WBSC/BWS/N0706: Prepare mehndi paste, make cones, and create simple Indian and Arabic
designs with care and workplace cleanliness.
- 7. WBSC/BWS/N0707: Plan to set up a small business related to beauty related services.
- 8. WBSC/BWS/N0708: Work in real job situation with emphasis on basic safety and hazards (Assistant
Beautician).
- 9. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Haircutting techniques
Create basic haircuts using special cutting techniques.

WBSC/BWS/N0702: Perform basic manicure and pedicure services.
Description
Perform basic manicure and pedicure services.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparation of the client & trolley for service.
Analysis of the hand & feet, filling the record card.
Basic hand tools (Nail file, cuticle cleaner and buffer, Nail cutter, nail cleaner) and their use.
Positioning of the client to ensure easy process and minimal injury.
Cleaning and drying of the hand/ feet of client as a part of preparation.
Application of cuticle softener product.
Removal of any excessive hard skin.
Usage of massage technique according to clients need.
Application of suitable base coat /nail polish & topcoat relevant to the clients need.
Cleaning of the treated area and use suitable soothing product.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Prepare the client & trolley for service.
PC2. Analyze the hand & feet, fill the record card.
PC3. Identify basic hand tools (Nail file, cuticle cleaner and buffer, Nail cutter, nail cleaner) and
their use.
PC4. Adjust the positioning of the client to ensure easy process and minimal injury.
PC5. Clean and dry the hand/ feet of client as a part of preparation.
PC6. Apply cuticle softener product during the cuticle cutting process and ensure no damage to the
cuticle and nail plate.
PC7. Remove any excessive hard skin using foot scrape among without discomfort to the client.
PC8. Use massage technique according to clients need. Apply suitable base coat /nail polish &
topcoat relevant to the clients need.
PC9. Clean the treated area and use suitable soothing product.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The importance of hand and nail care.
KU2. The structure, function and characteristics of nail and the process of nail growth.
KU3. Basic symptoms of different nail diseases.

Description
Carry out temporary removal of Superfluous hair, Threading, Tweezing and Bleaching.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Epilation and depilation services.
Hair structure & hair.
The raw materials required with correct specification.
The elements of a client consultation for hair removal.
The conditions that contraindicate hair removal.
The process of wax (Hot wax, cold wax, strips etc.)

The process of cream bleach.

PC7. Demonstrate the process Threading, Tweezing.
PC8. Demonstrate the process of cream bleach.
PC9. Carryout disposing of all the waste safety according to the salon’ standards.
