THC/Q0124: Hotel Reception (WorldSkills)
Brief Job Description
The role of Hotel Receptionist is key to every hotel establishment. At the hotel’s reception area guests
receive their first impression of the hotel and therefore of their probable hotel experience. The quality,
courtesy and promptness of service can make a great difference, positively or negatively, to the guest’s
relationship with the hotel and their satisfaction during their stay. This in turn affects the hotel’s reputation
and repeat business. The Hotel Receptionist works mainly in the hotel’s front office. Hotel receptionists
need to use a wide range of skills continuously. These may include knowledge of local and general tourism
information, good verbal and written English, computer literacy, good manners and conduct and grooming,
excellent communication and social skills, problem solving, competence with figures and cash handling,
and the application of procedures for reservations, reception, guest services and check out.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be polite, well-groomed, committed, and service-oriented with great
interpersonal skills and attention to details. The person should be able to work comfortably for long
duration.

THC/N0144: Maintain work organization and manage self (Hotel
Reception)
Description
This NOS emphasizes upon efficiently organizing and managing daily tasks at the hotel reception, ensuring
seamless guest interactions and maintaining a structured work environment
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Communicating effectively with guests and colleagues
Dealing effectively with guest queries and unforeseen situations
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Deal effectively with guest related unexpected situations
PC2. Maintain health and safety for guests, colleagues, and visitors
PC3. Identify oneself with one’s organization and its goals
PC4. Hand over to the next shift ensuring that all information is passed on
PC5. Prioritize work effectively
PC6. Ensure that the reception area is well presented in terms of appearance, signage, cleanliness
PC7. Apply rules and guidance relating to guests’ security
PC8. Maintain guests’ privacy
PC9. Apply all legislation relating to the sale and service of goods and services within hotel
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The services and facilities offered by hotels and their tariffs
KU2. Legal requirements of hotels regarding check-in, health and safety, discrimination, guests
property, guests behaviour, service of alcohol, sale of goods and services, data protection
KU3. The rules in the event of an evacuation of hotels and the systems of first point of call for
emergency services
KU4. The facilities available for guests with disabilities
KU5. The structure, role and requirements of front office operations within the hotel industry
KU6. Reporting structures, shift patterns and types of staff
KU7. The management of the guest cycle
KU8. The types of customer likely to use the hotel.
Generic Skills (GS)

THC/N0145: Communicate with customers and show interpersonal skills
Description
This NOS describes the importance and need to exhibit exceptional interpersonal skills and effective
communication with hotel guests, providing a positive and welcoming experience at the reception
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Implementing personalized and attentive communication strategies to cater to diverse guest needs
Implementing time-management strategies for efficient handling of guest
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Welcome, attend to and communicate with all guests depending on guest type and/or
situation, including those with disabilities and communication difficulties
PC2. Provide accurate and comprehensive information about hotels’ services and facilities
PC3. Maintain good professional relations and communications with colleagues and suppliers
PC4. Receive and deliver information from/to colleagues and suppliers
PC5. Communicate effectively and in a timely manner with other departments within hotels
PC6. Maintain good professional relations and communications with guests
PC7. Receive and deliver information from/to guests
PC8. Make and receive telephone calls and e-mails
PC9. Attend guests during stay and ensure their satisfaction
PC10. Maintain excellent personal presentation, abiding by the rules of the establishment regarding
uniform and personal appearance
PC11. Be aware of and react appropriately to non-verbal communications such as body language
and gestures
PC12. Apply listening techniques effectively
PC13. Engage in appropriate and professional conversation
PC14. Demonstrate self-confidence in dialogue
PC15. Demonstrate tact and diplomacy
PC16. Effectively resolve conflicts
PC17. Respond to special request
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. The importance of effective communications with guests
KU2. Procedures and guidance for communicating with guests

THC/N0146: Take hotel reservation
Description
This NOS describes the epitome of luxury and comfort with our hotel reservation service and allocates
rooms according to hotel policies and procedures
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Providing excellent customer service
Expanding to cover a diverse range of hotels and accommodations
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Take individual reservations in person, over the telephone, by e-mail, booking platforms or
letters
PC2. Take group reservations by telephone, e-mail, booking platforms or letters
PC3. Take reservations via approved agents or brokers and record appropriately
PC4. Accept walk-in bookings based on room availability, agreeing tariff and payment according to
hotels’ policies
PC5. Request and take deposits according to hotels’ policies
PC6. Allocate rooms according to hotels’ policies and procedures
PC7. Record requests for additional services or sales to ensure delivery and appropriate charging
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Room allocation procedures including density charts, conventional charts, software solutions
KU2. The place of central reservations within a hotel chain
KU3. Software systems used to record hotel reservations
KU4. The types and range of tariffs
KU5. The status of provisional, confirmed, and guaranteed bookings
KU6. Policies regarding deposits and procedures for taking a deposit at the point of booking
KU7. Release times for rooms
KU8. Hotels policies regarding overbooking
KU9. All documentation used within hotels relating to reservations
KU10. Hotels policies regarding yield management
KU11. Policies relating to the use of agents or brokers
Generic Skills (GS)

THC/N0147: Handle guest/group check-in
Description
This NOS emphasizes managing and facilitating guest and group check-ins with precision and warmth,
ensuring a seamless arrival experience that sets the stage for a delightful stay
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Follow appropriate check-in procedures for all types of guests
Handle and process group check-ins
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Check in guests according to hotels’ policies and procedures
PC2. Maintain all necessary documentation and information relating to guests
PC3. Issue room keys to guests
PC4. Provide directions to allocated rooms and information about hotel services and facilities
PC5. Request and take instructions for additional services and sales
PC6. Ensure payment to prepare for smooth check-outs
PC7. Advise on transferring guests luggage to rooms and organise transfers according to hotels’
policies
PC8. Prepare and deal efficiently with arrivals of groups
PC9. Handle efficiently overbooking and book-out situations
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Legal requirements regarding documentation and records for national and international hotel
guests
KU2. Electronic and manual systems for checking in guests
KU3. Procedures and protocols for issuing keys
KU4. Different types of traditional and electronic room keys
KU5. Registration requirements
KU6. The function and updating of guests histories
KU7. Usual guest requests such as early morning calls, newspapers, breakfast, room service
KU8. Automated check-in procedures
KU9. Procedures for dealing with guests luggage, belongings, and car parking
KU10. Group check-in procedures
KU11. Overbooking policies

THC/N0148: Handle administration and back-office procedures
Description
This unit ensures all the important back office and administrative tasks and paperwork are carried out
smoothly
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Keeping everything organized, managing paperwork, and making sure the hotel runs smoothly
Maintain credit systems in line
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Use computers and usual office software
PC2. File documents and data physically and electronically
PC3. Handle and account for cash and cash equivalents
PC4. Manage currency exchange as required
PC5. Conduct general office and administrative procedures such as filing, word processing,
databases, photocopying, and maintenance of records
PC6. Calculate currency exchanges and transactions with appropriate commission according to
hotels’ policies
PC7. Post charges to guests’ accounts accurately
