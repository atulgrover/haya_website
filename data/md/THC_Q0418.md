#### THC/Q0418: Bakery (WorldSkills)
Brief Job Description
The baker is a highly skilled professional, with a high level of knowledge about food and nutrition, who
produces a wide range of bread and pastry items.The baker produces all varieties of fresh and tasty
bakery products, made of any kind of grains. Different types of fermentation and other processes will be
used to turn the raw material into sweet or savory products. For example, wheat bread, rye bread, artisan
bread, enriched breads, laminated products, and ingredients to flavor their bakery products. Bakers also
produces elaborate displays of decorative breads using creative skills and knowledge.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have good eye sight, patience, attention to details, good communication
skills, and physical strength to work for long working hours.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. THC/N0440: Maintain work organization and communicate effectively (Bakery)
2. THC/N0442: Maintain Food hygiene and workplace health, safety, and environment (Bakery)
3. THC/N0445: Prepare enriched breads
4. THC/N0450: Prepare wheat Breads
5. THC/N0451: Prepare laminated Doughs/Pastries
6. THC/N0452: Prepare specialty breads
7. THC/N0453: Prepare decorative breads

doughs, ensuring consistent quality, appearance, and flavor through proper production techniques, frying,
baking, or boiling processes, effective use of flavorings, and meticulous finishing and display for sale
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Expertly prepare a diverse range of bakery products
Employ appropriate cooking methods
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Produce a range of sweet or enriched doughs
- PC2. Produce a range of bakery products using sweet or enriched dough
- PC3. Produce products in bulk to a consistent size, shape, appearance, flavor, and standard

- PC5. Use appropriate flavorings effectively
- PC6. Finish or decorate products as appropriate
- PC7. Display products for sale
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. The factors that impact on the performance of yeast
- KU2. The use of yeast in its various forms
- KU3. The range of sweet and enriched dough products
- KU4. How to produce and enriched dough enhancing with such ingredients as sugar, eggs, butter, and milk
- KU5. The impact of enriching ingredients on the proving process
- KU6. How sweet or enriched dough products should be displayed for sale
- KU7. The different techniques for shaping like braiding, moulding in different shapes, baking or
frying these products
- KU8. How sweet or enriched dough products should be store

Description
This NOS emphasis on creating and presenting a variety of high-quality bakery products, while prioritizing
safe and hygienic storage and appealing display for sale.

Ensure consistent quality, size, and produce a range of bulk products

- KU5. The range and uses of doughs and pastes used to produce bakery products
- KU6. The range of raw materials and how can they be worked into a filling
- KU7. Why fillings which are baked need to be stable at high temperature
- KU8. The effect of using seasonal fruits and vegetables for fillings
- KU9. The importance of appearance, texture, and taste
