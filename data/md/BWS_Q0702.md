#### BWS/Q0702: Assistant Tattoo Artist
Brief Job Description
An Assistant Tattoo Artist applies a permanent design directly to a client's body, for decorative or
ornamental design purposes. The Artist needs to be knowledgeable on health safety and hygiene, have
good hand-eye coordination and skills in a variety of art styles.
Personal Attributes
This job requires an individual with experience in body art to apply a permanent design efficiently and
effectively in a safe and hygienic working environment. The individual must exhibit a pleasant personality,
excellent interpersonal and communication skills and be sensitive when dealing with clients. The individual
must exhibit a neat personal appearance at all times have good hand-eye coordination. The individual
must be pleasant and tactful when dealing with clients and have a genuine interest in people.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. BWS/N0702: Apply permanent markings and images
2. BWS/N9001: Prepare and maintain work area
3. BWS/N9002: Maintain health and safety at the workplace

#### BWS/N0702: Apply permanent  markings and images
Description
This OS unit is about the application of a permanent design directly to a client's body, injecting ink under a
layer of skin.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Apply permanent markings and images
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Apply permanent markings and images
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. adhere to the health and safety standards laid out by the manufacturer and salon
- PC2. consult the client to identify any condition that causes issues when a tattoo is healing, such as
diabetes, anemia, or an immune deficiency disease
- PC3. discuss options and details related to the tattoo such as placement, size, colour and estimated
price of tattoo and clarify the client's understanding and expectation prior to commencement of
design application
- PC4. set up the tattoo machine and apparatus
- PC5. produce a traced outline for the tattoo based on the shape of the body part receiving the tattoo
as to ensure the finalized tattoo will not sway from the original vision of the client
- PC6. sanitize the hands and sterilize/ sanitize all equipment
- PC7. clean the clients skin and carry out a skin sensitivity test, if required
- PC8. stretch the skin of the client to provide a smooth clean line
- PC9. skillfully use electric needle and indelible ink to draw tattoo on specified part of clients body
- PC10. shade the skin starting from the darkest to the lighter zones
- PC11. position self and client throughout procedure to ensure privacy, comfort and wellbeing
- PC12. perform and adapt the procedure using materials, equipment and techniques correctly and safely to meet the needs of the client
- PC13. complete the procedure to the satisfaction of the client in a stipulated time as per clients
specific requirements
- PC14. apply an antiseptic cream and a bandage tattoo upon completion and provide client with tattoo
after-care knowledge
- PC15. record the procedure accurately and store information securely in line with the salons policies
- PC16. provide specific after-procedure, homecare advice and recommendations for product use and further treatments to the client

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

#### BWS/N9001: Prepare and maintain work area
Description
Prepare the equipment's/ tools/ products/machinery (if required any) and work area ahead of service/
session delivery to ensure the efficiently and effectiveness of conducting treatments/ sessions considering
the standards of operation of the organization/ salon/ beauty clinic.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
1. Prepare and maintain work area
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare and maintain work area
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. ensure that ambient conditions are suitable for the client and the service procedures to be
carried out in a hygienic, safe and disinfected environment such as using air purifiers to reduce dust, dander, smoke, allergens & odour resulting in a healthier, fresher & cleaner

Scope

manufacturers’ instructions
