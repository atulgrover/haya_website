#### SGJ/Q5201: Environmental Impact Assessor
Brief Job Description
The Environmental Impact Assessor evaluates the environmental effects of proposed projects to ensure
regulatory compliance. Key responsibilities include applying EIA and audit concepts, conducting project
screening and public consultations, monitoring baseline environments, and analyzing samples. The role
also involves using GIS tools and predictive models, facilitating stakeholder participation, and developing
Environmental Management Plans for sustainable project implementation.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to have analytical thinking, ethical responsibility, communication, problem-
solving, and project management skills, along with adaptability, teamwork, and an eye for detail to assess
environmental impacts effectively and ensure sustainable project outcomes.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)

#### SGJ/N6801: Introduce Environmental Acts, Rules, Regulations, and
Standards and concept of Environmental Impact Assessment  and Audit
Description
This NOS provides foundational knowledge on environmental concepts, key environmental laws and regulations, and the importance of tools like Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) and environmental
audits. It aims to develop awareness and understanding of sustainable practices and regulatory compliance in various sectors.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The Scope Covers basic environmental concepts, key environmental laws and standards, and the fundamentals of Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) and audits to promote regulatory
compliance and sustainable practices.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Environment.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain the components of the environment.
- PC2. Describe details about the atmosphere, hydrosphere, lithosphere, and biosphere.
- PC3. Highlight the necessity of resource management for sustainable development.
- PC4. Discuss types of pollution, including atmospheric pollution, water pollution, and land
pollution, along with their possible causes.
- PC5. Emphasize the quality of a healthy environment.
Introduction to various Environmental Acts & Rules, Regulations and Standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. Impart knowledge on important acts linked with EIA.
- PC7. Explain the roles of various authorities implementing the acts and rules.
- PC8. Discuss the latest amendments in rules.
- PC9. Highlight the connection of EIA to various acts.
Concept of Environment Impact Assessment, Environmental Audit, Environmental Impacts, Environmental
Impact Statement and role of EIA in society
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Understand the concept of Environment Impact Assessment.
- PC11. Gain a basic idea about Environmental Audit and various environmental impacts caused
by different activities.
- PC12. Explore the concept of Environmental Impact Statement.
- PC13. Discuss the connection of EIA to society.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

Categorization of Projects, and Environmental Clearance Procedures
Description
This NOS introduces the essential processes involved in Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA), including
project screening, scoping, public consultation, and environmental clearance. It also covers the basic
structure of EIA reports for various projects and familiarizes learners with key environmental attributes
assessed during the EIA process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The Scope Covers the following:
EIA process including screening, scoping, public consultation, project categorization, and clearance
procedures, along with the structure of EIA reports and key environmental attributes for impact
assessment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Concept of Screening, Scoping, Public consultation, categorization of projects & procedure for environmental clearance
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Gain a detailed understanding of project screening.
- PC2. Learn the categorization of projects with respect to the EIA Notification.
- PC3. Discuss the procedure and concept of public consultation and its application.
- PC4. Illustrate the clearance procedures and the role of different authorities.
Basic parts of EIA for different projects
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Explain the procedure of project categorization.
- PC6. Anticipate the environmental impacts.
- PC7. Identify project alternatives.
- PC8. Establish a monitoring program.
- PC9. Perform an analysis of cost-benefit.
- PC10. Prepare an Environment Management Plan.
- PC11. Apply methods of survey.
- PC12. Conduct survey practice at the project site.
- PC13. Implement monitoring practices at the site.
Environmental Attributes

- PC14. Identify the different types of attributes required for analysis.

#### SGJ/N6805: Conduct Field Surveys and Apply Prediction Methods and
Models for Environmental Impact
Description
This unit defines the skills and knowledge required to conduct field surveys and apply prediction methods
to assess potential environmental impacts of projects. It includes collecting baseline data, identifying
sources of pollution, using appropriate tools and models to predict impacts on air, water, soil, and
biodiversity, and interpreting the results to support environmental planning and mitigation strategies.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the procedures and techniques for conducting field surveys and applying prediction
models to assess environmental impacts. It includes collecting and analyzing environmental data,
identifying potential sources of impact, using modeling tools to forecast changes, and interpreting
results to support mitigation planning and informed decision-making.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Field visit to different project sites and survey.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Conduct survey work to gather essential data.
- PC2. Understand the nature of project activities and their implications.
- PC3. Anticipate the causes and sources of pollution associated with these activities.
Prediction Methods
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Gain practical experience in applying prediction methods, including the use of wind rose
diagrams.

| Sub-sector | Sub-sector is derived from a further breakdown based on the characteristics and interests of its components. |
| Occupation | Occupation is a set of job roles, which perform similar/ related set of functions in an industry. |
| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. Occupational Standards are applicable both in the Indian and global contexts. |
| Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |
| Qualifications Pack (QP) | QP comprises the set of OS, together with the educational, training and other criteria required to perform a job role. A QP is assigned a unique qualifications pack code. |
| Unit Code | Unit code is a unique identifier for an Occupational Standard, which is denoted by an ‘N’ |
| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
