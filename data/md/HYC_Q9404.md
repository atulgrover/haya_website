HYC/Q9404: Law Officer (Energy Sector)
Brief Job Description
A law Officer professional, often a Chief Legal Officer or General Counsel, oversees legal affairs within an
organization. Responsibilities include providing strategic legal advice, managing regulatory compliance,
negotiating contracts, and representing the organization in legal matters. This role requires strong
leadership, exceptional legal acumen, and the ability to align legal strategies with overall business
objectives.
Personal Attributes
The individual should have good sense of responsibility, a good organizer and time keeper. The person
should have the ability to work in a team and maintain excellent interpersonal relationships.

Writing
Description
This unit is about introducing the legal profession, legal research and writing.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Concepts of Law Legislations Statutes Kinds Laws and Remedies Core Legal Doctrines
Skills of Drafting Concept analysis and Legal Services such as Drafting and vetting
Role of Legal Team and Types of Legal Service(s) their Professional Legal Ethics & Conduct

Elements and Performance Criteria
Concepts of Law; Legislations; Statutes; Kinds Law/s and Remedies Core Legal Doctrines

HYC/N9410: Contracts and Business Law
Description
This unit is about basics of Contract law Components, Formation, Elements, and termination of contracts,
skills of Contractual Drafting, Review and Negotiation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Basics of Contract law Components, Formation, Elements, and termination of contracts.
Skills of Contractual Drafting, Review and Negotiation
Generic and Specific Contracts such as Indemnity Bailment Guarantee Pledge, Agency.
Difference between Contract, Agreement, MoU, MoA
Elements and Performance Criteria
Basics of Contract law; Components, Formation, Elements, and termination of contracts
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Know about key elements of consent; consideration; proposal; acceptance and
communication; valid and legal purpose which result in making legally valid contracts
PC2. Have awareness about the ways and mode of terminating a contract by express notice and
conduct
PC3. Know about the key elements of the Valid, Void and Voidable Contracts and the relevant
remedies available related to the same.
PC4. Ability to draft and review the contracts
Skills of Contractual Drafting, Review and Negotiation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Ability to draft contractual clauses with detailed responsibilities of both parties
PC6. Understanding about the key clauses of jurisdiction, indemnity; confidentiality and
severability.
PC7. Have understanding about the business terms and commercial concerns forming basis of the
multi-party negotiation/s
PC8. Know about key review parameters and conducting multiple party discussion for contract
finalization.
Generic and Specific Contracts such as Indemnity; Bailment; Guarantee; Pledge, Agency
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. Clearly understanding about various parties involved in specific contracts of Indemnity
Holder- Indemnifier; Guarantor- guarantee holder; Pledgor- pledgee; Principal and Agent.
PC10. Key differences between the two-party contract of Indemnity and tripartite contract of
Guarantee
PC11. Role of Bailor and Bailee
PC12. Responsibilities of Agent under Contracts

HYC/N9412: Administrative and Regulatory Law
Description
This unit is about overview of administrative and Regulatory Law
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Overview of Administrative Law
Roles and functions of Regulatory agencies Administrative Authorities Quasi- Judicial Bodies.
Principles of Natural Justice
Knowing various administrative procedures.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Overview of Administrative Law
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Know the difference between contractual and administrative function/s
PC2. Understand the powers and limitations of Competent Authority as per law and policy
PC3. Have conceptual understanding of reasoned administrative orders
PC4. Know about the appellate procedure in a given administrative set up.
Roles and functions of Regulatory agencies; Administrative Authorities; Quasi- Judicial Bodies.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. Understand the key roles, powers of a regulator and quasi- judicial authorities
PC6. Know the difference between an administrative and quasi- judicial exercise of power/
function
PC7. Know the difference between an judicial and quasi- judicial exercise of power/ function
Principles of Natural Justice
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. In-depth knowledge about the rule of fair play; opportunity of hearing and equal opportunity
PC9. Importance of issuance of Show Cause Notice(s)
PC10. Clear understanding about the doctrine and concept of proportionality
PC11. Conceptual knowledge about Double Jeopardy and ‘Being an interested party’
