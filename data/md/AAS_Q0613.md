AAS/Q0613: Airport Terminal Operations Manager
Brief Job Description
The Airport Terminal Operations Manager is responsible for overall management of Airport Operational
functions and to provide Safety and Security oversight. S/he is also responsible for managing
contingencies and enhance customer experience through process improvements. In the course of
performance of these functions, they are also expected to manage people and stakeholders both internal
and external.
Personal Attributes
The Airport Terminal Operations Manager should possess advanced communication skills, both verbal and
written, along with a core focus on customer orientation. S/he should possess good interpersonal skills and
should be able to manage diversity in the team, resolve conflicts as well make sound decisions under
pressure situations. S/he should be a good listener and display a penchant for innovation and technology
solutions. She/he should possess the leadership skills to develop people and build succession pipelines
through effective delegation of duties.

AAS/N0630: Manage operational performance and provide safety and
security oversight
Description
This unit describes the actions to be performed regarding the management of operations and provide
oversight of the security & safety functions by organizational and regulatory procedures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Manage operational performance
Provide safety and security oversight
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage operational performance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Manage & review operational performance that would include passenger facilitation,
enforcement of airside standards & practices daily
PC2. Manage daily operations, such as terminal management, landside/parking management
including ground transportation, parking bay allocation, baggage handling system support,
line management, airline support in terminal operations, flight inspection services
coordination, gate assignments and common use resource allocation
PC3. Manage, direct, decision making and assist with around the clock response oversight of the
24-hour integrated operations centre as related to terminal and landside operations
PC4. Manage the flow of VIP, CIP & maintain protocol as established
PC5. Monitor the update of airport flight information systems including arrival carousel allocation
PC6. Participate in the bid process along with the procurement team in providing inputs where the
process is being established for terminal & airside requirements
PC7. Coordinate closely with the engineering teams when there is a proposed construction activity
to ensure operational stability is maintained at all time
PC8. Monitor the administration of public announcements
PC9. Monitoring the resolution of fault logging and reporting by maintenance teams
PC10. Control access of vendors, contractors through the issuance of permits & airport passes
Provide safety & security oversight
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. Participate, develop, and implement the Safety Management System (SMS) for the safe
operations on the airport’s movement area on the airfield
PC12. Develop, implement & review the airfield driver’s training and mandatory training
programmes
PC13. Participate in creation, update & implementation of security protocols in collaboration with
security bodies to ensure compliance with international and national regulations
PC14. Develop & implement airport safety regulations in coordination with safety specialists

AAS/N0631: Manage contingencies and emergencies, Enhance customer
experience through process improvements
Description
This unit describes the actions to be performed to manage contingencies and emergencies. It also
describes the steps to be taken to enhance customer experience by effecting process improvements under
organizational and regulatory requirements/procedures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Contingency Management
Enhance customer experience through process improvements
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage contingencies & emergencies
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Act as the first point of contact for any airport emergencies, which may require activation of
the airport’s emergency or business continuity plans
PC2. Coordinate emergency response efforts
PC3. Assist the senior management in interagency coordination and notification of emergency
events
PC4. Develop & implement jointly with the emergency response teams the airport’s emergency
plans to the regulatory bodies as mandated under law
PC5. Review jointly the airlines emergency plans to ensure they comply with the airport’s
emergency plan and takes necessary action to rectify non-compliance
PC6. Identify risk through a structured ‘assessment framework’
PC7. Develop and implement the ‘risk mitigation framework’
PC8. Develop drills and exercises along with the ERT and conduct briefing & debriefing with
stakeholders on course corrections and learning
PC9. Respond to incidents and first-aid on the airport and complete all relevant paperwork
thoroughly and on time
Enhance customer experience through process improvements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. Plan deploy & critically review resource allocation both in landside & airside to ensure their
effective utilization
PC11. Evaluate, revise and/or develop department policies and procedures
PC12. Lead development and update of Standard Operations Procedures (sop) and checklists
PC13. Drive process audits to identify areas of improvement
PC14. Participate in technology improvement initiatives with Information Technology teams by
establishing valid use cases for improvement in process and enhancing customer experience

Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
Assessment Guidelines
- 1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will also lay
down the proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each PC.
- 2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on the knowledge bank of questions created by the
SSC.
- 3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected
elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
- 4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for the theory part for each
candidate at each examination/training centre (as per assessment criteria below).
- 5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training centre based on these criteria.
- 6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score a minimum of 70% of %
aggregate marks to successfully clear the assessment.
