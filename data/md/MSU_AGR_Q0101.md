#### MSU/AGR/Q0101: Large Cardamom  Organic Cultivation and Management
Brief Job Description
On completion of this course, the candidate will be able to select and prepare the area and soil for large
cardamom cultivation, carry out propagation activities, and perform essential maintenance during the
cultivation process. They will also be skilled in harvesting large cardamom produce and undertaking post-
harvest activities to enhance profitability. In addition, the candidate will be trained to manage organic crop
cultivation sustainably, ensuring health and safety standards are maintained throughout the cultivation
and processing phases.
Personal Attributes
Candidates for this role should possess strong analytical skills to assess soil and environmental conditions,
along with attention to detail for monitoring plant health. A problem-solving mindset is essential for
troubleshooting cultivation challenges. Effective communication and adaptability will aid in collaboration
and responding to changing conditions. A commitment to sustainability and organic farming practices is
vital, as is the physical stamina to handle demanding tasks. Strong time management skills and a
proactive attitude toward improving techniques are important, alongside a solid understanding of health
and safety regulations in agriculture.

Compulsory NOS:

#### MSU/AGR/N0107: Select and prepare area and soil for large cardamoms
cultivation
Description
In this OS unit, candidate will learn to select a site with suitable conditions for growing large cardamom,
prepare area for large cardamom cultivation and prepare soil to receive plants.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Select a site with suitable conditions for growing large cardamom
Prepare area for large cardamom cultivation
Prepare soil to receive plants
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Select a site with suitable conditions for growing large cardamom
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Research and identify potential areas available for large cardamom cultivation.
- PC2. Analyse the altitude, slope, rainfall, humidity, climatic events and ambient air temperature
for selecting an area for the cultivation of large cardamom.
- PC3. Get the soil tested from authorized centres.
- PC4. Determine if soil conditions are suitable for growing large cardamom. (Soil conditions:
fertility of the soil, the nature of the subsoil, soil depth, land topography and its effect on
water flow, no contamination in the soil such as heavy metals or pollutants, etc.)
- PC5. Analyse the availability of compatible shade trees in the selected area.
- PC6. Identify primary and secondary sources of water and the quality of water available at the
site.
Prepare area for large cardamom cultivation
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Identify the hazards of growing large cardamom and possible precautions to take.
- PC8. Design the layout of the farm providing windbreaks, plantation area as well as other
necessary facilities like water source, office space, nursery, irrigation channels, shade trees,
roads, fence, etc.
- PC9. Clear the site of unwanted plant material and general debris without burning organic matter.
- PC10. Return to the topsoil, the crop residue and other organic farm materials such as green
manure, organic mulches and poultry/cattle manure in judicious proportions to replace nutrients removed in the previous harvest.
- PC11. Interpret scale plans to identify dimensions, shapes and positions to be marked on the land
and features to be produced.
- PC12. Transfer various dimensions accurately from scale plans onto the land, using triangulation,
running lines and offsets. (Various dimensions: geometric shapes such as rectangles, circles, hexagons, and ellipses, construction of right angle, irregular shapes)

#### MSU/AGR/N0101: Carry out propagation activities for large cardamom.
Description
In this OS unit, the candidate will learn to procure and prepare propagation material, prepare primary and
secondary nurseries for propogating large cardamom seeds, transplant seedlings into the field and
propogate large cardamom seeds through suckers.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Procure and prepare propagation material
Prepare primary and secondary nurseries for propagating large cardamom through seeds
Transplant seedlings into the field
Propagate large cardamom through suckers
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Procure and prepare propagation material
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify local and improved varieties of cultivars that are disease resistant, high yield,
organic certified or chemically untreated and have been tested and practised in the area.
- PC2. Select the type of large cardamom that can be grown profitably based on market need,
prices, production costs, time to harvest and disease resistance.
- PC3. Identify suitable propagation material such as seeds, seedlings and suckers as per the type
of large cardamom and the agro-climatic conditions.
- PC4. Identify authorised vendors/suppliers of organic propagation material.
- PC5. Ascertain the quality of seeds, seedlings or suckers from each source (Quality: free from
pests and diseases, high survival rate, right age, high yield, etc.)
- PC6. Ascertain the prevailing market price for the propagation material.
- PC7. Procure the organic propagation material from the selected authorised vendor.
- PC8. Store the propagation material in a storage space that is free of infestation and has climatic
conditions suitable for the planting material.
- PC9. Prepare bio-inputs for propagation material treatment
- PC10. Implement the organic propagation material treatment process appropriately
- PC11. Apply appropriate organic pesticides and fungicides in the dosage recommended by
experts/manufacturers, to the propagation material as per the prevalent pests and diseases in a given agroclimatic zone.
Prepare primary and secondary nurseries for propagating large cardamom through seeds
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC12. Prepare and weather seed beds for the primary nursery.
- PC13. Mix organic manure into the soil.
- PC14. Make a surface bed of fine tilth.

#### MSU/AGR/N0102: Carry out aftercare or maintenance during cultivation of
large cardamom
Description
In this OS unit, candidate will learn to control and prevent potential threats to plants, manage soil
conditions, promote and maintain healthy growth, prepare irrigation schedule and irrigate the large
cardamom plants.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Control and prevent potential threats to plants
Manage soil conditions
Promote and maintain healthy growth
Prepare irrigation schedule
Irrigate the large cardamom plants
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Control and prevent potential threats to plants
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Identify potential threats to plant health for a specific agro-climatic zone. (Potential threats:
certain types of weeds, pests, diseases, disorders, unfavourable conditions, etc.)
- PC2. Prepare a pest calendar with all pest characteristics for large cardamom crops in relation to
different stages of growth and seasons.
- PC3. Implement organic preventive techniques which limit the development of weeds, pests and
diseases in a balanced nutrient management programme such as water logging, destruction of infected plant debris, pruning, etc.
- PC4. Check plants for any infestation of weeds, pests, diseases or disorders that adversely
impacts production.
- PC5. Estimate the type of existing infestation, its extent, stage and size.
- PC6. Control insect-pest and disease incidence at an early controllable stage by applying
Integrated Pest Management (IPM) and Integrated Disease Management (IDM)
- PC7. Manage and prevent viral diseases.
Manage soil conditions
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. Monitor top soil’s pH value, fertility and density against acceptable levels for the chosen crop
- PC9. Identify various methods of activating microbial activity in topsoil
- PC10. Prepare various organic inputs that can increase soil microbial activity
- PC11. Apply soil-activating inputs effectively
- PC12. Identify different plant nutritional requirements during the growing cycle and growing
conditions, using published data on species, historical records and experience.

| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
