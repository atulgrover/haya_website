TSC/Q8102: Operator - Pultrusion (Composites)
Brief Job Description
The individual at work is responsible for selecting raw materials, right tooling and processes, and ensuring
the proper operation of pultrusion machines for the quality manufacturing of Fiber Reinforced Polymer
(FRP) products. The key Duties include preparing and mixing resins, monitoring pot life, viscosity of the
binding system, and using it within prescribed time, handling and aligning fibers, monitoring and adjusting
machine settings, performing quality checks, and adhering to safety protocols to produce high-quality FRP
products.
Personal Attributes
The job requires the individual to be courteous, and physically fit to work in a factory environment with
good communication skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:

TSC/N8106: Carry out Shift change responsibilities in Pultrusion
Department
Description
This unit provides foundational understanding for Pultrusion Operators, covering the mission's overview,
the composite industry's scope, and the core responsibilities of an operator.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Taking charge of shift from pultrusion operator
Handing over the shift to operator
Elements and Performance Criteria
Taking charge of shift from pultrusion operator
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. come at least 10-15 minutes earlier to the work place
PC2. get instructions from supervisor
PC3. verify the operational status of the pultrusion machine and ancillary equipment.
PC4. review the shift logbook for updates, pending tasks, and machine settings
PC5. check the availability of raw materials, tools, and consumables required for the shift.
PC6. wear personal protective equipment (PPE) and ensure all safety measures are in place
PC7. inspect the workspace to confirm cleanliness and compliance with 5S principles
Handing over the shift to operator
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. provide a detailed handover report to the next operator, including production updates,
machine settings, and any maintenance issues
PC9. highlight any critical incidents, deviations, or quality concerns encountered during the shift.
PC10. store all tools and materials in their designated locations
PC11. confirm that the machine and work area are left in a safe and operational state
PC12. update the shift logbook with accurate information for continuity
PC13. get clearance from the incoming counterpart before leaving the work place
PC14. handover the shift to the incoming shift operator as per standard protocol
PC15. report to his/ her shift superiors as well as that of the incoming shift operator in case his/ her
counterpart doesn't report for the incoming shift
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. objectives and key benefits of the composite manufacturing sector.

TSC/N8107: Preparation for fabrication of pultruded composite parts
Description
This unit offers an in-depth understanding of the principles of composites and the pultrusion process,
covering material characteristics, resin types, equipment setup, and quality checks.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Preparation for part manufacturing
Selection of Raw Material and Testing
Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparation for part manufacturing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. inspect the pultrusion machine and ensure all systems are operational before starting the
shift.
PC2. inspect and confirm the readiness of tools, fixtures, and auxiliary equipment required for
manufacturing.
PC3. verify the availability of necessary tools, jigs, and consumables for part manufacturing.
PC4. review work instructions, technical drawings, and specifications
PC5. check alignment and tension of fiber spools or reinforcement materials before loading
PC6. set up and adjust the pulling system, die temperature, and resin bath according to
specifications.
PC7. conduct a dry run of the machine to identify any potential issues.
Selection of Raw Material and Testing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. identify the type and quantity of raw materials i.e., reinforce fiber, resin, additives etc.
PC9. conduct a visual inspection of raw materials to check for damage, contamination, or
inconsistencies.
PC10. perform quality tests such as resin gel time, cure rate, and fiber tensile strength.
PC11. record material properties and test results in compliance with documentation standards.
PC12. monitor the mixing of resin, hardener, and additives to ensure proper ratios and
homogeneity.
PC13. store and handle raw materials following workplace protocols to maintain quality.
PC14. coordinate with the quality control team to resolve any raw material-related issues.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

TSC/N8108: Carry out Pultrusion Production Activity
Description
This unit covers essential operational and maintenance procedures for pultrusion machinery, including
setting parameters, handling materials, and trial production with new molds.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform production activity of the pultruded composite parts
Quality check and finish products
Advancements in pultrusion technology
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform production activity of the pultruded composite parts
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. clean, adjust, and apply lubrication to equipment following the manufacturer's instructions
PC2. set the operating parameters of the machine as per SOP
PC3. apply proper procedure to startup and operate the machine to meet job specification
PC4. load and unload the mold safely from the machine
PC5. eject the product from the mold
PC6. verify that the dimensions, structural reinforcements, supports, and flanges conform to the
job and design specifications.
PC7. check and rectify manufacturing defects associated with mold design, mold manufacturing,
material properties, process parameters, and machine performance.
PC8. monitor complete machine to achieve standard cycle time
PC9. check production processes to reduce scrap and trim waste, ensuring compliance with
established workplace procedures.
PC10. identify the shutdown type and follow the prescribed procedures based on equipment
manuals and workplace protocols.
PC11. initiate equipment shutdown following the manufacturer's instructions and established
workplace protocols.
PC12. perform thorough and efficient cleaning of equipment using the specified methods and
materials, in strict accordance with the equipment’s operational guidelines and maintenance
instructions.
PC13. finalize shutdown documentation in accordance with workplace standards, ensuring all
procedures and details are accurately recorded.
PC14. clear and clean the work area following shutdown, ensuring it is properly prepared for the
next startup.
Quality check and finish products
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC15. perform standard shaping, trimming or cutting operations as per job specifications
