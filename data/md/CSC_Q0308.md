CSC/Q0308: Metal Fabrication Engineer
Brief Job Description
A Metal Fabrication Engineer is a professional who designs and creates metal parts and structures for
Capital Goods industries.
Personal Attributes
The person should be result oriented with good technical and analytical skills, should have Excellent
Interpersonal Skills, communication and presentation skills and a good team player. They should have
ability to manage projects, prioritizing of work and mentoring the budding engineers.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. CSC/N0327: Supervise the preparation and utilization of metal fabrication tools and associated
equipment
- 2. CSC/N0328: Develop 3D Prototype and implement suitable metal fabrication methods
- 3. CSC/N0329: inspect for Dimensional Defects & Document for Quality Assurance.
- 4. CSC/N1339: Collaboratively coordinate with the team
- 5. CSC/N0505: Follow health, safety and environment guidelines at workplace
- 6. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

CSC/N0327: Supervise the preparation and utilization of metal fabrication
tools and associated equipment
Description
This NOS unit is about to Supervise the preparation and utilization of metal fabrication tools and associated
equipment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operating diverse metalworking tools, including lathes and mills, and understanding equipment
setup procedures for precise machining.
Utilizing proper personal protective equipment, and ensuring machine guarding to prevent accidents
and injuries..
Optimizing processes for increased productivity and maintaining quality standards
Elements and Performance Criteria
Operating diverse metal working tools, including lathes and mills, and understanding equipment setup
procedures for precise machining.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Read engineering drawings, selecting materials, and planning the fabrication process.
PC2. Choose the right metalworking tools and configuring equipment like lathes, mills, and grinders
for specific tasks
PC3. Adhering to safety protocols, including proper use of personal protective equipment (PPE) and
machine guarding.
utilizing proper personal protective equipment, and ensuring machine guarding to prevent accidents and
injuries
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Perform cutting, shaping, and joining of metal materials with precision and efficiency
PC5. Conduct inspections to ensure fabricated components meet design specifications and quality
standards
PC6. Identify and address any issues or malfunctions during equipment setup or operation to
maintain workflow continuity.
Optimizing processes for increased productivity and maintaining quality standards
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Effectively communicating with team members and supervisors regarding progress,
challenges, and any adjustments needed in the fabrication process.
PC8. Actively seeking opportunities to enhance skills, optimize equipment setup
PC9. Setup procedures, and improve overall efficiency and quality in metalworking operations

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. Tool Functions: Understanding the purpose and capabilities of various metalworking tools
such as lathes, mills, drills, and grinders.
KU2. Equipment Setup Procedures: Knowing how to properly configure and calibrate machines for
specific fabrication tasks, including tool selection and alignment.
KU3. Material Properties: Familiarity with the characteristics and behavior of different metals,
including their machinability, hardness, and thermal conductivity.
KU4. Safety Protocols: Knowledge of safety regulations and procedures to prevent accidents and
injuries while operating metalworking equipmet
KU5. Reading Engineering Drawings: Ability to interpret engineering drawings, blueprints, and
schematics to understand dimensional specifications and manufacturing requirements
KU6. Tool Maintenance: Understanding basic maintenance procedures to ensure the proper
functioning and longevity of metalworking tools and equipment
KU7. Troubleshooting Skills: Ability to identify and resolve common issues or malfunctions that
may arise during equipment setup or operation.
KU8. Quality Control Measures: Understanding quality standards and inspection methods to verify
the accuracy and precision of fabricated components
KU9. Efficiency Optimization: Knowledge of techniques and best practices to maximize
productivity and minimize material waste during metalworking operations.
KU10. Continuous Learning: Awareness of advancements in metalworking technology and
techniques, and a willingness to continuously improve skills and knowledge in the field.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. read safety instructions/guidelines
GS2. modify work practices to improve them
GS3. work with supervisors/team members to carry out work related tasks
GS4. Complete task efficiently and accurate time
GS5. inform/report to concerned person in case of any problem
GS6. Make timely decision for efficient utilization of resources
GS7. write reports such as accident report, in at least English/regional language

CSC/N0328: Develop 3D Prototype and implement suitable metal
fabrication methods
Description
This unit is about to Develop 3D Prototype and implement suitable metal fabrication methods
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Attaining proficiency in a range of metal fabrication techniques including welding, cutting, and
bending to efficiently produce components to specifications
Ensuring accurate setup and execution of fabrication processes.
Ensure the well-being of oneself and colleagues during fabrication operations.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Attaining proficiency in a range of metal fabrication techniques including welding, cutting, and bending to
efficiently produce components to specifications
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Assess the type and properties of the metal to determine the most appropriate
fabrication technique
PC2. . Choose the suitable fabrication method based on factors such as material type,
thickness, and desired outcome
PC3. Select and prepared the necessary tools and equipment for the chosen fabrication
technique.
Ensuring accurate setup and execution of fabrication processes
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Ensure adherence to safety protocols and procedures while performing fabrication
tasks to prevent accidents and injuries.
PC5. Perform the selected fabrication technique with precision and accuracy.
PC6. Inspect fabricated components to ensure they meet design specifications and quality
standards.
Ensure the well-being of oneself and colleagues during fabrication operations
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Complete fabrication tasks within allocated timeframes while minimizing material
waste and optimizing workflow
PC8. Identify and addressing any issues or challenges encountered during the fabrication
process to maintain workflow continuity
PC9. . Documenting the fabrication process and reporting any deviations from specifications
or quality issues to relevant personnel.
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

KU7. Efficiency Optimization: Understanding techniques to optimize fabrication processes for

CSC/N0329: inspect for Dimensional Defects & Document for Quality
Assurance.
Description
This unit is about to Inspect for Dimensional Defects & Document for Quality Assurance.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Inspect components for dimensional accuracy using precise measurement tools to identify
deviations from specifications.
During inspections, noting any mistakes or differences to keep accurate quality records.
Ensure adherence to quality standards and regulations throughout the inspection process to uphold
product integrity and customer satisfaction.
Elements and Performance Criteria
Inspect components for dimensional accuracy using precise measurement tools to identify deviations
from specifications
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Accurately identifying dimensional defects such as deviations from specified measurements,
surface irregularities, or geometric inconsistencies
PC2. Utilizing appropriate measuring tools such as calipers, micrometers, or gauges to measure
dimensions with precision and accuracy
PC3. Recording inspection results and documenting dimensional defects, including their nature,
location, and severity, in a clear and detailed manner.
during inspections, noting any mistakes or differences to keep accurate quality records.
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. Ensuring inspection processes adhere to quality assurance standards, including relevant
regulations, specifications, and industry best practices
PC5. Investigating the underlying causes of dimensional defects to determine whether they result
from fabrication processes, material issues, or other factors
PC6. Implementing corrective actions to address identified dimensional defects and prevent
recurrence, such as adjusting fabrication processes or material specifications.
• Ensure adherence to quality standards and regulations throughout the inspection process to uphold
product integrity and customer satisfaction
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. Effectively communicating inspection findings and quality assurance documentation to
relevant stakeholders, including production teams, engineers, and management
PC8. Participating in ongoing efforts to improve inspection techniques, documentation practices,
and quality assurance processes to enhance overall product quality and customer satisfaction

Description
This OS unit is about following adequate safety procedures to make work environment healthy and safe
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This unit/task covers the following:
Adhere to standard safety procedures of the company
Follow healthy practices and posture
Practice waste management and recycling
Conserve material and resources
Elements and Performance Criteria
Adhere to standard safety procedures of the organisation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. comply with general safety procedures and those for handling equipment, tools, chemicals,
and hazardous material, as prescribed and followed in the organisation
PC2. remove finger rings or any other metal objects likely to interfere with the work
PC3. ensure that identification badge or any other object worn around the neck or on the clothing
does not get caught in any rotating machine, or otherwise interfere with the work
PC4. use appropriate safety devices such as goggles, gloves, ear plugs, caps, ESD pins, covers,
shoes, helmets etc. recommended for the work being performed
PC5. inform, escalate, or raise alarm about any suspicions, unaccounted hazardous material,
devices, or other objects found in the premises
PC6. inform, escalate, or raise alarm about any breach of safety or security procedure in the
organisation
PC7. help achieve zero accidents goals at work
PC8. avoid damage to sensitive electronic components due to negligence of ESD procedures
PC9. participate regularly in fire drills or other safety related workshops organised by the
organisation
PC10. follow strictly all access control and perimeter safety procedures in designated factory areas
such as robotic work stations, automated production lines, automated material movement
and other potentially risky operations
PC11. ensure that other people follow all access control and perimeter safety procedures in
designated factory areas and help avoid accidents
PC12. use emergency switches or other mechanisms of stopping a machine immediately in case
any emergency situation has developed or about to happen
PC13. ensure that electrical equipment are properly grounded
PC14. follow Cyber Security guidelines and be vigilant at workplace
