#### CON/Q4105: Concrete Construction Work (WorldSkills)
Brief Job Description
A Concrete Construction Worker generally works on commercial and residential projects. There is a direct
relationship between the nature and quality of the product required and the payment made by the
customer. Therefore, the Concrete Construction Worker has a continuing responsibility to work professionally in order to meet the requirements of the customer and thus maintain and grow the
business.
Personal Attributes
The person should be physically fit to work in varying locations and conditions. The person should be
organized, diligent, methodical, safety-conscious, and a prompt decision- maker. In addition to being a
team player, the individual should have good communication skills.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CON/N4101: Work Organization, Management and Communication
2. CON/N4103: Interpretation of Drawings
3. CON/N4104: Setting out and Measurement
4. CON/N4110: Concrete Construction Work (WorldSkills)
5. CON/N4105: Quality Check and Handover

#### CON/N4101: Work Organization, Management   and Communication
Description
This NOS deals with ensuring safe and healthy working environment at competition area by proper
utilization of safety gears and effective communication skill with juries and competitors.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Adopt healthy and safe work practices at competition area
Management of workplace
Effective communication at competition area
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Adopt healthy and safe work practices at competition area
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Explain health and safety legislation, obligations, and documentation
- PC2. check and ensure the availability of appropriate and usable Personal Protective Equipment
(PPE) at the workplace
- PC3. use the appropriate PPE according to the nature of work and ensure other personnel also do
the same
- PC4. follow and promote the appropriate measures to ensure hygiene in the workplace,
e.g.regular cleaning and sanitization
- PC5. follow the applicable emergency procedures and ensure the personnel also do the same
- PC6. Learn precautions for the safe use of power and cordless tools
- PC7. Apply standards and laws relating to security, safety, and hygiene in plastering and drywall
systems
Management of workplace

construction plan and equipment, organization and procedures, material management, staffing and timelines

methods of working in the construction industry and changing technologies, for example acoustics, sustainability and environmental impact
- PC12. Work effectively as part of teams
- PC13. Work effectively with other trades
- PC14. Establish and maintain high quality standards and working processes
- PC15. identify problems promptly and manage their resolution

#### CON/N4103: Interpretation of Drawings
Description
This NOS deals with understanding of the test project drawings along with its process of measurements.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Interpretation and execution of drawings as per standards
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Interpretation and execution of drawings as per standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Prepare site measurement drawings
- PC2. Accurately interpret all plans, elevations, sections and enlarged details
- PC3. Produce basic outline drawings (hand and CAD) including elevations, plans, and sections to
full size
- PC4. produce accurate complex drawings on wood to make figure on the wall/floor
- PC5. Identify drawing errors or items that require clarification
- PC6. Determine and check quantities of materials required
- PC7. Calculate a cost and price for the work
- PC8. Identify horizontal and vertical key dimensions and all angles
- PC9. Identify curved work and mortar joint finishes
- PC10. Interpret all project features and their required construction methods
- PC11. Establish any features that need special equipment or templates and source these
- PC12. Recognize specified bonding patterns and obey bonding rules during construction
- PC13. Provide advice and guidance to other professionals such as architects and quantity surveyors
- PC14. Keep essential notes on each installation process
- PC15. Explain complex specialist and technical information about installations to clients and other
professionals
- PC16. Apply mathematic geometry principles to the calculation of angles, areas, perimeters,
curves, arcs, volumes, ratios, etc.
- PC17. Prepare the materials requirements, taking into account increased requirements due to
compression, wastage, breakage, etc.
- PC18. Calculate formwork surfaces and materials requirements
- PC19. Calculate formwork surfaces and materials requirements for face concrete formwork
- PC20. Interpret, analyse, and work with construction plans (e.g. design plans, formwork plans,
reinforcement plans, detail drawings, etc.) and material and parts lists
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)

#### CON/N4104: Setting out and Measurement
Description
This NOS deals with measurements to be setout on competition area as per the drawing
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Implement techniques to setout the measurement
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Implement techniques to setout the measurement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Check measurements of the wall/floor conform to the drawing specifications
- PC2. Produce setting out for templates
- PC3. Visualize and think through projects, identifying potential challenges early and taking the
necessary preventative action
- PC4. Set out the locations, starting points and lines of projects according to plans and
specifications
- PC5. Set out highly technical designs including brick-on-end, brick-on edge, raked/inclined, curved
projecting, recessing brickwork, archways, corbelling, decorative bonding, and battered walling
- PC6. Accurately interpret the dimensions from drawings and ensure the design is set out within a
given tolerance
- PC7. Check all horizontal and vertical angles
- PC8. Accurately interpret dimensions from drawings and ensure designs are set out within a given
tolerances
- PC9. Produce any templates/building aids that may be helpful when constructing
- PC10. Set out datum points of reference for projects
- PC11. Carry out setting out work using the necessary surveying equipment (pocket rule, tape
measure, distance meter, set square, level, etc.)
- PC12. Measure predetermined structures, joints, and materials for the subsequent face concrete
surfaces (anchor holes, shuttering frames, board inserts, distribution and alignment of formwork boards, etc.)
- PC13. Create horizontal levels and measure heights using spirit levels, water level gauges, and
optical devices

#### CON/N4110: Concrete Construction Work (WorldSkills)
Description
This NOS deals with the construction of test project for Concrete Construction Work Skill.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Construction of formwork
Construction of reinforcement
Filling of formworks and treatment
Removal of formworks and reprocessing
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Construction of formwork
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Work manually with materials such as wood, metal, and plastic (for separating, reshaping,
connecting)
- PC2. Measure, lay out and cut wood and work with it manually and using machinery
- PC3. Make simple trestles, working platforms plus auxiliary equipment, set up protective nets and
use them in compliance with the relevant regulations
- PC4. Make and put together every type of formwork
- PC5. Make supports and reinforcements (concrete pressure)
- PC6. Make face concrete formwork
- PC7. Make slits, apertures, openings, and recesses
- PC8. Move anchors as directed
- PC9. Make various joints in combination with the appropriate joint sealants (profiles, sealing strips,
expansion joint tapes)
- PC10. Cut to length, bend, interweave, lay, and anchor structural steel according to bending and
reinforcement diagrams and in compliance with reinforcement directives (specifically those concerning bending, radius of curvature, end hooks, brackets, distributors, separators, joints,
and connection reinforcements)
- PC11. Prevent the following problems through correct construction
- PC12. Use scaffolding appropriately and safely and apply health and safety requirements and
legislation
Construction of reinforcement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC13. Make simple trestles, working platforms plus auxiliary equipment, set up protective nets and
use them in compliance with the relevant regulations

- PC15. Prevent the build-up of rust stains on vertical components and of traces of rust caused by

#### Assessment Guidelines

provided the same Mark Summary Form as used by the Experts. A master set of marking tools provided by
the Competition Organizer is available during familiarization. All measurements are taken using the master
set of measuring equipment. As per the Competition Rules a majority vote (50% +1) is needed to:
• Change the Marking Scheme (within limits specified in the Technical Description);
• Change competition sequence or content;
• Agree on a solution for disputes concerning marks awarded etc.
