#### CSC/Q0423: Advance  Mechatronics Simulation Engineer
Brief Job Description
Advanced Mechatronics Simulation Engineer is responsible for integration of mechanical, electrical, and
computer systems to create advanced automation solutions.
Personal Attributes
The person should be result oriented with good technical and analytical skills, should have Excellent
Interpersonal Skills, communication and presentation skills and a good team player. They should have
ability to manage projects, prioritizing of work and mentoring the budding engineers.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. CSC/N0479: Supervise programming Simulation of Mechatronics System using CAD inputs (CAD
Modelling of particular process)
2. CSC/N0480: Supervise testing, and Integration of all the sensor, actuators, control system and
mechanical components of the mechatronic system.
3. CSC/N1339: Collaboratively coordinate with the team
4. CSC/N0505: Follow health, safety and environment guidelines at workplace
5. DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)

#### CSC/N0479: Supervise programming   Simulation of Mechatronics System
using CAD inputs (CAD Modelling of particular process)
Description
This NOS unit is about Supervise programming Simulation of Mechatronics System using CAD inputs (CAD
Modelling of process).
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct development of Detailed Models mechatronic system components.
Implement simulations & analyze results to predict the performance and behavior of mechatronic systems
Perform integration, validation & optimization activities for models.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct development of Detailed Models mechatronic system components
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Proficiently use modeling and simulation software (e.g., MATLAB/Simulink, ANSYS,
SolidWorks) to create accurate and detailed models of mechanical, electrical, hydraulic, and control components.
- PC2. Develop understanding of the principles and interactions of mechanical, electrical, hydraulic,
and control systems within mechatronic applications.
- PC3. Identify and perform validation of the developed models against experimental data and real-
world scenarios to ensure their accuracy and reliability. Update models based on validation results.
Implement simulations & analyze results to predict the performance and behavior of mechatronic systems.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Configure and run simulations using advanced software tools (e.g., MATLAB/Simulink, ANSYS,
COMSOL Multiphysics) to model the behavior of mechatronic systems under various operating conditions.
- PC5. Analyze simulation results to understand the performance, efficiency, and potential failure
modes of mechatronic systems.
- PC6. Prepare detailed reports and visualizations to document the simulation process, results, and
conclusions. Effectively communicate findings to stakeholders, including engineers, project managers, and clients, providing clear recommendations for design improvements.
Perform integration, validation & optimization activities for models.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Integrate individual component models into comprehensive, system-level simulations to
assess overall system performance and interaction.
- PC8. Validate the integrated models by comparing simulation results with experimental data and
real-world performance.

- KU3. Mechatronics Fundamentals: Understanding of core concepts of mechatronics, including the integration of mechanical, electrical, hydraulic, and control systems. Key theories and
principles underpinning the behavior and interaction of mechatronic components.
- KU4. Advanced Modeling Techniques: Proficiency in techniques for developing accurate and detailed models of mechatronic components using software tools like MATLAB/Simulink,
ANSYS, SolidWorks, and COMSOL Multiphysics.
- KU5. Simulation Tools and Software: Efficient use of Proficient use of advanced simulation software
to conduct performance and behavior analyses of mechatronic systems. Understanding of simulation parameters, boundary conditions, and input variables necessary for accurate
model simulations.
- KU6. Data Analysis and Interpretation: Understanding the Techniques for analyzing simulation results to extract meaningful insights and performance metrics. Methods for identifying
trends, potential failure modes, and areas for optimization from simulation data.
- KU7. Validation and Verification Processes: Procedures for validating simulation models against
experimental and real-world data to ensure accuracy and reliability. Knowledge of techniques for identifying and correcting discrepancies between simulated and actual performance.
- KU8. System Integration: Knowledge of integrating component models into larger system-level simulations to assess overall system performance. Understanding of interdependencies and
interactions among various mechatronic components within a system
- KU9. Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration: Proficiency in collaborating with cross-functional teams,
including hardware, software, and design engineers, to ensure cohesive system development.
Understanding the importance of interdisciplinary knowledge in the development and simulation of mechatronic systems.

#### CSC/N0480: Supervise testing, and Integration of all the sensor,
actuators, control system and mechanical components of the mechatronic system.
Description
This unit is about to Supervise testing, and Integration of all the sensor, actuators, control system and
mechanical components of the mechatronic system.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Conduct testing of sensors, actuators, control systems, and mechanical components to meet performance specifications and functions.
Integrate tested components into a cohesive mechatronic system.
Perform system-level validation activities for continuous improvement
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Conduct testing of sensors, actuators, control systems, and mechanical components to meet performance
specifications and functions
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Develop detailed test plans and procedures that outline the specific tests to be conducted,
including performance criteria, testing conditions, and measurement methods
- PC2. Conduct tests systematically according to the established test plans, ensuring accurate and
consistent execution. Collect and record test data meticulously, ensuring that all relevant parameters and outcomes are documented for further analysis.
- PC3. Analyze the collected test data to determine whether the components meet the required
performance specifications and function correctly. Identify any deviations from expected performance, diagnose underlying issues, and recommend corrective actions or
improvements to enhance component reliability and effectiveness.
Integrate tested components into a cohesive mechatronic system.
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Develop integration plans that outline the step-by-step process for combining sensors,
actuators, control systems, and mechanical components into a unified system.
- PC5. Perform the integration of tested components according to the established plans, ensuring
proper physical and functional connections. Address and resolve compatibility issues that may arise during integration.
- PC6. Conduct comprehensive system-level tests to verify that the integrated mechatronic system
operates correctly and meets the overall design and performance specifications.
Perform system-level validation activities for continuous improvement
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Develop validation plans that include test objectives, methodologies, and success criteria for
evaluating the entire mechatronic system.

- KU3. Principles of Mechatronic Systems: In-depth understanding of Core concepts of mechatronics,
including the integration and interaction of mechanical, electrical, electronic, and control systems. Functional roles and characteristics of sensors, actuators, control systems, and
mechanical components in a mechatronic system.
- KU4. Testing Methods and Tools: Familiarity with Techniques and methodologies for testing the performance and functionality of individual components, such as sensors, actuators, and
control systems. Use of testing equipment and software tools (e.g., oscilloscopes, multimeters, signal generators, data acquisition systems) to perform accurate and reliable
tests.
- KU5. Integration Techniques: Knowledge of Best practices for integrating various components into a
unified mechatronic system, including mechanical assembly, electrical wiring, and software interfacing. Knowledge of communication protocols (e.g., CAN, I2C, SPI, UART) and data
exchange methods for ensuring seamless interaction between components.
- KU6. System-Level Design and Architecture: Understanding of overall design and architecture of mechatronic systems, including system schematics, block diagrams, and flowcharts.
- KU7. Validation and Verification Processes: Understanding of Procedures for validating the integrated system against design specifications and real-world performance requirements.
Techniques for verifying system functionality, reliability, and safety through comprehensive testing and analysis.
- KU8. Safety Standards and Regulations: Awareness of relevant safety standards, industry regulations, and compliance requirements for mechatronic systems. Understanding of risk
assessment and mitigation strategies to ensure safe operation of the integrated system.
