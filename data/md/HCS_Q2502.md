#### HCS/Q2502: Decorative Painter - Glassware
Brief Job Description
A decorative painter for glassware specialises in creating intricate designs and patterns on glass surfaces,
using various painting techniques to enhance the aesthetic appeal of items such as vases, bottles, and
tableware, while ensuring durability and vibrancy in the finished product.
Personal Attributes
A decorative painter for glassware should possess a strong artistic flair, exceptional attention to detail, and
patience, along with good manual dexterity and the ability to work meticulously to achieve high-quality,
visually stunning results.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. HCS/N2503: Prepare to make decorative designs on glass articles
2. HCS/N2504: Making decorative design on glass article

#### HCS/N2503: Prepare to make decorative designs on glass articles
Description
This OS unit is about making a decorative design on glass articles using a different types of glass colors as
per design specifications. To perform the work competently the person is required to work with precision in
delivering outcomes mentioned in this unit.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Review and Understand Work Instructions
Gather Tools, Materials, and Raw Materials
Inspect Materials and Plan the Work Sequence
Prepare the Work Area and Ensure Safety
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Review and Understand Work Instructions
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Review the job instructions and design specifications received from responsible personnel,
ensuring clarity on design, color palette, and areas to be painted.
- PC2. Confirm that the instructions and design details are complete and accurate.
- PC3. Clarify any uncertainties or missing information with the supervisor or manager before
proceeding.
Gather Tools, Materials, and Raw Materials
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Collect all required tools and equipment, ensuring they align with the job requirements and
approved procedures.
- PC5. Ensure all raw materials, including glass articles and paints, are available and in suitable
condition for the task.
- PC6. Check that all tools and materials are clean, undamaged, and fit for use in the design
process.
Inspect Materials and Plan the Work Sequence
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Visually inspect the glass articles for defects such as chips, cracks, or rough edges and
ensure they meet the design requirements.
- PC8. Plan the sequence of tasks for efficient execution, ensuring that drying times, material
curing, and other requirements are factored in.
- PC9. Ensure that the work plan aligns with safety protocols and follows the design specifications.
Prepare the Work Area and Ensure Safety
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Organize the work area to ensure it is clean, spacious, and well-ventilated for safe work
conditions.

#### HCS/N2504: Making decorative design on glass article
Description
Decorative glass painter makes required decorative design on glass articles with the help of glass colors.
To perform the work competently the person is required to work with precision in delivering outcomes with
the help of appropriate painting techniques.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
assess work requirements make decorative design on glass article work safely
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Assess work requirements
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Arrange the appropriate tools, materials, and accessories required for the task, including
color plates, scrapers, brushes, and markers, as well as raw materials such as glass articles and glass colors.
- PC2. Receive detailed job instructions and design guidelines from the personnel responsible,
including specifications on the design, color combinations to be used, the area to be painted, and any specific techniques to be applied.
- PC3. Read and confirm that the instructions given and design are complete and correct.
- PC4. Inspect glass articles visually for any possible defects as per required standard parameters.
- PC5. Identify the surfaces and dimensions of the article to be painted.
- PC6. Draw the design on the glass article with the help of the marker.
Make decorative design on glass article
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Identify a suitable type of colour to be used as per design instructions.
- PC8. Prepare the design on the surface of the glass article with base color.
- PC9. Perform step by step the colouring process on the glass article.
- PC10. Apply the final finishing coat on the glass article right after coat dries up and before keeping
in firing oven.
- PC11. Maintain required production output and product quality.
- PC12. Perform painting in a well-ventilated and lit place.
- PC13. Use proper painting techniques while painting the glass articles.
- PC14. Ensure the paint is spread evenly across the design as per requirement.
- PC15. Set the firing oven at the appropriate temperature for baking.
- PC16. Put and take out the glass articles in/from the firing oven after final coat at correct
temperature.

#### HCS/N9916: Follow basic health and safety practices for glassware crafts
Description
This unit covers health, safety and security at the workplace. This includes procedures and practices that
candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
General Safety Practices
Workplace Hazard Identification
Safe Work Practices
Fire Safety
First Aid and Emergency Response
Good Housekeeping Practices
Awareness and Compliance
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
General Safety Practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Handle glass, tools, materials, and chemicals with care to prevent injuries or damage.
- PC2. Wear appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE), such as gloves, goggles, and masks,
while working.
- PC3. Inspect tools, machines, and equipment for safety before use.
- PC4. Adhere to safe material handling practices to avoid breakage or injury.
- PC5. Dispose of broken glass, chemical waste, and other waste materials as per workplace
guidelines.
- PC6. Report accidents, near misses, or unsafe conditions immediately to the supervisor.
Workplace Hazard Identification
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC7. Identify potential hazards in the work area, such as sharp edges, hot surfaces, fumes, or
improper lighting.
- PC8. Take precautions while working with hazardous materials, including molten glass and
chemicals.
- PC9. Ensure proper ventilation in areas where glass cutting, heating, or chemical processes are
conducted.
Safe Work Practices
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC10. Follow safe handling and storage practices for glass and glassware tools.
- PC11. Maintain a clutter-free and well-organized workspace.
- PC12. Use machines and equipment only as per instructions and ensure proper maintenance.

#### Assessment Guidelines and Assessment Weightage
#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Criteria for assessment for each Qualification Pack will be created by the Sector Skill Council. Each
Element/ Performance Criteria (PC) will be assigned marks proportional to its importance in NOS. SSC will
also lay down proportion of marks for Theory and Skills Practical for each Element/ PC.
2. The assessment for the theory part will be based on knowledge bank of questions created by the SSC.
3. Assessment will be conducted for all compulsory NOS, and where applicable, on the selected elective/option NOS/set of NOS.
4. Individual assessment agencies will create unique question papers for theory part for each candidate at
each examination/training center (as per assessment criteria below).
5. Individual assessment agencies will create unique evaluations for skill practical for every student at
each examination/ training center based on these criteria.
6. To pass the Qualification Pack assessment, every trainee should score the Recommended Pass %
aggregate for the QP.
7. In case of unsuccessful completion, the trainee may seek reassessment on the Qualification Pack.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |

| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | Performance Criteria (PC) | Performance Criteria (PC) are statements that together specify the standard of performance required when carrying out a task. |
| National Occupational Standards (NOS) | NOS are occupational standards which apply uniquely in the Indian context. |

| Unit Title | Unit title gives a clear overall statement about what the incumbent should be able to do. |
| Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
