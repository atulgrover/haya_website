#### CUTM/AGR/Q0801: Topiary Garden Artist
Brief Job Description
The individual is responsible to understand quality parameters of Topiary Garden Artist. Trainee able to
disseminate the knowledge and skills related to topiary to new generations of gardeners. Handle different
artistic aspects of topiary techniques. Add value to the garden, parks and office gardens. Outcomes of
topiary maintenance encompass the preservation of design, plant health, long-term sustainability of living
sculptures and maintain their aesthetic and artistic roles in the garden or landscape in their specific
environments. Understand the roles and responsibilities as topiary trainer experts.
Personal Attributes
A Topiary Garden Artist should be creative, patient, and detail-oriented, with a strong aesthetic sense and
love for plants. They must possess good hand-eye coordination, physical stamina, and spatial awareness.
Discipline, precision, and a passion for garden design are essential, along with the ability to work
independently or in teams.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0801: Introduction to plants used in Topiary Gardening and
Gardening Tools
Description
This NOS introduces learners to topiary-friendly plants and the tools required for sculpting and maintaining
them. It covers plant characteristics, tool identification, and safe handling procedures, enabling learners to
prepare effectively for artistic plant shaping.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope includes identifying suitable topiary plants, understanding their care requirements, and
mastering the use of essential gardening tools. Learners will gain foundational knowledge of horticulture, safety protocols, and tool upkeep necessary for shaping and maintaining decorative
plant forms.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Introduction to the suitable plants for making topiary and role of Topiary Gardener
- PC2. Identify various types of garden tools
- PC3. Know the use of power tools and hand tools.
- PC4. Maintenance of the equipment.
- PC5. Know about the safety measures in using tools and equipment.
- PC6. Inculcate the knowledge regarding the roles and responsibilities of Topiary Gardener.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. different types of plants suitable for topiary.
- KU2. evergreen species commonly used in shaping.
- KU3. the basic principles of plant aesthetics and growth habits.
- KU4. climate adaptability of topiary plant species.
- KU5. tool types: shears, pruners, secateurs, loppers, etc.
- KU6. the maintenance techniques of gardening tools.
- KU7. the safety precautions in handling sharp tools.
- KU8. the seasonal requirements for pruning.
- KU9. the basic botany related to leaf retention and regeneration.
- KU10. signs of disease and pest issues in topiary plants.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0804: Plant Propagation Techniques
Description
This NOS equips learners with plant propagation knowledge essential for growing topiary plants. It focuses
on the techniques and conditions required for generating healthy plant material for artistic shaping and
landscaping.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Covers methods like cutting, layering, and grafting tools and care practices, and preparation for
transplanting. Learners will be able to propagate and manage plant stock effectively, ensuring a
sustainable supply for topiary creation.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Knowledge on plant propagation methods
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand different methods of propagation of plants for cost-effective plant production.
- PC2. Understand the preservation, perpetuation and conservation of the desirable traits in plants
through vegetative propagation.
- PC3. Understand the asexual method/vegetative method like stem cuttings, root cuttings and leaf
cutting methods of propagation.
Information about handling of fish and shellfish waste
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. Understand detailed information on planting space.
- PC5. Gain the knowledge on rapid plant multiplication by passing the lengthy seed germination
process and time-saving cultivation.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. sexual and asexual propagation methods.
- KU2. the propagation by cuttings, layering, grafting, and division.
- KU3. tools and materials used in propagation.
- KU4. environmental conditions for propagation success.
- KU5. about rooting hormones and their application.
- KU6. how to select mother plants for propagation.
- KU7. seed dormancy and germination processes.
- KU8. proper handling of seedlings and young plants.

#### CUTM/AGR/N0805: Topiary Designing and Preservation
Description
This NOS focuses on the artistic and technical aspects of topiary design and its long-term maintenance. It
equips learners to conceptualize, execute, and sustain decorative plant forms across various styles and
settings.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Covers the process of shaping plants, using frames, design sketching, and maintaining plant health
to retain form. Learners will gain skills in layout execution and preservation, ensuring long-term
appeal and structural integrity of topiary designs.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Pruning techniques
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Steps to create structured and symmetrical designs of living plants into desired forms to add
artistic expression in the garden.
- PC2. Traditional and modern topiary shapes.
- PC3. Practice on training and pruning techniques.
- PC4. Understand the pruning periods/months.
- PC5. Topiary gardens in India.

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. design principles in topiary art (form, balance, symmetry).

#### CUTM/AGR/N0806: Yard Waste Management
Description
This NOS focuses on managing waste generated from gardening and topiary work. It emphasizes the
importance of waste segregation, composting, and eco-friendly disposal methods to support sustainable
gardening practices.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Includes yard waste collection, composting, safety, and compliance with disposal norms. Learners
gain practical knowledge in waste minimization, recycling of organics, and environmental conservation through responsible garden waste management.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Clearing and recycling of yard waste
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Understand the significance of clearing of yard waste.
- PC2. Learn separation of wood logs for other valuable use.
- PC3. Yard waste drop off area selection.
- PC4. Learn about recycling of yard waste by composting, equipment operation and safety
consideration.
Personal safety skills
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. Sanitize equipment, tools and machinery before and after use.
- PC6. Learn about basic safety consideration before operation of all tools and machineries.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. types of garden and yard waste (leaves, trimmings, branches).
- KU2. segregation techniques: biodegradable vs non-biodegradable.
- KU3. composting techniques for garden waste.
- KU4. benefits of organic waste reuse.
- KU5. local rules for garden waste disposal.
- KU6. safety and hygiene in waste handling.
- KU7. seasonal waste volumes and management planning.
- KU8. basic equipment for waste collection and shredding.
- KU9. reusable plant materials.
- KU10. environmental impact of improper disposal.

#### CUTM/VSQ/N7803: Maintain Health and Safety at Work Place.
Description
This NOS focuses on ensuring a safe and healthy working environment by promoting hazard identification,
safe equipment handling, proper use of PPE, and adherence to workplace safety protocols. It involves
assessing and mitigating risks, responding to emergencies, and minimizing environmental and workplace
hazards to protect self, co-workers, and surroundings.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The scope of this NOS includes performing regular safety checks, using safety gear, handling hazardous materials responsibly, following emergency procedures, and maintaining hygiene and
cleanliness in the workplace. It aims to cultivate a culture of safety and environmental responsibility
while reducing risk, injury, and workplace accidents.

protective clothing or equipment is used in performing these duties in accordance with workplace policy.
- PC3. Reading and understanding the hazards of use and contamination mentioned on the labels of
pesticides/fumigants etc
- PC4. Assessing the risks prior to performing manual handling jobs and work according to currently
recommended safe practice.
- PC5. Use equipment and materials safely and correctly and return the same to designated storage
when not in use
- PC6. Dispose of waste safely and correctly in a designated area
- PC7. Recognize risks to bystanders and take action to reduce risk associated with jobs in the
workplace
- PC8. Perform your work in a manner, which minimizes environmental damage all procedures and
work instructions for controlling risk are followed closely.
- PC9. Report any accidents, incidents or problems immediately to an appropriate person and take
necessary immediate action to reduce further danger.
- PC10. Follow procedures for dealing with accidents, fires and emergencies, including
communicating location and directions to emergency.
- PC11. Follow emergency procedures to company standard / workplace requirements
