#### CUTM/AGR/Q1102: Smart Farming Supervisor
Brief Job Description
The individual manages the crop in the farm using smart tools and latest technology. He or she is
responsible to understand field variability using IoT based sensors and UAV. Further, he should devise
appropriate strategies to manage the identified variability precisely, synchronizing to the crop demand.
Personal Attributes
A Smart Farming Supervisor should be innovative, tech-savvy, and adaptable, with strong problem-solving
and decision-making skills. They must have the ability to operate and monitor modern agricultural
technologies such as IoT devices, drones, sensors, and automated equipment. Analytical thinking and data
interpretation skills are essential to optimize crop yield, resource use, and sustainability. Leadership
qualities and team management skills are needed to guide farm workers in adopting precision farming
methods. Good communication and planning abilities help in coordinating with stakeholders, agri-tech
companies, and farmers. Being environmentally conscious, detail-oriented, and committed to digital
transformation in agriculture are key attributes.

#### CUTM/AGR/N1105: Preparation of Land Using Smart Tools and Equipment
Description
This NOS focuses on preparing farmland using advanced smart technologies to ensure precision and
efficiency. It covers the use of drones with multispectral cameras for field imaging, image processing for
soil variability analysis, and selection of suitable tillage equipment for optimum seedbed preparation.
Learners are trained in installing and calibrating soil moisture sensors, operating IoT panels, and setting up
micro-irrigation systems aligned with crop needs. The NOS emphasizes integrating smart tools for uniform
fertilizer application, data storage on cloud platforms, and adopting practices that minimize water
stagnation, thereby enhancing productivity and sustainability.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS focuses on modern land preparation techniques integrating smart farming technologies such as drones, multispectral cameras, IoT-based soil moisture sensors, and image processing
software. It covers assessment of field variability, selection of appropriate tillage methods, creation
of optimum seedbeds, installation of micro-irrigation systems, and precise basal fertilizer application. The scope emphasizes sustainable practices, water management, and data-driven
decisions to enhance soil health, productivity, and resource efficiency, enabling technical managers
to optimize farm operations using advanced digital and mechanized tools.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Preparation of land using Smart Tools and Equipment
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Fly the drone equipped with multi spectral camera and capture images.
- PC2. Collect the captured images and perform image stitching.
- PC3. Process those stitched images in an image processing software.
- PC4. Based on the results obtained from image processing determine the right tillage equipment
and number of tillages required to obtain optimum tilth.
- PC5. Fix the appropriate gradient in the fields to avoid water stagnation.
- PC6. Divide the site into grids based on its variability for installation of soil moisture sensors.
- PC7. Calibrate the soil moisture sensors and fix a threshold for scheduling irrigation based on
crop.
- PC8. Understand circuit of IoT panel to which soil moisture sensors were connected.
- PC9. Fix the micro-irrigation set up along the gradient based on the crop.
- PC10. Connect the output points in the IoT panel to the motor connected to micro-irrigation set up.
- PC11. Assure uniform application of recommended fertilizers as basal dose.
- PC12. Check the sensor data is stored in cloud.

#### CUTM/AGR/N1106: Knowledge  of Smart Seed and Sowing
Description
This NOS focuses on the application of smart technologies and scientific methods for seed selection, crop
planning, and precision sowing practices. It involves identifying suitable high-value crops, understanding
seed characteristics, and determining appropriate sowing depth and spacing. The role emphasizes the use
of modern tools such as seed drills, IoT-enabled devices, and drones for ensuring uniform sowing, better
germination, and optimized plant population. It also integrates knowledge of crop modeling, data-driven
decision-making, and sustainable practices to enhance productivity, reduce resource wastage, and
improve farm profitability.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the competencies required for selecting, testing, and sowing seeds using smart technologies to ensure precision and higher productivity. It includes the ability to install and use
decision-support software for crop modelling, identify suitable high-value crops, and procure quality
seeds from reliable sources. Learners are trained to test seed purity, assess germination percentage, measure 1000-grain weight, and determine optimum sowing spacing. The NOS also
emphasizes calibration of seed drills or robotic planters, and executing sowing at the correct soil
depth using mechanized and IoT-enabled tools. The scope ensures that the trainee develops both theoretical knowledge and practical expertise in smart seed selection and sowing techniques,
thereby enhancing efficiency, yield potential, and resource optimization in modern farming systems.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Knowledge of Smart Seed and Sowing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Install decision support software for crop modelling.
- PC2. Perform crop modeling and select suitable high value crop.
- PC3. Procure the seed material from a reliable source.
- PC4. Test the purity and germination percentage.
- PC5. Measure the 1000 grain weight using seed counter.
- PC6. Fix the optimum spacing for sowing.
- PC7. Calibrate the seed drill/ robotic planter.
- PC8. Sow the seed at optimum soil depth.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. how to install and operate DSS software for crop modelling to select suitable high-value crops based on soil, climate, and market needs.

#### CUTM/AGR/N1107: Understand Smart  Nutrient Management
Description
This NOS focuses on the scientific and technology-driven management of soil nutrients to optimize crop
productivity. It involves the use of nutrient-specific sensors and IoT-based systems to monitor soil fertility
in real-time. The learner is expected to generate and analyze soil fertility maps using GIS tools, calibrate
Variable Rate Technology (VRT), and devise nutrient application maps tailored to crop needs. The role also
includes integrating data into decision support systems to determine the right source, dose, and timing of
nutrient application, ensuring precision, sustainability, and cost-effectiveness. This NOS empowers the
trainee to adopt smart nutrient management practices that enhance soil health, reduce wastage of inputs,
and improve overall farm efficiency.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
It covers the operation of nutrient-specific sensors, integration with IoT platforms, and development
of soil fertility maps through GIS tools. Learners are trained to calibrate Variable Rate Technology
(VRT) equipment and prepare nutrient application maps for site-specific fertilizer use. The scope also
includes data collection for decision support systems (DSS), using DSS to determine the right source,
type, dose, and timing of nutrients, and ensuring efficient and sustainable nutrient management
practices.
By applying precision farming tools, this NOS ensures improved crop yield, soil health maintenance,
and reduced input wastage, aligning with environmentally sustainable and economically viable smart farming practices.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand Smart Nutrient Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Install nutrient specific sensors.
- PC2. Connect the sensor data to IoT.
- PC3. Develop soil fertility maps using GIS software.
- PC4. Calibrate the Variable Rate Technology (VRT).
- PC5. Devise nutrient application map and feed it to VRT.
- PC6. Collect the data to train the decision support system.
- PC7. Use Decision Support Systems to identify the right source and dose of application of nutrients.
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. macro and micro-nutrients, their role in plant growth, and crop-specific nutrient needs.

to measure soil nutrient levels accurately.

#### CUTM/AGR/N1108: Study of Smart Irrigation Water and Weed
Management
Description
This NOS focuses on the application of smart technologies for efficient water use and weed control in
modern farming systems. It involves the installation and calibration of IoT-based soil moisture sensors,
adoption of suitable micro-irrigation methods, and programming irrigation schedules using variable rate
technology to optimize water distribution as per crop needs. Learners are trained to differentiate weeds
from crops using image processing software, spectral analysis, and remote sensing tools, enabling
targeted weed management. The NOS also emphasizes the judicious use of herbicides at the right time
and dosage, ensuring sustainable crop production. Through this NOS, trainees gain competence in
precision irrigation, water conservation, and integrated weed control measures, thereby enhancing
productivity while reducing input costs and environmental impacts.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
It covers the installation and calibration of IoT-based soil moisture sensors, scheduling irrigation
through micro-irrigation and variable rate technologies, and integrating data-driven systems for
precise water use. Learners will also gain expertise in distinguishing weeds from crops using image
processing software, spectral wavelength differences, and remote sensing tools, followed by the
application of suitable herbicides at the correct stage. The scope extends to enhancing water-use
efficiency, reducing crop stress, minimizing weed competition, and optimizing input costs, thereby
ensuring sustainable agricultural practices. This NOS is critical in promoting resource conservation,
higher productivity, and environmentally safe weed management strategies, aligning with modern precision farming systems.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Study of Smart Irrigation Water and Weed Management
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Install IoT based soil moisture sensors across the farm.
- PC2. Adopt the right method of micro-irrigation.
- PC3. Code the IoT based sensors for threshold and duration of irrigation schedule.
- PC4. Calibrate the system before installation.
- PC5. Install the calibrated irrigation system in the field.
- PC6. Schedule the application using variable rate technology.
- PC7. Distinguish weed species from crop by their differences in wavelength.
- PC8. Use image processing soft wares to differentiate weeds from crop.
- PC9. Ascertain the type of weeds that will affect the crop.
- PC10. Use appropriate herbicides in the appropriate time.

| Sector | Sector is a conglomeration of different business operations having similar business and interests. It may also be defined as a distinct subset of the economy whose components share similar characteristics and interests. |

| Job role | Job role defines a unique set of functions that together form a unique employment opportunity in an organisation. |
| Occupational Standards (OS) | OS specify the standards of performance an individual must achieve when carrying out a function in the workplace, together with the Knowledge and Understanding (KU) they need to meet that standard consistently. | Description | Description gives a short summary of the unit content. This would be helpful to anyone searching on a database to verify that this is the appropriate OS they are looking for. |
| Scope | Scope is a set of statements specifying the range of variables that an individual may have to deal with in carrying out the function which have a critical impact on quality of performance required. |
