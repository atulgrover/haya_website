#### MES/Q0523: Graphic Design Technology - Indiaskills Worldskills
Brief Job Description
Graphic designer creates visual concepts, using computer software or by hand, to communicate ideas that
inspire, inform, and captivate consumers. They develop the overall layout and production design for
various applications such as advertisements, brochures, magazines, and corporate reports etc. with high
visual impact.
Personal Attributes
The individual in this role must be creative with high degree of professional responsibility and timeliness
with deadlines. He/she in this role has to be open-minded, willing to try new things and comfortable taking
advice from unexpected sources. Individual must not be colour blind at any stage.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. MES/N0557: Work Organization and Management - Worldskills

#### MES/N0557: Work Organization and Management   - Worldskills
Description
Work organization and management involve structuring tasks, resources, and processes to achieve goals
efficiently. It encompasses task allocation, workflow design, resource management, time management,
communication, leadership, quality control, and problem-solving to optimize productivity and maintain a
positive work environment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Work organization and management involve structuring tasks, resources, and processes to achieve
organizational goals efficiently. This includes task allocation, workflow design, resource management, time management, communication, leadership, quality control, and problem-solving.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria

company

Communication and interpersonal skills involve effectively conveying information, actively listening, and
fostering positive interactions with others. These skills encompass verbal and written communication,
nonverbal cues, empathy, active listening, conflict resolution, and relationship building, essential for
successful collaboration and building strong professional relationships.

Communication and interpersonal skills encompass the ability to convey information effectively,
listen actively, and interact positively with others. This includes verbal and written communication,
nonverbal cues, empathy, active listening, conflict resolution, and relationship building. These skills
are essential for successful collaboration, teamwork, and building strong professional relationships.

#### MES/N0560: Innovation, creativity, and design- Worldskills
Description
Innovation, creativity, and design entail the process of developing novel ideas, concepts, and solutions to
address challenges or fulfill needs in innovative and aesthetically appealing ways.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This domain encompasses the generation of original concepts, products, services, or processes through creative thinking, experimentation, and exploration of possibilities. It involves combining
existing knowledge, skills, and resources in new ways to achieve desired outcomes or create value.
Innovation, creativity, and design are applicable across various fields, including technology, arts,
business, and social sciences, fostering progress, differentiation, and competitiveness. These capabilities drive advancements, inspire change, and enhance user experiences, contributing to
organizational growth, societal development, and individual fulfillment.
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. Create, analyses and develop visual responses to communication problems
- PC2. Create (including photography), manipulate, and optimize images for both
- PC3. Analyze target markets and the products being delivered
- PC4. Create ideas that are appropriate to target markets
- PC5. Take into consideration the impact of each element that is added during
- PC6. Use all the required elements to create designs
- PC7. Respect existing corporate identity guidelines and style guides
- PC8. Maintain original design concepts and improve the visual appeal
- PC9. Transform ideas into appropriate and creative designs

Technical aspects and output involve understanding and managing the practical elements and outcomes
of a process, project, or task, often within a specific field or discipline.
Scope

The scope of technical aspects and output encompasses various factors related to the implementation, execution, and results of a task or project. It includes considerations such as
technical specifications, requirements, constraints, and deliverables. Technical aspects may involve
hardware, software, tools, techniques, methodologies, or standards relevant to the domain. Output
refers to the tangible or intangible results, products, or outcomes produced through the application
of technical knowledge and skills. This domain emphasizes the importance of precision, accuracy,
efficiency, and effectiveness in achieving desired objectives and meeting stakeholders' expectations.

#### Assessment Guidelines
1. Assessment System Overview:
• Batches assigned to the assessment agencies for conducting the assessment on SDMS/SIP or email
• Assessment agencies send the assessment confirmation to VTP/TC looping SSC
• Assessment agency deploys the ToA certified Assessor for executing the assessment
• SSC monitors the assessment process & records
2. Testing Environment:
• Confirm that the centre is available at the same address as mentioned on SDMS or SIP
• Check the duration of the training.
• Check the Assessment Start and End time to be as 10 a.m. and 5 p.m.
