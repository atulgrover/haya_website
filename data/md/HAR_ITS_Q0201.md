DGT/VSQ/N0102: Employability Skills (60 Hours)
Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Introduction to Employability Skills
Constitutional values - Citizenship
Becoming a Professional in the 21st Century
Basic English Skills
Career Development & Goal Setting
Communication Skills
Diversity & Inclusion
Financial and Legal Literacy
Essential Digital Skills
Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
Getting ready for Apprenticeship & Jobs
Elements and Performance Criteria
Introduction to Employability Skills
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify employability skills required for jobs in various industries
PC2. identify and explore learning and employability portals

citizenship, responsibility towards society etc. and personal values and ethics such as
honesty, integrity, caring and respecting others, etc.

critical and adaptive thinking, problem-solving, creative thinking, social and cultural
awareness, emotional awareness, learning to learn for continuous learning etc. in personal
and professional life

Description
Provide knowlege of HTML and CSS for web page creation
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Design and Manage web pages
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Understand and explain structure and application of HTML and CSS
PC2. Define and use various tags, elements and attributes.
PC3. Design web page using lists, table, links and multimedia contents.
PC4. Explain and use CSS elements to create attractive webpage.
PC5. Explain and demonstrate techniques for responsive web design

KU1. The requirement & objective of organization and scope of work.
KU2. Structure and syntax applicable as per the requirement of the work
KU3. Basic web layout and content organization for clear and easy navigation.
KU4. Importance of visual appearance and consistant design.
KU5. Importance of collecting feedback and regular updation for enhancement.
Generic Skills (GS)
User/individual on the job needs to know how to:
GS1. Produce work output as per the predefined format and in a timely manner.
GS2. Listen actively and communicate with other team members in simmple and effective terms.
GS3. Seek inputs from clients and other team members for clear and impressive design.
GS4. Prioritise work based on urgency.
