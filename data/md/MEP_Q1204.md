MEP/Q1204: Business Support Practitioner
Brief Job Description
Business Support Practitioners are professionals entitled to provide tax planning, management, statutory
filing and financial services. Providing financial services and filing of statutory compliances fall under some
of the major responsibilities of a Business Support Practitioner
Personal Attributes
The individual must be passionate about the work, should have good communication skills, need to be an
attentive listener, should be well-groomed, skillful in multi-tasking, and well disciplined. The individual
must be well versed with computer and digital skills, along with an optimistic approach towards the work.
They must also be flexible and creative in handling the responsibilities.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. MEP/N1214: Perform Financial Management support activities of Business
- 2. MEP/N1215: Managing Tax Deducted at Source and Tax Collected by the Seller
- 3. MEP/N9903: Apply health and safety practices at the workplace
- 4. DGT/VSQ/N0103: Employability Skills (90 Hours)
Electives(mandatory to select at least one):
Elective 1: Taxation and Import Compliance
The individual is responsible for maintaining records of accounts for the purpose of preparing periodic
reports around indirect tax of an organization on timely basis. This Individual must also keep abreast with
the various tax rules and is required to perform functions relating to filling of returns by the applicable due
dates.
- 1. MEP/N1216: Manage Indirect Tax
- 2. MEP/N1217: Ensure Compliance of GST
- 3. MEP/N1218: Online Application of Customs

MEP/N1214: Perform Financial Management support activities of Business
Description
This unit covers how to make a systematic planning strategy for Financial Management. It discusses the
collection and verification of source documents and provides guidelines to handle Books of accounts. The
#### unit also provides guidelines to maintain financial statement.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Collect and verify the sourced documents
Prepare Books of Accounts
Maintain Financial Statement
Follow material and energy conservation practices
Elements and Performance Criteria
Collect and verify the sourced documents
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify and verify sourced documents
PC2. prepare invoice with details of sales
PC3. maintain invoice for purchases
PC4. execute the acquired tax details
PC5. . record digital and physical financial documents
Prepare Books of Accounts
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. ensure proper book keeping
PC7. . accurate drawing of ledger accounts
PC8. maintain perfect trail balance
PC9. prepare bank reconciliation statement
Maintain Financial Statement
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC10. examine the documents for financial statement
PC11. identify the elements for financial statement
PC12. prepare accurate financial statement
Follow material and energy conservation practices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. identify processes where material utilization can be optimized
PC14. use energy efficient equipment
PC15. ensure lights and equipment are switched off when not in use

MEP/N1215: Managing Tax Deducted at Source and Tax Collected by the
Seller
Description
This unit covers the detailed competencies required to manage the provident fund account of employees
and the processes of deductions and submission of returns.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Examine the document for filing
Submit online returns to EPFO
Ensure Inclusivity
Elements and Performance Criteria
Manage Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Verify the Tax Deduction Account number (TAN) and other documents
PC2. Create and maintain an account in TRACE portal
PC3. Register the TAN and other details for filing TDS
PC4. Maintain record of tax deducted at source
PC5. Fill the requisite forms and submit TDS through e-portal
File Tax Collected by Seller Returns (TCS)
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. Deduct TCS from various sources as applicable
PC7. Provide TCS collected details
PC8. . Prepare Statutory Report
PC9. File the return of TCS
PC10. Maintain the applicable records and registers

PC12. . use non- gendered statements in communication, viz. phone calls, e-mails
PC13. ensure respect for cultural diversity and persons with disability

KU1. understand the Provident Fund Act, 1952

MEP/N9903: Apply health and safety practices at the workplace
Description
This unit deals in detail with application of health and safety practices in the workplace
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Apply relevant health and safety practices at the workplace
Maintain a healthy and hygienic environment
Deal with emergency situations
Follow fire safety requirements
Elements and Performance Criteria
Apply relevant health and safety practices at the workplace
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify, control and report health and safety issues relating to immediate work environment
according to procedures
PC2. follow procedures and instructions for dealing with hazards, within the scope of
responsibilities and competencies
PC3. document and report all hazards, accidents and near-miss incidents as per set process
PC4. document safety records according to organisational policies
Maintain a healthy and hygienic environment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. maintain the work area in a clean and tidy condition
PC6. ensure that the work area is sanitised as and when required
PC7. maintain personal hygiene
PC8. use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) where required
PC9. wash hands using soap and water or alcohol based sanitiser
PC10. report hygiene related concerns promptly to the relevant authority
Emergencies, rescue and first-aid procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. administer appropriate first aid to victims wherever required e.g. in case of bleeding, burns,
choking, electric shock, poisoning etc.
PC12. respond promptly and appropriately to an accident situation or medical emergency in real or
simulated environments
PC13. perform rescue activity during an accident if applicable (e.g. if moving victim is advisable)
Follow fire safety requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC14. follow fire safety practices
PC15. identify the type of fire and its stage
