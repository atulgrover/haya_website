#### PSS/Q7003: Engineer - Transmission
Brief Job Description
Engineer-Transmission is responsible for power transmission systems erection and commissioning,
operation & maintenance, testing & inspection of grid substation, power transformer, transmission
network, O/H Line, U/G cabling, GIS, SCADA, automation system, smart grid, metering etc. including HV
and EHV systems protection.
Personal Attributes
Engineer Transmission should be alert to observe minute details at the work place. The job holder should
be able to cope with difficult work environment (such as heat, noise, height etc.). He/She must also have
physical ability and stamina to stand for long working hours, besides mental ability to respond to
emergency situation.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PSS/N1336: Working effectively with others
2. PSS/N2001: Use basic health and safety practices for power related work
3. PSS/N7003: Carry out erection & commissioning of transmission system
4. PSS/N7004: Carry out operation & maintenance of power transmission system

#### PSS/N1336: Working  effectively with others

in their behavior and interactions with others at the workplace. These cover areas such as communication
etiquette,discipline, listening, handling conflict and grievances.

#### PSS/N2001: Use  basic health and safety practices for power related work
Description
This unit covers health, safety and security for power related work. This includes procedures and practices
that candidates need to follow to help maintain a healthy, safe and secure work environment in a power
plant, power station/substation or on the field while working on power equipment. It covers responsibilities
towards self, others, assets and the environment
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Health and safety
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. use protective clothing/equipment for specific tasks and work conditions
- PC2. state the name and location of people responsible for health and safety in the workplace
- PC3. state the names and location of documents that refer to health and safety in the workplace
- PC4. identify job-site hazardous work and state possible causes of risk or accident in the
workplace
- PC5. follow electrical safe working procedures such as tag out/lock out and display ptw (permit to
work),
- PC6. follow warning signs (danger, out of service, etc.) while working with electrical systems
- PC7. use standard safe working practices when working at heights, confined areas and trenches
- PC8. test any electrical equipment and system using insulated testing devices before touching
them
- PC9. ensure positive isolation of electrical equipment & system as per given standards
- PC10. recognize any abnormalities in electrical equipment or system installed alarm annunciation
and/or noticing parameters from gauge/ indicator installed
- PC11. carry out safe working practices while dealing with hazards to ensure the safety of self and
others
- PC12. state methods of accident prevention in the work environment of the job role
- PC13. state location of general health and safety equipment in the workplace
- PC14. inspect for faults, set up and safely use of scaffolds and elevated platforms and ladder
- PC15. lift, carry and transport heavy objects & tools safely using correct procedures from storage to
workplace and vice versa
- PC16. inspect grid station and its equipment routinely for any signs of oil and water leakage
- PC17. store flammable materials and machine lubricating oil safely and correctly
- PC18. check that the emission and pollution control devices are working properly in line with
environmental policy standards
- PC19. ensure proper working condition of battery and battery charger
- PC20. maintain electrolyte level of each cell with distilled water
- PC21. maintain proper ventilation in battery room
- PC22. apply good housekeeping practices at all times
- PC23. identify common hazard signs displayed in various areas

#### PSS/N7003: Carry out erection & commissioning of transmission system
Description
Engineer-Transmission is responsible for transmission systems erection andcommissioning. In addition of
the above material management, resource mobilization and co-ordination with seniors shall also be part of
the job.
Scope
This unit/task covers the following:
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Installation of power transmission network
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. descibe power transmission system including types of transmission system
- PC2. differentiate various types of transmission system voltage level wise/technology (hvdc, hvac)
- PC3. oversee the civil foundation work
- PC4. draw and plan schematic diagrams, engineering drawings, and single line designs, diagrams,
lay out plans etc.
- PC5. plan, monitor and control installation of power transmission systems
- PC6. review erection of ehv cable-oil filled and gas filled cable
- PC7. monitor ehv cable joining process
- PC8. manage installation of communication system such as plcc and opgw
- PC9. prepare estimates and bill of quantities
- PC10. handle the procurement function (modes and types- turnkey, supply & services etc.);
material planning and handling; store handling methods
- PC11. supervise out route survey for o/h line or u/g lines and row
- PC12. supervise gis mapping of transmission assets
- PC13. supervise erection and commissioning of protection system and earthing for transmission
network
- PC14. prepare mis report
Installation of grid sub-station
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC15. plan, monitor and control erection and commissioning of of air insulated substation and gas
insulated substation (gis)
- PC16. oversee erection of power transformer, switchgear, lighting arrester, ct, pt, cvt etc.
- PC17. perform activities related to bus bar and bus coupler
- PC18. oversee prepare transformer foundation
- PC19. oversee installation of substation automation system (sas)
- PC20. conduct type testing and regular testing of various equipment and relevant specifications for
conducting them
