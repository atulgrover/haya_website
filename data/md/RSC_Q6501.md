RSC/Q6501: Junior Assistant - Rubber Plantation
Brief Job Description
The Junior Assistant Rubber Plantation supports supervisors in carrying out land preparation, planting, and
maintenance activities in rubber plantations. The role involves assisting in soil management, irrigation,
weeding, and basic plant health care to ensure healthy growth and sustainable plantation practices.
Personal Attributes
The individual should be physically fit to work in outdoor plantation environments and willing to perform
manual tasks for long hours under varying weather conditions. They must be observant, disciplined, and
attentive to instructions, with patience and care in handling plants and tools. Basic teamwork,
communication, and time management skills are essential, along with a positive attitude toward
sustainable agricultural practices.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. RSC/N6502: Perform land preparation and planning activities
- 2. RSC/N6503: Carry out planting of rubber plants and its maintenance
- 3. RSC/N5629: Follow safety, environmental and hygiene practices
- 4. DGT/VSQ/N0101: Employability Skills (30 Hours)

RSC/N6502: Perform land preparation and planning activities
Description
This NOS covers the skills and knowledge required to prepare plantation land and plan rubber plantation
activities effectively. It includes clearing and leveling land, preparing planting pits, arranging planting
materials and tools, and ensuring proper layout and resource utilization while adhering to safety and
environmental standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Demonstrate and carry out land preparation activities
Identify and apply planning methods for plantation layout and resource arrangement
Elements and Performance Criteria
Demonstrate and carry out land preparation activities
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify and clear unwanted vegetation, debris, and obstacles from the plantation area
PC2. demonstrate leveling of soil and preparation of planting pits according to plantation guideline
PC3. apply methods to ensure proper drainage and soil aeration in the prepared land
PC4. carry out marking of boundaries and rows for planting
PC5. show correct handling of soil, tools, and implements during preparation activities
PC6. recognize soil conditions and report unsuitable areas to the supervisor
PC7. ensure adherence to safety procedures while performing land preparation tasks
PC8. record and maintain details of land preparation activities in logs or reports
Identify and apply planning methods for plantation layout and resource arrangement
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. develop a plantation layout plan following recommended spacing and row alignment
PC10. identify and arrange required planting materials (seedlings, budded plants) and tools
PC11. apply work instructions to allocate resources efficiently across the plantation area
PC12. conduct discussions with supervisors to clarify plantation planning requirements
PC13. demonstrate correct placement of planting materials in the prepared pits
PC14. ensure optimal utilization of land and resources as per the plantation plan
PC15. recognize potential issues in layout or resource allocation and report for corrective action
PC16. maintain records of planning and resource arrangement activities for reference
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. importance of land preparation in successful rubber plantation establishment

RSC/N6503: Carry out planting of rubber plants and its maintenance
Description
This NOS outlines the competencies required to maintain a rubber plantation by performing weeding,
irrigation, soil enrichment, and basic plant health care to ensure sustained growth and productivity.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform and demonstrate routine upkeep of plantation area
Apply and ensure soil and nutrient management practices
Identify and conduct basic plant health maintenance
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform and demonstrate routine upkeep of plantation area
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. clear unwanted weeds, grasses, and debris from the plantation area using appropriate tools
PC2. demonstrate proper mulching techniques to retain soil moisture
PC3. loosen soil around the plant base to enhance aeration and root growth
PC4. carry out irrigation activities as per schedule, ensuring optimum water supply
PC5. ensure plantation paths and surroundings are kept clean and safe
PC6. follow work instructions to execute upkeep tasks within given timelines
PC7. use tools and implements safely while performing upkeep operations
PC8. dispose of weeds and plant residues in an eco-friendly manner
Apply and ensure soil and nutrient management practices
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. demonstrate preparation of organic manure or compost for soil enrichment
PC10. apply manure/fertilizers around plant roots at recommended doses and intervals
PC11. ensure soil moisture is maintained through mulching, irrigation, or cover cropping
PC12. recognize soil erosion-prone areas and apply corrective measures
PC13. follow instructions for safe storage, handling, and application of fertilizers
PC14. maintain records of fertilizer/manure application as per instructions
PC15. display awareness of sustainable practices to minimize environmental impact
PC16. use protective gear while handling chemical fertilizers, if applicable
Identify and conduct basic plant health maintenance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC17. regularly observe plantation for signs of pest or disease infestation
PC18. identify common symptoms such as leaf spots, wilting, or discoloration
PC19. perform basic removal of infected leaves/branches under supervision
PC20. apply safe and prescribed plant protection methods (e.g., neem oil, fungicide spray)

RSC/N5629: Follow safety, environmental and hygiene practices
Description
This NOS involves adopting safety practices, using protective equipment, maintaining personal hygiene,
and following environmentally sustainable practices in the nursery.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Follow health and safety procedures
Maintain hygiene and cleanliness
Practice environmental sustainability
Elements and Performance Criteria
Follow health and safety procedures
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. wear basic protective equipment such as gloves and boots
PC2. use tools and materials safely under guidance
PC3. follow safety instructions while handling fertilizers and pesticides
PC4. store tools and agrochemicals in designated places
Maintain hygiene and cleanliness
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. wash hands and maintain personal hygiene before and after work
PC6. keep nursery pathways and surroundings clean
PC7. dispose of nursery waste safely
Practice environmental sustainability
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. avoid wastage of water during irrigation
PC9. follow eco-friendly practices such as use of organic manure
PC10. segregate biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. importance of personal safety and protective gear
KU2. hazards related to improper handling of tools and agrochemicals
KU3. hygiene practices to prevent infections and diseases
KU4. safe methods of disposing waste
KU5. importance of water conservation and eco-friendly practices

Description
This unit is about employability skills, Constitutional values, becoming a professional in the 21st Century,
digital, financial, and legal literacy, diversity and Inclusion, English and communication skills, customer
service, entrepreneurship, and apprenticeship, getting ready for jobs and career development.

Financial and Legal Literacy

Entrepreneurship
Customer Service
