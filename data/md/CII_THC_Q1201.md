CII/THC/Q1201: Fundamental course in Culinary Operations
Brief Job Description
The Fundamental course in Culinary Operations, aligned with EHL standards, develops foundational and
intermediary culinary skills with emphasis on nutrition, hygiene, stewarding, and occupational health. It
prepares professionals to manage goods, plan menus, lead kitchen teams, and apply innovative cooking
techniques while ensuring food quality, safety, and presentation, enhancing guest experiences and
contributing to the success of hospitality establishments.
Personal Attributes
For culinary operations, one needs attention to detail, creativity, teamwork, time management, and
resilience to perform efficiently in high-pressure environments. The job also requires the individual to be
calm, composed, and able to work with strong team management, administrative, communication, and
planning skills, ensuring quality and consistency.

CII/THC/N1201: Fundamentals of Culinary Theory and Practice
Description
This NOS defines the core competencies required of culinary professionals at entry level. It covers
knowledge, skills, and abilities essential for working effectively in a professional kitchen, including kitchen
organization, food hygiene, cooking techniques, mise en place, ingredient handling, knife skills, stocks and
sauces, safe equipment use, and basic plating and presentation.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
This NOS covers the core competencies in basic culinary operations, including kitchen hygiene and
safety, mise en place, ingredient handling, cooking methods, knife skills, and preparation of simple
dishes. It also emphasizes portion control, plating, and safe use of kitchen equipment to ensure
quality and consistency in food production.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Identify and describe different kitchen layouts, workstations, and tools used in professional
kitchens.
PC2. Explain the principles of food hygiene, kitchen safety, and sanitation practices.
PC3. Identify various cooking methods (e.g., boiling, steaming, frying) and describe their
appropriate uses.
PC4. Explain the process of mise en place and its importance in culinary preparation.
PC5. Describe the basic characteristics, storage, and uses of primary ingredients such as
vegetables, meats, dairy, and grains.
PC6. Differentiate between moist heat and dry heat cooking techniques with examples.
PC7. Demonstrate the process of knife handling, including common cuts like julienne, brunoise,
and chiffonade.
PC8. Recognize the function and types of stocks, sauces, and soups in classical cuisine.
PC9. Prepare simple dishes using basic culinary techniques following standard recipes.
PC10. Demonstrate basic skills in seasoning, tasting, and adjusting food preparation.
PC11. Explain the basic concepts of portion control and plating presentation.
PC12. Identify common culinary terms and measurements used in recipe standardization.
PC13. Follow SOPs for safe use and cleaning of equipment like ovens, mixers, and blenders.
PC14. Identify various thickening agents and their use in sauce or soup preparation.
PC15. Explain the role of fats, oils, and emulsifiers in basic food preparation.
PC16. Demonstrate preparation of basic doughs and batters for breads and pastries.
PC17. Use standard cooking temperatures and timings to prepare a variety of dishes.
PC18. Identify common kitchen hazards and describe appropriate preventive measures.
PC19. Perform basic plating techniques to enhance dish presentation and appeal.

The individual on the job needs to know and understand:

GS1. Communicate clearly and follow instructions.
GS2. Use simple numeracy for measurements and portioning.
GS3. Maintain personal hygiene, grooming, and workplace discipline.
GS4. Work effectively in a team and support others in the kitchen.
GS5. Manage time and complete tasks efficiently.

CII/THC/N1202: Nutrition, Hygiene and Stewarding Practices
Description
This NOS covers the knowledge and skills required for ensuring proper nutrition, safe food handling, and
maintaining high standards of hygiene and stewarding practices in food service and kitchen operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The individual will be able to plan nutritionally balanced meals, calculate nutritional values, and
adapt menus to dietary needs and restrictions. They will ensure food safety, hygiene, storage, and
preservation practices to maintain quality and prevent risks. The individual will also perform
stewarding operations such as cleaning, sanitation, waste management, and equipment upkeep in
line with eco friendly and sustainable kitchen practices.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Explain the basic principles of nutrition and the role of macronutrients and micronutrients in
the human body.
PC2. Identify food ingredients rich in proteins, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, and minerals used in
balanced diets.
PC3. Plan a nutritionally balanced menu for different age groups or dietary needs.
PC4. Calculate calorific and nutritional value of food ingredients and meals
PC5. Identify food allergies, intolerances, and dietary restrictions, and adapt meal plans
accordingly
PC6. Explain the effects of poor nutrition on health and productivity
PC7. Apply proper storage and preservation methods to retain the nutritional quality of food.
PC8. Describe safe food handling practices to avoid contamination and nutrient loss
PC9. Follow hygiene protocols including personal hygiene, kitchen hygiene, and food safety
measures.
PC10. Identify and manage risks related to pests, spoilage, and cross-contamination in food
environments
PC11. Carry out routine cleaning and sanitation activities for kitchen surfaces, tools, and utensils
PC12. Operate dishwashing and cleaning equipment as per manufacturer’s instructions.
PC13. Use cleaning agents and chemicals safely, understanding labeling and hazard signs.
PC14. Maintain waste disposal and recycling systems as per kitchen SOPs and environmental
guidelines.
PC15. Maintain logs and checklists for hygiene and sanitation tasks for audit readiness.
PC16. Monitor inventory and supplies for stewarding and cleaning operations.
PC17. Perform basic maintenance checks on stewarding equipment and report any malfunctions
PC18. Follow standard procedures for energy-efficient and eco-friendly kitchen operations.

CII/THC/N1203: Hospitality Operations, Inventory Handling and Health
Practices
Description
This NOS defines the skills and knowledge required for an individual to deliver professional guest services,
follow SOPs for reservations, billing, and event support, and maintain confidentiality and service standards.
It also covers managing inventory through stock registers, storage, documentation, and audits while
adhering to FIFO/LIFO methods, safety protocols, and hygiene practices. Additionally, the individual will
follow occupational health, food safety, and workplace safety guidelines to ensure smooth, safe, and
sustainable hospitality operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
The individual will be able to provide professional guest services, follow hospitality SOPs, and
coordinate with departments for smooth operations. They will manage inventory by maintaining
stock, storage, and documentation as per standards. The individual will also ensure workplace
health, safety, and hygiene to support efficient and sustainable hospitality operations.
Elements and Performance Criteria
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. Describe the structure and departments of a hospitality establishment (e.g., hotel, resort,
restaurant).
PC2. Identify roles and responsibilities of different departments in delivering guest services.
PC3. Demonstrate standard guest interaction and communication etiquette.
PC4. Follow SOPs for check-in, check-out, reservation handling, and complaint resolution.
PC5. Assist in handling basic hospitality software or documentation related to bookings or billing
PC6. Coordinate with various departments to ensure smooth operations and guest satisfaction.
PC7. Support event setups and execution as per instructions (e.g., banquets, conferences).
PC8. Apply service recovery techniques in responding to guest dissatisfaction or feedback.
PC9. Maintain confidentiality, grooming, and service standards while representing the
establishment.
PC10. Demonstrate awareness of tourism trends, sustainability practices, and local culture for
guest interactions.
PC11. Prepare and update stock registers for kitchen and service materials.
PC12. Receive, inspect, and store goods as per FIFO/LIFO inventory methods.
PC13. Handle documentation for ordering, issuing, and recording goods.
PC14. Monitor temperature, labeling, and expiration dates for perishable items.
PC15. Identify different types of storage areas (dry, cold, frozen) and maintain them as per SOP.
PC16. Follow safety and hygiene protocols during receiving and storing of inventory.
PC17. Report damaged, expired, or low-stock materials as per the escalation matrix.

measures.

Scope

team members to meet timelines.
PC10. Support basic inventory tasks like counting, recording, and reporting stock levels to
supervisors.
PC11. Apply basic numeracy to measure quantities, portion sizes, and conversion of units in recipe
preparation.
