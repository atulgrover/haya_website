RSC/Q3602: Assistant Operator - Tyre Balancing & Wheel Alignment
Brief Job Description
The Assistant Operator - Tyre Balancing & Wheel Alignment supports trained technicians in carrying out
tyre balancing, wheel alignment, and inspection operations. The person prepares tools and equipment,
assists in machine operation, inspects tyres, and ensures cleanliness and safety of equipment. The role
requires basic understanding of automotive tyre services and adherence to standard operating
procedures.
Personal Attributes
This job requires the individual to be energetic, focused and attentive. He should be able to work
independently under the guidance of supervisor. He should possess physical stamina and be comfortable
in performing Physical labour intensive work. Being an individual seeking perfection in performing his job,
he should be willing to learn the emerging advance methods in his work area.

RSC/N3604: Prepare for Tyre Balancing and Wheel Alignment Operations
Description
This NOS covers the preparatory activities involved in tyre balancing and wheel alignment services,
including identifying job requirements, selecting tools, and ensuring proper setup of equipment.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Identify work requirements
Prepare work area and tools
Set up balancing and alignment machines
Elements and Performance Criteria
Identify work requirements
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. obtain job sheet or instructions from the supervisor
PC2. check vehicle type and tyre specifications
PC3. inspect tyres visually for damages or irregular wear
PC4. record initial tyre condition and note any abnormalities
Prepare work area and tools
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC5. clean the work area and remove obstructions
PC6. collect tools and PPE as per standard checklist
PC7. verify availability of balancing weights and alignment sensors
PC8. check tyre inflator and pressure gauge for proper function
Set up balancing and alignment machines
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. inspect the balancing machine and alignment console for readiness
PC10. perform pre-operation checks on sensors, clamps, and rollers
PC11. ensure machine calibration or alignment software update if needed
PC12. tag faulty or malfunctioning equipment for maintenance
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. basic tyre construction and types (radial, bias, tubeless)
KU2. purpose and importance of tyre balancing and alignment
KU3. common terms: toe, camber, caster, runout, vibration

RSC/N3605: Assist in Tyre Balancing and Wheel Alignment Operations
Description
This NOS is about supporting technicians in the actual process of wheel alignment and tyre balancing by
operating tools under supervision, positioning the vehicle, and following step-by-step procedures.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Assist in wheel balancing
Assist in wheel alignment
Follow safety and SOPs
Elements and Performance Criteria
Assist in wheel balancing
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. position the tyre on the balancing machine
PC2. input tyre size and specifications into the machine console
PC3. operate the balancing machine under guidance
PC4. apply balancing weights at specified locations
PC5. recheck balance and adjust if necessary
PC6. clean wheels post-operation
Assist in wheel alignment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC7. mount the alignment sensors or cameras
PC8. align the sensors with target on each wheel
PC9. operate alignment console to read values
PC10. assist technician in adjusting camber, caster, toe
PC11. observe vehicle behavior during test drive
PC12. report unresolvable issues to supervisor
Follow safety and SOPs
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. follow safety precautions during machine operations
PC14. use PPE and secure the vehicle during lifting
PC15. follow step-by-step instructions during live jobs
PC16. report tool malfunction or hazard immediately

RSC/N3606: Inspect the Tyres and Maintain the Equipment
Description
This NOS involves inspecting tyre and wheel conditions and performing basic maintenance of tools and
equipment used for balancing and alignment operations.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Inspect tyres and report issues
Maintain tools and machines
Ensure workplace safety and documentation
Elements and Performance Criteria
Inspect tyres and report issues
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. examine tyres for cracks, bulges, cuts, or worn tread
PC2. use tread depth gauge to measure tread wear
PC3. check tyre inflation with pressure gauge
PC4. verify valve and bead area for leakage
PC5. document inspection results
Maintain tools and machines
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. clean balancing and alignment machines after use
PC7. lubricate moving parts as per schedule
PC8. perform minor checks (cables, bolts, rollers)
PC9. tag defective equipment and store appropriately
PC10. replenish consumables (weights, clamps)
Ensure workplace safety and documentation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. follow safe disposal practices for damaged tyres
PC12. update maintenance logbook
PC13. ensure availability of basic spares and cleaning material
PC14. report safety hazards to authorized personnel
Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. types of tyre damage and their symptoms

RSC/N5007: Follow health & safety protocol
Description
This unit is about maintaining health and safety of self and others at workplace.
Scope
