TEL/Q7202: Assembly Process Sr. Technician - Wafer Thinning & Lapping
Brief Job Description
The individual for this job role is tasked with operating laser machines to accurately etch identification
marks on semiconductor wafers. The individual is also responsible to set up laser parameters, ensuring the
precision of markings, and maintaining equipment. In addition, he also need to inspect marked wafers for
quality and adherence to specifications, documenting the process and outcomes.
Personal Attributes
The individual must demonstrate attention to detail, analytical and problem-solving abilities, and effective
communication skills, both written and verbal. The individual should be adept at working collaboratively
with others.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
- 1. TEL/N7205: Thinning and Lapping Processes for Telecom Applications
- 2. TEL/N7206: Operating and Maintaining Equipment for Telecom Wafer Processing
- 3. TEL/N7207: Quality Control and Safety in Telecom Semiconductor Manufacturing

TEL/N7205: Thinning and Lapping Processes for Telecom Applications
Description
This OS unit equips technicians with the skills to perform wafer thinning and lapping using appropriate
techniques, ensuring consistent quality throughout the process.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Pre-Process Preparation
Process Operation and Monitoring
Post-Process Inspection and Documentation
Elements and Performance Criteria
Pre-Process Preparation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. interpret device specifications to determine target wafer thickness and surface finish
PC2. select the appropriate thinning and lapping based on wafer material properties and device
needs
PC3. prepare the equipment for operation following established procedures (e.g., loading abrasive
materials, preparing slurry solution, calibrating instruments)
Process Operation and Monitoring
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. securely mount the wafer onto the processing platform of the equipment
PC5. configure and set up process parameters based on the technique and target specifications
PC6. operate the equipment, adhering to all safety protocols and Standard Operating Procedures
(SOPs)
PC7. monitor the process using appropriate gauges and instruments to track critical parameters
PC8. analyze collected data to identify any deviations from desired specifications
PC9. recognize and troubleshoot minor equipment malfunctions during the thinning and lapping
process, following established procedures
PC10. calibrate measurement instruments periodically to ensure accuracy of data collected during
process monitoring
Post-Process Inspection and Documentation
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC11. carry out minor adjustments to process parameters based on real-time monitoring to ensure
consistent wafer quality
PC12. perform a final inspection of the wafer surface to identify any defects using appropriate tools
and techniques
PC13. document the process parameters, results, and any observations in the designated logbook
or electronic system

Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
KU1. relationship between wafer thickness and functionality of semiconductor devices
KU2. different surface finish requirements for various device applications
KU3. operating principles of different thinning and lapping techniques
KU4. impact of abrasive materials, slurry composition, and polishing pressure on thinning rate
KU5. influence of polishing pad type and polishing time on surface finish
KU6. functions and capabilities of equipment used in wafer thinning and lapping processes
KU7. safe handling procedures for wafers and hazardous materials used in the processes
KU8. common defects arising during thinning and lapping and their causes
KU9. safe mounting procedures for wafers onto processing platforms
KU10. standard operating procedures (SOPs) for setting up and configuring process parameters
KU11. functions and operating principles of measurement instruments used for monitoring critical
parameters
KU12. potential safety hazards associated with wafer thinning and lapping processes
KU13. personal protective equipment (PPE) required for safe operation of equipment
KU14. techniques for interpreting data collected during process monitoring to identify trends and
deviations
KU15. impact of process parameter variations on wafer quality (e.g., thickness uniformity, surface
roughness)
KU16. principles of troubleshooting minor equipment malfunctions during the thinning and lapping
process
KU17. techniques for performing final inspections of wafer surfaces to identify defects (e.g., visual
inspection, microscopy)
KU18. importance of accurate and complete documentation of process parameters, results, and
observations
KU19. record-keeping procedures for process data and quality control findings
Generic Skills (GS)

GS5. plan and prioritize tasks to ensure timely completion

GS8. take quick decisions to deal with workplace emergencies/ accidents

TEL/N7206: Operating and Maintaining Equipment for Telecom Wafer
Processing
Description
This OS unit equips technicians with the skills to operate wafer thinning and lapping equipment safely,
perform routine maintenance, and troubleshoot basic operational issues.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Operate Thinning & Lapping Equipment Safely
Perform Routine Equipment Maintenance
Troubleshoot Basic Equipment Issues
Elements and Performance Criteria
Operate Thinning & Lapping Equipment Safely
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. perform pre-operational checks on the equipment to ensure proper setup and functionality
PC2. wear and utilize appropriate PPE (e.g., gloves, safety glasses, respirators) based on the
materials and processes involved
PC3. load and unload wafers onto the processing platform following safe handling procedures
PC4. start, operate, and monitor the equipment during the thinning and lapping process, ensuring
adherence to established parameters
PC5. identify potential hazards associated with the operation of thinning and lapping equipment
(e.g., moving parts, electrical hazards, chemical exposure)
Perform Routine Equipment Maintenance
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. follow established maintenance schedules to perform cleaning tasks on the equipment (e.g.,
cleaning polishing pads, filters, waste disposal chambers)
PC7. lubricate moving parts according to manufacturer's recommendations
PC8. replace consumable items like abrasives, slurries, and filters as needed
PC9. inspect equipment components for signs of wear and tear (e.g., worn-out polishing pads,
damaged belts)
PC10. report any identified equipment issues or potential maintenance needs to the designated
personnel using appropriate documentation procedures
PC11. maintain accurate logs or records of completed maintenance tasks and identified equipment
issues
Troubleshoot Basic Equipment Issues
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC12. identify common operational problems encountered during the thinning and lapping process
PC13. analyze collected data to diagnose potential causes of the problem

TEL/N7207: Quality Control and Safety in Telecom Semiconductor
Manufacturing
Description
This OS unit equips technicians with the skills to perform quality control inspections of thinned and lapped
wafers, analyze data, and ensure a safe work environment in the semiconductor assembly line.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Perform Quality Control Inspections
Analyze Quality Control Data and Report Issues
Maintain a Safe Work Environment
Elements and Performance Criteria
Perform Quality Control Inspections
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. review quality control specifications for thinned and lapped wafer parameters
PC2. select appropriate inspection tools and measuring instruments based on the procedure
PC3. prepare a clean and well-lit work area for conducting visual inspections
PC4. perform visual inspections of thinned and lapped wafers to identify potential defects
PC5. utilize magnification tools for detailed inspection as needed
PC6. configure and calibrate measuring instruments according to established procedures (if
applicable)
PC7. carefully measure relevant parameters of the thinned and lapped wafers
PC8. document identified defects and measured data using standardized formats
Analyze Quality Control Data and Report Issues
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC9. compare collected data (visual inspection observations and measurement results) to quality
control specifications
PC10. analyze trends in data to identify potential quality control issues
PC11. document any identified deviations from specifications along with the specific wafers
affected
PC12. report quality control issues and deviations to designated personnel for further investigation
and potential corrective actions
Maintain a Safe Work Environment
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC13. attend and participate in mandatory safety training programs
PC14. maintain a clean and organized work area to minimize hazards
PC15. properly handle and dispose of hazardous materials according to SDS and waste disposal
procedures
