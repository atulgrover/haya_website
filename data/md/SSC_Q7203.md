SSC/Q7203: IT Technical Support Executive
Brief Job Description
An IT Technical Support Executive resolves customer queries and technical issues across various channels,
such as voice, non-voice (chat, email), or hybrid formats. The role involves troubleshooting
hardware/software problems, providing remote support, documenting resolutions, and ensuring a seamless
customer experience. Adherence to organizational protocols, strong communication skills, and the use of
technology, including Generative AI, are critical for success.
Personal Attributes
This role requires the individual to work independently while interacting with customers to address their
concerns. The ideal candidate should be result-oriented, possess strong logical thinking and interpersonal
skills, and effectively prioritize their workload. A willingness to work in a desk-based environment and a
commitment to delivering exceptional service are essential for success in this position.

SSC/N7202: Assist and Support Customers with Basic IT and Service
Queries
Description
This unit focuses on equipping individuals with the skills and knowledge to handle basic customer queries
and provide support through effective communication. It emphasizes understanding customer needs,
offering first-level assistance, and ensuring excellent customer service.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Proactive Customer Engagement
Personalized Service Delivery
Multichannel Support Management
Knowledge Base and Self-Learning
Collaboration and Team Coordination
Accessibility and Inclusivity in Support
Root Cause Analysis and Long-Term Solutions
Emotional Intelligence and Conflict Management
Elements and Performance Criteria
Proactive Customer Engagement
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. identify common IT service issues, such as connectivity problems or login errors, based on
customer usage trends
PC2. guide customers on preventive measures, such as password management or avoiding
phishing emails, to reduce basic IT issues
Personalized Service Delivery
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC3. adapt explanations of IT services or solutions to match the customer's technical skill level
PC4. use customer-specific details, such as device type or service history, to provide accurate and
relevant troubleshooting steps
PC5. provide follow-up assistance to ensure issues like basic software installations or configuration
changes are resolved effectively
Collaboration and Team Coordination
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. share insights with the team about recurring customer issues, such as email syncing errors
or slow device performance
PC7. support colleagues by sharing solutions to common IT problems or assisting during high
volumes of customer queries
Accessibility and Inclusivity in Support
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:

SSC/N7301: Incident and Request Management through Voice Support
Description
This National Occupational Standard (NOS) specifies the skills, knowledge, and attributes required for
effectively managing and resolving IT service requests and incidents remotely through voice support. It
focuses on handling customer queries, troubleshooting basic issues, and ensuring prompt resolution or
escalation as per organizational protocols.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Acknowledge and Categorize Service Requests/Incidents
Understand and Diagnose the Issue
Troubleshoot and Provide Remote Support
Escalate and Monitor Incidents
Resolution and Documentation
Customer Interaction Management
Compliance with Tools and Technology
Feedback and Continuous Improvement
Use Gen AI for Technical Support- Voice
Elements and Performance Criteria
Acknowledge and Categorize Service Requests/Incidents
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC1. acknowledge receipt of customer service requests/incidents
PC2. empathize for any inconvenience caused and reassure customers of a commitment to
resolve the issue
PC3. record and categorize service requests/incidents accurately using the organization’s incident
management tool.
Understand and Diagnose the Issue
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC4. obtain sufficient information from customers to understand the nature of the problem
PC5. perform an initial diagnosis of the reported issue to identify possible solutions
Troubleshoot and Provide Remote Support
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC6. guide customers remotely to test potential solutions and resolve basic issues
PC7. provide customers with a justifiable resolution time when an immediate solution cannot be
found.
Escalate and Monitor Incidents
To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
PC8. prioritize service requests/incidents according to organizational guidelines

Description
This unit covers handling basic IT service requests at the helpdesk, ensuring cybersecurity, and using soft
skills for domestic users. It includes writing clear chats/emails, using remote desktop tools, following
escalation protocols, and leveraging Gen AI for task automation. Complex issues are escalated to experts.
