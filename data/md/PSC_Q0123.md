#### PSC/Q0123: Plumbing  and Heating (WorldSkills)
Brief Job Description
A candidate who certified under the qualification Plumbing and Heating (WorldSkills) is primarily
responsible for installation, repair and maintenance of plumbing and heating systems in residential,
commercial and industrial settings. The technician will play a crucial role in ensuring the proper functioning
of water supply, drainage, gas, heating, dry wall systems, pump operations etc. and contributing to the
overall comfort and safety of occupants.
Personal Attributes
The individual must possess technical proficiency, mechanical aptitude, problem solving skills,
thoroughness and precision in measurement and levelling. The person should be good at following
instructions and a team player with a positive attitude.
Applicable National Occupational Standards (NOS)
Compulsory NOS:
1. PSC/N0178: Workplace Management and Handling of Tools, Equipment and Materials
2. PSC/N0179: Analysis of Test Project
3. PSC/N0180: Conduct Operations on Pipes, Fittings and other sanitary fixtures for installation
4. PSC/N0181: Install Sanitary and CP fittings on Normal and Drywall System
5. PSC/N0182: Understand Marking and Assessment Criteria and Finishing and Handover the Job
6. PSC/N0136: Apply Health and Safety Practices at the Workplace

#### PSC/N0178: Workplace  Management   and Handling of Tools, Equipment
and Materials
Description
This NOS deals with systematic organization, coordination and optimization of resources, processes and
people within the work environment to achieve specific goals and objectives as per the WorldSkills
standards.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand the workspace design and infrastructure
Optimize workflow and process
Identify tools, equipment and calculate quantity of materials as per drawing
Evaluate and monitor the job assigned
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand the workspace design and infrastructure
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. manage workstation area as provided
- PC2. implement solutions to enhance productivity
- PC3. maintain tidiness of work station assigned
- PC4. place tools, equipment and materials in proper stack as per the module assigned for the
project
Optimize workflow and process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC5. streamline work processes for efficiency
- PC6. identify and eliminate discrepancies, if any, with proper approval from team management
and juries
- PC7. implement best practices for workflow management
- PC8. keep tracking of timings as per the module assigned and complete the same within
scheduled time
Identify tools, equipment and calculate quantity of materials as per drawing
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC9. review the drawing to identify specific tasks and operations
- PC10. break down the materials into categories such as water supply, drainage, gas heating, towel
rails, sanitary and CP fittings etc.
- PC11. utilize measurements and dimensions provided in the drawing to calculate material
quantities
- PC12. create a comprehensive list that includes the name, quantity, specifications and any other
relevant details for each tool, equipment and material

#### PSC/N0179: Analysis of Test Project
Description
This NOS deals with analysis of a plumbing drawing / test project which is a crucial step in understanding
the layout and specifications for plumbing systems in a building or construction project.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Understand Symbols and Labels
Identify Pipe Materials and Fittings
Identify Parts of Sanitary and CP Fittings to Fix on Wall
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Understand Symbols and Labels
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. interpret plumbing symbols used in the drawing
- PC2. identify different components such as pipes, fixtures, valves and other plumbing elements
- PC3. recognize labels indicating materials, sizes and specification
Identify Pipe Materials and Fittings
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. determine types of pipes (e.g. water supply, drainage, gas heating and towel rail)
- PC5. identify pipe sizes, materials as per the project assigned
- PC6. examine arrows or indicators showing the flow direction of water or wastewater
- PC7. identify connection points between pipes and fixtures
Identify Parts of Sanitary and CP Fittings to Fix on Wall
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC8. analyze the locations of plumbing fixtures (sinks, toilets, showers, etc.)
- PC9. understand the connections and pathways between fixtures and the main plumbing system
as per drawing
- PC10. differentiate between hot and cold water pipes and connect to fixtures (wherever needed)
along with valves and pumps as mentioned on drawing
#### Knowledge and Understanding (KU)
The individual on the job needs to know and understand:
- KU1. understand the specifications for different materials used in the plumbing system (e.g., copper, PVC, PEX etc.)
- KU2. interpret isometric views to visualize the three-dimensional layout of the plumbing system
- KU3. testing tools and technologies

#### PSC/N0180: Conduct  Operations on Pipes, Fittings and other sanitary
fixtures for installation
Description
This NOS deals with the installation of water supply pipes, drainage pipes, gas heating and towel rails
which involves a systematic and precise process to ensure the efficient and safe conveyance of water
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Selection of materials as per the drawing and standards
Preparation for piping process
Conduct operations on pipes
Install Pipes with its fittings
Install Valves and Pumps
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Selection of materials as per the drawing and standards
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. examine the drawings and specifications provided
- PC2. identify the functional requirements of the part or product
- PC3. identify tools and materials for different modules as per the test project
Preparation for piping process
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. determine the fitting requirements to install the pipes
- PC5. measure and mark dimensions for fabrication on the pipes and fittings making allowances for
spring-back, distortion and assembly
Conduct operations on pipes Install Pipes with its fittings
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC6. cut various types of pipes to the length determined with proper tools
- PC7. cut threads on GI pipe ends to the appropriate length as per dimension and method of
assessment
- PC8. bend and form the pipes to specified angle and offsets as mentioned in drawing
- PC9. prepare pipe ends using techniques such as reaming, leveling, filing and grinding
- PC10. join different types of pipes using fittings and relevant techniques
- PC11. ensure the assembled pipe meets the required specifications
- PC12. report any difficulties that may arise and carry out remedial actions
- PC13. identify the area and route where the assembled pipes are to be installed on board
- PC14. install water supply pipes, drainage pipes, gas heating pipes and towel rail pipes and other
water supply components according to given specifications and site requirements without damage or interference to the surrounding structures

#### PSC/N0181: Install Sanitary and CP fittings on Normal and Drywall System
Description
This NOS deals in detail with installation of plumbing fixtures in accordance with industry standards and
project requirements on both normal and dry wall system.
Scope
The scope covers the following :
Prepare for installation of fixtures
Install sanitary fixtures, support and related accessories
Install drywall system
#### Elements and Performance Criteria
Prepare for installation of fixtures
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC1. tally the count and quality of fixtures, parts, support material provided in the packing with
the manufacturer’s list or order form
- PC2. select size, type and quantity of fixture and trim for specific application according to given
specifications
- PC3. locate and mark the position of fixtures and fixture supports in structures
Install sanitary fixtures, support and related accessories
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC4. cut the physical structures such as wooden walls with correct markings and specifications for
performing pipe installations
- PC5. install supports for plumbing and heating piping using appropriate fasteners to ensure grade,
spacing and alignment
- PC6. install fixtures as per specifications without damage to fixture, pipework, the surrounding
environment etc
- PC7. Install and secure the sensor for touchless fittings and fixtures in the slot provided slot (in
case added in the test project only)
- PC8. plumb and level the supports and fixtures installed to ensure alignment and balance
- PC9. identify and rectify faults that are within limits of expertise and authority and escalate others
to authorized personnel
- PC10. clear the work area and dispose, reuse or recycle left over materials according to regulations
and codes of practice
Install drywall system
#### To be competent, the user/individual on the job must be able to:
- PC11. perform measurements for cutting of dry wall channels
- PC12. drive screws into drywall panels
- PC13. remove any excess drywall paneling
